Ezekiyeli 17

Fumbo ye zi'ndi no muzabibu

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya,
2 “Gwe go munhu! Wuwalonjelaje Waisilayeli i fumbo,
3 ho wamanye vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, mono nghuwalonjela vyono, baho katali, kwali na 'ndi mbaha, yono yali yina mazoya maswanu ku wulangwe, hamonga ne mabawa mawaha muno, gono gatandalaga hanhu hawaha.

Ale, 'ndi yiyo, yaguluka yikabita sunga mu Matunda ga Lebanoni, na kuko,

yikapanyula cisonje co mubici wo muhagata,

4 yikacisola cisonje cico, yikavidumula vinhambi vyayo,

vyono vyali kucanyha yayo yikavitegula sunga ko ku yisi ye wasuluza,

yikaviwika mwi'kaya lye wakwawi.

5 Hanyuma kanyhi,

yikayisola mbeyu ya mu mbeyu za mu yisi ya Isilayeli,

yakayihadika i hanhu hono

ho wuli muhangha wono wina moso mwinji,

i hanhu hono hali ho gali ne malenga menji

nhondo zose go kuyimwajilila ho yisume kuwiluka.

6 Nayo mbeyu yiyo yikacindika,

mubici wukawiluka viswanu,

wukawa muzabibu wono wutulile viswanu, kanyhi wutindigaye.”

Ne manhambi gayo gakapasicila, gakawiluka,

gakayipitucila 'ndi, ne milela yayo yikehimbila muno hasi.

No muzabibu wukawa kupasicila manhambi menji,

wukatula ne mahamha menji.

7 “Hakawa na 'ndi yinji kanyhi,

yono nayo yali yina mabawa mawaha muno,

ne mazoya menji muno.

Alu, u muzabibu wukayilanzya milela yayo kuli 'ndi yiyo,

hamonga ne mahamha gayo,

wukawa kwihwesa nha vyono,

wotuga malenga menji kuciza malenga gono gali

mu busitani mono wali muhadikwe.

8 Ninga hamba nhavivyo,

u muzabibu nha wali wakondya kuhadikwa

mu muhangha wono wina moso mwinji,

hono ho gali ne malenga menji,

ho wusume kuwiluka no kulela zabibu,

no kuwa muzabibu muswanu.

9 “Holeka nane no Muwaha Mulungu nghwiwuza nghutya,

alu mbeka u muzabibu wuwu,

wokwikala wuwiluce hongo?

Alo'ndi nongozi, si yoyikula milela yayo,

no kuyidumula mikalo yayo,

no kugabenanjiza manhambi gayo ho wunyale?

Hanyuma wuwe kuwula vilungo,

mu nghango yono si wosaka kanyhi

wanhu wenji wawukule milela yayo.

10 Heya, mbeka, muzabibu wakondya kuhadikwa,

ninga wule, wojendelela kuwiluka hongo?

Hamba si wosuma kunyazwa ne mbeho ya kucilima kolo yeza kuwuputa?

Hamba lunji kunyalila baho hono ho wuli,

hono wukuwilucila?”

Nhunizo ye fumbo yiyo

11 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya,
12 “Ale, ta, wawuzaje wawo i wabedegazi, hono wawe wayimanyile nhunizo ye fumbo yiyi. Wuwalonjele nha vyono, u mutemi wa Babiloni yakaza ku Yelusalemi, yakamusola mutemi, hamonga ne wetumami wakwe wose, yakawategula sunga ko ku yisi yakwe ye Babiloni.
13 Yakamusola na wo lulelo lwe citemi, yakawika cilagane nayo, yakamwilahiza yamuhulice mwene. Yakawasola ne wanhu wa mu yisi wono wena vilungo,
14 ho yalilece likungugo lisina vilungo. I cilagane cakwe ho cisume kukamiswa.
15 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, u mutemi we yisi ya Yuda, yakafyula, yakatuma wetumami ku Misili, ho wamupele fwalasi hamonga ne lipuga yiwaha lye wasilikale. Ninga wule, alu mbeka Yuda kosuma kupona? Aa ng'o! Si kosuma kubena cilagane, kanyhi ho yalece kulipicizwa.”

16 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Ninga mbeka, nha vyono ne nili Mulungu mumi, u mutemi yuyo kolofwila ku Babiloni, vyono yakondya, kuwubena wilahize wakwe, hamonga ne cilagane cakwe, vyono yavinoza no mutemi we yisi ya Babiloni, mono yo yamuwika mwi'goda lyo wutemi.
17 Hamba nhavivyo, i vilungo vye lipuga lye wasilikale wa Falawo u mutemi we Misili, si vyosuma kumutaza cinji mu wulugu, hambi u mutemi we yisi ya Babiloni, nha kozenga magagala, hamonga ne magomawakali, ho yawawulaje wanhu wenji muno.
18 Vyono mwene mutemi wa Yuda kabena cilagane hamonga no wilahize wakwe, na ku soko yo wituma zizi zose, holeka nayo hamba si kopona yaye.”

19 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, “Nha vyono 'ne, nili Mulungu mumi, nomulipiciza mutemi wa Yuda, ku soko yo wubena cilagane, hamonga no wilahize wakwe, wono yelahiziza mwi'tagwa lyangu, nha vyono kowukamisa.
20 Nolutandaza lwavu lo mupelembi ho nimwibatile mumo mu lwavu lulo. Nimutegule kuko ku Babiloni, nghamulipicizize kuko. Hambi nha yawa si mwihuwilwa kuli'ne.
21 Ne wasilikale wakwe wose i waswanu, wose wowulajilwa mu wulugu. Na wono wosigala, wose wokwiyagalila mbavu zose ze yisi. Nanye baho nhondo, ho molomanyila vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu mono nizilonjile zizi.”

Witumbilo wono Mulungu yakuwihuwiza

22 Azi zo yakuzilonga u Muwaha Mulungu yakutya,

“Nocisola cisonje co mubici wo muhagata.

Nicibene i cinhambi cayo cidece.

Nghacihadice mucanyha ye litunda litali muno.

23 Mwi'tunda litali muno,

lya mu yisi ya Isilayeli.

Naco copasicila manhambi,

no kulela mbeyu,

no kuwa mubici muhagata muswanu muno.

Ne zindeje ze mawala gose zilekala mumo,

zolowona wuceho mu mubeho wayo.

24 Ne mibici yose ya mu yisi,

yolomanya vyono,

ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu,

mono niyidumulangaga mibici mitali,

no kuyinoza mibici midodo yiwiluce yiwe mitali.

Niyinyazaga mibici yono yidedewaye.

No kuyinoza mibici mikavu, yidedewale.

Ane yo nene u Muwaha Mulungu,

mono nizilonjile zizi,

nane nozifisiliza zizo zose,

nozituma nha vyono nonjile.”
