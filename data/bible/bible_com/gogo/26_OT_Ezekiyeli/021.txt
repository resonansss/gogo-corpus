Ezekiyeli 21

U mumaje wo Muwaha Mulungu

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya,
2 “Gwe go munhu, wuyitacilaje Yelusalemi. Wuhatacilaje i hanhu hose hono wanhu wahembelaga. Wuyisugule yisi ya Isilayeli.
3 Hambi ane no Muwaha Mulungu, nha neza nili mulugu wenyu. Nowusola mumaje wangu, nimuwulajenyi nyenye wose, i waswanu ne wabi, wose hamonga.
4 Nowitumamila mumaje wangu niwawulaje wanhu wose, ku wulawilila ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, sunga ko ku lubavu lwa kutakama.
5 Ne wanhu wose womanya vyono yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, yo ninuye mumaje wangu, nane hamba si nowulecela lukulu.

6 “Gwe go munhu, alu weze kugula nha munhu mono nhumbula yakwe yitamile, yikusaka kubahuka so wusungu. Wugule ko wukalale, wukagulile i hanhu hazelu, hono wanhu wose wowa kukuwona.
7 Nawo kolo wakukuwuza wakutya, nhawule lu wukugula, nagwe wuwalonjele nha vyono, wukugula ku soko ye zinghani zono zikuza. Na kolo zinghani zizo zikuza, i zinhumbula zawo zolomema woga, ne miwoko yawo yolonyenyanjika, no wusugu wawo wose wolomalika, ne mafugamilo gawo golocicima. Ne lipango lilyo lyaza, lyakondya kufika.” Vyo yakutya Muwaha Mulungu.

8 Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya,
9 “Gwe go munhu, alu wuwile. Wuwalonjele wanhu zono ane no Muwaha Mulungu nghutya, Wuwatye nhavi; Mumaje wakondya kunolwa, kanyhi wakwemwa wakondya.

10 Wanolwa ho witume mulimo wo kuwulaga,

wakwemwa ho wumulice nhe muli.

Holowa hasina hamba nyemo,

hambi i wanhu wangu nha si waceleleye bwete,

ze suguzi hamba zo wulipicizwe.

11 No mumaje wukondya kunolwa

ho wuwikanizwe ku witumamilwe,

mumaje wunolwa no kukwemwa

ho wupegwe mu miwoko yo muwulaji.

12 Gwe go munhu, wulile wuzozize muno ko wusungu,

hambi u mumaje wuwu,

nha wuwikanizwe so wanhu wangu,

na so walongola wose wa Isilayeli.

Hambi wose nha wowulagwa,

hamonga ne wanhu wose wono wasigaye.

Ale, witowaje mu mhambaga yako ko wusungu.

13 Nane nghuwajela du wanhu wangu,

na hono waleme kunena,

ale, zinghani zizi zose zowaziza.

14 “Alu, 'gwe go munhu, wuwile. Wutowe maganza gako, nawo mumaje wowa kucinya kenji na kenji. Hambi mumaje wuwo, nha mumaje wono uwulagaga, kanyhi wo mumaje wo kogofya muno, no kuwulaga.
15 Wo mumaje wono wowanoza i wanhu wangu wawule vilungo no kwikobala. Nane nghugogofya i makaya gawo so mumaje wono wukung'alala nhe muli, wono wuwikanizwe ho wuwulaje.
16 Alu 'gwe go mumaje mukali, weze kutema kulume no kucekulu!

“Weze kutema ho hose du, mu nghango yo wupitucile wako akuno na akuno.
17 Nane nhavivilya, notowa maganza gangu, ne likuwo lyangu nhondo ho lyomalika. Vyo nghulonga nene no Muwaha Mulungu.”

U mumaje wo mutemi wa ku Babiloni

18 Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya,
19 “Gwe go munhu, alu wuwicile vitanjilo mu nzila zimbaha nyejete, zono u mutemi wa ku Babiloni kosuma kuzikola, akuno yadinghuye no mumaje wakwe. Ninga zinzila zizo zose, zeze zilawiliye mu yisi monga. Kanyhi wuwicile ne zimbawo ze vitanjilo ha nzila panda ye zinzila zizo.
20 Citanjilo cimonga comulajila mutemi i nzila yono yibitile sunga kwi'kaya lye Waamoli lya ku Laba. Ne citanjilo ciyagwe naco, comulajila i nzila yono yibitile ku yisi ya Yuda, kwi'kaya lya Yelusalemi lyono lizenjeywe magomawakali.
21 Hambi u mutemi wa Babiloni, nha yemile ha nzila panda, ho yayimanye nzila yono koyityatya, yakuzisinjisa zisonga zakwe mwi'pongolo, yakutowa cisangu, yakuviwuza vihwani vyakwe ho vimulonjele, yakulilanga litoga lye nimu yono yilavigwe so likumbiko.
22 Ale! Luno, yayibata songa mu muwoko wakwe wo kulume yono yandicigwe ‘Yelusalemi!’ Nayo songa yiyo yikumulonjela yabite yakawikanize vinhu vyo kugakumulila majinji, yazoze lizozo lyo wulugu, yakawice mu milango vinhu vyo kukumulila majinji, yawice matuta go muhangha, yahimbe ne makombo.
23 Ninga i wanhu wa mwi'kaya lya Yelusalemi, hamba si wozihuwilila lukulu zizi, ku soko yo wilumbanize no mutemi wa Babiloni wono wakondya kwilumbaniza nayo. Ninga i nghani yiyi yowa nha suguzi yono yowakumbuciza zo wubi wawo, kanyhi yowasugula nha vyono wolotekwa.
24 Na azi kanyhi zo nghulonga, ane no Muwaha Mulungu nghutya, U wubi wenyu wakondya kugubulwa calinze. Ne wanhu wose nha wakondya kumanya vyono muli wasutyi. Hambi nha mukuwulajila wubi wenyu mu metumo genyu gose. Holeka alu mweza muli wanhu wa kuling'anizwa du, nane luno nomutajilenyi mu miwoko ye walugu wenyu.

25 “Agwe go mulongola we wanhu wa Isilayeli, go mulongola u mwituma zimbi, lizuwa lyako, lizuwa lyo wulipicizwe wako we ndumilizo, likuza.
26 Nane no Muwaha Mulungu, mbeka nalonga du nakondya. Alu wuwuse citambala cako, wuvule no mukumbo. Hambi nha si zowa kanyhi nha vyono zali. Alu, wuwenulaje waciwa, watuje lilumbo! Wuwawusize lilumbo wose wono watemile!
27 Matongo nye, woyi hee matongo! Heya mbeka, nolinoza likaya liwe matongo. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, zizi zose si zokuza, sunga kolo yakondya kuza u mutemi mono nomuhagula, ho yaze yaliling'anize likaya. Hanyuma ho nimupele mwene.”

U mumaje, ne Waamoni

28 “Gwe go munhu, ale wuwile. Wuwalonjele zono ane no Muwaha Mulungu, nghuwalonjela wawo Waamoni, wono wakuwatovulanga i Waisilayeli. Wuwalonjelaje wutye.

‘U mumaje wawikanizwa ho wongomeze.

Wakondya kukwemwa ho wuwulaje,

wumulice nhe limuli.’

29 Wugubulilwe wono mukutya mukugubulilwa, wugubulilwe wuwo wa wulambi. Sunga no wuwizi wenyu wose wa wulambi du. Hambi anye nha muli wetuma zimbi du, ninga lizuwa lyenyu lyasejelela, lizuwa lyo wulipicizwe wenyu we ndumilizo. Hambi u mumaje nha wozidumulanga zisingo zenyu.

30 “Ale, wugomoleze mumaje mu njasulu yayo! Nane nomuling'anizizenyi i hanhu hono mwawumbilwa, mu yisi yono mwalelelwa.
31 Nagwe koliwona likuwo lyangu, lono lyeza kukuziza nhe malambi go moto. Nane nokuwika mu miwoko ye wanhu i wakali wono wali nzizila, wono wamanyile mulimo wo kongomeza.
32 Nagwe kokongomezezwa mu moto. Ne sakami yako, yomwajicila mu yisi yako. Na ku wulawa lulo, howa hasina kanyhi mono kolokukumbucilila lukulu.” Vyo yakutya u Muwaha Mulungu.
