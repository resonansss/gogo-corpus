Ezekiyeli 25

Uwuwililwe we wana wa Amoni

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya,
2 “Gwe go munhu, wuwalonjelaje wanhu wa Amoni.
3 Wuwalonjeje vyono wahulicize zono nghuwalonjela, ane no Muwaha Mulungu nghutya nhavi, Anye mwendezwaga kuyiwona Nyumba yangu nyavufywe, no kuyiwona yisi ya Isilayeli yakondya kunozwa yitongo, no kuwawona i wanhu wa Yuda, wakusolwa wakutegulwa ku wuhamywe.
4 Alu, vyono nanye mwali kuzisangalalila zizo, holeka nane nowaleta we makungugo, go lubavu lwa ku nyika ya kucilima, waze wamutemenyi. Nawo kolo wakuza, wosita zinghambi zawo mu muno mono yisi yenyu, wekale mumo. Wolya ne mikalo, wong'wa ne mele, i vinhu vyono vyali vinojeye viwe vyenyu, muvitumamile nyenye.
5 Nane nolinoza likaya lya Laba, liwe hanhu ha wuwiko we zingamiya, ne yisi yose ya Amoni, hawe hanhu ha wuwiko we zingholo. Nanye ho musume kumanya nha vyono yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.

6 “U Muwaha Mulungu kanyhi yakuzilonga azino yakutya, Anye mwatowaga maganza, hamonga no kuzumha-zumha so nyemo. Mukayibedegala yisi ya Isilayeli.
7 Holeka ku soko ya zizo, nane nomuwicenyi mu miwoko ya we makungugo ganji, waze wamuhizizenyi no kumusolenyi mateka. Nane nomongomezezenyi lukulu, mulece kuwa kanyhi likungugo hamba hadodo, hamba kuwa na yisi yono kanyhi yowa yenyu nyo weneco. Nanye baho ho momanyila nha vyono yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”

Uwuwililwe we yisi ya Mowabu

8 U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Vyono Mowabu yikutya yisi ya Yuda, yihwanile zinghungugo zinji zose.
9 Ale, nane nogaleka i makaya gono gayipilime no kuyidima Mowabu mu mipaka yayo, gose ho gahomwe, hamonga ne makaya gose matambule ga Beti Yeshimoti, Baali Meyoni, hamonga ne likaya lya Kiliyatayimu.
10 Na kanyhi, nogaleka i makungugo, gono gekaye ku nyika ya ku lubavu lwa kucilima, gaze gayisole yisi ya Mowabu, hamonga ne yisi ya Amoni. Ne likungugo lye Wamowabi, hodu hobaho liwe kuduma.
11 Nane noyilipiciza Mowabu, nawo ho womanyila vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”

Uwuwililwe we yisi ya Edomu

12 U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “I wanhu wa Edomu, vyono wawanozeza ko wukali i wanhu wa Yuda, no kuwalipiciza, ne nghani yiyo, likawa yisutya yiwaha kuli wene Waedomu.
13 Nane ku soko yiyo, holeka noyilipiciza Edomu, nowawulaga wanhu wose hamonga ne vilimu. Noyinoza Edomu yiwe nyhiwa, ku wulawilila kwi'kaya lya Temani, sunga ko kwi'kaya lya Dedani, ne wanhu wose wowulajilwa ku wulugu.
14 Nawo i wanhu wangu Waisilayeli, wolowalipiciza ko wukali Waedomu ku soko yangu, no kuwanoza Waedomu wawuwone wukali we likuwo lyangu. Nawo Waedomu baho ho wolomanyila u wukali wo wulipicize wangu vyono winha.” Vyo yakutya u Muwaha Mulungu.

Uwuwililwe we yisi ye Wafilisiti

15 U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, “Vyono i Wafilisiti, wetuma nghani yo wilipicize, kuli walugu wawo we vilo vinji vyono vyapuluta, wakawongomezeza mwi'kuwo lyawo.
16 Holeka nane lu, noyihoma yisi ye Wafilisiti, niwawulaje wanhu wose, halece kusigala hamba munhu monga. Nowongomeza wanhu wose wono wowa wakali womi mu yisi yo wuhihi ye Wafilisiti.
17 Nowalipiciza ko wulipicizwe wuwaha muno, nowatacila ko wukali muno. Nawo woliwona likuwo lyangu vyono linha. Nhondo ho womanya vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”
