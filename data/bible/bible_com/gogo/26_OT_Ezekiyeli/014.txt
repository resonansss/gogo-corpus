Ezekiyeli 14

U Mulungu yakuwuhosa wuhembi we vihwani

1 Wanji wa muli walongola we Waisilayeli, wakanyiziza wanonjele za kuli wuhile wo Muwaha Mulungu.
2 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya,
3 “Gwe go munhu, i wanhu wawa, wakondya kuvyenda vihwani ne mitima yawo yose, navyo vyo vikuwatonga ku wituma wubi. Alu wakwihwesa vyono ane nane ndahile kuwapitula hongo?
4 Alu nagwe wuwalonjelaje Waisilayeli wose wuwatye nhavi, ‘Ane no Muwaha Mulungu, yo nene mono nomupitula munhu wo wose, mono yakondizye kuvipitucila vihwani no mutima wakwe, no kutongwa navyo ku wituma wubi, kanyhi ho kawa kuza kujela kumuwuza muwizi. Nomupitula nha vyono yanojeye, mu nghango yo winji we vihwani vyakwe!’
5 Ninga vihwani vivyo, hamba viwe vyakondya kuwanoza Waisilayeli ho wandece 'ne, ninga nitumbiye nha vyono lipitulo lyangu lyowanoza Waisilayeli wawe ne cogopo kanyhi kuli'ne.

6 “Holeka aluno, wuwalonjele Waisilayeli zono nghuwalonjela ane no Muwaha Mulungu, nghutya, ‘Ale mbuyilenyi 'nye nyo wanhu wangu, mumhitucile nene, muwuce mu vihwani vyenyu vye mawifyo.’

7 “Ninga yunji wo wose wa muli Waisilayeli, hamba wa muli wajenzi, wono wekalane hamonga ne wanhu we mhuga ye Waisilayeli, mono kondeka 'ne, no kuhemba vihwani, hanyuma kanyhi ho kawa kubita kuwuza kuli muwizi, nene no mweneco yo nomupitula munhu yuyo.
8 Nolewana no munhu yuyo, nimunoze yawe co citanjilo, kanyhi nimutemanulile lukulu na mu mhuga ye wanhu wangu, nanye ho mumanye nha vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.

9 “No muwizi hono yakocwe-kocwe, no kulonga nghani yono si ye mbeka, ale mumanye nha vyono nene no Muwaha Mulungu, yo nimukocize u muwizi yuyo. Nane nomongomeza no kumutemanulila lukulu mu wanhu wa Isilayeli.
10 U muwizi yuyo, hamonga no munhu yuyo, mono yabitile kumuwuza muwizi, wose vyono wejete, wotuga wulipicizwe wono wiling'aniye.
11 Nane nokwituma zizi, i Waisilayeli ho walece kundeka'ne, no kwiyavufya so wubi wawo. Hambi nha yiwanojeye wawe wanhu wangu, nane niwe Mulungu wawo.” Yakutya Muwaha Mulungu.

Noha, Danyeli na Jobu

12 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya,
13 “Hono yisi yinozeze wubi, no kuwa si nyihuwilwa kuli'ne, ale, nane nowugolola muwoko wangu, niyiwusize witumbilo wayo we zindigwa, niyiletele nzala, niwawulaje wanhu, hamonga ne mitugo.
14 Hamba i wanhu wawa wadatu nha cinji Noha, Danyeli, na Jobu, wali wawe wakali wekaye baho, u wuswanu wawo si wali wutaze cinji co cose, langa wuswanu wawo wali wuwaponye wumi wawo weneco du. Vyo yakutya u Muwaha Mulungu.
15 Hamba lunji, nosuma kutuma malimu ga mu mbago, gawahawanje wanhu, no kuyinoza yisi yiwe ya kogofya, no kuwanoza wanhu walece kujenda-jenda ako na ako.
16 Hamba i wanhu wawo wadatu, Noha, Danyeli, na Jobu, wali wawe wakali wekaye mumo, mbeka mbeka, vyono mumipaga, ane no Muwaha Mulungu, nawo nhavivilya, si wali wasume kuwaponya hamba ne wana wawo weneco. Langa wali weponye wumi wawo weneco du, nayo yisi yali yikale wuciwa.

17 “Hamba lunji nosuma kuleta wulugu mu yisi yiyo, hamonga ne vitwalo vyono vyongomezaga, ho viwamale i wanhu wose, hamonga ne mitugo zose.
18 Hamba iwanhu wawo wadatu, Noha, Danyeli, na Jobu, wali wawe wakali wekaye mumo, mbeka mbeka, vyono mumipaga ane no Muwaha Mulungu, si wali wasume kuwaponya hamba i wana wawo, langa wali weponye wumi wawo weneco du.

19 “Na hono nali ndete wutamwa wa tawuni, mu yisi yose, niwawulajile wanhu ne mitugo mwi'kuwo lyangu.
20 Na hamba Noha, Danyeli na Jobu, wali wawe wali mumo, mbeka mbeka, vyono mumipaga ane no Muwaha Mulungu, ane no Mulungu mumi, si wali wasume kwi'ponya weneco hamonga ne wana wawo. Langa u wuswanu wawo, wali wuwaponye wumi wawo weneco du.”
21 Na zino, zo Muwaha Mulungu yakulonga yakutya, “Noloyiletela Yelusalemi ndipiciza zangu yine, zinzito muno. Yinongozi, wulugu, ye kejete, nzala, ye kadatu, malimu makali ga mu mbago, na ye kane, litamwa, vize viwongomeze wanhu hamonga ne mitugo yose.
22 No munhu wo wose mono kopona hamonga ne wanagwe, ale wuwalanjililaje lono wakuza kuli'gwe. Wuyiwone nghango yo wubi wawo vyono winha. Baho nagwe ho komanyila nha vyono kumbe u wulipicize wono nghuwuleta mu Yelusalemi, wo wulipicizwe we ling'ani.
23 Kanyhi ho komanyila nha vyono kumbe, hali ho yili soko mbaha muli zose zono netuma.” U Muwaha Mulungu yo yalonjile zizi.
