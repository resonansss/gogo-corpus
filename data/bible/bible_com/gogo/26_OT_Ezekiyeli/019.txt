Ezekiyeli 19

U lwimbo lo wukalale

1 U Muwaha Mulungu, yakanonjela yakatya nimbe lwimbo lulu lwe cililo, niwembile i wawaha wa Isilayeli.

2 U nyina yenyu simba yo lukumyo mu zisimba zinji.

Yazilisaga zi nyanagwe haciligati ye zisimba zinume.

3 Yikayilisa nyanagwe monga,

yikayijiza no wupelemba, yikalya wanhu.

4 Nago makungugo,

gakazihulika zizo zinghani zayo,

wakayigwiza mwi'kombo.

Wakayibata ne zindobogo, wakayikwega,

wakayitegula sunga ku yisi ya Misili.

5 Nayo nyina yayo, yaza wona yayilindila sunga

no witumbilo wakwe wajilila.

Yikasola mwana yunji kanyhi wa muli zi nyanagwe,

yikayinoza yikawa simba nghali muno.

6 Nayo yali yeza kuwa yawiluka,

yikawa kujenda ako na ako muciligati ye zisimba.

Nayo yikeyijiza kupelemba, yikalya wanhu.

7 Yikagakumulanga i magomawakali gawo,

yikaganoza makaya gawo gakawa matongo.

Sunga ne wanhu wa mu'si, wakamema no woga,

mu mapango gose lono yikukoloma.

8 Hobaho we makungugo,

wakekongholela hamonga wakulawa mu zi'si zose,

ho wayihome.

Wakalutandaza u lwavu lwawo lo wupelembi,

wakayibata mu mutego wawo.

9 Wakayitajila mwi'tundu wakayitegula

ko mutemi we yisi ya Babiloni.

Wakayiwika hasi yo wulindilizi we vilungo,

i ngholoma yayo, ho yilece kuhulikwa kanyhi

mu matunda ga mu yisi ya Isilayeli.

10 Kanyhi u nyina yako,

yali yehwanile no mubici wo muzabibu,

wono muhadikwe behi ne malenga.

Na vyono hali hogali malenga menji,

holeka muzabibu wali wina mahamha menji,

kanyhi wukalela mikalo minji muno.

11 Ne manhambi gayo gali gali vilungo,

gakawiluka, gakawa nghome ze citemi.

Nawo mubici wo muzabibu,

wukatalimha wukawa mutali kuciza i mibici yinji.

Ne wanhu wose wakawuwona wutali wayo,

na vyono wali wudedewaye muno.

12 Ninga so likuwo,

wukakulwa hamonga ne milela yayo,

wukagwiswa hasi.

Ne mbeho ya kucilima,

yikayinyaza mikalo yayo.

Ne manhambi gayo,

gakabenanjika gose, gakanyala,

gakapembelwa mu moto.

13 Nalu, wuhadikwa mu mbago,

mu yisi nghavu yono yisina malenga.

14 Ne lisina lyo muzabibu lyapya no moto.

No moto wuwo wagatakamiza manhambi gayo,

hamonga ne mikalo yayo.

Holeka i manhambi gago,

si golowa na vilungo kanyhi,

hamba si golowa nghome kanyhi ze citemi.

Alu lo lwimbo lwa cililo

lono lukuwa kwimbwa kenji na kenji.
