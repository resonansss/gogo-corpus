Ezekiyeli 24

Sufuliya yono yina nghangaga

1 Na mwi'zuwa lye likumi, lya mu mwezi we likumi, wa mu mwaka we licenda, wa mu wuhamywe wetu, u Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakutya,
2 “Gwe go munhu, alu wulyandikaje lizuwa lili, hambi lizuwa lili, nha lye 'zuwa lyono umutemi wa Babiloni, kolokuziza yayipilime Yelusalemi.
3 Holeka i wanhu wangu wono wakondya kumbedegala, nghusaka wuwalonjele fumbo yiyi, yono ane no Muwaha Mulungu nghuwatya,

Wutelece sufuliya mu moto,

wuyimemye malenga twaa!

4 Wuyitajile mugati yayo zimhonje ziswanu ze zinyama,

zinyama ze viwoko ne vima,

wutajilize hamonga ne zinyama ziswanu zono wuhaguye,

zono zihangahanjile ne mafupa.

5 Ninga witumamilaje zinyama ze ngholo nhelevu,

hanyuma, wuyifwanghanize ne zingwagu nyinji sufuliya yiyo.

Wugalece malenga gahemule,

gahemuse mafupa hamonga ne zinyama zayo.”

6 Azi kanyhi zo Muwaha Mulungu yakulonga yakutya, “Woyi hee! Gwe go likaya lyo wuwulaji! Hambi nha wehwana sufuliya yono yina nghangaga, yono si yosumika hamba ku wukwemwe yaye. Ale, wunimule mhonje monga monga ya nyama mugati yayo, sunga halece kusigala hamba mhonje monga yaye.
7 Hambi mwi'kaya, nha mwali mowuli wuwulaji, ne sakami ye wanhu, hamba si yamwajika mu muhangha yaye, hono hogali manghundi ho yifucilice, langa yamwajika mucanyha ye litalawe.
8 Nane nghawa kuyileka sakami yiyo mucanyha ye litalawe, ho yilece kwihundika, ho likuwo lyangu likolele no kulipiciza.”

9 Azi nazo kanyhi zo Muwaha Mulungu yakulonga yakutya, “Woyi, hee! Gwe, 'gwe 'kaya lyo wuwulaji! Ane nane nokonjeleza zingwagu ziwe nyinji.
10 Lete ngwagu nyinji! Wufwanghanize moto! Telece nyama! Hemuse muhuzi! Takamize mafupa!
11 Hanyuma wuyijelece mu moto yiyo sufuliya ye shaba, yono yisina cinji mugati yayo, wuyilece yipye sunga yidunghuwale. Nawo wavufwe wayo, hamonga ne nghangaga yayo, vyowa vyakondya kukongojela, nayo yowa yezwa kanyhi,
12 hamba nghangaga yose yilece kuwucila lukulu mu moto.
13 Gwe Yelusalemi, i metumo gako go wusenyha, gakondya kukwavufya. Nane hamba niwe najela kukweza, ninga hamba nhavivyo vyo wukali si nela yaye. Nane manyile nha vyono si kokwelela lukulu, sunga langa kolo wakondya kuwuwona wukali we likuwo lyangu.
14 Vyo nghutya ane no Muwaha Mulungu. Nha vyono cidaha caza, nane nokwituma. Nane hamba si nowunenela wubi wako, hamba kukuwonela lusungu, hamba si nokuleka yaye. Langa nokulipiciza du ku soko ye metumo gako gono wetuma.” Vyo yakutya u Muwaha Mulungu.

U wufwe wo mugolece wo muwizi Ezekiyeli

15 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya,
16 “Gwe go munhu, ane nokuwusiza lwa kamonga u munhu mono wumwendile muno. Ninga wulanjilile, wulece kulila, hamba kulavya mahozi yaye.
17 Hamba cililo cako cilece kuhulikwa na yunji. Kanyhi wulece kuciwopola ne cipombelo cako, hamonga ne malatu gako ho ciwe nha citanjilo co wufwilwe wako. Hamba wulece kwigubiciza ku wusu, hamba kulya zindigwa nha zono walizaga wanhu wono wali mu wusungu wo wufwilwe.”

18 Ale, nane nghalongana ne wanhu hakali mitondo, ne lizuwa lyali lyeza kuswa, cinyamihe, u mugolece wangu yakawa kufwa. Nane wali weza kwela mitondo yaco, nghawa kwituma nha vyono u Muwaha Mulungu yandajiliza nitume.
19 Ninga nawo i wanhu wakambuza wakutya, “Alu nhawule wukwituma nhavivi?”

20 Nane nghapitula nghawalonjela nghatya, “U Muwaha Mulungu kanonjela nha
21 vyono, nimulonjelenyi anye nyo Waisilayeli wose, nghani azi, vyono u mwene yakutya nhavi, Anye mwidunhumulilaga i vilungo vya mu Nyumba yangu. Mwendile kubita kuyilanga no kuyiwona, ninga u Muwaha Mulungu koyavufya. Sunga ne wazelelo wa mu zimhuga zenyu, wono wolowa wakali wasigaye mumo mu Yelusalemi, wolowulajilwa wose ku wulugu.
22 Ale, nanye baho ho mulanozeza zizi zono nghuzinoza 'ne. Hambi nha si mologubiciza wusu wenyu, hamba kulya zindigwa nha zono walizaga wanhu wono wali mu wusungu wo wufwilwe.
23 Hamba si molojenda musina cipombelo mu mitwe yenyu, hamba si molojenda musina malatu mu migulu yenyu, hamba si mololila, hamba kwikunguza yaye. Langa wose molonyeta ku soko yo wubi wenyu, kanyhi molowa kugula munhu na muyagwe.
24 Hambi ane Ezekiyeli nha yo nolowa citanjilo kuli'nye, nanye molokwituma zose nha vyono nene nozize. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nha vyono, lulo kolo zejela, nanye ho molomumanyila nha vyono u mwene mbeka yo Muwaha Mulungu.”

25 U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, “Alu agwe go munhu, ane nowawusiza lukulu yiyo Nyumba yangu, yono yali kuwapela nyemo no widunhumule, yono wali wendile kuyilanga no kuyiwona. Kanyhi nolowasola ne wana wawo we cilume na we cicekulu.
26 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo lono niletumila zizi, yuyo munhu mono kolo kuza yapone mu wongomele wuwu, u munhu yuyo yo kolokuziza, yaze yakulonjele nha vyono ziweye.
27 Nagwe mwi'zuwa lilyo, ho kologomolelelwa kanyhi i vilungo vyako vyo kulonga, vyono wali wakondya kuvyajiliza. Nagwe ho kololongana no munhu yuyo. Na mu nzila yiyi, nagwe mo kolowela citanjilo kuli wanhu wa Isilayeli. Nawo baho nhondo, ho wolomanyila vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”
