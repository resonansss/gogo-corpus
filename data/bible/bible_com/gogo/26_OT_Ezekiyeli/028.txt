Ezekiyeli 28

U wuwililwe wo mutemi we yisi ya Tulo

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakalongana kanyhi nane yakatya,
2 “Gwe go munhu, alu, wumulonjelaje u mutemi we yisi ya Tulo, zono ane no Muwaha Mulungu nghumulonjela nghutya nhavi,

Alu nha vyono wukwikumya,

no kwiwona mu nhumbula yako

nha vyono agwe wuli mulungu.

Nalu ti, vyono wukwiwona nha wuli mulungu,

holeka wikaye mwi'goda lyo wutemi,

mu ciligati ye lilenga.

Heya, kosuma nhawule kwihwesa wuli mulungu,

kumbe nagwe ho wuli munhu du,

hamba si wuli mulungu yaye.

3 Kanyhi agwe,

wukwihwesa nha vyono wina mahala menji si nha Danyeli,

na vyono hasina nghani yo yose ye cinyele,

yono yowa hundikwe hali'gwe.

4 Na kanyhi wukwihwesa nha vyono,

agwe wina mahala, na kanyhi wuli munghula,

holeka ti wuketujila sawo nyinji,

hamonga na zahabu ne fweza.

5 Wukwihwesa kanyhi nha vyono so mahala gako,

wanoza wusuluza no kwitujila cambuzi ciwaha muno.

Wedunhumula muno so wugoli wako!

6 “Alu, 'lece nane lu nghulonjele,

ane mono yo nene Muwaha Mulungu,

nghukutya nhavi, Alu vyono agwe wedunhumula,

no kwihwesa vyono nagwe weza wina mahala nho mulungu,

7 Nane lu nokutumila i wanhu wakali,

wono wasina lusungu, waze wakuhome.

Nawo kolo wakuza,

wovyongomezeza lukulu i vinhu vyako vyose i viswanu,

vyono wavitujila mu wunghula ne mahala gako.

8 Nawo wokuwulaga wakugumile mu cisolo ce lilenga.

9 Alu wule, kojendelela du kwiyitya agwe wuli mulungu,

sunga na baho lono weza kukuwulaga?

Ninga wumanye nha vyono, agwe,

kowa vyo wuli munhu du kuli mono yakukuwulaga,

hamba si kowa nha wuli mulungu yaye.

10 Kanyhi kolofwa nha wuli 'mbwa,

mu miwoko ye wanhu wajenzi,

wono wasina hamba mulungu yaye.

Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu mono nghuzilajiliza zizi.”

U wugwe wo mutemi we yisi ya Tulo

11 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya,
12 “Gwe go munhu, wumulilile ko wusungu u mutemi wa Tulo, so zinghani zono zomwagana. Wumulonjele nha vyono, ane no Muwaha Mulungu nghumutya nhavi,

Agwe wali wuli musambo wo mugoloka.

Kanyhi wali wumemile mahala,

hamonga no wusikame wo wuswanu!

13 Hambi nha wekalaga mu Adeni,

busitani yo Mulungu,

wavwalaga vibwe vye mawala gose vyo wuguzi wukamu,

ne yakuti, ne alimasi, ne belulo, na zumaladi,

na shohamu, na yashibu, ne yakuti-samawi,

na zumaladi ne bahalamani, ne zahabu.

Na vyose vivyo,

wakunozeza gwegwe mwi'zuwa lyono wawumbilwa.

14 Nghakuwicila malayika wo wogofye,

ho yakudime.

Hambi agwe nha wekalaga mwi'tunda lyangu lyono lyelile,

wukawa kujendela mu ciligati ye mabwe go moto.

15 Ne mikalile yako,

yali miswanu ku wulawilila mwi'zuwa lyono wawumbilwa,

sunga mwi'zuwa lyono wanziza kwituma wubi.

16 Nagwe, wali wuli mwipiganzi muno,

mu mulimo wo kugula no kuguza.

Ne nghani yiyi, yikakutegula ku wuhizi wo wituma wubi.

Na ku soko yiyo,

holeka nane nghakuswaswaniza wuwuce mwi'tunda lyangu lyono lyelile.

Nayo malayika mono yakudimaga,

yakakuwinjila kutali, ku wulawa mu mabwe go moto.

17 Agwe wedunhumulilaga wuswanu wako,

ne lilumbo lyako likakunoza witume nghani zo wulele.

Holeka ku soko ya zizo, nane nokugwisa ho hasi,

nghulece wuwe suguzi kuli watemi wanji.

18 Na ku soko yo wukwawi wako,

wukahatajila wavufywe i hanhu hako hono helile.

Na ku soko yiyo,

nane nghakupembela mu moto wukatakamilila lukulu.

Na wose wono wowa kukulanga,

wowona nha vyono weza mavu du.

19 Sunga ne makungugo gose wono wazaga ho wakuwone,

wakondya kogofywa na zono zikwijeleye,

nalu wakukuhoweleza wakutya,

Hambi lu, zili hayi, hamba si kolokwijela kanyhi cibitilila.”

U wuwililwe we likaya lya Sidoni

20 Muwaha Mulungu yakanyitya,

21 “Gwe go munhu, wulimanyizaje likaya lya Sidoni.

22 Wuwalonjele i wanhu wono wekalaga mwi'kaya lilyo,

zono ane no Muwaha Mulungu nghuwalonjela nghutya nhavi,

Ane no Muwaha Mulungu nili mulugu wenyu anye wanhu wa Sidoni,

ne wanhu wose wolonghumya nene,

so zono nomutendelenyi.

Nawo baho ho wolomanyila

nha vyono yo nene Muwaha Mulungu,

kolo nakondya kwilajila nha vyono ane nelile,

mu nzila yo kuwalipiciza

i wanhu wono wekaye muli'gwe Sidoni.

23 Hambi nha nolokuletela matamwa,

noloyinoza sakami yimwajice mu zinzila zako zose,

ne wanhu wako wolowulagwa.

Nagwe ho kolomanyila nha vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”

Yisi ya Isilayeli yolomoteswa

24 Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Hasina kanyhi nghungugo hamba monga, muli zono ziyipilime yisi ya Isilayeli, zono zayitendelaga Isilayeli vibi, yono kanyhi yosuma kuwa nhe mamiya ne mabijili, gono goyitamisa kanyhi Isilayeli. Nawo ho womanya vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”

25 Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Nane nowagomola kanyhi Waisilayeli, ku wulawa mu makungugo, mono nali niweyagazize. Ne makungugo gose ho womanya nha vyono ane nelile. Nawo i wanhu wa Isilayeli, wolokwikala mu yisi yawo weneco, mu yisi yono namupela u mwitumami wangu Yakobo.
26 Nawo wolowa kwikala mumo wapolile. Wolozizenga zikaya zawo, no kulima migunda ye mizabibu. Nane nolowalipiciza i wekalani wayawo wose, wono wawatendelaga vibi, ninga Waisilayeli wolowa wapolile du. Nawo nhondo baho ho wolomanyila vyono ane yo nene u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo.”
