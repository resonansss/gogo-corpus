Ezekiyeli 10

Ulukumyo lo Muwaha Mulungu, lukuwuka mu Nyumba yo Mulungu

1 Nane nali neza kulanga ha mwanya wono wali wuli hacanyha ye makelubi, nghawona cinhu cinji cono cejelaga cihwanile libwe lye liwala lyo wulanga cejelaga mucanyha yawo, kotya wijele we hwani ye 'goda lye nhojo.
2 Baho Mulungu yakamulonjela yuyulya munhu mono yali yavwalile myenda ye bafuta, yakamutya, “Ale winjile haciligati ye maguludumu, hasi ye makelubi, wugasole makala go moto, wuyimemye miwoko yako yose vyono myejete, hanyuma wugeyagaze mwi'kaya lyose.”

Nane nghawa nimulanjiliye munhu yuyo lono yabitaga.
3 Nayo lono yenjilaga, i makelubi gali gemile ku lubavu lwa kutakama lwe Nyumba yo Mulungu, nalyo livunde likamema mwi'kumbo lya mugati.
4 Ne nhojo yo Mulungu, yikelajila yikulawa mu makelubi, yikabita sunga ko kunze ye Nyumba yo Mulungu. Hanyuma i Nyumba yose yo Mulungu, yikamemywa ne livunde lilyo. Nalyo likumbo lyose lyang'alalaga so wuzelu we nhojo yo Mulungu.
5 Ne cihumbi co mulilo we mabawa ge makelubi, cikahulikwa sunga mo mwi'kumbo lya kudeha. Hambi cihumbi nha cali cihwanile lizi lyo Mulungu Mudaha Vyose.

6 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yali yeza kumulonjela yuyulya munhu, mono yali yavwalile myenda ye bafuta, nha vyono yabite yakeme haciligati ye maguludumu, gono gali gali hasi ye makelubi, u munhu yuyo yakabita yakenjila, yakema behi ne liguludumu linji.
7 Nayo yumonga wa muli wawalya makelubi, yakatajila muwoko wakwe mu moto, wono wali wuli baho behi nawo, yakasola makala ganji, yakamupela mu miwoko munhu mono yali yavwalile myenda ye bafuta, nayo yali yeza kugabocela, hobaho yakawa kutuluka, yakawa kubita.
8 Nane nghawona makelubi gose, gali gena cinhu cono cejelaga nha muwoko wa munhu, hasi ye mabawa gayo gose.
9 Kanyhi, nghawona hali ho gali maguludumu mane gose gehwanile, yikelubi ne maguludumu lyayo lili ha mhelo yayo, no wijele we maguludumu wihwanile liwala lye libwe lya Belulo.
10 Nago maguludumu gago, gejelaga gakung'alala nha mabwe go wuguzi wukamu. Ne maguludumu gose, gali gena liguludumu linji, lyono lyali lili mugati ye liguludumu liyagwe, liguludumu ho lisume kubiduka viswanu, no kutyatya kono linojeye libite.
11 Nawo makelubi lono wabitaga, wasumaga kubita ako na ako, mu zimbavu zose vyono yine, hamba kupituka-pituka ako na ako yaye. Nawo wajendanaga hamonga du ko kose kono wasakaga kubita, hamba kwileka yaye, akuno wakawa wasina soko yo kupituka-pituka yaye.
12 Nayo imiwili yawo, ne vyanza vyawo, ne miwoko yawo, ne mabawa, ne maguludumu, vyose vyali vimemile meso kose kose.
13 Ne maguludumu gaga, gali gehwanile ne maguludumu gono nagawona ha wugubulilwe wangu wulongozi.

14 Ne makelubi gose, gali gena wusu wuli hane hane, wusu wulongozi, wali wusu wa ng'ombe nume, we kejete, wusu wa munhu, we kadatu, wusu wa simba, na we kane, wusu wa 'ndi.
15 (Ne makelubi gaga, gali gehwanile na gono nagawona he kolongo lya Kebali.) Nago makelubi lono gagulukaga mucanyha,
16 hobaho nago maguludumu vyagulukanaga mucanyha hamonga ne makelubi. Sunga na lono makelubi gatandakulaga mabawa gayo, gawa kuguluka, hamba nhavivyo, ne maguludumu nago vyo vyali vili hamonga nago du.
17 Na lono makelubi galekaga kujenda, nhavivyo nago maguludumu, galekaga kujenda. Na lono makelubi gagulukaga, ne maguludumu gago nago vikabitana hamonga. Hambi imakelubi nha go gagalongozaga i maguludumu.

18 Nayo nhojo yo Muwaha Mulungu yikawa kuwuka ha mulango we Nyumba yo Mulungu, yikabita kwima hacanyha ye makelubi.
19 Ale, baho, nago makelubi gakawa kutandakula i mabawa gawo, gakawa kuguluka lulo baho lono nali nghali nigalanjile, vikawa kubitana ne maguludumu hamonga. Gakema hadodo ha mulango wa kucilima we Nyumba yo Mulungu, ne nhojo yo Muwaha Mulungu yali yili hacanyha yawo.
20 Nane nghawa kwizuka nha vyono kumbe wo wa walya makelubi wono nawawona hasi yo Mulungu we Waisilayeli he kolongo lya Kebali.
21 Ne makelubi gago gose, gali gena wusu wuli hane hane, ne mabawa mane mane, hamonga ne cinhu cinji cono cejelaga nha muwoko wa munhu, hasika ye mabawa gawo gose.
22 Sunga no wusu wawo, wejelaga wihwanile no wusu wuwulya du wono nawuwona he kolongo lya Kebali. Nawo makelubi wawo, wajendanaga wose hamonga walanjile ku mwande du.
