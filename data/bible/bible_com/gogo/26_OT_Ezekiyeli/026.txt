Ezekiyeli 26

Uwuwililwe we likaya lya Tulo

1 Na mwizuwa lilongozi lyo mwezi mulongozi, mu mwaka we likumi na monga, wa mu wuhamywe wetu, u Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya,
2 “Gwe go munhu, ayi yo nghani yono yikuleka wanhu wa mwi'kaya lya Tulo wakuyisekanga Yelusalemi wakutya, ‘Ahee, nhondo Yelusalemi yibenanjika! Ne vilungo vyayo vyo wusuluza alu nhondo vyamalika! Hamba si cokwisumaniza kanyhi nayo!’

3 “Alu, azi nane nene no Muwaha Mulungu, zo nghukulonjela agwe go likaya lya Tulo, nha vyono ane neza nili mulugu wako. Nowaleta makungugo menji kuli'gwe, waze wakuhome, nawo kolo wakuza, wolokuziza ne cihumbi, nhe mawingo ge lilenga.
4 Kolo wakuza, wologamwagulila lukulu i majinji ge likaya lya Tulo, wayigwise ne minala yayo yose. Nane nologafwagulila lukulu manghundi gose gawuce, niyinoze yiwe nhe'talawe lili bwete.
5 Nawo i wanimula zisomba mwi'lenga, wolonyaziza zinyavu zawo mucanyha yayo. Vyo nghutya ane nene Muwaha Mulungu. Ne makungugo woloyiteka Tulo.
6 Wawawulaje i wanhu wose wono wali mu makaya gayo mu yisi nghavu. Nayo Tulo, baho ho yolomanya vyono, ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”

7 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Nomuleta Nebukadineza u mutemi muwaha mono yacizize watemi wose, u mutemi wa ku Babiloni, yayihome Tulo. Nebukadineza kolokuza ne lipuga yiwaha muno lye wasilikale, ne zifwalasi, ne matuka go wulugu, hamonga ne wasilikale wapanda fwalasi, yakulawilila ku lubavu lwa kusumuka.
8 Ne wanhu wose wono wekaye mu makaya gono gali mu yisi nghavu, wolowulajilwa mu wulugu. I walugu wolohimba makweme, hamonga no kuzenga magomawakali, wakunozeze ne magagala, wokuzenjela lijinji lya ngula, wakupilime mbavu zose.
9 Wologamwagulanga majinji gako ne matinde, no kuyikumulanga minala yako ne vyuma vizito.
10 Ne manghundi gono golowa kutimulwa ne zifwalasi zawo zi nyinji, golokufuniciza. Ne misindo ye mazozo ge wapanda zifwalasi, hamonga ne magali go wulugu gono gakukwegwa ne zifwalasi, gologasinjisa majinji gako, kolo yeza kwinjila mu milago yako, nha vyono wanhu wenjilaga mu kaya yono yibenigwe hanji.
11 Nawo i wapanda zifwalasi wawo, wolowa kukola ne cihumbi mu zinzila, wakuwawulaga wanhu wako kwe mimaje yawo. Ne masumbili ge vilungo vyako, gose gologwiswa sunga ho hasi.
12 Ne walugu wako, wolowusola wugoli wako wagawane, hamonga ne vinhu vyako vyose vyo wusuluza. Nawo wologakumulanga majinji gako, no kuzimwagulanga zikaya zako zono zinojeze muno. Kanyhi, wologasola i mabwe gayo, ne zimbawo zayo, ne zikokoto zayo, wavigumile vyose mwi'lenga.
13 Nane nologadumya i mazi ge zinyimbo zako, hamonga ne mazi ge masumbi gako, hamba si golohulikwa kanyhi yaye.
14 Nololileka litalawe lili bwete, i hanhu hono wanimula 'somba wowa kwanika zinyavu zawo. Ne likaya, si lilazenjelwa lukulu kanyhi yaye. Vyo nghutya ane no Muwaha Mulungu.”

15 U Muwaha Mulungu, yakali yena zinji kanyhi zono yakulilonjela likaya lya Tulo, yakutya, “Nagwe kolo wasumwa, i wanhu wako wono wekalaga mu mwalambalo we lilenga, wolokogofywa muno ne cililo ce wayawo wono wakuwulagwa.
16 Ne watemi wose we zinghungugo zono zikaye mu mwejelele we lilenga, wolowuka mu magoda gawo go wutemi. Wolovula ne vivwalo vyawo vye citemitemi, wolokwikala hasi mu muhangha, akuno wakucicima muno. Hambi nha wolokogofywa muno, wakwizina ku soko ya zizo zono zikuweleye, kanyhi hamba si wolosuma kwiyimila ho walece kujendelela kucicima.
17 Nawo wolokwimbila u lwimbo lwe maziko gako, wawe kutya,

Ale, lanjenyi ta ze likaya lye lilumbo

vyono lyahodanjika!

Ne zingalawa zalyo zose zahamywa mwi'lenga.

Hambi i wanhu we likaya lili,

nha wali walitemile lilenga.

Wakawa cogofyo kuli wose

wono wekalaga mu mwalambalo we lilenga.

18 Nalu mwi'zuwa lyo wugwe wako,

vizingwa vikucicima sunga ne wanhu wose wono wekalaga mumo,

wakutinuswa muno so wuhodanjice wako.”

19 U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Nane nokunoza wuwe kaya yo wambuce, wuwe nhe makaya ge matongo, gono si wekalaga wanhu. Nghugubicize ne lilenga liwaha kusi ku cisolo.
20 Nghutegule, nghufise sunga ko ku yisi ye wafu, wukekalane ne wanhu wono wekalaga mono yinze baho katali. Kanyhi nolokunoza wikale ku yisi yono yili kusi muno, i hanhu hono hena wambuce cibitilila, wikale hamonga ne wafu. Nagwe nhondo ho si koloyiwuyila kanyhi nhojo yako, no kwikala kanyhi hanhu hako, mu yisi ye womi.
21 Nane nolokunoza wuwe cinhu ca kogofya, na wuwo, wo wolowa wo mudumo wako. Ne wanhu walakulondola muno, ninga hamba si wulejelela lukulu.” Vyo yakutya u Muwaha Mulungu.
