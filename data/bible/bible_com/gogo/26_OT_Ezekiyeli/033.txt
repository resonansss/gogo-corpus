Ezekiyeli 33

U Mulungu yakumuwika Ezekiyeli yawe mulindilizi

(Eze 3:16-21)

1 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya,
2 “Gwe go munhu, wuwalonjele wanhu wako wawe wamanyile, zono zolokwijela kolo nghuleta wulugu mu yisi. Holeka i wanhu we yisi yiyo, wasole munhu monga wa muli wene, wamuwice yawe mulindilizi wawo.
3 Kolo yakuwawona walugu wakuza, mwene yatowe ndulele, yawasuguze wanhu.
4 Na kolo munhu yakuyihulika ndulele, nayo ho yalece kuyicelelela, na hono walugu waze wamuwulaje, yowa nghani yakwe mweneco, hambi nha keyambuza mweneco.
5 Kafwa so masutya gakwe mweneco, vyono si yawuceleleye wusugulwe wono yasuguywe ne ndulele. Ku soko hono yali yawucelelele wusugulwe, hamba si yali yafwe yaye.
6 Ninga nayo u mulindilizi, hono yawawone walugu wakuza, nayo ho yalece kuyitowa ndulele, ne walugu waze wamuwulaje yumonga wa muli wanhu wawa, u munhu yuyo mono kowulagwa, ayo kowa yafwa no wubi wakwe. Langa nane nomuwamba yuyo mulindilizi, nimulipicize so lifwa lyo munhu yuyo.

7 “Holeka agwe go munhu, ane nghukunoza agwe wuwe mulindilizi we nghungugo ya Isilayeli, ku wulawa lulu. Holeka ku wulawa lulu, weze kuwasugula wanhu kuli zono nowa kukutuma wuwasugule.
8 Na hono nghulonjele nha vyono u munhu ayo kofwa hono yalece kupituka no kuwuleka wubi wakwe, nagwe wikale si wumusuguye yaye yapituce, nayo lu ho yafwile mu wubi wakwe. Ale nane nokuwamba gwegwe, nghulipicize ku soko ye lifwa lyo munhu yuyo.
9 Ninga langa hono wuwe kumusugula u munhu mubi, nayo hamba kuhulika yaye, ayo langa kofwila mu wubi wakwe, nagwe kowa wusina wuhoswe wo wose.”

U munhu na lwakwe

10 Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya, “Gwe go munhu, wuwalonjele Waisilayeli kali kenji du. Hambi wene nha wakulonga wakutya, ‘U mutegulo wo wubi wetu ne masutya getu, wacilemela, alu ho copona wule?’
11 Ninga alu agwe, wuwalonjele vyono ane no Muwaha Mulungu, mono nili Mulungu mumi, mbeka si nghuwa kwendezwa kuwona u munhu mubi yakufwila mu wubi wakwe. Langa nyendile yapituce yawulece wubi wakwe yekale mumi. Holeka anye nyo wanhu wa Isilayeli, alu mupituce, mulece kwituma u wubi wenyu wono mwitumaga. Alo nhawulenyi mukusaka kufwa anye nyo wanhu wa Isilayeli?

12 “Gwe go munhu, alu, wuwalonjele i Waisilayeli nha vyono, u munhu muswanu, hono yetume wubi, i ziswanu zono yali kwituma, hamba si zomuponya yaye. Nayo u munhu mubi, hono yalece kwituma zo wubi wakwe, ayo si kolipicizwa yaye. Na hono u munhu muswanu yanze kanyhi kwituma wubi, ayo hamba si kopona ng'o.
13 Ane nosuma kumwihuwiza wumi, u munhu muswanu, ninga nayo hono yanze kwihwesa nha vyono i ziswanu zono yetuma katali zo zomutaza, hanyuma ho yanze kwituma zo wubi, yamanye nha vyono ane si nozikumbucila yaye i ziswanu zakwe zono yali yetuma. Langa mwene kofwa du ku soko yo wubi wakwe.
14 Heya, nosuma kumulonjela munhu mubi, nha vyono, agwe kofwa. Ninga hono yanene wubi wakwe no kwituma zo mugoloka na ze ling'ani,
15 nha cinji, hono yagomole cinhu cono yapinjicilwa, kanyhi yamugomolele munhu sawo yakwe yono yakopa, hamba cono yahiza, na hono yalece kwituma kanyhi wubi, yatyatye u wulajilize wo wumi, u munhu yuyo si kofwa yaye, langa kowumipa.
16 Nane nomunenela u wubi wakwe wose wono yetumaga, nayo kokwikala mumi, vyono lu yakwituma zo mugoloka na ze ling'ani.

17 “Kanyhi i wanhu wako, watezaga nha vyono ati ane si nitumaga ze ling'ani aa, ng'o! Langa i wene, wo wetumaga zono si zililing'ani yaye.
18 Holeka u munhu we ling'ani, hono yalece kwituma ze ling'ani, ho yanze kwituma zo wubi, munhu yuyo kofwa so wubi wakwe.
19 Nayo u munhu mubi hono yalece kwituma wubi, no kwituma zo mugoloka na ze ling'ani, yuyo kowuponya wumi wakwe.
20 Ninga anye nyo Isilayeli, mukali vyo mukutya du, vyono zono ane nghwituma si zili ling'ani yaye. Ninga nane nomuling'anizizenyi mu metumo genyu.”

I zinghani zo wugwe we likaya lya Yelusalemi

21 Na mwi'zuwa lye'hano, lya mu mwezi we likumi, wa mu mwaka we likumi na nyejete, wa mu wuhamywe wetu, u munhu yunji mono yehemya yakulawa ku Yelusalemi, yakanyiziza yakanonjela yakatya, nha vyono likaya lya Yelusalemi lyahoma.
22 Ninga nane, mu mapango ge cinyamihe, lono u munhu yuyo mono yehema yali yakali si nanyiziza, ane nane, nali nakondya kuviwona i vilungo vyo Muwaha Mulungu muli 'ne. Na lono u munhu yuyo mono yehemya yanyizizaga i mitondo yaco hakali mitondo, u Muwaha Mulungu, yali yakondya kumhela i vilungo vyo kusuma kulonga.

U wubi we wanhu

23 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya nhavi,
24 “Gwe go munhu, i wanhu wono wekaye mu matongo ge makaya ga mu yisi ya Isilayeli, wakutya, ‘Abulahamu, yali yo munhu mono yali yali wene du mono yapegwa yisi yiyi yose. Alu ase nha cili wenji, holeka ne yisi yiyi yose yetu.’

25 “Ninga lu agwe wuwalonjele wutye, Anye nyo Isilayeli, mulizaga nyama hamonga ne sakami yayo. Kanyhi muhembaga vihwani. Kanyhi muli wawulaji wa wanhu. Alu nghanici, yono yoleka sunga mwihwese nha vyono, yisi yiyi yenyu nyenye?
26 Anye nha mwitumbilaga i mimaje yenyu du. Ne metumo genyu gose nha metumo ga cevu du. Nanye wose muli wasenyha. Alu cono coleka sunga mwihwese nha vyono yisi yiyi yenyu, cicici cene lo?

27 “Ninga wuwalonjelaje nha vyono, ane no Muwaha Mulungu, mono nili Mulungu mumi, nghuwasugula nha vyono mbeka, i wanhu wose wono wekaye mu matongo ge makaya gose, wose wowulagwa. Nawo wono wekaye mwi'hala, wololigwa ne zinimu za mu mbago. Nawo wono wehundice mu matunda na mu mapango, wolowulagwa ne matamwa.
28 Nane noloyileka yisi yose yina wambuce wuwaha muno, novidumya ne vilungo vyono wedunhumulilaga. Ne matunda ga mu yisi ya Isilayeli, golowa gena wambuce wuwaha, hamba munhu wo wose si kolowa kusuma kukola mumo.
29 Nane kolo nakondya kuwalipiciza i wanhu so wubi wawo, no kuyinoza yisi yiwe wambuce, baho nawo nhondo, ho wolomanyila nha vyono, ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”

U witumwe we zinghani zo muwizi

30 Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Gwe go munhu, i wanhu wako, wakuduwila zinghani zako, lono wetang'anila mu majinji ge likaya, hamba mu milango ye zikaya zawo. Wakulongana munhu na muyagwe wakutya, ‘Nye, alenyi cibite cikahulicize zinghani zono zilawile kuli Muwaha Mulungu.’
31 Nawo baho wawa kwikonghola wenji du ha mwande yako ho wahulice zono kolonga, ninga hamba si wakwituma zono wukuwalonjela wetume. Wakulonga zinghani mu milomo yawo du, ninga vyo wajendeleye du no wituma zimbi.
32 Na langa, ta! Agwe lu kuli wene weza kotya lwimbo luswanu muno, lwa munhu mono yena yizi lyono likuwendeza, mono yakusuma kutowa lisumbi viswanu, wakuzihulika zinghani zako, ninga hamba si wakuzituma yaye.
33 Ninga kolo zinghani zako zakondya kufisilizwa, na mbeka vyo zowa, ale nhondo, nawo baho ho wolomanya vyono kumbe mbeka u muwizi yali yali hamonga nawo muli wene.”
