Ezekiyeli 30

U Muwaha Mulungu koloyilipiciza Misili

1 U Muwaha Mulungu yakalonga kanyhi yakatya,
2 “Gwe go munhu, wuwawilile, wuwalonjele zono ane no Muwaha Mulungu nghulonga, wuwalonjele wuzozize wutye,

I lizuwa lye nghakamo likuza!

3 Lizuwa lyasejelela,

lizuwa lyono u Muwaha Mulungu kolokwitumila zizo zose.

Lizuwa lye mavunde,

no wugaye kuli makungugo.

4 Kolowa na wulugu mu yisi ya Misili,

hamonga no wugaye wuwaha mu yisi ya Kushi.

Na mumo mu Misili, wanhu wenji wolowulagwa,

sunga ne ma'si menji golotekwa no kulekwa matongo du.

5 “No wulugu wuwo, wolowawulaga ne wasilikale wose wono wazimwe ku wulawa Kushi na Lidiya, na Libiya, na Alabiya, na Kubu, sunga na muli wasilikale wa mu wanhu wangu.”
6 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “I wanhu wose wono vilunzize ne yisi ya Misili, wono wakulawa ku Migidodi, ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, hamonga na wono wakulawa ku Aswani, ku lubavu lwa kutakama, wawo wose wolowulajilwa ku wulugu. Ne lipuga lye wasilikale lyono yisi ya Misili yilitumbiye muno, nalyo lyokongomezwa lyose. Vyo nghutya ane no Muwaha Mulungu.
7 Ne yisi ya Misili, yolowa yo yisi yono yina wambuce wuwaha muno, si nhe ma'si gose ga mono'nze. Ne makaya, nago golokwikala matongo du.
8 Nane kolo nakondya kuyipembela moto yisi ya Misili, no kuwawulaga wose wono vilunzize nayo, baho nhondo, ho wolomanyila vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.

9 “Ne lizuwa lilyo kolo lyaza, ne yisi ya Misili yakondya kongomezwa, nane nolotuma wanhu wazize mu zingalawa, wawatinuse no kuwogofya i wanhu wa Kushi. Ne lizuwa lilyo mbeka likuza!”
10 U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Nane nolomutuma Nebukadineza u mutemi wa Babiloni, yaze yawuwusize lukulu u wugoli wose we yisi ya Misili.
11 U mwene kolo yakuza, kolokuza hamonga ne Wasilikale wakwe i wakali, wono wasina lusungu, waze wayihodanje yisi. Woloyihoma Misili ne mimaje yawo. Ne yisi yose yolomema mivimba yose.
12 Nololinyaza likolongo lya Nile, no kuyiwika yisi ya Misili, mu miwoko ye wanhu wabi. Ne wanhu wajenzi, woloyongomeza yisi yose. Vyo nghutya, ane no Muwaha Mulungu.”

13 U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Nolovihodanjila lukulu vihwani vyose, hamonga ne milungu yose yo wulambi yono yili mu Memufisi. Na holowa hasina hamba munhu monga, mono koloyitema kanyhi yisi ya Misili, hambi nha nolowogofya i wanhu wose.
14 Noloyihodanga yisi ya Misili ku lubavu lwa kutakama, nilipembele moto ne likaya lya Zowani, lyono lili ku lubavu lwa kusumuka. Nize nililing'anize ne likaya lya Tabesi, i likaya liwaha.
15 Nololinoza Pelusiyumu, i ligomawakali liwaha lye yisi ya Misili, liwuwone u wukali we likuwo lyangu. Nolowuhodanjilila lukulu u wugoli wose wa Tabesi.
16 Noloyipembela moto yisi ya Misili, na Pelusiyumu i ligomawakali liwaha, lyolowa mu wusungu wuwaha muno. Ne majinji ge likaya lya Tabesi, golomwaguka, gaze gagwe ho hasi. Ne likaya nalyo lyolosolwa ne lihwazi lye malenga.
17 Ne wazelelo wose wa mu makaya ga Oni na Pi-beseti, wolofwila mu wulugu, ne wanhu wanji wose, wolotekwa mateka.
18 Ne citi colodinda mu'si, kolo nakondya kuwanyenyanga vilungo i Wamisili, no kuwawusiza lukumyo lono wedunhumulilaga. Ne livunde lyoloyigubika yisi yose ya Misili, ne wanhu wayo wose wa mu makaya gose, wolosolwa mateka.
19 Nane kolo nakondya kuyilipiciza yisi ya Misili mu nzila yiyi, nawo ho womanya vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”

U mutemi wa Misili, yakuwusizwa vilungo vyo wutemi

20 Na mwi'zuwa lyo mupunghati, lya mu mwezi mulongozi, wa mu mwaka we likumi na monga, wa mu wuhamywe wetu, u Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya,
21 “Gwe go munhu, ane nawubena u muwoko wa Falawo u mutemi wa Misili. Kanyhi hasina hamba munhu monga mono yabita ho yakamuwope ne bandeji, hamba kumutajila miti yaye, ho wupone, wutuje vilungo vyo kuwibatila mumaje kanyhi.
22 Holeka luno, azi zo yakulonga u Muwaha Mulungu, yakutya, nhavi, Ane nili mulugu wa Falawo u mutemi wa Misili, holeka nomubena i miwoko yakwe yose vyono myejete, u muwoko muswanu, kanyhi na wuwulya wono wali wubenece, no mumaje wolagala mu muwoko wakwe wugwe hasi.
23 Nane noweyagaziza Wamisili mu yinze yose.
24 Hanyuma niyinoze miwoko yo mutemi we Babiloni yiwe ne vilungo, nimupele no mumaje wangu yebate mu miwoko yakwe. Ninga i miwoko ya Falawo u mutemi wa Misili, noyibena, nayo kokugula no kufwa ha mwande yo mulugu wakwe.
25 Na heya, mbeka, u mwene Falawo, nomunyetya, no kumwinula u mutemi wa Babiloni. Nane kolo namupela mumaje wangu, nayo kolo yakuwusonha ku lubavu lwa Misili, baho nhondo i wanhu wose, ho wolomanyila vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.
26 Noloweyagaza i Wamisili, weyagalile mu yinze yose. Nawo ho waze wamanye vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”
