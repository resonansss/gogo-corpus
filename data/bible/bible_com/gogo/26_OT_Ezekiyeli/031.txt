Ezekiyeli 31

Yisi ya Misili, yikwihwananizwa no mubici wo Muhagata

1 Na mwi'zuwa lilongozi, lya mu mwezi we kadatu, wa mu mwaka we likumi na monga, wa mu wuhamywe wetu, u Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya,
2 “Gwe go munhu, wumulonjelaje u mutemi wa Misili hamonga ne wanhu wakwe wose, wumutye nhavi,

Alo agwe wihwanile nani mu vilungo vyako?

Hamba nghwihwananize na cinhu cilo?

3 Agwe, nghuwona wihwanile no mubici

wo muhagata wa ku Lebanoni,

wono wina manhambi gono gena mubeho muswanu,

U mubici mutali muno wuficile ko ku mavunde.

4 Hakawa ho gali ne malenga go kuwuwilusa.

Na kusi yayo, vikolongo vyo kuwulisa.

Navyo vikawa vikumwajila malenga

i hanhu hono mubici wali wucindice.

No kutegula malenga mu mabici gose ga mu mbago.

5 Nawo vyono wamwajilwaga viswanu,

holeka wukatalimha muno sinhe mibici yose.

Ne manhambi gayo gakakangala no kutalimha muno.

6 Ne zindeje ze mawala gose,

zikazenga manhuli mucanyha ye manhambi gayo.

Zinimu za mu mbago,

nazo zikalelela wana wayo mu mubeho wayo.

Ne makungugo ga kono'nze,

nago gakaceha mu mubeho wayo.

7 No mubici weneco, wali muswanu nye!

Wali mutali, wina manhambi matali.

Ne milela yayo,

yikawa yinjiye kusi muno muno

mu muhangha yikulonda vikolongo vye malenga.

8 Hasina mubici wo muhagata hamba monga

mu mugunda wo Mulungu,

wono wosuma kwihwananizwa nawo.

Hamba hasina musonobali wo wose

wono wina manhambi gono gehwanile gago,

hamba mubici wose wulya.

Na hakawa hasina mubici muswanu muno,

wono wihwanile wuwo mu mugunda wo Mulungu.

9 Ne manhambi gayo gono gali gakopile muno muno,

gakawunoza mubici wukawa muswanu muno.

Sunga ne mibici yose yono yali yili mumo

mu mugunda wo Mulungu

yikawuwonela wivu.

10 “Alu ane no Muwaha Mulungu, nokulonjela zono zolowijelela u mubici wuwo wono wawiluka, wukafika ko ku mavunde. Vyono, mu nghango yono wacilaga kuwiluka, yo nghango kanyhi vyo wacilaga kwidunhumula so wutali wayo.
11 Holeka nane lu nawulema, nawuleka wusolwe no munhu muwaha wa muli we makungugo. Nayo kowulipiciza mubici wuwo mu nghango yo wubi wayo.
12 Ne wanhu wakali muno wa muli we makungugo, wono wasina lusungu, wolowudumula wawugwise hasi. Ne manhambi gayo, hamonga ne vinhambi vyayo vyologwila mu matunda gose, na mu malolo ga mu'si. Ne makungugo gose gono gekalaga mu mubeho wayo, golowuka.
13 Ne zindeje zolokuza zizenje manhuli gazo mu mubici wuwo wono wugwiye. Nazo zinimu za mu mbago, zologakanyanjila manhambi gayo.
14 Holeka ku wulawa lulu, sunga mu vilo vyono vikuza, hasina kanyhi mubici wo wose, hamba wumwajilwe viswanu muno malenga, wono wulawiluka wutalimhe kanyhi no kufika ko ku mavunde nha vyono wali u mubici wuwu. Hambi i mibici yose nha yofwa, nha vyono wanhu wafwizaga, no kwilunza na wono wakubita ku yisi ye wafu.”

15 Na zizi zo yakulonga u Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Na mwi'zuwa lono u mubici weza kubita ku yisi ye wafu, nane noganoza i malenga gono gali hasi mu muhangha, gawugubice, ciwe co citanjilo co kuwulilila. Nologemila makolongo, hamonga ne vikolongo, ho vilece kuhuluma kanyhi. Hambi u mubici nha wolowa wakondya kunyala. Nololeta citi ciwaha, mu matunda ga Lebanoni, niyinyaze mibici yose ya mu mbago.
16 Nane kolo neza kuwutegula ku yisi ye wafu, i lizi lyo wongomele wayo, wogacicimya makungugo. Ne mibici yose ya Adeni, i mibici miswanu, ne mibici mimwajile viswanu ya Lebanoni, yono nayo yakondya kubita ku yisi ye wafu, nayo yokwendezwa du no wongomele wayo.
17 Nayo vyabitana hamonga no mubici wuwo, sunga ko ku yisi ye wafu, vikekalane na wawo wono wakondya kuwulagwa. Na wose wono wali wekaye mu mubeho wayo haciligati ye makungugo.

18 “U mubici wuwo, yo Falawo u mutemi wa Misili, mono hamba ne mibici ya mu Adeni, si yatalimha yaye no kunojeza. Ninga aluno, nagwe kotagwa ku yisi ye wafu, hamonga ne mibici ya Adeni. Nagwe kokwilunza ne wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu, hamonga na wono wawulagwa ku wulugu. Vyo yakutya u Muwaha Mulungu.”
