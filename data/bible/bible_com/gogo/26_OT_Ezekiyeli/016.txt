Ezekiyeli 16

Wuwula wihuwilo, wa wekazi wa mwi'kaya lya Yelusalemi

1 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya,
2 “Gwe go munhu, wuyimanyizaje Yelusalemi mawifyo gayo gono yitumile.
3 Wuyilonjele Yelusalemi zono u Muwaha Mulungu yakuyilonjela yakutya, ‘Agwe Yelusalemi, u wimo wako no lulelo lwako, walawa mu yisi ya Kanani. U sogo yali Mwamoli, u nyina yako yali Muhiti.
4 Nagwe lono walelelwa, hali hasina munhu mono yakudumula lunyeku, hamba kukuhovuga, hamba kukutajila mwino, hamba kukuwopa ne cimwenda yaye.
5 Hali hasina munhu wo wose mono yakuwonela lusungu yaye, ho yakutendele hamba monga bwete ya muli zizo. Na lono walelelwa, hali hasina hamba monga mono yali yakwendile. Ninga watagwa kunze mwi'balangu du.’

6 “Nane nali neza kukola baho, nghakuwona agwe wukugalangala mu sakami yako. Wali wukwiliye sakami yose muwili wose. Ninga nane, hamba si nakuleka yaye ho wufwe.
7 Langa nghakunoza wuwiluce nho mubici muswanu. Nagwe wukawiluka wina ne vilungo, wukatalimha muno, wukawa muhinza muswanu muno. Ne vitombo vyako, vikawa vimile viswanu muno, ne zimvwili zako nazo zikawiluka viswanu, ninga hamba nhavivyo wali wuli cipwi du.

8 “Ninga nali neza kukola lwe kejete, nghawona nha vyono, alu cipango cakondya kufika, co kukwenda. Hobaho nghawugubika muwili wako ne likoti lyangu, nghakwihuwiza, nghanoza cilagane nagwe nha vyono nokutola, nagwe ku wulawilila baho, wukawa wuli wangu.” Azi zo Muwaha Mulungu yakutya.

9 “Hodu, hobaho nghasola malenga nghakuhovuga, nghayeza sakami yose mu muwili wako. Nghakubaka mafuta.
10 Nghakuvwika musweko muhambwe, nghakupela ne malatu gono mahone ne nyhingo swanu muno, hamonga ne citambala ce bafuta co kwiwopa ku mutwe, no mwenda we halili.
11 Nghakuhamba ne vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu, nghakuvwika bangili mu miwoko yako, no mukufu mu singo yako.
12 Nghakupela sunga ne nyamwidole yo kuvwala mu mhula yako, ne viheleni, hamonga no mukumbo muswanu wo kuvwala.
13 Nhavivi vyo wahambwa zahabu ne fweza, ne vivwalo vyako vye bafuta swanu, hamonga no wihambo. Wukalya wusaje wuswanu wono wunamuce, hamonga no wuci ne mafuta. Wukawa muswanu muno, sunga wukawa mugolece wo mutemi.
14 Nagwe wukawa ne lilumbo yiwaha muno muli zinghungugo zose, ku soko yo wuswanu wako. Hambi nha yo nene mono nakunoza ho wijele wuli muswanu muno.” Azi zo yakulonga u Muwaha Mulungu.

15 “Ninga lu nagwe wukwiyitumbila so wuswanu wako, sunga nhondo aluno, weza kusenyha no munhu wo wose mono kopuluta baho.
16 Wukawa kusola sunga ne myenda yako yinji, no kuyihamba mu hanhu hako hono kuhembelaga, nha vyono wenha i wacekulu wasenyha. Wukawa kwilavya kuli munhu wo wose du.
17 Wukawa kuyisola zahabu, hamonga no wihambo wono nakupela, wukayinoza vihwani vye cilume wulawa kusenyha navyo.
18 Wukasola myenda yo wihambo yono nakupela, wukawa kuvivwika vihwani. Wukavilavizya vihwani, i mafuta hamonga no wuvumba wono nakupela.
19 Ane nghakupela ndigwa yo wusaje wono wunamuce, hamonga ne mafuta, no wuci, ninga 'gwe, wukavilavizya vihwani, ho wutuje wendezwe kuli vyene.” Azi zo yakutya u Muwaha Mulungu.

20 “Hanyuma kanyhi, wukawasola i wanago we cilume, ne we cicekulu wono wandelela, no kuwalavya kuli vihwani. Wule, zizi si zikulajila u wubi wono wukwituma, nha vyono weza si wuli mwihuwilwa kuli'ne,
21 hamba wali wulece kuwasola wanangu no kuwalavya kuli vihwani?
22 Na mu mapango gako gose ge mikalile yako, ye cevu yo wusenyha, hamba si wayikumbucilila lukulu i mikalile yako ya ha wudodo, nha vyono wali wuli cipwi, wukwigalagaza mu sakami yako.”

I mikalile yo wusenyha ya Yelusalemi

23 Muwaha Mulungu yakukutya, “Woyi hee! Woyi hee! Gwe Yelusalemi! Hambi nha wetuma wubi wuwo wose,
24 no kuzenga hanhu ho kuhembela vihwani, i hanhu hose, mu zinzila zimbaha zose, hamonga no kwituma makani go wusenyha.
25 Wakondya lu kuwutipiza wuswanu wako mu madabaga. Ku soko yo kwilavya gwegwe mweneco kuli munhu wo wose mono yakukola baho, no wusenyha wako wukawa wucizize nhondo zose.
26 Kanyhi wukagona ne Wamisili, no wusenyha wako wukanghoza muno'ne likuwo.

27 “Alu nane nakondya kukugololela muwoko wangu nghuling'anize, ne ndigwa yako ye nhondo zose, niyicepula, kuli'gwe. Nagwe lu, nghondya kukutajila mu miwoko ya wono wakusulile i Wafilisiti, wono wakugawonela cevu i metumo gako go wusenyha.
28 Wukasenyha ne wanji wose, hamba kwiguta yaye, hanyuma wukabita kuwatyatya Waashuli, wukasenyha nawo, ninga nawo hamba si wakwigusya yaye.
29 Wukabita ku Wababiloni i wanhu wasuluza wawaha, wukasenyha nawo, ninga hamba kukwigusya ng'o.
30 Alu, azi zo yakulonga u Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, ‘Agwe Yelusalemi, wetuma zizi zose nho mucekulu musenyha mono yasina hamba cevu yaye.
31 Na mu zinzila zose zimbaha, agwe wuzenjile hanhu ha wuhembelo we vihwani, no kwituma zo wusenyha. Ninga nghani yo wizine, agwe si lunji wusina mapesa nha vyono wenha i wasenyha wanji, holeka wukwituma zizo, ng'o, wisimajiye ne wasenyha wanji.
32 Langa winha nho mucekulu mutolwa, mono kasenyhaga ne wajenzi, si nho kumwenda mulume wakwe du yali wene.
33 Kanyhi cimanyile nha vyono i wacekulu wasenyha, wo wakuwa kuhongwa ne walume, ninga agwe, yo gwegwe mono kuwahongaga i wendece wako wose we cilume, kuwa kuwakwejeleza ku wulawa hanhu hose, ho waze wagonane nagwe.
34 Na mbeka agwe, zinghani zako zisimajiye na ze wacekulu wasenyha wayago, agwe wuli wene du. Hambi nha hasina mulume mono kakutyatyaga ho yasenyhe nagwe, langa gwegwe yo kwitegulaga ku walume no kuwalipa, ninga agwe si wakulipaga cinji yaye. Holeka mbeka, wisimajiye muno.’ ”

Wuling'anizi wo Mulungu kuli Yelusalemi

35 Holeka 'gwe go Yelusalemi go malaya! Alu wuhulicize zono Muwaha Mulungu yakulonga yakutya,

36 Azi zo yakulonga Muwaha Mulungu, “Alu vyono kuyigubula myenda yako, kuwa kwikala cipwi so wusenyha wako, kuwa kwiyilavya go mweneco kuli wendece wako, na kuli vihwani vyako vyose vye cevu, na kanyhi kuwa kuwawulaga sunga ne wanago, kuwalavya makumbiko kuli vihwani.
37 Ku soko ya zizo zose, nane lu, nowakonghola hamonga i wendece wako wose, wono wuwendile, na wono wuwasulile. Nowaleta hali'gwe niwatandalice, wakupilime, hanyuma, nghugubule myenda yako, niwalece waciwone cipwi cako.
38 Nane nokuling'aniza so wusenyha wako, no wuwulaji wako. Nghuwulajile mu wulipicize wukali we likuwo lyangu.
39 Nghutajile mu miwoko yawo, nawo wahakumulanjile lukulu, i hanhu hako hose ha wuhembelo, hono kwitumamilaga so wusenyha wako, no kuhemba vihwani. Nawo wolokusolela myenda yako yose, hamonga ne vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu, waze wakulece baho wuli cipwi.

40 “Wolokuletela mapuga ge wanhu, wakutowanje ne mabwe. Wolokutemasya nhugu nhugu ne mimaje yawo.
41 Wolozipembela zikaya zako, no kugaleka mapuga ge wacekulu, wawuwone wulipicizwe wako. Nane nolokupeza no wusenyha wako, hamonga no wuhonga i walume i wendece wako.
42 Nane baho likuwo lyangu ho lilamalicila, niwe kusenjela. Nane si nolokolwa kanyhi ne likuwo, nhondo no wivu wangu ho wokuwucila.
43 Ane nghuwona, 'mba, wasemwa zono nakutendela ha wudodo wako, holeka alu weza kwituma zinghani, zono zikunghoza'ne likuwo. Na yiyi yo yikaleka wutuje wulipicizwe ku soko ya zizo. Alu nhawule 'gwe, wukawa wukwihanza-hanza mu wusenyha wako, ne vinhu vye liwifyo?” Azi zo yakutya u Muwaha Mulungu.

Nha vyono yenha nyina yo munhu, nayo mwanagwe we cicekulu vyo kowa yenha.

44 Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “I wanhu, wosuma kuyitumamila i mbazi yiyi kuli'gwe Yelusalemi, yono yikutya, ‘Nha vyono yenha nyina yo munhu, ale, nayo mwanagwe we cicekulu vyo kowa yenha.’
45 Agwe mbeka wuli mwana we cicekulu wo nyina yako, mono yamusula u mulume wakwe hamonga ne wanagwe. Wihwanile i wanduguzo we cicekulu, wono nawo wawasula i walume zawo hamonga ne wana wawo. Nagwe Yelusalemi hamonga ne makaya mayago, u nyina yenyu yali Muhiti, no sogo yenyu yali Mwamoli.

46 “U lumbulyo muwaha, yo Samaliya, ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, hamonga ne vitumbi vyayo. Nayo u lumbulyo mudodo, yo Sodoma, hamonga ne vitumbi vyayo, ku lubavu lwa kutakama.
47 Ninga wule, wazityatya zinzila zawo hamba wetuma mu nghango ye mawifyo gawo? Aa, ng'o! Agwe si wetuma nhavivyo, langa nagwe wukahodanjika hadodo du, kuciza wene vyono wali wenha muli zose zono wetuma.
48 Ninga mbeka, nha vyono ninha ane no Mulungu mumi, u Muwaha Mulungu, nghutya nhavi, ‘U lumbulyo Sodoma hamonga ne vitumbi vyakwe vyose, si wetuma zimbi zono zinha zizi, zono agwe Yelusalemi wetuma, hamonga ne vitumbi vyako.
49 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, nayo Sodoma, hamonga ne wanagwe we cicekulu, wakedunhumula muno ku soko ye zindigwa nyinji zono wali wena, hamonga no wikale wawo wo mulamu no wucehe. Ninga si wawalanjilila i waciwa na wono wali wena wanjile.
50 Langa wene, wakawa wali mu widunhumuzi du, hamonga no wuwula cihuliko, no kujendelela kwituma zono 'ne nizisulile. Na ku soko yiyo, holeka nane nghawongomeza, nha vyono wumanyile nagwe.’

51 “Hamba Samaliya, si yetuma wubi wono wuficile nusu yo wubi wono agwe wetuma. Agwe Yelusalemi, wetuma makani ge mawifyo muno muno, si nha vyono Samaliya yetuma ku wulawa katali. Ne mahodango gako, gakawanoza wanduguzo we cicekulu, wejele wali ling'ani so mawipizo gako gono wetuma.
52 Ninga nalu, yikunojeye wucikangalile cevu cako. Hambi u wubi wako nha we wifyo muno, wucizize we wanduguzo we cicekulu, wono luno wakwijela hali'gwe wasina masutya. Holeka aluno, yikunojeye wucidinghule cevu cako, hambi nha yo gwegwe wuwanozize wanduguzo we cicekulu wejele wali ling'ani.”

I wanhu wa Sodoma, ne wanhu wa Samaliya wologomolwa

53 Muwaha Mulungu yakayilonjela Yelusalemi yakatya, “Ane nowapela kanyhi wumoteswe we yisi ya Samaliya, hamonga ne vitumbi vyayo vyose, nayo Sodoma, hamonga ne vitumbi vyayo vyose. Heya, sunga na gwegwe nagwe nokumotya nhavivyo.
54 Ninga, kolokolwa soni, ne cevu cako colowalajila i wanduguzo we cicekulu, nha vyono wene waswanu wasina masutya.
55 Nawo kanyhi wolomoteswa, ninga nagwe Yelusalemi, hamonga ne vitumbi vyako vyose vyologomolwa.
56 Ninga wule, agwe Yelusalemi, si wayisekaga Sodoma mu vilo vivilya, lono wali wukali mu wiwoni wako,
57 na lono zimbi zono wetuma, zali zikali sinamanyika yaye? Ninga aluno wose mwehwana. Naluno i Waedomu, ne Wafilisiti, hamonga ne wekalani wayago, wono mwikalane behi, wono wakusulile, nawo wokwanza kukusekanga nagwe.
58 Holeka yowa yikunojeye wugadinghule gwegwe i mabi gose go wusenyha wako, hamonga ne mawifyo gako gono wetuma.” Vyo yakutya u Muwaha Mulungu.

I cilagane cono cokwikala cibitilila

59 U Muwaha Mulungu yakulonga yakutya nhavi, “Ane nomutendelenyi nha vyono munojeye. Hambi nha mwabedegala wihuwizo wenyu, no kucibena cilagane.
60 Ninga ane nocikumbucila cilagane cono nacinoza nanye, lono mwali mukali wadodo. Na kanyhi nonoza cilagane nanye, cono cokwikala cibitilila.
61 Nanye mokumbucila ze metumo genyu gono mwetumaga lulo, muwe kukolwa cevu, kolo mwawagomola i wandugu zenyu we cicekulu, i wawaha ne wadodo. Ninga nane, nowanoza wawe wana wenyu we cicekulu, hamba yiwe zizi si zali zili ku lubavu lwe cilagane cangu, cono nghucinoza nanye.
62 Nocinoza wupya cilagane cangu kuli'nye. Nanye baho, ho momanyila vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.
63 Nane baho, ho nolomunenelenyi i masutya genyu gose gono mwetuma. Ninga anye molowa mugakumbuciye no kukolwa cevu, hamba no kulonga cinji kanyhi.” Vyo yakutya u Muwaha Mulungu.
