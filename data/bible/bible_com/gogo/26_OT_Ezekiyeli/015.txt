Ezekiyeli 15

I cihwanicizo co Muzabibu

1 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya,
2 “Gwe go munhu, mwene, u muzabibu wina lilumboci sinho mubici wunji? Hamba wuswanuci wono wuli mwi'nhambi lyo mubici wo muzabibu, hono wukuwuling'aniza ne mibici ya mu mbago?
3 Wule, kosuma kuwitumamila u mubici wayo, u mulimo wunji wo wose? Hamba kosuma kuwunoza cisomeko co kutumbicila vinhu vyo vyose?
4 Langa wene, muswanu du ku ngwagu, so wupembela moto. Nawo moto, kolo wazikongojeza zisonje zayo zose vyono nyejete, hasigala haciligati yayo, hono naho hakondya kutakamila, wule, kosuma kuwitumamila so wunozeza cinhu co cose mbi?
5 Ayo yowa nha nghani ya bwete du, vyono sunga na lono wali wukali si natakamila, vyo wali wusina mulimo wunji wo wose. Holeka lu lono wakondya kutakamila, ho wokwila mulimoci waco, langa yo nghani ya bwete du.”

6 Naluno u Muwaha Mulungu, vyo yakulonga yakutya, “Vyono, u muzabibu wusolwaga ku mbago, wuwa kutakamizwa mu moto, ale nane, vyo nowasola i wanhu wose wono wekaye mu Yelusalemi,
7 niwalipicize. Wene wakondya kwiponya mu wulawa mu moto, ninga luno, moto wowongomezeza lukulu. Nane kolo nghuwalipiciza, nagwe baho nhondo, ho kolomanyila nha vyono yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.
8 Hambi nha weza si wehuwilwa kanyhi kuli'ne, holeka nane noyinoza yisi yiwe nyhiwa.” Vyo yakutya u Muwaha Mulungu.
