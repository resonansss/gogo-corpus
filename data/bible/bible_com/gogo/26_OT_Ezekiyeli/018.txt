Ezekiyeli 18

U Munhu na lwakwe

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi
2 yakatya, “Alu mbazi yiyi yina nhunizoci, yono yikalaga yikulongwa mu yisi ya Isilayeli, yono yikutya,

‘Wababa, walya zabibu zindece,

ninga i wana, meno gawo gakafwa yitenene.’ ”

3 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, “Na mbeka vyono ane yo nene Mulungu mumi, holeka si mowuyajilila lukulu kanyhi kuyilonga mbazi yiyi.
4 Hambi u wumi we wanhu wose, nha wangu nene. U wumi wo mulezi, sunga no wumi wo mwana. Holeka u munhu mono kokwituma wubi, yuyo yo kofwa.

5 “Na lunji yisolaga, hawa ho yuli munhu muswanu kanyhi yali ling'ani, na kanyhi wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu.
6 Mono si kahembaga vihwani vye Waisilayeli, hamba kulya vye makumbiko malavye mu hanhu hono halemezwe, mu wuhembelo we wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu. Kanyhi yawe yakali namulonza mucekulu wa munhu yunji, hamba kugona no mucekulu wa munhu yunji, lono yali mu vilo vywakwe vyo mwezi.
7 Hamba si yamukonya munhu, langa yamulipa viswanu mono yamudayaga, hamba si yamuboka yunji sawo yakwe kwe vilungo. Kanyhi yawe yakuwalisa wono wadabuce, no kuwapela vivwalo wono wasakaze.
8 Kanyhi yawe munhu mono kakopeshaga, ninga si kadayaga ngomola. Mono si yendile kwituma zimbi, langa kanozaga wuling'anizi we ling'ani muli munhu no muyagwe.
9 No munhu we liwala lilyo, kagebataga malajilizo gangu, no kuwukamisa wulajilize wangu. U munhu yuyo yakuwa yali ling'ani, nayo holeka kowumipa.” Vyo yakutya u Muwaha Mulungu.

10 “Yisolaga na lunji, u munhu yuyu, kawa yena mwana. No mwana yuyo kawa muhizi, kanyhi muwulaji, mono yakwituma lunji likani limonga lya muli gago,
11 gono u sogwe si kagetumaga yaye. U mwana yuyo, yawe ya kulya ndigwa ze makumbiko malavye mu hanhu hono halemezwe, mu wuhembelo we wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu, na kanyhi akuno ho yakumulonza no mucekulu wo muyagwe.
12 Kanyhi yendile kuwakonya i waciwa, akuno muhizi, no kuwaboka wanhu sawo yawo kwe vilungo, hamba kugomola cono munhu yunji yacipinjice kuli mwene. Kanyhi, yawe yendile kubita ku wuhembelo we wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu, no kuhemba vihwani vye mawifyo.
13 Kanyhi yawe munhu mono kakopeshaga hanyuma kadaya ne ngomola. Mukwihwesa u munhu we liwala lili, kowumipa hongo? Nghukulonjela mbeka nha vyono si kowumipa yaye. Hambi nha kakondya kwituma zinghani zizi ze liwifyo, holeka kofwa du. Nayo kowa yeyambuza u wumi wakwe mweneco.

14 “Ninga kanyhi lunji citye, yisolaga u munhu yuyu we kejete, nayo kawa yena mwana. Nayo u mwana yuyu, kaziwonaga i zimbi zose zono u sogwe kazitumaga, ninga mwene mwana ho yalece kwiga musambo wuwo mubi wo sogwe.
15 Mwene mwana yuyo, yawe si yakuhemba vihwani vyono wakuvihemba Waisilayeli, hamba kulya zindigwa ze makumbiko, i malavye kuli wuhembelo we wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu. Kanyhi yawe mono si kalonzaga mucekulu wa yunji,
16 hamba kumwiwonela yunji, hamba kumuhiziza munhu yunji yaye. Kanyhi mwene yawe mono kawagomolelaga wanhu vinhu vyawo vyono wavipinjice kuli mwene. Kawalisaga wono wali nzala, no kuwapela vivwalo wono wasawuce.
17 Kanyhi mwene yawe si yendile kwituma zimbi, hamba kuwakopesha wanhu no kusaka ngomola. Na kanyhi mwene yawe yawibate wulajilize wangu, no kugakamisa malajilizo gangu. Ale, u munhu we liwala lilyo, si kofwa yaye, ku soko yo wubi wo sogwe, langa mwene kowumipa du.
18 Na ku lubavu lunji, u sogwe mweneco yo yawakonyaga wanhu no kuwahiziza, no kwituma nhondo zose i zimbi kuli wayagwe. Holeka yuyu sogwe yo, kofwa so wubi wakwe wono mweneco yali kuwituma.”

19 Na lunji kokwiwuza go mweneco wuwe kutya, “Alu, nhawule u mwana yuyo nayo, yalece kuwusola u wubi wo sogwe? Ninga lipitulo lyayo lyoli, yuyo u mwana, nha yetuma ziswanu na ze ling'ani. Yebata wulajilize wangu, no kuwutyatya viswanu, holeka mwene yimunojeye yawumipe.
20 Langa mono yakwituma wubi, ale, yuyo du yo kofwa. Umwana, si kofwa so wubi wo sogwe, hamba sogo yo munhu nayo, si kofwa yaye ku soko yo wubi wo mwanagwe. U munhu muswanu kohumbilizwa so wituma ziswanu wakwe. No munhu mwituma zimbi, nayo kofwa so wubi wono ketumaga.

21 “Na hono u munhu mubi yapituce, yalece kwituma zo wubi, yebate wulajilize wangu, no kwituma ziswanu na ze ling'ani, ayo si kofwa yaye, langa kowumipa.
22 No wubi wakwe wose wonenelwa, nayo kowa mumi, hambi nha ketuma ze ling'ani.”
23 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, “Wulenyi! Mukwihwesa vyono'ne niwusangalalilaga 'mbi u wufwe wo munhu mubi? Aa, ng'o! Ane nyendile munhu yuyo yanene wubi wakwe, yekale mumi.
24 Ninga langa, hono munhu we ling'ani yalece kwituma ziswanu, ho yanze kwituma zinghani zimbi, ze cevu, zono i wanhu wabi wakuzitumama, wule u munhu yuyo kowumipa 'mbi? Aa, ng'o! Ne ziswanu zose zono yetumaga, si zokumbucilwa yaye. Nayo kofwa du ku soko yo wubi wakwe no wu wula wihuwilwa wakwe.”

25 Ninga na lunji kotya nhavi, “Alu mwene zizi zono Muwaha Mulungu yakwituma, wule nghani zizi swanu? Alu muhulicize nimulonjelenyi anye nyo Waisilayeli. Alu anye mukwihwesa nha vyono zinzila zangu si zili ling'ani hongo? Ninga mumanye nha vyono zinzila zenyu zo si zili ling'ani yaye.
26 Kanyhi mumanye nha vyono, u munhu we ling'ani, hono yalece kwituma ziswanu, yanze kwituma zimbi, na hanyuma ho yafwe, kofwila mu wubi wakwe wono yali kwituma.
27 Ninga hono u munhu mubi yapituce, no kuwuleka wubi wakwe wono yali kwituma, ho yetume zo mugoloka na ze ling'ani, yuyo kowumipa.
28 Hambi nha yakumanya zo wubi wono yali kwituma, no kuleka kuwituma. Holeka u munhu yuyo si kofwa yaye, langa kowumipa.
29 Ninga anye nyo Waisilayeli mutezaga vyono, ‘Muwaha Mulungu ketumaga zo si zili ling'ani.’ Mwihwesaga nha vyono zinzila zangu si zili ling'ani? Ninga mumanye nha vyono zinzila zenyu nyenye zono si zili ling'ani yaye.

30 “Holeka ane no Muwaha Mulungu, nghumulonjelenyi anye nyo Waisilayeli, nha vyono, nomuling'anizenyi munhu na lwakwe, mu nghango ye metumo gakwe gono yetuma. Alu muwuye, mumhitucile, muwulece u wubi wono mwitumaga, mulece kuwuleceleza u wubi wenyu wumongomezenyi.
31 Muwutajilaje lukulu u wubi wenyu wose wono mwetumaga, mwinozeze nhumbula mhya, no muhe mupya. Alu anye nyo Waisilayeli, nhawulenyi mukwiyambalila ne lifwa?
32 Ninga ane, si nyendile yunji wo wose yafwe. Holeka mupituce muwulece u wubi wenyu, mwikale womi.” Vyo yakutya u Muwaha Mulungu.
