Ezekiyeli 11

Yelusalemi yikuling'anizwa

1 Kanyhi muhe wukanyinula wukandeta sunga ko ku mulango wo lubavu lwa kucilima ye Nyumba yo Mulungu. Nali neza kufika kuko, nghawona wanhu makumi mejete na wahano, wemile behi no mulango. Na muli wene, mwali mo yuli na Yazaniya u mwana wa Azuli, hamonga na Pelatiya u mwana we Benaya, i walongola wawaha we nghungugo.
2 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanyitya, “Gwe go munhu, i wanhu wawa, nha wakwihwesa zo wuhile wubi du, no kuwalanjiza i wanhu wa mwi'kaya lili, zinghani zono sizinojeye yaye.
3 Hambi wene nha wakutya ti, ‘Cozizenga kanyhi zikaya zetu musunho. Hambi likaya nha lyehwana nyungu nhelece mu moto, nase ceza nha nyama yono nhelekwe mu nyungu yiyo, ninga hamba nhavivyo, nyungu yiyo yicicitiye, ho cilece kupya no moto weneco.’
4 Holeka 'gwe go munhu, wuwahosaje so nghani.”

5 U muhe wo Muwaha Mulungu, wukanyiziza kwa vilungo, nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela niwalonjele wanhu zinghani zakwe azino; yakatya, “Anye nyo wanhu wa Isilayeli, ane manyile zose zono mukuzilonga, sunga na zose zono mukuziwikaniza.
6 Hambi nha mwakondya kuwawulaga wanhu wenji mu muno mwi'kaya lili, sunga ne likaya lyose, lyamema mivimba ya wanhu.

7 “Holeka nane no Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, nghumulonjelenyi nghutya nhavi. Mbeka likaya lili, lyaweza nyungu nhelece mu moto, ne wanhu wono wowa yo nyama yono yotelekwa mumo, yo yiyo mivimba ye wanhu wono mwakondya kuwawulaga! Ninga nanye lu, hamba si mokwikala mwi'kaya lili yaye, langa nomutajenyi kunze ye likaya!
8 Kanyhi 'nye muli wadodi ku mumaje, holeka 'ne, nowaleta wanhu wono wowa wena ne mimaje yawo, ho wamuhomenyi.
9 Nolomuwusenyi mwi'kaya, nimuwicenyi mu miwoko ye wanhu wajenzi. Kanyhi nakondya kumuling'anizenyi wufwe.
10 Nanye wose molowulajilwa mu wulugu, mu yisi yenyu nyenye nyo weneco. Na mu nzila yiyo, nhondo i wanhu wose ho wolomanyila vyono, ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.
11 Sunga ne likaya lili hamba si lyolomuponyenyi yaye, nha vyono nyungu yiyicitilaga moto, nyama yono nhelece mugati yayo. Ninga ane nolomulipicizenyi du hanhu ho hose hono molowa mu yisi ya Isilayeli.
12 Nanye nhondo, ho molomanyila vyono, ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, kanyhi na vyono lono mwali mwakondya kupegwa malajilizo gangu, hamba si mwagebata yaye, langa mukajendelela du, ku wituma ku hwani ye malajilizo ge makungugo gono gamupilimenyi.”
13 Nane, Ezekiyeli lono nawilaga, baho du Pelatiya yakagwa hasi yakafwa. Nane hobaho nghawa kwigwisa hasi wugubama, nghazoza nghatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, alu mwene, ho kowawulaga wanhu wose wono wakali wasigaye mu yisi ya Isilayeli?”

U wihuwizo wo Mulungu kuli Wahamywa

14 Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya,
15 “Gwe go munhu, i wanhu wono wekaye mu Yelusalemi, wakulonga i zako hamonga na ze wayago Waisilayeli wose wono wahamywa. Wakutya ti, ‘Alu i wanhu wose wono wahamywa, nha weza wali kutali muno, hamba si wosuma kanyhi kumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu. Holeka Muwaha Mulungu, yakondya kucipela yisi yiyi yose yiwe yetu sese.’

16 “Holeka, wuwalonjele wanduguzo wono wali kuko ku wuhamywe, wuwatye nha vyono, yo nene mono nawategula kuko, ho wakekale kutali mu makungugo. Kanyhi wuwalonjele nha vyono, hamba yiwe yo nene mono naweyagaziza mu ma'si ganji, ninga si lyopuluta yipango yiwaha muno, nane nowa nili hamonga nawo kanyhi, mumo mu ma'si kono wowa wekaye.
17 Holeka wuwalonjelaje zose zono nghulonga ane no Muwaha Mulungu. Hambi nha nowakongholeza wose hamonga, ku wulawa mu ma'si kono nali niweyagazize, niwagomolele kanyhi yisi yawo ya Isilayeli.
18 Na kolo wawuya ku yisi yawo, yolowa yiwanojeye waze wawusize lukulu vinhu vyose vyavu, hamonga ne vihwani vyose vye mawifyo, vyono wolovyagana mumo mu yisi yawo.
19 Nane nolowapela nhumbula mhya hamonga no wuhile wupya. Nolowawusiza lukulu u wu wula cihuliko cawo, hamonga ne nhumbula ye 'bwe yono yili muli wene. Nolowapela nhumbula yo winenenyhezi na ye cihuliko.
20 Nawo nhondo, ho wolowukamisa wulajilize wangu, no kugatyatya ko wihuwilwa malajilizo gangu. Nawo wolowa wanhu wangu, nane nolowa Mulungu wawo.
21 Ninga langa, nolowalipiciza i wanhu wose, wono mitima yawo yendile kuhemba vinhu vyavu, hamonga ne vihwani vye mawifyo. Nolowalipiciza mu nghango ye metumo gawo.” Nhavivi vyo yakutya u Muwaha Mulungu.

U lukumyo lo Mulungu lukuwuka mu Yelusalemi

22 Hobaho i makelubi nago, gakawa kuguluka, vikabitana hamonga ne maguludumu gagalya. Ne nhojo yo Mulungu wa Isilayeli, yali yili hacanyha yago.
23 Hobaho i nhojo yikawa yawuka mwi'kaya, yikabita ku lubavu lwa kucilima ye litunda.
24 Hanyuma kanyhi, mu wugubulilwe wangu, u muhe wo Mulungu wukandinghula kanyhi, wukangomola sunga ko ku wahamywa, ku Babiloni. Hobaho baho wugubulilwe wangu wukawa kumbucila.
25 Nane nghawa kuwalonjela wandugu zangu i wahamywa zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yandajila.
