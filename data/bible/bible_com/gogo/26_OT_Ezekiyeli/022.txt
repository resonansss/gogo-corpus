Ezekiyeli 22

U wubi we likaya lya Yelusalemi

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya,
2 “Gwe go munhu, alu wule, wakondya kwiwikaniza wuliling'anize likaya lya Yelusalemi, lyono limemile wuwulaji wose? Ale, wulimanyizaje ze makani gayo gose ge mawifyo, gono litumaga.
3 Wulilonjele likaya zono nghulonga, ane no Muwaha Mulungu, wulilonjele vyono, alu vyono wakondya kuwawulaga wanhu wenji wa mwi'kaya lilyo, na vyono wakondya kwiyavufya go mweneco so wuhemba vihwani wako 'gwe Yelusalemi, holeka lizuwa lyako nalyo lyasejelela.
4 Hambi nha wuli musutyi ku soko yo wuwulaji wako, hamonga no wiyavufya wuli wene so vihwani vyono wavinoza, na ku soko yiyo, lizuwa lyako lyasejelela, ne lipango lyako lyafika! Na yiyi yo soko yono nghawaleka we makungugo ganji wakusimanje, ne we ma'si ganji nawo, wakusekanje.
5 I ma'si ge behi, hamonga ne ma'si go kutali, gose gakukusekanga, ku soko ye mikalile yako mibi.
6 Ne walongola wose we Waisilayeli, nha weyitumbiye weneco vilungo vyawo, kanyhi wawulaji.
7 Kanyhi mwi'kaya, musina mwana hamba yumonga mono yawahulice walezi wakwe. Mukuwakoca-koca i wajenzi, no kwitujila cambuzi kuli wacekulu wafwile, na ku wana wono wasina walezi.
8 Na kanyhi musina hamba cogopo kuli hanhu hono helile, hamba si mukuzibata ne zisabato yaye.
9 Na wanji wa muli wanhu wako, wendile makani kuwakoweleza makani wayawo wanji, no kuwabalalila ne zinghani zo wulambi, ho wawambuze wawulagwe mbula soko. Na wanji, wakulya makumbiko ge vihwani, na wanji waceye no wetumaga zo wusenyha wawo du.
10 Wanji nawo wa muli wene, wagonaga ne wacekulu we wasogo zawo. Na wanji wendile kuwebata wacekulu lono wali mu vilo vyawo vyo mwezi no kugona nawo kwe vilungo.
11 Na wanji wasenyha, na wanji nawo, wawalonzaga i walamu zawo we cicekulu, hamba walumbu zawo wono wahanjile wasogo zawo, hamba wanyina zawo.
12 Na wanji wa muli wanhu wako wakuwa kusolwa no kulipwa malipo, wawa kutumwa wabite wakawulaje munhu. Na wanji wakudaya ngomola so migawa yono wandugu zawo Waisilayeli wawakopile, hanyuma wawa kwigolifya muno so cambuzi cono cikulawa kuli wandugu zawo. Wene wakondya kusemwa'ne.” Vyo yakutya no Muwaha Mulungu.

13 “Alu, lanje, nane nocitowela hamhelo, i cambuzi cono wucitujile mu zinzila zo wuhizi no wuwulaji.
14 Alu 'gwe mwene wukwihwesa nha vyono kosuma kuwa no wukangalile, hamba kuwa ne vilungo vyo kwinulila muwoko wako, ane kolo nakondya kukunozeza zizo hongo? Ane no Muwaha Mulungu vyo nghutya, kanyhi noyifisiliza du.
15 Ane noweyagaza i wanhu wako, weyagalile mu ma'si 'gose na mu makungugo, nane nogakongojeza ne metumo gako mabi.
16 Na mu nzila yiyo, ne makungugo gose gokubedegala. Ninga agwe ho kolomanyila vyono, ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”

Lityanilo lyo Mulungu lyo kwezeza

17 Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya,
18 “Gwe go munhu, alu Waisilayeli nha weza kanyhi wasina cambuzi kanyhi kuli'ne. Wehwana ne cuma cono cisina cambuzi, nha vyono shaba, hambe bati, hamba cuma, hamba lisasi, vyono vyalekwa, lono fweza yakondya kukwemwa mu moto.
19 Holeka luno ane no Muwaha Mulungu, nghuwalonjela nghutya nhavi, nawo vyo wenha, wasina cambuzi nha vivyo. Alu nane nowategula ku Yelusalemi wose hamonga,
20 nha vyono wakongholezaga mabwe ge fweza, ne shaba, ne cuma, ne lisasi, ne mabati, wavitajila mwi'tyanilo, ho vifugutizwe no moto no kuvyenjedula. Nhavivyo nane vyo nomukongholezezenyi mwi'kuwo lyangu na munhunduji yangu, nimwenjedulenyi.
21 Heya mbeka, nowakongholela wose ha Yelusalemi, niwapembele mu moto, niwenjedule ne likuwo lyangu.
22 Nawo wokwenjedulilwa mumo mu Yelusalemi, nha vyono fweza yenjedulagwa mu moto. Nawo baho nhondo, ho wolomanyila vyono, mbeka ali lyo likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu.”

U wubi we walongola we Waisilayeli

23 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya,
24 “Gwe go munhu, wuwalonjelaje i Waisilayeli nha vyono yisi yawo lu, yaweza nyavufywe, holeka nane lu, noyilipiciziza mwi'kuwo lyangu.
25 Hambi i walongola wayo nha wehwana zisimba zono zikolomelaga zinimu zono gakondya kuziwulaga. Hambi nha weza wawulaga wanhu, wakusola mapesa menji, hamonga ne vinhu vyo wuguzi wukamu. Na mu nzila ye wanhu wawulaji, wono wawapelaga pesa ho wawulaje wanhu, nalu wakondya kuleka wacekulu wenji wono wafwiliywe ne walume zawo.
26 Nawo i wakohani, wakondya kubena lilajilizo lyangu, sunga nalu, hamba si wakuvicelelela kanyhi i vinhu vyono vyelile yaye. Hamba si wakusuma kuwika wisimajile we vinhu vyono vyelile, ne vinhu vyono si vyelile. Hamba si wakusuma kwijiza wanhu, ho wasume kutemanula i vinhu vyono vyavufywe, ne vinhu vyono si vyavufywe. Na kanyhi wayibedegala ne Sabato. Na yiyi yo yikuleka sunga ne wanhu wa Isilayeli hamba si wanyogopile 'ne yaye.
27 Nawo i wetumami we selekali, wehwanile maminzi, gono gahawangaga zinimu zono gakondya kuziwulaga. Wakuwulaga wanhu, wene ho wagolipe.
28 Nawo i wawizi, wazihundice zo wubi wuwu, wehwana ne wanhu wono wahiliwaga swakala mwi'jinji. Wawonaga zo wugubulilwe wo wulambi, no kuwila zo wulambi du. Wene weyitezaga nha vyono wakuganula nghani yo Muwaha Mulungu, na kumbe nane no Muwaha Mulungu ho si niwatumile yaye.
29 Wakuhiza ko wusugu sawo ya wanji, no kuwaboka kwe vilungo. Wakuwatendela vibi i waciwa no kuwewonela i wajenzi.
30 Nane nghalanga-langa, ho nhuje munhu muli wene mono kosuma kulizenga lijinji, na mono kosuma kwima i hanhu hono majinji gamwaguce, ho yasume kuyitaza yisi, lono nane neza kuyongomeza yisi kwe likuwo lyangu. Ninga si namutuga yaye hamba monga.
31 Holeka ku soko yiyo, nane nowamwajila likuwo lyangu, niwakongoje mu moto, ku soko ye metumo gawo.” Vyo yakutya Muwaha Mulungu.
