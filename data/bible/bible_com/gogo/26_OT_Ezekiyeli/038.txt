Ezekiyeli 38

U mutemi Gogu yakwitumamilwa no Mulungu

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya, nhavi,
2 “Gwe go munhu, wumulonjelaje Gogu, u mutemi we zinghungugo za Mesheki na Tubali, mu yisi ya Magogu. Wumulonjele,
3 kanyhi wumumanyize nha vyono ane no Muwaha Mulungu, nili mulugu wakwe.
4 Holeka yamanye nha vyono nomupitula, nimutajile ndobogo mu zinzagasa zakwe, nimulavye nimukwejenhele kutali, hamonga ne mapuga gakwe ge wasilikale. I wasilikale wakwe, hamonga ne zifwalasi, wono si wavwalile myenda ye cisilikale, nawo wowa mhuga mbaha muno. Ne wasilikale wawo wose, wowa wadinghuye hamonga ne vitwalo vyawo, nha cinji, zingula, hamonga ne mimaje yawo.
5 Nawo i wanhu wa Pelisiya na Kushi hamonga na Libiya, wolowa wali hamonga nayo. Na wose wawo wolowa wadinghuye hamonga ne vitwalo vyawo, zingula ne zikofiya ze cuma.
6 Sunga ne wanhu vitung'ati we zi'si za Gomeli na Beti-togalima ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, wawo wose wowa hamonga nayo, hamonga ne wanhu wenji du wono wololawa mu zinghungugo zinji.
7 Holeka wumulonjele nha vyono, yimunojeye yewikanize hamonga ne mapuga gose ge wasilikale, wono wowa wali hasi yo wulindilizi wakwe.
8 Na kolo yakondya kupuluta myaka minji, ane nolomulajiliza, yabite yakayihome yisi, yono i wanhu wowa wakongholwa no kugomolwa hamonga, ku wulawa mu zinghungugo zinji, nawo luno wekaye wapolile wasina kanyhi woga wo wulugu. Kanyhi kologahoma ne matunda ga Isilayeli, gono gali gakondya kulekwa no kutuga wambuce vilo vinji muno, i hanhu hono aluno i wanhu wekaye wapolile.
9 Nayo mutemi Gogu ne lipuga lyakwe lye wasilikale, hamonga ne wanhu wenji wono wololawa mu makungugo menji, woloyihungucila yisi nhe mbeho mbaha yo mukukunzo, kanyhi wolotandala mu yisi yose nhe mavunde.”

10 Ne zinghani zizi, zo Muwaha Mulungu yakumulonjela Gogu yakutya, “Ne mapango gago kolo gakondya kuza, agwe Gogu kolokwanza kwihwesa zo wuhile wubi du mu nhumbula yako.
11 Hambi nha kolowa kwihwesa zo kuzihoma du, i zi'si zono zisina vilungo, zono i wanhu wayo wekaye wapolile, mu makaya gono gasina magomawakali, hamba milango.
12 Koloviteka vinhu hamonga ne wanhu wayo, wono wekaye mu makaya gono katali, gali matongo du. Wono wakondya kwikonghola ku wulawa mu makungugo. Nalu wakondya no kusawa mitugo ne zisawo zinji, nalu wekaye haciligati ye yinze.
13 Ne wanhu wanya Sheba na Dedani, hamonga ne wasuluza wa mu vitumbi vya kutakama ya Hisipaniya, wolokuwuza wawe kutya, ‘Alu mwene 'gwe Gogu, kuza akuno hamonga ne mapuga gako ge wasilikale ho wutece mateka lo? Yikwijela nha vyono wuhile wako, wutuje fweza, na zahabu, ne mitugo, hamonga ne zisawo zinji zose, wuvikunyhe wubite navyo vyose.’ ”

14 Hobaho u Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kunhegula, mbite nghamulonjele Gogu zose zono yasakaga yamulonjele. Nane nghamulonjela Gogu vyono u Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, “Alu vyono i wanhu wangu wekaye wapolile, nagwe lu wubite
15 ku wulawa ku yisi yako, ku lubavu lwa kusumuka kutali, wubite hamonga ne lipuga yiwaha muno lye wasilikale vitung'ati, wono wapandile fwalasi, wono wakulawa mu zinghungugo zose.
16 Nanye mowahoma i wanhu wangu Isilayeli, nhe libeho liwaha lyo mukukunzo lyono likudumula yisi. Na kolo lipango lyakondya kuza, ane nokutegula wukayihome yisi yangu, ho wuwamanyize we makungugo, wamanye vyono nene nani, na kanyhi wuwamanyize zo wele wangu, mu nzila yono nghwitumila muli'gwe.
17 Hambi agwe Gogu, nha yo gwegwe mono nalongaga i zako ku wulawa katali, mu nzila ye wetumami wangu i wawizi wose we Waisilayeli, na vyono na mu vilo vyono vikuza, nokwenda nize ndete munhu yayihome Isilayeli.” Vyo yakutya u Muwaha Mulungu.

Gogu yakulipicizwa no Mulungu

18 U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Mwi'zuwa lyono Gogu kolowahoma we yisi ya Isilayeli, nane nolowa mukali muno muno.
19 Nane nghulonjela mwi'kuwo lyangu likali, nha vyono, mwi'zuwa lilyo, holowa ne 'tulo yiwaha muno mu yisi ya Isilayeli.
20 Ne zisomba zose, ne zindeje ne zinimu zose zimbaha ne zindodo, sunga ne wanhu wose du wono wekaye mono'nze, wolocicima so woga, ha meso gangu. Ne matunda nago gologwa hasi, na hono henuce, holotenjelemulwa, ne majinji gose gologwa hasi.
21 Nane nolomogofya Gogu, ane no Muwaha Mulungu. Ne wanhu wakwe wolokwipitucila wawe kwihoma ne mimaje yawo weneco wose munhu na muyagwe.
22 Nane nolomulipiciziza Gogu mu nzila ye matamwa na mu ma'fwa. Nolotonyeza mvula yono yine 'beho na ye mabwe mawaha muno muno, no moto, ne cibiliti, muli mwene ne lipuga lye wasilikale wakwe, hamonga ne makungugo gose, gono gowa gali hamonga nayo.
23 Na mu nzila yiyo, mo nowamanyiziza we zinghungugo zose nha vyono, ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, kanyhi nelile. Nawo nhondo, baho ho womanyila vyono, ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”
