Ezekiyeli 23

I wahinza wetuma wubi

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya,
2 “Gwe go munhu, kwali na wana we cicekulu wejete.
3 Nawo lono wali wakali wadodo, wekalaga ku Misili. Wakawuhodanga wunyacipale wawo, wakawa malaya.
4 U muwaha yacemwaga Ohola, nha cinji yo Samaliya. Nayo u mwanakwawe, yacemwaga Oholiba, nha cinji yo Yelusalemi. Nawo vyono wejete, wakawa wangu wakandelela wana walume ne wacekulu.
5 Ninga Ohola, hamba yawe yali wangu, hamba nhavivyo yajendelela du no wumalaya wakwe. Yaketipiza muno ne wendece wakwe wa ku Ashuli.
6 I wasilikale wono wali wavwalile myenda yihwanile, ye liwala lyo lugandu, i wanhu we lilumbo, wono wena witumami we lilumbo liwaha. Na wawo wose wali wazelelo du wapanda fwalasi.
7 Yali malaya mono yasenyhaga ne wetumami wose we Ciashuli. No wusenyha wakwe, wukamwambuza yakeyavufya so wuhemba vihwani vye Waashuli.
8 Nayo yajendelela no wumalaya wakwe, nha vyono yali yenha du kuko ku Misili, kono yawucila no wunyacipale wakwe. Hambi nha ku wulawa mu wuhinza wakwe, i walume nha wagonaga nayo, wakamutendela nhe wacekulu wamalaya.
9 Holeka ku soko yiyo, nane nghamuwika mu miwoko ya wawo Waashuli i wendece wakwe, wono yali viyendile nawo.
10 Nawo wakamuvula myenda yakwe, yakekala yali cipwi, wakawasola i wanangwe we cilume na we cicekulu, u mwene wakamuwulaga ko mumaje. Ne wacekulu wakawa wakulonga i zakwe du hanhu hose.

11 “Ninga hamba nhavivyo, u mwanakwawe Oholiba, nayo yaziwona zizi, ninga nayo yakacila kuwa malaya muno sinha vyono yali yenha Ohola.
12 Nayo nhavivilya, yali yena masulumizi, yawasulumilaga i wanhu we lilumbo, i wetumami we Ciashuli, i wasilikale wono wali wavwalile myenda yihwanile ye liwala lyo lugandu, sunga ne wasilikale wawalya i wazelelo wono wejelaga nha wazelelo waswanu.
13 Nane nghamuwona vyono yakondya kwiyavufya, na wose vyono wejete, wakawa wabi wose, nghologo monga.

14 “Oholiba yakacila muno muno kwiyinjiza mu makani go wusenyha. Yakakwegwa muno ne zihwani ze wetumami wawaha wa Babiloni, zono zali nghakatwe mwi'jinji, hiliwe liwala lidunghu muno.
15 Wono wali wawopwe misipi mu viwino vyawo, ne vipombelo vye mawala mu mitwe yawo.
16 Nayo Oholiba yali yeza kuwawona du, hobaho baho yakebatwa no wusulumizi, yakawa kutuma wanhu wabite ku Babiloni.
17 Nawo wanya Babiloni, hobaho wakawa kuza kusenyha nayo. Wakasenyha nayo muno, no kumwavufya muno muno. Sunga hanyuma nhondo, wakawa kumuleka.
18 Yakegubula cipwi cakwe calinze, ne wanhu wose nhondo wakamanya nha vyono mwene musenyha muno muno. Holeka nane, nhondo nghawa kumuleka, nha vyono nali nakondya kumuleka u muwahawe Ohola.
19 Nayo yakacila muno konjeza zo wusenyha wakwe, yakakumbucila vilo vyo wuhinza wakwe, lono yetumaga metumo go wumalaya mu yisi ya Misili.
20 Yakamema wusulumizi muno muno wo wusenyha, yakawa kuwasulumila i walume wono wena miwili miwaha nhe miwili ye zindogowe, wono walavyaga mbeyu yo lulelo nha ye fwalasi nume.”

21 Nhavivi agwe Oholiba, vyo wawukumbucila wusenyha wono wetumaga ha wuhinza wako, lono wali ku Misili, lono walume wedawalilaga vitombo vyako, sunga no wunyacipale wako, wukawa kuwuka.

Wulipicize wo Mulungu kuli Oholiba

22 “Alu agwe Oholiba, azi zo Muwaha Mulungu yakukulonjela, yakutya nhavi. Alu, mwekatala na wawo wendece wako. Ninga 'ne nowanoza wakukolelwe 'kuwo, nowaleta wakupilime mbavu zose.
23 Nowaleta Wababiloni wose, hamonga ne Wakalidayo, ne wanhu walume, wono wolawa ku Pekodi, na Showa, na ku Kowa, hamonga ne Waashuli wose. Nowakongholela hamonga i wazelelo waswanu, wono wena lilumbo liwaha mu witumami wawo, hamonga ne wetumami walongola, ne wasilikale wapanda fwalasi, wono wena milimo ye lilumbo liwaha mu mulimo wawo.
24 Waze wakuhome ku wulawilila ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, wolokuza ne lipuga liwaha lye wasilikale, ne matuka go wulugu, hamonga ne magali. Wolowa wena ne zingula zawo, ne zikofiya ze cuma mu mitwe yawo, nawo wolokupilima mbavu zose. Nane nokukombola mu miwoko yawo, nawo wokuling'aniziza mu wityatye wo wulajilize wawo vyono winha.
25 Ku soko agwe Oholiba, mbeka wanghoza muno likuwo, holeka nane, nowaleka wawo, wakutendele mu nghango ye likuwo lyawo vyono lyowa. Ninga wumanye vyono kolo wakuza, wolokudumula mhula yako, hamonga ne makutu gako, hanyuma ho wawawulaje sunga ne wanago. Na mbeka, wowasola i wanago we cilume na we cicekulu wawakongoje womi mu moto.
26 Wolovidemulanga vivwalo vyako, wasole hamonga no wihambo wako wo wulenzi.
27 Nane baho nhondo, ho nolowudumizya lukulu wusulumizi wo wusenyha wako, hamonga ne makani gako ge cikomaza, gono wetumaga ku wulawilila lono wali wukali ku Misili. Nagwe ku wulawilila baho, nhondo si kolovinugulila kanyhi meso, i vihwani, hamba kwihwesa kanyhi za ku Misili.”

28 Azi kanyhi zo Muwaha Mulungu yakulonga yakutya, “Ane nokulavya kuli wanhu wono wuwasulile, kanyhi wono mwakondya kwileka nawo.
29 Nawo vyono wakusulile, holeka wovisola vinhu vyako vyose, vyono wavisawila mu vilungo vyako, wolokuvula myenda yako, wakulece wuli cipwi, ne zinghani zako zo wumalaya wako, nazo zolokwijela calinze. Alu u wusulumizi wako hamonga no wusenyha wako,
30 vyo vikuleteye zizi zose. Hambi nha wali wuli malaya muwaha wa muli makungugo gose agwe Oholiba, sunga nhondo wukawa kwiyavufya gwegwe go mweneco ne vihwani vyawo.
31 Hambi nha wukazityatya zinghanzo zo muwahawo Ohola, holeka nane nokung'wesa cikombe cicilya du co wulipicizwe, nagwe wucing'we.”

32 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi,

“Nagwe Oholiba,

kong'wela du mu cikombe co muwahawo Ohola,

naco ho ciwaha muno kanyhi citali.

Ne wanhu wose wokwizwajiza no kukubedegala,

naco cikombe cimemile muno.

33 Ne cikombe cico co muwahawo Samaliya,

i cikombe co woga no wuciwa,

cokunoza wugale muno, wuwe no wukalale.

34 Heya kocing'wa hamba si kosigaza cinji mugati yaco,

kovitafunya ne vibada vyayo,

ne matombo gako kogazewanga.

Vyo nalonga ane no Muwaha Mulungu.”

35 Azi nazo zo Muwaha Mulungu yakulonga yakutya, “Alu vyono agwe wakondya kusemwa'ne, wanhumbila mugongo, nagwe lu kotuga magazo ku soko yo wusulumizi wako hamonga no wumalaya wako.”

U wulipicize wo Mulungu kuli Ohola, na kuli Oholiba

36 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya, “Gwe go munhu, wule, wakondya kwiwikaniza ho wuwaling'anize Ohola na Oholiba? Ale wuwalonjelaje ze makani ge mawifyo gono wali kwituma.
37 Hambi nha wali kwituma makani go wusenyha, ne makani go wuwulaji. Wali kusenyha ne vihwani, no kuwawulaga i wana wawo weneco wono wandelela'ne. Wali kuwalavya makumbiko i wanangu kuli vihwani vyawo.
38 Kanyhi hamba si makani gago du gali wene gono wali kwituma. Langa wayavufya sunga ne Nyumba yangu, wakazibena ne zisabato zangu, zono nali nakondya kuzamula.
39 Na mwi'zuwa li lilya du lyono wawawulajila i wanangu, no kuwalavya makumbiko kuli vihwani, lyo lizuwa lilyo kanyhi, lyo wenjilila na mu Nyumba yangu no kuyavufya!

40 “Wakawalalika wanhu kenji na kenji, ku wulawa kutali muno, waze. Nawo wanhu wakaza. Nawo Ohola na Oholiba, wakehovuga, wakeyola ne lanji mu meso gawo, wakehamba no wihambo.
41 Wakekala mu magoda maswanu, na ha mwande yawo, wakatenjelwa meza yono yali yimemile vinhu viswanu muno, hamonga no wuvumba, ne mafuta gono nali niwapelile.
42 No winji we mazi ge wanhu wono wali wali mu wusangalale, gakahulikwa i mazi ge lipuga lye wanhu wono waletwa ku wulawa ku nyika. Nawo wakawavwika Ohola na Oholiba bangili mu miwoko yawo, hamonga ne vipombelo viswanu mu mitwe yawo.
43 Nane baho, nghawa kulonga nili wene mu nhumbula yangu nghutya, Alu lanje vyono wamwitumamilaga u mucekulu malaya, mono aluno yasakala ku soko yo wusenyha wakwe.
44 Hambi nha wabitaga kuli wamalaya wawa kali kenji muno, Wabitaga kuli Ohola, na kuli Oholiba, wawa i wacekulu wasenyha.
45 Holeka i wanhu we ling'ani, wowaling'aniza mu ning'aniza yono yiwanojeye i wacekulu wasenyha, na mu ning'aniza yono yiwanojeye i wacekulu wawulaji. Hambi nha wali kwituma mulimo wo wusenyha, sunga ne miwoko yawo yihodanjice ne sakami.”

46 Azi kanyhi zo yakulonga u Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Langa, ta, nane noleta lipuga lye wanhu, waze wawahungucile, no kuwaboka vinhu vyawo.
47 Ne lipuga lilyo lye wanhu, lyowatowanga ne mabwe, no kuwawulaga kwe mimaje yawo. Wawawulaje ne wana wawo, we cilume na we cicekulu, no kuzipembela zikaya zawo.
48 Nane nowupezezya lukulu wusenyha mu yisi yawo, i wacekulu wose ho wafundwe, walece kuzityatya kanyhi zinghani zo wusenyha, nha vyono wetumaga.
49 Ninga nanye Ohola na Oholiba, ane nomulipicizenyi ku soko yo wusenyha wenyu, na ku soko yo wubi wenyu wo kuhemba vihwani. Nanye baho ho momanyila vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”
