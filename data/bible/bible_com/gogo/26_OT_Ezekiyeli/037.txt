Ezekiyeli 37

I lilolo lye mafupa gono ganyalile

1 Nghaviwona vilungo vyo Muwaha Mulungu, no muhe wakwe wukanhegula kwi'lolo lyono lyali limemile mafupa.
2 Yakanghoza behi nago, yakamhilimya mbavu zose. Nane nghawona, mwali mogali mafupa menji muno muno, kanyhi gose ganyalile.
3 Yakanonjela yakatya, “Gwe go munhu, wule, mafupa gaga gosuma kuwumipa?”

Nane nghapitula nghatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulugu, agwe yo gwegwe du mono kozimanya zizo!”

4 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakanyitya, “Ale, lu, wugawilile mafupa gago. Wugalonjele mafupa gaga gono ganyalile, gayihulicize i nghani yo Muwaha Mulungu.
5 Wugalonjele nha vyono, ane no Muwaha Mulungu nghugalonjela nghutya nhavi, lanjenyi ta, ane notajila wehele muli'nye nanye mowumipa.
6 Nane nomupelenyi matimba hamonga ne nyama, hanyuma nimugubicenyi ne nghuli mucanyha yayo. Nomutajilenyi wehele muli'nye, nanye mowumipa. Nanye ho momanya vyono, ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”

7 Ale, baho, nane nghawa kuwila nha vyono nalonjelwa. Na lono nawilaga, nghahulika lizi likulila kotya kayamba, ne mafupa gakanza kwilunza yifupa ne 'fupa liyagwe.
8 Na lono nali nghali nanjile, hobaho, i mafupa gakawa kugubikwa ne matimba hamonga ne nyama, na hanyuma yayo, gakawa kugubikwa ne nghuli. Ninga hamba nhavivyo mwali mukali musina wehele mu miwili yiyo.

9 U Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya, “Gwe go munhu, aluno, wuyiwililaje mbeho. Wuyilonjele mbeho, nha vyono u Muwaha Mulungu yakukutya nhavi, alu wuze ku wulawa mu zimbavu zose vyono yine, wukayehelele i miwili yiyi yono yifwiye ho yiwumipe.”

10 Baho nane nghawa kuwila nha vyono nalonjelwa. No wehele wukawa kuyinjila i miwili yiyilya. Ne miwili yikawumipa, wakenuka wakema wima. Nawo wali wenji muno muno, wono wali wakwiliye kunoza lipuga yiwaha lye wasilikale wo wulugu.

11 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanyitya, “Gwe go munhu, i wanhu wa Isilayeli, wehwanile ne mafupa gagano. Hambi wene nha wakutya vyono, cakondya kunyala, nalu ceza cisina witumbilo, kanyhi cafwila lukulu!
12 Holeka wuwawililaje i wanhu wangu wa Isilayeli, wuwalonjele nha vyono, ane no Muwaha Mulungu, novidigula vitenjelo vyawo, niwawuse mumo mu vitenjelo, niwategule sunga ko ku yisi ya Isilayeli.
13 Nane kolo nakondya kuvidigula vitenjelo, mono i wanhu wazicigwe, no kuwalavyaniza kunze, nawo baho ho womanyila vyono, ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.
14 Nane notajila wehele muli wene, niwomifye, niwalece wekale mu yisi yawo. Nawo ho womanya vyono, ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu. Nihuwiza nha vyono nozituma zizi. Na mbeka nozituma. Vyo nghutya ane no Muwaha Mulungu.”

Yuda na Isilayeli mu wutemi wumonga

15 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya nhavi,
16 “Gwe go munhu, wusole cibici cimonga, wucandice mucanyha yayo zinghani azino, ‘U wutemi wa Yuda.’ Na hanyuma kanyhi wusole cibici cinji, naco wucandice mucanyha yayo zinghani azino, ‘U wutemi wa Isilayeli.’
17 Hanyuma wuvisole vibici vivyo vyose vyono vyejete, wuvihanze, wuvibate hamonga ku sonje yavyo, vijele nha kotya cibici cimonga.
18 Na kolo i wanhu wako wakukuwuza wakusaka wamanye nhunizo yayo nyhici.
19 Wuwalonjele nha vyono, ane no Muwaha Mulungu, nocisola cibici cono candicigwe Isilayeli, nicihanze hamonga ne cibici ciyagwe cono candicigwe Yuda. Nane novinoza viwe cibici cimonga nicibate mu muwoko wangu.

20 “Holeka luno, wuvibataje vibici vivyo vyejete mu muwoko wako, i wanhu wakuwone vyono wuvibate.
21 Hanyuma wuwalonjele nha vyono, ane no Muwaha Mulungu, nowakonghola wanhu wangu mu wulawa mu makungugo gose kono weyagaliye. Nowakonghola niwawice hamonga, niwagomole sunga ko ku yisi yawo.
22 Nane nowalunza wose wawe nghungugo monga du mu'si, mu matunda ga Isilayeli. Nawo wolowa na mutemi yumonga, mono kowatema wanhu wose hamonga. Nawo hamba si wolokwigawa kanyhi wawe makungugo mejete, hamba wutemi wejete.
23 Hamba si wolokwiyavufya weneco so wuhemba vihwani vye cevu, hamba kwiyinjiza mu wituma wubi yaye. Nane nolowawusiza lukulu mu zinzila zo wubi, na mu zinzila zono zosuma kuwambuza wangaluce'ne. Noloweza, nawo wolowa wanhu wangu, nane nolowa Mulungu wawo.
24 No mutemi mono yehwanile Dawudi u mwitumami wangu, yuyo yo kolowa mutemi wawo. Nawo wose wolowa wali hamonga hasi yo mutemi monga. Kanyhi wogebata ne malajilizo gangu ko wihuwilwa.

25 “Nawo wolokwikala mu yisi yono namupela mwitumami wangu Yakobo, i yisi yono wekalaga walezi wawo. Nawo wolokwikala mu yisi yiyo cibitilila, wene ne wana wawo, hamonga ne wana we wana wawo. No mutemi mono yehwanile u mwitumami wangu Dawudi, yuyo yo kolotema muli wene cibitilila.
26 Nane nolonoza cilagane co mulamu hamonga nawo, cowa cilagane ce cibitilila. Nowatandaza no kuwonjeleza wonjelezece wawe wenji. Noloyiwika ne Nyumba yangu mu yisi yawo, nayo yolokwikala mumo cibitilila.
27 Nane nolokwikala baho hamonga nawo, Ane nolowa Mulungu wawo, nawo wolowa wanhu wangu.
28 Nane kolo nakondya kuyiwika Nyumba yangu yikale muli wene cibitilila, hobaho i makungugo ho golomanyila vyono, yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, mono niwahaguye i wanhu wa Isilayeli, wawe wanhu wangu.”
