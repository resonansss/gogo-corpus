Ezekiyeli 29

U wuwililwe we yisi ya Misili

1 Na mwi'zuwa lye kumi na nyejete, lya mu mwezi we likumi, wa mu mwaka we likumi, wa mu wuhamywe wetu, u Muwaha Mulungu yakalongana nane yakatya,
2 “Gwe go munhu, wumulonjelaje u mutemi wa Misili, nha vyono yisi ya Misili yolipicizwa.
3 Wumulonjele nha vyono u Muwaha Mulungu yakumulonjela zizi u mutemi we yisi ya Misili, yakutya, ‘Ane nili mulugu wako, agwe go lizoka liwaha, mono wugonile mugati ye likolongo. Mono wukwiyitya nha vyono likolongo lya Nile lyako, na vyono ati yo gwegwe mono walinoza.
4 Alu nane notajila ndobogo za cuma mu zinzagasa zako, hanyuma nizinamatize zisomba za mu makolongo muli'gwe. Hanyuma niwe kukukwega ku wulawa mwi'kolongo lya Nile, hamonga ne zisomba zose zono zowa zikunamatiye.
5 Niwe kukutaga mu nyika, hamonga ne zisomba zizo. No muwili wako wogwa hasi, no kulekwa baho du, howa hasina munhu mono kowuzika. Langa nozilecela zindeje, hamonga ne malimu ga mu mbago go gakulye.’
6 Na baho, i wanhu wose wa mu yisi ya Misili, ho womanyila vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.” Na kanyhi u Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Anye nyo Wamisili, i Waisilayeli, wali wamwitumbilenyi nyenye vyono mowapela wutazi, ninga kumbe ho si mukwiliye cinji yaye, ciba ne nghome ye ligwami si nha'nye.
7 Hambi nha wali weza kumwegamilenyi, anye mukawa kubeneka, no kuwacinyanga mu mawega gawo gose, no kuwapela wusungu wuwaha mu migongo yawo.
8 Holeka aluno, nane no Muwaha Mulungu, nghumulonjelenyi nha vyono, ane nowatuma wanhu, waze wamuhomenyi ne mimaje. Nawo kolo wakuza, wowawulaga i wanhu wose, hamonga ne mitugo zose.
9 Nayo Misili, yolosigala yili bwete du musina cinji. Nagwe go yisi ya Misili, baho ho wulamanyila vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.

“Hambi agwe nha wali kutya, likolongo lye Nile lyako, ati kanyhi yo gwegwe mono walinoza.
10 Holeka ane nili mulugu wako, kanyhi nili mulugu we likolongo lya Nile. Nane noyinoza yisi ya Misili yiwe nyika no wambuce du, ku wulawilila kwi'kaya lya Migidoli ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, sunga ko kwi'kaya lya Aswani lyono lili ku lubavu lwa kutakama, sunga ko ku mupaka we yisi ya Kushi.
11 Na kanyhi, holowa hasina munhu mono kolokola mumo, hamba nimu yo yose. Molowa musina cinhu co cose cono cilekala mumo, sunga yize yipulute myaka makumi mane.
12 Nane noloyinoza Misili yiwe yo yisi yono yina wambuce muno si nhe zi'si zose za mono'nze. Ne makaya gose ge yisi ya Misili, golokwikala matongo mwi'daha lya myaka makumi mane. Nolowasola Wamisili, niweyagazize kutali ako na ako kuli zinghungugo zinji, wakekalane ne wanhu wanji.”

13 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Na kolo yeza kupuluta yiyo myaka makumi mane, nhondo ho nolowakonghola kanyhi Wamisili ku wulawa mu makungugo, kono nali niweyagazize.
14 Nize niwalece wekale ku Patilosi ku lubavu lwa kutakama ye yisi ya Misili, i hanhu hono walelelwa. Ninga kuko, wolowa wena wutemi wono wusoceye muno muno,
15 hamba si wologatemela lukulu kanyhi i makungugo ganji yaye. Nolowanoza wawe wanhu wono wasina lilumbo, wono si wolowa ne vilungo vyo kulisuma no kulitema kanyhi likungugo linji yaye.
16 Sunga ne Waisilayeli nawo, hamba si walawetumbila kanyhi i Wamisili so wutazi wo wose. Ne nghani yiyo yolowamanyiza Waisilayeli, nha vyono kumbe yali nghani 'mbi muno yono wetuma, yo kuwetumbila wawo Wamisili. Na baho nawo Waisilayeli, ho wolomanyila vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”

U mutemi Nebukadineza yo koloyitema Misili

17 Na mwizuwa lilongozi, lya mu mwezi mulongozi, wa mu mwaka we makumi mejete na mupunghati, wa mu wuhamywe wetu, u Muwaha Mulungu yakalongana nane yakatya,
18 “Gwe go munhu, Nebukadineza u mutemi wa Babiloni, yawetumamila wasilikale wakwe yakayihoma Tulo. Yawadinghuza wasilikale wakwe mitegulo mizito muno muno, sunga ne mitwe yawo yikakulunjika yikatuga mihalu, ne mawega gawo gakakubuka. Ninga u mutemi, hamonga ne wasilikale wakwe, si wapegwa co cose so mulimo wono wetuma.
19 Holeka nane no Muwaha Mulungu nghutya nhavi, Alu noyisola yisi ya Misili, nimupele mutemi Nebukadineza. Nayo koyihoma, yasole mateka vinhu vyose, ne sawo yose ya Misili, gawe go malipo kuli wasilikale wakwe.
20 Nomupela Misili gawe go malipo go mulimo wakwe, hambi i wasilikale wakwe, nha wanyitumamilaga nene. Ane no Muwaha Mulungu vyo nghutya.

21 “Na kolo zikwijela zizi, ane nowanoza i wanhu wa Isilayeli wawe ne vilungo. No kukuleka nagwe Ezekiyeli, wusume kuwalonjela Waisilayeli, nawo wokuhulika zono wukuwalonjela. Nawo ho wamanye vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”
