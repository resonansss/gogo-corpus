Danyeli 1

Danyeli ne wayagwe ha mwande ye mahakama ga Nebukadineza

1 Ale, mu mwaka we kadatu, wa mu wuteme wakwe Yehoyakimu mu yisi ya Yuda. Mutemi Nebukadineza wa ku Babiloni yakalipilima likaya lya Yelusalemi no kulihoma.
2 Muwaha Mulungu yakamutajila Yehoyakimu mu miwoko ya Nebukadineza, hamonga ne viya vinji vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. U mutemi Nebukadineza yakawasola wafungwa hamonga ne viya yakawa kuvitegula ku Babiloni, yono hanji yacemwaga shinali, yakaviwika mu vinyumba vye viwutwa vya mu nyumba ye milungu yakwe.

3 Mutemi yakamulonjela Ashipenazi, u muwaha we wapaswe wakwe, yawahagule wanji wa muli wana wa Isilayeli i wahamywa, wa mu lulelo lwe citemi-temi, na wa mu zindelo zinhambule.
4 Wawe wazelelo wono wena futa swanu, we mahala muno muno, kanyhi wono weyijize viswanu, wapelupelu ku wizuce, wono wasina hamba cilema mu miwili yawo. Ho wapegwe mulimo wo kuyidima nyumba yo wutemi. Nayo Ashipenazi yawalanjize no kusoma, no kwandika, mu nonga ye Cibabiloni.
5 Kanyhi mutemi yakalajiliza vyono, i wazalelo wawa, weze kupegwa nhondo zose zindigwa, hamonga ne divayi, nha vyono walizaga wa mu mhuga yo wutemi. Na kolo wakondya kwijizwa myaka midatu, wategulwe ha mwande yo mutemi.
6 Na muli wawo wono wahagulwa, yali Danyeli, Hananiya, Mishayeli na Azaliya. Na wose wawa, wali wo mulango wa Yuda.
7 Baho u mulongola muwaha yakawacemela matagwa mapya. Danyeli yakamucemela lya Beliteshaza, Hananiya yakamucemela lya Shedilaki, Mishayeli yakamucemela lya Meshaki na Azaliya yakamucemela lya Abedinego.

8 Baho Danyeli yakehwesa mu nhumbula yakwe, yakawona nha vyono sikosuma kwiyavufya, so wulya zindigwa no kung'wa divayi ya mu nyumba yo wutemi. Holeka yakawa kumulomba Ashipenazi yu yulya u muwaha we wapaswe, yamulece, yalece kulya ndigwa yono yomwavufya.
9 Nayo Mulungu yakamupela Ashipenazi nhumbula yo lusungu kuli Danyeli.
10 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, Ashipenazi yamogopaga mutemi, holeka yakamulonjela Danyeli yakamutya, “Alu mutemi nha yakondya kuwaga ndigwa yono mowa kulya no kung'wa, holeka hono wulece kwijela wujinile nhe wayago, mutemi kombulaga.”
11 Baho Danyeli yakamulonjela u mulongola mono yapegwa wuwaha na Ashipenazi yawe yo mulanjilizi wa mwene Danyeli na Hananiya, na Meshaki, na Azaliya, yakamutya,
12 “Dedede cikukunena wucijelaje mwi'daha lya vilo yikumi du, wucipelaje, mboga mboga du zo ciwe kulya, hamonga ne malenga du go kung'wa.
13 Hanyuma ho kociling'aniza ase ne wazelelo wayetu wono wakulya zindigwa ze nyumba yo wutemi, baho ho kowona vyono cikwijela ha mwande ye wayetu.”
14 Yuyo yakalumba wajele kulya nhavivyo mwi'daha lya vilo yikumi.
15 Ne vilo yikumi vyali vyeza kupuluta, wakejela wajinile, kanyhi watuga ne vilungo, kuciza i wazelelo walya wono walizaga zindigwa za mu nyumba yo wutemi.
16 Wulawilila baho u mulindilizi yakawawusiza lukulu mu zindigwa zono mutemi yali yalajilize, yakajendelela kuwapela mboga mboga du hamonga ne malenga go kung'wa.

17 Mulungu yakawapela wawo wazelelo wane wizuce wuwaha, hamonga no wumanya mahala, na mu wumanya kusoma no kwandika. Hamonga na zizo, Danyeli yakapegwa wizuce wo kulolosa zinhunizo ze zindoto no wugubulilwe we hayo hayo.
18 Ne myaka yiyilya midatu, yono yawagwa no mutemi yali yeza kupuluta, Ashipenazi yakawasola wazelelo wawa wose yakawategula kuli Nebukadineza.
19 Mutemi yakaduwana na wose yumonga yumonga. Ninga Danyeli, Hananiya, Mishayeli na Azaliya, wakamwendeza muno mutemi si nhe wayawo wose. Na ku soko yiyo, wakawikwa wawe wetumami wo mutemi.
20 Na muli nghani yo yose yo wizuce, yono mutemi yayiwuzaga, hamba nghani nghamu, wawa i wane, wakejela wo wena wizuce kali kumi kuciza waganga ne wahawi wa mu wutemi wakwe wose.
21 Danyeli yakajendelela kuwa kuko, sunga lono Kulo u mutemi wa Pelisiya yayihomela Babiloni no kuyisuma.


Danyeli 2

Ndoto ya Nebukadineza

1 Ale, mu mwaka we kejete wono Nebukadineza yali yakali mu wutemi wakwe, yalota ndoto. Ndoto yiyo yikamupela mahwele mawaha. Na mwi'zuwa lilyo, hamba si yasuma no kugona nhongo yaye.
2 Baho yakawacema watowa visangu, ne waganga, ne walotezi, hamonga ne wahawi, wose waze wamulonjele i nhunizo ye ndoto yiyo. Nawo wali weza kufika ha mwande yakwe,
3 yakawatya, “Ane nina mahwele ne ndoto yono ndotile zuwali, alu nghusaka manye nhunizo yayo.”
4 Nawo wakamupitula mutemi mu nonga ye Cishamu wakamutya, “Gwe mutemi, wuteme cibitilila! Ale wucilonjelaje ndoto yako co wetumami wako, nase cokulonjela nhunizo yayo.”

5 Mutemi yakawalonjela yakatya, “Ndoto yeneco nayisemwa, ninga nakondya kulamula vyono nyenye yo munojeye munonjele yiyo ndoto yangu, hanyuma munonjele ne nhunizo yayo. Na hono mulemegwe, modumulwa vibimha vibimha, nizinoze ne zikaya zenyu gawe matongo.
6 Ninga hono munonjele yiyo ndoto yangu, hamonga ne nhunizo yayo, nane nomupelenyi vihumbilizwa viwaha, hamonga ne lilumbo yiwaha. Holeka lu, munonjelaje ndoto yiyo ne nhunizo yayo.”
7 Ninga wene wakamupitula mutemi wakamutya, “Gwe mutemi, hono wucilonjele i ndoto yako, nase co watumami wako, cokuloloseza nhunizo yayo.”
8 Lulo, mutemi yakalonga yehindile yakatya, “Nakondya kumanya vyono mukusaka ho mutuje cilejeho. Hambi nha nakondya kulamula
9 nha vyono nomulipicizenyi anye wose wulipicizwe wono wiling'aniye, hono mulece kundoloseza i ndoto yiyo. Alu anye wose kumbe mwelumbaniza, ho munonjele zo walambi, akuno mukwihwesa nha vyono hanyuma nokawaniza wulonje wangu. Aluno mwamule munonjele ndoto yangu, nane ho nomanya vyono mbeka mosuma kunonjela ze nhunizo yayo.”

10 Nawo waganga wakamupitula mutemi wakamutya, “Hasina yunji mu yinze ayi, mono kosuma kukuloloseza Gwe mutemi, muli zizi zono wukusaka kumanya. Hamba hasina mutemi yunji wa muli watemi, hamba muli wono wena lilumbo liwaha, hamba muli wono wena wulajilize wuwaha, mono yawuza nghani yono yinha yiyi, kuli muganga hamba kuli wahawi ne walotezi.
11 Holeka cono wukuwuza Gwe mutemi, co cinhu cikamu muno muno, cono hasina munhu mono kosuma kukunozeza, langa milungu du, nayo lu, ho siyikalaga muli wanhu.”
12 Na so nghani yiyo, mutemi yakakolwa ne likuwo, no kulajiliza we mahala wose wa mu yisi ya Babiloni wawulagwe.
13 Lolulo lulo wulajilize wukanzwa nha vyono we mahala wose wawulagwe hamonga na Danyeli ne wayagwe.

Mulungu yakumulajila Danyeli nhunizo ye ndoto

14 Hobaho Danyeli yakamubitila Aliyoki, u muwaha we wasilikale walindilizi wo mutemi, mono yali yapejigwe mulimo wo kuwawulaga. Danyeli yakasejelela kwe cogopo na kwe cisindo muno, yakahagula ne zinghani zono kolonga nayo.
15 Yakamuwuza Aliyoki i soko yono yilecile mutemi yakulavya lilajilizo lilyo ko musunho. Hobaho Aliyoki yakamulonjela Danyeli zono zijeye.
16 Lulo Danyeli, yakabita mbela mbela kulomba yatuje cilejeho cinji co kumuwona mutemi, ho yasume kumuloloseza mutemi i nhunizo ye ndoto.
17 Hobaho Danyeli yakawuya ku kaya yakawalonjela ne wayagwe Hananiya, Mishayeli na Azaliya, zono zijeye.
18 Yakawalonjela vyono, yiwanojeye walombe kuli Mulungu wa ku wulanga, yawawonele lusungu, no kumulomba yawapele wumanye we nghani yiyo hundikwe, ho walece kuwulagwa hamonga na we mahala wa mu Babiloni.

19 Mu cilo cicilya, nghani yiyilya hundikwe, yikejela kuli Danyeli mu wugubulilwe. Lulo yakamukumya Mulungu wa ku wulanga yakatya,

20 “Mulungu yo we vilungo ne mahala!

Mukumyenyi cibitilila ne cibitilila.

21 Umwene kakawanizaga vidaha ne mapango.

Kawawikaga no kuwawusa watemi.

Yo mwene mono kawapelaga

wanhu wumanye no wizuce.

22 U mwene kanzalikaga vinhu vyono vihundice

yavimanyile na vyono vihundice mu citi.

Kanyhi mwene nha yapilimwe no wuzelu.

23 Nghukulumba no kukukumya,

gwe go Mulungu we walezi wangu.

Wamhela wumanye ne vilungo,

wahulika no wulombe wangu.

Wacilajila na zono comuloloseza Mutemi.”

Danyeli yakumulonjela Mutemi ndoto no kumuloloseza nhunizo yayo

24 Baho Danyeli yakabita kuli Aliyoki, mono u mutemi yali yamulajiliza yawawulaje we mahala wose wa he 'kulu. Yakamutya, “Wulece kuwawulaga. Langa wusolaje nene, wunhegule kuli mutemi, nane nomuloloseza ze nhunizo ye ndoto yakwe.”

25 Lo lulo lulo Aliyoki yakamusola Danyeli, no kumutegula ha mwande yo mutemi Nebukadineza. Yakamulonjela mutemi yakatya, “Nghondya kumutuga yumonga wa muli Wayahudi i wahamywa, mono kosuma kukuloloseza Gwe mutemi i nhunizo ye ndoto yako.”
26 Mutemi yakamutya Danyeli (Mono yacemwaga Beliteshaza). “Wudahile kunonjela ndoto yangu hamonga ne nhunizo yayo?”
27 Danyeli yakapitula yakatya, “Gwe mutemi, hasina muhawi, hamba mutowa cisangu, hamba we mahala go wizuce we zinyelezi, mono kosuma kuzilolosa zizi.
28 Langa Mulungu du wa ku wulanga, mono kagubulaga zono hundikwe no kuzilajila calinze. Alu kakulajila Gwe go mutemi zono zokwijelela agwe ku mwande. Holeka nokuloloseza ze ndoto hamonga no wugubulilwe wono wapegwa lono wali wugonile.

29 “Lono Gwe mutemi wali wugonile, walota za kumwande zono zokuziza. No Mulungu mono kagubulaga zihundikwe, yo yakulajiye zono zokwijela mu vilo vyono vikuza.
30 Alu, nghani yiyo hundikwe, yakondya kugubulwa kuli'ne. Ninga si vyono ane yo nina mahala menji kuciza wose ng'o. Langa du agwe go mutemi ho wusume kumanya ze nhunizo ye ndoto yiyi, nagwe ho wugamanye mahweso ga mu nhumbula yako.

31 “Gwe mutemi, mu wugubulilwe wako, wawona cihwani ca munhu, ciwaha muno ha mwande yako, cono cikung'alala kanyhi cikogofya na ku wulangwe.
32 Mutwe wayo wali wa zahabu nengu, mhambaga yayo ne miwoko yayo yali ya fweza, ciwino ne vima hamonga ne 'nda yayo, vyali vya shaba.
33 Migulu yayo ya cuma, ne zinzayo ze migulu yayo zali nusu, cuma, na nusu, lilongo linamu.
34 Na lono wali wujendeleye kucilanga cihwani cico, libwe liwaha muno, lyono hamba yunji si yalipamiza, likavulumuka, likayikumula migulu ye cuma ne lilongo linamu lye cihwani ci cilya, likacibenanga.
35 Lo lulo lulo cuma, ne lilongo linamu, ne shaba ne fweza hamonga ne zahabu, vikasajika, vikawa nhe mawaje ga mu cuga, ga mu vilo vye nhwanga. Mbeho yikavipemhelusa hamba si vyasigala yaye. Libwe lililya likawahapa likawa yitunda lyono likayimemya yinze yose.

36 “Ayi yali yo ndoto yako. Alu Gwe mutemi, nane nokuloloseza nhunizo yayo.
37 Gwe mutemi, agwe yo gwegwe mono wuli mutemi muwaha muli watemi wose. Mulungu wa ku wulanga yakupela wuwaha no wusume, ne vilungo, hamonga ne lilumbo.
38 Yakutemya agwe muli wanhu wose we zisi zose za mono yinze, hamonga ne vilimu vya mu mbago ne zindeje. Alu agwe, wo mutwe awulya we zahabu.
39 Na mu nyuma yako, kolotema mutemi yunji, mono sikolowa ne lilumbo liwaha nha vyono winha 'gwe. Hanyuma kanyhi wolokuza wutemi we kadatu, wo wutemi we shaba, wono wolotema yinze yose.
40 Kanyhi wolokuza wutemi we kane, wono wolowa ne vilungo nha cuma cono cikumulaga no kubenanjiza cinhu co cose. Wuwo wo wolowubenanjiza wutemi wose wono walongola.
41 Kanyhi mu ndoto yako wawona vyono, i migulu ne vidole kululu vyali nusu cuma na nusu lilongo linamu. Nhunizo yayo, wutemi wuwo wolowa wugawanuce. Ninga wolowa ne vilungo vya cuma. Ku soko cuma cali cihangahanjile ne lilongo linamu.
42 Vidole kululu navyo, vyali nusu cuma, na nusu lilongo linamu. Nhunizo yayo, lubavu lunji lo wutemi wolowa ne vilungo, no lubavu lunji wolowa wutimbile.
43 Kanyhi wukawona cuma cihangahanjile ne lilongo linamu. Nhunizo yayo, wolojela kwilunza mu wutemi wawo mu nzila zo witolanile, ninga hamba si walasumila lukulu, nha vyono cuma si cihangahangaga ne lilongo linamu.
44 Na mu vilo vyo wutemi wuwo wose, Mulungu wa ku wulanga, kolowusimya wutemi wunji wono siwuladuma yaye, hamba kusumwa yaye. No wutemi wuwo, wolowongomeza wutemi wose, ninga wene wokwikala cibitilila ne cibitilila.
45 Kanyhi wawona vyono libwe lyono si lyapamizwa na munhu, likapanduka wulawa mwi'talawe, likacitijinha cihwani ce cuma, ne shaba, ne lilongo linamu, ne fweza, hamonga ne zahabu. Muwaha Mulungu yakukulonjela Gwe mutemi, zono zokwijela mu vilo vyono vikuza. Alu ane nakondya kukuloloseza ze ndoto yako vyono yinha, hamonga ne nhunizo yayo ye mbeka.”

Mutemi yakumuhumbiliza Danyeli

46 Lo lulo lulo mutemi Nebukadineza yakafugama, yakenama, sunga no wusu wakwe wukapamiza ho hasi. Yakalajiliza vilavywa ne makumbiko vinozwe so Danyeli.
47 Mutemi yakatya, “Mbeka Mulungu wako yo Muwaha muli milungu yose. Yo Muwaha Mulungu muli watemi, yo mwene kagubulaga calinze zono hundikwe. Alu nakondya kuzimanya zizi, hambi nha wakondya kundoloseza nghani yiyi yono yali hundikwe.”
48 Hobaho yakamuwika Danyeli yawe muwaha mwi'tumbi lya Babiloni, no kumuhumbiliza vipegwa viswanu. Kanyhi yakamuwika yawe yo muwaha muli we mahala wa mu wutemi wakwe.
49 Na Danyeli yakamulomba mutemi, yakawawika Shedilaki, Meshaki na Abedinego, nawo wawe wawaha wa mu zinghani zose za mu matumbi ga mu Babiloni. Ninga mwene Danyeli, yakajendelela kwikala mwi'kulu.


Danyeli 3

Nebukadineza yakulajiliza wanhu wose wacihembe cihwani ce Zahabu

1 Mutemi Nebukadineza yakanoza cihwani ca zahabu, cono cali cina wutali wa mita makumi mejete na mupunghati, no wugazi wa mita yidatu. Yakacisimya mwi'lolo lya Dula, mwi'tumbi lya Babiloni.
2 Baho mutemi yakalajiliza i wetumami wakwe wose, wekongholele hamonga, i watemi, ne walongola, ne watazi wawo, na wose wono wena wulajilize, ne wawaha we mahala, ne walamuzi, ne wakamisa viwutwa, hamonga ne wahakimu, ne walongola wose wa mu matumbi, waze ha wusiwanulwe we cihwani cico, cono Nebukadineza yacisimya.
3 Na lono walongola wose wali wamema, so wusiwanulwe, wemile ha mwande ye cihwani,
4 hobaho munya kulalika yakawalonjela wanhu kwe yizi 'waha yakatya, “Anye nyo wanhu we zinghungugo zose, na we zi ndelo zose, na we milonjele yose.
5 Mohulika lizi lye ndulele ne filimbi, ne masumbi, ne mazeze, ne ndonondo, ne vidananda ne mihilimo yose ye masumbi, yono yowa yityatizye. Vyono mulilo wuwo wukwanza du, yinojeye anye wose mwiname ho hasi, mucihembe cihwani cono mutemi Nebukadineza yacisimizye.
6 No munhu wo wose mono sikokwinama no kucihemba cihwani cico, munhu yuyo kogumilwa mwi'lambi lyo moto wono wukwaka muno muno.”
7 Ale, lo lulo lulo wakahulika masumbi galila, wanhu wose wakenama no kucihemba cihwani ce zahabu, cono mutemi Nebukadineza yacisimizye.

Wanduguze wadatu wa Danyeli wakusitakwa

8 Hobaho wanji wa muli Wababiloni, wakawa kwanza kuwasonjeza Wayahudi.
9 Wakamulonjela mutemi Nebukadineza wakamutya, “Gwe mutemi wikale cibitilila na cibitilila.
10 Gwegwe go mutemi walavya lilajilizo, vyono, hono munhu wo wose yahulice lizi lye ndulele, ne zifilimbi, ne masumbi, ne sinjila ne ndonondo ne vidananda ne mihilimo yose ye masumbi vyeza kutowa, yimunojeye yafugame hasi, no kucihemba cihwani ce zahabu.
11 Na muli wose mono sikofugama no kucihemba cihwani cici, yimunojeye yagumilwe mwi'lambi lyo moto.
12 Alu ko wali wanji wa muli Wayahudi, wono wuwawicile nha walongola mu yisi ya Babiloni, Shedilaki, Meshaki na Abedinego wono sunga nalu wakuwubedegala wulajilize wako gwe go mutemi. Siwakusaka kumuhemba mulungu wako, hamba kufugama ha cihwani cono wacisimya.”
13 Lo lulo lulo Mutemi Nebukadineza yakakolwa ne likuwo muno muno, yakawa kulajiliza wa walya wanhu wadatu, waletwe ha mwande yakwe.
14 Yakawawuza yakawatya, “Shedilaki, Meshaki na Abedinego, ati zizi za mbeka, vyono anye mukulema kumuhemba mulungu wangu, no kucifugamila cihwani ce zahabu cono nacisimya?
15 Ale, 'lu, hono muhulice mililo ye zindulele, ne zifilimbi, ne masumbi, ne mazeze, ne zindonondo, ne vidananda, hamonga ne mihilimo yose ye masumbi, hobaho mufugame no kucihemba cihwani. Hono mulece kunoza nhavivyo, mowopwa no kugumilwa mwi'lambi lyo moto. Mukwihwesa 'lo ko yuli mulungu yunji mono komuponyenyi?”

16 Shedilaki, Meshaki na Abedinego wakamupitula wakamutya, “Gwe mutemi, ase sicokwilewela co weneco.
17 Langa u Mulungu wetu mono cikumuhemba, yo kosuma kucitaza mwi'lambi lyo moto, hamonga na mu vilungo vyako.
18 Na hono yalece kucitaza, ninga wutemi wako wumanye, vyono mbeka, ase sicosumila lukulu kumuhemba mulungu wako hamba si cocifugamila ne cihwani cako ce zahabu cono wacisimya.”

Wanduguze wadatu wa Danyeli wakuling'anizwa kufwa

19 Baho Nebukadineza yakakolwa ne likuwo muno, no wusu wakwe wukakununghala, yakawakolelwa likuwo yiwaha Shedilaki, Meshaki na Abedinego. Hobaho yakawalajiliza wanhu wakwe wonjeleze zingwagu, moto wuwe mukali muno kali mupunghati, kuciza vyono wakaga vilo vyose.
20 Baho yakawalajiliza wanhu wakwe, iwasilikale wo wulugu, wono wali wali vilungo muno muno, wawawope wanhu wa walya i wadatu no kuwagumila mwi'lambi lyo moto wono wakaga.
21 Hobaho wakawawopa, nawo wali wavwalile viswanu zishati, ne zikanzo ne zikofiya, wakawa kuwagumila mwi'lambi lyo moto wono wukwaka hamonga na vyose vyono wali wavwalile.
22 Kanyhi na vyono mutemi yali yalavya lilajilizo, nha vyono moto wonjelezwe muno muno, malambi-lambi gayo, gakawanyanya no kuwawulaga awalya wasilikale, wono wawagumila Shedilaki, na Meshaki, na Abedinego, mwi'lambi lyo moto.
23 Lulo Shedilaki, Meshaki na Abedinego, wali vyo wawopwe du, wakagumilwa haciligati ye lilambi lyo moto.
24 Mu cipango cico, Nebukadineza yakenuka mwi'goda ko wizine wuwaha. Yakawawuza wetumami wakwe yakawatya, “Mwene mbeka ciwawopa wanhu wadatu, ciwa kuwagumila mwi'lambi lyo moto?” Wakamupitula wakamutya, “Heya, vyo citendile gwe mutemi.”
25 Kanyhi yakawatya, “Alu nhawule nghuwona wanhu wane, wono wakujendajenda mu moto? Kanyhi hamba siwawopwe yaye, hamba siwakulajila vyono watamiswe. No muyawo we kane, yakwijela kotya nha malayika.”

Shedilaki, Meshaki na Abedinego, wakulavyanizwa mu moto no kupegwa wuwaha

26 Hobaho Nebukadineza yakenuka yakasejelela behi no moto, yakacemeleza kwe vilungo yakatya, “Shedilaki! Meshaki! Na Abedinego! Wetumami wo Mulungu mumi! Mulawile muze kuno!” Nawo wakalawaniza lwa kamonga.
27 Lulo, wose wa mwi'kulu, ne walongola wa mu matumbi, na wose wono wena wulajilize, hamonga ne wetumami wanji wose wo mutemi, wakekongholela hamonga, ho wawalanje wanhu wawa i wadatu wono hamba si watamiswe no moto yaye. Zimvwili zawo hamba sizatakamila, ne myenda nayo siyatakamila, hamba kutuga munungho du bwete we lyosi mu miwili yawo.
28 Mutemi yakatya, “Yalumbwe Mulungu wa Shedilaki, Meshaki na Abedinego! Hambi nha kamutuma Malayika wakwe, kawaponya wawa wanhu wakwe, wono wakumwitumamila no kumwihuwila. Walema malajilizo gangu, wawa kwisuza wumi wawo. Wawona ciba wafwe, si nho kumuhemba mulungu yunji, langa Mulungu wawo du.
29 Alu nane nghulajiliza vyono, munhu wo wose wa mu nghungugo yo yose, hamba wa mu lulelo lo lose, hamba wa mu milonjele yo yose, mono kolonga nghani yo yose yimbi kuli Mulungu wa Shedilaki na Meshaki na Abedinego, munhu yuyo kodumulwa vipiligwa piligwa, ne kaya yakwe yonozwa yiwe 'tongo. Ku soko nha kusina Mulungu yunji mono kosuma kuponya nhavivi.”
30 Lulo Mutemi yakawonjeleza lilumbo lyawo wa Shedilaki, Meshaki na Abedinego, wawe wawaha mu matumbi ga Babiloni.


Danyeli 4

Ndoto ye kejete ya Nebukadineza

1 Mutemi Nebukadineza yakategula mulamu kuli wanhu we makungugo gose, na we zimbeyu zose, na we milonjele yose wa mu yinze yose, yakatya, “Mulamu weze kuli nyenye.
2 Hulicizenyi zinghani ze vitanjilo, ne mezino, zono u Mulungu mumi yandajiye.

3 Vitanjilo vyakwe nha viwaha,

vyono Mulungu mumi yakucilajila!

Ne mezino ge vilungo nha mawaha gono yakwituma.

Mulungu yo Mutemi cibitilila,

kanyhi yo kolotema mu zindelo zose.”

4 Nene, Mutemi Nebukadineza nali nyhehile mu kaya yangu, nhulile mwi'kulu lyangu.
5 Na lono nali ngonile, nghalota ndoto, na mumo mu ndoto yangu, nghawona wugubulilwe wo kogofya muno.
6 Nghawa kuwalajiliza we mahala wose wa mu Babiloni, waze ho wandoloseze ze nhunizo ye ndoto yiyo.
7 Ale, i watowa visangu, ne wahawi, na we mahala go wizuce we zinyelezi, wakaletwa hali 'ne. Nane nghawaloloseza ze ndoto yangu, ninga hamba siwasuma kuyilolosa nhunizo yayo yaye.
8 Lulo lulo Danyeli yakawa kuza, (mono yakucemwa Beliteshaza, litagwa lyo mulungu wangu.) Kanyhi muli mwene wikaye muhe we milungu yono yelile. Nane hobaho nghamuloloseza ze ndoto yangu yono nghalota. Nghamutya,
9 “Gwe Beliteshaza go muwaha we watowa visangu, ane manyile vyono u muhe we milungu yono yelile yili muli 'gwe, na vyono kanyhi, wuzimanyile na zono zihundicigwe zose. Alu ayi yo ndoto yangu yono ndotile, nane nghusaka wunonjele nhunizo yayo.”
10 Nayo mutemi Nebukadineza yakamutya Beliteshaza, “Ane lono nali ngonile, natuga wugubulilwe, nghawona mubici mutali muno muno wono wali wuli haciligati ye yinze.
11 Wukawahapa muno muno, wukapamiza sunga ko ku wulanga. Nawo wejelaga kuli wanhu wose wa mono yinze.
12 Ne mahamha gayo, gali maswanu muno muno, nago gali gavunice mikalo, yono yali yikwiliye ku wulya wanhu wose we yinze. Vilimu vyose vya mu mbago, vikaceha mu mubeho wayo. Ne zindeje zikazenga manhuli gazo mu manhambi gayo, kanyhi ne viwumbwa vyose, vyalizaga mikalo yayo.

13 “Na lono nali nghali ngonile nghwihwesa zo wugubulilwe wuwu, nghamuwona malayika yakutima yakulawilila ku wulanga, mono yali yewikanize so suguzi.
14 Yakalonga kwe 'zi yiwaha yakutya, ‘Wudumulenyi mubici wuwu, mugapilulangaje ne manhambi gayo, muhepe ne mahamha gayo gose, muyiyagaze ne mikalo yayo; muviwinje ne vilimu viwuce mu mubeho wayo, hamonga ne zindeje zono zili mu manhambi gayo.
15 Langa mulilece lisina lyayo mu muhangha, liwe yiwopwe no munyolo wa cuma, ne shaba yeze yipilime munyolo. Mulilece baho mu mahanze madece ga mwi'lolo. Mumulekaje baho munhu yuyu yatuje malenga ge buhe ya ku wulanga. Mulece yekale hamonga ne malimu hamonga ne mahanze.
16 Na mu myaka mupunghati, nhumbula yakwe yikawanice, yilece kuwa nhumbula yo munhu, langa yapegwe nhumbula, ya nimu.
17 Awu wali wo wulamuzi wa Malayika yulya mono yali yewikanize so suguzi. Holeka 'lu, wanhu wose wamanye vyono, u Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye, yo yatemile mu wutemi wose wa mu wanhu, nayo kamupelaga mono mweneco yendile kumupela. Kanyhi kawapelaga wutemi sunga na wono wasoceye.’ ”
18 Baho mutemi Nebukadineza yakamutya Beliteshaza, “Ayino yo ndoto yono ndotile. Holeka lu Beliteshaza, wundolosezaje nhunizo ye ndoto yiyi. Hambi nha hasina yunji wa muli we mahala wa mu wutemi wangu, mono yasumile kundoloseza. Ninga agwe, kosuma kundoloseza, vyono muhe we milungu yono yelile yili muli gwegwe.”

Danyeli yakuyilolosa nhunizo ye ndoto

19 Muli yiyo, Danyeli mono kanyhi yacemwaga Beliteshaza, yali yeza kuzihulika zizo, baho yakakangawala, yakanyamala cipango cidodo. Mutemi yakamulonjela yakamutya, “Beliteshaza, wulece kukangawala so ndoto yiyi hamonga ne nhunizo yayo.” Beliteshaza yakapitula yakamutya, “Gwe mutemi, ane nghwenda ndoto yiyi ne nhunizo yayo, yeze yili muli walugu wako yilece kuwa kuli'gwe.
20 Holeka nhunizo ye ndoto yako yoyi, mubici wono wali mutali wupamize ku wulanga, wijeye kuli wose wa mu yinze yiyi.
21 Ne mahamha gayo, gali maswanu muno muno, kanyhi gali gena ne mikalo yono yali yikwiliye kuwalisa we yinze yose. Ne malimu ga mu mbago gakaceha mu mubeho wayo, ne zindeje nazo zikazenga manhuli gazo mu manhambi gayo.

22 “Gwe mutemi, agwe yo gwegwe wo mubici wuwo mutali muno, kanyhi wuli vilungo. No wuwaha wako wuwiluce sunga ko ku wulanga, ne vilungo vyako vitandaye sunga mu yinze yose.
23 Na lono agwe mutemi, wali wulanjiliye, Malayika yakatima kulawa ku wulanga, na hanyuma yakatya, ‘Dumulenyi mubici wugwe, hanyuma muwupilulaje. Langa mulece lisina mu muhangha. Muliwopeceze no munyolo we cuma, ne shaba yeze yipilime munyolo, mulilece kuko kwi'lolo lyono lina mahanze madece; Mumulekaje baho munhu yuyu, yatuje malenga ge buhe ya ku wulanga; Mumulece yekale hamonga ne malimu myaka mupunghati.’

24 “Ne nhunizo yayo yoyi, Agwe mutemi, azi zo yakukulajila Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye zono zokwijelela gwegwe ku mwande.
25 Kolowingwa mu wikalana ne wanhu wayago, kolokwikalana ne malimu. Nagwe kolowa kulya mahanze nhe zing'ombe, kanyhi kolowa kugona mwi'hala caka, kono wubuhe wono wukulawa ku wulanga, wowa kukugwila sunga yipulute myaka mupunghati. Baho nagwe ho kolomanyila vyono, u Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye yo yatemile mu wutemi wose wa mu wanhu. Kanyhi na vyono yo yadahile kumupela wutemi munhu wo wose mono mwene komuhagula.
26 Na zono Malayika yateye, nha vyono lisina lilekwe mu muhangha. Nhunizo yayo yoyi. Agwe kolowa mutemi kanyhi, wulaza kuwa wakondya kumanya vyono mbeka Mulungu yo yayitemile yinze yose.
27 Holeka Gwe mutemi, nghukunena wibataje nghani yangu yono nghukupela, nghutya nhavi, ‘Wulecele lukulu kwituma wubi, langa wanze kwituma ze ling'ani, kanyhi weze kuwawonela lusungu i waciwa.’ Baho ho kojendelela kwikala viswanu hamonga ne nyemo mu vilo vyose vyo wikale wako.”
28 Zizi zose zikamwijelela mutemi Nebukadineza.
29 Yaza kupuluta myezi yikumi na myejete, lono mwene yajendajendaga mwi'tembe mu kaya yakwe yo wutemi ha Babiloni.
30 Hambi nha yakatya, “Langa ta! Likaya lye citemitemi lya Babiloni vyono yiwaha! Yonene mono nalizenga ho wuwe wikalo wangu wo wutemi. Nane ho nhandaze u wuwaha wangu hamonga no wusume, ne vilungo vyo wutemi wangu.”

31 Na lono yali yakali yakuzilonga zinghani zizi, lizi likalongana nayo kulawa ku wulanga likatya, “Mutemi Nebukadineza wuhulicizaje lu, zono nane nghukulonjela! Alu, wakondya kuwusizwa vilungo vyo wutemi wako.
32 Nagwe kowinjilwa lukulu muli wanhu, wukekale ne vilimu vya mu mbago, kanyhi kowa kulya mahanze kotya ng'ombe sunga yipulute myaka mupunghati. Baho nagwe ho komanyila vyono, u Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye, yo yatemile mu wutemi wose wa mu wanhu, kanyhi na vyono yo yadahile kumupela wutemi munhu wo wose mono mweneco komuhagula.”
33 Vidaha vicefu du, vyali vyeza kupuluta, zinghani zizi zikejela za mbeka. Nebukadineza yakawingwa mu wanhu, yakawa kulya mahanze kotya ng'ombe. Wubuhe wukamugwila mu muwili wakwe, ne zimvwili zakwe zikawiluka, zikawa zinha mazoya ga numba. Ne makombazala gakwe, gakawa matali kotya nha ga ndeje.

Nebukadineza yakumukumya Mulungu

34 Mutemi yakatya, “Na lono myaka mupunghati yali yakondya kupuluta, nghenula meso gangu kucanyha, ne mahala gangu gakambuyila kanyhi, nghamulumba Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye, nghamupela lilumbo no lukumyo mwene mono kekalaga cibitilila.

“Mwene kolotema cibitilila.

No wutemi wakwe hamba siwuladuma yaye.

35 Mono yakuwalanga wanhu wa mono yinze kotya si cinji,

Wamalayika ku wulanga ne wanhu wose wa kono'nze,

wali mu wutonji wakwe.

Hamba hasina yunji mono kobena wulajilize wakwe

hamba kuziwuza zono mwene ketumaga.

36 “Na mwi'pango lilyo, mahala gangu gakambuyila kanyhi, hamonga ne lilumbo lyangu, no wutemi wangu, hamonga no lukumyo lo wutemi wangu, vyose vikambuyila. I wetumami wangu, hamonga ne wanhu we mahala wakambocela. Hobaho nghapegwa vilungo vyo wutemi, nawo wutemi wukawa ne vilungo ne lilumbo kuciza na ha mwamulizo.
37 Nene Nebukadineza, nghumulumbiza no kumupela cogopo, hamonga no lukumyo, u mutemi wa ku wulanga. Hambi zose zono ketumaga, nha za mbeka, kanyhi ze ling'ani. Kanyhi yo kamunyetyaga munhu wo wose mono yakwikumya.”


Danyeli 5

Sikukuu ya Belishaza

1 Mwi'zuwa linji cinyamihe, Mutemi Belishaza yakawalalika wanhu we mahala elufu monga, wabite walye sikukuu mbaha, no kung'wa divayi hamonga nayo.
2 Na lono wajendelelaga kung'wa, Belishaza yakalajiliza viletwe vikombe vya zahabu, na vye fweza, hamonga ne zibakuli, vyono u sogwe Nebukadineza yavisola wulawa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu ku Yelusalemi. Mutemi Belishaza, yakavisaka ho vize waving'wele divayi, mwene ne wawaha wose ne wagolece zakwe, hamonga ne wajitulwa wakwe.
3 Lulo lulo vikombe ne zibakuli vikaletwa, nawo wakanza kuving'wela,
4 akuno wakuyilumba milungu yono yali minozwe ne zahabu, ne fweza, ne shaba, ne cuma, ne mabici ne mabwe.

5 Lo lulo lulo muwoko wa munhu wukejela, wukanza kwandika mwi'jinji lye likaya lyo wutemi lyono lihiliwe swakala, hanhu hono zitala zamulikaga viswanu. Nayo mutemi yakawuwona muwoko wuwo lono wandikaga.
6 Hobaho wusu wakwe wukasininghala so woga, ne mafugamilo gakwe gakanza kucicima.
7 U mutemi Belishaza yakacemeleza kwe yizi yiwaha, waletwe, waganga ne walotezi, na we mahala go wizuce we zinyelezi ne watowa visangu. Nawo wali weza kuwa waletwa, mutemi yakawalonjela yakawatya, “Munhu wo wose, mono kosuma kusoma mandiko aga, no kunonjela nhunizo yayo, yuyo nomuvwika myenda ye citemi-temi, hamonga ne kanzo yo lugandu, no luvumbe lwe zahabu lwe lilumbo mu singo yakwe. Nayo kowa munhu we kadatu wa muli wono wena wulajilize mu wutemi wangu.”
8 Baho wanhu we mahala wa mwi'kulu wo walongola, ninga hasina hamba yumonga muli wene, mono yasuma kugasoma mandiko gago, hamba kuyilolosa nhunizo yayo.
9 Na ku soko yiyo, Mutemi Belishaza, yakasininghala muno muno, na we mahala wakwe wakawula yo yose yo kunoza.

10 U nyina yo mugolece wo mutemi, yakahulika zinghani zono mutemi yalongaga hamonga na we mahala wakwe. Hodu yakawa kwinjila mwi'kumbo lyo wulilo we sikukuu. Yakatya, “Gwe mutemi, wuteme cibitilila! Kanyhi nghukulomba wulece kugazika no kusininghala muno.
11 Hambi mu wutemi wako, nha mo yuli munhu yunji, mono yali hamonga ne myuhe ye milungu yono yelile. Lono u sogo mutemi Nebukadineza yali yatemile, munhu yuyo yalajila yihala yiswanu lyo wutanje, no wizuce ne mahweso, nha vyono milungu yikuwa kulajilwa. Lulo mutemi Nebukadineza u sogo, yakamunoza yawe yo muwaha we watowa visangu wose, ne wahawi, hamonga ne walotezi, na we mahala go wizuce we zinyelezi.
12 Mbeka yena wizuce no wumanye mu wulolosa zinhunizo ze zindoto, no kuzilolosa zinghani ze mafumbo, hamonga ne zinghani zose zono hundikwe. Holeka wutume yunji yakamulete Danyeli mono mutemi yamupela litagwa lya Beliteshaza, nayo kosuma kukulonjela nhunizo ya zizo zose.”

Danyeli yakugalolosa mandiko

13 Lo lulo lulo Danyeli yakaletwa ha mwande yo Mutemi, nayo mutemi yakamutya, “Yo gwegwe Danyeli, ayulya Muyahudi muhamywa, mono waletwa na baba ku wulawilila ku yisi ya Yuda lono baba yali mutemi?
14 Nahulika vyono muhe we milungu yono yelile yili muli'gwe. Na vyono agwe wina wumanye no wizuce ne mahala.
15 We mahala ne wahawi wose wacemwa, ho wasome mandiko agano no kundoloseza nhunizo yayo, ninga hamba si wasuma kuyimanya nhunizo yayo.
16 Alu nahulika vyono agwe wukusuma kuzimanya zinghani zono hundikwe, sunga no kuzilolosa zifumbo. Alu, hono wusome mandiko aga, no kundoloseza nhunizo yayo, kovwalizwa myenda ye citemi-temi yono miswanu, ne kanzo ye liwala lyo lugandu, kanyhi kovwalizwa no luvumbe lwe lilumbo mu singo yako. Nagwe kowa yo we kadatu muli wono wena wulajilize mu wutemi.”

17 Danyeli yakapitula yakatya, “Gwe mutemi, vipegwa vyako wikalaje navyo du, hamba lunji wumupelaje munhu wo wose. Ninga ane, nokusomela bwete du mandiko gaga, no kukuloloseza ze nhunizo yayo, gwe go mutemi.
18 U Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye, yo yamunoza sogo Nebukadineza yawe mutemi muwaha. Yakamupela ne nhojo ne lilumbo.
19 Nayo yali mogopwe ne wanhu wose we makungugo ne zimbeyu na we milonjele yose, wamogopaga muno muno no kucicima. Lono yasakaga kumuwulaga yunji, yamuwulagaga du. Na lono yasakaga kumuwopolela yunji yekale mumi, yamuwopolelaga du. Yawapelaga lilumbo wono yasakaga, na wono yasakaga kuwanyetye, yawanyetyaga.
20 Ninga 'lu, vyono yalajila wivuni no wukali, hamonga no wu wula cihuliko, hodu yakawa kuwuswa mu wutemi, no kuwajiliza mukumbo wakwe wo wutemi, yakalyajiliza ne lilumbo lyakwe.
21 Yakawinjilizwa, no kuwuswa mu wikalana ne wanhu, ne mahweso gakwe gakehwana na ge malimu. Yakawa kwikalana ne zinyhenje, yakalya mahanze nhe zing'ombe, yakawa yakugona cipwi mwi'wagalila lya mwi'lolo, yasina na co kumugubika muwili wakwe, ho yalece kudodowekwa ne buhe. Sunga nhondo, yakamanya vyono mbeka u Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye, yo yatemile wutemi wose mu wanhu, kanyhi yadahile kumupela wutemi munhu wo wose mono mwene yakumuhagula.

22 “Ninga lu, agwe nagwe go mwanagwe, hamba si wukwinyetya go mweneco, na kumbe nagwe ho wuzimanyile zizi zose.
23 Nagwe wetuma zono si zikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu wa ku wulanga, hambi nha kuvisola vikombe hamonga ne zibakuli, vyono sogo yavisola mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Hanyuma gwegwe ne wawaha, ne wagolece zako, hamonga ne wajitulwa wako, muwa kuvitumamila so wung'wela divayi, akuno mukuyikumya milungu yono minozwe ne zahabu, ne fweza, ne shaba hamonga ne cuma ne mabici ne mabwe, milungu yono siyihulikaga hamba kuwona yaye, yono siyimanyile hamba cinji co cose. Hamba si wusacile kumupela lilumbo ne cogopo u Mulungu mono yena no muhe wako mu mawoko gakwe, na mono kakulongozaga muli zose zono wukwituma.
24 Ku soko yiyo, holeka Mulungu kawutuma muwoko ho wandice zinghani zizino.

25 “Ne zinghani zono zali zandicigwe zali zo zino: MENE, MENE, TEKELI, PELESI.
26 Ne nhunizo yayo yoyi, MENE, wo wutya, Mulungu kaviwala vilo vyo wutemi wako, kakondya kuvidumya.
27 TEKELI, wo wutya, wakondya kupimwa mu muzani, nagwe wejela vyono wacepuka muno.
28 PELESI, wo wutya, wutemi wako wagawanulwa wapegwa Wamedi ne Wapelisiya.”
29 Lo lulo lulo Belishaza yakalajiliza wetumami wakwe, wamuvwalize Danyeli, kanzo ye citemi-temi ye liwala lyo lugandu, no kumuvwaliza mu singo luvumbe lwa zahabu lwe lilumbo. Yakamunoza yawe munhu we kadatu muli wono wena wulajilize wa mu wutemi wuwo.
30 Na mu cilo cico, Belishaza mutemi we Wababiloni yakawulagwa.
31 Hobaho Daliyo u Mumedi yakawusola wutemi wuwo, nayo lizika lyakwe yali yaficile myaka makumi mutandatu na myejete.


Danyeli 6

Wuhile wubi wo kumuwulaga Danyeli

1 Daliyo yakawona viswanu yawawice walongola, yigana limonga na makumi mejete, ho webate wulongola mu wutemi wakwe.
2 Muli wawo kanyhi, yakawahagulila Danyeli na wanji wayagwe wejete, wawe walanjilizi we walongola wawo wanji, ho wawe kulavya liwalo kuli mwene Danyeli ne wayagwe, ho sawo yo mutemi yilece kwajilila.
3 Danyeli yakalajila witumami wuswanu kuciza wawaha wayagwe, hamonga ne walongola. Na vyono yalajila milimo miswanu kuciza wayagwe, ku soko yiyo, mutemi yakawona viswanu yamunoze yawe yo muwaha mu wutemi wakwe wose.
4 Hobaho i walongola, hamonga ne wawaha, wakalondola nzila yo kuluhodanga lukumyo lwa Danyeli mu mulimo wakwe. Ninga si wasuma, hambi Danyeli nha yali mwihuwilwa, kanyhi siyetumaga nghani 'mbi hamba yisutya lyo lyose.
5 Holeka wakalongana weneco wose wakatya, “Nye hamba si cosuma kutuga nghani yo yose 'mbi muli mwene ho cimusonjeze, langa hono cilondole nghani yono vidomile no wulajilize wo Mulungu wakwe.”

6 Hobaho wakekonghola, wakabita kuli mutemi, wakamutya, “Gwe go Mutemi Daliyo, wuwumipe cibitilila!
7 Ase wose mono cikulongoza mu wutemi wako, co walanjilizi, ne walongola ne walamuzi, ne wanyamhala wa mu matumbi, na wose wono wena wulajilize na wose wa mu wulongola wuwu, cilumbaniza vyono, Agwe go mutemi, yikunojeye wulavye wulajilize, kanyhi wuwutyatilile viswanu. Alu wulavye lilajilizo wutye, mu vilo makumi madatu, kusina munhu mono kosuma kulomba nghani yo yose kuli mulungu yunji, hamba kuli munhu yunji wo wose, langa hono si kwako du gwegwe go mutemi. No munhu wo wose mono kobena wulajilize wuwo, yimunojeye yagumilwe mu mhango yono yimemile zisimba.
8 Holeka 'lu, 'gwe mutemi, wulavye lilajilizo lili, wuliwicile ne citanjilo ho liwe ne vilungo, lilece kukawanika, nha vyono winha wulajilize we Wamedi ne Wapelisiya wono si wukawanikaga.”
9 Lulo Mutemi Daliyo yakawuwicila citanjilo wulajilize wuwo.
10 Nayo Danyeli yali yeza kumanya vyono lilajilizo lilyo lyawicilwa ne citanjilo, yakawa kubita ku kaya yakwe. Na mu cinyumba ca kucanyha ku golofa ya mu kaya yakwe, cali cina madilisha gono vyali vidomile na Yelusalemi. Baho, yakafugama ha madilisha ge nyumba yakwe, vyono gali gali mwazi, yakamulomba Mulungu nghanghulo yidatu kwe zuwa, nha vyono yali yazoweye.

11 Nawo i walugu wa Danyeli, wali weza kumuwona yakumulomba Mulungu wakwe,
12 hodu wakekongholela wose hamonga, wakatuluka kumusonjeza Danyeli kuli mutemi, wakatya, “Gwe mutemi, wawicila citanjilo mwi 'lajilizo, vyono, mu vilo makumi madatu kweze kusina munhu wo wose mono komulomba mulungu yunji, hamba munhu yunji, hono si kuli 'gwe du. Na hono yejele munhu mono si kokamisa lilajilizo lili, yuyo yagumilwe mu mhango yono yimemile zisimba.” Mutemi yakawapitula yakawatya, “Heya, ne lilajilizo vyo likutya, vyono wulajilize we Wamedi ne Wapelisiya si wukawanikaga.”
13 Hobaho wakamulonjela mutemi wakamutya, “Danyeli yumonga wa muli wahamywa ku wulawilila Yuda, hamba si yakuwukumya wuwaha wako, hamba kuwibata no wulajilize wono walavya. Mwene kamuhembaga du Mulungu wakwe, nghanghulo yidatu nhondo zose.”
14 Lo lulo lulo mutemi yali yeza kuzihulika zizi, yakakalala muno muno, yakalondola nzila yo kumuponya Danyeli. Yakajendelela kuyilondola nzila, sunga ne lizuwa likaswa.
15 Hanyuma wanhu wawa, wakamuziza kanyhi mutemi, wakawuza wakamutya, “Gwe mutemi, wumanyile manyimanyi nha vyono, u wulajilize no wutemye we Wamedi na we Wapelisiya, wono wakondya kuwikwa no mutemi, vyono si wukawanikaga yaye.”

16 Hobaho mutemi yakalajiliza vyono Danyeli yebatwe. Hobaho Danyeli yakawa kwibatwa, yakagumilwa mu mhango ye zisimba. Mutemi yakamulonjela Danyeli yakamutya, “Ale, u Mulungu wako mono kumwitumamilaga nhondo zose, yo yuyo mono kokuponya.”
17 Hobaho, u mulomo we mhango wukawa kudindwa ne libwe yiwaha muno. Nayo mutemi, yakawa kutajila citanjilo cakwe mweneco, hamonga na ce wawaha wakwe mucanyha ye libwe, ho munhu wo wose yalece kuza no kumuponya Danyeli.
18 Hobaho mutemi yakawa kuwuya ku kaya yakwe yo wutemi, ninga mu cilo cico, siyagona nhongo yaye, hamba kulya ndigwa, hamba cinji co cose co kumusindamika.
19 Mutemi yakelawa hakali mitondo muno muno, yakabita ku mhango.
20 Yaza kufika ha mhango, yakacemeleza ko wukalale yakutya, “Danyeli, 'gwe go mwitumami wo Mulungu mumi! Wule, u Mulungu mono kumwitumamilaga kwe cogopo muno muno, kasuma kukuponya mu zisimba?”
21 Danyeli nayo yakamupitula yakamutya, “Gwe mutemi wuwumipe cibitilila!
22 Mulungu kandetela Malayika mono kazidinda milomo zisimba, ho zilece kuhawanga. Kanoza nhavivyo, vyono kawona ane sina yisutya lyo lyose kuli mwene, hamba kuli'gwe, go mutemi.”

23 Mutemi yakasangalala muno muno, yakalajiliza wamulavyanize Danyeli mu mhango. Hobaho wakamulavyaniza Danyeli kunze. Nayo Danyeli vyono yali yamwihuwiye Mulungu, yakejela yasina hamba nghomele yo yose mu muwili wakwe.
24 Lo lulolulo mutemi yakalajiliza webatwe, wawalya wose wono wamusonjeza Danyeli. Nawo wali weza kwibatwa, wakagumilwa wose mu mhango ye zisimba hamonga ne wagolece zawo, ne wana wawo. Nazo zisimba zikawahawanga no kuwabenanga mafupa gawo lono wali wakali hamba sinafika kusi ye mhango.
25 Baho mutemi Daliyo, yakawandicila baluwa zinghungugo zose, ne zimbeyu zose, na we milonjele yose ya kono'nze, yakawatya, “Mulamu kuli 'nye!”
26 Nene Daliyo, nghumulajilizenyi anye wose wa mu wutemi wangu, vyono wanhu wose, munojeye mumogope no kumupela cogopo Mulungu wa Danyeli.

“Mwene yo Mulungu mumi,

Mono kolotema cibitilila.

No wutemi wakwe, siwulahodanjika yaye,

ne vilungo vyakwe sivyoloduma yaye.

27 Mwene kaponyaga no kudima,

ketumaga mapoto no wizine ku wulanga na kono'nze.

Kamuponya Danyeli, yalece kuwulagwa ne zisimba.”

28 Nayo Danyeli yakamoteswa muno,

mu vilo vyo mutemi Daliyo na Kulo u Mupelisiya.


Danyeli 7

I malimu mane mawaha

1 Ale, mu mwaka mulongozi wono Belishaza yali yatemile ku Babiloni. Nalota ndoto, na mu ndoto yiyo, nghawona wugubulilwe mu cilo cico. Nane nghawa kuyandika ndoto yiyo,
2 nazo zono naziwona mu ndoto mu cilo cico, zo zino,

Mbeho yakukunzaga kulawa mbavu zose, yikwisumaniza mucanyha ye lilenga.
3 Hobaho malimu mawaha mane, gakebuka mucanyha ye lilenga. Nago gose gali gesimajiye hwani yago.
4 Lilongozi lyali linha simba, ninga lyali lina mabawa genha ga numba. Nane lono nalangaga, mabawa galyo, gakapunyulwa mazoya gayo. Lilimu lilyo likasimywa, likema kwe migulu yalyo myejete nha munhu. Likapegwa mutima nha wa munhu.

5 Ne lilimu lye kejete, lyali linhe dubu. Nalyo lyali limile kwe migulu yalyo ya ku nyuma. Kanyhi likawa lilumile mafupa madatu ga mbavu mu meno galyo. Hanyuma likaza lizi likalitya, “Winuce wulye nyama nyinji nha vyono wukusuma.”
6 Na lono nali nghali nanjile, hobaho nghawona kanyhi lilimu linji. Ne lilimu lilyo nalyo lyali lihwanile suwi, ninga lyene lyali lina mabawa mane mu mugongo walyo, genha mabawa ga ndeje, lina mitwe mine, likapegwa wulajilize.
7 Na lono nali nghali kanyhi nanjile, nghawona lilimu lye kane, lyali lina vilungo viwaha, lina wusume kanyhi lya kogofya. Nalyo lyali lina meno ga cuma, likalya no kubenanjiza, na vyono vyali visigaye likavikanyanjiza ne migulu yalyo. Nalyo lyali lisimajiye ne malimu mayagwe, kanyhi lyali lina mahembe yikumi.
8 Na lono nagasunjililaga mahembe gaga, nghawona kanyhi yihembe linji yidodo lyono lyakunghumukaga likulawila ha ciligati ye mayagwe. Likagakula i mahembe madatu ga muli gono gali gali baho wulawa mwamulizo. Ne lihembe lilyo lyali lina meso ga munhu, no mulomo wono walongaga ko widunhumuzi muno muno.

Wongomezwe we malimu ga galya mane

9 Na lono nali nghali nanjile, nghawona magoda go wutemi gakuwikwa hanhu hayo. Na mono kekalaga mumi cibitilila, yakekala mwi'goda limonga lya muli magoda gago. Nayo yali yavwalile myenda mizelu swee, kotya seluji. Ne zimvwili zakwe, zali zinha wukunzeje wa ngholo, wuswanu muno. Ne ligoda lyakwe, lyali yinozezwe maguludumu ga moto, gang'alalaga nha moto.
10 Ne cikolongo co moto, cahulumaga cikulawilila mwi'goda lilyo. Na kanyhi hakawa ho wali wanhu maelufu na maelufu wono wakumwitumamila, hamonga ne wanhu waficile mamilyoni na mamilyoni, wono wemile hali mwene wakumwitumamila. Nago mahakama gakawa kwanza milimo yayo yo wu wuling'anizi ne vitabu vikagubulwa.
11 Na lono nali nghali nanjile, nghahulika lihembe lililya lidodo, likwilumba no kwikumya lyeneco. Na lono nali nghali njendeleye kulanga du, nghawona lilimu lililya lye kane likuwulagwa, no mudunhu walyo, wukugumilwa mu malambi go moto no kongojezezwa lukulu.
12 Nago agalya malimu ganji, gakawusizwa vilungo vyago. Ninga gakakombolwa gekale momi cidaha cono gali gatemezwe.

13 Na mu wugubulilwe wuwu ne cilo, nghawona cinhu cinji kanyhi cono cali cihwanile munhu. Nayo munhu yuyo yakawa kusejelela hali'ne, akuno yapilimwe ne mavunde. Hanyuma yakawa kubita kuli mono kekalaga mumi cibitilila, yakelajila kuli mwene.
14 Yakawa kupegwa vilungo ne lilumbo, hamonga no wutemi, i wanhu wose we zinghungugo zose, na we zimbeyu zose, na we milonjele yose, ho wamwitumamile mwene. Ne vilungo vyakwe vyokwikala cibitilila, no wutemi wakwe si wuladuma yaye.

Wugubulilwe wulongozi wukuloloswa

15 No wugubulilwe wuwo wono nawuwona, wukanhinusa muno mu nhumbula yangu, nane nghagazika muno mu mutima wangu.
16 Nghawa kumubitila yumonga wa muli wono wali wemile baho, nghamulomba yandoloseze zose zo wugubulilwe wuwo. Nayo yakanonjela nhunizo yayo yakatya,
17 “Aga i malimu mane mawaha gono wukugawona, gago wo wutemi wune wono hanyuma wotema mono'nze.
18 Nawo i wanhu wo Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye, wolowubocela wutemi, no kutema cibitilila.”
19 Ninga hanyuma yayo, nane nghelowa nizimanye zinji kanyhi zi zilya ze lilimu lye kane, lyono si lyali lihwanile ne mayagwe gose. Lilimu lyono lyogofyaga, lyono lyabenanjiza zimhelembo zalyo kwe makombazala galyo ge shaba, hamonga ne meno galyo ge cuma, no kuvikanyanjiza ne migulu yalyo.
20 Na kanyhi nghasaka ho manye za ga galya i mahembe yikumi, gono gali gali mu mutwe walyo, hamonga na ze lihembe lililya lyono lyakunghumuka no kulawila, no kuganoza mahembe ga galya madatu gagwe. Na vyono kanyhi, lyali lina meso no mulomo, kanyhi likikumya ko widunhumuzi muno muno. Na kanyhi lyali yilimu lya kogofya muno si nhe mayagwe.

21 Na lono nali nghali njendeleye kulanga, lihembe lilyo, likanza kwihoma ne wanhu wo Mulungu, likawasuma.
22 Ninga yu yulya nayo mono kekalaga mumi cibitilila, yakawa kuza, yakawaling'aniza i wanhu wo Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye, mu nghango yo wendo wakwe kuli wene. Ne cipango nha cakondya kuza, co kupegwa wutemi i wanhu wo Mulungu.
23 Nayo munhu yuyo yakawa kundoloseza ze nhunizo yayo, yakatya, “Lilimu lilyo lye kane, wo wutemi we kane wono wolotema mono'nze, nawo wolowa wisimajiye no wutemi wunji wo wose. Nawo woloyitema yinze yose no kuyikanyanjila.
24 Nago agalya i mahembe yikumi, wo watemi yikumi, wono wolotema mu wutemi wuwo. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, kolokuza mutemi yunji kanyhi, mono si yehwanile na wawo watemi walongozi. Nayo mutemi yuyo, kolowasuma i watemi wadatu.
25 No mutemi yuyo, kolomutovula Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye no kuwewonela i wanhu wo Mulungu. Mutemi yuyo kolojela yawakawaniziza i wanhu wo Mulungu, u wulajilize wo wuhembi wawo, hamonga ne zisikukuu zawo. Nawo i wanhu wo Mulungu wolowa hasi yo wulajilize wakwe, myaka midatu na myezi mutandatu.
26 Nago mahakama ga ku wulanga, go gololavya u wuling'anizi, gomuwusiza vilungo vyakwe no kumongomezeza lukulu.
27 Baho, i vilungo no wuwaha wo wutemi wose wa mono'nze, wolopegwa i wanhu wo Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye. Ne vilungo vyo wutemi wawo, si viladuma yaye. Ne watemi wose wa mono'nze, wolowetumamila no kuwahulika.”
28 Awu wo mudumo we nghani yiyi. Ne nghani yiyi yikanyogofya muno, sunga no wusu wangu wukawa wusina nyemo. Ninga nghani yiyo, nghayiwika mu nhumbula yangu.


Danyeli 8

Wugubulilwe wa Danyeli we Nduwilu, ne Mhene

1 Na mu mwaka we kadatu wo wutemi wa Belishaza, nghawona wugubulilwe wunji kanyhi.
2 Na mumo mu wugubulilwe wuwo, nghewona nili mwi'kaya lya Susa, lya mu yisi ya Elamu lyono lipilimwe ne majinji. Nane nali nimile he 'kolongo lya Ulayi.
3 Na lono nali nili baho he kolongo lya Ulayi, nghawona nduwilu yono yina mahembe matali muno mejete. Na muli mahembe gago, limonga lyali yitali si nhe liyagwe, kanyhi lyali yipya, lyono lyacindika lono liyagwe lyali lyakondya kucindika.
4 Nane nghawa niyilanjiliye nduwilu yiyo, yikucinya ne mahembe gayo ku lubavu lwa kumwezi, na kusumuka, na kutakama. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, hali hasina nimu hamba monga, yono yasuma kuyimila, hamba kwiponya mu vilungo vye nduwilu yiyo. Langa yetuma nha vyono yali yendile yeneco, yikekumya mu nhumbula yayo yeneco.

5 Na lono nali nghali kwihwesa nghani yiyi nhunizo yayo nyhici lo. Hodu hobaho mvulati nghali yikawa kuza yifunyhile, yikulawilila ku lubavu lwa kumwezi, yali yimanyhile muno muno, sunga ne migulu yayo hamba si yapamizaga hasi yaye. Nayo mvulati yiyo, yali yina yihembe lyono lilawiye haciligati ye meso gayo.
6 Ale, du, mvulati yiyo yikawa kuza yihutile, yikawa kubita kono ko yili nduwilu yono nayiwona yimile behi ne likolongo, yikayitowanga nduwilu yiyo kwe vilungo muno muno.
7 Nane nghawa vyo niyilanjiliye mvulati yiyo du, vyono yikuyitowanga nduwilu. Mvulati yiyo yali yina yikuwo yiwaha, yikagabenanga sunga ne mahembe gagalya mejete ge nduwilu. Nayo nduwilu yali yisina vilungo, si yasuma hamba kwitaza bwete ng'o. Holeka yikagumilwa ho hasi no kukanyanjizwa, hakawa hasina hamba munhu mono kosuma kuyiponya yaye.
8 Nayo mvulati, yikedunhumula muno muno, na ku soko yo widunhumuzi wayo, lihembe lyayo likawa kubeneka.
9 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, likacindika kanyhi yihembe linji lidodo, mwi'hembe limonga lya muli gagalya mahembe mane. Ne vilungo vyayo vikatandala sunga ko ku lubavu lwa kutakama, no lubavu lwa kucilima, sunga ko ku yisi swanu muno.
10 Naco cihembe cico, cikawiluka muno, cikawa ne vilungo vyo kwihoma ne lipuga lya ku wulanga, nha cinji, zinyelezi, sunga likazigumila zinji ho hasi, likazikanyaga.
11 Hamba nhavivyo, likamwavufya u Muwaha we lipuga lya ku wulanga, likemila sunga ne vilavywa vye makumbiko ge nhondo zose, vyono wamulavizyaga, likayihodanga ne Nyumba yo Mulungu.

12 Na baho ha Nyumba yo Mulungu, i wanhu wakahanoza hakawa hanhu ho kwitumila wubi. Hakawa si hanhu kanyhi ho kulavizya vilavywa vye makumbiko vye nhondo zose. Wanhu wakawuleka sunga no wuhembi we mbeka. Lihembe lilyo likasuma kwituma nghani yo yose yono lyeneco lyasakaga kwituma.
13 Hanyuma yayo, nghahulika malayika yakumuwuza malayika muyagwe, yakutya, “Alo zizi zono zijeye mu wugubulilwe, ho zojendelela sunga nili lo? Hamba wuwu wubi wono wukuwimila u wulavye we makumbiko, nawo wojendelela sunga nili? Ma, lipuga lya ku wulanga nalyo, hamonga ne Nyumba yo Mulungu, navyo ho vyojendelela kukanyanjilwa sunga nili?”
14 Nghahulika malayika muyagwe nayo yakupitula yakutya, “Nghani zizi, zojendelela du, sunga vipulute vilo elufu monga na yigana limonga na makumi mahano. Na mu vilo vivi vyose, howa hasina wulavye we vilavywa vye makumbiko ge hakali mitondo ne cinyamihe. Ale, Nyumba yo Mulungu nhondo, ho yokwezwa.”

Malayika Gabuliyeli yakulolosa zo Wugubulilwe

15 Nane nghasaka ho manye nhunizo yo wugubulilwe wuwo, na kumbe munhu yunji yakema nhinicizo ha mwande yangu.
16 Hanyuma kanyhi nghahulika lizi likucema ku nhumbi ye likolongo lya Ulayi, likutya, “Agwe Gabuliyeli, wumulolosezaje yuyo nhunizo ya zono yaziwene.”
17 Nayo Gabuliyeli yakawa kuza kwima behi nane. Ninga 'ne nghenjilwa no woga wuwaha muno, nghagwa ho hasi.

Nayo Gabuliyeli yakanonjela yakatya, “Gwe go munhu, ale wuhulicizaje wuyimanye nhunizo yayo. Wugubulilwe wuwu, wukulajila ze ndumilizo ye yinze.”
18 Ninga lono yali yakali kulonga, ane nghagwa hasi nghawa kuzilika. Ninga mwene yakanyibata, yakanyinula, yakasimya nghema ne magulu gangu.
19 Hanyuma yakajendelela kutya, “Ane nghukulajila ze likuwo lyo Mulungu, vyono lyolowa linha mu vilo vya ku ndumo. Hambi wugubulilwe wuwo, nha wukulajila zono zolowa ku vilo vya ku ndumo.

20 “Alu, yiyilya nduwilu ye mahembe mejete yono wuyiwene, yiyo, wo wutemi wo Wumedi na Pelisiya.
21 Nayo mvulati yiyo, wo wutemi wo Wugiliki. Nalyo lihembe lyono lili haciligati ye meso, yo mutemi mulongozi.
22 Nago i mahembe mane gono gacindice lono lihembe lilongozi libeneceye, wo watemi wane wono wololawa mwi'kungugo limonga lyono lyologawanuka, kanyhi lyolowa lisina vilungo nha vyono wutemi wulongozi wono wali wina vilungo.

23 “Na lono mudumo we watemi wawa wuwe wasejelela, na kolo wakondya kusocela ho walipicizwe, ale baho ho holowa no mutemi mono kolowa yasina cihuliko, kanyhi mulambi, kanyhi mono kolowa yena mikalile ye cakacaka.
24 Nayo kolowa ne vilungo, kanyhi kololeta wongomele wuwaha, kolomoteswa muli zose zono kolowa kwituma. Kolowongomeza i wanhu we vilungo, hamonga ne wanhu wo Mulungu.
25 Kanyhi mwene vyono musugu, holeka kolomoteswa na mu zinzila zakwe zose zo wulambi. Kolowa mwidunhumuzi, kanyhi kolowongomeza wanhu wenji wono wali wazoweye kwikala wapolile. Kanyhi kolokwihoma no Mutemi we watemi. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, kolobenanjizwa, na holowa hasina munhu wo kumutaziza.
26 Nawo wugubulilwe wono wawona we vilavywa vye makumbiko ge cinyamihe, na ge hakali mitondo, azo za mbeka, nazo zolowa nha vyono wali wakondya kulolosezwa. Ninga agwe wuzikamisaje du mu nhumbula yako, hambi nha zosola vilo vinji muno muno sunga ho zize zificilicile.”
27 Nane Danyeli, nghakalala muno so zizo, nghatamwa no kutamwa vilo vinji muno. Nali neza kutuga ciba, nhondo ho nghawa kubita kuwituma mulimo wangu wono mutemi yali yamhelile niwe kuwituma. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, nghawizina muno wugubulilwe wuwo, nane si nasuma hamba kuwizuka yaye.


Danyeli 9

Danyeli yakuwalombela wanhu wakwe

1 Na mu mwaka mulongozi wo wutemi wa Daliyo, u mutemi we Wababiloni, wo lulelo lwe Cimedi, u mwana wa Ahasuwelo.
2 Nene Danyeli, nasomaga mu vitabu i visiwanulwe, akuno nghwihwesa ze myaka makumi mupunghati yono Yelusalemi yolowa yili mu wongomezwe, vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulonjela muwizi Yelemiya.
3 Nane nghamulomba Muwaha Mulungu ko winenenyhezi, nghaleka kulya, nghavwala magunila, nghawa kwikala mu mavu.
4 Nghamulomba Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wangu, nghawunena u wubi we wanhu wangu.

Nghalomba nghatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, yo gwegwe Muwaha, nase cikogopile gwegwe du. Nagwe wibate ne cilagane cako, kanyhi wuli lusungu kuli wono wakwendile, no kwituma zono kulajilizaga.

5 “Ase cetuma wubi, kanyhi cili wabi, cituma nghani 'mbi muno. Hambi nha cazilema zinzila zako, cakondya kuzileka ne ziswanu zono wacilajila.
6 Hamba si cawahuliciza sunga ne wetumami wako wawizi, wono wayilonjelaga nghani yako mwi'tagwa lyako kuli watemi wetu, na kuli walongola wetu, na kuli walezi wetu, sunga na kuli likungugo lyose.
7 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, agwe yo gwegwe mono kwitumaga nhondo zose ze ling'ani. Ninga ase yo ciyiletelaga cevu nhondo zose sese co weneco du. Na yiyi yo nghani ya mbeka kuli sese wose mono cikaye mu Yuda na mu Yelusalemi, sunga na kuli Waisilayeli wose, wono waweyagaziza mu ma'si ge behi na go kutali, vyono wali si wehuwilwa kuli'gwe.
8 Sunga ne watemi wetu, hamonga ne walongola wetu, sunga ne walezi wetu, wose wetuma nghani za cevu no kwituma wubi hali'gwe, go Muwaha Mulungu.
9 Ninga agwe, wuli lusungu, kanyhi wuli munenezi, hamba ciwe ase cakondya kukugaluka no kukuleka.
10 Nagwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wetu, lono wacilonjelela nha vyono, yicinojeye mikalile yetu, yityatye nha vyono winha wulajilize wako wono wacipelela muli wetumami wako i wawizi, ninga ase hamba si cakuhuliciza yaye.
11 Ne Waisilayeli wose, wakondya 'lu kuwubena wulajilize wako, hamba si wakuzityatya kanyhi yaye zono wawalonjela. Holeka ase cili wasutyi du hali'gwe, na ku soko yiyo, holeka nagwe wukaciletela malijito gose gago gono mandice mu Wulajilize wa Musa, u mwitumami wako.
12 Hambi 'gwe nha wakondya lu kwituma zono watya vyono kocitendela 'se, hamonga ne walongola wetu. Wukayilipiciza Yelusalemi kwe magazo mawaha gono gakali hamba si gananozwa mwi'kaya linji lyo lyose mono yinze yiyi.
13 Wukucilipiciza ko wulipicizwe wose, wono wukulongwa mu Wulajilize wa Musa. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wetu, nase cikali napituka, ciwulece wubi wetu hamba kuyityatya i mbeka yako, ho cikwendeze gwegwe.
14 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wetu, wali wiwikanize wucilipicize, na vyo wanoza. Hambi 'gwe nha kwitumaga ze ling'ani nhondo zose. Ninga ase hamba kukuhulika yaye.

15 “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu go Mulungu wetu, agwe walajila vilungo vyako, wukawawusa wanhu wako mu yisi ya Misili, ne vilungo vyako vikali vikumbuciywe. Ase mbeka cikusutizya, kanyhi cikuwuvumila wubi wetu.
16 Agwe wacidima mu vilo vyono vipulute, holeka cikukunena, wulece kuyikolelwa likuwo Yelusalemi. Hambi nha lye 'kaya lyako, litunda lyako lyono lyelile. Hambi i wanhu wose we ma'si gono gacipilime, nha wakuyihosa Yelusalemi, hamonga ne wanhu wako, ku soko yo wubi wetu, hamonga no wubi we walezi wetu wono wetuma.
17 Holeka 'gwe go Mulungu wetu, nghukunena wuhulice wulombe wangu, hamonga no wunene wangu. Nghukunena wuyinozeleze Nyumba yako, yono yali yakondya kuhodangwa. Wuyinozeleze, ne wanhu wose ho wamanye vyono agwe yo gwegwe go Mulungu.
18 Holeka nghukunena wucihulicize 'gwe go Mulungu, wucilanje, wuwuwone no wugaye wetu vyono cikugaya, ne magazo ge likaya lyono walicemela litagwa lyako. Nase cikukulomba, hambi nha cimanyile vyono agwe wuli lusungu, si cikukulomba vyono ase cituma ze ling'ani yaye.
19 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, cikukunena wucihulice. Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wucinenele wubi wetu. Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wucihulice, kanyhi wucipele zono cikukulomba. Na mu nzila yiyo, wanhu wose ho womanya vyono wuli Mulungu. Holeka wulece kutinya yaye! Hambi likaya lili nha lyako du gwegwe, hamonga ne wanhu wono wali mugati yalyo.”

Gabuliyeli yakulolosa Wuwizi

20 Nane Danyeli, nghajendelela du kulomba, nghunena wubi wangu, hamonga no wubi we wanhu wangu Waisilayeli, no kumulomba u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wangu yayinozeleze Nyumba yakwe.
21 Na lono nali nghali njendeleye mu wulombe wangu, hodu yu yulya Gabuliyeli mono namuwona mu wugubulilwe wangu wulongozi, yakawa kuza yaguluce, yakatima hono nali nimile. Ne madaha gago, gali go madaha go kulavya vilavywa vye makumbiko ge cinyamihe.
22 Ale, Gabuliyeli, yakandoloseza yakatya, “Gwe Danyeli, ane nghuziza, ho nghutaze, wuwizuce wuwizi.
23 Wukumbuciye vyono lono wanziza kumulomba Mulungu, nayo yakakupitula. Mwene yakwendile, holeka niza ho nghulonjele lipitulo lyakwe kuli'gwe. Holeka nghukunena wuhulicizaje muno, lono neza kukuloloseza wugubulilwe wuwo.

24 “Mu myaka makumi mupunghati kali mupunghati, wo wutali we cipango cono Mulungu yaciwika, co kuwimila wubi, hamonga ne masutya, muli wanhu wako na mwi'kaya lyako lyono lyelile. U wubi wolonenelwa, ne ling'ani ye cibitilila yolotema. Na mwi'pango lilyo, u wugubulilwe hamonga no wuwizi, ho vilawa vya mbeka. Ne Nyumba yo Mulungu nayo, yilasiwanulwa.
25 Holeka wuzimanyaje zizi, kanyhi wuzizuce, vyono ku wulawilila mu madaha lono u wulajilize wulalajilizwa, nha vyono Yelusalemi yizengwe kanyhi, sunga mu mapango gono kolokuziza u mulongola muwaha u muhagulwa wo Mulungu, yolowa yakondya kupuluta majuma mupunghati. Na Yelusalemi mu madaha gago, yolowa yakondya kuzengwa wupya, hamonga ne mabalabala gayo, yolowa yakondya kuzenjelwa sunga ne magomawakali gono gali vilungo. Nayo yolokondya kuzengwa mu majuma makumi mutandatu na nyejete. Ninga i vilo vivyo, vyolowa vilo vyo wugaye.
26 Na ku ndumilizo ye vilo vivyo, u mulongola muhagulwa wo Mulungu, kolowulagwa mbula soko. Nalyo likaya hamonga ne Nyumba yo Mulungu, vyolokongomezwa ne wasilikale wo mutemi we vilungo. Nayo ndumilizo yolokuza nhe lihwazi. Kanyhi wolokuza wulugu hamonga ne magazo, vyono Mulungu yakondya kuviwikaniza.
27 Nayo mutemi yuyo, kolokwilumbaniza ne wanhu, myaka mupunghati. Na kolo vikupuluta nusu ye vilo vivyo, kologawusiza lukulu makumbiko, hamonga ne vilavywa. Ne Nghani yo Wogofye muno, yolowikwa mu cizuli ce Nyumba yo Mulungu, nayo yolokwikala baho, sunga lono kolokuziza mono yayiwika baho, vitang'anile no wongomele wono Mulungu yakondya kumuwajila.”


Danyeli 10

Wugubulilwe wa Danyeli he kolongo lya Tigilisi

1 (Na mu mwaka we kadatu wa Kulo u mutemi wa Pelisiya, Danyeli mono yacemwa Beliteshaza, yagubulilwa nghani. Ne nghani yiyo yali ya mbeka, ninga yali nghani nghamu muno ku wizuce. Ninga yikaloloswa kuli mwene mu nzila yo wugubulilwe.)

2 Na mu mapango gago, ane Danyeli, nali nili mu cililo majuma madatu.
3 Nane mu vilo vivyo vyose, si nalizaga ndigwa swanu, hamba sinalizaga nyama yaye, hamba kung'wa divayi, hamba kucana zimvwili zangu, sunga gakapuluta majuma madatu.
4 Na mwi'zuwa lye makumi mejete na yine lya mu mwezi mulongozi wa mu mwaka wuwo, ane nali nimile ha nyenje ye likolongo liwaha lya Tigilisi.
5 Nali neza kugalamila kucanyha, nghawona munhu yunji yavwalile myenda ya bafuta, na mu ciwino, yewopile musipi wa zahabu.
6 No muwili wakwe, wang'alalaga nhe 'bwe lyono likucemwa Zabalajadi. No wusu wakwe, wang'alalaga kotya limuli, ne meso gakwe genha tala. Miwoko yakwe ne migulu, vyang'alalaga kotya shaba yono nghusulwe muno muno. Ne lizi lyakwe nalyo, lyahulicikaga kotya nhe lizi lye 'puga yiwaha lya wanhu.

7 Nane nene Danyeli du, mono nawuwona wugubulilwe wuwo nili wene. Ninga i wanhu wono wali wali hamonga nane, hamba si wawona cinji, langa wene wakatuga woga wuwaha, wakatiza, wakawa kwihundika.
8 Wakawa kundeka nene du nili wene, nane nghawa niyilanjiliye yiyo nghani yo wizine. Ninga nane nghawa sina vilungo muli'ne, sunga no wusu wangu wukesima so woga, sunga hakawa hasina munhu wo wose mono yali yanhanje vyono yo nene Danyeli.
9 Nane nali neza kulihulika lizi lyo munhu yuyo, nghagwa hasi wugubama nzilice.
10 Ninga hanyuma hadodo, u munhu yuyo yakanyibata no muwoko, yakawa kunyinula, yakawa kusimya fugame, ne miwoko yangu yibate hasi. Ninga mwi'daha lilyo, nali nghali du nghucicima.
11 Nayo malayika yakanonjela yakatya, “Gwe Danyeli, u Mulungu yakwendile muno. Holeka alu winukaje wime, wuhulicize viswanu kuli zono nghusaka nghulonjele, zono zindetile kuli'gwe.” Nayo malayika yali yeza kukondya kuzilonga zizo, hodu, nghawa kwinuka, ninga akuno vyo nali nghali nghucicima du.

12 Hanyuma malayika yakajendelela kulonga nane, yakatya, “Gwe Danyeli, wulece kogopa. Hambi Mulungu nha yakondya kuwuhulika wulombe wako, ku wulawilila lono wamulombela ha lulongozi, wukenenenyheza, ho wutuje wizuce. Holeka nane niza ho nghuletele lipitulo lyo wulombe wako.
13 Ninga malayika mulongola wo wutemi wa Pelisiya, yali yanyimiye vilo makumi mejete na cimonga. Ninga hanyuma Mikayeli, muyagwe ne walongola we Wamalayika, yakawa kuza kunhaziza, hambi nha nali nalekwa du nili wene mu yisi ya Pelisiya.
14 Nane lu, niza kuli'gwe, ho nghwizushe kuli zono zolowejelela wanhu wako mu vilo vyono vikuza. Na wuwu wo wugubulilwe we vilo vyono vikuza.”
15 Nayo malayika lono yalongaga zizo, ane kuno, nghawa nanjile meso gangu hasi du sina yo kulonga.
16 Hanyuma malayika yuyo mono yejelaga yehwanile munhu, yakawugolola muwoko wakwe, yakamhamiza mulomo wangu Nane nghamutya, “Gwe muwaha, wugubulilwe wuwu, wukunyhepula vilungo vyangu, si nghusuma no kwiyimila ho ndece kucicima.
17 Alu 'ne nha nehwana nho muwanda mono yemile ha mwande yo mutwawe. Alu ho nosuma kulongana nhawule nagwe? Hambi nha neza sina vilungo, no muhe wangu wukudinda.”
18 Hanyuma kanyhi malayika yakanyibata, na hobaho vilungo vyangu vikawa kumbuyila kanyhi.
19 Yakawa kunyitya, “U Mulungu yakwendile 'gwe Danyeli, holeka wulece kogofywa na cinji co cose, hamba kuwa na mahwele.”

Nayo yali yeza kukondya kuzilonga zizi, nane nghewona nhondo vilungo vyangu vyose vyakondya kumbuyila kanyhi, nane nghamutya, “Gwe muwaha wangu, wulonje zono wina, hambi nha wakondya kumhela vilungo.”

20 Nayo malayika yakanyitya, “Mwene Danyeli, wumanyile 'mbi cono cilecile nghukuziza? Ane nghuziza, ho nghugubulile zono zandicigwe mu Citabu, ze Mbeka. Nalu yinojeye mbuye kanyhi ku Pelisiya, cikalewane na malayika mulindilizi wa ku Pelisiya. Na kolo zizo zakondya, ale, nayo malayika mulindilizi wa ku Wugiliki kokuza.
21 Na lulu nhondo, nowa sina yunji mono kosuma kumhela wutazi, langa Mikayeli du, malayika mulindilizi we Waisilayeli, yuyo du yo komhela wutazi.”


Danyeli 11

1 Ninga ane no mweneco ku lubavu lwangu, mu mwaka mulongozi wo mutemi Daliyo Mumedi, neyilavya nimusikamye no kumupela vilungo.
2 Holeka aluno zono nokulonjela, zo ze mbeka.

Wutemi wa Misili na Siliya

Nayo malayika yakatya, “Ho wali watemi wanji kanyhi wadatu, wono wotema mu Pelisiya. No mutemi we kane, mono kowatyatya, yuyo kowa mugoli muno si nhe wanji wose. Nayo kolo yakutuga vilungo so wugoli wakwe, kokwisumaniza no wutemi wo Wugiliki.
3 Hanyuma kanyhi, u mutemi we vilungo nayo kolokwinuka. Nayo kolotema kwe vilungo no kwituma nha vyono yakusaka mweneco.
4 Ninga ku soko yo wuteme wakwe we vilungo, wutemi wakwe wolokwigawa no kulawa hane. Ne watemi wanji wono si wali mu lulelo lwakwe, wawo wo wolowuhala wutemi wakwe. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, si wolowa ne vilungo nha vyono yali yenha mwene.

5 “No mutemi wa Misili, yuyo langa yo kolowa yali vilungo. Na muli yumonga wa muli we citung'ati wakwe, yuyo yo kolowa ne vilungo kuciza, kanyhi yo kolotema wutemi wuwaha muno.
6 Ninga kolo yeza kupuluta myaka micefu, yuyo mutemi wa Misili, vyolokwilunza no mutemi wa Siliya, kolomukombola sunga no mwanagwe we cicekulu, vitole no mutemi wa Siliya. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, wilunze wawo wuwo, si wolojendelela vilo vinji ng'o. Vyono mwi'zuwa linji, yuyo muhinza, no mulume wakwe no mwanagwe, hamonga ne wetumami wono vikabitana nayo, wose wolowulagwa.
7 Na hanyuma ya zizo, yumonga wa muli wanduguze, yo kolotema. Nayo hanyuma kololihoma lipuga lye wasilikale wo mutemi wa Siliya, yenjile mu magomawakali gawo no kuwasuma.
8 Nayo kolosola vihwani vye milungu yawo, hamonga ne viya vyawo vye zahabu na vye fweza, vyono wali wavisiwanuliye yiyo milungu yawo, yavitegule vyose ku Misili. Na kolo yikupuluta myaka micefu yo mulamu,
9 nayo mutemi wa Siliya koloyihoma Misili, ninga kolowingwa yawuye ku yisi yakwe kanyhi.

10 “Nawo i wana wo mutemi wa Siliya, wolokonghola yipuga yiwaha muno lye wasilikale, wewikanize so wulugu. Nayo yumonga wa muli wene, kolokwipongholeza nhe lihwazi, yakagakundujize magomawakali ge walugu.
11 Nayo mutemi wa Misili, ku soko ye nghani yiyo, hobaho kolokolwa ne likuwo yiwaha, yabite vikehome no mutemi wa Siliya. Nayo mutemi wa Misili, kololiteka lipuga lyose lye wasilikale wo mutemi wa Siliya.
12 Nayo mutemi wa Misili kolokwidunhumula so wusume wakwe, hamonga no winji muno we wasilikale wa Siliya wono yawawulaje. Ninga hamba si kojendelela nhondo zose kuwa no wusume wuwo.

13 “Nayo mutemi wa Siliya, kolowuya yakakonghole lipuga yiwaha muno lye wasilikale, wacizize i walongozi. Na kolo vipango vyafika, kolobita kanyhi ku Misili yena ne lipuga yiwaha lye wasilikale vitung'ati.
14 Nawo wanhu wenji wolomugaluka mutemi wa Misili. Sunga ne wanhu wanji i wagazi, wa muli wanhu wa mu nghungugo yako, Danyeli, nawo wologaluka, ku soko yo wugubulilwe wono wawuwene. Ninga hamba nhavivyo vyo wolosumwa du.
15 Ale baho, nayo mutemi wa Siliya, ho kololipilima likaya lye magomawakali, no kulisola. Nawo wasilikale wa Misili, hamba si wolojendelela kanyhi no wihomi. Sunga ne wasilikale wawo vitung'ati, si wolowa ne vilungo kanyhi vyo kwihomela.
16 Nayo mutemi wa Siliya kolowatendela wanhu wa Misili, nha vyono kolowa yendile mweneco, na holowa hasina witazize. Kanyhi kolokwinjila mu yisi ye cilagane, no kuyitemela lukulu.

17 “Nayo mutemi wa Siliya, kolotyatilila, yetumamile wasilikale wakwe wose. Na kolo yakubita, si kolowongomeza yaye wutemi we walugu wakwe, langa kolonoza mulamu, kanyhi welunze nayo. Nayo mutemi wa Siliya, kolomukombola mwanagwe we cicekulu vitole no mutemi wa Misili. Ninga mahweso gakwe go wihomi, si goloficilicila kanyhi yaye.
18 Ninga hanyuma ya zizo, mutemi wa Siliya, kologahoma makungugo menji mu nzila ye lilenga. Nayo kolowasuma wenji wa muli wawo. Ninga hamonga na zizo, citung'ati yumonga mujenzi, yo kolomusuma, yawudumye no widunhumuzi wakwe wose, yamunyetye no widunhumuzi wakwe mutemi wa Siliya.
19 Nayo mutemi wa Siliya, kolowuya ku yisi yakwe mweneco mu magomawakali gakwe. Ninga kolowa yakondya kusumilwa lukulu. Na wuwo wo wolowa wo mudumo wakwe.

20 “Ninga kolotema mutemi yunji kanyhi mu nyuma yakwe, mono kolotegula mwitumami wakwe yabite yakawatoweze wanhu kodi, ho yonjeleze sawo ya mu wutemi wakwe. Ninga vilo si vyolopuluta vinji yaye, mutemi yuyo kolowulagwa. Ninga si kolowulajilwa hazelu yaye, hamba si kolowulajilwa mu wulugu ng'o.”

U mutemi mubi wa Siliya

21 Nayo malayika yakajendelela kulolosa yakutya, “Nayo mutemi wa Siliya mono kotyatya, kolowa munhu mubi muno muno, mono kolowa yasina lilumbo lyono limunojeye yawe mutemi. Langa kolowa munhu mono yabedegaywe muno, ninga kolowusola wutemi mu nzila yo wiyendezi.
22 No munhu wo wose mono kolojela kumwimila, hamba yawe mukohani Muwaha wo Mulungu, munhu yuyo kolofwagulwa no kuwusizwa lukulu.
23 Na mu wungwele wakwe, kowakonga-konga we zinghungugo zinji, nayo kolocila kuwa ne vilungo muno, hamba yiwe mwene yatemile mu nghungugo ndodo du.
24 Nayo koloyihoma nhinicizo yisi yono yina wugoli. Kanyhi kolokwituma nghani zono hamba ne walezi wakwe si wazituma yaye. Hanyuma kolozisola zisawo, zono yazitecile kuko ku wulugu, yawagawile i wanhu wakwe. Kanyhi kolokwiwikaniza ho yagakumulanje magomawakali, ninga kolowa yaceleza, i cipango cakwe colowa cakondya malika.

25 “Nayo kololiwikaniza lipuga yiwaha muno lye wasilikale, akuno yasina woga, yabite yakamuhome u mutemi wa Misili. Nayo mutemi wa Misili, kolokwiwikaniza ne lipuga lyakwe yiwaha lye wasilikale, ho vihome no mutemi wa Siliya. Ninga mutemi wa Misili, si kolomusuma mutemi wa Siliya. Hambi mutemi wa Misili nha kolokongwa-kongwa, ho yalece kuwufisiliza wuhile wakwe.
26 Ne walonjelezi wakwe mweneco, wo wolomuwulaga. Na wenji wa muli wasilikale wakwe wolowulagwa, ne lipuga lyakwe lyose lye wasilikale, wolofwagulilwa lukulu.
27 Ne watemi wawo wejete, wolokwikala wahanje ndigwa ha meza monga. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, mwi'pango lilyo, i mitima yawo, yowa yikali mu wihwesa wituma zimbi. Kanyhi wolowa kwilambila munhu no muyagwe. Ninga si wolocituga cono wakucisaka, hambi lidaha lyayo, nha lyowa likali si naza yaye.
28 Nayo mutemi wa Siliya, kolowuya kwakwe hamonga ne sawo yono yatecile, ninga kuno yena no wuhile wo kuyongomeza dini ye wanhu wo Mulungu. Kolokwituma nha vyono kolosaka mweneco, hanyuma hodu, kolowuya ku yisi yakwe mweneco.

29 “Ninga hanyuma yayo kanyhi, kolobita yakayihome Misili. Ninga muhinzo wuwu, nghani zolokwisima.
30 Hambi i Waloma nha wolokuziza mu zingalawa zawo, wamwimile, nayo kolokogofywa.

“Nayo kolowuya ku yisi yakwe yakoligwe ne likuwo muno muno, na kolojela kuyongomeza yisi ye wanhu wo Mulungu. Kolotyatya mahala ge wanhu wono wakondya kuyileka yisi yiyo.
31 Na wanji wa muli wasilikale wakwe, woloyavufya Nyumba yo Mulungu. Wolovimila vilavywa vye makumbiko, nawo wolowika I wifyo, mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
32 Na mu nzila yo kumukonga-konga mutemi, kolokwitujila watazi wenji wa muli wono wolowa wakondya kuyileka dini yawo. Ninga awalya wono wowa wakali wakumutyatya Mulungu, awo langa wolokwihoma nayo.
33 Ne walongola we wanhu wono wena mahala, wolowejiza wanhu wenji. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, wanji wa muli wene wolowulagwa mu wulugu, hamba wologumilwa mu moto, na wanji wolosolwa wawopwe mu jela.
34 Ninga mu mapango gago lono wuwulaji wolowa wukali wujendeleye, i wanhu wo Mulungu wolotuga wutazi wudodo, ne wenji wono wowa kwilunza nawo, wowa kwilunza ko wukoci du, ku so cambuzi cawo weneco.
35 Sunga na wanji wa muli walongola we mahala, nawo wolowulagwa. Na ku soko ya zizo, wanhu wolokwiyeza. Na zizi zolojendelela du, sunga kono wolokuziza mudumo, mu vilo vyono Mulungu yaviwaga.

36 “Nayo mutemi wa Siliya, kolokwituma nha vyono kolowa yendile mweneco. Kolokwidunhumula, no kwiwona vyono mwene yo yeza muwaha kuciza milungu yose, kanyhi yamucizize sunga no Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye. Nayo kolosuma kuzinoza zizo, sunga lidaha lifice lyono Mulungu kolomulipiciziza. Nayo Mulungu kolokwituma nha vyono yakondya kuwaga.
37 Nayo mutemi si koloyilumba kanyhi ng'o, i milungu yono walezi wakwe wayihembaga, hamba milungu yono yendigwe ne wacekulu. Nha cinji, si koloyilumbila lukulu milungu yose, hambi mwene nha kolokwihwesa nha vyono mwene yo muwaha yayicizize milungu yiyo yose.
38 Ninga langa, mwene koloyipela cogopo i milungu yono yigadimaga magomawakali du i milungu yono ne i walezi wakwe si wayihemba yaye. Kanyhi koyilavizya milungu yiyo zahabu, na fweza, ne vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu, hamonga ne vipegwa vinji vinji vyo wugoli.
39 Nayo kolowetumamila wanhu wono wakuyihemba milungu mijenzi, wagadime magomawakali gakwe. Kanyhi kolowapela lilumbo yiwaha muno, wose wono wolomubocela nha mutemi wawo. Kolowawika mu milimo ye lilumbo yiwaha. Kanyhi kolowagawila sunga ne yisi, yawafupe.

40 “Ne madaha ge ndumo go mutemi wa Siliya kolo geza kufika, u mutemi wa Misili kolomuhoma. Ninga mwene mutemi wa Siliya, nayo kolokwitaza, yehome no mutemi wa Misili kwe vilungo vyakwe vyose, akuno yakwitumamila ne matuka gakwe go wulugu, ne zifwalasi, hamonga ne zingalawa nyinji muno. Nayo kolozisuma zi'si nyinji, kolokwinjila mu zi'si zizo yakuhuluma nhe 'hwazi lya malenga.
41 Kanyhi koloyihoma sunga ne yisi ye Cilagane, kolowawulaga makumi na makumi ga maelufu. Ninga langa zi'si za Edomu, na Mowabu ne yisi ya Amoni yono yowa yikali yisigaye, azo langa zolopona,
42 na lono kowa kuzihoma zi'si zizo zose, sunga ne yisi ya Misili si koloyileka yaye.
43 Nayo kolosola viwutwa vyose vye Misili vyono vihundikwe, vye zahabu, ne fweza, hamonga ne vinhu vinji vyose vyo wuguzi wukamu. Kanyhi koloyisuma yisi ya Libiya na Kushi.
44 Ninga zinghani zono zolokuza zikulawa ku lubavu lwa kucilima na kusumuka, zizo zolomutajila woga mu nhumbula yakwe, nayo kolokwiyoha kwihoma kwe vilungo vyakwe vyose, kolowulaga wanhu wenji muno muno.
45 Kologahoma sunga ne mahema gakwe mawaha muno ge citemi-temi, haciligati ye lilenga ne litunda lyono lizenjigwe Nyumba yo Mulungu. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, vyo kolofwa du, na holowa baho, hasina hamba munhu wo wose mono kolomutaza.”


Danyeli 12

Lidaha lyo Mudumo

1 Nayo malayika mono yali yavwalile myenda ye bafuta, yakanonjela nene Danyeli, yakatya, “Mwi'pango lyono kolokuziza Mikayeli u mulongola we Wamalayika, mono kawadimaga wanhu wako, baho lyolowa yipago lyo wugaye wuwaha muno. Wugaye wono wukali nejela mu madaha go gose, ku wulawa vyono lyamuliziza likungugo, sunga mu madaha gago. Na kolo likuza lidaha lilyo, i wanhu we nghungugo yako, wono matagwa gawo gandicigwe mu citabu co Mulungu, wose wolopona.
2 Na wono wakondya kufwa, wose wolozukulwa. Wanji wolozukulilwa mu wumi we cibitilila, na wanji wolotuga cevu no wugaye we cibitilila.
3 Ninga i walongola we mahala, wolonga'lala nhe limuli lya ku wulanga. Na wono wawatongaga wanhu wenji, ho wetume ze ling'ani, awo wolong'alala nhe zinyelezi, cibitilila.”

4 Yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya, “Danyeli 'gwe, aluno zinghani zizi zeze za cinyele kuli 'gwe, wucigubikaje citabu, wuciwicile ne citanjilo, cilece kugubulwa kanyhi, sunga ku ndumilizo ye yinze. I wanhu wenji wolowa wena lumangho ako na ako, ne mahala gokonjezeka.”
5 Hanyuma ya zizo, nghawona wanhu wejete wemile ha nyenje ye likolongo, yumonga yemile mu nyenje ayi kuno, no muyagwe nayo mu nyenje yo lubavu luyagwe.
6 Na yumonga muli wene, yakamuwuza malayika mono lulo yali yemile mucanyha ye lilenga lye likolongo, yakamutya, “Alu yosola myaka milinga sunga zinghani zizi zo wizine zize zidume.”
7 Nayo malayika yuyo, yakenula miwoko yakwe yose ku wulanga, yakelahiza mwi'agwa lyo Mulungu, mono kekalaga cibitilila. Nane nghamuhulika yakutya, “Yowa myaka midatu na nusu. Na kolo wugazwe we wanhu wo Mulungu wapuluta, baho na zizi zose zolowa zakondya kwijela.”
8 Nane nghazihulika zono yalonga, ninga hamba si nasuma kuzizuka yaye. Holeka nghamuwuza nghamutya, “Gwe muwaha wangu, mudumo we zinghani zizi, zowa wule?”
9 Nayo malayika yakamhitula yakatya, “Alu Danyeli, wubite, hambi nghani zizi nha zowa za cinyele, kanyhi zowa hundikwe sunga lono mudumo wulaziza.
10 Ne wanhu wenji wolokwezwa. Ninga i wabi, hamba si wolozizuka zizo, langa wene wolocila kuwa wabi du. Langa we mahala wo wolozizuka.

11 “No wulawilila mu madaha gago, gono vilavywa vye nhondo zose vye makumbiko vyolokwimililwa, ne Liwifyo kolo lyeza kusimywa, vyolopuluta vilo elufu monga, na magana mejete na makumi yicenda.
12 Wo wamoti wawo wono wowa wehuwilwa, sunga vize vipulute vilo elufu monga na magana madatu, na makumi madatu na vihano!
13 Holeka nagwe Danyeli, weze wuli mwihuwilwa sunga ko ku ndumo. Heya, kofwa, ninga kolozuka kanyhi, wubocele ligawo lyako ha mudumo we vilo.”
