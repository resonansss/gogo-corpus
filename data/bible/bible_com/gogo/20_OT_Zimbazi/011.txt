Zimbazi 11

1 U Muwaha Mulungu,

sikendezwaga ne wanhu wono

wetumamilaga vipimo vyo wukoci.

Ninga mwene,

kendezwaga ne vipimo vye ling'ani.

2 Ne wanhu iwedunhumuzi,

wokozwa cevu kamonga.

Ninga mahala gali muli wenenenyhezi.

3 Na hono wuwe wuli munhu muswanu,

winenenyhezi wokutonga.

Ne wanhu wose wono si wehuwilwa,

wokongomezwa no wu wula kuwa no wihuwilwa.

4 Ninga wugoli hamba si wulakutaza cinji yaye

mwizuwa lyo kufwa.

Ninga wihuwilwa wokuponya wumi wako.

5 Wihuwilwa wuyinozaga mikalile yo munhu muswanu

yiwa miswanu.

Ninga u munhu mubi,

kolofwa ku soko yo wubi wakwe.

6 Wiling'ano wumuponyaga munhu mwinenenyhezi.

Ninga mono si mwihuwilwa,

kegwizaga mweneco no wubi wakwe.

7 Na lono munhu mubi yakufwa,

kafwizaga hamonga no witumbilo wakwe.

No witumbila zo wugoli,

wowa nghani ya bwete du.

8 Wanhu we ling'ani wakuwa kudimwa mu zimbali.

Langa i wabi,

zimbali ziwazizaga.

9 Zinghani za wono wasina cogopo co Muwaha Mulungu,

zosuma kukwajiliza.

Ninga mahala ga wono wali ling'ani,

gosuma kukuponya.

10 Ne likaya lisangalalaga lono we ling'ani

wakumota.

Na hono i wanhu wabi wafwe,

i wanhu wasangalalaga.

11 Ne likaya litogozwaga lono wanhu we ling'ani

wakumota.

Ninga likaya lihodanjikaga ku soko ye makani ge wanhu wabi.

12 Yo nghani ya wulele du yo kuwabedegala wanji.

Ninga hono wuwe munhu mono wina mahala,

konyamala twii.

13 No munhu muhesi,

si kalonjelwaga nghani ze cinyele.

Langa wumwitumbile munhu mwihuwilwa.

14 Ne likungugo ligwizaga hono hawe hasina wutonji.

Na mu winji we wanyamhala mo wuli wusume.

15 Na hono wihuwize vyono komulipila

mujenzi mugawa wakwe,

kokwitajila mu mbali bwete.

Ciba hono wulece kwiyinjiza mu nghani yiyo.

16 No mucekulu musindile,

yena lilumbo nhondo zose.

Ninga mucekulu mono si mutuvu,

yo nghani ya cevu du.

No munhu mudoba,

si katujilaga lukulu sawo.

Ninga munhu mupiligazi ku milimo,

kagolipaga.

17 Kosuma kwinozeza ziswanu go mweneco

hono wuwe no lusungu.

Ninga hono wuwe wuli jila,

kokwitamisa wuli wene.

18 Ne wanhu wabi,

si watugaga cambuzi co cose yaye.

Ninga hono witume ze ling'ani,

nagwe kohumbilizwa.

19 Mono yakamile mu wiling'ano kowumipa.

Ninga mono katyatyaga wubi, kofwa.

20 Muwaha Mulungu yawasulile i wanhu wo wuhile wubi.

Ninga yawendile wono wetumaga zo mugoloka.

21 Na mbeka i wabi wolipicizwa, ninga

we ling'ani woponywa.

22 U wuswanu we futa yo mucekulu,

hono wulece kuhanga no wuswanu we nyikala yakwe,

wuswanu wakwe wowa nhe nyamwidole ya zahabu

yono mvwice mu mhula ye ngubi.

23 Wuhile wa we ling'ani nhondo zose wowu,

wetume ziswanu du.

Ninga i wabi kolo watuga vyono wakusaka,

hodu wose wakukolwa likuwo.

24 Ko wali wanji wono wetumamilaga sawo zawo

nha vyono wendile,

ninga vyo wakucila kugolipa du.

Na ko wali wanji wono wetumamilaga sawo yawo

ko wulanjilizi muno,

ninga vyo wakucila kuciwapa muno.

25 Weze no wucemezi, nagwe komoteswa.

Weze kuwapela wanji wutazi,

holeka nagwe kotuga wutazi.

26 Wanhu wamulijitaga munhu

mono kahundikaga wuhemba,

yalindiye yatuje wuguzi wuswanu.

Ninga wamukumyaga mono kawaguzizaga wuhemba.

27 Hono wuwe wupiligaye mu wulondola ziswanu,

wanhu wokwenda.

Ninga hono wuwe wuli mulondola zimbi,

nazo zimbi zokutuga.

28 Nawo wono wetumbiye zisawo zawo,

wololagala nhe mahamha mwi'bahu.

Ninga we ling'ani womoteswa nhe mahamha mwi'fuku.

29 No munhu mono kaletaga ciyagalo mu kaya yakwe,

ku ndumilizo si kokwambulila cinji yaye.

Nawo walele nhondo zose wowa wetumami we wanhu we mahala.

30 Wiling'ano wuletaga wumi.

Ninga wugazi, wuwuwusaga.

31 Ne wanhu waswanu,

watugaga cihumbilizwa cawo baha hono yinze.

Holeka wumanye vyono i wabi hamonga ne wanyo wubi wose,

wololipicizwa.
