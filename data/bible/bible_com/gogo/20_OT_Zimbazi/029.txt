Zimbazi 29

1 Na hono wuwe wusina cihuliko,

nhondo zose lono wukutacilwa, lizuwa linji,

kolobenangwa hamba si koloponywa kanyhi.

2 Hono wundajile mutemi mono yali ling'ani,

nane nokulajila wanhu wono wena wusangalale.

Na hono wundajile u mutemi mubi,

nane nokulajila i wanhu wono wena wukalale.

3 Hono wuwe wendile mahala,

mbeka sogo kokwendezwa nagwe.

Kanyhi yo nghani ya wulele,

yo munhu kwitumamila sawo kuli wacekulu wasenyha.

4 Na hono mutemi yawe yali mu ling'ani,

sunga ne likungugo lyowa lili vilungo.

Ninga hono yawe mwenda sawo,

koyongomeza yisi yakwe.

5 Na hono wuwe wuli mwiyendezi kuli wendece wako,

wukwitega mutego go mweneco.

6 Ne wanhu wabi, weyigwizaga mu wubi wawo.

Akuno i wanhu we ling'ani, wasangalaye kanyhi welecele.

7 Nayo munhu we ling'ani yazimanyile

zono zimunojeye yawatendele waciwa.

Ninga i wanhu wabi, si wazimanyaga zizo.

8 Ne wanhu wono si webedegazi

wasagahazaga wuma mwi'kaya.

Ninga i wanhu we mahala, wapozaga makuwo.

9 No munhu we mahala, hono yamusitace mulele,

u mulele kowa kuseka du,

kanyhi kowa yazozize du akuno yakuliga.

10 I wanhu wono wendile wuwulaji,

wamusulaga u munhu we ling'ani.

Ninga we ling'ani wowucitila wumi wo munhu we liwala lilyo.

11 Ne wanhu walele, welajilaga makuwo gawo calinze.

Ninga we mahala, wali ngola, akuno wena no wiyimizi.

12 Na hono mutemi yawe muhulicila zo wulambi,

ne wetumami wakwe wose wowa walambi.

13 U muciwa no mugazi, wose yo nghologo monga,

u Muwaha Mulungu nha yawapela meso wose vyono wejete.

14 No mutemi mono kawaling'anizaga viswanu waciwa,

kotema vilo vinji muno.

15 Kutacila hamonga no kufunda,

co cinhu ciswanu kuli wana.

No mwana hono yalece kutendelwa zizo,

komukoza cevu nyina ku soko yakwe.

16 Na hono i wanhu wabi wawe wo watemi,

no wusutye wonjelezekaga.

Ninga we ling'ani wokwikala walanjiliye wufwe we wanhu wawo.

17 Wumufundaje mwanago,

nagwe kokwilumbila muli mwene.

Nayo hamba si kokukoza cevu yaye.

18 Ne likungugo hono lilece kuwa no wutonji wo Mulungu,

liwezaga yikungugo lyono lisina wulajilize.

Yo mumoti munhu mono yebate lilajilizo lyo Mulungu.

19 Si kosuma kumufunda mwitumami na nghani du,

ku soko hamba yawe yakukwizuka zono wukumulonjela,

ninga si kohulika.

20 Ciba kumwitumbila u munhu mulele,

si nha kumwitumbila u munhu mono kelonganjilaga du,

wu wula kwamula kwihwesa.

21 Na hono wumuzoweleze mwitumami wako,

ku wulawa wudodo wakwe,

wuwe kumupela du cono yakusaka,

mwi'zuwa linji,

kolo kwisolela du mweneco vinhu vyako.

22 Ne wanhu wono wena makuwo ge mbela mbela,

waletaga wuma hamonga ne mazozo.

23 Ne wanhu wedunhumuzi, wosuma kukuletela lifwa.

Ninga hono wuwe mwinenenyhezi, wokupela lilumbo.

24 Nayo u muyagwe no muhizi, kesuzaga wumi wakwe.

Na hono yalonje ze mbeka mu mahakama,

kotuga wulipicizwe.

Nayo hono yalece kwituma nhavivyo,

Mulungu komulijita.

25 Nawo woga wo kuwogopa wanhu, wuletaga mutego.

Ninga hono wumwitumbile Muwaha Mulungu,

kowa wupolile.

26 Ne wanhu wose wasakaga watuje wendezwe kuli mutemi.

Ninga kosuma kutuga ling'ani kuli Muwaha Mulungu du yali wene.

27 I wanhu we ling'ani, wawasulaga i wabi,

nawo i wanhu wabi, wawasulaga i wanhu we ling'ani.
