Zimbazi 19

1 Yili ciba wuwe muciwa, ninga wuli mwihuwilwa.

Si nho kuwa wuli mulambi, kanyhi wuli mulele.

2 No wilowe nawo, hono wuwe wusina wizuce, si wuswanu.

Nazo nghani zo musunho, zitajilaga munhu mu mbali.

3 Wanhu wanji nawo, weyihodangaga mu mikalile yawo weneco,

so metumo gawo go wulele, na hanyuma,

wawa kumuhosa Muwaha Mulungu.

4 Ne wanhu wagoli,

nawo watugaga wendece wawo nhondo zose.

Ninga i waciwa,

hamba ne wacefu wono wena nawo wakuwafutila.

5 Na hono wulonje zo wulambi mu mahakama,

kotuga wulipicizwe, kowa wusina nzila yo kuponela.

6 Munhu wo wose,

yendile yatuje wendezwe kuli wanhu we lilumbo.

Kanyhi wenji,

wono wendile wawe wendece kuli wono walavyaga vipegwa.

7 Sunga ne wanduguze wo muciwa,

wawezaga siwamwendile yaye.

Kanyhi kekalaga yasina wendece wakwe.

Hamba yahidimbale muno ho yawatuje,

ninga sikosuma yaye.

8 Wuhidimbale muno mu wiyijize, wuzikumbucile na

zono wukwiyijiza, nagwe komoteswa.

9 Na hasina munhu, mono kalongaga zo wulambi mu

mahakama, mono si kalipizizwaga no kongomela.

10 Ne walele, si wanojeye wekale mu winji we zisawo,

Ne wetumami, si wanojeye wateme mu wanhu we lilumbo.

11 Na hono wuwe wina mahala, kokwiyimila likuwo lyako.

Na hono yunji yakusutizye, yowa nghani swanu muno,

hono wulece kuzicelelela zizo, zono yakutendeye.

12 Likuwo lyo mutemi, lihwanile ngholoma ye lisimba.

Ninga wendezwe wakwe, winha buhe mu mahanze.

13 No mwana mulele, kamuletelaga sogwe wugaye.

Nayo mucekulu we liwanjilila,

yehwanile mvula ye lihwela.

14 Heya, munhu kosuma kuhala kaya,

hamonga ne zisawo ze walezi wakwe.

Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yali wene,

yo kosuma kumupela mugolece we mahala.

15 Ale du, weze mudoba hono gwegwe wendile kuwa mudoba,

wugone nhongo.

Ninga wumanye vyono nzala yokwinjila.

16 Wugakamisaje malajilizo go Mulungu,

nagwe holeka kokwikala vilo vinji mu wikale wako.

Ninga hono wugabedegale, ale, kofwa.

17 Na lono wukuwataza vinhu waciwa,

yinha kotya wukuviwika kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

Nayo Muwaha Mulungu,

hanyuma kanyhi yakukugomolela.

18 Wuwafundaje wanago lono wakali wadodo,

lono wena no wupelu wo kugabocela mafundo.

Ninga hono wulece kuwafunda,

wukuwapela cilejeho co kwihodanga weneco.

19 Na hone munhu yena likuwo lyono likwaka musunho,

agwe mulekaje,

hambi nha kokwidinghulila wulipicizwe wakwe mweneco.

Ninga hono wuwone vyono kosuma kumutaza kamonga mwi'kuwo lyakwe,

ale, wulece kukatala kumutaza.

20 Na hono wuwe wuli mupelu kuwuhuliciza wulanjize we wayago,

lizuwa linji nagwe kotuga mahala.

21 Heya, i wanhu wosuma kwiwicila nghani nyinji zo kwituma.

Ninga wuhile wo Muwaha Mulungu, wo wufisilizwaga du.

22 Yo nghani ya cevu ciwaha,

hono wuwe wuli cilya cilya.

Kanyhi ciba i waciwa, si nhe wanhu walambi.

23 Hono wumogope Muwaha Mulungu,

nagwe ho kokwikala vilo vinji mu wikale wako,

hamba nghani yo yose 'mbi,

si yokwagana yaye.

24 Wanhu wanji, mbeka wadoba muno muno,

wakufutigwa sunga kuwanyhile ndigwa mu milomo yawo.

25 I wanhu wedunhumuzi, yiwanojeye watuje wulipicizwe,

ho wanji nawo, wono si wazimanyile zono ziwanojeye kwituma,

ho wefundile mumo.

Na hono wuwe ne mahala, kokwifundila mu wulanjizwe.

26 No mwana mono kamugazaga sogwe no kumuwinga nyina.

Yo mwana mono kakozaga soni, no kuleta cevu.

27 Gwe mwanangu, hono wuleme mafundo,

nha cinji wukulema zinghani zo wumanye.

28 Hawezaga hasina ling'ani,

lono muhomelezi yakuwa yena wuhile wo kumutamisa yunji.

Ne wanhu wabi nawo, nha wendile muzinzi wo wubi.

29 Ne wanhu we lizeze, wewikanize so wutowangwe.
