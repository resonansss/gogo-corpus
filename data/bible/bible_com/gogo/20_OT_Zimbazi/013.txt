Zimbazi 13

1 U mwana we mahala,

kahulicizaga wijize wo sogwe.

Ninga mwana mubezi,

si kavumilaga masutya gakwe.

2 Ne wanhu waswanu,

wopegwa vihumbilizwa vyawo,

mu nghango ye mefumo gawo.

Ninga i walambi wekalaga,

wadabuciye wugazi du.

3 Wilanjilile kuli zono wukulonga.

Wuwukamise wumi wako.

Ninga munhu mono si kelanjililaga mu milonjele yakwe,

keyongomezaga mweneco.

4 Ne nhumbula yo munhu mudoba,

hamba yisulumile cinji,

si kosuma kucituga yaye.

Ninga munhu muhidimbazi,

katugaga vyose vyono kasakaga.

5 We ling'ani, yawusulile wulambi.

Ninga u mubi,

ketumaga makani ga cevu kanyhi ga soni.

6 Ling'ani, yiwacitilaga we ling'ani.

Ninga wubi, lyo lifwa lye wabi.

7 Wanhu wanji wenozaga nha wagoli,

ninga kumbe ho wanghwenzele.

Na wanji wenozaga nha wanghwenzele,

ninga kumbe ho wagoli.

8 U mugoli ketumamilaga sawo yakwe kwiponya wumi wakwe.

Ninga hasina mono kamogofyaga munghwenzele.

9 Ne wanhu we ling'ani wehwanile limuli lyono

likumulika muno.

Ninga i wabi, wehwanile tala yono yizimile.

10 Wusisi, wuletaga wiwanganile.

Holeka yili ciba kulondola wutazi,

hambi nha go mahala.

11 Mu nghango ye mbela mbela

yono wukuyisawila sawo yako,

yo nghango kanyhi yono koyajiliziza musunho.

Ninga mono kakongholaga ko wupiligazi,

kokonjelezeka.

12 No witumbilo wono wutinyaga kwijela,

wutamisaga nhumbula.

Ninga kolo yakutuga cono yakucisaka,

baho nhumbula yakwe yisangalalaga muno.

13 Na hono wugabeze mejizo maswanu,

ale kokwiletela wugaye.

Ninga hono wugavumile, kowa mu mulamu.

14 Ne mejizo ge mahala, wo muvwilo wo wumi.

Nago gokutaza wiponye wumi wako lono wuli mu mbali.

15 Wizuce wuswanu wuletaga mahweso ge lihewa.

Ninga wawo wono si wehuwilwa,

wali mu nzila yo wuhodanjice.

16 I wanhu we mahala,

nhondo zose wamulaga kusimya, wehwesa,

lono wakali si netuma cinji.

Ninga i walele weyagazaga wulele wawo.

17 U mutumwa mubi kaletaga wiwanganile.

Ninga wono wetumbilwaga waletaga mulamu.

18 Munhu wowose mono si kendaga kulanjizwa kowa muciwa

no kukozwa cevu.

Ninga mono kavumilaga wulanjizwe, kotuga lilumbo.

19 Ninga yo nghani swanu hono wucituje cono kusakaga!

Ninga i wanhu walele,

walemaga kuwuka mu wituma zimbi.

20 Wilunzaje na we mahala,

nagwe kotuga mahala.

Ninga hono wilunze ne walele,

kokwajilila.

21 Magaziko, gawatyatililaga i wabi,

hanhu hose.

Ninga we ling'ani,

wohumbilizwa ne vinhu viswanu.

22 No munhu muswanu kowa ne sawo yono kosuma

kuwalecela ne wezukuluwe.

Ninga sawo ye wabi yiwutilwaga we ling'ani.

23 Ne migunda yono si yilimwaga,

yolela ndigwa nyinji ku so waciwa.

Ninga i wanhu wono si wali ling'ani wahodangaga vinhu.

24 Na hono wulece kumutowa mwanago lono yakusutya,

wumusulile.

Ninga hono wuwe wumwendile, komufunda.

25 I we ling'ani wekalaga wena ndigwa yono yiwakwiliye,

Ninga i wabi, wekalaga wali nzala vilo vyose.
