Zimbazi 21

1 Muwaha Mulungu, kayitongaga nhumbula yo mutemi,

nha vyono kacihulumyaga cikolongo.

2 Nagwe kokwihwesa 'mba vyono, zose zono wukwituma,

wukwituma ze ling'ani.

Ninga wukumbucile vyono,

Muwaha Mulungu, yo kazijelaga zinzila zako.

3 Nhondo zose, weze kwituma ze ling'ani na zo mugoloka,

zono zikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu,

si nho kumulavizya makumbiko.

4 I wanhu wabi,

watongwaga no wiwoni hamonga no widunhumuzi wawo.

Ne nghani yiyi, wo wubi.

5 Hono wuwe wuli mwihwesa viswanu, kotuga vinji.

Ninga, hono wuwe wuli munhu we papala-papala,

hamba si kotuga vinji.

6 No wugoli wono kowutuga mu zinzila zimbi,

wokwajilila musunho, hambi nha wo mutego wako we lifwa.

7 Nawo i wabi, wongomezezwaga mu wugazi wawo weneco.

Hambi nha wakulema kwituma ze ling'ani.

8 Ne wanhu wahodanji,

wakolaga mu nzila yono yipetyapetizye,

Ninga we ling'ani, wene wetumaga ze ling'ani.

9 Yili ciba kwikala kunze mwi'tembe lye kaya,

si nho kuhanga kaya monga, no mucekulu mono yali zozo.

10 I wanhu wabi, nhondo zose wekalaga wadabuciye zimbi du.

Kanyhi wasina lusungu kuli munhu wo wose.

11 Na lono munhu mwidunhumuzi yakulipicizwa,

sunga no munhu mono yasina mahweso,

nayo mo keyijizizaga lifundo.

No munhu we mahala nayo,

kokwiyijiziza muli zono yafundwa.

12 Mulungu yo we ling'ani,

nayo yazimanyile zose zono zikwitumwa mu zikaya ze wanhu wabi.

Nayo kowaletela i wabi wose wongomezwe.

13 Na hono wuleme kuwahuliciza waciwa cililo cawo,

Nagwe cililo cako, co kulondola wutazi,

hamba si cohulikwa yaye.

14 Na hono yunji yakukolelwe likuwo,

cipegwa cono komupela cinyele,

comupoza likuwo lyakwe.

15 Na lono ling'ani yikwitumwa, i we ling'ani,

wakuwa kusangalala muno.

Ninga i wanhu wabi wakongomezwa.

16 Nalyo lifwa, liwalindiye wose wono wajiliye,

no kuyileka nzila yo wizuce.

17 Na hono wende ze cinyemo-nyemo, divayi,

ne ndigwa swanu muno, hamba si kogolipa yaye.

18 Nawo i wanhu wabi, ziwawuyilaga weneco i zimbi,

zono wakuwa kusaka kuwatendela we ling'ani.

19 Na mbeka, yili ciba kwikala ku nyika,

si nho kwikala no mucekulu we lizozo,

mung'ung'ulici, kanyhi mukali.

20 I wanhu we mahala, wekalaga mu wugoli,

hamonga ne nyemo.

Ninga i walele weyagazaga sawo zawo kolo wazituga.

21 Weze muswanu, kanyhi mwihuwilwa,

holeka kokwikala vilo vinji.

Sunga ne wanhu wanji,

wokupela cogopo no kukutendela viswanu.

22 No mucekulu we mahala,

yadahile kulisola likaya,

lyono lina no wulindilizi we vilungo,

no kugakumulanga ne majinji gono wagetumbiye.

23 Na hono wusace wileje ne mazozo,

ale, wilanjilile mu zinghani zako zono kulongaga.

24 Ale, agwe wundajile u munhu mwidunhumuzi,

nane nokulajila u munhu mwiwoni, no mwivuni,

mono si kawacelelelaga wanhu wanji.

25 No munhu mudoba, mono si kasakaga kwituma milimo,

ayo yakwiwulaga yali wene.

26 Na zizo zono yakwituma,

yakwihwesa 'mba mo kotujila vyono yakusaka.

Ninga u munhu we ling'ani,

kalavyaga hamba kwimila yaye.

27 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu,

hamba si yendile i wanhu wabi,

wamulavizye makumbiko, ko muno,

gono gakulawa mu wuhile wawo wubi.

28 No wuhomelezi wo munhu mulambi,

si witumbilwaga yaye.

Langa nghani yo munhu mwinenenyhezi, yovumilwa.

29 I wanhu we ling'ani, wena witumbilo.

Ninga i wabi, wajelaga kwinoza weneco,

ho wawe nha waswanu.

30 I mahala go munhu, hamba lukumyo,

hamba wizuce, si vyosuma kukutaza cinji,

hono Muwaha Mulungu yo yalece kuwa hamonga nagwe.

31 Kosuma kuyiwikaniza fwalasi yako so wulugu.

Ninga Muwaha Mulungu du, yo kaletaga wusume.
