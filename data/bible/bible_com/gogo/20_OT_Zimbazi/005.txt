Zimbazi 5

Suguzi kuli zo wusenyha

1 Gwe mwanangu,

wukamise no kuzihulika ze mahala gangu no wizuce.

2 Nagwe ho komanya nzila ze nyikala ye cogopo,

ne zinghani zako zokulajila vyono kumbe wina mahala.

3 Hambi milomo yo mugolece wo munhu yunji,

nha yiwezaga yili muzinzi nho wuci,

ne nonela yakwe,

yiteleye yinhe mafuta,

4 ninga kolo zose zakondya,

hodu mwene kawa kukuleka,

wina wusungu ne mawine.

5 Nayo kokukwenghweleza sunga yakufise ku yisi ye wafu,

hambi nzila yono yayikoleye,

nha nzila yono yibitile ku wufwe.

6 Hambi mwene nha si kajendelaga mu nzila yo wumi,

langa keyedyaga caka,

hamba kumanya zono zomwagana yaye.

7 Anye wanangu, ale ta,

muhulicizaje,

kanyhi mulece kuzisemwa zono nghulonga.

8 U mucekulu we liwala lilyo,

mumwilegaje ye!

Hamba no kusejelela ha mulango wakwe du,

mulecele lukulu.

9 Ku soko hono wunoze zono zinha zizo,

wanji wokusolela lilumbo lyako lyono wali wina nalyo,

nagwe kofwa wukali mudodo,

kanyhi kofwila mu miwoko ye wanhu wono wasina lusungu.

10 Nawo wajenzi woyisola sawo yako,

na vyose vyono wali wavituga mu milimo yako,

vyose vyosolwa no munhu yunji.

11 Nagwe kolofwila mu wusungu,

kolo muwili wako hamonga ne nghuli yako vikuligwa,

12 nagwe baho ho kolotela,

“Alu nye, nhawule si neyijiza?

Hamba nhawule si namuleka yunji yanhaze?

13 Hamba si nenda kuwahulika walimu wangu,

hamba kuwacelelela yaye.

14 Nane nghewona nhinicizo

vyono nakondya kukozwa cevu cagalanze.”

15 U mugolece wako yehwanile cisima ce malenga mapolo,

ale, weze wuli mwihuwilwa kuli mugolece wako,

no wendo wako weze wuli kuli mweneco du yali wene.

16 Ne wana nawo wono wulelile ako kudeha,

kuli mucekulu yunji,

wawo hamba si wokutaza cinji yaye.

17 Hambi i wanago,

nha wanojeye wawiluce,

ho wakutaze gwegwe,

ninga si wawo wajenzi yaye.

18 Holeka yikunojeye wumusangalalile mugolece wako,

wuwone vyono wina nyemo no muhinza mono mwitolile.

19 Hambi nha muswanu ku wulangwe,

kanyhi yakwendeza nha cizimba.

Wulece vitombo vyakwe vikusangalaze nhondo zose.

Nagwe wendezwe muno no wendo wakwe kuli'gwe.

20 Alo socici mwanangu,

wukuwugawa wendo wako kuli mucekulu yunji?

Hamba nhawule vitombo vyo mucekulu

wo munhu yunji vyo vikwendeze 'gwe?

21 Ninga wumanye vyono,

Muwaha Mulungu kaziwonaga zose zono kwitumaga.

Nayo kakulanjililaga ko kose kono wukubita.

22 Kanyhi wumanye vyono u wubi wo munhu mubi,

mutego kuli mwene.

Hambi nha kokwigwiza mu mutego wo wubi wakwe mweneco.

23 Hambi nha yakwiwulaga

mweneco so wu wula kwiyimila kwakwe.

No wulele wakwe,

wo womufisa ko ku citenjelo.
