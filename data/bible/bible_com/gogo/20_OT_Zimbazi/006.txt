Zimbazi 6

Suguzi kanyhi zinji

1 Ati mwanangu,

ho hali hanji hono wehuwiza

nha vyono komulipila yunji migawa yakwe?

2 Hamba ho lili lizuwa linji

lono wetega ne zinghani zako go mweneco,

wukebatwa ne zinghani zo mulomo wako zono wehuwiza?

3 Ale du kanyhi mwanangu,

alu weza wuli mu miwoko yo munhu yuyo.

Ninga aluno,

wunoze nhavi ho wiponye,

wumubitilaje mbela munhu yuyo,

wumulonjele yakulece.

4 Wulece kuleka meso gako gagone,

hamba nghope zako zituje nhongo.

5 Langa wisuhulaje mu mutego,

nha vyono ndeje,

hamba nzimba, yiponyaga mu miwoko yo mupelembi.

6 Nawo i wanhu wadoba,

yiwanojeye weyijizize mu nzila ye zisungusugu vyono zikalaga.

7 Zene zisungusungu zisina mulongola,

hamba munyamhala, hamba mutemi.

8 Ninga ziwicilaga zeneco ndigwa yazo,

mwi'pango lye cibahu,

ho ziwe ne ciwutwa mwi'pango lye cifuku.

9 Alo u munhu mudoba kogona sunga nili lo?

Hamba kosinhuka nili mu zinhongo zakwe?

10 Nayo mudoba,

yakupitula yakutya,

“Nghali hadodo,

wundece ta ngone ngone.

Ndekaje nghunye magulu gangu ngone ngone hadodo.”

11 Ninga lono yakali yali mu nhongo,

hobaho wuciwa wukumuziza nho muhizi mono yena vitwalo.

12 Mbeka i wanhu wabi wono si wakwiliye cinji,

wo wendile kubita ako na ako,

wawa kutandaza zo wulambi.

13 Wakonyezaga ne meso gawo,

akuno wakunoza vitanjilo ne vidole vyawo,

ho wakukonje-konje.

14 Nawo wekelaga wakwihwesa

zimbi du zo wupatuce mu zinhumbula zawo,

wakusagahaza wuma du hanhu hose.

15 Na ku soko yiyo,

magazo gowaziza gasina suguzi,

no wongomele wowagana.

16 Ko zili nghani mutandatu hamba mupunghati,

zono Muwaha Mulungu yazisulile,

17 Meso go widunhumuzi.

Lulimi lono lulongaga zo wulambi.

Miwoko yono yiwawulagaga wanhu we ling'ani.

18 Nhumbula yono yihwesaga mahweso mabi.

Magulu gono gamanyhilaga ku wituma zimbi.

19 Muhomelezi wo wulambi mono kekalaga yakulamba-lamba.

No munhu musomenghanya, mono kaletaga

lusulo muli wandugu.

Suguzi kuli zo wusenyha

20 Mwanangu, weze kwituma nha vyono sogo yakukulonjela,

hamba wulece kuzisemwa zono nyina yako yakakwijiza.

21 Wuzikamisaje zinghani zawo vilo vyose,

zikale mu nhumbula yako.

22 Ne mafundo gayo,

gokutonga lono wuli mu muhinzo,

kanyhi gokukamisa ku cilo.

23 Ne mejizo gayo,

nha lyo limuli lyako no wuzelu,

no wutonji wayo,

wokwijiza mu mikalile yako.

24 Nago gokucitila kuli mucekulu mubi,

na kuli zinghani zo wupepelezi,

za kuli wacekulu we wanhu wanji.

25 Holeka wulece kukwegwa no wuswanu wawo,

hamba wulece kugwizwa no wulembule we meso gawo.

26 Mulume kosuma kumutuga mucekulu

musenyha so pesa zono zikwiliye

wuguzi wa mukate wumonga du,

ninga mucekulu musenyha,

kosuma kumusolela mulume yuyo,

vyose vyono yena navyo.

27 Wule! Kosuma kujeleka moto mu mhambaga yako,

hamba kunyanya myenda yako?

28 Hambi kosuma kujenda mucanyha ye makala go moto,

hamba kupya migulu yako?

29 Na mbeka ku wugona no mucekulu wo

munhu yunji mo yili mbali mbaha muno.

Na wo wose mono kokwituma zizo,

kotuga wuhoswe.

30 Ne wanhu si wamubedegalaga muhizi,

lono yakuhiza ndigwa lono yadabuka.

31 Na hono yebatwe,

kolipa kali nghanghulo mupunghati muli vivyo vyono yahizize.

Na yowa yimunojeye yalavye vyose vyono yena navyo.

32 Ninga munhu mono yakusenyha,

ayo yasina mahala.

Hambi nha yakwihodanga mweneco du.

33 Kokwiwusiza lilumbo lyakwe,

no kutowangwa,

hamonga no kukozwa cevu.

34 Hambi mulume nha kokolwa muno likuwo,

ku soko yo wivu wa kuli mugolece wakwe,

nayo komulipiciza yasina lusungu.

35 Hamba si kovumila malipo go gose,

na howa hasina vipegwa vyo vyose,

vyono vyolipoza likuwo lyakwe.
