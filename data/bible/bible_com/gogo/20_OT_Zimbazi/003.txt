Zimbazi 3

Mafundo kuli wazelelo

1 Gwe mwanangu, wulece kuzisemwa zono nghukwijiza.

Kanyhi weze kuzikumbucila nhondo zose zono nghukulajiliza witume.

2 Ne mafundo gangu,

gokupela wumoteswe vilo vinji hamonga no mu mikalile yako.

3 Weze wuli mwihuwilwa,

kanyhi wuli cihuliko.

Wuwuwopeleze wihuwilwa ne cihuliko mu singo yako,

wuvyandice mu nhumbula yako mugati.

4 Nagwe hono witume zizo,

u Mulungu hamonga ne wanhu wokwendezwa nagwe.

5 Wumwitumbile Muwaha Mulungu kwe nhumbula yako yose.

Wulece kwiyitumbila mahala gako go mweneco.

6 Wumukumbucile Muwaha Mulungu,

muli zose zono wukwituma,

nayo kokutonga mu nzila yono yinojeye.

7 Ninga wulecele lukulu kwihwesa nha vyono,

agwe wuli we mahala go mweneco,

langa agwe weze ne cihuliko kuli Muwaha Mulungu du,

kanyhi wulemele lukulu kwituma zimbi.

8 Na hono wuwe ne cihuliko kuli Muwaha Mulungu,

ale, yowa nha miti miswanu,

yono yokuponya zinghomele zako no kukucepulila wusungu wako.

9 Wumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu,

mu nzila yo kumulavizya vilimwa viswanu vyono wuvitujile mu mugunda wako.

10 Hobaho nago madongha gako,

gomemywa wuhemba,

kanyhi kowa na divayi nyinji yono yokufutila na ha wuwiko wayo.

11 Mwanangu, kolo Muwaha Mulungu yakukufunda,

wulece kugabedegala mafundo gakwe,

langa wugabocele.

12 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu,

nha kawafundaga wose wono yawendile,

nha vyono sogo yo munhu kamufundaga mwanagwe mono yamwendile.

13 Yo mumoti munhu mono kawonaga mahala,

no munhu mono katugaga wizuce.

14 Hambi nha mo mocili cambuzi ciwaha muno si nhe cambuzi ca mu fweza.

Kanyhi mocili cambuzi ciwaha muno si nhe zahabu.

15 Mahala, mbeka gena sawilo mbaha muno,

si nhe vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu,

na hasina cinhu cinji co cose,

cono kocisaka cono kosuma kwihwananiza ne mahala.

16 Mahala gakupelaga vilo vinji vyo kwikala mumi mu mikalile yako,

hamonga no wugoli ne lilumbo.

17 Mahala gokupela nyemo mu mikalile yako hamonga no mulamu.

18 Na wose wono wena mahala,

wena nyemo.

Ne mahala gowapela wumi.

19 Muwaha Mulungu yayiwumba yinze,

kwe mahala gakwe.

Na ko wizuce wakwe,

yakawusimya wulanga.

20 Kanyhi mahala gakwe go gaganoza makolongo gahulume,

ne mavunde nago gayitonyeze mvula mono 'si.

21 Holeka agwe mwanangu,

wukamise mahala hamonga no wizuce.

Wulece kuvileceleza vikutije.

22 Hambi nha vyo vyokupela wumi,

no wendezwe,

hamonga ne mikalile ye nyemo.

23 Nagwe kowa kujenda wupolile mu zinzila zako,

hamba si kokwikobala yaye.

24 Kanyhi kowa wusina woga wo wose,

lono wukwinjila kugona mu citanda cako.

Heya ku cilo kogona nhongo zono zili muzinzi.

25 Hamba si kosuma kugacelelela magayo gono gokuziza nhinicizo,

hamba mukukunzo we wabi kolo wukukuziza.

26 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha kokukamisa.

Hamba si kokuleka yaye wugwile mu mutego.

27 Holeka agwe,

weze kuwatendela ziswanu wono wena wanjile,

mu nghango vyono kosuma.

28 Kanyhi wulecele lukulu kumutya muyago,

yalindile sunga mitondo yaco,

hono kumbe nagwe wali wusume kumupela wutazi lwa kamonga du.

29 Kanyhi wulece kumuwicila muyago cikobazo ho wumutamise,

hambi yuyo muyago,

nha mwikalene behi,

kanyhi mwene yakwitumbiye gwegwe.

30 Kanyhi wulece kwisumaniza no muyago mbula soko,

lono nayo hamba si yakunozize yinji 'mbi.

31 Wulece kuwawonela wivu,

i wanhu wagazi,

hamba wulece kuwega mu metumo gawo.

32 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu,

nha yawasulile i wanhu wetuma zimbi,

langa i weling'ani,

mwene yali hamonga nawo.

33 Muwaha Mulungu kazilijitizaga zikaya ze wanhu wabi,

ninga zikaya ze weling'ani,

mwene kazimotyaga.

34 Kanyhi mwene si kawasakaga i wanhu wedunhumuzi,

langa kawapelaga lihewa i weneneyhezi.

35 Iwe mahala wotuga lukumyo,

ninga walele, wokozwa cevu.
