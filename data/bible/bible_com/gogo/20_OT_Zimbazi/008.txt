Zimbazi 8

U lukumyo lwe Mahala

1 Ale ta muhulicize!

Mahala gakucemeleza.

No wizuce nawo wukulonga wihindile.

2 Mahala yemile mu matunda mucanyha behi ne nzila,

sunga na mono getang'aniye mabalabala.

3 Yakucemeleza yali ha ciligati ye likaya yakutya,

4 “Nghumucemenyi nyenye nyo wanhu wose mono'nze,

nghumunenenyi nyenye nyo wanhu.

5 Anye mono musina mahala,

mutuje wizuce,

nanye mo walele,

mweze ne nhumbula yo wumanye.

6 Muhulice, zinghani zangu ziswanu,

hambi zose zono nghumulonjelenyi,

nha ze ling'ani.

7 Na zono nghulonga,

nghulonga za mbeka,

hambi'ne nha si nyendile wulambi.

8 Holeka zose zono nghulonga,

nghulonga za mbeka musina nghani ya wulambi,

hamba ze liwanjilila.

9 Kuli munhu mono yena wizuce,

zizi zose zili calinze,

kanyhi zili ling'ani kuli wose wono wena wumanye.

10 Holeka ciba mugehaje mejizo gangu,

si nho kwihaga fweza.

Yili ciba mwihaje wizuce,

si nhe zahabu nengu.”

11 “Ane yo nene Mahala,

yo nene du mono nomukwilenyi,

si nhe vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu,

hamba hasina cinhu co cose cono kocisaka cono

cosuma kwihwananizwa na nene.

12 Ane yo nene Mahala,

kanyhi nina no wizuce,

kanyhi nina no wumanye hamonga ne ning'aniza.

13 I cogopo ca kuli Muwaha Mulungu,

ciletaga kusula wubi.

Nane hamba si niwendile widunhumuzi hamonga ne cibede,

hamonga ne zinzila zimbi,

ne zinghani zo wulambi.

14 Nane nghali kwihwesa,

ho niwawusize lukulu hambi

nha nina wizuce kanyhi nili vilungo.

15 Niwatazaga watemi ho wateme,

no kunoza wuling'anizi wuswanu.

16 I wawaha wakutema so wutazi wangu,

hamonga ne walongola,

sunga ne walamuzi wose we'nze.

17 Niwendile wono wanyendile

nayo mono yakunondola, kombona.

18 Ane nina wugoli wose ne lilumbo,

vyono nosuma kumugawilenyi,

no kumumotyenyi.

19 Na vyose vyono nomupelenyi,

nha viswanu vicizize

zahabu nengu,

hamba fweza swanu.

20 Nane niyityatyaga nzila yo mugoloka du,

nhyatyaga zi nzila ze ling'ani

21 ho niwapele zisawo wose wono wanyendile,

nizimemye zikaya zawo ne viwutwa.”

22 “U Muwaha Mulungu nha yamula kumumba'ne katali,

mu mwamulizo we milimo yakwe.

23 Ane nha nasimywa ku wulawa katali na katali

ha mwamulizo,

na lono yinze yali yikali si yinawa.

24 Ane nghalelwa lono ne lilenga lyali likali hasina

na lono vikolongo vye malenga vikali vili hasina.

25 Hambi 'ne nha nalelwa na lono matunda gali gakali,

ne vigongo vyali vikali nasimywa hanhu hayo.

26 Na lono Mulungu yali yakali

nayiwumba yinze ne mawimbi,

hamba muhangha mulongozi wa mu funzi.

27 Na lono yawikanizaga wulanga,

nane nali kuko,

na lono yawikaga cisolo ce lilenga.

28 Na lono yagawikaga mavunde mu mwanya,

nali kuko,

sunga na lono yadigulaga mivwilo ye lilenga.

29 Na lono yalilajilizaga lilenga hanhu hayo,

ho lilece kupuluta vyono yalilajilize.

Na lono yawusimizya musinji we yinze, ane nali kuko.

30 Hambi nha nali hamonga nayo,

nho mwitumami munghula.

Nghawa kumusangalalila nhondo zose,

nghawa kusecelela hali mwene.

31 Nghayisecelela yinze,

nghendezwa ne zinghungugo ze wanhu.”

32 “Holeka lu wanangu,

muhulicize 'ne mwitume nha vyono nimwijizenyi,

nanye mosangalala.

33 Mweze mwina cihuliko kuli zose zono mukwijizwa,

mweze ne mahala,

mulece kuzibedegala yaye.

34 Yo mumoti munhu mono yakuhuliciza'ne,

yalindilize ha milango ye kaya yangu.

35 Hambi mono yakumbona 'ne,

nha kawonaga wumi,

nayo Muwaha Mulungu kokwendezwa nayo.

36 ‘No munhu mono si yakumbona'ne,

yakwitamisa mweneco.

No munhu mono yasulile 'ne,

ayo yendile lifwa.’ ”
