Zimbazi 26

1 Lukumyo lo mulele, lwa bwete,

hambi nha lwihwanile nha seluji mu cibahu,

hamba mvula mwi'pango lye ndudula.

2 Nawo wulijitwe wono wusina soko,

si womugwila munhu,

langa hono wuwe wina soko yayo.

Hambi nha wowa wihwanile zindeje,

zono zigulukaga ako na ako, hamba kutula ng'o.

3 Fwalasi yikunagwa, nayo ndogowe yiwopwaga no lubano.

Nayo mulele, wumukune no mukotya.

4 Wulece kumupitula mulele mu wulele wakwe,

ho wulece kwihwana nayo.

5 Wumupitule mulele mu nghango yo wulele wakwe,

ho yalece kwiyihwesa vyono we mahala.

6 Na hono wumutume mulele yakutegulile nghani,

kowa nho munhu mono wukwidumula magulu gako no kwipela wambuce du.

7 Kosuma kucitumamila cihwanicizo co munhu mulele,

kuli munhu we citewe,

mono kosuma kuyitumamila migulu yakwe.

8 Na hono wumupele mulele lilumbo,

wehwana no munhu mono yakuwicila libwe mu manati.

9 Ne zinghani zo mulele,

zono yakuzisolela kuli munhu we mahala,

zihwanile u munhu mugalo 'jimbi,

mono yakujela kuhomola limiya,

lyono limuhomile mu muwoko wakwe.

10 No munya mulimo,

mono yakwilumbaniza mu mulimo no munhu mulele,

mono yakupuluta,

ale, yuyo welumbanizaga no munhu wo wose mono yakupuluta.

11 U munhu mulele,

kawuyilaga kanyhi kwituma zinghani zo wulele,

nhe libwa lyono liwuyilaga madeko galyo.

12 No munhu mulele muno, yuyo yali ciba,

si nho munhu mono kehwesaga yena mahala,

kumbe ho yasina.

13 Alu mwene, nhawule u munhu mudoba,

si kalawilaga kunze?

Alo kowa yakogopa simba?

14 Nayo munhu mudoba, kebiducizaga mu citanda du,

yehwanile nho mulango wono wuninyinaga mu zibawaba zayo.

15 Ne wanhu wanji wadoba muno,

wakulemegwa sunga no kutajila ndigwa mu milomo yawo.

16 No munhu mudoba, kehwesaga nha vyono yena mahala,

kuciza wanhu mupunghati,

wono wosuma kulavya mahweso maswanu.

17 Nawo u witajila mu nghani zono si zako,

wihwanile no wubita mu nzila,

kuwa kugebata makutu ge libwa nhinicizo.

18 No munhu mono komwajiliza nghani muyagwe,

hanyuma kanyhi ho yatye yali kumwizavya,

19 yuyo yehwanile munhu wo lukwale,

mono yakwidawalila cipe ciwulaji.

20 Hono hasina ngwagu, moto si wukolelaga,

na hono hasina wusomenghanya, hawezaga hasina mazozo.

21 Makala, go galekaga mavu gawa gali moto.

Nazo zingwagu, zo zilekaga moto wukwaka.

Nayo musomenghanya,

yo kalekaga mazozo gakujendelela.

22 Ne zinghani ze maheho, zili muzinzi!

Nase, wulenyi vyono cizendile!

23 Ne zinghani zo wulambi,

zihundikaga mahweso ge mbeka,

zihwanile ne nyungu swanu ye lilongo,

yono hiliwe na fweza kunze yayo.

24 Nayo munhu mukoci,

kahundicizaga lusulo lwakwe,

mu zinghani zo wiyendezi.

25 Ne zinghani zakwe, zijelaga nha swanu,

ninga wulece kumwitumbila.

Hambi nhumbula yakwe, nha yimemile lusulo du.

26 Kosuma kujela kuluhundika lusulo lwakwe ko wusugu,

ninga i wanhu wose woziwona i zimbi zono yakwituma.

27 Ne wanhu wono wawategaga wayawo mitego,

wagwilaga kanyhi weneco.

Ne wanhu wono wabidusaga libwe,

ho liwagwile wayawo,

libwe liwuyaga no kuwagwila weneco.

28 No lulimi lo wulambi,

luwasulaga wono lukuwatamisa.

Ne zinghani zo wulambi, ziletaga wongomele.
