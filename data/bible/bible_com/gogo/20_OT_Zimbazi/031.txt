Zimbazi 31

Wulanjizwe kuli Mutemi

1 Azi zo nghani zono mutemi Lamuyeli yafundwa no nyina, yatya,

2 “Agwe yo gwegwe du mwanangu we cilume monga,

mono namulomba kuli Mulungu,

nayo yakamhela.

Alu magu, hamba nghulonjele nityeci?

3 Ninga 'ne nghukutya vyono,

wulecele lukulu kumalila vilungo vyako vyose mu wusenyha,

ne sawo yako yose mu wacekulu,

hambi zizo nha zawahodangaga sunga ne watemi.

4 Holeka wuhulicize gwe mwanangu Lamuyeli,

i watemi si wanojeye wawe wagalo

'jimbi, hamba kuwa no wusulumizi wo wugalo 'jimbi.

5 Ku soko hono wawe wagala wujimbi,

hobaho hamba si wakukumbucila kanyhi zo wulajilize,

kanyhi hamba si wakucelelela ze ling'ani ye wanhu.

6 “Holeka, wugalo 'jimbi,

wuwanojeye wanhu wono wakufwa,

na wono wali mu wukalale.

7 Wawo langa wo wanojeye wang'we,

ho wasemwe zo wuciwa wawo,

no wukalale wawo.

8 Wulonje zo wuling'anize,

so wono si wosuma kwilonjela weneco.

Wuwataze wono wasina wutazi,

ho watuje ling'ani yawo.

9 Holeka, wulonje ku soko yawo zinghani zawo,

kanyhi weze wuli muling'anizi we ling'ani.

No kuwatendela ze ling'ani i waciwa na wono wanjiye.”

U mugolece muswanu mono yakwiliye

10 Na mbeka kukamu kumutuga mugolece mono yakwiliye! Hambi nha yena wuguzi wukamu muno si nha vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu!
11 Nayo mugolece we liwala lilyo, sunga no mulume wakwe, kawezaga yamwitumbiye. Kanyhi hamba si kolo ciwapa yaye.

12 Na mu myaka yose yo wikalane wawo, hasina

hambe 'zuwa lyono mugolece yamukalaza mulume wakwe.

13 Langa mwene vilo vyose, kekalaga yakunoza myenda

yo wukunzeje we zingholo, hamonga na ye bafuta.

14 Kanyhi kakongholaga ndigwa ako na ako,

yakuyileta mu kaya, kanozaga nhe wasuluza

wa mu ngalawa yo wukwawi.

15 Na kanyhi kelawaga kwinuka hakali mitondo muno

muno, ho yawikanize ndigwa so we mhuga yakwe,

hamonga no kumugawila u mwitumami wakwe we cicekulu,

milimo yono kokwituma.

16 Kalondolaga mugunda, kawa kuwugula ne zipesa

zono yali yakwawa, hanyuma kawa kuhadika zabibu.

17 Kanyhi muhidimbazi muno mu milimo,

yali vilungo kanyhi mwitumi.

18 Nayo kawezaga yamanyile ne vifo vye vinhu vyose

vyono kanozaga.

Nayo ketumaga milimo sunga lizuwa ligwila.

19 Mwene kabotaga mweneco kwe miwoko yakwe,

no kuhona myenda yakwe kwe miwoko yakwe.

20 Kanyhi kawawonelaga lusungu i waciwa

na wono wali mu wanjile.

21 Na mu madaha ge cinyuli, mwene hamba si

kehwelelaga yaye, hambi we mhuga yakwe wose,

nha wena myenda yono yowapela civuce.

22 Kahonaga mweneco ne mitote yo kutandika mucanyha

ye vitanda, kanyhi, kavwalaga myenda yo lugandu.

23 No mulume wakwe, yo munhu we lilumbo.

Kanyhi yo yumonga wa muli walongola mu'si.

24 Kanyhi kahonaga myenda ne misipi, kawa

kuwaguziza i wasuluza.

25 Yo mucekulu mono yali vilungo, kanyhi yena

lilumbo, hamba si kehwelelaga za mu vilo vyono vikuza.

26 Kanyhi, yendile kulonga ko wupolo zinghani ze mahala.

27 Kanyhi mwene kekalaga yakwipiganza, yakuwalondolela

visakwa we mhuga yakwe.

28 Ne wana nawo, wendezwaga muno, nayo mulume wakwe,

kamulumbaga muno.

29 Mulume wakwe katezaga, “I wacekulu wenji,

mbeka wagolece waswanu, ninga agwe

wuwacizize wawo wose.”

30 Nawo wuhijila mbejele, wina zo wukoci,

nawo wuswanu wo wulangwe, wa bwete.

Ninga mucekulu mono kamogopaga Muwaha Mulungu,

yuyo langa, kotuga lilumbo.

31 Wumulumbaje mugolece wako, muli zose zono

yakwituma. Nayo, holeka kotuga lukumyo hali wanhu wose.
