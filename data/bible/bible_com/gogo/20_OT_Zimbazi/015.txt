Zimbazi 15

1 Wupitule we mhola mhola, wutuzaga likuwo

Ninga nghani yono yikutamisa, yisagahazaga likuwo.

2 Wulonje we wanhu we mahala,

wuwezaga wuswanu wo wizuce.

Ninga i walele, walongaga zo wulele.

3 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, kaziwonaga zose hanhu hose.

Kacigozaga wose, hono cikwituma ziswanu hamba 'mbi.

4 Zinghani ze ngola, ziletaga wumi.

Ninga zinghani zo wiwanganile, zihodangaga nhumbula.

5 Yo nghani yo wulele, kuzibedegala zono sogo yakufunda.

Ninga yo nghani swanu kugabocela mafundo gono yakukufunda.

6 Wanhu we ling'ani, wazilanjililaga zisawo zawo.

Ninga i wabi, wajilizaga zisawo

zawo mu cipango co wugaye.

7 We mahala, wo weyagazaga zo wumanye, sinhe walele.

8 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu,

kendezwaga no wulombe we wanhu we ling'ani.

Ninga yagasulile makumbiko gono

wamulavizyaga i wanhu wabi.

9 Zinzila ze wanhu wabi, Muwaha Mulungu yazisulile.

Mwene yawendile wono wetumaga zo mugoloka.

10 Na hono witume zimbi, kotamiswa ko wulipicizwe.

Na hono wulece kuvumila wufundwe, kofwa.

11 Kanyhi wumanye vyono, hamba yisi ye wafu,

siyosuma kumwimila Muwaha Mulungu,

ho yalece kusuma kuzimanya, zono zili mumo yaye.

Alu munhu ho kosuma nhawule,

kumuhundicila Mulungu, mahweso gakwe?

12 Ne wanhu wabedegazi, si wendaga wufundwe yaye,

kanyhi hamba si wendile kuwuza nghani kuli we mahala.

13 Ne wanhu nawo hono wawe wena nyemo,

kowawona wusu wawo wusangalaye.

Ninga hono wawe wena wukalale, wakwijela wasininghaye.

14 Ne wanhu wono wena wizuce, wendaga kwiyijiza,

Ninga i walele, weyigutyaga no wulele wawo du.

15 Mikalile yo muciwa, vilo vyose yiwezaga ya magayo.

Ninga i wanhu waseci, wekalaga nhondo zose wamotile.

16 Yili ciba hono wuwe muciwa, akuno wumogopile Muwaha

Mulungu, si nha kuwa mugoli hamonga no wugaye.

17 Kanyhi yili ciba kulya ndigwa ye mboga ye sunga du,

hamonga ne wanhu wono mwiyendile.

Sinho kulya nyama yono yijinile ha ciligati yo lusulo.

18 Makuwo galetaga matemanuko.

Ninga wukangalizi, wuletaga mulamu.

19 Na hono wuwe mudoba,

kokwitang'anila ne zimbali hanhu hose.

Ninga hono wuwe wuli muhidimbazi,

hamba si kogazika yaye.

20 Nayo mwana we mahala, kamusangalazaga sogwe.

Ninga mwana mulele, kamukalazaga nyina yakwe.

Ninga we mahala, wetumaga zo mugoloka.

21 Ne wanhu walele, wawusangalalilaga wulele wawo.

Ninga we mahala, wetumaga zo mugoloka.

22 Weze wuli mupelu ku wubocela zo wufundwe,

holeka komoteswa.

Ninga hono wuwe wuli zeze,

kokwiyambuza go mweneco.

23 Yiwezaga nyemo mbaha lecenyi, hono wupitule nghani

yono yinojeye, mu cipango cono cinojeye!

24 Ne wanhu we mahala,

wayityatyaga nzila yono yiwatonganga ko ku wumi.

Ninga si wayityatyaga nzila yono yowatonga sunga kwi'fwa.

25 U Muwaha Mulungu kozimwagulanga

zikaya ze wanhu wedunhumuzi.

Langa sawo yo mucekulu mufwile, koyidima.

26 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yagasulile mahweso ge wanhu wabi.

Langa mwene kendezwaga ne zinghani za mu wendo.

27 Na hono wijelele witujile sawo mu nzila yono si yinojeye,

wumanye vyono wukuwenjiza we mhuga yako mu magayo.

Holeka wulecele lukulu kulya mafupo,

holeka nagwe kowumipa vilo vinji.

28 I wanhu we ling'ani, wamulaga kwihwesa, ho wapitula

Ninga i wabi wapitulaga musunho, no kuleta mazozo.

29 I wanhu we ling'ani lono wakulomba,

Muwaha Mulungu kawahulicizaga.

Langa i wabi, si kawacelelelaga yaye.

30 No wusu wono wina nyemo, wusangalazaga nhumbula.

Nazo zinghani ziswanu, ziletaga wusangalavu.

31 Hono wuwe mupelu kuhulika zo wufundwe, wina mahala.

32 Ninga hono wuwe wuli ng'anje ku wufundwe,

kokwitamisa go mweneco.

Na hono wuvumile kufundwa, kowa na nhumbula swanu.

33 Ne cogopo ca kuli Muwaha Mulungu,

wo wijize we mahala.

Yikunojeye weze wuli mwinenenyhezi

lono wukali sinatuga lukumyo.
