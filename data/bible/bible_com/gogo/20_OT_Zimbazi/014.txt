Zimbazi 14

1 Ne zikaya ziwezaga swanu so mahala go mucekulu.

Ninga wulele wuzihodangaga.

2 Weze mwihuwilwa, akuno wukulajila cogopo cako

kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

3 No widunhumuzi wo munhu mulele,

wumupelaga wulonji winji.

Ninga zinghani zo munhu we mahala, zimukamisaga.

4 Hono hawe hasina ng'ombe zo kukwega lisili,

madongha gako gowa gasina cinji.

Ninga u winji we ndudula, wulajilaga vyono

ng'ombe ko zili.

5 U muhomelezi mwihuwilwa,

si kalongaga wulambi yaye.

Ninga mono si mwihuwilwa,

si kalongaga cinji, langa zo wulambi du.

6 Ne wanhu wedunhumuzi,

si watugaga mahala yaye.

Ninga wono wezuce,

weyijizaga mbela mbela.

7 Wilegaje kuli i wanhu walele,

hambi nha wasina cinji cono wosuma kukwijiza.

8 Alo nhawule mono yena wizuce kawezaga yena mahala?

Ku soko vyono mwene, yamanyile zono yakwituma.

H amba nhawule munhu mulele nayo, kawa yasina mahala?

Ku soko vyono nayo yakwiwona yamanyile.

9 Ne wanhu walele, na hono wetume wubi,

hamba si wacelelelaga yaye.

Ninga we mahala, walondolaga wunenelwe.

10 Wusangalale, wo wako.

No wusungu, nawo wako.

Hasina hamba yunji mono mohanga nayo muli zizo.

11 Ne kaya yo munhu muswanu yikalaga yimile du,

lono kaya yo munhu mubi yakondya kumwagulwa.

12 Ne nzila yono wukwihwesa yo nzila swanu,

kumbe yo nzila yono yokutegula kwi'fwa.

13 Nalo luseko luhundikaga wukalale.

Ninga kolo nyemo yikuwuka,

wukalale wuwezaga vyo wuli baho du.

14 Nawo i wanhu wabi, wopegwa wulipicizwe wawo.

Nawo i wanhu waswanu,

wohumbilizwa nha vyono genha metumo gawo.

15 U munhu mulele, kehuwilaga zinghani zose.

Ninga i wanhu we mahala,

wayilanjililaga muno i mijendele yawo.

16 I wanhu we mahala,

welanjililaga walece kwinjila mu wubi.

Ninga i walele, wajendaga wedunhumuye wasina mahwele.

17 Ne wanhu wono wena likuwo lyono lyakaga musunho,

wetumaga nghani za wulele.

Ninga we mahala wekalaga wapolile.

18 I walele, wohala zo wulele wawo.

Ninga we mahala, wohumbilizwa wizuce.

19 Nawo i wanhu wabi,

wolofugama ha mwande ya we ling'ani,

no kulomba wendezwe wawo.

20 Munhu muciwa, yakuwa kusulwa ne wanhu wose,

sunga ne wekalani wayagwe wose.

Ninga i wagoli wendigwe na wanhu wenji.

21 Ninga hono wusace kuwa no wusangalale,

ale wuwendaje waciwa.

Kanyhi wumanye vyono wubi lono kuwabedegala wayago.

22 Na hono wuwe wukwituma viswanu, kotuga lilumbo,

no kwitumbilwa ne wayago.

Ninga hono wuwe wukwituma zimbi, ale, wukusutya.

23 Wuhidimbale kwituma milimo, nagwe kotuga cambuzi.

Ninga hono wuwe wikaye du wina mulomo, kowa muciwa.

24 Ne wanhu we mahala, wahumbilizwaga sawo.

Ninga i walele, wamanyicilaga mu wulele wawo.

25 Nayo muhomelezi,

kaponyaga wumi hono yalonje ze mbeka.

Ninga hono yalonje zo wulambi,

yakuwasonjeza wanhu.

26 Na mu cogopo co Muwaha Mulungu, mo mo wuli

witumbilo, no wucitilwe wo munhu, na we mhuga yakwe.

27 Hono wuwe kusaka kwilega lifwa, yikunojeye wuwe

ne cogopo kuli Muwaha Mulungu, mono yo muvwilo wo wumi.

28 Ne lilumbo lyo mutemi lili mu winji we wanhu

wono yatemile. Ninga hono yawe ne wanhu wacefu,

wutemi wakwe wukuwa wa bwete.

29 Munhu we mahala,

si yakuwa kukolwa ne likuwo musunho.

Ninga hono wuwe kukolwaga ne likuwo mbela,

alu agwe wukwilajila vyono wuli mulele.

30 No mulamu wa mu nhumbula,

wuwunozaga muwili wuwa muswanu.

Ninga wivu, wihwanile lilonda libi.

31 Na hono wuwewonele waciwa,

wukumutovula Mulungu mono yawawumba.

Ninga mono yakuwawonela lusungu waciwa,

yuyo yakumukumya Mulungu.

32 I wanhu wabi,

weletelaga lifwa weneco ku soko yo wubi wawo.

Ninga i wanhu waswanu,

wena witumbilo sunga ko ku wufwe wawo.

33 Ne mahala gekalaga mu mahweso gose go

munhu mono yena wizuce.

Ninga i walele, si wazimanyile ze mahala yaye.

34 Ling'ani yinozaga likungugo liwe ne 'lumbo.

Ninga wubi, co cevu ciwaha kuli makungugo gose.

35 I watemi nawo,

wendezwaga ne wetumami wono wakwituma no wizuce.

Ninga wakuwa kuwalipiciza wono milimo yikuwafutila.
