Zimbazi 18

1 Wanhu wono wetemanulaga ne wayawo,

walondolaga ze masulumizi gawo weneco.

Wesumanizaga na zono wayawo wakuwona zo swanu.

2 No mulele si kacelelelwaga, hono yawe yakwizuka hamba ng'o.

Langa mwene kasakaga kwilajila du, vyono mumanya vyose.

3 Nawo wubi ne cevu, vibitanaga hamonga.

Ne lilumbo hono likuwucile, wuzaga wubedegalwe.

4 Ne zinghani zo munhu, gawezaga go mahala,

Genha malenga ge cisolo, nhe 'kolongo likuhuluma.

5 Si viswanu kumuhijila mbejele u munhu musutyi,

no kwajiliza ling'ani yo munhu we ling'ani.

6 Na kolo zinghani zo wiwanganile zo wulele zikwanza,

Mwene yakulonga zo wutowe.

7 No munhu mono yasina mahala,

kalongaga zinghani zo kwiyambuza mweneco.

Nayo kebatilwaga mu zinghani zakwe mweneco.

8 Ne zinghani zo kupwepwelwa, ziwezaga zili muzinzi.

Nase cendile muno kuzihulicila!

9 No mudoba ku milimo, yo munhu mubi muno,

Yehwanile no munhu muhodanji.

10 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, wo munala we vilungo,

hono i wanhu we ling'ani,

wosuma kwihundika no kuwa wapolile.

11 Nawo wagoli, wawonaga nha vyono,

sawo yawo yosuma kuwadima nhe lilulu litali,

kanyhi nhe majinji matali,

gono gali vilungo, gono galipilime likaya.

12 U munhu mwinenenyhezi, yo katugaga lilumbo.

Ninga i wedunhumuzi, wali mu nzila yo wongomele.

13 Weze wina ngola, lono wukali si napitula cinji,

Ninga hono wuwe wuli musupu ku wupitule,

kokwiyinjiza mu wu wula mahala no wutovulwe.

14 U muhe wako wo wokusikamya mu wusocele wako.

Ninga hono wuwusuputule,

no witumbilo wako wokwajilila.

15 I wanhu wono wena wizuce,

nhondo zose wekalaga wahidimbazi,

kanyhi wendile no kwiyijiza.

16 Na hono wuwe kusaka mwiwone no munhu we lilumbo,

agwe mutegulilaje cipegwa du, hodu,

kowa kupelu kwiwona nayo.

17 No munhu mono kalongolaga kupitula zinghani mu mahakama,

nhondo zose mwene, kewonanga yali ling'ani,

sunga kolo mono yakumudaya, yakumuwuza mawuzo.

18 Na hono wanhu wejete we vilungo,

wawe wena wiwanganile mu mahakama,

ale, kula yo yosuma kuzilamula zinghani zizo.

19 Wumutazaje nduguyo, nayo kolokucitila,

kolowa nhe lijinji lyono likamile kuli 'gwe.

Ninga hono mugombane nayo,

ale, nayo hamba si kolokupela wutazi yaye.

20 Ne mikalile yako,

yomema mikalo ye zinghani zono kuwa kuzilonga.

21 Ne zinghani zono wukuwa kuzilonga,

zosuma kuwukamisa wumi wako hamba kuwongomeza.

Holeka kobocela nha vyono zinha zinghani zako.

22 Hono wumutuje mugolece muswanu,

mbeka watuga cinhu ciswanu.

Nayo yikulajila vyono, Muwaha Mulungu yakwendile.

23 Na lono munhu muciwa yakulomba,

kalombaga yakwineneleza.

Ninga munhu mugoli, kapitulaga ko wukali du.

24 Wiyendi wunji, si wujendelelaga vilo vinji.

Ninga wiyendi wunji nawo, wucizaga na wono muli ndugu.
