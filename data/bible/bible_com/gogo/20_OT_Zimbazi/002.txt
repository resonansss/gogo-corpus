Zimbazi 2

Cambuzi ce Mahala

1 Gwe mwanangu, wiyijizaje zono nghukulonjela,

kanyhi wulece kuzisemwa zono nghulonjela witume.

2 Hulicize kuli ze mahala, wuzimanye.

3 Heya, wulombe wutuje wumanye,

no kuwucemeleza wizuce.

4 Wulondole wuhidimbaye,

nha vyono wali wuyilondole fweza,

hamba sawo yono hundikwe.

5 Na hono wulondole wuhidimbaye,

kosuma kuyimanya nhunizo ye cogopo co Muwaha Mulungu,

nagwe baho, ho kosuma kuzimanya za kuli Mulungu.

6 Hambi nha yo Muwaha Mulungu du,

mono kawapelaga wanhu mahala,

na muli mwene, mo vilawaga wumanye wose no wizuce.

7 Kanyhi mwene, yo kalavyaga wutazi no wucitilwe,

kuli wose we ling'ani, ne wanhu wehuwilwa.

8 Kanyhi, yo mweneco du mono kawacitilaga wose,

wono wawatendelaga wayawo ko wukali,

no kuwadima wose wono welavizye kuli mwene.

9 Holeka, hono wuhulicize 'ne,

baho ho komanyila zono zili mugoloka,

na ze ling'ani, kanyhi swanu.

Kanyhi kosuma kuzimanya zono zikunojeye witume.

10 Kowa wina mahala,

no wizuce wako wokupela wusangalale mu mutima wako.

11 Wizuce wako no wumanye wako,

vyokukamisa

12 no kukwimila, ho wulece kwituma zimbi yaye.

Navyo vyokutemanula ne wanhu wono wendile makani-kani,

kuli zono walongaga

13 i wanhu wono wakondya kuyileka mikalile ye ling'ani,

no kwikala mu citi co wubi,

14 wanhu wono wendezwaga mu wituma zimbi,

wono wasangalilaga mu zimbi,

15 wanhu wono wayisina mbeka,

wono si wosuma hamba kwitumbilwa yaye.

16 Kosuma kumwilega mucekulu musenyha,

mono yakujela kukupepeleza kwe zinghani zakwe ndece,

17 mono si mwihuwilwa na kuli mulume wakwe mwenco,

mono kanyhi, yakusemwa no wilahize wakwe.

18 Na hono wubite ku kaya yakwe,

ale, wumanye nha vyono,

wutyatizye nzila ye lifwa.

Na hono wubite, nha cinji,

wukuyisejelela yisi ye wafu.

19 Hambi nha hasina munhu mono yabita kumulamusa,

mono kanyhi yawuya.

Hambi nha si kowuyajila kanyhi mu nzila yo wumi.

20 Holeka agwe, yikunojeye wutyatye musambo we wanhu waswanu,

wono wekaye mu mikalile ye ling'ani.

21 Hambi i wanhu we ling'ani,

kanyhi wono wakwilaniciye,

nha wokwikala mu yisi yetu yiyi.

22 Langa i wabi, Mulungu kolowawusiza lukulu mu yisi,

yawakulanje i wanyo wubi,

nha vyono wavikulaga vilimwa mu muhangha.
