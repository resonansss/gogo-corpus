Zimbazi 9

Mahala no Wulele

1 Mahala, yazenga kaya yakwe,

yakayiseseza ne masumbili mupunghati.

2 Yakatemanga ng'ombe yakwe so sikukuu,

yakayihanzahanza ne divayi yakwe,

yakavitenga vyose ha meza.

3 Yakawa kuwatuma wetumami wakwe we cicekulu,

wabite wakeme hacanyha,

i hanhu hono henucile muno mwi'kaya,

wawalalice wanhu wawe kutya,

4 “Ale muze akuno,

anye mo wanhu mono musina mahala!”

Na kuli munhu mulele yakutya,

5 “Ale wuze akuno wulye ndigwa yangu

no kung'wa divayi yangu yono hanzahanzwe.

6 Muwuce mwi'puga lye wanhu walele,

ho muwumipe.

Mujendele mu nzila yo wizuce.”

7 Na hono wumufunde munhu mubezi,

kokwitujila soni go mweneco.

Na hono wumutacile munhu mubi,

kokwitamisa go mweneco.

8 Holeka yili ciba wulece kumufunda u munhu mubezi,

hambi nha kokusula.

Ninga hono wumufunde munhu we mahala,

ayo langa kokuhulika.

9 Ne nghani yo yose yono koyilonga kuli munhu we mahala,

ayo yomunoza yawe ne mahala muno.

Ne nghani yo yose yono komulonjela munhu we ling'ani,

yiyo yomonjeleza wizuce wakwe.

10 I cogopo co Muwaha Mulungu,

wo mwamulizo we mahala.

Na hono wumumanye Mono Yelile,

ale kowa watuga wizuce.

11 Nago mahala gokonjelezeza myaka yo wikale wako.

12 Nagwe kowa mwi'walo lye wanhu wono wena cambuzi,

hono wuwe wina mahala. Ninga hono wugaleme mahala,

ale, kowa wuli mwi'puga lya wono wotuga magazo.

13 Nawo wulele,

wihwanile ne lizozo, no wu wula mahala,

wo mucekulu mono yasina cevu.

14 Hambi mwene nha kekalaga ha mulango we kaya yakwe,

hamba lunji mwi'goda lyono liwicigwe

i hanhu hono henucile muno mwi'kaya,

15 na kolo yakuwawona i wanhu wono wakupuluta baho,

wono wali mu zawo,

nalu wakuwuya ku zikwawo,

kawa kuwacemeleza yakutya,

16 “Alu, muze akuno anye nyo wanhu mono musina mahala!”

Na kuli walele yakutya,

17 “Malenga go kuhiza gali muzinzi.

Ne ndigwa yono yikuligwa cinyele,

nayo swanu muno.”

18 Ninga munhu yuyo,

yakuwa si yamanyile vyono wanhu wose wono wenjilaga mu kaya

yo mucekulu yuyo, wafwizaga.

Na vyono wose wono wakondya kwinjila,

alu wali kusi ye yisi ye wafu.
