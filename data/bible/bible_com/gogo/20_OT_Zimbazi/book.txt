Zimbazi 1

Zimbazi

1 Azi zo zimbazi za Solomona u mwana wa Dawudi, u mutemi wa Isilayeli.
2 Zo mbazi zono zosuma kukutaza wumanye mahala, no kutuga wulanjizwe wuswanu, no kumanya zinonga zono zina nhunizo muno muno.
3 Nazo zokwijiza nha vyono kosuma kwikala kwa mahala, no kuwa mwihuwilwa, kanyhi wuli ling'ani, hamonga no wuswanu.
4 Zokunoza wuwuce mu wulele, wuwe munhu mono kosuma kuwejiza wazelelo, ho wawe no wumanye.
5 Ne zimbazi zizi, zosuma konjeleza wumanye kuli wanhu we mahala, no kuwapela wutonji wa mu wumanye wawo,
6 ho wasume kuzimanya muno zinhunizo za mugati muno, ze zimbazi, hamonga ne vidalicizo vye wanhu wawo we mahala.

Mejizo kuli wazelelo

7 I cogopo co Muwaha Mulungu, wo wusimyo wo wumanye. Ninga i walele wabedegalaga mahala ne mafundo.

8 Mwanangu, wuhulice mafundo gono sogo

no nyina yako wakwijizaga wulece kugaleka.

9 Hambi mafundo gawo nha gokupela nyikala swanu.

Gowa nha mukumbo we lihewa,

mu mutwe wako,

hamba nho mukufu wa mu singo yako.

10 Mwanangu, wanyo wubi hono wakukwejeleze ko wusugu,

wulece kulumbila lukulu.

11 Hono watye,

“Ale cibitane cikamulondole munhu cimuwulaje!

Cibitane cikawahome wanhu wono wasina masutya,

wuwe nha wo mwidawalo wetu!

12 Nha vyono yinha, cowamela wakali womi nhe yisi ye wafu.

Kotya wakugwila mwi'kweme.

13 Nase cozilondola sawo zose ze wagoli,

ciwe kuzimemya zikaya zetu na vyono cowa caviteka!

14 Holeka wuze cilunze nase,

nase cogawana vinhu vyose vyono covihiza.”

15 Ninga 'ne nghukutya nhavi agwe mwanangu,

vyono, wulecele lukulu kwilunza ne wanhu we liwala lilyo,

hamba wulece kuwatyatya.

16 Hambi wene nha si walindilaga kuwituma zimbi,

nhondo zose wekalaga wewikanize so wuwulaji du.

17 Kanyhi wumanye vyono,

si viswanu kuyitega ndeje no lwavu,

lono nayo yikunuguliye yikukuwona,

18 hambi wanhu we liwala lilyo,

nha wategaga mutego,

kanyhi wawa kwigwiza weneco,

mu mutego wono wokwiwulaga wali wene.

19 Kanyhi, wumanye vyono wuhizi,

wusakaga wumi wo muhizi.

Ne nghani yiyi, yikuwa kumwijelela munhu wo wose,

mono kekalilaga muli zo wuwulaji.

Mahala gakucema

20 Alu wuhulicize!

Mahala gakucemeleza mu zinzila na mu zi sokoni,

21 gakucemeleza gehindile,

sunga na mu malango ge likaya,

sunga ne hanhu ho hose,

hono wanhu wendile kwihanguliza hamonga.

22 Gakucemeleza gakutya,

“Nye nyo wanhu walele!

Alu ho mojendelela kwikala muli walele sunga nili?

Alu si mosuma kwiyijiza hamba hadodo lo?

23 Ale lu, muhulicize lono nghumutacilenyi.

Hambi 'ne nha nghumulanjizenyi viswanu,

no kumugubulilenyi ze mahala gangu.

24 Nasola katali nghumucemelezenyi,

nghumusacenyi ho munyizize,

ninga nanye hamba kuhulika yaye.

25 Mwakondya kuwubedegala wulanjize wangu wose,

hamba si mwendile ne mafundo gangu.

26 Ninga nanye kolo mwenjila mu magazo,

nane nomusekanjenyi muno muno.

Kanyhi nomwizwajizenyi kolo zimbi zikumwaganenyi.

27 Kolo magazo gago gakumuzizenyi nho mukukunzo,

gawe kumuletelenyi libeho lyo wugaye,

nanye lono mowa muli mu wusungu no wukalale.

28 Ale baho nanye, ho mulagalondolela mahala,

ninga nane hamba si nolomutondowozenyi ng'o.

Nanye molonondola hanhu hose,

ninga hamba si molombona yaye.

29 Hambi nha mwawusula wumanye,

hamba kusaka nhondo zose kumuhulika Muwaha Mulungu yaye.

30 Kanyhi hamba si mwasacila lukulu kugabocela mafundo gangu,

hamba kugatununhicila bwete.

31 Holeka luno, zomwaganenyi zono ziwanojeye,

ne metumo genyu gomupelenyi mhungo.

32 Holeka i wanhu walele,

wofwila mu wulele wawo,

hambi nha si wakusaka mahala.

Vyono i wanhu wono wasina mahala,

nha wakuwa kongomezwa no wuwula mahala wawo.

33 Ninga munhu wo wose mono kohuliciza'ne,

yuyo kokwikala yapolile,

kowa mutulano,

hamba kowa yasina soko yo kogopa cinji co cose yaye.”


Zimbazi 2

Cambuzi ce Mahala

1 Gwe mwanangu, wiyijizaje zono nghukulonjela,

kanyhi wulece kuzisemwa zono nghulonjela witume.

2 Hulicize kuli ze mahala, wuzimanye.

3 Heya, wulombe wutuje wumanye,

no kuwucemeleza wizuce.

4 Wulondole wuhidimbaye,

nha vyono wali wuyilondole fweza,

hamba sawo yono hundikwe.

5 Na hono wulondole wuhidimbaye,

kosuma kuyimanya nhunizo ye cogopo co Muwaha Mulungu,

nagwe baho, ho kosuma kuzimanya za kuli Mulungu.

6 Hambi nha yo Muwaha Mulungu du,

mono kawapelaga wanhu mahala,

na muli mwene, mo vilawaga wumanye wose no wizuce.

7 Kanyhi mwene, yo kalavyaga wutazi no wucitilwe,

kuli wose we ling'ani, ne wanhu wehuwilwa.

8 Kanyhi, yo mweneco du mono kawacitilaga wose,

wono wawatendelaga wayawo ko wukali,

no kuwadima wose wono welavizye kuli mwene.

9 Holeka, hono wuhulicize 'ne,

baho ho komanyila zono zili mugoloka,

na ze ling'ani, kanyhi swanu.

Kanyhi kosuma kuzimanya zono zikunojeye witume.

10 Kowa wina mahala,

no wizuce wako wokupela wusangalale mu mutima wako.

11 Wizuce wako no wumanye wako,

vyokukamisa

12 no kukwimila, ho wulece kwituma zimbi yaye.

Navyo vyokutemanula ne wanhu wono wendile makani-kani,

kuli zono walongaga

13 i wanhu wono wakondya kuyileka mikalile ye ling'ani,

no kwikala mu citi co wubi,

14 wanhu wono wendezwaga mu wituma zimbi,

wono wasangalilaga mu zimbi,

15 wanhu wono wayisina mbeka,

wono si wosuma hamba kwitumbilwa yaye.

16 Kosuma kumwilega mucekulu musenyha,

mono yakujela kukupepeleza kwe zinghani zakwe ndece,

17 mono si mwihuwilwa na kuli mulume wakwe mwenco,

mono kanyhi, yakusemwa no wilahize wakwe.

18 Na hono wubite ku kaya yakwe,

ale, wumanye nha vyono,

wutyatizye nzila ye lifwa.

Na hono wubite, nha cinji,

wukuyisejelela yisi ye wafu.

19 Hambi nha hasina munhu mono yabita kumulamusa,

mono kanyhi yawuya.

Hambi nha si kowuyajila kanyhi mu nzila yo wumi.

20 Holeka agwe, yikunojeye wutyatye musambo we wanhu waswanu,

wono wekaye mu mikalile ye ling'ani.

21 Hambi i wanhu we ling'ani,

kanyhi wono wakwilaniciye,

nha wokwikala mu yisi yetu yiyi.

22 Langa i wabi, Mulungu kolowawusiza lukulu mu yisi,

yawakulanje i wanyo wubi,

nha vyono wavikulaga vilimwa mu muhangha.


Zimbazi 3

Mafundo kuli wazelelo

1 Gwe mwanangu, wulece kuzisemwa zono nghukwijiza.

Kanyhi weze kuzikumbucila nhondo zose zono nghukulajiliza witume.

2 Ne mafundo gangu,

gokupela wumoteswe vilo vinji hamonga no mu mikalile yako.

3 Weze wuli mwihuwilwa,

kanyhi wuli cihuliko.

Wuwuwopeleze wihuwilwa ne cihuliko mu singo yako,

wuvyandice mu nhumbula yako mugati.

4 Nagwe hono witume zizo,

u Mulungu hamonga ne wanhu wokwendezwa nagwe.

5 Wumwitumbile Muwaha Mulungu kwe nhumbula yako yose.

Wulece kwiyitumbila mahala gako go mweneco.

6 Wumukumbucile Muwaha Mulungu,

muli zose zono wukwituma,

nayo kokutonga mu nzila yono yinojeye.

7 Ninga wulecele lukulu kwihwesa nha vyono,

agwe wuli we mahala go mweneco,

langa agwe weze ne cihuliko kuli Muwaha Mulungu du,

kanyhi wulemele lukulu kwituma zimbi.

8 Na hono wuwe ne cihuliko kuli Muwaha Mulungu,

ale, yowa nha miti miswanu,

yono yokuponya zinghomele zako no kukucepulila wusungu wako.

9 Wumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu,

mu nzila yo kumulavizya vilimwa viswanu vyono wuvitujile mu mugunda wako.

10 Hobaho nago madongha gako,

gomemywa wuhemba,

kanyhi kowa na divayi nyinji yono yokufutila na ha wuwiko wayo.

11 Mwanangu, kolo Muwaha Mulungu yakukufunda,

wulece kugabedegala mafundo gakwe,

langa wugabocele.

12 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu,

nha kawafundaga wose wono yawendile,

nha vyono sogo yo munhu kamufundaga mwanagwe mono yamwendile.

13 Yo mumoti munhu mono kawonaga mahala,

no munhu mono katugaga wizuce.

14 Hambi nha mo mocili cambuzi ciwaha muno si nhe cambuzi ca mu fweza.

Kanyhi mocili cambuzi ciwaha muno si nhe zahabu.

15 Mahala, mbeka gena sawilo mbaha muno,

si nhe vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu,

na hasina cinhu cinji co cose,

cono kocisaka cono kosuma kwihwananiza ne mahala.

16 Mahala gakupelaga vilo vinji vyo kwikala mumi mu mikalile yako,

hamonga no wugoli ne lilumbo.

17 Mahala gokupela nyemo mu mikalile yako hamonga no mulamu.

18 Na wose wono wena mahala,

wena nyemo.

Ne mahala gowapela wumi.

19 Muwaha Mulungu yayiwumba yinze,

kwe mahala gakwe.

Na ko wizuce wakwe,

yakawusimya wulanga.

20 Kanyhi mahala gakwe go gaganoza makolongo gahulume,

ne mavunde nago gayitonyeze mvula mono 'si.

21 Holeka agwe mwanangu,

wukamise mahala hamonga no wizuce.

Wulece kuvileceleza vikutije.

22 Hambi nha vyo vyokupela wumi,

no wendezwe,

hamonga ne mikalile ye nyemo.

23 Nagwe kowa kujenda wupolile mu zinzila zako,

hamba si kokwikobala yaye.

24 Kanyhi kowa wusina woga wo wose,

lono wukwinjila kugona mu citanda cako.

Heya ku cilo kogona nhongo zono zili muzinzi.

25 Hamba si kosuma kugacelelela magayo gono gokuziza nhinicizo,

hamba mukukunzo we wabi kolo wukukuziza.

26 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha kokukamisa.

Hamba si kokuleka yaye wugwile mu mutego.

27 Holeka agwe,

weze kuwatendela ziswanu wono wena wanjile,

mu nghango vyono kosuma.

28 Kanyhi wulecele lukulu kumutya muyago,

yalindile sunga mitondo yaco,

hono kumbe nagwe wali wusume kumupela wutazi lwa kamonga du.

29 Kanyhi wulece kumuwicila muyago cikobazo ho wumutamise,

hambi yuyo muyago,

nha mwikalene behi,

kanyhi mwene yakwitumbiye gwegwe.

30 Kanyhi wulece kwisumaniza no muyago mbula soko,

lono nayo hamba si yakunozize yinji 'mbi.

31 Wulece kuwawonela wivu,

i wanhu wagazi,

hamba wulece kuwega mu metumo gawo.

32 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu,

nha yawasulile i wanhu wetuma zimbi,

langa i weling'ani,

mwene yali hamonga nawo.

33 Muwaha Mulungu kazilijitizaga zikaya ze wanhu wabi,

ninga zikaya ze weling'ani,

mwene kazimotyaga.

34 Kanyhi mwene si kawasakaga i wanhu wedunhumuzi,

langa kawapelaga lihewa i weneneyhezi.

35 Iwe mahala wotuga lukumyo,

ninga walele, wokozwa cevu.


Zimbazi 4

Cambuzi ce mahala

1 Nye wanangu, muhulicize zono sogo yenyu yakumufundenyi.

Mugebate,

mafundo gakwe ho mowa no wizuce.

2 Hambi 'ne zono nghumwijizenyi,

nha nghani swanu,

holeka muzikumbucile zose.

3 Hambi ane nha nali mwana kuli sogo yangu.

Mudodo mwendece we ciwene mwa yaya.

4 Baba yafundaga yatezaga,

“Ale, wukumbucile kuli zono nghukulonjela,

wulece kuzisemwa.

Weze kwituma nha vyono nghukulajiliza,

nagwe holeka kowumipa.

5 Wutuje mahala,

hamonga no wizuce! Wulecele lukulu kusemwa,

hamba kuzibedegala zinghani zangu.

6 Wulece kumuleka mahala,

nayo kokukamisa.

Wumwende, nayo kokucitila,

wikale wupolile.

7 Kutuga mahala yo nghani mbaha yono yicizize,

yono yikunojeye wuyitume.

Heya, hamonga ne zisawo zako zose,

ninga wunojeye witujile mahala.

8 Wende mahala,

nayo kokupela lilumbo.

Wumwibatilize,

nayo kokuletela lukumyo.

9 Nayo kowa wo mukumbo wo lukumyo mu mutwe wako.”

10 Gwe mwanangu, wuhulicizaje,

wuzikamisizaje lukulu zose zono nghukulonjela,

nagwe holeka kokwikala vilo vinji mono'nze.

11 Ane nakondya kukwijiza mahala,

hamonga ne nzila swanu yono koyikola mu mikalile yako.

12 Kanyhi hasina cinji co cose cono cokwimila mu zinzila zako,

hono wuwe kujenda kwe mahala,

na kanyhi hamba si kokwikobala yaye lono wukumangha.

13 Nhondo zose weze wibate zono weyijiza,

hambi mejizo gako,

nha wo wumi wako,

holeka wugalanjililaje galece kukutiga.

14 Wulece kwinjila mu zinzila wetuma zimbi.

Wulecele lukulu kuyityatya misambo ye wanhu wabi,

15 Wulece kwituma zizo,

wuwucile lukulu mu zimbi!

Wuzilemaje zimbi,

wilegaje mu zimbi.

16 Nawo i wanhu wabi,

hamba si wagonaga nhongo sunga langa wetume zimbi du.

Wagonaga meso,

sunga watamise munhu yunji.

17 Nago metumo mabi kuli wene yo ndigwa yawo, hamonga.

No wukali, nawo kuli wene,

vihwanile no wugali ne malenga go kung'wa.

18 Nayo nzila yono wakolaga wanhu we ling'ani,

yihwanile wuzelu we lizuwa lono likusosomoka,

nalyo liwa kujendelela kutuga wuzelu muno sunga kolo welelecela.

19 Ninga nzila ye wanhu wabi,

yina wutitu, yihwanile cilo.

Nawo wakwikobaza hamba kumanya cono ciwakobaze yaye.

20 Holeka agwe mwanangu,

wukamise zono nghulonga,

wuzihulice zinghani zangu.

21 Wulece kuzileceleza zikutije,

wuzikumbucilaje,

wuzikamise mu nhumbula yako.

22 Nazo zomupela wumi hamonga ne vilungo vyo muwili

munhu wo wose mono yakuzizuka.

23 Holeka wuyikamise nhumbula yako,

hambi mumo nha mo yilawaga mivwilo yo wumi.

24 Wulece kulonga wulambi.

Wilegaje muli zinghani zo wulambi,

hamonga ne zinghani ze liwanjilila.

25 Meso gako,

galanje ku mwande du,

hamonga no witumbilo,

wulece kwikoza cevu.

26 Weze wuli mulanjilizi muno,

muli zose zono wukwituma,

wukumanya vyono,

zono wukwituma zose swanu.

27 Wilegaje mu zimbi,

meso gako galanje ku mwande du.

Hamba nghanzo yako monga du,

yilece kupatuka mu nzila.


Zimbazi 5

Suguzi kuli zo wusenyha

1 Gwe mwanangu,

wukamise no kuzihulika ze mahala gangu no wizuce.

2 Nagwe ho komanya nzila ze nyikala ye cogopo,

ne zinghani zako zokulajila vyono kumbe wina mahala.

3 Hambi milomo yo mugolece wo munhu yunji,

nha yiwezaga yili muzinzi nho wuci,

ne nonela yakwe,

yiteleye yinhe mafuta,

4 ninga kolo zose zakondya,

hodu mwene kawa kukuleka,

wina wusungu ne mawine.

5 Nayo kokukwenghweleza sunga yakufise ku yisi ye wafu,

hambi nzila yono yayikoleye,

nha nzila yono yibitile ku wufwe.

6 Hambi mwene nha si kajendelaga mu nzila yo wumi,

langa keyedyaga caka,

hamba kumanya zono zomwagana yaye.

7 Anye wanangu, ale ta,

muhulicizaje,

kanyhi mulece kuzisemwa zono nghulonga.

8 U mucekulu we liwala lilyo,

mumwilegaje ye!

Hamba no kusejelela ha mulango wakwe du,

mulecele lukulu.

9 Ku soko hono wunoze zono zinha zizo,

wanji wokusolela lilumbo lyako lyono wali wina nalyo,

nagwe kofwa wukali mudodo,

kanyhi kofwila mu miwoko ye wanhu wono wasina lusungu.

10 Nawo wajenzi woyisola sawo yako,

na vyose vyono wali wavituga mu milimo yako,

vyose vyosolwa no munhu yunji.

11 Nagwe kolofwila mu wusungu,

kolo muwili wako hamonga ne nghuli yako vikuligwa,

12 nagwe baho ho kolotela,

“Alu nye, nhawule si neyijiza?

Hamba nhawule si namuleka yunji yanhaze?

13 Hamba si nenda kuwahulika walimu wangu,

hamba kuwacelelela yaye.

14 Nane nghewona nhinicizo

vyono nakondya kukozwa cevu cagalanze.”

15 U mugolece wako yehwanile cisima ce malenga mapolo,

ale, weze wuli mwihuwilwa kuli mugolece wako,

no wendo wako weze wuli kuli mweneco du yali wene.

16 Ne wana nawo wono wulelile ako kudeha,

kuli mucekulu yunji,

wawo hamba si wokutaza cinji yaye.

17 Hambi i wanago,

nha wanojeye wawiluce,

ho wakutaze gwegwe,

ninga si wawo wajenzi yaye.

18 Holeka yikunojeye wumusangalalile mugolece wako,

wuwone vyono wina nyemo no muhinza mono mwitolile.

19 Hambi nha muswanu ku wulangwe,

kanyhi yakwendeza nha cizimba.

Wulece vitombo vyakwe vikusangalaze nhondo zose.

Nagwe wendezwe muno no wendo wakwe kuli'gwe.

20 Alo socici mwanangu,

wukuwugawa wendo wako kuli mucekulu yunji?

Hamba nhawule vitombo vyo mucekulu

wo munhu yunji vyo vikwendeze 'gwe?

21 Ninga wumanye vyono,

Muwaha Mulungu kaziwonaga zose zono kwitumaga.

Nayo kakulanjililaga ko kose kono wukubita.

22 Kanyhi wumanye vyono u wubi wo munhu mubi,

mutego kuli mwene.

Hambi nha kokwigwiza mu mutego wo wubi wakwe mweneco.

23 Hambi nha yakwiwulaga

mweneco so wu wula kwiyimila kwakwe.

No wulele wakwe,

wo womufisa ko ku citenjelo.


Zimbazi 6

Suguzi kanyhi zinji

1 Ati mwanangu,

ho hali hanji hono wehuwiza

nha vyono komulipila yunji migawa yakwe?

2 Hamba ho lili lizuwa linji

lono wetega ne zinghani zako go mweneco,

wukebatwa ne zinghani zo mulomo wako zono wehuwiza?

3 Ale du kanyhi mwanangu,

alu weza wuli mu miwoko yo munhu yuyo.

Ninga aluno,

wunoze nhavi ho wiponye,

wumubitilaje mbela munhu yuyo,

wumulonjele yakulece.

4 Wulece kuleka meso gako gagone,

hamba nghope zako zituje nhongo.

5 Langa wisuhulaje mu mutego,

nha vyono ndeje,

hamba nzimba, yiponyaga mu miwoko yo mupelembi.

6 Nawo i wanhu wadoba,

yiwanojeye weyijizize mu nzila ye zisungusugu vyono zikalaga.

7 Zene zisungusungu zisina mulongola,

hamba munyamhala, hamba mutemi.

8 Ninga ziwicilaga zeneco ndigwa yazo,

mwi'pango lye cibahu,

ho ziwe ne ciwutwa mwi'pango lye cifuku.

9 Alo u munhu mudoba kogona sunga nili lo?

Hamba kosinhuka nili mu zinhongo zakwe?

10 Nayo mudoba,

yakupitula yakutya,

“Nghali hadodo,

wundece ta ngone ngone.

Ndekaje nghunye magulu gangu ngone ngone hadodo.”

11 Ninga lono yakali yali mu nhongo,

hobaho wuciwa wukumuziza nho muhizi mono yena vitwalo.

12 Mbeka i wanhu wabi wono si wakwiliye cinji,

wo wendile kubita ako na ako,

wawa kutandaza zo wulambi.

13 Wakonyezaga ne meso gawo,

akuno wakunoza vitanjilo ne vidole vyawo,

ho wakukonje-konje.

14 Nawo wekelaga wakwihwesa

zimbi du zo wupatuce mu zinhumbula zawo,

wakusagahaza wuma du hanhu hose.

15 Na ku soko yiyo,

magazo gowaziza gasina suguzi,

no wongomele wowagana.

16 Ko zili nghani mutandatu hamba mupunghati,

zono Muwaha Mulungu yazisulile,

17 Meso go widunhumuzi.

Lulimi lono lulongaga zo wulambi.

Miwoko yono yiwawulagaga wanhu we ling'ani.

18 Nhumbula yono yihwesaga mahweso mabi.

Magulu gono gamanyhilaga ku wituma zimbi.

19 Muhomelezi wo wulambi mono kekalaga yakulamba-lamba.

No munhu musomenghanya, mono kaletaga

lusulo muli wandugu.

Suguzi kuli zo wusenyha

20 Mwanangu, weze kwituma nha vyono sogo yakukulonjela,

hamba wulece kuzisemwa zono nyina yako yakakwijiza.

21 Wuzikamisaje zinghani zawo vilo vyose,

zikale mu nhumbula yako.

22 Ne mafundo gayo,

gokutonga lono wuli mu muhinzo,

kanyhi gokukamisa ku cilo.

23 Ne mejizo gayo,

nha lyo limuli lyako no wuzelu,

no wutonji wayo,

wokwijiza mu mikalile yako.

24 Nago gokucitila kuli mucekulu mubi,

na kuli zinghani zo wupepelezi,

za kuli wacekulu we wanhu wanji.

25 Holeka wulece kukwegwa no wuswanu wawo,

hamba wulece kugwizwa no wulembule we meso gawo.

26 Mulume kosuma kumutuga mucekulu

musenyha so pesa zono zikwiliye

wuguzi wa mukate wumonga du,

ninga mucekulu musenyha,

kosuma kumusolela mulume yuyo,

vyose vyono yena navyo.

27 Wule! Kosuma kujeleka moto mu mhambaga yako,

hamba kunyanya myenda yako?

28 Hambi kosuma kujenda mucanyha ye makala go moto,

hamba kupya migulu yako?

29 Na mbeka ku wugona no mucekulu wo

munhu yunji mo yili mbali mbaha muno.

Na wo wose mono kokwituma zizo,

kotuga wuhoswe.

30 Ne wanhu si wamubedegalaga muhizi,

lono yakuhiza ndigwa lono yadabuka.

31 Na hono yebatwe,

kolipa kali nghanghulo mupunghati muli vivyo vyono yahizize.

Na yowa yimunojeye yalavye vyose vyono yena navyo.

32 Ninga munhu mono yakusenyha,

ayo yasina mahala.

Hambi nha yakwihodanga mweneco du.

33 Kokwiwusiza lilumbo lyakwe,

no kutowangwa,

hamonga no kukozwa cevu.

34 Hambi mulume nha kokolwa muno likuwo,

ku soko yo wivu wa kuli mugolece wakwe,

nayo komulipiciza yasina lusungu.

35 Hamba si kovumila malipo go gose,

na howa hasina vipegwa vyo vyose,

vyono vyolipoza likuwo lyakwe.

U lukumyo lwe Mahala

no kunoza wuling'anizi wuswanu.

16 I wawaha wakutema so wutazi wangu,

hamonga ne walongola,

sunga ne walamuzi wose we'nze.

17 Niwendile wono wanyendile

nayo mono yakunondola, kombona.

18 Ane nina wugoli wose ne lilumbo,

vyono nosuma kumugawilenyi,

no kumumotyenyi.

19 Na vyose vyono nomupelenyi,

nha viswanu vicizize

zahabu nengu,

hamba fweza swanu.

20 Nane niyityatyaga nzila yo mugoloka du,

nhyatyaga zi nzila ze ling'ani

nizimemye zikaya zawo ne viwutwa.”

mu mwamulizo we milimo yakwe.

23 Ane nha nasimywa ku wulawa katali na katali

ha mwamulizo,

na lono yinze yali yikali si yinawa.

24 Ane nghalelwa lono ne lilenga lyali likali hasina

na lono vikolongo vye malenga vikali vili hasina.

25 Hambi 'ne nha nalelwa na lono matunda gali gakali,

ne vigongo vyali vikali nasimywa hanhu hayo.

26 Na lono Mulungu yali yakali

nayiwumba yinze ne mawimbi,

hamba muhangha mulongozi wa mu funzi.

nane nali kuko,

na lono yawikaga cisolo ce lilenga.

28 Na lono yagawikaga mavunde mu mwanya,

nali kuko,

sunga na lono yadigulaga mivwilo ye lilenga.

29 Na lono yalilajilizaga lilenga hanhu hayo,

ho lilece kupuluta vyono yalilajilize.

Na lono yawusimizya musinji we yinze, ane nali kuko.

30 Hambi nha nali hamonga nayo,

nho mwitumami munghula.

nghawa kusecelela hali mwene.

31 Nghayisecelela yinze,

nghendezwa ne zinghungugo ze wanhu.”

32 “Holeka lu wanangu,

muhulicize 'ne mwitume nha vyono nimwijizenyi,

nanye mosangalala.

33 Mweze mwina cihuliko kuli zose zono mukwijizwa,

mweze ne mahala,

mulece kuzibedegala yaye.

ayo yendile lifwa.’ ”


Zimbazi 9

Mahala no Wulele

1 Mahala, yazenga kaya yakwe,

yakayiseseza ne masumbili mupunghati.

2 Yakatemanga ng'ombe yakwe so sikukuu,

yakayihanzahanza ne divayi yakwe,

yakavitenga vyose ha meza.

3 Yakawa kuwatuma wetumami wakwe we cicekulu,

wabite wakeme hacanyha,

i hanhu hono henucile muno mwi'kaya,

wawalalice wanhu wawe kutya,

4 “Ale muze akuno,

anye mo wanhu mono musina mahala!”

Na kuli munhu mulele yakutya,

5 “Ale wuze akuno wulye ndigwa yangu

no kung'wa divayi yangu yono hanzahanzwe.

6 Muwuce mwi'puga lye wanhu walele,

ho muwumipe.

Mujendele mu nzila yo wizuce.”

7 Na hono wumufunde munhu mubezi,

kokwitujila soni go mweneco.

Na hono wumutacile munhu mubi,

kokwitamisa go mweneco.

8 Holeka yili ciba wulece kumufunda u munhu mubezi,

hambi nha kokusula.

Ninga hono wumufunde munhu we mahala,

ayo langa kokuhulika.

9 Ne nghani yo yose yono koyilonga kuli munhu we mahala,

ayo yomunoza yawe ne mahala muno.

Ne nghani yo yose yono komulonjela munhu we ling'ani,

yiyo yomonjeleza wizuce wakwe.

10 I cogopo co Muwaha Mulungu,

wo mwamulizo we mahala.

Na hono wumumanye Mono Yelile,

ale kowa watuga wizuce.

11 Nago mahala gokonjelezeza myaka yo wikale wako.

12 Nagwe kowa mwi'walo lye wanhu wono wena cambuzi,

hono wuwe wina mahala. Ninga hono wugaleme mahala,

ale, kowa wuli mwi'puga lya wono wotuga magazo.

13 Nawo wulele,

wihwanile ne lizozo, no wu wula mahala,

wo mucekulu mono yasina cevu.

14 Hambi mwene nha kekalaga ha mulango we kaya yakwe,

hamba lunji mwi'goda lyono liwicigwe

i hanhu hono henucile muno mwi'kaya,

15 na kolo yakuwawona i wanhu wono wakupuluta baho,

wono wali mu zawo,

nalu wakuwuya ku zikwawo,

kawa kuwacemeleza yakutya,

16 “Alu, muze akuno anye nyo wanhu mono musina mahala!”

Na kuli walele yakutya,

17 “Malenga go kuhiza gali muzinzi.

Ne ndigwa yono yikuligwa cinyele,

nayo swanu muno.”

18 Ninga munhu yuyo,

yakuwa si yamanyile vyono wanhu wose wono wenjilaga mu kaya

yo mucekulu yuyo, wafwizaga.

Na vyono wose wono wakondya kwinjila,

alu wali kusi ye yisi ye wafu.


Zimbazi 10

Zimbazi za Solomona

1 Azi zo mbazi za Solomona, yakutya,

U mwana mono yena mahala,

kayisangalazaga nhumbula yo sogwe.

Ninga umwana mulele,

kamuletelaga nyina wusungu wukali muno.

2 Sawo yono koyitujila mu nzila zo wubi,

yisina cambuzi.

Ninga wiling'ano,

wuponyaga mu wufwe.

3 Muwaha Mulungu si kowaleceleza

i wanhu waswanu wawe wali nzala.

Langa kowaleka i wabi wekale si wavitujile vyono wakuvisaka.

4 Hono wuwe wuli mudoba,

kokwiletela wuciwa,

ninga hono wuwe mupiligazi ku milimo, kogolipa.

5 U munhu we mahala,

kakongholaga mbela nhwanga yakwe lono yakondya kupya.

Ninga yo nghani ya cevu,

munhu kuwa wugonile,

mu madaha ge mhwanga.

6 U munhu muswanu katugaga wumoteswe.

Ninga zinghani zo munhu mubi,

zigubikaga wugazi.

7 Ne wanhu waswanu wowa kukumbucilwa nha wumoteswe.

Ninga i wanhu wabi,

matagwa gawo gowa kuwola kamonga du.

8 Ne wanhu we mahala,

wabocelaga mafundo maswanu kamonga.

Ninga i wanhu wono walongaga zo wulele,

awo wokongomezwa.

9 I wanhu we ling'ani, wali mu mulamu,

kanyhi wali mu wucitilwe.

Ninga wono wasina cihuliko, womanyika.

10 No munhu mono si yakuyityatya mbeka,

kaletaga mazozo.

Ninga mono yakutacila calinze,

kaletaga mulamu.

11 Ne zinghani zo munhu muswanu,

wo muvwilo wo wumi.

Ninga zinghani zo munhu mubi,

zigubukaga wugazi.

12 Lusulo, luletaga wiwanganile.

Ninga wendo, wugubikaga masutya.

13 Iwanhu wono wena wizuce,

walongaga ze mahala.

Ninga i wanhu walele,

awo wakusaka kulipicizwa du.

14 Nawo we mahala,

wakuwa kutuga wumanye mu nghango yawo.

Ninga zinonga ze walele,

ziletaga mazozo mbela mbela.

15 Wugoli wuwatazaga wagoli,

nawo wuciwa wuwahodangaga i waciwa.

16 Ne cipegwa so mulimo muswanu, wo wumi.

Ninga wubi wonjelezaga mukwelekwele wo kujendelela kwituma wubi.

17 Ne wanhu wono wazibocelaga ze mafundo,

wawo wowumipa.

Ninga wanhu wono si wendile kuvumila ze masutya gawo,

wali mu mbali.

18 No munhu mono kahundikaga simbili yakwe,

yuyo mulambi.

No munhu mono yendile kukumusa nghani zo wulambi,

munhu yuyo mulele.

19 Mu winji we zinghani zako,

no wubi mo winjililaga mumo.

Ninga hono wuwe wina mahala,

yili ciba wuwe si wuli mulonji muno.

20 Ne zinghani zo munhu muswanu,

zihwanile fweza swanu.

Ninga mahweso go munhu mubi,

gasina cambuzi.

21 Ne zinghani zo munhu muswanu,

ziwaletelaga cambuzi wanhu wenji.

Ninga kosuma kwiwulaga wuli wene,

so nghani za wulele du.

22 U wumoti wo Muwaha Mulungu,

wo wugolifyaga.

Ninga wituma milimo kwe vilungo,

si wokugolifya yaye.

23 Kanyhi yo nghani ya wulele kusangalalila mu zimbi.

Ninga i wanhu wono wena wizuce,

wendezwaga ne mahala.

24 Nawo we ling'ani,

watugaga vyono wasakaga.

Ninga iwabi, watugaga cono wakucogopa.

25 Na hono civulavumbi cize,

ciwakwenghwelezaga wanhu wabi.

Ninga wanhu we ling'ani,

wekalaga nhondo zose, wapolile du.

26 Wulece kumupela mulimo wako munhu mudoba yakwitumile,

hambi u munhu yuyo nha kokupaya mu meno gako nha pilipili,

kanyhi kowa nha malyosi gono gokufunyhila mu meso gako.

27 Holeka agwe,

wumuhulice Muwaha Mulungu,

holeka kokwikala vilo vinji mono'nze.

Ninga i wanhu wabi wafwizaga na lono vipango vyawo vikali nafika.

28 No witumbilo we wanhu waswanu,

wuwapelaga wusangalale.

Ninga i wanhu wabi,

wawezaga wasina witumbilo wo wose.

29 Muwaha Mulungu kawadimaga wanhu we ling'ani.

Ninga kawongomezaga wose i wetuma zimbi.

30 Iwanhu we ling'ani,

wokwikala mu wudimwe vilo vyose.

Ninga i wanhu wabi,

si wowumipa mono yinze.

31 Ne wanhu we mahala,

walongaga zinghani ze mahala,

ninga u lulimi lono lulongaga zimbi,

lodumulwa.

32 Kanyhi i wanhu we ling'ani,

wakuwa kumanya ziswanu zono ziwanojeye walonje.

Ninga i wanhu wabi nhondo zose,

walongaga zinghani zono zidahile kuwatamisa wanhu.


Zimbazi 11

1 U Muwaha Mulungu,

sikendezwaga ne wanhu wono

wetumamilaga vipimo vyo wukoci.

Ninga mwene,

kendezwaga ne vipimo vye ling'ani.

2 Ne wanhu iwedunhumuzi,

wokozwa cevu kamonga.

Ninga mahala gali muli wenenenyhezi.

3 Na hono wuwe wuli munhu muswanu,

winenenyhezi wokutonga.

Ne wanhu wose wono si wehuwilwa,

wokongomezwa no wu wula kuwa no wihuwilwa.

4 Ninga wugoli hamba si wulakutaza cinji yaye

mwizuwa lyo kufwa.

Ninga wihuwilwa wokuponya wumi wako.

5 Wihuwilwa wuyinozaga mikalile yo munhu muswanu

yiwa miswanu.

Ninga u munhu mubi,

kolofwa ku soko yo wubi wakwe.

6 Wiling'ano wumuponyaga munhu mwinenenyhezi.

Ninga mono si mwihuwilwa,

kegwizaga mweneco no wubi wakwe.

7 Na lono munhu mubi yakufwa,

kafwizaga hamonga no witumbilo wakwe.

No witumbila zo wugoli,

wowa nghani ya bwete du.

8 Wanhu we ling'ani wakuwa kudimwa mu zimbali.

Langa i wabi,

zimbali ziwazizaga.

9 Zinghani za wono wasina cogopo co Muwaha Mulungu,

zosuma kukwajiliza.

Ninga mahala ga wono wali ling'ani,

gosuma kukuponya.

10 Ne likaya lisangalalaga lono we ling'ani

wakumota.

Na hono i wanhu wabi wafwe,

i wanhu wasangalalaga.

11 Ne likaya litogozwaga lono wanhu we ling'ani

wakumota.

Ninga likaya lihodanjikaga ku soko ye makani ge wanhu wabi.

12 Yo nghani ya wulele du yo kuwabedegala wanji.

Ninga hono wuwe munhu mono wina mahala,

konyamala twii.

13 No munhu muhesi,

si kalonjelwaga nghani ze cinyele.

Langa wumwitumbile munhu mwihuwilwa.

14 Ne likungugo ligwizaga hono hawe hasina wutonji.

Na mu winji we wanyamhala mo wuli wusume.

15 Na hono wihuwize vyono komulipila

mujenzi mugawa wakwe,

kokwitajila mu mbali bwete.

Ciba hono wulece kwiyinjiza mu nghani yiyo.

16 No mucekulu musindile,

yena lilumbo nhondo zose.

Ninga mucekulu mono si mutuvu,

yo nghani ya cevu du.

No munhu mudoba,

si katujilaga lukulu sawo.

Ninga munhu mupiligazi ku milimo,

kagolipaga.

17 Kosuma kwinozeza ziswanu go mweneco

hono wuwe no lusungu.

Ninga hono wuwe wuli jila,

kokwitamisa wuli wene.

18 Ne wanhu wabi,

si watugaga cambuzi co cose yaye.

Ninga hono witume ze ling'ani,

nagwe kohumbilizwa.

19 Mono yakamile mu wiling'ano kowumipa.

Ninga mono katyatyaga wubi, kofwa.

20 Muwaha Mulungu yawasulile i wanhu wo wuhile wubi.

Ninga yawendile wono wetumaga zo mugoloka.

21 Na mbeka i wabi wolipicizwa, ninga

we ling'ani woponywa.

22 U wuswanu we futa yo mucekulu,

hono wulece kuhanga no wuswanu we nyikala yakwe,

wuswanu wakwe wowa nhe nyamwidole ya zahabu

yono mvwice mu mhula ye ngubi.

23 Wuhile wa we ling'ani nhondo zose wowu,

wetume ziswanu du.

Ninga i wabi kolo watuga vyono wakusaka,

hodu wose wakukolwa likuwo.

24 Ko wali wanji wono wetumamilaga sawo zawo

nha vyono wendile,

ninga vyo wakucila kugolipa du.

Na ko wali wanji wono wetumamilaga sawo yawo

ko wulanjilizi muno,

ninga vyo wakucila kuciwapa muno.

25 Weze no wucemezi, nagwe komoteswa.

Weze kuwapela wanji wutazi,

holeka nagwe kotuga wutazi.

26 Wanhu wamulijitaga munhu

mono kahundikaga wuhemba,

yalindiye yatuje wuguzi wuswanu.

Ninga wamukumyaga mono kawaguzizaga wuhemba.

27 Hono wuwe wupiligaye mu wulondola ziswanu,

wanhu wokwenda.

Ninga hono wuwe wuli mulondola zimbi,

nazo zimbi zokutuga.

28 Nawo wono wetumbiye zisawo zawo,

wololagala nhe mahamha mwi'bahu.

Ninga we ling'ani womoteswa nhe mahamha mwi'fuku.

29 No munhu mono kaletaga ciyagalo mu kaya yakwe,

ku ndumilizo si kokwambulila cinji yaye.

Nawo walele nhondo zose wowa wetumami we wanhu we mahala.

30 Wiling'ano wuletaga wumi.

Ninga wugazi, wuwuwusaga.

31 Ne wanhu waswanu,

watugaga cihumbilizwa cawo baha hono yinze.

Holeka wumanye vyono i wabi hamonga ne wanyo wubi wose,

wololipicizwa.


Zimbazi 12

1 Munhu wo wose mono yendile wumanye,

yuyo kendaga no kulanjizwa lono yasutya.

Kanyhi yo nghani ya wulele,

munhu mono si yendile kulanjizwa.

2 Muwaha Mulungu kendezwaga ne wanhu waswanu.

Ninga kawahosaga i wehwesa zimbi.

3 Wubi si wumusikamyaga munhu yaye.

Ninga i we ling'ani,

wekalaga wasikame.

4 U mucekulu musindile,

lye 'lumbo ne nyemo kuli mulume wakwe,

ninga mucekulu mono kamukozaga cevu u mulume wakwe,

yehwanile lilonda libi muno,

mu mafupa gakwe.

5 I weling'ani, wokutendela viswanu.

Ninga i wabi, wosaka wakukonje du.

6 Ne zinghani ze wanhu wabi, ziwulagaga.

Ninga zinghani za we ling'ani,

ziwaponyaga wono wakuzibocela.

7 Ne wanhu wabi wafwizaga wakuleka wasina wana.

Ninga kaya ya we ling'ani, yokwikala.

8 Hono wuwe wina mahala, wanhu wokulumba.

Ninga hono wuwe wuli mulele, wanhu wokubedegala.

9 Yili ciba wuwe nha vyono winha,

wukwiyitujila ndigwa mu milimo yako.

Si nho kwinoza nha wuli munhu we lilumbo,

akuno ho wuli nzala.

10 No munhu muswanu,

kazidimaga viswanu mitugo zakwe.

Ninga i wanhu wabi,

wali jila ku zi mitugo zawo.

11 No munhu muhidimbazi kulima,

kekalaga yena ndigwa.

Ninga kwajiliza vipango so nghani zono zisina cambuzi,

yiyo yo nghani ya wulele du.

12 Ne wanhu wabi,

wose walondolaga zo wituma makani mabi du.

Ninga we ling'ani wekalaga wasikame.

13 No munhu mubi kegwizizaga mu zinghani zakwe mweneco.

Ninga we ling'ani kokwiponya mu wugaye.

14 Ne cihumbilizwa cako,

cowa cili mu nghango ya zono kuzilongaga,

na zono kwitumaga.

Kopegwa vyono yikunojeye.

15 Ne wanhu walele,

nhondo zose wene wehwesaga nha vyono wali ling'ani.

Ninga we mahala wahulicizaga kuli zo wulanjizwe.

16 Likuwo lyo mulele kalilajilaga calinze.

Ninga we mahala,

si wendaga kwikoza cevu yaye.

17 Na lono wukulonga ze mbeka,

ling'ani yikunozwa.

Ninga muhomelezi wo wulambi,

kayajilizaga ling'ani.

18 Ne zinghani ze caka,

zikuwa kucinya nho mumaje.

Ninga zinghani zo munhu we mahala, ziponyaga.

19 No munhu mulambi,

kekalaga vilo vicefu mono'nze.

Ninga munhu mono kalongaga ze mbeka,

kekalaga cibitilila.

20 Wukoci wuli mu zinhumbula za wono wehwesaga zimbi,

ninga kuli wono wetumaga ku so ziswanu, wowona nyemo.

21 Kanyhi hasina cinji cibi

cono cowaziza i wanhu we ling'ani.

Ninga i wabi, wambulilaga magayo du.

22 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yawasulile i wanhu walambi.

Ninga kendezwaga na wono wetumaga ze mbeka.

23 I wanhu we mahala,

wahundikaga zono wazimanyile.

Ninga i walele,

wanzalikaga zo wulele wawo.

24 No wituma milimo ko wupiligazi,

wokupela wutule.

Ninga wudoba, wokunoza wuwe muwanda.

25 Ne mahwele,

gosuma kukuwusiza nyemo yako.

Ninga nghani swanu,

zosuma kukusangalaza.

26 U munhu we ling'ani,

yo kawezaga mutonji wo muyagwe.

Ninga zinzila zo munhu mubi,

ziwatongaga ku wajilile.

27 Nagwe hono wuwe mudoba,

hamba si kosuma kwiwutila vya kumwande.

Ninga hono wuwe wuli mupiligazi mu milimo,

kosuma kwiwutila vya kumwande.

28 U wiling'ano, yo nzila yo wumi.

Ninga wubi, yo nzila ye lifwa.


Zimbazi 13

1 U mwana we mahala,

kahulicizaga wijize wo sogwe.

Ninga mwana mubezi,

si kavumilaga masutya gakwe.

2 Ne wanhu waswanu,

wopegwa vihumbilizwa vyawo,

mu nghango ye mefumo gawo.

Ninga i walambi wekalaga,

wadabuciye wugazi du.

3 Wilanjilile kuli zono wukulonga.

Wuwukamise wumi wako.

Ninga munhu mono si kelanjililaga mu milonjele yakwe,

keyongomezaga mweneco.

4 Ne nhumbula yo munhu mudoba,

hamba yisulumile cinji,

si kosuma kucituga yaye.

Ninga munhu muhidimbazi,

katugaga vyose vyono kasakaga.

5 We ling'ani, yawusulile wulambi.

Ninga u mubi,

ketumaga makani ga cevu kanyhi ga soni.

6 Ling'ani, yiwacitilaga we ling'ani.

Ninga wubi, lyo lifwa lye wabi.

7 Wanhu wanji wenozaga nha wagoli,

ninga kumbe ho wanghwenzele.

Na wanji wenozaga nha wanghwenzele,

ninga kumbe ho wagoli.

8 U mugoli ketumamilaga sawo yakwe kwiponya wumi wakwe.

Ninga hasina mono kamogofyaga munghwenzele.

9 Ne wanhu we ling'ani wehwanile limuli lyono

likumulika muno.

Ninga i wabi, wehwanile tala yono yizimile.

10 Wusisi, wuletaga wiwanganile.

Holeka yili ciba kulondola wutazi,

hambi nha go mahala.

11 Mu nghango ye mbela mbela

yono wukuyisawila sawo yako,

yo nghango kanyhi yono koyajiliziza musunho.

Ninga mono kakongholaga ko wupiligazi,

kokonjelezeka.

12 No witumbilo wono wutinyaga kwijela,

wutamisaga nhumbula.

Ninga kolo yakutuga cono yakucisaka,

baho nhumbula yakwe yisangalalaga muno.

13 Na hono wugabeze mejizo maswanu,

ale kokwiletela wugaye.

Ninga hono wugavumile, kowa mu mulamu.

14 Ne mejizo ge mahala, wo muvwilo wo wumi.

Nago gokutaza wiponye wumi wako lono wuli mu mbali.

15 Wizuce wuswanu wuletaga mahweso ge lihewa.

Ninga wawo wono si wehuwilwa,

wali mu nzila yo wuhodanjice.

16 I wanhu we mahala,

nhondo zose wamulaga kusimya, wehwesa,

lono wakali si netuma cinji.

Ninga i walele weyagazaga wulele wawo.

17 U mutumwa mubi kaletaga wiwanganile.

Ninga wono wetumbilwaga waletaga mulamu.

18 Munhu wowose mono si kendaga kulanjizwa kowa muciwa

no kukozwa cevu.

Ninga mono kavumilaga wulanjizwe, kotuga lilumbo.

19 Ninga yo nghani swanu hono wucituje cono kusakaga!

Ninga i wanhu walele,

walemaga kuwuka mu wituma zimbi.

20 Wilunzaje na we mahala,

nagwe kotuga mahala.

Ninga hono wilunze ne walele,

kokwajilila.

21 Magaziko, gawatyatililaga i wabi,

hanhu hose.

Ninga we ling'ani,

wohumbilizwa ne vinhu viswanu.

22 No munhu muswanu kowa ne sawo yono kosuma

kuwalecela ne wezukuluwe.

Ninga sawo ye wabi yiwutilwaga we ling'ani.

23 Ne migunda yono si yilimwaga,

yolela ndigwa nyinji ku so waciwa.

Ninga i wanhu wono si wali ling'ani wahodangaga vinhu.

24 Na hono wulece kumutowa mwanago lono yakusutya,

wumusulile.

Ninga hono wuwe wumwendile, komufunda.

25 I we ling'ani wekalaga wena ndigwa yono yiwakwiliye,

Ninga i wabi, wekalaga wali nzala vilo vyose.


Zimbazi 14

1 Ne zikaya ziwezaga swanu so mahala go mucekulu.

Ninga wulele wuzihodangaga.

2 Weze mwihuwilwa, akuno wukulajila cogopo cako

kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

3 No widunhumuzi wo munhu mulele,

wumupelaga wulonji winji.

Ninga zinghani zo munhu we mahala, zimukamisaga.

4 Hono hawe hasina ng'ombe zo kukwega lisili,

madongha gako gowa gasina cinji.

Ninga u winji we ndudula, wulajilaga vyono

ng'ombe ko zili.

5 U muhomelezi mwihuwilwa,

si kalongaga wulambi yaye.

Ninga mono si mwihuwilwa,

si kalongaga cinji, langa zo wulambi du.

6 Ne wanhu wedunhumuzi,

si watugaga mahala yaye.

Ninga wono wezuce,

weyijizaga mbela mbela.

7 Wilegaje kuli i wanhu walele,

hambi nha wasina cinji cono wosuma kukwijiza.

8 Alo nhawule mono yena wizuce kawezaga yena mahala?

Ku soko vyono mwene, yamanyile zono yakwituma.

H amba nhawule munhu mulele nayo, kawa yasina mahala?

Ku soko vyono nayo yakwiwona yamanyile.

9 Ne wanhu walele, na hono wetume wubi,

hamba si wacelelelaga yaye.

Ninga we mahala, walondolaga wunenelwe.

10 Wusangalale, wo wako.

No wusungu, nawo wako.

Hasina hamba yunji mono mohanga nayo muli zizo.

11 Ne kaya yo munhu muswanu yikalaga yimile du,

lono kaya yo munhu mubi yakondya kumwagulwa.

12 Ne nzila yono wukwihwesa yo nzila swanu,

kumbe yo nzila yono yokutegula kwi'fwa.

13 Nalo luseko luhundikaga wukalale.

Ninga kolo nyemo yikuwuka,

wukalale wuwezaga vyo wuli baho du.

14 Nawo i wanhu wabi, wopegwa wulipicizwe wawo.

Nawo i wanhu waswanu,

wohumbilizwa nha vyono genha metumo gawo.

15 U munhu mulele, kehuwilaga zinghani zose.

Ninga i wanhu we mahala,

wayilanjililaga muno i mijendele yawo.

16 I wanhu we mahala,

welanjililaga walece kwinjila mu wubi.

Ninga i walele, wajendaga wedunhumuye wasina mahwele.

17 Ne wanhu wono wena likuwo lyono lyakaga musunho,

wetumaga nghani za wulele.

Ninga we mahala wekalaga wapolile.

18 I walele, wohala zo wulele wawo.

Ninga we mahala, wohumbilizwa wizuce.

19 Nawo i wanhu wabi,

wolofugama ha mwande ya we ling'ani,

no kulomba wendezwe wawo.

20 Munhu muciwa, yakuwa kusulwa ne wanhu wose,

sunga ne wekalani wayagwe wose.

Ninga i wagoli wendigwe na wanhu wenji.

21 Ninga hono wusace kuwa no wusangalale,

ale wuwendaje waciwa.

Kanyhi wumanye vyono wubi lono kuwabedegala wayago.

22 Na hono wuwe wukwituma viswanu, kotuga lilumbo,

no kwitumbilwa ne wayago.

Ninga hono wuwe wukwituma zimbi, ale, wukusutya.

23 Wuhidimbale kwituma milimo, nagwe kotuga cambuzi.

Ninga hono wuwe wikaye du wina mulomo, kowa muciwa.

24 Ne wanhu we mahala, wahumbilizwaga sawo.

Ninga i walele, wamanyicilaga mu wulele wawo.

25 Nayo muhomelezi,

kaponyaga wumi hono yalonje ze mbeka.

Ninga hono yalonje zo wulambi,

yakuwasonjeza wanhu.

26 Na mu cogopo co Muwaha Mulungu, mo mo wuli

witumbilo, no wucitilwe wo munhu, na we mhuga yakwe.

27 Hono wuwe kusaka kwilega lifwa, yikunojeye wuwe

ne cogopo kuli Muwaha Mulungu, mono yo muvwilo wo wumi.

28 Ne lilumbo lyo mutemi lili mu winji we wanhu

wono yatemile. Ninga hono yawe ne wanhu wacefu,

wutemi wakwe wukuwa wa bwete.

29 Munhu we mahala,

si yakuwa kukolwa ne likuwo musunho.

Ninga hono wuwe kukolwaga ne likuwo mbela,

alu agwe wukwilajila vyono wuli mulele.

30 No mulamu wa mu nhumbula,

wuwunozaga muwili wuwa muswanu.

Ninga wivu, wihwanile lilonda libi.

31 Na hono wuwewonele waciwa,

wukumutovula Mulungu mono yawawumba.

Ninga mono yakuwawonela lusungu waciwa,

yuyo yakumukumya Mulungu.

32 I wanhu wabi,

weletelaga lifwa weneco ku soko yo wubi wawo.

Ninga i wanhu waswanu,

wena witumbilo sunga ko ku wufwe wawo.

33 Ne mahala gekalaga mu mahweso gose go

munhu mono yena wizuce.

Ninga i walele, si wazimanyile ze mahala yaye.

34 Ling'ani yinozaga likungugo liwe ne 'lumbo.

Ninga wubi, co cevu ciwaha kuli makungugo gose.

35 I watemi nawo,

wendezwaga ne wetumami wono wakwituma no wizuce.

Ninga wakuwa kuwalipiciza wono milimo yikuwafutila.


Zimbazi 15

1 Wupitule we mhola mhola, wutuzaga likuwo

Ninga nghani yono yikutamisa, yisagahazaga likuwo.

2 Wulonje we wanhu we mahala,

wuwezaga wuswanu wo wizuce.

Ninga i walele, walongaga zo wulele.

3 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, kaziwonaga zose hanhu hose.

Kacigozaga wose, hono cikwituma ziswanu hamba 'mbi.

4 Zinghani ze ngola, ziletaga wumi.

Ninga zinghani zo wiwanganile, zihodangaga nhumbula.

5 Yo nghani yo wulele, kuzibedegala zono sogo yakufunda.

Ninga yo nghani swanu kugabocela mafundo gono yakukufunda.

6 Wanhu we ling'ani, wazilanjililaga zisawo zawo.

Ninga i wabi, wajilizaga zisawo

zawo mu cipango co wugaye.

7 We mahala, wo weyagazaga zo wumanye, sinhe walele.

8 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu,

kendezwaga no wulombe we wanhu we ling'ani.

Ninga yagasulile makumbiko gono

wamulavizyaga i wanhu wabi.

9 Zinzila ze wanhu wabi, Muwaha Mulungu yazisulile.

Mwene yawendile wono wetumaga zo mugoloka.

10 Na hono witume zimbi, kotamiswa ko wulipicizwe.

Na hono wulece kuvumila wufundwe, kofwa.

11 Kanyhi wumanye vyono, hamba yisi ye wafu,

siyosuma kumwimila Muwaha Mulungu,

ho yalece kusuma kuzimanya, zono zili mumo yaye.

Alu munhu ho kosuma nhawule,

kumuhundicila Mulungu, mahweso gakwe?

12 Ne wanhu wabedegazi, si wendaga wufundwe yaye,

kanyhi hamba si wendile kuwuza nghani kuli we mahala.

13 Ne wanhu nawo hono wawe wena nyemo,

kowawona wusu wawo wusangalaye.

Ninga hono wawe wena wukalale, wakwijela wasininghaye.

14 Ne wanhu wono wena wizuce, wendaga kwiyijiza,

Ninga i walele, weyigutyaga no wulele wawo du.

15 Mikalile yo muciwa, vilo vyose yiwezaga ya magayo.

Ninga i wanhu waseci, wekalaga nhondo zose wamotile.

16 Yili ciba hono wuwe muciwa, akuno wumogopile Muwaha

Mulungu, si nha kuwa mugoli hamonga no wugaye.

17 Kanyhi yili ciba kulya ndigwa ye mboga ye sunga du,

hamonga ne wanhu wono mwiyendile.

Sinho kulya nyama yono yijinile ha ciligati yo lusulo.

18 Makuwo galetaga matemanuko.

Ninga wukangalizi, wuletaga mulamu.

19 Na hono wuwe mudoba,

kokwitang'anila ne zimbali hanhu hose.

Ninga hono wuwe wuli muhidimbazi,

hamba si kogazika yaye.

20 Nayo mwana we mahala, kamusangalazaga sogwe.

Ninga mwana mulele, kamukalazaga nyina yakwe.

Ninga we mahala, wetumaga zo mugoloka.

21 Ne wanhu walele, wawusangalalilaga wulele wawo.

Ninga we mahala, wetumaga zo mugoloka.

22 Weze wuli mupelu ku wubocela zo wufundwe,

holeka komoteswa.

Ninga hono wuwe wuli zeze,

kokwiyambuza go mweneco.

23 Yiwezaga nyemo mbaha lecenyi, hono wupitule nghani

yono yinojeye, mu cipango cono cinojeye!

24 Ne wanhu we mahala,

wayityatyaga nzila yono yiwatonganga ko ku wumi.

Ninga si wayityatyaga nzila yono yowatonga sunga kwi'fwa.

25 U Muwaha Mulungu kozimwagulanga

zikaya ze wanhu wedunhumuzi.

Langa sawo yo mucekulu mufwile, koyidima.

26 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yagasulile mahweso ge wanhu wabi.

Langa mwene kendezwaga ne zinghani za mu wendo.

27 Na hono wijelele witujile sawo mu nzila yono si yinojeye,

wumanye vyono wukuwenjiza we mhuga yako mu magayo.

Holeka wulecele lukulu kulya mafupo,

holeka nagwe kowumipa vilo vinji.

28 I wanhu we ling'ani, wamulaga kwihwesa, ho wapitula

Ninga i wabi wapitulaga musunho, no kuleta mazozo.

29 I wanhu we ling'ani lono wakulomba,

Muwaha Mulungu kawahulicizaga.

Langa i wabi, si kawacelelelaga yaye.

30 No wusu wono wina nyemo, wusangalazaga nhumbula.

Nazo zinghani ziswanu, ziletaga wusangalavu.

31 Hono wuwe mupelu kuhulika zo wufundwe, wina mahala.

32 Ninga hono wuwe wuli ng'anje ku wufundwe,

kokwitamisa go mweneco.

Na hono wuvumile kufundwa, kowa na nhumbula swanu.

33 Ne cogopo ca kuli Muwaha Mulungu,

wo wijize we mahala.

Yikunojeye weze wuli mwinenenyhezi

lono wukali sinatuga lukumyo.


Zimbazi 16

1 Ase cihwesaga zetu,

ninga Mulungu yo yena nghani ye ndumilizo.

2 Agwe wukwihwesa nha vyono zose zono kwitumaga,

kunozaga viswanu.

Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yo kazijelaga zinhunizo zayo.

3 Holeka yikunojeye wumukoze Muwaha Mulungu metumo gako.

Ne mahweso gako ho gokangazwa.

4 U Muwaha Mulungu,

yaviwumba vinhu vyose ko wuhile wakwe mweneco.

Ninga wuhile we wabi, wobedyanjizwa.

5 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu, si yendile wanhu wedunhumuzi yaye.

Holeka si komuleka munhu we nhavivyo yalece kulipicizwa.

6 Holeka weze na cihuliko, kanyhi mwihuwilwa.

Nayo Mulungu kokunenela wubi wako.

Weze na cihuliko kuli Muwaha Mulungu wako,

holeka nagwe si zokwijelela i zimbi.

7 Na hono wuwe kumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu,

kosuma kuwanoza ne walugu wako mwiyende nawo.

8 Yili ciba wuwe na cidodo cono wina,

cono wucitujiye mu nzila ye ling'ani,

si nho kuwa na vinhu vinji vyono wuvitujiye

mu nzila yono si ye ling'ani.

9 Agwe kosuma kuwikaniza mahweso gako.

Ninga Mulungu yo katongaga mu witume wayo.

10 Mutemi yo munya kudumula zinghani zose.

Ne zinghani zakwe, si kasutyaga mu wuling'anize.

11 U Muwaha Mulungu kendezwaga ne vipimo vye ling'ani

no wuguzi wono wuling'aniciye.

12 Ne watemi nawo yiwanojeye weleje na zo wubi.

Hambi ling'ani nha yo yinozaga wutemi wuwa wuli vilungo.

13 No mutemi yendile kuzihulika ze mbeka.

Nayo yawendile muno wono walongaga zo mugoloka.

14 No munhu we mahala,

kajelaga muno kumusangalaza mutemi.

Vyono hono mutemi yakolwe ne likuwo, ale, yunji kofwa.

15 No wendezwe wo mutemi,

wihwanile mavunde gono gatonyezaga mvula ye mafuso,

namo mo mo wuli wumi.

16 Na mbeka, yili ciba wuwe na mahala hamonga no wizuce,

si nho kuwa na zahabu hamonga na fweza.

17 Ne wanhu we ling'ani, si wakolaga mu zinzila zo wubi.

Holeka nagwe wiyihengaje go mweneco,

wiwone kono wudomile kwihi.

Hambi nha yokuponya wumi wako.

18 Wiwoni, wulongozaga ku wuhodanjice.

Nawo widunhumuzi kwi'fwa.

19 Yili ciba winenenyheze no kwikala mu wuciwa.

Si nho kuwa mwi'puga lye wedunhumuzi,

no kuhanga nawo muli vyono wapumhile.

20 Wuzibate zono wukwijizwa, holeka nagwe komoteswa.

Wumwitumbile Muwaha Mulungu, nagwe kosangalala.

21 U we mahala mu nhumbula,

kamanyicilaga mu wutanje wakwe.

No muzinzi we zinghani zakwe wonjelezaga wumanye.

22 Ne mahala, wo muvwilo wo wumi kuli we mahala.

Ninga hono wujele kuwejiza i walele,

wokwajiliziza bwete vipango vyako.

23 Ne wanhu wono wena wizuce,

wamulaga kwihwesa, ho walonga.

Na kolo wakulonga, nghani zawo zikwendeza.

24 Ne zinghani ziswanu zihwanile wuci.

Ku wujeze zili muzinzi, kanyhi swanu na mu muwili wako.

25 Ne nzila yono agwe wukwihwesa yo swanu,

Ninga yokulongoza kwi'fwa.

26 Ne nzala yo mwitumami,

yimunozaga ho yetume yahidimbaye.

Hambi nha yakuhila yegutye nzala yakwe.

27 Ne wanhu wabi nhondo zose walondolaga nzila zo

kuwatamisiza wanji.

Sunga ne zinghani zawo, zinyanyaga vibi nha moto.

28 Ne maheho, gatandazwaga ne wanhu wabi.

Hambi nha wekalaga wakusagahaza zimbi,

no kusonjeza wanhu, no kuwatemanula.

29 I wanhu wagazi, wawakongaga wendece wawo,

no kuwenjiza mu wongomele.

30 Wawalanjile wanhu wono wakusekanga,

akuno wakunghonyeza ne meso, wakunyihweseza i zimbi.

31 No wikala mumi vilo vinji,

co cihumbilizwa ce wanhu we ling'ani.

Ne zimvwi nazo, wo mukumbo wo lukumyo.

32 Yili ciba hono wuwe si wuli mupelu kwi'kuwo,

si nho munhu wa vilungo.

Kanyhi, yili ciba wuwe no wiyimizi,

si nho kutema mu makaya gose.

33 I wanhu watowaga kula,

ho wawumanye wuhile wo Mulungu.

Ninga Mulungu mweneco, yo yena lipitulo.


Zimbazi 17

1 Ciba kulya nhonje cilumanga hamonga

no mulamu mu nhumbula.

Si nho kulya ndigwa nyinji swanu,

mu kaya yono yina no wiwanganile.

2 U mwitumami mono ketumaga no wulula,

komutema no mwana wo mutwawe mono si kazicelelelaga yaye.

Kanyhi kohala ne lifungu lyakwe.

3 Zahabu na fweza, vikuwa kujelwa no moto.

Nayo nhumbula yo munhu yijelwaga no Muwaha Mulungu.

4 I wanhu wabi, wahulicizaga mahweso mabi.

Nawo i walambi, wahulicizaga mahweso go wulambi.

5 Na hono wuwe kuwasekanga i waciwa, ale,

wumanye vyono wukumutovula Mulungu mono yawawumba.

Na mono kasecelelaga wugazice wo munhu yunji, kolipicizwa.

6 I wakombi, weyilumbilaga muli wezukulu zawo,

nha vyono nawo wazelelo welumbilaga muli wasogo zawo.

7 Ne wanhu we lilumbo,

si walongaga zo wulambi yaye.

Nawo walele,

wasina hamba nghani ye cambuzi yono wolonga.

8 Wanhu wanji wehwesaga vyono,

mulimo wo kulavya mafupo,

wuwezaga nha wuganga,

nawo wehuwiye vyono wosuma kuwapela wutazi.

9 Na hono wusace wanhu wakwende, ale,

weze kuwanenela kuli zono wakukusutizya.

Akuno wukukumbucila nha vyono masutya gawulagaga ndugu.

10 U munhu wo wizuce, keyijizizaga mu wutacilwe,

ninga mulele, keyijizizaga mu wutowe nghanghulo yigana.

11 Nalyo lifwa, lyowaziza ko wukali i wanhu wabi,

wono waletaga mazozo nhondo zose.

12 Kanyhi ciba, hono mwitang'anile ne dubu,

lyono walihizize mana galyo,

si nho kwitang'anila no munhu mulele,

mono yakwipiganza ne zinghani zo wulele.

13 Hono wuwe kulipaga ziswanu ku zimbi,

nagwe si kotujila lukulu yinji 'mbi mu kaya yako.

14 Mwamulizo wo wuma,

wihwanile munhu mono yakujida malenga.

Holeka ciba kuwuleka wiwanganile,

lono mukali si nafikana kutali.

15 Mono yakumuhosa we ling'ani,

na mono yakutya mubi yo weling'ani.

Wose wawo vyono wejete,

yiwifyo kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

16 Si viswanu munhu mulele,

yetumamile sawo so wulondola wizuce,

hambi nha yasina mahala.

17 Wono weyendile,

walajilaga nhondo zose zo wendo wawo.

Na we ndugu nawo wetazilizaga mu wugaye.

18 Ninga u munhu mono yasina mahala,

yo kehuwizaga vyono komulipila yunji mugawa wakwe.

19 Nawo i wenda wubi, wo wenda wugaye.

Na mono konjelezaga wutali wo mululu wakwe,

kelondolelaga wugazice.

20 Na mono kehwesaga zimbi no kulonga makani mabi,

si kotuga ziswanu, langa wongomele.

21 Ne wana wono wendile kwituma zinghani zo wulele,

co cevu no wukalale kuli sogo yawo.

22 Wuseci nawo, wokupela muwili muswanu,

wono wusina wunyoganyoga.

No wukalale we mapango gose,

wo wufwa mhola mhola.

23 Mahakimu wabi, wabocelaga mafupo cinyele,

ho wazajilize nzila zo wuling'anizi.

24 Munhu mono yena wizuce,

kehwesaga kwituma ze mahala.

Ninga mono yasina mahala, kebitilaga caka du.

25 No mwana mulele, kamuletelaga sogwe wusungu du.

Hamonga no wukalale wuwaha kuli nyina yakwe.

26 Kanyhi si viswanu yaye,

kumulifya fayini munhu mono yasina lisutya.

Ne ling'ani yikuwa kulekwa,

lono wanhu waswanu wakulipicizwa.

27 No munhu mono kewonaga yali mu mbeka,

si kalonganjikaga nghani nyinji yaye mapango gose.

Ne wanhu wono wendile kwikala no mulamu,

wena wizuce we mbeka.

28 Ninga kanyhi, hamba munhu mono yasina mahala mulele,

kosuma kwihwesa nha vyono yena mahala no wizuce,

hono yawe nayo kekalaga yanyamaye,

no kuwimila mulomo wakwe wulece kulonga-longa.


Zimbazi 18

1 Wanhu wono wetemanulaga ne wayawo,

walondolaga ze masulumizi gawo weneco.

Wesumanizaga na zono wayawo wakuwona zo swanu.

2 No mulele si kacelelelwaga, hono yawe yakwizuka hamba ng'o.

Langa mwene kasakaga kwilajila du, vyono mumanya vyose.

3 Nawo wubi ne cevu, vibitanaga hamonga.

Ne lilumbo hono likuwucile, wuzaga wubedegalwe.

4 Ne zinghani zo munhu, gawezaga go mahala,

Genha malenga ge cisolo, nhe 'kolongo likuhuluma.

5 Si viswanu kumuhijila mbejele u munhu musutyi,

no kwajiliza ling'ani yo munhu we ling'ani.

6 Na kolo zinghani zo wiwanganile zo wulele zikwanza,

Mwene yakulonga zo wutowe.

7 No munhu mono yasina mahala,

kalongaga zinghani zo kwiyambuza mweneco.

Nayo kebatilwaga mu zinghani zakwe mweneco.

8 Ne zinghani zo kupwepwelwa, ziwezaga zili muzinzi.

Nase cendile muno kuzihulicila!

9 No mudoba ku milimo, yo munhu mubi muno,

Yehwanile no munhu muhodanji.

10 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, wo munala we vilungo,

hono i wanhu we ling'ani,

wosuma kwihundika no kuwa wapolile.

11 Nawo wagoli, wawonaga nha vyono,

sawo yawo yosuma kuwadima nhe lilulu litali,

kanyhi nhe majinji matali,

gono gali vilungo, gono galipilime likaya.

12 U munhu mwinenenyhezi, yo katugaga lilumbo.

Ninga i wedunhumuzi, wali mu nzila yo wongomele.

13 Weze wina ngola, lono wukali si napitula cinji,

Ninga hono wuwe wuli musupu ku wupitule,

kokwiyinjiza mu wu wula mahala no wutovulwe.

14 U muhe wako wo wokusikamya mu wusocele wako.

Ninga hono wuwusuputule,

no witumbilo wako wokwajilila.

15 I wanhu wono wena wizuce,

nhondo zose wekalaga wahidimbazi,

kanyhi wendile no kwiyijiza.

16 Na hono wuwe kusaka mwiwone no munhu we lilumbo,

agwe mutegulilaje cipegwa du, hodu,

kowa kupelu kwiwona nayo.

17 No munhu mono kalongolaga kupitula zinghani mu mahakama,

nhondo zose mwene, kewonanga yali ling'ani,

sunga kolo mono yakumudaya, yakumuwuza mawuzo.

18 Na hono wanhu wejete we vilungo,

wawe wena wiwanganile mu mahakama,

ale, kula yo yosuma kuzilamula zinghani zizo.

19 Wumutazaje nduguyo, nayo kolokucitila,

kolowa nhe lijinji lyono likamile kuli 'gwe.

Ninga hono mugombane nayo,

ale, nayo hamba si kolokupela wutazi yaye.

20 Ne mikalile yako,

yomema mikalo ye zinghani zono kuwa kuzilonga.

21 Ne zinghani zono wukuwa kuzilonga,

zosuma kuwukamisa wumi wako hamba kuwongomeza.

Holeka kobocela nha vyono zinha zinghani zako.

22 Hono wumutuje mugolece muswanu,

mbeka watuga cinhu ciswanu.

Nayo yikulajila vyono, Muwaha Mulungu yakwendile.

23 Na lono munhu muciwa yakulomba,

kalombaga yakwineneleza.

Ninga munhu mugoli, kapitulaga ko wukali du.

24 Wiyendi wunji, si wujendelelaga vilo vinji.

Ninga wiyendi wunji nawo, wucizaga na wono muli ndugu.


Zimbazi 19

1 Yili ciba wuwe muciwa, ninga wuli mwihuwilwa.

Si nho kuwa wuli mulambi, kanyhi wuli mulele.

2 No wilowe nawo, hono wuwe wusina wizuce, si wuswanu.

Nazo nghani zo musunho, zitajilaga munhu mu mbali.

3 Wanhu wanji nawo, weyihodangaga mu mikalile yawo weneco,

so metumo gawo go wulele, na hanyuma,

wawa kumuhosa Muwaha Mulungu.

4 Ne wanhu wagoli,

nawo watugaga wendece wawo nhondo zose.

Ninga i waciwa,

hamba ne wacefu wono wena nawo wakuwafutila.

5 Na hono wulonje zo wulambi mu mahakama,

kotuga wulipicizwe, kowa wusina nzila yo kuponela.

6 Munhu wo wose,

yendile yatuje wendezwe kuli wanhu we lilumbo.

Kanyhi wenji,

wono wendile wawe wendece kuli wono walavyaga vipegwa.

7 Sunga ne wanduguze wo muciwa,

wawezaga siwamwendile yaye.

Kanyhi kekalaga yasina wendece wakwe.

Hamba yahidimbale muno ho yawatuje,

ninga sikosuma yaye.

8 Wuhidimbale muno mu wiyijize, wuzikumbucile na

zono wukwiyijiza, nagwe komoteswa.

9 Na hasina munhu, mono kalongaga zo wulambi mu

mahakama, mono si kalipizizwaga no kongomela.

10 Ne walele, si wanojeye wekale mu winji we zisawo,

Ne wetumami, si wanojeye wateme mu wanhu we lilumbo.

11 Na hono wuwe wina mahala, kokwiyimila likuwo lyako.

Na hono yunji yakusutizye, yowa nghani swanu muno,

hono wulece kuzicelelela zizo, zono yakutendeye.

12 Likuwo lyo mutemi, lihwanile ngholoma ye lisimba.

Ninga wendezwe wakwe, winha buhe mu mahanze.

13 No mwana mulele, kamuletelaga sogwe wugaye.

Nayo mucekulu we liwanjilila,

yehwanile mvula ye lihwela.

14 Heya, munhu kosuma kuhala kaya,

hamonga ne zisawo ze walezi wakwe.

Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yali wene,

yo kosuma kumupela mugolece we mahala.

15 Ale du, weze mudoba hono gwegwe wendile kuwa mudoba,

wugone nhongo.

Ninga wumanye vyono nzala yokwinjila.

16 Wugakamisaje malajilizo go Mulungu,

nagwe holeka kokwikala vilo vinji mu wikale wako.

Ninga hono wugabedegale, ale, kofwa.

17 Na lono wukuwataza vinhu waciwa,

yinha kotya wukuviwika kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

Nayo Muwaha Mulungu,

hanyuma kanyhi yakukugomolela.

18 Wuwafundaje wanago lono wakali wadodo,

lono wena no wupelu wo kugabocela mafundo.

Ninga hono wulece kuwafunda,

wukuwapela cilejeho co kwihodanga weneco.

19 Na hone munhu yena likuwo lyono likwaka musunho,

agwe mulekaje,

hambi nha kokwidinghulila wulipicizwe wakwe mweneco.

Ninga hono wuwone vyono kosuma kumutaza kamonga mwi'kuwo lyakwe,

ale, wulece kukatala kumutaza.

20 Na hono wuwe wuli mupelu kuwuhuliciza wulanjize we wayago,

lizuwa linji nagwe kotuga mahala.

21 Heya, i wanhu wosuma kwiwicila nghani nyinji zo kwituma.

Ninga wuhile wo Muwaha Mulungu, wo wufisilizwaga du.

22 Yo nghani ya cevu ciwaha,

hono wuwe wuli cilya cilya.

Kanyhi ciba i waciwa, si nhe wanhu walambi.

23 Hono wumogope Muwaha Mulungu,

nagwe ho kokwikala vilo vinji mu wikale wako,

hamba nghani yo yose 'mbi,

si yokwagana yaye.

24 Wanhu wanji, mbeka wadoba muno muno,

wakufutigwa sunga kuwanyhile ndigwa mu milomo yawo.

25 I wanhu wedunhumuzi, yiwanojeye watuje wulipicizwe,

ho wanji nawo, wono si wazimanyile zono ziwanojeye kwituma,

ho wefundile mumo.

Na hono wuwe ne mahala, kokwifundila mu wulanjizwe.

26 No mwana mono kamugazaga sogwe no kumuwinga nyina.

Yo mwana mono kakozaga soni, no kuleta cevu.

27 Gwe mwanangu, hono wuleme mafundo,

nha cinji wukulema zinghani zo wumanye.

28 Hawezaga hasina ling'ani,

lono muhomelezi yakuwa yena wuhile wo kumutamisa yunji.

Ne wanhu wabi nawo, nha wendile muzinzi wo wubi.

29 Ne wanhu we lizeze, wewikanize so wutowangwe.


Zimbazi 20

1 Wung'wela wo wujimbi, wuletaga mazozo,

no kumunoza munhu kawa mulele.

Kanyhi yo nghani ya wulele du,

munhu kuwa mung'wela.

2 Yikunojeye wumogope mutemi,

lono yakolwa ne likuwo,

nha vyono kuyogopaga simba,

lono yikukoloma.

Na hono wumukoze likuwo,

wukwiwulaga go mweneco.

3 No wu wula mahala wose,

wusagahizaga wiwanganile.

Ninga hono wileje muli zizo,

alo lo lukumyo luswanu.

4 No mulimi mudoba,

hono yalece kulima migunda yakwe,

mu vipango vyono vinojeye,

hamba si kokwambulila cinji.

5 Ne mahweso go munhu, gehwanile ne malenga,

gono gali mwi'sima litali muno.

Ninga hamba nhavivyo, u munhu we mahala,

vyo kosuma kuganega du.

6 Ne wanhu wose, wendile kwiwona wali cihuliko,

kanyhi wehuwilwa.

Ninga u mwihuwilwa, yo nani mono komuwona!

7 Wamotile wana, wono wena sogo yawo mwihuwilwa,

kanyhi mono yakwituma ze ling'ani.

8 U mutemi, kekalaga mwi'goda lyakwe

lyo wuling'anizi yawumanyile wubi,

no kuwuwona ne meso gakwe.

9 Alu mwene, ho yuli mbeka munhu, mono kosuma kutya,

vyono si yakuhoswa ne ciselinji cakwe,

mono yakondya kwiyeza wubi wakwe?

10 Muwaha Mulungu,

si yakwendezwa ne wanhu wono wakwitumamila vipimo,

ne vijelo vyono si vili ling'ani.

11 Sunga no mwana mudodo,

kelajilaga nha vyono yenha,

mu metumo gakwe.

Nagwe kosuma kumumanya,

hono mwana yuyu muswanu na yali ling'ani.

12 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu,

yacipelile meso go kuwonela,

ne makutu go kuhulicila.

13 Na hono witumamile vipango vyako vyose mu nhongo,

na mbeka kociwapa.

Holeka yikunojeye witume milimo,

holeka nagwe kotuga ndigwa nyinji.

14 No munhu wo wose mono kagulaga cinhu,

nhondo zose katezaga wuguzi wukamu.

Ninga kolo yawuya kwakwe kukaya,

nhondo ho yakulumba.

15 Na hono yiwe nhondo zose,

kuzimanyaga zono kulongaga ale,

kowa wina cambuzi ciwaha si nhe zahabu ne fweza.

16 No munhu mono kowa mulele, sunga yehuwize,

nha vyono komulipila mujenzi migawa yakwe,

ayo yimunojeye, sawo yakwe yisolwe yipinjikwe.

17 Ne cinhu co cose, cono kocitujila mu zinzila zimbi,

ha lulongozi, kosuma kucitumamila nha ndigwa swanu,

ninga hanyuma, cowa nha musawawa mu mulomo wako.

18 Weze kuzibocela zinghani ziswanu,

holeka nagwe komoteswa.

Ninga wulece kwinjila caka mu wulugu,

lono wukali nehwesa viswanu.

19 Nago maheho, si gahundikaga ze cinyele,

holeka agwe, wilegaje ne wanhu walonji-lonji.

20 Na hono wuwalijite walezi wako,

wumi wako wozima,

nha vyono tala yizimaga, kuwa kutitu tuku.

21 Mu nghango yo kuyitujila musunho sawo yako,

yo nghango yiyo kanyhi du, vyo yomalika,

hamba kukutaza cinji.

22 Wulece kumulipiciza yunji,

so wubi wono yakutendeye.

Ninga agwe, wumwitumbile du Muwaha Mulungu,

nayo yo kokulipila.

23 Muwaha Mulungu, si yakwendezwa ne wanhu,

wono wakwitumamila vipimo, ne vijelo,

vyono si vili ling'ani.

24 Muwaha Mulungu, yo yaciwicila zinzila zetu,

alu nani kanyhi, mono kosuma kuyimanya,

nzila yo wumi wakwe mweneco?

25 Weze kwamula kwihwesa viswanu,

lono wukali si nehuwiza,

i cinhu cono komulavizya Mulungu.

Hanyuma yilece kukupela wukalale.

26 U mutemi we mahala,

kosuma kumumanya kamonga du u munhu mono yasutizye,

nayo komulipiciza yasina lusungu.

27 Muwaha Mulungu, yacipela mutima ne ciselinji,

holeka si cidahile kwihundiciza i zetu yaye.

28 Nayo mutemi, kajendelelaga no wutemi wakwe du,

hono yawe yatemile mu ling'ani, ne mbeka, no lusungu.

29 Cikuwa kuvizina vilungo vyo wuzelelo,

no kuwa ne cogopo kuli zimvwi ze wakombi.

30 Hanji kuwezaga, wusungu wuwaha,

kucinoza cikawanize i mijendele yetu.


Zimbazi 21

1 Muwaha Mulungu, kayitongaga nhumbula yo mutemi,

nha vyono kacihulumyaga cikolongo.

2 Nagwe kokwihwesa 'mba vyono, zose zono wukwituma,

wukwituma ze ling'ani.

Ninga wukumbucile vyono,

Muwaha Mulungu, yo kazijelaga zinzila zako.

3 Nhondo zose, weze kwituma ze ling'ani na zo mugoloka,

zono zikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu,

si nho kumulavizya makumbiko.

4 I wanhu wabi,

watongwaga no wiwoni hamonga no widunhumuzi wawo.

Ne nghani yiyi, wo wubi.

5 Hono wuwe wuli mwihwesa viswanu, kotuga vinji.

Ninga, hono wuwe wuli munhu we papala-papala,

hamba si kotuga vinji.

6 No wugoli wono kowutuga mu zinzila zimbi,

wokwajilila musunho, hambi nha wo mutego wako we lifwa.

7 Nawo i wabi, wongomezezwaga mu wugazi wawo weneco.

Hambi nha wakulema kwituma ze ling'ani.

8 Ne wanhu wahodanji,

wakolaga mu nzila yono yipetyapetizye,

Ninga we ling'ani, wene wetumaga ze ling'ani.

9 Yili ciba kwikala kunze mwi'tembe lye kaya,

si nho kuhanga kaya monga, no mucekulu mono yali zozo.

10 I wanhu wabi, nhondo zose wekalaga wadabuciye zimbi du.

Kanyhi wasina lusungu kuli munhu wo wose.

11 Na lono munhu mwidunhumuzi yakulipicizwa,

sunga no munhu mono yasina mahweso,

nayo mo keyijizizaga lifundo.

No munhu we mahala nayo,

kokwiyijiziza muli zono yafundwa.

12 Mulungu yo we ling'ani,

nayo yazimanyile zose zono zikwitumwa mu zikaya ze wanhu wabi.

Nayo kowaletela i wabi wose wongomezwe.

13 Na hono wuleme kuwahuliciza waciwa cililo cawo,

Nagwe cililo cako, co kulondola wutazi,

hamba si cohulikwa yaye.

14 Na hono yunji yakukolelwe likuwo,

cipegwa cono komupela cinyele,

comupoza likuwo lyakwe.

15 Na lono ling'ani yikwitumwa, i we ling'ani,

wakuwa kusangalala muno.

Ninga i wanhu wabi wakongomezwa.

16 Nalyo lifwa, liwalindiye wose wono wajiliye,

no kuyileka nzila yo wizuce.

17 Na hono wende ze cinyemo-nyemo, divayi,

ne ndigwa swanu muno, hamba si kogolipa yaye.

18 Nawo i wanhu wabi, ziwawuyilaga weneco i zimbi,

zono wakuwa kusaka kuwatendela we ling'ani.

19 Na mbeka, yili ciba kwikala ku nyika,

si nho kwikala no mucekulu we lizozo,

mung'ung'ulici, kanyhi mukali.

20 I wanhu we mahala, wekalaga mu wugoli,

hamonga ne nyemo.

Ninga i walele weyagazaga sawo zawo kolo wazituga.

21 Weze muswanu, kanyhi mwihuwilwa,

holeka kokwikala vilo vinji.

Sunga ne wanhu wanji,

wokupela cogopo no kukutendela viswanu.

22 No mucekulu we mahala,

yadahile kulisola likaya,

lyono lina no wulindilizi we vilungo,

no kugakumulanga ne majinji gono wagetumbiye.

23 Na hono wusace wileje ne mazozo,

ale, wilanjilile mu zinghani zako zono kulongaga.

24 Ale, agwe wundajile u munhu mwidunhumuzi,

nane nokulajila u munhu mwiwoni, no mwivuni,

mono si kawacelelelaga wanhu wanji.

25 No munhu mudoba, mono si kasakaga kwituma milimo,

ayo yakwiwulaga yali wene.

26 Na zizo zono yakwituma,

yakwihwesa 'mba mo kotujila vyono yakusaka.

Ninga u munhu we ling'ani,

kalavyaga hamba kwimila yaye.

27 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu,

hamba si yendile i wanhu wabi,

wamulavizye makumbiko, ko muno,

gono gakulawa mu wuhile wawo wubi.

28 No wuhomelezi wo munhu mulambi,

si witumbilwaga yaye.

Langa nghani yo munhu mwinenenyhezi, yovumilwa.

29 I wanhu we ling'ani, wena witumbilo.

Ninga i wabi, wajelaga kwinoza weneco,

ho wawe nha waswanu.

30 I mahala go munhu, hamba lukumyo,

hamba wizuce, si vyosuma kukutaza cinji,

hono Muwaha Mulungu yo yalece kuwa hamonga nagwe.

31 Kosuma kuyiwikaniza fwalasi yako so wulugu.

Ninga Muwaha Mulungu du, yo kaletaga wusume.


Zimbazi 22

1 Ciba kuhagula litagwa yiswanu, si nho wugoli.

No wendezwe, si nha fweza na zahabu.

2 U mugoli no muciwa, wose wehwanile du.

Hambi Muwaha Mulungu,

nha yo yawawumba wose vyono wejete.

3 Ne wanhu wono wena wizuce,

wawonaga mbali zikuza, wawa kwilega.

Ninga mono yasina mahweso, keyinjizaga,

na hanyuma kawa yali mu cililo.

4 Wumuhulice Muwaha Mulungu,

weze wuli mwinenenyhezi,

nagwe kogolipa, kotuga lilumbo,

kanyhi kokwikala vilo vinji mono'nze.

5 Na hono wuwe wuwendile wumi wako,

ale, wileje ne mitego yono yiwagwizaga i wabi mu zinzila.

6 Wumwijize mwana mudodo,

mikalile yono yimunojeye.

Nayo kozikumbucila mu mikalile yakwe yose.

7 Nawo waciwa wezaga wo wetumami we wagoli.

Kanyhi vyo yinha, hono wukope ca munhu, hodu,

nagwe weza wuli muwanda wo munhu yuyo.

8 Na hono wuhadice mbeyu zono si ze ling'ani,

magayo gokuziza.

Ne nghani yo kuwewonela wanji, yowuka.

9 Weze wuli lusungu, muhanje ndigwa ne waciwa.

Nagwe mbeka komoteswa.

10 Wumuwingaje u munhu mwidunhumuzi,

mu widunhumuzi wakwe.

No wiwanganile wose womalika,

hamonga no wuma wose.

11 Na hono wuwe wina wendo wo wele wa mu nhumbula,

na ku so lihewa lye zinghani zako,

ale, mutemi kowa mwendece wako.

12 I meso go Muwaha Mulungu,

gayiwonaga i mbeka hono ho yili,

ninga zinghani ze walambi kazilemaga.

13 Nayo munhu mudoba, kekalaga yekaye ha kaya du,

hamba kulonga viswanu.

34 Nagwe kokwiwona nha munhu mono wuli mwi'lenga, wukudeka,

Zimbazi 25

Mbazi zinji kanyhi za Solomona

1 Azino nazo zo mbazi za Solomona,

zono munhu yunji yazisolela mu kaya ya Hezekiya,

u mutemi wa Yuda.

2 Cikumutogoza Mulungu,

so zono kazigubicizaga,

cikuwatogoza watemi,

so zose zono wazilolosaga.

3 Nagwe hamba si kozimanya,

zono mutemi yakwihwesa.

Hambi mahweso gakwe, nha gacicizize'se,

gehwanile no wulanga vyono wuli kutali nase,

hamba cisolo ce lilenga.

4 Wuwuse makwe gawuce mu fweza,

holeka nayo munghula konoza cinhu ciswanu.

5 Wawusaje i wabi ho mutemi holeka no wutemi,

wakwe womanyika so ling'ani yayo.

6 Nagwe kolo wema ha mwande yo mutemi,

wulece kujela kwiyendeceza hali mutemi,

no kwinoza nha wuli munhu we lilumbo.

7 Ciba wulindile sunga wuwikwe hanhu he lilumbo,

si nha kulonjelwa, wuwuce hono wikaye,

wumutelele yunji mono yena lilumbo.

8 Na hono wuwone nghani,

wulece kubita mbela mbela mu mahakama so nghani yiyo.

Ku soko hono yaze muhomelezi yunji yahomelele vyono zizo si za mbeka,

alu 'gwe ho konozaci?

9 Na hono wuwe wina nghani no mwikalani muyago,

muzimalaje nyenye du muli wejete,

ninga wulece kuzigubula ze cinyele za yunji.

10 Na hono nhavivyo, wose womanya vyono agwe wuli 'hede,

si kuhundikaga ze cinyele.

No wubi wuwo, si wokuwucila yaye.

11 Ne nghani yono yikulonjelwa hono hanojeye,

yihwanile ne mikalo ye zahabu mu nhoto za fweza.

12 No wutacile wono wukulavywa no munhu we mahala,

kuli munhu mono yendile kuhuliciza,

wuswanu, wucizize nyamwidole za zahabu,

hamba vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu,

vyono vinozigwe ne zahabu nengu.

13 U mwitumami mwihuwilwa,

kamwendezaga mono yakumutuma,

nhe malenga ge mbeho, mu madaha ge nhwanga,

mwi'zuwa likali.

14 Ne wanhu wono wehuwizaga vinhu ko wulambi,

wehwanile mavunde ne mbeho, vyono visina mvula.

15 Zinghani ze ngola,

ziwusaga wiwanganile we vilungo no kutuza watemi.

16 Wulece kukomba wuci winji kuciza,

hono wucizilize, kodeka.

17 Wulece kubita ku kaya yo mwikalani muyago kali kenji.

Hambi nha lyowa yikataza kuli mwene,

na hanyuma kokusula.

18 Na mono yakumubalalila muyagwe,

yuyo muwulaji, yenha mumaje,

ne nhumbi, hamba songa nghali.

19 Hono wumwitumbile munhu mono si mwihuwilwa,

mu vilo vyo wugaye,

yowa nha munhu mono yakujela kutafunya ndigwa ne lino lyono libenece,

hamba kujenda no mugulu wono wutinghuce.

20 Na hono wumwimbile zinyimbo munhu mono yena wukalale,

yowa nha wukumuvula myenda yakwe,

mwi'zuwa lyono lina mbeho nghali,

hamba kotya nho kuwukusuwa mwino mu nghomele.

21 Na hono mulugu wako yawe yadabuce, wumulisaje.

Na hono yakaluce, mupelaje malenga yang'we.

22 Na hono wumutendele zizo,

komukoza cevu muno.

Nagwe Muwaha Mulungu kokuhumbiliza.

23 Maheho, galetaga makuwo,

nha vyono mbeho ya kusumuka yiletaga mvula.

24 Yili ciba kwikala kunze mwi'tembe mucanyha,

si nho kuwa mu kaya monga,

no mucekulu mono yali zozo.

25 No wuhulika nghani swanu,

ku wulawa ku yisi yo kutali,

yihwanile no kung'wa malenga ge zizizi,

lono wukaluce.

26 No munhu muswanu lono yakuhanga no munhu mubi,

yakwihwana no munhu we ling'ani,

mono yakuwuya ne nyuma no kuhanga ne wabi,

kawa nho muvwilo wono wanhu watibuye ne magulu,

hamba cisima cono civizice.

27 Si viswanu kuli'gwe kulya wuci winji.

Weze no wiyimile mu wituma nghani.

28 Na hono wulece kusuma kulimila likuwo lyako,

kokwihwana ne likaya lyono lisina magomawakali,

nalyo liwezaga yipelu no kuhomwa.


Zimbazi 26

1 Lukumyo lo mulele, lwa bwete,

hambi nha lwihwanile nha seluji mu cibahu,

hamba mvula mwi'pango lye ndudula.

2 Nawo wulijitwe wono wusina soko,

si womugwila munhu,

langa hono wuwe wina soko yayo.

Hambi nha wowa wihwanile zindeje,

zono zigulukaga ako na ako, hamba kutula ng'o.

3 Fwalasi yikunagwa, nayo ndogowe yiwopwaga no lubano.

Nayo mulele, wumukune no mukotya.

4 Wulece kumupitula mulele mu wulele wakwe,

ho wulece kwihwana nayo.

5 Wumupitule mulele mu nghango yo wulele wakwe,

ho yalece kwiyihwesa vyono we mahala.

6 Na hono wumutume mulele yakutegulile nghani,

kowa nho munhu mono wukwidumula magulu gako no kwipela wambuce du.

kuli munhu we citewe,

mono kosuma kuyitumamila migulu yakwe.

wehwana no munhu mono yakuwicila libwe mu manati.

zihwanile u munhu mugalo 'jimbi,

mono yakujela kuhomola limiya,

lyono limuhomile mu muwoko wakwe.

mono yakwilumbaniza mu mulimo no munhu mulele,

mono yakupuluta,

ale, yuyo welumbanizaga no munhu wo wose mono yakupuluta.

kawuyilaga kanyhi kwituma zinghani zo wulele,

nhe libwa lyono liwuyilaga madeko galyo.

12 No munhu mulele muno, yuyo yali ciba,

si nho munhu mono kehwesaga yena mahala,

kumbe ho yasina.

13 Alu mwene, nhawule u munhu mudoba,

si kalawilaga kunze?

Alo kowa yakogopa simba?

14 Nayo munhu mudoba, kebiducizaga mu citanda du,

yehwanile nho mulango wono wuninyinaga mu zibawaba zayo.

15 Ne wanhu wanji wadoba muno,

wakulemegwa sunga no kutajila ndigwa mu milomo yawo.

16 No munhu mudoba, kehwesaga nha vyono yena mahala,

kuciza wanhu mupunghati,

wono wosuma kulavya mahweso maswanu.

17 Nawo u witajila mu nghani zono si zako,

wihwanile no wubita mu nzila,

kuwa kugebata makutu ge libwa nhinicizo.

18 No munhu mono komwajiliza nghani muyagwe,

hanyuma kanyhi ho yatye yali kumwizavya,

19 yuyo yehwanile munhu wo lukwale,

mono yakwidawalila cipe ciwulaji.

20 Hono hasina ngwagu, moto si wukolelaga,

na hono hasina wusomenghanya, hawezaga hasina mazozo.

21 Makala, go galekaga mavu gawa gali moto.

Nazo zingwagu, zo zilekaga moto wukwaka.

Nayo musomenghanya,

yo kalekaga mazozo gakujendelela.

22 Ne zinghani ze maheho, zili muzinzi!

Nase, wulenyi vyono cizendile!

23 Ne zinghani zo wulambi,

zihundikaga mahweso ge mbeka,

zihwanile ne nyungu swanu ye lilongo,

yono hiliwe na fweza kunze yayo.

24 Nayo munhu mukoci,

kahundicizaga lusulo lwakwe,

mu zinghani zo wiyendezi.

25 Ne zinghani zakwe, zijelaga nha swanu,

ninga wulece kumwitumbila.

Hambi nhumbula yakwe, nha yimemile lusulo du.

26 Kosuma kujela kuluhundika lusulo lwakwe ko wusugu,

ninga i wanhu wose woziwona i zimbi zono yakwituma.

27 Ne wanhu wono wawategaga wayawo mitego,

wagwilaga kanyhi weneco.

Ne wanhu wono wabidusaga libwe,

ho liwagwile wayawo,

libwe liwuyaga no kuwagwila weneco.

28 No lulimi lo wulambi,

luwasulaga wono lukuwatamisa.

Ne zinghani zo wulambi, ziletaga wongomele.


Zimbazi 27

1 Wulece kwidunhumulila kuli zono kozinoza mitondo,

10 Wulece kuwaleka weyendi wayago,

hamba weyendi wo sogo.

Na kanyhi hono wuwe wuli mu wugaye,

wulece kulondola wutazi kuli nduguyo.

Kanyhi wukumbucile nha vyono,

mwiyendi muyago mono mwikalane behi nayo,

yuyo yo kosuma kukutaza, si

nho nduguyo mono yekaye kutali nagwe.

11 Gwe mwanangu, weze ne mahala,

nane holeka nosangalala.

Nane nowa nina lipitulo,

kuli munhu wowose mono kohosa 'ne.

12 Ne wanhu wo wizuce, wawonaga zimbali zikuwaziza,

hodu, wawa kwilega.

Ninga u munhu mono yasina mahweso,

mwene kokwiyinjiza,

ninga hanyuma kanyhi yawe kukalala.

13 No munhu mono kowa mulele,

sunga yehuwize nha vyono komulipila u mujenzi migawa yakwe,

ayo yuyo yimunojeye sawo yakwe yisolwe,

yipinjikwe so migawa yiyo.

14 Na mono yendile kwilawa no kumwinusa mwendece wakwe

ne 'zi yikali muno muno,

wuwo wowalwa nha wulijite kuli mwene.

15 No mugolece mono yali zozo,

yehwanile nhe mvula ye lihwela.

16 Alu kosuma kumunyamaza na nzila ci?

Wule, hono wukulanga,

wujelile ne 'zuwa linji kuyimila mbeho,

hamba kufumbata mafuta mu muwoko wako?

17 Ninga nase co wanhu,

ciyijizizaga kuli wanhu wayetu,

nha vyono cuma cinolaga cuma ciyagwe.

18 Na hono wuwukamise mukuyu wako,

holeka kolya ne mana gayo.

No mwitumami nayo,

hono yamulanjilile viswanu muwaha wakwe,

ale, nayo kotuga lilumbo.

19 Nha vyono kusumaga kwiwona wusu wako mu malenga,

lono wukugalanga.

Ale, nhavivyo nagwe,

vyo kosuma kwiwona mu nhumbula yako go mweneco.

20 No wusulumizi wo munhu,

wihwanile ne yinze ye wafu, yono si yigutaga.

21 Lilumbo lyo munhu, nalyo likuwa kujelwa,

nha vyono moto wujelaga zahabu na fweza.

22 Hamba wumutowe mulele,

sunga yawe yali behi no kufwa,

ninga si kosuma kuwutowa wulele wakwe sunga wumuwucile.

23 Wuhidimbale kuzidima ngholo ne zing'ombe zako,

ko wulanjilizi muno.

24 Hambi sawo, nha si yikalaga cibitilila.

Hamba makungugo du nago,

si gekalaga cibitilila.

25 Na lono wukutema mahanze go kunyaza so ng'ombe,

kanyhi mu cipango cico,

weze kutema ne mahanze ga mu vigongo,

lono wukali wulindiye mahanze kanyhi gawiluce.

26 Nagwe kosuma kwinozeza myenda,

mu wulawa mu wukunzeje we zingholo zako.

Na kosuma kugula mugunda,

ne zipesa zono zolawa mu wuguzi we zimhene zako zinji.

27 Ne zimhene zono zosigala,

kowa kuzikama mele,

gono gomukwilenyi nyenye wose,

mu mhuga yako,

hamonga ne wetumami wako we cicekulu.


Zimbazi 28

1 Nawo i wabi, watizaga wakumangha,

na hono hasina munhu wo wose mono yakuwawinga.

Ninga u munhu we ling'ani, yapetuce yenha simba.

2 Nalyo likungugo,

hono liwe lyenjila mu wituma zimbi,

likawanizaga watemi walyo.

Ninga hamba nhavivyo,

likungugo lyowa lili vilungo,

hono liwe lina walongola we mahala no wizuce.

3 No munhu mono yena wulajilize,

kanyhi ho yawe kuwewonela i waciwa,

yehwanile mvula mbaha,

yono yihodangaga vilimwa.

4 Na hono wuwe si kuwucelelelaga wulajilize,

nagwe wehwana i wanhu wabi.

Ninga hono wuwe wukuwutyatya wulajilize,

si kohoswa yaye,

5 Ne wanhu wabi,

hamba si wayimanyaga ling'ani yaye.

Ninga wono wamuhembaga Muwaha Mulungu,

wayimanyiye lukulu.

6 Ciba hono wuwe muciwa, ninga wuli mwihuwilwa,

si nha kuwa wuli mugoli, na kuno si wuli mwihuwilwa.

7 No muzelelo mono yakuwuhulika

no kuwutyatya wulajilize,

yuyo yena wizuce.

Ninga mono yakwilunza ne wanhu wono si wanojeye,

kamuletelaga sogwe cevu.

8 Na hono wuwe kutugaga wugoli wako,

mu nzila yo konjeleza cambuzi hamonga no wukonji kuli wanhu,

ale, wumanye vyono, sawo yako yiyo,

yolohamila kuli yunji mono yali lusungu ku waciwa.

9 Na hono wulece kuwutyatya wulajilize,

nayo Mulungu kowuwona wulombe wako si wuswanu,

hamba si kowuhulika yaye.

10 Hono wumukwejeleze munhu we ling'ani,

no kumugwisiza mu wubi,

nagwe kogwila mu mutego wako go mweneco.

Ne wanhu wono wasina wusutye,

wohumbilizwa cihumbilizwa ciswanu muno.

11 Nawo wanhu wagoli, nhondo zose,

wene wehwesaga nha vyono wena mahala.

Ninga kumbe u muciwa,

mono yena wizuce mu mikalile yakwe,

yuyo yo yamumanyile mugoli viswanu.

12 Ne wanhu waswanu lono wakutema,

wanhu wose wakuwasangalalila.

Ninga kolo wanhu wabi wakutema,

i wanhu wakwihundika.

13 Na hono wujendelele kuwuhundika wubi wako,

mbeka hamba si komoteswa mu mikalile yako.

Holeka yikunojeye wunene wubi wako, wuwulece,

nayo Mulungu kokuwonela lusungu.

14 Weze wuli cihuliko kuli Muwaha Mulungu nhondo zose,

nagwe kowona nyemo.

Ninga hono wuwe wusina cihuliko,

mbeka kokongomezwa.

15 Ne wanhu waciwa,

si watugaga wutazi kuli mutemi mubi,

hambi nha mukali,

yehwanile simba yono yikukoloma,

hamba dubu yono yidabuce.

16 No mutemi mono yasina wizuce,

kawezaga mugazi muno.

Ninga mono yawusulile wusulumizi,

yuyo kotema vilo vinji.

17 No munhu muwulaji,

kehimbilaga citenjelo cakwe musunho.

Holeka wulece kumwimila.

18 Weze mwihuwilwa,

holeka nagwe kokwikala wupolile.

Ninga hono wuwe si wuli mwihuwilwa,

kogwa musunho.

19 Nayo mono kalimaga yahidimbaye,

kawezaga na ndigwa nyinji.

Ninga i wanhu wono wajilizaga vipango bwete,

wowa kwikala vyo waciwa du.

20 Ne wanhu wehuwilwa,

wowa wena mikalile yono yina wusangalale.

Ninga mono yakuseka ho yatuje wugoli musunho,

mbeka kolipicizwa.

21 Nako kuwahijila mbejele wanhu, si kuswanu ng'o.

Ninga mahakimu wanji,

wakonga-kongwaga mu nzila yo kupegwa cinhu cidodo du ca mafupo.

22 Ne wanhu wabugu, weyohaga kwitujila sawo,

hamba kumanya zono zowagana hanyuma.

23 Wumutacilaje munhu, hanyuma nayo kokwendezwa,

si nha agwe kwiyendeza kuli mwene.

24 Na mono yakwihwesa vyono,

si'sutya kuwahiziza walezi wakwe,

yuyo yakusutya, hambi wuhizi,

nha wuhizi du nhe wahizi wanji.

25 No munhu mubugu, kasagahizaga mazozo.

Ninga agwe, wiyohe kumwitumbila Muwaha Mulungu du.

26 Kanyhi yo nghani ya wulele,

hono wuwe kuyiwambaga zono watemya gwegwe du.

Holeka wilanjilile, vyono yili ciba,

hono wuwe kuwuhulika wulanjize we wanhu we mahala.

27 Wuwapele waciwa, nagwe hamba si kokwanjila cinji yaye.

Ninga hono wulece kuwataza waciwa,

wanhu wenji wokulijita.

28 Wanhu wehundikaga, lono wanhu wabi wakutema.

Ninga wawo kolo wawuswa mu wutemi,

i wanhu we ling'ani kanyhi wo wotema.


Zimbazi 29

1 Na hono wuwe wusina cihuliko,

nhondo zose lono wukutacilwa, lizuwa linji,

kolobenangwa hamba si koloponywa kanyhi.

2 Hono wundajile mutemi mono yali ling'ani,

nane nokulajila wanhu wono wena wusangalale.

Na hono wundajile u mutemi mubi,

nane nokulajila i wanhu wono wena wukalale.

3 Hono wuwe wendile mahala,

mbeka sogo kokwendezwa nagwe.

Kanyhi yo nghani ya wulele,

yo munhu kwitumamila sawo kuli wacekulu wasenyha.

4 Na hono mutemi yawe yali mu ling'ani,

sunga ne likungugo lyowa lili vilungo.

Ninga hono yawe mwenda sawo,

koyongomeza yisi yakwe.

5 Na hono wuwe wuli mwiyendezi kuli wendece wako,

wukwitega mutego go mweneco.

6 Ne wanhu wabi, weyigwizaga mu wubi wawo.

Akuno i wanhu we ling'ani, wasangalaye kanyhi welecele.

7 Nayo munhu we ling'ani yazimanyile

zono zimunojeye yawatendele waciwa.

Ninga i wanhu wabi, si wazimanyaga zizo.

8 Ne wanhu wono si webedegazi

wasagahazaga wuma mwi'kaya.

Ninga i wanhu we mahala, wapozaga makuwo.

9 No munhu we mahala, hono yamusitace mulele,

u mulele kowa kuseka du,

kanyhi kowa yazozize du akuno yakuliga.

10 I wanhu wono wendile wuwulaji,

wamusulaga u munhu we ling'ani.

Ninga we ling'ani wowucitila wumi wo munhu we liwala lilyo.

11 Ne wanhu walele, welajilaga makuwo gawo calinze.

Ninga we mahala, wali ngola, akuno wena no wiyimizi.

12 Na hono mutemi yawe muhulicila zo wulambi,

ne wetumami wakwe wose wowa walambi.

13 U muciwa no mugazi, wose yo nghologo monga,

u Muwaha Mulungu nha yawapela meso wose vyono wejete.

14 No mutemi mono kawaling'anizaga viswanu waciwa,

kotema vilo vinji muno.

15 Kutacila hamonga no kufunda,

co cinhu ciswanu kuli wana.

No mwana hono yalece kutendelwa zizo,

komukoza cevu nyina ku soko yakwe.

16 Na hono i wanhu wabi wawe wo watemi,

no wusutye wonjelezekaga.

Ninga we ling'ani wokwikala walanjiliye wufwe we wanhu wawo.

17 Wumufundaje mwanago,

nagwe kokwilumbila muli mwene.

Nayo hamba si kokukoza cevu yaye.

18 Ne likungugo hono lilece kuwa no wutonji wo Mulungu,

liwezaga yikungugo lyono lisina wulajilize.

Yo mumoti munhu mono yebate lilajilizo lyo Mulungu.

19 Si kosuma kumufunda mwitumami na nghani du,

ku soko hamba yawe yakukwizuka zono wukumulonjela,

ninga si kohulika.

20 Ciba kumwitumbila u munhu mulele,

si nha kumwitumbila u munhu mono kelonganjilaga du,

wu wula kwamula kwihwesa.

21 Na hono wumuzoweleze mwitumami wako,

ku wulawa wudodo wakwe,

wuwe kumupela du cono yakusaka,

mwi'zuwa linji,

kolo kwisolela du mweneco vinhu vyako.

22 Ne wanhu wono wena makuwo ge mbela mbela,

waletaga wuma hamonga ne mazozo.

23 Ne wanhu wedunhumuzi, wosuma kukuletela lifwa.

Ninga hono wuwe mwinenenyhezi, wokupela lilumbo.

24 Nayo u muyagwe no muhizi, kesuzaga wumi wakwe.

Na hono yalonje ze mbeka mu mahakama,

kotuga wulipicizwe.

Nayo hono yalece kwituma nhavivyo,

Mulungu komulijita.

25 Nawo woga wo kuwogopa wanhu, wuletaga mutego.

Ninga hono wumwitumbile Muwaha Mulungu,

kowa wupolile.

26 Ne wanhu wose wasakaga watuje wendezwe kuli mutemi.

Ninga kosuma kutuga ling'ani kuli Muwaha Mulungu du yali wene.

27 I wanhu we ling'ani, wawasulaga i wabi,

nawo i wanhu wabi, wawasulaga i wanhu we ling'ani.


Zimbazi 30

Zinghani za Aguli

1 Azi zo nghani zono yazibocela Aguli u mwana

wa Yake, yakutya, “U Mulungu yakondya kundeka,

u Mulungu yakondya kundeka.

Neza sina wutazi wo wose.

2 Nane luno, nehwana lilimu, si nha munhu.

Naluno, neza sina wizuce wono wunojeye munhu yawe yena nawo.

3 Nghali neyijiza ze mahala,

hamba sinizimanyile zinghani zo Mulungu yaye.

4 Alo nani 'mbi mono yagatema mahala ga ku wulanga?

Hamba nani mono yasuma kuyifumbata mbeho

mu muwoko wakwe?

Hamba mono yasuma kugavunga malenga mu citema?

Hamba mono yawika mipaka ye yinze nani?

Alo yo nani mwene, hono wumumanyile?

Ne litagwa lyo mwanagwe nani?

5 “Ninga Mulungu kawukamisaga wihuwizo wakwe

wono kawihuwizaga.

Mwene yehwanile ngula,

kuli wose wono walondolaga wucitilwe kuli mwene.

6 Wulece konjeleza zinghani zakwe, mwene kokuleka,

no kukuwona vyono wuli mulambi.”

Mbazi zinji kanyhi

7 Gwe go Mulungu,

ane nghukulomba wumhele nghani nyejete lono nghali mumi.

8 Wumbusize kutali ne zinghani ze bwete na zo wulambi.

Na kanyhi wulece kumhela wuciwa hamba wugoli.

Ninga hamba nhavivyo,

nghulomba wumhele ndigwa yono yinghwiliye.

9 Ku soko hono niwe nina ndigwa nyinji,

ndahile kutya “Muwaha Mulungu yo nani?”

Ninga kanyhi hono niwe nili muciwa,

nohiza no kulitoyula litagwa lyakwe.

10 Wulece kumubalalila bwete u mwitumami,

kuli muwaha wakwe.

Vyono hono wunoze nhavivyo,

agwe kolijitwa no kutuga magayo so nghani yiyo.

11 Kanyhi ko wali wanhu,

wono walijitaga wasogo zawo,

no kuwakununghalila i wanyina zawo.

12 Ko wali wanhu wanji, wono wewonaga wazelu,

ninga kumbe wene ho watipiye du ne makwe gawo.

13 Ko wali wanhu wanji, wono wewonaga waswanu,

Lanje ta, vyono wakwidunhumula weneco!

14 Ko wali wanhu wanji, wono wawewonelaga waciwa,

na wono wena wanjile, no kuwasolela vinhu vyawo,

wakwitujila ndigwa yawo mu nzila yiyo.

15 Ling'onyo lya mu malenga, lyono lyonghaga sakami,

lina wana we cicekulu wejete.

Na wose vyono wejete,

wakucemwa, “Wumhelaje.”

Na ho vili vinhu vine vyono si vigutaga yaye,

16 I yinze ye wafu,

Mucekulu mugumba,

Muhangha mukavu wono wukusaka mvula.

No moto wono si wusumikaga kuzimywa.

17 No munhu mono kamusekangaga sogwe,

hamba mono yakumubedegala nyina mu wukombi wakwe,

munhu yuyo yanojeye yaligwe ne zi 'ndi,

hamba meso gakwe gakopozolwe ne mahowe.

18 Kanyhi, hovili vinhu vidatu hamba vine vye cinyele kuli'ne,

vyono hamba si nivimanyile yaye, navyo vyovi:

19 Wuguluce we 'ndi mu mwanya,

wujende we lizoka mwi'talawe,

na vyono ngalawa yisumaga kuyiwona nzila yayo mwi'lenga.

Na vyono wiyende wo mulume no muhinza wuwezaga.

20 Na yiyi yo nzila yono mucekulu

mono si mwihuwilwa kayitumamilaga.

Mwene kolo yasenyha, kawa kwihovuga,

hanyuma kawa kutya,

“Ninga ane, hamba si nitumile nghani yinji 'mbi yaye.”

21 Na kozili nghani yine

zono yinze si yosuma kuzidinghula nazo zozi:

22 U muwanda hono yawe mutemi,

U munhu mulele, hono yawe yakwizize ndigwa.

23 No mucekulu mono yasuligwe, hono yatolwe.

No mwitumami we cicekulu,

hono yamupeme mugolece munya kaya.

24 Na mono yinze,

mo vili viwumbwa vine

vyono vina miwili midodo muno muno,

ninga vina mahala menji.

25 Sungusungu, vyo viwumbwa vidodo,

ninga vikuwa kusuma kwiwicila ciwutwa ca ndigwa mu cibahu.

26 Mhimbi, nazo zisina vilungo,

ninga zikuwa kwinozeza wikalo wazo mu matalawe.

27 Nzije, nazo zisina mutemi,

ninga zitulukaga ziwumbatiye hamonga du.

28 Nyhodo nazo ndodo,

kosuma hamba kuyifumbata monga mwi'ganza,

ninga zikalaga mu makulu.

29 Kanyhi, ho vili vinhu vine, vyono vyendezaga,

hono wukuvilanga vikujenda, navyo vyovi:

30 Simba, lyono lina vilungo kuciza malimu gose,

lyono silyogopaga cinji.

31 Mvulati ne zinzogolo,

zono zijendaga zikwitogola.

Ne watemi lono wali ha mwande ye wanhu wawo.

32 Na hono nagwe wali wenjila mu wulele wo widunhumuzi,

ale, wiyihengaje!

33 Hono wugasuce mele, kotuga tilazi,

hono wumutowe yunji mu mhula,

mafununghula golawila,

ninga honowulisagahize likuwo,

kokwiyinjiza mu mbali.


Zimbazi 31

Wulanjizwe kuli Mutemi

1 Azi zo nghani zono mutemi Lamuyeli yafundwa no nyina, yatya,

2 “Agwe yo gwegwe du mwanangu we cilume monga,

mono namulomba kuli Mulungu,

nayo yakamhela.

Alu magu, hamba nghulonjele nityeci?

3 Ninga 'ne nghukutya vyono,

wulecele lukulu kumalila vilungo vyako vyose mu wusenyha,

ne sawo yako yose mu wacekulu,

hambi zizo nha zawahodangaga sunga ne watemi.

4 Holeka wuhulicize gwe mwanangu Lamuyeli,

i watemi si wanojeye wawe wagalo

'jimbi, hamba kuwa no wusulumizi wo wugalo 'jimbi.

5 Ku soko hono wawe wagala wujimbi,

hobaho hamba si wakukumbucila kanyhi zo wulajilize,

kanyhi hamba si wakucelelela ze ling'ani ye wanhu.

6 “Holeka, wugalo 'jimbi,

wuwanojeye wanhu wono wakufwa,

na wono wali mu wukalale.

7 Wawo langa wo wanojeye wang'we,

ho wasemwe zo wuciwa wawo,

no wukalale wawo.

8 Wulonje zo wuling'anize,

so wono si wosuma kwilonjela weneco.

Wuwataze wono wasina wutazi,

ho watuje ling'ani yawo.

9 Holeka, wulonje ku soko yawo zinghani zawo,

kanyhi weze wuli muling'anizi we ling'ani.

No kuwatendela ze ling'ani i waciwa na wono wanjiye.”

U mugolece muswanu mono yakwiliye

10 Na mbeka kukamu kumutuga mugolece mono yakwiliye! Hambi nha yena wuguzi wukamu muno si nha vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu!
11 Nayo mugolece we liwala lilyo, sunga no mulume wakwe, kawezaga yamwitumbiye. Kanyhi hamba si kolo ciwapa yaye.

12 Na mu myaka yose yo wikalane wawo, hasina

hambe 'zuwa lyono mugolece yamukalaza mulume wakwe.

13 Langa mwene vilo vyose, kekalaga yakunoza myenda

yo wukunzeje we zingholo, hamonga na ye bafuta.

14 Kanyhi kakongholaga ndigwa ako na ako,

yakuyileta mu kaya, kanozaga nhe wasuluza

wa mu ngalawa yo wukwawi.

15 Na kanyhi kelawaga kwinuka hakali mitondo muno

muno, ho yawikanize ndigwa so we mhuga yakwe,

hamonga no kumugawila u mwitumami wakwe we cicekulu,

milimo yono kokwituma.

16 Kalondolaga mugunda, kawa kuwugula ne zipesa

zono yali yakwawa, hanyuma kawa kuhadika zabibu.

17 Kanyhi muhidimbazi muno mu milimo,

yali vilungo kanyhi mwitumi.

18 Nayo kawezaga yamanyile ne vifo vye vinhu vyose

vyono kanozaga.

Nayo ketumaga milimo sunga lizuwa ligwila.

19 Mwene kabotaga mweneco kwe miwoko yakwe,

no kuhona myenda yakwe kwe miwoko yakwe.

20 Kanyhi kawawonelaga lusungu i waciwa

na wono wali mu wanjile.

21 Na mu madaha ge cinyuli, mwene hamba si

kehwelelaga yaye, hambi we mhuga yakwe wose,

nha wena myenda yono yowapela civuce.

22 Kahonaga mweneco ne mitote yo kutandika mucanyha

ye vitanda, kanyhi, kavwalaga myenda yo lugandu.

23 No mulume wakwe, yo munhu we lilumbo.

Kanyhi yo yumonga wa muli walongola mu'si.

24 Kanyhi kahonaga myenda ne misipi, kawa

kuwaguziza i wasuluza.

25 Yo mucekulu mono yali vilungo, kanyhi yena

lilumbo, hamba si kehwelelaga za mu vilo vyono vikuza.

26 Kanyhi, yendile kulonga ko wupolo zinghani ze mahala.

27 Kanyhi mwene kekalaga yakwipiganza, yakuwalondolela

visakwa we mhuga yakwe.

28 Ne wana nawo, wendezwaga muno, nayo mulume wakwe,

kamulumbaga muno.

29 Mulume wakwe katezaga, “I wacekulu wenji,

mbeka wagolece waswanu, ninga agwe

wuwacizize wawo wose.”

30 Nawo wuhijila mbejele, wina zo wukoci,

nawo wuswanu wo wulangwe, wa bwete.

Ninga mucekulu mono kamogopaga Muwaha Mulungu,

yuyo langa, kotuga lilumbo.

31 Wumulumbaje mugolece wako, muli zose zono

yakwituma. Nayo, holeka kotuga lukumyo hali wanhu wose.
