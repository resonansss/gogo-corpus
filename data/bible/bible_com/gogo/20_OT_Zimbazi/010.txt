Zimbazi 10

Zimbazi za Solomona

1 Azi zo mbazi za Solomona, yakutya,

U mwana mono yena mahala,

kayisangalazaga nhumbula yo sogwe.

Ninga umwana mulele,

kamuletelaga nyina wusungu wukali muno.

2 Sawo yono koyitujila mu nzila zo wubi,

yisina cambuzi.

Ninga wiling'ano,

wuponyaga mu wufwe.

3 Muwaha Mulungu si kowaleceleza

i wanhu waswanu wawe wali nzala.

Langa kowaleka i wabi wekale si wavitujile vyono wakuvisaka.

4 Hono wuwe wuli mudoba,

kokwiletela wuciwa,

ninga hono wuwe mupiligazi ku milimo, kogolipa.

5 U munhu we mahala,

kakongholaga mbela nhwanga yakwe lono yakondya kupya.

Ninga yo nghani ya cevu,

munhu kuwa wugonile,

mu madaha ge mhwanga.

6 U munhu muswanu katugaga wumoteswe.

Ninga zinghani zo munhu mubi,

zigubikaga wugazi.

7 Ne wanhu waswanu wowa kukumbucilwa nha wumoteswe.

Ninga i wanhu wabi,

matagwa gawo gowa kuwola kamonga du.

8 Ne wanhu we mahala,

wabocelaga mafundo maswanu kamonga.

Ninga i wanhu wono walongaga zo wulele,

awo wokongomezwa.

9 I wanhu we ling'ani, wali mu mulamu,

kanyhi wali mu wucitilwe.

Ninga wono wasina cihuliko, womanyika.

10 No munhu mono si yakuyityatya mbeka,

kaletaga mazozo.

Ninga mono yakutacila calinze,

kaletaga mulamu.

11 Ne zinghani zo munhu muswanu,

wo muvwilo wo wumi.

Ninga zinghani zo munhu mubi,

zigubukaga wugazi.

12 Lusulo, luletaga wiwanganile.

Ninga wendo, wugubikaga masutya.

13 Iwanhu wono wena wizuce,

walongaga ze mahala.

Ninga i wanhu walele,

awo wakusaka kulipicizwa du.

14 Nawo we mahala,

wakuwa kutuga wumanye mu nghango yawo.

Ninga zinonga ze walele,

ziletaga mazozo mbela mbela.

15 Wugoli wuwatazaga wagoli,

nawo wuciwa wuwahodangaga i waciwa.

16 Ne cipegwa so mulimo muswanu, wo wumi.

Ninga wubi wonjelezaga mukwelekwele wo kujendelela kwituma wubi.

17 Ne wanhu wono wazibocelaga ze mafundo,

wawo wowumipa.

Ninga wanhu wono si wendile kuvumila ze masutya gawo,

wali mu mbali.

18 No munhu mono kahundikaga simbili yakwe,

yuyo mulambi.

No munhu mono yendile kukumusa nghani zo wulambi,

munhu yuyo mulele.

19 Mu winji we zinghani zako,

no wubi mo winjililaga mumo.

Ninga hono wuwe wina mahala,

yili ciba wuwe si wuli mulonji muno.

20 Ne zinghani zo munhu muswanu,

zihwanile fweza swanu.

Ninga mahweso go munhu mubi,

gasina cambuzi.

21 Ne zinghani zo munhu muswanu,

ziwaletelaga cambuzi wanhu wenji.

Ninga kosuma kwiwulaga wuli wene,

so nghani za wulele du.

22 U wumoti wo Muwaha Mulungu,

wo wugolifyaga.

Ninga wituma milimo kwe vilungo,

si wokugolifya yaye.

23 Kanyhi yo nghani ya wulele kusangalalila mu zimbi.

Ninga i wanhu wono wena wizuce,

wendezwaga ne mahala.

24 Nawo we ling'ani,

watugaga vyono wasakaga.

Ninga iwabi, watugaga cono wakucogopa.

25 Na hono civulavumbi cize,

ciwakwenghwelezaga wanhu wabi.

Ninga wanhu we ling'ani,

wekalaga nhondo zose, wapolile du.

26 Wulece kumupela mulimo wako munhu mudoba yakwitumile,

hambi u munhu yuyo nha kokupaya mu meno gako nha pilipili,

kanyhi kowa nha malyosi gono gokufunyhila mu meso gako.

27 Holeka agwe,

wumuhulice Muwaha Mulungu,

holeka kokwikala vilo vinji mono'nze.

Ninga i wanhu wabi wafwizaga na lono vipango vyawo vikali nafika.

28 No witumbilo we wanhu waswanu,

wuwapelaga wusangalale.

Ninga i wanhu wabi,

wawezaga wasina witumbilo wo wose.

29 Muwaha Mulungu kawadimaga wanhu we ling'ani.

Ninga kawongomezaga wose i wetuma zimbi.

30 Iwanhu we ling'ani,

wokwikala mu wudimwe vilo vyose.

Ninga i wanhu wabi,

si wowumipa mono yinze.

31 Ne wanhu we mahala,

walongaga zinghani ze mahala,

ninga u lulimi lono lulongaga zimbi,

lodumulwa.

32 Kanyhi i wanhu we ling'ani,

wakuwa kumanya ziswanu zono ziwanojeye walonje.

Ninga i wanhu wabi nhondo zose,

walongaga zinghani zono zidahile kuwatamisa wanhu.
