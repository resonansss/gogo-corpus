Zimbazi 7

1 Gwe mwanangu,

wukamise zono nghulonga,

wulece kusemwa zono nghukulajiliza witume.

2 Wibataje malajilizo gangu ho wuwumipe.

Wibate mejizo gangu ko wulanjilizi muno,

nha vyono kugalanjililaga meso gako.

3 Weze wugebate mapango gose,

wugandice mu nhumbula yako mugati.

4 Wuganoze mahala gawe yo lumbulyo,

no wizuce nawo wuwe yo mwendece wako.

5 Navyo vyokucitila kuli wacekulu we wanhu,

na kuli wacekulu wono wena nghani zo wupepelezi.

Mucekulu we mikalile yo wusenyha

6 Mwi'zuwa linji,

nali sunjiliye mwi'dilisha lya mu kaya yangu,

7 nane nghawawona wazelelo wenji walele,

na mumo muli wazelelo wawo,

nghamuwona muzelelo yunji mulele.

8 Muzelelo yuyo,

yakolaga mu nzila,

yono yikolile behi ne kaya,

yono yekalaga mucekulu yunji.

Nayo muzelelo yuyo,

yakolaga behi ne kaya yo mucekulu yuyo,

9 mwi'pango lye cinyamihe,

lono kwali kwatukuwala.

10 Nayo muzelelo yuyo,

vikawa kwitang'anila no mucekulu yuyo,

nayo mucekulu yuyo,

yali yavwalile kotya mucekulu musenyha,

akuno yena no wiwikanize wakwe.

11 Yena meso makali,

yasina ne cevu,

yali yazoweye kujendajenda mu zinzila,

hamba

12 kwima ha cipeto,

yalindilize walume.

Na hanji mumo mu zinzila,

hamba mu zi sokoni.

13 Mucekulu yuyo,

yali yeza kwitang'anila no muzelelo yuyo mulele,

yakawa kumukumbatila muzelelo yuyo,

yakawa kumunonela,

akuno yamulanjile ku meso, no kumutya,

14 “Ane zuwali ndavya vilavywa,

holeka ku kaya yangu ko yili nyama ye makumbiko.

15 Holeka nane niwa kuza ku kuno kono nzila,

ho nghulondole.

Nali kusaka nghuwone,

kumbe nalu nakondya kukuwona!

16 Alu ku kaya yangu,

nakondya kutandika citanda cangu ne mitote ye mawala,

yono yilawaga ku Misili.

17 Nakondya kucimiziza citanda cangu vye mhunjile swanu,

hamonga no wugutika,

no wudi no mudalasini.

18 Holeka lu, kenhe cibite,

cikagone sunga wele mitondo hakali mitondo.

Ciyisangalazize mu wikumbatile wetu.

19 Hambi mulume wangu nha yakali yali hasina.

Yabita muhinzo wa kutali muno.

20 Yabita kulondola sawo,

na holeka si kowuya mbela,

sunga vipulute vilo yikumi na vine ho yawuye.”

21 Hodu mucekulu yuyo,

yakamukwega muzelelo yuyo kwe vitombo vyakwe,

akuno yakumupepeleza na nonga swanu muno.

22 Hanyuma hadodo,

nghawona muzelelo yuyo yeza kumutyatya mucekulu yuyo,

nha ng'ombe yono yikubita kuwulagwa,

hamba nzimba yono yikwibananjila mu mutego,

23 lono yakondya kugumwa,

ne songa yapuguza ko ku nhumbula kugati.

Na mbeka, muzelelo yuyo,

yali yehwanile nhe ndeje yono yikwitegula ku lwavu.

Hambi nha si yasuma kumanya vyono wumi wakwe wuli mu mbali.

24 Holeka wanangu, muhulicize 'ne.

Mutejeleze muno kuli zono nghulonga.

25 Mulecele lukulu kumuleceleza mucekulu we liwala lilyo,

yamusumenyi,

hamba mulece kumusejelela yaye.

26 Hambi nha yawagwisize wenji,

kanyhi yawambuze wanhu wenji wafwe.

27 Holeka hono mwijelele du mubite ku kaya yakwe,

ale, mowa mwenjila mu nzila yono yibitile ku yisi ye wafu.

Nayo yo nzila ye ndumula yo kufwa kamonga.
