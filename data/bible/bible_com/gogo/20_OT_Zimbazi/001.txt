Zimbazi 1

Zimbazi

1 Azi zo zimbazi za Solomona u mwana wa Dawudi, u mutemi wa Isilayeli.
2 Zo mbazi zono zosuma kukutaza wumanye mahala, no kutuga wulanjizwe wuswanu, no kumanya zinonga zono zina nhunizo muno muno.
3 Nazo zokwijiza nha vyono kosuma kwikala kwa mahala, no kuwa mwihuwilwa, kanyhi wuli ling'ani, hamonga no wuswanu.
4 Zokunoza wuwuce mu wulele, wuwe munhu mono kosuma kuwejiza wazelelo, ho wawe no wumanye.
5 Ne zimbazi zizi, zosuma konjeleza wumanye kuli wanhu we mahala, no kuwapela wutonji wa mu wumanye wawo,
6 ho wasume kuzimanya muno zinhunizo za mugati muno, ze zimbazi, hamonga ne vidalicizo vye wanhu wawo we mahala.

Mejizo kuli wazelelo

7 I cogopo co Muwaha Mulungu, wo wusimyo wo wumanye. Ninga i walele wabedegalaga mahala ne mafundo.

8 Mwanangu, wuhulice mafundo gono sogo

no nyina yako wakwijizaga wulece kugaleka.

9 Hambi mafundo gawo nha gokupela nyikala swanu.

Gowa nha mukumbo we lihewa,

mu mutwe wako,

hamba nho mukufu wa mu singo yako.

10 Mwanangu, wanyo wubi hono wakukwejeleze ko wusugu,

wulece kulumbila lukulu.

11 Hono watye,

“Ale cibitane cikamulondole munhu cimuwulaje!

Cibitane cikawahome wanhu wono wasina masutya,

wuwe nha wo mwidawalo wetu!

12 Nha vyono yinha, cowamela wakali womi nhe yisi ye wafu.

Kotya wakugwila mwi'kweme.

13 Nase cozilondola sawo zose ze wagoli,

ciwe kuzimemya zikaya zetu na vyono cowa caviteka!

14 Holeka wuze cilunze nase,

nase cogawana vinhu vyose vyono covihiza.”

15 Ninga 'ne nghukutya nhavi agwe mwanangu,

vyono, wulecele lukulu kwilunza ne wanhu we liwala lilyo,

hamba wulece kuwatyatya.

16 Hambi wene nha si walindilaga kuwituma zimbi,

nhondo zose wekalaga wewikanize so wuwulaji du.

17 Kanyhi wumanye vyono,

si viswanu kuyitega ndeje no lwavu,

lono nayo yikunuguliye yikukuwona,

18 hambi wanhu we liwala lilyo,

nha wategaga mutego,

kanyhi wawa kwigwiza weneco,

mu mutego wono wokwiwulaga wali wene.

19 Kanyhi, wumanye vyono wuhizi,

wusakaga wumi wo muhizi.

Ne nghani yiyi, yikuwa kumwijelela munhu wo wose,

mono kekalilaga muli zo wuwulaji.

Mahala gakucema

20 Alu wuhulicize!

Mahala gakucemeleza mu zinzila na mu zi sokoni,

21 gakucemeleza gehindile,

sunga na mu malango ge likaya,

sunga ne hanhu ho hose,

hono wanhu wendile kwihanguliza hamonga.

22 Gakucemeleza gakutya,

“Nye nyo wanhu walele!

Alu ho mojendelela kwikala muli walele sunga nili?

Alu si mosuma kwiyijiza hamba hadodo lo?

23 Ale lu, muhulicize lono nghumutacilenyi.

Hambi 'ne nha nghumulanjizenyi viswanu,

no kumugubulilenyi ze mahala gangu.

24 Nasola katali nghumucemelezenyi,

nghumusacenyi ho munyizize,

ninga nanye hamba kuhulika yaye.

25 Mwakondya kuwubedegala wulanjize wangu wose,

hamba si mwendile ne mafundo gangu.

26 Ninga nanye kolo mwenjila mu magazo,

nane nomusekanjenyi muno muno.

Kanyhi nomwizwajizenyi kolo zimbi zikumwaganenyi.

27 Kolo magazo gago gakumuzizenyi nho mukukunzo,

gawe kumuletelenyi libeho lyo wugaye,

nanye lono mowa muli mu wusungu no wukalale.

28 Ale baho nanye, ho mulagalondolela mahala,

ninga nane hamba si nolomutondowozenyi ng'o.

Nanye molonondola hanhu hose,

ninga hamba si molombona yaye.

29 Hambi nha mwawusula wumanye,

hamba kusaka nhondo zose kumuhulika Muwaha Mulungu yaye.

30 Kanyhi hamba si mwasacila lukulu kugabocela mafundo gangu,

hamba kugatununhicila bwete.

31 Holeka luno, zomwaganenyi zono ziwanojeye,

ne metumo genyu gomupelenyi mhungo.

32 Holeka i wanhu walele,

wofwila mu wulele wawo,

hambi nha si wakusaka mahala.

Vyono i wanhu wono wasina mahala,

nha wakuwa kongomezwa no wuwula mahala wawo.

33 Ninga munhu wo wose mono kohuliciza'ne,

yuyo kokwikala yapolile,

kowa mutulano,

hamba kowa yasina soko yo kogopa cinji co cose yaye.”
