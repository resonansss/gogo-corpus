Zimbazi 27

1 Wulece kwidunhumulila kuli zono kozinoza mitondo,

Hambi nha si wuzimanyile,

zono zokwijela haciligati ya lulu ne mitondo.

2 Kanyhi, wulece wanji wo wakukumye,

ninga wulece kwikumya wuli wene.

3 Libwe yizito, nawo musawawa muzito.

Ninga u munhu mulele mono yendile kusagahaza nghani,

muzito muno yacizize vivyo vyose.

4 Likuwo si litetelaga, kanyhi lihodangaga.

Ninga hamba nhavivyo,

si lyokwihwana no munhu we mvivi.

5 Ciba hono wumutacile munhu calinze,

si nha kumuleka yehwese vyono agwe si wumuceleleye.

6 No mwendece wako hamba yakutamise,

nagwe hamba si kowona vibi yaye.

Ninga hono mulugu wako yakujelece muwoko wakwe mwi'wega,

komutya, “Wuka 'gwe!”

7 Na hono wukali wigute, hamba wuci si kowusaka yaye.

Ninga kolo wadabuka, hamba ndigwa yiwe yili cisungu,

agwe koyiwona yili muzinzi du.

8 No munhu mono sikekalaga ha kaya yakwe yatule,

yehwanile ndeje yono yikuyumba-yumba ako na ako,

kutali ne linhuli lyayo.

9 Nago mafuta go muzinzi,

hamonga ne mhunjile swanu,

visangalazaga nhumbula.

Ninga mazozo, gahodangaga mulamu wa mu nhumbula.

10 Wulece kuwaleka weyendi wayago,

hamba weyendi wo sogo.

Na kanyhi hono wuwe wuli mu wugaye,

wulece kulondola wutazi kuli nduguyo.

Kanyhi wukumbucile nha vyono,

mwiyendi muyago mono mwikalane behi nayo,

yuyo yo kosuma kukutaza, si

nho nduguyo mono yekaye kutali nagwe.

11 Gwe mwanangu, weze ne mahala,

nane holeka nosangalala.

Nane nowa nina lipitulo,

kuli munhu wowose mono kohosa 'ne.

12 Ne wanhu wo wizuce, wawonaga zimbali zikuwaziza,

hodu, wawa kwilega.

Ninga u munhu mono yasina mahweso,

mwene kokwiyinjiza,

ninga hanyuma kanyhi yawe kukalala.

13 No munhu mono kowa mulele,

sunga yehuwize nha vyono komulipila u mujenzi migawa yakwe,

ayo yuyo yimunojeye sawo yakwe yisolwe,

yipinjikwe so migawa yiyo.

14 Na mono yendile kwilawa no kumwinusa mwendece wakwe

ne 'zi yikali muno muno,

wuwo wowalwa nha wulijite kuli mwene.

15 No mugolece mono yali zozo,

yehwanile nhe mvula ye lihwela.

16 Alu kosuma kumunyamaza na nzila ci?

Wule, hono wukulanga,

wujelile ne 'zuwa linji kuyimila mbeho,

hamba kufumbata mafuta mu muwoko wako?

17 Ninga nase co wanhu,

ciyijizizaga kuli wanhu wayetu,

nha vyono cuma cinolaga cuma ciyagwe.

18 Na hono wuwukamise mukuyu wako,

holeka kolya ne mana gayo.

No mwitumami nayo,

hono yamulanjilile viswanu muwaha wakwe,

ale, nayo kotuga lilumbo.

19 Nha vyono kusumaga kwiwona wusu wako mu malenga,

lono wukugalanga.

Ale, nhavivyo nagwe,

vyo kosuma kwiwona mu nhumbula yako go mweneco.

20 No wusulumizi wo munhu,

wihwanile ne yinze ye wafu, yono si yigutaga.

21 Lilumbo lyo munhu, nalyo likuwa kujelwa,

nha vyono moto wujelaga zahabu na fweza.

22 Hamba wumutowe mulele,

sunga yawe yali behi no kufwa,

ninga si kosuma kuwutowa wulele wakwe sunga wumuwucile.

23 Wuhidimbale kuzidima ngholo ne zing'ombe zako,

ko wulanjilizi muno.

24 Hambi sawo, nha si yikalaga cibitilila.

Hamba makungugo du nago,

si gekalaga cibitilila.

25 Na lono wukutema mahanze go kunyaza so ng'ombe,

kanyhi mu cipango cico,

weze kutema ne mahanze ga mu vigongo,

lono wukali wulindiye mahanze kanyhi gawiluce.

26 Nagwe kosuma kwinozeza myenda,

mu wulawa mu wukunzeje we zingholo zako.

Na kosuma kugula mugunda,

ne zipesa zono zolawa mu wuguzi we zimhene zako zinji.

27 Ne zimhene zono zosigala,

kowa kuzikama mele,

gono gomukwilenyi nyenye wose,

mu mhuga yako,

hamonga ne wetumami wako we cicekulu.
