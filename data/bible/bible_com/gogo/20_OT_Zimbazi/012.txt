Zimbazi 12

1 Munhu wo wose mono yendile wumanye,

yuyo kendaga no kulanjizwa lono yasutya.

Kanyhi yo nghani ya wulele,

munhu mono si yendile kulanjizwa.

2 Muwaha Mulungu kendezwaga ne wanhu waswanu.

Ninga kawahosaga i wehwesa zimbi.

3 Wubi si wumusikamyaga munhu yaye.

Ninga i we ling'ani,

wekalaga wasikame.

4 U mucekulu musindile,

lye 'lumbo ne nyemo kuli mulume wakwe,

ninga mucekulu mono kamukozaga cevu u mulume wakwe,

yehwanile lilonda libi muno,

mu mafupa gakwe.

5 I weling'ani, wokutendela viswanu.

Ninga i wabi, wosaka wakukonje du.

6 Ne zinghani ze wanhu wabi, ziwulagaga.

Ninga zinghani za we ling'ani,

ziwaponyaga wono wakuzibocela.

7 Ne wanhu wabi wafwizaga wakuleka wasina wana.

Ninga kaya ya we ling'ani, yokwikala.

8 Hono wuwe wina mahala, wanhu wokulumba.

Ninga hono wuwe wuli mulele, wanhu wokubedegala.

9 Yili ciba wuwe nha vyono winha,

wukwiyitujila ndigwa mu milimo yako.

Si nho kwinoza nha wuli munhu we lilumbo,

akuno ho wuli nzala.

10 No munhu muswanu,

kazidimaga viswanu mitugo zakwe.

Ninga i wanhu wabi,

wali jila ku zi mitugo zawo.

11 No munhu muhidimbazi kulima,

kekalaga yena ndigwa.

Ninga kwajiliza vipango so nghani zono zisina cambuzi,

yiyo yo nghani ya wulele du.

12 Ne wanhu wabi,

wose walondolaga zo wituma makani mabi du.

Ninga we ling'ani wekalaga wasikame.

13 No munhu mubi kegwizizaga mu zinghani zakwe mweneco.

Ninga we ling'ani kokwiponya mu wugaye.

14 Ne cihumbilizwa cako,

cowa cili mu nghango ya zono kuzilongaga,

na zono kwitumaga.

Kopegwa vyono yikunojeye.

15 Ne wanhu walele,

nhondo zose wene wehwesaga nha vyono wali ling'ani.

Ninga we mahala wahulicizaga kuli zo wulanjizwe.

16 Likuwo lyo mulele kalilajilaga calinze.

Ninga we mahala,

si wendaga kwikoza cevu yaye.

17 Na lono wukulonga ze mbeka,

ling'ani yikunozwa.

Ninga muhomelezi wo wulambi,

kayajilizaga ling'ani.

18 Ne zinghani ze caka,

zikuwa kucinya nho mumaje.

Ninga zinghani zo munhu we mahala, ziponyaga.

19 No munhu mulambi,

kekalaga vilo vicefu mono'nze.

Ninga munhu mono kalongaga ze mbeka,

kekalaga cibitilila.

20 Wukoci wuli mu zinhumbula za wono wehwesaga zimbi,

ninga kuli wono wetumaga ku so ziswanu, wowona nyemo.

21 Kanyhi hasina cinji cibi

cono cowaziza i wanhu we ling'ani.

Ninga i wabi, wambulilaga magayo du.

22 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yawasulile i wanhu walambi.

Ninga kendezwaga na wono wetumaga ze mbeka.

23 I wanhu we mahala,

wahundikaga zono wazimanyile.

Ninga i walele,

wanzalikaga zo wulele wawo.

24 No wituma milimo ko wupiligazi,

wokupela wutule.

Ninga wudoba, wokunoza wuwe muwanda.

25 Ne mahwele,

gosuma kukuwusiza nyemo yako.

Ninga nghani swanu,

zosuma kukusangalaza.

26 U munhu we ling'ani,

yo kawezaga mutonji wo muyagwe.

Ninga zinzila zo munhu mubi,

ziwatongaga ku wajilile.

27 Nagwe hono wuwe mudoba,

hamba si kosuma kwiwutila vya kumwande.

Ninga hono wuwe wuli mupiligazi mu milimo,

kosuma kwiwutila vya kumwande.

28 U wiling'ano, yo nzila yo wumi.

Ninga wubi, yo nzila ye lifwa.
