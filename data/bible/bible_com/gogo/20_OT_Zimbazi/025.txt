Zimbazi 25

Mbazi zinji kanyhi za Solomona

1 Azino nazo zo mbazi za Solomona,

zono munhu yunji yazisolela mu kaya ya Hezekiya,

u mutemi wa Yuda.

2 Cikumutogoza Mulungu,

so zono kazigubicizaga,

cikuwatogoza watemi,

so zose zono wazilolosaga.

3 Nagwe hamba si kozimanya,

zono mutemi yakwihwesa.

Hambi mahweso gakwe, nha gacicizize'se,

gehwanile no wulanga vyono wuli kutali nase,

hamba cisolo ce lilenga.

4 Wuwuse makwe gawuce mu fweza,

holeka nayo munghula konoza cinhu ciswanu.

5 Wawusaje i wabi ho mutemi holeka no wutemi,

wakwe womanyika so ling'ani yayo.

6 Nagwe kolo wema ha mwande yo mutemi,

wulece kujela kwiyendeceza hali mutemi,

no kwinoza nha wuli munhu we lilumbo.

7 Ciba wulindile sunga wuwikwe hanhu he lilumbo,

si nha kulonjelwa, wuwuce hono wikaye,

wumutelele yunji mono yena lilumbo.

8 Na hono wuwone nghani,

wulece kubita mbela mbela mu mahakama so nghani yiyo.

Ku soko hono yaze muhomelezi yunji yahomelele vyono zizo si za mbeka,

alu 'gwe ho konozaci?

9 Na hono wuwe wina nghani no mwikalani muyago,

muzimalaje nyenye du muli wejete,

ninga wulece kuzigubula ze cinyele za yunji.

10 Na hono nhavivyo, wose womanya vyono agwe wuli 'hede,

si kuhundikaga ze cinyele.

No wubi wuwo, si wokuwucila yaye.

11 Ne nghani yono yikulonjelwa hono hanojeye,

yihwanile ne mikalo ye zahabu mu nhoto za fweza.

12 No wutacile wono wukulavywa no munhu we mahala,

kuli munhu mono yendile kuhuliciza,

wuswanu, wucizize nyamwidole za zahabu,

hamba vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu,

vyono vinozigwe ne zahabu nengu.

13 U mwitumami mwihuwilwa,

kamwendezaga mono yakumutuma,

nhe malenga ge mbeho, mu madaha ge nhwanga,

mwi'zuwa likali.

14 Ne wanhu wono wehuwizaga vinhu ko wulambi,

wehwanile mavunde ne mbeho, vyono visina mvula.

15 Zinghani ze ngola,

ziwusaga wiwanganile we vilungo no kutuza watemi.

16 Wulece kukomba wuci winji kuciza,

hono wucizilize, kodeka.

17 Wulece kubita ku kaya yo mwikalani muyago kali kenji.

Hambi nha lyowa yikataza kuli mwene,

na hanyuma kokusula.

18 Na mono yakumubalalila muyagwe,

yuyo muwulaji, yenha mumaje,

ne nhumbi, hamba songa nghali.

19 Hono wumwitumbile munhu mono si mwihuwilwa,

mu vilo vyo wugaye,

yowa nha munhu mono yakujela kutafunya ndigwa ne lino lyono libenece,

hamba kujenda no mugulu wono wutinghuce.

20 Na hono wumwimbile zinyimbo munhu mono yena wukalale,

yowa nha wukumuvula myenda yakwe,

mwi'zuwa lyono lina mbeho nghali,

hamba kotya nho kuwukusuwa mwino mu nghomele.

21 Na hono mulugu wako yawe yadabuce, wumulisaje.

Na hono yakaluce, mupelaje malenga yang'we.

22 Na hono wumutendele zizo,

komukoza cevu muno.

Nagwe Muwaha Mulungu kokuhumbiliza.

23 Maheho, galetaga makuwo,

nha vyono mbeho ya kusumuka yiletaga mvula.

24 Yili ciba kwikala kunze mwi'tembe mucanyha,

si nho kuwa mu kaya monga,

no mucekulu mono yali zozo.

25 No wuhulika nghani swanu,

ku wulawa ku yisi yo kutali,

yihwanile no kung'wa malenga ge zizizi,

lono wukaluce.

26 No munhu muswanu lono yakuhanga no munhu mubi,

yakwihwana no munhu we ling'ani,

mono yakuwuya ne nyuma no kuhanga ne wabi,

kawa nho muvwilo wono wanhu watibuye ne magulu,

hamba cisima cono civizice.

27 Si viswanu kuli'gwe kulya wuci winji.

Weze no wiyimile mu wituma nghani.

28 Na hono wulece kusuma kulimila likuwo lyako,

kokwihwana ne likaya lyono lisina magomawakali,

nalyo liwezaga yipelu no kuhomwa.
