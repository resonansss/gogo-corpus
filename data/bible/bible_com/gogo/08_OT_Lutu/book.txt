Lutu 1

Elimeleki ne mhuga yakwe wakubita ku Mowabu

1 Baho katali, mu vilo vyono Waisilayeli wali wakali siwanawa na mutemi, kwali koyili nzala mu'si. No munhu mono yacemagwa Elimeleki, mono yali we mhuga ya Efulata, mono yekalaga Betelehemu mu Yuda,
2 yakabita hamonga no mugolece wakwe Nawomi ne wana wawo wejete Maloni na Ciliyoni, yekale mu yisi ya Mowabu. Na lono wekalaga kuko,
3 Elimeleki yakafwa, Nawomi yakalekwa yali wene hamonga ne wanagwe wejete,
4 Nawo wa Maloni na Ciliyoni wakawa kutola i wahinza we Cimowabu, Olipa na Lutu. Kotya myaka yikumi yali yeza kupuluta,
5 Maloni na Ciliyoni nawo wakawa kufwa, Nawomi yakalekwa yali wene yasina mulume hamba wana wa cilume.

Nawomi na Lutu wakuwuya ku Betelehemu

6 Hanyuma yayo, Nawomi yakahulika vyono Muwaha Mulungu yawamotya i wanhu wakwe ko kuwapela nhwanga mbaha; hobaho yakawa kwiwikaniza ho yawuce ku Mowabu hamonga ne wakwewe.
7 Wakawuka hamonga wawuye ku yisi ya Yuda. Na lono wali wali mu nzila,
8 Nawomi yakawatya wakwewe, “Nye wanangu, muwuye kwenyu, mukekale hamonga ne wanyina zenyu. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu kowa muswanu kuli'nye, nha vyono anye mwali waswanu kuli'ne na kuli wawo wono wakondya kufwa.
9 No Muwaha Mulungu yamutazenyi, mutolwe kanyhi mutuje nyumba.” Baho Nawomi yakawa kuwanonela no kuwelaga. Ninga wene wakanza kulila.
10 Wakamutya, “Ng'o! Ase langa, cobitana nagwe du kuli wanhu wako.”
11 Nawomi yakawapitula. Yakawatya, “Yinojeye muwuye kwenyu wanangu.” hawule mukusaka cibitane hamonga nane? Mukwihwesa ane, nosuma kulela kanyhi wana wanji we cilume, wono womutolenyi?
12 Holeka muwuye ku zikaya zenyu wanangu, hambi ane nha neza nili mukombi, si nosuma kutolwa kanyhi. Hamba nali nitye kowuli witumbilo, vyono hono nholwe ne cilo cici notuga wana,
13 wule, mwali muwalindile sunga wawiluce? Yiyi yimwimilenyi ho mulece kutolwa na yunji? Ng'o wanangu, mumanyile nha vyono siyosumika. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha kamhitucila, ane nane nghumuwonelenyi lusungu wanangu.

14 Kanyhi wene wakanza kulila. Ninga Olipa yakawa kumunonela mukwewe, yakawa kuwuya ku kaya, ninga Lutu, vikekalana no mukwewe.
15 Nayo, Nawomi yakamutya, “Lutu, lanje ta mutozi muyago, yawawuyila i wanhu wakwe, na ko mulungu wakwe. Nagwe le, muwuyane wose ku kaya.”

16 Ninga Lutu yakamupitula Nawomi, yakamutya,

“Gwe yaya! Wulece kuneneleza 'ne ho nghulece!

Wundekaje 'ne cibitane na gwegwe.

Kono kobita, nane nobita;

na kono kokwikala, nane nokwikala.

Ne wanhu wako, nane wowa wanhu wangu,

no Mulungu wako nane kowa Mulungu wangu.

17 Na ho hose hono kofwila nane ho nofwila,

no kuzikwa baho,

no Muwaha Mulungu yanhendele nhavivyo no kuciza,

langa lifwa du lyo lyolocitemanula nagwe.”

18 Nawomi yali yeza kuwona vyono Lutu yendile wabitane, hodu nayo yakawa kunyamala.
19 Wakawa kujendelela no muhinzo wose vyono wejete, sunga wakafika ku Betelehemu. Nawo lono waficililaga, mwi'kaya lya Betelehemu, likaya lyose likenjila sasamo mbaha, ne wacekulu wakalonga wakutya, “Mwene 'nye, yuyu mbeka yo Nawomi?”

20 Nayo Nawomi yakawatya, “Mulece nyhema Nawomi, langa munyhemaje nene Mala, (nhunizo yayo cinhu cono cina cisungu,) hambi u Mulungu Mudaha Vyose mono yali vilungo, nha kayinoza mikalile yangu yiwe ya wusungu sungu du.
21 Hambi lono nawukaga baha, nha nali nina vyose, ninga Muwaha Mulungu, yangomola luno nili bwete. Holeka socici munyheme nene Nawomi, lono Muwaha Mulungu Mudaha Vyose, yakondya kuning'aniza nha nili musutyi, no kumhela magayo?”
22 Nhavivi vyo Nawomi yawuya ku wulawa Mowabu hamonga na Lutu, mukwewe u Mumowabu. Wakafika mu Betelehemu, mu mwamulizo we ndudula yo wuhemba.


Lutu 2

Lutu yakwituma mu mugunda wa Bowazi

1 Nawomi yali yena nduguye mono yacemwaga Bowazi, munhu mugoli kanyhi munhu muwaha, mono yali we mhuga yo mulume wakwe Elimeleki.
2 Ale, mwi'zuwa linji, Lutu yakamulonjela Nawomi yakatya, “Lece 'ne mbite ku migunda nghehalalize wuhemba wono wakuwusigaza lono wakududula. Manyile nha vyono notuga mono komvumila nitume hamonga nayo.” Nayo Nawomi yakamupitula yakamutya, “Ale, wubite mwanangu.”
3 Lutu yakawa kubita ku migunda, yakawa yakutyatya mu nyuma ye waduduzi, no kuhalaliza wuhemba wono wasigazaga. Yikejela vyono kumbe yali yali mu mugunda wono wali wa Bowazi mono yali we mhuga ya Elimeleki.

4 Hanyuma yayo Bowazi mweneco yakaza ku mugunda ku wulawa ku Betelehemu yakawalamusa i waduduzi. Yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu yeze muli nyenye.” Nawo wakamutya, “Muwaha Mulungu nagwe yakulugute.”
5 Bowazi yakamuwuza mulanjilizi we waduduzi yakatya, “Alu yuyu muhinza mwana nani?”
6 Mulanjilizi yakapitula yakatya, “Ayuno muhinza, mujenzi, mono yazize ku wulawa Mowabu hamonga na Nawomi.
7 Kanyitya nimulece yawatyatye mu nyuma i waduduzi, yahalaliza wuhemba. Kaswa yakwituma ku wulawa hakali mitondo, alu yacehile du hadodo mu cibanda.”

8 Baho Bowazi yakamulonjela Lutu yakatya, “Hulicize mwanangu, wulece kanyhi kwihalaliza wuhemba mu migunda yinji, langa mu mugunda wuwu du. Witumaje baha, hamonga ne wetumami wangu wawa we cicekulu,
9 na lono wukuza mono mugunda weze kwamula kulanga, wuwone kono we cicekulu wakududula. Nagwe weze wuli hamonga du ne wahinza wawa. Nane nakondya kuwalajiliza wawo we cilume walece kukugaza. Na hono wuwone nyhilu, wubite ku viya wukang'we malenga gono wanejile.”
10 Lutu yakenamya wusu wakwe ho hasi, yakamutya Bowazi, “Alo nhawule wukwigaza ku soko yangu? Hamba nhawule wendile kuwanozeza viswanu wajenzi?”
11 Nayo Bowazi yakamutya Lutu, “Ane nakondya kuzihulika zose ziswanu zono wetuma kuli mukwewo Nawomi, ku wulawa lono mulume wako yafwila. Kanyhi manyile na vyono, wawaleka u sogo no nyina yako ne yisi yako, na vyono waza kwikala muli wanhu wono wali si wuwamanyile yaye.
12 Holeka Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli, yakumotye so zizo zono wetuma. Kanyhi wutuje cihumbilizwa cono cimemeleceye, ku wulawa kuli yuyo Muwaha Mulungu we Waisilayeli, yuyo mono wumuzize ho yakukamise.”

13 Nayo Lutu yakamupitula Bowazi yakamutya, “Gwe muwaha, kuhonjela muno, kuli zono wunhedeye, no mwitumami wako. Hambi nha wanoza nane nihulice viswanu, vyono kulonga nane, kwa wupolo, hamba nha yiwe ane si nihwanile na yunji wa muli wetumami wako.”
14 Na mu cipango co kulya zindigwa, Bowazi yakamutya Lutu, “Wuze nagwe wusole nhonje wulilize mu divayi.” Hodu Lutu yakawa kwikala hasi hamonga ne waduduzi, nayo Bowazi yakawa kumupela mbonho zo wuhemba. Nayo Lutu yakalya sunga yakeguta, no kusigaza ndigwa zinji.
15 Nayo yali yeza kwinuka ho yabite yakahalalize wuhemba. Bowazi yakawalajiliza wetumami wakwe yakawatya, “Mumulekaje Lutu yahalale sunga na mu madulu, mulece kumwimila.
16 Hamonga na zizo, mucepule wunji ku wulawa mu madulu muwulece, nayo yetondoleze.”

17 “Nayo Lutu yakawa kujendelela no wihalalizi wakwe wo wuhemba mu mugunda, sunga likaswa. Nayo yali yeza kuwutwanga wono yahalaye, yakatuga kilo yikumi limonga.”
18 Yakawutegula wuhemba ku kaya yakamulajila mukwewe vyono yahalaye. Nayo Nawomi yakawa kumupela Lutu wukoko wono mwene Nawomi kasigaza mu ndigwa ye misi.
19 Hanyuma, Nawomi yakamuwuza Lutu yakatya, “Alo zuwali kwihi kono wuwuhalaye wuhemba wose wuwu? Hamba wali mugunda wa nani, mono wali kwihalaliza? Mulungu yamumotye munhu yuyo mono yendezwe nagwe. Nayo, Lutu yakawa kumulonjela Nawomi, nha vyono yali kuhalala mu mugunda wa munhu mono yakucemwa Bowazi.”
20 Nayo Nawomi yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu yamumotye Bowazi!” Muwaha Mulungu mono kawukamisaga wihuwizo wakwe kuli womi na kuli wono wafwiye. Yakajendelela kanyhi kutya, “Munhu yuyo ndugu yetu, kanyhi yo munhu muyagwe na wono wena ling'ani yo wuhale.”

21 Nayo Lutu u Mumowabu yakatya, “Na yono swanu kuciza zose, kanonjela katya, neze kuhalala wuhemba mumo du mu mugunda wakwe, hamonga ne waduduzi wakwe, sunga lono walamalila ndudula.”
22 Nawomi yakamutya Lutu. “Heya mwanangu, yowa viswanu kuli'gwe wuwe kwituma hamonga ne wetumami we cicekulu wono wakwituma mu mugunda wa Bowazi. Hambi nha manyile vyono, hono wubite wukahalale mu mugunda wa munhu yunji, munya mugunda wuwo, koswa yakukugaza bwete.”
23 Nayo Lutu yakajendelela kwituma hamonga nawo no kuhalala wuhemba, sunga ndudula yose yo wuhemba yikawa yamalika. Nayo Lutu yakajendelela du kwikala no mukwewe.


Lutu 3

Lutu yakutolwa

1 Hanyuma Nawomi yakamutya Lutu, “Mwanangu, ane nghuwona vyono yowa viswanu, hono nghulondolele mulume yakutole, ho wutuje nyumba yako go mweneco.
2 Wukumbucile vyono munhu ayu Bowazi, mono mwetumanaga hamonga ne wetumami wakwe we cicekulu mu mugunda, ase ndugu yetu. Alu hulicize, cinyamihe cici, mwene kowa kwela wuhemba.
3 Holeka wihovugaje no kwibaka mafuta ge mhunjile swanu, wuvwale ne vivwalo vyako viswanu. Hanyuma wubite kuko ku cuga kono yakwelela wuhemba. Ninga mwene yalece manya vyono nagwe wuli baho, sunga kolo yamala kulya no kung'wa.
4 Wumulanjililaje hono yakubita kugona, na kolo yakondya kugona nhongo, nagwe wubite wukesosomece mu mwenda wakwe wo kwifunika ku lubavu lwa ku migulu yakwe, wuwe kugona baho. Nayo mwene kokulonjela cono wunoze.”
5 Lutu yakamupitula Nawomi yakatya, “Ale viswanu yaya, ane nokwituma zose nha vyono wunonjeye.”

6 Hobaho, Lutu yakawa kubita ku cuga no kwituma zose zono mukwewe yamulonjela.
7 Bowazi yaza kondya kulya no kung'wa yakawa na nyemo mu nhumbula yakwe. Yakawa kubita he 'lundo lyo wuhemba no kugona nhongo. Nayo Lutu yakawa kubita mholamhola, yakawa kuwugubula mwenda yakawa kwisosomeka no kugona ha magulu ga Bowazi.
8 Na mucilo mugati, Bowazi yakenuka nhinicizo, no kupituka, yakezina muno kumuwona munhu wa cicekulu yagonile baho.
9 Yakamuwuza yakatya, “Alu gwegwe nani?” Nayo Lutu yakapitula yakatya, “Gwe muwaha wangu, nene Lutu no mwitumami wako. Hambi nha yo gwegwe mono wuli ndugu ye behi, kanyhi mono wudahile kundima. Holeka nghukunena wuhalaje.”

10 Nayo Bowazi yakamutya, “Ale Muwaha Mulungu yakumotye mwanangu. Hambi nha wukulajila cogopo ciwaha ca mu mhuga yenyu, muli zono wukwituma aluno, kuciza zono wetuma kuli mukwewo Nawomi. Hamba nhavivyo, yali yinojeye wubite wukamulondole mulume muzelelo, hamba yawe mugoli hamba muciwa. Ninga agwe vyo si wunozize nhavivyo.
11 Holeka Lutu, wulece kwihwelela. Nane nokunozeza zose zono wukuhila, hambi wanhu wose wa mwi'kaya, nha wakumanyile vyono wuli mucekulu muswanu.
12 Mbeka ane nili ndugu ye behi, na kanyhi ndahile kukudima. Ninga koyali munhu yunji, we ndugu ye behi, kuciza ane.
13 Holeka wugone baha sunga wele, nane kolo wela, ho nowona hono munhu yuyo kosuma kukudima hamba ng'o. Na hono yiwe vyo yinha, ale yowa nghani swanu, na hono yilece kusumika, ale hodu. Nane nghwilahiza hali Muwaha Mulungu mumi, nha vyono nene nokudima.”

14 Hodu Lutu yakawa kugona baho ha magulu ga Bowazi. Ninga yakelawa kwinuka lono kukali kutitu titu, ho yalece kuwonwa na yunji, vyono Bowazi si yasaka munhu wo wose yamanye vyono Lutu yali yali baho.
15 Bowazi yakamutya Lutu, “Ale wulete mwenda wako wono wuvwalile wuwibate mu mawoko.” Nayo Lutu yakawa kuwibata, Bowazi yakawa kulunda kilo makumi mejete go wuhemba mu mwenda, yakawa kumutwika Lutu mu mutwe. Yakawa kubita nawo ku kaya kwawo.
16 Yali yeza kufika ku kaya, umukwewe Nawomi yakamuwuza yakutya, “Alo mwanangu, wuhemba kuwusola hayi?” Nayo Lutu yakawa kumuloloseza mukwewe zose zono Bowazi yamunozeze.
17 Kanyhi, “Kanyitya, ‘Si yowa viswanu mbuye kuli'gwe akuno sina cinji, holeka kawa kumhela no wuhemba wuwu wose kilo makumi mejete nghuletele.’ ”
18 Nayo Nawomi yakamutya, “Alu mwanangu Lutu, weze no wukangalile, sunga wuziwone zizi zose vyono zowa. Manyile vyono Bowazi sikoceha zuwali, sunga yayimale nghani yiyi.”


Lutu 4

Bowazi yakumutola Lutu

1 Bowazi yakawa kubita kwi'kaya, yakawa kwikala ha mulango we hanhu hono wetang'anililaga mu mikung'ano. Hanyuma u nduguye we behi muno wa Elimeleki, mono Bowazi yakawa yamutambula, nayo yakawa yakukola baho, nayo Bowazi yakawa kumucema, yakutya, “Gwe muyangu wuze ahano, mwendece wangu wikale hasi ta hadodo.” Yakawa kubita no kwikala.
2 Hobaho Bowazi yakasola wanhu yikumi wawaha wa mu 'kaya no kuwatya waze wehangulize wose baho. Nawo lono wali wekala,
3 mwene Bowazi yakawa kumulonjela nduguye yakatya, “Alu, Nawomi yawuya ku wulawa Mowabu, yakusaka kuguza mugunda wono wali we ndugu yetu Elimeleki,
4 nane nghwihwesa vyono, yikunojeye wuzimanye zizi. Holeka aluno hono wukuwusaka, wuwugulaje ha mwande ye wanhu awa wono wali bahano. Ninga hono wuwe si wukuwusaka, ale, wulonje, ku soko ling'ani yo kuwugula yikunojeye gwegwe wuwe mulongozi wo kuwugula, na hanyuma ho lwangu.” Nayo munhu yu yulya yakatya, “Nowugula nene.”

5 Bowazi yakamutya, “Ale viswanu! Ninga hono kuwugula mugunda wuwo ku wulawa kwa Nawomi, wumanye vyono wukumuguliliza na Lutu u mu Mowabu mufwile, no mugunda wikale mu mhuga yo munhu mono yafwiye.”
6 Nayo munhu yuyo yakapitula yakatya, “Hono yili nhavivyo, nokulecela ling'ani yangu yo kuwugula mugunda, ku soko yikwijela vyono iwanangu, si wowuhala mugunda wuwo. Holeka ciba wuwugule go mweneco.”
7 Mu vilo vye katali vye Waisilayeli, lono munhu yaguzaga, hamba kukawaniza sawo, awu wali wo moto kuli yuyo mono yaguzaga, yasolaga lilatu lyakwe, no kumupela mono yakugula, na mu nzila yiyi muli Isilayeli yalajilaga nha vyono nghani yimalika.

8 No munhu yuyo yaza kuwa yalonga na Bowazi, yakamutya, “Wugulaje gwegwe.” Hobaho yakasola lilatu lyakwe, no kumupela Bowazi.
9 Baho Bowazi yakawalonjela i wawaha na wose wono wali wali baho yakatya, “Anye wose muli wahomelezi zuwali, vyono ane nghondya kuvigula ku wulawa kuli Nawomi vinhu vyose vyono vyali vya Elimeleki hamonga na vye wanagwe Ciliyoni na Maloni.”
10 Kanyhi na mu wityatye wo moto wetu, Lutu u mu Mowabu, u mulekwa na Maloni, kowa mugolece wangu. Na mu nzila yiyi, sawo yokwikala mu mawoko ge mhuga yo munhu mono yafwiye, no wityatye wo lulelo lwe mhuga yakwe, lojendelela muli wanhu wakwe, na mwi' kaya lyakwe. Nanye yo nyenye wahomelezi muli zizi izuwali.
11 Nawo wawaha, hamonga ne wanhu wose wakatya, “Heya, ase cili wahomelezi. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yamunoze mucekulu wako yawe nha Laheli na Leya, wono walela wana wenji kuli Yakobo, nagwe wuwe mugoli mu lulelo lwa Efulata, no kuwa munhu muwaha mu Betelehemu.
12 Ne wana wono Muwaha Mulungu kokupela agwe kuli muhinza yuyu, woyinoza mhuga yako yiwe nha mhuga ya Pelezi, mwana wa Yuda na Tamali.”

Bowazi no lulelo lwakwe

13 Hodu, Bowazi yakamusola Lutu no kumutegula ku kaya yakwe yakawa mugolece wakwe. Muwaha Mulungu yakamumotya, nayo yakadinghula nda, yakalela mwana wa cilume.
14 Nayo Lutu yakamutya Nawomi, “Yalumbwe Muwaha Mulungu mono yakupelile mwizukulu zuwali mono kokudima agwe. No muzelelo yuyu, yeze yamanyice muli Isilayeli.
15 No mukwewo yakwendile muno, kanyhi yakondya kukwitumila zinyinji kuciza wana we cilume mupunghati. Nalu yakupela mwizukulu mono koleta mikalile mipya no kukudima mu wukombi wako.”
16 Nawomi yakamusola u mwana no kumupagata, yakawa yo mukoci wakwe.

17 Nawo i wacekulu wekalani wayagwe, wakamucemela umwana litagwa lya Obedi. Nawo wakawa kuwalonjela wanhu wose nha vyono lu, u mwana we cilume kalelwa kuli Nawomi. Obedi yakawa yo sogo wa Yese mono yali sogo wa Dawudi.
18 Ayi yo mhuga yo lulelo lono lulawiliye kuli Pelezi sunga kuli Dawudi; wawo wo wana wa Pelezi.
19 Pelezi yakamulela Hezeloni, Hezeloni yakamulela Lamu,
20 Lamu yakamulela Aminadabu; Aminadabu yakamulela Nasoni,
21 Nasoni yakamulela Salimona, Salimona yakamulela Bowazi; Bowazi yakamulela Obedi;
22 Obedi yakamulela Yese; Yese yakamulela Dawudi.
