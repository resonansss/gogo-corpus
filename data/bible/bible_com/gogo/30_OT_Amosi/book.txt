Amosi 1

U muwizi Amosi

1 Azi zo nghani za Amosi, u mudimi munya kwi kaya lya Tekowa. Myaka myejete lono litulo lya mu'si lyali likali si naza, mu vilo vya Uziya lono yali mutemi wa Yuda, na lono Yelobowamu mwana wa Yowashi yali mutemi wa Isilayeli.
2 Amosi yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu yakukoloma kuwulawa kwitunda lya Sayuni, ne lizi lyakwe wulawa ku Yelusalemi. No wudimo weza musina, ne lihanze mwitunda lye kalimeli lyanyala.”

Wuling'anizi wo Mulungu kuli makaya gono gali gali behi behi na Isilayeli

Damesiki

3 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Wanhu wa Damesiki wetuma wubi kali kenji, na ku soko yiyo nane nowalipiciza. Hambi nha wawatendela vibi no kuwatamisa muno muno wanhu wa Gileyadi.
4 Holeka nane nolipembela moto likaya lyo wutemi, lyono mutemi Hazayeli yazenga, kanyhi nogakongoga makaya go mutemi Benehadadi.
5 Nogabenanjiza ne malango ge likaya lya Damesiki no kuwawusa wono wekaye mwi'lolo lya Aveni, hamonga no mutemi wa Betedeni. Ne wanhu wa Siliya, wolosolwa nha wafungwa sunga ko ku yisi ya Kili.”

Filisiti

6 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Iwanhu wa Gaza, wetuma wubi muno muno. Na ku soko yiyo, nane nowalipiciza wene. Ku soko wene wayisola nghungugo yose, no kuwahamizizya kuli wanhu wa Edomu wawe wawanda.
7 Holeka notegula moto nigatakamize majinji ge likaya lye Gaza no kugatakamiza makaya gayo.
8 Nowawusa wono wekaye mu makaya ga Ashidodi na Ashikeloni, hamonga ne watemi wawo. Kanyhi nolilipiciza likaya lya Ekiloni, ne wafilisiti wose wono wasigala, wofwa.”

Tulo

9 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Iwanhu wa Tulo, nawo wetuma wubi kali kenji. Na ku soko yiyo, nane nowalipiciza. Hambi nha wayisola nghungugo yose no kuyihamizizya ku yisi ya Edomu wawe wawanda, hamba siwacikumbucila ne cilagane ce ndugu cono wacinoza.
10 Holeka nane notegula moto mu majinji ge likaya lya Tulo, no kugatakamiza makaya gayo.”

Edomu

11 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Wanhu wa Edomu, wetuma wubi kali kenji. Na ku soko yiyo, nane nowalipiciza. Hambi nha wawatendela vibi wandugu zawo, Waisilayeli, hamba kulajila lusungu yaye. Ne likuwo lyawo, likawa vyo lijendeleye du lisina wumalice yaye.
12 Holeka nane notegula moto mwi'kaya lya Temani, no kugatakamiza makaya ga Bozila.”

Amoni

13 Muwaha yakutya, “Wanhu wa Amoni, wetuma wubi kali kenji, na ku soko yiyo, nane nowalipiciza. Hambi mu wulugu wawo, nha wawatumbulaga i wacekulu we mida, so wusaka kugazimhya mipaka ye zi'si zawo.
14 Holeka nane notegula moto mu majinji ge Likaya lya Laba no kugatakamiza zikaya, makaya gayo. Na kolowa kolili izozo mwi'zuwa lilyo lya wulugu, no wihomi wolowa wuwaha, kotya mukukunzo we livulavumbi.
15 U mutemi we Amoni hamonga ne wawaha, wolohamywa no kutegulwa kutali.”


Amosi 2

Mowabu

1 Muwaha yakutya, “Wanhu wa Mowabu, wetuma wubi kali kenji, na ku soko yiyo, nane nowalipiciza. Hambi nha si wali na cogopo kuli mafupa go mutemi wa Edomu, wene kwa kuganyanya wagatakamiza sunga gakawa mavu.
2 Nane notegula moto mu yisi ya Mowabu, no kugatakamiza makaya ga keliyoti. Ne wanhu wa Mowabu wolofwa ku soko ye nyhinyinho ne lizozo lyo wulugu lono wasilikale wolowa wakuzoza, hamonga ne mazi ge zindulele.
3 Nane nolomuwulaga mutemi wa Mowabu, hamonga ne walongola wose we yisi.”

Yuda

4 Muwaha yakutya, “Wanhu wa Yuda, wetuma wubi kali kenji na ku soko yiyo, nane nowalipiciza. Hambi nha wehepa mafundo gangu, hamba siwebate ne malajilizo gangu. Hambi nha wakondya kwajiliza so wutyatye milungu yo wulambi yono wasogo zawo wayitumila.”
5 Holeka nane notegula moto kuli Yuda, no kuganyanya makaya ga Yelusalemi.

Uling'anizi wo Mulungu kuli Isilayeli

6 Muwaha yakutya, “Wanhu wa Isilayeli, wetuma wubi kali kenji nane ku soko yiyo, nowalipiciza. Wawaguza ku wuwanda iwanhu wehuwilwa wono siwasuma kulipa migawa yawo, i wanhu waciwa wono si wali wasumile kulipa hamba uguzi wa malatu.”
7 Wakuwewonela wanhu wono wasoceye, na wono wasina wutazi, no kumwivyajiza nzila muciwa. U munhu no sogwe, wose wakuhanga kugona no muhinza yumonga muwanda no kulyavufya litagwa lyangu lyono lyelile.
8 Ne hanhu ho hose hono wakuhembela, wakuyigonela myenda yono wayisolile kulawa kuli waciwa, yono wayipinjice so migawa yawo. Mu wuhembelo wo Mulungu wawo wakung'wa divayi yono wayisolile kulawa kuli wawo wono wawadayaga mapesa.
9 “Holeka, 'nye nyo wanhu wangu, ku soko yenyu, yo yaleka nane nghawongomeza lukulu Waamoli, wanhu wono wali watali kotya mibici ye mihagata, kanyhi we vilungo kotya mibici ye minghola.
10 Nane nghamuwusenyi mu yisi ye Misili, nghamutonjenyi mu nyika myaka makumi mane, kanyhi nghawa kumupelenyi ne yisi ya Waamoli yiwe yisi yenyu.
11 Nghawahagula muli wana wenyu, wanji wawe wawizi, na muli wanhu wenyu iwazelelo, wanji wawe wanazili. Wulenyi, azi si zili mbeka, anye nyo wanhu wa Isilayeli? Ane no Muwaha Mulungu vyo nghutya.
12 Ninga nye mwawanoza wanazili wang'we divayi, no kuwalajiliza i wawizi, walece kulonga zinghani zangu.
13 Holeka 'lu nane, nomusindanjilenyi ho hasi, nanye motulilwa nho mukokoteni wono wudinghuye wuhemba.
14 Hamba no munhu mumanyhi sikolotiga yaye, no munhu we vilungo kolowula, vilungo vyakwe, no musilikale sikolosuma kuponya wumi wakwe.
15 No munhu mugumi we zisonga, sikolokwima, no mumanyhi nayo sikolotiza, ne wanhu wa mu zifwalasi siwolotiga hamonga no wumi wawo.
16 Mwi'zuwa lilyo, sunga ne wasilikale we nhumbula ye cilume-lume, wolotaga vitwalo vyawo vyo wulugu, no kutiza.” Muwaha Mulungu vyo yakutya.


Amosi 3

1 Muhulicizenyi nghani ayi, anye nyo wanhu wa Isilayeli yono Muwaha Mulungu yayilonga kuli'nye, anye nyo we likungugo lyose lyono yaliwusa ku Misili yakatya,
2 “Muli makungugo gose ga mono yinze, ane nghamumanyenyi nyenye no kumukamisenyi. Na yiyi yo yikuleka no wubi wenyu wukuwa wakogofya. Holeka nane yinojeye nimulipicizenyi so masutya genyu.”

Mulimo wo muwizi

3 Alu 'mbi wanhu wejete wosuma kujendana hamonga muhinzo lono siwalagane?
4 Hambi simba yikolomaga bwete du mu mbago, lono si yitujile mhelembo? Hamba nyana ye simba yikolomaga mu mwina wayo, lono si yibate cinji?
5 Hamba ndeje yigwilaga 'mbi mu cidoga, lono cidoga cisina cikocezo? No mutego nawo ma, wutegukaga 'mbi lono siwuteguywe na cinji?
6 Hamba ndulele yo wulugu ma, yotowa mu kaya, wanhu walece kogopa? Hamba nghani 'mbi ma, yosuma kwinjila mwi'kaya lono Muwaha Mulungu si yayitemizye?
7 U Muwaha siketumaga cinji co cose lono siyawagubuliye wetumami wakwe wawizi.
8 Lono simba yikukoloma, nani 'mbi mono si kebatwaga no woga? Na lono Muwaha yakulonga, alo nani mono kosuma kuleka kuyiganula inghani yakwe?

Wongomezwe wa Samaliya

9 Muwalonjele wawo wono wekaye mu makaya go wutemi, ge Misili na Ashidodi, muwatye, “Mwihangulize hamonga mu matunda gono gayipilime Samaliya nanye mowona wusagahale hamonga no wubi wono wukwitumwa mumo.”
10 Muwaha yakutya, “Wanhu awa, wakumevya mu zinhumba zawo vinhu vyo wuhizi no wuwulaji, hamba siwakumanya vyono yiwanojeye wawe wehuwila.
11 Holeka walugu woyipilima yisi yawo, no kugahodanga magomawakali gawo, no kuwaboka makaya gawo.”
12 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Nha vyono mudimi kakugaga magulu mejete, hamba yikutu lye ngholo yono yaligwa ne simba, nhavivyo vyo wosigala wacefu du muli wanhu wa Samaliya, wono wakugona mu vitanda vye cinyemo-nyemo.
13 Alu muhulicize, mukawakaje wo lulelo lwa Yakobo zo yakutya, Muwaha Mulungu u Mulungu we Mihanga.
14 Mwi'zuwa lyono nolowalipiciziza wanhu wa Isilayeli so wubi wawo, nolovihodanga vikumbicilo vya Beteli. Ne zinhuzi zose ze cikumbicilo vyose zolobenangwa no kuga hasi.
15 Nane nolozihodanga zinyumba ze madaha ge cinyuli na ze madaha ge livuce. Ne zinyumba zo wihambo we meno ge zinhembo, zolokongomezwa ne zikaya zose zimbaha, zolohodanjicila lukulu.”


Amosi 4

1 Hulicizenyi zizi, anye nyo wacekulu wa Samaliya, mono mujinaga nhe zing'ombe za Bashani, mono mukuwatendela vibi wono wasoceye, no kuwewonela i waciwa, no kusaka walume zenyu wamupelenyi wujimbi we mawala gose mung'we.
2 Nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu mono yelile, yelahiza yakatya, “Lizuwa lyolokuza lono nolomukwejenyi ne ndobogo, na wose muli nyenye molowa nha somba mu cilowelo.
3 Nanye molokwegwa sunga behi na hono hamwaguywe mwi'jinji no kutagwa kunze.”

Waisilayeli walemegwa kwiyijiza

4 U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Nye nyo Waisilayeli, ale, mubite hanhu hono helile mu Beteli muketume wubi, hono muwe munojeye! Mubite sunga sunga ko ku Giligali, muketume zo wubi mu nghango yono mukusaka nyenye nyo weneco. Kanyhi mujendelele ku wuleta nhondo zose hakali mitondo mitugo zenyu so likumbiko. Kanyhi nhondo zose mwi'zuwa lye kadatu, mweze kulavya zaka.
5 Mujendelele no kulavya mikate yenyu yo wulumbize kuli Mulungu, no kwilumbila mu vilavya vyono vizize ko winji vyono mukuleta! Ayi yo nghani yono anye mwendile kwituma.

6 “Nane yo nene mono naleta nzala mu makaya genyu gose, ninga hamba simwambuyila yaye.
7 Nane nghayimila mvula yilece kuvitonyela vilimwa vyenyu lono vilimwa vyayisakaga muno. Nghayitonyeza mvula mwi'kaya ali, na linji nghalizumha. Mvula yikatonya mu mugunda awu, ninga mugunda muyagwe wono vihegame, wukanyala.
8 Wanhu wakasocela so nyhilu, wanhu we makaya ganji wakabita mwi'kaya linji lyono wakwihwesa wotuga malenga, ninga hamba kuko kwali kusina gono gakukondya ku wung'we. Hamba nhavivyo si mwambuyila yaye.

9 “Nghayileta mbeho, yivinyaze vilimwa vyenyu. Ne zinzije zikalya migunda yenyu yose ne mizabibu, mikuyu yenyu hamonga ne mizeyituni. Ninga hamba simwambuyila yaye.
10 Nghamuletelenyi lihuhuji kotya nha lyono nategula ku Misili. Nghawawulaga i wazelelo wenyu ku wulugu, no kuzisola zifwalasi zenyu. Nghamevya munungho we mivimba ye wanhu wono wafwiye mu zimhula zenyu, mu zinghambi zenyu. Ninga hamba simwambuyila yaye.

11 “Nghawahodanga wanji muli'nye, nha vyono nayihodanga Sodoma na Gomola. Wawo wono wasigala muli'nye, wakawa nha cibici cinyanywe, cono ciponile ku wulawa mu moto. Ninga hamba simwambuyila yaye.” Vyo yakutya Muwaha Mulungu.
12 “Holeka anye nyo Waisilayeli, nane nomulipicizenyi. Holeka vyono nomunozezenyi zizi, ale nanye, mwiwikanizaje kuwitang'anila no wuling'anizi wangu!”
13 Mulungu yo yanoza matunda no kuwumba mbeho. Mwene kaganozaga mahweso gakwe, gamanyice kuli munhu. Kakawanizaga misi ciwa cilo, no kuhakanyaga hono henuce mu yinze. Ali lyo litagwa lyakwe, Muwaha Mulungu mono yali vilungo!


Amosi 5

Cililo ca Amosi

1 Muhulicize anye nyo Waisilayeli, lwimbo lwe maziko, lono nghumwimbilenyi!

2 U Mwana we cicekulu wa Isilayeli yakondya kugwa. Hamba sikokwinuka kanyhi! Yakondya kulekwa yagwiye hasi, Hasina hamba mono yakumwinusa.

3 Holeka Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Iikaya lya mu Isilayeli, lyotegula wasilikale elufu monga, ninga wowuya igana limonga du. Ne likaya linji nalyo, lyotegula wasililale yigana limonga, ninga wowuya yikumi limonga du.”
4 Muwaha Mulungu yakuwalonjela Waisilayeli, yakutya, “Muze kuli'ne, nanye mowumipa.
5 Mulece kubita kanyhi kuhemba ku Belisheba. Hamba mulece kujela kunondola ku Beteli. Hambi Beteli nha yolokwambuka bwete. Hamba mulece kubita ku Giligali, hambi wanhu wayo nha wolosolwa wategulwe ku wuwanda.”

6 Mumubitilaje Muwaha Mulungu, nanye mowumipa. Ninga hono mwikale simumubitiye, mwene komukongojezenyi ne likuwo lyakwe nha moto nyo Waisilayeli. Moto wolowanyanya ne wanhu wa Beteli, hasina hamba yunji mono kolosuma kuwuzimya.
7 Woyi he kuli'nye. Anye mono muyikawaniza ling'ani no kuwawusiza wanhu ling'ani yawo!

8 Muwaha Mulungu mono yanoza zinyelezi ne cilimila ne mwacinghunghu. Kapitusaga citi ciwa wuzelu, ne misi ciwa cilo. Kacemaga malenga ga mwi'lenga no kugamwaga kunze mu yisi litagwa lyakwe Muwaha Mulungu.
9 Kaletaga wuhodanjice muli wawaha, na muli wanhu wawo we vilungo.

10 Anye wanhu mumusulile mono kawatacilaga wono si wali ling'ani, mono kulongaga mbeka yose ha mahakama.
11 Mukuwatema i waciwa no kuwahiziza wuhemba wawo. Na ku soko yiyo nanye simokwikala mu zikaya ziswanu zono muzenjile ne mabwe maswanu, hamba kung'wa divayi yono yikulawa mu zizabibu zenyu ziswanu zono mulimile.
12 Hambi nane nha manyile vyono wubi wenyu wukogofya hamonga ne masutya genyu vyono menji, gono muwituma. No kuwewonela wanhu we ling'ani. Mukubocela mafupo, no kwimila i ling'ani ye waciwa, yono wakuyituga mu mahakama.
13 Holeka, u wukanganizi wa mu vidaha vye zimbali, yo nghani swanu muno ku witume.
14 Munoze wuhile wenyu, wuwe kwituma zono zili ling'ani, hamba si zimbi yaye, holeka mokwikala womi. No Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga, kowa hamonga nanye nha vyono mwihuwiye, vyono, mwene yali baho.
15 Musule zimbi, mwende ziswanu no kwituma ze ling'ani mu mahakama. Nha lunji Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga kowa no lusungu kuli wanhu we likungugo lili, wono wakali wasigaye womi.

16 Holeka Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga yakutya, “Kolowa kocili cililo co wusungu, mu zinzila za mwi'kaya. Na wono wakulima, wolocemwa waze wamulilile mono yafwiye, hamonga na wawo wono wakulipwa ho walile citalo.
17 Kolowa na cililo mu migunda yose ye mizabibu. Azi zose zolokwijela ku soko, ane nghuza nimuling'anizenyi.” Vyo yakutya Muwaha Mulungu.
18 Wolowa wogofye wuwaha kuli'nye mono mukusulumila lizuwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu! Kolowa kowuli wuswanuci kuli'nye mwi'zuwa lilyo? Hambi kuli'nye nha lyolowa lizuwa lye citi, hamba si lya wuzelu yaye.
19 Yolowa nha munhu mono yakumangha yakutiza simba, no kwitang'anila ne dubu! Hamba yolowa nha munhu mono yakwinjila mukaya, no kwegamika muwoko mwijinji, nzoka yiwa muluma!
20 Lizuwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu, lyololeta citi hamba si wuzelu yaye. Lyolowa yizuwa lya wutitu wuwaha, lyono lisina hamba wuzelu bwete.
21 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Ane singhwendezwa ne zi sikukuu zenyu zo wuhembi, hamba sinosuma kuzikangalila yaye!
22 Hamba mundavizye vilavywa vinyanye na vyo 'saje, ane sinovibocela yaye, sinozibocela hamba ne zimitugo zenyu zono zijinile zono mukundavizya.
23 Mulece kunzozeza 'ne, ne zinyimbo zenyu, nane singhusaka kuwuhuliciza hamba no mulilo we masumbi genyu.
24 Langa muwulece wiling'ano wuhulume kotya muvwilo, ne ling'ani kotya likolongo lyono silinyalaga.
25 Anye nyo Waisilayeli, ane sinasaka makumbiko ne vilavywa vyenyu, lono nawalongoza mu nyika mu myaka makumi mane.
26 Alu ninga vyono mwavihemba ivihwani vye milungu vya Sakuti, mulungu wo mutemi wenyu, no mulungu wa Kayiwani, mulungu we nyelezi, molosoleleza vihwani vivyo,
27 kolo neza kumutegulenyi ku wuwanda, ku nhumbi ya Damesiki,” vyo yakutya Muwaha Mulungu, mono litagwa lyakwe, Mulungu we Mihanga.


Amosi 6

Wongomele we Waisilayeli

1 Yolowa nghani ya kogofya muno, kuli 'nye mono mwina mikalile miswanu nhavi mu Siyoni, na kuli nye mono mukwihwesa nha mwikaye mupolile mu Samaliya, anye mo wanhu wawaha mu nghungugo mbaha ya Isilayeli, anye mono wanhu wa Isilayeli wakuwa kumuzizenyi wakulondola wutazi kuli'nye!
2 Mubite mulanje ta mwikaya lya Kaline. Hamba mwi'kaya liwaha lya Hamati, sunga ko kwi'kaya lya Gati, mu yisi ye Wafilisiti. Wulenyi, wena cinji cono cicizize wutemi wa Yuda na Isilayeli? Hamba yisi yawo, wuwaha wayo wucicize yisi yenyu?
3 Anye mono si mukwenda kuvumila vyono lizuwa libi lyokuza, ninga zizo zono mukwituma, zo zikulisejeleza behi lizuwa lilyo.
4 Magu, 'mba yolowa wogofyeci kuli'nye, mono mukugona mu vitanda vyenyu vye cinyemo-nyemo vyono vihambigwe ne meno ga nhembo, akuno mukulya zisikukuu ze zinyama ze zing'ombe, hamonga na ze zingholo za mwi'tewa!
5 Anye mono mwendile kutula zinyimbo, nha vyono Dawudi yanoza, no kwimba hamonga ne mazi ge masumbi.
6 Anye mono mukung'wela divayi mu zibakuli, hamonga no kwibaka mafuta ge mhunjile swanu muno muno, hamba simukulila so wongomezwe we Waisilayeli.
7 Holeka mowa walongozi kubita ku wuhamywe. Ne zisikukuu zenyu, hamonga ne indigwa ze cinyemo-nyemo, vyose vyoloduma.

8 Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga yo yacipelile wulajilize wo wukagwe, yakutya, “Nane siniwendile widunhumuzi we wanhu wa Isilayeli. Kanyhi nizibedegaye muno ne zikaya zawo ze cinyemo-nyemo. Holeka nowapela walugu wawo likaya lyawo liwaha hamonga ne vinhu vyose vyono vili mumo.”
9 Nahono hawe ho wali wanhu yikumi wono wasigaye mu mhuga, nawo wolofwa.
10 Na munya ndugu wo munhu mono yafwiye, hamonga no mwimilizi we maziko, kolo wakuwulavya muwili wa malehemu kunze ye kaya ho wakawuzice, wolokwamula waceme, kuli mono yakali yasigaye mukaya wawe kutya, “Wule ko yuli munhu yunji mono yakali yasigaye hamonga nagwe mumo mu kaya?” Ne lizi lyopitula litye, “Aa! Ng'o.” Nayo munya ndugu kotya, “Hodu wanyamale twii! Hambi nha yicinojeye cilanjilile cilece kulitambula litagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu.”

11 Lono Muwaha Mulungu yakulavya lilajilizo, zinyumba zimbaha ne zindodo obenanjika tici tici.
12 Wule! Zifwalasi zomangha mu canyha ye matalawe? Ma! Wanhu walimaga ne zing'ombe mwi'lenga? Ninga anye mwayipitula ling'ani, yawa sumu, ne mbeka nayo yawa yisutya.
13 Anye mono mukwilumba so wibata likaya lya Lodeba, mukwilumba mukutya, “Ase cali cili vilungo, lanje vyono cikasuma kulisola likaya lya Kalinayimu.”
14 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga mweneco yakutya, “Anye nyo Waisilayeli, ane nowaleta wasilikale we likungugo lye cijenzi, wayisole yisi yenyu, nawo womugazenyi wulawa ku Hamati ku winjililo wa kusumuka, sunga ko ku cikolongo ca Alaba, mhelo ya kutakama.”


Amosi 7

Wulajilwe we ning'aniza

Wulajilwe wulongozi: zinzije

1 Nane nghatuga wulajilwe ku wulawa kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Muli zizo, nghamuwona yakuwumba yitewa lya nzije lono u mulimo wo kutema mahanze go mutemi, go kulisa zing'ombe zakwe wali wakondya, ne lihanze lyali lyanza kuwiluka kanyhi.
2 Mu wulajilwe wuwo, nghawona nzije zikulya vinhu vyose vyono videdewaye mu'si. Nane hanyuma nghatya, “Gwe Muwaha Mulungu nghukunena, uwanenelaje wanhu wako! Alu wanhu wako ho wowumipa vye wule? Hambi nha wacefu, kanyhi wasoceye!”
3 Hodu Muwaha Mulungu yakakawaniza nghani yiyo no kutya. “Azi zono wuziwene, hodu sizokwijela yaye.”

Wulajilwe we kejete: Moto

4 Nane nghatuga wulajilwe wunji kanyhi, ku wulawa kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Muli wuwo, nghamuwona mwene yakwiwikaniza yawongomeze wanhu wakwe ko moto. No moto wuwo wukalinyanya lilenga liwaha ku'si ye'si, kanyhi wukawa kwanza uyinyanya ne yisi.
5 Nane nghatya, “Gwe Muwaha Mulungu! Nghukunena wuwanenelaje wanhu wako! Alu wanhu wako u lulelo lwa Yakobo ho wowumipa vye wule?” Hambi nha wacefu kanyhi wasoceye!
6 Hodu Muwaha Mulungu yakakawaniza kanyhi nghani yiyo no kutya, “Nghani yiyi nayo, hodu si yowa yaye.”

Wulajilwe we kadatu: Cipimilo

7 Nane nghatuga wulajilwe wunji kanyhi, ku wulawa kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Muli wuwo, nghamuwona mwene Mulungu, yali yemile behi ne lijinji, lyono lyali lizenjigwe ko wutazi we cipimilo, ne cipimilo cali cili mu muwoko wakwe.
8 Hodu Muwaha Mulungu yakambuza yakatya, “Amosi, wukuwona cici?” Nane nghamupitula nghatya, “Nghuwona cipimilo.” Nayo yakanyitya, “Ane nghwitumamila cipimilo cici, kulajila vyono wanhu wangu wenha lijinji lyono si ligoloce libinuce, yono lili kunze yo mucilili. Holeka nane sinokawaniza yaye wuhile wangu kanyhi, wo kusaka kuwongomeza wene.
9 Ne hanhu hono lulelo lwa Isaka wahembelaga, naho holo kongomezwa. Ne hanhu hose hono helile ha Isilayeli, holekwa matongo du. Nane noloyidumya ne mhuga yo mutemi Yelobowamu.”

Amosi na Amaziya

10 Amaziya u mukohani wa Beteli, yakamutegulila zinghani Yelobowamu, u mutemi wa Isilayeli, yakutya, “Amosi yakutajila nghani za wusonjezi muli wanhu wa Isilayeli. No wulonje wakwe wohodanga yisi.
11 Hambi mwene nha yakulonga yakutya, ‘Yelobowamu kolofwila ku wulugu, ne wanhu wa Isilayeli wolosolwa ku wulawa ku yisi yawo, wategulwe kutali nha wawopwe.’ ”
12 Nayo Amaziya yakamulonjela Amosi yakatya, “Gwe go muwizi, hodu zakondya, alu, wuwuye ko ku Yuda, wukaganule luganuzi lwako kuko. Nawo wokulisa so mulimo wuwo.
13 Ninga wulece kuwila kanyhi ahano hono Beteli. Hambi aha, nha ho hanhu hono mutemi kahembelaga ho ha wuhembelo we likungugo.”

14 Amosi yakapitula yakamutya Amaziya, “Ane si nili muwizi we, liwala lye wawizi wono wakuwila ho walipwe. Ane nili mudima mitugo kanyhi nili mulanjilizi we mikuyu.
15 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu, yasola ane ku wulawa mu mulimo wangu wo wudimi, no kunhuma nize kuwila kuli wanhu wakwe Isilayeli.
16 Holeka alu, wuhulicize vyono Muwaha Mulungu yakutya. Agwe wukunyitya vyono ndece kulavya wuwizi hamba kuganula kuli Waisilayeli.
17 Holeka lu agwe Amaziya. Muwaha Mulungu yakutya kuli'gwe, ‘U mugolece wako, kowa musenyha mumuno mwikaya lili, ne wanago nawo walume ne wacekulu, wolowulagwa ku wulugu. Ne yisi yako nayo, yologawilwa wanji. Nagwe go mweneco, kolofwila mu yisi yo wavufywe. Ne Waisilayeli, mbeka wolosolwa no kuwuswa mu yisi yawo, wategulwe ku yisi yo wujenzi.’ ”


Amosi 8

Wulajilwe we kane: Nhoto ye mikalo

1 Kanyhi nghalajilwa ku wulawa kuli Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga. Muli wuwo nghawona nhoto yono yina mikalo.
2 Muwaha Mulungu yakambuza yakatya, “Amosi, wukuwona cici?” Nane nghamupitula nghatya, “Nghuwona nhoto ye mikalo.” Muwaha Mulungu yakanyitya, “U mudumo we wanhu wangu Isilayeli, wakondya kuza. Nane sinokawaniza yaye wuhile wangu wo kuwongomeza wene.
3 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo zinyimbo za mu kaya yo wutemi, zolowa vililo vyo wusungu wuwaha. Ne miwili ye wanhu wono wafwiye, yolowa yiyagaye caka hanhu hose. Hamba lizozo si lilahulikwa yaye.”

Mulungu koyongomeza Isilayeli

4 Muhulicize zizi anye mono mukuwewonela i waciwa, no kujela kuwongomeza i waciwa wa mu'si.
5 Mukutya nyo weneco, “Colindila sunga kolo vyapuluta i vilo vye ndawo yo mwezi, ho cosuma kuguza vilimwa vyetu. Alu sabato yomalika nili nase ho cisume kwanza kuguza kanyhi? Hambi baho nha colosuma kuguza na kumhelo, mu nzila yo kwitumila vipimo vyo wulambi, no kuvipitula vipimilo, ho ciwahizize wanyo kugula.
6 Na baho ho colosuma kuguza ngano yono si swanu ko wuguzi wuwaha. Nase comutuga munhu muciwa, mono sikosuma kulipa mugawa wakwe, hamba kulipa wuguzi we vilatu bwete, nase baho comugula yawe muwanda.”
7 Muwaha Mulungu we Waisilayeli yelahiza yakutya, “Hamba sinosemwa i metumo gawo mabi.
8 Na ku soko yiyo, holeka yisi yocicima, ne wanhu wose wa mu'si yose, wowa mu wugaye. Ne yisi yose yolosinjisika; yolokwibuka no kung'welela, nhe likolongo lye Nile ku Misili.
9 I cipango cikuza lono nilanoza lizuwa ligwile, lono yikali misi. Ne yisi yolotukuwala mwi'zuwa lilyo. Nene, no Muwaha Mulungu vyo nghutya.
10 Nolopitula zi sikukuu zenyu ziwe wukalale, ne zinyimbo zenyu ze nyemo ziwe cililo no wusungu. Nolomunozenyi munyonyole mitwe yenyu, no kuvwala magunila. Nanye molowa nha walezi wono wakulila so mwana wawo monga we cilume. Ne lizuwa lilyo, lyolowa lya wusungu sunga ko ku mudumo wayo.

11 “Ne cipango cikuza lono nilaleta nzala mu'si, wanhu wolowa na nzala, ninga si nzala ya ndigwa, kanyhi wolowa na nyhilu, ninga si nyhilu ya malenga. Langa wolowa na nzala ne nyhilu so nghani yono yikulawa kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Nane yo nene no Muwaha Mulungu mono nghutya.
12 Ne wanhu wolopilima-pilima, ku wulawa kwi'lenga alino sunga ko kwilenga alino, na kanyhi, ku wulawa lubavu lwa kusumuka, sunga ko ku lubavu lwa kucilima, wolomangha no kubita ako na ako wakuyilondola nghani yo Muwaha Mulungu, ninga hamba siwalayituga ng'o.
13 Mwi'zuwa lilyo wazelelo ne wahinza waswanu, wolozilika so nyhilu.
14 Na wono welahilaga vihwani vye milungu vye Samaliya, wono watezaga, ‘Ko mulungu wako Dani,’ hamba, ‘Ko mulungu wa Belisheba!’ Wanhu wawo wologwa, na hamba si wolokwinuka kanyhi yaye.”


Amosi 9

Wuling'anizi wo Muwaha Mulungu

1 Nane namuwona Muwaha Mulungu yemile ha cikumbicilo. Yakanyitya, “Wutowe mu midumo ye masumbili gono gawukamizye wuhembelo, ne likumbo lyose lyosinjisika. Nagwe wugabenanjize no kugaleka gawagwile wanhu mu mitwe ye wanhu. Ne wanhu wono wasigaye ku wulugu ane nolowawulaga no mumaje. Hasina hamba munhu monga mono yalatiga, hamba yumonga mono yalapona muli wene.
2 Hamba wahimbe nzila zawo hasi, sunga zifice ko ku yinze ye wafu. Ane nowebata du. Hamba wabite ko ku wulanga, ane nowatimya hasi.
3 Hamba nha wehundice ku cizuli ce litunda lya Kalimeli, ane nowalondola sunga niwebate. Hono wehundice mu visolo vye lilenga, nolilajiliza lilimu liwaha lya mwilenga liwalume.
4 Na hono wasolwe wawopwe ne walugu wawo, nowalonjela wawo walugu wawo wawawulaje. Nane hamba si nowataza yaye, anga ndumuye nghani niwongomeze du.”

5 Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga kayipamizaga yisi,

nayo yinze yiwa kucicima,

na wose wono wekaye mumo wolila.

Ne yinze yose yolokwibuka no kug'welela kotya

nhe likolongo lye Nile ku Misili.

6 Muwaha Mulungu kazengaga wikalo wakwe ku wulanga,

no kuwika misinji yo mwanya mono'si.

Mwene kacemaga malenga

ge lilenga no kugakupa mu'si,

Muwaha Mulungu, lye 'tagwa lyakwe!

7 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Nye nyo Waisilayeli, ane nghuwehwesa wanhu wa Etiyopiya, nha vyono nghumwihwesenyi nyenye. Ane nawatonga Wafilisiti, ku wulawa ku Kilete, nawo Waashuli, ku wulawa ku Kili, nha vyono namutonjenyi 'nye ku wulawa ku Misili.
8 Nane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, mono nghuwulanga wutemi wa Isilayeli, wono wina wubi, nane nowongomezeza lukulu mono yinze. Ninga hamba nhavivyo si nolongomeza lulelo lose lwa Yakobo.

9 “Langa nolavya wulajilize no kuwapemenha wanhu wa Isilayeli, nha vyono wapemenhaga wuhemba mu luheneko. Nowapemenha wene, ku wulawa mu makungugo, no kuwawusa wose wono wasina cambuzi.
10 Na wanyo wubi wa muli wanhu wangu, wolowulagwa ku wulugu, na wose wono wakutya, ‘Mulungu sikoleka zimbi zicagane.’ ”

Mhonelo yono yowaziza Waisilayeli

11 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Lizuwa likuza lyono ane nolowusimya wutemi wa Dawudi, wono winha kaya yono yigwiye so wongomezwe. Nolonozeleza majinji gose gono gamwaguka, no kugasimya kanyhi. Noloyizenga no kuyinoza yiwe nha vyono yali katali.
12 Holeka wanhu wa Isilayeli, wolowatema i wanhu wono wali wasigaye mu yisi ya Edomu, na wa muli makungugo gose wono ku wulawa ha mwamulizo wali wangu.” Vyo yakutya Muwaha mono kolozinoza zizi zijele.
13 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya,

“Ivilo vikuza.

Lono vilimwa vilawiluka musunho kuciza

mu nghango yono vyowa vyisumice ku wududule

ne zizabibu zolowiluka mbela mbela kuciza

mu nghango yono divayi yikusuma kunozwa,

ne matunda golosuluza divayi swanu,

ne vigongo vyose vyolosipa divayi.

14 Nane nolowagomola

wanhu wangu sunga ko ku yisi yawo.

Wolozenga makaya gawo

gono gahodangwa no kwikala mumo,

wolopanha migunda ye mizabibu

no kung'wa divayi wolopanha migunda

no kulya vilimwa vyono walimile.

15 Nane nolowagomola no kuwawika wanhu wangu, mu'si yono nawapela,

hamba si wolowuswa kanyhi yaye.”

Muwaha Mulungu,

u Mulungu wenyu, vyo yakutya.
