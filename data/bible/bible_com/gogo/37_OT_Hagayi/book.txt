Hagayi 1

Wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu wo kuyizenga kanyhi Nyumba yo Mulungu

1 Mu mwaka we kejete wo wutemi wa Daliyo mu Pelisiya, mwi'zuwa lilongozi lyo mwezi wo mutandatu, Muwaha Mulungu yalonga mu nzila yo muwizi Hagayi. Yamulonjele Zelubabeli mwana wa Shelitiyeli u mulongola we yisi ya Yuda, hamonga no Mukohani Muwaha Yoshuwa mwana wa Yehozadaki.

2 Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga yakamutya Hagayi, “Wanhu awa wakutya cipango aci co si cipango co kuyizenjela Nyumba yo Mulungu.”
3 Muwaha Mulungu kanyhi, yakawalonjela wanhu nghani ayi muli muwizi Hagayi yakatya,
4 “Nye nyo wanhu wangu, alo nhawulenyi, anye mwikaye mu zikaya zinzenje viswanu, lono Nyumba yangu yo wuhembelo yikaye mu wuhodanjice?
5 Ma, si mukuziwona zono zomutujenyi.”
6 Mulanje ta, anye mwahadika mbeyu nyinji, ninga mwadudula wucefu du, mukulya, ninga simukwiguta yaye, mukung'wa divayi, ninga hamba simukuca ng'o, mukuvwala myenda, ninga siyikukondya kumupenyi civuce. No munhu mono yakwituma milimo, u musahala wakwe, wukuwa nha wutajilwa mu mufuko wono wutoboce-toboce.
7 Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga yakutya, Alu nhawule si mukusuma kumanya, ku sokoci zizino zikwijela?
8 Alu mubite mu matunda, mukateme zimbawo, muyizenje Nyumba yo Mulungu, holeka nane nokwendezwa, kanyhi ho nowa kuhembwa nha vyono nojeye kuhembwa.

9 “Mwali mwitumbiye vyono motuga nhwanga mbaha, ninga yipituka yiwa ndodo. Nanye mwali mweza kugomola nhwanga yenyu mu zikaya, nane nghayipemhelusa. Na ku sokoci nghazinoza zizo? Vyono Nyumba yangu yikaye mu wuhodanjice, akuno anye mukujendelela kwipiganza mu zikaya zenyu, munhu na kaya yakwe.
10 Na yiyi yo soko yono yikuleka mvula si yikutonya, hamba kusina cinji cono cikucindika.
11 Nane nghayimila mvula yilece kutonya mu yisi, na mu matunda gayo, na mu migunda yo wuhemba, na mu migunda ye zabibu, na mu migunda ye mibici yono yilelaga mikalo, muli vilimwa vyose vyono yisi yilavyaga, na muli wanhu, ne zinimu, na muli vyose vyono mukuhila kuviwilusa.”

Wanhu wakulihulika lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu

12 Hobaho Zelubabeli na Yoshuwa ne wanhu wose wono walawa ku wuhamywe ku Babiloni, waketuma nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu wawo yawalonjela wetume. Nawo wakamogopa no kumuhulika muwizi Hagayi, mutumwa wo Muwaha Mulungu.
13 Baho Hagayi yakawalonjela wanhu nghani yo Muwaha Mulungu, yakawatya, “Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, awu wo wihuwizo wangu, vyono ane nowa hamonga nanye.”
14 U Muwaha Mulungu yakaweza mitima wanhu wose wetume mulimo wo kuyizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu, Zelubabeli u mulongola we yisi ya Yuda, na Yoshuwa u Mukohani Muwaha, ne wanhu wose wono wawuya ku wulawa ku wuhamywe. Wakawa kwanza kuyizenga kanyhi Nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga, u Mulungu wawo.
15 Mwi'zuwa lye makumi mejete na yine, lyo mwezi wo mutandatu, wo Mwaka we kejete, wo wuteme wakwe Daliyo.


Hagayi 2

U wuswanu we Nyumba 'mhya yo Mulungu

1 Mwi'zuwa lye makumi mejete na monga, lyo mwezi wo mupunghati, mu mwaka wuwo, Muwaha Mulungu yalonga kanyhi mu nzila yo muwizi Hagayi.
2 Yakamulonjela Hagayi, yalonje na Zelubabeli u mulongola we yisi ya Yuda, na kuli Yoshuwa Mukohani Muwaha, na kuli wanhu, yawalonjele yatye.
3 “Wulenyi, koyuli yunji muli'nye mono yakumukumbucila wuswanu we Nyumba yo Mulungu vyono wali winha? Alu ninga wulenyi, mukuyiwona yinha wule zuwali? Yidahile kwijela nha cinhu ca caka caka du.
4 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, yunji yalece kutukuwika mutima. Langa anye mwitumaje du mulimo muhidimbaye, hambi ane nha nili hamonga nanye.
5 Nanye lono mwawukaga ku wulawa Misili, ane nghamwihuwizenyi nha vyono cipango cose nowa hamonga nanye, sunga nalu ane nghali nili hamonga nanye. Holeka mulece kogopa.

6 “Hanyuma nosinjisa wulanga ne yinze, yisi ne lilenga.
7 Nane nogacicimya makungugo gose, no wugoli wawo wose, wolosolwa no kuletwa bahano, ne Nyumba yo Mulungu yolomemywa no wugoli.
8 Fweza zose na zahabu za mono yinze, nha zangu.
9 Nyumba 'mhya yo Mulungu, yolowa swanu muno muno kuciza yinongozi. Nane baho nolowapela wanhu wangu wumoteswe no mulamu.” Vyo yakutya Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga.

I wanhu wose wavufywe

10 Mwi'zuwa lye makumi mejete na yine, lyo mwezi we licenda, mu mwaka we kejete wo wutemi wa Daliyo, u Muwaha Mulungu kanyhi yalonga kuli muwizi Hagayi.
11 Yakatya, “Wuwawuzaje ta Wakohani wulajilize vyono wukutya mu nghani ayino.
12 Hono munhu yunji yasole nyama yono yelile ku wulawa mu cikumbicilo, no kuyidinghula mwi'mhembo lyo mwenda wakwe. Na hono yaleceleze mwenda wakwe wupamize mukate, hamba ndigwa yono nhelece, hamba divayi, hamba mafuta ge mizeyituni, hamba ndigwa yo yose, wule! Ndigwa yiyo nayo yowa yanozwa yelile?”

Ne liwuzo lilyo lyali lyeza kuwuzwa kuli wakohani, nawo i wakohani wakatya, “Aa ng'o si yonozwa yelile.”
13 Hanyuma Hagayi kanyhi yakawawuza yakutya, “Ma, hono yiwe munhu yeyavufya ku soko yo kuwupamiza muvimba. Na hono yapamize cinhu co cose ca muli zindigwa zizo, wule, kowa yazitajila wavufywe zindigwa zizi?” Nawo i wakohani wakapitula wakatya, “Heya, kowa yavitajila wavufywe.”

14 Hobaho Hagayi yakawatya, “U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, azi vyo zinha kuli wanhu we likungugo lili, na kuli vilimwa vyose vyono wakuvilima, na kuli vinhu vyose vyono wakundavizya mu cikumbicilo, vyose vyakondya kwavufywa.”

Muwaha Mulungu yakuwehuwiza wulugutwe wakwe

15 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Ma, simwawona zono zejela kuli'nye?” Lono mwali mukali nanza kuyizenga kanyhi Nyumba yo Mulungu,
16 mwabita ku migunda kududula wuhemba, akuno mukulanjilila vyono motuga kilo magana mejete, ninga wono wali wuli baho, wali kilo yigana limonga du. Kanyhi mwabita ho muneje divayi lita yigana mu zinjilo, ninga mwatuga lita makumi mane du.
17 Nategula mbeho yono yanyaza, ne mvula ye mabwe, yikavihodanga vinhu vyose vyono mwahilaga kuviwilusa. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, si mwanena yaye.
18 Zuwali lye 'zuwa lye makumi mejete na yine, lya mu mwezi we licenda, ku wulawa lizuwa lyono musinji we Nyumba yo Mulungu wakondelecela. Alu mulanjilile cono cokwijela ku wulawa lulu sunga hanyuma yayo.
19 Hamba nha yiwe aluno musina wuhemba wono wusigaye mu madongha genyu, ne zizabibu, ne mikuyu ne mikomamanga, ne mizeyituni nayo si yikulela, ninga ku wulawa lulu, ane nomulugutizenyi.

Wihuwizo wo Muwaha Mulungu kuli Zelubabeli

20 Mwi'zuwa lilyo lye makumi mejete na yine, lyo mwezi, Muwaha Mulungu yamupela Hagayi nghani lwe kejete.
21 Yabite yakamulonjele Zelubabeli u mulongola we yisi ya Yuda yamutye, “Ane nowucicimya wulanga ne yisi,
22 kanyhi nowuwusa no wutemi wose, no kuvidumya vilungo vyayo. Noyibidusa mituka yawo yo wulugu, hamonga na wono wakuyijendya. Zifwalasi nazo zofwa, hamonga na wono wakuzijendela, wokwiwulaga weneco wose.
23 Mwi'zuwa lilyo, nokusola agwe Zelubabeli go mwitumami wangu, nokuhagula wuteme mwi'tagwa lyangu. Hambi nha yo gwegwe du mono nghuhaguye.” Vyo yakutya Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga.
