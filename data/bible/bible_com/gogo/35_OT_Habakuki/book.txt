Habakuki 1

1 Ayi yo nghani yono Muwaha Mulungu yamugubulila muwizi Habakuki.

Habakuki si yakwendezwa no wuhizi

2 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, alu nondole wutazi kuli'gwe sunga nili nagwe ho wuhulice. Hamba cokulilila sunga nili ho wuciponye mu wukali we wanhu wabi?
3 Alu nhawule wukunoza 'ne nigawone magayo gono genha gaga? Hamba kosuma nhawule kujendelela ku wulanga wubi wono winha wuwu? Hambi wuhodangwe, ne vilungo, nha vyose vimhilime nene, kanyhi ko wuli wuma no wugombane hanhu hose.
4 No wulajilize weza wusina vilungo kanyhi, sunga ne ling'ani yeza yili musina. I wanhu wabi wakuwapilima we ling'ani, holeka no wuling'anizi weza wusina vilungo.

Lipitulo lyo Muwaha Mulungu

5 U Muwaha Mulungu yakalonga kuli wanhu wakwe yakutya, “Mugalangaje makungugo gono gamupilimenyi, nanye mokwizina kuli zono moziwona. Nane nokwituma nghani yono si mokwihuwilila lukulu kolo mweza kuyihulika.
6 Nane nghuwanoza Wababiloni wawe ne vilungo kanyhi wakali, kanyhi wali nghakamo. Nawo wokola haciligati ye yinze, no kutema zi'si zi'si.
7 Kanyhi wowogofya wanhu no woga wuwaha, no wulajilize wowa wuli mu wiwoni wawo weneco.

8 “Ne zifwalasi zawo zowa zikumangha mbela mbela zicizize masuwi, kanyhi nghali, zicizize ne maminzi gono gadabuce. Ne wajendela zifwalasi wawo, wokuza wakuzimasha ku wulawa ma'si go kutali, fwalasi zawo zikwipalila hasi. Zikukola musunho muno muno kotya numba vyono yibataga mapelembo gayo.
9 Ne wasilikale wawo, wena vilungo vyo wusume, ne wanhu wose wakuwogopa kolo wakuwasejelela behi. Ne mateka wono wowateka, wowa wenji nha musawawa.
10 Wowabedegala watemi no kuwaseka i wawaha. Hamba hasina igomawakali lyono lyosuma kuwemila, hambi nha wolilundila muhangha likaya no kulisola.
11 I wanhu wawa wono i vilungo vyawo yo mulungu wawo, wopuluta musunho nha mbeho, hanyuma wawe kubita.”

Habakuki yakulajila wu wula kwendezwa wakwe kanyhi hali Muwaha Mulungu

12 Muwaha Mulungu ku wulawa mwamulizo yo gwegwe Mulungu, agwe yo wuli Mulungu wangu, mono welile na we cibitilila, Muwaha Mulungu wangu no mudimi wangu. Alu wawahagula i Wababiloni no kuwanoza wawe ne vilungo, ho wasume kucilipiciza ase.
13 Ninga socici wukusuma kwima kuli wanhu awa wakoci, kanyhi wabi? Ne meso gako nha gelile, ku wulanga zo wubi, hamba si kosuma kwima ku lubavu lwe wanhu wetuma zimbi. Alu socici wunyamaye lono wakuwahodanga wanhu wono wali ling'ani si nha wene?
14 Hamba wukusuma nhawule kuwatendela wanhu nha somba, hamba kotya litewa lye mang'onyo, gono gasina wulajilize wono wukugalongoza?
15 Wababiloni wakuwagwiza wanhu na ndobogo, nha kotya somba. Hanyuma wawa kuwakwejenha hasi mu zinyavu no kuzoza so nyemo mu wibatwe wawo!
16 Sunga wakuzihemba zinyavu zawo, no kuzilavizya makumbiko, vyono zinyavu zawo zikuwapela iviswanu vyose.
17 Alu wule, wojendelela kwitumamila mimaje yawo cibitilila, no kujendelela kugahodanga makungugo wu wula lusungu?


Habakuki 2

Lipitulo lyo Muwaha Mulungu kuli Habakuki

1 Nobita mucanyha yo munala wangu, nindile mbone zono Muwaha Mulungu kononjela, ho nonje kanyhi, mbone lipitulo lyono komhela muli zizo zono singhuzenda.

Mulungu yakumupitula Habakuki

2 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamhela lipitulo ali yakatya, “Ale, wandice calinze mu vibawo zono nghukugubulila, ho zisume kusomeka calinze.
3 Wuzandikaje, hambi cipango nha cikali cono zizo zowela. Ninga cipango cikuza mbela, na zono nghukulajila zokuza. Ne cipango cico cokwijela nha cikucela ku wuze, ninga agwe weze wulindiye du, naco cokuza, hamba si cotinya yaye.
4 ‘I wabi wose si wokwikala womi yaye, langa we ling'ani, wo wokwikala womi, hambi nha wehuwilwa kuli Mulungu.’ ”

Wongomezwe kuli wono si weling'ani

5 Hamonga na zizo, divayi yajilizaga, no munhu mwidunhumuzi, si kekalaga vilo vinji. Mwene yewikanize kuwamela wanhu wanji nha vyono yinha yisi ye wafu. Holeka nayo si kegutaga yaye nha vyono linha lifwa, mwene kekongholelaga i makungugo gose, hamonga ne wanhu wose wawa nha sawo yakwe.
6 Ninga iwanhu wono wakuwasuma, nawo wolowazwajiza wono wawasumize, kanyhi wolowabedegala. Wolotya, “Mwavisolaga vyono si vyali vyenyu, ninga 'lu woyi hee kuli'nye! Hamba mojendelela wule 'lu kanyhi, mu wugoli wono mwetujila mu nzila yo kuwadaya kwa vilungo wono wali wakopile kuli'nye ho wamulipenyi?”
7 Ninga na lono mowa mukali namanya, wawo wono mwawasolela vinhu vyawo, nawo wolomuzizenyi nhinicizo welipicize. I walugu wolomuzizenyi no kumogofyenyi. Kanyhi wolomubocenyi ne sawo yenyu kwe vilungo!
8 Anye mwawaboka wanhu we makungugo menji sawo zawo, ninga aluno wawo nawo wono wasigaye, womubocenyi nanye zisawo zenyu, ku soko yo wuwulaji wono mwetuma, na ku soko ye mawonezi genyu kuli wanhu we yinze ne makaya gawo.

9 Woyi hee kuli nye! Mono mwazigolifya zimhuga zenyu ne sawo yono muyibocile kwe vilungo, akuno mukuzinoza zikaya zenyu ziwe zipolile zisina mbali!
10 Na mu nzila yiyo, mukunoza vibi, hambi nha mweletela cevu mu zikaya zenyu, namo mu wuhomanga makungugo menji, mweyongomeza muli wene.
11 Alu sunga ne mabwe ga mwi'jinji, gakulila so nyenye, no mucici nawo wukupitula.
12 Woyi hee kuli'nye! Mwalisimya likaya ko wavu, mukalizenga ko wuwulaji.
13 Ne makungugo gono mwagatema, gakawa nha gekataze no mulimo wa bwete, ne mazengo gose gono wali wazenga, gakaticimila no moto. No Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga yo yetuma zizo.
14 Ninga yinze yolowa yimemile wumanye wo lukumyo lo Mulungu nha vyono lilenga limemile malenga.

15 Woyi hee kuli'nye! Anye mono mwi'kuwo lyenyu, mukawanoza wayenyu wabedegalwe no kuwakoza cevu, kanyhi mukawanoza wakawa kupebesuka-pebesuka nha wanhu wono wagalile wujimbi.
16 Ninga zizo zomuwuyilenyi nyo weneco, ne wanhu si womupelenyi cogopo yaye, nanye momema cevu ciwaha. Nanye nyo weneco mologala wujimbi no kupebesuka-pebesuka. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu kolomung'wesenyi cikombe co wulipicizwe nyenye nyo weneco. Ne cogopo cenyu colopituka ciwe cevu.
17 Nha vyono mwayitemanga mbago ya ku Lebanoni, nanye 'lu vyo motemangwa nhavivyo. Kanyhi nha vyono mwaviwulaga vilimu, nanye vilimu vyomogofyenyi. Na zizi zokwijela ku soko yo wuwulaji wono mwetuma, na ku soko yo wongomeze we wanhu wa mono'nze hamonga ne makaya gayo.

18 Hambi cihwani cina cambuzici? Nha cinhu du cono cinozigwe no munhu, kanyhi hamba si cosuma kukulonjela cinji co cose, langa wulambi du. Hamba hasina nghani yono cico cihwani, cimutendelaga munya kucisesa ho cisume kwitumbilwa, hambi cihwani, nha si mulungu, hamba si cosuma no kulonga yaye!
19 Woyi hee kuli 'nye! Mono mulilonjelaga libici mukutya, “Usinhuce!” Hamba kwi'bwe lyono si likulonga mukutya, “Winuce!” Alo mukwihwesa vyono cihwani cosuma kumugubulilenyi nghani yo yose lo? Heya, cidahile kuwa cihiliwe ne fweza hamonga ne zahabu, ninga hamba nhavivyo vyo cisina wumi du.

20 U Muwaha Mulungu yali mu Nyumba yakwe yono Yelile, holeka wanhu wose wa mono'nze, yiwanojeye watule hali mwene.


Habakuki 3

Lilombo lya Habakuki

1 Awu wo wulombe wo muwizi Habakuki,

2 Gwe Muwaha Mulungu,

ane hulika zono wetuma,

nane nhuga woga wuwaha.

Ale, alu'kanyhi mu vilo vyetu,

witumaje metumo mawaha gono wagetuma lulo.

Na lono wuli mwi'kuwo lyako,

ninga weze no lusungu.

3 Mulungu yakuza kanyhi ku wulawa Edomu,

Mulungu mono yelile yakuza

ku wulawa ku matunda ge Palani.

Lukumyo lwakwe lugubice wulanga,

ne yisi yamemile lukumyo lwakwe.

4 Kazaga hamonga no wuzelu we limuli,

no wuzelu wubulilaga

ku wulawa mu muwoko wakwe,

baho ho vihundikwe vilungo vyakwe.

5 Mwene kalongozaga wutamwa

ku mwande yakwe,

kanyhi kalilajilizaga lifwa lize mu nyuma yayo.

6 Hono yakwima,

yisi yikusinjisika,

na mu wulanje wakwe,

makungugo gakucicima.

Ne matunda ge cibitilila gakubeneka.

Ne vigongo vye cibitilila vikudodomela hasi,

vigongo vyono mwene yakolaga mu vilo vye katali.

7 Ane nghawona wanhu wa Kushani wali

mu magayo ne wanhu wa Midiyani wakucicima.

8 Alo Muwaha Mulungu kwali

ko gali makolongo gono gakaleka agwe wukolwe 'kuwo?

Hamba kwali ko lili lilenga

lyono likukozize agwe likuwo?

Agwe wajendelaga mucanyha ye mavunde,

mavunde gali yo mituka yako yo wulugu,

lono wawaletela wanhu wako wusume.

9 Ale wiwikanize wuwitumamile wupinde wako,

ho wuwe wiwikanize

ku wugumila zisonga zako.

Ne mamuli gako gakuyidemula yisi.

10 Ne matunda nago,

kolo gakukuwona, gakucicima,

ne malenga nago,

gakumwajika ku wulawa ku wulanga,

ne malenga gono gali hasi ye'si,

gakukoloma hawaha.

11 Na mu wung'alale

wo wugumile we zisonga zako,

no wung'alale wo wuzelu we migoha yako,

lizuwa no mwezi vyene

vyo vimile du ha wikalo wavyo.

12 Na mwi'kuwo lyako wukakola mu yisi,

na mu wukali wako,

wukawapondanjiza makungugo.

13 Wukabita kuwaponya wanhu wako,

no kumuponya mutemi wako

mono wamuhagula.

Wukawagwisa hasi walongola we wabi,

wukawapondanjiza i watyatyi wakwe.

14 Ne zisonga zako,

zikamuguma musilikale mulongola

we wasilikale wakwe wo wulugu,

lono wakuza nhe 'wingo yiwaha

ho waciyagaze 'se,

wakucisimanga nhe wanhu

wono wakuwewonela

i waciwa mu nzila ye cinyele.

15 Walikanyanjiza lilenga ne zifwalasi zako,

sunga ne malenga gakatumba.

16 Nane nghuzihulika zizi zose,

kuno nghucicima,

no mulomo wangu wuleceye wukucicima so woga.

No muwili wangu wose wanyong'onyela,

sunga ne magulu gangu nhondo,

gakucicima lono nimile.

Ninga ne, nolindila du nhulile,

sunga lizuwa lize

lyono Mulungu kolowalipiciza wose

wono wakuwa kucihoma.

17 Ninga hamba mikuyu yilece kubonhola maluwa,

ne zizabibu yilece kulela zabibu,

hamba mizeyituni nayo yiwe si yikujela, zeyituni,

nayo migunda yiwe si yikulavya wuhemba,

hamba ne zingholo nazo ziwe zose zafwiza,

ne mabululu ge zimitugo geza gali bwete.

18 Ane nosangalala no kusecelela

vyono Muwaha Mulungu yo muponyi wangu.

19 U Muwaha Mulungu yo kamhelaga 'ne vilungo.

Kayinozaga migulu yangu yiwa nhe migulu ye nzela,

kanyhi kamhelaga vilungo

vyo kukolela mu canyha ye matunda.
