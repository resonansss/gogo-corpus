Wuwale 1

Wuwalwe wulongozi we Waisilayeli

1 Mwi'zuwa lilongozi lya mu mwezi we kejete, wa mu mwaka we kejete, ku wulawa lizuwa lyono Waisilayeli wawucila ku Misili, Muwaha Mulungu yakalongana na Musa, mwi 'Hema lyakwe lyono Lyelile, lono yali yali mu nyika ya Sinayi. Muwaha Mulungu yalonga yakatya,
2 “Agwe Musa hamonga na Haluni, muwawale wanhu wose wa Isilayeli mu nghango vyono wenha mu milango yawo na mu mhuga zawo. Mwandice matagwa ge we cilume wose, wono waficile myaka makumi mejete,
3 no kuciza wono mowona wakwiliye kwinjila mu mulimo wo wusilikale wo kwihoma ku wulugu.
4 Agwe na Haluni, muwalombaje walongola monga monga, mu wulawa mu milango yose ye Waisilayeli, nawo wawo wo womutazenyi mu mulimo wuwo.”
5 Na agano go matagwa ge walongola wa mu milango yawo; wono mokwitaziliza nawo mu mulimo wuwo.

Wo mulango wa Lubeni, umulongola yo Elizuli

mwana wa Shedewuli.

6 Wo mulango wa Simiyoni, u mulongola yo

Shelumiyeli mwana wa Zulishadayi.

7 Wo mulango wa Yuda, u mulongola yo Nashoni

mwana wa Abinadabu.

8 Wo mulango wa Isakali, u mulongola yo Nataneli

mwana wa Zuwali.

9 Wo mulango wa Zabuloni, u mulongola yo

Eliyabi, mwana wa Heloni.

10 Wo mulango wa Efulayimu, u mulongola yo

Elishama mwana wa Amihudi.

11 Wo mulango wa Benyamini, u mulongola yo

Abidani mwana wa Gidiyoni.

12 Wo mulango wa Dani, u mulongola yo

Ahiyezeli, mwana wa Amishadayi.

13 Wo mulango wa Asheli, u mulongola yo

Pagiyeli mwana wa Ocilani.

14 Wo mulango wa Gadi, u mulongola yo

Eliyasafu, mwana wa Dewuyeli.

15 Wo mulango wa Nafutali, u mulongola yo

Ahila mwana wa Enani.

16 Awa wo wacemwa mu wulawa mu zimhuga zose, ze milango yose ye wasogo zawo, ho wawe walongola wa mu milimo yawo.

17 Ne wanhu wawa we cilume we likumi na wejete, wakawataza muno Musa na Haluni.
18 Wakawakongholela hamonga mhuga yose ya Waisilayeli, mwi'zuwa lilongozi lyo mwezi we kejete. Wakawa kuwandika Waisilayeli wose, mu nghango vyono wenha mu milango yawo, na mu mhuga zawo. Wakawandika we cilume wose wono waficile myaka makumi mejete, hamonga na wose wono wena myaka yono yicizize yiyo, wakandikwa no kuwalwa liwalo lyawo,
19 nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yali yalajilize. Musa yakawawala wanhu no kuwandika, lono yali yali mwi 'nyika lya Sinayi.

20 Nawo i wanhu we cilume wose wono wali we lizika lya myaka makumi mejete hamba kuciza, wono wawonwa wakwiliye kwinjila mu mulimo wo wusilikale wo kwihoma ku wulugu, wose matagwa gawo gakandikwa, mu nghango vyono wenha mu milango yawo na mu mhuga zawo, wakamulilwa we mhuga ya Lubeni u mwana wa Yakobo, u mulongozi kulelwa. Ne liwalo lyawo lyoli:
21 Wo mulango wa Lubeni, wali waficile elufu makumi mane na mutandatu, na magana mahano.

22 Wo mulango wa Simiyoni, wali waficile
23 elufu makumi mahano ne cenda, na magana madatu.

24 Wo mulango wa Gadi, wali waficile,
25 elufu makumi mane ne 'hano, na magana mutandatu na makumi mahano.

26 Wo mulango wa Yuda, wali waficile,
27 elufu makumi mupunghati na yine, na magana mutandatu.

28 Wo mulango wa Isakali, wali waficile
29 elufu makumi mahano na yine, na magana mane.

30 Wo mulango wa Zabuloni, wali waficile,
31 elufu makumi mahano na mupunghati, na magana mane.

32 Wo mulango wa Yusufu, wo wana wa Efulayimu, wali waficile,
33 elufu makumi mane na magana mahano.

34 Wo mulango wa Manase, wali waficile
35 elufu makumi madatu na nyejete na magana mejete.

36 Wo mulango wa Benyamini, wali waficile,
37 elufu makumi mutandatu ne 'hano, na magana mane.

38 Wo mulango wa Dani, wali waficile,
39 elufu makumi madatu na nyejete, na magana mupunghati.

40 Wo mulango wa Asheli, wali waficile,
41 elufu makumi mane na monga na magana mahano.

42 Wo mulango wa Nafutali, wali waficile,
43 elufu makumi mahano na yidatu, na magana mane.

44 Ne liwalo lyawo wose hamonga wali waficile
45 wanhu elufu magana mutandatu na yidatu,
46 na magana mahano, na makumi mahano.

47 Na mwi'walo lilino, Walawi si wawalilizwa yaye wahanzwe na we milango yinji.
48 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yamulonjela Musa vyono,
49 lono weza kuwawala wanhu wono wokwila kwinjila mu mulimo wo wusilikale, wulece kuwenjiza wo mulango wa Lawi mu mulimo wuwo.
50 Langa wene Walawi, wawikaje wawe walanjilizi du we Lihema lyangu lyono Lyelile, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose vyono vili mumo. Wawe kwitumama mumo mugati, kanyhi wakome mahema gawo walipilime Lihema lyono Lyelile.
51 Na mu cipango co cose lono mweza kuyihamya nghambi yenyu, wawo Walawi wowa wo mulimo wawo kulimwagula Lihema. Kanyhi wo weneco du wono wowa kulikoma Lihema lyono Lyelile kolo mwafika hanhu hono moyisimya nghambi yenyu. Kanyhi mulanjilile, hono munhu wo wose yejelele yasejelele behi ne Lihema lyono Lyelile, munhu yuyo yawulagwe.
52 Ne Waisilayeli nawo, mulimo wawo wowa kukoma mahema gawo vibuto vibuto, munhu na mhuga yakwe, munhu na bendela yakwe.
53 Ninga Walawi wanojeye wakome mahema gawo gapilime Lihema ho wasume kulidima, munhu wo wose ho yalece kulisejelela Lihema no kunghoza likuwo, nane niwatowe mhuga ya Isilayeli.
54 Nawo wanhu Waisilayeli wakanoza nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.


Wuwale 2

Nzila yo wukoma mahema, wanhu no mulango wawo mu nghambi

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakawalajiza Musa na Haluni nzila yono yiwanojeye wayityatye, lono weza kukoma mahema mu nghambi, yakatya,
2 Kolo Waisilayeli weza kukoma mahema mu nghambi, yiwanojeye munhu na lwakwe, mu mulango wakwe, yakome lihema lyakwe hasi ye bendela, i hanhu hono yalajiywe. Kanyhi yalajile ne citanjilo ce wasogwe. Ne mahema ganojeye gakomwe gawe gapilime mbavu zose ze Lihema lyono Lyelile, viwe vidomile ne Lihema.

3 Na ku lubavu lwe cilima, wawo wono wali hasi ye bendela ya wo mulango wa Yuda, hamonga na wono vyokwihegama nawo, yiwanojeye wakome mahema gawo wawe wegawile mu vipuga vipuga, mu nghango ye liwalo lye walongola wawo, mu zimhuga zawo nha vyono nhavi.

4 Wo mulango wa Yuda, u mulongola yo Nahashoni

mwana wa Aminadabu, wanhu elufu makumi mupunghati

na yine, na magana mutandatu.

5 Wo mulango wa Isakali, u mulongola yo Nataneli

mwana wa Zuwali,

6 wanhu elufu makumi mahano na yine,

na magana mane.

7 Wo mulango wa Zabuloni, u mulongola yo Eliyabi

mwana wa Heloni,

8 wanhu elufu makumi mahano na mupunghati na

magana mane.

9 Ne liwalo lyawo wose hamonga wono wawalwa

wa mu nghambi ya Yuda, wali waficile wanhu

elufu yigana limonga na makumi munana na

mutandatu, na magana mane.

Nawo wawo wo walongole kutuluka.

10 Nawo wono wali hasi ye bendela ya wo mulango

wa Lubeni, hamonga na wono vyokwihegama nawo,

yiwanojeye wakome mahema

gawo ku lubavu lwe takama,

wawe wegawile mu vipuga vipuga, mu nghango ye

liwalo lye walongola wawo mu zimhuga zawo

nha vyono nhavi.

11 Wo mulango wa Lubeni u mulongola yo Elizuli

mwana wa Shedewuli, wanhu elufu makumi mane

na mutandatu na magana mahano. Vihegame na:

12 Wo mulango wa Simiyoni, u mulongola yo Shelumiyeli

mwana wa Zelishadayi,

13 wanhu elufu makumi mahano ne 'cenda na magana madatu.

14 Wo mulango wa Gadi, u mulongola yo Eliyasafu

mwana wa Lewuyeli,

15 wanhu elufu makumi mane na yihano, na

magana mutandatu, na makumi mahano.

16 Ne liwalo lyawo wose hamonga, waficile

wanhu elufu yigana limonga na makumi mahano

na monga, na magana mane na makumi mahano.

Nawo wawo, wo weze we kejete ku wutuluce.

17 Hanyuma yayo, kolo wawo we zimhuga zi nyejete,

mhuga ya Yuda ne mhuga ya Lubeni, wakondya kutuluka,

ale, we mhuga ye Walawi nawo ho wawe kutyatya mu nyuma

yawo, akuno walidinghuye ne Lihema lyono Lyelile.

Kanyhi yinojeye mhuga zose, wajende wetyatizye,

nha vyono winha wityatye wawo wo wukoma

mahema gawo mu nghambi, wanhu na mhuga yawo

hasi ye bendela yawo.

18 Nawo wono wali hasi ye bendela ya

wo mulango wa Efulayimu, yiwanojeye wakome mahema

gawo ku lubavu lwe mwezi, hamonga na wono vyokwihegama

nawo, wawe wegawile vipuga

vipuga, mu nghango ye liwalo lye walongola

wawo, mu zimhuga zawo nha vyono nhavi.

19 Wo mulango wa Efulayimu, u mulongola yo

Elishama mwana wa Amihudi,

wanhu elufu makumi mane na magana mahano. Vihegame na:

20 Wo mulango wa Manase, u mulongola yo Gamaliyeli

mwana wa Pedazuli,

21 wanhu elufu makumi madatu na nyejete na magana mejete.

22 Wo mulango wa Benyamini, u mulongola yo Abidani

mwana wa Gidiyoni,

23 wanhu elufu makumi madatu na yihano, na magana mane.

24 Ne liwalo lyawo wose hamonga waficile wanhu

elufu yigana limonga na munana, ne 'gana limonga.

Nawo wawano wo we kadatu mu wutuluce.

25 Nawo wono wali hasi ye bendela ya wo mulango

wa Dani, na wono vyokwihegama nawo, yiwanojeye

wakome mahema gawo ku lubavu lwe sumuka,

weze wegawile vipuga vipuga, mu nghango ye

liwalo lye walongola wawo, mu milango yawo

nha vyono nhavi.

26 Wo mulango wa Dani, u mulongola yo Ahiyezeli

mwana wa Amishadayi, wanhu elufu makumi mutandatu

na nyejete, na magana mupunghati. Vihegame na:

27 Wo mulango wa Asheli, u mulongola yo Pagiyeli

mwana wa Ocilani

28 wanhu elufu makumi mane na monga, na magana mahano.

29 Wo mulango wa Nafutali, u mulongola yo Ahila mwana

wa Enani,

30 wanhu elufu makumi mahano na yidatu,

na magana mane.

31 Ne liwalo lyawo wose hamonga, waficile wanhu

elufu yigana limonga na makumi mahano na mupunghati,

na magana mutandatu. Nawo wa wano weze wo we

ndumilizo kutuluka.

32 Ne liwalo lye wanhu Waisilayeli, wose wono wandikwa mu nghango ye zi mhuga zawo, cipuga na cipuga, wakawa waficile liwalo lyawo, wanhu elufu magana mutandatu na yidatu, na magana mahano na makumi mahano.
33 Ninga Walawi si wenjila mwi'walo hamonga ne Waisilayeli.

34 Nawo Waisilayeli, wakanoza zose nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa. Wose wakakoma mahema gawo wanhu na bendela yawo. Na lono watulukaga, wajendaga wanhu na mhuga zawo ze wasogo zawo.


Wuwale 3

I wana wa Haluni

1 Awa wali zo ndelo za Haluni na Musa, lono Muwaha Mulungu yalongana na Musa he 'tunda lya Sinayi.
2 Haluni yali yena wana wane, mwana mulongozi kulelwa yali Nadabu, hanyuma ho Abihu, na Eleyezali, na Itamali.
3 Ne wana wawano nawo, waketilwa mafuta go witile, wakasiwanulwa wawe wakohani.
4 Ninga Nadabu na Abihu wakawulagwa, lono wamulavizya Muwaha Mulungu moto wono si wali welile, lono wali wali mu nyika ya Sinayi. Kanyhi wene wali wasina wana. Holeka Eleyezali hamonga na Itamali, wo wajendelela kwituma mulimo wo wukohani mu madaha gose, lono sogo yawo Haluni yali cawumi.

Walawi wakuwikwa wawetumamile wakohani

5 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
6 “Wasejelezaje wo mulango wa Lawi, wuwawice wamwitumamile Haluni mukohani.
7 Wetume milimo yose yono yikusakwa so Lihema lyono Lyelile, ho walanjilile u wilecele we wakohani mu milimo yawo, hamonga no wilecele we mhuga yose.
8 Nawo yiwanojeye walanjilile no kuvikamisa vitumamilwa vyose vyono vili mwi 'Hema lyono Lyelile, webate no wulindilizi we wanhu Waisilayeli.
9 Ninga mulimo monga du wono Walawi wanojeye wawitume, wo mulimo wo kumwitumamila Haluni du, hamonga ne wanagwe.
10 Nagwe wuwawice Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wo wetume mulimo wuwo wo wukohani. Ninga hono munhu yunji wo wose yejelele yetume mulimo wuwo, munhu yuyo yawulagwe.”

11 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
12 “Ku wulawa lulu, awano Walawi, weza wanhu wangu. Hambi nha, lono nawawulajila i wana walongozi kulelwa we Wamisili, nha ho nawasiwanulila wawano Walawi, wawe yo mwanangu mulongozi kulelwa kuli mhuga zose ze Waisilayeli, sunga na kuli nyana zinongozi kulelwa ze mitugo.
13 Holeka luno, hanhu ho kuwa ne wana wangu i walongozi kulelwa mu wana we Waisilayeli, alu nakondya kuwa na wawano Walawi, wo wowa wanhu wangu. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”

U wuwalwe we Walawi

14 Na lono wali wali mu nyika ya Sinayi, Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajiliza Musa,
15 yawawale Walawi, yawawale mu nghango ye wasogo zawo ne zimhuga zawo, kanyhi yawandice wana wose we cilume wono waficila mwezi monga, sunga na wono wacizize.
16 Nayo Musa yaketuma nha vyono yalajilizwa no Muwaha Mulungu.
17 Nayo Lawi yali yena wana wadatu, Gelishoni, Kohati, na Melali. Wawa wali wo wa kuku za munhu, mu milango ye wasogo zawo.
18 Nayo Gelishoni, yakawa na wana we wejete, Libuni na Shimeyi.
19 Nayo Kohati, yakawa na wana we wane, Amulamu, Izihali, Hebuloni na Uziyeli.
20 Nayo Melali yakawa na wana wejete, Mahili na Mushi. Nawo wawa wakawa wo we mhuga ze walawi ku mhuga zawo.

21 Nawo we mhuga ya Gelishoni, wali wo mwamulizo we zimhuga zi nyejete, mhuga ye Walibuni, ne mhuga ye Washimeyi.
22 Ne liwalo lye wana we cilume wono wandikwa, wono waficile mwezi monga na wono wacizize, wakawa waficila elufu mupunghati na magana mahano.
23 Ne mhuga yiyo, wo wakawa wanojeye wakome mahema gawo ku lubavu lwe mwezi, ku mhilimo ye Lihema lyono Lyelile,
24 wakawa hamonga na Eliyasafu u mwana wa Layeli, u mulongola wawo wa mu mhuga yiyo ye Wagelishoni.
25 Nawo wakawa wo walanjilizi we Lihema, hamonga ne luwa yayo, ne Lihema ne ligubiko lyayo, no wucitizo wa ha mulango we Lihema.
26 Hamonga ne myenda ye lijinji lye luwa, hamonga ne cikumbicilo, ne myenda ya ha mulango we luwa. Nawo wo walanjilizi wa mu witumamilwe we vinhu vivino vyose.

27 Nawo we mhuga ya Kohati, wali wo mwamulizo we zi mhuga za Amulamu na Izihali na Hebuloni na Uziyeli. Wawa wakawa wo we mhuga ye Wakohati.
28 Ne liwalo lye wana we cilume wono wandikwa, wono waficila mwezi monga na wono wacizize, wakawa waficile elufu munana na magana mutandatu.
29 Na we mhuga yiyo wo wakawa wanojeye wakome mahema gawo ku lubavu lwe takama ye Lihema,
30 wakawa hamonga na Elizafani, u mwana wa Uziyeli, u mulongola wawo mu mhuga yiyo ye Wakohati.
31 Nawo wakawa wo walindilizi we Lisanduku lye Cilagane, ne meza, hamonga ne tala, ne vikumbicilo, ne viya vyono vyelile, vyono wetumamilaga wakohani i Hanhu hono helile, ne libupa lyo mwenda, lyono litemanuye i Hanhu hono helile. Kanyhi wakawa wo walanjilizi wo wuhembi wose wono i viya vivyo vyetumamilwaga.

32 Nayo Eleyezali u mwana wa Haluni mukohani, yali yo muwaha wa mu wawaha we Walawi. Nayo yali yo mulanjilizi wa wose wono wali wena wilecele wo kwituma mumo mu Hanhu hono helile.

33 Nawo we mhuga ya Melali, wali wo mwamulizo we zi mhuga za Mahili na za Mushi.
34 Ne liwalo lye wana we cilume wono wandikwa, wono waficila mwezi monga na wono wacizize, wakawa waficile elufu mutandatu na magana mejete.
35 Ne mhuga yiyo, wo wakawa wanojeye wakome mahema gawo ku lubavu lwe sumuka ye Lihema. Nawo wakawa hamonga na Zuliyeli u mwana wa Abihayili, u mulongola wawo mu mhuga yiyo.
36 Nawo wakawa wo walindilizi we zifulemu ze Lihema, hamonga ne mahapa gayo, ne micici yayo, ne masumbili gayo, ne mawicilo gayo gose. Kanyhi wakawa wo walanjilizi wa mu witumamilwe we vinhu vivyo vyose.
37 Kanyhi wakawa wo walanjilizi we masumbili gose, ne mawicilo gayo, ne zimambo zayo, ne milejehe yayo.

38 Nayo Musa hamonga na Haluni ne wanagwe, wene wakakoma mahema gawo ku lubavu lwe cilima, ku mwande ye Lihema. Nawo wakawa wo wetumami wo kulongoza wuhembi, so wanhu wa Isilayeli mu Hanhu hono helile. No munhu yunji wo wose mono yasaka yejelele yalongoze wuhembi, munhu yuyo yawulagwa.

39 Ne liwalo lye Walawi wana we cilume wono wali waficile mwezi monga, na wono wacizize, wono Musa yawandika mu nghango nha vyono wenha mu mhuga zawo, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yali yalajiliza, wali waficile wanhu elufu makumi mejete na nyejete cititila.

Walawi wakuhomela wulongola wo wulelwe we wana walongozi kulelwa

40 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “I wana wose we cilume we Waisilayeli, awo wangu. Holeka wandice matagwa gawo wose i wana we cilume we Waisilayeli. I walongola kulelwa, wono waficila mwezi monga na wono wacizize.
41 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, ane nghuwanoza i Walawi wose, wo wahomele hanhu hawo, wo wakuwa wanhu wangu! Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu! Kanyhi sunga ne mitugo ze Walawi zose, nazo nhavivyo, nghuzinoza zihomele hanhu ha mitugo zinongozi kulelwa ze Waisilayeli.”
42 Nayo Musa, yakawa du ne cihuliko, yakawa kuwandika i wana we cilume wose i walongozi kulelwa,
43 wono wali waficila mwezi monga na wono wali wacizize. Ne liwalo lyawo likawa lificile elufu makumi mejete na nyejete, na magana mejete na makumi mupunghati na wadatu.

44 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela kanyhi Musa yakatya,
45 “Alu, wasiwanulaje Walawi wawe wanhu wangu, wahomele hanhu he wana we cilume i walongozi kulelwa we Waisilayeli. Kanyhi zisiwanule sunga ne mitugo ze Walawi zihomele hanhu ha mitugo zinongozi kulelwa ze Waisilayeli.
46 Ninga vyono yijeye, ko wali wana we cilume we Waisilayeli magana mejete na makumi mupunghati na wadatu, wono wacizize mwi'walo lya muli awalya i wana we cilume we Walawi. Holeka i wana wawo langa, kosuma kuwakomboza.
47 Nagwe wulavye wukombozwe wa fweza yihano yihano so mwana monga monga, mu nghango ye mapesa gono gakwitumamilwa mwi 'Hema lyono Lyelile.
48 Ne mapesa gago mupelaje Haluni ne wanagwe.”
49 Nayo Musa yakawa ne cihuliko, yakawa kusola,
50 fweza elufu monga na magana madatu na makumi mutandatu na yihano,
51 yakawa kumupela Haluni ne wanagwe.


Wuwale 4

Milimo ye Walawi, we mhuga ye Wakohati

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
2 “Yawawale Walawi wose we mhuga ye Wakohati, mu nghango ye milango ye wasogo zawo ne zimhuga zawo,
3 yawandice wanhu we cilume wose wono waficile ku wulawilila myaka makumi madatu sunga makumi mahano, wono wowa wanojeye ku wituma milimo ya mwi 'Hema lyono Lyelile.
4 Hambi witumami wawo nha wowa wuli mu vinhu vyono vyelile.”

5 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajiliza Musa yakatya, “Kolo weza kuwuka mu nghambi yiyo, ale, Haluni ne wanagwe we cilume, wanojeye wenjile mwi 'Hema, wapambule libupa lyo mwenda lyono lili ha mwande ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane. Wawe kulivunjiliza Lisanduku lye Cilagane no mwenda wuwo.
6 Hanyuma wawe kuligubika ne nguwo yono yitelemeye, kanyhi wawe kuwugubika no mwenda we liwala lye buluu mucanyha yalyo. Hanyuma ho wawe kugakoza mu zinyamwidole i mabici go kulidinghulila.

7 “Hanyuma kanyhi, wasole meza ye mikate milavye kuli Muwaha Mulungu, watandice mucanyha yayo mwenda we liwala lye buluu, ho wawe kujeleka mucanyha yayo viya vyayo, nha cinji visahani vyo kuwicila wuvumba, ne vikombe, hamonga ne vidumu, vyo kujidila vilavywa vye divayi. Kanyhi yinojeye mikate yiwe miwice mucanyha ye meza nhondo zose.
8 Hanyuma ho wawe kuvigubiciza vinhu vivyo vyose no mwenda we liwala lidunghu, hanyuma ho wawe kugubika mucanyha yayo ne nguwo swanu yono yitelemeye. Hanyuma wawe kugakoza mu zi nyamwidole i mabici go kuyidinghulila.

9 “Hanyuma kanyhi, wasole mwenda we liwala lye buluu, wawuvunjilize wuwiko we tala, ne zitala zayo, ne mibato yayo, ne visahani vyayo, hamonga ne viya vyayo vyose vyo kuwicila mafuta.
10 Ne vinhu vivyo vyose, wavivunjilizaje mu nguwo swanu yono yitelemeye, na hanyuma wawe kuvijeleka mu wudinghulilo wavyo.

11 “Walaza kuwa wazikondya zizo, ale, wasole kanyhi mwenda we liwala lye buluu, wawugubice mucanyha ye cikumbicilo ce zahabu, wawe kucigubika mucanyha yayo ne nguwo swanu yono yitelemeye, hanyuma wawe kugakoza mu zinyamwidole zayo i mabici go kucindinghulila.
12 Hanyuma wawe kusola viya vyose vyono vikwitumamilwa i Hanhu hono helile, wawe kuvipalinjiza vyose hamonga mu mwenda we liwala lye buluu, hanyuma wawe kuvivunjiliza mu nguwo swanu yono yitelemeye, wawe kuvijeleka mu wudinghulilo wavyo.
13 Hanyuma wawe kugawusa mavu mu cikumbicilo, wawe kutandika mwenda we liwala lyo lugandu mucanyha yaco.
14 Hanyuma wawe kupanga mucanyha yaco i vinhu vyose vyono vitumamilwaga mu wuhembi mu cikumbicilo cico, nha cinji, vyungu so mavu, ne zikoleyo, ne mabeseni, ne vicinyilo, hamonga ne visahani vyo kupalila moto. Hanyuma wawe kuvivunjiliza mu nguwo swanu vyono yitelemeye, wawe kugakoza mu zi nyamwidole i mabici gayo go kucidinghulila.
15 Nayo Haluni ne wanagwe, kolo wakondya kuvivunjiliza no kuviwopeleza vinhu vyose, vyono vikwitumamilwa i Hanhu hono helile, lono weza kutuluka wakulawa mu nghambi yiyo, ale, i wana wa Kohati, wo wavidinghule i viya vivyo. Langa walanjilile walece kuvipamiza viya vivyo, na hono wejelele du wavipamize, wofwa.

“No mulimo wuwo wo wudinghuzi we viya vivyo, wowa wo mulimo wawo du i wana we Wakohati, ho hose hono mowa kulihamizya Lihema lyono Lyelile.
16 Nayo Eleyezali u mwana wa Haluni mukohani, yo kowa mulanjizi we Lihema lyose, ne mafuta go kwasiza zitala, no wuvumba, ne cilavywa co wusaje, ne mafuta go witile, hamonga ne vinji vyose vyono visiwanuywe ku so Muwaha Mulungu vyono vili mwi 'Hema mugati.”

17 Muwaha Mulungu yakawalonjela Musa na Haluni yakawatya,
18 “Mulece kuwaleceleza wawa i wana wa Kohati wafwe,
19 so wusejelela behi ne vinhu vivi vyono vyelile. Na hono musace muyimile mbali yiyi ho yilece kuwejelela, yinojeye Haluni ne wanagwe, wawe kwinjila weneco mugati ye Lihema, wawa kuwagawila wawo wana we Wakohati, munhu na mutegulo wakwe, wono kodinghula, wawalajile ne cidinghulile cayo.
20 Kanyhi mulanjilile, vyono hono wejelele wenjile du mwi Hema, no kuvilanga viya vivyo vyono vyelile, lono Wakohani wakali wakuviwopeleza, mbeka wofwa.”

U mulimo we Walawi, i wana we Wagelishoni

21 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa,
22 “Yawawale Walawi i wana we Wagelishoni, mu nghango ye milango yawo vyono yinha ku wasogo zawo, mu mhuga zawo.
23 Yawandice wanhu wose we cilume wono waficila ku wulawilila myaka makumi madatu sunga myaka makumi mahano. Wono wowa wakwiliye ku wituma milimo ya mwi Hema lyono Lyelile.
24 Nawo wowa wo wadinghuzi we viya avi vyono vityatizye.
25 Lihema lyono Lyelile, ne ligubiko lyayo lya mugati yayo, ne ligubiko lyayo lya kunze yayo, ne nguwo swanu yono yitelemeye, yono yikugubikwa mucanyha yayo, hamonga no wucitizo wa ha mulango we Lihema lyono Lyelile,
26 ne myenda misimye ye lijinji, lye luwa ye Lihema lyono Lyelile, ne cikumbicilo, ne myenda ya ha mulango we luwa, hamonga ne milejehe yose yono yikwitumamilwa mu wukoma Lihema lyono Lyelile. Nawo wo wetume zose zono zikusakwa mu mulimo wa muli viya vivyo.
27 Nayo Musa na Haluni, wowa wo walanjilizi wono wolanjilila no kuwona vyono, i wana we Wagelishoni, wetuma zose, wadinghula ne vinhu vyose vyono Haluni ne wanagwe wawalonjeye wadinghule.
28 Na wuwu wo mulimo we wana we wa Gelishoni wa mumo mwi 'Hema lyono Lyelile. Nawo wokwituma zizo zose mu wutonji wa Itamali u mwana wa Haluni mukohani.”

U mulimo we Walawi, i wana we mhuga ya Melali

29 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa, “Yawawale Walawi i wana we Wamelali, mu nghango ye milango yawo vyono yinha ku wasogo zawo, mu mhuga zawo.
30 Yawandice wanhu wose we cilume wono waficila ku wulawilila myaka makumi madatu sunga myaka makumi mahano. Wono wowa wakwiliye ku wituma milimo ya mwi 'Hema lyono Lyelile.
31 Nawo wawo wo wowa wo wadinghuzi we zifulemu, ne mahapa gayo, ne masumbili gayo, ne mawicilo ge Lihema.
32 Hamonga ne masumbili gono gapilime Lihema, ne zimambo zayo ne milejehe yo kuwopela no kulipilimya Lihema lyose. Hamonga ne milejehe yose yono yikwitumamilwa so wukoma Lihema. Kanyhi yinojeye munhu wowose, yeze kudinghula cinhu cico cimonga du nhondo zose, yalece kukawaniza kawaniza.
33 No mulimo wuwuno wo wuwanojeye wawo du i wana wa Melali, mu wuhembi wawo mwi 'Hema lyono Lyelile. Nawo wokwituma zizi zose mu wutonji wa Itamali u mwana wa Haluni mukohani.”

U wuwalwe we Walawi

34 Nawo Musa na Haluni, hamonga ne walongola we zimhuga ze wanhu, wakawa kwanza kuwawala wanhu, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yali yalajilize. Wakawawala i wanhu we zimhuga yidatu za Walawi, nha cinji mhuga ye Wakohati.
35 Wakawawala mu nghango ye milango yawo vyono yinha ku wasogo zawo, mu mhuga zawo. Hanyuma wakawa kuwandika mu citabu, i wanhu wose wono waficila ku wulawilila myaka makumi madatu, sunga myaka makumi mahano. Wono wowa wakwiliye ku wituma milimo ya mwi 'Hema lyono Lyelile.
36 Ne liwalo lyawo munghango ye zimhuga zawo wali waficile wanhu elufu nyejete na magana mupunghati na makumi mahano.

37 Ali lyali lyo liwalo lya we mhuga ye Wakohati wono watumaga mwi 'Hema lyono Lyelile,

38 Wo mulango we Wagelishoni, ne zimhuga zawo,

39 wono wali waficile myaka makumi madatu na we makumi mahano, wono wali wakwiliye kwituma mwi 'Hema lyono Lyelile.

40 Elufu nyejete na magana mutandatu

na makumi madatu.

41 Ali lyali lyo liwalo lya we mhuga ye Wagelishoni wose wono wetumaga mwi Hema lyono Lyelile.

42 Wo mulango we Wamelali, ne zimhuga wazo,

43 wono wali waficile myaka makumi madatu sunga makumi mahano, wono wefumaga mwi 'Hema lyono Lyelile

44 wali waficile

Elufu yidatu na magana mejete cititila.

45 Ali lyali lyo liwalo lya we mhuga ye Wamelali wono Musa nu Haluni wawawala.
46 Ne liwalo lyawo wose hamonga, wakawa waficile wanhu elufu munana na magana mahano na makumi munana, wono wali waficile myaka makumi madatu, sunga makumi mahano, wono wali wakwiliye kwituma no kudinghula vinhu vya mwi Hema lyono Lyelile.

47 Ne wanhu wose wakandikwa mu citabu, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.
48 Kanyhi ne wanhu wose, wakagawilwa munhu na mulimo wakwe,
49 u mulimo wono kowa kwituma, hamba cinhuci cono kowa kudinghula.


Wuwale 5

Kuli ze wanhu i wavufywe

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
2 “Wuwalonjele Waisilayeli vyono, munhu wo wose mono kowa yatuga wutamwa wo wukoma, munhu yuyo yimunojeye yawuce mu nghambi yeleje mu wayagwe. Hamba mono yakulavya wufila mu muwili wakwe, hamba mono yapamize muvimba.
3 I wanhu wose we liwala lilyo wono weyavufye, wunojeye wuwakome, wuwategule kunze ye nghambi, ho walece kuyihodanga nghambi, mono nikaye 'ne, hamonga ne wanhu wangu.”
4 Nawo Waisilayeli wakawa na cihuliko, wakawa kuwawusa wose mu nghambi nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.

Vilavywa so wusutye

5 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajiliza Musa,
6 “Yawalonjele Waisilayeli, vyono, Hono munhu wo wose yawe si mwihuwilwa kuli Muwaha Mulungu, ho yamusutizye muyagwe,
7 ale, u munhu yuyo yowa yimunojeye yanene wubi wakwe, kanyhi yalavye kuli munhu yuyo mono yamusutizye, wulavye wono wukwiliye hamonga no wonjeleze wa mekumi mejete kwe 'gana.
8 Na hono lunji munhu yuyo mono kasutizywa yawe yafwiza, na kuno yawe yasina ndugu we behi, mono kusuma kubocela cilavywa cico co wusutye, hodu cilavywa cico, cosuma kulavywa kuli Muwaha Mulungu, ciwe so wakohani. Ne cilavywa ci cino co wusutye, cikuwalilizwa hamonga ne nduwilu yono yitumamiywe so wilamule wo munhu yuyo musutyi.
9 Kanyhi sunga ne vilavywa vyose vyo winulwe, vyono Waisilayeli wakulavya kuli Muwaha Mulungu, vyowa sawo yo mukohani mono wakumulavizya.
10 No mukohani wowose kosuma kusola mweneco i vilavywa vyono wakumulavizya.”

Kuli ze walume, wono si wawetumbiye wagolece zawo

11 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajiliza Musa,
12 “Yawalonjele wanhu Waisilayeli wityatye wuwu. Vyono, lunji munhu si yamwitumbiye mugolece wakwe, yawe yakumuwona nha si mwihuwilwa kuli mwene.
13 Kanyhi yawe kumwihweseza nha vyono lunji yakondya kwiyavufya so wugona no mulume yunji, ninga kanyhi akuno u mulume wakwe, yawe si yamanyiye lukulu. Holeka ku soko ye cevu co mugolece wakwe, cinojele mulume yuyo, yekale nazo du, ziwe za cinyele mu nhumbula yakwe. Hambi nha zisina wuhomelezi wo wose, no mugolece nayo nha si yebatwe mwitumo lilyo.
14 Na lunji, u mulume kawa yena mahwele no mugolece wakwe, ninga kumbe nayo mugolece wakwe vyo mwihuwilwa du.
15 Ale, mu nghani yono yinha yiyi, u mulume yuyo, yowa yimunojeye yamulete mugolece wakwe kuli mukohani. Kanyhi yaleteleze hamonga ne cilavywa cono cosakwa, ca wusaje wa shayili, kilo monga, wono si mujidile mafuta, hamba wuvumba. Hambi aco nha cilavywa cono cikulavywa ku wulawa ko mulume, mono yena wivu we maganigani mabi kuli mugolece wakwe. Naco cilavywa cico, cikulavywa, i mbeka ho yiwe calinze.

16 “Nayo mukohani yamusejeleze yuyo mucekulu, yeme ha mwande ye cikumbicilo.
17 Hanyuma mukohani yasole malenga gono gelile, yagatajile mu ciya ce lilongo, hanyuma kanyhi yasole manghundi, gono gali baho hasi, mugati ye Lihema lyono Lyelile, yagatajile mumo mu malenga, ne malenga gago nago, gokalipa.
18 Hanyuma mukohani yamudalangule zimvwili mucekulu yuyo. Mukohani yasole wusaje wu wulya we cilavywa, yamupele mucekulu yawudinghule mu mawoko gakwe. Nayo mukohani yebate ciya ce lilongo, cono cina malenga gagalya makali, gono gakusuma kumuletela munhu wulijitwe.
19 Hanyuma mukohani yamwilahize yuyo mucekulu ko wilahize awuno yatye, ‘Hono yiwe mbeka si witumile nghani yiyo yo wusenyha, i malenga gaga go kulijitila, si gosuma kukuletela wulijitwe.
20 Ninga hono wuwe kwituma nghani yiyi yo wusenyha,
21 ale, Muwaha Mulungu yakunoze wuwe mulijitwe muli wanhu wako. Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu yakunoze cipwi cako ciwombe, ne 'nda yako yitumbe.
22 Ne malenga gagano, genjile mu'nda yako, gayitumvye, ne cipwi cako ciwombe.’ Nayo mucekulu yuyo yavumize yatye, ‘Heya nghuvumila. Muwaha Mulungu yanhendele nhavivyo.’

23 “Hanyuma mukohani yagandice malijito gaga mu citabu, yawe kugazuguza mandiko gago no kugasuluziza mu ciya ce lilongo mu malenga makali.
24 Na lono mukohani yakali namung'wesa yuyo mucekulu, malenga gago makali, gono gomuletela wusungu wukali,
25 mukohani yamule yawusole wusaje we cilavywa co wivu ku wulawa mu mawoko go mucekulu, yawusinjise hali Muwaha Mulungu, yawe kuwuleta ha cikumbicilo.
26 Yawe kunega yiganza limonga lyo wusaje wuwo, wuwe nha citanjilo ce cilavywa, yawe kuwukongoga mu cikumbicilo. Nayo kolo yazikondya zizo, ho yawe kumung'wesa mucekulu yuyo malenga gago makali.
27 Na hono yiwe mbeka mucekulu yuyo ketuma zo wusenyha, i malenga gago gomupela wusungu wukali muno muno mu 'nda yakwe, ne 'nda yakwe yotumba, ne cipwi cakwe cowomba, nayo kowa wulijite muli wanhu wakwe.
28 Ninga hono yiwe mbeka si yazitumile zizo zo wusenyha, si kosuma kutuga mbali yo yose, kosuma du no kulela wana.

29 “Ale, alino lyo lilajilizo lya kuli zinghani ze liwala lilyo, lono mulume yawe yali wivu, mwenda maganigani mabi kuli mugolece wakwe, no kumwihweseza vyono kagona no mulume yunji.
30 U mucekulu yuyo yaletwe yeme ha cikumbicilo, nayo mukohani konoza wityatye wuwu wo wuhembi.
31 Ninga u mulume wakwe, kowa yasina nghani yaye. Langa u mucekulu hono yawe musutyi, kokwidinghulila wulipicizwe wakwe, mweneco.”


Wuwale 6

Wulajilize wa kuli zo Wunazili

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa,
2 “Yawalonjele wanhu Waisilayeli wulajilize wuwu. Hono munhu wo wose mulume hamba mucekulu, yelahize yawe Munazili, yelavye mweneco kuli Muwaha Mulungu,
3 hodu munhu yuyo yalecele lukulu kung'wa wujimbi hamba divayi. Yalecele lukulu kung'wa cing'wegwa cono cinozigwe mu wulawa mu zabibu, hamba kulya zabibu zono zinyazigwe, hamba zono si zinyazigwe.
4 Na mu vilo vyakwe vyose vyono kojendelela kuwa Munazili, yalecele lukulu kulya cinhu cocose cono cikulawa mu zabibu, hamba zimhece zayo, hamba mabada gayo.

5 “Na mu vilo vyakwe vyose vyono kowa yakali mu wilahize wo kuwa Munazili, yalece kunyonyola zimvwili zakwe, hamba kuziceta yaye. Hambi mwene, nha muwopwe no wilahize mu mapango gose, lono yakali yesiwanuliye kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Holeka yajendelele kuzileka zimvwili zakwe ziwiluce, hamonga ne zindevu zakwe.
6 Hambi zimvwili zakwe, nha co citanjilo co wisiwanulile wakwe, kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
7 Holeka munhu we liwala lilyo, si yimunojeye yeyavufye so wusejelela hono howuli muvimba, hamba wuwe muvimba wo sogwe mulezi, hamba wo nyina, hamba wo lumbulye, hamba wo muwahawe, hamba wo mwanakwawe.
8 Hambi i vilo vyose vyono yakali Munazili nha musiwanulilwe kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

9 “Ne zimvwili zo Munazili zono siwanulwe, hono zavufywe no munhu yunji mono yafwiye musunho behi nayo, nayo ho yeyitipize mutwe wakwe wo wisiwanule wakwe so wufwe wo musunho wo munhu yuyo, ale, yowa yimunojeye yalindile sunga vilo mupunghati vipulute, hanyuma yazinyonyole zimvwili zakwe, yacete ne zindevu zakwe. Nayo ho kowa yezwa yakondya.
10 Na mwi'zuwa lyo munana, yalete nghunda, hamba nziwa nyejete kuli mukohani, ha mulango we Lihema lyono Lyelile.
11 Nayo mukohani yayilavye nziwa hamba nghunda, monga yiwe cilavywa co wubi, ne nzagwe yiwe cilavywa cinyanywe, yamunozeze wilamule so wubi wakwe wo kwibata muvimba. Na mwi'zuwa lilyo du, u munhu yuyo yazisiwanule kanyhi zimvwili zakwe,
12 kanyhi yesiwanulile mweneco kuli Muwaha Mulungu, yawe Munazili mu vilo vyakwe vyose. Ninga i vilo vyakwe vya kunyuma, vyono yali Munazili, si vyowalwa kanyhi, vyono zimvwili zakwe zono zali siwanulwe, nha zali zakondya kuhodanjika. Kanyhi yimunojeye yalete nyana ye ngholo ya mwaka monga yiwe so cilavywa co wusutye.

13 “Nayo Munazili kolo yakondya kumala vilo vyakwe vyo wisiwanule wakwe, ale, yanoze wuhembi. Yimunojeye yabite ha mulango we Lihema lyono Lyelile,
14 yamulavizye Muwaha Mulungu mitugo midatu, nhelevu, zono zisina cilema co cose. Yalete nyana nume monga, ya ngholo ya mwaka monga, yiwe so cilavywa cinyanywe, na nyana nyhekulu monga ya ngholo ya mwaka monga, yiwe so cilavywa co wubi. Hamonga na nduwilu monga ya mwaka monga, yiwe so cilavywa co mulamu.
15 Kanyhi yalavye nhoto monga ya mikate yono yisina cisase, ne maandazi gono gatiwigwe hamonga ne mafuta, na capati zono njidile mafuta, ne vilavywa vyono vikusakwa vyo wusaje hamonga ne divayi.

16 “Nayo mukohani yavilavye vivi vyose kuli Muwaha Mulungu, yalavye ne cilavywa co wubi, hamonga ne cilavywa cinyanywe.
17 Mukohani yamulavizye Muwaha Mulungu likumbiko lya nduwilu, yiwe cilavywa co mulamu. Nayo yayilavye hamonga ne nhoto ye mikate yono yisina cisase. Kanyhi yalavye hamonga ne cilavywa co wusaje ne divayi.
18 Nayo Munazili, lono yali baho ha mulango we Lihema, yazinyonyole zimvwili zakwe zose, yazigumile mucanyha yo moto wono wuli mwi'vungu mono cikunyanyilwa cilavywa co mulamu.

19 “Na kolo ciwoko ce nduwilu catelekwa cakondya kupya, mukohani yacisole no mukate monga mu nhoto, wono wusina cisase, na capati monga, yawe kumwibatiza yuyo Munazili mu mawoko gakwe.
20 Hanyuma mukohani yawe kuvisinjisa hali Muwaha Mulungu, ciwe cilavywa cisinjiswe hali Muwaha Mulungu. Navyo vikuwa vilavywa vyono visiwanuliywe mukohani, hamonga ne nyama ye ng'hwa nyumvywe, hamonga ne cima cimonga ce nduwilu cinulwe, cono mu wulajilize, yo ling'ani yapegwe mukohani. Nazo kolo zakondya zizo, hodu Munazili kosuma kung'wa divayi.
21 Nago gaga go malajilizo ga kuli Wanazili. Na hono munhu Munazili yehuwize kulavya cilavywa, kuciza vyono wukusakwa wihuwize wakwe yalavye, ale, yowa yimunojeye yawufisilize du wihuwizo wakwe nha vyono yehuwize.”

Wulugutize wono wunojeye Wakohani wawalugutize wanhu

22 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajiliza Musa yakatya,
23 “Wumulonjele Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe wutye, lono wakuwalugutiza, wanhu Waisilayeli, wanojeye

wetumamile zinghani azino wawe kutya,

24 ‘Muwaha Mulungu yakulugutize, no kukukamisa

25 Muwaha Mulungu yakubulilile no wuzelu wo wusu wakwe,

kanyhi yawe muswanu kuli'gwe.

26 Muwaha Mulungu yakuwonele lusungu

yakupele mulamu.’ ”

27 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Hono waliwice litagwa lyangu kuli Waisilayeli. Ale nane nowalugutiza.”


Wuwale 7

I vilavywa vye walongola we wanhu

1 Na mwizuwa lyono Musa yakondezya mulimo wo kukoma Lihema lyono Lyelile, hobaho yakawa kulitila Lihema i mafuta go witile, yakalisiwanula, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose, yakacisiwanula ne cikumbicilo, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose.
2 Hanyuma i wawaha we milango, wono wali wo walongola mu milango ye Waisilayeli, wawalya wono wali walanjilizi mu mulimo wo wu wala liwalo lye wanhu,
3 wakaleta vilavywa vyawo kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Waleta mikokoteni mutandatu. Wakawa wakulavya walongola wejete, mukokoteni monga, walongola wejete, mukokoteni monga. Kanyhi wawo walongola, wakalavya na nghongolo yikumi na nyejete, walavya munhu nghongolo monga, munhu nghongolo monga. Nawo wali weza kukondya kulavya vinhu vivyo,
4 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
5 “Ale, wuvibocelaje vipegwa vivyo vitumamilwe mu mulimo we Lihema lyono Lyelile. Nagwe wuwapele Walawi vinhu vivyo ho vitumamilwe mu mulimo wono wukusakwa witumwe.”
6 Musa yakawa kuwapela Walawi i mikokoteni yiyo hamonga ne zinghongolo.
7 Yakawapela we mhuga ye Wagelishoni mikokoteni myejete, hamonga na nghongolo yine.
8 Nawo we mhuga ye Wamelali, yakawapela mikokoteni mine, hamonga na nghongolo munana. Ne milimo yawo yose, yikawa yikwitumwa hasi yo wulanjilizi wa Itamali u mwana wa Haluni u mukohani.
9 Ninga Musa si yawapela mikokoteni hamba nghongolo we mhuga ye Wakohati, vyono wene wali walanjilizi we vinhu vyono vyelile, kanyhi wali wo wadinghuzi we vinhu vivyo, wavipikaga mu mawega gawo.

10 I walongola wa wano, wakaleta kanyhi vilavywa vinji so mulimo wo wuhembi wo kucisiwanula cikumbicilo. Na lono wali wewikaniza ku wulete vilavywa vyawo ku cikumbicilo,
11 Hanyuma Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “U mulongola yumunga yumonga yalavye cilavyocakwe, munhu ne zuwa lyakwe, so wusiwanule we cikumbicilo.”
12 Nayo mono yalavya cilavya cakwe mwi zuwa lilongozi, yali Nasoni u mwana wa Binadabu wo mulango wa Yuda.
13 Ne cilavya cakwe cali sahani monga ya fweza, wuzito wayo kilo monga, na nusu, bakuli monga ya fweza, wuzito wa yogulamu magana munana mu nghango ye cipimo cono cikwitumamilwa mwi hema lyono lyelile, sahani ne bakuli yiyo vyali vimemile wusaje wono wunamuce wono wuhanzahanza ne mafuta, so cilavywa co wuseje.
14 Cisahani cimonga ca fweza wuzito wa yo gulamu yigana limonga ne kumi, cimemile wuvumba.
15 Nzeku monga, nduwilu monga, nyana ya ngholo nume monga ya mwaka monga; so cilavywa linyanywe.
16 Mvulati monga so cilalvywa co wubi!
17 Nzeku nyejete, nduwilu yi hano, mvulati yihano, hamonga na nyana za nghalo nume yihano za mwaka monga, so likambiko lye cilavya co mulamu. Acico cilavywa ca Nasoni mwana wa Aminadabu.
18 Mwi'zuwa lye kejete, yali Nataneli u mwana wa Suwali, u mulongola womulango wa Isakali, cilavywa cakwe,

Wuwale 8

Wuwikwe we zitala

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
2 “Mulonjelaje Haluni, vyono, mu cipango cono kowa yeza kuziwicila zitala mupunghati mu wuwiko wayo, yakumbucile yaziwicile mu nzila yono zowa kumulika lubavu lwa kumwande.”
3 Nayo Haluni yakawa na cihuliko, yakawa kuziwika zitala zikalanga ku mwande yo wuwiko wayo.
4 Nawo wuwiko we tala, wose wali mutyane na zahabu nengu, mu cihwani cono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.

U wezwe no wusiwanulwe we Walawi

5 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
6 “Ale wawagulaje i Walawi muli Waisilayeli, kanyhi, wuweze.
7 Nagwe wuwezeze mu nzila ayi: Wuwamizize malenga go wilamule, wuwalonjele wecete mu miwili yawo mose, wakanze ne myenda yawo. Nawo wowa wezwa.
8 Hanyuma walete nzeku yono yikali ndodo ndodo, hamonga ne cilavywa cono cikusakwa co wusaje wono wunamuce, weze wusaje wono wuhanjile ne mafuta. Nagwe wulete nzeku yinji kanyhi so cilavywa co wubi.
9 Hanyuma wuwakonghole hamonga i mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli. Ho wuwawagule Walawi, wuwawice weme ku mwande ye Lihema lyono Lyelile.
10 Hanyuma i Waisilayeli, wayijelece miwoko yawo mu mitwe ye Walawi.
11 Nayo Haluni ho yawe kuwalavya Walawi wawe cilavywa hali Muwaha Mulungu, cisinjiswe mu wulawa mu Waisilayeli, ho wanyitumamile.
12 Hanyuma kanyhi Waisilayeli ho wayijelece miwoko yawo mu mitwe ya zizo zi nzeku zi nyejete. Nzeku monga ho yiwe so cilavywa co wubi. Nayo ye kejete yiwe so cilavywa cinyanywe kuli Muwaha Mulungu, wuwanozeze Walawi wilamule.

13 “Wawalavye Walawi wawe cilavywa cisinjiswe kuli'ne. Wuwawice Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wawe wo walanjilizi wawo.
14 Nagwe wuwawagulilaje Walawi muli Waisilayeli mu nzila yiyi, ho wawe wanhu wangu.
15 Nagwe kolo wakondya kuweza Walawi, no kuwalavya cilavywa cisinjiswe kuli 'ne, nhondo ho wowa weza wakwiliye, mu wituma mulimo wangu, wa mwi Hema lyono Lyelile.
16 Nane nghondya kuwanoza wahalile hanhu ha wana walongozi kulelwa we Waisilayeli. Holeka awa weza wangu du nene no mweneco.
17 Hambi lono nawawulajila iwana wose walongozi kulelwa we Wamisili, nha ho nawasiwanulila wawe wo wana wangu wo wudagwe, nha cinji walongozi kulelwa wa mu zimhuga zose ze Waisilayeli, sunga na mu zinyana zinongozi kulelwa ze mitugo.
18 Ninga aluno, nghuwasola wawano Walawi, ho wahalile i hanhu ha wana wose we Waisilayeli i walongozi kulelwa.
19 Nane nghuwawika wawa Walawi mu miwoko ya Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wawe nha cipegwa cono cilawile kuli Waisilayeli, ho wetume mulimo wa mwi Hema lyono Lyelile ku soko ye wanhu wa Isilayeli, no kuwanozeza wilamule. Ho wawadime Waisilayeli, ku wulawa mu matamwa ge cimali, gono gosuma kuwaziza Waisilayeli, hono wejelele wasejelele behi ne Hanhu hono helile.”

20 Hobaho, Musa na Haluni, hamonga ne wanhu Waisilayeli, wakawa kuwasiwanula Walawi, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.
21 Nawo Walawi wakawa kwiyeza weneco mu wubi wawo, wakawa kuyikanza ne myenda yawo. Nayo Haluni yakawa kuwalavya wawe cilavywa cisinjiswe hali Muwaha Mulungu. Hanyuma yakawa kuwanozeza wilamule so wezwe wawo.
22 Nawo wanhu wakanoza zose, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa kuli za wawo Walawi. Na mu nzila yiyo, Walawi wakawa wakondya kukwilanizwa so wituma mulimo mwi Hema lyono Lyelile, mu wulanjilizi wa Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe.

23 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
24 “Mu Walawi wose, u munhu mono kowa yakondya kuficila myaka makumi mejete na mihano, yuyo yo kosuma kwinjila mu wilecele wo wituma mulimo wa mwi Hema lyangu lyono Lyelile.
25 Nayo munhu yuyo, yimunojeye yalece kujendelela no mulimo wuwo kolo yaficila lizika lya myaka makumi mahano.
26 Ninga hamba nhavivyo kosuma kujendelela kuwatazataza wayagwe Walawi, mu milimo yawo ya mwi Hema lyono Lyelile. Ninga si yinojeye mwene yalongoze wuhembi wowose mweneco yali wene du. Nagwe nhavivi, vyo kosuma kuyilanjilila milimo ye Walawi.”


Wuwale 9

Pasaka ye kejete

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakalongana na Musa mu nyika ya Sinayi, mu mwezi mulongozi wa mu mwaka we kejete, lono wanhu wa Isilayeli wali wakondya kuwuka ku Misili. Yalonga yakatya,
2 “Mwi'zuwa lye likumi na yine lyo mwezi wuwu, cinyamihe,
3 i wanhu Waisilayeli, wayinoze Pasaka mu nghango nha vyono winha wulajilize, hamonga no wityatye wayo.”
4 Nayo Musa yakawa kuwalonjela wanhu wa Isilayeli wayinoze Pasaka na kuyikamisa.
5 Na mwi'pango lye cinyamihe lya mwizuwa lye likumi na yine lya mu mwezi mulongozi, wakawa kuyinoza Pasaka, lono wali wali mu nyika ya Sinayi. Wanhu wakazinoza zose nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.

6 Ninga mwizuwa lilyo hakawa ho wali wanhu wanji wono wali wakondya kwiyavufya so wupamiza muvimba. Holeka si wasuma kuyinoza yiyo Pasaka mwi'zuwa lilyo. Wawo wakamubitila Musa hamonga na Haluni wakawa kutya,
7 “Ase heya cili wavufywe, hambi nha cipamiza muvimba. Ninga alu, nhawule cikwimilwa, ho cilece kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu cilavywa, hamonga ne Waisilayeli wayetu.”

8 Nayo Musa yakawapitula yakawatya, “Ale lecenyi ta, sunga nhuje lipitulo ku wulawa kuli Muwaha Mulungu.”

9 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa
10 yawalonjele Waisilayeli yawatye nhavi, “Lono muyenyu monga muli nyenye, hamba muli wana wenyu, yaweza mwavufywe so wupamiza muvimba, na lunji mu cipango cico, yali mu muhinzo kutali, ninga akuno ho yena no wuhile wo kuyinoza Pasaka.
11 Ale, howuli wukombolwe wo kuyinoza Pasaka, kolo wakondya kupuluta mwezi monga, ku wuhalize we lizuwa lilyo. Ninga wuyinoze cinyamihe ca mwizuwa lye likumi na yine, lya mu mwezi we kejete. Wuyinozaje hamonga no mukate wono wusina cisase, hamba mboga zono zisina cisungu.
12 Wulece kuyisigaza ndigwa yisigale so mitondo, hamba wulece kubena lifupa lyo lyose lyo mutugo. Langa wunoze Pasaka mu wulunji du wo wulajilize wayo vyono winha.
13 Ninga langa, munhu wo wose mono si yeyavufye, kanyhi hamba si yali mu muhinzo kutali, nayo ho yalece mweneco ne nyono kuyinoza Pasaka. U munhu yuyo, si kowalilizwa hamonga ne wanhu wangu yaye. Hambi nha si yandavizye cilavywa cangu mu cipango cayo. Holeka munhu yuyo kokwidinghulila wulipicizwe wo wubi wakwe mweneco.

14 “Na hono mujenzi mono mwikalane, nayo yawe yena wuhile wo kuyinoza Pasaka, yimunojeye yayinozeze mu wutyatya wulajilize wayo, hamonga no wityatye wayo wose vyono winha. Hambi u wulajilize wuwo, nha wuli so wanhu wose, kuli wanyawusi sunga na kuli wajenzi.”

Livunde lyo Moto

(Wut 40:34-38)

15 Na mwizuwa lyono lyakomelwa Lihema lyono Lyelile, likaza livunde likaligubika. Na mu cilo, livunde lyejelaga nha moto sunga wusiku wukela.
16 Na vyo lyejelaga nhondo zose livunde, lyagubikaga Lihema ku misi, na ku cilo lyejelaga nha moto.
17 Na mu cipango co cose cono livunde lilyo lyanyasulwaga hacanyha ye Lihema, nawo Waisilayeli wayimwagulaga nghambi. Na hanhu hono livunde lyemaga, baho nawo ho wayisimyaga nghambi yawo.
18 Ne wanhu wayimwagulaga nghambi mu wutyatya lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu du. Na mu wulajilize wakwe, wakayisimya nghambi. Na mu nghango yono livunde lyajendelelaga kwikala hacanyha ye Lihema, nawo vyo wajendelelaga kuwa na cihuliko du kuli Muwaha Mulungu, hamba si wawuka hanhu baho yaye.
19 Na mu mapango ganji livunde lyekalaga hacanyha ye Lihema sunga vilo vipuluta vinji. Nawo wajendelelaga kuwa mu nghambi yiyo, hamba kuwuka ng'o, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yawalajilizaga.
20 Mu mapango ganji livunde lyekalaga hacanyha ye Lihema vilo vicefu du, nawo wajendelela kuwa mu nghambi so lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu. Kanyhi wasumaga kutuluka no kuwuka baho, so lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu du.
21 Hanji na hanji, livunde lyekalaga baho ku wulawa cinyamihe sunga hakali mitondo du. Ninga yali yiwanojeye nawo watuluce lulo baho du, lono livunde likunyasulwa. Na mu madaha go gose du lono livunde lyanyasulwaga, nawo wakawa wanojeye watuluce.
22 Hamba hono yisole vilo vyejete, hamba lunji mwezi monga, hamba lunji mwaka monga. Yikawa du nha vyono livunde lyojendelela kwikala hacanyha ye Lihema, nawo si watulukaga yaye, na lono livunde lyanyasulwaga, nawo watulukaga.
23 Wayisimyaga nghambi yawo, no kuyimwagula mu nzila yo wibata malajilizo gono Muwaha Mulungu yawalajiliziza muli Musa.


Wuwale 10

Ndulele za fweza

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
2 “Ale, wunoze ndulele nyejete za fweza yono nhyane. Nagwe wuzitumamile so wucemela wanhu wekongholele hamonga, na so wumwagula nghambi no kutuluka.
3 Na kolo zindulele zose vyono nyejete, zikujendelela kulilila hamonga yidaha mututu, mhuga yose ye wanhu, wekongholele hali 'gwe, ha mulango we Lihema lyangu lyono Lyelile.
4 Ninga langa, hono yilile ndulele monga du yidaha mututu, yiyo yowa so lucemo lwe walongola we milango, wekongholelele hamonga ha mwande yako.
5 Na hono ndulele zose zililile hamonga cidaha cidodo du, ale, we milango ya wono wekaye ku lubavu lwe cilima, watuluce.
6 Na kolo ndulele zikulila kanyhi lwe kejete, cidaha cidodo, ale, we milango ya wono wekaye ku lubavu lwe takama, nawo watuluce. Holeka wumanye vyono, ndulele zose zilile cidaha cidodo, so wumwagula nghambi no kutuluka.
7 Ninga lono wukuwacema mhuga yose hamonga, zindulele zilile sunga yidaha mututu.
8 Nazo zindulele, zeze kutowa ne wana wa Haluni i wakohani.

“Kanyhi no wulajilize wuwu, wunojeye wukamiswe mu zindelo zenyu, sunga na mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza.
9 Mukumbucile vyono, hono wulugu wuwe wenjila mu yisi yenyu, nanye muwe kwihoma ne walugu wenyu wawo wono wakumwiwonelenyi, ale, mutowe zindulele ciwe co citanjilo ca vyono wulugu wenjila. Nane, no Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, nomuletelenyi wutazi, kanyhi nomuponyenyi mu wulawa mu miwoko ye walugu wenyu.
10 Na mu zi nyemo ze zi sikukuu zenyu, nha vyono mu myamulizo ye myezi, sunga na mu vilo vye zi sikukuu zinji zose ze ci dini. Mweze kutowa zindulele zizino, lono mweza kulavya vilavywa vyenyu vinyanywe, hamonga ne vilavywa vyenyu vyo mulamu. Nane nomupelenyi wutazi mwi'pango lilyo. Ane no Muwaha, u Mulungu wenyu.”

Waisilayeli wakuyimwagula nghambi

11 Na mwizuwa lye makumi mejete lyo mwezi we kejete wa mu mwaka we kejete, ku wulawa mwi'zuwa lyono i wanhu wa Isilayeli wawucila mu yisi ya Misili, livunde lya hacanyha ye Lihema lyono Lyelile, likawa kunyasulwa.
12 Nawo i wanhu Waisilayeli, wakawa kutuluka muhinzo wawo ku wulawa mu nyika ya Sinayi. Nalyo livunde likajendelela sunga likafika mu nyika ya Palani, likawa kwima baho.

13 Nawo wakawa kutulucila mu wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu wono yawalajiliziza muli Musa.
14 Na mu mapango gose lono watulukaga, watulukaga wetyatizye du mu wityatye wawo nha vyono wawezaga nhondo zose. Nawo wono wali wali hasi ye bendela ye cipuga, cono calongozwaga na wo mulango wa Yuda, wo wakawa walongozi ha wutuluce. Wakawa kwityatya vipuga vipuga hasi yo wulanjilizi wa Nahashoni u mwana wa Aminadabu.
15 Nayo Netaneli u mwana wa Zuwali, yakawa yo mulanjilizi wa wo mulango wa Isakali.
16 Nayo Eliyabi u mwana wa Heloni, yakawa yo mulanjilizi wa wo mulango wa Zabuloni.

17 Lihema likamwagulwa, ne wana we Wagelishoni ne Wamelali, wono wali wo wadinghuzi, wakawa kutuluka.

18 Hanyuma nawo wono wali wali hasi ye bendela ye mhuga ya wo mulango wa Lubeni, wakawa kutuluka wali vipuga vipuga, wali wali hamonga na Elizuli u mwana wa Shedewuli, yakawa yo mulanjilizi wawo.
19 Nayo Shelumiyeli, u mwana wa Zulishadayi, yakawa yo mulanjilizi wa wo mulango wa Simiyoni.
20 Nayo Eliyasafu u mwana wa Dewuyeli yakawa yo mulanjilizi wa wo mulango wa Gadi.

21 Hanyuma ho Walawi i wana we cilume we Wakohati, nawo ho watuluce, akuno wadinghuye hamonga ne vinhu vyono vyelile. Na mwipango lyono woficila ku nghambi yono wakubita, wokwagana Lihema nalyo lyowa lyakondya kukomwa.

22 Hanyuma kanhyi, nawo wono wali hasi ye bendela ye cipuga cono cikulongozwa na wo mulango wa Efulayimu, nawo watuluce wali mu vipuga vipuga, wawe wali mu wulanjilizi wa Elishama u mwana wa Amihudi.

23 Nayo Gamaliyeli u mwana wa Pedazuli, yakawa yo mulanjilizi wa wo mulango wa Manase.
24 Nayo Abidani u mwana wa Gidiyoni, yakawa yo mulanjilizi wa wo mulango wa Benyamini.

25 Na ha ndumilizo yayo, wono wali wali hasi ye bendela ye we mhuga yono yalongozwaga na wo mulango wa Dani, wono wali nha wo wetumami wono wawalanjililaga no kuwadima i wayawo wose, nawo ho wakawa kutuluka wali vipuga vipuga, wakawa hamonga na Ahiyezeli u mwana wa Amishadayi, yakawa yo mulanjilizi wawo.
26 Nayo Pagiyeli u mwana wa Ocilani, yakawa yo mulanjilizi wa wo mulango wa Asheli.
27 Na Ahila u mwana wa Enani, yakawa yo mulanjilizi wa wo mulango wa Nafutali.
28 Na wuwu wukawa wo wityatye wo wutuluce wawo mhuga na mhuga, lono Waisilayeli wayimwagulaga nghambi, na lono watulukaga.

29 Nayo Musa yakamulonjela mulamuwe Hobabu, u mwana wa Yetelo Mumidiyani, yakamutya, “Gwe mbuyane, alu ceza cili behi no wutuluce, wo kubita ku yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu yacilonjela vyono kocipela. Hambi nha yehuwiza vyono kowamotya muno Waisilayeli. Holeka wuze cibitane nagwe, nase cohanga nagwe wumoteswe wetu.”

30 Nayo Hobabu yakapitula yakatya, “Ng'o mbuyane, ane langa, nowuya du ku yisi yangu.”

31 Nayo Musa yakatya, “Gwe mbuyane, dedede, cilece kwileka ye. Hambi agwe nha wuhamanyile ne hanhu hono coyisimya nghambi yetu mu nyika, holeka agwe yo kowa mutonji wetu.
32 Hono cibitane nagwe, mbeka cosuma kumota hamonga nagwe, u wulugutwe wono u Muwaha Mulungu kocimotya.”

Wutuluce we wanhu

33 Nawo wanhu wali weza kuwuka he 'tunda lyono lyelile lya Sinayi, wajenda vilo vidatu. Nalyo Lisanduku lye Cilagane lyo Muwaha Mulungu, nhondo zose lyawalongolelaga ku mwande, ho liwatonje i hanhu hono woyisimya nghambi yawo.
34 Nawo lyono wawukaga mu nghambi ayi no kubita kanyhi mu nghambi yinji, livunde lyo Muwaha Mulungu lyekalaga lili hacanyha yawo mu misi yose.

35 Na mwipango lono Lisanduku lye Cilagane lyatulukaga, Musa yalongaga yakutya, “Ale, winuce go Muwaha Mulungu, wuweyagaze walugu wako, nawo wono wakusulile, wose wakutize 'gwe!”
36 Na lono likwima, yalongaga yakutya, “Ale, Muwaha Mulungu wuwawuyilaje i maelufu na maelufu ge wanhu we zimhuga ze Waisilayeli.”


Wuwale 11

I hanhu hono hakucemwa Tabela

1 Nawo wanhu, wakawa kwanza kumung'ung'ulicila Muwaha Mulungu, ku soko ye magayo gawo. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yali yeza kuwahulika, hobaho yakakolwa ne likuwo muno muno, yakawa kuwagumilila moto wanhu. Nawo moto wukawanyanya wayawo wanji, wukayiticimiza ne nghambi yawo lubavu lunji.
2 Nawo wanhu wakawa kumulilila Musa wakusaka wutazi. Nayo Musa yakamulomba Muwaha Mulungu, nawo moto hobaho wukawa kuzima.
3 Holeka i hanhu baho, hakacemwa Tabela, hambi baho nha ho hanhu hono moto wo Muwaha Mulungu, wawanyanyila wayawo wanji.

Musa yakuwahagula walongola makumi mupunghati

4 Na mu muhinzo wuwo, mwali mowali ne wajenzi wono vyajendana hamonga ne Waisilayeli. Nawo wakawa wena nyhilu yo kulya nyama, sunga na weneco Waisilayeli, nawo wakawa kwanza kung'ung'ulika wakutya, “Nye, alu nyama, hamba ciyiwone hayi!
5 Lono cekalaga ku Misili, calizaga somba mu nghango nha vyono casakaga, kanyhi zali za bwete du. Cikumbuciye sunga ne matanga, ne matijiti, ne zimboga ze mahamha, ne vitungulu, sunga ne vitungulu sawumu, vyono calizaga.
6 Ninga aluno casocela. Hambi nha ceza cisina cinji cono colya, langa hono si Mana yiyo du nhondo zose!”

7 (Nayo yiyo Mana yali yihwanile mbeyu ndodo ndodo, ne liwala lyayo lyali linha liwala lye zinhafuta.
8 Nayo yalagalaga ku cilo hamonga ne buhe.
9 Na kolo wela, i wanhu wawa kupilimapilima ako na ako wakuyizola. Hanyuma wawa kuyisaga mu zi nzala, hamba lunji kuyibunda mu matuli wuwa wusaje, hanyuma wawa kuyiteleka no kuyinoza capati. Nayo u munzinzi wayo, wukawa wihwanile no munzinzi wo mukate mutelecele mafuta.)

10 Nayo Musa yakawahulika wanhu vyono wang'ung'ulikaga, lono wali welundice munhu na mhuga yakwe mu milango ye mahema gawo. Musa yakagazika muno, vyono Muwaha Mulungu yali yawakolelwa likuwo muno muno.
11 Hobaho Musa yakawa kumulonjela Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, alo nhawule kunhendela vibi nhavivi? Hamba nhawule lu wasula? Hamba nhawule wukamhela wulanjilizi wa muli wanhu wawa?
12 Alu ane, nha si nawawumba nene, hamba kuwanoza kose du ho walelwe! Alu nhawule wumheye nitume mulimo wuwu nha nili mukoci wa wana, ho niwadinghule nha wagalika mu mawoko gangu mu nzila yose, sunga niwafise ko ku yisi yono wawehuwiziza walezi wawo?
13 Alo, hayi hene hono ane noyituga nyama yono yowakwila wanhu wose wawa? Alu 'mbi, nha vyo wakali wakung'ung'ulika du wakulomba nyama.
14 Nane, hamba si nghusuma nili wene, kuwa mulanjilizi we wanhu wawa, hambi nha mulimo muwaha muno muno kuli'ne!
15 Na hono wuwe wukali kunhendela nhavivi, nghukulomba wumbonelaje lusungu, kanyhi ciba mbulagaje du kamonga. Hambi ane nha si nosuma kujendelela kuwukangalila wukali wako.”

16 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Ale, nghongholelaje wanhu makumi mupunghati wa muli walongola we Waisilayeli, i wanhu wono wena lilumbo yiswanu, kanyhi wono wamanyice vyono mbeka wena wuwe wo wulongoza wanhu, wundetele ha mulango we Lihema lyangu lyono Lyelile. Hanyuma wuwatye weme baho hamonga nagwe.
17 Nane notima, cilongane nagwe baho. Nane nosola muhe wunji wono wuli muli'gwe wono nakupela, ho niwagawile nawo. Nawo ho wosuma kukutaziliza mu wituma mulimo wo wulanjilizi we wanhu wawa, nagwe holeka si kowudinghula mulimo wuwo kanyhi wuli wene yaye.
18 Alu, walonjele wanhu wutye, ‘Ale mwiyezaje, mwiwikanize, vyono i mitondo, motuga nyama mulye. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha kakondya kuwuhulika wung'ung'ulice wenyu, hamonga no wulonje wenyu wo kutya vyono mwina wuhile wo kulya nyama, na kanyhi vyono, mwamotaga lono mwali ku Misili. Aluno Muwaha Mulungu komupelenyi nyama, nanye moyilya.
19 Ninga si moyilya, mwi'zuwa limonga du hamba vilo vyejete, hamba vilo vihano, hamba vilo yikumi, hamba vilo makumi mejete du, a, a, ng'o.
20 Langa mojendelela kulya nyama sunga mwezi mututu. Sunga nyama yilawile mu zi mhula zenyu, sunga muwe kudeka no kudeka. Nazo zizi zomwijelelenyi, vyono anye, mwamulema Muwaha Mulungu wenyu mono yali bahano hamonga nanye, kanyhi mukung'ung'ulika no kutya, vyono, yali yiwe yili ciba hono mwali mwikale ku Misili.’ ”

21 Musa yakamulonjela Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Alu ahano, nina wanhu elufu magana mutandatu cititila, wono nghuwalongoza, nagwe ho wukutya vyono kowapela nyama yono yowakwila walye mwezi monga?
22 Wukwihwesa yali yiwe yili ciba wawulajilwe ng'ombe hamba ngholo, ho ziwakwile? Hamba somba zose za mwi'lenga zowakwila?”

23 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamupitula Musa yakamutya, “Wukwihwesa vilungo vyangu ane, vina cimizi? Nagwe kowona lulu baha du vyono, zono nonjile zokwijela hamba ng'o!”

24 Musa yakawa kubita kuwalonjela zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yazilonjile. Yakawakonghola walongola makumi mupunghati, yakawatandalika wose wapilime Lihema.
25 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kuziza mwi'vunde, yakawa kulongana na Musa. Muwaha Mulungu yakasola muhe wunji muli Musa, wono yali yamupelile Musa. Yakawa kuwagawila wa walya walongola we makumi mupunghati. Kukawa, muhe wali weza kuwatimila, hobaho wakawa kwanza kuwila nhe wawizi, ninga si wasola yidaha mututu yaye.

26 Nawo wanhu wejete wa muli walongola wawalya we makumi mupunghati, Elidadi no muyagwe Medadi, wene si walawila mu nghambi ho wabite kwi 'Hema. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, muhe yawatimila nawo, nawo nhavivyo wakawa kwanza kuwila.
27 U muzelelo yunji yakawa kubita musunho kuli Musa, yakawa kumumanyiza zizo za Elidadi hamonga na Medadi vyono wakuwila mu nghambi.

28 Nayo Yoshuwa u mwana wa Nuni, mono ku wulawa wudodo wakwe yali yo mutazi wa Musa, yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Muwaha wangu Musa, wuwalemezaje ye!”

29 Nayo Musa yakapitula yakatya, “Mwene Yoshuwa, alu wukumbonela wivu, hamba nhawule? Ane nyendile muno Muwaha Mulungu yawapele muhe wakwe wanhu wakwe wose, yawanoze wose wawe wawizi!”
30 Hobaho Musa, hamonga ne walongola wawalya makumi mupunghati, wakawa kuwuya ku nghambi.

Muwaha Mulungu yakuleta Nghwale

31 Muwaha Mulungu yakaleta musunho mbeho mbaha, ne mbeho yiyo yikaleta nghwale ku wulawa kwi 'lenga, nazo zikawa ziguluciye hasika sika muno muno, kotya nha mita monga du ku wulawa hasi. Zikawa kumema mu nghambi mbavu zose, zibitile du muhinzo we'zuwa limonga winji wazo, ku wulawa ha nghambi.
32 Holeka mwi'zuwa lilyo, wanhu wajendelela kwibata nghwale du sunga likaswa, cilo cose sunga wukela, sunga ne mitondo yaco. Hamba hasina munhu monga mono yebata nyhefu hasi ya kilo elufu monga. Wakawa kuzitandalika mwi'zuwa mu nghambi yose ho zinyale.
33 Na lono wali wakali watuga nyama nyinji zo kulya, Muwaha Mulungu yakawakolelwa muno yikuwo, yakawahuga muno no wutamwa we lihuhuji.
34 Ne litagwa lye hanhu baho, hakacemwa Kibuloti-hatava (wo wutya, “Vitenjelo vye Wasulumizi.”) wazikwa i wanhu wo wusulumizi wa nyama.

35 Wali weza kuwuka baho, wakabita ku Hazeloti wakayisimya nghambi kuko.


Wuwale 12

Milyamu yakulipicizwa

1 Musa yali yatolile mucekulu we Cikushi. Ninga Milyamu na Haluni wene si wendezwa ne nghani yiyo.
2 Holeka wakawa kulongana wakutya, “Alo Muwaha Mulungu kalongana na Musa du yali wene, hamba kulonga nase ng'o?” Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakazihulika zono walonganaga.
3 (Ninga Musa yali yo munhu mupolo muno, kuciza wanhu wose wono wali mononze.)

4 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakalonga nawo nhinicizo, Musa, Haluni na Milyamu, yakawatya, “Ale, ane nghumusacenyi nyenye wose vyono muli wadatu, muze kwi Hema lyangu lyono Lyelile.” Nawo wakawa kubita.
5 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakaziza mwi'vunde lyono lipalinjile, yakema ha mulango we Lihema, yakacema yakutya, “Haluni! Milyamu!” Nawo vyono wejete wakalawila mwi Hema.
6 Muwaha Mulungu yakawatya, “Alu, muhulice ta zono nghumulonjelenyi! Hono ho wali wawizi muli'nye, ane yo nene mono nghwilajila kuli wene mu nzila yo wugubulilwe, kanyhi nghulonganila nawo mu zindoto.
7 Ninga lono nghulongana no mwitumami wangu Musa, nghulongana nayo mu nzila yono yisimajiye. Hambi nha nimuwicile mwene yawe yo mulanjilizi muli wanhu wose wa Isilayeli.
8 Holeka lono nghulongana nayo, nghulongana nayo ciwonelela, kanyhi calinze du, hamba si mu nzila yo wufumbe ng'o. Nayo yakondya kumbona sunga ne cihwani cangu! Alu nhawule si mwali kogopa kumulonganga mwitumami wangu Musa?”

9 Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kuwa kolelwa likuwo muno muno, yakawa kuwuka yakawaleka.
10 Ne livunde likawa kuwuka he Hema. No muwili wa Milyamu wukatuga lwa kamonga wutamwa wo wukoma. No muwili wakwe wose wukawa kuswelulila swee, nha mabwe ga mvula. Nayo Haluni yali yeza kumuwona Milyamu vyono yakondya kwibatwa no wukoma,
11 yakawa kumulonjela Musa yakutya, “Go muwaha wangu, dedede, wulece kucileceleza citamiswe no wulipicizwe wuwu, so wubi wetu we cilelelele.
12 Dedede, wulece kumuleka Milyamu, yawe nha cinhu cono cileligwe cifwiye, akuno yena nghuli yono yilijigwe lubavu lunji.”

13 Nayo Musa yakamulomba Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Gwe go Mulungu, nghukunena wumuponye Milyamu!”

14 Muwaha Mulungu yakapitula yakatya, “Hono sogwe yali yamufunyile mate mu wusu wakwe, yali yakozwe cevu vilo mupunghati. Holeka mulekaje Milyamu yekale kunze ye nghambi vilo mupunghati. Na kolo vyapuluta vilo vivyo vyo mupunghati, ale ho kosuma kugomolwa kanyhi mono nghambi.”
15 Hobaho Milyamu yakawa kudindilwa kunze ye nghambi, nayo yakawa kuko kunze vilo mupunghati. Nawo wanhu, hamba si watuluka yaye, sunga Milyamu yakagomolwa mu nghambi.
16 Hanyuma ho wakawa kutuluka ku wulawa baho ha Hazeloti, wakabita sunga ko ku nyika ya Palani, wakayisimya nghambi yawo baho.


Wuwale 13

Watandizi

(Kum 1:19-33)

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
2 “Ale wuhagule walongola monga monga, mu wulawa mu milango yose yikumi na myejete ye Waisilayeli. Wuwatume wabite nha watandizi, wakayilanje yisi ya Kanani vyono yinha, yisi yono nghuwapela i Waisilayeli.”
3 Nayo Musa yakawa ne cihuliko, yakawatuma ku wulawa ku nyika ya Palani i walongola awa nha vyono wetyatizye ahano.
4 Wo mulango wa Lubeni, yakabita Shamuwa u mwana wa Zakuli:

5 Wo mulango wa Simiyoni, yakabita Shafati u mwana wa Holi.

6 Wo mulango wa Yuda, yakabita Kalebi u mwana wa Yefune.

7 Wo mulango wa Isakali, yakabita Igali u mwana wa Yusufu.

8 Wo mulango wa Efulayimu, yakabita Hosheya u mwana wa Nuni.

9 Wo mulango wa Benyamini, yakabita Paliti u mwana wa Lafu.

10 Wo mulango wa Zabuloni, yakabita Gadiyeli u mwana wa Sodi.

11 Wo mulango wa Yusufu, (nha cinji wono wakulawa mu mulango wa Manase,) yakabita Gadi, u mwana wa Susi.

12 Wo mulango wa Dani, yakabita Amiyeli u mwana wa Gemali.

13 Wo mulango wa Asheli, yakabita Setuli u mwana wa Mikayeli.

14 Wo mulango wa Nafutali, yakabita Nahibi u mwana wa Vofisi.

15 Wo mulango wa Gadi, yakabita Gewuyeli u mwana wa Maci.

16 Wawo wo wanhu wono Musa yawatuma, wabite wakayilanje yisi ya Kanani vyono yinha. Yakawa kumukawaniza litagwa Hosheya u mwana wa Nuni, yawe Yoshuwa.

17 Nayo Musa lono yawatumaga, yakawalonjela yakatya, “Kolo mweza kutuluka, mwinjililaje ku lubavu lwa kusumuka ye yisi, muwe kujendelela sunga mufice ku lubavu lwe takama ye yisi, mukole sunga na mu matunda.
18 Muyilanje yisi yiyo yose ya Kanani, muyiwone vyono yinha, kanyhi mumanye ne liwalo lye wanhu wayo wono wekaye mumo, na hono wali vilungo, hamba watimbile.
19 Kanyhi muyilangaje yisi hono swanu hamba ng'o. Na hono wanhu wayo wekaye mu zikaya zono zizenjigwe mwi'hala, hamba nzenje mugati ye magomawakali.
20 Kanyhi muwulanje no muhangha wayo, hono wina moso hamba ng'o, na hono mogali mabici hamba yihala du. Kanyhi kolo mweza kuwuya akuno, mukumbucile muleteleze ne mikalo yono wayilimaga kuko.” (Naco cali co cipango co wupilo we zi zabibu.)

21 Hobaho wanhu wawo wakawa kutuluka, wakakolela ku lubavu lwe sumuka, wakayilanga yisi ku wulawilila mu nyika ya Zini, sunga ko ku takama ku Lehobu, behi na Hamati-pasi kono sumuka.
22 Ninga kanyhi, wakamula kubita ku lubavu lwe takama lwe yisi, wakakolela ku Hebuloni, mu yisi ya we mhuga ya Ahimoni na Sheshayi, na Talamayi, we ndelwa ye mbeyu ye manhu gono gali gali vilungo, gono gacemwaga Waanaki, wo wekalaga kuko. (Nayo Hebuloni, yo yalongola kuzengwa. Yali yeza kupuluta myaka mupunghati, ho yikazengwa nayo Zowani yono yili mu Misili.)
23 Hodu, wakawa kuza sunga ko kwi'lolo lya Eshikoli, nawo wali weza kufika mwi'lolo lilyo, wakatema linhambi lina yivunda limonga lya zabibu. Nalyo likawa yizito muno muno, sunga yikawa cinojele lisomekwe mwi 'bici, kanyhi lidinghulwe na wanhu wejete wetembase. Wakaleta ne makomamanga ne zinghuyu.
24 Hanhu baho hakacemwa Lilolo lya Eshikoli, vyono i Waisilayeli, baho ho watema linhambi lye zabibu.

25 Nawo wali weza kukondya kuyilanga yisi mu vilo makumi mane, hodu, wakawa kuwuya
26 kuli Musa na Haluni, hamonga ne mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli kuko ku Kadeshi, mu nyika ya Palani. Wakalonga zose zono waziwona, wakalajila sunga ne mikalo yono waleteleze.
27 Wakamulonjela Musa wakutya, “Ciyipilima yisi yose, ciyiwona vyono yinha, ayino hano ne mikalo yono yikulimwa mu yisi yiyo.
28 Langa i wanhu wono wekaye mu yisi yiyo, wo wanhu wono wali vilungo muno muno. Ne makaya gawo mawaha muno muno, kanyhi gose mazenje mugati ye magomawakali. Kanyhi ciwona ne zindelo ze manhu gono gali vilungo kuko, gono gacemwaga Waanaki.
29 Nawo Waamaleki wekaye ku lubavu lwe takama ye yisi. Nawo Wahiti ne Wayebusi hamonga ne Waamoli, wekaye mu yisi ye matunda. Nawo Wakanani, wekaye mu mwalambalo we lilenga lya Meditelaneya, na ku lubavu lwe likolongo lya Yoludani.”

30 Nayo Kalebi yakawa kuwanyamaza wanhu wono wamung'ung'ulicilaga Musa, yakatya, “Alenyi 'nye, cikayisole yisi, hambi 'se nha cina vilungo vyo kuyisolela.”

31 Ninga wanhu wono wabitana hamonga na Kalebi wakatya, “Nye, ase cisina vilungo vyo kuwasumila wanhu wa walya, hambi wene nha wali vilungo wacicizize 'se.”
32 Holeka wakatandaza nghani za wulambi muli Waisilayeli, kuli ze yisi yiyo yono wabita kuyilanga. Kanyhi wakacila konjeleza wakutya, “Ne yisi yiyo hamba si yikusuma no kulela zindigwa zono ziwakwiliye wanhu wose wono wekaye mumo. Kanyhi wanhu wose wono ciwawene, wose watali muno muno.
33 Ciwawona kanyhi kuko sunga ne Wanefili, we zindelo ze Waanaki. Nase ciwa kwiwona cili wadodo du kotya mhanzi, hambi nha vyo cali kwijela ha mwande yawo.”


Wuwale 14

Wanhu wakung'ung'ulika

1 Ne wanhu wakajendelela kulila ko wusungu wuwaha muno, cilo cose.
2 Wakawa wakumung'ung'ulicila Musa na Haluni wakutya, “Yali yiwe yili ciba le, hono cali cifwile ku Misili, hamba bahano mono nyika!
3 Alu so cici lo Muwaha Mulungu yakusaka yacitegule kuko ku yisi yiyo? Hambi nha cofwila mu wulugu, ne wagolece wetu nawo, hamonga ne wana wetu, wosolwa mateka. Alu si yali yiwe yili ciba le, hono cali ciwuye ne nyuma ku Misili?”
4 Wakawa kulongana weneco wose wakutya, “Nye, alenyi cihagule mulongola mono kocigomola ne nyuma ku Misili.”

5 Hobaho Musa na Haluni wakenama ho hasi, ha mwande ye wanhu wose.
6 Nayo Yoshuwa u mwana wa Nuni, hamonga na Kalebi u mwana wa Yefune i watandizi wejete, wakayidemulanga myenda yawo ko wukalale,
7 wakawa kuwalonjela wanhu wakutya, “Yisi yono cayitandila, yo yisi yo swanu muno muno.
8 Na hono Muwaha Mulungu yendezwe nase, kocitegula sunga ko ku yisi yiyo, yacipele yisi yiyo yono yina moso hamonga no wugoli winji.
9 Holeka mulece kuwa wafyuzi hali Muwaha Mulungu, hamba mulece kuwogopa i wanhu wono wekaye mu yisi yiyo. Cowasumiza musunho du, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yali hamonga nase. Nayo nha yakondya kuyisumiza milungu yawo yono wayitumbiye, holeka mulece kuwa na woga.”
10 Ne mhuga yose wali wewikaniza ho wawatowanje ne mabwe Yoshuwa na Kalebi sunga wafwe. Ninga wanhu wakaluwona nhinicizo u lukumyo lo Muwaha Mulungu lukwijela hacanyha ye Lihema lyono Lyelile.

Musa yakuwalombela wanhu

11 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Alu mwene, i wanhu wawa wojendelela kumbedegala sunga nili? Hamba wojendelela kulema kunyitumbila 'ne sunga nili, hamba niwe netuma mapoto menji hali wene?
12 Hodu, nane nowaletela lihuhuji liwongomeze. Ninga agwe Musa, nokunoza wuwe we nghungugo mbaha muno, yono yowa yili vilungo muno muno si nha wene!”

13 Ninga Musa yakamulonjela Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, yo gwegwe mono wawawusa wanhu wawa ku Misili kwe vilungo viwaha muno. Holeka hono Wamisili wahulice zono wawatendela wanhu wako,
14 na mbeka wosuma kuwalonjela i wanhu wono wekaye mu yisi yiyi. Hambi i wanhu wawo, nha wakondizye kumanya vyono, agwe go Muwaha Mulungu wuli hamonga nase, nagwe mbeka wukwijela ciwonelela, lono livunde lyako limile hacanyha yetu, na vyono kanyhi agwe, wukucilongolela ku mwande yetu ne lisumbili lye livunde ku misi, na ku cilo nako wukucilongolela ne lisumbili lyo moto.
15 Alu hono wuwawulaje wanhu wako wose, i makungugo wono wazihulika zo lukumyo lwako koleka watye,
16 kuwawulajila wanhu wako mu nyika, vyono kulemegwa kuwafisa sunga ko ku yisi yono wawehuwiza vyono kowapela.
17 Holeka alu go Muwaha Mulungu, nghukulomba, wucilajile vilungo vyako, witume nha vyono wehuwiza lono watya nhavi,
18 ‘Ane no Muwaha Mulungu, si nili mupelu kukolwa ne likuwo mbela, kanyhi ndajilaga zo lusungu lwangu luwaha, hamonga no wihuwilwa wangu, kanyhi nili munenezi wo wubi, hamonga no wufyule. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, nowalipiciza wana hamonga ne wezukulu, sunga ko ku lulelo lwe kadatu na lwe kane so wubi we walezi.’
19 Holeka alu 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, ku so wuwaha wako, no lusungu lwako, lono silupitukaga, nghukulomba wuwanenelaje wanhu wawa wubi wuwu, nha vyono kuwanenelaga du, ku wulawa lono wawuka ku yisi ya Misili.”

20 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamupitula Musa yakatya, “Ale, hodu, nowanenela nha vyono wulombile.
21 Ninga nghwihuwiza vyono, nha vyono ane nikalaga mumi, na kanyhi nha vyono lukumyo lwangu lumemile mono yinze yose,
22 hasina munhu hamba monga wa muli wawa, mono kokwikala mumi sunga yaze yenjile mu yisi yiyo. Hambi nha waluwona lukumyo lwangu lono lukundajila vyono nili baho, kanyhi wakagawona ne mapoto gono naganoza mu yisi ya Misili, sunga na mono nyika, kanyhi wakanjela no wukangalizi wangu kali kenji, ninga hamba nhavivyo, si wahulika 'ne yaye.
23 Holeka si wokwinjilila lukulu mu yisi yono nawehuwiza walezi wawo. Kanyhi hasina hamba monga wa muli wawo wono wakumbeza, mono kosuma kwinjila mu yisi yiyo.
24 Ninga lu, ku soko yo mwitumami wangu Kalebi, mono yena muhe wunji, kanyhi vyono yajendeleye kuwa ne cihuliko kuli'ne, yuyo langa yo nosuma kumufisa hamonga ne zindelo zakwe sunga ko ku yisi yono yabita kuyitandila, nawo woyihala yisi yiyo,
25 ya mu malolo mono Waamaleki, hamonga ne Wakanani wekaye. Holeka i mitondo lu, mupituce mubite mu nyika, mutyatye nzila yono yibitile kwi'lenga lya Shamu.”

U Muwaha Mulungu, yakuwalipiciza wanhu, so mang'ung'uliko gawo

26 Muwaha Mulungu, yakawalonjela Musa na Haluni yakawatya,
27 “Alu mwene, i wanhu wawa wabi, wojendelela kung'ung'ulicila sunga nili lo? Hambi nha nacilwa muno ne mang'ung'uliko gawo gaga!
28 Holeka aluno, wuwapitulaje wutye nhavi: Nghwilahiza nha vyono mbeka, ane nikalaga mumi, nokwituma nha vyono wulombile. Ane no Muwaha Mulungu, vyo natya.
29 Anye mofwa, ne mivimba yenyu yokwiyagalila mu muno mono nyika du. Hambi nha mukung'ung'ulicila muno. Holeka hasina hamba monga wa muli nyenye, mono yaficile myaka yono yicizize myaka makumi mejete, mono kosuma kwinjila mu yisi yiyo.
30 Nali nihuwize vyono, anye wose mokwikala mu yisi yiyo, ninga luno, hasina hamba monga wa muli nyenye mono kosuma kwinjila mu yisi yiyo, langa Kalebi du na Yoshuwa, wo wosuma kwinjila.
31 Kanyhi anye mukutya vyono, i wana wenyu, wosolwa mateka, ninga ane nowafisa sunga ko ku yisi yiyo yono anye mwakondya kuyilema, nayo yisi yiyo, yowa yo yisi yawo.
32 Ninga anye mofwila mumuno du mono nyika yi yino.
33 Ne wana wenyu nawo, woyegayega mumuno mono nyika yiyi myaka makumi mane, hamonga ne magayo, ku soko yo wula wihuwilwa wenyu, sunga kolo yafwiza u muyenyu we ndumilizo.
34 Nanye motuga magayo ku soko yo wubi wenyu, mu myaka makumi mane. Nayo yowa, mwaka monga wukwinjila mwi'zuwa limonga, lya mu vilo vivilya makumi mane vyono mwetumamila, lono mwayitandilila yisi. Nanye ho mosuma kumanya i cambuzi co kunema 'ne!
35 Nane nghwilahiza vyono nomutendelenyi zizi, anye mo wanhu wabi, mono mwekongholela hamonga ho muneme 'ne. Nanye mbeka wose mofwila mono nyika du. Ane no Muwaha Mulungu nalonga.”

36 Nawo wanhu wono Musa yawatuma wabite wakayitandile yisi, wawo wo waleta zinghani zimbi, zono zambuza sunga ne wanhu wamung'ung'ulicile Muwaha Mulungu.
37 Holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawatowa ne lihuhuji, wakafwa.
38 Na muli wawalya wanhu we likumi na wejete, wono watumwa wabite wakayitandile yisi, nawo wose wakafwa, wakasigala Yoshuwa na Kalebi du.

Wujele wulongozi wo kuyihoma yisi

(Kum 1:41-46)

39 Nayo Musa, yali yeza kuwalonjela i Waisilayeli zono Muwaha Mulungu yalonjile, Oo, wakalila muno ko wukalale.
40 Ne mitondo yaco, wakelawa hakali mitondo muno muno, wakawa kwanza kuyihoma yisi ye matunda akuno wakutya, “Alu cakondya kwiwikaniza ku winjila i hanhu hono u Muwaha Mulungu yacihuwiza. Ninga cikuvumila vyono cili wasutyi.”

41 Ninga Musa yakawatya, “Alu nye, nhawulenyi musina cihuliko kuli Muwaha Mulungu? Hambi nha si mosuma cinji yaye!
42 Holeka mulecele lukulu kubita, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha si yali hamonga nanye. Ne walugu wenyu womusumenyi du.
43 Na kolo mweza kwihoma ne Waamaleki hamonga ne Wakanani, anye wose mowulagwa mumalicile mu wulugu. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha si kowa yali hamonga nanye, hambi 'nye nha mwalema kumutyatya mwene.”

44 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, wene vyo wakazezepa du, wakawa kubita ku yisi ye matunda. Ninga Lisanduku lye Cilagane hamonga na Musa, wasigala ku nghambi du.
45 Hobaho, Waamaleki hamonga ne Wakanani, wono wekalaga mu yisi yiyo ye matunda, wakawaziza, wakawahoma, wakawasumiza, wakawawulaga, akuno wakuwawinjiliza sunga ko ku Holema.


Wuwale 15

Wulajilize wa kuli ze makumbiko

1 Muwaha Mulungu, yakamulajiliza Musa,
2 yawalonjele wanhu Waisilayeli, malajilizo gono wanojeye wagakamise, mu yisi yono yakuwapela.
3 Yiwanojeye wamulavizye Muwaha Mulungu nghongolo, hamba ngholo, hamba mhene, yiwe so cilavywa cinyanywe, hamba so cilavywa co wufisilize wo wilahize, hamba so cilavywa ce lihewa, hamba so cilavywa cono wazoweye kulavya mu zi sikukuu zenyu ze nhondo zose, ne mhunjile ye vilavywa vye ndigwa, yikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu.
4 No munhu wowose, mono kowa kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu cilavywa cinyanywe ce ngholo, hamba ce mhene, yowa yimunojeye yalavye hamonga no wusaje wa kilo monga wono wuhanzahanze ne mafuta lita monga,
5 ciwe cilavywa co wusaje munamu, hamonga na divayi lita monga.
6 Na hono yiwe nduwilu yo yikulavywa, ale yinojeye yilavywe hamonga no wusaje wa kilo nyejete wono wuhanzahanze ne mafuta lita monga na nusu, nawo wulavywe ciwe cilavywa co wusaje,
7 hamonga na divayi lita monga na nusu. Ne mhunjile ye vilavywa vyono vinha vivino, yikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu.
8 Na hono yiwe nghongolo yo yikulavywa kuli Muwaha Mulungu nha cilavywa cinyanywe, hamba nha cilavywa co wufisilize wo wilahize, hamba lunji so cilavywa co mulamu,
9 ale, yowa yinojeye wulavywe hamonga no wusaje wa kilo yidatu wono wuhanzahanze ne mafuta ga lita nyejete,
10 hamonga na divayi lita nyejete. Ne mhunjile ye likumbiko lyono linha lili, likumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu.

11 Nayo vyo yowa yinha mu wulavye we nghongolo yoyose, hamba we nduwilu yoyose, hamba we ngholo yoyose, hamba we mhene yoyose.
12 Na hono hawe hoyili mitugo minji yono yikulavywa lwa kamonga, ale, mitugo zose, monga monga, zinozezwe nha vyono winha wityatye wuwo.
13 No Mwiisilayeli wo wose munya wusi, yimunojeye yanoze nhavivi lono yakulavya cilavywa ce ndigwa. Ne mhunjile yayo yikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu.
14 Na mwi'pango lyo lyose, mujenzi mono mwikalane, hono yawe mwikazi we 'daha yidodo du, hamba muhamizi, yalavye cilavywa ce ndigwa, ye mhunjile yono yikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu, u munhu yuyo yanojeye yatyatye wulajilize vyono winha.
15 No wulajilize wuwu, wunojeye wukamiswe na nyenye, hamonga ne wajenzi wono mwikalane nawo, sunga mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza. Hambi wose anye na wene, nha co cimonga du hali Mulungu.
16 Hambi malajilizo gose hamonga no wityatye wayo, nha vili ku soko yenyu na kuli wene.

17 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa,
18 “Yawalonjele Waisilayeli, u wulajilize awuno, wawukamise mu yisi yono yakuwapela.
19 Na lono mweza kulya zindigwa za mu yisi, mweze kuwika zinji ha mhelo, ziwe cilavywa cinulwe hali Muwaha Mulungu.
20 Na lono mweza kuteleka mikate mu wulawa mu wuhemba wupya, ale, mumanye vyono, u mukate mulongozi kutelekwa, wuwo wunojeye wulavywe ciwe cilavywa cinulwe hali Muwaha Mulungu. Nanye molavya mu nzila yiyilya du, nha vyono mulavyaga cilavywa cinulwe co wuhemba mu wulawa mu vyuga vyenyu.
21 Ne cilavywa cicino cinulwe co mukate, cinojeye cilavywe kuli Muwaha Mulungu mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza.

22 “Na lunji, so wu wula kumanya, yunji kekala si yebate wulajilize wu wuno, wono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajilize Musa.
23 Na kanyhi lunji mu vilo vyono vikuza, mhuga yilece kusuma kuzituma zose, zono Muwaha Mulungu yawalajiliziza muli Musa.
24 Na hono mhuga yisutye so wu wula kumanya, ale, wosuma kulavya nghongolo yiwe cilavywa cinyanywe, ne mhunjile yayo, yikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga ne cilavywa co wusaje ne divayi. Hamonga na zizo, wowa wanojeye walavye mvulati, yiwe so cilavywa co wubi.
25 Nayo mukohani, yanoze wilamule so mhuga yose. Nawo wonenelwa, hambi lisutya lyawo, nha wasutizya mu wu wula kumanya, na kanyhi luno, wakondya kulavya ne cilavywa co wubi yiwe co cilavywa ce ndigwa kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
26 Nayo mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli wonenelwa, hamonga ne wajenzi wono vikalane nawo. Hambi nha wose, wali, wali nghologo monga mu wusutye.

27 “Na hono munhu wowose yasutye wula kumanya, ale munhu yuyo yalavye mhene nhogota ya mwaka monga so cilavywa co wubi.
28 Nayo mukohani yamunozeze wilamule hali Muwaha Mulungu, u munhu yuyu mu wubi wakwe, nayo munhu yuyo konenelwa.
29 No wityatye wuwu, wowa so wanhu wose wono wakwinjila mu masutya wula kumanya, hono yawe munyawusi Mwiisilayeli, hamba yawe mujenzi muhamizi.

30 “Ninga munhu wowose mono yakwituma wubi ne nyono yakwe, hono yawe munyawusi, hamba yawe mujenzi, ayo yakuwa musutyi so wubedegala u Muwaha Mulungu. Holeka munhu yuyo yawulagwe.
31 Hambi nha kabedegala kuli zono Muwaha Mulungu yalonjile, kanyhi, kabena ne nyono, limonga lya muli malajilizo go Mulungu. Holeka munhu yuyo yimunojeye yafwe.”

Muli zo munhu mono koyibena Sabato

32 Mwi'zuwa linji, lono Waisilayeli wali wakali wali mu nyika, u munhu yunji yakejela yakutenya ngwagu mwi'zuwa lye Sabato.
33 U munhu yuyo yakawa kusolwa, yakategulwa kuli Musa na Haluni na hali mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli.
34 Nawo wakawa kumudindila mu jela, hambi nha yali yikali si namanyika calinze yono wosuma kumutendela so lisutya lilyo.
35 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “U munhu yuyo, yanojeye yawulagwe, yategulwe kunze ye nghambi, ne mhuga yose wamutowangaje ne mabwe sunga yafwe.”
36 Hobaho mhuga yose yikawa kumusola, yikawa kumutegula kunze ye nghambi, yikawa kumutowanga ne mabwe sunga yakafwa, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yali yalajilize.

Wulajilize wa kuli ze masune

37 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajiliza Musa yakatya,
38 “Yawalonjele wanhu wa Isilayeli yatye, ‘Ale, muhoneleze masune mu mamhembo ge myenda yenyu, kanyhi na mu masune ge macindo gose, muwicile mulejehe we liwala lye buluu. Nanye munojeye muwe kunoza nhavivi mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza.
39 Nago masune gago, gowa co cikumbucizo cenyu, nanye kolo mukugawona, mogakumbucila malajilizo gangu gose, nanye kolo mwakumbucila, si mosuma kanyhi kundeka, no kutyatya zi nzila zenyu nyo weneco, hamonga ne masulumizi genyu.
40 Nago kanyhi masune gago, gowa kumukumbucizenyi, mujendelele kugebata malajilizo gangu gose. Nanye mowa muli wanhu wangu nene du.
41 Ane yo nene no Muwaha u Mulungu wenyu, mono namuwusenyi mu yisi ya Misili niwe Mulungu wenyu. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.’ ”


Wuwale 16

Wufyule wa Kola, na Datani, na Abilamu

1 Nayo Kola u mwana wa Izihali, mulawa mu Walawi we ndelwa ye Wakohati, yakawubedegala wulongola wa Musa. Mwene, yakelunza ne wayagwe wanji wadatu, wo mulango wa Lubeni, nha cinji, Datani na Abilamu i wana wa Eliyabi, hamonga na Oni u mwana wa Peleti.
2 Hamonga na wanji we wanhu Waisilayeli, waficile wanhu magana mejete na makumi mahano, wanhu watambule, walongola, wahagulwa ne mhuga ye Waisilayeli.
3 Wakekongholela hali Musa na Haluni, wakawa kutya, “Alu nanye nhondo mwacila muno! I wanhu wose, nha wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu, nayo Muwaha Mulungu nha yali hamonga na sese wose. Alu 'gwe Musa, nhawule wukwilongoleza hali mhuga yiyi yose yo Muwaha Mulungu?”

4 Nayo Musa yali yeza kuzihulika zizi, hobaho yakagwa gubama, yakawa kumulomba Mulungu.
5 Hanyuma, yakawa kumulonjela Kola, hamonga ne watyati wakwe, yakawatya, “Ale, i mitondo hakali mitondo, u Muwaha Mulungu kocilajila, nani mono munhu wakwe, kanyhi wa hayi mono wakwe, nha cinji mono yamuhagula, komusejeleza ha cikumbicilo.
6 Holeka i mitondo hakali mitondo, gwegwe Kola, hamonga ne watyati wako wose, musole vifusizo muvitajile moto, muwutajile no wuvumba muli vyene, hanyuma muvilete ku cikumbicilo.
7 Ale baho ho cowonela, nani wa muli sese mono Muwaha Mulungu komuhagula, yuyo yo yelile. Ane nghuwona vyono yiyo langa yo yomukwilenyi nyo Walawi!”

8 Musa yakajendelela kulongana na Kola yakutya, “Ale ta, muhulicize anye nyo Walawi.
9 Alu mwene, anye mukwihwesa nha vyono nghani yiyi ndodo hongo, vyono u Mulungu we Waisilayeli yamusiwanulenyi nyenye mu wulawa mu mhuga ye wanhu, ho mumusejelele no kunoza wuhembi wenyu mwi 'Hema lyo Muwaha Mulungu, no kuwetumamila mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli lo?
10 Mulungu yo yamunozenyi nyenye, hamonga ne Walawi wanji, ho muwe ne lilumbo lilino. Ninga luno, anye mukuhila muwutuje no mulimo wuwu wo wukohani hongo!
11 Holeka lono mukumung'ung'ulicila Haluni, mukumung'ung'ulicila Muwaha Mulungu, na mu nzila yiyo, nyenye, hamonga ne watyati wenyu wose, mweza muli wabedegazi.”

12 Hanyuma Musa yakatuma wanji wawaceme Datani na Abilamu i wana wa Eliyabi, waze kuli mwene. Ninga wene wakalema kuza, wakatya, “Ase si cokuza!
13 Agwe wukwihwesa nghani yiyi ndodo yo kuciwusa mu yisi yono yina moso, yisi ya Misili, no kucileta ahano hono nyika, ho wuciwulaje? Alu hongo wukusaka kwitemezya muli sese?
14 Na mbeka, wukali nacifisa mu yisi yono yina moso, hamba wukali nacipela migunda, hamba migunda ya zabibu, nha vyono cali cina navyo ku Misili. Naluno agwe, wukujela kucikonga du bwete. Holeka nase calema, hamba si cokuza yaye!”

15 Hobaho nayo Musa yakawa kukolwa ne likuwo, yakawa kumulonjela Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wulecele lukulu kubocela cilavywa ce liwala lyolyose lyono wanhu wawano wokuletela. Ane nghali si namusutizya yunji wowose wa muli wene. Nane nghali si nasola hamba ndogowe yawo monga.”

16 Hobaho Musa yakawa kumutya Kola, “Imitondo, gwegwe, hamonga ne watyati wako wose, magana mejete na makumi mahano, nhondo muze he Hema lyono Lyelile lyo Muwaha Mulungu, na Haluni nayo kowa yali baho.
17 Na muli nyenye wose, yumonga yumonga, nhondo yasoleleze ne cifusizo cakwe, yatajile no wuvumba mucanyha yaco, hanyuma yacilete ha cikumbicilo.”
18 Hodu, wose wakasola cifusizo munhu na cakwe, wakacitajila ne makala hamonga no wuvumba mucanyha yayo. Wakawa kwima ha mulango we Lihema, hamonga na Musa na Haluni.
19 Nayo Kola, yakawa kuwakonghola hamonga, mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli, nawo wakatandala wawalanjile Musa na Haluni, ha mulango we lihema. No lukumyo lo Muwaha Mulungu lukejela nhinicizo kuli mhuga yose.
20 No Muwaha Mulungu yakalongana na Musa na Haluni yakawatya,
21 “Ale, mutele, mwime cidaha ne wanhu wawa, nane nowongomeza lwa kamonga.”

22 Ninga Musa na Haluni wakenama sunga no wusu wawo wukapamiza ho hasi, wakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, nha yo gwegwe du mono yo ndawililo yo wumi wose. Alu hono munhu monga yasutye, agwe hodu kowakolelwa likuwo mhuga yose?”

23 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kumutya Musa,
24 “Wuwalonjele wanhu hodu wahame, wawuse mahema gawo behi ne mahema ga Kola, na Datani, na Abilamu.”

25 Hodu Musa yakawa kutuluka hamonga ne walongola we Waisilayeli, wakawa kubita kuli Datani na Abilamu.
26 Musa yakawa kuwalonjela wanhu yakatya, “Ale muhame, muwuse mahema genyu behi ne mahema ge wanhu wawa wabi. Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kupamiza cinhu cawo cinji co cose. Vyono hono mupamize, hodu mokongomezezwa lukulu hamonga nawo mu wubi wawo.”
27 Hodu wanhu wakawa kuwuka behi ne mahema ga Kola, na Datani, na Abilamu.

Nawo Datani hamonga na Abilamu, wakalawila kunze ye mahema gawo, wakawa wemile ha milango ye mahema gawo, hamonga ne wagolece zawo ne wana wawo.
28 Nayo Musa yakawa kuwalonjela wanhu yakatya, “Na mu nzila yiyi, mo momanyila vyono, Muwaha Mulungu yo yanhuma 'ne ho nizitume zizino zose, holeka ane si nghwitumila zizi mu vilungo vyangu no mweneco yaye.
29 Holeka i wanhu wawa hono wafwe lifwa lyono wanhu wose wafwizaga, walece kufwila mwi'fwa lyono Muwaha Mulungu kowalipiciziza, Muwaha mulungu yeze si yanhuma.
30 Ninga hono Muwaha Mulungu yanoze nghani yono yikali hamba sinejela, yisi yahame, yiwamele wene, hamonga ne vinhu vyawo vyose vyono wena navyo, nawo wenjile hasi, kusi, vyo womi du, wafice ku yisi ye wafu, baho langa ho momanyila vyono i wanhu wawa, wamulema Muwaha Mulungu.”

31 Nayo Musa, yali yeza malila wulonje wakwe wuwo, hodu ho baho baho, i hanhu hono wali wemile wa Datani na Abilamu, yisi yikawa kwahama.
32 Yikawa kuwamela Datani na Abilamu, ne zimhuga zawo wose, hamonga na Kola ne mhuga yakwe wose ne watyati wa Kola wose hamonga ne vinhu vyawo vyose.
33 Hodu wakawa wabita ku yisi ye wafu, akuno vyo womi du, hamonga ne vinhu vyawo vyose. Nayo yisi hodu yikawa kudinda, nawo hodu wukawa wo mudumo wawo.
34 Ne wanhu wose Waisilayeli wono wazilanjililaga zizo, wali weza kucihulika cililo cawo, vyono wawowaga wa Kola hamonga ne wayagwe, hobaho wene wakawa kwitula, wakawa kutiza, akuno wazozize wakutya, “Nye, alenyi citize! Yisi yidahile kucimela sunga na sese!”

35 Hanyuma Muwaha Mulungu yakaleta moto, wukawakongoga wawalya wanhu magana mejete na makumi mahano wono walavyaga wuvumba.

36 Hanyuma ya zizo, Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
37 “Wumulonjele Eleyezali u mwana wa Haluni mukohani, yawuse vifusizo vye shaba mu wulawa mu mivimba ye wanhu wono moto wuwakongoje, hanyuma yasole mavu gono gali mumo mu vifusizo, yagamwaje hanhu ho hose du, hambi vifusizo vivyo nha vyelile.
38 Hambi nha vyezwa du, lono vyali vyeza kuletwa ha cikumbicilo co Muwaha Mulungu. Holeka lu, wuvisole vifusizo vivyo vye wanhu wawo wono wawulagwe so wubi wawo, wuvityane viwe nha vibati, ho vitumamilwe so wugubika cikumbicilo. Ne nghani yiyi yowa suguzi kuli Waisilayeli.”
39 Nayo Eleyezali u mukohani, yakawa kuvisola vifusizo, yakawa kuvityana viwe nha vibati, ho vitumamilwe so wugubika cikumbicilo.
40 Ne nghani yiyi, yikawa suguzi kuli Waisilayeli, wakamanya vyono, kumbe si yinojeye munhu wowose mono si we ndelwa ye Walawi, yasejelele ha cikumbicilo, yafuse wuvumba kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Na vyono hono yunji yejelele yanoze nhavivyo, u munhu yuyo kokongomezwa nha vyono yongomezwa Kola, hamonga ne wanhu wakwe. Na zose ziketumwa nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliziza Eleyezali muli Musa.

Haluni yakuwaponya wanhu

41 Na mwizuwa lye kejete, mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli wakanza kumung'ung'ulicila Musa na Haluni wakutya, “Alu anye, ta mulanje, mwakondya kuwawulaga wanhu wanji wo Mulungu.”
42 Nawo lono wali wekongholela hamonga, lono wamulemaga Musa na Haluni, wali weza kwi'lenga kunyuma, kono kolili Lihema, wakaliwona livunde ligubice mwi 'Hema, hamonga no lukumyo lo Muwaha Mulungu lukwijela.
43 Musa na Haluni wakawa kubita, wakema ha mwande ye Lihema lyono Lyelile,
44 nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
45 “Ale mutele, mwime cidaha ne wanhu wawa, nane niwongomeze lwa kamonga!”

Nawo wose vyono wejete wakenama sunga no wusu wawo wukapamiza ho hasi,
46 Musa yakamutya Haluni, “Ale wusole cifusizo cako, wucitajile ne makala gono gakulawa mu cikumbicilo, hanyuma, wutajile wuvumba mucanyha yayo. Wuwabitile wanhu musunho, wukawanozeze wilamule. Ale Wiyohaje! Hambi likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu nha lyakolela muno, ne lihuhuji lyakondya kuza.”

47 Nayo Haluni yakawa na cihuliko, yakasola cifusizo cakwe lwa kamonga, yakawa kumanyhila ha ciligati ye wanhu lono wali wehangulize hamonga. Nayo yali yeza kuwona vyono wutamwa we lihuhuji wakondya kwanza muli wanhu, hobaho yakawa kuwutajila wuvumba mu moto, yakawa kuwanozeza wanhu wilamule.
48 Ne litumo lilyo likemila lihuhuji lilece kujendelela. Haluni yakekala vyo yemile du ha ciligati ya wono wali wakondya kufwa, na wono wali wakali womi.
49 Ne liwalo lye wanhu wono wali wakondya kufwa, lyali lificile wanhu elufu yikumi na yine, na magana mupunghati. Awalya wono wafwa so cibede ca Kola, awo si wawalilizwe mwi'walo lilino.
50 Na lono lihuhuji lyali lyapuluta, Haluni nayo yakawa kuwuya kuli Musa, ha mulango we Lihema.


Wuwale 17

Nghome ya Haluni

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
2 “Walonjele wanhu Waisilayeli, wakupele nghome yikumi na nyejete, zeze nghome monga monga ku wulawa mu walongola we milango yose vyono yikumi na myejete. Nagwe wandice munhu ne litagwa lyakwe mu nghome yakwe.
3 Ninga mu nghome yono yowa so Walawi, wandice litagwa lya Haluni. Nazo zowa nghome monga monga nha vyono linha liwalo lye walongola we milango yawo.
4 Wuzisole wuzilete mwi 'Hema lyangu lyono Lyelile, wuziwice ha mwande ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane, hono nghwitang'anilila nagwe.
5 Ne nghome yo munhu mono nimuhaguye nene, yotula. Na mu nzila yiyi, mo nowudumizya wung'ung'ulice wono si wukuduma, wono wakukung'ung'ulicila wawa Waisilayeli.”

6 Nayo Musa yakawalonjela Waisilayeli. Nawo walongola wawo, wose wakamuletela Musa nghome monga monga, munhu na mulango wakwe, wakaleta nghome yikumi na nyejete zose hamonga. Ne nghome ya Haluni nayo, wakayihanziliza hamonga na zizo.
7 Nayo Musa yakawa kuziwika mwi 'Hema, ha mwande ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane.

8 Ne mitondo yaco. Lono Musa yali yali mwi Hema, yakawona vyono nghome ya Haluni, yono yali so mhuga ye Walawi, yakondya kutula, yibonhoye, kanyhi yilelile malozi!
9 Nayo Musa yakawa kuzisola nghome zose vyono zinha, yakawa kuwalajila Waisilayeli. Nawo Waisilayeli wakaziwona zono ziweye. Nawo walongola wakawa kuzisola zinghome munhu na nghome yakwe.
10 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Ale, wuyigomolezaje nghome ya Haluni ha mwande ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane. Nayo wuyilece yiwe kwikala baho du, yiwe yo suguzi kuli Waisilayeli wono wali cibede, hambi nha wofwa du, hono wawe wakali wajendeleye ne mang'ung'uliko gawo.”
11 Nayo Musa yakanoza du, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza.

12 Nawo wanhu Waisilayeli, wakamulonjela Musa wakatya, “Alu, awu nhondo, weza wo mudumo wetu!
13 Hambi hono yeza vyono, munhu wo wose mono kosejelela behi ne Lihema kofwa, alu kumbe ase wose cofwa du!”


Wuwale 18

Milimo ye Wakohani ne Walawi

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Haluni yakamutya, “Agwe hamonga ne wanago we cilume, hamonga ne Walawi wose, munojeye mwidinghulile masutya genyu nyo weneco, i masutya gogose gono mowa kusutya, mu witumami wenyu wa mwi 'Hema lyangu lyono Lyelile. Ninga kanyhi, agwe Haluni hamonga ne wanago, modinghula nyo weneco masutya genyu ga mu witumami wenyu wo wukohani.
2 Holeka wuwalete wanduguzo, hamonga ne mhuga ye Walawi, ho mwitumane nawo, wawe kukutaza lono agwe hamonga ne wanago we cilume mukwitumama mwi 'Hema.
3 Nawo wakamise wulindilizi wawo ko wulajilize wako, hamonga no wulajilize wawo kuli ze Lihema. Ninga langa, walece kwituma mulimo wunji wowose, wono wudomile kuli vinhu vyono vyelile vye Hanhu hono helile, hamba vinhu vye cikumbicilo. Na hono wejelele wanoze nhavivyo, wumanye vyono gwegwe hamonga na wene, mowulagwa.
4 Wene wanojeye wetumane hamonga nagwe, wawufisilize no mulimo wono wapejigwe wa mu witumami wa mwi 'Hema. Ninga munhu wowose mono si yakwiliye, ayo langa mulece kwitumana nayo.
5 Gwegwe go mweneco, hamonga ne wanago, yo nyenye mono munojeye muwitume mulimo wuwo we Hanhu hono helile, hamonga ne cikumbicilo. Likuwo lyangu ho lilece kuwaziza kanyhi i wanhu Waisilayeli.
6 Hambi nha yo nene mono nawahagula i wanduguzo Walawi, ku wulawa mu Waisilayeli, wawe nha cipegwa kuli'gwe. Nawo lu, weza wasiwanulilwe kuli'ne, ho wetume mulimo wo witumami wawo mwi Hema.
7 Ninga agwe hamonga ne wanago, yo nyenye du mono mokwituma u mulimo wo wukohani, u mulimo wa ha cikumbicilo, hamonga ne milimo yose ye Hanhu hono helile muno. I milimo yiyo, yowa yo milimo yenyu nyenye, hambi nha nakondya kukupela i cipegwa co Wukohani. Ninga u munhu wo wose mono si yakwiliye, mono kosejelela yaze behi ne vinhu vyono vyelile, u munhu yuyo yawulagwe.”

I vilavywa vyono viwanojeye Wakohani

8 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela kanyhi Haluni yakamutya, “Ale, wukumbucile vyono ane, nghukupela gwegwe i vilavywa vyose vyono wakundavizya nha yikumbiko lye cilavywa cinyanywe. Ane nghukupela gwegwe, hamonga na we zindelo zako, ho viwe vyako cibitilila.
9 Na kuli vilavywa vyono vyelile, vyono si vinyanywe mu cikumbicilo, navyo vyowa vyako, navyo vyovi: Vilavywa vyo wusaje, vilavywa vyo wubi, ne vilavywa vyo wusutye. Cinhu co cose cono celile cono colavywa kuli'ne, aco cowa cako, hamonga ne wanago.
10 Ninga yikunojeye weze kuvilila i Hanhu Hono Helile i vinhu vivino. Kanyhi wulanjilile, vyono i wanhu we cilume du, wo wanojeye walye vinhu vivyo. Hambi nha vyelile.

11 “Hamonga na zizi, yowa sunga hamonga ne vilavywa visinjiswe vyono Waisilayeli wakundavizya, vyose vyowa vyako du. Ane nghukupela gwegwe, hamonga ne wanago we cilume, na we cicekulu, sunga mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza. No munhu wowose wa mu nyumba yako, mono si yeyavufye, yuyo kosuma kulya.

12 “Nane kanyhi, nghukupela sunga ne mana malongozi ga mu'si, gono Waisilayeli, wakumhela mu mwaka na mwaka, nha cinji, mafuta, divayi, hamonga no wuhemba.
13 Vivyo vyose, vyowa vyako gwegwe. No munhu wo wose wa mu nyumba yako mono si yeyavufye, yuyo kosuma kulya.

14 “Vinhu vyo vyose vyono visina wulajilize, vyono visiwanuliywe kuli'ne, vyose vyowa vyako du gwegwe.
15 Mwana wowose u mulongozi kulelwa, hamba yiwe nyana yo mutugo, yono Waisilayeli wondavizya, yiyo yowa yako. Ninga langa, yinojeye wubocele nghomboza so mwana mulongozi kulelwa. Kanyhi yowa yikunojeye wubocele nghomboza nha vivilya so zinyana zinongozi kulelwa ze zinimu, zono nyavufywe.
16 I wana wadodo wanojeye wakombozwe lono wakondya kuficila mwezi monga. Wakombozwe ko wuhalize wa fweza yihano, mu nghango ye mapesa gono gakwitumamilwa mwi 'Hema lyono Lyelile.
17 Ninga zinyana zinongozi kulelwa ze zing'ombe, na ze zingholo, na ze zimhene, azo si zinojeye zikombozwe yaye, hambi azo nha zangu nene no mweneco, hambi nha zinojeye zilavywe yikumbiko. Ne sakami yazo wunojeye wuyimizize ha cikumbicilo, ninga mhama yayo, wuyikongoje mu moto, yiwe cilavywa ce ndigwa, ne mhunjile yayo yikunyendeza ane muno.
18 Ninga langa nyama yayo, ayo yowa sawo yako, nha vyono nyama ye nghwa hamonga ne cima co mugulu wo kulume ce cilavywa cisinjiswe.

19 “Ne vilavywa vinulwe vye vinhu vyono vyelile, vyose vye mawala gose, vyono Waisilayeli wowa kundavizya, vyose nghukupela gwegwe, vyowa vyako gwegwe hamonga ne wanago we cilume, na we cicekulu, sunga mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza. Na ci cino cowa co cilagane, cono si cosuma kubenwa yaye, cilagane cono nakondya kucinoza kuli'gwe, hamonga no lulelo lwako lose.”

20 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Haluni kanyhi yakatya, “Wulece kubocela, hamba kusola sawo ye liwala lyo lyose, yiwe nha wuhazi wako. Hamba hasina yigawo lyolyose lye ciwanza, cono kosuma kusola ku wulawa mu yisi ye Waisilayeli. Hambi ane nha yonene Muwaha Mulungu mono nili yigawo lyako, no wuhazi wako.”

I vilavywa vyono viwanojeye Walawi

21 Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Nawapela Walawi vilavywa vyose vye zaka, vyono i wanhu wa Isilayeli wakundavizya. Nayo zaka yiyo, liwe lye 'fungu lyawo, so witumami wawo wa mwi Hema lyono Lyelile.
22 Ninga Waisilayeli wanji wose, walecele lukulu kulisejelela behi Lihema. Hono walisejelele behi, wokwiletela weneco wulipicizwe we lifwa.
23 Holeka ku wulawa zuwali, Walawi wali wene du, wo wowa kwituma mwi 'Hema, no kulanjilila milimo yose yono yili mumo. No wutemye wuwu, wo wutemye we cibitilila sunga na kuli zindelo zenyu zose. Ninga wene Walawi, wowa wasina wuhazi wo wose muli Waisilayeli.
24 Hambi nha nakondya kuwapela wene vilavywa vyose vye zaka, vyono wakundavizya Waisilayeli viwe vyawo. Na yiyi yo soko yono yikuleka niwalonjela vyono, wene wowa wasina wuhazi wo wose muli Waisilayeli.”

Zaka so Walawi

25 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajiliza Musa,
26 “Yawalonjele Walawi yatye, ‘Lono mukubocela zaka ku wulawa kuli Waisilayeli, zaka yono Muwaha Mulungu yakumupelenyi yiwe sawo yenyu, mowa munojeye nanye, mumulavizye Muwaha Mulungu cilavywa cinulwe, mu wulawa mu zaka yiyo, yiwe zaka yono yilawile mu zaka.
27 Ne cilavywa cenyu cico cinulwe, cowa nha co cilavywa cono munhu mulimi, kalavyaga mu wulawa mu wuhemba wupya, na mu divayi mhya.
28 Na mu nzila yiyi, nanye mo molavizya vilavywa vyose vinulwe, vyono vikulavizywa Muwaha Mulungu, vyono vikulawa mu zaka zose zono mukuzibocela ku wulawa kuli Waisilayeli. Nanye mumupele Haluni mukohani i vilavywa vivino vyose.
29 Ninga mumulavizyaje i viswanu ku wulawa muli vyono muboceye.
30 Na kolo mwakondya kulavya viswanu, nanye mosuma kusigala na vyono visigaye, nha vyono yinha, munhu kasigazaga nayo cinji, lono yakondya kulavya cilavywa cakwe.
31 Nanye mosuma kuvilila hanhu ho hose vivyo vyono visigaye, gwegwe, hamonga na we mhuga yako wose. Hambi nha lyo lifungu lyenyu so witumami wenyu wa mwi Hema.
32 Nanye si mowa muli wasutyi yaye, so wulavya vivyo vyono visigaye, hambi nha mowa mwakondya kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, i viswanu vyose. Ninga mulanjilile, mulecele lukulu kuvyayufya i vilavywa vyono vyelile vye Waisilayeli, so wulongola kulya cilavywa cocose, lono vivilya vilavywa viswanu vikali si vinasiwanulwa no kulavywa. Na hono mwijelele du munoze nhavivyo, langa mofwa.’ ”


Wuwale 19

Mavu ge ndama ye liwala lidunghu.

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajiliza Musa na Haluni yakawatya,
2 wawapele Waisilayeli wulajilize wuwu, wawatye. Ale muwaletele Musa na Haluni ndama ye liwala lidunghu, ndama nhelevu yono yisina cilema co cose, yono yikali sinawicilwa lubano, no kwituma mulimo wunji yaye,
3 wamupele Eleyezali u mukohani. Nayo ndama yiyo yitegulwe kunze ye nghambi, yikawulajilwe ha mwande ya Eleyezali mukohani.
4 Nayo Eleyezali mukohani, yayisole sakami yayo ndodo ne cidole cakwe, yayimize kali mupunghati ku deha ye Lihema.
5 Nayo nyama yose ye ndama yiyo, hamonga no mukunza wayo, ne sakami yayo, hamonga no wutumbu wayo, vyose vikongogwe mu moto ha mwande yo mukohani.
6 Nayo mukohani yasole cinhambi co mubici wo muhagata, no mulejehe musisili mudunghu, ne cinhambi ca ezobu, yavigumile vyose mu moto wuwo.
7 Na kolo zakondya zizo, yimunojeye mukohani yehovuje muwili wakwe, yakanze sunga ne myenda yakwe, ho yenjile mu nghambi. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, mwene kokwikala mwavufywe du sunga kono lyoswela lizuwa.
8 No munhu mono kowituma mulimo wuwo wo kuyikongoga ndama yiyo, u munhu yuyo yimunojeye nayo yehovuje muwili wose, kanyhi yakanze ne myenda yakwe. Ninga nayo nhavivilya kokwikala mwavufywe du sunga kono lyoswela lizuwa.
9 Hanyuma u munhu mono si mwavufywe, yagazole gago mavu ge ndama, yagalunde i hanhu hono helile kuko kunze ye nghambi. I hanhu hono hanojeye galundwe, hono mhuga ye Waisilayeli wosuma kugasola wagetumamile so wuwikaniza malenga go weze. No wuhembi wuwu wonozwa so cilavywa co wubi.
10 No munhu yuyo mono kozola i mavu gago ge ndama, yimunojeye yakanze myenda yakwe, ninga kanyhi kokwikala mwavufywe du sunga kono lyoswela lizuwa. No wulajilize wuwu, wojendelela du sunga mu vilo vyono vikuza, nawo wowa kuli Waisilayeli sunga na kuli wajenzi wono wekalane nawo.

Kuli zo wupamiza muvimba

11 Munhu wowose mono kowupamiza muvimba, hodu kowa yeyavufya mweneco, sunga vilo mupunghati vipulute.
12 Nayo yowa yimunojeye yeyezeze mweneco mu malenga gago go kwiyezeza, mwi'zuwa lye kadatu, na mwi'zuwa lyo mupunghati, nayo kowa yakondya kwezwa. Ninga hono yalece kwiyezeza mweneco mu malenga gago, mwi'zuwa lye kadatu na mwi'zuwa lyo mupunghati, hodu kokwikala vyo mwavufywe du.
13 Munhu wowose mono kowupamiza muvimba, kanyhi yalece kwiyeza mweneco, hodu kokwikala vyo mwavufywe du. Vyono i malenga go weze si getiywe muli mwene. Nayo yakulyavufya Lihema lyo Muwaha Mulungu, hamba si kowalilizwa ne wanhu wo Mulungu.

14 Na hono munhu yafwile mwi'hema mugati, u munhu mono kowa yali mumo mugati ye lihema, mwi'pango lono yuyo yakufwa, hamba sunga na mono kohelela du mwi'hema lilyo, hodu kowa yeyavufya, sunga vilo mupunghati vipulute.
15 Sunga ne zinjilo zose, hamonga ne viya vyose vyono vili mwazi si vigubucize, vyowa vyavufywa.
16 Na hono munhu yamupamize munhu mono yawulagwe, hamba mono yefwiliye mweneco du, kunze ye milango ye mahema, hamba hono munhu yunji yapamize mafupa go mufu, hamba citenjelo, u munhu yuyo kowa yakondya kwiyavufya sunga vilo mupunghati vipulute.

17 No wezwe wo wavufywe, wowa wuli nhavi: Gasolwe i mavu gagalya ge ndama ye liwala lidunghu, yono yakongogwa so cilavywa co wubi, gatajilwe mu cungu. Ganegwe ne malenga gono gakuhuluma, gahanzwe mumo mu cungu.
18 Hanyuma nghani nongozi, u munhu mono si mwavufywe, yasole cinhambi ca ezobu, yacizabice mumo mu malenga gago, yagamize mwi 'Hema, sunga na muli vinhu vyose vyono vili mumo mugati yalyo, hamonga ne wanhu wose wono wowa wali baho. Ninga ku lubavu lunji, u munhu mono si mwavufywe, yamumizize malenga u munhu mono yagapamize mafupa go mufu, hamba mufu, hamba citenjelo.
19 Na mwi'zuwa lye kadatu, na mwizuwa lyo mupunghati, u munhu yuyo mono si mwavufywe, yamumizize malenga u munhu mono yeyavufye. Na mwi'zuwa lyo mupunghati, ale, ho yamweze munhu yuyo, kolo yayikanza myenda yakwe, no kwihovuga muwili wose, nayo kowa yezwa.

20 No munhu wo wose mono kowa yakondya kwiyavufya, na kuno ho si yakwiyeza ng'o, ale munhu yuyo, kojendelela kuwa mwavufywe, vyono i malenga go wezwe si gamizizwe muli mwene, hambi munhu yuyo nha yakulyavufya Lihema lyo Muwaha Mulungu. Na ku soko yiyo, hamba si kowalilizwa ne wanhu wo Mulungu.
21 Holeka yikunojeye wuwukamise muno muno wulajilize wu wuno, sunga mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza. No munhu yuyo nayo, mono kowituma mulimo wuwo wo kumiza malenga go wezwe, yimunojeye nayo yakanze myenda yakwe. No munhu wo wose mono kogapamiza malenga gago, nayo kowa yakondya kwiyavufya sunga kono lyoswela lizuwa.
22 Na muli cinhu co cose cono kocipamiza u munhu mwavufywe, hodu ne cinhu cico naco cowa cakondya kwavufywa. Nayo munhu mono kocipamiza cinhu cico, nayo kowa yakondya kwiyavufya. Holeka kokwikala mwavufywe, sunga kono lyoswela lizuwa.


Wuwale 20

Kuli zono zejela ku Kadeshi

(Wut 17:1-7)

1 Na mu mwezi mulongozi, i mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli, wakafika mu nyika ya Zini, wakayisimya nghambi yawo mu Kadeshi. Na lono wali wali baho, Milyamu yakawa kufwa, yakazikwa baho.

2 Ne hanhu baho hono wayisimya nghambi yawo, hakawa hasina malenga, hakawa na nyhilu mbaha muno. Hobaho, wanhu wakekongholela hamonga wamupilime Musa na Haluni.
3 Wakawa kwanza kung'ung'ulika wakutya, “Nye! Yali yiwe yili ciba le, cifwile ha mwande ye Lihema lyo Muwaha Mulungu, ne Waisilayeli wayetu.
4 Alu nhawulenyi nye muwa kucileta hanhu baha mono nyika yiyi? Anye kumbe mucileta bahano ho cifwe sese hamonga ne mitugo zetu.
5 Alo nhawulenyi mukaciwusa ku yisi ya Misili, nalu mwacileta mu yisi yiyi mbi nhavi, yono yisina hamba wucindya cinji co cose? Hasina hamba wuhemba bwete, hasina mikuyu, hasina zabibu, hamba makomamanga du bwete. Hamba malenga du go kung'wa, hasina!”
6 Nayo Musa hamonga na Haluni, wakawuka hali wanhu wakabita wose vyono wejete, wakema ha mulango we Lihema. Wakenamya wusu wawo sunga wukapamiza ho hasi. Nalo lukumyo lo Muwaha Mulungu lukawalawila.

7 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
8 “Ale wusole nghome yako yono yili ha mwande ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane. Hanyuma agwe hamonga na Haluni, muwakongholele hamonga wose we mhuga ye Waisilayeli. Nanye mulilonjelaje litalawe lilyo ha mwande yawo, nalyo litalawe lilyo lyolavya malenga. Na mu nzila yiyo, mosuma kulavya malenga, mu wulawa mumo mwi'talawe, nawo wanhu wotuga malenga, wong'wa weneco hamonga ne mitugo zawo zose.”
9 Musa yakawa kuyisola yiyo nghome, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza.

10 Musa na Haluni, wakawa kuwakongholela mhuga yose hamonga ha mwande ye litalawe. Nayo Musa yakawa kuwalonjela wanhu yakatya, “Ale ta muhulicize, anye mono musina cihuliko! Wulenyi, cimuletelenyi malenga galawe mwitalawe lili?”
11 Hanyuma Musa yakawa kuyinula nghome, yakalitowa litalawe kali kejete. Malenga gakalawila menji muno muno nha muvwilo. Ne wanhu wose wakang'wa wakaca, hamonga sunga ne mitugo zawo zose.

12 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakawahosa Musa na Haluni, yakawatya, “Alu vyono si munyihuwiye 'ne, na kanyhi vyono si musacile no kuvilajila vilungo vyangu viwaha vyono vyelile, hali wanhu wawa Waisilayeli, hodu, nanye si mowalongoza wanhu wawa, sunga muwafise ku yisi yono nawehuwiza vyono nowapela.”

13 Na zizi zejela lono wali wali ha muvwilo wa Meliba, i hanhu hono i wanhu wa Isilayeli wamung'ung'ulicila Muwaha Mulungu, na kanyhi ho hanhu hono Muwaha Mulungu yawalajilila vyono mwene yo Yelile.

U mutemi wa Edomu yakuwemila Waisilayeli walece kupuluta

14 Ale, Musa yakatuma wanhu kuli mutemi wa Edomu ku wulawa baho ha Kadeshi. Yakutya, “Wanhu wawa, wakulawa kuli sese co wanduguzo we nghungugo ya Isilayeli. Nitumbiye vyono nagwe wakondya kumanya ze magayo getu, na vyono cikugazika,
15 na vyono walezi wetu wabita kuko ku Misili, i hanhu hono cekala myaka minji muno muno. Na vyono Wamisili wawagaza muno walezi wetu, sunga na sese wose.
16 Nase cikamulilila Muwaha Mulungu yacipele wutazi. Nayo mbeka yakacihulika cililo cetu, yakaciletela Malayika mono yakaciwusa ku yisi yiyo ya Misili. Ninga aluno, cili mono yisi ya Kadeshi, kaya yono yili mu mupaka we yisi yako.
17 Holeka cikukulomba wucikombole hamonga ne mitugo zetu cikolele mu yisi yako. Nase hamba sicosuma kuyileka balabala no kukola caka mu mawimbi gako, hamba mu migunda yako ye zabibu, hamba si cong'wa ne malenga ga mu masima gako yaye. Langa 'se cotyatya balabala du sunga lono cowa cawuka mu yisi yako.”

18 Ninga Waedomu wakapitula wakatya, “Ase langa calema! Hamba si comukombolenyi yaye ho mukolele mono yisi yetu! Na hono mwijelele mutye mukole, cokuza cimuhomenyi.”

19 Nawo Waisilayeli wakapitula wakatya, “Nye wayetu, ase cowa cityatizye balabala du, na hono sese, hamba mitugo zetu, zing'we malenga genyu, ase cohaliza. Ninga cono cikulomba, cipulute du.”

20 Nawo Waedomu wakapitula wakatya, “Ase calema le!” Hodu ho baho Waedomu wakawa kubita hamonga ne wasilikale wenji muno, wakawahome Waisilayeli.
21 Alu vyono Waedomu walema kuwakombola Waisilayeli wakolele mu yisi yawo, hodu nawo Waisilayeli wakawa kuwuya ne nyuma, wakakolela nzila yinji.

U wufwe wa Haluni

22 Ne mhuga yose ya Isilayeli, wakawuka ku Kadeshi, wakafika kwi'tunda lya Holi,
23 ha mupaka wa Edomu. Na lono wali wali baho, u Muwaha Mulungu yakawalonjela Musa na Haluni yakatya,
24 “Ayu Haluni si kosuma kwinjila mu yisi yono nawehuwiziza Isilayeli vyono nowapela, langa mwene kofwila mu nzila du. Hambi nyenye vyono muli wejete, nha mwabedegala lilajilizo lyangu akulya ku muvwilo wa Meliba.
25 Holeka lu, Musa, wumusolaje Haluni hamonga na Eleyezali u mwanagwe, wuwategule kucanyha ye litunda lya Holi.
26 Nagwe kolo weza kufika kuko, wumuvulaje Haluni myenda yakwe yose ye cikohani, wumuvwice Eleyezali. Nayo Haluni hodu kofwila baho.”
27 Nayo, Musa yakanoza nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza. Nawo wose wakawa kubita kwi'tunda lya Holi kucanyha, akuno ne wanhu wose wakawa wawalanjiliye du.
28 Musa yakawa kumuvula Haluni i myenda yose ye cikohani, yakawa kumuvwika Eleyezali. Nayo Haluni hodu hobaho yakawa kufwa kuko kucanyha ye litunda. Nawo Musa hamonga na Eleyezali wakawa kutima hasi ye litunda.
29 Ne mhuga yose ya Isilayeli, wakamanya vyono Haluni yakondya kufwa. Holeka wose wakamulilila vilo makumi madatu.


Wuwale 21

Waisilayeli wakuwasumiza Wakanani

1 Nayo Mutemi we Wakanani, mono yacemwaga Aladi, mono yali wo lubavu lwe takama lwe yisi ya Kanani, yali yeza kuhulika du vyono Waisilayeli wakuza, wakoleye nzila ye Atalimu, hobaho yakawa kuwabitila yakawa kuwahoma, sunga yakasola wayawo wanji mateka.
2 Nawo Waisilayeli wakelahiza hali Muwaha Mulungu wakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, hono wucitaze ciwasume wanhu wawa, nase cowongomezeza lukulu hamonga ne makaya gawo.”
3 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawahulika wulombe wawo, yakawataza, nawo wakawasuma mbeka i Wakanani. Waisilayeli wakawongomezeza lukulu Wakanani hamonga ne makaya gawo gose. Wakahacema hanhu baho Holema.

I nzoka ya shaba

4 Nawo Waisilayeli, wakawa kutuluka ku wulawa he 'tunda lya Holi, wakatyatya balabala yono yibitile ku Agaba, ho wayipilime yisi ye Waedomu. Ninga mumo mu nzila, wanhu wakatimba muno muno so mujendo,
5 hobaho wakawa kumung'ung'ulicila Mulungu hamonga na Musa, wakutya, “Alu nhawule wukaciwusa mu yisi ya Misili ho cifwile mumu mu nyika yiyi, i hanhu hono hasina ndigwa, hamba malenga bwete go kung'wa. Nase si cosuma hamba hadodo kukangalila kujendelela kulya ndigwa yiyi mhelu mhelu!”
6 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kuwagumilila Waisilayeli mazoka. Na wenji wa muli wene wakalumwa ne mazoka gago wakafwa.
7 Hobaho i wanhu wakawa kumuziza Musa wakamutya, “Gwe Musa, ase cisutya vyono cimung'ung'ulicila Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga sunga na gwegwe. Alu cikukulomba, wumulombe Muwaha Mulungu yagawuse mazoka gaga.” Nayo Musa yakawa kuwalombela wanhu kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
8 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kumulonjela Musa yanoze nzoka ya shaba, yayitumbice mwi'bici, no munhu wo wose mono lizoka lyomuluma, hono yagalamile yayilanje nzoka yiyo, munhu yuyo kopona.
9 Hobaho Musa yakawa kuyinoza nzoka ye shaba, yakayitumbika mwi'bici. No munhu wo wose mono yalumwa ne zinzoka zi zilya, nayo yakagalamila no kuyilanga nzoka ye shaba, hobaho munhu yuyo yakapona.

Ku wulawa kwi'tunda lya Holi, sunga mwi'lolo lya Mowabu

10 Nawo Waisilayeli wakajendelela no muhinzo wawo wakafika ku Aboti.
11 Wali weza kuwuka hanhu baho, wakayisimya nghambi yawo ha Abalimu mu nyika ye cilima ye yisi ye Wamowabi.
12 Hanyuma kanyhi wakayisimya nghambi yawo mwi'lolo lya Zeledi.
13 Wali weza kulawa baho, wakajendelela kanyhi sunga wakafika kusumuka ye likolongo lya Alinoni, mu nyika yono yitandaye yificile sunga ko ku yisi ye Waamoli, wakayisimya nghambi yawo baho. (Nayo yiyo Alinoni, wali wo mupaka we yisi ye Wamowabi ne yisi ye Waamoli.)
14 Na yiyi yo soko yono yikuleka i citabu co Muwaha Mulungu co wulolose wo wulugu, cikulonga cikutya,

“Likaya lye Wahebu mu yisi ya Sufa,

hamonga ne malolo,

likolongo lya Alinoni,

15 hamonga no mwalambalo we malolo,

wono wutandaye sunga ko kwi'kaya lya Ali,

sunga ko ku mupaka wa Mowabu.”

16 Na ku wulawilila baho, wakajendelela no muhinzo wawo sunga wakafika ha Beyeli i hanhu hono hakucemwa Cisima, i hanhu hono Muwaha Mulungu yalonganila na Musa yakatya, “Ale wakongholelaje wanhu wose hamonga, nane nowapela malenga wang'we.”
17 Na mwipango lilyo i Waisilayeli wakemba lwimbo alu wakutya,

“Ale go cisima wuvwile malenga.

Nase cocilamusiza mu wimbe.

18 Go cisima cono wahimbwa ne wana wo mutemi,

Hamonga ne walongola we wanhu,

Cono cahimbwa ne nghome yo wutemi

Hamonga ne zinghome zawo.”

Nawo wakawa kutuluka ku wulawa mumo mu nyika, wakawa kubita sunga ko ku Matana.
19 Kanyhi wali weza kulawa baho, wakabita sunga ko ku Nahaliyeli. Na ku wulawa Nahaliyeli, wakabita sunga ko ku Bamoti,
20 na ku wulawa Bamoti, wakabita sunga ko kwi'lolo lya mu yisi ye Wamowabi, hasika ye cizuli ce litunda lya Pisiga, wakawa wakulilanga linyika hasi yayo.

Waisilayeli wakumusuma u mutemi Sihoni no mutemi Ogu

(Kum 2:26–3:11)

21 Hanyuma i wanhu wa Isilayeli, wakatuma wanhu wabite kuli Waamoli ko mutemi Sihoni wakamulonjele watye,
22 “Cikukulomba wucikombole sese hamonga ne mitugo zetu, cikolele mu yisi yako. Nase hamba si cosuma kuyileka balabala, no kukola caka mu mawimbi gako, hamba mu migunda yako ye zabibu, si cong'wa ne malenga ga mu masima gako yaye. Langa ase cotyatya balabala du, sunga lono cowa cawuka mu yisi yako.”
23 Ninga mutemi Sihoni hamba si yawakombola i wanhu wa Isilayeli ho wakolele mu yisi yakwe. Langa mwene yakawakonghola hamonga wasilikale wakwe wo wulugu, yakawa kubita kuli Yahazi ku nyika, yakawa kuwahoma Waisilayeli.
24 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, Waisilayeli wakawawulaga wanhu wenji wa muli walugu wawo mumo mu wulugu. Wakayisola ne yisi yawo ku wulawilila kwi'kolongo lya Alinoni ku lubavu lwe sumuka, sunga ko ku Yaboki, nha cinji sunga ko kuli Waamoli, vyono mupaka we Waamoli wali wina wulindilizi we vilungo viwaha.
25 Holeka i Waisilayeli wakazisuma zisi zose ze Waamoli no kuzisola, hamonga ne Heshiboni, sunga ne makaya gose gono gali gapilime. Wakawa kwikala mu zisi zizo.
26 Nalyo likaya lya Heshiboni, lyali lyo likaya liwaha, lyo mutemi Sihoni mutemi we Waamoli, mono vikehoma no mutemi mulongozi we Wamowabi, nayo yakamusuma yakayiteka sunga ne yisi yakwe yose, ku wulawilila kwi'kolongo lya Alinoni.
27 Na yiyi yo yaleka i mbazi yikulonga yikutya,

“Ale, wuze ku Heshiboni,

kwi'kaya lyo mutemi Sihoni!

Cokwenda ciliwone likuzengwa kanyhi

no kusimywa.

28 Hambi wasilikale wa Sihoni wo wulugu,

nha wenjila lwa kamonga nha moto mwi'kaya lya Heshiboni.

Wakalyongomeza likaya lya Ali ya Mowabu.

Hamonga ne wawaha we vigongo vya Alinoni.

29 Woyi he anye mo wanhu wa Mowabu.

Anye mono muhembaga mulungu Cemoshi,

alu mongomezwa!

Mulungu wenyu yawambuza wanhu

waweza watizizi.

Ne wacekulu nawo wasolwa mateka

no mutemi we Waamoni.

30 Ninga aluno i wana wawo nawo wongomezwa ku wulawa mu

nzila yose, wulawa Heshiboni sunga ko ku Diboni. Na ku

wulawa Nashimu sunga ko ku Nofa behi na Medeba.”

31 Holeka i wanhu wa Isilayeli, wakekala mu yisi ye Waamoli.
32 Nayo Musa yakawatuma wanhu wanji, wakalanje nzila swanu yono yowataza ho walihome likaya lya Yezeli. Waisilayeli wakaliteka likaya lilyo, hamonga ne vikaya vyose vyono vyali vilipilime, wakawawinga ne Waamoli wose wono wekalaga mumo.

33 Hanyuma Waisilayeli wakawuya ne nyuma, wakayityatya balabala yono yibitile ku Bashani. Nayo mutemi Ogu wa Bashani, yakabita hamonga ne wasilikale wakwe wo wulugu, ho vikehome nawo ha Edileyi.
34 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Wulecele lukulu kumogopa Ogu, ane nokupela wusume kuli mwene, na kuli wanhu wakwe wose, na kuli yisi yakwe nhavivyo. Ho wumunoze nha vyono wamunoza Sihoni, u mutemi we Waamoli, mono yali yatemile mu Heshiboni.”
35 Nawo Waisilayeli wakamuwulaga Ogu hamonga ne wanagwe we cilume, sunga ne wanhu wose du, si yasigala yunji hamba yumonga. Hanyuma wakayiteka ne yisi yakwe yose.


Wuwale 22

U mutemi wa Mowabu yakumucema Balamu

1 Nawo Waisilayeli, wakajendelela no muhinzo wawo, sunga wakafika ku yisi yo wuhihi, mu yisi ya Mowabu, kucilima ya Yoludani, wakayisimya nghambi yawo baho vidomile ne Yeliko.

2 Nayo Balaki u mutemi wa Mowabu, u mwana wa Zipola, yakazihulika zo winji we Waisilayeli, sunga na zose zono Waisilayeli wali wazituma kuli Waamoli.
3 Holeka, mwene hamonga ne wanhu wakwe wose, wakenjilwa no woga wuwaha muno muno ku soko ye Waisilayeli.
4 Nawo Wamowabi wakawa kuwalonjela walongola we Wamidiyani wakawatya, “Nye wayetu, lipuga lili lye Waisilayeli, yiwaha muno, lyowa nha vyono nghambaku yisosolaga mahanze mu mawimbi.”
5 Holeka mutemi Balaki, yakatuma wanhu wabite wakamuceme Balamu u mwana wa Beyoli, mono yekalaga ku Petoli, behi ne likolongo lya Efulata, mu yisi ya Amawu. Nawo wakabita, wakamulonjela Balamu zinghani zono watumwa na Balaki, wakamutya, “Ane nghukusaka wumanye vyono, likungugo lyose lye Waisilayeli lyaza ku wulawa ku Misili. Ne wanhu wayo weyagaye hanhu hose, nane nghogopa, wadahile kuyisola yisi yetu.
6 Kanyhi wenji muno muno kuciza ne liwalo lyetu. Holeka nghukunena wuze wundijitile wanhu wawa. Na lunji holeka cosuma kuwasuma no kuwawinga wawuce mono yisi yetu. Hambi nha nghumanyile vyono agwe, hono wuwalugute wanhu, hodu wakulugutwa, na hono wuwalijite, hodu wakuwa walijitwa.”

7 Nawo Wamowabi hamonga ne Wamidiyani, wakasoleleza hamonga ne malipo so gago malijito, wakawa kubita kuli Balamu, wakawa kumulonjela zose zono Balaki yawatumile kuli mwene.
8 Nayo Balamu yakawatya, “Ale, izuwali mugone baha, kolo wela i mitondo, ho nomulonjelenyi vyono Muwaha Mulungu kowa yanonjela.” Nawo walongola we Wamowabi wakalumba zizo, wakagona baho ha Balamu.

9 Nayo Mulungu yakawa kumuziza Balamu, yakamuwuza yakutya, “Alu Balamu, wawano wanhuci wono wali hono hako?”

10 Nayo Balamu yakapitula yakatya, “U mutemi we Wamowabi yo yawatumile waze wanonjele,
11 vyono i wanhu wono wazize wakulawa ku Misili, watandaye mu yisi yose. Holeka yakusaka 'ne nimulijitile wanhu wawo, ho yasume kuwahoma, yawawinje na mu yisi yakwe.”

12 Nayo Mulungu yakamulonjela Balamu yakatya, “Gwe Balamu, wulecele lukulu kubitana ne wanhu wawa ye, hamba wulece kuwalijita i Waisilayeli, hambi 'ne nha nakondya kuwalugutiza.”

13 Wali weza kwela hakali mitondo, Balamu yakawa kuwabitila wawalya wono watumwa na Balaki, yakawalonjela yakatya, “Ale, muwuye kuli Balaki, hambi Mulungu nha kanemeza ho ndece kubitana hamonga nanye.”
14 Hobaho nawo wakawa kuwuya kuli Balaki, wakawa kumulonjela zose, vyono Balamu kalema kuza hamonga nawo.

15 Hobaho, nayo Balaki yali yeza kuzihulika zizo, yakawa kuwatuma kanyhi walongola wenji muno, wono wali wena lilumbo si nha awalya walongozi.
16 Nawo wakawa kumubitila Balamu, wakawa kumulonjela zose zono watumigwe na Balaki, wakatya, “Gwe muwaha, dedede, wulece kuleceleza nghani yinji yikwimile, ho wulece kuza kono kwangu ye!
17 Nane nokupela cihumbilizwa ciwaha muno, kanyhi nokunozeza cinhu co cose cono kosaka. Holeka dedede nghukunena, wuze wundijitile wanhu wawa.”

18 Ninga nayo Balamu yakapitula yakatya, “Hamba Balaki yamhele fweza yakwe yose hamonga ne zahabu yose yono yili mwi'kulu lyakwe, ane si nosumila lukulu kubena lilajilizo lyo Muwaha u Mulungu wangu so cinhu cidodo du.
19 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, ta mulindile, mugone bahano nha vyono wanoza wayenyu, nane ho sume kumanya hono Muwaha Mulungu kowa yena yinji kanyhi yo kunonjela hamba ng'o.”

20 Na mu cilo cico, Mulungu yakamuziza Balamu, yakamutya, “Ale, hono wanhu wawa wazize kukusola ho mubitane, hodu wiwikanizaje mubitane nawo. Ninga kolo wafika kuko, witumaje zono nghukulonjela nene du.”
21 Hodu wali weza kwela mitondo yaco, Balamu yakawa kuyiwikaniza ndogowe yakwe, yakawa kubitana ne walongola we Wamowabi.

Balamu ne ndogowe yakwe

22 Nayo Mulungu yakamukolelwa likuwo muno muno Balamu lono yabitaga. Na lono Balamu yali yapandile mu ndogowe yakwe, yali yali hamonga ne wetumami wakwe wejete. Malayika wo Mulungu yakema ha ciligati ye balabala, yamwimile ho yalece kupuluta.
23 Nayo ndogowe yali yeza kumuwona malayika yemile mu balabala, akuno yebate no mumaje mu muwoko wakwe, hobaho ndogowe yikaleka balabala, yikawa kwinjila mu mawimbi. Nayo Balamu yakayitowa ndogowe, yakayigomoleza kanyhi mwi'balabala.
24 Nayo malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu yakatela, yakabita kwima i hanhu hafinyu hono balabala yipajigwe ne migunda ye zabibu akuno na akuno, kanyhi hakawa ne majinji ga mabwe akuno na akuno.
25 Ndogowe yikamuwona malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu, yikawa kwifininyhiza kwi'jinji, yikamukuluza Balamu mugulu wakwe mwi'jinji. Nayo Balamu yakayitowanga kanyhi ndogowe.
26 Malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu yakatela kanyhi ku mwande, yakabita kwima i hanhu hafinyu muno muno, hono hali hasina cilejeho hamba cidodo co kupulutila akuno hamba akuno.
27 Nhondo mu nghanghulo yiyi, ndogowe yali yeza kumuwona malayika, hodu yikawa kubundyagala hasi. Nayo Balamu yali yeza kuziwona zizo, yakakolwa muno muno ne likuwo, yakawa kuyitowanga muno ndogowe ne nghome yakwe.
28 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu nhondo yakawa kuyipela ndogowe wusume wo kulonga. Ndogowe yikawa kumulonjela Balamu yikutya, “Mwene 'gwe Balamu, ane nghusutizya cicici? Alu nhawule wukunhowanga du sunga zafika nghanghulo yidatu?”

29 Nayo Balamu yakayipitula ndogowe yakamitya, “Nghukutowanga, vyono agwe kunozeza ane nghani ya wulele muno muno! Na hono nali niwe nina mumaje, mbeka nali nghuwulaje zuwali.”

30 Ndogowe nayo yikapitula yikamutya, “Ale nha yo nene no ndogowe yako du, mono kujendelaga mucanyha yangu mu vilo vyose vyo wikale wako? Wule, ho lili 'zuwa linji lyono nowa nakutendela nhavivi?”

Nayo Balamu yakayipitula yakatya, “Aa ng'o.”

31 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu nhondo ho yakawa kumwenzelula Balamu i meso gakwe, yakawa kumuwona malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu, vyono yemile mu balabala, akuno yebate no mumaje wakwe mu muwoko wakwe. Hobaho Balamu yakawa kugwa wugubama, no wusu wakwe wukapamiza ho hasi.
32 Nayo malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kumuwuza Balamu yakutya, “Alu 'gwe Balamu, so cici kuwa kuyitowanga nhavivi ndogowe yako, sunga nghanghulo yidatu? Ane niza ho nghwimile mu nzila yako, vyono si yali yikunojeye agwe wubite mu muhinzo wuwu.
33 Ninga alu ndogowe yako yo yimbwene, sunga yiwa kusuma kuyileka nzila mu nghanghulo zizi yidatu. Na hono ndogowe yako yali yilece kunoza nhavivyo, mbeka nali nghuwulaje gwegwe, ninga ndogowe si nali niyiwulaje yaye.”

34 Nayo Balamu yakapitula, “Gwe muwaha, ane sutya, hambi nha si nali manyile vyono agwe wali wimile mu balabala ho wunyimile ndece kupuluta. Ninga aluno hono wuwe wukwihwesa vyono si yinojeye njendelele no muhinzo wuwu, hodu nowuya ne nyuma mbuye kwetu.”
35 Ninga malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu yakamupitula yakatya, “Hodu, kosuma kujendelela, mubitane hamonga ne wanhu wawa. Ninga wubite wukalonje zono nene no mweneco nokulonjela.” Nhodo Balamu, yakawa kubitana ne wanhu wawalya.

Balaki yakumubocela Balamu

36 Nayo Balaki yali yeza kuhulika vyono Balamu yakuza, hobaho yakawa kubita kumuhinjiliziza ku Ali, likaya lyono lili he kolongo lya Alinoni ha mupaka wa Mowabu.
37 Balaki yakawa kumuwuza Balamu yakatya, “Alu nhawule si waza lululya lono nakutumila wanhu ha lulongozi? Hamba lunji wukehwesa vyono ane si nosuma kukupela cihumbilizwa ciwaha?”

38 Nayo Balamu yakamupitula yakamutya, “Gwe Balaki, lanje ta, alu ane naza. Ninga ane sina vilungo vyo kulonjela cinji cocose. Langa ane, nolonga du zono Mulungu kononjela nizilonje.”
39 Nawo wose vyono wejete, Balamu na Balaki, wakawa kubitana sunga ko ku Kiliyati-huzoti.
40 Nawo wali weza kufika baho, hobaho Balaki yakawa kuwulaga ng'ombe monga na ngholo monga, yakawa kumupela Balamu zi nyama zinji, hamonga ne walongola wono wali wali hamonga nayo.

Wuwile wulongozi wa Balamu

41 Na mwi'zuwa lye kejete, Balaki yakawa kumusola Balamu, yakamutegula ko ku vigongo vya Bamoti-baali. Na kuko ko Balamu yasuma kuyiwonela nghango ndodo ye wanhu wa Isilayeli.


Wuwale 23

1 Nayo Balamu yakamulonjela Balaki yakatya, “Nghukulomba, wunzenjele bahano vikumbicilo mupunghati, hanyuma wundetele nghongolo mupunghati na nduwilu mupunghati.”

2 Nayo Balaki yaketuma nha vyono yalonjelwa na Balamu. Nawo wose vyono wejete, Balamu na Balaki, wakalavya nghongolo monga monga, ne zinduwilu monga monga mu vikumbicilo vyose.
3 Hanyuma, Balamu yakamulonjela Balaki yakatya, “Balaki 'gwe, ale wime baho ha cilavywa cako cinyanywe, lono nane nghali kubita nghamanye, hono Muwaha Mulungu konyiziza hamba ng'o.” Ninga nane nokulonjela du zose zono mwene kononjela. Hobaho yakawa kubita kucanyha ye litunda yali wene.
4 Nayo Mulungu vikawa kwitang'anila nayo kuko. Balamu yakamulonjela Mulungu yakatya, “Ane nazenga nakondya vikumbicilo vili mupunghati, kanyhi nakondya no kulavya nghongolo monga monga, hamonga na nduwilu monga monga mu vikumbicilo vyose.”

5 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Balamu zose zono yanojeye yakazilonje. Yakawa kumukombola yabite kuli Balaki yakamulonjele zono yamulonjeye yalonje.
6 Nayo Balamu yakawa kuwuya kuli Balaki, yakamwagana Balaki yakali vyo yemile bahalya du ha cilavywa cakwe cinyanywe, yali hamonga ne walongola we Wamowabi.

7 Hobaho Balamu yakawa kwanza kuzilonga zo wuwile wakwe yakutya,

“Balaki u mutemi we Wamowabi kanyhema,

ku wulawilila ku Siliya,

mu matunda ga kucilima,

Kanyitya, ‘Wuze wundijitile Waisilayeli.’

8 Alu nosuma kuwalijita wule, wono Mulungu yakondya

kuwaluguta. Hamba kulonga zo wongomezwe lono Mulungu

si yateye nhavivyo?

9 Nane nghusuma kuwawona ku wulawilila

mu vizuli vye mabwe.

Nghuwalanga ku wulawa mu matunda mucanyha

Lyo likungugo lyono likalaga ciwene.

Ninga wamanyile vyono wene walugutwe kuciza

we makungugo ganji gose.

10 Ne zindelo za Isilayeli, wenji kotya manghundi.

Wenji muno muno, hamba si wakusumika na ku wuwale.

Wundece nivimalile vilo vyangu

nha yumonga wa muli wanhu wo Mulungu.

Wundece nifwe ko mulamu nhe

wanhu we ling'ani.”

11 Nayo Balaki yakamulonjela Balamu yakatya, “Alu Balamu, ho kunhendela nhawule kanyhi? Ane nha nghuleta agwe bahano ho wundijitile i walugu wangu. Ninga agwe kuwalugutiza.”
12 Nayo Balamu yakamupitula yakatya, “Ane nghulonga du zono Muwaha Mulungu yakunonjela nonje.”

Wuwile we kejete wa Balamu

13 Hanyuma kanyhi, Balaki yakamulonjela Balamu yakatya, “Ale Balamu, wuze le, cibitane hamonga hanhu hanji kanyhi, i hanhu hono kosuma kuwawona Waisilayeli wanji. Nagwe ho wundijitile wene ku wulawilila hanhu baho.”
14 Hobaho yakawa kumusola, wakawa kubitana sunga kwi'hala lya Zofimu kucanyha ye litunda lya Pisiga. Na kuko nako yakazenga kanyhi vikumbicilo mupunghati, yakalavya kanyhi vilavywa vya nghongolo monga monga, na nduwilu monga monga, mu vikumbicilo vyose.

15 Balamu yakamulonjela kanyhi Balaki yakatya, “Ale wime baho ha cilavywa cako cinyanywe, lono nane nghali kubita nghewone no Mulungu.”

16 Muwaha Mulungu nayo vikawa kwiwona na Balamu. Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kumulonjela Balamu, zono Balamu kolonga. Hanyuma yakawa kumukombola yawuye kuli Balaki yakamulonjele zono yamutumile yalonje.
17 Nayo Balamu yakawa kuwuya kuli Balaki, yakamwagana Balaki yakali vyo yemile bahalya du ha cilavywa cakwe cinyanywe, yali hamonga ne walongola we Wamowabi. Nayo Balaki yakawa kuwuza vyono Muwaha Mulungu yateye.
18 Balamu yakawa kwanza kuzilonga zo wuwile wakwe yakutya,

“Gwe Balaki, wuze go mwana wa Zipola,

Wuhulicize kuli zono nozilonga.

19 Mulungu si yehwanile wanhu wono walambaga.

Hamba si yehwanile wuwe wo wuhile wo

munhu wono wupitukaga,

Ninga mwene ketumaga zose zono yehuwize,

Mwene kalongaga ko mulomo,

nazo ziwa kwijela.

20 Ane nonjelwa vyono, nize niwalugutize.

Na lono Mulungu yakondya kulugutiza,

ane si nosuma kugomoleza.

21 Nizimanyile zinghani ze

Waisilayeli za ku mwande.

Vyono si wokwinjila mu wukalale hamba mu magayo,

Hambi u Muwaha Mulungu nha yali hamonga nawo.

Nawo wakumwanza vyono mwene yo Mutemi wawo.

22 Mulungu yo yawawusa ku yisi ya Misili.

Nayo yakuwalewanila, kotya

likambaku lya mu mbago.

23 Hasina mhiji zo wuhawi, hamba waganga,

Wono wosuma kwitumamilwa ne nghungugo ya Isilayeli

Sunga ne wanhu wowa kuziganula zinghani ze

Waisilayeli, wakutya,

‘Lanjenyi ta, zono Mulungu yetumile!’

24 Ne likungugo lya Isilayeli lihwanile simba

yono yili vilungo muno muno.

Yono si yicehaga, langa sunga

yidemulanje no kulya,

sunga ying'we ne sakami ya vyono liwulaje.”

25 Nayo Balaki yakamulonjela Balamu yakatya, “Alu agwe walema kundijitila i wanhu wa Isilayeli, holeka, wulece hamba kuwalugutiza yaye!”

26 Balamu nayo yakapitula yakamutya, “Ninga ane si nghulonjela nitya, vyono nosuma kwituma zono Muwaha Mulungu yakunonjela du?”

Wuwile we kadatu wa Balamu

27 Nayo Balaki yakatya, “Alu nhondo wunhyatyaje kanyhi, nane nokutegula kanyhi hanhu hanji. I hanhu hono lunji Mulungu kokwenda kukukombola ho wundijitile wanhu wawo ku wulawilila hanhu baho.”
28 Hobaho yakawa kumusola Balamu, wakawa kubitana sunga ko kucanyha ye litunda lya Peyoli, akuno wakawa kulilanga li nyika hasi yawo.
29 Nayo Balamu yakamulonjela Balaki yakatya, “Ale wunzenjelaje kanyhi bahano vikumbicilo mupunghati, wundetele na nghongolo mupunghati, hamonga na nduwilu mupunghati.”
30 Nayo Balaki yakazituma zose nha vyono yali yalajilizwe, yakawa kulavya nghongolo mupunghati, hamonga na nduwilu mupunghati, nghongolo monga monga, na nduwilu monga monga, mu vikumbicilo vyose.


Wuwale 24

1 Nhondo nayo Balamu yakawa yamanya vyono Muwaha Mulungu, yamusakaga yawalugutize i Waisilayeli. Holeka si yabita kanyhi kuwulondola mhande nha ha lulongozi. Langa hobaho du yakawa kubita ku nyika.
2 Yakawagana kuko i wanhu Waisilayeli wasitile nghambi, mulango na nghambi yawo. U Muhe wo Mulungu yakawa kumutonga,
3 yakawa kuwila yakutya,

“Azi zo nghani zono yakulonga Balamu u mwana wa Beyoli.

Zo nghani zo munhu mono yakuziwona calinze.

4 U munhu mono yakusuma kuzihulika

zono Mulungu yakuzilonga.

Nane ko wulanje du,

nghusuma kuciwona citanjilo

wugubulilwe wono wukulawa kuli Muwaha Mulungu,

U Mudaha Vyose

5 Ne mahema ge Waisilayeli,

Ka! Nye! Maswanu si!

6 Genha kotya micilili mitali ye mitende

Hamba kotya nha busitani zono

zili ha nhumbi ye likolongo.

Genha kotya wudi wono

wuhadikwe no Muwaha Mulungu.

Hamba kotya nha mihagata yono yili behi ne malenga.

7 Nawo wotuga mvula mbaha muno muno.

Wohadika ne zimbeyu zawo mu migunda yono

yitonyeywe malenga menji muno.

No mutemi wawo kowa yena lilumbo yiwaha muno

si nha Agagi.

No wutemi wakwe wotandala, wusole yisi nyinji.

8 Mulungu yo yawawusa mu yisi ya Misili.

Nayo yakuwalewanila muno, yehwanile

nghambaku ya mu mbago.

Nawo wowongomeza i walugu wawo wose.

Wowatijinha mafupa gawo no kuzibenanjiza

zisonga zawo.

9 Ne likungugo lilino, lili vilungo muno muno

lihwanile simba.

Nalyo hono lyagona, hasina kanyhi mono

kokwijelela yalisinhuse.

No munhu wo wose mono komulugutiza

Isilayeli, u munhu yuyo nayo kolugutizwa.

No munhu wo wose mono kumulijitiza Isilayeli,

u munhu yuyo nayo kolijitwa.”

10 Hobaho Balaki yakawa kumukolelwa likuwo yiwaha muno muno Balamu. Yakawa kuzifumbata zi ngumi ze miwoko yakwe kwe 'kuwo, yakawa kumulonjela Balamu yakutya, “Agwe Balamu, ane nakucema ho wuze wuwalijite wawo walugu wangu, ninga alu zawa nghanghulo yidatu, agwe wukuwalugutiza, hamba si wukuwalijita ng'o.
11 Hodu lu wuwuce na baha, wuwuye kwenyu! Ane nali nehuwiza nha vyono nokupela cihumbilizwa, ninga alu Muwaha Mulungu yakwimila ho wulece kucituga.”

12 Nayo Balamu yakamupitula yakatya, “Mwene 'gwe Balaki, alo siwukumbuciye vyono nawalonjela i wanhu wono wanhumila, nghatya,
13 hamba wumhele fweza yako yose, hamonga ne zahabu yako yose yono yili mwi'kulu lyako, ane si nolumbila lukulu kubena lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, so wituma cinji cocose cono cili cambuzi kuli'ne. Kanyhi na vyono ane nolonga zono Muwaha Mulungu yakunonjela, zizo zo nolonga du?”

Wuwile we ndumilizo wa Balamu

14 Hanyuma kanyhi Balamu yakawa kumulonjela Balaki yakutya, “Alu ane neza kuwuya kwetu kuli wanhu wangu. Ninga lono nghali sinatuluka, nghukusugula no kukumanyiza, ze Waisilayeli, vyono wolowatendela i wanhu wako mu vilo vyono vikuza.”
15 Hobaho Balamu yakawa kuwila yakutya,

“Azi zo nghani zono yakulonga Balamu, u mwana wa Beyoli,

zo nghani zo munhu mono yakuziwona calinze.

16 U munhu mono yakusuma kuzihulika zono

Mulungu yakuzilonga.

Kanyhi mono yakusuma kulibocela sunga ne

lihala lyono likulawa kuli

Muwaha Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye.

Nane ko wulanje du, nghusuma kuciwona citanjilo.

Wugubulilwe wono wukulawa kuli

Muwaha Mulungu U Mudaha Vyose.

17 Nane nghuziwona za ku mwande,

Kanyhi nghuliwona likungugo lye Waisilayeli.

No mutemi mono yakung'alala nha nyelezi,

Kolokwijela ku wulawa mwi'kungugo lilyo.

Kolokuza ku wulawa mwi'kungugo lye Waisilayeli

yenha nyelezi yo mucila.

Kolowatowanga i walongola we Wamowabi,

Kanyhi kolowasuma i wanhu wose wa Seti.

18 Nayo kolowasuma i walugu wakwe wose

wono wekaye ku Edomu.

Kanyhi koyisola ne yisi yawo yiwe yakwe.

Lono Waisilayeli wakujendelela kuwa no wusume.

19 Likungugo lye Waisilayeli,

lyolowasumila lukulu.

No kuwongomezeza lukulu na wono wowa

wakali wasigaye wose.”

20 Na mu wugubulilwe wuwo, Balamu kanyhi

yakawawona Waamaleki.

Hobaho yakawa kuwila yakutya,

“Ati mulanje, wawa Waamaleki, lyali lyo

likungugo lyono lyali lili vilungo muno

kuciza wose.

Ninga ku ndumilizo, lyolokwajililila lukulu.”

21 Na mu wugubulilwe wuwo kanyhi,

yakawawona i Wakeni, nayo yakawawilila yakutya,

“I hanhu hono mwikaye, ho hanhu hono hapolile,

Hasina vitamisi, hehwanile ne linhuli lyono

yitumbice mucanyha ye vizuli vye mabwe,

22 Ninga hamba nhavivyo,

anye mo Wakeni, molokongomezezwa lukulu,

Kolo Waashuli wakumusolenyi mateka.”

23 Hanyuma ya zizo, Balamu kanyhi

yakawila yakutya,

“Alo wawa wo wanhuci wono wekongholeye

hamonga kono lubavu lwe sumuka?

24 Ninga i wanhu we cibano, wolokuziza

mwi'lenga wakulawa Kupulo.

Nawo woloyisumiza Ashuli

hamonga na Ebeli.

Ninga nawo wongomele wawo we cibitilila wolowaziza.”

25 Balamu yali yeza kukondya kuzilonga zizo, nhondo ho yakawa kutuluka yakawa kuwuya kwawo. Nayo Balaki yakawa kubita ne zakwe.


Wuwale 25

I Waisilayeli ha Peyoli

1 Nawo Waisilayeli lono wali wali mu nghambi mwi'lolo lya Shitimu, i wanhu we cilume, wakawa wakusenyha ne wahinza we Cimowabu wono wali wali mumo.
2 Hambi nha wawacemelaga mu zindigwa ze makumbiko ge milungu yawo ye Wamowabi yono wayihembaga. Nawo Waisilayeli, wakalya zindigwa zizo, kanyhi wakamuhemba no mulungu wawo Baali-peyoli.
3 Na ku soko yiyo, Muwaha Mulungu yakawakolelwa Waisilayeli likuwo muno muno,
4 yakawa kumulonjela Musa yakatya, “Ale wasolaje walongola we Waisilayeli, na ku so cihuliko cako, wuwawulajile wose he'zuwa, nane ho ndece kuwakolelwa likuwo Waisilayeli.”
5 Nayo Musa yakawa kuwalonjela i wawaha yakawatya, “Wose muli nyenye, muwawulagaje wanhu wose, munhu na wo mulango wakwe wose, i wanhu wono wakumuhemba Baali-peyoli.”

6 Nawo lono wali wehangulize mhuga yose, wakulila ha mulango we Lihema lyono Lyelile, walanje nhavi, yumonga wa muli Waisilayeli, yakamusola mucekulu we Cimidiyani, yakawa kumwinjiza mwi'hema lyakwe ciwonelela, ha meso ga Musa na hali wanhu wose.
7 Nayo Finehasi u mwana wa Eleyezali, kanyhi yo mwizukulu we cilume wa Haluni u mukohani, yali yeza kuziwona zizo, yakawa kwinuka, yakawa kuwuka ha wihangulize. Yakasola mugoha,
8 yakawa kumulonda yu yulya munhu, hamonga no mucekulu yuyulya, mumulya mwi'hema, yakawa kuwahoma no mugoha yakawabanhiza wose vyono wejete lwa kamonga, yakawawulaga. Na mu nzila yiyo, li lilya litamwa lye lihuhuji, lyono lyawamalaga Waisilayeli, hodu nalyo likawa kuwuka.
9 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, lyali lyakondya kuwawulaga wanhu makumi mejete na yine elufu.

10 Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kumulonjela kanyhi Musa yakatya,
11 “Alu ku soko ya zizo zono, yetumile Finehasi, nane hodu si nojendelela kanyhi kuwakolelwa likuwo wanhu wa Isilayeli yaye. Hambi Finehasi nha si yawene viswanu kuwukangalila wuhembi we liwala lyo lyose lyo wuhemba milungu yinji, langa wahembe nene du. Na yiyi yo yikuleka nane si nghusaka niwongomezeze mwi 'kuwo lyangu.
12 Holeka wumulonjele Finehasi nha vyono, alu neza kunoza cilagane co mulamu.
13 Mwene, hamonga ne wanagwe wose, mu ndelo zakwe zose, ane nakondya kuwawika kuwa wakohani mu vilo vyose. Hambi mwene nha si yali kusuma kuvikangalila i vinhu vyose vyono vikujela kwisumaniza nane. Kanyhi kasuma kuwanozeza wanhu wa Isilayeli wilamule.”

14 Ne litagwa lyo Mwiisilayeli mono yawulagwa hamonga no mucekulu Mumidiyani, yali Zimuli u mwana wa Salu, u mulongola we mhuga yo mulango wa Simiyoni.
15 Ne litagwa lyo mucekulu yuyo we Cimidiyani, yacemwaga Kozibi. Nayo Zulu u sogwe wo mucekulu yuyo, yali yo mulongola muwaha we mhuga ye Wamidiyani.

16 Hobaho Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kumulajiliza Musa yakutya,
17 “Ale, wahomaje wawo Wamidiyani wuwongomezeze lukulu,
18 ku soko yo wubi wono wamutendelenyi, lono wakamukonjenyi akulya ku Peyoli, kanyhi na ku soko ya Kozibi mono yakawulagwa kuko ku Peyoli, mwipango lye litamwa lye lihuhuji lono lyali ligwiye.”


Wuwale 26

Wuwale we wanhu lwe kejete

1 Litamwa lye lihuhuji lyono lyawamala wanhu, lyali lyeza kuwa lyapuluta, Muwaha Mulungu yakawalonjela Musa na Eleyezali u mwana wa Haluni, yakawatya,
2 “Alu, mwandice liwalo lye zimhuga zose ze milango ye Waisilayeli, liwalo lye walume wose, wono waficile myaka makumi mejete hamba wono wacizize, wono wowa wakwiliye ku wituma mulimo wo wusilikale wo wulugu.”
3 Nayo Musa na Eleyezali mukohani, wakawa na cihuliko kuli Muwaha mulungu, wakawa kuwakongholela hamonga i walume wose we lizika lilyo. Wekongholelela mu yisi yo wuhihi ya Mowabu, lono wukulowoka likolongo lya Yoludani ku wulawa Yeliko.
4 Wakawa kuwawala wanhu wono waficile myaka makumi mejete, na wono wacizize, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.

Ne liwalo lye wanhu Waisilayeli wono wakawa baho, wono walawa ku Misili nha lyoli.

5 Wo mulango wa Lubeni u mwana wa Yakobo we mhuga ya Hanoki na ya Palu,
6 na ya Hezeloni, hamonga na ya Kalimi.
7 Ne liwalo lya wose hamonga wo mulango wa Lubeni, wali waficile walume wose wanhu makumi mane ne 'datu elufu, na magana mupunghati na makumi madatu.
8 Nawo i wana wa Palu, wowa: Eliyabi,
9 hamonga ne wanagwe Nemuyeli, na Datani, na Abilamu. (Wawano Datani hamonga na Abilamu wo wali wo wahagulwe ne mhuga yawo. Nawo wawa wo wesumaniza na Musa na Haluni, wakelunza ne watyatyi wa Kola, lono wamubezeza Muwaha Mulungu.
10 Nayo yisi yikahama, yikawamela wose, wakafwa, Kola, hamonga ne watyatyi wakwe wose. Na lono mhuga yiyo yali yakondya kufwa, moto nawo wukawatakamiza wanhu walume waficile magana mejete na makumi mahano, wakawa suguzi kuli wanhu.
11 Ninga langa i wana we cilume wa Kola, awo si wawulagwa.)

12 Wo mulango wa Simiyoni nawo wowa; We mhuga ya Nemuyeli, na ya Yamini, na ya Yacini,
13 na ya Zela, hamonga na ya Shawuli.
14 Ne liwalo lya wose hamonga wo mulango wa Simiyoni, wali waficile wanhu walume, makumi mejete na nyejete elufu, na magana mejete.

15 Wo mulango wa Gadi nawo wowa: We mhuga ya Zefoni, na ya Hagi, na ya Shuni,
16 na ya Ozini, na ya Eli,
17 na ya Alodi, hamonga na ya Aleli.
18 Ne liwalo lyawo wose, hamonga, wo mulango wa Gadi, wali waficile wanhu walume makumi mane elufu, na magana mahano.

19 Wo mulango wa Yuda, nawo wowa:
20 We mhuga ya Shela, na ya Pelezi, na ya Zela,
21 na ya Hezeloni, na ya Hamuli, (hamonga ne wana wejete we cilume Eli na Onani, wono wali wakondya kufwila ku yisi ya Kanani.)
22 Ne liwalo lyawo wose hamonga wo mulango wa Yuda, wali waficile wanhu walume makumi mupunghati na mutandatu elufu, na magana mahano.

23 Wo mulango wa Isakali nawo wowa: we mhuga ya Tola, na ya Puwa,
24 na ya Yashubu, hamonga na ya Shimuloni.
25 Ne liwalo lyawo wose hamonga, wo mulango wa Isakali, wali waficile wanhu walume makumi mutandatu na yine elufu na magana madatu.

26 Wo mulango wa Zabuloni nawo wowa: We mhuga ya Seledi, na ya Eloni, hamonga na ya Yehaleleli:
27 Ne liwalo lyawo wose hamonga wo mulango wa Zabuloni, wali waficile wanhu walume makumi mutandatu elufu, na magana mahano.

28 Wo mulango wa Yusufu, Yusufu mono yali yo sogo ye wana we cilume wejete, Manase na Efulayimu.

29 Wo mulango wa Manase nawo wowa: Macili u mwana wa Manase, yali yo mulezi wa Gileyadi, hamonga ne wana wanji wono nawo wakawa wa mu mhuga ya Gileyadi ku lubavu lwe wakuku zawo, nawo wowa:
30 We mhuga ya Lezeli, na ya Heleki,
31 na ya Asiliyeli, na ya Shekemu,
32 na ya Shemida, na ya Hefa.
33 Nayo Zelofehadi u mwana wa Hefa, yakawa yasina wana we cilume, langa yakawa na wana we cicekulu du, nawo matagwa gawo wowa: Mahila, na Nowa, na Hogila, Milika, na Tiliza.
34 Ne liwalo lyawo wose hamonga, wo mulango wa Manase, wali waficile wanhu walume makumi mahano na nyejete elufu, na magana mupunghati.

35 Wo mulango wa Efulayimu nawo wowa: We mhuga ya Shutela na ya Bekeli, na ya Tahani.
36 Wanhu we mhuga ya Elani, wono walawa mu lulelo lwe wana wa Shutela.
37 Ne liwalo lyawo wose hamonga, wo mulango wa Efulayimu, wali waficile wanhu walume makumi madatu na nyejete elufu, na magana mahano.

Nayo yiyo yo milango yono yilawiliye muli wana wa Yusufu.

38 Wo mulango wa Benyamini nawo wowa: We mhuga ya Bela, na ya Ashibeli, na ya Ahilamu,
39 na ya Shefufamu, na ya Hufamu.
40 We mhuga ya Alidi na Naamani, walawilila mu lulelo lwa Bela.
41 Ne liwalo lyawo wose hamonga wo mulango wa Benyamini, wakawa waficile walume makumi mane ne'hano elufu, na magana mutandatu.

42 Wo mulango wa Dani, nawo wowa: We mhuga ya Shushamu,
43 wono wali waficile liwalo lyawo, wanhu walume makumi mutandatu na yine elufu, na magana mane.

44 Wo mulango wa Asheli, nawo wowa: We mhuga ya Imuna, na ya Ishivi, na ya Beliya.
45 We mhuga ya Habeli, na ya Maliciyeli, wono walawilila mu lulelo lwa Beliya.
46 Asheli yali yena mwana we cicekulu litagwa lyakwe Sela.
47 Ne liwalo lyawo wose hamonga wo mulango wa Asheli, wakawa waficile walume makumi mahano na yidatu elufu, na magana mane.

48 Wo mulango wa Nafutali, nawo wowa: We mhuga ya Yahizeyeli, na ya Guni,
49 na ya Yezeli, na ya Shilemu.
50 Ne liwalo lyawo wose hamonga wakawa waficile wanhu walume makumi mane na yihano elufu, na magana mane.

51 Ne liwalo lye Waisilayeli hamonga, wakawa waficile wanhu walume magana mutandatu na monga elufu, na magana mupunghati na makumi madatu.

52 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
53 “Alu, wuyigawaje yisi, wuwagawile we milango yiyino yose yiwe mulango na yisi yawo, mu nghango yo winji we liwalo lyawo vyono wenha, wanhu na mulango wawo.
54 Ninga yisi wuyigawaje mu nzila yo kutowa kula,
55 wuwapele nha vyono winha u winji we wanhu wa mu milango yawo,
56 wo mulango wono wina wanhu wenji, watuje yisi mbaha, nawo wo mulango wono wina wanhu wacefu watuje yisi ndodo.”

57 Wo mulango wa Lawi, walawilila mu mhuga ya Gelishoni, na ya Kohati, na ya Melali.
58 Ne zindelo zawo, zilawiliye mu zimhuga ndodo ndodo za Libina, na Hebuloni, na Mahili, na Mushi, na Kola. Nayo Kohati yali yo sogo wa Amulamu,
59 mono hanyuma yakamutola Yekobedi u mwana we cicekulu wa Lawi, u mulelelwa ku Misili. Nayo Yekobedi yakamulelela Amulamu wana wejete we cilume, yo Haluni na Musa hamonga no lumbu lyawo Milyamu.
60 Nayo Haluni yakawa na wana we cilume wane, yo Nadabu, na Abihu, na Eleyezali, na Itamali.
61 Nadabu na Abihu, wawo wo wafwa lwa kamonga, mwi'zuwa lililya lono walavizya kuli Muwaha Mulungu, u moto wono si wali welile mu cikumbicilo.
62 Ne liwalo lye wana we cilume we Walawi, i wana wono wali waficile mwezi monga, hamonga na wono wali wacizize, wose hamonga wali waficile elufu makumi mejete na yidatu cititila. Wene wakawalwa, ninga si wawalilizwa hamonga ne Waisilayeli wose, vyono wene wali wasina ling'ani yo kupegwa wuhazi wa cinhu cocose mu Isilayeli.

63 Ne milango yiyo yose. Musa na Eleyezali wo wayiwala no kuyandika liwalo lyayo, lono wetumamila mulimo wo kwandika liwalo lye Waisilayeli wose, lono wali wali mu yisi yo wuhihi ya Mowabu, ku nhumbi ye likolongo lya Yoludani ku wulawa Yeliko.
64 Hamba hali hasina munhu monga mono si yawalwa, muli wanhu wose wono Musa na Haluni wawawala ha wuwale wulongozi lono wali wali mu nyika ya Sinayi.
65 Ninga Mulungu yali yakondya kulonga nha vyono, wose muli wene, wofwila mu nyika. Hasina hamba munhu monga mono kosigala, langa kosigala Kalebi du u mwana wa Yefune, hamonga na Yoshuwa u mwana wa Nuni.


Wuwale 27

Iwana we cicekulu wa Zelofehadi

1 Mahila na Nowa, na Hogila, na Milika hamonga na Tiliza, wali wo wana we cicekulu wa Zelofehadi, u mwana wa Hefeli, u mwana wa Gileyadi, u mwana wa Macili, u mwana wa Manase, u mwana wa Yusufu.
2 Wawo wakamubitila Musa na Eleyezali mukohani, ne walongola wose, ne mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli, ha mulango we Lihema lyono Lyelile, wakawa kutya,

3 “Alu ase, u sogo yetu yafwila mu nyika, yakafwa yasina hamba wana we cilume yaye. Nayo hamba si yali mutyatyi wa Kola. Kola mono yamubeza Muwaha Mulungu. Nayo yakafwa so wubi wakwe mweneco.
4 Alu vyono mwene yali yasina wana we cilume, alu ase ma, hamba ciwe cili wana we cicekulu, nhawule litagwa lyo sogo yetu lyajililile lukulu mu Isilayeli? Holeka cikulomba wucigawile nase sawo mu wulawa muli zi sawo ze wanduguze zo sogo yetu.”

5 Nayo Musa yakawa kuyiwika nghani yiyi kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
6 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamupitula yakatya,
7 “Zizo zono wawo wana we cicekulu wa Zelofehadi wakuziwuza, na mbeka. Holeka heya wuwapelaje nawo sawo yono yikulawa muli wanduguze zo sogo yawo. Wuhazi wo sogo yawo wuwe wawo.
8 Holeka wuwalonjele Waisilayeli wose, vyono, u munhu wo wose hono yafwe si yalecile mwana we cilume i wanagwe we cicekulu wo wowa wahazi we sawo yakwe yose.
9 Na hono munhu yuyo yafwe si yalecile hamba mwana we cicekulu, hodu yinojeye muwahawe hamba mwanakwawe yo yawe muhazi we sawo yiyo.
10 Na hono yawe yasina hamba muwahawe hamba mwanakwawe, ale u muwahawe hamba u mwanakwawe wo sogwe yo kowa muhazi we sawo yiyo.
11 Na hono yawe yasina muwahawe hamba mwanakwawe wo sogwe, hamba mwihwawe, ale nduguye mono vili behi nayo, yo kowa muhazi we sawo yiyo, nayo yowa yawa sawo yakwe du. Nawo Waisilayeli, yiwanojeye wazikamise zizi, ziwe wo wityatye we ling'ani, wono wunojeye wawutyatye muli vinhu vivyo vye ling'ani, nha vyono ane no Muwaha Mulungu nghukulajiliza.”

Yoshuwa yakuhagulwa yahalile hanhu ha Musa

(Kum 31:1-8)

12 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Alu wubite kucanyha ye matunda ga Abalimu, na ku wulawa kuko, wuyilanje yisi yose yono nghuwapela Waisilayeli.
13 Nagwe kolo wakondya kuyilanga no kuyiwona vyono yinha, hodu wuwe kufwila baho, nha vyono muwahawo Haluni yafwa,
14 Hambi agwe go mweneco, hamonga no muwahawo Haluni, nha mwalibedegala lilajilizo lyangu akulya ku nyika ya Zini. Lono mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli wang'ung'ulicilaga bahalya ha muvwilo wa Meliba. Nagwe baho walema kulajila ze vilungo vyangu vyono vyelile hali wene.” (Nayo Meliba co cikolongo ce malenga cono cili ku Kadeshi mu nyika ya Zini.)

15 Nayo Musa hobaho yakawa kufugama, yakawa kulomba yakutya,
16 “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go ndawililo yo wumi we wanhu wose, nghukunena, wuhagule gwegwe, u munhu mono kosuma kuwatonga Waisilayeli,
17 kanyhi mono kosuma kuwa mulongola wawo mu wulugu. I litewa lyako ho lilece kuwa nha ngholo zono zisina mudimi.”

18 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamupitula Musa yakatya, “Ale musolaje Yoshuwa u mwana wa Nuni, u munhu mono yena muhe, u muwicile miwoko yako mu mutwe wakwe.
19 Wumunoze yawe muwaha hali Eleyezali mukohani, hamonga ne mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli. Nagwe wumuwice ha mwande yawo wose, wuwalonjele vyono, yuyu Yoshuwa yo yakuwuhalila u mulimo wako.
20 Wumulecele wulajilize wako wunji. I wanhu wa Isilayeli ho wamuhulice.
21 Nayo kowa yali hamonga na Eleyezali u mukohani, mono kosuma kuwumanya wuhile wangu mu nzila yo witumamila Ulimu na Tumimu. Na mu nzila yiyo, Eleyezali kosuma kumutonga Yoshuwa, hamonga ne mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli, muli zinghani zawo zose.”

22 Nayo Musa yakazinoza zose nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza. Yakamunoza Yoshuwa yeme hali Eleyezali mukohani, sunga na hali mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli.
23 Musa yakawa kuyiwicila miwoko yakwe mu mutwe wa Yoshuwa nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza. Yakawa kumuwika ha mwande, yakawa kuwalonjela wanhu, vyono Yoshuwa yo yakondya kuwuhalila u mulimo wakwe.


Wuwale 28

I vilavywa vye nhondo zose

(Wut 29:38-46)

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajiliza Musa.
2 Yawalajilize Waisilayeli vyono, yiwanojeye wamulavizye Mulungu vilavywa vye ndigwa, vyono vikumwendeza, wavilavye mu vilo vyono vitemezwe kulavywa.

3 Navyo avino vyo vilavywa vye ndigwa, vyono vinojeye wamulavizye Muwaha Mulungu so cilavywa cinyanywe. Weze kulavya nyana za ngholo nume nyejete za mwaka monga, nhelevu, zono zisina cilema co cose.
4 Weze kulavya nyana monga ya ngholo hakali mitondo, ne nzagwe nayo cinyamihe.
5 Weze kuzilavya hamonga ne cilavywa co wusaje. Wusaje wa kilo monga, wono muhanzahanze ne mafuta malengu lita monga.
6 Naco cici, cowa co cilavywa ce nhondo zose, cono cowa cinyanywe, cilavywa cono calavywa halulongozi nha cilavywa ce ndigwa he 'tunda lya Sinayi. Ne mhunjile yayo yikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu.
7 Alu nha vyono yinha, yinojeye divayi, yilavywe hamonga ne ngholo nongozi, holeka wujidile divayi mu cikumbicilo lita monga.
8 Na mwi'pango lye cinyamihe, wuyilavye ngholo ye kejete, wuyilavizye mu nzila yiyo du nha vyono wuyilavizye ngholo ya hakali mitondo, hamonga ne cilavywa cayo ce divayi. Naco co cilavywa ce ndigwa, ne mhunjile yayo yikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu.

I vilavywa vya mwi'zuwa lye Sabato

9 Na mwizuwa lye Sabato, wulavye nyana za ngholo nume nyejete, nhelevu, zono zisina cilema co cose, hamonga no wusaje kilo nyejete muhanzahanze ne mafuta, wuwe cilavywa co wusaje.
10 Ne cilavywa cicino cinyanywe, ceze kulavywa mu Sabato zose, hamonga ne divayi, nha vyono yinha du mu cilavywa ce nhondo zose.

I cilavywa ca mwi'zuwa lilongozi lyo Mwezi

11 Weze kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu cilavywa cinyanywe mwi'zuwa lilongozi lya mu myezi yose. Weze kulavya nzeku nyejete, hamonga ne nduwilu monga, hamonga na nyana za ngholo nume za mwaka monga, zili mupunghati, nazo zose zeze nhelevu, zono zisina cilema co cose.
12 Na vyono co cilavywa co wusaje, holeka wulavye wusaje wono muhanzahanze ne mafuta. Wulavye kilo yidatu zo wusaje, hamonga na nzeku monga monga, nzeku na kilo yidatu za wusaje. Nazo zinduwilu, ziweze nduwilu monga na kilo nyejete zo wusaje.
13 Ninga kuli zi nyana ze zingholo, azo wuzilavye hamonga na kilo monga monga du ya wusaje. Navyo vilavywa vivino vinyanywe, vyo vilavywa vye ndigwa. Ne mhunjile yayo yikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu.
14 Ne cilavywa cono cikwilaniciye ce divayi, ciweze lita nyejete za divayi, hamonga na nzeku monga monga, nha cinji nzeku na lita zayo nyejete. Ninga kuli nduwilu, wulavye mafuta lita monga na nusu du. Na lita monga monga du, kuli ziyana ze zingholo. Ngholo na lita yayo. Na wuwu wo wulajilize kuli ze cilavywa cinyanywe, ca mwi'zuwa lilongozi lya mu myezi yose, mu mwaka wose mututu.
15 Kanyhi weze kulavya mvulati so cilavywa co wubi hamonga ne cilavywa cinyanywe ce nhondo zose, hamonga ne cilavywa cayo ce divayi.

I vilavywa vya mu sikukuu yo mukate wono wusina cisase

(Lawi 23:5-14)

16 Nayo sikukuu ye Pasaka yo kumupela lilumbo Muwaha Mulungu, yinojeye yinozwe mwi'zuwa lye likumi na yine lya mu mwezi mulongozi.
17 Na mwi'zuwa lye likumi na yihano, sikukuu ye ci dini yikwanza, nayo yijendelele sunga vilo mupunghati. Na mu vilo vivyo, yeze kuligwa mikate yono yisina cisase du, yono yiwikanizwe so mulimo wuwo.
18 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo lilongozi lye sikukuu, anye munojeye mwihangulizize hamonga so wuhembi. Na mwi'zuwa lilyo mulecele lukulu kwituma mulimo wunji wowose.
19 Kanyhi mumulavizyaje Muwaha mulungu cilavywa cinyanywe ciwe co cilavywa ce ndigwa kuli mwene. Mumulavizye nzeku nyejete, na nduwilu monga, hamonga na nyana ze zingholo nume za mwaka monga, zili mupunghati, nazo zose zeze nhelevu zono zisina cilema co cose.
20 Kanyhi mulavye cilavywa cono cikwilaniciye co wusaje, ciwe co wusaje muhanzahanze ne mafuta, wuwe kilo yidatu yidatu mu nzeku monga monga,
21 kilo monga monga, so zinyana ze zingholo monga monga.
22 Kanyhi mulavye mvulati monga yiwe so cilavywa co wubi. Na mu nzila yiyi mo komunozezenyi wilamule.
23 Nazo zizi muzilavye hamonga ne cilavywa cinyanywe ce nhondo zose hakali mitondo.
24 Na mu nzila yiyi du, yo mweze kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu cilavywa ce ndigwa, mu vilo mupunghati. Ne mhunjile yayo yikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu. Nanye mweze kulavya nhavivi, hamonga ne cilavywa cinyanywe ce nhondo zose, hamonga ne cilavywa ce divayi.
25 Mweze kwihanguliza hamonga mwizuwa lyo mupunghati so wuhembi, kanyhi mulecele lukulu kwituma mulimo wunji mwi'zuwa lilyo.

I vilavywa vye sikukuu ye nhwanga

(Lawi 23:15-22)

26 Na mwi'zuwa lilongozi lya mu sikukuu ye nhwanga, lono mukumuletela Muwaha Mulungu cilavywa co wuhemba wupya, munojeye mwihangulize wose hamonga so wuhembi, ninga mulece kwituma mulimo wunji mwizuwa lilyo.
27 Mulavye cilavywa cinyanywe, cilavywa ca nzeku nyejete, na nduwilu monga, hamonga na nyana za ngholo, nume, mupunghati za mwaka monga nhelevu, zono zisina cilema co cose.
28 Kanyhi mulavye cilavywa cono cikwilaniciye co wusaje, wusaje wono muhanzahanze ne mafuta. Weze wusaje wa kilo yidatu yidatu mu nzeku zose monga monga, na kilo nyejete so nduwilu,
29 na kilo monga monga so nyana ze zingholo monga monga.
30 Kanyhi mulavye mvulati monga yiwe so cilavywa co wubi. Na mu nzila yiyi, mo komunozezenyi wilamule.
31 Nanye muzilavye zizi hamonga ne cilavywa ce divayi, hamonga ne cilavywa cinyanywe ce nhondo zose, hamonga ne cilavywa co wusaje.


Wuwale 29

I vilavywa vye sikukuu yo mwaka mupya

(Lawi 23:23-25)

1 Na mwi'zuwa lilongozi lya mu mwezi wo mupunghati, mweze kwihanguliza wose so wuhembi. Ninga mulece kwituma mulimo wunji mwi'zuwa lilyo. Na mwizuwa lilyo munojeye mutowe ndulele.
2 Kanyhi mulavye ne cilavywa cinyanywe kuli Muwaha Mulungu, ne mhunjile yayo, yikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu. Mulavye nzeku monga, nduwilu monga, hamonga na nyana za ngholo, nume, mupunghati za mwaka monga, nhelevu zono zisina cilema co cose.
3 Kanyhi mulavye cilavywa cono cikwilaniciye, cilavywa co wusaje wono muhanzahanze ne mafuta. Weze wusaje wa kilo yidatu so nzeku yiyo monga, na kilo nyejete so nduwilu,
4 na kilo monga monga so nyana ze zingholo, vyono nyejete.
5 Kanyhi mulavye mvulati monga yiwe so cilavywa co wubi. Na mu nzila yiyino, mo komunozezenyi wilamule.
6 Mweze kulavya vilavywa vivi hamonga na cilavywa cinyanywe ce nhondo zose, mwi'zuwa lilongozi lyo mwezi, hamonga ne cilavywa cayo co wusaje, hamonga ne cilavywa ce divayi. Ne vilavywa vivino vye ndigwa, vikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu so mhunjile yayo.

I vilavywa vya mwi'zuwa lyo Wilamule

(Lawi 23:26-32)

7 Kanyhi, mweze kwihanguliza hamonga, mwizuwa lye likumi lyo mwezi wo mupunghati, so wuhembi. Na mwizuwa lilyo, mulece kulya cinji hamba kwituma mulimo wunji.
8 Mumulavizye Muwaha Mulungu cilavywa cinyanywe, hambi mhunjile yayo nha yikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu. Mweze kumulavizya nzeku monga, nduwilu monga, hamonga ne nyana nume mupunghati za ngholo za mwaka monga, nhelevu zono zisina cilema co cose.
9 Mumulavizye cilavywa cono cikwilaniciye ce cilavywa co wusaje wono muhanzahanze ne mafuta, wuwe kilo yidatu so nzeku, na kilo nyejete so nduwilu,
10 na kilo monga monga so zingholo zose monga monga.
11 Kanyhi mulavye mvulati monga yiwe so cilavywa co wubi, hamonga ne cilavywa co wubi co wilamule, ne cilavywa cinyanywe ce nhondo zose, hamonga ne cilavywa cayo co wusaje ne cilavywa ce divayi.

I vilavywa vye sikukuu ye vibanda

(Lawi 23:33-44)

12 Mweze kwihanguliza hamopnga so wuhembi, mwi'zuwa lye likumi ne'hano lyo mwezi wo mupunghati. Munoze sikukuu yiyi, vilo mupunghati, mu cogopo co Muwaha Mulungu. Na mwi'zuwa lilyo, mulecele lukulu kwituma mulimo wunji.
13 Na mwi'zuwa lili lilongozi, munojeye mumulavizye Muwaha Mulungu cilavywa ce ndigwa. Hambi mhunjile yayo nha yikumwendeza muno Muwaha Mulungu, mumulavizye nzeku yikumi ne 'datu, nduwilu nyejete, hamonga na nyana za ngholo nume, yikumi na yine za mwaka monga, zose nhelevu, zisina cilema co cose.
14 Mumulavizye cilavywa cono cikwilaniciye co wusaje wono muhanzahanze ne mafuta, wuwe wusaje wa kilo yidatu yidatu so nzeku zose, kilo nyejete nyejete so nduwilu zose,
15 na kilo monga monga so ngholo zose, hamonga ne visakwa vye vilavywa vye divayi.
16 Kanyhi mulavye mvulati monga, yiwe so cilavywa co wubi. Muvilavye vivino, hamonga ne cilavywa cinyanywe ce nhondo zose, ne cilavywa cayo co wusaje ne divayi.

17 Na mwi'zuwa lye kejete, mulavye nzeku yikumi na nyejete, na nduwilu nyejete, hamonga na nyana za ngholo nume yikumi na yine za mwaka monga, zose nhelevu, zisina cilema co cose.
18 Mulavye vilavywa vivyo, cilavywa co wusaje ne divayi, so nzeku, na so zinduwilu ne zinyana ze zingholo, hamonga ne vilavywa vyose vyono vikusakwa nha mwi'zuwa lilongozi.
19 Na mvulati monga ciwe cilavywa co wubi, kuhanga ne cilavywa cinyanywe ce nhondo zose, ne cilavywa co wusaje ne divayi.

20 Na mwi'zuwa lye kadatu, mulavye nzeku yikumi na monga na nduwilu nyejete, hamonga na nyana za ngholo nume, yikumi na yine za mwaka monga, zose nhelevu, zisina cilema co cose.
21 Mulavye vilavywa vivyo hamonga ne divayi so zinzeku, na zo zinduwilu na so zinyana ze zingholo, nha vyono linha liwalo lyazo.
22 Na mvulati monga ciwe cilavywa co wubi, hamonga ne vilavywa vyose vyono vikusakwa, nha mwi'zuwa lilyo lilongozi.

23 Na mwi'zuwa lye kane, mulavye nzeku yikumi limonga, na nduwilu nyejete, hamonga na nyana za ngholo nume, yikumi na yine za mwaka monga zose nhelevu, zisina cilema co cose.
24 Mulavye vilavywa viyo,
25 hamonga ne vilavywa vinji vyono vikusakwa mwi'zuwa lilyo.

26 Na mwizuwa lye 'hano, mulavye nzeku yicenda, na nduwilu nyejete, hamonga na nyana za ngholo nume, yikumi na yine, za mwaka monga, zose nhelevu, zisina cilema co cose.
27 Mulavye vilavywa vivyo,
28 hamonga ne vilavywa vinji vyono vikusakwa mwizuwa lilyo.

29 Na mwizuwa lyo mutandatu, mulavye nzeku munana, na nduwilu nyejete, hamonga na nyana za ngholo nume, yikumi na yine za mwaka monga, zose nhelevu, zisina cilema cocose.
30 Mulavye vilavywa vivyo,
31 hamonga ne vilavywa vyono vikusakwa mwizuwa lilyo.

32 Na mwizuwa lyo mupunghati, mulavye nzeku mupunghati, na nduwilu nyejete, hamonga na nyana za ngholo nume, yikumi na yine za mwaka monga, zose nhelevu, zisina cilema co cose.
33 Mulavye vilavywa vivyo,
34 hamonga ne vilavywa vyono vikusakwa mwizuwa lilyo.

35 Na mwi'zuwa lyo munana, mwikongholele wose hamonga so wuhembi. Na mwi'zuwa lilyo mulecele lukulu kwituma mulimo wunji.
36 Mulavye cilavywa cinyanywe kuli Muwaha Mulungu, yiwe nha cilavywa ce ndigwa kuli Muwaha Mulungu, ne mhunjile yayo yikumwendeza muno. Mumulavizye nzeku monga, na nduwilu monga, hamonga na nyana za ngholo nume, zili mupunghati, nhelevu zisina cilema co cose.
37 Mulavye vilavywa vivyo,
38 hamonga ne vilavywa vinji vyono vikusakwa mwi'zuwa lilyo.

39 Na wuwu wo wityatye wa kuli vilavywa vinyanywe, ne vilavywa vyo wusaje, ne vilavywa vye divayi, hamonga ne vilavywa vyo mulamu, vyono mowa kumunozeza Muwaha Mulungu, mu zisikukuu ze mazuwa gayo mahagulwe. No wityatye wuwu, wowa sunga na kuli vilavywa vyenyu vyono mukulavya, so wufisilize wo wilahize wenyu, hamba mu vilavywa vye lihewa.

40 Nayo Musa, yakawa kuwaloloseza wanhu Waisilayeli zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza.


Wuwale 30

Wulajilize wa kuli zo wilahize

1 Nayo Musa, yakawaloloseza walongola we milango ye Waisilayeli, wityatye wono wanojeye wawutyatye, kuli zo wilahize, yakawatya,
2 “Hono munhu yelahize vyono komulavizya Muwaha Mulungu cinhu cinji, hamba hono yelahize wilahize wo kwiyimila cinhu cinji, yanojeye yelanjilile yalece kubena wihuwize wakwe. Langa yowa yimunojeye yawufisilize du, nha vyono yali yakondya kwihuwiza, vyono kokwituma nghani yiyo.

3 “Na hono mwana we cicekulu, mono yakali yali mukaya mo sogwe, hono yelahize vyono komulavizya cinji Muwaha Mulungu, hamba yehuwize kwiyimila cinhu cinji.
4 Ale yowa yimunojeye yetume nha vyono yelahize, hamba vyono yehuwize, langa hono sogwe yalete cimizi kolo yazihulika zizo.
5 Na hono sogwe kolo yakuhulika, yamwimile ho yalece kuwufisiliza wilahize wakwe, ale hodu yalece. Muwaha Mulungu komunenela, hambi sogwe nha yo yamwimiye yalece kuwibata wilahize wakwe.
6 Na hono mucekulu mutolwe yelahize mweneco du, hamba hono yelahize wula kumanya, hamba hono yehuwize kwiyimila cinhu cinji.

7 “Ale u mucekulu yuyo, yowa yimunojeye yazifisilize zose zono yelahize, hamba zono yehuwize, langa mulume wakwe hono yalete cimizi kolo yazihulika.
8 Na mulume wakwe hono yamwimile ho yalece kufisiliza wilahize wakwe kolo yazihulika, ale hodu yalece. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu komunenela.
9 Nayo mucekulu mufwile, hamba mono yalecigwe, ayo yimunojeye yafisilize du wilahize wakwe wose, wono kokwilahiza, sunga no wihuwize wo kwiyimila cinhu cinji.

10 “Na hono mucekulu mutolwa yelahize, hamba yehuwize kwiyimila cinhu cinji
11 nayo yowa yimunojeye yafisilize du wilahize wakwe, hamba wihuwize wakwe, langa hono mulume wakwe yalete cimizi kolo yazihulika.
12 Na hono mulume wakwe kolo yazihulika zizo, ho yamulemeze, hodu yazilekaje. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu komunenela, hambi nha mulume wakwe mono yamwimiye, ho yalece kuwufisiliza wihuwize wakwe.
13 Hambi u mulume wakwe, nha yena ling'ani yo kuwukangaza hamba kuwubena wilahize wo wose, hamba wihuwize wo wose wono yawunozize.
14 Ninga hono mulume wakwe yanyamale twii mwi'zuwa lilyo lyono yazihuliciye zo wilahize wo mugolece wakwe, nayo hamba si yalonjile cinji, hodu mucekulu kosuma kuzifisiliza zose zono yali yelahize, hamba zono yali yehuwize. Hambi mulume wakwe nha si yalavizye cimizi co cose, mwi'zuwa lyono yazihuliciye zizo.
15 Ninga hanyuma hono mulume kanyhi yabene mweneco wilahize wo mugolece wakwe, ale mulume yuyo kowudinghula mweneco wubi wo mugolece wakwe, hambi nha kalemwa kuwufisiliza wilahize.
16 Nawo wuwu, wo wulajilize wono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa, na kuli zo wilahize, wono wukunozwa no mucekulu mono si mutolwe mono yali mu nyumba yo sogwe, hamba kuli mucekulu mutolwa.”


Wuwale 31

U wulugu wono welile kuli Wamidiyani

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
2 “Walipicizaje Wamidiyani ku soko ya zono wawatendeye wanhu Waisilayeli. Na kolo wakondya kuzituma zizi, nagwe nhondo ho wuwe kufwa.”

3 Nayo Musa yakawa kuwalonjela wanhu yakatya, “Ale, mwiwikanizaje so wulugu, ho musume kuwahoma Wamidiyani, muwalipicize ku soko ye zinghani zono wamutendela Muwaha Mulungu.
4 Holeka nghumulombenyi muwikanize wanhu walume, mu wulawa mu milango yenyu yose, wono wobita ku wulugu. Na mu mulango monga, walawe wanhu elufu monga.”

5 Hobaho wakawa kuwahagula wanhu walume elufu monga monga mu wulawa mu milango yawo yose, wakawa kuwawikaniza ho wabite ku wulugu.
6 Nayo Musa yakawa kuwasola, yakawa kuwategula ku wihomi, hasi yo wulanjilizi wa Finehasi u mwana wa Eleyezali u mukohani, mono yali yo mulanjilizi we viya vyose vyono vyelile, hamonga ne zindulele zono wetumamilaga so kuwasugula nha vyono zinha.
7 Hodu, wakawa kuwahoma Wamidiyani, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa, nawo wakawawulaga wanhu wose we cilume,
8 hamonga ne watemi wahano we Wamidiyani, Evi, Lekemu, Zuli, Huli, hamonga na Leba. Wakamuwulaga nhavivilya na Balamu u mwana wa Beyoli.

9 Wanhu Waisilayeli, wakawasola mateka i wacekulu wose we Cimidiyani, hamonga ne wana wawo wose. Wakazisola ne zing'ombe, hamonga ne matewa gawo gose, wakazisola ne zisawo zawo zose.
10 Hanyuma wakawa kugapembela moto ne makaya gawo hamonga ne zinghambi zawo.
11 Hodu wakawa kuvikunza vinhu vyose vyono wali waviteka, hamonga ne wafungwa, ne mitugo zose.
12 Wakawa kuvileta vinhu vyose kuli Musa, na Eleyezali, na kuli mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli, lono wali wali mu nghambi zawo mu yisi yo wuhihi ya Mowabu, ku nhumbi ye likolongo lya Yoludani ku wulawa Yeliko.

Wasilikale wo wulugu wakuwuya

13 Nayo Musa na Eleyezali hamonga ne walongola we mhuga yose ye wanhu, wakawa kubita kunze ye nghambi kuwahinjiliza wasilikale wo wulugu.
14 Nayo Musa yakawa kuwakolelwa yikuwo muno muno i wawaha walongola we vipuga vipuga vye wasilikale, wono wawuya ku wulawa ku wulugu.
15 Yakawawuza yakatya, “Alu nye, nhawule muwaleka i wacekulu womi?
16 Ninga mukumbucile vyono wo wawa wacekulu wono wagatyatya mafundo ga Balamu, sunga wakawa kuwakwega wanhu wamubedegale Muwaha Mulungu. Na yiyi yikawa yo soko yono yikaleka lihuhuji likawaziza i wanhu wo Mulungu.
17 Holeka luno, muwawulagaje i wacekulu wose hamonga ne wazelelo wose wono wakondya kuzimanya zinghani ze ligona.
18 Ninga langa, mwisigazize nyo weneco i wahinza wose wono wakali namanya walume.
19 Ninga kanyhi hamba nhavivyo, anye wose, mono kowa yawulaje munhu wo wose, hamba lunji kapamiza muvimba, yowa yimunojeye yekale kunze ye nghambi vilo mupunghati. Na mwizuwa lye kadatu, na mwizuwa lyo mupunghati, mwiyeze nyo weneco hamonga ne wacekulu wono muwatecile.
20 Kanyhi mowa munojeye muyeze ne myenda yenyu yose, sunga ne myenda yono mihonwe ko wukunzeje we zinguwo ze zimhene, sunga ne vinhu vyose vyono vinozwe kwe mabici.”

21 Nayo Eleyezali u mukohani, yakawalonjela wanhu walume wono walawa ku wulugu yakawatya, “Awu wo wulajilize wono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.
22 Cinhu co cose cono si cosuma kutakamila mu moto, nha vyono yinha zahabu, fweza, shaba, cuma, hambe 'bati,
23 hamba lisasi, vivyo vinojeye vikozwe mu moto ho viwe vyezwa. Ninga i vinji vyose vinojeye vyezwe kwe malenga, holeka viwe vyezwa.
24 Na mwi'zuwa lyo mupunghati, mokanza myenda yenyu, nanye mowa mwezwa, hanyuma nhondo ho mosuma kukombolwa mwinjile mu nghambi.”

Wugawane we mateka

25 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
26 “Agwe na Eleyezali u mukohani, hamonga ne walongola wose we mhuga, muviwale vinhu vyose vyono muvitecile ku wulugu, hamonga ne wafungwa, ne mitugo zose.
27 Muvigawe mafungu mejete, lifungu limonga liwe lye wasilikale, ne lifungu liyagwe, liwe lye mhuga yose ye wanhu.
28 Ninga mwifungu lyono lyowa lyo lye wasilikale, musole kodi ku so Muwaha Mulungu. Mweze kusola cinhu cimonga cimonga mu makumi mahano mahano, ku wulawa mu wafungwa, mitugo, ndogowe, ngholo, sunga ne zimhene.
29 Ne vinhu vivyo vyose, mumupele Eleyezali u mukohani, viwe vilavywa kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
30 Na mwi'fungu lyono muwapelile mhuga yose ye wanhu, namo mumo, musole cinhu cimonga cimonga mu makumi mahano mahano, ku wulawa mu wafungwa, mitugo, ndogowe, ngholo, sunga ne zimhene. Ne vinhu vivi vyose, muwapele Walawi wono wena mulimo wo kulilanjilila Lihema lyo Muwaha Mulungu.”
31 Nayo Musa na Eleyezali u mukohani, waketuma nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalajiliza.

32 Na alino lyo liwalo lye vinhu vyono wasilikale waviteka, hamonga ne vinhu vyono wasolaga weneco. Ngholo hamonga ne zimhene, magana mutandatu na makumi mupunghati na yihano elufu.
33 Ng'ombe, makumi mupunghati na nyejete elufu.
34 Ndogowe, makumi mutandatu na monga elufu.
35 Ne wacekulu, wono wali wakali namanya walume, wali wali makumi madatu na nyejete elufu.
36 Ne nusu yono wasola wasilikale, yali yificile mhene magana madatu na makumi madatu na mupunghati elufu, na ngholo magana mahano.
37 Nazo ze magana mutandatu na makumi mupunghati na yihano, zo zasolwa zikawa zo kodi yo Muwaha Mulungu.
38 Ng'ombe, makumi madatu na mutandatu elufu. Ze makumi mupunghati na nyejete zikawa yo kodi yo Muwaha Mulungu.
39 Nazo zindogowe, zali zili makumi madatu, elufu, na magana mahano. Na muli zizo, zikasolwa makumi mutandatu na monga, zikawa yo kodi yo Muwaha Mulungu.
40 Nawo wacekulu wono wali wakali namanya walume, wono wagawilwa i wasilikale, wali waficile wacekulu yikumi na mutandatu elufu. Na muli wawo, wakasolwa wacekulu makumi madatu na wejete, wakawa yo kodi yo Muwaha Mulungu.
41 Nayo Musa, yakamupela Eleyezali u mukohani vinhu vivyo vyose vye kodi, i cilavywa cinulwa kuli Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.

42 Ne ligawo lye mhuga ye wanhu wose, lyali viling'aniye u winji wayo, ne ligawo lyono wagawilwa wasilikale,
43 nazo zali nhavi: Ngholo hamonga ne zimhene zali zili magana madatu na makumi madatu na mupunghati elufu, na magana mahano.
44 Nazo ng'ombe, zali zili makumi madatu na mutandatu elufu.
45 Ndogowe, nazo zali zili makumi madatu elufu na magana mahano.
46 I wacekulu wono wali wakali namanya walume, nawo wali waficile yikumi na mutandatu elufu.
47 Na mwi'fungu lili nalyo, Musa yakasola cinhu cimonga cimonga mu wulawa mu vinhu makumi mahano mahano, mu wulawa mu wanhu, na mu mitugo. Musa yakawa kuwapela Walawi wono wali wo wetuma mulimo wo wulanjilizi we Lihema lyo Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yali yamulajilize Musa.

48 Hanyuma nawo wono wali walongola we vipuga vipuga vye wasilikale, wakamubitila Musa
49 wakamulonjela wakutya, “Gwe muwaha, ase cakondya kunoza liwalo lye wasilikale wose wono wali wali hasi yo wulanjilizi wetu, nase ciwona vyono, hasina hamba monga mono yajiliye.
50 Holeka nase luno, cikukuletela i vinhu vyono nase cavisola yumonga yumonga muli sese. Navyo vyovino: Wihambo wa zahabu, wudodi, bangili, nyamwidole ze vitanjilo, viheleni, hamonga ne mikufu. Nase cikumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu, cinoze wilamule nayo so wumi wetu. Ho yajendelele kucidima mu mikalile yetu.”
51 Musa hamonga na Eleyezali u mukohani, wakawa kuyibocela zahabu, hamonga ne vinhu vyose vyo wihambo.
52 Ne vilavywa vyose hamonga vyono wawo wawaha walongola wavileta, vyali vina wuzito wa behi ya kilo magana mejete.
53 Ninga awalya wono wali wo si wawaha walongola, awo wakekala navyo du i vinhu vyono wali waviteka.
54 Hodu nayo Musa na Eleyezali u mukohani, wakawa kuyisola zahabu yose wakawa kuyitegula mwi Hema. Muwaha Mulungu ho yawadime i Waisilayeli.


Wuwale 32

We milango ya ku lubavu lwa kucilima ye likolongo lya Yoludani

(Kum 3:12-22)

1 Nawo wo mulango wa Lubeni, na wo mulango wa Gadi, wakawa wena matewa mawaha ge zimitugo. Holeka wali weza kuyiwona yisi ya Yezeli, ne yisi ya Gileyadi, vyono yo yowakwila ku so mitugo zawo,
2 hobaho wakawa kumubitila Musa na Eleyezali u mukohani, hamonga ne wawaha wanji we mhuga ye Waisilayeli, wakawatya,
3 “Yisi yiyi yono Muwaha Mulungu yawatazize Waisilayeli, no kuwapela, i makaya ga Ataloti, Diboni, Yezeli, Nimulahi, Heshiboni, Eleyalehi, Sibumahi, Nebo, hamonga na Beyoni,
4 yo yisi swanu muno so mitugo, nase luno ho cina matewa mawaha muno muno ge mitugo.
5 Holeka cikukunena wucipelaje yisi yiyino yo yiwe yetu, hamba wulece kucilowosa likolongo lili lya Yoludani ho cibite cikekale ku nhumbi nzagwe.”

6 Nayo Musa yakawapitula Wagadi ne Walubeni, yakawatya, “Alu mwene 'nye, anye ho mosaka muwe mwikaye bahano, lono wayenyu wanji wakubita ku wulugu?
7 Nhawulenyi 'lu mukwijelela kuwadumula mitima i wanhu we Isilayeli, ho walece kulowoka likolongo lya Yoludani, wenjile mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu yawapelile?
8 Mwene mukutanga! Zizi zo wetuma wasogo zenyu, lono nawatumila ku wulawa Kadeshi Balineya, ho wabite wakayitandile yisi.
9 Nawo wabita, sunga wakafika mwi'lolo lya Eshikoli, wakayilanga yisi, no kuyiwona vyono yinha. Ninga wali weza kuwuya ku wulawa kuko, wakaza wakawadumula wanhu mitima, ho walece kwinjila mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu yawapela.
10 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakakolwa ne likuwo muno muno mwi'zuwa lilyo, sunga yakehuwiza yakatya,
11 ‘Alu i wanhu wawa vyono si wamhelile cogopo, holeka nane nghwilahiza vyono, hasina hamba monga we cilume wa muli wene, mono yaficile myaka makumi mejete hamba kuciza, muli wono walawile ku Misili, mono kusuma kwinjila mu yisi yono namwihuziza Abulahamu na Isaka na Yakobo.’
12 Ne nghani yiyi yiwagwiliye wose. Kalebi du u mwana wa Yefune u Mukenizi, hamonga na Yoshuwa u mwana wa Nuni, wo wali musina mu nghani yiyo. Hambi wene nha wo weneco du wono wena cihuliko ca kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
13 Muwaha Mulungu yakawakolelwa likuwo muno muno wanhu, sunga yakawaleka wayegale ako na ako du mu nyika myaka makumi mane, sunga lulelo lu lulya lose lono si lukamwendeza, wakafwa wose.
14 Alu nanye mwazihalila ze walezi wenyu, anye nyo lulelo lupya, lulelo lwe wanhu wabi, mwewikaniza nanye mulilete kanyhi likuwo liwaha lyo Muwaha Mulungu kuli Waisilayeli.
15 Holeka nanye nyo wanhu wa Lubeni hamonga ne wanhu wa Gadi, hono muwe mwalemela lukulu kumuhulika Muwaha Mulungu lulu, nayo kowalecela lukulu i wanhu wawa wayegayeje ako na ako mono nyika. No wongomele wawo wowa mu mitwe yenyu.”

16 Nawo wakamusejelela Musa wakamutya, “Nghani nongozi, cikukulomba wucikombole cisite bahano mululu so zing'ombe zetu, kanyhi cizenje ne makaya ge magomawakali ku so wana wetu.
17 Nase ho cowa cakondya kwiwikaniza ku wubitana hamonga ne Waisilayeli wayetu ku wulugu. Nase cowa hamonga nawo du, sunga kono cowasumila walugu, no kuwaleka wawe wakondya kwikala mu yisi yono yowa yo yawo. Akuno nako, i wana wetu wojendelela kwikala baha mu makaya gaga ge magomawakali, wowa wapolile du, ku soko ye wanhu we yisi yiyi.
18 Nase hamba si cowuyila lukulu ku kuno, sunga langa kolo Waisilayeli wose wakondya kuyisolela lukulu yisi, no kuyihala, mulango no wuhazi wawo.
19 Nase kanyhi, hamba si cosolela lukulu sawo yawo yo yose kuko ku nhumbi nzagwe ya Yoludani, hambi ase nha cakondya kulibocela lifungu lyetu kukuno kono nhumbi ye cilima ya Yoludani.”

20 Nayo Musa yakawapitula yakatya, “Hono zono mukuzilonga, mukulonga za mbeka, ale, mwiwikanizaje le, baha hali Muwaha Mulungu, mubite ku wulugu.
21 Ale, alu vitung'ati wenyu wose walowoce likolongo lya Yoludani, nawo, so wulonje wo Muwaha Mulungu, wosuma kuwahoma walugu wetu sunga Muwaha Mulungu yawasume,
22 sunga ne yisi yisolwe. Na hanyuma ya zizi, anye ho mowuya kono kwenyu, hambi nha mowa mwakondya kucifisiliza cilagane cenyu no Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga na kuli wayenyu Waisilayeli. Holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu koyimanya yisi yiyi, yono yili ku lubavu lwe cilima ye likolongo lya Yoludani, vyono yisi yenyu.
23 Ninga hono mubene wihuwizo wenyu, mbeka nghumusugulenyi, vyono, mowa mwamusutizya Muwaha Mulungu. Holeka mulecele lukulu kusutya muli yiyi, hambi hono mubene wihuwizo wenyu, nha molipicizwa ku soko yo wubi wenyu.
24 Holeka aluno, ale muzenje makaya genyu, musite ne milulu yenyu so mitugo zenyu. Ninga mukumbucile du, mwitume nha vyono mwihuwize!”

25 Nawo wanhu wa Gadi na wa Lubeni wakapitula wakatya, “Gwe muwaha, ase cokwituma du nha vyono wulajilize.
26 I wagolece zetu, ne wana wetu, ne zing'ombe zetu, ne zingholo zetu, avyo vyose, vyosigala bahano mu makaya gaga ga Gileyadi.
27 Ninga ase wose ciwikanize ku wubita ku wulugu, hasi yo wulanjilizi wo Muwaha Mulungu wetu. Nase colowoka likolongo lya Yoludani, cikehome nha vyono wulonjile.”

28 Nayo Musa yakawa kumulajiliza Eleyezali u mukohani, hamonga na Yoshuwa, malajilizo gaga gose, hamonga ne walongola wanji we Waisilayeli, yakatya,
29 “Hono wanhu wa Gadi ne wanhu wa Gileyadi walowoce likolongo lya Yoludani, wawe wewikaniza so wihomi, mu wutonji wo Muwaha Mulungu, na hono wamutazenyi sunga muyisume yisi no kuyisola, ale, nanye muwalecelaje yisi ya Gileyadi yiwe yawo.
30 Ninga langa, hono walece kulowoka likolongo lya Yoludani, no kubitana hamonga nanye ku wulugu, ale, motemana nawo du yisi ya Kanani.”

31 Nawo wanhu wa Gadi ne wanhu wa Lubeni wakapitula wakatya, “Gwe muwaha, ase cokwituma du nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalajilize.
32 Ase colowoka Yoludani ko wutonji wakwe, sunga cinjile mu yisi ya Kanani mu wulugu, nase holeka cosuma kwisigaziza sawo yetu co weneco, akuno kono cilima ye likolongo lya Yoludani.”

33 Hodu nayo Musa yakawa kuwapela i wana wa Gadi ne wana wa Lubeni hamonga ne nusu ye wana wa Manase, yisi yose yo mutemi Sihoni, mutemi we Waamoli, hamonga ne yisi yo mutemi Ogu mutemi wa Bashani, hamonga ne makaya gose ne vi'si vyose vyono viwapilime.
34 Nawo i wana wa Gadi, wakawa kugazenga kanyhi i makaya ga Diboni, na Ataloti, na Aloweli,
35 na Atiloti Shofani, na Yezeli, na Yogibeha,
36 na Beti Nimulahi, hamonga na Beti Halani.
37 Nawo i wana wa Lubeni wakagazenga makaya ga Heshiboni, na Eleyalehi, na Kiliyatayimu,
38 na Nebo, na Baali Meyoni, (litagwa lili lyakawanizwa), na Sibumahi. Nawo wali weza kukondya kugazenga makaya gago, hodu wakawa kugacemela matagwa ganji mapya.

39 I wana wa Macili, u mwana wa Manase, wakabita ku yisi ya Gileyadi, wakayiboka, wakawawinga Waamoli wono wekalaga mumo, wakayisola.
40 Holeka nayo Musa yakawa kuwapela i wana wa Macili yisi yiyo ya Gileyadi, nawo hodu wakawa wekala mumo.
41 Nayo Yayili, wo mulango wa Manase, yakawahoma, yakazisola zikaya zawo zinji, yakazicemela litagwa lya “Vikaya vya Yayili.”
42 Nayo Noba, yakayihoma Kenati, yakayisola, hamonga ne vikaya vyayo, nayo yakahacemela litagwa lya Noba, litagwa lyakwe mweneco.


Wuwale 33

Muhinzo wo kulawa ku Misili kubita ku Mowabu

1 Ali lyo liwalo lye matagwa ge hanhu hono Waisilayeli wasitaga zinghambi zawo, lono wawucila ku Misili, mu nghango ye milango yawo, hasi yo wutonji wa Musa na Haluni.
2 Musa yakasuma kwandika matagwa ge hanhu hose hono wasitaga zinghambi, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza.

3 I Waisilayeli, wawuka ku Misili mwi'zuwa lye likumi ne'hano lya mu mwezi mulongozi wa mu mwaka, mwi'zuwa lye Pasaka nongozi. Nawo wawuka mwi'kaya lya Lamesesi ha meso ge Wamisili,
4 lono wene Wamisili wali wali mu mulimo wo kuzika i wana wawo we cilume i walongozi kulelwa, wono Muwaha Mulungu yawawulaga. Na mu wunoza zizi, Muwaha Mulungu yalajila vyono mwene yali vilungo muno muno kuciza milungu ya Misili.

5 Nawo Waisilayeli wawuka ku Lamesesi, wakasita nghambi yawo ku Sukoti.
6 Ne nghambi yawo yono yatyatya, yali yili ku Etamu, ha mwejelele we nyika.
7 Na ku wulawa baho, wakawuya ne nyuma, wakabita ku Habiloti, kulubavu lwe cilima ya Baali Zefoni, wakasita nghambi yawo behi na Migidoli.
8 Wakawuka ha Pi-hahiloti, wakakolela he 'lenga lya Shamu, mu nyika ya Shuli, nawo wali weza kujenda muhinzo wa vilo vidatu, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Mala.
9 Na ku wulawa baho, wakabita Elimu, wakasita nghambi yawo baho, hambi nha hali na vikolongo vye malenga yikumi na vyejete, hamonga ne mitende makumi mupunghati.

10 Wakawuka Elimu, wakasita nghambi he'lenga lya Shamu.
11 Ne nghambi yawo yono yatyatya yali ya mu nyika ya Zini.
12 Hanyuma wakasita nghambi yawo ha Dofeka.
13 Wali weza kuwuka baho, wakabita Alusi.
14 Na yono yatyatya, yali Lefidimu, i hanhu hono hali ho yili nyhilu mbaha ye malenga go kung'wa wene.

15 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Lefidimu, wakasita nghambi yawo mu nyika ya Sinayi.
16 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa mu nyika ya Sinayi, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Kibeloti-hatava.
17 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Kibeloti-hatava wakasita nghambi yawo ha Hazeloti.
18 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Hazeloti, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Litima.
19 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Litima, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Limoni-pelezi.
20 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Limoni-pelezi, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Libina.
21 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Libina, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Lisa.
22 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Libina, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Kehelata.
23 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Kehelata, wakasita nghambi yawo mwi'tunda lya Shefeli.
24 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa he 'tunda lya Shefeli, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Halada.
25 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Halada, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Makiloti.
26 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Makiloti, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Tahati.
27 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Tahati, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Tela.
28 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Tela, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Mitika.
29 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Mitika, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Hasimona.
30 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Hasimona, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Moseloti.
31 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Moseloti, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Beneyakani.
32 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Beneyakani, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Holi-hagidigadi.
33 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Holi-hagidigadi, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Yotibata.
34 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Yotibata, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Abilona.
35 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Abilona, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Eziyoni-gebeli.
36 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Eziyoni-gebeli, wakasita nghambi yawo mu nyika ya Zini, (Nha cinji nyika ya Kadeshi.)
37 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Kadeshi wakasita nghambi yawo he'tunda lya Holi, ku mhelo ya Edomu.
38 Haluni u Mukohani yakabita kucanyha ye litunda lya Holi nha vyono yalajilizwa no Muwaha Mulungu, yakawa kufwila kuko, mu mwaka we makumi mane ku wulawa mu mwaka wono Waisilayeli wawucila ku Misili, mu mwezi we hano, mwi'zuwa lilongozi lyo mwezi.
39 Lulo, Haluni yali yaficila myaka yigana limonga na makumi mejete na midatu, lono yafwilila mwi'tunda lya Holi.

40 U mutemi we Cikanani, mono yali yatemile ku Aladi, mono yekalaga kutakama mu yisi ya Kanani, yakazihulika zo wuze we Waisilayeli.
41 Nawo Waisilayeli wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Holi, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Zalimoni.
42 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Zalimoni, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Oboti.
43 Wakajenda muhinzo ku wulawa Punoni wakasita nghambi yawo ha Oboti.
44 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Oboti, wakasita nghambi yawo ha Iyeyabalimu, mu mupaka wa Mowabu.
45 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Iyimu wakasita nghambi yawo ha Diboni-gadi.
46 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Diboni-gadi wakasita nghambi yawo ha Alimoni-dibulatayimu.
47 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa Alimoni-dibulatayimu, wakasita nghambi yawo mu matunda ga Abalimu, vidomile na Nebo.
48 Wakajenda muhinzo wulawa ha matunda ga Abalimu, wakasita nghambi yawo mu yisi yo wuhihi ya Mowabu, ha Yoludani, behi na Yeliko.
49 Wakasita nghambi yawo ha Yoludani wulawa Beteyeshimoti, sunga ko ku Abeli-shitimu, mu yisi yo wuhihi ya Mowabu.

Wulolosezwe lono wakali nalowoka Yoludani

50 Na lono wali wakali wali baho, mu yisi yo wuhihi ya Mowabu, ku nhumbi ya Yoludani behi na Yeliko, Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa
51 yawaloloseze Waisilayeli wityatye awu, yakatya, “Kolo mweza kulowoka likolongo lya Yoludani, mukwinjila mu yisi ya Kanani,
52 Ale, munojeye muwawinje wekazi wose wa mu yisi yiyo. Muvyongomezeze lukulu i vihwani vyawo viseswe, hamonga ne hanhu hawo hose ho wuhembelo wawo.
53 Muyisole yisi, mwikale mumo, hambi ane nha nimupelenyi nyenye yisi yiwe yenyu.
54 Hanyuma muwe kuyigawa yisi mu nzila yo kutowa kula, milango yi yino yose, yituje mulango na yisi yawo, mu nghango yo winji we liwalo lyawo vyono winha, wanhu na mulango wawo.
55 Ninga mulanjilile! Hono mulece kuwawinga i wekazi wose wa mu yisi yiyo, wawo wono mowasigaza, wowa nha mamiya mu meso genyu, na mu zi mbavu zenyu. Nawo wowa yitimvya yiwaha muno muno muli nyenye.
56 Na hono mulece du kuwawinga, ane yo nomongomezenyi nyenye, nha vyono anye mwali mwiwikanize ho muwongomeze wene.”


Wuwale 34

I mipaka ye yisi

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa
2 wulajilize awuno so Waisilayeli yakatya, “Kolo mweza kwinjila mu yisi yono nghumupelenyi, yisi ya Kanani, ale, mipaka ye yisi yenyu yowa nhavi:
3 Ku lubavu lwa kutakama, mupaka wowa mugazi wulawiliye mu nyika ya Zini, mu mupaka wa Edomu. Nawo wokwanziza ku lubavu lwe ndumilizo ya kutakama, ye lilenga lyo mwino kucilima yayo.
4 Hanyuma wojendelela sunga ko ku lubavu lwa kutakama ku wubita ku Akelabimu Pasi, nawo wojendelela wukole mu Zini ku lubavu lwa kutakama ye Kadeshi Balineya. Hanyuma kanyhi, mupaka wojendelela sunga ko ku lubavu lwe mwezi ye sumuka ku Hazali Adali, nawo wobita sunga ko ku Azimoni,
5 hanyuma ho wokunga wulanje kwi'lolo, ku mupaka wa Misili, nawo wodumiliziza mwi'lenga lya Meditelaneya.

6 “Holeka mupaka wo lubavu lwa kumwezi, wowa lilenga lya Meditelaneya.
7 Nawo mupaka wo lubavu lwa kusumuka, awo wowa wugolocile du ku wulawa lilenga lya Meditelaneya sunga ko kwi'tunda lya Holi.
8 Na ku wulawa baho, wojendelela sunga ko ku Hamati Pasi. Kanyhi wojendelela sunga ko ku Zedadi,
9 sunga ku Zifoloni, nawo wodumiliziza ku Hazali Enani.

10 “Nawo mupaka wa ku lubavu lwa kucilima, wowa wugolocile du, ku wulawa Hazali Enani sunga ko ku Shefamu.
11 Hanyuma wojendelela sunga ko kutakama ku Halibeli, kucilima ye Ayini, sunga mu matunda, ku mhwani ya kucilima ye lilamba lya Galilaya.
12 Hanyuma kanyhi wojendelela sunga kutakama kwi'kolongo lya Yoludani sunga kwi'lenga lyo mwino.

“Nayo mipaka yiyi, yowa mipaka mine ye yisi yenyu.”

13 Nayo Musa yakawa kuwaloloseza zizi Waisilayeli yakutya, “Ayi yo yisi yono mosuma kugawana mu nzila yo kutowa kula, yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu yakondya kuyilavya ho yiwe so we milango yenyu yicenda na nusu.
14 Hambi i we milango ya Lubeni na ya Gadi, hamonga ne nusu ya wo mulango wa Manase, wono wali ku lubavu lwa kucilima, awo wose nha wakondya kulituga ligawo lyawo, nawo wakagawilwa mu nghango yo winji wa mu zimhuga zawo,
15 ku lubavu lwa kucilima ye likolongo lya Yoludani, vidome na Yeliko.”

I walongola wono wapegwa mulimo wo kuyigawa yisi

16 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
17 “Eleyezali mukohani, hamonga na Yoshuwa u mwana wa Nuni, wawo wo wetumaje mulimo wuwo wo kuwagawila wanhu yisi.
18 Kanyhi wuhagule wanhu wanji monga monga mu wulawa mu milango yose nha vyono yinha, ho wawataze mu mulimo wuwu wo wugawa yisi.”
19 Nawo wanhu wono Muwaha Mulungu yawahagula so mulimo wuwo, wowano:

Wo mulango wa Yuda,

Kalebi mwana wa Yefune.

20 Wo mulango wa Simiyoni,

Shelumiyeli u mwana wa Amihudi.

21 Wo mulango wa Benyamini,

Elidadi u mwana wa Cisiloni.

22 Wo mulango wa Dani,

Buki u mwana wa Yogili.

23 Wo mulango wa Manase,

Haniyeli, umwana wa Efodi.

24 Wo mulango wa Efulayimu,

Kemuyeli u mwana wa Shifatani.

25 Wo mulango wa Zabuloni,

Zelizafani u mwana Palinaki.

26 Wo mulango wa Isakali,

Pelitiyeli u mwana wa Azani.

27 Wo mulango wa Asheli,

Abihudi u mwana wa Shelomi.

28 Wo mulango wa Nafutali,

Pedaheli u mwana wa Amihudi.

29 Wawa wo wanhu wono Muwaha Mulungu yawahagula, wetume mulimo wo kugawa yisi kuli wanhu wa Isilayeli, mu yisi ya Kanani.


Wuwale 35

I makaya gono wapegwa Walawi

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakalongana na Musa lono yali yali mu yisi yo wuhihi ya Mowabu, ku nhumbi ya Yoludani behi na Yeliko, yakatya,
2 “Ale, walonjelaje Waisilayeli, vyono yiwanojeye wawagawile nawo Walawi makaya ganji, nawo wekale mumo, no wudimilo we mitugo zawo, wuwe behi behi ne makaya gago.
3 Ne makaya gago gowa sawo ye Walawi, nawo wokwikala mumo. Nawo wudimilo wowa wudimilo we zing'ombe zawo hamonga ne mitugo zinji zose.
4 Nawo wudimilo weze hanhu hagazi ku wulawilila mu majinji ge likaya. Weze wugazi wa mita magana mane na makumi mahano mu wupilima likaya mbavu zose.
5 Na mu nzila yiyo, wudimilo wowa no wugazi wa mita magana yicenda mu mbavu zose, ne likaya lyowa lili haciligati.
6 Kanyhi muwapele Walawi makaya ganji, gali mutandatu, gawe hanhu ha wutizizo wo munhu hono lunji yawulaje munhu mhalanyuka. Hamonga na zizo, wawapelaje kanyhi makaya ganji makumi mane na mejete,
7 hamonga no wudimilo wayo, nago mu wutowelele wago, gowa gaficile makumi mane na munana.
8 Ne liwalo lye makaya ge Walawi, gono wosuma kwikala, geze gakwiliye mu nghango ye yisi yawo vyono yinha.”

Makaya go Wutizizo

(Kum 19:1-13; Yos 20:1-9)

9 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa
10 yawalonjele Waisilayeli yatye, “Kolo mweza kulowoka likolongo lya Yoludani, na kolo mwenjila mu yisi ya Kanani,
11 ale mugawagule makaya ganji gawe so wutizizo, hono munhu yawulaje yunji mhalanyuka, ho yasume kutiziza mumo no kwiponya.
12 Mumo mo kokwiponezya kuli wandugu za mono yafwiye, hono wasace kwilipiciza so ndugu yawo. No munhu hono yasitakwe vyono kawulaga munhu, yalece kuwulagwa musunho, sunga calo cakwe yaling'anizwe calinze hali wanhu.
13 Holeka muhagule makaya mutandatu,
14 madatu gawe ku lubavu lwa kucilima ya Yoludani, ne madatu, gawe mu yisi ya Kanani,
15 Nago gosuma kuwa ho hanhu ha wutizizo we Waisilayeli, hamonga ne wajenzi wono mwikaye nawo nha waduwi du, hamba wono mwikalane nawo, wono si wolohama kanyhi. Nayo mono komuwulaga munhu mhalanyuka, yowa yimunojeye yatizize muli limonga lya mu makaya gaga.

16 “Na hono munhu yetumamile citwalo ca cuma
17 hamba yibwe,
18 hambe 'bici, yawulaje munhu, yuyo kowalwa vyono muwulaji, nayo munhu yuyo yanojeye yawulagwe.
19 No nduguye wa mono yawulagwe, yuyo yo kowa yanojeye yamuwulaje munhu yuyo u muwulaji. Nayo yamuwulaje du kolo vikwitang'anila nayo.

20 “Na hono munhu yawe yamusulile muyagwe, ho yamuwulaje mu nzila yo kumubamiliza hasi, hamba lunji mu nzila yo kumutowa na cinhu cinji,
21 hamba lunji yamutowanje na funzi, ale, munhu yuyo kowalwa vyono muwulaji, nayo yanojeye yawulagwe. No nduguye wa mono yawulagwe, yuyo yo kowa yanojeye yamuwulaje munhu yuyo u muwulaji. Nayo yamuwulagaje du kolo vikwitang'anila nayo.

22 “Lunji munhu hono yamuwulaje muyagwe mhalanyuka, mono si yali musili wakwe, lunji yamuwulaje mu nzila yo kumubamiliza hasi, lunji mu nzila yo kumugumilila cinhu cinji.
23 Hamba lunji munhu, kagumila yibwe wula kuwona, nalyo limutowa munhu kafwa, nayo yawe siyaligumiye libwe ne nyono, langa yali mhalanyuka du, no munhu yuyo yawe si yali musili wakwe yaye.
24 Ale mu zinghani ze liwala lili, yinojeye mhuga yose hamonga, yo yizilamule mu nzila yo kumuwonela lusungu u munhu mono yawulaje. Ninga yilece kuwa mu nzila yo wuwonela lusungu ayulya mono yakondya kufwilwa no nduguye, hambi mwene yuyo nha kosaka du yelipicize, so lifwa lyo nduguye.
25 Yinojeye mhuga yose yimuponye u munhu mono yetumile lisutya lyo wuwulaji mu miwoko yo ndugu yo munhu mono yafwiye. Na yowa yinojeye u munhu yuyo yagomolezwe ku kaya yo wutizizo, kono yali yakondya kutiziza. Nayo yekalaje kuko du, sunga kono kofwila u Mukohani Muwaha mono yetilwa mafuta gono gelile, mono yali baho lulo.
26 Na hono munhu mono yawulaje, yawuce mwi'kaya lyono yali yatiziza,
27 na hono munya nduguye mono yawulagwe yamuwone mumo mwihala, yamuwulaje, ale wilipicize wuwo si wowa wuwulaji yaye.
28 Holeka yinojeye u munhu muwulaji yekale mumo du mwi'kaye lyo wutizizo, sunga kono kolofwila Mukohani Muwaha mono yali baho mu vilo vivyo. Na kolo mukohani yakondya kufwa, hodu kosuma kuwuya ku kaya yakwe.
29 No wutemye wuwu wuli so nyenye na mu ndelo zenyu zose, mu wikalo wenyu wose.

30 “No munhu hono yawulaje munhu muyagwe, yuyo kowa muwulaji, nayo yimunojeye yawulagwe mu nzila ye wahomelezi wejete, hamba kuciza. Hambi wuhomelezi wo munhu monga, nha si wukwiliye yaye kuhomelela wufwe wo munhu.
31 Nayo mono yakuwulaga munhu, yanojeye nayo yawulagwe. Hamba mulecele lukulu kubocela malipo ge mapesa kuli munhu yuyo muwulaji, ho yeponye mu wulipicizwe wakwe ku so wuwulaji.
32 Na hono munhu yawe yatiziza kwi'kaya lyo wutizizo, mulecele lukulu kumulumbiza yalipe pesa, ho yakombolwe yawuye ku kaya yakwe, lono Mukohani Muwaha yakali si nafwa.
33 Na hono mwijelele du munoze nha vivyo, hodu mowa mwayavufya yisi yono mwikaye. Hambi wuwulaji nha wukuwa kuyavufya yisi, hamba hasina cilavywa cinji cono cosuma kuyeza yisi, ku soko yo wuwulaji wono witumiywe muli yene, langa hono si lifwa lya yuyo munhu muwulaji du, sunga yawulagwe.
34 Holeka mulecele lukulu kuyavufya yisi yono mwikaye, hambi ane nha yonene Muwaha Mulungu, nane nikaye hamonga ne wanhu wa Isilayeli.”


Wuwale 36

U wuhazi we wacekulu wono watoligwe

1 Iwawaha we zi mhuga mu wana wa Gileyadi, u mwana wa Macili mwizukulu wa Manase, u mwana wa Yusufu, wakamubitila Musa, hamonga ne walongola wanji.
2 Wakamutya, “Muwaha Mulungu yakulajiliza wuyigawe yisi kuli wanhu Waisilayeli mu nzila yo kutowa kula. Kanyhi yakakulajiliza vyono wuwapele sawo yo ndugu yetu Zelofehadi i wanagwe we cicekulu.
3 Ninga wukumbucile, vyono hono watolwe no munhu mono si wo mulango wetu, ne sawo yiyo si yoleka kuwa yo mulango wuwo kuko kono kotolwa, na baho ligawo lyono cali cigawiywe hodu lyocepuka.
4 Na mu mwaka we makumi mahano we sikukuu lono sawo yose yono yali yiguzigwe yeza kugomolwa kuli wanya sawo, yiyo sawo ye wana we cicekulu wa Zelofehadi, yowa yeza sawo yo mulango wuwo du kono watoligwe, nayo lu, yowa yakondya kwajililila lukulu, ku wulawa mu mulango wetu.”

5 Nayo Musa yakawa kuwaloloseza wanhu wa Isilayeli wulajilize wuno, wono Muwaha Mulungu yalajiliza, yakawatya, “I nghani yono u mulango wa Manase wakuyilonga, yo nghani ya mbeka,
6 holeka Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, vyono i wana we cicekulu wa Zelofehadi, heya wadahile kutolwa no munhu wo wose, ninga watolwe no munhu wa mu mhuga yo mulango wawo du.
7 Hambi sawo yo Mwiisilayeli wo wose, nha yinojeye yikale mu wo mulango wakwe du.
8 No mucekulu wo wose mono kohala sawo mu wulawa mu sawo ye Waisilayeli, yimunojeye yatolwe no munhu wa mu mhuga yo mulango wo Mwiisilayeli du. Na mu nzila yiyi, Mwiisilayeli wowose, kosuma kujendelela kuhala sawo ye walezi wakwe,
9 sunga ne sawo nayo si yosuma kwilecelwa ku wulawa mu mulango awuno, yibite kanyhi ku mulango awulya, aa, ng'o. Langa milango yose yojendelela kwilecela zisawo zawo weneco wose du.”

10 Holeka Mahila, na Tiliza, na Hogila, na Milika, hamonga na Nowa, i wana we cicekulu wa Zelofehadi,
11 wakanoza nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa. Wose wakatolwa ne wahizi zawo.
12 Watolwa ne wana we mhuga ya Manase u mwana wa Yusufu. Ne sawo yawo yikekala mu mhuga yo mulango wo sogo yawo.

13 Aga go malajilizo no wutemye, wono Muwaha Mulungu yawapelela Waisilayeli muli Musa, lono wali wali mu yisi yo wuhihi ya Mowabu, ku nhumbi ye likolongo lya Yoludani behi na Yeliko.
