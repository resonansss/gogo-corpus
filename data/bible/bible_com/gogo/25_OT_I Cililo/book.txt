I Cililo 1

Wukalale wa Yelusalemi

1 Lanje lu, likaya lili lya Yelusalemi vyono lyekala yiciwa,

ninga ho lyali limemile

wanhu wenji muno muno.

Nalyo lyali lina

ne lilumbo yiwaha mono yinze yose,

ninga alu mulanje,

lyehwana no mucekulu mufwile.

Na mu makaya gose,

lyene lyali nho mwana we cicekulu wo mutemi.

Ninga alu lyakondya kusolwa no kutegulwa ku wuwanda.

2 Alu Yelusalemi kekalaga yakulila cilo cose,

ne mahozi gakuhuluma mu meso.

Ne wendece wakwe wose we katali,

wakondya kumuleka, hasina hamba monga mono yakumutuza.

Na wono vyali vilunzize nayo, alu wamugaluka,

wose weza walugu wakwe.

3 Nawo wanhu wa Yuda, weza wawanda lu,

wono wasina wutazi wo wose.

Hambi lu nha wakondya kuhamywa ku zikaya zawo.

Weza wekala mu zi'si zinji,

wasina hamba welecele wo wose.

Wapilimwe ne walugu mbavu zose,

wasina hamba nzila yo kwiponezya.

4 Nalu hasina hamba munhu monga kanyhi,

mono kosuma kubita kuhemba ku Nyumba yo Mulungu

mu vilo vye zi sikukuu.

Ne wahinza wose wono wembaga,

alu wali mu wugaye,

nawo wakohani wakugula du.

Ne milango ye likaya, yeza mu wambuce,

nayo Sayuni yili mu wusungu wuwaha muno.

5 Ne walugu wa Yelusalemi wakondya kuyisuma

no kuyibata mu vilungo vyawo.

Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yayileka yinjile mu magazo,

ku soko yo wubi wayo winji.

Ne wanagwe nawo, wakondya kusolwa no kutegulwa kutali.

6 No lukumyo lwa Yelusalemi,

aluno lwaweza cinhu cono capuluta katali.

Ne walongola wayo wehwana ne zinzimba zono zigandile so nzala,

Nalu wakondya kubita wasina vilungo,

wakuhwagwa kumwande ya wono wakuwatyatya mu nyuma yawo.

7 Na mu wutamiswe wuwo,

alu Yelusalemi yikukumbucila zo lukumyo lwayo lwe katali.

Na lono yagwizaga mu miwoko ye walugu,

hali hasina hamba munya kumutaziza.

Ne walugu wakwe wakasecelela muno so wugwe wakwe.

8 No lukumyo lwa Yelusalemi lwakondya kuwuka,

alu yeza yali cipwi, yebatwe ne cevu.

Nalu yakugula yehundicize wusu wakwe, yakuwona soni.

Yelusalemi yetipiza mweneco so wubi wakwe.

9 No wubi wakwe wijeye calinze,

ninga mwene yakwilajila nha kotya si yali muli zizo yaye.

Holeka ku soko yiyo, no wugwe wakwe wa mbuko,

hamba hasina mono kosuma kumusindamika yaye.

Ne walugu wakwe wamusuma,

nalu mwene yakumulilila Muwaha Mulungu yamuwonele lusungu.

10 Ne walugu wakwe wakamuhiziza sawo yakwe yose.

Nayo yakawawona ne meso gakwe mweneco,

vyono wakwinjila mu Nyumba yo Mulungu,

mono Muwaha Mulungu yali yawalemeze

we makungugo walece kwinjila.

11 Nawo wanhu wakwe wose wakugula, wakulondola ndigwa.

Wakukawa vinhu vyawo na ndigwa, ho walece kufwa.

Holeka likaya likulila likutya,

“Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu wunghumbucile,

wugalanje magayo gangu.

12 “Alu Yelusalemi yikumulilila munhu wo wose mono yakupuluta, yikutya,

‘Ta muwone! Hasina hamba yunji

mono yatuga magayo mawaha gono genha gaga nha vyono ane ninha.’

Magayo gono Muwaha Mulungu yamheleye mwi'kuwo lyakwe.

13 “Kagumila moto ku wulawa kucanyha,

moto wono wukunyanya muli'ne.

Kanhega no mutego, kangwisa ho hasi.

Nalu yakondya kundeka nili wene, nili mu wusungu wuwaha.

14 “No wubi wangu wose mwene yawuwene,

kanyhi kawuwopeleza wose hamonga,

kawutumbika mu singo yangu.

Nane nghuwula vilungo, ku soko yo wuzito wayo.

Muwaha Mulungu kanyinjiza mu miwoko ye walugu wangu,

nane niwa sina wo kunhaza.

15 “Hambi Muwaha Mulungu

nha kawanoza vitung'ati wangu wawe si cinji kuli'ne.

Hambi nha katuma wasilikale wawongomeza wazelelo wangu.

Muwaha Mulungu kawakanyanjiza wanhu wangu,

nha vyono wakobongaga zabibu mu ciya co kuminyila.

16 “Na yo soko yono yikuleka meso gangu gakulawa mahozi.

Hasina hamba monga mono konhuza,

hamba kusikamya.

Hambi walugu wangu nha wakondya kusuma 'ne,

ne wanhu wangu wasina hamba cono ciwasigaliye.

17 “Nane ngoloye mawoko gangu,

ninga hasina hamba monga mono yakumhela wutazi.

Hambi Muwaha Mulungu

nha kandetela walugu ku wulawa mbavu zose.

Nawo wakunhendela nha cinhu cono citipiye.

18 “Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yo we ling'ani,

hambi nha yo nene mono si nali kwenda kumuhulika.

Holeka muhulicizaje anye wanhu wose,

muwuwone wugaye wangu.

Hambi i wazelelo wangu hamonga ne wacekulu,

nha wakondya kusolwa no kutegulwa kutali.

19 “Nane nghawacema wono cali cilunzize nawo,

ho waze wamhele wutazi,

ninga wakalema kuza.

I wakohani ne walongola,

wose wakafwila mwi'kaya,

lono walondolaga ndigwa, walece kufwa.

20 “Alu 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wulangaje ta wusungu

wono nina nawo mu nhumbula yangu!

Ne nhumbula yangu nha yatulilwa muno no wukalale,

ku soko yo wubi wangu.

Alu mu zinzila, mweza mowuli wuwulaji,

na mu milango namo, molili yifwa.

21 “Alu wulanje vyono, sunga na mu wugule wangu,

hasina hamba monga mono yakumhela wutuzwe.

Nawo walugu wangu wasangalaye,

vyono agwe kundetela wugaye wono winha wuwu.

Holeka lize mbela lizuwa lyako lyono wehuwiza,

liwanoze walugu wangu,

nawo wenjile mu magayo,

wagaye nha vyono ane nghugaya.

22 “Wuwaling'anize so wubi wawo wose.

Wuwalipicize nawo,

nha vyono wundipicize nene so wubi wangu.

Nghugula ko wusungu muno,

ne nhumbula yangu yizilice.”


I Cililo 2

Muwaha Mulungu yakuyilipiciza Yelusalemi

1 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yayifunika

Sayuni no wutitu mwi'kuwo lyakwe.

No lukumyo lwayo lwa ku wulanga, yaluhodanjila lukulu,

Na mwi'zuwa lye likuwo lyakwe,

yayileka sunga ne Nyumba yakwe.

2 Muwaha Mulungu yavyongomeza vitumbi vyose vya Yuda,

hamba kuviwonela lusungu yaye.

Yakumulanga ne magomawakali gose gono gayicitilaga yisi.

Yawaletela cevu ciwaha i watemi,

hamonga no wutemi wawo.

3 Na mwi'kuwo lyakwe, yavidumulanga vilungo vya Isilayeli.

Yalema kucipela wutazi, lono walugu waciziza.

Yacitakamiza nha vyono moto wutakamizaga vyose.

4 Yacilunga ne zisonga zakwe nha mulugu,

yawawulaga wose, wono wali wendeze ha meso getu.

Nase amuno mono Yelusalemi,

cikaliwona likuwo lyakwe vyono likwaka.

5 Muwaha Mulungu, yawahodanga Waisilayeli nha mulugu.

Yagaleka magomawakali gayo ne hanhu hayo vihodangwe.

Yawaletela wanhu wa Yuda wusungu wono si wolomalika.

6 Kayikumulanga Nyumba yo Mulungu,

yono cenjilaga cikumuhemba.

Yaciwusiza ne vilo vyetu vye zi sikukuu na vye zi Sabato.

I watemi hamonga ne wakohani,

wose wawuwona wukali we likuwo lyakwe.

7 U Muwaha Mulungu yakondya kucilema cikumbicilo cakwe,

no kuyileka Nyumba yakwe yono yelile.

Kakondya kuwaleka walugu wagakumulanje majinji gayo gose.

Wakuzoza na nyemo yo wusume, i hanhu hono katali,

cenjilaga cikuhemba na nyemo.

8 Muwaha Mulungu yali yasimbiliye,

vyono majinji ga Sayuni ganojeye gakumulangwe du.

Yakamula kuwalanga,

ho yawaletele wuhodanjice wono wuwakwiliye.

Majinji hamonga ne minala yayo, vyose vigwiye,

nalu gawa matongo.

9 Nayo milango yayo, mifucizwe mu malowanjila,

ne makomeyo gayo gose gabenanjice.

U mutemi hamonga ne wanhu wakwe,

wose wali ku wuhamywe.

Alu nhondo, hamba Wulajilize weza si wukwijizwa yaye,

ne wawizi nawo hamba si wakutuga wugubulilwe

ku wulawa kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

10 Nawo i wanhu wakombi wa mu Yelusalemi,

wekaye wanyamaye twii,

akuno weyitiye manghundi mu mitwe yawo, wavwalile magunila.

Nawo i wahinza, wenamizye mitwe yawo hasi.

11 Woyi, he! Nhondo ne meso gangu, gapaya so cililo,

ne nhumbula yangu nayo, yili mu wusungu wuwaha.

No wusungu wanhamisa muno,

ku soko yo wuhodanjice we wanhu wangu.

Naluno i wana wadodo hamonga ne wagalika,

wakuzilika du mu zinzila ze likaya.

12 Wakulilila wanyina zawo, so nzala ne nyhilu ye malenga.

Alu wakugwanga du mu zinzila,

wenha wanhu wono wena nghomele.

Hanyuma hodu,

wawa kufwila mu mawoko ge wanyina zawo mhola mhola.

13 Woyi he gwe Yelusalemi! Go mwendece wangu Yelusalemi!

Alu waco ho nolonga cici kanyhi?

Ho nosuma wule kukusindamika?

Hambi nha hasina munhu hamba monga,

mono yatamiswa nha vivi.

Hambi magayo gako nha gasina mupaka nhe lilenga.

Hasina hamba witumbilo kanyhi.

14 Sunga ne wawizi wako nawo,

weza wasina cinji cono wokulonjela,

langa zo wulambi-lambi du.

No wuganule wawo wukukongaga du,

hamba si wukuwa kukulajila zo wubi wako.

Na mu nzila yiyo, wakukunoza agwe,

ho wiwone nha vyono wusina soko yo kunena.

15 Ne wanhu wono wakupuluta wakolile mwi'kaya,

wakukulanga ko wubedegale,

wawa kusinjisa ne mitwe yawo akuno wakugaseka matongo ga Yelusalemi,

wakutya, “Mwene 'nye, mbeka lili lyo likaya liswanu,

lyono lyali likumuce mu yinze yose?”

16 Ne walugu wako wose,

alu wakukwizwajiza no kukunugulila ko lusulo,

akuno wakuyibobotola ne milomo yawo,

no kukubedula wakutya,

“Hee!

Cilihodanga si!

Ali lyo lizuwa lyono cali cililanjiliye muno!”

17 Alu Muwaha Mulungu,

nhondo ketuma zono yali yazitemizye kuzituma.

Kacihodanga yasina lusungu nha vyono yacilonjela.

Kawapela walugu wetu wusume,

kawapela wusangalale so wutamiswe wetu.

18 Gwe Yelusalemi,

wugalece majinji gako gamulilile Muwaha Mulungu!

Galekaje mahozi gako gasulule misi ne cilo.

Wisakaze ne cililo, hamonga no wusungu!

19 Na mu cilo cose,

weze kwinuka kali kenji wukumulilila Muwaha Mulungu,

wumujidile mutima wako nha malenga u Muwaha Mulungu,

wumulombe yawawonele lusungu wanago.

I wanago wono wakufwa ne nzala mu zinzila zose!

20 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wuwalanje wawo ta,

wono wukuwatamisa, wo wa nani wene?

Alu wulanje vyono sunga ne wacekulu nhondo,

weza kulya ne miwili ye wana wawo wono wawendile!

Sunga ne wakohani hamonga ne wawizi,

nawo nhondo weza kuwulajilwa mu Nyumba yako mugati!

21 Alu i wadodo ne wakombi,

wose nha wagwiye wafwiye mu zinzila.

I wazelelo hamonga ne wacekulu,

wose wakondya kuwulagwa ne mimaje ye walugu.

Kuwawulajila mwi'zuwa lye likuwo lyako,

wusina hamba lusungu.

22 Wukawalalika walugu wangu ku sikukuu,

ho woga wangu wijele mbavu zose.

Hasina hamba mono kolopona mwi'zuwa lilyo lye likuwo lyako.

Hambi nha wawawulaga wanangu wono nawawilusa no kuwenda.


I Cililo 3

Wulipicizwe no Wunene no Witumbilo

1 Ane yo nene mono newonelela no mweneco,

u wulipicizwe wo Mulungu vyono winha.

2 Yanyinjiza mu wutitu muno muno.

3 Yanhowanga kali kenji muno ko wukali.

4 Yandeka muwili wangu wugandile kanyhi wutimbile,

yagabenanga ne mafupa gangu.

5 Yandindila mu jela yo wukalale ne cevu.

6 Yambika nikale mu wutitu we lifwa wono wikalaga.

7 Yambopa ne manyolo,

nalu nawa nili muwopwe sina hamba witumbilo wo kupona yaye.

8 Nane nghulilila wutazi,

ninga Mulungu si yakusaka kuhuliciza ng'o.

9 Nane nghupebesuka-pebesuka lono nghujenda,

hambi majinji ge mabwe,

nha ganhejeye mu zinzila zose kose kono nghubita.

10 Yanhejela nha dubu, kanzumhila kotya simba.

11 Kanghundujiziza ha mhelo ye nzila,

kanhemasha nha nili nyama,

kawa kundeka baho.

12 Kawutawika wupinde wakwe,

kanoza ane yo niwe munhu wo kulungwa ne zisonga zakwe.

13 Kanguma,

ne zisonga zakwe zinjila mu muwili wangu.

14 Ne wanhu wakusekanga misi yose,

nawa nili munhu wo kuzwajizwa kuli wene wose.

15 Nayo kamhela magayo mawaha,

yiwe yo ndigwa yangu, ne malenga go kung'wa.

16 Kanghuluza wusu wangu hasi,

kagabena ne meno gangu mu masakalawe.

17 Nane lu nakondya no kuvisemwa na vyono vinha,

u wumoteswe, no mulamu, hamonga no wusangalale.

18 No wikala nili mumi, weza wudodo.

No witumbilo wangu wa kuli Muwaha Mulungu, wawuka.

19 Na kolo nghwihwesa zo wusungu wangu,

na vyono kanyhi neza sina kaya,

yikuwa nha sumu nghali kuli'ne.

20 Nane nikalaga nghuzihweza zizo,

ne nhumbula yangu yina wukalale wuwaha.

21 Ninga kanyhi witumbilo wangu wukuza,

kolo nghuzikumbucila zinghani azino zono zikutya,

22 U lusungu no wendo wo Muwaha Mulungu,

si vidumaga yaye.

23 Vikalaga vyo vipya du nhondo zose,

nha vyono yinha hakali mitondo lono lizuwa likusosomoka,

wihuwilwa wakwe wo wuwaha muno muno.

24 Holeka ane, Muwaha Mulungu lyo lifungu lyangu.

Nane nimwitumbiye mweneco du.

25 U Muwaha Mulungu,

muswanu kuli wose wono wakumwitumbila.

26 Holeka yowa nghani swanu kuli'se cimulindile

ko wukangalizi, yaciponye.

27 Kanyhi yicinojeye ciyijize wukangalizi

wuwu lono cikali wadodo.

28 Na lono cenjila mu magayo,

yicinojeye cikale cibembo cili wene,

ciwe cinyamaye ko wukangalile.

29 Yicinojeye ciyilavye mu nzila yo kumwinamila,

hambi nha ko kowuli witumbilo kuli mwene.

30 Hamba hono citowe, hamba kutovulwa,

yicinojeye ciwe kuzibocela zose du.

31 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu wetu, nha yali lusungu,

hamba si kocileka cibitilila.

32 Hanji yadahile kuciletela wukalale,

ninga wendo wakwe kuli'se wo we mbeka,

kanyhi wuli vilungo.

33 Kanyhi mwene,

si kendezwaga yaye yaciletele wukalale no wusungu.

34 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu,

yamanyile lono zinhumbula zetu ziwopigwe mu jela,

zikupondangwa.

35 Kanyhi yamanyile na lono cikuzilema ze ling'ani

zono yacipela.

36 Ne ling'ani lono si yikwitumwa mu mahakama,

mwene yamanyile du.

37 U wuhile wo Muwaha Mulungu du,

wo wunojeye wuwe kwitumwa nhondo zose.

38 I ziswanu ne zimbi,

zose zili mu wulajilize wakwe.

39 Alu mwene nhawule cendile kung'ung'ulika,

lono cikulipicizwa ku soko yo wubi wetu?

40 Ninga aluno, yicinojeye ciyihenje mu mikalile yetu,

cimuwuyile Muwaha Mulungu wetu.

41 Cimwinulile Mulungu zinhumbula zetu ku wulanga,

cimulombe.

42 “Ase mbeka cetuma wubi no kukuleka,

ninga nagwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

hamba si wacinenela.

43 “Wacityatya wukaciwulaga,

hambi lusungu lwako nha lwali lwihundice mwi'kuwo lyako.

44 No wulombe wetu hamba si wakuficila yaye

ku soko ye livunde lizito lyono walicita.

45 Wacinoza ciwe cili manghundi, mono yinze.

46 “Nalu cikutovulwa no kuzwajizwa ne walugu wetu wose.

47 Ceza cili mu magayo hamonga no wongomele,

cikaye mu zi mbali no woga.

48 Ne meso gangu, gakuhuluma makolongo ge mahozi,

ku soko yo wongomele we wanhu wangu.

49 “Ne mahozi gangu,

gojendelela du kuhuluma nhe cikolongo.

50 Sunga lono Muwaha Mulungu,

kolo yakulanga hasi ku wulawa ku wulanga yaciwone.

51 Ne nhumbula yangu, yikalaye muno,

lono nghuziwona zono zawejelela wacekulu wa mwi'kaya.

52 “Walugu wangu wakangwiza

mu mutego mbula soko yo kusulila.

53 Wakangumila mwi'kombo vyo nili mumi,

hanyuma wakawa kubidusiza libwe,

no kuwudinda mulango we likombo.

54 Ne malenga gakawa kwanza kuhulumila mumo mwi'kombo,

nghawona vyono lifwa lyasejelela.

55 “Nane nghakulilila go Muwaha Mulungu lono

nili mumo mwi'kombo.

56 Nane nali neza kukulomba wuhulice cililo cangu,

nagwe wukahulika.

57 Wukamhitula, wukanonjela vyono wulece kogopa.

58 “Wukaza wukamhonya 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu wangu,

wukawuponya wumi wangu.

59 Holeka wuning'anize nghani yangu,

hambi nha wuzimanyile zimbi zono zetumwa kuli'ne.

60 Hambi agwe nha wumanyile,

vyono walugu wangu wasulile,

na vyono wakwihehemeza ku soko yangu.

61 “Hambi nha kuwahulikaga vyono wakunhovula

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

kanyhi wumanyile no wihehemeze wawo wose.

62 Misi yose walongaga zangu du,

no kwilumbaniza zo wuhile wawo wubi kuli'ne.

63 Na ku wulawilila hakali mitondo sunga lyoswa,

wekalaga wakusekanga du.

64 “Gwe Muwaha Mulungu,

wuwalipicize mu nghango ye metumo gawo.

65 Wuwalijite, kanyhi wuwapele no wukamu we zinhumbula.

66 Wuwapelembe, wuwapatile lukulu mono yinze!”


I Cililo 4

Yelusalemi lono yakondya kugwa

1 Wung'alale we zahabu yetu hamba si wejela,

ne mabwe gono gali mazenjele Nyumba yo Mulungu,

gakeyagala caka mu zinzila.

2 Ne wazelelo wa Sayuni,

wali wo we cifo cikamu nha zahabu,

ninga luno, wakutendelwa nha viya vye lilongo.

3 Sunga ne macewe nha gakuwa kosa mana gayo.

Ninga wanhu wangu weza wakali,

wenha nyacihema ya mu nyika.

4 Wakuwaleka wana wawo wagalika wakufwa ne nzala,

hamonga ne nyhilu ye malenga.

Wana nawo wakwilombeleza ndigwa,

yono hasina hamba mono kosuma kuwapela.

5 Sunga ne wanhu wono walizaga ndigwa swanu,

wose nha wafwila mu zinzila ku soko ye nzala.

Na wono wekalaga wamotile,

alu wakugaya so wulondola ndigwa.

6 Ne wanhu wangu watuga wulipicizwe wuwaha muno

si nhe wanhu wa Sodoma,

wono wepamila ne lifwa lye nhinicizo mu muwoko wo Mulungu.

7 Ne wawaha wetu wali waswanu kanyhi wazelu wenha seluji,

wali vilungo, wahidimbazi ku milimo,

kanyhi wena miwili miswanu yono yijinile.

8 Alu lanje, wagonile mu zinzila du,

hamba kumanyika na munhu yaye,

wakondya kufwa, no wusu wawo watukuwala,

ne zinghuli zawo zanyala nha ngwagu,

zinyaliliye ne mafupa gawo.

9 Na wono wafwila mu wulugu nha wali ciba,

si nha wono wakafwa munyuma yayo,

wono wafwizaga mhola mhola so nzala,

wono wali wasina ndigwa yo kulya.

10 Ne magayo gono gawaziza wanhu wangu,

galeta wogofye wuwaha muno.

Hambi i wacekulu wono wali wawendile muno wana wawo,

nha wakawateleka wana wawo, ho watuje ndigwa yo kulya.

11 Muwaha Mulungu yakalifisiliza likuwo lyakwe liwaha,

yakayipembela moto Sayuni,

yikapya yikakongojela ho hasi.

12 Hali hasina hamba munhu monga,

hamba na muli walongola we makungugo majenzi,

mono yali yehuwiye vyono wulugu wosuma

kwinjila mu milango ya Yelusalemi.

13 Heya, zizi zejela, ku soko wawizi wawo wetuma wubi,

ne wakohani nawo wakasutya, no kwambuza lifwa,

kuli wanhu wono wasina masutya.

14 Ne walongola wawo,

wakawa kuyumba-yumba du mu zinzila wenha wabofu.

Na ku soko yiyo wakekwizanga sakami,

mu nghango yono sunga yunji si yasuma kuwapamiza.

15 Sunga ne wanhu, wakawa kuwawinga wakutya,

“Bitenyi ako!

Hambi nha mweyavufya!

Holeka muwuce,

mulece hamba kucipamiza'se!”

Hodu, nawo wakawa kutuluka,

wakabita ako na ako mu makungugo ganji,

ninga hakawa hasina hamba yunji kuko,

mono yawabocela yaye.

16 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, hamba si yawatununhicila yaye,

Nhondo ho yakacila kuweyagaza.

Hamba si yawakumbucila yaye i wakohani wetu,

hamonga ne walongola.

17 Nase cikalanjilila, lunji cotuga wutazi,

ninga calanjilila sunga cikakatala,

hamba kuwutuga yaye.

Cikajela kanyhi kulindila,

lunji cotuga wutazi ku wulawa mwi'kungugo linji,

ninga hamba si cawutuga yaye.

18 Nawo walugu wetu, vyo wali wacilanjiliye du,

hamba si casuma no kujenda-jenda ako na ako mu zinzila.

Ne vilo vyetu vikawa vyamalika, no mudumo wetu wafika.

19 Nawo wono wacilondaga mu nyuma,

wali wali musunho muno kuciza numba ya mu mwanya.

Wakacityatya mu matunda, wakacisola,

wakacitegula ku nyika.

20 Wakamusola u Wehele wo wumi wetu,

u Mutemi, mono Muwaha Mulungu yamuhagula.

U munhu mono cali cimwitumbiye,

nha vyono yo kocitaza muli walugu wetu.

21 Ale, musece du, no kusangalala mu nghango yenyu,

anye nyo wanhu wa Edomu na Uzi.

Ninga mumanye nha vyono,

nanye magazo gakumuzizenyi nhavivyo,

nanye molopuwa,

muwe jenda cipwi mukoligwe ne cevu.

22 Alu Sayuni yakondya kulipicizwa so wubi wayo.

Holeka Muwaha Mulungu,

hamba si kucileka cijendelele kuwa mu wuhamywe yaye.

Langa agwe Edomu, u Muwaha Mulungu kokulipiciza du,

kanyhi kowanzalika no wubi wako.


I Cililo 5

Kulomba lusungu

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wukumbucile zono zacagana,

wucilanje, wuciwone ne cevu cetu.

2 Hambi zisawo zetu zose,

nha zeza zili mu miwoko ye wajenzi.

Kanyhi wawo wajenzi, wo wekaye na mu zikaya zetu.

3 Ne wasogo zetu wose,

nha wakondya kuwulagwa ne walugu wetu.

Ne wanyina zetu wekala wafwile.

4 Alu lanje, ne malenga gose go kung'wa, cikugula,

sunga ne zingwagu zose cikugula.

5 Wacimasha duu, kotya cili madogowe hamba ngamiya,

sunga nhondo cakatala.

Ninga hamba nhavivyo, si wakucileka ciceheze yaye.

6 Na lono ciwe cikusaka ndigwa zono zicikwiliye,

sunga cibite du cikelombeleze ku Misili, hamba ku Siliya.

7 Walezi wetu, heya wasutya, ninga aluno wakondya kufwa,

ninga ase luno, ceza kugaya ku soko yo wubi wawo.

8 Nalu citemigwe ne wanhu wawanda,

na hasina mono kociponya mu miwoko yawo.

9 Ne wawulaji wakukoloma mu nyika.

Nase cikuwa kuwusuza wumi wetu,

lono cikubita kulondola ndigwa.

10 Ne nzala nayo yacinoza zinghuli zetu zawawuka

ne zimhungo,

sunga ne miwili yetu nayo, yeza yili moto kotya mafigwa.

11 Ne wagolece zetu nawo, wakwibatwa ne walume,

no kugona nawo kwe vilungo mwi'tunda lya Sayuni,

na mu vitumbi vyose vya Yuda.

Ne wana wetu we cicekulu,

nawo wakudindimizwa ho wagone ne walume kwe vilungo.

12 I walongola wetu nawo, wakwibatwa, wakunyongwa.

I wakombi wetu, si wakupegwa cogopo yaye.

13 Nawo i wazelelo wetu,

wakudindimizwa wakuzule wuhemba mu matuli nha wawanda.

Nawo i wana wetu wadodo we cilume,

wapebesukaga-pebesukaga so wudinghula

mitegulo mizito ye zingwagu.

14 I wakombi nawo,

wapela ku wubita kwikala mu milango ye likaya.

I wazelelo nawo, wapela ku wubita ku ng'oma.

15 I nyemo ya mu zinhumbula zetu, yaduma.

Ne midawalo yetu yapituka cawa cililo.

16 Hasina cinji cono cisigaye,

muli vyose vyono celumbilaga.

Heya cetuma wubi, nalu congomezwa.

17 Holeka zinhumbula zetu zizilice.

Na ku soko ya zizo, ne meso getu si gakuwona viswanu.

18 Hambi litunda lye Sayuni nha lyalekwa lyekala lili wene,

ne macewe gakupilima-pilima mu matongo galyo.

19 Ninga agwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

yo gwegwe du wuli mutemi cibitilila,

nagwe kotema sunga ko ku ndumo ye yinze.

20 Alu nha wacileka sunga vilo vyeza vinji muno?

Alo hamba si kocikumbucila kanyhi ng'o?

21 Cikukulomba wucigomole kuli'gwe,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

cikunena wucigomole!

Wucigomolele kanyhi lukumyo lwetu lwe katali.

22 Hamba hodu wacilemela lukulu cibitilila?

Alu kocikolelwa likuwo lyako sunga nili lo?
