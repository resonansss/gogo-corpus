2 Zinghomo 25

Amaziya u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 14:2-6)

1 Amaziya lono yatemela, yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na mihano, yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi mejete ne'cenda. U nyina yacemwaga Yehoyadini munya Yelusalemi,
2 Amaziya yetuma zono zamwendeza Muwaha Mulungu, ninga si yetuma na nhumbula nhuvu yaye.
3 Nayo yali yeza kutuga vilungo mu wutemi wakwe, hobaho yakawawulaga i wetumami wawalya wono wamuwulaga sogwe u mutemi Yowashi.
4 Ninga i wana wawo, awo langa si yawawulaga yaye, yatyatya zono Muwaha Mulungu yalajiliza mu Wulajilize wa Musa, zono zikutya, “I walezi si wofwa ku soko yo wubi we wana wawo, hamba wana nawo si wofwa ku soko yo wubi we walezi wawo. Langa munhu kofwa ku soko yo wubi wakwe mweneco du.”

U wihomi ne Waedomu

(2Wat 14:7)

5 U mutemi Amaziya, yakawawikaniza wanhu wo mulango wa Yuda na wa Benyamini, yakawenjiza mu mulimo wo wusilikale, yakawawika mu vipuga vipuga, mhuga na cipuga cayo, mu nghango ye zimhuga zawo vyono zali. Hanyuma yakawa kuwawika wawaha walanjilizi, we vipuga vye wanhu elufu monga monga, na we vipuga vya wanhu yigana limonga limonga. Ne wanhu wawa, wali wahangahanjile hamonga, ku wulawilila wono wali waficile myaka makumi mejete, hamonga ne wanhu mututu, ne liwalo lyawo wose hamonga, wali waficile wanhu elufu magana madatu cititila. Wawo wakasolwa, wakategulwa ku wulugu. Nawo wali wanhu wono wazoweye muno mu witumamila migoha, hamonga ne zingula.
6 Hamonga na wawo, yakalomba ne wasilikale wanji ku wulawa ku Isilayeli, yakawalipa fweza zo wuzito wa kilo elufu yidatu na magana mane, ho waze wamutaze.
7 Ninga ho bahobaho, u muwizi yakamubitila mutemi Amaziya, yakamulonjela yakamutya, “Gwe mutemi, wulecele lukulu kuwasola wasilikale wawa we Ciisilayeli. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yakondya kuwaleka wanhu wawo wo wutemi wa ku lubavu lwa kusumuka.
8 Agwe wukwihwesa nha vyono wawo wokutaza mu milewo, ninga yo Mulungu du, mono yena vilungo vyo wusume hamba vyo wusumwe, nayo yali wene du mono kosuma kuwaleka walugu wako wakusume.”

9 Nayo Amaziya, yakamuwuza muwizi, yakatya, “Alu, ma, zifweza zangu zono nakondya kuwalipa, ho zobita du za bwete?”

Muwizi nayo yakamupitula Amaziya yakatya, “Wukumbucile nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu kosuma kukuhaliza nyinji zicizize zizo!”
10 Hodu, Amaziya nhondo yakawa kuwaluwula wawalya wasilikale, yakawakombola wawuye ku zikwawo. Nawo wakavumila ninga kunji si wendile, wakatuluka wakomaye, ku soko ye wanhu wa Yuda.

11 Amaziya yakawa kwikamiliza, yakawa kuwategula wasilikale wakwe ko kwi'lolo lyo Mwino. Wakawa kwanza kwihoma kuko ne wasilikale we Ciedomu, ne wasilikale we Ciedomu wakawulagwa waficile elufu yikumi.
12 Na wanji wakatekwa nawo waficile elufu yikumi. Wakawa kuwasola wasilikale wawo wono wawateka, wakawategula kwi'kaya lya Sela, wakawawika mu vizuli vye mabwe matali, wakawa kuwagumila hasi. Ne wasilikale wose, wakavulumuka wakwitowezanga mu mabwe, wose, wakafwa, hamba yunji kusigala yaye.

13 Akuno kono nyuma nako, wasilikale wawalya we Ciisilayeli, wono Amaziya yawalema walece kubitana nayo ku wulugu, nawo wakabita wakagahoma makaya ga Yuda haciligati ya Samaliya na Bete-holoni, wakawulaga wanhu elufu yidatu, wakaviteka ne vinhu vinji muno.

14 Nayo Amaziya lono yawuyaga ku wulawa kuwahoma no kuwasuma Waedomu, yakasoleleza ne vihwani vyawo, yakavisimya, yakavihemba, yakawa yakuvifusiza no wuvumba.
15 Ne nghani yiyi, yikamukoza muno likuwo Muwaha Mulungu. Yakawa kumutuma muwizi kuli Amaziya. Nayo muwizi yakamutya Amaziya, “Alu 'gwe Amaziya, ho kosuma nhawule kuyitumbila milungu ye wanhu wanji, yono si yosuma hamba kuwaponya wanhu wayo, mu vilungo vyako?”

16 Nayo Amaziya yakapitula musunho, yakatya, “Mwene 'gwe, ku wulawa na nili cikakuwika wuwe mulonjelezi wo mutemi? Holeka wunyamale lulu baha. Na hono wujendelele, ane nokuwulaga!” Nayo muwizi yakatya, “Ale hodu'ne nanyamala. Ninga manyiye lukulu vyono Mulungu yakondya kudumula nghani, yakongomeze, ku soko ya zizo zono witumile, na vyono kanyhi, kuyibedegala ne nghani yangu.”

U wihomi ne Waisilayeli

(2Wat 14:8-20)

17 U mutemi Amaziya wa Yuda, hamonga ne walonjelezi wakwe, wakelumbaniza wabite wakayihome yisi ya Isilayeli. Na ku soko yiyo yakatuma wanhu wabite kuli Yehowashi u mutemi wa Isilayeli, u mwana wa Yehoyahazi, wabite wakamusagale ne nyono, ho wehome.
18 Nayo Yehowashi yakamupitula Amaziya yakatya, “Mwi'zuwa linji, i cibici co mufuku, cono cali cili mu matunda ga Lebanoni, cawulonjela mubici wo muhagata, cikatya, ‘Wumhelaje mwanago we cicekulu, yatolwe no mwanangu.’ Ninga lilimu likali likakola baho, likacibenanjiza cibici co mufuku.
19 Mwene Amaziya, alu wedunhumula, vyono wawona wawasuma Waedomu hongo. Ane nghuwona vyono, ciba wulece kwikumya, wucehe du ha kaya yako. Alu 'gwe nhawule wukusaka wiyambuze bwete, sunga wuwaletele wanhu wako wongomele wuwaha?”

20 Ninga mwene Amaziya, yakalemela lukulu kuzihuliciza zizo. Hambi nha wali wo wuhile wo Mulungu, Amaziya yasumwe, vyono yali yakondya kwiyinjiza mu wuhemba vihwani vye Waedomu.
21 Nayo Yehowashi u mutemi wa Isilayeli, yakawa kwinjila mu wihomi na Amaziya mutemi wa Yuda. Waketang'anilila ha Bete-shemeshi, mu yisi ya Yuda.
22 Ninga i wasilikale wa Yuda wakasumwa, wakawa kutiziza ku zikaya zawo.
23 Yehowashi, yakamuteka Amaziya, yakamutegula ku Yelusalemi. Nayo Yehowashi, yali yeza kufika ku Yelusalemi, yakagakumulanga majinji ge likaya, ku wulawilila ku mulango wa Efulayimu, sunga ko ku mulango wa ha cipeto, wugazi wa kotya mita magana mejete nhavi.
24 Yakazisola ne zizahabu zose, ne zifweza za mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, ne viya vyose vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, vyono vyali vili mu wulanjilizi we wana wa Obedi Edomu, ne viwutwa vyose vya mu nyumba yo wutemi, yakavisola vyose hamonga ne wanhu wose wono yawateka.

25 Nayo Amaziya mutemi wa Yuda, yajendelela kuwumipa myaka yikumi na mihano, lono Yehowashi u mutemi wa Isilayeli yali yakondya kufwa.

26 Ne zinghani zose zono Amaziya yetuma ku wulawilila mwamulizo, sunga ku ndumo yo wutemi wakwe, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce Zinghomo ze watemi wa Yuda na Isilayeli.
27 Ninga ku wulawa bahalya, lono yamubezeza Muwaha Mulungu, hodu wakamunozeza mvivi mu Yelusalemi. Sunga nayo yakawa kutiziza kwi'kaya lya Lakishi. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, i walugu wakwe, wakamulonda wakamuwulajila kuko.
28 Langa muwili wakwe wakawudinghulila mu fwalasi, wakawuleta ku Yelusalemi. Wakamuzika mu vitenjelo vye watemi, mwi'kaya lya Dawudi.
