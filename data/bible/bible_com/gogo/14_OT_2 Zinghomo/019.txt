2 Zinghomo 19

U muwizi yakumukalipila Yehoshafati

1 Yehoshafati u mutemi wa Yuda, yakawuya ku kaya yakwe yo wutemi ku Yelusalemi yapolile.
2 Nayo muwizi Yehu u mwana wa Hanani, yakawa kubita kumuhinjiliza mutemi, yakamulonjela yakatya, “Alu wukwihwesa, vyono viswanu kuwataza i wabi, no kuwenda wono wamusulile Muwaha Mulungu? Holeka zizo zono witumile zo zilecile Muwaha Mulungu kakukolelwa likuwo.
3 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, zinji ziswanu, zikali zili muli'gwe. Vyono waviwusa vihwani vyose vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela mu yisi yose, na kanyhi vyono wukali wujendeleye kuwutyatya wuhile wo Mulungu.”

Wunozeleze wa Yehoshafati

4 U mutemi Yehoshafati, hamba yawe yekalaga mu Yelusalemi, ninga yawajendelaga wanhu wakwe kali kenji, ku wulawilila ku Belisheba ku lubavu lwa kutakama, sunga ko ku ndumilizo ye yisi ye matunda ya Efulayimu, ku lubavu lwa kusumuka. Ho yasume kuwakwega wanhu wamuwuyile u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo.
5 Nayo yakawika mahakimu mu makaya gose ge magomawakali, mu yisi yose ya Yuda,
6 yakawalonjela wawo mahakimu yakawatya, “Ale, mulanjilile mu wuling'anizi wenyu, vyono anye si mukumwitumila munhu, langa mukumwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu, nayo yali baha hamonga nanye mu wuling'anizi wenyu.
7 Holeka munojeye mumogope Muwaha Mulungu, mulanjilile mu witume wenyu, hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, nha si kawukangalilaga wungwele, hamba wuhijila mbejele kuli munhu, hamba wubocela mafupo.”

8 Na mu Yelusalemi namo, mutemi Yehoshafati, yakawika Walawi ne Wakohani, hamonga ne wawaha wa mu zi ndelwa wa mu zimhuga, mu zimhuga zawo, wawe waling'anizi wa mu vyalo, kuli zo wubena Lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, hamba muli zinghani zono wakwisoka, i wekazi wa mwi'kaya.
9 Nawo yakawalonjela yakawatya, “Nanye munojeye mwitume milimo yenyu kwe cogopo hali Muwaha Mulungu, mweze kumuhulika mwene ko wihuwilwa muli zose zono mowa kwituma.
10 Na hono wandugu zenyu wono wekaye mu makaya ganji ganji, wamuzizenyi wena vyalo vyo wuwulaji, hamba nghani yo yose yo wubena wulajilize, hamba zo wubena Lilajilizo, ale, anye muwalolosezaje mugolile, sunga wezuce viswanu vyono wosuma kucinoza calo cico, mwi'daha lyo wuling'anizwe. Ho walece kuwalilwa masutya go kumunozeza wubi Muwaha Mulungu. Na hono mulece kunoza nhavivyo, ale, nyenye, hamonga ne wandugu zenyu, wose nyenye, likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, lyomuzizenyi. Ninga hono mwitume viswanu milimo yenyu, hamba si mowa wasutyi yaye.
11 Nayo Amaliya u Mukohani Muwaha, kowa yo mudumula nghani ye ndumilizo mu vyalo vyose vye ci dini. Nayo Zebadiya u mwana wa Ishumayeli u mulongola wa wo mulango wa Yuda, nayo kowa yo mudumula nghani ye ndumilizo, mu vyalo vyose vye selekali. Nawo Walawi, wowa wo walanjilizi we zinghani zose, zono zikuling'anizwa mu mahakama, hono ziwe zikudumulwa muling'ano. Holeka mwisindamikaje, muzikamise zizi zono nimulonjelenyi. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yawe yali hamonga nanye mu wituma ziswanu.”
