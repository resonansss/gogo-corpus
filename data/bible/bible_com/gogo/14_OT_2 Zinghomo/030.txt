2 Zinghomo 30

Wuwikanize so Pasaka

1 I wanhu si wasuma kuyinoza Sikukuu ye Pasaka, mu vipango vyono vyali vinojeye, nha cinji, mu mwezi mulongozi, vyono wakohani wali wakali si neyeza wiyeze wono wukwiliye. Kanyhi ne wanhu wenji wali wakali nehanguliza ku Yelusalemi.
2 Holeka mutemi Hezekiya, ne wetumami wakwe, hamonga ne wanhu wa Yelusalemi, wakelumbaniza vyono wayinoze sikukuu yiyo mu mwezi we kejete.
3 Nayo mutemi yakawa kuwalalika wanhu wose wa Isilayeli na Yuda. Ninga wo mulango wa Efulayimu na wa Manase, u mutemi yakawona ciba yawandicile baluwa, yawalalice nawo waze ku Nyumba yo Mulungu, ku Yelusalemi, ho wayinoze sikukuu ye Pasaka hamonga nawo, wamuhembe Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli.
4 Mutemi hamonga ne wanhu wakwe, wakawona vyono, lihweso lyawo lilyo yiswanu.
5 Holeka wakawa kuwalalika Waisilayeli wose ku wulawilila Dani, ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, sunga ko ku Belisheba ku lubavu lwa kutakama, waze wose ku Yelusalemi wayinoze wose sikukuu ye Pasaka, nha vyono winha Wulajilize.
6 Nawo wanhu wono watumwa no mutemi hamonga ne wetumami wakwe, wakawa kutuluka kubita ku wulalici, mu yisi yose ya Yuda na Yelusalemi. Wakatumwa wakawalonjele wanhu vyono mutemi yakutya, “Nye nyo wanhu wa Isilayeli, mono mwapona mu miwoko ye Waashuli, wono wayisuma yisi. Aluno mumuwuyile Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Abulahamu, na Isaka na Yakobo, nayo komuwuyilenyi.
7 Mulece kwiga mikalile ye walezi wenyu, hamba ye wandugu zenyu Waisilayeli, wono wali si wehuwilwa kuli Muwaha Mulungu u Mulungu wawo. Ninga nanye mukuwona nha vyono yawalipiciza ko wukali muno.
8 Holeka nanye mulece kuwa musina cihuliko, nha vyono wene wali wenha, langa anye muweze ne cihuliko kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Holeka luno, wose muze ku Nyumba yo Mulungu ku Yelusalemi, i hanhu hono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yaheza cibitilila, muze mumuhembe, nayo ho yalece kanyhi kumukolelwenyi likuwo.
9 Nanye hono mumuwuyile Muwaha Mulungu, na wa walya nawo wono wawasola wandugu zenyu no kuwategula kutali nha wafungwa, nawo wowawonela lusungu, wawalecele wawuye ku zikwawo. Hambi u Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, nha yena lihewa no lusungu. Holeka hono mumuwuyile, nayo komubocelenyi.”

10 Nawo wanhu wono watumwa, wakawa kutuluka wakawa kubita mu makaya gose ge yisi ya wo mulango wa Efulayimu na wa Manase, wakafika sunga ko ku sumuka ku wo mulango wa Zabuloni. Ninga wanhu wakawa kuwasekanga, no kuwazwajiza.
11 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, hali ho wali wanji wa mu mulango wa Asheli na wa Manase, hamonga na wo mulango wa Zabuloni, wono wakenda kuza ku Yelusalemi.
12 Nayo Mulungu yakawapela Wayuda wose i nhumbula ye cihuliko kuli malajilizo go mutemi ne wawaha wakwe mu nghango yo wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu.

Pasaka yikunozwa

13 Ne wanhu wenji muno, wakekongholela mu Yelusalemi, mu mwezi we kejete, ho wayinoze sikukuu yo mukate wono wusina cisase.
14 Wakawa kuvidinghula vikumbicilo vyose vyono vyetumamilwaga mu Yelusalemi so wulavizya makumbiko, no wufusa wuvumba, wakawa kuvileta mwi'lolo lya Kidiloni.
15 Na mwizuwa lye likumi na yine, lyo mwezi we kejete, wakawulaga nyana ye ngholo, ye Pasaka. Nawo wakohani ne Walawi, wono wali wakali neyeza, wakakolwa ne cevu ciwaha, wakawa kwiyeza, nhondo ho wakawa kulavya vilavywa vinyanywe mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
16 Waketuma milimo yawo mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, nha vyono winha wulajilize, wono wuli mwi Lajilizo lya Musa, u munhu wo Mulungu. I Walawi, wakayisola sakami ye makumbiko, wakawapela wakohani, nawo wakohani wakayimiza mu cikumbicilo.
17 Na vyono wanhu wenji wali si weyezize, holeka Walawi wakaziwulaga zinyana ze zingholo ze Pasaka, ho waweze kuli Muwaha Mulungu, wawo wono wali wakali neyeza.
18 Hamonga na zizo, i wanhu wose wono wakaza ku wulawa mu mulango wa Efulayimu, na wa Manase, na wa Isakali, na wa Zabuloni, wali wakali hamba neyeza, ninga wakayilya Pasaka nha vyono si yandikwa yaye. Nayo mutemi Hezekiya, yakawalombela kuli Mulungu, yakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,
19 go Mulungu we walezi wetu, ko wuswanu wako, nghukulomba wuwanenele wose wono wali baha, wono wakukuhemba kwe zinhumbula zawo zose, hamba wawe si weyezize ng'o.”
20 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawuvumila wulombe wa Hezekiya, yakawa kuwanenela wanhu, hamba si yawatamisa yaye.
21 Ne wanhu wose, wono waza baho ha Yelusalemi, ho wayinoze sikukuu yo mukate wono wusina cisase, mu vilo mupunghati, wali wena nyemo mbaha muno. Nawo Walawi hamonga ne wakohani, wembaga kwe vilungo vyawo vyose, wakumulumba Muwaha Mulungu.
22 Nayo mutemi Hezekiya, yakawalumba muno Walawi so wuhidimbazi wawo wuwaha, mu wulongoza wuhembi wo Muwaha Mulungu.

Wunoze we Sikukuu lwe kejete

Vyali vyeza kupuluta vilo mupunghati, vyono walavyaga makumbiko, wakumulumba Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo,
23 wakawona viswanu wonjeleze kanyhi vilo vinji mupunghati, wajendelele kuyinoza sikukuu. Holeka wakajendelela kuyinoza na nyemo mbaha.
24 Mutemi Hezekiya yakalavya ng'ombe elufu monga, hamonga na ngholo elufu mupunghati, ho wanhu walye. Wetumami wo mutemi, nawo wakalavya ng'ombe elufu monga nhavivilya, hamonga na ngholo elufu yikumi. Ne wakohani wenji wakeyeza.
25 I wanhu wa Yuda, ne wakohani, ne Walawi, ne wanhu wono walawa kusumuka, hamonga ne wajenzi, wono wali wahamiye mu Yuda na mu Yelusalemi, wose wakawa na nyemo mbaha muno.
26 Ne likaya lyose lya Yelusalemi, likamema nyemo mbaha muno, vyono zono zinha zizi, zali zikali nejela yaye, ku wulawa mu vilo vyo mutemi Solomona, u mwana wa Dawudi.
27 Nawo wakohani ne Walawi, wakawa kuwalombela wanhu u wumoteswe wa kuli Mulungu. Nayo Mulungu wulombe wawo kuko kono yekaye ku wulanga, no kuwuvumila.
