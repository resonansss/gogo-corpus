2 Zinghomo 15

Wunozeleze wa Asa

1 Lulo u Muhe wo Mulungu, wukamuziza Azaliya u mwana wa Obedi.
2 Nayo yakawa kubita kwitang'anila no mutemi Asa. Yakawa kumulonjela yakutya, “Gwe go mutemi Asa, hamonga nanye mo wanhu wa Yuda na Benyamini, ta muhulicizaje! U Muwaha Mulungu yali hamonga nanye, lono nanye mukali mukumuhulika mwene. Na hono mujendelele kumulondola, nayo kokwijela hali'nye, ninga hono mumulece, nayo komulecenyi.
3 Hambi nha wulawa vilo vinji, Waisilayeli wali wekaye wasina Mulungu we mbeka, hamba Wakohani, wono wali wawejize, kanyhi wali wasina hamba wulajilize, wono wali wawutyatye.
4 Ninga wali weza kwinjila mu magazo, hobaho wakamuwuyila Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli. Wakamulondola, wakamuwona.
5 Na mu vilo vivyo, hali hasina munhu mono yalawilaga, hamba mono yenjilaga ko mulamu, hambi nha hali hasina mulamu. Hakawa na mahwele mawaha, muli wanhu wose wono wekaye mu'si zose.
6 Wakawa wakwiyihoma weneco wose, nghungugo na nghungugo, yikaya ne'kaya, hambi u Mulungu nha yo yawapela magayo gago gose.
7 Ninga anye mwisindamice, mulece kogopa. Vyono u mulimo wono mukuwituma, nha wowa na cihumbilizwa.”

8 Mutemi Asa, yali yeza kuzihulika zo muwizi Azaliya u mwana wa Obedi, zono yazilonjile, yakapegwa vilungo. Yakaviwusiza lukulu vihwani vyose vye mawipizo mu yisi yose ya Yuda na Benyamini, na mu makaya gose gono yagasola, ga mu yisi ye matunda ya Efulayimu. Yakacinozeleza kanyhi ne cikumbicilo co Muwaha Mulungu cono cali cili ha mwande ye likumbo lye Nyumba yo Mulungu.

9 Ne wanhu wenji wono walawa ku yisi ya Efulayimu, na Manase, na Simiyoni, hamonga ne wajenzi wose wono wekalaga mu wutemi wakwe, wali weza kuwona nha vyono Asa yali hamonga no Muwaha Mulungu, hobaho wose wakawa kumuziza Asa. Nayo Asa yakawakonghola wose, hamonga ne wanhu wa Yuda na Benyamini.
10 Wakekongholela ku Yelusalemi mu mwezi we kadatu wa mu mwaka we likumi ne'hano wo wutemi wa Asa.
11 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo, wakalavya makumbiko mu wulawa mu vinhu vyono waviteka ku wulugu, ng'ombe magana mupunghati, na ngholo elufu mupunghati.
12 Wakanoza cilagane, nha vyono womuhemba Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo, kwe zinhumbula zawo zose.
13 No munhu wo wose mudodo hamba muwaha, we cilume hamba wecicekulu, mono si komuhemba Muwaha Mulungu, ayo yawulagwe.
14 Nawo wakamwilahiza Muwaha Mulungu kwe lizi yiwaha muno, nha vyono wocibata cilagane. Hanyuma wakazoza muno akuno wakutowa ne zindulele.
15 Ne wanhu wose wa Yuda, wakawa no wusangalale muno, vyono wacinoza cilagane cico kwe zinhumbula zawo zose. Wakamuhemba Muwaha Mulungu ko wendezwe wawo wose, nayo yakawavumila, yakawapela mulamu mbavu zose.

16 Mutemi Asa, yakawa kumuwusiza lilumbo u nyina yakwe Maaka, lyo kucemwa u nyina yo mutemi, ku soko yanoza cihwani ce liwifyo co mulungu we cicekulu Ashela. Asa yakacidumula cihwani cico, yakacipondanjizanga, yakacikongoga mu moto mwi'lolo lya Kidiloni.
17 Asa hamba yawe si yahongomezeza lukulu hanhu hose ho wuhembelo we milungu yinji mu yisi yose, ninga mwene, yali vyo mwihuwilwa du kuli Muwaha Mulungu, mu mikalile yakwe yose.
18 Asa yavigomoleza kanyhi vinhu vyose vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, vyono u sogwe Abiya, yali yavisiwanuye kuli Mulungu, hamonga ne viya vye zahabu, na vye fweza, vyono mweneco nayo yavisiwanula.
19 Na kwali kusina milewo kanyhi, sunga mwaka we makumi madatu ne'hano wo wuteme wakwe Asa.
