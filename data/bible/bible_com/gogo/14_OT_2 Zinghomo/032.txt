2 Zinghomo 32

Waashuli wakuyogofya Yelusalemi

(2Wat 18:13-37; 19:14-19, 35-37; Isa 36:1-22; 37:8-38)

1 Zali zeza kupuluta zizo, zo mutemi Hezekiya vyono yamwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu ko wihuwilwa muno, hobaho Senakelibu, u mutemi wa Ashuli, yakawa kuza kuyihoma Yuda. Yakagapilima i makaya gose ge magomawakali, yakawalajiliza wasilikale wakwe watobole milango mu majinji, wakole wenjile mugati ye makaya.
2 Ninga nayo Hezekiya yali yeza kumanya nha vyono Senakelibu yena wuhile wo kuyihoma Yelusalemi nhavivilya,
3 hobaho mwene hamonga ne wetumami wakwe, wakawona vyono yowa viswanu wagemile malenga ge mivwilo yono yali kunze ye likaya, Waashuli ho walece kutuga malenga go gose kolo wakulisejelela likaya lya Yelusalemi.
4 Hodu wakawa kwikonghola wanhu wenji muno, wakawa kuyimila mivwilo yose ye malenga, hakawa hasina kanyhi malenga go gose gono gahulumaga mu mivwilo yiyo.
5 Mutemi yakalinoza likaya liwe no wulindilizi wono wukamile, yakaganozeleza majinji gono gali gamwaguce, no kuzenga minala mucanyha yayo. Kanyhi yakazenga ne lijinji lye kejete yakalipilimya kunze yayo. Hamonga na zizo, yakaganozeleza ne magomawakali gono gali mazenje mu yisi yose, ga ku lubavu lwa kucilima ya Yelusalemi. Kanyhi yakawa yena migoha minji muno hamonga ne zingula.
6 Yakawawika wasilikale vitung'ati mwi'kaya, wakawa wali hasi yo wulanjilizi we wetumami wawaha we wasilikale. Yakawa kulajiliza wose wekongholele ha wuhihi, ha mulango we likaya. Hanyuma yakawa kuwalonjela yakatya,
7 “Mweze ne vilungo, ne nhumbula yo wutung'ati. Mulece kumogopa mutemi we Waashuli, hamba lipuga lyakwe lye wasilikale lyono yena nalyo. Hambi ase ku lubavu lwetu, nha cina vilungo muno cimucizize mwene.
8 Hambi mwene nha yetumbiye vilungo vye cimunhu-munhu, ninga ase, cina Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, mono kocipela wutazi no kucilewela mweneco mu milewo yetu.” Nawo wanhu wakasikamywa muno muno ne zinghani zo mutemi.

9 Hanyuma kanyhi, Senakelibu lono yali yali ku Lakishi, yakatuma wanhu wabite kuli mutemi Hezekiya na kuli wanhu wose wa Yuda, wono wali wali hamonga na Hezekiya mu Yelusalemi, wakawalonjele watye nhavi,
10 “Ane yo nene Senakelibu u mutemi wa Ashuli, nghuwuza, cicici cono cikumupelenyi vilungo, anye nyo wanhu ho mujendelele kwikala mu Yelusalemi mupilimwe ne magomawakali.
11 Anye hongo mwazitumbila zono Hezekiya yakumutyenyi, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu komuponyenyi mu miwoko yetu. Ninga mumanye nha vyono, Hezekiya yakumukonjenyi bwete, komwambuzenyi bwete mufwe ne nzala ne nyhilu ye malenga.
12 Yuyu Hezekiya, yo yuyo mono yahahodanga i hanhu hose ho wuhembelo, hamonga ne vikumbicilo vyayo, yakawa kuwalonjela i wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, vyono yiwanojeye wahembe no kufusa wuvumba hanhu hamonga du.
13 Kanyhi anye, si mumanyile lo, i walezi wangu na nene, vyono cawanoza wanhu we zinghungugo zinji? Wulenyi, hali ho yili hamba milungu yinji yo yose, yono yawaponya i wanhu wayo mu vilungo vyo mutemi wa Ashuli?
14 Mukwihwesa ho gali mapango ganji go gose, lono milungu yiyo ye ma'si gago, yasuma kuziponya zi'si zizo mu miwoko yetu? Alu cono cikumupelenyi witumbilo, nha vyono yuyo Mulungu wenyu, kosuma kumuponyenyi, cicici?
15 Holeka yuyo Hezekiya, yalece kumukonjenyi no kumwajilizenyi bwete. Mulecele lukulu kumwitumbila! Hambi nha hakali nejela yaye, mulungu we nghungugo yo yose, mono yasuma kuwaponya wanhu wakwe, mu wulawa mu miwoko yo mutemi wo wose wa Ashuli, ku wulawa wulawo. Holeka nanye u Mulungu wenyu, hamba si kosuma kumuponyenyi yaye.”

16 I wetumami we Ciashuli wakacila kulonga makani mabi kuli Muwaha Mulungu, sunga na kuli Hezekiya u mwitumami wo Muwaha Mulungu.
17 Nayo mutemi wa Ashuli, yakandika baluwa, yono yali yina zinghani zo kumutovula Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli. Ne baluwa yiyo yali yilonjile yikutya, “I milungu ye makungugo gose, si yawaponya wanhu wayo mu miwoko yangu, holeka hamba no Mulungu wa Hezekiya nayo, sikosuma kuwaponya yaye wanhu wakwe mu miwoko yangu.”

18 Nawo wetumami wo mutemi wa Ashuli, wakawa kuzanza zinghani zizo wazozize muno muno, wakulonga mu nonga ye Cihebelaniya, ho wawogofye no kuwapela mahwele, i wanhu wa Yelusalemi, wono wali wali mucanyha ye majinji ge likaya.
19 Wakawa kumulonga Mulungu wa Yelusalemi mu nzila yi yilya du yono wayilongaga milungu ye makungugo ganji, i milungu ye vihwani vyono vinozigwe ne miwoko ye wanhu.

20 Ninga mwene mutemi Hezekiya, hamonga no muwizi Isaya u mwana wa Amozi, wakamulomba Mulungu no kumulilila yawapele wutazi.
21 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawuhulika wulombe wawo, yakawa kumutuma malayika wakwe, yakabita yakawa kuwawulaga wasilikale wose we Ciashuli, hamonga ne walongola wawaha wose we lipuga lye wasilikale. Hodu, u mutemi wa Ashuli, yakawa kuwuya ku Ashuli ne cevu ciwaha. Na mwi'zuwa linji, lono yali yali mugati mu wuhembelo we milungu yakwe, i wanagwe we cilume wakenjila, wakawa kumuwulaga ne mimaje yawo.

22 Nhavivyo vyo Muwaha Mulungu yamuponezya mutemi Hezekiya, hamonga ne wanhu wa Yelusalemi, mu miwoko ya Senakelibu u mutemi wa Ashuli, sunga na muli walugu wawo wanji. Yakawaleka wanhu wekale welamuye ne wakalani wayawo wose we zi'si zinji.
23 Ne wanhu wenji, wakajendelela kuza ku Yelusalemi kuleta vilavywa vyawo kuli Muwaha Mulungu, nayo Hezekiya wakamuletela vipegwa. Na ku wulawilila baho, zinghungogo zose zikamupela cogopo Hezekiya u mutemi wa Yuda.

Wutamwa wa Hezekiya hamonga no wikumyi wakwe

(2Wat 20:1-3, 12-19; Isa 38:1-3; 39:1-8)

24 Na mu vilo vivyo, Hezekiya yakatamwa, yakawa yali behi no kufwa. Yakamulomba Mulungu, nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakamulajila citanjilo nha vyono kopona mu wutamwa wakwe.
25 Ninga mwene Hezekiya yakamema wikumyi mu nhumbula yakwe, hamba kulajila lilumbo kuli Muwaha Mulungu muli zose zono Mulungu yamutendeye. Na ku soko yiyo, Muwaha Mulungu yakamukolelwa likuwo, hamonga ne wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi.
26 Ninga ku ndumilizo yayo, Hezekiya hamonga ne wanhu wa Yelusalemi, wakenenenyheza hali Mulungu, holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu, hodu, si yawalipiciza wanhu yaye, sunga Hezekiya yakafwa.

U wugoli wa Hezekiya, hamonga no lukumyo lwakwe

27 Nayo mutemi Hezekiya yakawa na sawo nyinji muno, kanyhi yakawa ne lilumbo yiwaha. Yakawa yena zikaya zo kuwicila zahabu, fweza, vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu, na vye mhunjile swanu, ne zingula, hamonga ne vinhu vinji muno vyo wuguzi wukamu.
28 Kanyhi hamba nhavivyo, yakawa yena kaya zinji zono yawikaga wuhemba, ne divayi, ne mafuta. Kanyhi ne mabululu ge zing'ombe zakwe, ne makumbo ge zingholo.
29 Hamonga na vivyo vyose, Mulungu yakamupela matewa mawaha ge zingholo, ne zing'ombe, hamonga na sawo zinji, kanyhi nyinji muno muno. Holeka nayo yakasuma kuzenga makaya menji muno.
30 Kanyhi yo yuyu Hezekiya, mono yawudinda muvwilo we malenga wa kucanyha wa Gihoni, yakaganozeza malenga nzila yono yikolile hasi kwa hasi, sunga gakenjila mu Yelusalemi. Nayo mutemi Hezekiya yakamoteswa muli zose zono yetuma.
31 Ninga lono watemi wa Babiloni, watumila wanhu waze kuli Hezekiya, wamuwuze ze vitanjilo vyo wizine, vyono vyejela mu'si, baho langa Mulungu yakamuleka Hezekiya yetume nha vyono kowona mweneco, ho yamumanye zono zili mu nhumbula yakwe.

Ndumilizo yo wutemi wa Hezekiya

(2Wat 20:20-21)

32 Ne zinghani zose zono mutemi Hezekiya yetuma, hamonga no wuhidimbazi wakwe muli zo wuhembi wo kumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu, zose zandicigwe mu citabu co wugubulilwe wo muwizi Isaya, u mwana wa Amozi, hamonga na mu citabu ce Zinghomo ze watemi wa Yuda na Isilayeli.
33 Hezekiya yakafwa, yakazikwa ha kacanyha ye vitenjelo vye watemi. Ne wanhu wose wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, wakamunozeza lilumbo yiwaha muno, lono yali yakondya kufwa. Nayo Manase u mwanagwe, yakahala wutemi wakwe.
