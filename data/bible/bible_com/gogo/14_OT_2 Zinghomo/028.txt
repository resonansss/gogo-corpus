2 Zinghomo 28

Ahazi mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 16:1-4)

1 Ahazi yakatema lono yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete cititila. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka yikumi na mutandatu. Ninga nayo hamba si yatyatya musambo muswanu wo kukuye Dawudi. Mwene yetuma zono si zamwendeza Muwaha Mulungu.
2 Mwene yatyatya i metumo mabi ge watemi wa Isilayeli. Hambi nha yali yena vihwani vye shaba vya Baali.
3 Yakawa yakufusa wuvumba mwi'lolo lyo mwana wa Hinomu, hamonga no kuwalavya wanagwe, wawe makumbiko ge cilavywa cinyanywe kuli vihwani. Yakawa yakwiga makani ge cevu, gono wetumaga wanhu wono Muwaha Mulungu yawawinga mu yisi, lono Waisilayeli wenjilaga.
4 Yakawa yakulavya makumbiko no kufusa wuvumba, sunga na mu wuhembelo wa wono si wamumanyile Mulungu, mu matunda, na hasi ye mibeho ye mibici yono yina mahamha gono gadedewaye.

Wulugu we Wasiliya ne Waisilayeli

(2Wat 16:5)

5 Alu vyono mutemi Ahazi yetuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe, holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamuleka yasumwe no mutemi wa Siliya. Nayo mutemi wa Siliya, yali yeza kumusuma Ahazi, yakawa kuwasola ne wanhu wa Yuda wenji muno, yakawategula ku Damesiki nha wafungwa.
6 Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu, yakamuleka Peka u mwana wa Lemaliya, yabite yakamuhome Ahazi, no kuwawulaga mwi'zuwa limonga, wasilikale vitung'ati wa Yuda, waficile elufu yigana limonga na makumi mejete. Na zizi zaletwa no Muwaha Mulungu, vyono wene i wanhu wa Yuda, wali wakondya kumuleka Mulungu.
7 Nayo musilikale yunji we Ciisilayeli, litagwa lyakwe yacemwaga Zikili, yakamuwulaga Maseya u mwana wo mutemi Ahazi, hamonga na Azilikamu u mululicizi we kaya yo wutemi, hamonga na Elikana mono yali yo we kejete ko mutemi Ahazi.
8 Ninga hamba yiwe wawo wa Yuda wali wandugu zawo, ninga Waisilayeli wakawasola wacekulu wono wali waficile elufu magana mejete cititila, hamonga ne wana wose, wakasolwa nha wafungwa. Wakawagomola ku Samaliya, hamonga ne vinhu vinji muno vyono waviteka.

U muwizi Obedi

9 Munhu yunji litagwa lyakwe Obedi, u muwizi wo Muwaha Mulungu, munhu yuyu, yekalaga mwi'kaya lya Samaliya. Yuyu Obedi, viketang'anila ne lipuga lye wasilikale we Ciisilayeli behi ne likaya, wakuwuya, wena ne wafungwa wawo we Ciyuda, yakawalonjela yakatya, “Lanjenyi ta! U Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wenyu, yali yawakolelwa likuwo wanhu wa Yuda, na holeka yamulecenyi 'nye mubite muwahome, muwasume. Ninga aluno, Muwaha Mulungu kakondya kuhulika vyono muwawulaganga mu nzila 'mbi muno muno.
10 Sunga nalu kanyhi, vyo mukali du, mukusaka muwanoze i wanhu wa Yelusalemi na Yuda, i walume ne wacekulu, wawe wawanda wenyu. Alu si mukumanya nha vyono sunga na nyenye mwakondya kwituma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu lo?
11 Alu muhulicize ta! Mumanye vyono, wafungwa wawa wo wandugu zenyu ne walumbu zenyu. Holeka ciba muwalecele wabite. Na hono mulece kuwalecela, ale, Muwaha Mulungu komulipicizizenyi mwi'kuwo lyakwe.”

12 Na mwipuga lyawo, mwali na wanhu wane, wono wali wo walongola wawo, wono walawa ku wutemi wa ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, yali, Azaliya u mwana wa Yehohanani, na Belekiya u mwana wa Meshilemoti, na Yehizikiya u mwana wa Shalumu, hamonga na Amasa u mwana wa Hadilayi, nawo wawa si walivumila litumo lye Wasilikale lyo kuwasola wafungwa.
13 Holeka nawo wakawalonjela wasilikale wawo, wakawatya, “Mulece kuwaleta wawo wafungwa akuno! Hambi ase mbeka nha cakondya kwituma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, sunga nalu cakondya kumukoza likuwo, nalu, yewikanize yacilipicize. Nalu kanyhi mukusaka mwitume nghani yinji yono yowonjeleza wubi wetu.”
14 Hodu lipuga lye wasilikale, likawa kuwakombola wafungwa wose kuli walongola wawo, hamonga ne vinhu vyose vyono wali waviteka.
15 Nawo wawalya i wanhu wane, wakawikwa wawagawile wafungwa, myenda yono yali yili mu vinhu vyono waviteka. Nawo wakawa kuwapela myenda, ne malatu, wasume kuvwala. Wakawapela ndigwa wakalya, wakang'wa. Wakawatajila ne mafuta mu zinghomele zawo. Na walya wono wali wasocela hamba si wakusuma no kujenda, wakawasola, wakawapaciza mu zindogowe. Hanyuma wakawa kuwagomola wafungwa wose ku yisi ya Yuda ku Yeliko, mwi'kaya lye mibici minji ye mitende. Hodu, Waisilayeli wali weza kuwafisa, wene wakawa kuwuya kwawo ku Samaliya.

Ahazi yakulondola wutazi kuli Waashuli

(2Wat 16:7-9)

16 Mu madaha gago, mutemi Ahazi, yakatuma wanhu kuli Tigilati Pilesa, u mutemi wa Shamu, ho yamutaze.
17 Hambi Waedomu, nha wali waza kanyhi kuwahoma Wayuda, no kusola wafungwa wenji.
18 Na mu madaha gago kanyhi, nawo Wafilisiti wali wenjila no kugahoma makaya ga ku lubavu lwa ku mwezi ga mu matunda, hamonga na ga mu yisi ya Yuda ku lubavu lwa kutakama. Nawo wali wakondya kugasola makaya ga Beti Shemeshi, na Ayijaloni, na Gedeloti, hamonga ne makaya ga Soko, na Timuna, na Gimuzo, hamonga ne vitumbi vyayo vyose. Nawo wakawa kuhamilila lukulu mumo.

19 Alu, vyono Ahazi mutemi wa Yuda, yali yakondya kuyihodanga ling'ani ye wanhu wakwe, na vyono kanyhi yali yakondya kulyavufya litagwa lyo Muwaha Mulugu, holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakawaletela Wayuda magazo mawaha.
20 Nayo mutemi wa Siliya, hamba si yamuletela Ahazi wutazi yaye, langa yamuletela magazo.
21 Nayo Ahazi yali yeza kuwona vyono mutemi Pigilati Pilesa yakumugaza, yakawa kuyisola zahabu yose yono yali yili mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, na mu nyumba yo wutemi, na mu zikaya ze walongola we wanhu, yakawa kumupela mutemi Pigilati Pilesa. Ninga vivyo vyose hamba si vyakwila cinji yaye.

U wubi wa Ahazi

22 Na lono magazo gakwe Ahazi gacilaga kuwa mabi muno muno, nayo Ahazi ho yakacila muno kwituma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu si nha halulongozi.
23 Yakalavya makumbiko kuli milungu ye Waashuli wono wamusuma. Hambi nha yatya, vyono “I milungu ye Waashuli, yiwataza watemi wa Siliya, holeka nane hono niyilavizye makumbiko, nayo yomhela wutazi nha vivilya.” Ne nghani yiyi, yikamuletela wongomezwe, mwene hamonga ne likungugo lyose lye Waisilayeli.
24 Hamonga na zizo, yakasola viya vyose vyono vyali mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakavibenanga vyose. Yakayidinda milango ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakawika vikumbicilo hanhu hose mu Yelusalemi.
25 Yakazenga hanhu ho wuhembelo we milungu yo wulambi. No wuvumba wukawa kufuswa kuli milungu yinji. Na muli zizo, yakeletela mweneco likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo.

26 Ne zinghani zose zono Ahazi yetuma, ku wulawa mwamulizo wo wutemi wakwe sunga ko ku ndumilizo, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce Zinghomo ze watemi wa Yuda na Isilayeli.
27 Mutemi Ahazi yakafwa, yakazikwa mu Yelusalemi. Ninga si wamuzika mu vitenjelo vye watemi. Nayo Hezekiya mwanagwe, yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.
