2 Zinghomo 1

Mutemi Solomona yakulomba mahala

(1Wat 3:1-15)

1 Solomona u mwana wo mutemi Dawudi, yakekangaza mu wutemi wakwe. No Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe, yakamumotya, no kumunoza yawe ne vilungo vinji mu wutemi wakwe.

2 Hanyuma Solomona yakawalonjela wetumami wose, wono wali walanjilizi we vipuga vye wasilikale elufu monga monga, na we ligana limonga limonga, hamonga ne walongola wose we selekali, hamonga ne wawaha wose we zimhuga, hamonga ne wanhu wose,
3 yakawalajiliza wabitane nayo hanhu ho kuhembela ku Gibeyoni. Wabita kuko, vyono ho hanhu hono hali holili Lihema lyono Lyelile, lyono Musa u mwitumami wo Muwaha Mulungu, yalinozeza mu nyika.
4 (Ninga Lisanduku lye Cilagane, lulo lyali lili ku Yelusalemi, liwicigwe mugati ye lihema lyono mutemi Dawudi yalikoma, lono yaliletela Lisanduku lye Cilagane, ku wulawa ku Kiliyati-yeyalimu.
5 Ne cikumbicilo ce shaba cono yacinoza Bezaleli, u mwana wa Uli, kanyhi yo mwizukulu wa Huli, naco cali cikali cili kuko ku Gibeyoni, cisimye ha mwande ye Lihema lyono Lyelile. Nayo mutemi Solomona, hamonga ne wanhu wose, wakamuhemba kuko Muwaha Mulungu.)
6 Mutemi Solomona, yakamulavizya Muwaha Mulungu makumbiko mu cikumbicilo cico ce shaba, cono cali ciwice baho ha mwande ye Lihema lyono Lyelile. Yakawulaga mitugo elufu monga, yakayilavya cilavywa cinyanywe, yakayikongojeza mucanyha ye cikumbicilo cico.

7 Na mu cilo cico, Mulungu yakamuziza Solomona, yakamutya, “Wulombe cono wukusaka nghupele.”

8 Nayo Solomona yakapitula yakamutya Mulungu, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wamunozeza sogo yangu Dawudi lusungu luwaha, nalu kunhemya 'ne, halile wutemi wakwe.
9 Holeka alu go Muwaha Mulungu, nghukulomba wufisilize wihuwizo wako wono wamwihuwiziza baba Dawudi. Hambi nha wanhemya niwe mutemi muli wanhu wawa wono wenji muno, hamba si wakuwala yaye.
10 Holeka ane, nghukulomba wumhele mahala no wizuce, wono wonghwila kunhaza, ho sume kuwatema wanhu wawa. Hono si nhavivyo, ane ho nosuma wule kuwatema wanhu wako wawa i wenji muno?”

11 Mulungu nayo yakamupitula Solomona yakamutya, “Heya, kulomba nghani swanu muno. Hambi nha si wulombile sawo, hamba wugoli, hamba nhojo, hamba lifwa kuli walugu wako, hamba siwisaciye mikalile ya vilo vinji. Langa kulomba mahala no wizuce, ho wusume kuwatema viswanu wanhu wangu, wono nghunozize agwe ho wuwe mutemi wawo.
12 Holeka nane lu, nghukupela mahala no wizuce. Ninga hamonga na zizo, ane nokupela sunga ne sawo, no wugoli, ne nhojo, kuciza vyono wali wena navyo i watemi wose wono wakulongolela, lono agwe wali wukali natema. Hamba wono wotema hanyuma yako, howa hasina hamba monga, mono kolowa yena vinhu vinha vivyo.”

Vilungo vyo mutemi Solomona hamonga no wugoli wakwe

(1Wat 10:26-29)

13 Hodu, nhondo, Solomona ho yakawa kuwuka i hanhu ho wuhembelo, kuko ku Gibeyoni, hono lyali likomigwe Lihema lyono Lyelile, yakawa kuwuya ku Yelusalemi.
14 Solomona yakakonghola matuka menji go wulugu, gaficile elufu monga na magana mane, hamonga ne wasilikale wapanda fwalasi, waficile elufu yikumi na nyejete. Yakawawika wanji mu Yelusalemi, na wono wasigala, yakawagawa, yakawawika mu makaya ganji hayo hayo.
15 Na mu vilo vyo wutemi wakwe, azi fweza hamonga ne zahabu, vyali vinhu vyono wanhu wali wavizowela muno mu Yelusalemi, ku soko yo winji muno wavyo, vikawa nha mabwe du. Ne mibici ye mihagata, nayo yikawa minji muno muno, ne wanhu nhondo wakayizowela nhe mibici yinji yose yono wayiwonaga nhondo zose.
16 Nawo wono wamuletelaga mutemi zizo zifwalasi, wene wazigulaga ku wulawa Misili na Kilikiya.
17 Nago matuka go wulugu ku wulawa ku Misili, gaguzwaga limonga fweza magana mutandatu, na fwalasi monga, yaguzwaga fweza yigana limonga na makumi mahano. Na wawo wono waziguzaga, wakawaguziza ne watemi wose we Wahiti, ne watemi wa Siliya.


2 Zinghomo 2

Wuwikanize wo wuzenji we Nyumba yo Mulungu

(1Wat 5:1-18)

1 Mutemi Solomona yakadumula nghani vyono, yanojeye yazenje Nyumba yo Mulungu, yono yowa ho hanhu hono wowa kumuhembela Muwaha Mulungu, na kanyhi na mweneco nayo, yezenjele ne likulu lyakwe.
2 Na hobaho du, yakawawika wanhu elufu makumi mupunghati so mulimo wo kuvidinghula no kuvileta vinhu vyo kuzenjela lizengo lilyo, na wanhu elufu makumi munana so mulimo wo kusesa mabwe. Hanyuma yakawawika wanhu elufu yidatu na magana mutandatu wawe wo walanjilizi we milimo yose.

3 Hanyuma Solomona yakawa kutuma wanhu kuli Hilamu, mutemi wa Tulo, wakamulonjele watye, “Nghukulomba, wunhendele nane nha vyono wamutendela baba Dawudi, vyono wamuguzizaga mabici go muhagata, so wuzenjela kaya yakwe ye likulu.
4 Alu nane nanza mulimo wo kuzenga Nyumba yo Mulungu wangu. Ne Nyumba yiyo, howa ho hanhu hono helile hono i wanhu wangu, hamonga na nene, cowa kumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, akuno cikufusa no wuvumba we mhunjile swanu, no kumulavizya vilavywa vyo mukate wono welile vilo vyose. Kanyhi ciwe kumulavizya sunga ne vilavywa vinyanywe nhondo zose, hakali mitondo ne cinyamihe. Sunga na mu vilo vyose vye Sabato, na mu zisikukuu ze ndawo yo mwezi, na mu vilo vinji vyose vyono vyelile, ciwe kumupela lilumbo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu. Hambi nha vyo yawalajiliza Waisilayeli, weze kunoza nhavivi cibitilila.
5 Holeka nane, nakondya kudumula nghani, nha vyono nzenje Nyumba yo Mulungu, yono yowa mbaha muno muno, hambi mwene Mulungu wetu, nha yo Muwaha, yacizize milungu yinji yo yose.
6 Hamba nhavivyo, hasina munhu mono kosuma kumuzenjela Mulungu Nyumba yono kosuma kwinjila, vyono sunga no wulanga, hamonga no mwanya wayo si vikumukwila yaye. Holeka 'ne waco nene nani 'mbi, ho sume kumuzenjela Mulungu Nyumba yono yowa swanu, langa, howa hanhu du, ho kufusiza wuvumba hali mwene.
7 Holeka nghukulomba wundetele munhu munghula, we milimo ye zahabu ne fweza ne shaba, ne vyuma, na mu wunoza myenda ye liwala lye buluu, ye liwala lyo lugandu, na ye liwala lidunghu. Kanyhi yasume kwitumana hamonga ne wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, wono baba Dawudi yawawika.
8 Nane nha manyile wunghula we wanhu wako vyono wenha. Holeka wundetelaje mabici go muhagata, ne mibeloshi, ne mabici go musandali, ku wulawa ku Lebanoni. Nane nakondya kwiwikaniza, nghuletele wanhu wangu, ho vitazilize ne wanhu wako,
9 ho wasume kuwikaniza mbawo nyinji muno, hambi nha nadumula nghani vyono yinojeye nzenje Nyumba yo Mulungu mbaha muno muno, kanyhi yono yowa yina lukumyo luwaha.
10 Na langa ta, ane nowapela wetumami wako, wuhemba, tani elufu nyejete, na shayili, tani elufu nyejete, na divayi, lita elufu magana mane, hamonga na mafuta, nago lita elufu magana mane.”

11 Ale, du! Nayo mutemi Hilamu, yakamwandicila baluwa Solomona, yakamupitula yakatya, “Gwe Solomona, Muwaha Mulungu vyono yawendile wanhu wakwe, holeka yakunoza agwe wuwe mutemi wawo.
12 Holeka, yalumbwe Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we wa Isilayeli, mono yawumba wulanga ne yinze! Hambi nha yamupela Dawudi mwana we mahala, mono yena wizuce wuwaha, kanyhi munghula, mono yakondya kudumula nghani yamuzenjele Muwaha Mulungu Nyumba yakwe.
13 Holeka nane aluno, nghukuletela munhu munghula muno, litagwa lyakwe Hulamu.
14 U nyina wa Hulamu, mwene yo mulawa mu mulango wa Dani. U sogwe nayo, munya Tulo. Mwene Hulamu, yo munghula muno mu milimo ye zahabu, na ye fweza, na ye shaba, na ye vyuma, na ye mabwe, sunga na ye mabici. Kanyhi mwene kosuma kwituma sunga ne milimo yo wunoza myenda ye liwala lye buluu, na ye liwala lyo lugandu, na ye liwala lidunghu. Kanyhi yo munhu mono kosuma kwituma milimo yose yo wulula, hambi nha kosuma kwiga cihwani co cose cono kolajilwa. Holeka wumulece vitumane ne wanhu wako i walula, hamonga ne wanhu wono wetumaga kuli sogo mutemi Dawudi.
15 Holeka aluno, heya, wuciletelaje vinhu vyono wehuwiza, wuhemba ne shayili, ne divayi, hamonga ne mafuta.
16 Nase cobita mwi'tunda lya Lebanoni, cikakutemele mabici go muhagata, mu nghango nha vyono gwegwe kosaka. Hanyuma cogawopeleza hamonga, cigojeze mwi'lenga, sunga ko ku Yopa. Nagwe kogasolela baho, wugategule ku Yelusalemi.”

Wuzenji we Nyumba yo Mulungu wukwanza

(1Wat 6:1-38)

17 Mutemi Solomona, yakawawala i wanhu wose wajenzi wono wekalaga mu Isilayeli, nha vyono sogwe Dawudi yanoza mu vilo vyakwe. Na wakejela wajenzi waficile wanhu elufu yigana limonga na makumi mahano na wadatu, na magana mutandatu.
18 Yakawasola wose, yakawika wanhu elufu makumi mupunghati, mu mulimo wo wudinghula vinhu vyo kuzenjela, no kuvileta. Na wanji elufu makumi munana, yakawawika mu mulimo wo wusesi we mabwe mu matunda. Na wanji kanyhi elufu yidatu na magana mutandatu, yakawawika wawe wo walanjilizi we milimo yose, i milimo ho yitumwe viswanu.


2 Zinghomo 3

1 Ninga mutemi Dawudi, u sogwe wa Solomona, yali yakondya kuhawikaniza i hanhu hono yozengwa yiyo Nyumba yo Mulungu. Ne hanhu heneco, hali ha Yelusalemi, he 'tunda lya Moliya, hono Muwaha Mulungu yelajilila kuli Dawudi, i hanhu hono Olinani u Muyebusi, yahetumamilaga nha cuga cakwe.
2 Mutemi Solomona yakalwanza luzengo lulo mu mwezi we kejete, wa mu mwaka we kane, wa mu wutemi wakwe.
3 Ne Nyumba yo Mulungu yono mutemi Solomona yayizenga, yali yina wutali wo mulandala wayo, wa mita makumi mejete na mupunghati, no wugazi wayo, wali mita yicenda,
4 Ne likumbo lyono lyali lili ku mwande yayo, mulandala walyo, wali winha wugazi we Nyumba, lyali lina mita yicenda, no wutali wo kubita kucanyha, wali mita makumi mahano na yine. Ne likumbo lilyo, lyali yihiliwe zahabu nengu mugati yalyo.
5 Ne nyumba mbaha, yakayifunika ne zimbawo zo muhagata, zono hiliwe ne zahabu nengu. Yakayihamba mitende ne minyolo.
6 Mutemi Solomona, yakayihamba Nyumba yo Mulungu ne vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu, hamonga ne zahabu yono yaletwa ku wulawa ku Palevayimu.
7 Yakayitumamila zahabu yiyo mu wuhiliwa majinji gose ge Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne micici yayo, ne zifulemu zayo, hamonga ne milango yayo. Na mu majinji, yakawika vihwani vye makelubi.
8 Cumba ca haciligati, cono cikucemwa i Hanhu Hono helile helile muno, cali na wutali wa mita yicenda, no wugazi wa mita yicenda. Nawo wali wo wugazi we Nyumba yo Mulungu. Yakasola zahabu ya wuzito wa tani makumi mejete, yakayitumamila so wuhiliwe we majinji gose ge Hanhu Hono Helile Helile, muno.
9 Kanyhi yakasola zahabu yo wuzito wa kilo magana mahano na makumi mupunghati, yakayitumamila so wutyana misumali. Ne majinji ge vyumba vya ku golofa, navyo vyose, vikahiliwa zahabu.

10 Kanyhi mutemi Solomona, yakawalajiliza wetumami wakwe wanoze vihwani vye makelubi mejete, wakagafunika na zahabu, wakagawika i Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno,
11 gakawa gose galanjile ku mulango. Nago gose vyono mejete, gali gena mabawa mejete mejete. Ne libawa limonga, lyali lina wutali wa mita nyejete, na lobo.
12 Ne mabawa gayo gali matandakule, gepamizize haciligati ye cumba, na ku lubavu luyagwe, libawa lye kejete, lyali lipamize kwi 'jinji.
13 Mabawa gago ku wutandakule wayo, gali gakwiliye wugazi we cumba cose mita yicenda.
14 Yakanoza ne libupa lyo mwenda we citani ciswanu, we liwala lye buluu, no lugandu, hamonga ne liwala lidunghu, yakahonela ne cihwani ce makelubi, yakaliwika licitize i Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno.

Masumbili mejete ge shaba

(1Wat 7:15-22)

15 Mutemi Solomona, yakanoza masumbili mejete, limonga lina wutali wa mita yikumi na yihano na nusu. Yakagasimya ku mwande ye Nyumba yo Mulungu. Ne lisumbili limonga lyali lina mukumbo wa mita nyejete na lobo wutali wayo.
16 Na ku visonje, kucanyha ye masumbili, kwali kuhambwe na minyolo, hamonga ne makomamanga yigana limonga ga shaba.
17 Ne masumbili gago, gali masimye akuno, na akuno ha mulango we Nyumba yo Mulungu, limonga ku lubavu lwa kutakama yo mulango, walicemaga Yakini, ne liyagwe nalyo lyo lubavu lwa kusumuka, walicemaga Bowazi.


2 Zinghomo 4

I vinhu vyo kuzenjela Nyumba yo Mulungu

(1Wat 7:23-51)

1 Mutemi Solomona yali yena cikumbicilo ca shaba, cono cali cina wugazi wayo mita yicenda mbavu zose, no wutali wayo wa kucanyha mita yine na nusu.
2 Kanyhi yakanoza yitanji lya shaba, lyono lyali no wupilime walyo wa mu mugo walyo, mita yine na nusu. Na mita nyejete na lobo, wima walyo, na mita yikumi ne'datu na nusu, wuwaha wo wupilime walyo.
3 Yakawicila micilili myejete yo wihambo mumo mwitanji, wunji hasika yayo, no muyagwe nawo ha kacanyha yayo. No wihambo wuwo, wukawa wa hwani ya ng'ombe, wono wali mulunzize lukulu ne litanji.
4 Yakalijeleka mucanyha ye migongo ye vihwani vye zing'ombe yikumi na nyejete ze shaba. Nazo zikawa zimile yidatu yidatu zilanjile yidatu ne langa lyazo. Yidatu zilanjile ku sumuka, yidatu, zilanjile ku mwezi, yidatu zilanjile kutakama ne zidatu zikawa zilanjile kucilima.
5 Ne cuma cono canozwa litanji, cali cina wukangazi wa sentimita mupunghati na nusu. No mugo wayo wali munoze nha mugo we cikombe, winhe 'luwa. Ne litanji lilyo, lyenjilaga lita za malenga elufu makumi mutandatu.
6 Yakanoza na mabeseni yikumi, yakawika mahano lubavu lwa kutakama lwe Nyumba yo Mulungu, ne mahano nago lubavu lwayo lwa kusumuka. Ne mabeseni gago, getumamilwaga so mulimo wo kukwema zinyama zinji ze mitugo ze makumbiko ge vilavywa vinyanywe. Ninga malenga gono gekalaga mwi'tanji liwaha, ago wezuguzaga wakohani du wali wene.

7 Kanyhi yakanoza viwicilo vya tala, vili 'kumi vya zahabu, mu nghango nha vyono yalajilizwa, yakaviwika mwi'kumbo lye Nyumba yo Mulungu, vihano lubavu lwa kutakama, ne vihano lubavu lwa kusumuka. Hanyuma kanyhi yakanoza mabeseni yigana limonga ga zahabu.
8 Yakanoza na meza yikumi, yakaviwika vyose mugati ye cumba ca haciligati, mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Yakawika viwicilo vye tala vihano, hamonga na meza yihano lubavu aluno na viwicilo vye tala vihano kanyhi na meza yihano, navyo yakaviwika lubavu luyagwe mumo mugati. Kanyhi yakanoza na bakuli yigana za zahabu.

9 Kanyhi yakanoza na ha wuduwilo we wakohani, hamonga no wuduwilo wuwaha, yakawicila ne milango yayo. Ne milango yono yali yili haciligati ya ha wuduwilo, yose yali mihiliwe shaba.
10 Nalyo litanji lilyo lye malenga, lyali yiwikwe behi ne nhuzi yo lubavu lwa kucilima ye takama ye Nyumba yo Mulungu.

11 Hulamu, yakanoza zinyungu, ne masili, ne zibakuli. Nhondo yakakondya kuvinoza vinhu vyose, vyono yehuwiza kuli Mutemi Solomona, nha vyono kovinoza so Nyumba yo Mulungu navyo vyali vyovi:

12 Masumbili mejete

Mikumbo myejete, yono yali mucanyha ye masumbili,

zi nyavu nyejete zo kufunika

mucanyha ye masumbili mejete.

13 Ne makomamanga magana mane ga shaba

ga mu zinyavu zi nyejete, zali micilili myejete myejete,

mhilimye mwi'sumbili limonga limonga kucanyha yayo.

14 Mikokoteni yo kudinghulila, yikumi.

Mabeseni go kwizuguziza, yikumi, ne Litanji limonga.

15 Ne vihwani vye ng'ombe za shaba yikumi na nyejete,

zono zajelekwa litanji.

16 Zinyungu, masili, hamonga ne viya vyayo vyose. Nayo yuyo Hulamu u munghula, yakanoza vinhu vyose, kwe shaba nhelemeze viswanu muno, nha vyono mutemi Solomona yamulajiliza, so witumamilwa mu Nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu.

17 Mutemi yakavinoza vinhu vivyo vyose, mwi'longo linamu lya mu nyika ya mwi'lolo lya Yoludani, muciligati ya Sukoti na Zeleda.
18 Ne viya vivyo vyose vyono vyanozwa, hasina hamba munhu monga, mono yasuma kumanya wuzito we shaba yono yetumamilwa, vyono wali winha.

19 Mutemi Solomona, yakanoza ne viya vye zahabu so Nyumba yo Mulungu, nha cinji, cikumbicilo, hamonga ne zimeza zo kuwika mikate yono yali milavywe kuli Mulungu.
20 Hamonga no wuwiko we zitala, ne zitala zono zali nozwe ne zahabu nengu lwe, ziwe kwaka ku mwande ye Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno, nha vyono zategwa.
21 Hamonga ne maluwa go kuhamba, ne zitala, ne vibatilo,
22 ne visimizyo vye zitala ne zibakuli, ne mitela yo kufusiza wuvumba, hamonga ne mazaye go kupalila makala go moto. Vinhu vivino vyose, vyali vinozwe ne zahabu nengu. I milango ya kunze ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne milango yo kwinjilila i Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno, yose yali mihiliwe zahabu.


2 Zinghomo 5

1 U mutemi Solomona, yali yeza kukondya u mulimo we Nyumba yo Mulungu, hanyuma yakakongholeza vinhu vyose vyono sogwe Dawudi yavisiwanula kuli Muwaha Mulungu, yakaviwika mu viwutwa vye Nyumba yo Mulungu yo kuwika viwutwa, nha cinji fweza, zahabu, hamonga ne vinhu vinji vyose.

Lisanduku lye Cilagane likwinjizwa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu

(1Wat 8:1-9)

2 Hanyuma yayo, u mutemi Solomona, yakawa kuwacema walongola wose wa mu milango ye Waisilayeli, hamonga ne wawaha we zimhuga, wekongholele ku Yelusalemi, ho walisole Lisanduku lye Cilagane lyo Mulungu ku wulawa ku Sayuni mwi'kaya lya Dawudi, walinjize mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
3 Na wose wakekongholela hamonga, mu cipango ce sikukuu ye vibanda.
4 Nawo walongola wose wali weza kukondya kwikonghola hamonga, hobaho Walawi wakalidinghula Lisanduku lye Cilagane,
5 wakalinjiza mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Hanyuma, wakohani hamonga ne Walawi, wakalimwagula Lihema lyono Lyelile, wakalisola hamonga ne vinhu vyayo vyose, wakavinjiza mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
6 Nayo mutemi Solomona, hamonga ne wanhu wose wa Isilayeli, wakekongholela ha mwande ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane, wakalavya makumbiko ga ngholo nyinji, hamonga na ng'ombe nyinji muno, hamba si za kuwala.
7 Hanyuma ya zizo, nhondo i wakohani ho wakawa kulidinghula Lisanduku lye Cilagane lyo Mulungu, wakalinjiza mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, wakaliwika i Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno, hasi ye Makelubi.
8 Ne mabawa ge Makelubi, gakalifuniciza Lisanduku, hamonga ne mabici gayo go kulidinghulila.
9 Ninga mabici go kulidinghulila Lisanduku vyono gali matali, holeka gasumaga kwijela kuli munhu wo wose mono kokwima vidomile no mulango we Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno. (Ne mabici gago, gakali gali baho sunga nalu).
10 Na mumo mugati ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane, mwali musina cinji, langa mwali mozili zi mbawo zinyejete ze mabwe, zono Musa yazitajila mumo, lono yali yali he 'tunda lya Sinayi, i hanhu hono Muwaha Mulungu, yanozeza cilagane hamonga ne wanhu wakwe Waisilayeli, lono wawuyaga wakulawa ku Misili.

U lukumyo lo Muwaha Mulungu

11 Ne wakohani wose wono wali wali baho, si wali wehangulize wanhu na cipuga cawo, langa wose wali wahangahanjile du, kanyhi wose wali wakondya kwiyeza.
12 Ne Walawi iwembi, wose kono walila, wa Asafu, na Hemani, na Yedutuni, hamonga na wo lulelo lwawo, wose wali wavwalile vivwalo vizelu 'swe. Nawo wawa Walawi, wakema ku lubavu lwa kucilima, behi ne cikumbicilo, akuno webate ne misesele ne masumbi ne zindonondo. Kanyhi hakawa na wakohani yigana limonga na makumi mejete, wono watowaga zindulele.
13 Nawo i wembi, wembaga wose hamonga kwe mazi maswanu muno, hamonga ne zindulele ne misesele, ne masumbi, hamonga ne vinhu vinji vinji vyono watowaga, wakumukumya Muwaha Mulungu wakutya,

“Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe, hambi nha muswanu.

No lusungu lwakwe nha lwa cibitilila.”

Likaza yivunde kwa nhinicizo, likayimemya nhojo yo Muwaha Mulungu mumo mu Nyumba yakwe.
14 Sunga ne wakohani, si wasuma kanyhi kujendelela no kuwulongoza wuhembi yaye. Hambi Nyumba yo Mulungu, nha yali yamema nhojo yo Muwaha Mulungu.


2 Zinghomo 6

Zinghani zo mutemi Solomona kuli wanhu

(1Wat 8:12-21)

1 Hanyuma yayo, mutemi Solomona yakalomba yakutya,

“Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu wehagulila go mweneco

kwikala mu mavunde na mu wutitu.

2 Ninga alu nakondya kukuzenjela Nyumba yako,

i hanhu hako hono kosuma kwikala cibitilila.”

3 Ne wanhu wose Waisilayeli, wali wemile baho. Mutemi yakawapitucila, yakawalugutiza.
4 Na hanyuma yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli! Hambi nha kawukamisa wihuwizo wono yamwihuwiza baba Dawudi, lono yalonjela yakatya,
5 ‘Ku wulawa lono nawawusiza wanhu wangu ku Misili, sunga nalu, nali nghali sinahagula likaya lyo lyose mu yisi ya Isilayeli, hawe ho hanhu hono yosuma kuzengwa Nyumba yangu, honiwe kuhembelwa. Hamba nali nghali sinamuhagula munhu yunji wo wose, yawalongoze wanhu wangu Waisilayeli.
6 Ninga aluno, nakondya kuyihagula Yelusalemi, hawe ho hanhu hono nosuma kuhembelwa. Nagwe, agwe Dawudi, yo kowatema wanhu wangu.’ ”

7 Solomona yakajendelela kulonga yakutya, “U sogo yangu Dawudi, yali yakondya kwihwesa nha vyono ciba yazenje Nyumba yo Mulungu, i hanhu hono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli kowa kuhembelwa.
8 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela yakatya, ‘Agwe Dawudi, heya kunoza viswanu vyono wihwesize wunzenjele Nyumba,
9 ninga'lu, agwe si kosuma kunzenjela, langa Solomona, u mwanago wako, yuyo langa yo kolonzenjela Nyumba yangu.’ ”

10 “Nalu u Muwaha Mulungu, kakondya kuwufisiliza wihuwizo wakwe. Nane luno mbeka, nawuhala wutemi wa baba Dawudi muli Waisilayeli. Kanyhi nakondya no kuyizenga Nyumba, i hanhu hono cowa kumuhembela Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli.
11 Kanyhi nakondya no kulinjiza Lisanduku lye Cilagane mumo mugati, na mugati yalyo, movili vibawo vyejete vye mabwe, vyono vyandicigwe icilagane cono u Muwaha Mulungu yacinoza ne wanhu wakwe Waisilayeli.”

Wulombe wa Solomona

(1Wat 8:22-53)

12 Hanyuma, Solomona yakema ha cikumbicilo, ha mwande ye wanhu wose, yakayinyasula miwoko yakwe kucanyha, yakalomba.
13 (Solomona yanoza ne shaba, i hanhu hono henuce ho kulonjela, wima wayo mita monga na lobo, no wugazi wayo, mita nyejete na lobo mbavu zose. Yakahawika haciligati yo wuduwilo. Yakema mucanyha yayo, yakafugama ha mwande ye wanhu wose, yakayinyasula miwoko yakwe kucanyha.)
14 Yakalomba yakutya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu we Waisilayeli, hambi nha hasina Mulungu yunji kanyhi mono yehwanile gwegwe, ku wulanga hamba mono'si hasi. Agwe kukamisaga cilagane cako kuli wanhu wako, kanyhi kuwalajilaga zo lusungu lwako wanhu wako, wono wakujendela muli'gwe, wakuhulice kwe zinhumbula zawo zose.
15 Hambi 'gwe, nha kufisiliza cilagane cono wacinoza kuli baba Dawudi. Naluno nghani zose zakondya kufisilizwa.
16 Holeka aluno go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu we Waisilayeli, nghukunena, wufisilize ne cilagane ciyagwe naco, cono wamwihuwiziza baba Dawudi, lono wamulonjela wukatya, vyono vilo vyose howa ho yuli monga wa muli wanagwe, mono kowatema Waisilayeli. Ninga nghani mbaha, walanjilile, webate Lilajilizo lyako, nha vyono mwene Dawudi yali yebate.
17 Holeka aluno go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu we Waisilayeli, nghukunena wuzifisilizaje zose du, nha vyono wamwihuwiziza u mwitumami wako Dawudi.

18 “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, alu mbeka kosuma kwikala mono'nze, hamonga ne wanhu walume ne wacekulu? Sunga no wulanga nha si wukukwiliye ku wikala mumo. Alu 'mbi Nyumba yako yiyi yono nghuzenjeye, hongo yowa mbaha yono yikukwiliye?
19 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wangu, ane nili mwitumami wako. Nghukunena wuhulice wulombe wangu, wumhele zono nghukulomba.
20 Wuyilanje yiyi Nyumba yako, misi ne cilo. Agwe wehuwiza nha vyono, bahano howa ho hanhu hono i wanhu wako wowa kukuhembela. Holeka weze kuhulika lono nghulomba ndomizye wusu wangu kono ko yili Nyumba yako yiyi.
21 Wuhulice wulombe wangu, hamonga no wulombe we wanhu wako Waisilayeli, lono nawo wakukulomba akuno walanzizye wusu wawo hanhu baha. Holeka cikukunena, wucihulice, kuko ku wulanga, wucinenele.

22 “Na lono munhu yasitakwe vyono kamusutizya muyagwe, na hono yaletwe hono cikumbicilo yelahizwe mono Nyumba yako, na hono yelahe nha vyono mwene si musutyi.
23 Agwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wuhulice kuko ku wulanga, wuyiling'anize nghani yiyo muli wanhu wako. Wumulipicize mono yena lisutya, mu nghango ye masutya gakwe, no kumuleka ayulya mono yasina lisutya ku soko yo wihuwilwa wakwe.

24 “Na lono wanhu wako Waisilayeli wasumwe ne walugu wawo, vyono wene wakusutizya'gwe, na hono wakuzize waze mono Nyumba yiyi, no kukulomba ko winenenyhezi, wakusaka wuwanenele,
25 ale, wuwahulice kuko ku wulanga, wuwanenele wanhu wako wubi wawo, wuwagomole ku yisi yono wawapela wene, hamonga ne walezi wawo.

26 “Na lono wuwe wuyimiye mvula yilece kutonya, vyono i wanhu wako wetuma wubi hali'gwe, na kolo wakunena, no kulanzya wusu wawo ku Nyumba yiyi, no kukulomba ko winenenyhezi,
27 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wuwahulice kuko ku wulanga, wuwanenele wubi wawo i wanhu wako, i wanhu wa Isilayeli, wuwejize wetume ziswanu. Hanyuma, Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wuyitonyeze mvula mono yisi yako yiyi, yono wawapela wanhu wako, yiwe yawo cibitilila.

28 “Na hono nzala yigwe mu'si, hamba lihuhuji, hamba lunji vilimwa vihodangwe ne mbeho mbaha, hamba kuligwa ne zinzije. Hamba lunji wanhu wako lono wakuhomwa ne walugu wawo, hamba lunji matamwa gawe gagwiye, hamba lunji mhungo ziwe ziwagwiliye,
29 nghukunena wuhulice wulombe wawo. No munhu wo wose wa muli wanhu wako Waisilayeli, hono yawe yena nghani yo yose yono yimutamise mu nhumbula yakwe, hono yakulombe, akuno yayigololeye miwoko Nyumba yiyi, ale,
30 wuhulice wulombe wakwe. Wuhulice wulombe wawo kuko ku wulanga, wuwanenele. Hambi nha yo gwegwe du wuli wene, mono wumanyile mahweso ga mu nhumbula yo munhu. Wumuling'anizize munhu mu nghango vyono genha metumo gakwe.
31 Nawo i wanhu wako ho wokupela cogopo, hamonga no kukuhulika mu vilo vyose vyono wokwikala mu yisi yono wawapela i walezi wetu.

32 “Na hono mujenzi mono yekaye kutali ne yisi yiyi, nayo yahulice zo wuwaha wako ne vilungo vyako, na vyono wiwikanize mu nyinhuma yako nhondo zose, na hono nayo yaze yakulombe mono Nyumba yiyi,
33 ale, wumuhulikaje wulombe wakwe kuko ku wulanga kono wikaye, wumupele ne visakwa vyakwe vyono yakukulomba, i wanhu wose wa mono yinze, ho wakumanye gwegwe, no kukuhulika, nha vyono i wanhu wako Waisilayeli. Kanyhi ho womanya nha vyono Nyumba yiyi yono nayizenga, ho hanhu du hali wene ho kukuhembela gwegwe.

34 “Na hono wuwalajilize wanhu wako, wabite wakehome ne walugu wawo, nawo hono wakulombe, ho hose du hono howa ho wali, akuno wadomizye wusu wawo kone 'kaya lili lyono walihagula, hamonga ne Nyumba yako yiyi yono nakuzenjela,
35 ale, wuhulice wulombe wawo. Wuwahulice kuko ku wulanga, wuwapele wusume.

36 “Na hono wanhu wako wetume wubi hali'gwe, na hawe hasina hamba monga mono si yetumile wubi. Na mwi'kuwo lyako, nagwe wuwalece wasumwe ne walugu wawo, wawasole nha wafungwa, no kuwategula ku yisi yinji, hamba yisi yiyo yiwe yili kutali muno muno,
37 wuhulice wulombe wawo. Hono lono wali ku yisi yiyo, wanene no kukulomba, wavumile wubi wawo, hamonga ne masutya gono wetumile, ale, Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu wuhulice wulombe wawo.
38 Na hono yiwe mbeka, lono wali ku yisi yiyo, wema mu mbeka, kanyhi wanena no wubi wawo, ho wakulombe akuno wadomizye wusu wawo kono yisi yiyi yono wawapela i walezi wetu, mwi'kaya lilino lyono gwegwe walihagula, na kono Nyumba yako yiyi yono nakuzenjela,
39 ale, wuhulice wulombe wawo, ku wulawa kuko kono wikaye ku wulanga, wuwawonele lusungu, wuwanenele wanhu wako wubi wawo.

40 “Nalu go Muwaha Mulungu wangu, wucilanje ase, kanyhi wuhulice sunga no wulombe wetu wono cikulombile i hanhu baha.

41 Alu winuce gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

winjile mu Nyumba yako,

hamonga ne Lisanduku lye Cilagane,

lyono co cihwani ce vilungo vyako,

wikale mumo cibitilila.

Wuwalugutize wakohani mu milimo yawo yose,

ne wanhu wako wose wasangalale

ku soko yo wuswanu wako kuli wene.

42 Kanyhi go Muwaha Mulungu,

wulece kumulema mutemi mono wamuhagula gwegwe.

Wukumbucile wendo wono wali wina nawo

kuli Dawudi mwitumami wako.”


2 Zinghomo 7

Wusiwanule we Nyumba yo Mulungu

(1Wat 8:62-66)

1 Nayo mutemi Solomona yali yeza kukondya wulombe wakwe wuwo, hobaho moto wukatima wulawa ku wulanga, wukakongojeza makumbiko gose manyanywe, gono gali malavywe baho. Ne nhojo yo Muwaha Mulungu, yikamema mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
2 Hamba ne wakohani, si wasuma kwinjila mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hambi Nyumba yo Mulungu, nha yali yimemile nhojo yo Muwaha Mulungu.
3 Nawo wanhu wa Isilayeli wali weza kuwuwona moto wukugwa wulawa ku wulanga, hamonga no wuzelu wamema mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hobaho wose wakafugama hasi, no wusu wawo wukapamiza ho hasi, wakamuhemba Mulungu no kumulumba so wuswanu wakwe hamonga no wendo wakwe we cibitilila.
4 Hanyuma ya zizo, mutemi Solomona, hamonga ne wanhu wose, wakawa kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu makumbiko.
5 Mutemi Solomona, yakalavya makumbiko, ng'ombe elufu makumi mejete na nyejete, hamonga na ngholo elufu yigana limonga na makumi mejete, vilavywa vyo mulamu. Hanyuma nhondo u mwene hamonga ne wanhu wakwe ho wakawa kuyisiwanula Nyumba yo Mulungu.
6 Nawo wakohani wakema i hanhu hono wali wawiciywe, nawo Walawi wakawa wedomilenawo, akuno, wakamukumya Muwaha Mulungu mu viya vyo kwimbila, vyono mutemi Dawudi yavilavya, wakwimba ne zinyimbo wakutya, “Lusungu, lwakwe lwikalaga cibitilila!” Nha vyono walajilizwa no mutemi Dawudi. Nawo wakohani wakazitowa zindulele ha mwande yawo, ne Waisilayeli wose wali wemile wima.

7 Mutemi Solomona, yakahasiwanula ha wuduwilo, wono wali haciligati, ha mwande ye Nyumba yo Mulungu. Hanyuma yakalavya ne vilavywa vinyanywe, ne vilavywa vyo wusaje hamonga ne mafuta ga mu vilavywa vyo mulamu. Nayo yanoza nhavivyo, vyono ci cilya cikumbicilo ce shaba cono yacinoza, cali cidodo muno, si cali cikwile ku wulavizya vilavywa vyose vivyo.

8 Nayo Solomona, hamonga ne wanhu wose Waisilayeli, wakayinoza sikukuu yiyi ye vibanda, vilo mupunghati. Na hakawa na wanhu wenji muno muno, wanji wali walawiliye kutali, ku Hamati Pasi, ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, na ku mupaka we yisi ya Misili, ku lubavu lwa kutakama.
9 Nawo wose, wakawa baho du vilo mupunghati, so wusiwanule we cikumbicilo, hanyuma kanyhi vilo mupunghati so sikukuu yeneco. Na mwi'zuwa lye ndumilizo, wakawa no wudumilize wo wuhembi wo wusiwanuzi,
10 na mitondo yaco, lizuwa lye makumi mejete na yidatu lya mu mwezi wo mupunghati, mutemi Solomona, nhondo ho yakawa kuwakombola wanhu wawuye ku zikwawo. Nawo wanhu wose wakawa no wusangalale wuwaha muno, ku soko yo wulugutwe wono Muwaha Mulungu yawapelile wanhu wakwe Waisilayeli, hamonga na Dawudi, na Solomona.

Mulungu yakwilajila kanyhi kuli Solomona

(1Wat 9:1-9)

11 Nayo mutemi Solomona, lono yali yayikondya i milimo yose yo wuzenji we Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne kaya yo wutemi, nha vyono yali yehwesize mu nhumbula yakwe,
12 mwizuwa linji ne cilo, Muwaha Mulungu yakamulawila, yakamulonjela Solomona yakutya, “Gwe Solomona, ane niwuhulika wulombe wako, nane niyibocela Nyumba yiyi, hawe ho hanhu hono mowa kundavizya makumbiko.
13 Na mu mapango go gose hono niyimile mvula yilece kutonya, hamba hono niwaletele nzije zilye vilimwa vyawo, hamba ndete lihuhuji muli wanhu wangu,
14 nawo wanhu wangu wono wakucemwa mwi'tagwa lyangu hono, wenenenyheze no kulomba kuli'ne, no kunondola kanyhi wagalece ne metumo gawo mabi gono wali kwituma, nane nowahulika kuko ku wulanga, nowanenela no wubi wawo, no kuyimotya kanyhi yisi yawo.
15 Nane noyilanjilila muno Nyumba yiyi, kanyhi nowa niwikanize kuwuhuliciza wulombe wose wono wowa kuwulombela hanhu baha.
16 Hambi nha yo nene mono nahahagula hanhu baha no kuhasiwanula, hawe ho hanhu hono nowa kuhembelwa cibitilila. Holeka nowa nihalanjiliye, no kuhadima mu mapango gose.
17 Nagwe hono wunyitumamile ko wihuwilwa nha vyono sogo Dawudi yanyitumamila, wugebate malajilizo gangu, kanyhi witume zose zono nghulajilize,
18 nane nowufisiliza wihuwizo wangu, wono namwihuwiza sogo Dawudi, lono namulonjela nha vyono, Waisilayeli wowa kutemwa ne wana wawo du vilo vyose.
19 Ninga wulanjilile! Hono gwegwe, hamba wanhu wako, muwubene wulajilize wangu hamonga ne malajilizo gangu gono namupelenyi, nanye muwe kuyitumamila no kuyihemba milungu yinji,
20 nane nomuwusenyi mu yisi yono namupelenyi, kanyhi noyileka ne Nyumba yiyi yono nene no mweneco nayisiwanula, yiwe ho hanhu hono nowa kuhembelwa. Nane noyinoza yiwe nha mbazi, ne wanhu wose woyihong'onga, wayisece, no kuyibedegala.

21 “Kwa luno, Nyumba yiyi yo Mulungu, yina cogopo muno kuli wanhu wose. Ninga hanyuma, munhu wo wose mono kolowa kukola behi nayo, kolokwizina muno no kuwuza yawe kutya, ‘Alu socici lo Muwaha Mulungu ketuma zono zinha zizi mu yisi yiyi, na mono Nyumba yiyi?’
22 Nawo wanhu wolopitula watye, ‘Ku soko i Waisilayeli wakondya kumuleka Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo, mono yawawusa i walezi wawo ku yisi ya Misili, wakegwadilila ne milungu yinji no kuyihemba. Na yiyi yo soko yono yaleka Muwaha Mulungu yakuwagumilila magazo gaga muli wene.’ ”


2 Zinghomo 8

I milimo yono yetuma Solomona

(1Wat 9:10-28)

1 Mutemi Solomona, yayizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu hamonga ne kaya yakwe yo wutemi, myaka makumi mejete, ho yakawa yakondya.
2 Kanyhi yakagazenga sunga ne makaya gono mutemi Hulamu yamupela yakawenjiza Waisilayeli wekale mu makaya gago.
3 Yakayiteka yisi ya Hamati, ne yisi ya Zoba,
4 yakalizenjela ligomawakali likaya lya Palimila lya mu nyika. Yakagazenga ne makaya gose ga mu Hamati, wukawa wo wuwiko wakwe we viwutwa.
5 Mutemi Solomona, yakagazenga sunga ne makaya gaga gose gono getyatizye, Bete-holoni ya kucanyha, na Bete-holoni ya kusika, (yakagazenjela ne magomawakali makaya gose, yakawicila ne milango ho yiwe ne vidindilo.)
6 Hamonga ne likaya lya Baalati, ne makaya gose gono yali yawicile viwutwa, na mu makaya gose gono yali yaziwicile zifwalasi zakwe, hamonga ne matuka gakwe go wulugu. Yakawa yena mahweso go wuzenga vyono yasakaga mwene mu Yelusalemi, na mu Lebanoni, sunga na mu yisi yose yono yali yayitemile mwene.
7 Solomona yakawasola wanhu wose we Cikanani wono wasigala, wono si wo lulelo lwe Waisilayeli, wono Waisilayeli si wasuma kuwawulajila lukulu, lono wayisolela yisi yiyo, yakawenjiza mu witumami, wakawa wetumami mu yisi yakwe.
8 Ne wanhu wawa, wali wo Wahiti, Waamoli, Wapelizi, Wahivi, hamonga ne Wayebusi, na wawo wose, sunga ne wana wawo, wakajendelela kuwa wetumami wa mu yisi yiyo, sunga mu vilo vivi vye behi.
9 Ninga wene Waisilayeli, si wetumywaga nha wetumami, langa wene wetumaga milimo yo wusilikale, na yo wulongola, na yo wulanjilizi we matuka go wulugu, na yo wulanjilizi we wapanda zifwalasi.
10 Hali ho wali wanhu magana mejete na makumi mahano, wono wali wawicigwe wawe wo walanjilizi we wetuma milimo yose yo wuzenji, wono walanjililaga wuzenji wose we mazengo ge hayo hayo.

11 Mutemi Solomona, yakamusola mugolece wakwe u mwana we cicekulu wo mutemi wa Misili, yakamuhamya mwi'kaya lya Dawudi, yakamwinjiza mu kaya yakwe yono yamuzenjela. Hambi nha yehwesa yakatya, “Si yowa viswanu mugolece wangu yekale mwi'kulu lya Dawudi u mutemi we Waisilayeli. Vyono ho hose hono lyowa liwicigwe Lisanduku lye cilagane, hanhu baho haweza helile.”

12 Mutemi Solomona, yakamulavizya Muwaha Mulungu makumbiko, mu cikumbicilo cono yacizenga ha mwande ye Nyumba yo Mulungu.
13 Yakalavya vilavywa vinyanywe mu nghango nha vyono Wulajilize wa Musa wali wulajilize mu vilo vyose vyono vyelile. Yakalavya mu vye zi Sabato, na so sikukuu ze ndawo yo mwezi, na so zi sikukuu yidatu za mu mwaka, nha cinji, sikukuu ye mikate yono yisina cisase, ne sikukuu ye nhwanga, ne sikukuu ye vibanda.
14 Solomona, yakatyatya wityatye wono sogwe Dawudi yawuwika. Nayo yakayiwikaniza i milimo ye nhondo zose ye wakohani mu wiwalile wawo, hamonga na ye Walawi wono wawatazaga wakohani mu wimbe we zinyimbo, hamonga na mu witume we milimo yawo. Kanyhi yakawawikaniza i walindilizi we Nyumba yo Mulungu, mu hanhu hayo hayo, ho wasume kwituma viswanu i milimo yawo ye nhondo zose, yo wulindiliza milango, nha vyono Dawudi u munhu wo Mulungu yalajiliza.
15 Ne malajilizo gose gono mutemi Dawudi yawalajiliza wakohani, hamonga ne Walawi, kuli ze zinyumba zo kuwicila viwutwa, hamonga ne milimo yinji, wulajilize wuwo wose, wukafisilizwa.

16 Na mu mapango gaga, i milimo yose yono yali yayihwesize Solomona yayitume, nhondo yikawa yakondya. Ku lubavu lo wuzenji we Nyumba yo Mulungu, ku wulawa musinji wayo, sunga yikakondya yose, zose ziketumwa viswanu muno.

17 Na hanyuma ya zizo, Solomona yakabita ku Eziyoni-gebeli na ku Elati, ha mhwani ya Agaba, mu yisi ya Edomu.
18 Nayo mutemi Hulamu, yakamuletela magalawa mu miwoko ye wetumami wakwe. Ne wetumami wakwe wakajendela mwi'lenga, hamonga ne wetumami wa Solomona wakafika ku yisi ya Ofili. Nawo wakasola zahabu kuko ku Ofili zificile kilo elufu yikumi ne'hano, wakamuletela mutemi Solomona.


2 Zinghomo 9

Mutemi we cicekulu wa Sheba yakumubitila Solomona

(1Wat 10:1-13)

1 No mutemi we cicekulu wa Sheba, yaza kuzihulika zo lukumyo lo mutemi Solomona, hodu, yakawa kutuluka yakawa kubita ku Yelusalemi, yabite yakamujele mutemi Solomona mu mawuzo makamu. Na lono yabitaga, yakabitana hamonga ne watyatyi wakwe wenji muno, yakateguliza no muhalaka, ne vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu muno, hamonga na zahabu nyinji muno. Na vivi vyose, vyali vipacizwe mu zingamiya. Nayo yali yeza kwiwona na Solomona, hobaho yakawa kumuwuza nghani nyinji muno mu nghango vyono yali yehwesize.
2 Ninga nayo Solomona, yakamupitula nghani zose, hamba hasina nghani yinji yono yamulemela kuyipitula.
3 Nayo mutemi we cicekulu wa Sheba, yakagawona mahala ga Solomona, ne kaya yo wutemi yono yayizenjile.
4 Yakaziwona ne zindigwa zono yatenjelwaga ha meza, no wikalo wa wetumami wakwe hono wekalaga, no wityatye wa wetumami wa mwi'kulu, ne vivwalo vye milimo vyono wavwalaga, hamonga ne myenda yono wavwalaga i wetumami wono wamwitumamilaga ha zindigwa ze zisikukuu, na mu makumbiko gono yalavyaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Yali yeza kuziwona zizo zose, yakezina muno muno.

5 Hobaho yakawa kumulonjela mutemi Solomona yakatya, “Kumbe zako zono hulikaga lono nili ku yisi yangu, hamonga na ze mahala gako, zose za mbeka!
6 Ninga ane, si nehuwilaga zono wanonjelaga, sunga nhondo niza kwiwonelela no mweneco ne meso gangu. Nalu nakondya kuwona vyono kumbe, nali nghali si nahulika hamba nusu bwete ze mahala gako. Kumbe 'gwe wina mahala menji muno, kuciza no wulonje wono wanhu wakulonga.
7 Holeka wamoti ne wanhu wono wakukwitumamila, wono wemaga ha mwande yako nhondo zose, wakukuhuliciza ze mahala gako!
8 Ale, Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako yalumbwe! Mono yakulajila vyono yendezwa nagwe, holeka yakawa kukunoza wuwe mutemi, wuteme mwi'tagwa lyakwe. Hambi nha yawendile wanhu wakwe Waisilayeli, nayo yakusaka yawakamise cibitilila. Nayo yakakunoza wuwe mutemi we Waisilayeli, ho wunoze ling'ani no wiling'ano.”

9 Hodu mutemi we cicekulu wa Sheba, yakawa kumupela Solomona, vipegwa vyono yamuletela, nha cinji kilo elufu yine za zahabu, no muhalaka mwinji muno, hamonga ne vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu. Hamba hakali kanyhi si nawa no muhalaka wunji muswanu muno, nho muhalaka wono mutemi we cicekulu wa Sheba, yamupela mutemi Solomona.

10 (Ne wanhu wo mutemi Hulamu, hamonga na wo mutemi Solomona, wono waleta zahabu ku walawa ku Ofili, nawo waleteleza ne mibici yo musandali, hamonga ne vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu.
11 Mutemi Solomona yakayitumamila mibici ye misandali so wuzenjela ngazi ze Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga na likulu. Yakasesa masumbi hamonga ne zindonondo, ku so wembi. Na hakawa hasina kanyhi masumbi ne zindonondo zono zejela mu yisi ya Yuda, zono zali zihwanile zizo.)

12 Nayo mutemi Solomona, yakamupela yuyo mutemi we cicekulu wa Sheba, vyose vyono yavisakaga. Hamonga kanyhi ne vinhu vyono wakakawana, kuli avilya vyono mwene mutemi we cicekulu wa Sheba, yamuletela Solomona. Hanyuma ya zizo, mutemi we cicekulu wa Sheba, hamonga ne wetumami wakwe, wakawa kuwuya kwawo ku yisi ya Sheba.

Sawo yo mutemi Solomona

(1Wat 10:14-25)

13 Na mu mwaka na mwaka, mutemi Solomona yabocelaga zahabu kilo elufu makumi mejete ne'datu,
14 ku watowelele hamonga ne kodi yono walavyaga iwasuluza ne wakwawi. I watemi wa ku Alabiya, hamonga ne walongola we zisi ze Waisilayeli, wose wakamuletela Solomona zahabu na fweza.
15 Mutemi Solomona yakanoza ngula zimbaha zificile magana mejete, yakazihiliwa zahabu ya wuzito wa kilo mupunghati.
16 Kanyhi yakanoza na ngula zinji zi ndodo ndodo, zificile magana madatu, nazo yakazihiliwa zahabu ya wuzito wa kilo yidatu. Yakazisola zizo ngula zose, yakaziwika mu nyumba yono yacemelwa litagwa lya Nyumba ya mu Mbago ya Lebanoni.

17 Hanyuma kanyhi, mutemi Solomona yakanoza yigoda yiwaha lya mbali za nhembo, yakalihiliwa zahabu nengu.
18 Ne ligoda lilyo, likawa na ngazi mutandatu, zono zetumamilwaga, ho wuhaficile hono holili ligoda lyeneco. Kanyhi mu mhelo ye zingazi, mukawa na mbawo zo kwibata akuno na akuno, nazo hiliwe zahabu.
19 Kanyhi hakawa na hwani ya simba zono zali mbice zimile akuno na akuno. Na ku wulawilila baho, hakawa hasina kanyhi wutemi wo wose wono wali wina ligoda lyono lihwanile lilyo.

20 Ne vikombe vyose vyo mutemi Solomona, vyali vya zahabu du, sunga ne vitumamilwa vyose vyono vyali vili mu Nyumba ya mu Mbago ya Lebanoni, vyose vyali vya zahabu nengu. Ninga fweza mu vilo vyo mutemi Solomona, yali si cinhu cono cikamile yaye.
21 Mutemi Solomona, yali yena ngalawa zono zabitaga ku Talishishi hamonga ne zingalawa zo mutemi Hulamu. Na mu myaka midatu, ngalawa zo mutemi Solomona, zaletaga zahabu, fweza, mbali za nhembo, ne zinyani, hamonga ne zindeje.

22 Nayo mutemi Solomona, yakagolipa muno muno, yakawa yo munhu we mahala menji si nho mutemi wo wose wa mono'nze.
23 Na wose wakawa wakumuziza wakumuwuza wuza, no kumanya zo winji we mahala gakwe, gono Mulungu yamupelile.
24 Na wose wono wamuzizaga, wamuletelaga ne vipegwa vye vinhu vye fweza, na vye zahabu, hamba vivwalo, ne vitwalo, na vye mhunjile swanu, ne zifwalasi, hamonga ne zinyumbu. Ne nghani yiyo, yajendelela mwaka na mwaka.

25 Mutemi Solomona, kanyhi yali yena mabanda elufu yine go kuwicila matuka gakwe go wulugu, hamonga ne zifwalasi zakwe. Kanyhi yali yena ne wapanda zifwalasi, waficile elufu yikumi na nyejete. Wanji wa muli wawa, yakawawika mu Yelusalemi, na wono wasigala yakawawika mu makaya hayo hayo.
26 Yakawa mutemi muwaha muli watemi wose wa mu'si, ku wulawa kwi 'kolongo lya Efulata, sunga ko ku yisi ye Wafilisiti, ko ku mupaka we yisi ya Misili.
27 Na mu vilo vyo wutemi wakwe, fweza yali nha cinhu ca bwete du mu Yelusalemi, yikawa nha mabwe du. Ne mibici ye mihagata, nayo yali minji muno, yikawa nhe mibici ye mikuyu yono yili mu mwalambalo we matunda ga Yuda.
28 Wakamuletela Solomona, zifwalasi ku wulawa ku Misili, na ku zi'si zinji.

Wulolose we ndumula, kuli zo wutemi wa Solomona

(1Wat 11:41-43)

29 Ne zinghani ze ndawililo yo Wutemi wa Solomona, ku wulawilila mwamulizo, sunga ko ku ndumo yakwe, zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo za Natani muwizi, na mu wuwizi wa Ahiya munya Shilo, sunga na mu wugubulilwe wa Ido u muwizi, kanyhi mono yali na mu vilo vya Yelobowamu u mutemi wa Isilayeli.
30 Solomona yatema Waisilayeli wose mu Yelusalemi, myaka makumi mane.
31 Solomona yakafwa, yakazikwa mwi'kaya lya Dawudi. Na Lehobowamu u mwanagwe, yakahala wutemi wakwe.


2 Zinghomo 10

I we milango ya wono wekalaga ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, wakugaluka

(1Wat 12:1-20)

1 Ale, Lehobowamu yakawa kubita ku Shekemu, kono wali wehangulizize wanhu wose wa Isilayeli, we milango ya wono wekalaga ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, ho wamunoze yawe mutemi.
2 Ninga Yelobowamu u mwana wa Nebati, mono yamutiza Solomona, yakawa kutiziza ku Misili, yali yeza kuzihulika zizo, hodu yakawa kuwuya kukaya.
3 Nawo i wanhu wose we milango ye Waisilayeli, wono wekalaga ku lubavu lwe sumuka, wakamucema Yelobowamu, yakaza, hanyuma wose hamonga na Yelobowamu, wakawa kumubitila Lehobowamu, wakawa kumutya,
4 “U sogo Solomona, yacitumyaga vibi muno, no kucidinghuza mutegulo muzito. Holeka agwe hono wucicepulile wuwanda wuwo wo sogo, no mutegulo wakwe muzito, wono yacidinghuzaga, ale, nase cokwitumamila.”

5 Nayo Lehobowamu yakawatya, “Ale mumhelaje vilo vidatu, ane nihwese nghani yiyi.” Hanyuma ho muze kanyhi nimupenyi lipitulo. Hodu nawo wanhu wakawa kwiyagala.
6 Mutemi Lehobowamu, yakawawuza i wanyamhala wono wamutazaga u sogwe mu zinghani, lono yali yakali mumi, yakawatya, “Ale, ta, munhazaje, mumhele lipitulo lyono nowapitula wanhu wawa.”

7 Nawo i wanyamhala, wakamupitula wakamutya, “Agwe hono wusace wuwetumamile wanhu wawa viswanu, ale, weze kuwapela mapitulo gono gowakwila mu visakwa vyawo, holeka nawo wojendelela nhondo zose kukuhulika.”

8 Ninga mwene Lehobowamu, yakazibedegala zinghani ze wanyamhala, yakawa kuwawuza wazika wayagwe, wono lulo wali wo watazi wakwe mu zinghani, yakawatya,
9 “Alu, wulenyi, nanye mukuwona wule? ‘Alu mbite nghawapitule, nitye nhawule, vyono wene wakusaka niwacepulile mutegulo?’ ”

10 Nawo i wazelelo, i wazika wayagwe, wakamupitula wakamutya, “Alu nagwe wubite wukawalonjele wutye nhavi, ‘Cidole nene cangu, ciwaha cicizize ciwino ca baba Solomona.
11 Holeka hono yawe baba yali kumudinghuzenyi mutegulo muzito, alu ane nowonjeleza mutegulo wenyu wuwe muzito kuciza. Na hono yali kumukunenyi no mukotya, ane nomukunenyi muno muno no mujeledi we mamiya!’ ”

12 Vyali vyeza kupuluta vilo vidatu, Yelobowamu hamonga ne wanhu wose we Waisilayeli, wakawa kumubitila kanyhi mutemi Lehobowamu, nha vyono yali yawalonjeye.
13 Ninga mutemi Lehobowamu, vyono yazibedegala zinghani ze wanyamhala, yakawa kulongana ne wanhu ko wukali muno muno. Yakagatyatya mahweso ge wazelelo, i wazika wayagwe, yakawa kuwalonjela wanhu yakutya,
14 “Baba Solomona, yamudinghuzajenyi mutegulo muzito, heya, ninga ane nomonjelezenyi mutegulo wenyu wuwe muzito muno. Mwene yali kumukunenyi no mukotya, ninga ane nomukunenyi muno muno no mujeledi we mamivya.”
15 Na wuwo, wali wo wuhile wo Muwaha Mulungu, ziwe nhavivyo, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulonjela Yelobowamu u mwana wa Nebati, mu nzila yo muwizi Ahiya munya Shilo. Na yiyi yo yaleka mutemi Lehobowamu si yasaka yazihulicize ze wanhu.

16 Nawo wanhu waza kuwona nha vyono mutemi Lehobowamu si yakuwahuliciza yaye, hodu, wakazozeza wose hamonga wakutya,

“Mwene nye,

ase cina yigawoci ne mhuga ya Dawudi!

Hamba wuhazici wono cina muli mwana wa Yese?

Nye nyo wanhu wa Isilayeli,

alenyi ciyagale, ciwuye ku zikaya zetu!

Mulece kumutyatya kanyhi Lehobowamu,

mumulekaje yali wene!”

Hodu, hobaho i wanhu wa Isilayeli, wakawa kumugaluka mutemi Lehobowamu.
17 Wakawa kumuleka Lehobowamu yawe mutemi we wanhu wono wekalaga mu yisi ya Yuda du.
18 Hanyuma mutemi Lehobowamu, yakamutuma Adonilamu, mono yali yo mulanjilizi muwaha wa wetuma milimo wose, yabite kuli Waisilayeli. Ninga Adonilamu yali yeza kufika kuko, Waisilayeli wakamutowanga ne mabwe sunga yakafwa. Na ku soko ya zizo, hodu Lehobowamu yakawa kupanda musunho mwi'tuka lyakwe lyo wulugu, yakawa kutiziza ku Yelusalemi.
19 Na ku wulawa mwizuwa lilyo, hodu i Waisilayeli wo wutemi wa ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, wakawa kuyigaluka nyumba ya Dawudi sunga nalu.


2 Zinghomo 11

U wuwizi wa Shemaya

(1Wat 12:21-24)

1 Nayo mutemi Lehobowamu yali yeza kufika ku Yelusalemi, yakawakonghola wasilikale vitung'ati wo wulugu, waficile elufu yigana limonga na makumi munana, mu wulawa mu wo mulango wa Yuda na wa Benyamini. Yakawa yena wuhile wo kubita wakehome na we milango ye Waisilayeli, wono wali wali ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, ho yawugomole kanyhi wutemi wakwe.
2 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu, yakamulonjela muwizi Shemaya vyono
3 yabite kuli mutemi Lehobowamu na kuli wanhu wose we milango ya Yuda na Benyamini, yawalonjele yatye nhavi,
4 “Mulecele lukulu kwihoma ne wandugu zenyu Waisilayeli. Langa wose muwuye ku zikaya zenyu, hambi azi zono zijeye, nha wo wuhile wangu nene no mweneco.” Hodu, nawo wakawa walihulika lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, na ku soko yiyo, si wabita kanyhi kwihoma na Yelobowamu.

Lehobowamu yakugazenjela makaya magomawakali

5 Lehobowamu yakekala mu Yelusalemi. Yakagazenjela magomawakali i makaya ga Yuda na Benyamini.
6 Ne makaya gono yagazenjela magomawakali gali goga: Betelehemu, Etamu, Tekowa,
7 Betizuli, Soko, Adulamu,
8 Gati, Melesha, Zifu,
9 Adolayimu, Lakishi, Azeka,
10 Zola, Ayijaloni na Hebuloni.
11 Yagazenjela magomawakali gono gakangaye muno muno, yakawika ne wasilikale walanjilizi mu makaya gose. Na mu makaya gose gago, yakawika ndigwa nyinji, ne mafuta, hamonga ne divayi.
12 Yakawika ngula, ne migoha. Na mu nzila yiyi, yakasuma kuwatema viswanu i wanhu wa Yuda na Benyamini.

Iwakohani hamonga ne Walawi, wakuza ku Yuda

13 Ale, i wakohani wose ne Walawi, wono wali wali ku mbavu ze yisi yose ya Isilayeli, wose wakaza kutakama, ku yisi ya Yuda.
14 Walawi wakawuleka wudimilo wawo we mitugo, hamonga ne hanhu hono wali wapejigwe wekale, wakahamila ku yisi ya Yuda na Yelusalemi, vyono mutemi Yelobowamu, we Waisilayeli hamonga ne wahazi wakwe, si wawakombola Walawi, ho wetume milimo yawo yo wukohani, kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
15 Hodu, nayo Yelobowamu yakawa kwiwicila wakohani wakwe mweneco, wono wosuma kwituma mulimo wo wukohani kuli milungu mu wuhembelo wawo, no kuhemba masetani, ne vihwani vyono yavinoza mweneco Yelobowamu, mu hwani ye zindama.
16 Ninga u wulawa mu milango yose ye Waisilayeli, wono zinhumbula zawo zasaka kumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu du, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli, wene wawo wakawatyatya i Walawi, vikabitana nawo sunga ko ku Yelusalemi, ho wakamuhembe Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo, no kumulavizya makumbiko.
17 Ne nghani yiyi, yikawupela vilungo muno wutemi wa Yuda. Wakamutaza muno Lehobowamu u mwana wa Solomona. Kanyhi vikekalana viswanu muno nha vyono wali wali mu wutemi wo mutemi Dawudi no mutemi Solomona.

We mhuga ya Lehobowamu

18 Lehobowamu yakatola mucekulu litagwa lyakwe Mahalati, u mwana we cicekulu wa Yelimoti, u mwana wa Dawudi. U nyina wa Mahalati, yacemwaga Abihayili u mwana we cicekulu wa Eliyabi, kanyhi yo mwizukulu wa Yese.
19 Nawo wakalela wana wadatu, Yewushi, na Shemaliya, na Zahamu.
20 Hanyuma yayo kanyhi, yakamutola Maka u mwana we cicekulu wa Abusalomu, wakalelana wana wane, Abiya na Atayi, na Ziza, na Shelomiti.
21 Hamba nhavivyo, Lehobowamu yakajendelela kutola, sunga yakafisa wagolece yikumi na munana. Kanyhi yakawa na wajitulwa waficile makumi mutandatu. Yakalela wana we cilume waficile makumi mejete na munana, ne wana we cicekulu nawo waficile makumi mutandatu. Ninga muli wacekulu wose wawo, hamonga ne wajitulwa wose, mwene Lehobowamu, yakamwenda muno muno Maaka, si nha wawo wose.
22 Kanyhi yakendezwa muno muno na Abiya u mwanagwe u mulelwa na Maaka, si nhe wanagwe wanji wose. Yakamuhagula yo yawe yo muhazi wakwe wo wutemi.
23 Kanyhi Lehobowamu yanoza cihalahala, yakaweyagaza i wanagwe wose mu yisi yose ya Yuda na Benyamini, yakawapela wuwaha mu makaya gose ge magomawakali. Yakawapela na ndigwa nyinji, yakawalondolela ne wagolece wenji.


2 Zinghomo 12

Wamisili wakuyihoma yisi ya Yuda

(1Wat 14:25-28)

1 Kukawa lono wutemi wa Lehobowamu wali wakondya kukama, nayo yatuga vilungo, hodu, mwene hamonga ne wanhu wakwe wakawa kulileka Lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu.
2 Na mu mwaka we 'hano wa mu wuteme wakwe Lehobowamu, u Muwaha Mulungu yakawaletela wulipicizwe ku soko yo wula cihuliko wawo. U mutemi Shishaki wa Misili, yakaza yakayihoma Yelusalemi,
3 yakawa yena lipuga yiwaha lye wasilikale, lyono lyali lina matuka go wulugu, gaficile elufu monga na magana mejete, hamonga ne wapanda fwalasi, waficile wasilikale elufu makumi mutandatu, ne wasilikale wenji muno muno wasina ne liwalo. Wakawa wahangahanjile ne wasilikale wono walawa ku Libiya ne Wasuki, ne Wakushi.
4 Yakagasola makaya ga Yuda gono gali mazenjele magomawakali, yakajendelela sunga ko ku Yelusalemi.

5 Na lono mutemi Lehobowamu, hamonga ne walongola wose wa mu yisi ya Yuda, wali wekongholeye ha Yelusalemi wakumutiza Shishaki, muwizi Shemaya yakawabitila, yakawalonjela yakatya, “Ayi yo nghani yo Muwaha Mulungu kuli'nye, yakutya, ‘Vyono anye mwandeka, ane nane nakondya kumulecenyi mu miwoko ya Shishaki.’ ”

6 Nayo mutemi Lehobowamu, hamonga ne walongola wose, wakavumila nha vyono mbeka wetuma wubi. Wakawa kutya “Mbeka zono Muwaha Mulungu yakwituma, zo ze ling'ani.”

7 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yali yeza kuziwona zizo, hodu yakamutuma kanyhi muwizi Shemaya, yakumutya, “Alu, vyono wanhu wawa wakondya kuwuvumila wubi wawo, hodu, nane' lu si nowongomeza yaye. Ninga langa, kolo mutemi Shishaki yeza kuwahoma, ane nowaponya. Nayo Yelusalemi si yoliwona likuwo lyangu.
8 Ninga Shishaki kowasuma, nawo baho ho womanyila wisimajile wo kuwahemba nene, no kuhemba watemi wa mono yisi.”

9 Nayo mutemi Shishaki, yakawa kuza ku Yelusalemi, yakawa kuyisola sawo yose yono yali mbice mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga na mu kaya yo wutemi. Yakasola vinhu vinji du, hamonga ne zahabu, na ngula zono mutemi Solomona yazinoza.
10 Nayo mutemi Lehobowamu, yakanoza ngula zinji za shaba, yakaziwika mu miwoko ye walindilizi we milango ye likulu.
11 Na lono yenjilaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, i walindilizi wakwe, wazisolaga ngula, wawa kubitana nayo. Na lono yalawilaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, i walindilizi wakwe nawo wazigomolaga ngula hanhu hayo ho wuwiko wayo.
12 Na vyono mutemi Lehobowamu yakacila kwinenenyheza hali Muwaha Mulungu, hodu, ne likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, si lyamongomeza yaye. Ne zinghani zose zikawa kujendelela viswanu mu yisi ya Yuda.

Wulolose we ndumula, kuli zo wutemi wa Lehobowamu

13 Lehobowamu yakatema mu Yelusalemi, ne vilungo vyo wutemi wakwe, vikonjelezeka muno. Nayo yali yaficile myaka makumi mane na monga lono yatemela. Yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka yikumi na mupunghati, mu kaya yono Muwaha Mulungu yayihagula mu wulawa mu yisi yose ye Waisilayeli, hawe ho hanhu hono mweneco kowa kuhembelwa. U nyina wa Lehobowamu, yacemwaga Naama, munya yisi ya Amoni.
14 Lehobowamu yetuma zimbi, vyono si yasaka mweneco kuwulondola wuhile wo Muwaha Mulungu.

15 Na zose zono yetuma Lehobowamu, ku wulawilila mwamulizo, sunga ko ku ndumilizo, hamonga ne zinghani ze we mhuga yakwe, zose zijeye mu citabu ce zinghomo co muwizi Shemaya, hamonga na mu nghomo yo muwizi Ido. Nayo Lehobowamu na Yelobowamu, wekalaga wakwihoma du vilo vyose.
16 Lehobowamu yakafwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi mu kaya ya Dawudi. Abiya u mwanagwe yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.


2 Zinghomo 13

Abiya wakwihoma na Yelobowamu

(1Wat 15:1-8)

1 Na mu mwaka we likumi na munana wo wutemi wa Yelobowamu muli Isilayeli, nayo Abiya, ho yatemela mu Yuda.
2 Abiya yatema myaka midatu du mu Yelusalemi. U nyina wa Abiya yacemwaga Mikaya u mwana we cicekulu wa Wuliyeli, munya kwi'kaya lya Gibeya.

Hodu, wukawa kugwa wulugu, Abiya na Yelobowamu, wakawa kwanza kwihoma.
3 Abiya yakawawikaniza wasilikale waficile elufu magana mane liwalo lyawo. Ninga Yelobowamu, nayo yakawikaniza wasilikale elufu magana munana liwalo lyawo, kuciza u winji we wasilikale wa Abiya.

4 Hodu, wasilikale wawo, wakawa kwitang'anilila mu yisi ye matunda ya Efulayimu. Mwene mutemi Abiya, yakawa kubita mucanyha ye litunda lya Zemalayimu, yakawa kwanza kumucemeleza Yelobowamu hamonga ne wanhu wakwe Waisilayeli, yakutya, “Ale, muhulicizaje ta!
5 Alo mwene si mumanyile yaye, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli, yawika cilagane na Dawudi cono si cilabeneka yaye, nha vyono, i watemi wose wa Isilayeli wolowa mu lulelo lwakwe cibitilila?
6 Ninga Yelobowamu u mwana wa Nebati, u mwitumami wa Solomona, yakamugaluka mutemi wakwe Solomona.
7 Hanyuma yakawakonghola hamonga mhuga ye wanhu we caka caka, wono wasina mahala, wono welajila nha wali vilungo ha mwande ya Lehobowamu u mwana wa Solomona, mono lulo yali yakali muzelelo mudodo, yasina wuzowele wuwaha ho yasume kuwemila.
8 Alu anye mukusaka mwisumanize ne vilungo vyo Muwaha Mulungu vyono yawapela wana wa Dawudi no lulelo lwakwe. Kanyhi mukwijela muza muli yipuga yiwaha muno lye Wasilikale, akuno mwina ne vihwani vye zindama ze zahabu, zono Yelobowamu yamunozezenyi, ziwe milungu yenyu.
9 Mukawawinga i wakohani wo Muwaha Mulungu, i wana wa Haluni, kanyhi mukawawinga ne Walawi. Nanye mukawawika wakohani wanji nha vyono wakunoza we zinghungugo zinji, ho wahalile hanhu hawo. Kanyhi no munhu wo wose hono koleta ndama, hamba nduwilu mupunghati, ayo langa kosuma kwisiwanula yawe mukohani we milungu yenyu.

10 “Ninga'se, cikali cikumwitumamila u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, cikali namuleka yaye. Ne wakohani wana wa Haluni, wo wakwituma milimo yawo yo wukohani, nawo Walawi, wakuwataziliza.
11 Na nhondo zose, hakali mitondo ne cinyamihe, wakumulavizya Mulungu, wuvumba hamonga ne vilavywa vinyanywe vye zimitugo. Kanyhi wakulavya ne vilavywa vye mikate yono yelile, wakuyijeleka mu meza. Kanyhi nhondo zose cinyamihe, wakukoleleza zitala mucanyha yo wuwiko wayo wa zahabu. Nase cikwituma nhondo zose, zono Muwaha Mulungu yalajiliza, ninga anye mwakondya kumuleka.
12 Kanyhi ase co wayenyu, u Mulungu yo mutonji wetu, ne wakohani wakwe wali baha cina nawo, wena ne zindulele, wewikanize wazitowe, ho waciceme cinjile mu milewo yo kwihoma nanye. Holeka anye nyo wanhu wa Isilayeli, mulecele lukulu kwihoma no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wanyu! Ninga hamba nhavivyo, si mosumila lukulu!”

13 Ninga kumbe Yelobowamu mwi'pango lilyo, yakawa yakondya kuwategula wasilikale wakwe wanji wakondya kuwapilima wanhu wa Yuda, ku migongo yawo. Na wono wasigala, vikawa vidomile.
14 Nawo wa Yuda, wali weza kupituka wakawona vyono wakondya kupilimwa ne walugu wawo. Hodu wakawa kumulilila Muwaha Mulungu yawataze, nawo wakohani wakatowa zindulele.
15 Nawo wa Yuda wakazozeza wose hamonga, hobaho wakawa kuwazumhila Waisilayeli no kuwahomanga, hasi yo wutonji wa Abiya. Nayo Mulungu yakamusuma Yelobowamu hamonga ne lipuga lyakwe lye Wasilikale lye Waisilayeli.
16 Waisilayeli hobaho wakawa kutiza ha mwande ye Wayuda. Nayo Mulungu yakawapela Wayuda cisumisumi, wakawasumiliza Waisilayeli.
17 Abiya ne wanhu wakwe, wakawahomanga muno mono Waisilayeli no kuwasuma. Na mwizuwa lilyo, wakawulagwa wasilikale vitung'ati we Waisilayeli, waficile elufu magana mahano.
18 Nhavivi vyo wasuma wanhu wa Yuda kuli Waisilayeli. Hambi nha wali wamwitumbiye Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo.

19 Abiya yakamulonda Yelobowamu ne wasilikale wakwe, yakagasola ne makaya gakwe ganji, Beteli, Yeshana, na Efuloni. Hamonga ne vitumbi tumbi vyose vyono vigapilime makaya gago.
20 Na mu vilo vyo wutemi wa Abiya, Yelobowamu si yatuga vilungo kanyhi. Sunga ndumilizo, Muwaha Mulungu yakamutowa Yelobowamu, yakafwa.

21 Nayo mutemi Abiya, yakajendelela kuwa ne vilungo. Abiya yali yatolile wacekulu yikumi na wane, yakawa yelelile wana we cilume makumi mejete na wejete, ne wana we cicekulu yikumi na mutandatu.
22 Ne zinghani za Abiya zono zisigaye, zono yalonga na zono yetuma, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce nghomo yo muwizi Ido.


2 Zinghomo 14

U mutemi Asa, yakuwasuma Wakushi

1 U mutemi Abiya, yakafwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi, mwi'kaya lya Dawudi. Na Asa u mwanagwe, yakahala wutemi wakwe. Na mu cipango co wutemi wa Asa, wanhu wakawa wena mulamu, sunga yikapuluta myaka yikumi.
2 Asa yakamwendeza muno Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe, so wituma ziswanu na ze ling'ani.
3 Yakaviwusa vikumbicilo vijenzi, hamonga ne hanhu hose ho wuhembelo we wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu. Yakagabenanga masumbili ge mabwe, yakavidumulanga vihwani vyose vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela.
4 Yakawalajiliza wanhu wose wa Yuda wetume wuhile wo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo, wagebate ne mejizo gakwe, hamonga no wulajilize wakwe.
5 Hambi nha yakondya luno, kuhahodanga hanhu hose ho wuhembelo we wanhu wono si wamumanyle Mulungu, hamonga ne vikumbicilo vyose vyo kufusiza wuvumba, hanhu hose mu makaya gose ga Yuda. No wutemi wukawa wutulile mu vilo vyo wutemi wakwe.
6 Yakazenga magomawakali mu makaya ga Yuda, mu vilo vyakwe. Na hakawa hasina wulugu wo wose sunga myaka yikatela, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yamupela mulamu.
7 Nayo yakawalonjela wanhu wa Yuda yakawatya, “Alenyi cigazenje makaya, cigapilimizye magomawakali, cigazenjele ne minala, ne milango, hamonga ne mapajicilo, ho ciwe kuyidinda milango no kuyipajika. Hambi ase nha cikuyilongoza yisi, vyono cikwituma zo wuhile wo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu. Nayo yakucidima, kanyhi yakucicitila mbavu zose.” Hodu wakawa kuzenga, no kutuga wumoteswe.
8 Nayo mutemi Asa, yali yena mhuga ye wasilikale, walawa mu yisi ya Yuda, wena ne zingula hamonga ne migoha, waficile elufu magana madatu. Na wanji walawa ku yisi ya Benyamini, nawo waficile elufu magana mejete na makumi munana, nawo wena ne zingula zawo, hamonga no wupinde wawo. Ne wasilikale wawa wose, wali wejizwe muno mulimo wo wusilikale wo wulugu, kanyhi vitung'ati.

9 Hakawa ho yuli Mukushi, litagwa lyakwe Zela, munhu yuyo, yakaza hamonga ne lipuga lyakwe lye wasilikale wo wulugu, waficile milyoni monga, hamonga ne matuka go wulugu, gaficile magana madatu, yakayihoma yisi ya Yuda, yakajendelela sunga ko ku Malesha.
10 Hodu, Asa nayo yakawa kubita vikehome nayo. Nawo wose mbavu zose, wakawa kutandala mwilolo lya Zefata, behi na Melesha, wanhu na lubavu lwawo.
11 Asa yakawa kumulomba Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe, yakutya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, agwe yo gwegwe du, mono wudahile kuwapela wutazi lipuga lyono lisina vilungo, kuli lyono lili vilungo. Alu, wucitaze 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wetu, hambi ase nha cikwitumbiye gwegwe du. Na mwi'tagwa lyako, holeka nase cijelela kuza cihome ne lipuga lili liwaha. Agwe go Muwaha Mulungu, agwe yo gwegwe yo wuli Mulungu wetu, hamba hasina mono kokwihwesa nha vyono yadahile yakusume agwe.”

12 Nayo Asa yali yeza kwanza kwihoma ne Wakushi, u Muwaha Mulungu yakawasuma Wakushi, wakawa kutiza.
13 Nayo Asa, hamonga ne lipuga lyakwe lye wasilikale, wakawalondelela Wakushi sunga wakawafisa ku Gelali. Na mu nzila yiyo, wasilikale wenji we Cikushi wakawulagwa muno, hambi nha wali wawula vilungo vyo kwihomela. Muwaha Mulungu yali yakondya kuwawusiza vilungo vyawo. Ne lipuga lye wasilikale wa Asa, wakasola vinhu vinji vye walugu.
14 Hanyuma ya zizo, wakagongomeza makaya gose, gono gali gayipilime Gelali, hambi i wanhu wose wa mumo, nha wali wakondya kugwilwa ne cogofyo co Muwaha Mulungu. Holeka lipuga lye wasilikale likasuma kuvisola vinhu vyose vyono vyali vili mu makaya gose.
15 Wakazihoma sunga ne zinghambi ze wadimi, wakasola mumo zingholo nyinji muno, hamonga na ngamiya nyinji. Hanyuma ya zizo, nhondo wakawa kuwuya ku Yelusalemi.


2 Zinghomo 15

Wunozeleze wa Asa

1 Lulo u Muhe wo Mulungu, wukamuziza Azaliya u mwana wa Obedi.
2 Nayo yakawa kubita kwitang'anila no mutemi Asa. Yakawa kumulonjela yakutya, “Gwe go mutemi Asa, hamonga nanye mo wanhu wa Yuda na Benyamini, ta muhulicizaje! U Muwaha Mulungu yali hamonga nanye, lono nanye mukali mukumuhulika mwene. Na hono mujendelele kumulondola, nayo kokwijela hali'nye, ninga hono mumulece, nayo komulecenyi.
3 Hambi nha wulawa vilo vinji, Waisilayeli wali wekaye wasina Mulungu we mbeka, hamba Wakohani, wono wali wawejize, kanyhi wali wasina hamba wulajilize, wono wali wawutyatye.
4 Ninga wali weza kwinjila mu magazo, hobaho wakamuwuyila Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli. Wakamulondola, wakamuwona.
5 Na mu vilo vivyo, hali hasina munhu mono yalawilaga, hamba mono yenjilaga ko mulamu, hambi nha hali hasina mulamu. Hakawa na mahwele mawaha, muli wanhu wose wono wekaye mu'si zose.
6 Wakawa wakwiyihoma weneco wose, nghungugo na nghungugo, yikaya ne'kaya, hambi u Mulungu nha yo yawapela magayo gago gose.
7 Ninga anye mwisindamice, mulece kogopa. Vyono u mulimo wono mukuwituma, nha wowa na cihumbilizwa.”

8 Mutemi Asa, yali yeza kuzihulika zo muwizi Azaliya u mwana wa Obedi, zono yazilonjile, yakapegwa vilungo. Yakaviwusiza lukulu vihwani vyose vye mawipizo mu yisi yose ya Yuda na Benyamini, na mu makaya gose gono yagasola, ga mu yisi ye matunda ya Efulayimu. Yakacinozeleza kanyhi ne cikumbicilo co Muwaha Mulungu cono cali cili ha mwande ye likumbo lye Nyumba yo Mulungu.

9 Ne wanhu wenji wono walawa ku yisi ya Efulayimu, na Manase, na Simiyoni, hamonga ne wajenzi wose wono wekalaga mu wutemi wakwe, wali weza kuwona nha vyono Asa yali hamonga no Muwaha Mulungu, hobaho wose wakawa kumuziza Asa. Nayo Asa yakawakonghola wose, hamonga ne wanhu wa Yuda na Benyamini.
10 Wakekongholela ku Yelusalemi mu mwezi we kadatu wa mu mwaka we likumi ne'hano wo wutemi wa Asa.
11 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo, wakalavya makumbiko mu wulawa mu vinhu vyono waviteka ku wulugu, ng'ombe magana mupunghati, na ngholo elufu mupunghati.
12 Wakanoza cilagane, nha vyono womuhemba Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo, kwe zinhumbula zawo zose.
13 No munhu wo wose mudodo hamba muwaha, we cilume hamba wecicekulu, mono si komuhemba Muwaha Mulungu, ayo yawulagwe.
14 Nawo wakamwilahiza Muwaha Mulungu kwe lizi yiwaha muno, nha vyono wocibata cilagane. Hanyuma wakazoza muno akuno wakutowa ne zindulele.
15 Ne wanhu wose wa Yuda, wakawa no wusangalale muno, vyono wacinoza cilagane cico kwe zinhumbula zawo zose. Wakamuhemba Muwaha Mulungu ko wendezwe wawo wose, nayo yakawavumila, yakawapela mulamu mbavu zose.

16 Mutemi Asa, yakawa kumuwusiza lilumbo u nyina yakwe Maaka, lyo kucemwa u nyina yo mutemi, ku soko yanoza cihwani ce liwifyo co mulungu we cicekulu Ashela. Asa yakacidumula cihwani cico, yakacipondanjizanga, yakacikongoga mu moto mwi'lolo lya Kidiloni.
17 Asa hamba yawe si yahongomezeza lukulu hanhu hose ho wuhembelo we milungu yinji mu yisi yose, ninga mwene, yali vyo mwihuwilwa du kuli Muwaha Mulungu, mu mikalile yakwe yose.
18 Asa yavigomoleza kanyhi vinhu vyose vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, vyono u sogwe Abiya, yali yavisiwanuye kuli Mulungu, hamonga ne viya vye zahabu, na vye fweza, vyono mweneco nayo yavisiwanula.
19 Na kwali kusina milewo kanyhi, sunga mwaka we makumi madatu ne'hano wo wuteme wakwe Asa.


2 Zinghomo 16

Yuda na Isilayeli zikwitemanuka

(1Wat 15:17-22)

1 Na mu mwaka we makumi madatu na mutandatu, wo wuteme wo mutemi Asa wa Yuda, baho Baasha mutemi wa Isilayeli yakayihoma yisi ya Yuda, yakazenga Lama, ho yawemile wanhu walece kulawila hamba kwinjila mu yisi ya Yuda.
2 Nayo Asa yali yeza kuziwona zizo, yakasola fweza hamonga na zahabu, mu wulawa mu viwutwa vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu na vya mwi'kulu, yakawa kuvitegula ku Damesiki kuli mutemi Benihadadi wa Siliya, yakamutya,
3 “Ane nghukunena, cilunze hamonga nagwe, nha vyono i wasogo zetu nawo wali welunzize. Nane nghuletela fweza yiyo, hamonga ne zahabu, viwe cipegwa cako du. Holeka aluno nghukunena, wiwusaje mu wilunze wako na Baasha u mutemi we Waisilayeli, nayo ho kosuma kugawusa mapuga gakwe ge wasilikale mu yisi yangu.”

4 Nayo Benihadadi, yakayivumila nghani yiyo yono Asa yayilonga. Hodu ho baho, yakawatuma wetumami wakwe, hamonga ne mapuga ge wasilikale wakwe, wabite wakagahome makaya ge Waisilayeli. Nawo wakabita, wakayisola lyoni, Dani, Abeli-mayimu, hamonga ne makaya gose ge yisi ya Nafutali, i hanhu hono vyali viwicigwe viwutwa.
5 Nayo mutemi Baasha yali yeza kuyihulika nghani yiyo, hodu, hobaho yakawa kulecelela kuyizenga Lama, yakawuleka mulimo wakwe.
6 Hanyuma mutemi Asa yakawakonghola hamonga wanhu wose ku wulawa mu'si yose ya Yuda, yakawalajiliza wakagasole mabwe gose hamonga ne mabici gose gono Baasha yazenjelaga ku Lama. Wakagetumamila mu wuzenjela Geba na Mizipa.

U muwizi Hanani

7 Na mu madaha gago, u muwizi Hanani yakamubitila Asa mutemi wa Yuda, yakamutya, “Alu vyono kumwitumbila mutemi we Siliya, hamba si wumwitumbiye yaye Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako, holeka mapuga ge wasilikale wo mutemi wa Siliya waponile mu muwoko wako.
8 Ninga wule i Wakushi hamonga ne Walibiya, si wali wena mhuga mbaha muno ye wasilikale? Ninga vyono agwe wali wumwitumbiye Muwaha Mulungu, nayo yakakupela wusume wuwaha muli wene, hamba wawe wali wenji muno.
9 Ninga wumanye nha vyono, Muwaha Mulungu kayilangaga yinze yose, ho yawapele vilungo wose wono wamogopile mwene. Ninga agwe kunoza wulele. Holeka ku soko yiyo agwe ku wulawa lulu, kokwikalila milewo du.”
10 Ne nghani yiyi, yikamukoza Asa likuwo yiwaha kuli muwizi Hanani. Hodu yakawa kumwibata Hanani, yakamuwopa ne manyolo. Na mu madaha gago Asa ho yanziza kuwewonela wanhu no kuwatendela ko wukali muno.

Ndumilizo yo wutemi wa Asa

(1Wat 15:23-24)

11 Ne zinghani zose zono mutemi Asa yetuma, ku wulawilila mwamulizo sunga ko ku ndumilizo yakwe, zose zandicigwe mu citabu Zinghomo ze ce metumo ge watemi wa Yuda na Isilayeli.
12 Na mu mwaka we makumi madatu ne cenda, lono Asa yali yakali yatemile, yakebatwa no wutamwa wa migulu. Ninga hamba nhavivyo si yasuma kumuwuyila Muwaha Mulungu ho yatuje wutazi, langa mwene yakacila kubita kwilaguliza ku waganga.
13 Yaza kupuluta myaka makumi mane na monga yo wutemi wakwe myejete, hodu Asa yakawa kufwa.
14 Yakazikwa mu citenjelo ce'bwe, cono yewikaniziza mweneco mwi'kaya lya Dawudi, lono yali yakali cawumi. Wakawutajila muwili wakwe wuvumba ne mafuta ge mhunjile swanu so maziko gakwe. Wakakoleleza moto muwaha muno ku soko ye lilumbo lyakwe.


2 Zinghomo 17

Yehoshafati yakuwa mutemi

1 Yehoshafati yakahala wutemi wo sogwe Asa, yakawa ne vilungo muli Isilayeli.
2 Yakawika mapuga ge wasilikale mu makaya ge magomawakali gose ga mu yisi ya Yuda, sunga na mu makaya ga mu yisi ya Efulayimu gono Asa yagasola.
3 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakamumotya muno Yehoshafati, vyono Yehoshafati yatyatya musambo muswanu we mikalile miswanu yo sogwe Asa, ya halulongozi, hamba si yahemba Baali.
4 Mwene yamwitumamila du u Mulungu wo sogwe, yakagebata malajilizo go Mulungu, hamba si yetuma nha vyono i watemi we Waisilayeli wetuma.
5 Na ku soko yiyo, Muwaha Mulungu nayo, yakamupela Yehoshafati wusume wuwaha wo kusuma kuwulongoza wutemi wa Yuda. Ne wanhu wose wakamuletela vipegwa. Na ku soko yiyo Yehoshafati yakawa mugoli muno, kanyhi munhu we 'lumbo yiwaha muno.
6 Nayo yakahidimbala muno kumwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu, yakahongomeza i hanhu hose hono wahembelaga i wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu, hamonga ne vihwani vyose vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela mu yisi ya Yuda.

7 Na mu mwaka we kadatu, wa mu wuteme wakwe, yakawatuma wanhu awano, wakawejize wanhu mu makaya ga Yuda, nha cinji Benihayili, Obadiya, Zekaliya, Netaneli, hamonga na Mikaya.
8 Ne wanhu wawo mu wubite wawo, wakabitana hamonga ne Walawi, wali'cenda, ne Wakohani wejete. Nawo i Walawi yali Shemaya, Netaniya, Zebadiya, Asaheli, Shemilamoti, Yehonatani, Adoniya, Tobiya, Tobadoniya. Nawo wakohani wali wowa, Elishama na Yeholamu.
9 Wakacisola citabu co Wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu, wakabita mu makaya gose ga Yuda, wakawejiza wanhu.

Wukumuce wa Yehoshafati

10 Muwaha Mulungu yakawanoza wanhu wose wo wutemi wono wupilime, wogope kubita kwihoma no mutemi Yehoshafati.
11 I Wafilisiti wanji, wakamuletela Yehoshafati fweza nyinji muno, hamonga ne vipegwa vinji. Nawo Walabu wanji, wakamuletela ngholo zificile elufu mupunghati na magana mupunghati, hamonga na mhene nazo elufu mupunghati, na magana mupunghati.
12 Na ku soko yiyo, Yehoshafati yakacila muno kuwa ne vilungo mu wutemi wakwe. Yakazenga magomawakali hamonga ne makaya, mu yisi yose ya Yuda.
13 Nayo yakawika mumo viwutwa vinji muno muno.

Namo mu Yelusalemi, yakawika wanhu vitung'ati, wono wamanyile milewo,
14 mu nghango ye zimhuga zawo. Adina yali yo muwaha we mhuga ye wasilikale we mhuga ya Yuda, nayo yali yena wasilikale waficile elufu magana madatu hasi yakwe.
15 Na we kejete yali Yehohanani, nayo yali yena wasilikale waficile elufu magana mejete na makumi munana.
16 Na we kadatu, yali Amasiya u mwana wa Zicili, nayo yali yena wasilikale waficile elufu magana mejete cititila. (Yuyu Amasiya, yali yelavizye du, yamwitumamile Muwaha Mulungu.)
17 No muwaha we mhuga ye wasilikale wono walawa mu mhuga ya Benyamini, yali Eliyada, munhu citung'ati, nayo yali yena wasilikale waficile elufu magana mejete du cititila, wono wali wena ne zingula zawo, hamonga no wupinde wawo.
18 No mutyatyi wakwe we kejete, yali Yehozabadi, nayo yali yena wasilikale waficile elufu yigana limonga na makumi munana.
19 Ne wanhu wawo wose, wamwitumamilaga mutemi mumo mu Yelusalemi, hamonga ne wasilikale wanji kanyhi, wono yawawika mu makaya ganji ge magomawakali ga mu yisi ya Yuda.


2 Zinghomo 18

Muwizi Mikaya yakumusugula Ahabu

(1Wat 22:1-28)

1 Nayo Yehoshafati u mutemi wa Yuda, lono yali yagolipa muno, kanyhi yena yilumbo yiwaha, mwi'zuwa linji, yakamutoza yumonga wa muli we mhuga yakwe ne we mhuga ya Ahabu u mutemi we Waisilayeli.
2 Yaza kupuluta myaka, Yehoshafati yakawa kubita kwi'kaya lya Samaliya, yakamulamuse Ahabu. Nayo Ahabu yali yeza kumuwona mujenzi wakwe, hodu, yakawa kumuwulajila ngholo na ng'ombe nyinji. Ahabu yakanoza nhavivyo, ho yamupepeleze Yehoshafati, vibitane wakalihome likaya lya Lamoti lya ku Gileyadi.
3 Ahabu yakamuwuza Yehoshafati yakatya, “Wule Yehoshafati, cobitana wukanhaze ciyihome Lamoti?”

Nayo Yehoshafati yakamupitula yakamutya, “Heya, kolo wewikaniza, cobitana na nene, hamonga ne wasilikale wangu, ase wose cokutaziliza du.
4 Ninga langa camule cimuwuze Muwaha Mulungu mu nghani yiyi.”

5 Hobaho Ahabu yakakonghola wawizi waficile magana mane, yakawa kuwawuza yakutya, “Ati, wulenyi, nosuma kubita nghayihome Lamoti, hamba ng'o?” Nawo wawizi wakamupitula wakamutya, “Wubite wukayihome, nayo Mulungu kokupela wusume du.”

6 Ninga Yehoshafati yakenjilila hagati, yakawuza yakatya, “Wulenyi, hasina kanyhi muwizi yunji mono nayo cosuma kumuwuza zo wuhile wo Muwaha Mulungu?”

7 Nayo Ahabu yakapitula yakatya, “Heya, yakali ho yuli yunji yali baho, yakucemwa Mikaya u Mwana wa Imula. Ninga ane nimusulile muno muno, vyono mwene yakali nambilila nghani swanu hamba kamonga bwete, langa mwene kambililaga nghani 'mbi du nhondo zose.” Yehoshafati yakamutya, “Wulecele lukulu kulonga nhavivyo.”

8 Hodu Ahabu yakamutuma mwitumami wakwe yabite yakamuceme Mikaya, yaze mbela mbela.

9 Nawo watemi wawa vyono wejete, nha cinji Ahabu u mutemi we Waisilayeli, hamonga na Yehoshafati u mutemi we Wayuda, wose wakekala mu magoda gawo ge citemi ha cuga, ha winjililo wo mulango we deha wa Samaliya. Ne wawizi wose, wakawa wakuwila ha mwande yawo.
10 No muwizi monga wa muli wene, litagwa lyakwe Zedekiya u mwana wa Kenana, yakenozeza mahembe ga cuma, yakamulonjela Ahabu yakutya, “Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, ‘Mu mahembe gaga, kosuma kuwahoma wanhu wa Siliya, nagwe kowasumila lukulu.’ ”
11 Ne wawizi wanji wose wakalonjela kumonga wakatya, “Agwe wubite du wukayihome Lamoti, no Muwaha Mulungu kokupela wusume wuwaha.”

12 Hanyuma nayo mwitumami mono yatumwa yakamuceme Mikaya, yakamulonjela Mikaya yakutya, “I wawizi wayago wanji wose, wamuwilila mutemi nha vyono, mutemi kosuma du. Holeka nagwe, wubite wukalonje nhavivyo.”

13 Ninga Mikaya yakamupitula yakamutya, “Ane nghwihuwiza mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu mumi, nha vyono nolonga du zono mweneco Muwaha Mulungu kononjela nizilonje!”

14 Nayo Mikaya yali yeza kufika hali mutemi Ahabu, mutemi Ahabu yakamuwuza yakutya, “Ati Mikaya, Mutemi Yehoshafati hamonga nane, cosuma kubita cikayihome Lamoti, hamba ng'o?”

Mikaya yakamupitula yakamutya, “Heya wubite, nane nitumbiye nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu kokupela wusume.”

15 Ninga Ahabu yakamupitula Mikaya yakamutya, “Gwe Mikaya, hono wuwe wukulonganila nane mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu, yikunojeye wunonjele ze mbeka! Ne nghani yiyi, nha nghulonjeye kali kenji muno?”

16 Nayo Mikaya yakamupitula yakatya, “Ane nghusuma kuwawona Waisilayeli, wose weyagaye mu matunda wenha zingholo zono zisina mudimi. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, ‘I wanhu wawa, wasina mulongola wawo, holeka muwalece wawuye ku zikaya zawo wapolile.’ ”

17 Nayo Ahabu, yakamulonjela Yehoshafati yakatya, “Ale, wawona ta! Zizi zo nghulonjeye, nha vyono ayu Mikaya, ane si kambililaga ziswanu ng'o, langa kambililaga zimbi du nhondo zose!”

18 Nayo Mikaya yakajendelela kulonga du yakutya, “Alu Ahabu, wuhulicize ta, zono Muwaha Mulungu yakuzilonga. Nane namuwona Muwaha Mulungu yekaye mwi'goda lyakwe ku wulanga, hamonga ne wamalayika wakwe wamupilime.
19 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakuwawuza yakutya, ‘Alo nani mono komukoca Ahabu, ho yabite ku Lamoti yakawulagwe?’ Nawo wamalayika wakegawa hejete, wanji wakalonga nhavi, na wanji nhavi.
20 Nhondo likenuka licisi, likema hali Muwaha Mulungu, likatya, ‘Ane nobita nghamukoce.’ Muwaha Mulungu yakaliwuza yakatya, ‘Alo komukoca wuwe kutya nhawule?’
21 Nalyo licisi likapitula likatya, ‘Ane nobita, nghawanoze wawizi wose wa Ahabu, walonje zo wulambi.’ Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakalitya, licisi, ‘Ale wubite wukamukocekoce, nane nitumbiye vyono komusuma.’ ”

22 Kanyhi Mikaya yakonjeza yakatya, “Ne nghani yiyi vyo yiweye. Muwaha Mulungu kawanoza wawizi wako wakulonjele zo wulambi. Ninga mweneco u Muwaha Mulungu yo yalonga, nha vyono magazo si goleka kukwagana!”

23 Hobaho muwizi Zedekiya, yakamuzumhila Mikaya, yakamutowa kofi mu wusu wakwe, akuno yakumutya, “Alu 'gwe Mikaya, ku wulawa na nili, muhe wo Muwaha Mulungu lono wukandeka 'ne, ho wulongane nagwe?”

24 Mikaya yakamupitula Zedekiya yakatya, “Agwe kowona kolo weza kubita kwihundika mu cinyumba cimonga ca mu vyumba vya ku mhilimo.”

25 Hobaho mutemi Ahabu yakamulajiliza yumonga wa muli wetumami wakwe, yakatya, “Musolaje Mikaya, wumutegule kuli Amoni u muwaha we likaya, na kuli Yowashi u mwana wo mutemi.
26 Wuwalonjele wamudindile mu jela, wawe kumulisa mukate mukavu na malenga, sunga lono nilawuyila mholile.”

27 Nayo Mikaya yakamupitula Ahabu yakatya, “Hono wuze wuwuye wupolile, Muwaha Mulungu yeze si yalongane nane! Holeka nanye nyo wanhu wose, muhulice zono nonjile!”

U wufwe wa Ahabu

(1Wat 22:29-35)

28 Ale, mutemi Ahabu wa Isilayeli, hamonga no mutemi Yehoshafati wa Yuda, wakabita kuyihoma Lamoti ya Gileyadi.
29 Ahabu yakamulonjela Yehoshafati, yakamutya, “Alu lono ceza kubita ku wulugu, ane nokwipitula ninoze ndece kumanyika, ninga agwe, wuvwale vivwalo vyako vyo wutemi.” Hodu Ahabu mutemi we Waisilayeli, yakabita ku wulugu yepituye.

30 Nayo mutemi we yisi ya Siliya, yakawalajiliza wawaha we matuka go wulugu makumi madatu na mejete, nha vyono walece kumuhoma munhu yunji wo wose, langa wamuhomaje du Ahabu, u mutemi we Waisilayeli.
31 Ninga nawo wali weza kumuwona Yehoshafati, u mutemi wa Yuda, wakehwesa 'mba yo mutemi Ahabu, holeka wakanza kumuhoma. Ninga nayo Yehoshafati, yali yeza kuzoza, Muwaha Mulungu yakamuponya, yakawagomola ne nyumba wono wasakaga wamuhome.
32 Nawo wawaha walongola, we matuka go wulugu, waza kuwona vyono yo si mweneco mutemi we Waisilayeli, hodu wakawa kuleka kumutyatya.
33 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, u musilikale yunji we wanhu wa Siliya, yakayiguma songa yakwe ko wuganicize, na kumbe songa yikamuguma Ahabu u mutemi we Waisilayeli, ha wulunzizo we vivwalo vyakwe vye cuma. Ahabu yakamulonjela u mujendi wo mutuka wakwe wo wulugu, yakatya, “Wuwi! Natamiswa! Alu wuwuye ne nyuma, ciwuce haciligati yo wulugu!”
34 No wihomi lono wali wujendeleye, mwene mutemi Ahabu, yali yali mu mutuka wakwe yawalanjile Wasiliya. Na lono lizuwa lyagwililicilaga, nayo hobaho yakawa kufwa.


2 Zinghomo 19

U muwizi yakumukalipila Yehoshafati

1 Yehoshafati u mutemi wa Yuda, yakawuya ku kaya yakwe yo wutemi ku Yelusalemi yapolile.
2 Nayo muwizi Yehu u mwana wa Hanani, yakawa kubita kumuhinjiliza mutemi, yakamulonjela yakatya, “Alu wukwihwesa, vyono viswanu kuwataza i wabi, no kuwenda wono wamusulile Muwaha Mulungu? Holeka zizo zono witumile zo zilecile Muwaha Mulungu kakukolelwa likuwo.
3 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, zinji ziswanu, zikali zili muli'gwe. Vyono waviwusa vihwani vyose vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela mu yisi yose, na kanyhi vyono wukali wujendeleye kuwutyatya wuhile wo Mulungu.”

Wunozeleze wa Yehoshafati

4 U mutemi Yehoshafati, hamba yawe yekalaga mu Yelusalemi, ninga yawajendelaga wanhu wakwe kali kenji, ku wulawilila ku Belisheba ku lubavu lwa kutakama, sunga ko ku ndumilizo ye yisi ye matunda ya Efulayimu, ku lubavu lwa kusumuka. Ho yasume kuwakwega wanhu wamuwuyile u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo.
5 Nayo yakawika mahakimu mu makaya gose ge magomawakali, mu yisi yose ya Yuda,
6 yakawalonjela wawo mahakimu yakawatya, “Ale, mulanjilile mu wuling'anizi wenyu, vyono anye si mukumwitumila munhu, langa mukumwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu, nayo yali baha hamonga nanye mu wuling'anizi wenyu.
7 Holeka munojeye mumogope Muwaha Mulungu, mulanjilile mu witume wenyu, hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, nha si kawukangalilaga wungwele, hamba wuhijila mbejele kuli munhu, hamba wubocela mafupo.”

8 Na mu Yelusalemi namo, mutemi Yehoshafati, yakawika Walawi ne Wakohani, hamonga ne wawaha wa mu zi ndelwa wa mu zimhuga, mu zimhuga zawo, wawe waling'anizi wa mu vyalo, kuli zo wubena Lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, hamba muli zinghani zono wakwisoka, i wekazi wa mwi'kaya.
9 Nawo yakawalonjela yakawatya, “Nanye munojeye mwitume milimo yenyu kwe cogopo hali Muwaha Mulungu, mweze kumuhulika mwene ko wihuwilwa muli zose zono mowa kwituma.
10 Na hono wandugu zenyu wono wekaye mu makaya ganji ganji, wamuzizenyi wena vyalo vyo wuwulaji, hamba nghani yo yose yo wubena wulajilize, hamba zo wubena Lilajilizo, ale, anye muwalolosezaje mugolile, sunga wezuce viswanu vyono wosuma kucinoza calo cico, mwi'daha lyo wuling'anizwe. Ho walece kuwalilwa masutya go kumunozeza wubi Muwaha Mulungu. Na hono mulece kunoza nhavivyo, ale, nyenye, hamonga ne wandugu zenyu, wose nyenye, likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, lyomuzizenyi. Ninga hono mwitume viswanu milimo yenyu, hamba si mowa wasutyi yaye.
11 Nayo Amaliya u Mukohani Muwaha, kowa yo mudumula nghani ye ndumilizo mu vyalo vyose vye ci dini. Nayo Zebadiya u mwana wa Ishumayeli u mulongola wa wo mulango wa Yuda, nayo kowa yo mudumula nghani ye ndumilizo, mu vyalo vyose vye selekali. Nawo Walawi, wowa wo walanjilizi we zinghani zose, zono zikuling'anizwa mu mahakama, hono ziwe zikudumulwa muling'ano. Holeka mwisindamikaje, muzikamise zizi zono nimulonjelenyi. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yawe yali hamonga nanye mu wituma ziswanu.”


2 Zinghomo 20

Mutemi Yehoshafati yakwihoma ne wanhu wa Edomu

1 Hanyuma yayo, i mapuga ge wasilikale we Wamowabi, na ge Waamoni, hamonga ne wayawo Wameyunimu, wakaza ho wayihome yisi ya Yuda.
2 Wanhu wanji wakamubitila mutemi Yehoshafati, wakamulonjela wakutya, “Likuza yipuga yiwaha muno lye wasilikale likulawa ku Edomu, ku nhumbi nzagwe ye lilenga lya Shamu, likusaka lize likuhome. Sunga nalu, wakondya kuyisola Hazazoni Tamali.” (Ali lyo litagwa linji lye likaya lya Engedi.)
3 Nayo Yehoshafati yaza kuzihulika zizo, yakenjilwa no woga wuwaha muno, yakawa kulomba kuli Muwaha Mulungu yamupele wutonji. Hanyuma yakalajiliza yisi yose walece kulya.
4 Ne wanhu mu yisi yose ya Yuda, wakawa kubita musunho ku Yelusalemi wakamulombe Muwaha Mulungu yawalajile wutonji wakwe.
5 Nawo waza kufika ku Yelusalemi, wakehanguliza he 'kumbo lya hadeha ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga na wanya baho ha Yelusalemi. Ale, nayo mutemi Yehoshafati, yakabita yakema ha mwande ye wanhu wose.
6 Yakalomba kwe'zi waha yakutya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu we walezi wetu, mono wutemile muli makungugo gose ga mono'nze, kuko ku wulanga. Yo gwegwe du mono wuli vilungo, hamba hasina munhu wo wose mono kosuma kukwimila nghani yo yose.
7 Agwe yo gwegwe go Mulungu wetu. Kanyhi na lono i wanhu wako Waisilayeli wahamilila mono yisi yiyi, agwe wukawawinga wanhu wose wono wekalaga mumo, wukawapela i wana wa Abulahamu u mwendece wako yisi yiyi, ho yiwe yawo cibitilila.
8 Nawo wekala mumu, sunga wazenga ne Nyumba yako, ho walajile cogopo cawo ca kuli'gwe, akuno
9 wakumanya nha vyono, hono magazo go gose gawazize so wulipicizwe, lunji wulugu, hamba lihuhuji, hamba lizala, nawo wosuma kuza no kwima ha mwande ye Nyumba yako yiyi, i hanhu hono agwe wukuhembelwa. Wakulombe wutazi ku so magazo gono gawatuga, na vyono nagwe kowahulika no kuwaponya.

10 “Alu wulanje ta, i Waamoni ne Wamowabi, hamonga ne we yisi ya Edomu, wakondya kwanza kucihoma. Na lono walezi wetu wawucila ku yisi ya Misili, agwe si wawakombola i walezi wetu wenjile mu zisi zizo, yikawa yiwanojeye wapilime, wawalece, hamba kuwongomeza yaye.
11 Alu, wulanje vyono wene wakusaka wacitendele vibi, wakuza waciwinje mu yisi yiyi yono agwe wacipela yiwe yetu.
12 Agwe yo gwegwe Mulungu wetu! Holeka cikukulomba gwegwe wuwalipicize. Ase nha cisina vilungo vyo kwihomela ne lipuga lili liwaha lye wasilikale, lyono lyeza kuza kucihoma. Ase cisina yinji yono cosuma kwituma, langa cilanjiliye du wutazi wulawe kuli'gwe.”

13 Ne wanhu wose wa Yuda, hamonga ne wagolece zawo, ne wana wawo, wose wali wemile baho du ha Nyumba yo Mulungu.
14 Na mwi'daha lilyo du, u muhe wo Muwaha Mulungu, wukamuziza Mulawi yumonga mono yali yali hamonga nawo, baho mu wihangulize wuwo. Litagwa lyakwe yacemwaga Yahazayeli, u mwana wa Zekaliya, u mwene yali we mhuga ya wana wa Asafu, yali mwana wa Asafu, ku wulawilila mu ndelwa ya Mataniya na Yayiyeli, na Benaya.
15 Ale yuyo Yahaziyeli, yakatya, “Gwe Mutemi, hamonga nanye nyo wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, u Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, ‘Mulece kogopa hamba kwihwelela kwihoma ne lipuga lilyo liwaha lye Wasilikale. Hambi wulugu wuwu nha si wenyu nyenye, langa wo Muwaha Mulungu.
16 Holeka i mitondo anye muwabitilaje mukawahome, lono wowa wafika ha Zizi. Nanye mwitang'anile nawo ha ndumilizo ye lilolo, ha mwande ye nyika behi na Yeluweli.
17 Nanye kolo mwawagana, mulece kwihoma nawo. Langa anye mutandale du hanhu henyu, muwe kulindila, nanye mowona vyono Muwaha Mulungu yakumulewelenyi, no kumupelenyi wusume. Holeka anye nyo wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, mulece kuwa na woga. Anye mubite du ku wulugu, ninga Muwaha Mulungu kowa hamonga nanye!’ ”

18 Hobaho mutemi yehoshafati yekenama no wusu wakwe wukapamiza ho hasi, ne wanhu wose nawo nhavivyo, wakenama no wusu wawo wukapamiza ho hasi, wakamuhemba Muwaha Mulungu.
19 Nawo we mhuga ye Walawi, we mhuga ye Wakohati, na we mhuga ye Wakola, wakema wima, wakamulumba Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli kwe'zi yiwaha muno muno.

20 No wusiku wali weza kwela, i mitondo yaco, hakali mitondo muno muno, i wanhu wose wakawa kubita ku nyika ya Tekowa. Nawo lono wali wakondya kwiwikaniza ho watuluce, mutemi Yehoshafati, yakawalonjela yakatya, “Anye nyo wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi! Mumwitumbilaje Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kanyhi mweze mukamile. Kanyhi mwihuwile zinghani zono wawizi wakwe womulonjelenyi, nanye holeka motuga wuswanu.”
21 Nayo mutemi yali yeza kukondya kuwaloloseza wanhu zizo, hobaho yakalajiliza i wembi, wavwale myenda yawo yono wayitumamilaga mu zisikukuu ze vilo vyono vyelile. Hanyuma walongole ku mwande ye lipuga lyose lye wasilikale wawe kwimba wakutya, “Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe! Hambi lusungu lwakwe nha lwa cibitilila!”

22 Nawo iwembi wali weza kwanza kwimba, u Muwaha Mulungu yakawagumilila walugu wawo mahwele no woga wuwaha.
23 Hodu hobaho i Waamoni ne Wamowabi, wakawa kuwahoma Waedomu no kuwawulaga wose. Hanyuma nawo, wakawa kwigalucila weneco wose, wakehoma wulugu wukali muno.
24 Nawo wasilikale wa Yuda, wali weza kufika ha munala wa mu nyika, walanje nhavi, wakawawona walugu wawo wagwiye wose hasi, wafwiye. Na hakawa hasina hamba yumonga mono yasigala.

25 Hodu, Yehoshafati, hamonga ne lipuga lyakwe lye wasilikale, wakawa kubita kusola vinhu vye walugu wawo. Na vyali vinhu vinji muno, zali ng'ombe, na myenda hamonga ne vinhu vinji du vyo wuguzi wukamu. Wakekala baho vilo vidatu wena mulimo wo kulundika du vinhu. Hambi nha vyali vinji muno. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, si wasuma kuvisola vinhu vyose, hambi nha vyali vinji muno.
26 Na mwi'zuwa lye kane, wakekongholela he 'lolo lya Balaka, wakamulumba Muwaha Mulungu so zose zono yawatendeye. Ale, yiyo yo soko yono yikaleka i hanhu baho hakacemwa “Balaka sunga nalu.”
27 Mutemi Yehoshafati, nhondo yakawa kuwuya ku Yelusalemi, ko wusume wuwaha, hamonga ne wanhu wakwe, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yakondya kuwasumila i walugu wawo.
28 Nawo wali weza kufika he 'kaya, wakabita sunga ko ku Nyumba yo Mulungu, wakwimba akuno wakutowa ne zindonondo hamonga ne zindulele.
29 Ne makungugo gose gono gahulika vyono Muwaha Mulungu kawasumila Waisilayeli kuli walugu wawo, nawo hobaho wakenjilwa no woga wuwaha muno.
30 Ku wulawilila baho, nhondo Yehoshafati, yakatema ko mulamu, nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamudima mbavu zose.

Ndumilizo yo wutemi wa Yehoshafati

(1Wat 22:41-50)

31 Yehoshafati u mutemi wa Yuda lono yatemela, yali yaficile myaka makumi madatu na mihano. Yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi mejete na mihano. Unyina yacemwaga Azuba, u mwana we cicekulu wa Shilihi.
32 Nayo Yehoshafati yaketuma ziswanu hali Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono yetuma u sogwe Asa, mono yamulongolela kutema.
33 Ninga hanhu ho wuhembelo ha wanhu wono si wamumanyile u Muwaha Mulungu si hawuswa yaye. Ne wanhu wakekala si wamuwuyiye Muwaha Mulungu kwe zinhumbula zawo zose, na mu wuhembi wo kumuhemba u Mulungu we walezi wawo.

34 Ne zinghani zose zono Yehoshafati yetuma, ku wulawilila mwamulizo wo wutemi wakwe, sunga ndumilizo yayo, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce nghomo ya Yehu, u mwana wa Hanani. Kanyhi zo monga ne zinghomo ze watemi we Waisilayeli.

35 Na mu madaha ganji, u mutemi Yehoshafati wa Yuda, vikelunza no mutemi Ahaziya we Waisilayeli, mono yaketuma zimbi nyinji.
36 Wakanoza ngalawa zo kubitila ku Eziyoni-gebeli.
37 Ninga Eleyezali u mwana wa Dodavahu, munya cikaya ce Malesha, yakamusugula Yehoshafati, yakamutya, “Alu vyono mwelunza na Ahaziya, ale nayo Muwaha Mulungu kozibenanjiza zingalawa zako zono wuzinozize.” Na mbeka zingalawa zikabenanjika, hamba kwituma cinji yaye.


2 Zinghomo 21

1 Yehoshafati yakafwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi, mu kaya ya Dawudi. Yeholamu u mwanagwe yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Yeholamu u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 8:17-24)

2 Yeholamu u mwana wa Yehoshafati u mutemi wa Yuda, yali yena wanduguze wali mutandatu, Azaliya, na Yehiyeli, na Zekaliya, na Azaliyahu, na Mikayeli, hamonga na Shefatiya.
3 U sogo yawo, yakawapela vipegwa vinji vya zahabu, na fweza, hamonga ne vinhu vinji vyo wuguzi wukamu. Kanyhi yakawawika wawe wawaha mu makaya ga Yuda, gono gena magomawakali, munhu ne kaya lyakwe. Ninga Yeholamu vyono yali yo mwana mulongozi kulelwa, Yehoshafati yakamunoza yawe yo muhazi wo wutemi wakwe.
4 Baho Yeholamu wutemi wakwe wali weza kutuga vilungo, hodu, yakawa kuwawulaga i wanduguze wose, hamonga ne wetumami wanji we Ciisilayeli.

5 Yeholamu yatema yaficile myaka makumi mejete na myejete. Yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka munana du.
6 Ninga mwene yakatyatya zinzila zimbi za Ahabu na ze watemi wa Isilayeli, na vyono yali yinha nyumba ya Ahabu. Yaketuma zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu.
7 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu si yenda yawongomeze wutemi wa Dawudi, hambi nha yali yehuwiza kuli mwitumami wakwe Dawudi, nha vyono lulelo lwakwe, lojendelela kutema vilo vyose.

8 Na mu vilo vyo mutemi Yeholamu, wanhu wa Edomu, wakayigaluka yisi ya Yuda, wakamutemya mutemi wawo weneco.
9 Yeholamu yakatuluka hamonga ne matuka gakwe gose go wulugu, yakabita yakawahoma Waedomu wono wali wamupilime u mwene, ne matuka gakwe go wulugu, hamonga ne wawaha we matuka.
10 Nayo yisi ya Edomu yikayigaluka yisi ya Yuda, sunga nalu. Na mwipango lilyo du, i wekazi wa Libina nawo wakayigaluka Yuda, vyono Yeholamu yali yakondya kumuleka Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wakwe.
11 Na kanyhi yakazenga ne hanhu henji ho wuhembelo we wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu mu yisi ya Yuda. Yakawa kuwanoza i wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, waketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu.

12 U muwizi Eliya, yakamwandicila Yoholamu baluwa, yono yali yayandice nhavi, “Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wo mulezi wako Dawudi, yakukuhosa, vyono agwe si wawutyatya musambo muswanu wo sogo mutemi Yehoshafati, hamba musambo muswanu wo kukuyo mutemi Asa.
13 Ninga agwe, kutyatya musambo mubi we watemi wa Isilayeli, no kuwatonga wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, wawe si wehuwilwa kuli Mulungu, nha vyono Ahabu yanoza hamonga ne wahazi wakwe, wono wawatonga vibi Waisilayeli, wakawa si wehuwilwa. Kanyhi agwe kuwawulaga ne wanduguzo, nawo kumbe wo wali wanhu waswanu si nha gwegwe.
14 Holeka lu, ku soko ya zizo, nayo Muwaha Mulungu, kowalipiciza i wanhu wako ko wulipicizwe wuwaha muno muno, hamonga ne wana wenyu, ne wagolece zenyu, no kuvyongomezeza lukulu vinhu vyenyu vyose.
15 Nagwe go mweneco, kotuga wutamwa wubi wa munda, wono wojendelela konjezeka izuwa ne'zuwa sunga no wutumbu wako wulawile kunze.”

16 Hali ho wali Wafilisiti wanji, hamonga ne Walabu, wono wekalaga behi ne hanhu hono wali wekaye Wakushi mu mwalambalo we lilenga. Muwaha Mulungu yakawasagahiza wawo, wabite vikehome na Yeholamu.
17 Wakayihoma Yuda, wakayisola kaya yo wutemi, wakawasoleleza nha wafungwa i wagolece wose wo mutemi, hamonga ne wanagwe wose, ninga Ahaziya u mwanagwe u mudodo, yuyo langa wakamuleka.

18 Hanyuma ya zizo, Muwaha Mulungu yakamuletela mutemi Yeholamu wutamwa wo wusungu wukali mu'nda yakwe wono si wopona.
19 No wutamwa wuwo wukacila konjezeka mhola mhola, myaka myejete. Sunga nhondo, mutemi yakawa kufwa ko wusungu wuwaha muno muno. Ne wanhu wakwe si wakoleleza moto wuwe muwaha so kumulilila, nha vyono wawanozezaga i walezi wakwe.

20 Yeholamu yatema lono yali yaficile myaka makumi madatu na myejete, yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka munana du. Na hasina hamba munhu monga mono yamukalalila lono yafwila. Wakamuzika mu kaya ya Dawudi, ninga si wamuzika mu vitenjelo vye watemi yaye.


2 Zinghomo 22

Ahaziya u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 8:25-29; 9:21-28)

1 I walabu wanji wali, wakondya za nhinicizo no kuwawulaga i wana wose wo mutemi Yeholamu, langa Ahaziya u mwana mudodo, ayo si wamuwulaga yaye. Holeka i wanhu wa Yelusalemi, wakawa kumunoza Ahaziya, yawe mutemi yahale wutemi wo sogwe.
2 Ahaziya yatema lono yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na myejete, nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi mwaka monga du. Ninga nayo Ahaziya yatyatya musambo mubi we mhuga yo mutemi Ahabu, vyono u nyina Ataliya, u mwana we cicekulu wo mutemi Ahabu, kanyhi mwizukulu wo mutemi Omuli wa Isilayeli,
3 wamufunda vibi, wakamulongoza ku wituma zimbi.
4 Yaketuma zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu, vyono lono sogwe yakondya kufwa, mwene yakasola wanji wa mu mhuga yo mutemi Ahabu, yakawanoza wawe wo watazi wakwe mu zinghani, nawo wakamulongoza sunga ko ku wongomele.
5 Nayo yakatyatya wutonji wawo, yakelunza no mutemi Yolamu wa Isilayeli mu milewo, wakehoma na Hazayeli mutemi wa Siliya ku Lamoti ya Gileyadi. Ninga mutemi Yolamu yakatamiswa muno, yakatuga nghomele kuko ku wulugu.
6 Holeka yakawa kuwuya, yakabita kwikala kwi'kaya lya Yezeleli, ho wamutamye zinghomele zakwe. Nayo mutemi Ahaziya, yakabita kumulanga.

7 Ale, no wongomele wa Ahaziya, waletwa no Mulungu, vyono Ahaziya yakabita kumulanga Yolamu. Vyono lono Ahaziya yali yali kuko kwa Yolamu, vikatulukana na Yolamu, wakawa kubita kuli Yehu u mwana wa Nimushi. U munhu mono Muwaha Mulungu yamuhagula yawongomezeze lukulu we nyumba ya Ahabu.
8 Na lono Yehu yanozaga wuling'anizi wo kuyilipiciza nyumba ya Ahabu, hobaho yakawagana walongola we Wayuda, hamonga ne wana we wanduguze wa Ahaziya wono vikazana na Ahaziya wamulanje Yolamu. Hobaho Yehu yakawa kuwawulaga wose.
9 Ninga Ahaziya mweneco, yakehundika, nawo wakamulondola sunga wakamwagana yehundice ku Samaliya. Wakamusola wakamutegula kuli Yehu, wakawa kumuwulaga. Ninga wakamuzika kwe 'lumbo, vyono u kukuye u mutemi Yehoshafati, yamwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu kwe nhumbula yakwe yose.

Na ku wulawa baho, hakawa hasina kanyhi wa mu nyumba ya Ahaziya mono yawibata wutemi.

Ataliya, u mutemi we cicekulu wa Yuda

(2Wat 11:1-3)

10 Nayo Ataliya, u nyina yo mutemi Ahaziya, yali yeza kumanya nha vyono mwanagwe Ahaziya yakondya kuwulagwa, hobaho nayo, yakalajiliza nha vyono i wana wose we citemi we nyumba ya Yuda wawulagwe.
11 Nayo Ahaziya yali yena u lumbulye mono yacemwaga Yehosheba, mono yali mutolwe no mukohani, litagwa lyakwe Yehoyada. Ale yuyo Yehosheba yakamuponya yumonga wa muli wana wa Ahaziya, mono yacemwaga Yowashi ho yalece kuwulagwa na Ataliya. Mwene yakamusola, yakamuwusa mwi'puga lye wana we watemi wono wali wawikanizwe wawulagwe, yakamuwika mu citanda cono cali cili mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakamuhundika mumo, yakamuponya, ho yalece kuwulagwa na Ataliya.
12 Yakamuhundika sunga yikapuluta myaka mutandatu, mu vilo vyono Ataliya yali yakali yatemile.


2 Zinghomo 23

Ataliya yakugalukwa

(2Wat 11:4-16)

1 Ninga mu mwaka wo mupunghati, Yehoyada mukohani, yakawalajiliza wetumami walongola we walindilizi wo mutemi. Yakanoza cilagane ne wawaha wahano we wasilikale, Azaliya u mwana wa Yelohamu, Ishumayeli u mwana wa Yehohanani, Azaliya u mwana wa Obedi, Maseya u mwana we cilume wa Adaya, hamonga na Elishafati u mwana wa Zikili.
2 Wakagajendela makaya gose ga Yuda, wakawakonghola Walawi, hamonga ne wawaha wose we milango ye Waisilayeli, wakawaleta ku Yelusalemi.

3 Wose wakawa kwikongholela mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, wakanoza cilagane na Yowashi u mwana wo mutemi Ahaziya. Nayo Yehoyada, yakawalonjela yakatya, “Nye wayangu! Ayuno ahano yo mwana wa malehemu mutemi Ahaziya. Nayo 'luno yo kowa mutemi, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yehuwiza vyono u lulelo lo mutemi Dawudi, wo wowa kutema.
4 Nase yiyi yo coyinoza. Holeka, kolo wakohani ne Walawi wakuza mu milimo yawo, mwizuwa lye Sabato, wanji wolindiliza milango ye Nyumba yo Mulungu,
5 na wanji wolilindiliza likulu. Na wono wowa wasigaye, awo wowikwa ha mulango wo musinji. Ne wanhu wose wokwihanguliziza hamonga mwi'kumbo lye Nyumba yo Mulungu.
6 Kanyhi howa hasina munhu wo wose mono kokwinjila mu Nyumba yo Mulungu hamba kulawaniza, langa wawo du wakohani ne Walawi, wono wowa wali mu wipiganze. Wawo du wo wosuma kwinjila, hambi wene nha welile. Ninga wanhu wose wono wasigaye, yiwanojeye webate muno muno wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu, wekale kunze.
7 Nawo i Walawi wowa wemile wamupilime mutemi wakumudima, wowa webate ne vitwalo mu miwoko yawo. Yowa yiwanojeye wawe hamonga no mutemi du ko kose kono kowa kubita. No munhu wo wose mono kokwijelela yenjile mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, ayo kowulagwa.”

8 Nawo Walawi, hamonga ne wanhu wose wa Yuda, wakanoza nhavivyo nha vyono yawalajiliza Yehoyada. Ne wanhu si wakombolwa weyagale yaye, i wawaha wakawa kusola munhu na wanhu wakwe, muli wono wokwinjila mu mulimo mwi'zuwa lye Sabato, hamonga na wono wowa wacehile mwi'zuwa lilyo, wawo wose wakajendelela no mulimo. Hambi u mukohani Yehoyada, nha si yawakombola wawe no wilecele.
9 Baho, mukohani Yehoyada, yakawa kuwapela wawaha wose, migoha hamonga ne zingula, zono zali zo mutemi Dawudi, zono zali mbice mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
10 Yakawika wanhu wono wali webate ne mimaje mu miwoko yawo, wakatandala wapilime ha mwande ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, ho wamudime mutemi.
11 Hanyuma Yehoyada mukohani, yakawa kumulavya Yowashi hanze, yakamuvwika mukumbo mu mutwe wakwe, yakamwibatiza mu miwoko, u walaka wo wulajilize, wono womutonga mu wutemi wakwe. Na hobaho yakawa yanozwa kuwa mutemi. Hanyuma Yehoyada mukohani, hamonga ne wanagwe, wakawa kumwitila Yowashi mafuta go witile, ne wanhu wose wakazozeza hamonga wakatya, “Mutemi yawumipe!”

12 Nayo Ataliya yali yeza kuhulika mazi ge wanhu, hamonga ne mazi ge walindilizi, wakumukumya mutemi, hobaho mwene yakenjila musunho mu Nyumba yo Mulungu kono wanhu wali wekongholeye.
13 Na kuko, yakamuwona mutemi mupya, yemile ha mulango we Nyumba yo Mulungu, behi ne lisumbili lyono lyawikwa so watemi du, yapilimwe ne wawaha we wasilikale, akuno ne zindulele zikutowa. Ne wanhu wose wazozize muno so nyemo, akuno wakutowa ne zindulele, hamonga ne wembi wa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, nawo wakawa wakuzilongoza zinyimbo zo lukumyo. Hobaho mwene Ataliya, yakawa kuvidemulanga vivwalo vyakwe ko wusungu wuwaha muno, akuno yazozize yakutya, “Wugaluci! Wugaluci!”

14 Ninga Yehoyada mukohani, si yenda Ataliya yawulajilwe mu ciwanza ce Nyumba yo Mulungu. Holeka yakawacema i wawaha we wasilikale, yakawatya, “Ale, musolenyi mumuwice haciligati ye walindilizi, kanyhi mweze mwiwikanize, mumuwulaje munhu wo wose mono kojela kumutaziza.”

15 Hobaho wakawa kumwibata, wakawa kumutegula sunga ko kwi kulu, wakamuwulajila ha mulango we zifwalasi.

Wunozeleze wa Yehoyada

(2Wat 11:17-20)

16 Mukohani Yehoyada, yakamusola mutemi Yowashi, hamonga ne wanhu wose, wakanoza cilagane hamonga nayo, nha vyono wowa wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu.
17 Hanyuma wakawa kubita wose hamonga sunga ko ku wuhembelo wa Baali wakawumwagulanga. Wakavitulanga vikumbicilo, hamonga ne vihwani vyose, wakamuwulaga na Matani, u mukohani wa Baali, wakamuwulajila ha mwande ye vikumbicilo.
18 Yehoyaga mukohani, yakawawika wakohani ne Walawi, wawe wo walanjilizi we milimo yose ya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Nawo wakawa wakwituma milimo yawo, nha vyono wawicilwa no mutemi Dawudi, hamonga no kunyanya makumbiko i malavye kuli Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono winha wulajilize wa Musa. Kanyhi wakawa wo walanjilizi we zinyimbo hamonga no wityatye we zisikukuu zose.
19 Kanyhi Yehoyada mukohani, yakawawika walindilizi, walindilize ha milango ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, ho walanjilile munhu wo wose mono mwavufywe, yalece kwinjila.

20 Nawo i wawaha we wasilikale, ne walongola we wanhu hamonga ne wetumami wose ne wanhu wose wono wasigaye, wawo wose, wakelunza na Yehoyada mukohani, wakawa kujenda watunjile wose hamonga, wakumuusindiciza mutemi, ku wulawa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, wakumtegula ku kaya yakwe yo wutemi. Nawo wali weza kufika kwi'kulu, wakenjilila ku mulango muwaha. Nayo mutemi yakawa kwikala mwi'goda lyakwe lyo wutemi.
21 Ne wanhu wose wakawa na nyemo mbaha muno, ne likaya likawa litulile muno, hambi Ataliya nha yali yakondya kuwulagwa.


2 Zinghomo 24

Yowashi u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 12:1-16)

1 Yowashi, yakatema mu yisi ya Yuda, lono yali yaficile myaka mupunghati du. Yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi mane cititila. U nyina wa Yowashi yacemwaga Zibiya munya kwi'kaya lya Belisheba.
2 Nayo Yowashi, mu vilo vyo wutemi wakwe yaketuma zono zamwendezaga Muwaha Mulungu, mu vilo vyose vyo wikale wa Yehoyada mukohani, lono yakali mumi.
3 Nayo Yehoyada mukohani, yakamuhagulila mutemi Yowashi wacekulu wejete, yakawa kuwatola. Nawo wacekulu wawo wakamulelela Yowashi wana we cilume na we cicekulu.

4 Nayo mutemi Yowashi, yali yeza kwikala vilo vilo mu wutemi wakwe, yakawona viswanu yayinozeleze Nyumba yo Mulungu, mono yihodanjice.
5 Na ku soko yiyo, yakawatuma wakohani, hamonga ne Walawi, wabite mu yisi yose ya Yuda, wawalonjele wanhu, wahangulize zipesa zono zokwila mu wunozeleze we Nyumba yo Mulungu. Yakawalonjela nha vyono, wayinoze nghani yiyo mbela mbela, ninga Walawi wakacela.
6 Hobaho yakamucema Yehoyada mukohani u mulongola wawo, yakamuwuza yakatya, “Alo socici si wawatyatilila i Walawi, ho wasume kukonghola kodi, mu yisi ya Yuda na Yelusalemi, kodi yono Musa u mwitumami wo Muwaha Mulungu, yayikongholaga kuli wanhu, izipesa zizo, ho zisume kutaza milimo ya mwi'hema lyono lyelile?”

7 (Hambi i watyatyi wa Ataliya, u mucekulu yulya mubi, nha wali wakondya kuyimwagula Nyumba yo Mulungu, wakasola viya vinji muno vyono vyelile, wakavitumamila mu wuhembi we Mabaali.)

8 Nayo mutemi Yowashi, yakawalajiliza i Walawi, wanoze lisanduku, lyono lyokwitumamilwa so kulavizya vilavywa, waliwice ha mulango we Nyumba yo Mulungu.
9 Nawo Walawi, wakawa kuwalalika wanhu wose wa Yelusalemi na Yuda, vyono yiwanojeye wose wamulavizye kodi Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono Musa u mwitumami wo Mulungu, yayikonghola kodi ha lulongozi mu nyika.
10 Ne nghani yiyi, yikawendeza wanhu wose hamonga ne walongola wawo, nawo wakawa kuzileta zipesa zawo ze kodi, wakalimemya lisanduku twaa.
11 Na nhondo zose lono lisanduku lyakondya kumema, i Walawi walidinghulaga no kulitegula kuli wetumami wo mutemi, wono wali wo walanjilizi. Nayo u mwandici wo mutemi, hamonga no mulindilizi wo Mukohani Muwaha, wawa kuzijida zipesa, no kuligomola kanyhi lisanduku hanhu hayo. Na mu nzila yiyo, wakonghola pesa nyinji muno muno.

12 Ale u mutemi nayo, hamonga na Yehoyada mukohani, wawa kuzisola zipesa zizo, wawa kuwapela i walanjilizi wo mulimo wo wunozelezi we Nyumba yo Mulungu, nawo wawa kuwalipa i wazenji ne waselemala, hamonga ne watyani wono watyanaga vyuma, ne shaba, wono wayinozelezaga Nyumba yo Mulungu.
13 Ne wazenji wawa wose, wetumaga wahidimbaye muno mu milimo yawo, wakayinozeleza viswanu muno Nyumba yo Mulungu, yikawa nha vyono yali halulongozi.
14 Nawo wunozelezi we Nyumba yo Mulungu wali weza kukondya, zahabu yose yono yasigala, hamonga ne fweza, wakayileta kuli mutemi hamonga na Yehoyada mukohani. Nawo wakanoza zibakuli ne viya vye hayo hayo so Nyumba yo Mulungu.

Zinzila za Yehoyada mukohani zikukawanizwa

Mu vilo vyose vyono Yehoyada yali yakali cawumi, makumbiko galavizwaga nhondo zose ha Nyumba yo Mulungu.
15 Ninga Yehoyada yali yeza kuficila mu myaka yigana limonga na makumi madatu, yakawa yakombipa muno, yakawa kufwa.
16 Wakamuzika mu vitenjelo vye watemi mwi'kaya lya Dawudi, ku soko vyono yetuma ziswanu muli Waisilayeli, na kuli Mulungu, hamonga na mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.

17 Ninga Yehoyada mukohani yali yeza kufwa, hodu, i walongola we Wayuda, wakawa kubita kumupepeleza mutemi Yowashi, nayo yakawahulicila zinghani zawo.
18 Na ku wulawa baho, i wanhu wakawa kupela ku wubita kuhemba ku Nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo, wakawa kwanza kuhemba vihwani, hamonga ne cihwani co mulungu we cicekulu Ashela. Na ku soko yo wubi wuwo, likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu likawaziza wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi.
19 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, u Muwaha Mulungu, yakawategulila wawizi, wawasugule wamuwuyile mwene. Ninga i wanhu wakalema hamba si wawahuliciza yaye.
20 Hanyuma u muhe wo Mulungu wukamuziza Zekaliya u mwana wa Yehoyada mukohani. Hobaho Zekaliya, yakabita yakema i hanhu hono kosuma kuwa yejeye hali wanhu wose, yakawa kuwalonjela wanhu yakutya, “Nye wayangu, u Muwaha Mulungu yakumuwuzenyi, nhawule mwagabeza i malajilizo gakwe, nalu mweza kwiyiletela wongomezwe nyenye nyo weneco! Hambi nha mwakondya kumuleka mwene, holeka nayo yakondya kumulecenyi nhavivyo!”
21 Nayo mutemi Yowashi yakelunza ne wanhu ho wamutendele zimbi Zekaliya. Hobaho mutemi yakalajiliza wanhu wamutowanje Zekaliya ne mabwe. Nawo wakawa kumutowanga no kumuwulajila he'kumbo lye Nyumba yo Mulungu.
22 Ati hobaho du, mutemi Yowashi yakawa yakondya kuzisemwa zo wuswanu zose, zono Yehoyada u sogwe wa Zekaliya yamutendela, hobaho yakawa kumukombola Zekaliya yawulagwe. Ninga nayo Zekaliya lono yafwizaga, yalonga yakatya, “Ale du, u Muwaha Mulungu yaziwonaje zono mukuzituma, nanye yamulipicizenyi!”

Ndumilizo yo wutemi wa Yowashi

23 Na ha ndumilizo yo mwaka wuwo, i wasilikale wanya Siliya, wakaza, wakayihoma Yuda na Yelusalemi, wakawawulaga i walongola wose, wakateka vinhu vinji muno, wakabita navyo ku Damesiki.
24 Ne lipuga lye wasilikale wa Siliya, lyali yipuga yidodo du, ninga Muwaha Mulungu, yakawapela wusume wo kuwasuma wasilikale wenji wa Yuda, vyono wene wali wakondya kumuleka Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo. Na mu nzila yiyi, mutemi Yowashi mo yalipicizizwa.
25 Nayo Yowashi, yakatuga nghomele mbaha, nawo i walugu wali weza kuwaleka, wanji wejete wa muli wetumami wakwe, wakamugaluka wakamuwulaga lono yali yagonile mu citanda cakwe, wakawa kwilipiciza ku soko yo wuwulagwe wa Zekaliya u mwana wa Yehoyada mukohani. Yowashi yakazikwa mwi'kaya lya Dawudi, ninga si wamuzika mu vitenjelo vye watemi.
26 (Ne wanhu wono wamugaluka, yali Zabadi u mwana wo mucekulu we Ciamoni mono yacemwaga Shimeyati, hamonga na Yehozabadi u mwana wo mucekulu we Cimowabu mono yacemwaga Shimuliti.)
27 Na mu mandiko gono gali mu citabu ce Zinghomo ze metumo ge watemi, mumo mo mozili ne zinghani ze wana wa Yowashi, no wuwizi wono Yowashi yawililwa, hamonga na ze nyituma yakwe, yo mulimo wo wunozelezi we Nyumba yo Mulungu.

Nayo Amaziya u mwanagwe, yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.


2 Zinghomo 25

Amaziya u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 14:2-6)

1 Amaziya lono yatemela, yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na mihano, yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi mejete ne'cenda. U nyina yacemwaga Yehoyadini munya Yelusalemi,
2 Amaziya yetuma zono zamwendeza Muwaha Mulungu, ninga si yetuma na nhumbula nhuvu yaye.
3 Nayo yali yeza kutuga vilungo mu wutemi wakwe, hobaho yakawawulaga i wetumami wawalya wono wamuwulaga sogwe u mutemi Yowashi.
4 Ninga i wana wawo, awo langa si yawawulaga yaye, yatyatya zono Muwaha Mulungu yalajiliza mu Wulajilize wa Musa, zono zikutya, “I walezi si wofwa ku soko yo wubi we wana wawo, hamba wana nawo si wofwa ku soko yo wubi we walezi wawo. Langa munhu kofwa ku soko yo wubi wakwe mweneco du.”

U wihomi ne Waedomu

(2Wat 14:7)

5 U mutemi Amaziya, yakawawikaniza wanhu wo mulango wa Yuda na wa Benyamini, yakawenjiza mu mulimo wo wusilikale, yakawawika mu vipuga vipuga, mhuga na cipuga cayo, mu nghango ye zimhuga zawo vyono zali. Hanyuma yakawa kuwawika wawaha walanjilizi, we vipuga vye wanhu elufu monga monga, na we vipuga vya wanhu yigana limonga limonga. Ne wanhu wawa, wali wahangahanjile hamonga, ku wulawilila wono wali waficile myaka makumi mejete, hamonga ne wanhu mututu, ne liwalo lyawo wose hamonga, wali waficile wanhu elufu magana madatu cititila. Wawo wakasolwa, wakategulwa ku wulugu. Nawo wali wanhu wono wazoweye muno mu witumamila migoha, hamonga ne zingula.
6 Hamonga na wawo, yakalomba ne wasilikale wanji ku wulawa ku Isilayeli, yakawalipa fweza zo wuzito wa kilo elufu yidatu na magana mane, ho waze wamutaze.
7 Ninga ho bahobaho, u muwizi yakamubitila mutemi Amaziya, yakamulonjela yakamutya, “Gwe mutemi, wulecele lukulu kuwasola wasilikale wawa we Ciisilayeli. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yakondya kuwaleka wanhu wawo wo wutemi wa ku lubavu lwa kusumuka.
8 Agwe wukwihwesa nha vyono wawo wokutaza mu milewo, ninga yo Mulungu du, mono yena vilungo vyo wusume hamba vyo wusumwe, nayo yali wene du mono kosuma kuwaleka walugu wako wakusume.”

9 Nayo Amaziya, yakamuwuza muwizi, yakatya, “Alu, ma, zifweza zangu zono nakondya kuwalipa, ho zobita du za bwete?”

Muwizi nayo yakamupitula Amaziya yakatya, “Wukumbucile nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu kosuma kukuhaliza nyinji zicizize zizo!”
10 Hodu, Amaziya nhondo yakawa kuwaluwula wawalya wasilikale, yakawakombola wawuye ku zikwawo. Nawo wakavumila ninga kunji si wendile, wakatuluka wakomaye, ku soko ye wanhu wa Yuda.

11 Amaziya yakawa kwikamiliza, yakawa kuwategula wasilikale wakwe ko kwi'lolo lyo Mwino. Wakawa kwanza kwihoma kuko ne wasilikale we Ciedomu, ne wasilikale we Ciedomu wakawulagwa waficile elufu yikumi.
12 Na wanji wakatekwa nawo waficile elufu yikumi. Wakawa kuwasola wasilikale wawo wono wawateka, wakawategula kwi'kaya lya Sela, wakawawika mu vizuli vye mabwe matali, wakawa kuwagumila hasi. Ne wasilikale wose, wakavulumuka wakwitowezanga mu mabwe, wose, wakafwa, hamba yunji kusigala yaye.

13 Akuno kono nyuma nako, wasilikale wawalya we Ciisilayeli, wono Amaziya yawalema walece kubitana nayo ku wulugu, nawo wakabita wakagahoma makaya ga Yuda haciligati ya Samaliya na Bete-holoni, wakawulaga wanhu elufu yidatu, wakaviteka ne vinhu vinji muno.

14 Nayo Amaziya lono yawuyaga ku wulawa kuwahoma no kuwasuma Waedomu, yakasoleleza ne vihwani vyawo, yakavisimya, yakavihemba, yakawa yakuvifusiza no wuvumba.
15 Ne nghani yiyi, yikamukoza muno likuwo Muwaha Mulungu. Yakawa kumutuma muwizi kuli Amaziya. Nayo muwizi yakamutya Amaziya, “Alu 'gwe Amaziya, ho kosuma nhawule kuyitumbila milungu ye wanhu wanji, yono si yosuma hamba kuwaponya wanhu wayo, mu vilungo vyako?”

16 Nayo Amaziya yakapitula musunho, yakatya, “Mwene 'gwe, ku wulawa na nili cikakuwika wuwe mulonjelezi wo mutemi? Holeka wunyamale lulu baha. Na hono wujendelele, ane nokuwulaga!” Nayo muwizi yakatya, “Ale hodu'ne nanyamala. Ninga manyiye lukulu vyono Mulungu yakondya kudumula nghani, yakongomeze, ku soko ya zizo zono witumile, na vyono kanyhi, kuyibedegala ne nghani yangu.”

U wihomi ne Waisilayeli

(2Wat 14:8-20)

17 U mutemi Amaziya wa Yuda, hamonga ne walonjelezi wakwe, wakelumbaniza wabite wakayihome yisi ya Isilayeli. Na ku soko yiyo yakatuma wanhu wabite kuli Yehowashi u mutemi wa Isilayeli, u mwana wa Yehoyahazi, wabite wakamusagale ne nyono, ho wehome.
18 Nayo Yehowashi yakamupitula Amaziya yakatya, “Mwi'zuwa linji, i cibici co mufuku, cono cali cili mu matunda ga Lebanoni, cawulonjela mubici wo muhagata, cikatya, ‘Wumhelaje mwanago we cicekulu, yatolwe no mwanangu.’ Ninga lilimu likali likakola baho, likacibenanjiza cibici co mufuku.
19 Mwene Amaziya, alu wedunhumula, vyono wawona wawasuma Waedomu hongo. Ane nghuwona vyono, ciba wulece kwikumya, wucehe du ha kaya yako. Alu 'gwe nhawule wukusaka wiyambuze bwete, sunga wuwaletele wanhu wako wongomele wuwaha?”

20 Ninga mwene Amaziya, yakalemela lukulu kuzihuliciza zizo. Hambi nha wali wo wuhile wo Mulungu, Amaziya yasumwe, vyono yali yakondya kwiyinjiza mu wuhemba vihwani vye Waedomu.
21 Nayo Yehowashi u mutemi wa Isilayeli, yakawa kwinjila mu wihomi na Amaziya mutemi wa Yuda. Waketang'anilila ha Bete-shemeshi, mu yisi ya Yuda.
22 Ninga i wasilikale wa Yuda wakasumwa, wakawa kutiziza ku zikaya zawo.
23 Yehowashi, yakamuteka Amaziya, yakamutegula ku Yelusalemi. Nayo Yehowashi, yali yeza kufika ku Yelusalemi, yakagakumulanga majinji ge likaya, ku wulawilila ku mulango wa Efulayimu, sunga ko ku mulango wa ha cipeto, wugazi wa kotya mita magana mejete nhavi.
24 Yakazisola ne zizahabu zose, ne zifweza za mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, ne viya vyose vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, vyono vyali vili mu wulanjilizi we wana wa Obedi Edomu, ne viwutwa vyose vya mu nyumba yo wutemi, yakavisola vyose hamonga ne wanhu wose wono yawateka.

25 Nayo Amaziya mutemi wa Yuda, yajendelela kuwumipa myaka yikumi na mihano, lono Yehowashi u mutemi wa Isilayeli yali yakondya kufwa.

26 Ne zinghani zose zono Amaziya yetuma ku wulawilila mwamulizo, sunga ku ndumo yo wutemi wakwe, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce Zinghomo ze watemi wa Yuda na Isilayeli.
27 Ninga ku wulawa bahalya, lono yamubezeza Muwaha Mulungu, hodu wakamunozeza mvivi mu Yelusalemi. Sunga nayo yakawa kutiziza kwi'kaya lya Lakishi. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, i walugu wakwe, wakamulonda wakamuwulajila kuko.
28 Langa muwili wakwe wakawudinghulila mu fwalasi, wakawuleta ku Yelusalemi. Wakamuzika mu vitenjelo vye watemi, mwi'kaya lya Dawudi.


2 Zinghomo 26

Uziya u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 14:21-22; 15:1-7)

1 I wanhu wose wa Yuda, wakawa kumwihaga Uziya u mwana wa Amaziya, yaficile myaka yikumi na mutandatu, yahale wutemi wo sogwe.
2 (Yali yeza kufwa Amaziya, ho baho Uziya ho yayisolela kanyhi Elati, no kulizenga kanyhi likaya.)

3 Uziya yatema lono yali yaficile myaka yikumi na mutandatu, nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi mahano na myejete. U nyina yacemwaga Yekoliya munya ku Yelusalemi.
4 Nayo Uziya yakatyatya musambo muswanu wo sogwe Amaziya. Yakamwendeza Muwaha Mulungu mu witume wakwe.
5 Na mu mapango gose, lono Zekaliya yali yakali mumi, mono yamutazaga muli ze nghani yo Mulungu, Uziya yakamwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu ko wihuwilwa, na ku soko yiyo, nayo Mulungu yakamulugutiza.

6 Hanyuma mwi'zuwa linji, Uziya yakabita kwihoma ne Wafilisiti. Yakagamwagulanga majinji ge makaya ga Gati, na Yamuniya na Ashidodi. Mwene yakazenga makaya ganji ge magomawakali, behi na Ashidodi, hamonga na ku lubavu lono lwasigala lwe yisi ye Wafilisiti.
7 Nayo Mulungu, yakamupela wusume wuwaha kuli Wafilisiti, hamonga ne Walabu wono wekalaga ku Gulibali, hamonga ne Wameyuni.
8 Sunga ne Waamoni wakalipa kodi kuli Uziya. Nayo yakawa ne vilungo viwaha, ne lilumbo lyakwe likatandala sunga ko ku Misili.

9 Mutemi Uziya, yakalizenjela magomawakali likaya lya Yelusalemi, yakazenga ne minala ha mulango we cipeto, na ha mulango wa kwi'lolo, sunga ne hanhu hose hono lijinji lyali lina cipeto.
10 Kanyhi yakazenga minala mu nyika, yakahimba ne malamba menji muno, hambi nha yali yena matewa mawaha ge zimitugo, mu mwalambalo we matunda go lubavu lwa kumwezi, sunga na mu yisi yo wuhihi. Hambi mwene nha yali mulimi muno, na ku soko yiyo i wanhu nawo wakakwegwa no mulimo wuwo wo kulima, wakalima zabibu mu yisi ye matunda, no kuyilima yisi yono yina moso mwinji.

11 Kanyhi yali yewikanizize mhuga mbaha muno ye wasilikale, wono wali wewikanizize lukulu so milewo. Ne zinghani zose, zandikwaga ne wandici wakwe, Yeyiyeli, na Maseya, mu wulanjilizi wa Hananiya, yumonga wa muli wetumami wo mutemi.
12 Ne mhuga yiyo mbaha ye wasilikale, yali yili hasi yo wulanjilizi wa wanhu elufu nyejete na magana mutandatu.
13 Nawo wawo, wali walanjilizi we wasilikale wono waficile elufu magana madatu na mupunghati, na magana mahano, wono wali vitung'ati muno mu wulugu, wono wamutazaga mutemi kuli walugu wakwe.
14 Nayo mutemi Uziya, yakawagawila wasilikale wose, ngula, ne migoha, ne zikofiya ze cuma, hamonga ne vicitizo vya cuma, no wupinde, ne zisonga zayo, ne mabwe ge zinghomango, go kugumila kwa nhago.
15 Na mumo mu Yelusalemi, i wanghula wakwe, wakanoza mitambo yono yikusuma kugumila zimhagali, hamonga ne mabwe mawaha mu wulawa mu minala, na mu vipeto vye lijinji lye likaya. Ne lilumbo lyakwe, likatandala hanhu hose, nayo yakacila kuwa ne vilungo mu wutemi wakwe, ku soko yo wutazi wuwaha wono yawutuga kulawa kuli Mulungu.

Uziya yakulipicizwa ku soko yo wiwoni wakwe

16 Ninga u mutemi Uziya, yali yeza kuwona vyono yatuga vilungo, hodu yakawa kwinjilwa no wikumye. No wikumye wuwo, wukawa kumwinjiza mu wituma zimbi. Yakamubedegala Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe, so winjila mweneco mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, no kufusa wuvumba, mu cikumbicilo co kufusiza wuvumba.
17 Ninga Azaliya mukohani, hamonga ne wakohani wayagwe waficile makumi munana, vitung'ati wo Muwaha Mulungu, kanyhi wasina woga, wakamutyatya mutemi Uziya kuko ku cikumbicilo,
18 wakamwimila wakatya, “Gwe Uziya! Yo si ling'ani yako 'gwe, kufusa wuvumba hali Muwaha Mulungu. Hambi nha yiwanojeye wakohani wono walecelwa na Haluni, kanyhi wakasiwanulwa, wawo du wo wanojeye wetume mulimo wuwu. Holeka wuwuce lulu baha i hanhu baha hono helile. Nalu wakondya kwituma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu. Holeka hamba si kojendelela kuwa no wulugutwe wakwe kanyhi.”

19 Nayo Uziya yakawa vyo yemile baho du ha mhelo ye cikumbicilo mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, akuno vyo yebate cifusizo co wuvumba mu muwoko wakwe. Yakawakolelwa likuwo liwaha wawo wakohani. Na lulo baho du yakatuga wutamwa wo wukoma mu cihanda co wusu wakwe.
20 Nayo Azaliya mukohani, hamonga ne wakohani wayagwe, wakawa wamulanjile Uziya ku wusu, ko woga wuwaha muno. Na hanyuma nhondo, wakawa kumulavyaniza kunze, yawuce mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Nayo yakasunha muno kulawila kunze, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yali yamulipiciza.

21 Na mu wikale wakwe, we vilo vyakwe vyono vyali visigaye, mutemi Uziya, yali yeyavufye hali Muwaha Mulungu, ku soko yo wukoma, wono yali yena nawo. Na ku soko yiyo, si yasuma kanyhi kwinjila mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Mwene yekalaga du mu kaya yakwe, hamba si yasuma kanyhi kwituma no mulimo wakwe wo wutemi. Yotamu u mwanagwe, yo yajendela kuyitema yisi.

22 Nayo muwizi Isaya, u mwana wa Amozi, yo yazandika zinghani zinji, zono mutemi Uziya yetuma mu wutemi wakwe.
23 Nhondo Uziya yakawa kufwa, yakazikwa behi no mugunda wo kuzika watemi, ku soko yo wutamwa wakwe wo wukoma, holeka si wamuzika mu vitenjelo vye watemi. Na Yotamu u mwanagwe yakahala wutemi wakwe.


2 Zinghomo 27

Yotamu u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 15:32-38)

1 Yotamu yatema lono yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na mihano, nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi, myaka yikumi na mutandatu. U nyina yacemwaga Yelusha, u mwana we cicekulu wa Zadoki.
2 Nayo yaketuma zono zamwendezaga Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono sogwe Uziya yetuma. Ninga mwene si yamusutizya Muwaha Mulungu so wufusa wuvumba mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Langa wanhu wo wamusutizya Muwaha Mulungu.

3 Yuyu Yotamu, yo yazenga mulango wo lubavu lwa kusumuka we Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne lijinji lya mwi'kaya lya Yelusalemi, ku lubavu lono lukucemwa Ofeli.
4 Kanyhi yakazenga ne makaya mucanyha ye matunda ga Yuda. Yakazenga ne magomawakali, hamonga ne minala mu mbago.
5 Kanyhi yakehoma no mutemi we Waamoni, hamonga ne wasilikale wakwe, yakawasuma. Yali yeza kulawa baho, yakawa kuwafwanghaniza i Waamoni, wawe kumulipa kodi myaka midatu. Mwaka mulongozi wamulipe fweza kilo elufu yidatu na magana mane, hamonga na ngano, tani elufu monga, hamonga na shayili, nazo tani elufu monga. Wawe kumulipa liwalo lilyo myaka yose, sunga yifice myaka midatu.
6 Nayo Yotamu yakajendelela kuwa ne vilungo mu wutemi wakwe, vyono yali yamuhulice ko wihuwilwa u Muwaha Mulungu wakwe.
7 Ne zinghani zinji zono yetuma Yotamu lono yali yatemile, hamonga ne milewo yakwe, ne zinzila zakwe, vyose vyandicigwe mu citabu ce Zinghomo ze watemi wa Isilayeli na Yuda.
8 Yotamu yakatema lono yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na mihano. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi, myaka yikumi na mutandatu.
9 Yotamu yakafwa, yakazikwa mwi'kaya lya Dawudi. Na Ahazi u mwanagwe, yakahala wutemi wakwe.


2 Zinghomo 28

Ahazi mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 16:1-4)

1 Ahazi yakatema lono yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete cititila. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka yikumi na mutandatu. Ninga nayo hamba si yatyatya musambo muswanu wo kukuye Dawudi. Mwene yetuma zono si zamwendeza Muwaha Mulungu.
2 Mwene yatyatya i metumo mabi ge watemi wa Isilayeli. Hambi nha yali yena vihwani vye shaba vya Baali.
3 Yakawa yakufusa wuvumba mwi'lolo lyo mwana wa Hinomu, hamonga no kuwalavya wanagwe, wawe makumbiko ge cilavywa cinyanywe kuli vihwani. Yakawa yakwiga makani ge cevu, gono wetumaga wanhu wono Muwaha Mulungu yawawinga mu yisi, lono Waisilayeli wenjilaga.
4 Yakawa yakulavya makumbiko no kufusa wuvumba, sunga na mu wuhembelo wa wono si wamumanyile Mulungu, mu matunda, na hasi ye mibeho ye mibici yono yina mahamha gono gadedewaye.

Wulugu we Wasiliya ne Waisilayeli

(2Wat 16:5)

5 Alu vyono mutemi Ahazi yetuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe, holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamuleka yasumwe no mutemi wa Siliya. Nayo mutemi wa Siliya, yali yeza kumusuma Ahazi, yakawa kuwasola ne wanhu wa Yuda wenji muno, yakawategula ku Damesiki nha wafungwa.
6 Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu, yakamuleka Peka u mwana wa Lemaliya, yabite yakamuhome Ahazi, no kuwawulaga mwi'zuwa limonga, wasilikale vitung'ati wa Yuda, waficile elufu yigana limonga na makumi mejete. Na zizi zaletwa no Muwaha Mulungu, vyono wene i wanhu wa Yuda, wali wakondya kumuleka Mulungu.
7 Nayo musilikale yunji we Ciisilayeli, litagwa lyakwe yacemwaga Zikili, yakamuwulaga Maseya u mwana wo mutemi Ahazi, hamonga na Azilikamu u mululicizi we kaya yo wutemi, hamonga na Elikana mono yali yo we kejete ko mutemi Ahazi.
8 Ninga hamba yiwe wawo wa Yuda wali wandugu zawo, ninga Waisilayeli wakawasola wacekulu wono wali waficile elufu magana mejete cititila, hamonga ne wana wose, wakasolwa nha wafungwa. Wakawagomola ku Samaliya, hamonga ne vinhu vinji muno vyono waviteka.

U muwizi Obedi

9 Munhu yunji litagwa lyakwe Obedi, u muwizi wo Muwaha Mulungu, munhu yuyu, yekalaga mwi'kaya lya Samaliya. Yuyu Obedi, viketang'anila ne lipuga lye wasilikale we Ciisilayeli behi ne likaya, wakuwuya, wena ne wafungwa wawo we Ciyuda, yakawalonjela yakatya, “Lanjenyi ta! U Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wenyu, yali yawakolelwa likuwo wanhu wa Yuda, na holeka yamulecenyi 'nye mubite muwahome, muwasume. Ninga aluno, Muwaha Mulungu kakondya kuhulika vyono muwawulaganga mu nzila 'mbi muno muno.
10 Sunga nalu kanyhi, vyo mukali du, mukusaka muwanoze i wanhu wa Yelusalemi na Yuda, i walume ne wacekulu, wawe wawanda wenyu. Alu si mukumanya nha vyono sunga na nyenye mwakondya kwituma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu lo?
11 Alu muhulicize ta! Mumanye vyono, wafungwa wawa wo wandugu zenyu ne walumbu zenyu. Holeka ciba muwalecele wabite. Na hono mulece kuwalecela, ale, Muwaha Mulungu komulipicizizenyi mwi'kuwo lyakwe.”

12 Na mwipuga lyawo, mwali na wanhu wane, wono wali wo walongola wawo, wono walawa ku wutemi wa ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, yali, Azaliya u mwana wa Yehohanani, na Belekiya u mwana wa Meshilemoti, na Yehizikiya u mwana wa Shalumu, hamonga na Amasa u mwana wa Hadilayi, nawo wawa si walivumila litumo lye Wasilikale lyo kuwasola wafungwa.
13 Holeka nawo wakawalonjela wasilikale wawo, wakawatya, “Mulece kuwaleta wawo wafungwa akuno! Hambi ase mbeka nha cakondya kwituma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, sunga nalu cakondya kumukoza likuwo, nalu, yewikanize yacilipicize. Nalu kanyhi mukusaka mwitume nghani yinji yono yowonjeleza wubi wetu.”
14 Hodu lipuga lye wasilikale, likawa kuwakombola wafungwa wose kuli walongola wawo, hamonga ne vinhu vyose vyono wali waviteka.
15 Nawo wawalya i wanhu wane, wakawikwa wawagawile wafungwa, myenda yono yali yili mu vinhu vyono waviteka. Nawo wakawa kuwapela myenda, ne malatu, wasume kuvwala. Wakawapela ndigwa wakalya, wakang'wa. Wakawatajila ne mafuta mu zinghomele zawo. Na walya wono wali wasocela hamba si wakusuma no kujenda, wakawasola, wakawapaciza mu zindogowe. Hanyuma wakawa kuwagomola wafungwa wose ku yisi ya Yuda ku Yeliko, mwi'kaya lye mibici minji ye mitende. Hodu, Waisilayeli wali weza kuwafisa, wene wakawa kuwuya kwawo ku Samaliya.

Ahazi yakulondola wutazi kuli Waashuli

(2Wat 16:7-9)

16 Mu madaha gago, mutemi Ahazi, yakatuma wanhu kuli Tigilati Pilesa, u mutemi wa Shamu, ho yamutaze.
17 Hambi Waedomu, nha wali waza kanyhi kuwahoma Wayuda, no kusola wafungwa wenji.
18 Na mu madaha gago kanyhi, nawo Wafilisiti wali wenjila no kugahoma makaya ga ku lubavu lwa ku mwezi ga mu matunda, hamonga na ga mu yisi ya Yuda ku lubavu lwa kutakama. Nawo wali wakondya kugasola makaya ga Beti Shemeshi, na Ayijaloni, na Gedeloti, hamonga ne makaya ga Soko, na Timuna, na Gimuzo, hamonga ne vitumbi vyayo vyose. Nawo wakawa kuhamilila lukulu mumo.

19 Alu, vyono Ahazi mutemi wa Yuda, yali yakondya kuyihodanga ling'ani ye wanhu wakwe, na vyono kanyhi yali yakondya kulyavufya litagwa lyo Muwaha Mulugu, holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakawaletela Wayuda magazo mawaha.
20 Nayo mutemi wa Siliya, hamba si yamuletela Ahazi wutazi yaye, langa yamuletela magazo.
21 Nayo Ahazi yali yeza kuwona vyono mutemi Pigilati Pilesa yakumugaza, yakawa kuyisola zahabu yose yono yali yili mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, na mu nyumba yo wutemi, na mu zikaya ze walongola we wanhu, yakawa kumupela mutemi Pigilati Pilesa. Ninga vivyo vyose hamba si vyakwila cinji yaye.

U wubi wa Ahazi

22 Na lono magazo gakwe Ahazi gacilaga kuwa mabi muno muno, nayo Ahazi ho yakacila muno kwituma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu si nha halulongozi.
23 Yakalavya makumbiko kuli milungu ye Waashuli wono wamusuma. Hambi nha yatya, vyono “I milungu ye Waashuli, yiwataza watemi wa Siliya, holeka nane hono niyilavizye makumbiko, nayo yomhela wutazi nha vivilya.” Ne nghani yiyi, yikamuletela wongomezwe, mwene hamonga ne likungugo lyose lye Waisilayeli.
24 Hamonga na zizo, yakasola viya vyose vyono vyali mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakavibenanga vyose. Yakayidinda milango ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakawika vikumbicilo hanhu hose mu Yelusalemi.
25 Yakazenga hanhu ho wuhembelo we milungu yo wulambi. No wuvumba wukawa kufuswa kuli milungu yinji. Na muli zizo, yakeletela mweneco likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo.

26 Ne zinghani zose zono Ahazi yetuma, ku wulawa mwamulizo wo wutemi wakwe sunga ko ku ndumilizo, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce Zinghomo ze watemi wa Yuda na Isilayeli.
27 Mutemi Ahazi yakafwa, yakazikwa mu Yelusalemi. Ninga si wamuzika mu vitenjelo vye watemi. Nayo Hezekiya mwanagwe, yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.


2 Zinghomo 29

Hezekiya u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 18:1-3)

1 Hezekiya yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na mihano, lono yanziza kutema mu Yuda. Nayo yatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi mejete ne'cenda. U nyina yacemwaga Abiya u mwana we cicekulu wa Zekaliya.
2 Mwene Hezekiya yetuma zono zikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu, yakatyatya musambo muswanu wo kukuye mutemi Dawudi.

U wezwe we Nyumba yo Mulungu

3 Na mu mwezi mulongozi wa mu mwaka, lono Hezekiya yakondya kutema, Hezekiya yakayidigula kanyhi i milango ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakayinozeleza.
4 Yakawakonghola wakohani ne Walawi, wali cipuga, yakawahanguliziza mwi'kumbo lyo lubavu lwa kucilima ye Nyumba yo Mulungu.
5 Yakawa kulongana nawo, yakawatya, “Nye nyo Walawi, mwiyeze zinhumbula zenyu, ho muyeze ne Nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wenyu. Muviwuse vinhu vyose mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, vyono vikuyavufya.
6 Hambi i walezi wenyu, nha si wali wehuwilwa kuli Muwaha Mulungu, na ku soko yiyo waketuma zono si zamwendezaga mwene. Wakamuleka, hamba si wakasaka no kuhalanga bwete, ne hanhu hono kekalaga.
7 Wakayidinda milango ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, wakazileceleza zitala zikazima, hamba si wasuma no kufusa wuvumba, hamba kulavya vilavywa vinyanywe mu Nyumba yo Mulungu we Waisilayeli,
8 na ku soko ya zizo, holeka Muwaha Mulungu yakawakolelwa likuwo, i wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, na zono yawanozeza zawapela wanhu wose wucicime no woga wuwaha. Nanye wose muzimanyiye lukulu zizi zose.
9 I wasogo zetu, wawulagwa ku wulugu, i wagolece zetu nawo, hamonga ne wana wetu, wakasolwa nha wafungwa no kutegulwa kutali.

10 “Aluno mbona viswanu cinoze cilagane no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli, ho yaze yalece kucikolelwa likuwo kanyhi.
11 Holeka wanangu, mulece kucitaga cilejeho. Hambi nha yo nyenye du, mono Muwaha Mulungu yamuhagulenyi muwe kufusa wuvumba hali mwene, hamonga no kuwalongoza wanhu lono wakumuhemba.”
12 Na alino lyo liwalo lye Walawi wono wali wali baho:

Ku wulawa mu mhuga ya Kohati,

yali Mahati, u mwana wa Amasayi,

hamonga na Yoweli, u mwana wa Azaliya.

Ku wulawa mu mhuga ya Melali,

yali Kishi, u mwana wa Abudi,

hamonga na Azaliya, u mwana wa Yehaleleli.

Ku wulawa mu mhuga ya Gelishoni,

yali Yowa, u mwana wa Zimuna.

Hamonga na Edeni, u mwana wa Yowa.

13 Ku wulawa mu mhuga ya Elizafani,

yali Shimuli na Yeyuweli.

Ku wulawa mu mhuga ya Asafu,

yali Zekaliya, na Mataniya.

14 Ku wulawa mu mhuga ya Hemani,

yali Yehuyeli, na Shimeyi.

Ku wulawa mu mhuga ya Yedutuni,

yali Shemaya, na Uziyeli.

15 Nawo wanhu wawa, wakawa kuwakonghola i Walawi wayawo, wakawa kwiyeza wose. Hanyuma ho wakawa kubita ku Nyumba yo Mulungu, nha vyono mutemi yawalajiliza, ho wayeze nha vyono winha wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu.
16 Wakohani wakenjila mugati ye Nyumba yo Mulungu wayeze. Wali weza kwinjila, wakaviwusa vinhu vyose, vyono vyayafuvyaga Nyumba yo Mulungu, wakavitaga kwi'kumbo lya kunze lye Nyumba yo Mulungu. Nawo Walawi, wakawa kuvidinghula, wakavitegula kunze ye likaya, kwi'lolo lya Kidiloni.

17 U mulimo wuwo, wawanza kuwituma mwi'zuwa lilongozi lya mu mwezi mulongozi. Wakawumalila mwi'zuwa lyo munana. Na mwi'zuwa lilyo lyo munana, wakawa wafika kwi'kumbo lye Nyumba yo Mulungu. Hanyuma kanyhi, ho wakajendelela no mulimo wuwo mu vilo kanyhi vinji munana, wakawa wakunozeleza Nyumba yo Mulungu, no kuyiwikaniza so wuhembi. Nha cinji wajendelela no mulimo, sunga mwi'zuwa lye kumi na mutandatu lyo mwezi.

Nyumba yo Mulungu kanyhi yikusiwanulwa

18 Nawo Walawi wali weza kuwukondya mulimo wuwo, wakawa kumubitila mutemi Hezekiya, wakamulonjela wakutya, “Ase cakondya kuyeza Nyumba yose yo Mulungu, ne cikumbicilo ce vilavywa vinyanywe, ne meza ye mikate yono yelile, hamonga ne viya vyayo vyose.
19 Kanyhi, cakondya kuvigomola hamonga ne viya vyose vyono mutemi Ahazi, yaviwusa mumo mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, mu mapango lono mwene si yali mwihuwilwa kuli Mulungu. Nase cakondya no kuyisiwanula kanyhi Nyumba yo Mulungu. Ne vinhu vyose civiwicile ha mwande ye cikumbicilo co Muwaha Mulungu.”

20 Nayo mutemi Hezekiya yali yeza kuzihulika zizo, lolulo lulo, yakawa kuwakonghola hamonga i wawaha wose wa mwi'kaya, wakawa kubita wose hamonga ku Nyumba yo Mulungu.
21 Wakaleta ng'ombe zinume mupunghati, ne zingholo zinduwilu mupunghati, ne zinyana zayo nazo mupunghati, na mvulati mupunghati, ziwe cilavywa co wubi ku so mhuga yo mutemi, na ku soko ye wanhu wa Yuda, hamonga no wezwe we Nyumba yo Mulungu. Mutemi yakawalajiliza wakohani i wana wa Haluni, wazilavye zimitugo zizo zose, makumbiko mu cikumbicilo.
22 Nawo wakohani, wakawa kuziwulaga zimitugo zose. Ninga wakanza ku wulawa zing'ombe, hanyuma zikatyatya zingholo, na hanyuma yayo ho zikatyatya zi nyana ze zingholo. Wakasola sakami ye zimitugo zose ze makumbiko, wakayimiza mu cikumbicilo, mutugo na sakami yayo.
23 Hanyuma wakazisola zimhene zimvulati zono zaletwa so cilavywa co wubi wakazileta ha mwande yo mutemi ne wahembi wose. Nawo wose wakawa kuzijeleka miwoko yawo.
24 Nawo wakohani wakaziwulaga, wakayisola sakami yazo, wakayimiza mu cikumbicilo, yiwe cilavywa co wubi ho wawanozeze Waisilayeli wose mulamu. Hambi mutemi nha yali yalajiliza nha vyono, yinojeye vilavywa vinyanywe, hamonga ne vilavywa so wubi, vilavywe ku so Waisilayeli wose.

25 Nayo mutemi Hezekiya, yakazinoza zose nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza mutemi Dawudi, muli Gadi u muwizi wo mutemi, kanyhi na muli muwizi Natani. Hezekiya yakawawika Walawi mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne watowa masumbi, ne ndonondo ne misesele.
26 Vinha nha vyono mutemi Dawudi yavitumamilaga. Ne wakohani nawo, wakema baho wena ne zindulele zawo.
27 Hanyuma, mutemi Hezekiya, yakalajiliza vilavywa vinyanywe vyanze kulavywa. Ne vilavywa vyali vyeza kwanza kulavywa, i wanhu nawo wakawa wakwimba wakumukumya Muwaha Mulungu. Nawo i wembi, wakawa kwanza kutowa zindulele hamonga ne viya vyose vyo kwimbila.
28 Ne wanhu wose wono wali wali baho, wakelunza mu wuhembi. Nawo i wembi, wakemba, na we zindulele nawo wakatowa, zikajendelela zose sunga wakakondeleza ku wulavya vilavywa vinyanywe.
29 Hanyuma ya zizo, u mutemi Hezekiya, hamonga ne wanhu wose, wakafugama hasi, wakamuhemba Mulungu.
30 Hanyuma, mutemi hamonga ne walongola wose, wakawalomba Walawi wajendelele kwimba, wamwimbile Muwaha Mulungu zinyimbo zo kumukumya, zono zandikwa na Dawudi, na zono yazandika Asafu u muwizi. Ne wanhu wose wakemba na nyemo mbaha muno. Lono wali wakali wafugame, wakamuhemba Mulungu.

31 Mutemi Hezekiya yakawalonjela wanhu yakawatya, “Alu vyono wose mwakondya kwiyeza, alu mulete makumbiko hamonga ne vilavywa vyo kumulumba Muwaha Mulungu.” Nawo wanhu wose wakayilumbiliza nghani yiyo. Na lulo baho du wanji wakawa kulavya zimitugo zawo ko mutima muzelu, ho ziwe makumbiko ge vilavywa vinyanywe.
32 Wakaleta ng'ombe makumi mupunghati, na ngholo yigana limonga, hamonga na nyana za ngholo, magana mejete, vikawa so vilavywa vinyanywe kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
33 Kanyhi wakaleta na ng'ombe magana mutandatu na ngholo elufu yidatu, gawe makumbiko gono wanhu walye.
34 Ninga 'lu nhondo, wakohani wakawa wacefu so kuwulaga no kuhinza zimitugo zizo zose. Holeka Walawi nawo, wakenjila kuwataza, sunga mulimo wose wukamalika. Na baho ho wakohani wanji nawo weyezeza. (Hambi i Walawi nha wali wehuwilwa mu wiyeze, sinha vyono wakohani wali wenha.)
35 Hamonga na zizo, lono vilavywa vinyanywe vyalavywaga, i wakohani wo wali wena mulimo wo kukongoga mhama, yono yalavywaga mu wulawa mu makumbiko, gono galizagwa ne wanhu, hamonga no wujidila divayi mu vilavywa vinyanywe vyono vyalavywaga.

Na hanyuma ya zizo, wuhembi wukajendelela kanyhi mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
36 Mutemi Hezekiya, hamonga ne wanhu wose, wakasangalala muno, vyono Mulungu kawapela wutazi wo kwitumila zizi zose mbela mbela.


2 Zinghomo 30

Wuwikanize so Pasaka

1 I wanhu si wasuma kuyinoza Sikukuu ye Pasaka, mu vipango vyono vyali vinojeye, nha cinji, mu mwezi mulongozi, vyono wakohani wali wakali si neyeza wiyeze wono wukwiliye. Kanyhi ne wanhu wenji wali wakali nehanguliza ku Yelusalemi.
2 Holeka mutemi Hezekiya, ne wetumami wakwe, hamonga ne wanhu wa Yelusalemi, wakelumbaniza vyono wayinoze sikukuu yiyo mu mwezi we kejete.
3 Nayo mutemi yakawa kuwalalika wanhu wose wa Isilayeli na Yuda. Ninga wo mulango wa Efulayimu na wa Manase, u mutemi yakawona ciba yawandicile baluwa, yawalalice nawo waze ku Nyumba yo Mulungu, ku Yelusalemi, ho wayinoze sikukuu ye Pasaka hamonga nawo, wamuhembe Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli.
4 Mutemi hamonga ne wanhu wakwe, wakawona vyono, lihweso lyawo lilyo yiswanu.
5 Holeka wakawa kuwalalika Waisilayeli wose ku wulawilila Dani, ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, sunga ko ku Belisheba ku lubavu lwa kutakama, waze wose ku Yelusalemi wayinoze wose sikukuu ye Pasaka, nha vyono winha Wulajilize.
6 Nawo wanhu wono watumwa no mutemi hamonga ne wetumami wakwe, wakawa kutuluka kubita ku wulalici, mu yisi yose ya Yuda na Yelusalemi. Wakatumwa wakawalonjele wanhu vyono mutemi yakutya, “Nye nyo wanhu wa Isilayeli, mono mwapona mu miwoko ye Waashuli, wono wayisuma yisi. Aluno mumuwuyile Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Abulahamu, na Isaka na Yakobo, nayo komuwuyilenyi.
7 Mulece kwiga mikalile ye walezi wenyu, hamba ye wandugu zenyu Waisilayeli, wono wali si wehuwilwa kuli Muwaha Mulungu u Mulungu wawo. Ninga nanye mukuwona nha vyono yawalipiciza ko wukali muno.
8 Holeka nanye mulece kuwa musina cihuliko, nha vyono wene wali wenha, langa anye muweze ne cihuliko kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Holeka luno, wose muze ku Nyumba yo Mulungu ku Yelusalemi, i hanhu hono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yaheza cibitilila, muze mumuhembe, nayo ho yalece kanyhi kumukolelwenyi likuwo.
9 Nanye hono mumuwuyile Muwaha Mulungu, na wa walya nawo wono wawasola wandugu zenyu no kuwategula kutali nha wafungwa, nawo wowawonela lusungu, wawalecele wawuye ku zikwawo. Hambi u Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, nha yena lihewa no lusungu. Holeka hono mumuwuyile, nayo komubocelenyi.”

10 Nawo wanhu wono watumwa, wakawa kutuluka wakawa kubita mu makaya gose ge yisi ya wo mulango wa Efulayimu na wa Manase, wakafika sunga ko ku sumuka ku wo mulango wa Zabuloni. Ninga wanhu wakawa kuwasekanga, no kuwazwajiza.
11 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, hali ho wali wanji wa mu mulango wa Asheli na wa Manase, hamonga na wo mulango wa Zabuloni, wono wakenda kuza ku Yelusalemi.
12 Nayo Mulungu yakawapela Wayuda wose i nhumbula ye cihuliko kuli malajilizo go mutemi ne wawaha wakwe mu nghango yo wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu.

Pasaka yikunozwa

13 Ne wanhu wenji muno, wakekongholela mu Yelusalemi, mu mwezi we kejete, ho wayinoze sikukuu yo mukate wono wusina cisase.
14 Wakawa kuvidinghula vikumbicilo vyose vyono vyetumamilwaga mu Yelusalemi so wulavizya makumbiko, no wufusa wuvumba, wakawa kuvileta mwi'lolo lya Kidiloni.
15 Na mwizuwa lye likumi na yine, lyo mwezi we kejete, wakawulaga nyana ye ngholo, ye Pasaka. Nawo wakohani ne Walawi, wono wali wakali neyeza, wakakolwa ne cevu ciwaha, wakawa kwiyeza, nhondo ho wakawa kulavya vilavywa vinyanywe mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
16 Waketuma milimo yawo mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, nha vyono winha wulajilize, wono wuli mwi Lajilizo lya Musa, u munhu wo Mulungu. I Walawi, wakayisola sakami ye makumbiko, wakawapela wakohani, nawo wakohani wakayimiza mu cikumbicilo.
17 Na vyono wanhu wenji wali si weyezize, holeka Walawi wakaziwulaga zinyana ze zingholo ze Pasaka, ho waweze kuli Muwaha Mulungu, wawo wono wali wakali neyeza.
18 Hamonga na zizo, i wanhu wose wono wakaza ku wulawa mu mulango wa Efulayimu, na wa Manase, na wa Isakali, na wa Zabuloni, wali wakali hamba neyeza, ninga wakayilya Pasaka nha vyono si yandikwa yaye. Nayo mutemi Hezekiya, yakawalombela kuli Mulungu, yakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,
19 go Mulungu we walezi wetu, ko wuswanu wako, nghukulomba wuwanenele wose wono wali baha, wono wakukuhemba kwe zinhumbula zawo zose, hamba wawe si weyezize ng'o.”
20 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawuvumila wulombe wa Hezekiya, yakawa kuwanenela wanhu, hamba si yawatamisa yaye.
21 Ne wanhu wose, wono waza baho ha Yelusalemi, ho wayinoze sikukuu yo mukate wono wusina cisase, mu vilo mupunghati, wali wena nyemo mbaha muno. Nawo Walawi hamonga ne wakohani, wembaga kwe vilungo vyawo vyose, wakumulumba Muwaha Mulungu.
22 Nayo mutemi Hezekiya, yakawalumba muno Walawi so wuhidimbazi wawo wuwaha, mu wulongoza wuhembi wo Muwaha Mulungu.

Wunoze we Sikukuu lwe kejete

Vyali vyeza kupuluta vilo mupunghati, vyono walavyaga makumbiko, wakumulumba Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo,
23 wakawona viswanu wonjeleze kanyhi vilo vinji mupunghati, wajendelele kuyinoza sikukuu. Holeka wakajendelela kuyinoza na nyemo mbaha.
24 Mutemi Hezekiya yakalavya ng'ombe elufu monga, hamonga na ngholo elufu mupunghati, ho wanhu walye. Wetumami wo mutemi, nawo wakalavya ng'ombe elufu monga nhavivilya, hamonga na ngholo elufu yikumi. Ne wakohani wenji wakeyeza.
25 I wanhu wa Yuda, ne wakohani, ne Walawi, ne wanhu wono walawa kusumuka, hamonga ne wajenzi, wono wali wahamiye mu Yuda na mu Yelusalemi, wose wakawa na nyemo mbaha muno.
26 Ne likaya lyose lya Yelusalemi, likamema nyemo mbaha muno, vyono zono zinha zizi, zali zikali nejela yaye, ku wulawa mu vilo vyo mutemi Solomona, u mwana wa Dawudi.
27 Nawo wakohani ne Walawi, wakawa kuwalombela wanhu u wumoteswe wa kuli Mulungu. Nayo Mulungu wulombe wawo kuko kono yekaye ku wulanga, no kuwuvumila.


2 Zinghomo 31

Wunozeleze wo Wuhembi

1 U wuhembi wali weza malika, i wanhu Isilayeli wose wakawa kwiyagala mu makaya gose mu Yuda, wakagabenanga masumbili gose ge mabwe, wakavidumulanga ne vihwani vyose vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, wakagabenanga ne makumbicilo, hamonga ne hanhu hose hono hetumamilwaga so wuhembi we milungu yo wulambi. Wakanoza nhavivi mu yisi ya Yuda, na mu zisi za Benyamini, na Efulayimu na Manase. Wali weza kukondya, hodu wakawa kuwuya ku kaya.
2 Mutemi Hezekiya, yakawika, no kunozeleza wiwalile we milimo ye Wakohani ne Walawi, nha vyono wali wanojeye munhu na mulimo wakwe. Hamonga no mulimo wo wulavya vilavywa vinyanywe hamonga ne vilavywa vyo mulamu. No mulimo wo wulongoza wuhembi mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Hamonga no kumulumba no kumukumya Muwaha Mulungu, mu Nyumba yakwe.
3 Mwene Hezekiya, yakalavya mitugo ku wulawa mwi'tewa lye zimitugo zakwe, so vilavywa vinyanywe, hakali mitondo ne cinyamihe. Hamonga na zono zalavywaga mu mazuwa ge Sabato, na za mu Ndawo yo Mwezi, sunga na muli vilavywa vya mu zisikukuu zinji, nha vyono yandikwa mu Wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu.

4 Hamonga na zizo, mutemi yakawalonjela wanhu wose wa Yelusalemi, walete vilavywa vyono vyali viwanojeye wakohani ne Walawi wapegwe, ho wasume kujendelela viswanu, wapiligaye mu Wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu.
5 No mutemi yali yeza kulavya wulajilize wuwo, hobaho lwa kamonga du, wanhu wa Isilayeli, wakawa kuleta vipegwa vyo wuhemba wawo wuswanu, ne divayi ne mafuta, no wuci, hamonga ne vilimwa vinji du vya mu migunda yawo. Wakalavya ne zaka ku wulawa muli vinhu vyawo vyose vyono wali wena navyo.
6 Ne wanhu wose wono wekalaga mu makaya ga Yuda, nawo wakalavya zaka ku wulawa mu zing'ombe zawo, ne zingholo. Wakaleta vipegwa vinji muno muno, wakamusiwanulila Muwaha Mulungu.
7 Vipegwa vivyo, wanhu wakanza kuvileta mu mwezi we kadatu, navyo vikajendelela kuletwa du, sunga yikapuluta myezi mine, nhondo vipegwa vikawa vyamema muno muno.
8 Nayo mutemi Hezekiya, hamonga ne wetumami wakwe, wali weza kugawona malundo gago ge vinhu vivyo vyono wanhu walavya, wakamulumba Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga ne wanhu wose wa Isilayeli.
9 U mutemi Hezekiya, yakawa kuwawuza Wakohani hamonga ne Walawi, kuli ze vinhu vivi vinji muno.
10 Nayo Azaliya u Mukohani Muwaha, wo lulelo lwa Zadoki, yakamupitula yakatya, “Ku wulawa wanhu lono wanziza kuleta vipegwa vyawo kono Nyumba yo Mulungu, waleta vinji muno vyono vikwiliye kulya no kusigala vinji du. Na zizi zose ziwa nhavivi, vyono Muwaha Mulungu yakondya kuwalugutiza wanhu wakwe.”

11 Hanyuma mutemi yakalajiliza wanozeleze hanhu ho kuwika viwutwa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
12 Nawo wakawa kuhanozeleza, wakawa kuviwika vipegwa vyose mumo, hamonga ne Zaka, ho vikale mutulano. Wakamuwika Mulawi monga, litagwa lyakwe Konaniya, yawe yo mulanjilizi, hamonga no nduguye Shimeyi yawe yo mutazi wakwe.
13 Hanyuma kanyhi, wakawikwa Walawi waficile yikumi limonga, wawe hasi yawo, nawo wo awa: Yehiyeli, Azaziya, Nahati, Asaheli, Yelimoti, Yozabadi, Eliyeli, Isimaciya, Mahati, hamonga na Benaya. Na wose wawa, wali wali mu wulajilize wo mutemi Hezekiya, hamonga na Azaliya u Mukohani Muwaha.
14 Nayo Kole, u mwana wa Imuna, u Mulawi, mono yali yo mulanjilizi muwaha wa ku mulango wa kucilima we Nyumba yo Mulungu, yuyo yali yo mulanjilizi wo kubocela vipegwa vyose vyono wamulavizyaga Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga no kuvigawa.
15 Na mu makaya ganji, mono wekalaga wakohani, hakawa na Edeni, na Miniyamini na Yeshuwa, na Shemaya, na Amaliya, hamonga na Shekaniya, wono wamutazaga mu mulimo wuwo ko wihuwilwa muno. Wakawa wakugawa ndigwa muling'ano kuli wandugu zawo Walawi, mu nghango yo wiwalile wawo vyono wali winha.
16 Wakawagawila ndigwa we cilume wose wono wali waficile myaka makumi madatu hamba kuciza, wono nhondo zose wetumaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, mu nghango ye milimo yawo vyono wali wapejigwe.
17 I wakohani wakapegwa ligawo lyawo, mu nghango ye zimhuga zawo vyono zinha. I Walawi nawo wono waficile myaka makumi mejete na wono wacizize, nawo wakapegwa ligawo lyawo, mu nghango ye vipuga vyawo vye milimo, nha vyono vyali vinha.
18 Na wose wakandikwa hamonga ne wagolece zawo, ne wana, hamonga na wose wono wadimwaga nawo, hambi wose nha wali wanojeye weyeze, ho wetume milimo yono yelile.
19 Kanyhi hakawa ho wali wanhu wono wawikwa, ho wawe kuwagawila ligawo lye zindigwa i wakohani wo lulelo lwa Haluni, wono wekalaga mu makaya, hamba na walya wono wali wali ku migunda yo kudimila ya mu makaya gago. Wanhu wawo wakawagawila ndigwa i walume wose wa mu zimhuga ze Wakohani, na kuli wanhu wose wono wali wawalilizwe hamonga ne zimhuga ze Walawi.

20 Nayo mutemi Hezekiya, zose zono yetuma mu yisi yose ya Yuda, yazituma mu ling'ani, kanyhi yetuma zose zono zikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe.
21 Na holeka yakamoteswa muno muno, vyono mwene zose zono yetuma mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamba zo kuwibata Wulajilize, zose yazituma kwe nhumbula yono yimogopile Mulungu, hamonga no mutima we cihembihembi kuli Mulungu wakwe.


2 Zinghomo 32

Waashuli wakuyogofya Yelusalemi

(2Wat 18:13-37; 19:14-19, 35-37; Isa 36:1-22; 37:8-38)

1 Zali zeza kupuluta zizo, zo mutemi Hezekiya vyono yamwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu ko wihuwilwa muno, hobaho Senakelibu, u mutemi wa Ashuli, yakawa kuza kuyihoma Yuda. Yakagapilima i makaya gose ge magomawakali, yakawalajiliza wasilikale wakwe watobole milango mu majinji, wakole wenjile mugati ye makaya.
2 Ninga nayo Hezekiya yali yeza kumanya nha vyono Senakelibu yena wuhile wo kuyihoma Yelusalemi nhavivilya,
3 hobaho mwene hamonga ne wetumami wakwe, wakawona vyono yowa viswanu wagemile malenga ge mivwilo yono yali kunze ye likaya, Waashuli ho walece kutuga malenga go gose kolo wakulisejelela likaya lya Yelusalemi.
4 Hodu wakawa kwikonghola wanhu wenji muno, wakawa kuyimila mivwilo yose ye malenga, hakawa hasina kanyhi malenga go gose gono gahulumaga mu mivwilo yiyo.
5 Mutemi yakalinoza likaya liwe no wulindilizi wono wukamile, yakaganozeleza majinji gono gali gamwaguce, no kuzenga minala mucanyha yayo. Kanyhi yakazenga ne lijinji lye kejete yakalipilimya kunze yayo. Hamonga na zizo, yakaganozeleza ne magomawakali gono gali mazenje mu yisi yose, ga ku lubavu lwa kucilima ya Yelusalemi. Kanyhi yakawa yena migoha minji muno hamonga ne zingula.
6 Yakawawika wasilikale vitung'ati mwi'kaya, wakawa wali hasi yo wulanjilizi we wetumami wawaha we wasilikale. Yakawa kulajiliza wose wekongholele ha wuhihi, ha mulango we likaya. Hanyuma yakawa kuwalonjela yakatya,
7 “Mweze ne vilungo, ne nhumbula yo wutung'ati. Mulece kumogopa mutemi we Waashuli, hamba lipuga lyakwe lye wasilikale lyono yena nalyo. Hambi ase ku lubavu lwetu, nha cina vilungo muno cimucizize mwene.
8 Hambi mwene nha yetumbiye vilungo vye cimunhu-munhu, ninga ase, cina Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, mono kocipela wutazi no kucilewela mweneco mu milewo yetu.” Nawo wanhu wakasikamywa muno muno ne zinghani zo mutemi.

9 Hanyuma kanyhi, Senakelibu lono yali yali ku Lakishi, yakatuma wanhu wabite kuli mutemi Hezekiya na kuli wanhu wose wa Yuda, wono wali wali hamonga na Hezekiya mu Yelusalemi, wakawalonjele watye nhavi,
10 “Ane yo nene Senakelibu u mutemi wa Ashuli, nghuwuza, cicici cono cikumupelenyi vilungo, anye nyo wanhu ho mujendelele kwikala mu Yelusalemi mupilimwe ne magomawakali.
11 Anye hongo mwazitumbila zono Hezekiya yakumutyenyi, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu komuponyenyi mu miwoko yetu. Ninga mumanye nha vyono, Hezekiya yakumukonjenyi bwete, komwambuzenyi bwete mufwe ne nzala ne nyhilu ye malenga.
12 Yuyu Hezekiya, yo yuyo mono yahahodanga i hanhu hose ho wuhembelo, hamonga ne vikumbicilo vyayo, yakawa kuwalonjela i wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, vyono yiwanojeye wahembe no kufusa wuvumba hanhu hamonga du.
13 Kanyhi anye, si mumanyile lo, i walezi wangu na nene, vyono cawanoza wanhu we zinghungugo zinji? Wulenyi, hali ho yili hamba milungu yinji yo yose, yono yawaponya i wanhu wayo mu vilungo vyo mutemi wa Ashuli?
14 Mukwihwesa ho gali mapango ganji go gose, lono milungu yiyo ye ma'si gago, yasuma kuziponya zi'si zizo mu miwoko yetu? Alu cono cikumupelenyi witumbilo, nha vyono yuyo Mulungu wenyu, kosuma kumuponyenyi, cicici?
15 Holeka yuyo Hezekiya, yalece kumukonjenyi no kumwajilizenyi bwete. Mulecele lukulu kumwitumbila! Hambi nha hakali nejela yaye, mulungu we nghungugo yo yose, mono yasuma kuwaponya wanhu wakwe, mu wulawa mu miwoko yo mutemi wo wose wa Ashuli, ku wulawa wulawo. Holeka nanye u Mulungu wenyu, hamba si kosuma kumuponyenyi yaye.”

16 I wetumami we Ciashuli wakacila kulonga makani mabi kuli Muwaha Mulungu, sunga na kuli Hezekiya u mwitumami wo Muwaha Mulungu.
17 Nayo mutemi wa Ashuli, yakandika baluwa, yono yali yina zinghani zo kumutovula Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli. Ne baluwa yiyo yali yilonjile yikutya, “I milungu ye makungugo gose, si yawaponya wanhu wayo mu miwoko yangu, holeka hamba no Mulungu wa Hezekiya nayo, sikosuma kuwaponya yaye wanhu wakwe mu miwoko yangu.”

18 Nawo wetumami wo mutemi wa Ashuli, wakawa kuzanza zinghani zizo wazozize muno muno, wakulonga mu nonga ye Cihebelaniya, ho wawogofye no kuwapela mahwele, i wanhu wa Yelusalemi, wono wali wali mucanyha ye majinji ge likaya.
19 Wakawa kumulonga Mulungu wa Yelusalemi mu nzila yi yilya du yono wayilongaga milungu ye makungugo ganji, i milungu ye vihwani vyono vinozigwe ne miwoko ye wanhu.

20 Ninga mwene mutemi Hezekiya, hamonga no muwizi Isaya u mwana wa Amozi, wakamulomba Mulungu no kumulilila yawapele wutazi.
21 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawuhulika wulombe wawo, yakawa kumutuma malayika wakwe, yakabita yakawa kuwawulaga wasilikale wose we Ciashuli, hamonga ne walongola wawaha wose we lipuga lye wasilikale. Hodu, u mutemi wa Ashuli, yakawa kuwuya ku Ashuli ne cevu ciwaha. Na mwi'zuwa linji, lono yali yali mugati mu wuhembelo we milungu yakwe, i wanagwe we cilume wakenjila, wakawa kumuwulaga ne mimaje yawo.

22 Nhavivyo vyo Muwaha Mulungu yamuponezya mutemi Hezekiya, hamonga ne wanhu wa Yelusalemi, mu miwoko ya Senakelibu u mutemi wa Ashuli, sunga na muli walugu wawo wanji. Yakawaleka wanhu wekale welamuye ne wakalani wayawo wose we zi'si zinji.
23 Ne wanhu wenji, wakajendelela kuza ku Yelusalemi kuleta vilavywa vyawo kuli Muwaha Mulungu, nayo Hezekiya wakamuletela vipegwa. Na ku wulawilila baho, zinghungogo zose zikamupela cogopo Hezekiya u mutemi wa Yuda.

Wutamwa wa Hezekiya hamonga no wikumyi wakwe

(2Wat 20:1-3, 12-19; Isa 38:1-3; 39:1-8)

24 Na mu vilo vivyo, Hezekiya yakatamwa, yakawa yali behi no kufwa. Yakamulomba Mulungu, nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakamulajila citanjilo nha vyono kopona mu wutamwa wakwe.
25 Ninga mwene Hezekiya yakamema wikumyi mu nhumbula yakwe, hamba kulajila lilumbo kuli Muwaha Mulungu muli zose zono Mulungu yamutendeye. Na ku soko yiyo, Muwaha Mulungu yakamukolelwa likuwo, hamonga ne wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi.
26 Ninga ku ndumilizo yayo, Hezekiya hamonga ne wanhu wa Yelusalemi, wakenenenyheza hali Mulungu, holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu, hodu, si yawalipiciza wanhu yaye, sunga Hezekiya yakafwa.

U wugoli wa Hezekiya, hamonga no lukumyo lwakwe

27 Nayo mutemi Hezekiya yakawa na sawo nyinji muno, kanyhi yakawa ne lilumbo yiwaha. Yakawa yena zikaya zo kuwicila zahabu, fweza, vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu, na vye mhunjile swanu, ne zingula, hamonga ne vinhu vinji muno vyo wuguzi wukamu.
28 Kanyhi hamba nhavivyo, yakawa yena kaya zinji zono yawikaga wuhemba, ne divayi, ne mafuta. Kanyhi ne mabululu ge zing'ombe zakwe, ne makumbo ge zingholo.
29 Hamonga na vivyo vyose, Mulungu yakamupela matewa mawaha ge zingholo, ne zing'ombe, hamonga na sawo zinji, kanyhi nyinji muno muno. Holeka nayo yakasuma kuzenga makaya menji muno.
30 Kanyhi yo yuyu Hezekiya, mono yawudinda muvwilo we malenga wa kucanyha wa Gihoni, yakaganozeza malenga nzila yono yikolile hasi kwa hasi, sunga gakenjila mu Yelusalemi. Nayo mutemi Hezekiya yakamoteswa muli zose zono yetuma.
31 Ninga lono watemi wa Babiloni, watumila wanhu waze kuli Hezekiya, wamuwuze ze vitanjilo vyo wizine, vyono vyejela mu'si, baho langa Mulungu yakamuleka Hezekiya yetume nha vyono kowona mweneco, ho yamumanye zono zili mu nhumbula yakwe.

Ndumilizo yo wutemi wa Hezekiya

(2Wat 20:20-21)

32 Ne zinghani zose zono mutemi Hezekiya yetuma, hamonga no wuhidimbazi wakwe muli zo wuhembi wo kumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu, zose zandicigwe mu citabu co wugubulilwe wo muwizi Isaya, u mwana wa Amozi, hamonga na mu citabu ce Zinghomo ze watemi wa Yuda na Isilayeli.
33 Hezekiya yakafwa, yakazikwa ha kacanyha ye vitenjelo vye watemi. Ne wanhu wose wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, wakamunozeza lilumbo yiwaha muno, lono yali yakondya kufwa. Nayo Manase u mwanagwe, yakahala wutemi wakwe.


2 Zinghomo 33

Manase u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 21:1-9)

1 Manase yali yaficile myaka yikumi na myejete lono yatemela mu Yuda. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi mahano na mihano.
2 Ninga Manase yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, vyono yetuma makani ge cevu gono wetumaga i wanhu we makungugo wono si wamumanyile Mulungu, wono Muwaha Mulungu yawawinga mu yisi, lono wanhu wakwe Mulungu, wenjilaga mumo.
3 Hambi nha yakahazenga kanyhi i hanhu ho wuhembelo ho kuhembela milungu yinji, hono sogwe Hezekiya yahamwagulanga mose. Yakazenga cikumbicilo so wuhembi wo kumuhemba Baali, yakasesa ne vihwani vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, yakawa yakuhemba zinyelezi.
4 Kanyhi yakazenga vikumbicilo vyo wuhembi we milungu yinji, mugati ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, i hanhu hono Muwaha Mulungu yatya, hawe ho hanhu hono mweneco kowa kuhembelwa cibitilila.
5 Kanyhi, yakazenga vikumbicilo vyo kuzihembela zinyelezi, mu makumbo mejete ga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
6 Yakawalavya wanagwe, makumbiko mwi'lolo lya Hinomu, wawe vilavywa vinyanywe. Kanyhi yetowelezaga cisangu na zo wuganga, hamonga no kwilaguliza kuli wono wena macisi, hamonga no wuganga. Yaketuma wubi muno hali Muwaha Mulungu, no Muwaha Mulungu, yakamukolelwa likuwo muno muno.
7 Yakawika cihwani mugati ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, i hanhu hono Muwaha Mulungu yamulonjela Dawudi, hamonga na Solomona u mwanagwe, yakawatya, “Bahano hono Yelusalemi, mu Nyumba yangu yiyi, ho hanhu hono nakondya kuhahagula ku soko ye yisi yose ye milango yose, yikumi na myejete ya Isilayeli, hawe ho hanhu hono nene no mweneco du nowa kuhembelwa.
8 Na hono wanhu wa Isilayeli wagebate malajilizo gangu, kanyhi wawukamise hamonga no Wulajilize wangu wose, wono Musa u mwitumami wangu yawapela, ale, nane hamba si nowaleka wawingwe mu yisi yiyo yono nakondya kuwapela walezi wawo.”
9 Manase yakawanoza wanhu wetume wubi hali Mulungu, waketuma wubi kuciza wubi wono makungugo gawituma, gono Muwaha Mulungu yagawinga mu yisi, lono wanhu wakwe Mulungu wenjilaga.

Manase yakunena hali Mulungu

10 Holeka hamba yawe Muwaha Mulungu yamusugula Manase hamonga ne wanhu wakwe, ninga wene wakalema, hamba si wasaka kumuhuliciza yaye.
11 Holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakawa kuwaleka wasilikale wa Ashuli wayihome Yuda. Wakamuteka Manase, wakamuwicila vidobogo vya cuma mu zimhula zakwe, wakamuwopa ne manyolo, wakamutegula ku Babiloni.
12 Na mumo mu wudumyanzwe wakwe, yakenjilwa no winenenyhezi wuwaha muno, yakamuwuyila Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe, yakamulomba Mulungu yamutaze.
13 Nayo Mulungu, yakawuhulika wulombe wakwe, yakamugomola yawuye ku Yelusalemi, yakawe mutemi kanyhi. Ne nghani yiyo, yikamunoza Manase yamanye nha vyono, u Muwaha Mulungu, yo Mulungu du yali wene.

14 Na hanyuma ya zizo, Manase yakalyonjeza lijinji lya kunze wutali wayo, lijinji lya kucilima ye likaya lya Dawudi, ku wulawilila mwi'lolo behi no muvwilo we malenga ga Gihoni, ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, sunga ko ku Mulango wa Somba, sunga mo mu yisi ya Ofeli, mu ciwanza ce Nyumba yo Mulungu. Kanyhi yakawawika wawaha we wasilikale, wawe walanjilizi we vipuga vye hayo hayo, mu makaya gose ga Yuda gono gena magomawakali.
15 Yakayiwusa milungu mijenzi mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne vihwani vyono yawika mweneco, hamonga ne vikumbicilo vye wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu vyono yali yavizenjile mu cigongo, hono hali yizenjigwe Nyumba yo Mulungu. Yakavitegula vyose vivino kunze ye likaya, yakavitaga kuko.
16 Yakacinozeleza ne cikumbicilo i hanhu hono Muwaha Mulungu yanojeye yahembelwe. Yakawa kulavya ne makumbiko ge cilavywa co mulamu, hamonga ne vilavywa vyo wulumbize mucanyha yayo. Yakawa kuwalajiliza i wanhu wa Yuda wamuhembe Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli.
17 Hamba yiwe wanhu wajendelela kulavya makumbiko mu wuhembelo hanji ako na ako, ninga wajendelela kumuhemba du Muwaha Mulungu.

Ndumilizo yo wutemi wa Manase

(2Wat 21:17-18)

18 Na zinji zose zono Manase yetuma, hamonga ne malombo gakwe kuli Mulungu wakwe, ne zinghani zose ze wawizi, wono wakalongana no mwene Manase, mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli. Zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce Zinghomo ze metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.
19 Na vyono mutemi yalomba, na vyono Mulungu yakamupitula, hamonga du no wubi wono Manase yetuma, lono yali yakali si nanena, hamonga ne zimbi zose zono yetuma, ze hanhu ho wuhembelo we wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu, ne vihwani vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, vyono yasesa, hamonga ne vihwani viseswe, vyono yavihemba, lanje, zose zizo zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze wawizi.
20 Manase yakafwa, yakazikwa behi ne kaya yo wutemi. Nayo Amoni, u mwanagwe yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Mutemi Amoni wa Yuda

(2Wat 21:19-26)

21 Amoni yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na myejete, lono yatemela mu Yuda. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi, myaka myejete du.
22 Nayo nhavivyo yaketuma wubi, nha vyono yetuma u sogwe Manase. Yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, hambi nha yakahemba vihwani vyono u sogwe yavihembaga.
23 Kanyhi si yali yehwanile sogwe mono yenenenyheza, yakamuwuyila Muwaha Mulungu. Mwene Amoni, yetuma wubi muno kuciza no wubi wono sogwe yetuma.
24 Nawo wetumami wakwe wamugaluka, wakamuwulajila mu kaya yo wutemi.
25 I wanhu wa Yuda wakawawulaga i watyatyi wose wa Amoni, wakawa kumuwika Yosiya u mwanagwe yahale wutemi wakwe.


2 Zinghomo 34

Yosiya u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 22:1-2)

1 Yosiya yali yaficile myaka munana du, lono yanziza kutema mu Yuda. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi madatu na wumonga.
2 Na mu wuteme wakwe, yaketuma zono zamwendezaga Muwaha Mulungu, yakatyatya musambo muswanu wo kukuye mutemi Dawudi, yakawukamisa wulajilize wose wo Mulungu.

Yosiya yakuwiyagaza wuhembi we milungu yinji

3 Na mu mwaka wo munana wa mu wutemi wakwe, lono yali yakali mudodo, yakawa kwanza kumuhemba Mulungu wo kukuye mutemi Dawudi. Na ku wulawa baho, yali yeza kupuluta kanyhi myaka mine, Yosiya yakawa kwanza kuhakumulanga i hanhu hose hono wahembelaga wuhembi we wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu, hamonga ne vihwani vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, hamonga ne vihwani viseswe vyose.
4 Yakawatuma wanhu wakwe, wakavimwagula vikumbicilo vyose, hanhu hose, hono wamuhembelaga Baali. Yakavimwagula sunga ne vikumbicilo vyo kufusiza wuvumba, vyono vyali vizenjigwe behi ne hanhu hose hono wamuhembelaga Baali. Wakavipondanjiza vihwani vyose vya mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, wakavitindya vikawa manghundi, hamonga ne vihwani vyose viseswe. Wakagasola manghundi gago, wakageyagaziza mucanyha ye vitenjelo vye wanhu wono walavywa kuli milungu yiyo.
5 Yakagasola mafupa ge wakohani we milungu yiyo, yakagakongonjela mu vikumbicilo vyawo. Na mu wituma zizi zose, yakawanoza i wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, weyeze kanyhi.
6 Yakanoza nhavivi, sunga na mu makaya ne hanhu hose, hono hali hakondya kuhodangwa, nha cinji, yisi ya Manase, na Efulayimu, na Simiyoni, sunga ko kusumuka ku yisi ya Nafutali.
7 Yakavitulanjiza vikumbicilo vyose mu yisi yose yo wutemi wo lubavu lwa kusumuka, hamonga ne vihwani vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, yakavitindya vihwani vyose viseswe vikawa manghundi. Yakavibenanjiza vikumbicilo vyose vyono wafusizaga wuvumba. Hanyuma hodu yakawa kuwuya ku Yelusalemi.

I citabu co Wulajilize cikwijela

(2Wat 22:3-20)

8 Na mu mwaka we likumi na munana, wa mu wutemi wakwe, lono yali yakondya kuyeza yisi hamonga ne Nyumba yo Mulungu, no kuwudumya wuhembi we wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu. Hobaho mutemi Yosiya, yakawa kuwatuma wanhu wadatu, wayinozeleze Nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu, nawo wali awa, Shafani u mwana wa Azaliya, na Maseya u muwaha we likaya lya Yelusalemi, na Yowa u mwana wa Yowahazi u mwitumami mulongola.
9 Ne zipesa zono Walawi i walindilizi wazikonghola mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, wakamupela Hilikiya u Mukohani Muwaha yaziwice. (Zipesa zizi, zakongholwaga ku wulawa kuli wanhu wa Efulayimu, na Manase, hamonga na kuli wanhu wono wasigala ku wutemi wa ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, na kuli wanhu wa Yuda na Benyamini, na Yelusalemi.)
10 Hanyuma kanyhi zipesa zizo, nayo Hilikiya Mukohani Muwaha, yakawapela wawalya wanhu wadatu wono wali wo walanjilizi wo mulimo wo kuyinozeleza Nyumba yo Mulungu, nawo wakawa kuziwika mu miwoko ye waselemala, hamonga ne wazenji, ho wagule mabwe hamonga ne zimbawo,
11 i vinhu vyono viketumamilwa mu wunozelezi we mazengo gono watemi wa Yuda, wagaleceleza gakahodanjika.
12 Ne wanhu wawo wono wawituma mulimo wuwo, wakawituma ko wihuwilwa muno, hasi yo wulanjilizi we Walawi wane, Yahati, na Obadiya, we mhuga ya Melali, na Zekaliya, hamonga na Meshulamu we mhuga ya Kohati. (Ne Walawi wawo i walanjilizi wo mulimo, wali wanghula muno mu wimbi.)
13 Ne Walawi wanji nawo, wakawikwa wawe walanjilizi wo wudinghuzi we vinhu vyo kuzenjela, hamonga no kuwalanjilila wetumami mu milimo hayo hayo. Wanji nawo, wakawa wo wandici, hamonga no kwituma mulimo wo wulanjilizi.

14 Ne mapesa gago, gali geza kuwuswa gose mu cinyumba co kuwicila viwutwa, hobaho Hilikiya u Mukohani Muwaha, yakaciwona citabu co Wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu, u Wulajilize wono Mulungu yamupela Musa.
15 Hilikiya yakamulonjela Shafani yakatya, “Ane niciwona citabu co Wulajilize mu muno mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.” Hodu yakawa kumupela Shafani, citabu cico.
16 Nayo Shafani, yakawa kucitegula ko mutemi Yosiya, yakawa kumulonjela mutemi yakatya, “Cakondya kuzinoza zose zono wulajilize,
17 cakondya kuziwusa zipesa zose zono zali mbice mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, ciwa kuwapela i wetumami, hamonga ne walanjilizi wawo.
18 Kanyhi hamonga na zizo, nina citabu acino cono Hilikiya yamhelile.” Hodu yakawa kucisoma ha mwande yo mutemi kwe'zi waha.

19 Nayo mutemi Yosiya, yali yeza kuzihulika zono zikusomwa mu citabu, hobaho yakawa kuvidemulanga vivwalo vyakwe ko wusungu wuwaha muno.
20 Yakawa kuwalonjela Hilikiya na Ahikamu u mwana wa Shafani, na Abudoni, u mwana wa Mikaya, na Shafani u mwandici, hamonga na Isaka, u mwitumami wo mutemi, yakawatya,
21 “Alu, mubite mukambuzize kuli Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga ne wanhu wono wakali wasigaye mu Isilayeli na mu Yuda. Muzimanye ze mejizo gono gali mu citabu cici. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yacikoleywe likuwo, vyono i walezi wetu si wenda kuyihulika nghani yo Muwaha Mulungu hamba kwituma zono zinojeye wazitume, nha vyono zandicigwe mu citabu cicino.”

22 Nayo Hilikiya hamonga ne wayagwe, wakawa kubita kumuwuza mucekulu yunji litagwa lyakwe Hilida, u muwizi, mono yekalaga mu hanhu hapya mu Yelusalemi, nha vyono mutemi yawalajiliza. (U mulume wo mucekulu yuyo yacemwaga Shalumu u mwana wa Tikiva, kanyhi mwizukulu wa Balihasi, yali yo mulanjilizi we vivwalo vyono wetumamilaga ku wulongoza wuhembi mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.) Nawo wali weza kumwagana, wakamuloloseza zose zono zijeye.
23 Nayo yakawalonjela wawuye ko mutemi, wakamulonjele
24 nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yakulonga yakutya, “Ane no Muwaha Mulungu, noyilipiciza Yelusalemi, hamonga ne wanhu wayo, kwe malijito gono gandicigwe mu citabu cico, cono wamusomeye mutemi.
25 Hambi nha wakondya kunema'ne, no kuyilavizya makumbiko milungu yinji. Holeka nane, wakondya kunghoza likuwo so zose zono wetumile. Nakondya kuyikolelwa likuwo Yelusalemi, ne likuwo lyangu, hamba si lyowuka yaye.
26 Nayo mutemi, mumulonjele mutye, ‘Agwe Yosiya, vyono kuzihulika zono zali kusomwa mu citabu
27 na kanyhi vyono kunena, kanyhi kuwa kwinenenyheza hali 'ne, kuwa kuwulajila wunene wako, mu nzila yo kudemulanga vivwalo vyako. Kuwa kulila, weza kuhulika nha vyono noyilipiciza Yelusalemi, hamonga ne wanhu wayo. Holeka nane lu, no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, niwuhulika wulombe wako.’
28 Holeka luno, wulipicize wono nali niwalipicize wanhu wa Yelusalemi, alu hodu, si nowalipiciza, sunga kolo agwe wakondya kufwa. Ninga nokuleka wufwe ko mulamu.” Nawo wanhu wa walya, wakawa kuwuya kuli mutemi Yosiya, hamonga ne zinghani zizo.

Yosiya yakunoza Cilagane ce cihuliko kuli Muwaha Mulungu

(2Wat 23:1-20)

29 Mutemi Yosiya, yakawa kuwakonghola i walongola wose we Wayuda na Yelusalemi.
30 Nawo, hamonga no mutemi, wakawa kwinjila mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne wakohani, ne Walawi, hamonga ne wanhu wose wono wasigala, i wagoli ne waciwa. Mutemi yakawa kwima ha mwande ye wanhu wose, yakawa kucisoma citabu cose ce cilagane, cono cejela mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
31 Yakema he sumbili lye watemi, yakanoza cilagane no Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono komuhulika no kwibata wulajilize, hamonga ne malajilizo gakwe, kwe nhumbula yakwe yose, hamonga na ko mutima wakwe wose. Na kanyhi, kokwituma zose nha vyono zinha mu cilagane, nha vyono zandicigwe mu citabu.
32 Yakawasaka wanhu wose, sunga ne wanji wose du, wono wali wali mumo mu Yelusalemi, wehuwize nha vyono wokwibata cilagane. Nawo wanhu wa Yelusalemi wakavihulika visakwa vye cilagane, cono wacinozize no Mulungu we walezi wawo.
33 Hodu mutemi Yosiya, yakawa kuviwusa i vihwani vyose vye mawifyo, vyono vyali vye wanhu wa Isilayeli, vyono vyali vitagaliye mu yisi yose. Na mu nghango yo wikale wakwe wose, yajendelela kuwakong'onheza wanhu, wamwitumamile Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo.


2 Zinghomo 35

Yosiya yakuyinoza Sikukuu ye Pasaka

(2Wat 23:21-23)

1 Mutemi Yosiya, yakayinoza sikukuu ye Pasaka kuko ku Yelusalemi. Mwi'zuwa lye likumi na yine lyo mwezi mulongozi, lyo yaziwulajila mitugo so Pasaka yiyo.
2 Yakawawika wakohani munhu no mulimo wakwe mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, ho wetume viswanu.
3 Yakawaloloseza ne Walawi wono wawejizaga Waisilayeli, wono wali wakondya kwiyilavya kuli Muwaha Mulungu, yakawatya, “Alu musole Lisanduku lye Cilagane, mulinjize mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, yono yazengwa no mutemi Solomona u mwana wa Dawudi. Vyono aluno, howa hasina soko kanyhi, yo kulihamya hamya ako na ako. Langa aluno, mulimo wenyu, wowa wo kumwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, hamonga ne wanhu wakwe Waisilayeli.
4 Holeka 'luno, mwibataje milimo yenyu ya mu Nyumba yo Mulugu, wanhu na mulango wawo, mu nghango nha vyono mwawicilwa no mutemi Dawudi, hamonga na Solomona u mwanagwe.
5 Mwiyigawe nyenye nyo weneco, ho musume kuwataza we zimhuga zose ze wanhu wa Isilayeli.
6 Kanyhi yo nyenye mono moziwulaga zingholo, hamonga ne zimhene, so sikukuu ye Pasaka. Holeka aluno, munojeye mwiyeze, ho muwikanize makumbiko, nawo wandugu zenyu Waisilayeli, ho wasume kugatyatya mejizo gono Muwaha Mulungu yawapelela muli Musa.”

7 Mutemi Yosiya yakalavya mitugo ku wulawa mwi'tewa lyakwe, ho wanhu walye mwi'zuwa lye sikukuu ye Pasaka. Yakalavya ngholo hamonga ne zinyana ze zingholo, ne zimhene, zificile zose hamonga elufu makumi madatu, yakalavya ne zing'ombe, nazo zificile elufu yidatu.
8 Iwetumami wakwe, nawo wakalavya so wanhu ne wakohani, hamonga ne Walawi, ho walye. Nayo Hilikiya u Mukohani Muwaha, hamonga na Zekaliya na Yehiyeli, wono wali wo walanjilizi we Nyumba yo Mulungu, nawo wakawapela i wakohani, nyana ze zingholo, hamonga na mhene, zificile elufu nyejete na magana mutandatu, wakawapela ne zing'ombe, nazo zificile magana madatu. Zikawa so makumbiko mwizuwa lye sikukuu ye Pasaka.
9 Nayo Konaniya, na Shemaya, no nduguye Netaneli, na Hashabiya, na Yeyiyeli, hamonga na Yozabadi, wono wali wo walongola we Walawi, nawo wakalavya nyana ze zingholo, hamonga ne zimhene, zificile elufu yihano, wakalavya ne zing'ombe, nazo zificile magana mahano, wakawapela Walawi wazilavye makumbiko.

10 Ne vinhu vyose, vyali vyeza kuwa vyawikanizwa vyakondya, i Wakohani ne Walawi, wakawa kwigawa mu milimo yawo, nha vyono mutemi yalajiliza.
11 Zimhene ne zingholo zali zeza kukondya kuwulagwa, i Walawi wakazihinza, nawo wakohani wakayisola sakami, wakayimiza mu cikumbicilo.
12 Hanyuma, wakawa kuwagawila wanhu zimitugo, so cilavywa cinyanywe, mu nghango ye zimhuga zawo vyono zali zinha, mhuga monga monga. Ho wazilavye, nha vyono winha wulajilze wa Musa.
13 I Walawi wakazikasika mu moto, zinyama ze makumbiko ge Pasaka, nha vyono wali winha wulajilize, hamonga no kuteleka vilavywa vyono vyelile mu zinyungu, na mu masefuliya mawaha muno, na mu zinghalingo. Hanyuma, wakawa kuwagawila wanhu nyama mbela mbela.
14 Na lono zizi zali zeza kukondya, i Walawi nawo, ho wakawa kwigawila nyama weneco, hamonga ne wakohani wo lulelo lwa Haluni. Hambi i wakohani nha wepiganzaga muno, sunga cilo cinala, waswezaga wakukongoga mitugo mututu ye vilavywa vinyanywe, hamonga ne zimhama ze makumbiko.
15 Nawo i Walawi i wembi, we mhuga ya Asafu, wali wali mu milimo yawo du, nha vyono walajilizwa no mutemi Dawudi, nha cinji, Asafu, Hemani, hamonga na Yedutuni u muwizi wo mutemi. Ne walindilizi we milango ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, wali wasina soko yo kuwulecelela mulimo wawo wo wulindilizi, vyono Walawi wanji, wo wawawikanizizaga Pasaka.
16 Nazo zose ziketumwa du, nha vyono mutemi Yosiya yali yalajilize mwizuwa lilyo, so wuhembi wo Muwaha Mulungu, na so wunoze we sikukuu ye Pasaka, na so wulavya vilavywa vinyanywe mu cikumbicilo.
17 Ne wanhu wose wa Isilayeli wono wali wehangulize, wakajendelela ne sikukuu ye Pasaka vilo mupunghati, hamonga ne sikukuu ye mikate yono yisina cisase,
18 Na mu wulawa mu vilo vyo muwizi Samwili hali hasina sikukuu yinji yo yose ya Pasaka, yono yanozwa na sasamo mbaha nhe sikukuu yiyi. Sunga hamba na muli watemi walongozi wose, hasina hamba monga mono yayinoza sikukuu ye Pasaka yiwe mbaha, nha vyono yayinoza mutemi Yosiya, hamonga ne wakohani, ne Walawi, ne wanhu wa Yuda, ne Waisilayeli, hamonga ne wanhu wose wa Yelusalemi,
19 mu mwaka we likumi limonga na munana wa mu wuteme wakwe mutemi Yosiya.

Ndumilizo yo wutemi wa Yosiya

(2Wat 23:28-30)

20 Mutemi Yosiya yali yeza kuzituma zizi mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, nayo Neko u mutemi wa Misili, yakawa kutegula lipuga lye wasilikale wakwe, wakayihome Kalikemishi, he 'kolongo lya Efulata, ninga Yosiya yakajela kumwimila.
21 Nayo Neko, yakamutumila Yosiya munhu, yakamulonjela yakatya, “Gwe Yosiya wulugu wuwu, nha si nghwihoma na gwegwe yaye, agwe go mutemi wa Yuda. Hamba ane si nizize cihome na gwegwe yaye, ane langa nghusaka cihome ne walugu wangu. Nayo Mulungu kanonjela nha vyono ndece kucela yaye. Kanyhi Mulungu yali ku lubavu lwangu, holeka agwe wulece kunyimila ng'o, na hono wunyimile du, ale, Mulungu kokongomeza.”
22 Ninga mwene Yosiya, hamba si yasaka kuzihulika zinghani zo Mulungu, zono yazilonjela muli mutemi Neko. Langa yakawa yadumuye nghani vyono vikehome du. Hodu yakawa kubita ku wulugu, mu yisi yono wuhihi ya Megido.

23 Na mumo mu wulugu wuwo, mutemi Yosiya, yakagumwa ne zisonga ze Wamisili. Hodu yaza kuwona vyono yatamiswa, yakawa kuwalonjela wetumami wakwe yakutya, “Mbusenyi baha mbela, hambi nha nakondya kutamiswa muno muno!”
24 Hobaho, nawo, wakawa kumuwusa mwi'tuka lyo wulugu, lyono yali yapandile, wakamuwika mwi'tuka linji lyo wulugu, lyono wali wena nalyo behi baho, wakawa kumugomola ku Yelusalemi. Yosiya yakawa kufwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi. Ne wanhu wose wa Yuda na Yelusalemi wakamulilila.

25 Nayo muwizi Yelemiya, yakatula lwimbo lo kumulilila mutemi Yosiya. Nawo wukawa wo moto we wembi wose mu yisi yose ya Isilayeli, we cilume na we cicekulu kulwimba lwimbo lulo, lono wakumulilila Yosiya. No lwimbo lulu, luli mu citabu ce Cililo.

26 Ne zinghani zose za Yosiya zono yetuma, hamonga ne ling'ani yakwe kuli Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga no wibate we Lilajilizo,
27 hamonga ne zinghani zakwe zo wulawa mwamulizo, sunga ko ku mudumo, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce Zinghomo ze watemi wa Isilayeli na Yuda.


2 Zinghomo 36

Yowahazi u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 23:30-35)

1 I wanhu wa Yuda wakamuhagula Yowahazi, u mwana wa Yosiya, wakawa kumunoza yawe mutemi mu Yelusalemi.
2 Nayo Yowahazi yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na midatu lono yanziza kutema mu Yuda. Yakatema myezi midatu du, mu Yelusalemi.
3 Hambi Neko u mutemi wa Misili, nha yamusola nha mufungwa, yakayinoza Yuda yimulipe Neko kodi ya fweza zificile kilo elufu yidatu na magana mane, hamonga na zahabu kilo makumi madatu na yine.
4 Mutemi Neko, yakamutemya Eliyakimu u mwana wa Yosiya, yawe mutemi wa Yuda. Yakalikawaniza ne litagwa lyakwe, yawe Yehoyakimu. Mwene Neko, yakamusola Yowahazi yakamutegula ku Misili.

Yehoyakimu mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 23:36–24:7)

5 Yehoyakimu yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na mihano, lono yanziza kutema mu Yuda. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi, myaka yikumi na monga. Ninga yaketuma zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe.
6 Holeka Nebukadineza u mutemi wa ku Babiloni, yakaza yakayihoma Yuda, yakamusola Yehoyakimu muwopwe ne manyolo nha mufungwa, yakamutegula ku Babiloni.
7 Nebukadineza, yakasoleleza hamonga ne viya vinji vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakavitegula ku Babiloni, yakaviwika mu kaya yo wutemi.
8 Ne zinghani zose zono Yehoyakimu yetuma, hamonga ne makani gose mabi gono yetuma go wuhemba vihwani, zose zizo zandicigwe mu citabu ce Zinghomo ze watemi wa Isilayeli na Yuda.

Na Yehoyakimu u mwanagwe yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Yehoyakimu u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 24:8-17)

9 Yehoyakimu yali yaficile myaka yikumi na munana, lono yanziza kutema mu Yuda. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myezi midatu, na vilo yikumi du. Nayo nhondo ho yakacila muno kumusutizya Muwaha Mulungu.
10 Mwaka wali weza malika, Nebukadineza yakabita yakamusola Yehoyakimu mufungwa, yakamutegula nayo ku Babiloni, yakasoleleza hamonga ne viya vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakavitegula kuko ku Babiloni. Yakawa kumutemya Zedekiya u nduguye, yo yawe mutemi mu Yuda na Yelusalemi.

Zedekiya u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 24:18-20; Yel 52:1-3a)

11 Zedekiya yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na wumonga, lono yanziza kutema mu Yuda. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi, myaka yikumi na wumonga.
12 Ninga nayo nhavivyo, yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, hamba si yasakaga yamuhulicize ko winenenyhezi u muwizi Yelemiya mono yamulonjelaga zinghani zo Muwaha Mulungu.

U wugwe wa Yelusalemi

(2Wat 25:1-21; Yel 52:3b-11)

13 Ninga Zedekiya yakamubeza mutemi Nebukadineza, hamba yiwe Nebukadineza yakajela kumudindimiza yelahize mwi'tagwa lyo Mulungu, nha vyono kowa yali cihuliko kuli mwene. Zedekiya yakawa yasina hamba cihuliko, hamba si yenda ho yanene, yamupitucile Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli.
14 Hamonga na zizo, sunga ne walongola wose wa Yuda, hamonga ne wakohani wose, sunga ne wanhu wose, wakawa kuwutyatya musambo mubi we makungugo gono gali gawapilime, wakawa nawo wakuhemba vihwani. Na mu nzila yiyo, wakayavufya Nyumba yo Mulungu, yono u Muwaha Mulungu mweneco yayeza yiwe yelile.
15 Ninga u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo, yakajendelela kuwatumila wawizi, wawasugule wanhu wakwe. Hambi nha yakawawonela lusungu wanhu wakwe, hamonga ne Nyumba yakwe.
16 Ninga wene wakawazwajiza i watumwa wo Mulungu, no kuzibedegala zinghani zakwe, no kuwasekanga wawizi wakwe, sunga nhondo likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu likawacila muno wanhu wakwe, na wakawa wasina nzila yinji kanyhi yo kwilega.

17 Na ku soko yiyo, u Muwaha Mulungu, yakawa kumutegula mutemi wa Babiloni yabite yakawahome. Nayo mutemi yakawawulaga wazelelo wose wa Yuda, sunga na wono wali mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Yali yasina lusungu kuli munhu wo wose we cilume hamba kuli we cicekulu, hamba kuli wazelelo ne wahinza, hamba kuli wono wali watamigwe hamba wono wali waswanu. Nayo Mulungu yakawatajila wanhu wakwe mu miwoko ya Nebukadineza.
18 Nebukadineza mutemi wa Babiloni, yakavisola vinhu vyose vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne sawo yose, hamonga ne sawo yose ya mu kaya yo wutemi, hamonga ne sawo ye wetumami wakwe. Yakawa kuvitegula vyose ku Babiloni.
19 Yakayinyanya Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne likaya lyose lya Yelusalemi, hamonga ne vinhu vyose vyono vyali vili mumo, yakagakumulanga ne majinji gose ge likaya.
20 Ne wanhu wose wono wapona mu mbali yiyo, yakawasola wose, yakawategula ku Babiloni. Na kuko, wakawa wetumami wakwe hamonga ne wanagwe, sunga mu cipango cono wenjilila wutemi wa Pelisiya.
21 Na zizo zikawa ho yifisilizwe nghani yono Muwaha Mulungu yali yakondya kuyilonjela muli muwizi Yelemiya, lono yatya, “Ne yisi nayo yokwikala hamba si yolimwa mu myaka makumi mupunghati mu nghango ye myaka yo wucehe yono si yanozwa yaye.”

Mutemi Koleshi, yakuwakombola Wayahudi wawuye kwawo

(Ezi 1:1-4)

22 Na mu mwaka mulongozi, lono Koleshi mutemi wa Pelisiya yatemela, hobaho yakayifisiliza nghani yono Muwaha Mulungu yayilonjela muli muwizi Yelemiya. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yayisinhusa nhumbula yo mutemi Koleshi, yakalajiliza no kuwandika wulajilize wuwo, ho wusomwe kwe lizi yiwaha muno mu wutemi wakwe wose, yakutya,

23 “Awu wo wulajilize wangu ane Koleshi, u mutemi wa Pelisiya. U Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa ku wulanga, kanoza niwe mutemi we yinze yose. Kanyhi yanghomboye nene nimuzenjele ne Nyumba yakwe ku Yelusalemi mu Yuda. Holeka aluno, anye wose mono muli wanhu wo Mulungu, mubite kuko ku Yelusalemi, nayo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yawezane nanye.”
