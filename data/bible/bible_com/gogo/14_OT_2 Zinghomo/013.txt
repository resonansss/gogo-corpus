2 Zinghomo 13

Abiya wakwihoma na Yelobowamu

(1Wat 15:1-8)

1 Na mu mwaka we likumi na munana wo wutemi wa Yelobowamu muli Isilayeli, nayo Abiya, ho yatemela mu Yuda.
2 Abiya yatema myaka midatu du mu Yelusalemi. U nyina wa Abiya yacemwaga Mikaya u mwana we cicekulu wa Wuliyeli, munya kwi'kaya lya Gibeya.

Hodu, wukawa kugwa wulugu, Abiya na Yelobowamu, wakawa kwanza kwihoma.
3 Abiya yakawawikaniza wasilikale waficile elufu magana mane liwalo lyawo. Ninga Yelobowamu, nayo yakawikaniza wasilikale elufu magana munana liwalo lyawo, kuciza u winji we wasilikale wa Abiya.

4 Hodu, wasilikale wawo, wakawa kwitang'anilila mu yisi ye matunda ya Efulayimu. Mwene mutemi Abiya, yakawa kubita mucanyha ye litunda lya Zemalayimu, yakawa kwanza kumucemeleza Yelobowamu hamonga ne wanhu wakwe Waisilayeli, yakutya, “Ale, muhulicizaje ta!
5 Alo mwene si mumanyile yaye, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli, yawika cilagane na Dawudi cono si cilabeneka yaye, nha vyono, i watemi wose wa Isilayeli wolowa mu lulelo lwakwe cibitilila?
6 Ninga Yelobowamu u mwana wa Nebati, u mwitumami wa Solomona, yakamugaluka mutemi wakwe Solomona.
7 Hanyuma yakawakonghola hamonga mhuga ye wanhu we caka caka, wono wasina mahala, wono welajila nha wali vilungo ha mwande ya Lehobowamu u mwana wa Solomona, mono lulo yali yakali muzelelo mudodo, yasina wuzowele wuwaha ho yasume kuwemila.
8 Alu anye mukusaka mwisumanize ne vilungo vyo Muwaha Mulungu vyono yawapela wana wa Dawudi no lulelo lwakwe. Kanyhi mukwijela muza muli yipuga yiwaha muno lye Wasilikale, akuno mwina ne vihwani vye zindama ze zahabu, zono Yelobowamu yamunozezenyi, ziwe milungu yenyu.
9 Mukawawinga i wakohani wo Muwaha Mulungu, i wana wa Haluni, kanyhi mukawawinga ne Walawi. Nanye mukawawika wakohani wanji nha vyono wakunoza we zinghungugo zinji, ho wahalile hanhu hawo. Kanyhi no munhu wo wose hono koleta ndama, hamba nduwilu mupunghati, ayo langa kosuma kwisiwanula yawe mukohani we milungu yenyu.

10 “Ninga'se, cikali cikumwitumamila u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, cikali namuleka yaye. Ne wakohani wana wa Haluni, wo wakwituma milimo yawo yo wukohani, nawo Walawi, wakuwataziliza.
11 Na nhondo zose, hakali mitondo ne cinyamihe, wakumulavizya Mulungu, wuvumba hamonga ne vilavywa vinyanywe vye zimitugo. Kanyhi wakulavya ne vilavywa vye mikate yono yelile, wakuyijeleka mu meza. Kanyhi nhondo zose cinyamihe, wakukoleleza zitala mucanyha yo wuwiko wayo wa zahabu. Nase cikwituma nhondo zose, zono Muwaha Mulungu yalajiliza, ninga anye mwakondya kumuleka.
12 Kanyhi ase co wayenyu, u Mulungu yo mutonji wetu, ne wakohani wakwe wali baha cina nawo, wena ne zindulele, wewikanize wazitowe, ho waciceme cinjile mu milewo yo kwihoma nanye. Holeka anye nyo wanhu wa Isilayeli, mulecele lukulu kwihoma no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wanyu! Ninga hamba nhavivyo, si mosumila lukulu!”

13 Ninga kumbe Yelobowamu mwi'pango lilyo, yakawa yakondya kuwategula wasilikale wakwe wanji wakondya kuwapilima wanhu wa Yuda, ku migongo yawo. Na wono wasigala, vikawa vidomile.
14 Nawo wa Yuda, wali weza kupituka wakawona vyono wakondya kupilimwa ne walugu wawo. Hodu wakawa kumulilila Muwaha Mulungu yawataze, nawo wakohani wakatowa zindulele.
15 Nawo wa Yuda wakazozeza wose hamonga, hobaho wakawa kuwazumhila Waisilayeli no kuwahomanga, hasi yo wutonji wa Abiya. Nayo Mulungu yakamusuma Yelobowamu hamonga ne lipuga lyakwe lye Wasilikale lye Waisilayeli.
16 Waisilayeli hobaho wakawa kutiza ha mwande ye Wayuda. Nayo Mulungu yakawapela Wayuda cisumisumi, wakawasumiliza Waisilayeli.
17 Abiya ne wanhu wakwe, wakawahomanga muno mono Waisilayeli no kuwasuma. Na mwizuwa lilyo, wakawulagwa wasilikale vitung'ati we Waisilayeli, waficile elufu magana mahano.
18 Nhavivi vyo wasuma wanhu wa Yuda kuli Waisilayeli. Hambi nha wali wamwitumbiye Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo.

19 Abiya yakamulonda Yelobowamu ne wasilikale wakwe, yakagasola ne makaya gakwe ganji, Beteli, Yeshana, na Efuloni. Hamonga ne vitumbi tumbi vyose vyono vigapilime makaya gago.
20 Na mu vilo vyo wutemi wa Abiya, Yelobowamu si yatuga vilungo kanyhi. Sunga ndumilizo, Muwaha Mulungu yakamutowa Yelobowamu, yakafwa.

21 Nayo mutemi Abiya, yakajendelela kuwa ne vilungo. Abiya yali yatolile wacekulu yikumi na wane, yakawa yelelile wana we cilume makumi mejete na wejete, ne wana we cicekulu yikumi na mutandatu.
22 Ne zinghani za Abiya zono zisigaye, zono yalonga na zono yetuma, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce nghomo yo muwizi Ido.
