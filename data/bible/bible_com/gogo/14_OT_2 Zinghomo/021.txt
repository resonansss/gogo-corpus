2 Zinghomo 21

1 Yehoshafati yakafwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi, mu kaya ya Dawudi. Yeholamu u mwanagwe yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Yeholamu u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 8:17-24)

2 Yeholamu u mwana wa Yehoshafati u mutemi wa Yuda, yali yena wanduguze wali mutandatu, Azaliya, na Yehiyeli, na Zekaliya, na Azaliyahu, na Mikayeli, hamonga na Shefatiya.
3 U sogo yawo, yakawapela vipegwa vinji vya zahabu, na fweza, hamonga ne vinhu vinji vyo wuguzi wukamu. Kanyhi yakawawika wawe wawaha mu makaya ga Yuda, gono gena magomawakali, munhu ne kaya lyakwe. Ninga Yeholamu vyono yali yo mwana mulongozi kulelwa, Yehoshafati yakamunoza yawe yo muhazi wo wutemi wakwe.
4 Baho Yeholamu wutemi wakwe wali weza kutuga vilungo, hodu, yakawa kuwawulaga i wanduguze wose, hamonga ne wetumami wanji we Ciisilayeli.

5 Yeholamu yatema yaficile myaka makumi mejete na myejete. Yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka munana du.
6 Ninga mwene yakatyatya zinzila zimbi za Ahabu na ze watemi wa Isilayeli, na vyono yali yinha nyumba ya Ahabu. Yaketuma zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu.
7 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu si yenda yawongomeze wutemi wa Dawudi, hambi nha yali yehuwiza kuli mwitumami wakwe Dawudi, nha vyono lulelo lwakwe, lojendelela kutema vilo vyose.

8 Na mu vilo vyo mutemi Yeholamu, wanhu wa Edomu, wakayigaluka yisi ya Yuda, wakamutemya mutemi wawo weneco.
9 Yeholamu yakatuluka hamonga ne matuka gakwe gose go wulugu, yakabita yakawahoma Waedomu wono wali wamupilime u mwene, ne matuka gakwe go wulugu, hamonga ne wawaha we matuka.
10 Nayo yisi ya Edomu yikayigaluka yisi ya Yuda, sunga nalu. Na mwipango lilyo du, i wekazi wa Libina nawo wakayigaluka Yuda, vyono Yeholamu yali yakondya kumuleka Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wakwe.
11 Na kanyhi yakazenga ne hanhu henji ho wuhembelo we wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu mu yisi ya Yuda. Yakawa kuwanoza i wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, waketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu.

12 U muwizi Eliya, yakamwandicila Yoholamu baluwa, yono yali yayandice nhavi, “Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wo mulezi wako Dawudi, yakukuhosa, vyono agwe si wawutyatya musambo muswanu wo sogo mutemi Yehoshafati, hamba musambo muswanu wo kukuyo mutemi Asa.
13 Ninga agwe, kutyatya musambo mubi we watemi wa Isilayeli, no kuwatonga wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, wawe si wehuwilwa kuli Mulungu, nha vyono Ahabu yanoza hamonga ne wahazi wakwe, wono wawatonga vibi Waisilayeli, wakawa si wehuwilwa. Kanyhi agwe kuwawulaga ne wanduguzo, nawo kumbe wo wali wanhu waswanu si nha gwegwe.
14 Holeka lu, ku soko ya zizo, nayo Muwaha Mulungu, kowalipiciza i wanhu wako ko wulipicizwe wuwaha muno muno, hamonga ne wana wenyu, ne wagolece zenyu, no kuvyongomezeza lukulu vinhu vyenyu vyose.
15 Nagwe go mweneco, kotuga wutamwa wubi wa munda, wono wojendelela konjezeka izuwa ne'zuwa sunga no wutumbu wako wulawile kunze.”

16 Hali ho wali Wafilisiti wanji, hamonga ne Walabu, wono wekalaga behi ne hanhu hono wali wekaye Wakushi mu mwalambalo we lilenga. Muwaha Mulungu yakawasagahiza wawo, wabite vikehome na Yeholamu.
17 Wakayihoma Yuda, wakayisola kaya yo wutemi, wakawasoleleza nha wafungwa i wagolece wose wo mutemi, hamonga ne wanagwe wose, ninga Ahaziya u mwanagwe u mudodo, yuyo langa wakamuleka.

18 Hanyuma ya zizo, Muwaha Mulungu yakamuletela mutemi Yeholamu wutamwa wo wusungu wukali mu'nda yakwe wono si wopona.
19 No wutamwa wuwo wukacila konjezeka mhola mhola, myaka myejete. Sunga nhondo, mutemi yakawa kufwa ko wusungu wuwaha muno muno. Ne wanhu wakwe si wakoleleza moto wuwe muwaha so kumulilila, nha vyono wawanozezaga i walezi wakwe.

20 Yeholamu yatema lono yali yaficile myaka makumi madatu na myejete, yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka munana du. Na hasina hamba munhu monga mono yamukalalila lono yafwila. Wakamuzika mu kaya ya Dawudi, ninga si wamuzika mu vitenjelo vye watemi yaye.
