2 Zinghomo 24

Yowashi u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 12:1-16)

1 Yowashi, yakatema mu yisi ya Yuda, lono yali yaficile myaka mupunghati du. Yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi mane cititila. U nyina wa Yowashi yacemwaga Zibiya munya kwi'kaya lya Belisheba.
2 Nayo Yowashi, mu vilo vyo wutemi wakwe yaketuma zono zamwendezaga Muwaha Mulungu, mu vilo vyose vyo wikale wa Yehoyada mukohani, lono yakali mumi.
3 Nayo Yehoyada mukohani, yakamuhagulila mutemi Yowashi wacekulu wejete, yakawa kuwatola. Nawo wacekulu wawo wakamulelela Yowashi wana we cilume na we cicekulu.

4 Nayo mutemi Yowashi, yali yeza kwikala vilo vilo mu wutemi wakwe, yakawona viswanu yayinozeleze Nyumba yo Mulungu, mono yihodanjice.
5 Na ku soko yiyo, yakawatuma wakohani, hamonga ne Walawi, wabite mu yisi yose ya Yuda, wawalonjele wanhu, wahangulize zipesa zono zokwila mu wunozeleze we Nyumba yo Mulungu. Yakawalonjela nha vyono, wayinoze nghani yiyo mbela mbela, ninga Walawi wakacela.
6 Hobaho yakamucema Yehoyada mukohani u mulongola wawo, yakamuwuza yakatya, “Alo socici si wawatyatilila i Walawi, ho wasume kukonghola kodi, mu yisi ya Yuda na Yelusalemi, kodi yono Musa u mwitumami wo Muwaha Mulungu, yayikongholaga kuli wanhu, izipesa zizo, ho zisume kutaza milimo ya mwi'hema lyono lyelile?”

7 (Hambi i watyatyi wa Ataliya, u mucekulu yulya mubi, nha wali wakondya kuyimwagula Nyumba yo Mulungu, wakasola viya vinji muno vyono vyelile, wakavitumamila mu wuhembi we Mabaali.)

8 Nayo mutemi Yowashi, yakawalajiliza i Walawi, wanoze lisanduku, lyono lyokwitumamilwa so kulavizya vilavywa, waliwice ha mulango we Nyumba yo Mulungu.
9 Nawo Walawi, wakawa kuwalalika wanhu wose wa Yelusalemi na Yuda, vyono yiwanojeye wose wamulavizye kodi Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono Musa u mwitumami wo Mulungu, yayikonghola kodi ha lulongozi mu nyika.
10 Ne nghani yiyi, yikawendeza wanhu wose hamonga ne walongola wawo, nawo wakawa kuzileta zipesa zawo ze kodi, wakalimemya lisanduku twaa.
11 Na nhondo zose lono lisanduku lyakondya kumema, i Walawi walidinghulaga no kulitegula kuli wetumami wo mutemi, wono wali wo walanjilizi. Nayo u mwandici wo mutemi, hamonga no mulindilizi wo Mukohani Muwaha, wawa kuzijida zipesa, no kuligomola kanyhi lisanduku hanhu hayo. Na mu nzila yiyo, wakonghola pesa nyinji muno muno.

12 Ale u mutemi nayo, hamonga na Yehoyada mukohani, wawa kuzisola zipesa zizo, wawa kuwapela i walanjilizi wo mulimo wo wunozelezi we Nyumba yo Mulungu, nawo wawa kuwalipa i wazenji ne waselemala, hamonga ne watyani wono watyanaga vyuma, ne shaba, wono wayinozelezaga Nyumba yo Mulungu.
13 Ne wazenji wawa wose, wetumaga wahidimbaye muno mu milimo yawo, wakayinozeleza viswanu muno Nyumba yo Mulungu, yikawa nha vyono yali halulongozi.
14 Nawo wunozelezi we Nyumba yo Mulungu wali weza kukondya, zahabu yose yono yasigala, hamonga ne fweza, wakayileta kuli mutemi hamonga na Yehoyada mukohani. Nawo wakanoza zibakuli ne viya vye hayo hayo so Nyumba yo Mulungu.

Zinzila za Yehoyada mukohani zikukawanizwa

Mu vilo vyose vyono Yehoyada yali yakali cawumi, makumbiko galavizwaga nhondo zose ha Nyumba yo Mulungu.
15 Ninga Yehoyada yali yeza kuficila mu myaka yigana limonga na makumi madatu, yakawa yakombipa muno, yakawa kufwa.
16 Wakamuzika mu vitenjelo vye watemi mwi'kaya lya Dawudi, ku soko vyono yetuma ziswanu muli Waisilayeli, na kuli Mulungu, hamonga na mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.

17 Ninga Yehoyada mukohani yali yeza kufwa, hodu, i walongola we Wayuda, wakawa kubita kumupepeleza mutemi Yowashi, nayo yakawahulicila zinghani zawo.
18 Na ku wulawa baho, i wanhu wakawa kupela ku wubita kuhemba ku Nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo, wakawa kwanza kuhemba vihwani, hamonga ne cihwani co mulungu we cicekulu Ashela. Na ku soko yo wubi wuwo, likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu likawaziza wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi.
19 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, u Muwaha Mulungu, yakawategulila wawizi, wawasugule wamuwuyile mwene. Ninga i wanhu wakalema hamba si wawahuliciza yaye.
20 Hanyuma u muhe wo Mulungu wukamuziza Zekaliya u mwana wa Yehoyada mukohani. Hobaho Zekaliya, yakabita yakema i hanhu hono kosuma kuwa yejeye hali wanhu wose, yakawa kuwalonjela wanhu yakutya, “Nye wayangu, u Muwaha Mulungu yakumuwuzenyi, nhawule mwagabeza i malajilizo gakwe, nalu mweza kwiyiletela wongomezwe nyenye nyo weneco! Hambi nha mwakondya kumuleka mwene, holeka nayo yakondya kumulecenyi nhavivyo!”
21 Nayo mutemi Yowashi yakelunza ne wanhu ho wamutendele zimbi Zekaliya. Hobaho mutemi yakalajiliza wanhu wamutowanje Zekaliya ne mabwe. Nawo wakawa kumutowanga no kumuwulajila he'kumbo lye Nyumba yo Mulungu.
22 Ati hobaho du, mutemi Yowashi yakawa yakondya kuzisemwa zo wuswanu zose, zono Yehoyada u sogwe wa Zekaliya yamutendela, hobaho yakawa kumukombola Zekaliya yawulagwe. Ninga nayo Zekaliya lono yafwizaga, yalonga yakatya, “Ale du, u Muwaha Mulungu yaziwonaje zono mukuzituma, nanye yamulipicizenyi!”

Ndumilizo yo wutemi wa Yowashi

23 Na ha ndumilizo yo mwaka wuwo, i wasilikale wanya Siliya, wakaza, wakayihoma Yuda na Yelusalemi, wakawawulaga i walongola wose, wakateka vinhu vinji muno, wakabita navyo ku Damesiki.
24 Ne lipuga lye wasilikale wa Siliya, lyali yipuga yidodo du, ninga Muwaha Mulungu, yakawapela wusume wo kuwasuma wasilikale wenji wa Yuda, vyono wene wali wakondya kumuleka Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo. Na mu nzila yiyi, mutemi Yowashi mo yalipicizizwa.
25 Nayo Yowashi, yakatuga nghomele mbaha, nawo i walugu wali weza kuwaleka, wanji wejete wa muli wetumami wakwe, wakamugaluka wakamuwulaga lono yali yagonile mu citanda cakwe, wakawa kwilipiciza ku soko yo wuwulagwe wa Zekaliya u mwana wa Yehoyada mukohani. Yowashi yakazikwa mwi'kaya lya Dawudi, ninga si wamuzika mu vitenjelo vye watemi.
26 (Ne wanhu wono wamugaluka, yali Zabadi u mwana wo mucekulu we Ciamoni mono yacemwaga Shimeyati, hamonga na Yehozabadi u mwana wo mucekulu we Cimowabu mono yacemwaga Shimuliti.)
27 Na mu mandiko gono gali mu citabu ce Zinghomo ze metumo ge watemi, mumo mo mozili ne zinghani ze wana wa Yowashi, no wuwizi wono Yowashi yawililwa, hamonga na ze nyituma yakwe, yo mulimo wo wunozelezi we Nyumba yo Mulungu.

Nayo Amaziya u mwanagwe, yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.
