2 Zinghomo 17

Yehoshafati yakuwa mutemi

1 Yehoshafati yakahala wutemi wo sogwe Asa, yakawa ne vilungo muli Isilayeli.
2 Yakawika mapuga ge wasilikale mu makaya ge magomawakali gose ga mu yisi ya Yuda, sunga na mu makaya ga mu yisi ya Efulayimu gono Asa yagasola.
3 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakamumotya muno Yehoshafati, vyono Yehoshafati yatyatya musambo muswanu we mikalile miswanu yo sogwe Asa, ya halulongozi, hamba si yahemba Baali.
4 Mwene yamwitumamila du u Mulungu wo sogwe, yakagebata malajilizo go Mulungu, hamba si yetuma nha vyono i watemi we Waisilayeli wetuma.
5 Na ku soko yiyo, Muwaha Mulungu nayo, yakamupela Yehoshafati wusume wuwaha wo kusuma kuwulongoza wutemi wa Yuda. Ne wanhu wose wakamuletela vipegwa. Na ku soko yiyo Yehoshafati yakawa mugoli muno, kanyhi munhu we 'lumbo yiwaha muno.
6 Nayo yakahidimbala muno kumwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu, yakahongomeza i hanhu hose hono wahembelaga i wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu, hamonga ne vihwani vyose vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela mu yisi ya Yuda.

7 Na mu mwaka we kadatu, wa mu wuteme wakwe, yakawatuma wanhu awano, wakawejize wanhu mu makaya ga Yuda, nha cinji Benihayili, Obadiya, Zekaliya, Netaneli, hamonga na Mikaya.
8 Ne wanhu wawo mu wubite wawo, wakabitana hamonga ne Walawi, wali'cenda, ne Wakohani wejete. Nawo i Walawi yali Shemaya, Netaniya, Zebadiya, Asaheli, Shemilamoti, Yehonatani, Adoniya, Tobiya, Tobadoniya. Nawo wakohani wali wowa, Elishama na Yeholamu.
9 Wakacisola citabu co Wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu, wakabita mu makaya gose ga Yuda, wakawejiza wanhu.

Wukumuce wa Yehoshafati

10 Muwaha Mulungu yakawanoza wanhu wose wo wutemi wono wupilime, wogope kubita kwihoma no mutemi Yehoshafati.
11 I Wafilisiti wanji, wakamuletela Yehoshafati fweza nyinji muno, hamonga ne vipegwa vinji. Nawo Walabu wanji, wakamuletela ngholo zificile elufu mupunghati na magana mupunghati, hamonga na mhene nazo elufu mupunghati, na magana mupunghati.
12 Na ku soko yiyo, Yehoshafati yakacila muno kuwa ne vilungo mu wutemi wakwe. Yakazenga magomawakali hamonga ne makaya, mu yisi yose ya Yuda.
13 Nayo yakawika mumo viwutwa vinji muno muno.

Namo mu Yelusalemi, yakawika wanhu vitung'ati, wono wamanyile milewo,
14 mu nghango ye zimhuga zawo. Adina yali yo muwaha we mhuga ye wasilikale we mhuga ya Yuda, nayo yali yena wasilikale waficile elufu magana madatu hasi yakwe.
15 Na we kejete yali Yehohanani, nayo yali yena wasilikale waficile elufu magana mejete na makumi munana.
16 Na we kadatu, yali Amasiya u mwana wa Zicili, nayo yali yena wasilikale waficile elufu magana mejete cititila. (Yuyu Amasiya, yali yelavizye du, yamwitumamile Muwaha Mulungu.)
17 No muwaha we mhuga ye wasilikale wono walawa mu mhuga ya Benyamini, yali Eliyada, munhu citung'ati, nayo yali yena wasilikale waficile elufu magana mejete du cititila, wono wali wena ne zingula zawo, hamonga no wupinde wawo.
18 No mutyatyi wakwe we kejete, yali Yehozabadi, nayo yali yena wasilikale waficile elufu yigana limonga na makumi munana.
19 Ne wanhu wawo wose, wamwitumamilaga mutemi mumo mu Yelusalemi, hamonga ne wasilikale wanji kanyhi, wono yawawika mu makaya ganji ge magomawakali ga mu yisi ya Yuda.
