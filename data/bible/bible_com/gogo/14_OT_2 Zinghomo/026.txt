2 Zinghomo 26

Uziya u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 14:21-22; 15:1-7)

1 I wanhu wose wa Yuda, wakawa kumwihaga Uziya u mwana wa Amaziya, yaficile myaka yikumi na mutandatu, yahale wutemi wo sogwe.
2 (Yali yeza kufwa Amaziya, ho baho Uziya ho yayisolela kanyhi Elati, no kulizenga kanyhi likaya.)

3 Uziya yatema lono yali yaficile myaka yikumi na mutandatu, nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi mahano na myejete. U nyina yacemwaga Yekoliya munya ku Yelusalemi.
4 Nayo Uziya yakatyatya musambo muswanu wo sogwe Amaziya. Yakamwendeza Muwaha Mulungu mu witume wakwe.
5 Na mu mapango gose, lono Zekaliya yali yakali mumi, mono yamutazaga muli ze nghani yo Mulungu, Uziya yakamwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu ko wihuwilwa, na ku soko yiyo, nayo Mulungu yakamulugutiza.

6 Hanyuma mwi'zuwa linji, Uziya yakabita kwihoma ne Wafilisiti. Yakagamwagulanga majinji ge makaya ga Gati, na Yamuniya na Ashidodi. Mwene yakazenga makaya ganji ge magomawakali, behi na Ashidodi, hamonga na ku lubavu lono lwasigala lwe yisi ye Wafilisiti.
7 Nayo Mulungu, yakamupela wusume wuwaha kuli Wafilisiti, hamonga ne Walabu wono wekalaga ku Gulibali, hamonga ne Wameyuni.
8 Sunga ne Waamoni wakalipa kodi kuli Uziya. Nayo yakawa ne vilungo viwaha, ne lilumbo lyakwe likatandala sunga ko ku Misili.

9 Mutemi Uziya, yakalizenjela magomawakali likaya lya Yelusalemi, yakazenga ne minala ha mulango we cipeto, na ha mulango wa kwi'lolo, sunga ne hanhu hose hono lijinji lyali lina cipeto.
10 Kanyhi yakazenga minala mu nyika, yakahimba ne malamba menji muno, hambi nha yali yena matewa mawaha ge zimitugo, mu mwalambalo we matunda go lubavu lwa kumwezi, sunga na mu yisi yo wuhihi. Hambi mwene nha yali mulimi muno, na ku soko yiyo i wanhu nawo wakakwegwa no mulimo wuwo wo kulima, wakalima zabibu mu yisi ye matunda, no kuyilima yisi yono yina moso mwinji.

11 Kanyhi yali yewikanizize mhuga mbaha muno ye wasilikale, wono wali wewikanizize lukulu so milewo. Ne zinghani zose, zandikwaga ne wandici wakwe, Yeyiyeli, na Maseya, mu wulanjilizi wa Hananiya, yumonga wa muli wetumami wo mutemi.
12 Ne mhuga yiyo mbaha ye wasilikale, yali yili hasi yo wulanjilizi wa wanhu elufu nyejete na magana mutandatu.
13 Nawo wawo, wali walanjilizi we wasilikale wono waficile elufu magana madatu na mupunghati, na magana mahano, wono wali vitung'ati muno mu wulugu, wono wamutazaga mutemi kuli walugu wakwe.
14 Nayo mutemi Uziya, yakawagawila wasilikale wose, ngula, ne migoha, ne zikofiya ze cuma, hamonga ne vicitizo vya cuma, no wupinde, ne zisonga zayo, ne mabwe ge zinghomango, go kugumila kwa nhago.
15 Na mumo mu Yelusalemi, i wanghula wakwe, wakanoza mitambo yono yikusuma kugumila zimhagali, hamonga ne mabwe mawaha mu wulawa mu minala, na mu vipeto vye lijinji lye likaya. Ne lilumbo lyakwe, likatandala hanhu hose, nayo yakacila kuwa ne vilungo mu wutemi wakwe, ku soko yo wutazi wuwaha wono yawutuga kulawa kuli Mulungu.

Uziya yakulipicizwa ku soko yo wiwoni wakwe

16 Ninga u mutemi Uziya, yali yeza kuwona vyono yatuga vilungo, hodu yakawa kwinjilwa no wikumye. No wikumye wuwo, wukawa kumwinjiza mu wituma zimbi. Yakamubedegala Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe, so winjila mweneco mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, no kufusa wuvumba, mu cikumbicilo co kufusiza wuvumba.
17 Ninga Azaliya mukohani, hamonga ne wakohani wayagwe waficile makumi munana, vitung'ati wo Muwaha Mulungu, kanyhi wasina woga, wakamutyatya mutemi Uziya kuko ku cikumbicilo,
18 wakamwimila wakatya, “Gwe Uziya! Yo si ling'ani yako 'gwe, kufusa wuvumba hali Muwaha Mulungu. Hambi nha yiwanojeye wakohani wono walecelwa na Haluni, kanyhi wakasiwanulwa, wawo du wo wanojeye wetume mulimo wuwu. Holeka wuwuce lulu baha i hanhu baha hono helile. Nalu wakondya kwituma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu. Holeka hamba si kojendelela kuwa no wulugutwe wakwe kanyhi.”

19 Nayo Uziya yakawa vyo yemile baho du ha mhelo ye cikumbicilo mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, akuno vyo yebate cifusizo co wuvumba mu muwoko wakwe. Yakawakolelwa likuwo liwaha wawo wakohani. Na lulo baho du yakatuga wutamwa wo wukoma mu cihanda co wusu wakwe.
20 Nayo Azaliya mukohani, hamonga ne wakohani wayagwe, wakawa wamulanjile Uziya ku wusu, ko woga wuwaha muno. Na hanyuma nhondo, wakawa kumulavyaniza kunze, yawuce mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Nayo yakasunha muno kulawila kunze, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yali yamulipiciza.

21 Na mu wikale wakwe, we vilo vyakwe vyono vyali visigaye, mutemi Uziya, yali yeyavufye hali Muwaha Mulungu, ku soko yo wukoma, wono yali yena nawo. Na ku soko yiyo, si yasuma kanyhi kwinjila mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Mwene yekalaga du mu kaya yakwe, hamba si yasuma kanyhi kwituma no mulimo wakwe wo wutemi. Yotamu u mwanagwe, yo yajendela kuyitema yisi.

22 Nayo muwizi Isaya, u mwana wa Amozi, yo yazandika zinghani zinji, zono mutemi Uziya yetuma mu wutemi wakwe.
23 Nhondo Uziya yakawa kufwa, yakazikwa behi no mugunda wo kuzika watemi, ku soko yo wutamwa wakwe wo wukoma, holeka si wamuzika mu vitenjelo vye watemi. Na Yotamu u mwanagwe yakahala wutemi wakwe.
