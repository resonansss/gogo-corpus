2 Zinghomo 23

Ataliya yakugalukwa

(2Wat 11:4-16)

1 Ninga mu mwaka wo mupunghati, Yehoyada mukohani, yakawalajiliza wetumami walongola we walindilizi wo mutemi. Yakanoza cilagane ne wawaha wahano we wasilikale, Azaliya u mwana wa Yelohamu, Ishumayeli u mwana wa Yehohanani, Azaliya u mwana wa Obedi, Maseya u mwana we cilume wa Adaya, hamonga na Elishafati u mwana wa Zikili.
2 Wakagajendela makaya gose ga Yuda, wakawakonghola Walawi, hamonga ne wawaha wose we milango ye Waisilayeli, wakawaleta ku Yelusalemi.

3 Wose wakawa kwikongholela mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, wakanoza cilagane na Yowashi u mwana wo mutemi Ahaziya. Nayo Yehoyada, yakawalonjela yakatya, “Nye wayangu! Ayuno ahano yo mwana wa malehemu mutemi Ahaziya. Nayo 'luno yo kowa mutemi, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yehuwiza vyono u lulelo lo mutemi Dawudi, wo wowa kutema.
4 Nase yiyi yo coyinoza. Holeka, kolo wakohani ne Walawi wakuza mu milimo yawo, mwizuwa lye Sabato, wanji wolindiliza milango ye Nyumba yo Mulungu,
5 na wanji wolilindiliza likulu. Na wono wowa wasigaye, awo wowikwa ha mulango wo musinji. Ne wanhu wose wokwihanguliziza hamonga mwi'kumbo lye Nyumba yo Mulungu.
6 Kanyhi howa hasina munhu wo wose mono kokwinjila mu Nyumba yo Mulungu hamba kulawaniza, langa wawo du wakohani ne Walawi, wono wowa wali mu wipiganze. Wawo du wo wosuma kwinjila, hambi wene nha welile. Ninga wanhu wose wono wasigaye, yiwanojeye webate muno muno wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu, wekale kunze.
7 Nawo i Walawi wowa wemile wamupilime mutemi wakumudima, wowa webate ne vitwalo mu miwoko yawo. Yowa yiwanojeye wawe hamonga no mutemi du ko kose kono kowa kubita. No munhu wo wose mono kokwijelela yenjile mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, ayo kowulagwa.”

8 Nawo Walawi, hamonga ne wanhu wose wa Yuda, wakanoza nhavivyo nha vyono yawalajiliza Yehoyada. Ne wanhu si wakombolwa weyagale yaye, i wawaha wakawa kusola munhu na wanhu wakwe, muli wono wokwinjila mu mulimo mwi'zuwa lye Sabato, hamonga na wono wowa wacehile mwi'zuwa lilyo, wawo wose wakajendelela no mulimo. Hambi u mukohani Yehoyada, nha si yawakombola wawe no wilecele.
9 Baho, mukohani Yehoyada, yakawa kuwapela wawaha wose, migoha hamonga ne zingula, zono zali zo mutemi Dawudi, zono zali mbice mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
10 Yakawika wanhu wono wali webate ne mimaje mu miwoko yawo, wakatandala wapilime ha mwande ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, ho wamudime mutemi.
11 Hanyuma Yehoyada mukohani, yakawa kumulavya Yowashi hanze, yakamuvwika mukumbo mu mutwe wakwe, yakamwibatiza mu miwoko, u walaka wo wulajilize, wono womutonga mu wutemi wakwe. Na hobaho yakawa yanozwa kuwa mutemi. Hanyuma Yehoyada mukohani, hamonga ne wanagwe, wakawa kumwitila Yowashi mafuta go witile, ne wanhu wose wakazozeza hamonga wakatya, “Mutemi yawumipe!”

12 Nayo Ataliya yali yeza kuhulika mazi ge wanhu, hamonga ne mazi ge walindilizi, wakumukumya mutemi, hobaho mwene yakenjila musunho mu Nyumba yo Mulungu kono wanhu wali wekongholeye.
13 Na kuko, yakamuwona mutemi mupya, yemile ha mulango we Nyumba yo Mulungu, behi ne lisumbili lyono lyawikwa so watemi du, yapilimwe ne wawaha we wasilikale, akuno ne zindulele zikutowa. Ne wanhu wose wazozize muno so nyemo, akuno wakutowa ne zindulele, hamonga ne wembi wa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, nawo wakawa wakuzilongoza zinyimbo zo lukumyo. Hobaho mwene Ataliya, yakawa kuvidemulanga vivwalo vyakwe ko wusungu wuwaha muno, akuno yazozize yakutya, “Wugaluci! Wugaluci!”

14 Ninga Yehoyada mukohani, si yenda Ataliya yawulajilwe mu ciwanza ce Nyumba yo Mulungu. Holeka yakawacema i wawaha we wasilikale, yakawatya, “Ale, musolenyi mumuwice haciligati ye walindilizi, kanyhi mweze mwiwikanize, mumuwulaje munhu wo wose mono kojela kumutaziza.”

15 Hobaho wakawa kumwibata, wakawa kumutegula sunga ko kwi kulu, wakamuwulajila ha mulango we zifwalasi.

Wunozeleze wa Yehoyada

(2Wat 11:17-20)

16 Mukohani Yehoyada, yakamusola mutemi Yowashi, hamonga ne wanhu wose, wakanoza cilagane hamonga nayo, nha vyono wowa wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu.
17 Hanyuma wakawa kubita wose hamonga sunga ko ku wuhembelo wa Baali wakawumwagulanga. Wakavitulanga vikumbicilo, hamonga ne vihwani vyose, wakamuwulaga na Matani, u mukohani wa Baali, wakamuwulajila ha mwande ye vikumbicilo.
18 Yehoyaga mukohani, yakawawika wakohani ne Walawi, wawe wo walanjilizi we milimo yose ya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Nawo wakawa wakwituma milimo yawo, nha vyono wawicilwa no mutemi Dawudi, hamonga no kunyanya makumbiko i malavye kuli Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono winha wulajilize wa Musa. Kanyhi wakawa wo walanjilizi we zinyimbo hamonga no wityatye we zisikukuu zose.
19 Kanyhi Yehoyada mukohani, yakawawika walindilizi, walindilize ha milango ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, ho walanjilile munhu wo wose mono mwavufywe, yalece kwinjila.

20 Nawo i wawaha we wasilikale, ne walongola we wanhu hamonga ne wetumami wose ne wanhu wose wono wasigaye, wawo wose, wakelunza na Yehoyada mukohani, wakawa kujenda watunjile wose hamonga, wakumuusindiciza mutemi, ku wulawa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, wakumtegula ku kaya yakwe yo wutemi. Nawo wali weza kufika kwi'kulu, wakenjilila ku mulango muwaha. Nayo mutemi yakawa kwikala mwi'goda lyakwe lyo wutemi.
21 Ne wanhu wose wakawa na nyemo mbaha muno, ne likaya likawa litulile muno, hambi Ataliya nha yali yakondya kuwulagwa.
