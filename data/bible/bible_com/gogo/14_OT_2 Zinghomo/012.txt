2 Zinghomo 12

Wamisili wakuyihoma yisi ya Yuda

(1Wat 14:25-28)

1 Kukawa lono wutemi wa Lehobowamu wali wakondya kukama, nayo yatuga vilungo, hodu, mwene hamonga ne wanhu wakwe wakawa kulileka Lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu.
2 Na mu mwaka we 'hano wa mu wuteme wakwe Lehobowamu, u Muwaha Mulungu yakawaletela wulipicizwe ku soko yo wula cihuliko wawo. U mutemi Shishaki wa Misili, yakaza yakayihoma Yelusalemi,
3 yakawa yena lipuga yiwaha lye wasilikale, lyono lyali lina matuka go wulugu, gaficile elufu monga na magana mejete, hamonga ne wapanda fwalasi, waficile wasilikale elufu makumi mutandatu, ne wasilikale wenji muno muno wasina ne liwalo. Wakawa wahangahanjile ne wasilikale wono walawa ku Libiya ne Wasuki, ne Wakushi.
4 Yakagasola makaya ga Yuda gono gali mazenjele magomawakali, yakajendelela sunga ko ku Yelusalemi.

5 Na lono mutemi Lehobowamu, hamonga ne walongola wose wa mu yisi ya Yuda, wali wekongholeye ha Yelusalemi wakumutiza Shishaki, muwizi Shemaya yakawabitila, yakawalonjela yakatya, “Ayi yo nghani yo Muwaha Mulungu kuli'nye, yakutya, ‘Vyono anye mwandeka, ane nane nakondya kumulecenyi mu miwoko ya Shishaki.’ ”

6 Nayo mutemi Lehobowamu, hamonga ne walongola wose, wakavumila nha vyono mbeka wetuma wubi. Wakawa kutya “Mbeka zono Muwaha Mulungu yakwituma, zo ze ling'ani.”

7 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yali yeza kuziwona zizo, hodu yakamutuma kanyhi muwizi Shemaya, yakumutya, “Alu, vyono wanhu wawa wakondya kuwuvumila wubi wawo, hodu, nane' lu si nowongomeza yaye. Ninga langa, kolo mutemi Shishaki yeza kuwahoma, ane nowaponya. Nayo Yelusalemi si yoliwona likuwo lyangu.
8 Ninga Shishaki kowasuma, nawo baho ho womanyila wisimajile wo kuwahemba nene, no kuhemba watemi wa mono yisi.”

9 Nayo mutemi Shishaki, yakawa kuza ku Yelusalemi, yakawa kuyisola sawo yose yono yali mbice mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga na mu kaya yo wutemi. Yakasola vinhu vinji du, hamonga ne zahabu, na ngula zono mutemi Solomona yazinoza.
10 Nayo mutemi Lehobowamu, yakanoza ngula zinji za shaba, yakaziwika mu miwoko ye walindilizi we milango ye likulu.
11 Na lono yenjilaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, i walindilizi wakwe, wazisolaga ngula, wawa kubitana nayo. Na lono yalawilaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, i walindilizi wakwe nawo wazigomolaga ngula hanhu hayo ho wuwiko wayo.
12 Na vyono mutemi Lehobowamu yakacila kwinenenyheza hali Muwaha Mulungu, hodu, ne likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, si lyamongomeza yaye. Ne zinghani zose zikawa kujendelela viswanu mu yisi ya Yuda.

Wulolose we ndumula, kuli zo wutemi wa Lehobowamu

13 Lehobowamu yakatema mu Yelusalemi, ne vilungo vyo wutemi wakwe, vikonjelezeka muno. Nayo yali yaficile myaka makumi mane na monga lono yatemela. Yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka yikumi na mupunghati, mu kaya yono Muwaha Mulungu yayihagula mu wulawa mu yisi yose ye Waisilayeli, hawe ho hanhu hono mweneco kowa kuhembelwa. U nyina wa Lehobowamu, yacemwaga Naama, munya yisi ya Amoni.
14 Lehobowamu yetuma zimbi, vyono si yasaka mweneco kuwulondola wuhile wo Muwaha Mulungu.

15 Na zose zono yetuma Lehobowamu, ku wulawilila mwamulizo, sunga ko ku ndumilizo, hamonga ne zinghani ze we mhuga yakwe, zose zijeye mu citabu ce zinghomo co muwizi Shemaya, hamonga na mu nghomo yo muwizi Ido. Nayo Lehobowamu na Yelobowamu, wekalaga wakwihoma du vilo vyose.
16 Lehobowamu yakafwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi mu kaya ya Dawudi. Abiya u mwanagwe yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.
