2 Zinghomo 16

Yuda na Isilayeli zikwitemanuka

(1Wat 15:17-22)

1 Na mu mwaka we makumi madatu na mutandatu, wo wuteme wo mutemi Asa wa Yuda, baho Baasha mutemi wa Isilayeli yakayihoma yisi ya Yuda, yakazenga Lama, ho yawemile wanhu walece kulawila hamba kwinjila mu yisi ya Yuda.
2 Nayo Asa yali yeza kuziwona zizo, yakasola fweza hamonga na zahabu, mu wulawa mu viwutwa vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu na vya mwi'kulu, yakawa kuvitegula ku Damesiki kuli mutemi Benihadadi wa Siliya, yakamutya,
3 “Ane nghukunena, cilunze hamonga nagwe, nha vyono i wasogo zetu nawo wali welunzize. Nane nghuletela fweza yiyo, hamonga ne zahabu, viwe cipegwa cako du. Holeka aluno nghukunena, wiwusaje mu wilunze wako na Baasha u mutemi we Waisilayeli, nayo ho kosuma kugawusa mapuga gakwe ge wasilikale mu yisi yangu.”

4 Nayo Benihadadi, yakayivumila nghani yiyo yono Asa yayilonga. Hodu ho baho, yakawatuma wetumami wakwe, hamonga ne mapuga ge wasilikale wakwe, wabite wakagahome makaya ge Waisilayeli. Nawo wakabita, wakayisola lyoni, Dani, Abeli-mayimu, hamonga ne makaya gose ge yisi ya Nafutali, i hanhu hono vyali viwicigwe viwutwa.
5 Nayo mutemi Baasha yali yeza kuyihulika nghani yiyo, hodu, hobaho yakawa kulecelela kuyizenga Lama, yakawuleka mulimo wakwe.
6 Hanyuma mutemi Asa yakawakonghola hamonga wanhu wose ku wulawa mu'si yose ya Yuda, yakawalajiliza wakagasole mabwe gose hamonga ne mabici gose gono Baasha yazenjelaga ku Lama. Wakagetumamila mu wuzenjela Geba na Mizipa.

U muwizi Hanani

7 Na mu madaha gago, u muwizi Hanani yakamubitila Asa mutemi wa Yuda, yakamutya, “Alu vyono kumwitumbila mutemi we Siliya, hamba si wumwitumbiye yaye Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako, holeka mapuga ge wasilikale wo mutemi wa Siliya waponile mu muwoko wako.
8 Ninga wule i Wakushi hamonga ne Walibiya, si wali wena mhuga mbaha muno ye wasilikale? Ninga vyono agwe wali wumwitumbiye Muwaha Mulungu, nayo yakakupela wusume wuwaha muli wene, hamba wawe wali wenji muno.
9 Ninga wumanye nha vyono, Muwaha Mulungu kayilangaga yinze yose, ho yawapele vilungo wose wono wamogopile mwene. Ninga agwe kunoza wulele. Holeka ku soko yiyo agwe ku wulawa lulu, kokwikalila milewo du.”
10 Ne nghani yiyi, yikamukoza Asa likuwo yiwaha kuli muwizi Hanani. Hodu yakawa kumwibata Hanani, yakamuwopa ne manyolo. Na mu madaha gago Asa ho yanziza kuwewonela wanhu no kuwatendela ko wukali muno.

Ndumilizo yo wutemi wa Asa

(1Wat 15:23-24)

11 Ne zinghani zose zono mutemi Asa yetuma, ku wulawilila mwamulizo sunga ko ku ndumilizo yakwe, zose zandicigwe mu citabu Zinghomo ze ce metumo ge watemi wa Yuda na Isilayeli.
12 Na mu mwaka we makumi madatu ne cenda, lono Asa yali yakali yatemile, yakebatwa no wutamwa wa migulu. Ninga hamba nhavivyo si yasuma kumuwuyila Muwaha Mulungu ho yatuje wutazi, langa mwene yakacila kubita kwilaguliza ku waganga.
13 Yaza kupuluta myaka makumi mane na monga yo wutemi wakwe myejete, hodu Asa yakawa kufwa.
14 Yakazikwa mu citenjelo ce'bwe, cono yewikaniziza mweneco mwi'kaya lya Dawudi, lono yali yakali cawumi. Wakawutajila muwili wakwe wuvumba ne mafuta ge mhunjile swanu so maziko gakwe. Wakakoleleza moto muwaha muno ku soko ye lilumbo lyakwe.
