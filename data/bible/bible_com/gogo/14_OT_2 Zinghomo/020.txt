2 Zinghomo 20

Mutemi Yehoshafati yakwihoma ne wanhu wa Edomu

1 Hanyuma yayo, i mapuga ge wasilikale we Wamowabi, na ge Waamoni, hamonga ne wayawo Wameyunimu, wakaza ho wayihome yisi ya Yuda.
2 Wanhu wanji wakamubitila mutemi Yehoshafati, wakamulonjela wakutya, “Likuza yipuga yiwaha muno lye wasilikale likulawa ku Edomu, ku nhumbi nzagwe ye lilenga lya Shamu, likusaka lize likuhome. Sunga nalu, wakondya kuyisola Hazazoni Tamali.” (Ali lyo litagwa linji lye likaya lya Engedi.)
3 Nayo Yehoshafati yaza kuzihulika zizo, yakenjilwa no woga wuwaha muno, yakawa kulomba kuli Muwaha Mulungu yamupele wutonji. Hanyuma yakalajiliza yisi yose walece kulya.
4 Ne wanhu mu yisi yose ya Yuda, wakawa kubita musunho ku Yelusalemi wakamulombe Muwaha Mulungu yawalajile wutonji wakwe.
5 Nawo waza kufika ku Yelusalemi, wakehanguliza he 'kumbo lya hadeha ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga na wanya baho ha Yelusalemi. Ale, nayo mutemi Yehoshafati, yakabita yakema ha mwande ye wanhu wose.
6 Yakalomba kwe'zi waha yakutya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu we walezi wetu, mono wutemile muli makungugo gose ga mono'nze, kuko ku wulanga. Yo gwegwe du mono wuli vilungo, hamba hasina munhu wo wose mono kosuma kukwimila nghani yo yose.
7 Agwe yo gwegwe go Mulungu wetu. Kanyhi na lono i wanhu wako Waisilayeli wahamilila mono yisi yiyi, agwe wukawawinga wanhu wose wono wekalaga mumo, wukawapela i wana wa Abulahamu u mwendece wako yisi yiyi, ho yiwe yawo cibitilila.
8 Nawo wekala mumu, sunga wazenga ne Nyumba yako, ho walajile cogopo cawo ca kuli'gwe, akuno
9 wakumanya nha vyono, hono magazo go gose gawazize so wulipicizwe, lunji wulugu, hamba lihuhuji, hamba lizala, nawo wosuma kuza no kwima ha mwande ye Nyumba yako yiyi, i hanhu hono agwe wukuhembelwa. Wakulombe wutazi ku so magazo gono gawatuga, na vyono nagwe kowahulika no kuwaponya.

10 “Alu wulanje ta, i Waamoni ne Wamowabi, hamonga ne we yisi ya Edomu, wakondya kwanza kucihoma. Na lono walezi wetu wawucila ku yisi ya Misili, agwe si wawakombola i walezi wetu wenjile mu zisi zizo, yikawa yiwanojeye wapilime, wawalece, hamba kuwongomeza yaye.
11 Alu, wulanje vyono wene wakusaka wacitendele vibi, wakuza waciwinje mu yisi yiyi yono agwe wacipela yiwe yetu.
12 Agwe yo gwegwe Mulungu wetu! Holeka cikukulomba gwegwe wuwalipicize. Ase nha cisina vilungo vyo kwihomela ne lipuga lili liwaha lye wasilikale, lyono lyeza kuza kucihoma. Ase cisina yinji yono cosuma kwituma, langa cilanjiliye du wutazi wulawe kuli'gwe.”

13 Ne wanhu wose wa Yuda, hamonga ne wagolece zawo, ne wana wawo, wose wali wemile baho du ha Nyumba yo Mulungu.
14 Na mwi'daha lilyo du, u muhe wo Muwaha Mulungu, wukamuziza Mulawi yumonga mono yali yali hamonga nawo, baho mu wihangulize wuwo. Litagwa lyakwe yacemwaga Yahazayeli, u mwana wa Zekaliya, u mwene yali we mhuga ya wana wa Asafu, yali mwana wa Asafu, ku wulawilila mu ndelwa ya Mataniya na Yayiyeli, na Benaya.
15 Ale yuyo Yahaziyeli, yakatya, “Gwe Mutemi, hamonga nanye nyo wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, u Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, ‘Mulece kogopa hamba kwihwelela kwihoma ne lipuga lilyo liwaha lye Wasilikale. Hambi wulugu wuwu nha si wenyu nyenye, langa wo Muwaha Mulungu.
16 Holeka i mitondo anye muwabitilaje mukawahome, lono wowa wafika ha Zizi. Nanye mwitang'anile nawo ha ndumilizo ye lilolo, ha mwande ye nyika behi na Yeluweli.
17 Nanye kolo mwawagana, mulece kwihoma nawo. Langa anye mutandale du hanhu henyu, muwe kulindila, nanye mowona vyono Muwaha Mulungu yakumulewelenyi, no kumupelenyi wusume. Holeka anye nyo wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, mulece kuwa na woga. Anye mubite du ku wulugu, ninga Muwaha Mulungu kowa hamonga nanye!’ ”

18 Hobaho mutemi yehoshafati yekenama no wusu wakwe wukapamiza ho hasi, ne wanhu wose nawo nhavivyo, wakenama no wusu wawo wukapamiza ho hasi, wakamuhemba Muwaha Mulungu.
19 Nawo we mhuga ye Walawi, we mhuga ye Wakohati, na we mhuga ye Wakola, wakema wima, wakamulumba Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli kwe'zi yiwaha muno muno.

20 No wusiku wali weza kwela, i mitondo yaco, hakali mitondo muno muno, i wanhu wose wakawa kubita ku nyika ya Tekowa. Nawo lono wali wakondya kwiwikaniza ho watuluce, mutemi Yehoshafati, yakawalonjela yakatya, “Anye nyo wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi! Mumwitumbilaje Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kanyhi mweze mukamile. Kanyhi mwihuwile zinghani zono wawizi wakwe womulonjelenyi, nanye holeka motuga wuswanu.”
21 Nayo mutemi yali yeza kukondya kuwaloloseza wanhu zizo, hobaho yakalajiliza i wembi, wavwale myenda yawo yono wayitumamilaga mu zisikukuu ze vilo vyono vyelile. Hanyuma walongole ku mwande ye lipuga lyose lye wasilikale wawe kwimba wakutya, “Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe! Hambi lusungu lwakwe nha lwa cibitilila!”

22 Nawo iwembi wali weza kwanza kwimba, u Muwaha Mulungu yakawagumilila walugu wawo mahwele no woga wuwaha.
23 Hodu hobaho i Waamoni ne Wamowabi, wakawa kuwahoma Waedomu no kuwawulaga wose. Hanyuma nawo, wakawa kwigalucila weneco wose, wakehoma wulugu wukali muno.
24 Nawo wasilikale wa Yuda, wali weza kufika ha munala wa mu nyika, walanje nhavi, wakawawona walugu wawo wagwiye wose hasi, wafwiye. Na hakawa hasina hamba yumonga mono yasigala.

25 Hodu, Yehoshafati, hamonga ne lipuga lyakwe lye wasilikale, wakawa kubita kusola vinhu vye walugu wawo. Na vyali vinhu vinji muno, zali ng'ombe, na myenda hamonga ne vinhu vinji du vyo wuguzi wukamu. Wakekala baho vilo vidatu wena mulimo wo kulundika du vinhu. Hambi nha vyali vinji muno. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, si wasuma kuvisola vinhu vyose, hambi nha vyali vinji muno.
26 Na mwi'zuwa lye kane, wakekongholela he 'lolo lya Balaka, wakamulumba Muwaha Mulungu so zose zono yawatendeye. Ale, yiyo yo soko yono yikaleka i hanhu baho hakacemwa “Balaka sunga nalu.”
27 Mutemi Yehoshafati, nhondo yakawa kuwuya ku Yelusalemi, ko wusume wuwaha, hamonga ne wanhu wakwe, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yakondya kuwasumila i walugu wawo.
28 Nawo wali weza kufika he 'kaya, wakabita sunga ko ku Nyumba yo Mulungu, wakwimba akuno wakutowa ne zindonondo hamonga ne zindulele.
29 Ne makungugo gose gono gahulika vyono Muwaha Mulungu kawasumila Waisilayeli kuli walugu wawo, nawo hobaho wakenjilwa no woga wuwaha muno.
30 Ku wulawilila baho, nhondo Yehoshafati, yakatema ko mulamu, nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamudima mbavu zose.

Ndumilizo yo wutemi wa Yehoshafati

(1Wat 22:41-50)

31 Yehoshafati u mutemi wa Yuda lono yatemela, yali yaficile myaka makumi madatu na mihano. Yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi mejete na mihano. Unyina yacemwaga Azuba, u mwana we cicekulu wa Shilihi.
32 Nayo Yehoshafati yaketuma ziswanu hali Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono yetuma u sogwe Asa, mono yamulongolela kutema.
33 Ninga hanhu ho wuhembelo ha wanhu wono si wamumanyile u Muwaha Mulungu si hawuswa yaye. Ne wanhu wakekala si wamuwuyiye Muwaha Mulungu kwe zinhumbula zawo zose, na mu wuhembi wo kumuhemba u Mulungu we walezi wawo.

34 Ne zinghani zose zono Yehoshafati yetuma, ku wulawilila mwamulizo wo wutemi wakwe, sunga ndumilizo yayo, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce nghomo ya Yehu, u mwana wa Hanani. Kanyhi zo monga ne zinghomo ze watemi we Waisilayeli.

35 Na mu madaha ganji, u mutemi Yehoshafati wa Yuda, vikelunza no mutemi Ahaziya we Waisilayeli, mono yaketuma zimbi nyinji.
36 Wakanoza ngalawa zo kubitila ku Eziyoni-gebeli.
37 Ninga Eleyezali u mwana wa Dodavahu, munya cikaya ce Malesha, yakamusugula Yehoshafati, yakamutya, “Alu vyono mwelunza na Ahaziya, ale nayo Muwaha Mulungu kozibenanjiza zingalawa zako zono wuzinozize.” Na mbeka zingalawa zikabenanjika, hamba kwituma cinji yaye.
