2 Zinghomo 36

Yowahazi u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 23:30-35)

1 I wanhu wa Yuda wakamuhagula Yowahazi, u mwana wa Yosiya, wakawa kumunoza yawe mutemi mu Yelusalemi.
2 Nayo Yowahazi yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na midatu lono yanziza kutema mu Yuda. Yakatema myezi midatu du, mu Yelusalemi.
3 Hambi Neko u mutemi wa Misili, nha yamusola nha mufungwa, yakayinoza Yuda yimulipe Neko kodi ya fweza zificile kilo elufu yidatu na magana mane, hamonga na zahabu kilo makumi madatu na yine.
4 Mutemi Neko, yakamutemya Eliyakimu u mwana wa Yosiya, yawe mutemi wa Yuda. Yakalikawaniza ne litagwa lyakwe, yawe Yehoyakimu. Mwene Neko, yakamusola Yowahazi yakamutegula ku Misili.

Yehoyakimu mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 23:36–24:7)

5 Yehoyakimu yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na mihano, lono yanziza kutema mu Yuda. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi, myaka yikumi na monga. Ninga yaketuma zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe.
6 Holeka Nebukadineza u mutemi wa ku Babiloni, yakaza yakayihoma Yuda, yakamusola Yehoyakimu muwopwe ne manyolo nha mufungwa, yakamutegula ku Babiloni.
7 Nebukadineza, yakasoleleza hamonga ne viya vinji vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakavitegula ku Babiloni, yakaviwika mu kaya yo wutemi.
8 Ne zinghani zose zono Yehoyakimu yetuma, hamonga ne makani gose mabi gono yetuma go wuhemba vihwani, zose zizo zandicigwe mu citabu ce Zinghomo ze watemi wa Isilayeli na Yuda.

Na Yehoyakimu u mwanagwe yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Yehoyakimu u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 24:8-17)

9 Yehoyakimu yali yaficile myaka yikumi na munana, lono yanziza kutema mu Yuda. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myezi midatu, na vilo yikumi du. Nayo nhondo ho yakacila muno kumusutizya Muwaha Mulungu.
10 Mwaka wali weza malika, Nebukadineza yakabita yakamusola Yehoyakimu mufungwa, yakamutegula nayo ku Babiloni, yakasoleleza hamonga ne viya vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakavitegula kuko ku Babiloni. Yakawa kumutemya Zedekiya u nduguye, yo yawe mutemi mu Yuda na Yelusalemi.

Zedekiya u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 24:18-20; Yel 52:1-3a)

11 Zedekiya yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na wumonga, lono yanziza kutema mu Yuda. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi, myaka yikumi na wumonga.
12 Ninga nayo nhavivyo, yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, hamba si yasakaga yamuhulicize ko winenenyhezi u muwizi Yelemiya mono yamulonjelaga zinghani zo Muwaha Mulungu.

U wugwe wa Yelusalemi

(2Wat 25:1-21; Yel 52:3b-11)

13 Ninga Zedekiya yakamubeza mutemi Nebukadineza, hamba yiwe Nebukadineza yakajela kumudindimiza yelahize mwi'tagwa lyo Mulungu, nha vyono kowa yali cihuliko kuli mwene. Zedekiya yakawa yasina hamba cihuliko, hamba si yenda ho yanene, yamupitucile Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli.
14 Hamonga na zizo, sunga ne walongola wose wa Yuda, hamonga ne wakohani wose, sunga ne wanhu wose, wakawa kuwutyatya musambo mubi we makungugo gono gali gawapilime, wakawa nawo wakuhemba vihwani. Na mu nzila yiyo, wakayavufya Nyumba yo Mulungu, yono u Muwaha Mulungu mweneco yayeza yiwe yelile.
15 Ninga u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo, yakajendelela kuwatumila wawizi, wawasugule wanhu wakwe. Hambi nha yakawawonela lusungu wanhu wakwe, hamonga ne Nyumba yakwe.
16 Ninga wene wakawazwajiza i watumwa wo Mulungu, no kuzibedegala zinghani zakwe, no kuwasekanga wawizi wakwe, sunga nhondo likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu likawacila muno wanhu wakwe, na wakawa wasina nzila yinji kanyhi yo kwilega.

17 Na ku soko yiyo, u Muwaha Mulungu, yakawa kumutegula mutemi wa Babiloni yabite yakawahome. Nayo mutemi yakawawulaga wazelelo wose wa Yuda, sunga na wono wali mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Yali yasina lusungu kuli munhu wo wose we cilume hamba kuli we cicekulu, hamba kuli wazelelo ne wahinza, hamba kuli wono wali watamigwe hamba wono wali waswanu. Nayo Mulungu yakawatajila wanhu wakwe mu miwoko ya Nebukadineza.
18 Nebukadineza mutemi wa Babiloni, yakavisola vinhu vyose vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne sawo yose, hamonga ne sawo yose ya mu kaya yo wutemi, hamonga ne sawo ye wetumami wakwe. Yakawa kuvitegula vyose ku Babiloni.
19 Yakayinyanya Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne likaya lyose lya Yelusalemi, hamonga ne vinhu vyose vyono vyali vili mumo, yakagakumulanga ne majinji gose ge likaya.
20 Ne wanhu wose wono wapona mu mbali yiyo, yakawasola wose, yakawategula ku Babiloni. Na kuko, wakawa wetumami wakwe hamonga ne wanagwe, sunga mu cipango cono wenjilila wutemi wa Pelisiya.
21 Na zizo zikawa ho yifisilizwe nghani yono Muwaha Mulungu yali yakondya kuyilonjela muli muwizi Yelemiya, lono yatya, “Ne yisi nayo yokwikala hamba si yolimwa mu myaka makumi mupunghati mu nghango ye myaka yo wucehe yono si yanozwa yaye.”

Mutemi Koleshi, yakuwakombola Wayahudi wawuye kwawo

(Ezi 1:1-4)

22 Na mu mwaka mulongozi, lono Koleshi mutemi wa Pelisiya yatemela, hobaho yakayifisiliza nghani yono Muwaha Mulungu yayilonjela muli muwizi Yelemiya. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yayisinhusa nhumbula yo mutemi Koleshi, yakalajiliza no kuwandika wulajilize wuwo, ho wusomwe kwe lizi yiwaha muno mu wutemi wakwe wose, yakutya,

23 “Awu wo wulajilize wangu ane Koleshi, u mutemi wa Pelisiya. U Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa ku wulanga, kanoza niwe mutemi we yinze yose. Kanyhi yanghomboye nene nimuzenjele ne Nyumba yakwe ku Yelusalemi mu Yuda. Holeka aluno, anye wose mono muli wanhu wo Mulungu, mubite kuko ku Yelusalemi, nayo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yawezane nanye.”
