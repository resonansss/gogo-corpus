2 Zinghomo 11

U wuwizi wa Shemaya

(1Wat 12:21-24)

1 Nayo mutemi Lehobowamu yali yeza kufika ku Yelusalemi, yakawakonghola wasilikale vitung'ati wo wulugu, waficile elufu yigana limonga na makumi munana, mu wulawa mu wo mulango wa Yuda na wa Benyamini. Yakawa yena wuhile wo kubita wakehome na we milango ye Waisilayeli, wono wali wali ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, ho yawugomole kanyhi wutemi wakwe.
2 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu, yakamulonjela muwizi Shemaya vyono
3 yabite kuli mutemi Lehobowamu na kuli wanhu wose we milango ya Yuda na Benyamini, yawalonjele yatye nhavi,
4 “Mulecele lukulu kwihoma ne wandugu zenyu Waisilayeli. Langa wose muwuye ku zikaya zenyu, hambi azi zono zijeye, nha wo wuhile wangu nene no mweneco.” Hodu, nawo wakawa walihulika lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, na ku soko yiyo, si wabita kanyhi kwihoma na Yelobowamu.

Lehobowamu yakugazenjela makaya magomawakali

5 Lehobowamu yakekala mu Yelusalemi. Yakagazenjela magomawakali i makaya ga Yuda na Benyamini.
6 Ne makaya gono yagazenjela magomawakali gali goga: Betelehemu, Etamu, Tekowa,
7 Betizuli, Soko, Adulamu,
8 Gati, Melesha, Zifu,
9 Adolayimu, Lakishi, Azeka,
10 Zola, Ayijaloni na Hebuloni.
11 Yagazenjela magomawakali gono gakangaye muno muno, yakawika ne wasilikale walanjilizi mu makaya gose. Na mu makaya gose gago, yakawika ndigwa nyinji, ne mafuta, hamonga ne divayi.
12 Yakawika ngula, ne migoha. Na mu nzila yiyi, yakasuma kuwatema viswanu i wanhu wa Yuda na Benyamini.

Iwakohani hamonga ne Walawi, wakuza ku Yuda

13 Ale, i wakohani wose ne Walawi, wono wali wali ku mbavu ze yisi yose ya Isilayeli, wose wakaza kutakama, ku yisi ya Yuda.
14 Walawi wakawuleka wudimilo wawo we mitugo, hamonga ne hanhu hono wali wapejigwe wekale, wakahamila ku yisi ya Yuda na Yelusalemi, vyono mutemi Yelobowamu, we Waisilayeli hamonga ne wahazi wakwe, si wawakombola Walawi, ho wetume milimo yawo yo wukohani, kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
15 Hodu, nayo Yelobowamu yakawa kwiwicila wakohani wakwe mweneco, wono wosuma kwituma mulimo wo wukohani kuli milungu mu wuhembelo wawo, no kuhemba masetani, ne vihwani vyono yavinoza mweneco Yelobowamu, mu hwani ye zindama.
16 Ninga u wulawa mu milango yose ye Waisilayeli, wono zinhumbula zawo zasaka kumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu du, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli, wene wawo wakawatyatya i Walawi, vikabitana nawo sunga ko ku Yelusalemi, ho wakamuhembe Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo, no kumulavizya makumbiko.
17 Ne nghani yiyi, yikawupela vilungo muno wutemi wa Yuda. Wakamutaza muno Lehobowamu u mwana wa Solomona. Kanyhi vikekalana viswanu muno nha vyono wali wali mu wutemi wo mutemi Dawudi no mutemi Solomona.

We mhuga ya Lehobowamu

18 Lehobowamu yakatola mucekulu litagwa lyakwe Mahalati, u mwana we cicekulu wa Yelimoti, u mwana wa Dawudi. U nyina wa Mahalati, yacemwaga Abihayili u mwana we cicekulu wa Eliyabi, kanyhi yo mwizukulu wa Yese.
19 Nawo wakalela wana wadatu, Yewushi, na Shemaliya, na Zahamu.
20 Hanyuma yayo kanyhi, yakamutola Maka u mwana we cicekulu wa Abusalomu, wakalelana wana wane, Abiya na Atayi, na Ziza, na Shelomiti.
21 Hamba nhavivyo, Lehobowamu yakajendelela kutola, sunga yakafisa wagolece yikumi na munana. Kanyhi yakawa na wajitulwa waficile makumi mutandatu. Yakalela wana we cilume waficile makumi mejete na munana, ne wana we cicekulu nawo waficile makumi mutandatu. Ninga muli wacekulu wose wawo, hamonga ne wajitulwa wose, mwene Lehobowamu, yakamwenda muno muno Maaka, si nha wawo wose.
22 Kanyhi yakendezwa muno muno na Abiya u mwanagwe u mulelwa na Maaka, si nhe wanagwe wanji wose. Yakamuhagula yo yawe yo muhazi wakwe wo wutemi.
23 Kanyhi Lehobowamu yanoza cihalahala, yakaweyagaza i wanagwe wose mu yisi yose ya Yuda na Benyamini, yakawapela wuwaha mu makaya gose ge magomawakali. Yakawapela na ndigwa nyinji, yakawalondolela ne wagolece wenji.
