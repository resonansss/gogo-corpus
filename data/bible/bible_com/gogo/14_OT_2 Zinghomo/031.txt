2 Zinghomo 31

Wunozeleze wo Wuhembi

1 U wuhembi wali weza malika, i wanhu Isilayeli wose wakawa kwiyagala mu makaya gose mu Yuda, wakagabenanga masumbili gose ge mabwe, wakavidumulanga ne vihwani vyose vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, wakagabenanga ne makumbicilo, hamonga ne hanhu hose hono hetumamilwaga so wuhembi we milungu yo wulambi. Wakanoza nhavivi mu yisi ya Yuda, na mu zisi za Benyamini, na Efulayimu na Manase. Wali weza kukondya, hodu wakawa kuwuya ku kaya.
2 Mutemi Hezekiya, yakawika, no kunozeleza wiwalile we milimo ye Wakohani ne Walawi, nha vyono wali wanojeye munhu na mulimo wakwe. Hamonga no mulimo wo wulavya vilavywa vinyanywe hamonga ne vilavywa vyo mulamu. No mulimo wo wulongoza wuhembi mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Hamonga no kumulumba no kumukumya Muwaha Mulungu, mu Nyumba yakwe.
3 Mwene Hezekiya, yakalavya mitugo ku wulawa mwi'tewa lye zimitugo zakwe, so vilavywa vinyanywe, hakali mitondo ne cinyamihe. Hamonga na zono zalavywaga mu mazuwa ge Sabato, na za mu Ndawo yo Mwezi, sunga na muli vilavywa vya mu zisikukuu zinji, nha vyono yandikwa mu Wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu.

4 Hamonga na zizo, mutemi yakawalonjela wanhu wose wa Yelusalemi, walete vilavywa vyono vyali viwanojeye wakohani ne Walawi wapegwe, ho wasume kujendelela viswanu, wapiligaye mu Wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu.
5 No mutemi yali yeza kulavya wulajilize wuwo, hobaho lwa kamonga du, wanhu wa Isilayeli, wakawa kuleta vipegwa vyo wuhemba wawo wuswanu, ne divayi ne mafuta, no wuci, hamonga ne vilimwa vinji du vya mu migunda yawo. Wakalavya ne zaka ku wulawa muli vinhu vyawo vyose vyono wali wena navyo.
6 Ne wanhu wose wono wekalaga mu makaya ga Yuda, nawo wakalavya zaka ku wulawa mu zing'ombe zawo, ne zingholo. Wakaleta vipegwa vinji muno muno, wakamusiwanulila Muwaha Mulungu.
7 Vipegwa vivyo, wanhu wakanza kuvileta mu mwezi we kadatu, navyo vikajendelela kuletwa du, sunga yikapuluta myezi mine, nhondo vipegwa vikawa vyamema muno muno.
8 Nayo mutemi Hezekiya, hamonga ne wetumami wakwe, wali weza kugawona malundo gago ge vinhu vivyo vyono wanhu walavya, wakamulumba Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga ne wanhu wose wa Isilayeli.
9 U mutemi Hezekiya, yakawa kuwawuza Wakohani hamonga ne Walawi, kuli ze vinhu vivi vinji muno.
10 Nayo Azaliya u Mukohani Muwaha, wo lulelo lwa Zadoki, yakamupitula yakatya, “Ku wulawa wanhu lono wanziza kuleta vipegwa vyawo kono Nyumba yo Mulungu, waleta vinji muno vyono vikwiliye kulya no kusigala vinji du. Na zizi zose ziwa nhavivi, vyono Muwaha Mulungu yakondya kuwalugutiza wanhu wakwe.”

11 Hanyuma mutemi yakalajiliza wanozeleze hanhu ho kuwika viwutwa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
12 Nawo wakawa kuhanozeleza, wakawa kuviwika vipegwa vyose mumo, hamonga ne Zaka, ho vikale mutulano. Wakamuwika Mulawi monga, litagwa lyakwe Konaniya, yawe yo mulanjilizi, hamonga no nduguye Shimeyi yawe yo mutazi wakwe.
13 Hanyuma kanyhi, wakawikwa Walawi waficile yikumi limonga, wawe hasi yawo, nawo wo awa: Yehiyeli, Azaziya, Nahati, Asaheli, Yelimoti, Yozabadi, Eliyeli, Isimaciya, Mahati, hamonga na Benaya. Na wose wawa, wali wali mu wulajilize wo mutemi Hezekiya, hamonga na Azaliya u Mukohani Muwaha.
14 Nayo Kole, u mwana wa Imuna, u Mulawi, mono yali yo mulanjilizi muwaha wa ku mulango wa kucilima we Nyumba yo Mulungu, yuyo yali yo mulanjilizi wo kubocela vipegwa vyose vyono wamulavizyaga Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga no kuvigawa.
15 Na mu makaya ganji, mono wekalaga wakohani, hakawa na Edeni, na Miniyamini na Yeshuwa, na Shemaya, na Amaliya, hamonga na Shekaniya, wono wamutazaga mu mulimo wuwo ko wihuwilwa muno. Wakawa wakugawa ndigwa muling'ano kuli wandugu zawo Walawi, mu nghango yo wiwalile wawo vyono wali winha.
16 Wakawagawila ndigwa we cilume wose wono wali waficile myaka makumi madatu hamba kuciza, wono nhondo zose wetumaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, mu nghango ye milimo yawo vyono wali wapejigwe.
17 I wakohani wakapegwa ligawo lyawo, mu nghango ye zimhuga zawo vyono zinha. I Walawi nawo wono waficile myaka makumi mejete na wono wacizize, nawo wakapegwa ligawo lyawo, mu nghango ye vipuga vyawo vye milimo, nha vyono vyali vinha.
18 Na wose wakandikwa hamonga ne wagolece zawo, ne wana, hamonga na wose wono wadimwaga nawo, hambi wose nha wali wanojeye weyeze, ho wetume milimo yono yelile.
19 Kanyhi hakawa ho wali wanhu wono wawikwa, ho wawe kuwagawila ligawo lye zindigwa i wakohani wo lulelo lwa Haluni, wono wekalaga mu makaya, hamba na walya wono wali wali ku migunda yo kudimila ya mu makaya gago. Wanhu wawo wakawagawila ndigwa i walume wose wa mu zimhuga ze Wakohani, na kuli wanhu wose wono wali wawalilizwe hamonga ne zimhuga ze Walawi.

20 Nayo mutemi Hezekiya, zose zono yetuma mu yisi yose ya Yuda, yazituma mu ling'ani, kanyhi yetuma zose zono zikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe.
21 Na holeka yakamoteswa muno muno, vyono mwene zose zono yetuma mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamba zo kuwibata Wulajilize, zose yazituma kwe nhumbula yono yimogopile Mulungu, hamonga no mutima we cihembihembi kuli Mulungu wakwe.
