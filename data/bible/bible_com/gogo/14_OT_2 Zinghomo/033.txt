2 Zinghomo 33

Manase u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Wat 21:1-9)

1 Manase yali yaficile myaka yikumi na myejete lono yatemela mu Yuda. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi mahano na mihano.
2 Ninga Manase yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, vyono yetuma makani ge cevu gono wetumaga i wanhu we makungugo wono si wamumanyile Mulungu, wono Muwaha Mulungu yawawinga mu yisi, lono wanhu wakwe Mulungu, wenjilaga mumo.
3 Hambi nha yakahazenga kanyhi i hanhu ho wuhembelo ho kuhembela milungu yinji, hono sogwe Hezekiya yahamwagulanga mose. Yakazenga cikumbicilo so wuhembi wo kumuhemba Baali, yakasesa ne vihwani vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, yakawa yakuhemba zinyelezi.
4 Kanyhi yakazenga vikumbicilo vyo wuhembi we milungu yinji, mugati ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, i hanhu hono Muwaha Mulungu yatya, hawe ho hanhu hono mweneco kowa kuhembelwa cibitilila.
5 Kanyhi, yakazenga vikumbicilo vyo kuzihembela zinyelezi, mu makumbo mejete ga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
6 Yakawalavya wanagwe, makumbiko mwi'lolo lya Hinomu, wawe vilavywa vinyanywe. Kanyhi yetowelezaga cisangu na zo wuganga, hamonga no kwilaguliza kuli wono wena macisi, hamonga no wuganga. Yaketuma wubi muno hali Muwaha Mulungu, no Muwaha Mulungu, yakamukolelwa likuwo muno muno.
7 Yakawika cihwani mugati ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, i hanhu hono Muwaha Mulungu yamulonjela Dawudi, hamonga na Solomona u mwanagwe, yakawatya, “Bahano hono Yelusalemi, mu Nyumba yangu yiyi, ho hanhu hono nakondya kuhahagula ku soko ye yisi yose ye milango yose, yikumi na myejete ya Isilayeli, hawe ho hanhu hono nene no mweneco du nowa kuhembelwa.
8 Na hono wanhu wa Isilayeli wagebate malajilizo gangu, kanyhi wawukamise hamonga no Wulajilize wangu wose, wono Musa u mwitumami wangu yawapela, ale, nane hamba si nowaleka wawingwe mu yisi yiyo yono nakondya kuwapela walezi wawo.”
9 Manase yakawanoza wanhu wetume wubi hali Mulungu, waketuma wubi kuciza wubi wono makungugo gawituma, gono Muwaha Mulungu yagawinga mu yisi, lono wanhu wakwe Mulungu wenjilaga.

Manase yakunena hali Mulungu

10 Holeka hamba yawe Muwaha Mulungu yamusugula Manase hamonga ne wanhu wakwe, ninga wene wakalema, hamba si wasaka kumuhuliciza yaye.
11 Holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakawa kuwaleka wasilikale wa Ashuli wayihome Yuda. Wakamuteka Manase, wakamuwicila vidobogo vya cuma mu zimhula zakwe, wakamuwopa ne manyolo, wakamutegula ku Babiloni.
12 Na mumo mu wudumyanzwe wakwe, yakenjilwa no winenenyhezi wuwaha muno, yakamuwuyila Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe, yakamulomba Mulungu yamutaze.
13 Nayo Mulungu, yakawuhulika wulombe wakwe, yakamugomola yawuye ku Yelusalemi, yakawe mutemi kanyhi. Ne nghani yiyo, yikamunoza Manase yamanye nha vyono, u Muwaha Mulungu, yo Mulungu du yali wene.

14 Na hanyuma ya zizo, Manase yakalyonjeza lijinji lya kunze wutali wayo, lijinji lya kucilima ye likaya lya Dawudi, ku wulawilila mwi'lolo behi no muvwilo we malenga ga Gihoni, ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, sunga ko ku Mulango wa Somba, sunga mo mu yisi ya Ofeli, mu ciwanza ce Nyumba yo Mulungu. Kanyhi yakawawika wawaha we wasilikale, wawe walanjilizi we vipuga vye hayo hayo, mu makaya gose ga Yuda gono gena magomawakali.
15 Yakayiwusa milungu mijenzi mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne vihwani vyono yawika mweneco, hamonga ne vikumbicilo vye wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu vyono yali yavizenjile mu cigongo, hono hali yizenjigwe Nyumba yo Mulungu. Yakavitegula vyose vivino kunze ye likaya, yakavitaga kuko.
16 Yakacinozeleza ne cikumbicilo i hanhu hono Muwaha Mulungu yanojeye yahembelwe. Yakawa kulavya ne makumbiko ge cilavywa co mulamu, hamonga ne vilavywa vyo wulumbize mucanyha yayo. Yakawa kuwalajiliza i wanhu wa Yuda wamuhembe Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli.
17 Hamba yiwe wanhu wajendelela kulavya makumbiko mu wuhembelo hanji ako na ako, ninga wajendelela kumuhemba du Muwaha Mulungu.

Ndumilizo yo wutemi wa Manase

(2Wat 21:17-18)

18 Na zinji zose zono Manase yetuma, hamonga ne malombo gakwe kuli Mulungu wakwe, ne zinghani zose ze wawizi, wono wakalongana no mwene Manase, mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli. Zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce Zinghomo ze metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.
19 Na vyono mutemi yalomba, na vyono Mulungu yakamupitula, hamonga du no wubi wono Manase yetuma, lono yali yakali si nanena, hamonga ne zimbi zose zono yetuma, ze hanhu ho wuhembelo we wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu, ne vihwani vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, vyono yasesa, hamonga ne vihwani viseswe, vyono yavihemba, lanje, zose zizo zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze wawizi.
20 Manase yakafwa, yakazikwa behi ne kaya yo wutemi. Nayo Amoni, u mwanagwe yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Mutemi Amoni wa Yuda

(2Wat 21:19-26)

21 Amoni yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na myejete, lono yatemela mu Yuda. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi, myaka myejete du.
22 Nayo nhavivyo yaketuma wubi, nha vyono yetuma u sogwe Manase. Yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, hambi nha yakahemba vihwani vyono u sogwe yavihembaga.
23 Kanyhi si yali yehwanile sogwe mono yenenenyheza, yakamuwuyila Muwaha Mulungu. Mwene Amoni, yetuma wubi muno kuciza no wubi wono sogwe yetuma.
24 Nawo wetumami wakwe wamugaluka, wakamuwulajila mu kaya yo wutemi.
25 I wanhu wa Yuda wakawawulaga i watyatyi wose wa Amoni, wakawa kumuwika Yosiya u mwanagwe yahale wutemi wakwe.
