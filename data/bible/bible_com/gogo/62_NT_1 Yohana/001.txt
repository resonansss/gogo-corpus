1 Yohana 1

I nghani yo wumi

1 Cikukwandicila zizi so i nghani yo wumi yono yali yili baho ku wulawa mwamulizo. Ase cayihulika no kuyiwona ne meso getu sese co weneco, cayiwona no kuyibata kwe miwoko yetu sese co weneco.
2 No wumi wuwo wali weza kwanzalikwa, ase cawuwona, nalu cikulonga, kanyhi cikumulonjelenyi zo wumi wuwo we cibitilila, wono wali ko Sogo yo munhu, hanyuma wukanzalikwa kuli'se.
3 Cico cono cawona, na cono cahulika, co cikumwanzizenyi nanye, nanye ho muhanje nase mu wumonga wono cina nawo no Sogo yo munhu no Mwanagwe Yesu Kilisito.
4 Cikumwandicilenyi nanye zizi, i nyemo yenyu ho yimemelecele.

U Mulungu wo wuzelu

5 I nghani yono cayihulika kuli Yesu, yono cikumulonjelenyi, nha yoyi, u Mulungu wo wuzelu, na muli mwene, musina hamba citi.
6 Holeka hono ciwe kutya cili wumonga nayo, na kuno ho cikwikala mu wutitu, cowa calonga wulambi mu wulonje na mu metumo.
7 Langa hono cikale mu wuzelu, nha vyono u mwene yali mu wuzelu, baho langa cowa no wumonga sese kwa sese, ne sakami yakwe Yesu Kilisito u Mwanagwe, yocikwema mu wubi wose.
8 Na hono ciwe kutya cisina wubi, cikwikonga co weneco, ne mbeka muli sese musina.
9 Langa hono ciwuvumile u wubi wetu, u mwene u Mulungu yo mwihuwilwa, kanyhi yali ling'ani, nayo kocinenela u wubi wetu wose, no kucikwema ne masutya getu gose.
10 Na hono ciwe kutya cikali sinetuma wubi, cowa camunoza u Mulungu nha mulambi, ne nghani yakwe yili musina muli sese.
