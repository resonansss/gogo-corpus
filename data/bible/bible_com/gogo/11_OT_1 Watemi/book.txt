1 Watemi 1

Mu vilo vyo wukombi wo mutemi Dawudi

1 U mutemi Dawudi, nhondo yakawa yakombipa muno muno. Sunga ne wetumami wakwe wamugubikaga ne mabulanjeti ho yatuje civuce. Ninga hamba nhavivyo si yacitugaga civuce yaye.
2 Hodu, nhondo i wetumami wakwe wakawa kumutya, “Gwe Mutemi, wulece cikakulondolele muhinza mono kowa kukulindiliza. Kanyhi yawe kugona mu mhambaga yako, ho yakupele civuce.”
3 Hobaho wakawa kwanza kumulondola mu Isilayeli yose, u muhinza muswanu muno ku wulangwe, hanyuma wakamuwona muhinza litagwa lyakwe Abishagu, munya Shunami. Wakawa kumuleta kuli mutemi.
4 Nayo muhinza yuyo mbeka yali muswanu muno muno, yakawa kumukamisa mutemi no kumwitumamila, ninga mutemi hamba si yagona nayo ng'o.

Adoniya yakusaka ligoda lyo wutemi

5 Ale, du, lono Abusalomu yali yakondya kufwa, Adoniya u mwana we cilume wa Dawudi, na Hajiti, mono yali yo mwana mulongozi kulelwa, yakawa yo yasigaye yakali mumi. Nayo yali munhu mono yena mumbwa swanu muno.
6 Hamba Dawudi yali yakali namulonga nghani yo yose yinji 'mbi yaye, nayo Adoniya, yasulumilaga yawe mutemi. Hobaho yakewikaniziza matuka go wulugu, ne zifwalasi, hamonga ne wasindicizi, waficile wanhu makumi mahano.
7 Adoniya yakalongana na Yowabu (mu nghalo nyina Zeluya) hamonga na Abiyatali u mukohani, nawo wakavumila kumutaziliza mu nghani yakwe yiyo.
8 Ninga Zadoki u mukohani, na Benaya u mwana wa Yehoyada, na Natani u muwizi, na Shimeyi, na Leyi, hamonga ne walindilizi wa Dawudi, awa wose wakalema kuhanga na Adoniya mu zinghani zakwe zizo.

9 Ale, mwi'zuwa linji, Adoniya yakawulaga ngholo na ng'ombe, hamonga na ndama zono zijinile, yakalavya likumbiko i He'bwe lye Zinzoka, behi ne cisima ca Enilojeli. Yakawacemela ne wana wanji wo mutemi Dawudi, hamonga ne wetumami wo mutemi wono walawilila ku Yuda, wakaza so ndigwa yiyo ye likumbiko.
10 Ninga si yasaka yamucemele Solomona u muwahawe, hamba Natani u muwizi, hamba Benaya, hamba i walindilizi wo mutemi yaye.

Solomona yakunozwa yawe mutemi

11 Hanyuma Natani u muwizi, yakabita kuli Batisheba u nyina wa Solomona yakamuwuza yakatya, “Ati, hongo nagwe si wumanyile yaye nha vyono Adoniya u mwana mulelwe na Hajiti, yenoza mweneco yawa mutemi? Sunga no mutemi Dawudi si yayimanyile nghani yiyi yaye!
12 Holeka hono wusace wiponye go mweneco, hamonga no mwanago Solomona, ale, ane nghukupela yihala,
13 Wubite lulu baha kuli mutemi Dawudi wukamutye, ‘Gwe Mutemi! Wule, si gwegwe mono wanyihuwiza nha vyono Solomona u mwanangu yo kolohala wutemi wako? Alu nhawule kanyhi nghuhulika nha vyono Adoniya yo yawa mutemi?’ ”
14 Natani yakajendelela kumulonjela Betisheba yakutya, “Ninga, nokuza nghwagane wukali kulongana no mutemi nane ho nghangaze zo wulonje wako.”

15 Hobaho Batisheba yakawa kubita kuli mutemi, yakamwagana mu cumba cakwe co kugona. Nayo mutemi yali yeza mukombi muno muno. Nayo Abishagu, u muhinza mulawa Shunami, yali yali baho yakumulanjilila mutemi.
16 Batisheba yakenama ha mwande yo mutemi, nayo mutemi yakamuwuza yakatya, “Alu Batisheba, wina nghanici?”

17 Batisheba yakapitula yakatya, “Gwe Mutemi! Agwe wanyihuwiza mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako, nha vyono, Solomona u mwanangu, yo kolohala wutemi wako.
18 Ninga alu, Adoniya yakondya kutema, ninga nagwe hamba si wuyimanyile nghani yiyi.
19 Mwene kakondya kulavya ne likumbiko lya ng'ombe nyinji, ne zingholo, hamonga ne zindama zono zijinile, kanyhi kawalalika sunga ne wanago na Abiyatali u mukohani, hamonga na Yowabu, u muwaha we wasilikale wako wo wulugu wabite kuko ku ndigwa ye likumbiko. Ninga si yamulalice Solomona u mwanago yaye.
20 Ninga Gwe go Mutemi! I wanhu wose wa Isilayeli wakulanjiliye gwegwe, wuwalonjele nani mwene mono kohala wutemi wako.
21 Na hono wulece kuyinoza nghani yiyi mbela mbela, lono agwe go mutemi, mulume wangu wukali mumi, wumanye vyono kolo wafwa, ane hamonga na Solomona u mwanangu, cotendelwa vibi kotya cili wagaluci.”

22 Nayo Batisheba lono yali yakali yakulonga,
23 Mutemi yakalonjelwa nha vyono Natani muwizi yali baho. Natani yakenjila, yakenama hamwande yo mutemi.
24 Hanyuma yakatya, “Gwe Mutemi! Yo gwegwe mono wulonjile vyono Adoniya yo yahale wutemi wako?
25 Hambi nhe zuwalili du, ho yabitile kulavya likumbiko lya ng'ombe nyinji, hamonga ne zingholo, ne zindama zono zijinile hamonga na Yowabu u muwaha we wasilikale wako wo wulugu, na Abiyatali u mukohani. Na mwi'pango lili baha, wali mu wulye we zindigwa nayo,
26 Ninga ane hamba si yandalice yaye, hamba Zadoki mukohani, hamba Benaya, hamba Solomona.
27 Alu, Gwe Mutemi ho kwituma zizi zose wuli wene, hamba kuwalonjela bwete i wetumami wako wamumanye u munhu mono kohala u wutemi wako?”

28 Mutemi Dawudi yakatya, “Ale, wumulonjele ta Batisheba yenjile kono cumba.” Hodu Batisheba yakenjila, yakema hamwande yo mutemi.
29 Nayo mutemi yakamulonjela Batisheba yakatya, “Ane nghukwihuwiza mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu mumi, mono yamhonya ku wulawa mu magayo gose,
30 nha vyono izuwali, nokwibata wihuwizo wangu wono nakwihuwiziza gwegwe, mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we wa Isilayeli, nha vyono Solomona u mwanago yo kohala wutemi wangu.”

31 Nayo Batisheba, yakenama hamwande yo mutemi, yakatya, “Muwaha wangu, u mutemi Dawudi yekale cibitilila!”

32 Hobaho mutemi Dawudi, yakawa kuwacema Zadoki na Natani, na Benaya. Nawo wali weza kuza, yakawalonjela yakatya,
33 “Ale, mubitane ne wetumami wangu, mumupandize Solomona mwanangu mucanyha ye nyumbu yangu, mumutegule sunga ku cikolongo ca Gihoni,
34 nayo Zadoki u mukohani, hamonga na Natani u muwizi, wamwitile mafuta baho, yawe mutemi wa Isilayeli. Hanyuma muwe kuzitowa zindulele akuno mukuzoza mukutya, ‘Go mutemi Solomona, wikale cibitilila!’
35 Hanyuma muwe kuzana nayo sunga hono hangu, lono yakuza kwikala mwigoda lyangu lyo wutemi. Mwene yo kohalila u wutemi wangu, hambi nha yo yuyo du mono nimuhaguye yawe mutemi wa Isilayeli na Yuda.”

36 Benaya yakapitula yakatya, “Heya vyo yonozeka, no Muwaha Mulungu yayikangaze nghani yiyi.
37 Alu, u Muwaha Mulungu nha vyono yali yali hamonga nagwe go mutemi, ale, nhavivyo vyo kowezana na Solomona, kanyhi yawumotese no wutemi wakwe muno, si nha vyono wali no wutemi wako.”

38 Nayo Zadoki, na Natani, na Benaya, hamonga ne walindilizi wo mutemi, wakawa kumupandiza Solomona mu nyumbu yo mutemi Dawudi, wakamutegula sunga ku cikolongo ca Gihoni.
39 Nayo Zadoki, yali yena ne lihembe lyono lyali lina mafuta, lyono yalisola mwi Hema lyono Lyelile yakawa kumwitila Solomona. Wakatowa ndulele, ne wanhu wose wakazoza wakutya, “Go mutemi Solomona, wikale Cibitilila!”
40 Na hanyuma ya zizi, wanhu wose wakamutyatya Solomona mu nyuma yakwe, akuno wazozize muno muno so nyemo, wakutowa ne zifilimbi. Wakawa wazozize muno lizozo yiwaha, sunga ne yisi yikabocelela.

41 Nayo Adoniya hamonga ne wajenzi wakwe lono wamalilaga kulya zindigwa zawa, hobaho wakahulika mazozo mawaha. Na Yowabu nayo yali yeza kugahulika mazi ge zindulele, yakawuza yakutya, “Alu lizozo lilyo lyono lyabuma muno mwi'kaya, lina nhunizoci?”
42 Nayo Yowabu lono yali yakali yakulonga, hobaho baho Yonazani u mwana wa Abiyatali u mukohani, yakawa kufika baho. Adoniya yakamutya, “Winjile! Agwe go munhu muswanu. Nitumbiye nha vyono kowa wukuciletela nghani swanu.”

43 Nayo Yonazani yakamupitula Adoniya yakamutya, “Ane nghukulonjela mbeka, nha vyono mutemi Dawudi, yakondya kumunoza Solomona yawe mutemi.
44 Kawatuma Zadoki na Natani, na Benaya hamonga ne walindilizi wo mutemi, wamutegula sunga ha cikolongo ca Gihoni. Nawo wamupandiza Solomona mu nyumbu yo mutemi.
45 Nawo Zadoki hamonga na Natani, wamwitila mafuta, wawa kumutemya. Weza kuzikondya zizo, wawa kwinjila mwi'kaya akuno wazozize so nyemo. Holeka wanhu wose wazozize muno. Nayo yo soko ye lizozo lyono wukulihulika.
46 Alu Solomona yo yeza mutemi.
47 Hamonga na zizo, i wetumami wose, wakondya kumubitila Solomona, no kulajila cogopo cawo kuli u Mulumbwe, mutemi Dawudi, akuno wakutya, ‘Ale, u Mulungu wako yamunoze Solomona yawe ne lilumbo yiwaha muno yakucize na gwe gwe, no wutemi wakwe wumoteswe muno kuciza no wutemi wako.’ Nayo mutemi Dawudi, kawa kwinama yakuhemba yali mu citanda cakwe,
48 yakutya, ‘Alenyi cimukumye Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, mono izuwali kamunoza yumonga wa muli wanangu, yahale wutemi wangu, kanyhi na vyono yanghamise niwe mumi sunga niziwona zizi!’ ”

49 Hodu, i wajenzi wa Adoniya wali weza kuzihulika zizo, wakenjilwa no woga wuwaha muno, wakawa kwinuka wose wakawa kutuluka wakawa kuwuya munhu na kwawo.
50 Nayo Adoniya so woga wo kumogopa Solomona, hodu yakawa kumanyhila kwi Hema lyono Lyelile, yakawa kwinjila, yakawa kugebata mahembe ge cikumbicilo.
51 U mutemi Solomona yakalonjelwa nha vyono, Adoniya yakumogopa muno muno, na vyono Adoniya kenjila mwi'Hema lyono Lyelile, alu yagebate mahembe ge cikumbicilo, kanyhi kalonga katya, “Nghani nongozi, nghuhila mutemi Solomona yanyilahile nha vyono sikombulaga.”

52 Nayo Solomona yakapitula yakatya, “Hono yawe munhu mwihuwilwa, hamba luvwili lumonga lwa mu mutwe wakwe si lopamizwa yaye. Ninga hono yalece kuwa mwihuwilwa, langa kofwa.”
53 Mutemi Solomona yakatuma wanhu wakamulete Adoniya. Wakabita, wakamusola baho ha cikumbicilo, wakamuleta. Adoniya yakamubitila mutemi Solomona, yakenama ha mwande yo mutemi. Nayo mutemi yakamutya Adoniya, “Ale wuwuye kukaya kwako.”


1 Watemi 2

Malajilizo ge ndumilizo ga Dawudi kuli Solomona

1 Nayo Dawudi lono yali yeza behi no kufwa, yakamucema Solomona u mwanagwe, yakawa kumulajiliza yakutya,
2 “Gwe mwanangu Solomona, alu cipango cangu co kufwa, cafika. Holeka agwe weze wuli mupiligazi, kanyhi wuli cilume-lume,
3 kanyhi weze kwituma zose zono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako yakukulajiliza. Weze na cihuliko kuli zo Wulajilize wakwe wose, hamonga ne malajilizo gakwe, nha vyono gandicigwe mu Wulajilize wa Musa, ho wuwe no wumoteswe mu milimo yako yose.
4 Na hono wumuhulice du Muwaha Mulungu, nayo kowufisiliza wihuwizo wakwe wono yawihuwiza, lono yanonjela nha vyono, i wanangu, wo wotema muli Isilayeli vilo vinji, mu nghango nha vyono wowa kucila, kugakamisa Malajilizo gakwe ko wihuwilwa, kwe zinhumbula zawo zose, na kwe mitima yawo yose.

5 “Kanyhi ho yili nghani yinji nayo, yono nghwenda wuyimanye. Wukumbuciye vyono Yowabu mwana wa Seluya yanozeza, Yowabu yawawulaga i wetumami wangu wejete, Abineli u mwana wa Neli, hamonga na Amasa u mwana wa Yeteli, wono wali wo wawaha we wasilikale Waisilayeli wo wulugu. Kanyhi wule, wukumbuciye nha vyono yawawulaga mwi'pango lyo mulamu, yakwilipiciza ku soko ye mafwa menji gono ganozwa na wene mu cipango co wulugu. Yawawulaga du mbula soko wanhu wono wasina masutya, nane nghayisola nghani yiyo yiwe yangu, kuli zono yetuma, zono aluno zandelela magaziko mawaha.
6 Holeka nagwe lu, witumaje nha vyono kowona gwegwe wulece kumuleka yefwile mweneco.

7 “Ninga langa nghukunena, wulece kuwanozeza vibi i wana wa Balizilayi munya Gileyadi, langa wuwadimaje, hambi wene nha wanhendela viswanu muno lono namutizaga Abusalomu u nduguyo.

8 “Kanyhi ho yuli yunji mono yakucemwa Shimeyi, u mwana wa Gela, u Mubenyamini munya cikaya ca Bahulimu Mwene yuyo, yandijitiza ko wusungu muno, mwizuwa lyono nabitaga ku Mahanayimu, ninga cali ceza kwitang'anila nayo he 'kolongo lya Yoludani, nghamwihuwiza mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu nha vyono si nomuleka yawulagwe yaye.
9 Ninga wulece kumuleka yekale si yalipicizwe yaye. Nagwe wumanyile go mweneco yono kokwituma, kanyhi yikunojeye wuwone nha vyono kakondya kuwulagwa.”

Wufwe wa Dawudi

10 Dawudi yakafwa nha vyono wasogwe wafwa, yakazikwa nayo mwi'kaya lya Dawudi.
11 Mwene Dawudi, yatema mu Isilayeli, myaka makumi mane. Na ku Hebuloni, yakatema myaka mupunghati, nako ku Yelusalemi, yakatema myaka makumi madatu na midatu.
12 Nayo Solomona yakahala u wutemi wo sogwe Dawudi, no wutemi wakwe wukajendela kuwa ne vilungo muno.

Wufwe wa Adoniya

13 Hanyuma Adoniya munghalo nyina Hajiti, yakabita kuli Batisheba u nyina ya Solomona. Nayo Batisheba yakamuwuza yakatya, “Wule, kuza no mulamu?” Nayo Adoniya yakamupitula yakatya, “Heya ane niza no mulamu.”
14 “Ninga hamba nhavivyo niza nina nghani kuli'gwe yono nghusaka nghuwuze.” Batisheba yakatya, “Alo nghanici?”

15 Adoniya yakapitula yakatya, “Agwe wumanyile nha vyono ane, nali niwe yo nili mutemi, na kanyhi nha vyono i wanhu wose wa Isilayeli, wali wayilanjiliye muno muno nghani yiyo. Ninga alu yijela nghani yono yisimajiye muno, nha vyono ndugu yangu Solomona, kawa yo mutemi, hambi nha wali wo wuhile wo Muwaha Mulungu.
16 Ninga luno, nina nghani monga du kuli'gwe, yono nghukulomba wunyitumile, holeka wulece kunema yaye.”

Batisheba yakamutya, “Alo nghanici yene?”
17 Adoniya yakatya, “Nghukunena wumbuzize kuli mutemi Solomona, vyono ane nyendile yanghombole nimutole Abishagu, yuyulya muhinza munya Shunemu. Nane manyile vyono agwe hono wulonje nayo, mwene si kokulema yaye.”

18 Batisheba yakatya, “Ale viswanu, nane nobita nghamulonjele mutemi nha vyono wunhumile.”

19 Ale, Batisheba yakawa kubita kuli mutemi Solomona, kumulonjela zose, nha vyono yatumigwe na Adoniya. Nayo mutemi yakenuka yakamulamusa nyina, yakenama ha mwande yo nyina. Hanyuma yakawa kwikala mwi'goda lyakwe lyo wutemi. Yakamutenjela nyina ligoda linji, u nyina yakekala ku muwoko wo kulume wo mutemi.
20 Hanyuma Batisheba yakawa kumutya mutemi, “Ane nina nghani ndodo du yono nghusaka nghulonjele, ninga dedede wulece kunema.” Mutemi yakatya, “Alo nghanici yaya? Nane sinokulema yaye.”

21 Batisheba yakatya, “Mulece nduguyo Adoniya yamutole Abishagu.”

22 Nayo mutemi yakamupitula yakatya, “Alu yaya, nhawule wukunyitya nimukombole Adoniya yamutole Abishagu. Ane nghuwona hanyuma konyitya nimukombolele no wutemi nhavivilya. Hamba nhavivyo, mwene Adoniya yo muwaha wangu. Abiyatali u mukohani hamonga na Yowabu wose wali ku lubavu lwakwe!”
23 Hodu Solomona yakawa kwilahiza mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Ale, u Mulungu yambulagaje zuwali du, hono ndece kumuwulaga Adoniya ku soko ye nghani yakwe yiyi yono yakuyisaka!
24 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu mumi, nha kakondya kufisiliziza, kanyikaza mwi'goda lyo wutemi, lya baba Dawudi, yawukamisa wihuwizo wakwe, sunga yakondya kumhela wutemi hamonga ne wanangu. Holeka nane nghwilahiza hali Muwaha Mulungu mumi, nha vyono Adoniya izuwalili du kofwa!”

25 Hodu hobaho, mutemi yakamulajiliza Benaya yabite yakamuwulaje Adoniya. Benaya yakabita yakamuwulaga.

Uwulipicizwe wa Abiyatali, hamonga no wufwe wa Yowabu.

26 Hanyuma ya zizo, mutemi yakamulonjela Abiyatali u mukohani yakatya, “Alu, wuwuye kwenyu ku yisi yenyu, ku Anatoli. Hambi nagwe nha wunojeye wuwulagwe, ninga aluno ane si nokuwulaga yaye, hambi agwe nha wali wuli mulanjilizi we Lisanduku lyo Muwaha Mulungu lye Cilagane, lono wali wuli hamonga na baba Dawudi, kanyhi wawezaga wuli hamonga nayo mu magayo gakwe gose.”
27 Hobaho Solomona yakawa kumuwusa Abiyatali mu mulimo wo wukohani wo kumwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu. Yakazifisiliza zinghani zono Muwaha Mulungu yazilonga ku Shilo, za Eli mukohani, hamonga ne wanagwe, zikawa za mbeka.

28 Yowabu nayo yakazihulika zono zali zejela. (Hambi mwene nha yali yali ku lubavu lwa Adoniya, ninga si ku lubavu lwa Abusalomu yaye.) Holeka yakawa kutiza, yakawa kwinjila mwi Hema lyono Lyelile, yakawa kugebata mahembe ge cikumbicilo.
29 Nayo mutemi Solomona, yali yeza kuhulika nha vyono Yowabu katiziza mwi'Hema, nalu yanamatiye ha cikumbicilo, hodu Solomona yakawa kutuma munhu kuli Yowabu, yakamuwuze Yowabu, isoko yono yilecile kawa kutiziza ku cikumbicilo. Nayo Yowabu yakapitula nha vyono katiziza kuli Muwaha Mulungu, hambi nha yakumogopa Solomona. Hodu Solomona yakamutuma Benaya yabite yakamuwulaje Yowabu.
30 Nayo Benaya yakabita kuko kwi Hema lyono Lyelile, yakamutya Yowabu, “U mutemi yakukutya wulawile kononze.” Yowabu yakapitula yakatya, “Nalema! Ane langa nofwila bahano du.”

Ale, Benaya yakabita kuli mutemi yakamulonjela nha vyono Yowabu yalonjile.

31 Nayo mutemi Solomona yakamutya Benaya, “Ale wunoze nha vyono mweneco Yowabu yakusaka. Wubite wumuwulaje, wumuzice. Ho nane hamonga no lulelo lwa Dawudi, cilece kuwalilwa masutya, so lisutya lyono yetuma Yowabu, lyo kuwawulaga i wanhu wono wali wasina masutya yaye.
32 Ninga u Muwaha Mulungu komulipiciza Yowabu so wuwulaji wuwo wono yetuma cizilago, hamba baba Dawudi si yali yamanyile yaye. Hambi Yowabu nha yawawulaga wanhu wejete we ling'ani, i wanhu wono wali waswanu kuciza mwene nha cinji Abineli, u muwaha we wasilikale wa Isilayeli, hamonga na Amasa u muwaha we Wasilikale wa Yuda.
33 Holeka wulipicizwe ku soko yo wuwulagwe wa wawo, wogwila muli Yowabu, hamonga no lulelo lwakwe cibitilila. Ninga u Muwaha Mulungu kowa kuwamotya nhondo zose wo lulelo lwa Dawudi wono wekaye mwi'goda lyakwe.”

34 Hobaho Benaya mwana wa Yehoyada, yakawa kubita kwi Hema lyono lyelile, yakawa kumuwulaga Yowabu, nayo yakazikwa ku kaya yakwe mwi'hala.
35 Mutemi yakawa kumuwika Benaya yo yawe muwaha we wasilikale, yakahalila hanhu ha Yowabu, yakamuwika Zadoki yawe mukohani, yahalile hanhu ha Abiyatali.

Wufwe wa Shimeyi

36 Hanyuma mutemi Solomona yakatuma munhu yabite yakamulonjele Shimeyi yatye, “Alu wizenjelaje kaya go mweneco ku Yelusalemi. Hanyuma wuwe kwikala mumo du mwi'kaya, wulecele lukulu kulawila kunze ye likaya.
37 Hono wijelele du wulawile, wulowoce cikolongo ca Kidiloni, ale, wumanye vyono mbeka wafwa, ne lisutya lilyo lyowa lyako gwegwe go mweneco.”

38 Nayo Shimeyi yakatya, “Nghukulumba muno muno Gwe go mutemi, nane nokwituma nha vyono wulonjile.” Yakajendelela kwikala mu Yelusalemi vilo vinji muno muno.
39 Ninga yali yeza kupuluta myaka midatu, i wetumami wejete wa Shimeyi, wakamanyhila kuli Akishi u mwana wa Maka u mutemi we yisi ya Gati. Nayo Shimeyi yali yeza kuhulika nha vyono wetumami wakwe wali ku Gati,
40 hobaho yakawikaniza ndogowe yakwe, yakawa kubita kuli mutemi Akishi wa Gati kuwalondola wetumami wakwe. Na mbeka yakawagana, hodu yakawagomola kanyhi ku kaya kwakwe.
41 Nayo mutemi Solomona yakahulika zizo zono Shimeyi yetumile,
42 hobaho yakatuma munhu kuli Shimeyi yakamulonjele yatye, “Alu ane nha nakwihuwiza mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono wulecele lukulu kuwuka mwi'kaya lya Yelusalemi. Kanyhi nghakusugula nha vyono, hono wuwuce du mwi'kaya, hodu kofwa. Nagwe wukalumba nha vyono kowa ne cihuliko kuli'ne.
43 Alu nhawule kanyhi kuwubena wihuwizo wako, kuwa kulibedegala ne lilajilizo lyangu?
44 Si kotya wumanyile manyi manyi, i zimbi zose zono wamutendela baba Dawudi. Holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu kokulipiza ku soko ya zizo.
45 Ninga ane, u Muwaha Mulungu kondugutiza, kanyhi kowunoza wutemi wa Dawudi wulawilicile cibitilila.”

46 Hodu, hobaho mutemi yakamulajiliza Benaya yakamuwulaje Shimeyi. Nayo yakabita yakamuwulaga. Nhondo no wutemi wukawa kutula mu muwoko wa Solomona.


1 Watemi 3

Solomona yakulomba mahala kuli Mulungu

(2Zin 1:3-12)

1 U mutemi Solomona, wakelunza na Falawo u mutemi wa Misili, munzila yo kumutola mwana we cicekulu wa Falawo. Yakamuwika yekale mu kaya ya Dawudi, sunga kolo yakondya kuzenga likulu lyakwe mweneco, ne Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne lijinji lyono lipilime Yelusalemi.
2 Hambi mwipango lilyo, i Nyumba yo Mulungu, nha yali yakali nazengwa yaye, holeka ne wanhu wali wakali wakulavya makumbiko gawo mu vigongo vye hayo hayo.
3 Nayo Solomona yali yamwendile muno Muwaha Mulungu, kanyhi yakawa yebate muno muno malajilizo gono yalajilizwa no sogwe Dawudi. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, nayo yawulagaga mitugo, kawa kulavizya makumbiko gakwe mu vigongo vye hayo hayo.

4 Mwi'zuwa linji, Solomona yakabita ku Gibeyoni ho yakalavye makumbiko kuko, hambi kuko ku Gibeyoni, nha ho hali hocili cikumbicilo cono cali cikumucile muno. Nayo halulongozi, yali yakondya kulavya kuko vilavywa vinyanywe magana na magana.
5 Na mwizuwa lilyo ne cilo, u Muwaha Mulungu yakelajila kuli mwene mu ndoto, yakamuwuza yakatya, “Alu kokwenda ane nghupele cici?”

6 Nayo Solomona yakapitula yakatya, “Nhondo zose, walajilaga wendo wuwaha, kuli baba Dawudi u mwitumami wako, nayo yakawa munhu muswanu, mono yali cihuliko, kanyhi yakawa no winenenyhezi wuwaha kuli'gwe. Nagwe wukajendelela kumulajila zo lusungu lwako luwaha, wukamupela mwana we cilume, mono aluno yawuhaliye wutemi wakwe.
7 Gwe go Mulungu, go Muwaha Mulungu, mono wumheye wusume wo kuhalila wutemi wa baba Dawudi, hamba yiwe ane nghali nili mudodo, mono si manyile na vyono wutemi winha.
8 Nane ahano nili hamonga ne wanhu wono wawahagula wawe wako, i wanhu wono wenji muno muno, wono si wakusumika na kuwuwalwe.
9 Holeka nghukunena wumhele mahala. Hambi nha nghuhila niwateme wanhu wako mu ling'ani, kanyhi sume kumanya zimbi ne ziswanu. Hambi 'ne nosuma wule kuwatema wanhu wako wenji muno wono wenha wawa?”

10 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakendezwa muno, yali yeza kuwona vyono, Solomona yakulomba yatuje mahala.
11 Hobaho yakamutya, “Alu, vyono wulombile mahala, ho wusume kutema kwe ling'ani, hamba si wukulomba ho wikale mumi vilo vinji, hamba lunji wuwe mugoli muno, hamba lunji walugu wako wafwe.
12 Ale nane nokutendela nha vyono wulombile. Nokupela mahala menji, hamonga no wizuce wuwaha wono munhu wo wose yakali si nawa nawo, hamba mono kolowa nawo.
13 Kanyhi nokonjeleza na zono si wuzilombile. Nagwe kowa no muwili mutelevu mu wikale wako wose, hamonga ne lilumbo yiwaha muno, kuciza watemi wose.
14 Na hono wujendelele ne cogopo cako kuli'ne, no kwibata Wulajilize wangu, hamonga ne malajilizo gangu, nha vyono sogo Dawudi yanoza, ale, nane nokupela vilo vinji vyo wikale wako.”

15 Solomona nhondo yakawa kusinhuka mu zinhongo, ninga yakamanya nha vyono, Mulungu kalonganila nayo mu zindoto. Hodu, yakawa kubita ku Yelusalemi, yakema ha mwande ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane, yakamulavizya Muwaha Mulungu vilavywa vinyanywe, hamonga ne vilavywa vyo mulamu. Hanyuma ya zizo, yakawanozeza sikukuu mbaha ya ndigwa i wetumami wakwe.

Solomona yakulamula icalo cikamu muno

16 Ale, mwizuwa linji, wakaza wacekulu wasenyha, wajete, kuli mutemi Solomona.
17 Na yumonga wa muli wene yakatya, “Gwe go Mutemi, mucekulu ayu hamonga nane, wose cikalane nyumba monga, ane nghasawa mwana wa cilume, lono nayo yali baho mu nyumba.
18 Vyali vyeza kupuluta vilo vyejete kulawa lono nalelela, muyangu nayo yakawa kulela mwana wa cilume. Nase wose vyono cili wejete, cili wene du mu nyumba, hasina hamba yunji wo wose mono yaza ha kaya yetu.
19 Na mu cilo ce zuwali, muyangu kagonela mwanagwe kawa kumuwulaga.
20 Nayo yeza kwinuka ne cilo, kawa kumusola mwanangu lono nali ngonile nhongo, kawa kamuwusa ku lubavu lono nali nimuwicile, kawa kumuwika mu citanda cakwe, hanyuma kawa kumusola u mwanagwe mono yafwiye, kawa kumuwika mu citanda cangu.
21 Nali neza kwinuka mu nhongo, lono wela hakali mitondo, niwa kusaka nimose mwanangu, ninga niwamuwona yafwiye. Neza kumulanga viswanu, niwa kuwona vyono kumbe ayu yo si mwanangu.”

22 Ninga ayulya mucekulu yunji, yakatya, “Ng'o, vyo si vinha, langa u mwana mono mumi yo wangu, ayo mono yafwiye yo wako!” Nayo mucekulu mulongozi yakatya, “Gwe, ng'o! U mwana mono yafwiye yo wako, ayo mumi yo wangu!”

Wakawa kujendelela kuwangana hamwande yo mutemi Solomona.

23 Nayo mutemi Solomona yakawatya, “Alu, 'nye, wose vyono muli wejete, ayu yakutya, u mwana mono yali mumi yo wangu, na mono yafwiye yo wako.”
24 Hodu mutemi yakasaka wamuletele mumaje. Nawo mumaje wali weza kuletwa,
25 mutemi yakatya, “Alu, mumusanhaje mwana ayu mumi yalawe hejete, muwagawile mucekulu na lusanho lwakwe.”

26 Baho u mucekulu mono yali yili mbeka yo munya mwana, nhumbula yakwe yikapaya muno, yakawa kumutya mutemi, “Gwe go mutemi, nghukunena dedede, wulece kumuwulaga mwana yuyu! Langa wumupelaje yuyo muyangu!”

Ninga ayulya mucekulu yunji, yakatya, “Aa, ng'o, wulece kumupela hamba yumonga wa muli sese, langa wumusanhaje du yalawe hejete.”

27 Hodu mutemi Solomona yakawa kutya, “Ale, hodu, mulece kanyhi kumuwulaga mwana yuyo! Langa mumupele mucekulu mulongozi, hambi nha yo nyina we mbeka wo mwana yuyo.”

28 Nawo i wanhu wa Isilayeli wali weza kuhulika zo wuling'anizi wo mutemi Solomona, wose wakawa na cogopo ciwaha muno kuli mwene. Hambi wose nha wakamanya vyono, Mulungu yo yamupelile mahala go kuling'aniziza zinghani viswanu.


1 Watemi 4

I wetumami wa Solomona

1 Solomona yakawa yo mutemi we yisi yose ya Isilayeli,
2 na awano, wali wo wetumami wakwe wawaha.

Azaliya u mwana wa Zadoki, yali yo mukohani,
3 Eliholefu na Ahiya i wana wa Shawu, wali wo wandici Yehoshafati u mwana wa Ahiludi, yali yo mulanjilizi we zinghani zo wikumbucize.
4 Benaya u mwana wa Yehoyada, yali yo muwaha we wasilikale wo wulugu. Zadoki na Abiyatali wali wo wakohani
5 Azaliya u mwana wa Natani, yali yo muwaha we wetumami wose.

Zabudi u mwana wa Natani, yali yo mukohani kanyhi yo mutazi muwaha wo mutemi.
6 Ahishali, yali yo mulanjilizi we wetumami wose wa mwi'kulu. Adonilamu u mwana wa Abuda, yali yo mulanjilizi wa wetumami wose we milimo, wono wetumywaga kwe vilungo.

7 Solomona yakawika wanhu yikumi na wejete, wawe wo wawaha mu zisi zose ze Waisilayeli, munhu na yisi yakwe. Nawo wakawa wo waleta zindigwa kuli mutemi, ku wulawa mu zisi zawo, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo. Wakawa wakwilecela munhu mwezi monga, sunga mwaka wumalika.
8 Aga go matagwa gawo hamonga ne matagwa ge zisi zono wagawilwa.

Benihuli, yakapegwa yisi ye matunda ya Efulayimu.
9 Bendekeli, yakapegwa makaya ga Mazi, nha cinji Shalibimu, na Beti Shemeshi, na Eloni na Beti Hanani.
10 Nayo Benihesedi, yakapegwa makaya gose ga Alububoti na Soko hamonga ne yisi yose ya Hefeli.
11 Benabinadabu, mono yali yamutolile mwana we cicekulu wa Solomona, mono yakucemwa Tafati, yakapegwa yisi yose ya Doli.
12 Bana, u mwana wa Ahiludi, yakapegwa makaya ga Tanaki, na Megido, sunga ne ma'si gose behi na Bete-shani, behi ne likaya lya Zeletani, ku lubavu lwa kutakama ye likaya lya Yezeleli, sunga ko kwi'kaya lya Abeli Mahola, ne likaya lya Yokemeyamu.
13 Nayo Bengebeli yakapegwa likaya lya Lamoti ya ku Gileyadi, hamonga ne vitumbi vyayo vya mu Gileyadi, vyono vyali vya we mhuga ya Yayili, i wana wa Manase, hamonga ne yisi ya Aligobu ya ku Bashani, gakawa makaya mawaha makumi mutandatu, gose hamonga. Na gose hamonga, gakawa mazenjele magomawakali, gena ne malango ga cuma.
14 Abinadabu u mwana wa Ido, yakapegwa yisi ya Mahanayimu.
15 Ahimazi mono yali yamutolile Basemati u mwana yunji kanyhi we cicekulu wa Solomona, mwene yakapegwa yisi ya Nafutali.
16 Bana, u mwana wa Hushayi, yakapegwa yisi ya Asheli, hamonga ne likaya lya Beyaloti.
17 Yehoshafati, u mwana wa Paluya, yakapegwa yisi ya Isakali.
18 Shimeyi, u mwana wa Ela, yakapegwa yisi ya Benyamini.
19 Gebeli, u mwana wa Uli, yakapegwa yisi ya Gileyadi yono yali yitemigwe no mutemi Sihoni we Waamoli, yono yali yo mutemi Ogu wa Bashani, ninga hamonga na wawa yikumi na wejete, hali ho yuli mulanjilizi wawo yumonga du, mono yawalanjililaga mu yisi yose.

Wumoteswe wo wutemi wa Solomona

20 Nawo wanhu wa Yuda, hamonga ne wanhu wa Isilayeli, wakawa wenji muno nho musawawa wa mu mwejelele we lilenga, walizaga, wakung'wa, kanyhi wakawa na nyemo mbaha muno.
21 Nawo wutemi wa Solomona, wukawa wutandaye muno sunga na mu makungugo ganji, ku wulawilila he 'kolongo lya Efulata, sunga ko ku yisi ye Wafilisiti, sunga ko ku mupaka we yisi ya Misili. Nawo wawa wose, wamulipaga kodi Solomona. Kanyhi wakajendelela kumwitumamila mu mikalile yakwe yose.

22 Ne visakwa vyono Solomona yavitumamilaga nhondo zose, vyali vyo avi. Makapo elufu yihano ga wusaje wono wunamuce, na makopo elufu yikumi ga wusaje wono wubalanjice,
23 na ng'ombe yikumi zono zijinile, na ng'ombe makumi mejete zono zikuliswa, na ngholo yigana limonga. Hamonga na vivyo vyose, kanyhi vikawa hamonga na nzela, na haluzi, hamonga ne zinghuku zono zijinile.

24 Solomona yakatema yisi yose yo lubavu lwa kumwezi ye likolongo lya Efulata, ku wulawa Tifusa ha Efulata, sunga ko ku mwezi kwi 'kaya lya Gaza. Ne watemi wose wa ku lubavu lwa ku mwezi ye likolongo lya Efulata, wose wakawa wetumami wakwe, nayo yakawa yena mulamu ne zisi zose zono zali zimupilime.
25 Na mu wikale wakwe wose, i wanhu wose wa Yuda na wa Isilayeli, wakekala wapolile mu mhuga zawo, hamonga ne mizabibu yawo ne mikuyu yawo.

26 Kanyhi Solomona, yali yena mabanda elufu makumi mane, go wulisizo we zifwalasi zakwe zo kukwega mikokoteni yo wulugu, kanyhi yali yena wasilikale wapanda fwalasi, waficile elufu yikumi na nyejete.
27 Nawo i wawaha yikumi na wejete we zi'si, wewalaga yu monga yu monga, munhu mwezi monga, wakutegulaga ko mutemi Solomona, ndigwa yono yalizaga mweneco, hamonga na wose wono walizanaga nayo mwi'kulu. Wawo walavyaga vinhu vyose vyono vyasakwaga nhondo zose.
28 Nawo wose mu wilecele wawo, walavyaga yumonga yumonga, shayili ne mahanze, vyono vyasakwaga ku soko ye zifwalasi zono zakwegaga mikokoteni yo wulugu, hamonga ne zinimu zinji.

29 Muwaha Mulungu yakamupela Solomona mahala menji, hamonga no wizuce wuwaha muno, muno hamonga no wuhile wuwaha wo witumi.
30 Solomona yakawa munhu we mahala kuciza wanhu wose we mahala wa ku lubavu lwa kucilima, hamba i wanhu we mahala wa ku Misili.
31 Solomona yakawa yo munhu we mahala muli wanhu wose, yakawa we mahala kuciza Etani munya Ezelahi, hamonga na Helimani, na Kalikoli, na Daleda i wana wa Maholi. Ne zinghani zakwe Solomona, zikakumuka mu ma'si gose gono gapilime.
32 Solomona yakandika mbazi elufu yidatu, hamonga na nyimbo kuciza elufu monga.
33 Na mumo mu mbazi zakwe, yakalonga ze mabici, hamonga ne vilimwa vyose, ku wulawa mu mihagata ya Lebanoni, sunga ko ku cibici ca ezobu cono citulaga no kutambala mu majinji. Yakalonga ze zinimu, na ze zindeje, na ze vitambala hasi, na ze zisomba.
34 Sunga ne watemi, wose mu wulawa mu yinze yose, wose wakazihulika ze mahala gakwe, wakawa wakutuma wanhu wabite wakamuhulicize.


1 Watemi 5

Solomona yakwiwikaniza yazenje Nyumba yo Mulungu

(2Zin 2:1-18)

1 Hilamu u mutemi wa Tulo, yali mwendece wo mutemi Dawudi mu vilo vinji muno, holeka yali yeza kuhulika nha vyono Solomona yo yahala wutemi wo sogwe Dawudi, yakatuma wanhu kuli Solomona.
2 Nayo Solomona yakatuma nghani kuli Hilamu yakatya,
3 “Agwe wumanyile nha vyono baba Dawudi mu cipango cakwe, wulugu wali wumupilime kose kose, nayo yakawa yena mulimo wo wihomi ne walugu wa mu ma'si gono gali gamupilime. Holeka hamba si yasuma kuzenga Nyumba yo Mulungu, yo kumuhembela Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe, sunga kolo Muwaha Mulungu yakondya kumupela wusume kuli walugu wakwe wose.
4 Ninga aluno, u Muwaha Mulungu wangu, yakondya kumhela mulamu mu mipaka yangu yose. Aluno neza sina kanyhi walugu, hamba cinji co cose cibi.
5 Muwaha Mulungu yamwihuwiza baba Dawudi yakatya, ‘U mwanago mono nomuhaza wutemi wako, yuyo yo konzenjela ne Nyumba yangu.’ Holeka nane nadumula nghani, niyizenje Nyumba yo kumuhembela Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wangu.
6 Holeka nghukunena, wutume wanhu wako, wabite ku Lebanoni wakanhemele mabici go muhagata. Ne wanhu wangu nawo, wowa wali hamonga nawo. Nane nowalipa wanhu wako, mu nghango nha vyono kowona viswanu gwegwe. Hambi nagwe nha wumanyile vyono i wanhu wangu hamba si wamanyile na vyono wayitemaga mibici, nha vyono wanhu wako wamanyile.”

7 Nayo Hilamu, yakendezwa muno muno yali yeza kuzituga zinghani zizo ku wulawa kuli Solomona, nayo yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe, mono yamupelile Dawudi u mwana we mahala mono yenha yuyu Solomona, ho yahale wutemi wakwe we likungugo liwaha lyono linha lili!”
8 Nayo Hilamu, yakatuma nghani kuli Solomona yakutya, “Nakondya kugabocela malajilizo gako gono wulajilize, nane nokwituma nha vyono wunonjeye. Nane nolavya mibici yo muhagata hamonga ne mibici yo mubeloshi.
9 Ne wanhu wangu woyitimya mibici yiyo, ku wulawa Lebanoni sunga kwi'lenga, nawo woyiwopa hamonga, wayojeze mwi'lenga, wayilete sunga hanhu hono kosaka gwegwe. Nawo woyiwopola, i wanhu wako nawo ho wayilanjilile. Ninga agwe nokusaka wuwalise ndigwa du wanhu wangu hodu.”

10 Hobaho Hilamu yakawa kumupela Solomona mabici menji go muhagata, hamonga ne mabici ge mibeloshi, mu nghango nha vyono yasakaga.
11 Nayo Solomona yakamupela Hilamu mwaka na mwaka madebe magana mane go wuhemba, hamonga na galoni yigana limonga ne kumi, za mafuta, so ndigwa ye wanhu wakwe.

12 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawukamisa wihuwizo wakwe, yakamupela Solomona mahala menji. Na hakawa no mulamu muno hagati ya Hilamu na Solomona, wakanoza cilagane vyono wejete.

13 Mutemi Solomona yakakonghola wanhu wetuma milimo, ku wulawa mu yisi yose ya Isilayeli, waficile wanhu elufu makumi madatu.
14 Yakamuwika Adonilamu yawe yo mulanjilizi wawo. Ninga yakawagawa wakalawa mapuga madatu, mhuga monga wanhu elufu yikumi. Ne zimhuga zizi zose, zewalilaga mwezi monga monga ku wubita ku Lebanoni. Mwezi monga wakuwa ku Lebanoni ne myezi myejete wawa wali ku zikaya zawo.
15 Mutemi Solomona yakawa yena wanhu elufu makumi munana, mu yisi ye matunda, wono wapondaga mabwe, na wanhu elufu makumi mupunghati, wono wagakunghaga mabwe.
16 Yakawika na wanji wanhu elufu yidatu na magana madatu, wono wawalindilizaga no kuyilanga milimo vyono wetumaga.
17 Na so wulajilize wo mutemi Solomona, wanhu wakaponda mabwe maswanu muno go kuzenjela musinji we Nyumba yo Mulungu.
18 I wazenji wa Solomona ne wazenji wa Hilamu, hamonga ne wanhu wono wakalawa kwi 'kaya lya Babiloni, wakawikaniziza lukulu mabwe, hamonga ne mabici go kuzenjela Nyumba yo Mulungu.


1 Watemi 6

Solomona yakuyizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu

1 Mutemi Solomona yakanza mulimo wo kuyizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu, lono yali yakondya kupuluta myaka magana mane na makumi munana, ku wulawa wanhu wa Isilayeli, lono wawucila ku Misili, mu mwaka we kane wo wutemi wakwe muli Isilayeli, mu mwezi we kejete, mwezi wa Zivu.
2 Ne Nyumba yono Solomona yamuzenjela Muwaha Mulungu, yali yina wutali wayo wa mugati mita makumi mejete na mupunghati, wugazi mita yicenda, no wutali wayo wo wubita kucanyha wali mita yikumi ne 'datu na nusu.
3 Ne likumbo lyali no wutali wayo wa mugati mita yine na nusu, no wugazi mita yicenda nho wugazi we hanhu hono helile.
4 Ne majinji ge Nyumba yo Mulungu, gali gena madilisha go kwinjiza wuzelu.
5 Na kunze ye majinji gose ge Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakazenga vinyumba, vipilime sunga na ku mhilimo yayo, ne viyumba vyose vyali vina wutali wo kubita kucanyha, mita nyejete na lobo.
6 Ne vinyumba vya hasi navyo, vyali vina mita nyejete na lobo wugazi wayo. Ne cinyumba ca ha ciligati, cali cina mita nyejete na lobo yidatu wugazi wayo, ne cinyumba ca hacanyha, mita yidatu wugazi wayo. Ne majinji ge Nyumba yo Mulungu yakagacepula wugazi kucanyha mbavu zose, vinyumba ho vikale mwi'jinji visina micici yayo.

7 Ne mabwe gose gono wazenjela Nyumba yo Mulungu, gaseseswaga kono wagahimbulaga du, ho halece kuwa ne zozo lya nyondo hamba nhemo, hamba citumamilwa co cose ca cuma, lono Nyumba yo Mulungu yazengwaga.

8 U mulango we cinyumba ca kusi, wukawikwa ku lubavu lwa kutakama ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, zikazengwa ne zi ngazi zo kubitila ku vinyumba vya kucanyha.
9 Mutemi Solomona, yakayizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu yakayikondya, yakayifunika ne mibici ne zimbawo zo muhagata.
10 Yakazenga vinyumba vili hadatu mu mhelo ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, ne vinyumba vyose vyali no wutali wa mita nyejete na lobo, navyo vikalunzwa hamonga ne Nyumba yo Mulungu kwe micici ye mibici yo muhagata.

11 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Solomona yakatya,
12 “Hono wibate wulajilize wangu hamonga ne malajilizo gangu gose, ale, nane nokunozeza zose nha vyono namwihuwiza sogo Dawudi.
13 Nane nokwikala hamonga ne wanhu wangu Waisilayeli, mumu mono Nyumba yangu yono wukunzenjela, nane si nowaleka kanyhi yaye.”

14 Solomona yakayizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu yakayikondeleza.

Vitumamilwa vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu

(2Zin 3:8-14)

15 Mugati ye majinji, yakagafunika na mbawo zo muhagata, ku wulawilila hasi ye nyumba, sunga ko kwi'jinji lya kucanyha. Na hasi yayo yakafunika na mbawo za mibeloshi.
16 Ne cinyumba ca hasi, cono cacemwa i Hanhu Hono Helile, helile muno, cikazengwa kumhilimo ye Nyumba yo Mulungu. Naco cali no wutali wa mita yicenda, cali citemanuywe ne libupa lya mbawo zo muhagata, zilawiliye hasi, sunga ko kucanyha.
17 Ne cinyumba ca kono mwande ye Hanhu Hono Helile, helile muno, cali no wutali wa mita yikumi na munana.
18 Nazo zizo mbawo zo muhagata, zali zihambigwe no wukakate we zimboga ne maluwa. Na mugati mose, mukahambwa ne zimbawo zo muhagata, ho mabwe galece kwijela.

19 Yakanoza cinyumba ca kugati mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, ho yawice mumo Lisanduku lye Cilagane.
20 Ne cinyumba cico, cali cina wutali wa mita yicenda, no wugazi wa mita yicenda, no wutali wo kubita kucanyha nawo mita yicenda, yakacihiliwa zahabu nengu. Naco cikumbicilo cali cifunicizwe na mbawo zo muhagata.
21 Na mugati ye Nyumba yo Mulungu mwali muhiliwe zahabu, mose, ne minyololo ya zahabu yikawikwa yikuninyina yidumulashe mulango we cinyumba cico ca kugati, cono naco cali cihiliwe zahabu.
22 Ne Nyumba yo Mulungu yose, mugati yali hiliwe zahabu, hamonga ne cikumbicilo ce Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno.

23 Yakanoza vihwani vya makelubi mejete ko mubici wo muzeyituni, yakagawika Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno, u wutali wayo limonga mita yine na nusu.
24 Ne vihwani vye makelubi gose, gakawa gehwanile wuwaha no wutali.
25 Limonga lina mabawa mejete, libawa limonga lina wutali wa mita nyejete na lobo,
26 no wutali wa ku wulawa libawa limonga sunga kwi'bawa liyagwe, wukawa mita yine na nusu.
27 Navyo vihwani vivyo vye makelubi vikawikwa akuno na akuno mugati ye Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno, ho mabawa gayo mejete gasume kwipamiza hagati ye cinyumba, ne mabawa ganji mejete gakapamiza lijinji.
28 Nago makelubi, gakahiliwa zahabu gose.

29 Ne lijinji lye cinyumba ciwaha, na lye cinyumba ca kugati, gose gakahambwa no wukakate we vihwani vye makelubi, ne mitende, hamonga ne maluwa kunze na ku gati.
30 Sunga na hasi du mu sakafu, mose mwali muhiliwe zahabu.

31 Wukanozwa mulango, wumonga we milango myejete ko mubici wo muzeyituni, wukawicilwa ha mulango we Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno, hamonga ne zimbawo nyejete zo wima ze zifulemu, na ye cipinga ya hacanyha yayo.
32 Ne milango nayo yikahambwa ko wukakate we vihwani vye makelubi, ne mitende, hamonga ne maluwa. Nawo mulango weneco, hamonga ne vihwani vye makelubi, ne mitende, vyose vikahiliwa zahabu.
33 Yakanoza kanyhi fulemu ko mubici wo muzeyituni nha vyono fulemu nongozi, yakanoza ku so mulango muwaha we nyumba mbaha yo wihangulizize.
34 Hakawa na milango yinji myejete yo kugodya, minozwe no mubici wo mubeloshi,
35 nayo nhavivyo yikahambwa no wukakate we vihwani vye makelubi, hamonga ne mitende, ne maluwa, yakayihiliwa zahabu mu wukakate.

36 Ne likumbo lya kugati, lyali lizenjigwe ha mwande ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, yipilimywe majinji gono gali mazenje micilili midatu, ya mabwe, na mucanyha yo mucilili monga monga we mabwe, wakajeleka mucilili wumonga wa mbawo zo muhagata sunga micilili yose vyono midatu ye mabwe.

37 No musinji we Nyumba yo Mulungu, wukasimywa mu mwezi we kejete, mwezi wa Zivu, mu mwaka we kane wo wutemi wa Solomona.
38 Na mu mwezi wo munana, mwezi wa Buli, wa mu mwaka we kumi na monga wo wutemi wa Solomona, ho Nyumba yo Mulungu yakondezya lukulu kuzengwa, nha vyono yasakwaga yiwe. Ale, Solomona yayizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu myaka mupunghati, nayo yikawa yakondya.


1 Watemi 7

Likulu lya Solomona

1 Solomona yakezenjela likulu, nalyo yakalizenga myaka yikumi na midatu.
2 Hanyuma yakayizenga Nyumba yinji yono yikacemwa “Nyumba ya mu mbago ya Lebanoni.” Nayo yali yina wutali wa mita makumi mane na yine na nusu, no wugazi wa mita makumi mejete na nyejete no lobo, no wutali wo kubita kucanyha, wali mita yikumi na yidatu na nusu. Yakayizenga mucanyha ye micilili midatu ye masumbili go mubici wo muhagata, no mucilili monga, wali mutandalice masumbili yikumi na mahano, ne micici yo mubici wo mupaga, yikawa mijelece mu masumbili gago.
3 Yikafunikwa mucanyha no mubici wo muhagata, mucanyha ye vinyumba vyono vyali vizenje mucanyha ye masumbili ga galya makumi mane na mahano.
4 Na mu zimbavu zose vyono nyejete ze lijinji, hakawa na madilisha micilili midatu midatu.
5 I milango yose hamonga ne madilisha gose, gali manoze gena nhuzi yine yine zi fulemu zayo. Ne micilili ye madilisha, nayo vyali visonjile muling'ano na mu wima wayo nhavivyo.

6 Yakalinozeza masumbili, wutali wayo mita makumi mejete na nyejete na lobo, no wugazi mita yikumi ne 'datu na nusu Likawa lina ne likumbo lyayo libatwe ne masumbili.

7 Nyumba yono yali yiwicigwe Ligoda lyo wutemi, hanji yacemwaga Nyumba yo kuling'aniziza, i hanhu hono Solomona yaling'anizizaga vyalo, naho hali hawiciywe mbawo zo muhagata ku wulawilila mu sakafu sunga ko ku canyha mu mahapa.

8 Ne kaya yakwe Solomona yono yekalaga, yali yizenjigwe ha mhilimo ye Nyumba yo kuling'aniziza, ninga yali yizenjigwe yihwanile nhe zikaya zinji zose. Kanyhi Solomona yakazenga nyumba yinji yono vihwanile ne zinyagwe, yakamuzenjela mugolece wakwe, u mwana we cicekulu wo mutemi wa Misili.

9 Ne mazengo gose gago, hamonga ne Nyumba yo kuling'aniziza, gose gali mazenje kwe mabwe maswanu muno ku wulawilila ku musinji sunga ko ku canyha. Ne mabwe gagano gali mapandulwe viswanu ko musumeno mbavu zose, nha vyono vinha vipimo vyayo.
10 No musinji wayo, wukawa muzenje ne mabwe mawaha muno, gono mapandulwe viswanu, sunga ne mabwe ganji gali gena wutali wa mita yidatu na nusu, na ganji mita yine.
11 Na mucanyha yago, gakajelekwa mabwe ganji, gono gali mapandulwe kwa vipimo, hamonga ne mabici go muhagata.
12 I likumbo liwaha lye likulu, hamonga ne cinyumba ca mugati mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne cinyumba ca ha mulango we Nyumba yo Mulungu, mose mwali mogali majinji, gono gali gena gose mucilili monga monga wa micici ya mbawo zo muhagata, micilili midatu ya mabwe, hanyuma mucilili monga wa micici ya mbawo zo mupagata.

Mulimo wa Hulamu

13 Mutemi Solomona, yakatuma munhu yakamulete Hulamu, u mutyani, mono yekalaga kwi kaya lya Tulo, munhu munghula muno mu wutyani we vyuma.
14 Na malehemu sogwe, nayo yali mwikala kuko kwi 'kaya lya Tulo nhavivilya. Nayo yakawa munghula mu mulimo wuwo wo wutyana vyuma. U nyina yali wo mulango wa Nafutali. Yuyo Hulamu, yali yo munhu we mahala muno, mono yali yena wizoweleze wuwaha muno mu mulimo wuwo wo wutyani. Nayo yo munhu mono yali yalanjiliye, vyono Solomona komusaka yawe mulanjilizi we milimo yose ye vyuma.

Masumbili mejete ga cuma

(2Zin 3:15-17)

15 Hulamu yo yanoza masumbili mejete ge vyuma, na gose vyono mejete, limonga lyali lina wutali wa mita munana, no wupilime wayo mita yihano na nusu, yakagasimya ha mulango we Nyumba yo Mulungu.
16 Kanyhi yakanoza mikumbo myejete ya vyuma, yono yali yina wutali wa mita nyejete na lobo, yikajelekwa mucanyha ye masumbili.
17 Ne masumbili gose vyono mejete, gali mahambwe mucanyha yayo, na mikumbo mitawe ya mikufu.
18 Hamonga na micilili myejete myejete ya makomamanga ga vyuma.

19 Ne mikumbo yiyo, yali mihambwe ne maluwa go wutali wa mita monga na lobo yidatu,
20 nayo yikajelekwa mucanyha ye mikufu mitawe yono yali yili mucanyha ye masumbili. Na hakawa na makomamanga magana mejete gali mu micilili myejete, gapilime mu masumbili gose limonga limonga.

21 Hulamu yakagawicila ha mulango we Nyumba yo Mulungu i masumbili gago mejete ge vyuma, limonga lya ku lubavu lwa kutakama likacemwa Yakini, ne liyagwe lya ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, likacemwa Bowazi.
22 Ne mikumbo ye vyuma vye maluwa yikawa yili mucanyha ye masumbili. No mulimo wo wuwicila masumbili, wukawa wakondya.

Wunoze we litanji lye malenga

(2Zin 4:2-5)

23 Hulamu kanyhi yakanoza litanji lya shaba, lyo kuwicila malenga. Wutali wayo wa kusi, mita nyejete na lobo, na mita yine na nusu wugazi walyo, na mita yikumi na yidatu na nusu wupilime walyo.
24 Na mu mugo we litanji, yakawicila mayungu ga shaba, gono gali menjedulwe, malunze hamonga ne litanji mu mugo wayo.
25 Litanji likajelekwa mucanyha ye vihwani vye zing'ombe yikumi na nyejete za shaba, zono zali zilipituciye viwino, yidatu yidatu, zilanjile mbavu zose vyono yine.
26 Wukangazi we licuma lye shaba lye litanji, wali sentimita mupunghati na nusu. U mugo wayo wali winha mugo wa cikombe, na kunze yo mugo, munoze kotya liluwa liswanu muno. Ne litanji lilyo lyenjilaga lita elufu makumi mane za malenga.

Mikokoteni ye vyuma

27 Hulamu yakanoza mikokoteni yikumi ya vyuma, no mukokoteni monga, wali wina wutali wa mita monga na lobo yidatu, no wugazi wayo nawo, mita monga na lobo yidatu, no wubita kucanyha, mita monga na lobo.
28 Yali minozeze mbawo ngazi, zono zali ziling'aniye wutali no wugazi wayo kose kose, nazo zikawicilwa mu zifulemu.
29 Na mu mbavu zayo, mwali mwandicigwe vihwani vya simba, ng'ombe, ne makelubi. Na mucanyha, mwali mocili cibatilo, na hasika ye cihwani ce simba ne ng'ombe, hakawa hakakatwe mikumbo ye maluwa.
30 Ne mikokoteni yose, yali yina maguludumu mane mane ga vyuma, hamonga ne micici yayo ya vyuma. Na mu zimbavu zayo zose vyono yine, yali yina mebato, gono gali gebate bakuli, nago mebato gago, gali mahambe no wihambo we maluwa.
31 Na mucanyha ye bakuli yiyo hali na mugo wono wupilime. No mugo wuwo, wukawicilwa wulawiliciye ha kacanyha, kotya nhe nusu mita nhavi ku wulawa hacanyha yo mukokoteni, na mita monga na nusu ha kasika mugati yayo. Nayo yikawa nghakakate mu mbavu zayo zose.
32 Maguludumu gayo gali gena wutali wa lobo yidatu ya mita, ne vibatilo vye mikokoteni, vikawa vilunze hamonga ne mikokoteni.
33 Ne maguludumu gayo gakawa gehwanile ne maguludumu ge matuka go wulugu, micici yayo ne migo yayo, ne mataluma gayo, hamonga ne vikombe vye maguludumu, vyose vyali vya cuma ca shaba.
34 Hakawa na mebatilo mane mane mu mikokoteni yose, gono gali malunze mu zinhuzi zose vyono yine yine ze mikokoteni.
35 Hakawa na vilunzizo mu mikokoteni yose, vyo wugazi wa lobo ya mita. Wibato wayo hamonga ne zimbawo za hasi yo mukokoteni, navyo vyali vilunze hamonga no mukokoteni.
36 Na kunze yo wibato, ne zimhelo zayo, Hulamu yakakakata vihwani vye makelubi na simba, hamonga ne mitende wumonga wumonga, yakapilimya ne maluwa.
37 Na yiyi yo nzila yono yetumamila mu wunoza mikokoteni. Ne mikokoteni yose, yikawa yina hwani monga du, wuwaha wayo, hamonga ne cinozeze cayo.

38 Hulamu yakanoza mabakuli yikumi, mukokoteni ne bakuli lyayo. Ne libakuli limonga lyali lina wugazi wa mita monga na lobo yidatu, ne libakuli limonga, lyenjilaga lita magana munana ga malenga.
39 Yakayiwika mikokoteni mihano ku lubavu lwa kutakama ye Nyumba yo Mulungu. Ne mikokoteni yinji mihano, yakayiwika ku lubavu lwa kusumuka. Ninga litanji, yakaliwika ku nhuzi ya ku lubavu lwa kutakama ye cilima.

Liwalo lye vitumamilwa vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu kwa ndumula

(2Zin 4:11–5:1)

40 Hulamu yakanoza mabakuli ne makoleyo, hamonga ne viya vyo kumiziza malenga. Yakawa yayikondeleza milimo yose yono yatumwa no Mutemi Solomona, ku soko ye Nyumba yo Mulungu. Ne vitumamilwa vyono yavinoza vyovino:

41 Masumbili mejete, mabakuli mejete ga mucanyha ye masumbili. I mikufu mitawe yono yikawikwa mucanyha ye masumbili.
42 Makomamanga magana mane ga shaba, gono yagawicila micilili myejete myejete ya makomamanga yigana yigana, gakapilimywa mu masumbili.
43 Mikokoteni yikumi, hamonga ne mabakuli yikumi.
44 Litanji lye malenga, hamonga ne vihwani vye zing'ombe yikumi na nyejete zono zajelekwa litanji mucanyha yazo.
45 Mabakuli ne makoleyo, ne viya vyo kwitumamilwa, vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, vyono Hulamu yamunozeza mutemi Solomona. Navyo vyose vyali vinozwe na shaba yono yang'alalaga.
46 U mutemi Solomona yavinozeza vinhu vivino vyose haciligati ya Sukoti na Zeletani, mwi'lolo lye likolongo lya Yoludani.
47 Mutemi Solomona hamba si yasuma kulituga liwalo, hamonga no wuzito we shaba yono yetumamilwa mu wunoza vinhu vivyo vyose, hambi na vyali vinji muno muno, holeka ne liwalo lyayo hamba si lyamanyika yaye.

48 Solomona yali yena ne vitumamilwa, vyono vyali vinozwe na zahabu, ku soko ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, navyo vyovino: Cikumbicilo, meza ye mikate yono milavywe kuli Mulungu,
49 viwicilo yikumi vye zitala, vyono vyawikwaga hamwande ye Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno, navyo vikawa vihano ku lubavu lwa kutakama, ne vihano ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, ne maluwa, zitala, mibato,
50 vikombe, tala, mabakuli, mitela, ne zikoleyo zo kupalila makala, ne zibawaba so milango ye Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno, hamonga ne milango ya kunze ye Nyumba yo Mulungu. Ne vinhu vivino vyose vyali vinozwe na zahabu.

51 Nayo mutemi Solomona yali yeza kuyikondya milimo yose yo kuyizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu, hodu, yakawa kuviwika mugati ye vinyumba, i vinhu vyose vyono sogwe Dawudi yavisiwanulila kuli Muwaha Mulungu, nha cinji, fweza, zahabu, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vinji vyose.


1 Watemi 8

Lisanduku lye Cilagane, likwinjizwa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu

(2Zin 5:2–6:2)

1 Mutemi Solomona yakawacema i walongola we milango, na we zimhuga ze wanhu wose wa Isilayeli, wamuzize ku Yelusalemi, waliwuse Lisanduku lye Cilagane ku Siyoni mu kaya ya Dawudi, wakalinjize mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
2 Na wose wakekongholela hamonga, mu cipango ce sikukuu ye vibanda mu mwezi wo mupunghati, wo mwezi wa Etanimu.
3 Nawo walongola wose wali weza kuwa wekonghola, i wakohani wakalidinghula Lisanduku lye Cilagane,
4 wakalinjiza mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Walawi hamonga ne wakohani, nawo wakalikula Lihema lyono Lyelile, wakalisola hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyose, wakavinjiza mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
5 Nayo mutemi Solomona, hamonga ne wanhu wose wa Isilayeli, wakekongholela ha mwande ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane, wakalavya makumbiko ge zingholo nyinji muno, hamonga ne zing'ombe nyinji muno, hamba si za kuwala.
6 Nawo wakohani wono wali walidinghuye Lisanduku lye Cilagane, wakalinjiza mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, wakaliwika Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno, hasi ye makelubi.
7 Ne makelubi, gali gagoloye mabawa gayo mucanyha ye Lisanduku, gakagafunika ne mabici gayo gono wali walidinghuliye.
8 Ninga mabici vyono gali matali, masonje gayo gejelaga hono munhu yawe yemile ha mwande ye Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno, nago gali baho du sunga nalu.
9 Na mugati ye Lisanduku mwali musina cinji, langa mabwe mejete du go wupamba, gono Musa yagawika mumo mugati, kuko kwi'tunda lya Sinayi, lono Muwaha Mulungu yanozeza Cilagane ne wanhu wa Isilayeli, lono wawucila mu yisi ya Misili.

10 Ne wakohani lono wawukaga mugati ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, i Nyumba yo Mulungu yikamema nhinicizo livunde,
11 likung'alala nhojo yo Muwaha Mulungu, sunga ne wakohani siwasuma kanyhi kwinjila ho wetume milimo yawo.

12 Nayo Solomona yakalomba yakutya,

“Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

yo gwegwe mono walisimya lizuwa mu mwanya,

ninga hamba nhavivyo wukahagula go

mweneco wikale mu mavunde no wutitu.

13 Ninga aluno nakondya kukuzenjela

nyumba yako yo lukumyo lwako,

i hanhu hono kokwikala cibitilila.”

Zinghani za Solomona kuli wanhu

(2Zin 6:3-11)

14 Nawo wanhu lono wali wakali wemile baho, mutemi Solomona yakawapitucila, yakawalugutiza.
15 Hanyuma yakawa kutya, “Yalumbwe Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli! Hambi nha yakamisa wihuwizo wakwe wono yamwihuwiziza baba Dawudi lono yatela.
16 ‘Ku wulawa lono nawawusiza wanhu wangu ku Misili, nali nghali nalihagula likaya mu yisi yose ya Isilayeli, i hanhu hono hanojeye Nyumba yangu yizengwe, i hanhu hono howa hanojeye ane hembelwe. Ninga nghakuhagula agwe Dawudi, wuteme muli wanhu wangu.’ ”

17 Kanyhi Solomona yakajendelela kutya, “Baba Dawudi yali yehwesize yazenje Nyumba yo Mulungu yo kumuhembela Muwaha Mulungu wa Isilayeli,
18 ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakamutya, ‘Heya, mahweso gako maswanu go kuhila wunzenjele nyumba,
19 ninga'lu agwe yo si koyizenga yaye. Langa mwanago mono komulela, yuyo yo kolonzenjela Nyumba yangu.’ ”

20 “Alu nalu u Muwaha Mulungu kuwakamisa wihuwizo wakwe. Nghani nongozi, nakondya kuwuhala wutemi wa baba Dawudi, wo kuwatema Waisilayeli, kanyhi nakondya kuzenga ne Nyumba yo Mulungu yo kumuhembela Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli.
21 Nakondya kuwika ne hanhu ho kuliwika Lisanduku lye Cilagane mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, lyono mugati yilyo, lina mabwe mejete du go wupamba, ge cilagane cono Muwaha Mulungu yalaganila ne walezi wetu lono yawawusiza ku Misili.”

Wulombe wa Solomona

(2Zin 6:12-42)

22 Solomona yakawa kubita kwima ha Cikumbicilo, ha meso ge wanhu wose, yakawa kuyigolola miwoko yakwe kucanyha,
23 yakalomba yakutya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wa Isilayeli, hasina Mulungu yunji mono yehwanile gwegwe kuko ku wulanga, hamba mono 'si hasi! Hambi agwe nha kukamisaga wihuwizo wako kuli wanhu wako, no kuwulajila u lusungu lwako kuli wene, lono wakukuhulika kwe zinhumbula zawo zose.
24 Kanyhi kuwukamisa no wihuwizo wako wono wamwihuwiziza baba Dawudi, nalu kwi zuwali zinghani zose zakondya kuficilicila.
25 Nalu go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wa Isilayeli, alu kanyhi nghukulomba wufisilize no wihuwizo wono wamwihuwiza baba Dawudi, lono wamulonjela nha vyono vilo vyose wa mu lulelo lwako wojendelela kutema muli Isilayeli, hono wakuhulice gwegwe nha vyono mwene Dawudi yakuhulika.
26 Holeka aluno Gwe go Mulungu wa Isilayeli, nghukulomba wuzinoze zose ziwe za mbeka, nha vyono wamwihuwiza baba Dawudi u mwitumami wako.

27 “Gwe go Mulungu, alu mbeka, kosuma kwikala mono yinze? Wulanje vyono wulanga hamba si wukukwiliye yaye, alu Nyumba yiyi yono nzenjile yo yokukwila?
28 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wangu, ane nili mwitumami wako. Holeka wuhulice wulombe wangu, wumhele ne visakwa vyangu vyono nghukulomba izuwali.
29 Weze kuyilanga Nyumba yiyi misi ne cilo, i hanhu baha hono gwegwe wahahagula, ho wuwe kuhembelwa. Alu, wuhulicize 'ne, lono cidomile ne Nyumba yako yiyi nghukulomba.
30 Wuhulice wulombe wangu, hamonga no wulombe we wanhu wako, lono nawo wowa wakukulomba vidomile wusu wawo ne hanhu baha. Gwe go Mulungu mono wikaye kuko ku wulanga, wucihulice kanyhi wucinenele.

31 “Na hono munhu yasitakwe vyono kamusutizya yunji, na hono yaletwe ha cikumbicilo mu Nyumba yako, yaze yelahize nha vyono mwene yasina masutya.
32 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wuhulice kuko ku wulanga wumuling'anize mwitumami wako. Wumulipicize musutyi, wumulecele ayulya mono yasina lisutya.

33 “Ne wanhu wako wa Isilayeli, kolo wasumwa ne walugu wawo ku soko vyono wakusutizya, ninga kolo wakukuwuyila, no kuza mono Nyumba yiyi, wakukulomba ko winenenyhezi, wakuhila wuwanenele,
34 ale wuwahulikaje kuko ku wulanga. Wuwanenele wanhu wako wubi wawo, wuwagomole kanyhi ku yisi yono wawapela walezi wawo.

35 “Na hono wikale si wutonyeze mvula ku soko yo wubi we wanhu wako wono wakutendeye, ninga kolo wakunena, wedome ne Nyumba yiyi wakulombe ko winenenyhezi,
36 wuwahulicizaje kuko ku wulanga. Wuwunenele wubi wo mutemi, sunga na we wanhu wa Isilayeli. Wuwejize wetume ze ling'ani. Hanyuma wutonyeze mvula mu yisi yako yono wawapela wanhu wako yiwe yawo.

37 “Na hono yigwe nzala mu yisi, hamba lihuhuji, lunji gagwe mawumbi ga nzije, hamba lunji wanhu wako wahomwe ne walugu wawo, hamba lunji hono hawe ho gali matamwa, hamba wutamwa muli wene,
38 wuwahulikaje wulombe wawo. Na hono hawe ho yuli yunji wa muli wanhu wako wa Isilayeli yena wusungu mu nhumbula yakwe, wagolole miwoko yawo wakulombe baha hono Nyumba yako,
39 wuwahulikaje wulombe wawo, wuwahulice kuko ku wikalo wako ku wulanga, wuwanenele, kanyhi wuwataze. Nagwe nha yo gwegwe du wuli wene mono wugamanyile mahweso ga mu nhumbula yo munhu. Holeka wumulangaje munhu wo wose nha vyono yimunojeye,
40 I wanhu wako ho wakuhulice gwegwe vilo vyose vyo wikale wawo wa mono yisi, yono wawapela walezi wetu.

41 “Na kolo mujenzi mono yekaye ku yisi ya kutali, yakuhulika zo lukumyo lwako, hamonga na ze mawaha gono wawatendela wanhu wako,
42 nayo yawe kuza kukuhemba no kulomba mu Nyumba yiyi,
43 wumuhulice wulombe wakwe. Wamuhulice wulombe wakwe kuko ku wulanga kono wikaye, kanyhi wumutendele zose zono yakukulomba. Ne wanhu wose wa mono'nze holeka wakumanye no kukuhulika gwegwe, nha vyono wanhu wako wa Isilayeli wakukuhulika. Kanyhi ho womanya nha vyono Nyumba yiyi yono nzenjile, ho hanhu hono hanojeye wakuhembele gwegwe.

44 “Nagwe lono wukuwalajiliza wanhu wako wabite ku wulugu wakehome ne walugu wawo, na hono wakulombe wemile ho hose hono wowa, ninga wawe walilanjile likaya lili lyono walihagula, ne Nyumba yako yono nakuzenjela,
45 wuhulice wulombe wawo. Wuwahulice kuko ku wulanga, wuwapele wusume kuli walugu wawo.

46 “Na kolo wanhu wako wakukusutizya, hambi nha hasina munhu mono si ketumaga wubi, nagwe mwi'kuwo lyako wuwalece walugu wawo wawasume, kanyhi wawasole wenha wafungwa wawategule ku yisi yinji, hamba yisi yiyo yiwe yili kutali muno muno,
47 ninga wuhulice wulombe we wanhu wako. Na hamba wawe wali ku yisi yiyo, ninga hono wanene, wakulombe, wakuvumila nha vyono mbeka wetuma wubi kanyhi wali cibede, ale wuhulice wulombe wawo Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu.
48 Na hono wanene kwe nhumbula ye mbeka lono wali ku yisi yiyo, ho wakulombe akuno wadomizye wusu wawo ku lubavu lwa kono yisi yiyi, yisi yono wawapela i walezi wetu, ne likaya lili lyono gwegwe walihagula, ne Nyumba yiyi yono nakuzenjela gwegwe,
49 ale wuhulicize wulombe wawo. Wuwahulice kuko kono wikaye ku wulanga, kanyhi wuwawonele lusungu.
50 Wuwanenele wubi wawo wose, hamonga ne cibede cawo ca kuli'gwe, kanyhi wuwanoze walugu wawo wawatendele viswanu.
51 Hambi wene nha wanhu wako wono wawawusa ku yisi ya Misili mwi 'lambi lyo moto.

52 “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, nghukulomba wuwalanje wanhu wako, hamonga no mutemi wawo, ko lusungu, wuhulice no wulombe wawo lono wakukulomba wutazi.
53 Agwe yo gwegwe mono wawahagula ku wulawa muli wanhu wose, ho wawe wanhu wako, nha vyono wawalonjela muli Musa u mwitumami wako, lono wawawusiza i walezi wetu ku Misili.”

Lilombo lya Solomona, lye ndumilizo

54 Nayo Solomona yali yeza kukondya kumulomba Muwaha Mulungu, yakema ha mwande ye cikumbicilo, baho hono yali yafugame akuno yagoloye ne miwoko yakwe.
55 Yakenula lizi lyakwe muno muno, yakawa kuwa lugutiza wanhu wose wono wali wehangulize baho, yakatya,
56 “Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe mono yawapelile wanhu wakwe mulamu, nha vyono yehuwiza yiwe. Nayo kawukamisa wihuwizo wakwe wono yehuwiziza muli Musa u mwitumami wakwe.
57 Muwaha Mulungu wetu yeze yali hamonga nase, na vyono yali yali hamonga ne walezi wetu, yalece kucilecela lukulu, hamba kucisemwa yaye.
58 Yacipele cogopo ca kuli mwene, nhondo zose, ho cikale mu mikalile yono yikumwendeza mwene, kanyhi cibate no wulajilize wakwe, hamonga ne malajilizo gakwe, gono yawapela walezi wetu.
59 Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, yeze kuwukumbucila wulombe wetu, hamonga ne malombo gose gaga gono nimulombile. Nhondo zose, yeze no lusungu kuli wanhu wakwe Waisilayeli na kuli mutemi wawo, muli visakwa vyawo vye nhondo zose.
60 Ne makungugo gose ga kononze ho gomanya nha vyono, Muwaha Mulungu yo Mulungu du yali wene, hasina yunji kanyhi.
61 Holeka nanye mo wanhu wakwe, munojeye mweze wehuwilwa kuli Muwaha Mulungu u Mulungu wenyu, mweze na cihuliko kuli mulajilize wakwe hamonga ne malajilizo gakwe nha vyono munozize zuwali.”

Wusiwanulwe we Nyumba yo Mulungu

(2Zin 7:4-10)

62 Hanyuma mutemi Solomona, hamonga ne wanhu wose wono wali wali baho, wakamulavizya Muwaha Mulungu makumbiko.
63 Yakalavya yitewa lya ng'ombe zificile elufu makumi mejete na nyejete, hamonga na ngholo elufu yigana limonga na makumi mejete, ku so cilavywa co mulamu. Hanyuma nhondo, mutemi hamonga ne wanhu wose, wakayisiwanula Nyumba yo Mulungu.
64 Na mwizuwa lilyo du, yakalisiwanula ne likumbo lya hagati, lyono lyali ha mwande ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakalavya baho ne makumbiko ge cilavywa cinyanywe, ne vilavywa vyo wusaje, ne mhama ye zimitugo so cilavywa co mulamu. Nayo yanoza zizino vyono cikumbicilo ce shaba cali cidodo ku wulavizya vilavywa vivino vyose.

65 Nayo Solomona hamonga ne Waisilayeli wose, wakayisangalalila sikukuu ye vibanda, vilo mupunghati baho ha Nyumba yo Mulungu, kanyhi yakonjeza ne vilo mupunghati vinji, vikawa hamonga vilo yikumu na vine. Ne wanhu wono wali wali baho, wali wanhu wenji muno muno, wanji walawilila kutali muno muno kotya wanya Hamati Pasi, kusumuka, ne Wamisili wulawa ku mupaka wa kutakama.
66 Na mwi'zuwa lyo munana nhondo Solomona ho yakawa kuwakombola wanhu wawuye ku zikwawo. Ne wanhu wose wakamulumba muno Solomona, wakawuya ku zikwawo wena nyemo mbaha, ku soko yo wulugutwe wose wono Muwaha Mulungu yamupela Dawudi mwitumami wakwe, hamonga ne wanhu wakwe wa Isilayeli.


1 Watemi 9

Mulungu yakwilajila kanyhi kuli Solomona

(2Zin 7:11-22)

1 Mutemi Solomona, yali yeza kukondya kuyizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne likulu, sunga na vyose vyono yasakaga yazenje,
2 Muwaha Mulungu yakelajila kuli Solomona lwe kejete, nha vyono yelajila kuli mwene, lono yali yali ku Gibeyoni.
3 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela yakatya, “Nakondya kuwuhulika wulombe wako, kanyhi nakondya kuyisiwanula Nyumba yangu yiyi yono wanzenjela, yiwe ho hanhu hono nowa kuhembelwa cibitila. Nane noyilanjilila no kuyidima vilo vyose.
4 Nagwe hono wunyitumamile kwe cogopo na ko wihuwilwa, nha vyono sogo Dawudi yanoza, na hono wuwukamise wulajilize wangu no kwituma zose nha vyono nakulajiliza,
5 nane nowufisiliza wihuwizo wangu wono namwihuwiziza sogo Dawudi, lono namulonjela vyono, Isilayeli wowa kutemwa ne wanagwe cibitilila.
6 Ninga gwegwe hambe wanago, hono mulece kunhyatya, na hono mulece kuwa na cihuliko kuli wulajilize wangu, hamonga ne malajilizo gangu gono nakupela, hanyuma muhembe milungu yinji,
7 ale, nane nowahamya wanhu wa Isilayeli niwawuse mu yisi yono nawapela. Noyileka sunga ne Nyumba yangu yiyi yono nakondya kuyisiwanula hawe hanhu hangu ho kuhembelwa. Ne wanhu wose hohose wowaseka Isilayeli no kuwatendela ko wubedegazi.
8 Sunga ne Nyumba yiyi yo Mulungu, yopituka yiwe yilundo lya matongo, ne wanhu wose wono wowa kupuluta baho, wowa kwiwuza wakutya, ‘Nhawule Muwaha Mulungu kayinoza nhavivi yisi yiyi ne Nyumba yiyi?’
9 Nawo wanhu wowa kupitula wakutya, ‘Ku soko vyono wamuleka Muwaha Mulungu u Mulungu wawo, mono yawawusa walezi wawo ku yisi ya Misili. Wakayipitucila milungu yinji no kuyitamamila no kuyihemba. Na yiyi yo soko yono yikuleka Muwaha Mulungu kawaletela wongomele wuwu.’ ”

Wilumbanize wa Solomona na Hilamu

(2Zin 8:1-2)

10 Mutemi Solomona yayizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu hamonga ne likulu myaka makumi mejete.
11 Nayo Mutemi Hilamu mutemi we yisi ya Tulo yo yamupela mibici yose yo muhagata, hamonga ne zahabu yose yono yasakaga so mulimo wo wuzenji. Ninga wuzenji wali weza kukondya, mutemi Solomona yakamupela Hilamu makaya gono gali gali mu yisi ya Galilaya.
12 Nayo Hilamu yakabita kugalanga makaya gago, ninga si yagenda yaye.
13 Hodu yakawa kumubitila Solomona yakamutya, “Alu ndugu yangu, aga go makaya mucici gono wukumhela!” Na ku soko yiyo, holeka hanhu baho hakali hakucemwa yisi ya Kabuli sunga nalu.
14 Hilamu yali yakondya kumutegulila Solomona zahabu ya wuzito wono wucizize kilo elufu yine.

Milimo yinji miwaha yono yetuma Solomona

(2Zin 8:3-18)

15 Mutemi Solomona, yewetumya kwe vilungo i wetuma milimo, mu wuzenji we Nyumba yo Mulungu hamonga ne likulu, no kumemya muhangha makombo gono gali gali kucilima ye likaya, hamonga no kuzenga magomawakali ge likaya. Kanyhi yakawetumamila so wuzenga makaya ga Hazoli, Megido hamonga na Gezeli.
16 Ninga mutemi wa Misili, yali yakondya kuyihoma Gezeli no kuyisola, yakawawulaga Wakanani wose i wekazi wa mwi'kaya, yakalipembela likaya no moto, hanyuma, yakawa kumupela mwanagwe we cicekulu likaya lilyo, nha cipegwa ce halusi lono yatolelwa na Solomona.
17 Nayo Solomona ho yakawa kulizenga kanyhi likaya lilyo lya Gezeli. Yaketumamila wanhu wakwe. Solomona yo yazenga ne Beti Holoni yono yili kusika
18 na Balati, na Tamali, mu nyika ya Yuda,
19 i makaya gono yagetumamilaga so wuwika vitumamilwa vyakwe. Go makaya gono yawikaga zifwalasi zakwe, ne matuka go wulugu, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyose vyono yasakaga so wuzenjela mu Yelusalemi, hamba mu Lebanoni, ne hanhu hanji hose du mu wutemi wakwe.
20 I wanhu wono wasigala, i Wakanani, wono Waisilayeli si wawawulaga lono wayisolela yisi yawo,
21 hamonga ne Waamoli, ne Wahiti, ne Wapelizi, ne Wahivi, ne Wayebusi wono wasigala, wakajendelela du kuwa wetumami sunga nalu. Ale wawo wo Solomona yawetumamilaga.
22 Ninga Solomona si yawanoza Waisilayeli wawe wetumami. Langa wene wetumaga mulimo wo wusilikale no kuwa walongola, ne wawaha we wasilikale, ne walongola we matuka go wulugu, hamonga ne wajendela zifwalasi.

23 Hali ho wali wawaha walanjilizi we milimo ya Solomona yu wuzenji, wali waficile magana mahano na makumi mahano, wono wemilizaga milimo hayo.

24 Solomona yakamemya muhangha, na mu makombo gono gali gali kucilima ye likaya, lono mugolece wakwe, u mwana we cicekulu wo mutemi wa Misili, yali yakondya kuhama mu kaya ya Dawudi, no kwinjila mwi'kulu lyono Solomona yamuzenjela. Hobaho ho yayizenjela na Milo.

25 Nayo Solomona yalavyaga nghanghulo yidatu mu mwaka, vilavywa vinyanywe, hamonga ne vilavywa vyo mulamu, mu cikumbicilo cono yamuzenjela Muwaha Mulungu. Yakawa yakufusa no wuvumba ku so Muwaha Mulungu. Nayo yakawa yakondya kuyizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu.

26 Mutemi Solomona, kanyhi yakanoza ngalawa ku Eziyoni-gebeli, yono yili behi na Eloti ha mhwani ya Agaba, mu yisi ya Edomu.
27 Mutemi Hilamu, yakawategula wanhu wakwe kuli Solomona, wono wali wazoweye kujendya ngalawa mwi'lenga, ho waketaze ne wanhu wa Solomona.
28 Wakajenda sunga wakafika ku Ofili, na kuko, wakasola zahabu yono yali yicizize wuzito wa kilo elufu yikumi na yine, wakamuletela Solomona.


1 Watemi 10

U wulamuse wa Sheba u mutemi we cicekulu, kuli Solomona

(2Zin 1:1-12)

1 No mutemi we cicekulu wa Sheba, yali yaza kuhulika zo lukumyo lwa Solomona, yakawa kubita kuli Solomona ku Yelusalemi ho yamujele mu mawuzo ge zinghani zinghamu.
2 Yakaza ku Yelusalemi hamonga ne wetumami wakwe wenji muno, hamonga ne zingamiya zono zidinghuye hati ye zimboga, hamonga ne zahabu nyinji muno no wihambo we vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu. Nayo yali yeza kwitang'anila na Solomona, yakaduwana nayo zose zono yali yena nazo mu nhumbula yakwe.
3 Nayo Solomona yakamupitula zose, na hakawa hasina hamba nghani monga yono yamufutila ku wupitule.
4 Mutemi we cicekulu wa Sheba, yakagawona mahala ga Solomona, hamonga ne likulu lyono yazenga.
5 Yakayiwona ne ndigwa yono Solomona yatenjelagwa ha meza, ne zikaya ze wetumami wakwe, hamonga no wityatye wa zose zono zetumwaga ne wetumami wa mwi'kulu, e vivwalo vyono wavwalaga, ne wetumami wono wamutenjelaga zindigwa mu mapango ge zi sikukuu, hamonga ne makumbiko gono yalavyaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Yali yeza kukondya kuziwona zizo zose, yakezina muno muno ne nhumbula yakwe yikazima.
6 Hanyuma yakawa kumuwuza Solomona yakatya, “Kumbe zose za mu yisi yako, na ze mahala gako, zose za mbeka!
7 Ane si nali kwihuwila yaye, sunga neza kuza no kwiwonelela zose nene no mweneco. Kumbe hamba nhavivyo, nali nghali sinazihulika zose yaye, hamba nusu yayo bwete, hambi mahala gako hamonga no wumoteswe wako, nha vicizize muno kuciza nha vyono nali nonjeywe.
8 Wamoti wagolece zako! Kanyhi wamoti ne wetumami wako, wono wali hamonga nagwe vilo vyose, wakuhulika ze mahala gako!
9 Yalumbwe Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako! Hambi nha yalajila nha vyono yendezwe nagwe, no kukunoza wuwe mutemi muli Isilayeli. Hambi wendo wakwe kuli Isilayeli, nha wa cibitilila, nayo yakakunoza wuwe mutemi wawo, ho wusume kunoza ling'ani ne mbeka.”

10 Mutemi we cicekulu Sheba, yakawa kumupela Solomona vipegwa vyono yamuletela, navyo vyali vicizize kilo elufu yine za zahabu, hamonga no winji we hati ye zimboga, no wihambo we vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu. No winji we hati ye zimboga wono yamupela, wali mwinji muno muno mu nghango yono Solomona yali yakali hamba si nawutuga mwi'zuwa linji lyolyose.

11 (Ne zingalawa za Hilamu zono zategula zahabu wulawa Ofili, nazo zikaleta wulawa Ofili mabici ge minghulwa, hamonga no wihambo we vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu.
12 Solomona yaketumamila mabici gago ge minghulwa yikawa zo sito zo kuwopela lijinji lye luwa ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne likulu. Hanyuma kanyhi, yakayitumamila kunozeza ndonondo ne masumbi so kwaya. Nayo yali yo mibici ye minghulwa miswanu muno, yono yali yikali si naza hamba kamonga ku Isilayeli, hamba hasina kanyhi yono yolokwijela yinha yiyi.)

13 Nayo mutemi Solomona, yakamupela mutemi we cicekulu wa Sheba, vinhu vyose vyono yavisakaga, hamonga na zizo, Solomona yakamupela kuciza na vyono yali yanojeye yamupele. Hanyuma ya zizo, u mutemi we cicekulu wa Sheba, hamonga ne wetumami wakwe, wakawa kuwuya ku yisi yawo ya Sheba.

Wugoli wo mutemi Solomona

(2Zin 9:13-28)

14 Mutemi Solomona myaka yose, yabocelaga kilo za zahabu zono zicizize elufu makumi mejete na yidatu,
15 hamonga ne kodi yono walavyaga wagoli, ne cambuzi cono walavyaga wasuluza, ne kodi yono walavyaga watemi we Calabu, hamonga ne wawaha we zi'si ze Waisilayeli.

16 Solomona yakanoza ngula mbaha muno magana mejete za zahabu ngula monga yikawa nozezwe zahabu ya wuzito wono wucizize kilo mupunghati.
17 Hanyuma kanyhi yakanoza ngula ndodo ndodo zificile magana madatu, za zahabu, ngula monga yikawa nozezwe zahabu ya wuzito wono wucizize kilo nyejete. Ne zingula zizino zose, yakaziwika mu “Nyumba ya mu mbago ya Lebanoni.”

18 Hanyuma kanyhi mutemi, yakanoza ligoda lyo wutemi, lubavu yakalihiliwa na mbali za nhembo, no lubavu lunji, yakalihiliwa na zahabu nengu.
19 Nalyo ligoda lilyo, lyali lina ngazi mutandatu, kanyhi likawa liwiciywe cihwani ciseswe ca simba mu zi mbavu zose ze zingazi akuno na akuno, nazo simba, zali yikumi na nyejete.
20 Na ha mugongo we ligoda lyo wutemi, hali hocili cihwani ciseswe ca mutwe wa ng'ombe. Na mu zimbavu zose vyono nyejete ze miwoko ye ligoda, hali ho cili cihwani ciseswe ca simba. Hakawa hasina hamba ligoda linji kanyhi, lyono lyali lihwanile lilino, mu wutemi wunji wowose.

21 Ne vikombe vyono Solomona yang'welaga, vyali vya zahabu vyose, sunga ne vitumamilwa vyono vyali viwice mu Nyumba ya mu Mbago ya Lebanoni, navyo vyali vya zahabu vyose. Hali hasina ciya co cose ca fweza, cono cetumamilwaga, vyono fweza mu vilo vya Solomona cali nha cinhu cono cisina cambuzi.
22 Hambi mutemi Solomona, nha yali yena ngalawa zono zajendaga mwi'lenga hamonga ne zingalawa za Hilamu. Na mu myaka yose, mu mwaka we kadatu, ngalawa zakwe zawuyaga zidinghuye zahabu, fweza, meno ga nhembo, ne manyani, hamonga ne majedengwa.

23 Nayo mutemi Solomona, yali mugoli muno muno, kanyhi yali munhu we mahala kuciza watemi wayagwe wanji.
24 Sunga ne wanhu wose wa mononze, wendaga kuza wamuhulicize ze mahala gono Mulungu yamupela.
25 No munhu wo wose mono yazaga kumulamusa, yakamuletela hamonga ne vipegwa vye vitumamilwa vya fweza na zahabu, ne vivwalo, ne vitwalo, ne zihati ze zimboga, ne zifwalasi, hamonga ne zinyumbu.

26 Mutemi Solomona yakakonghola matuka go wulugu, gaficile elufu yikumi na yine, hamonga ne wapanda zifwalasi, waficile elufu yikumi na nyejete, yakawawika mu makaya mono yali yagawicile matuka go wulugu. Wanji yakawawika mu Yelusalemi hamonga no mutemi, na wanji yakawawika ako na ako au makaya ganji.
27 Na mu mapango go wutemi wakwe, fweza yejelaga nha mabwe du mu Yelusalemi. Nago mabici go muhagata, gali menji gakehwana ne mabici ge mikuyu, gono gali menji mu mwalambalo we matunda ga Yuda.
28 Ne wakwawi wo mutemi, wagulaga zifwalasi ku wulawa Misili na ku Kilikiya,
29 no kuwaguziza i watemi wose we Wahiti, ne Wasiliya. Wa waguzizaga matuka go wulugu, fweza magana mutandatu kwe'tuka limonga, na fweza yigana limonga na makumi mahano kwa fwalasi monga.


1 Watemi 11

Solomona yakumuleka Mulungu

1 Mutemi Solomona yakawa kwenda wacekulu wenji wono si Wayahudi. Hamonga no kuwa yali yamutolile u mwana we cicekulu wo mutemi wa Misili, ninga yakatola kanyhi ne wacekulu we nghungugo ye Wahiti, na ye Wamowabi na ye Waamoni, na ye Waedomu, na ye Wazidoni.
2 Mwene yawatola du hamba yawe Mulungu yalajiliza nha vyono Waisilayeli walece kwitolanila ne wanhu wawa, hambi nha wowanoza Waisilayeli wahembe milungu yinji.
3 Mutemi Solomona yali yatolile wacekulu magana mupunghati, kanyhi yakawa yena wajitulwa magana madatu. Ne wacekulu wawa, wakamunoza Solomona yamulece Mulungu.
4 Na mu wukombi wakwe, wakamutonga mu wuhemba milungu yinji. Yakawa si mwihuwilwa kuli Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe, yakawa si yenho sogwe Dawudi mono yali mwihuwilwa.
5 Solomona yahembaga Ashitoleti, mulungu mucekulu wa wanya Sidoni, hamonga na Moleki, mulungu we cevu ce Waamoni.
6 Yakamusutizya Muwaha Mulungu, yakawa si mwihuwilwa, yakawa si yenha sogwe Dawudi mono yali mwihuwilwa.
7 Solomona yakazenga hanhu ho kumuhembela mulungu Cemoshi, mu matunda, ku lubavu lwa kucilima lwa Yelusalemi, we cevu ce Waamoni.
8 Kanyhi Solomona yakazenga hanhu henji ho kuhembela, hono i wagolece zakwe wono si Wayahudi wowa kufusiza wuvumba no kulavya makumbiko kuli milungu yawo.

9 Ninga hamba yawe Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli yelajila kejete kose kuli Solomona, no
10 kumukong'onheza yalece kuhembela lukulu milungu yinji, ninga Solomona si yamuhulika Muwaha Mulungu, mwene yakamuleka Mulungu. Holeka Muwaha Mulungu nayo yakamukolelwa likuwo Solomona,
11 yakamutya, “Alu vyono wakondya kucibena ne nyono cilagane cangu nagwe, wagabedegala ne malajilizo gangu, nane nghwihuwiza nha vyono nokuwusiza wutemi, nimupele yumonga wa muli wetumami wako.
12 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, ku soko yo sogo Dawudi, si nosuma kuzinoza zizi mu vilo vyako lono wukali mumi, langa mu cipango co wutemi wo mwanago.
13 Nane si nowuwusa lukulu wutemi wose kuli mwene, langa nomusigaziza mwene likungugo limonga, ku soko ya Dawudi mwitumami wangu, na ku soko ye likaya lya Yelusalemi lyono nalihagula liwe lyangu.”

I walugu wa Solomona

14 Hobaho, Muwaha Mulungu yakamunoza Hadadi wo lulelo lo wutemi wa Edomu, yamuhome Solomona.
15 Baho katali, lono zizi zikali, lono Dawudi yayisumila Edomu, Yowabu u muwaha we wasilikale wakwe wo wulugu, yabita kuko yakazika wafu.
16 Kanyhi mwene Yowabu hamonga ne wanhu, wakekala kuko ku Edomu myezi mutandatu, na mu vilo vivyo vyono yekalila kuko, yawawulaga i wanhu wose we cilume mumo mu Edomu,
17 ninga Hadadi hamonga ne wetumami wo sogwe, i Waedomu watiziza ku Misili. (Na mwidaha lilyo, Hadadi yali yakali mudodo muno muno.)
18 Wakawuka ku Midiyani, wakabita ku Palani, ne wanhu wanji wanya Palani wakelunza nawo. Wakawasoleleza ne wanhu wawo, wakajendelela sunga wakafika ku Misili ko mutemi Falawo. Wakamubitila u mutemi, nayo mutemi Falawo, yakamupela Hadadi ciwanza, hamonga ne kaya yo kwikala, yakawa kumulisa ne zindigwa.
19 Nayo Hadadi vikehulika muno muno no mutemi Falawo, nayo mutemi Falawo yakamupela Hadadi u mulamuwe we cicekulu, u mwanakwawe wo mugolece wakwe Tapenesi ho yamutole.
20 Nayo yuyo u mwanakwawe wa Tapenesi yakamulelela Hadadi mwana we cilume, yakacemwa litagwa, Genubati. U mwana yuyu yakawilusizwa baho mwi'kulu no mugolece wo mutemi Falawo, yakawa vikalane ne wana we cilume wo mutemi Falawo.

21 Nayo Hadadi lono yali yali ku Misili, yakahulika nha vyono Dawudi yakondya kufwa, na vyono na Yowabu u muwaha we wasilikale, nayo yakondya kufwa. Hodu Hadadi yakamulonjela mutemi Falawo yakatya, “Alu nghukunena wunghombole mbuye ku yisi yangu.”

22 Mutemi Falawo yakamuwuza yakutya, “Ku sokoci? Alo vyono ane nalemwa kukupela visakwa vyako, holeka alu weza kusaka kuwuya kwenyu?”

Hadadi nayo, yakamupitula mutemi Falawo yakatya, “Ane si nyanjiye cinji, langa nghukunena wunghombolaje mbite.” Hodu Hadadi yakawa kuwuya ku yisi yakwe. Alu yikawa, nha vyono mutemi wa Edomu yali yawasulile Waisilayeli, ale nhavivyo sunga na Hadadi, yali munhu mubi muno, yakawa mulugu muwaha Waisilayeli.

23 Mulungu kanyhi yakamunoza Lezoni u mwana wa Eliyada, nayo yawe mulugu wa Solomona. Yuyo Lezoni, yatiza ko muwaha wakwe u mutemi Hadadezeli wa Zoba,
24 yakawa mulongola we cipuga ce wanhu wagaluci. (Na zizi zejela lono Dawudi yamusumila Hadadezeli, yakawongomeza ne wanhu wa ku Siliya, wono wali vilunzize nayo.) Nayo Lezoni hamonga ne wanhu wakwe, wakabita kwikala ku Damesiki, i hanhu hono wanhu wakwe wakamunoza yawe mutemi wa Siliya.
25 Nayo Lezoni yakawa mulugu muwaha we Waisilayeli mwipango lyose lyono Solomona yali yakali mumi.

Wihuwizo wo Mulungu kuli Yelobowamu

26 Munhu yunji kanyhi, mono yamugaluka Solomona, yali Yelobowamu mwana wa Nebati, Mwefulayimu munya Seleda, mono yali muyagwe ne wetumami wakwe, u nyina yacemwaga zeluya.
27 Ne nghani yiyi yali nghani 'mbi muno muno. Ne nghani yono yaleka Yelobowamu yakumugaluka Solomona yali yoyi.

Lono Solomona yamemyaga muhangha mu makombo ku lubavu lwa kucilima ye likaya lya Yelusalemi ku Milo, no kunozeleza majinji ge likaya lyo sogwe Dawudi.
28 Nayo Yelobowamu lulo, yali muzelelo we vilungo viwaha. Nayo Solomona yali yeza kumuwona vyono yetumaga milimo ko wuhidimbazi, hodu yakawa kumuwika yawe mulanjilizi we wetuma milimo wose, mu yisi ye zimbeyu za ya Manase na Efulayimu.
29 Na mwi'zuwa linji, lono Yelobowamu yali yali mu muhinzo, yakulawa ku Yelusalemi, viketang'anila yali wene no muwizi Ahiya mu nzila, yakulawa ku Shilo.
30 Hobaho Ahiya, yakaciwopola civwalo cipya cono yali yavwalile, yakacidemulanga vitema yikumi na vyejete,
31 yakamutya Yelobowamu, “Ale, wusole vitema yikumi viwe vyako, hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli nha yakukutya, ‘Ane nowuwusa wutemi kuli Solomona, nokupela agwe we milango yikumi.
32 Nayo Solomona, kosigala na wo mulango monga du, ku soko ya Dawudi u mwitumami wangu, na ku soko ya Yelusalemi, likaya lyono nalihagula liwe lyangu mu wulawa mu yisi yose ya Isilayeli.
33 Nane nghwituma zizi, vyono Solomona yakondya kunema 'ne, alu yakuhemba milungu yinji, ye Asitalite, u mulungu mucekulu wa wanya Sidoni, hamonga no mulungu Cemoshi, u mulungu we Wamowabi, na Moleki u mulungu we Waamoni. Alu Solomona yakondya kungaluka, yetuma vibi, kanyhi hamba si yawibate no wulajilize wangu, hamonga ne malajilizo gangu, nha vyono u sogwe Dawudi yali yagebate.
34 Ninga si nomuwusiza Solomona wutemi wose ng'o, langa nomuleka yajendelele kuwa mutemi, mu vilo vyose vyo wikale wakwe. Na zizi nozituma ku soko yo mwitumami wangu Dawudi mono namuhagula, nayo yakawa munhu mono yali yebate wulajilize wangu hamonga ne malajilizo gangu.
35 Nane nowusola wutemi mu muwoko wo mwana wa Solomona, nghupele agwe milango yikumi.
36 Langa nomulecela wo mulango monga du u mwana wa Solomona. Nane nhondo zose, ho niwe nina mwana wo mwitumami wangu Dawudi, mono kowa yatemile mwi'kaya lya Yelusalemi, likaya lyono nalihagula hawe ho hanhu hono nowa kuhembelwa.
37 Nagwe Yelobowamu, nokunoza wuwe mutemi wa Isilayeli, nagwe kotema mu yisi yose yono kohila gwegwe.
38 Hono wuhulice 'ne ko mutima wako wose, kanyhi hono wugakamise ne malajilizo gangu, witume nha vyono nowa kukulajiliza witume, nha vyono yanozaga mwitumami wangu Dawudi, ale, nane nowa nili hamonga nagwe vilo vyose. Nokunoza wuwe mutemi we Waisilayeli. Nane nolanjilila sunga mbone, vyono ne wanago wakuwuhala wutemi wako, nha vyono netuma kuli Dawudi.
39 Langa nowalipiciza wo lulelo lwa Dawudi ku soko yo wubi wa Solomona, ninga si nowalipiciza vilo vyose yaye.’ ”

40 Na ku soko yiyo Solomona yakajela yamuwulaje Yelobowamu, ninga Yelobowamu yakatiziza kuli mutemi Shishaki wa ku Misili, yakekala kuko sunga Solomona yakafwa.

Wufwe wa Solomona

(2Zin 9:29-31)

41 Ne zinghani zose zono Solomona yetuma, ne mikalile yakwe, hamonga ne mahala gakwe, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce Zinghomo ze Metumo ga Solomona.
42 Solomona yali mutemi mu Yelusalemi mu Waisilayeli yose, myaka makumi mane.
43 Nayo yakafwa, yakazikwa mu kaya ya Dawudi. Nayo Lehobowamu u mwanagwe, yo yakahala wutemi wo sogwe Solomona.


1 Watemi 12

We milango ya ku lubavu lwa kusumuka wakugaluka

(2Zin 10:1-19)

1 Nayo Lehobowamu u mwana wa Salomona yakabita ku Shekemu, kono wali wehangulizize i wanhu wose wa Isilayeli, wa ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, ho wamutemye yawe mutemi.
2 Ninga nayo Yelobowamu u mwana wa Nebati, mono yali yali ku Misili so kumutiza Solomona, yakazihulika zizo.
3 Nawo i wanhu wose wa ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, wakatuma wanhu wabite ku Misili wakamuceme Yelobowamu. Wakawa kubitana wose hamonga na Yelobowamu kuli Lehobowamu, wakamutya,
4 “U sogo Solomona yacitumyaga vibi muno muno, no kucipela mutegulo muzito. Holeka agwe, hono wucepule mutegulo wuwo, no kucitumya viswanu, nase cokwitumamila.”

5 Lehobowamu yakawapitula yakatya, “Ale, nhondo muze kolo vyapuluta vilo vidatu, nane ho nomupenyi lipitulo.” Hodu nawo wakawa kwiyagala.
6 Mutemi Lehobowamu yakawawuza i wanyamhala wono wamutazaga u sogwe Solomona mu zinghani lono yali yakali mumi, yakawatya, “Ale, ta, munhazaje lipitulo lyono nowapitula wanhu wawa.”

7 Nawo wanyamhala wakamupitula wakatya, “Agwe hono wusace wuwetumamile wanhu wawa viswanu, ale, weze kuwapela mapitulo gono gowakwila mu visakwa vyawo, holeka nawo wojendelela nhondo zose kukuhulika.”

8 Ninga mwene Lehobowamu, yakazibedegala zinghani ze wanyamhala, yakawa kuwawuza wazika wayagwe, wono luno wali wo watazi wakwe mu zinghani, yakawatya,
9 “Alu wulenyi! Nanye mukutya wule? Mbite nghawapitule nhawule wanhu, vyono wene wakuhila niwacepulile mutegulo?”

10 Nawo wazelelo, i wazika wayagwe wakamupitula wakamutya, “Alu nagwe wubite wukawalonjele wutye nhavi, ‘Cidole nene cangu ciwaha, cicizize ciwino ca baba Solomona.’
11 Holeka hono yawe baba yamudinghuzajenyi mutegulo muzito, ane nowonjeleza mutegulo wenyu, na hono baba yali kumukunenyi na mukotya, ane nomukunenyi muno muno no mujeledi.”

12 Vyali vyeza kupuluta vilo vidatu, Yelobowamu hamonga ne wanhu wose wa Isilayeli, wakamubitila kanyhi mutemi Lehobowamu, nha vyono yali yawalonjeye.
13 Ninga mutemi Lehobowamu vyono yazibedegala zinghani ze wanyamhala, yakawa kulongana ne wanhu ko wukali muno muno, yakagatyatya mahweso ge wazika wayagwe, yakawalonjela wanhu yakutya,
14 “Baba Solomona yamudinghuzajenyi mutegulo muzito, ninga ane nowonjeleza mutegulo wenyu wuwe muzito muno muno. Mwene yali kumukunanjenyi na mukotya, ninga ane nomukunenyi muno muno no mujeledi!”
15 Na wuwo wali wo wuhile wo Muwaha Mulungu ziwe nhavivyo, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulonjela Yelobowamu, mu nzila yo muwizi Ahiya wa Shilo. Na yiyi yo yaleka mutemi Lehobowamu si yasaka yazihulicize ze wanhu.

16 Nawo wanhu waza kuwona vyono mutemi Lehobowamu hamba si yakuwahuliciza yaye, hodu, wakazozeza wose hamonga wakutya,

“Mwene nye, ase cina yigawo ne mhuga ya Dawudi!

Hamba wuhazici wono cina muli mwana wa Yese?

Nye nyo wanhu wa Isilayeli,

alenyi ciyagale ciwuye ku zikaya zetu.

Mulece kumutyatya kanyhi Lehobowamu mumulekaje yali wene!”

Hodu hobaho i wanhu wa Isilayeli wakawa kumugaluka mutemi Lehobowamu.
17 Wakawa kumuleka mutemi Lehobowamu yawe mutemi kwe wanhu wono wekalaga mu yisi ya Yuda du.

18 Hanyuma mutemi Lehobowamu, yakamutuma Adonilamu, mono yali yo mulanjilizi muwaha wa wetuma milimo wose, yabite kuli Waisilayeli, ninga Adonilamu yali yeza kufika kuko, Waisilayeli wakamutowanga ne mabwe sunga yakafwa. Na ku soko ya zizo, hodu Lehobowamu yakawa kupanda musunho mwi'tuka lyakwe lyo wulugu, yakawa kutiziza ku Yelusalemi.
19 Na ku wulawa mwizuwa lilyo, i wanhu wa Isilayeli wo wutemi wa ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, wakawa kuyigaluka nyumba ya Dawudi sunga nalu.

20 Nawo i wanhu wa Isilayeli waza kuhulika nha vyono Yelobowamu yakondya kuwuya ku wulawa Misili, wakawa kumulalika yaze ku wihangulize wawo wo mukung'ano, nawo wakawa kumunoza yawe mutemi wa Isilayeli. Langa wo mulango ya Yuda du, wo wasigala, wakayityatya nyumba ya Dawudi wali wene.

U wuwizi wa Shemaya

(2Zin 11:1-4)

21 Nayo Lehobowamu yali yeza kufika ku Yelusalemi, yakawakonghola wasilikale vitungh'ati mu wulugu, waficile elufu yigana limonga na makumi munana, mu wulawa mu milango ya Yuda na Benyamini. Yakawa yena wuhile yabite vikehome na we milango ya Isilayeli wono wali wali ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, ho yawugomole kanyhi wutemi wakwe.
22 Ninga Mulungu yakamulonjela muwizi Shemaya,
23 yabite yakamulonjele Lehobowamu, hamonga ne wanhu wose we zimbeyu za Yuda na Benyamini, yakawatye,
24 “Mulecele lukulu kubita muwahome wandugu zenyu, i wanhu wa Isilayeli. Holeka muwuye wose ku zikaya zenyu. Hambi zizi zose zono zijeye, nha wo wuhile wangu nene.” Nawo wakawa na cihuliko kuli nghani yiyo yo Muwaha Mulungu, hodu wakeyagala, wakawuya ku zikwawo.

Yelobowamu yakumuleka Muwaha Mulungu

25 Mutemi Yelobowamu, wa Isilayeli yakazenga likaya lya Shekemu ya mu yisi ya mu matunda ya Efulayimu, yakekala kuko cipango. Hanyuma kanyhi yakahama kuko, yakazenga likaya lya Penuweli.
26 Yakawa kwihwesa mu nhumbula yakwe yakutya, “Alu nha vyono yinha,
27 hono niwaleceleze wanhu wangu, wawe kubita ku Yelusalemi kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu makumbiko ku Nyumba yo Mulungu kuko, wopituka wamuwuyile Lehobowamu mutemi wa Yuda, hanyuma ane wambulaje.”

28 Nayo yali yeza kwihwesa nhavivyo, hodu yakanoza vihwani vya ndama nyejete za zahabu, yakawalonjela wanhu wakwe yakutya, “Mwali kwikataza no muhinzo mutali muno wo kubita ku Yelusalemi, ku wuhembi. Ninga alu mulanje, 'nye nyo wanhu wa Isilayeli, ayi yo milungu yenyu yono yamuwusenyi ku Misili!”
29 Nayo yakawika cihwani ca ndama monga ya zahabu ku Beteli, ne cihwani ce ndama nzagwe naco, yakaciwika ku Dani.
30 Ne nghani yiyi, yikawenjiza wanhu mu wubi. Hambi wanhu nha wabitaga kuhemba hali cihwani cimonga ca muli zindama zizo, na wanji wabitaga ku Beteli, na wanji ku Dani.
31 Yelobowamu yakazenga nhavivilya wuhembelo mu vigongo mucanyha. Yakawawika ne wakohani wanhu caka du, wono si wali wo mulango wa Lawi.

Wuhembi wa ku Beteli wukulemezwa

32 Yelobowamu u mwana wa Nebali, yakanoza sikukuu ye cidini mwi'zuwa lye likumi ne 'hano lya mu mwezi wo munana, nha vyono yinha lizuwa lye sikukuu yono yili ku Yuda. Yelobowamu, yakavilavizya makumbiko mu vigongo ku Beteli i vihwani vye zindama vyono yayinoza, yakawika ne wakohani kuko ku Beteli wawe kwituma mulimo wo kulongoza wuhembi mu hanhu hono yahazenga.
33 Na mwi'zuwa lye kumi ne hano, lya mu mwezi wo munana, lizuwa lyono mweneco yaliwaga, ale, yakabita ku Beteli, yakawa mweneco yakulavya likumbiko mu cikumbicilo, so wunoza sikukuu yono mweneco yayiwaga so wanhu wa Isilayeli.


1 Watemi 13

1 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakalajiliza muwizi ku wulawa ku Yuda, yakabita ku Beteli. Muwizi yakafika ku Beteli mwi'daha lono Yelobowamu yali yemile ha cikumbicilo yafuse wuvumba.
2 Hobaho muwizi yakalonga ku so lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, yakatya, “Gwe go cikumbicilo, go cikumbicilo, Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, ‘Kololelwa mwana we cilume mu nyumba ya Dawudi, litagwa lyakwe Yosiya.’ Na yuyo Yosiya, kolowawulajila muli gwegwe i wakohani wose wono wakwituma mu vigongo, mu makumbicilo ge milungu yo wulambi, wono wakulavya ne makumbiko muli gwegwe, ne mafupa gawo kologakongojela muli gwegwe.”
3 Nayo muwizi yakamulonjela kanyhi Yelobowamu yakatya, “Ne citanjilo cayo cocino, i cikumbicilo cici, copanduka cilawe hejete, ne mavu gono gali mumo gomwajika geyagale caka. Nagwe baho ho komanyila nha vyono, mbeka Muwaha Mulungu kalonjela muli'ne.”

4 Nayo mutemi Yelobowamu yali yeza kuzihulika zizi, hodu yakagolola muwoko wakwe, yakamusonha ne cidole, yakatya, “Ale, mwibatenyi munhu yuyo!” Hobaho baho u muwoko wo mutemi wukanyalilila vyo wugoloce du, hamba si yasuma kuwugomola kanyhi yaye.
5 Cikumbicilo cikapanduka nhinicizo cikalawa hejete, ne mavu gayo gakamwajika hasi. Yikawa nha vyono muwizi yalonjela mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu.
6 U mutemi Yelobowamu yakamulonjela muwizi, yakatya, “Dedede wunombele kuli Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako, yamhonye muwoko wangu!”

Nayo muwizi yakawa kumulomba Muwaha Mulungu, no muwoko wo mutemi wukawa kunoga.
7 Hanyuma mutemi Yelobowamu yakamulonjela muwizi yakatya, “Alu cibitane nane ku kaya yangu, wukatuje ndigwa wulye, kanyhi nghupele ne cihumbilizwa ku soko ya zono wumhendeye.”

8 Ninga muwizi yakamupitula yakatya, “Ane, hamba wumhele nusu ye sawo yako, hamba si cobitana nagwe, hamba si nolya, hamba kung'wa nagwe co cose.
9 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha kandajiliza vyono ndecele lukulu kulya, hamba kung'wa co cose, hamba kuwuyila nzila yono nizize.”
10 Holeka hamba si yawuyila kanyhi nzila yono yaziza, langa yakawuyila nzila yinji.

U muwizi mukombi munya Beteli

11 Na mu mapango gago, kwali na muwizi yunji mukombi, mono yekalaga baho ha Beteli. I wana wo muwizi yuyo mukombi, wakaza wakamulonjela sogo yawo, zose zono muwizi mono yalawile ku Yuda yetumile mu Beteli mwi'zuwa lilyo, na zono yalonjile kuli mutemi Yelobowamu.
12 Nayo u muwizi mukombi, yakawawuza wanagwe yakatya, “Alu kowa kakolela nzilaci lono yatuluciye?” Nawo wakamulajila nzila.
13 Hobaho yakawalonjela wamuwikanizize ndogowe yakwe, nawo wakanoza nha vyono yawalonjela.
14 Yakapanda mu ndogowe, yakawa kutuluka yatyatizye nzila, yakamulonda yu yulya muwizi mono yalawile ku Yuda, yakamwagana yacehile mu mubeho hasi yo mubici wo munghola. Yakawa kumuwuza yakatya, “Yo gwegwe go muwizi mono wulawile ku Yuda?” Nayo yakatya, “Heya yo nene.”

15 U muwizi mukombi yakamutya, “Ale cibitane ku kaya yangu cikalizane zindigwa.”

16 Ninga u muwizi mono yakalawa ku Yuda yakapitula yakatya, “Ane hamba si nosuma kubitana nagwe ku kaya yako, hamba kulizana nagwe ndigwa, hamba kung'wa co cose hamonga nagwe bahano.
17 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha kandajiliza vyono, ndecele lukulu kulya, hamba kung'wa co cose, hamba kuwuyila nzila yono nizize.”

18 Nayo u muwizi mukombi munya Beteli yakamupitula yakatya, “Gwe muyangu, ane nane nha nili muwuzi cinya gwegwe, na ko wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu, malayika kanonjela nize nghusole cibitane ku kaya yangu, nghakupele ndigwa wulye.” Ninga hamba nhavivyo, yuyo muwizi mukombi yalambaga du, hamba si zali za mbeka yaye.

19 Hobaho, u muwizi mono yakalawa ku Yuda, yakawa kubitana no muwizi mukombi ku kaya yakwe, wakalizana zindigwa.
20 Nawo lono wali wakali wekaye ha meza, i nghani yo Muwaha Mulungu yikamuziza u muwizi mukombi,
21 yakamulonjela kwe lizi yiwaha u muwizi mono yalawa ku Yuda yakumutya, “Muwaha Mulungu yakukutya nhavi, alu wakondya kumubedegala, hamba si witumile nha vyono yakulajilize.
22 Agwe kuwuya ne nyuma, kuwa kulya ndigwa i hanhu hono yali yakondya kukulajiliza nha vyono wulecele lukulu kuko kulya. Holeka ku soko yiyo, alu kowulagwa, no muwili wako, si kozikwa mu vitenjelo vya we mhuga yako yaye.”

23 Nawo wali weza kukondya kulya, u muwizi mukombi yakamuwikaniziza ndogowe, u muwizi mono yalawa ku Yuda.
24 Nayo yakayipanda, hodu yakawa kutuluka. Na mumo mu nzila, viketang'anila na simba yikamwibata yikamuwulaga. No muwili wakwe wukekala wugwiye baho mu nzila. Ninga ndogowe hamonga ne simba, vikawa vimile baho du ha mhelo viwulanjile muwili wo muwizi.
25 Nawo wanhu wanji wapulutaga baho, wakawuwona muwili wo muwizi wugwiye mu nzila, ne lisimba nalyo limile behi baho. Wanhu wawo wakawa kubita ku Beteli, wakalolosa zose zono waziwene mu nzila.

26 Nayo u muwizi mukombi yaza kuzihulika zizi, yakatya, “Ayo yo muwizi mono yalibedegala lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu! Holeka Muwaha Mulungu kamutumila lisimba limuwulaje, nha vyon Muwaha Mulungu yali yatemizye.”

27 Hobaho u muwizi mukombi, yakawa kuwalonjela wanagwe yakawatya, “Ale, mumbikanizizaje ndogowe yangu.” Nawo wakanoza nha vyono yawatuma.
28 Nayo yakatuluka yali mucanyha ye ndogowe, yakawagana muwili wo muwizi mono yakalawa ku Yuda, wuli mu nzila ne ndogowe hamonga ne simba, navyo vyo vimile baho du ha mhelo. Nalyo lisimba hamba si lyawulya u muwili wo muwizi, hamba kuyilya ndogowe yaye.
29 Hobaho, u muwizi mukombi yakawusola muwili yakawupacila mu ndogowe, yakawugomola ku Beteli ho wakawuzice, no kumulilila.
30 Yakawuzika mu citenjelo cakwe ca we mhuga yakwe. U mwene hamonga ne wanagwe wakamulilila, wakutya, “O 'gwe ndugu yangu, go ndugu yangu!”
31 Nawo waza kukondya kumuzika, u muwizi mukombi yakawalonjela wanagwe yakawatya, “Anye wanangu, nane kolo nafwa, munzikaje nane mu citenjelo cicino du, muwuzice muwili wangu viwe vihegame no muwili wakwe.
32 Hambi zinghani zono yazilonjela mwi'lajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu kuli cikumbicilo ca ku Beteli, na kuli ze hanhu hose ho wuhembelo wa mu vigongo mu makaya ga Samaliya, mbeka mbeka zoficilicila.”

U wubi wo wiyongomeze wo mutemi Yelobowamu

33 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, u mutemi Yelobowamu u mutemi wa Isilayeli, hamba si yasuma kuzileka zi nzila zakwe zimbi, langa mwene ho yakacila kuwawika wakohani wanhu we caka, wono walawaga mu zimhuga zono si zo mulango wa Lawi, ho wetume mulimo mumo mu vigongo. Yakesolela munhu wo wose du mono mwene yamusakaga hodu kawa kumunoza kawa mukohani.
34 No wubi wuwu, ku lubavu lwakwe yakeletela wuhodanjice no wongomele hamonga no kwiwembula ha meso ge yisi.


1 Watemi 14

U wufwe wo mwana wa Yelobowamu

1 Na mu mapango gago, Abiya u mwana wa Yelobowamu yakawa yatamwa.
2 Nayo Yelobowamu yakamulonjela mugolece wakwe yakamutya, “Alu winozaje wuwe nha wuli yunji, wanhu ho walece kukumanya, wubite ku Shilo kono kekalaga muwizi Ahiya, mono yanonjela vyono ane nowa mutemi wa Isilayeli.
3 Wumutegulilaje mikate yikumi na keki nyhefu, hamonga ne njilo yo wuci. Wubite wukamuwuze cono comwijelela mwana wetu Abiya, nayo kokulonjela.”

4 Hodu yuyo mugolece wakwe, yakawa kumubitila Ahiya ku Shilo. Nayo Ahiya yali yakondya kubofula so wukombi wakwe.
5 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yali yakondya kumulonjela nha vyono, u mugolece wa Yelobowamu yakuza kumuwona, ku soko yo mwanagwe mono yatamigwe. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Ahiya zono kolonga.

Nayo mugolece wa Yelobowamu yali yeza kufika, yakawa yenozize nha munhu yunji.
6 Ninga Ahiya yali yeza kuhulika sesele ha mulango, yakamanya vyono yo mweneco yaza, holeka yakamutya, “Wuhelele. Ane manyile vyono yo gwegwe mugolece wa Yelobowamu, alu nhawule wukwinoza nha wuli munhu yunji? Ninga ane nina nghani kuli'gwe yono si yokwendeza yaye.
7 Wubite wukamuloloseze Yelobowamu nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli yakumutya nhavi, ‘Ane nakuhagula muli wanhu, nghakunoza wuwe mutemi muli wanhu wangu Waisilayeli.
8 Nghawuwusa wutemi kuli lulelo lwa Dawudi, nghawa kukupela agwe. Ninga nagwe si wasaka wihwane nho mwitumami wangu Dawudi, mono yali yahulicile muno muno, kanyhi yetuma zo wuhile wangu.
9 Ninga agwe wakondya kwituma wubi kuciza wose wono wakulongolela kutema. Agwe wakondya kunema'ne, kanyhi wakondya kulisagala likuwo lyangu so wunoza vihwani viseswe, hamonga ne vihwani vyenjedulwe, no kvuihemba.
10 Holeka nane ku soko yiyo, nokuletela wongomele munyumba yako, nowawulaga we cilume wose wa mu nyumba yako, i wana wadodo, hamonga ne wanhu mututu wose. Nane nowaleka wa mu mhuga yako wafwagulwe nhe matakataka.
11 No munhu wo wose wa mu mhuga yako mono kofwila mwi'kaya, muwili wakwe woligwa ne mabwa, nayo mono kofwila mwi'hala, muwili wakwe woligwa ne zi'ndi. Nene no Muwaha Mulungu vyo nakondya kutya.’ ”

12 Ahiya yakajendelela kumulonjela mugolece wa Yelobowamu, yakutya, “Alu mudala, wuwuye ku kaya, ninga vyono wukulikanyaga likaya du, baho baho no mwanago kofwa.
13 Ne wanhu wa Isilayeli wose womuzika no kumulilila. Nayo kowa yo yuyo yumonga du wa mu mhuga ya Yelobowamu mono kozikwa, hambi nha yo yuyo du mono u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli yendezwe nayo.
14 U Muwaha Mulungu komwinulila mutemi yunji muli Waisilayeli mono kowudumizya lukulu wutemi wa Yelobowamu.
15 Muwaha Mulungu kolowalipiciza Waisilayeli, kolowasinjisa nha vyono cikolongo cisinjisaga madete mu'gati yaco. Kowawusa Waisilayeli mu yisi yiyi swanu muno, yono yawapela walezi wawo. Nayo koloweyagaziza ku nhumbi ye likolongo lya Efulata, hambi nha walisagala likuwo lyakwe, so wusesa vihwani vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela.
16 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu kolowaleka Waisilayeli vyono Yelobowamu yetuma wubi no kuwanoza Waisilayeli wetume wubi nhavivyo.”

17 U mugolece wa Yelobowamu yakawa kutuluka, yakafika ku Tiliza. Ninga yali yeza kufika du ha mulango, nayo mwana yakawa kutajika.
18 I wanhu wa Isilayeli wakamuzika, kanyhi wakamulilila muno, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalonjela muli mwitumami wakwe, u muwizi Ahiya.

U wugwe wa Yelobowamu

19 Ne zinghani zose zo mutemi Yelobowamu, zinghani zo wulugu wono yehomaga, na zo wuteme wakwe, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo go mutemi Yelobowamu wa Isilayeli.
20 Nayo Yelobowamu yatema myaka makumi mejete na myejete. Yakafwa, yakazikwa. No mwanagwe mono yacemwaga Nadabu yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.

U mutemi Lehobowamu wa Yuda

(2Zin 11:5–12:5)

21 Lehobowamu u mwana wa Solomona, yali yaficila myaka makumi mane na monga, lono yatemela mu Yuda. Nayo yakatema myaka yikumi na mupunghati mu Yelusalemi, mwi' kaya lyono Muwaha Mulungu yalihagula mu wulawa mu yisi yose ya Isilayeli, hawe ho hanhu hakwe hono kowa kuhembelwa. U nyina wa Lehobowamu yacemwaga Nama, munya Amoni.

22 I wanhu wa Yuda nawo wakamusutizya Muwaha Mulungu, wakamusagahiza Mulungu likuwo lyakwe, yakawakolelwa likuwo muno ku soko yowubi wono wetuma kuciza ne walezi wawo.
23 Hambi nha wazenga hanhu ho kuhembela milungu yo wulambi, wakasimya masumbili ga mabwe, hamonga ne cihwani co mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, no kucihemba mumo mu vigongo vitali, na mu mibeho hasi ye mibici.
24 Ne nghani yono yali 'mbi muno, hakawa ho wali walume wono wagonaga ne walume wayawo, hamonga ne wacekulu wasenyha, wono wetumamaga mu wuhembelo we milungu yo wulambi. I wanhu wa Yuda waketuma makani ga cevu muno, gono wetumaga i wanhu wono Muwaha Mulungu yawawinga mu yisi, lono Waisilayeli wenjilaga mu yisi yiyo.

25 Na mu mwaka we hano wo wutemi wa Lehobowamu, Shishaki u mutemi wa Misili, yakayihoma Yelusalemi.
26 Yakayisola sawo yose ya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne sawo yose ya mwi'kulu, hamonga ne zingula zose ze zahabu zono Solomona yazinoza.
27 U mutemi Lehobowamu, yakanoza ngula zinji za shaba yakahaliza. Yakawalajiliza wetumami wakwe wawaha, walindilize milango ye likulu.
28 Na mu mapango gose gono mutemi yenjilaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, i walindilizi nawo wazidinghulaga zingula, na hanyuma kanyhi wawa kuzigomola mu cinyumba co kuzikamisiza.

29 Ne zinghani zose zono mutemi Lehobowamu yetuma, zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ga watemi wa Yuda.
30 Na mu mapango gose gago, Lehobowamu na Yelobowamu, wekalaga wakwihoma du.
31 Lehobowamu yakafwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye citemi, mu kaya ya Dawudi. Na Abiya u mwanagwe yakahala wutemi wakwe.


1 Watemi 15

U mutemi Abiya wa Yuda

(2Zin 13:1-14)

1 Na mu mwaka we likumi na munana wa Yelobowamu u mwana wa Nebati u wutemi wa Isilayeli, baho nayo Abiya ho yatemela mu Yuda.
2 Abiya yatema myaka midatu ku Yelusalemi. U nyina wa Abiya yacemwaga Maka u mwana we cicekulu wa Abusalomu.
3 Ninga nayo Abiya yaketuma wubi wu wulya wose nha vyono yetuma sogwe Lehobowamu, hamba si yamuhulika Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe, nha vyono kukuye Dawudi yali yamuhulice.
4 Ninga du ku soko ya Dawudi, u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe, yakamupela mwana wa cilume yuyo mono yakacemwa Abiya, ho yahale wutemi wakwe mu Yelusalemi, no kuyidima yikale yipolile.
5 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yetuma zizo, vyono Dawudi yetuma zono zamwendezaga Mulungu, kanyhi yakagakamisa muno ne malajilizo gakwe gose. Langa nghani monga du yono yasutya, yo nghani ya Uliya Muhiti.
6 Nawo wulugu wono wali wakondya kwanza hagati ya Lehobowamu na Yelobowamu, wukajendelela sunga na mu vilo vyose vyo wikale wa Abiya.
7 Ne zinghani zose zono mutemi Abiya yetuma zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Yuda.

8 Abiya yakafwa, yakazikwa mu kaya ya Dawudi. No mwanagwe mono yacemwaga Asa, yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Asa mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 15:16–16:6)

9 Mu mwaka we makumi mejete wo mutemi wa Isilayeli Yelobowamu, Asa nayo lo yatemela mu Yuda.
10 Nayo Asa yatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi mane na monga. U mamaye wa Asa u mulelo sogwe, yacemwaga Maka u mwana we cicekulu wa Abusalomu.
11 Yuyo Asa, yaketuma ziswanu zono zikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu nha vyono yetuma kukuye Dawudi, u mulelo kukuye Abusalomu.
12 Yakawawinga mu yisi i walume nono wagonaga walume wayawo, hamonga ne wecekulu wasenyha, wono wetumaga mu wuhembelo we milungu yo wulambi. Yakavitulanga ne vihwani vyose vye milungu, vyono wavinoza i watemi wayagwe, wono wamulongolela.
13 Yakamuwusa mamaye Maka mwi'lumbo lyakwe lyo kuwa nyina we likulu, ku soko yo yanoza cihwani co mulungu we cicekulu Ashela. Asa yakaciwusa cihwani cico, yakacikongoga mu moto ha cikolongo ca Kidiloni.
14 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, Asa si yahahodanga i hanhu hose ha wakumbicilo we milungu yo wulambi mu vigongo yaye. Langa mwene yekala mwihuwilwa du kuli Muwaha Mulungu, mu mikalile yakwe yose.
15 Asa yakavihaliza mu Nyumba yo Mulungu i vitumamilwa vyose nha vyono vyali vinha, vyono sogwe yavisiwanulila Mulungu, hamonga ne zahabu, ne fweza, vyono mweneco nayo yakavisiwanula.

16 U mutemi Asa wa Yuda, no mutemi Baasha wa Isilayeli, wekalaga wakwihoma du wulugu mu vilo vyose vya mu wutemi wawo.
17 Baasha yakayihoma Yuda, yakazenga Lama, ho yawemile wanhu walece kwinjila, hamba kulawila wabite kuli Asa u mutemi we wa Yuda.
18 Nayo Asa yakawusa zifweza zose hamonga ne zahabu yono yali yili mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga na yono yali yili mwi'kulu, yakawatuma i wetumami wakwe, wakayitegula ku Damesiki kuli mutemi Benihadadi wa Siliya, u mwana wa Tabulimoni u mwizukulu wa Heziyoni. Yakatumiliza hamonga ne zinghani azi, yakatya,
19 “Alu 'gwe muyangu, nase cilunzaje nha vyono wasogo zetu wali welunzize hamonga. Nane nghukuleta yiyo fweza hamonga ne zahabu, viwe cipegwa cako. Nane nghukunena wuwubenaje wilunze wenyu no mutemi Baasha wa Isilayeli, nayo ho yagawuse mapuga gakwe mu wasilikale mu yisi yangu.”

20 Nayo mutemi Benihadadi yakalivumila lihweso lilyo lya Asa, hodu yakawa kutegula wawaha wakwe we wasilikale, hamonga ne mapuga gawo ge wasilikale wo wulugu, wakawa kubita kugahoma makaya ge Waisilayeli. Nawo wakawasuma Waisilayeli, wakasola ne makaya gawo, likaya lya Lyoni, na Dani, na Abeli-beti-maka, yisi yono yili behi ne lilamba lya Galilaya, sunga ne yisi yose ya Nafutali.
21 Nayo mutemi Baasha yali yeza kuzihulika nha vyono zinha, yakaleka kujendelela kuzenga Lama, hodu yakawa kubita kwikala ku Tiliza.

22 Hanyuma mutemi Asa yakatuma wanhu wabite mu yisi ya Yuda, wawalalice wanhu wose, wabite ku Lama, wakadinghule mabwe gose hamonga ne zimbawo zose, zono Baasha yetumamila mu wuzenga yiyo Lama. Nayo Asa yakavisola vinhu vivyo, yakazenjela Mizipa na Geba, likaya lya mu yisi ya Benyamini.

23 Ne zinghani zose zono mutemi Asa yetuma, hamonga ne cibali-bali cakwe, ne makaya gono yazenga, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Yuda. Ninga Asa mu wukombi wakwe, yakawa yena wutamwa wa mu migulu.
24 Asa yakafwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo mu kaya ya Dawudi. Na Yehoshafati u mwanagwe, yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Nadabu u mutemi wa Isilayeli.

25 Na mu mwaka we kejete wo mutemi Asa wa Yuda, nayo Nadabu u mwa wa Yelobowamu ho yanziza kutema muli wa Isilayeli. Nayo yatema myaka myejete du.
26 Ninga yakehwana sogwe Yelobowamu. Nayo yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, kanyhi yakawanoza sunga ne Waisilayeli wamusutizye Mulungu.

27 Nayo Baasha u mwana wa Ahiya wo mulango wa Isakali, yakawa no wuhile wubi kuli. Nadabu, hanyuma yakamuwulaga Nadabu, lono Nadabu hamonga ne wasilikale wakwe, wali walipilima walihodanje likaya lya Gibetoni, mu yisi ye Wafilisiti.
28 Na zizi zejela mu mwaka we kadatu wo wutemi wa Asa, mutemi wa Yuda. Na ku soko yiyo, Baasha yakawa yamusuma Nadabu u mutemi wa Isilayeli.
29 Hobaho Baasha yakawa kuwawulaga wose we mhuga ya Yelobowamu, yakatyatya zinghani zono Muwaha Mulungu yazilonjela muli mwitumami wakwe u muwizi Ahiya munya Shilo. Holeka we mhuga yose ya Yelobowamu, wakawulagwa wose, hakawa hasina hamba monga mono yasigala yaye.
30 Na zizi zikejela, vyono Yelobowamu yamukoza likuwo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, ku so wubi wono yetuma, na vyono, yawanoza ne Waisilayeli nawo wetume wubi.

31 Ne zinghani zose ze metumo gono yetuma Nadabu, zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.
32 U mutemi Asa wa Yuda, no mutemi Baasha wa Isilayeli, wekalaga wakwihoma du wulugu, mu vilo vyose vya mu wuteme wawo.

Baasha u mutemi wa Isilayeli

33 Na mu mwaka we kadatu wo mutemi Asa wa Yuda, Baasha u mwana wa Ahiya, yakawa mutemi wa Isilayeli, yakatema ku Tiliza myaka makumi mejete na mine.
34 Ninga nayo Baasha yakamusutizya Muwaha Mulungu, kanyhi yakawanoza i wanhu wa Isilayeli wetume wubi, nha vyono yanoza mutemi Yelobowamu mono yamulongolela kutema.


1 Watemi 16

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamutuma muwizi Yehu, u mwana wa Hanani, yabite yakamulonjele Baasha yamutye nhavi:
2 “Agwe Baasha, wali si wuli munhu mono wumanyice yaye, ninga ane nghukunoza wuwe wuli mulongola muli wanhu wangu Waisilayeli. Ninga agwe lu, wakondya kwituma wubi nha vyono yetuma Yelobowamu, yakawanoza ne wanhu wangu wetume wubi. No wubi wawo wukanghoza likuwo,
3 holeka nagwe nokutendela nha vyono namutendela Yelobowamu.
4 Ne wanhu wa mu mhuga yako, wono wofwila mwi'kaya, i miwili yawo yoligwa ne mabwa, nawo wono wofwila mwihala, i miwili yawo yoligwa ne zi'ndi.”

5 Ne zinghani zose zono yetuma mutemi Baasha ne milimo yakwe ye cibali-bali, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.
6 Baasha yakafwa, yakazikwa ku Tiliza. Na Ela u mwanagwe, yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.

7 I nghani yo Muwaha Mulungu kuli Baasha hamonga ne mhuga yakwe Muwaha Mulungu yamutuma muwizi Yehu u mwana wa Hanani, ku soko yo wubi wono yetuma Baasha hali Muwaha Mulungu. Baasha yakamukoza Muwaha Mulungu likuwo, ninga si ku soko yo wubi du wono yetuma, nha vyono yetuma mutemi Yelobowamu, mono yamulongolela mu wutemi, langa na ku soko vyono, yayongomeza ne mhuga yose ya Yelobowamu.

Ela u mutemi wa Isilayeli

8 Na mu mwaka we makumi mejete wo mutemi Asa wa Yuda, nayo Ela u mwana wa Baasha ho yakawa kutema muli Waisilayeli. Nayo Ela yakatema ku Tiliza myaka myejete du.
9 Hanyuma Zimuli mono yali yumonga wa wetumami wakwe, mono yali yo mulanjilizi muwaha we nusu ye matuka gose go mutemi go wulugu, yakawa no wuhile wubi kuli Ela. Na mwi'zuwa linji lono Ela yang'wezaga sunga yakagala mu kaya ya Aliza, mono yali yo mulanjilizi muwaha we likulu,
10 hobaho Zimuli yakenjila mumo mu kaya yiyo, yakamuhungucila Ela, yakamubogolanga sunga yakafwa. Mwene Zimuli yakawa kuwusola wutemi. Na zizi zose zejela mu mwaka we makumi mejete wo wuteme wo mutemi Asa wa Yuda.

11 Nayo Zimuli yaza kutema, hodu hobaho du, yakawa kuwawulaga wose we nyumba ya Baasha. Yakawawulaga i walume wose wono wali wali cidugudugu, hamonga ne wendece wa Baasha.
12 Holeka yikawa nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalonga kuli Baasha ko mulomo wo muwizi Yehu, holeka Zimuli yawawulaga we mhuga yose ya Baasha.
13 Na ku soko ye masutya gakwe Baasha, na ku soko yo kuwanoza Waisilayeli wetume wubi, Baasha hamonga na Ela u mwanagwe, wakamukoza likuwo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli.
14 Ne zinghani zose zono yetuma Ela, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.

Zimuli u mutemi wa Isilayeli

15 Na mu mwaka we makumi mejete na mupunghati, wo wutemi wa Asa mutemi wa Yuda, nayo Zimuli ho yatemela ku Tiliza muli Waisilayeli. Nayo yatema vilo mupunghati du. I Mapuga ge wasilikale Waisilayeli, wakalipilima likaya lya Gibetoni lya ku yisi ye Wafilisiti,
16 nawo wali weza kumanya nha vyono, Zimuli yali yena wuhile wubi kuli mutemi Ela, sunga kawa kumuwulaga, hodu nawo ku wulawa baho, wakawa kumwihaga Omuli u muwaha wawo we mapuga ge wasilikale, wakawa kumunoza yawe mutemi wa Isilayeli.
17 Nayo Omuli hamonga ne mapuga gakwe ge wasilikale, wakawuka kuko ku Gibetoni, wakawa kubita kuyipilima Tiliza.
18 Nayo Zimuli, yaza kuwona vyono likaya lyakondya kusolwa, hodu, mwene yakawa kubita kwihundika mu cinyumba, cono cali cizenjigwe mugati mwigomawakali lye likulu. Yakawa kulikoleleza likulu no moto, nayo yakawa kufwila mumo.
19 Na zizi zose zejela ku soko yo wubi wono yamusutizya Muwaha Mulungu. Yakehwana na Yelobowamu mono yamulongolela, nha vyono si yamwendeza Muwaha Mulungu so wubi wakwe wono yetuma, hamonga no kuwanoza Waisilayeli nawo wetume wubi.
20 Ne zinghani zose zono yetuma Zimuli, hamonga na zo wuhile wakwe mubi, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.

Omuli u mutemi wa Isilayeli

21 Hanyuma yikawa, i wanhu wa Isilayeli wakegawa hejete, wanji wamusakaga Tibuni u mwana wa Ginati, yo yawe mutemi wawo. Na wanji nawo wakawa wamwendile Omuli.
22 Ninga ku ndumilizo, awalya wono wamusakaga Omuli, wakawasuma wayawo. Tibuni yakafwa, na Omuli yakawa yo mutemi.
23 Holeka mu mwaka we makumi madatu na monga wo mutemi Asa, mutemi wa Yuda. Omuli ho yanozezwa yawe mutemi wa Isilayeli. Nayo Omuli yakatema muli Waisilayeli myaka yikumi na myejete. Mu myaka mutandatu milongozi, yakawa yatemile ku Tiliza.
24 Ninga hanyuma yakagula cigongo ca Samaliya, fweza elufu mutandatu, kuli munhu litagwa lyakwe Shemeli. Omuli yakazenga kaya mumo mu cigongo cico, yakayicemela litagwa Samaliya, nha cinji Shemeli, i litagwa lya mono yali munya cigongo cico.

25 Omuli yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, si nha watemi wose wono wamulongolela.
26 Yakawa yehwanile Yelobowamu mono yamulongolela kutema. Nayo Omuli yakamukalaza muno Muwaha Mulungu ku soko yo wubi wakwe meneco, hamonga no kuwanoza Waisilayeli nawo wetume wubi.
27 Ne zinghani zose zono Omuli yetuma, hamonga ne masutya gono yetuma, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.
28 Nayo Omuli yakafwa, yakazikwa kuko ku Samaliya. Nayo Ahabu u mwanagwe, yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Ahabu u mutemi wa Isilayeli

29 Na mu mwaka we makumi madatu na munana wo wuteme wa Asa, u mutemi wa yuda, nayo Ahabu u mwana wa Omuli, ho yatemela muli Waisilayeli, yakatema ku Samaliya myaka makumi mejete na myejete.
30 Ahabu yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu kuciza watemi wose wono wamulongolela.
31 Kanyhi mwene Ahabu, hamba si yetuma wubi nha vyono Yelobowamu yetuma du, langa yakaciziliza ku soko yo kumutola Yezebeli, u mwana we cicekulu wo mutemi Etibali, mutemi wa ku Sidoni, sunga yakahemba Baali.
32 Ahabu yakamuzenjela Baali nyumba yo wuhembelo ku Samaliya, yakamuzenjela ne cikumbicilo, yakaciwika mumo mu nyumba yo wuhembelo.
33 Yakawika ne cihwani co mulungu we cicekulu Ashela. Ahabu yanoza nghani nyinji du zimbi, sunga yakamukoza likuwo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, kuciza i watemi wose wa Isilayeli wono wamulongolela mu wutemi.
34 Na mwipango lyo wutemi wa Ahabu. Hiyeli munya Beteli, yakalizenga likaya lya Yeliko. Ninga Hiyeli yakafwilwa na Abilamu u mwanagwe mulongozi kulelwa, hamonga na Segubu u mwanagwe mudodo, lono yazengaga malango ge likaya. Nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yali yakondya ku zilonjela muli Yoshuwa u mwana wa Nuni.


1 Watemi 17

Eliya ne lizala liwaha

1 Kwali no muwizi litagwa lyakwe Eliya, mulawa ku Tishibi mu yisi ya Gileyadi. Muwizi Eliya yakamulonjela mutemi Ahabu yakamutya, “Ane nghukulonjela mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu mumi, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, mono ane nghumwitumamila, nha vyono, kowa kusina buhe, hamba mvula yono yotonya, sunga yipulute myaka myejete, hamba midatu, sunga nene kanyhi kono nolotela.”

2 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Eliya yakatya,
3 “Ale, wuwuce bahano, wubite ku lubavu lwa kucilima, wukehundice behi ne cikolongo ca Celiti, kucilima ye likolongo lya Yoludani.
4 Nagwe kowa kung'wa malenga ga mu cikolongo cico. Nane nakondya kuzituma zihowe ziwe kukuletela ndigwa kuko.”

5 Nayo Eliya yakawa ne cihuliko nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza. Yakatuluka, yakabita yakekala behi ne cikolongo ca Celiti.
6 Yakawa yakung'wa malenga ga mu cikolongo cico, nazo zihowe, zikawa zikumuletela mukate ne nyama, hakali mitondo ne cinyamihe, nhondo zose.
7 Ninga hanyuma hadodo, i cikolongo naco cikanyala ku soko vyono mvula yali si yikutonya.

Eliya no mucekulu mufwile wa ku Zalefati

8 Hanyuma Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Eliya yakatya,
9 “Alu, wubite ku cikaya ca Zalefati behi na Sidoni wukekale kuko. Nakondya kumulonjela mucekulu yunji mufwile mwikala baho vyono yakulise.”
10 Ale, Eliya yakawa kubita kuko ku Zalefati. Nayo yali yeza kufika ha mulango we cikaya, yakagana baho mucekulu yunji mufwile yakutenya ngwagu. Eliya yakamutya, “Nghukunena wumhele malenga go kung'wa.”
11 Nayo mucekulu yali yeza kutuluka yakubita, Eliya kanyhi yakamucemeleza yakutya, “Gwe yaya, nhondo ndetelezezaje no mukate nilye.”

12 Nayo mucekulu mufwile yakamupitula yakatya, “Nghukwilahiza mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu mumi, u Mulungu wako, nha vyono, 'ne sina hamba cibegulwa ca mukate yaye. Ane nina wusaje wudodo vivi du mu bakuli, hamonga ne mafuta yibanhi limonga du mu ciya. Nalu niza nhondole vigwagu, mbite navyo nghatelecele, hambe cidodo vyono cowa cinha, ho nilye ane no mwanangu, ndigwa yetu ye ndumilizo, hanyuma hodu cilindile cifwe ne nzala.”

13 Eliya yakamupitula yakatya, “Gwe yaya, wulece kwihwelela yaye, ninga agwe nghani nongozi, wubite wukanozeze mukate mudodo, wundetele, na hanyuma ho wubite wukenozeze nagwe hamonga na yuyo mwanago mu wusaje wono wosigala.
14 Hambi u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, nha yakutya nhavi, ‘Wusaje wuwo wono wuli mu bakuli, hamba si womalika yaye, ne mafuta gono gali mu ciya, nago hamba si gomalika yaye, sunga kolo nilatonyezeza mvula, nene Muwaha Mulungu.’ ”

15 Ale mucekulu mufwile, yakabita yakanoza nha vyono Eliya yamulonjela. Nawo wose wakawa wena ndigwa yono yawakwila vilo vinji.
16 No wusaje wa mu bakuli hamba si wamalika, ne mafuta gono gali gali mu ciya, nago hamba si gamalika yaye.

17 Hanyuma yayo u mwana wo mucekulu yuyo mufwile, yakawa kutamwa, no wutamwa wakwe wukacila muno yakawa mutamwa muno muno, na hanyuma yakawa kufwa.
18 Mucekulu mufwile yakamulonjela Eliya yakutya, “Gwe go munhu wo Mulungu, alo nhawule kunhendela nhavivi lo? Kunyiziza lo, u wubi wangu ho wukumbucilwe no Mulungu, wumbulajile mwanangu?”

19 Nayo Eliya yakamutya, “Ale, ta muletaje mwanago akuno.” Eliya yakamubocela mwana mu mawoko go mucekulu, yakamutegula ku cinyumba ca ku golofa kono mwene yekalaga, yakamuwika mwana yagone mu citanda cono mwene yagonelaga.
20 Yakamulomba Mulungu kwe 'zi yiwaha yakutya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wangu, alo nhawule kumutendela mucekulu yuyu mufwile nghani yono yinha yiyi? Mwene kanhendela viswanu, yakundima. Ninga alu 'gwe kanyhi wamuwulajila mwanagwe!”
21 Hanyuma Eliya yakalambalala mu canyha yo mwana nghanghulo yidatu, akuno yakulomba yakutya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wangu, nghukunena wumugomolele mwana yuyu wumi wakwe!”
22 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakawuvumila wulombe wa Eliya, wumi wo mwana wukamuwuyila, yakawa mumi kanyhi.

23 Eliya yakamusola mwana, yakatima nayo ku wulawa ku golofa sunga kuli nyina. Eliya yakamulonjela nyina yakamutya, “Lanje ta, alu mwanago mumi!”

24 Nayo mucekulu mufwile yakamupitula yakatya, “Alu nhondo ho namanya he, vyono agwe mbeka wuli munhu wo Mulungu, kanyhi na vyono Muwaha Mulungu yakulonjela muli gwegwe!”


1 Watemi 18

Eliya ne wakohani wa Baali

1 Hanyuma yaza kuwa mu mwaka we kadatu we lizala, Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Eliya yakamutya, “Alu, wubite wukelajile kuli mutemi Ahabu, nane notonyeza mvula.”
2 Nayo Eliya yakawa kuwanza muhinzo wo kubita.

3 Ahabu yakamucema Obadiya mono yali yo mulanjilizi we likulu. Nayo Obadiya yali yo munhu mono yali yena cogopo muno kuli Muwaha Mulungu,
4 na lono Yezebeli yawawulagaga i wawizi wo Muwaha Mulungu, mwene Obadiya yakawahundika wawizi wanji mu zimhango, waficile wawizi yigana limonga. Yakawatemanula wakawa mu mapuga mejete ga wanhu makumi mahano mahano, yakawa yakuwalisa ne zindigwa mumo mu zimhango zizo, hamonga ne malenga go kung'wa.
5 Hobaho, Ahabu yakamutya Obadiya, “Alu, alenyi cibite mu mivwilo yose ye malenga na mu vikolongo vyose vya mu yisi yose, lunji cotuga mahanze so zifwalasi, hamonga ne zinyumbu, ho zilece kufwa, cilece kufwilwa ne mitugo zetu.”
6 Wakawa kwilumbaniza no lubavu lono wobita munhu na lwakwe wakelondolele mahanze. Hobaho wakawa kwiyagala mu yisi yose ako na ako munhu na lwakwe.

7 Nayo Obadiya lono yali yali mu nzila yakubita, waketang'anila mu nzila nhinicizo na Eliya. Nayo Obadiya yali yeza kumumanya vyono yo Eliya, hodu yakenama hamwande ya Eliya, yakawuza yakutya, “Alu muwaha, yo gwegwe mbeka wuli Eliya?”

8 Nayo Eliya yakatya, “Heya yo nene. Alu wubite wukamumanyize muwaha wako u mutemi Ahabu nha vyono ane nili bahano.”

9 Nayo Obadiya yakamupitula yakatya, “Alu ane sutya nghanici, yono yoleka agwe wunhajile mu mbali nhavivi, mbite nghawulagwe no mutemi Ahabu?
10 Nane nghukwilahila mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu mumi, u Mulungu wako, nha vyono, mutemi yakuwa kukulondola muno mu ma'si gose ge yinze yose. Na hono mutemi we yisi yiyo yatye, vyono agwe wuli musina mu yisi yakwe, hodu Ahabu komusaka mutemi yuyo yelahize hono mbeka wuli musina.
11 Alu agwe ho wukusaka 'ne mbite nghamulonjele nha vyono wuli bahano hongo?
12 Alu nha baha kolo neza kuwuka, nagwe kono nyuma hono muhe wo Muwaha Mulungu wukusole, wukutegule kunji kono si kumanyice, nane kumbe nowa nakondya kumulonjela Ahabu nha vyono wuli bahano, nayo lu kumbe ho yekale si yakuwene, langa baho nhondo kombulaga. Agwe Eliya, wukumbucile nha vyono ane nili mogopa Muwaha Mulungu ku wulawilila wudodo wangu.
13 Hamba agwe wukali si nahulika vyono, lono Yezebeli yawawulagaga wawizi wo Muwaha Mulungu, ane nghawahundika mu mapango wawizi wanji yigana limonga, nghawatemanula wakalawa mapuga mejete, nghawa kuwahundika wanhu makumi mahano mahano, nghawa kuwalisa ne zindigwa hamonga ne malenga go kung'wa mumo mu zimhango?
14 Alu ho konhuma wule mbite nghamulonjele mutemi nha vyono agwe wuli bahano? Na mbeka kombulaga!”

15 Eliya yakamupitula yakamutya, “Ane nghwilahiza muli Muwaha Mulungu mumi, mono nimwitumamilaga, nha vyono nene no mweneco du, nobita nghelajile kuli mutemi izuwali.”

16 Hodu, Obadiya yakabita kuli mutemi Ahabu, yakawa kumulonjela nha vyono Eliya yali bahano. Nayo Ahabu yakawa kubita kumuhinjiliza Eliya.
17 Nayo Ahabu yali yeza kumuwona Eliya, yakamutya, “Hee! Kumbe waza go munhu mubi mono wukucipela magazo mu yisi ya Isilayeli!”

18 Eliya yakamupitula yakatya, “Gwe mutemi, ane yo si nene mono nghumuletelenyi magazo, langa yo gwegwe hamonga no sogo Omuli, mono mukugaleta magayo gaga. Yo nyenye no sogo mono si mwasaka kugebata malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu, kanyhi mukahemba vihwani vya Baali.
19 Alu nghukunena, wuwakonghole wanhu wose wa Isilayeli, nitang'anile nawo he'tunda lya Kalimeli. Kanyhi wuwalete ne wawizi wa Baali waficile magana mane na makumi mahano, hamonga ne wawizi magana mane wo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, wono wakumutaza Yezebeli.”

20 Hodu Ahabu yakawakonghola wa Isilayeli wose hamonga ne wawizi wose wa Baali, wetang'anile he tunda lya Kalimeli.
21 Nayo Eliya yakawabitila wanhu yakawatya, “Alu mwene, mojendelela kukangamyanga mu mahweso mejete sunga nili? Hono Muwaha Mulungu yo Mulungu, ale muhembenyi yuyo du, na hono mukuwona vyono Baali nayo Mulungu, ale muhembenyi yuyo!” Ninga wanhu wose wakanyamala twii.
22 Hanyuma Eliya yakatya, “Ane yo nene du muwizi wo Muwaha Mulungu mono sigaye, ninga howali wawizi magana mane na makumi mahano wa Baali.
23 Holeka alu mulete ng'ombe nyejete, wawizi wa Baali wasole monga wayiwulaje, wayitemashe, wayijelece nyama yiyo mucanyha ye zingwagu, ninga walece kupembela moto yaye. Ane nane noyisola ng'ombe ye kejete niyinoze nhavivyo nha vyono wanozize wene.
24 Hanyuma wawizi wa Baali wamulombe mulungu wawo Baali, ane nane nomulomba u Muwaha Mulungu, hanyuma ciwone mulungu mono kopitula yalete moto, ale yuyo yo Mulungu.”

Nawo wanhu wose wakapitula wazozize wakutya, “Nghani yiyo langa swanu cayilumba.”

25 Hodu nayo Eliya yakawalonjela i wawizi wa Baali yakawatya, “Alu anye vyono muli wenji, yo nyenye mwanze kuyisola ng'ombe muyiwulaje. Hanyuma mumulombe mulungu wenyu, ninga langa mulece kwasa moto mu zingwagu.”

26 Ale, nawo wawizi wa Baali wakayisola ng'ombe yono wali waletelwa, wakayiwulaga. Wakalomba kuli Baali, sunga mu misi mugati. Wazozaga wakutya, “Gwe Baali wucipitulaje!” Nawo walombaga nhavivyo, akuno wakuzumhazumha wakupilima cikumbicilo cono wazenga. Ninga hakawa hasina hamba yipitulo lyolyose lyono watuga.

27 Na lulo yali misi mugati. Nayo Eliya yakaseka yakutya, “Ale, mumuceme muno muno! Mwene nha yo mulungu! Na lunji yali mu ndoto za misi, hamba lunji yacehile, lunji kabita muhinzo, lunji yagonile, holeka mumusinhusaje!”
28 Ale, du, wawo wawizi wakacila kulomba wazozize, akuno wakwisalanga ne mamaje ne zisunja, nha vyono winha moto wawo wo wuhembi, sunga ne sakami yikawa kulawila mu miwili yawo.
29 Nawo wakajendelela du kuzoza no kwinoza kotya wamangha lukwale, sunga mu mapango ge makumbiko ge cinyamihe. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, hasina hamba lipitulo lyolyose lyono walituga, hamba lizi bwete kuhulikwa.

30 Hodu, Eliya yakawatya wanhu “Ale, musejelele akuno,” na wose wakasejelela wakamupilima. Nayo yakalinoza likumbicilo lyo Muwaha Mulungu lyono lyali lyamwaguka.
31 Yakasola mabwe yikumi na mejete, kwi walo lye milango ye wana yikumi na wejete wa Yakobo, mono Muwaha Mulungu yamupela litagwa lya Isilayeli.
32 Yakawa kuzenga ne mabwe gago, cikumbicilo co kumuhembela Muwaha Mulungu. Yakahimba musinji mu zimbavu zose ze cikumbicilo, wono zokwinjila lita yikumi na yine za malenga.
33 Yakasola zingwagu, yakazipanga mucanyha ye cikumbicilo, yakayitemasha ng'ombe, yakayijeleka nyama yose mucanyha ye zingwagu. Hanyuma yakawatya, “Ale, mumemye njilo yine malenga mugamwaje mucanyha ye likumbiko hamonga ne zingwagu zayo. Nawo wakanoza nhavivyo.”
34 Nayo yakawatya, “Ale, monjeleze malenga ganji kanyhi lwe kejete.” Nawo wakatajila kanyhi lwe kejete. Yakawatya, “Monjeleze ganji kanyhi lwe kadatu.” Nawo wakatajila lwe kadatu.
35 Nhondo malenga gakamema mu cikumbicilo gakapilima sunga musinji wose.

36 Na yikawa mu wulavya likumbiko lye cinyamihe, u muwizi Eliya, yakasejelela ha cikumbicilo yakalomba yakutya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wa Abulahamu na Isaka na Yakobo, nghukulomba zuwali, yimanyice nha vyono agwe yo gwegwe go Mulungu wa Isilayeli, na vyono nane nili mwitumami wako. Nane nizinoza zizi zose ku soko ye nghani yako.
37 Holeka nghukunena wuhulice Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wuhulice ho wanhu wawa nawo womanya nha vyono u Muwaha Mulungu, yo Mulungu mbeka, na vyono agwe wukuwapitula zinhumbula zawo wakuwuyile gwegwe.”

38 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakacikoleza moto cikumbicilo, nawo moto wukavitakamiza vyose, likumbiko ne zingwagu hamonga ne mabwe gayo. Wukanyanya muhangha no kuganyaza malenga gose mu musinji.
39 Nawo wanhu wali weza kuziwona zizi, wakebamiliza hasi, wakazoza wakutya, “Muwaha Mulungu yo Mulungu, u Muwaha Mulungu yo Mulungu yali wene du!”

40 Hobaho Eliya yakawatya, “Ale, muwebate wawizi wose wa Baali, mulece kumuleceleza hamba yumonga yatije!” Nawo wanhu wakawebata wose, nayo Eliya yakawategula sunga ko kwi'kolongo lya Kishoni, yakawawulajila kuko wose.

Ndumilizo ye lizala

41 Hanyuma Eliya yakamulonjela mutemi Ahabu yakatya, “Alu, wubite wukalye zindigwa. Hambi ane nha nghuhulika mvula yikudidima, yasejelela.”
42 Na lono Ahabu yali yabita ku wulya zindigwa, mwene Eliya yakabita kucanyha ye cizuli ce litunda lya Kalimeli, yakenama ho hasi, no mutwe wakwe yakawujeleka mu mafugamilo gakwe.
43 Hanyuma yakamulonjela mwitumami wakwe yakatya, “Ale ta wubite wukalanje ku lubavu lwa kwi'lenga.”

Mwitumami yakabita yakawuya, yakaleta lipitulo yakatya, “Ako hamba si mbwene cinji yaye.” Nayo Eliya yakamulonjela yabite kanyhi na kanyhi sunga zikafika nghanghulo mupunghati.
44 Langa mu nghanghulo yo mupunghati, u mwitumami yakatya, “Mbona yivunde yidodo linha muwoko wa munhu likulawa mwi'lenga.”

Hobaho, Eliya yakamulonjela mwitumami wakwe yakatya, “Alu wubite kuli mutemi Ahabu wukamutye yenjile mbela mwi'tuka lyakwe lyo wulugu, yamanyhile ku kaya, lono mvula yikali namudindila.”

45 Na hanyuma hadodo du, wulanga wukanza kudinda ne mavunde matitu, ne mbeho yikanza kuputa, hobaho ne mvula mbaha muno yikanza kutonya. Ahabu yakenjila mwituka lyakwe lyo wulugu, yakawa kutuluka yakubita ku Yezeleli.
46 I vilungo vyo Muwaha Mulungu vikamuziza Eliya, yaketunga myenda yakwe mu ciwino, yakebata lumangho ku mwande ya Ahabu, yakamangha nzila yose, sunga yakafika ku Yezeleli.


1 Watemi 19

Eliya mwi'tunda lya Sinayi

1 Nayo mutemi Ahabu yakamulonjela Yezebeli mugolece wakwe, zose zono Eliya yetumile, na vyono yawawulaje wawizi wose wa Baali.
2 Hobaho nayo Yezebeli yakatuma munhu yabite kuli Eliya yakamulonjele yatye, “Hono yifice mitondo kwinha vivi, niwe nghali hamba si nakuwulaga nha vyono agwe wawawulaga wawizi, hodu ciba nane milungu yimbulaje.”
3 Nayo Eliya, ku so nghani yiyo, hodu yakawa kutiza ho yeponye wumi wakwe. Yakamusola no mwitumami wakwe, wakawa kutiziza ku Belisheba ya ku yisi ya Yuda.

Eliya yakamuleka mwitumami wakwe,
4 mwene yakawa kujendelela no muhinzo misi yose mu nyika. Nayo yali yeza kufika ha mubici wunji, yakekala mu mubeho hasi yo mubici, yakasulumila ciba yafwe. Ale baho yakalomba yakutya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, alu nghani yiyi nha yeza nzito muno kuli'ne, holeka wumbulagaje, ciba nifwe, si nho kwikala mumi!”

5 Hodu, yakawa kutambalala baho hasi yo mubici, zinhongo zikawa kumwibata. Malayika yakaza yakamupamiza nhinicizo, yakamutya, “Gwe Eliya, ale winukaje wulye.”
6 Nayo yali yeza kwinuka mu zinhongo, yalanje nhavi, yakawona mukate hamonga ne malenga gali mu cibuzi, behi ha mutwe wakwe. Hodu yakawa kulya, yakang'wa ne malenga, hanyuma kanyhi yakawa kugona nhongo.
7 Nayo malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu, yakaza kanyhi lwe kejete yakamusinhusa, yakamutya, “Gwe Eliya, winukaje wulye, hambi muhinzo nha wowa muwaha muno kuli'gwe.”
8 Nayo Eliya yakenuka, yakalya, yakang'wa. Ndigwa yiyo yikamupela vilungo vyo kujenda vilo makumi mane ku wufika sunga ko kwi'tunda lya Sinayi, litunda lyono lyelile.
9 Yali yeza kufika he 'tunda, yakenjila mu mhango, yakagona mumo cilo cico.

Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakalongana nayo nhinicizo, yakamutya, “Alu Eliya, wukwitumaci baha?”

10 Nayo Eliya yakapitula yakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu we mihanga, ane nhondo zose, nali kukwitumamila gwegwe du wuli wene. Ninga i wanhu wa Isilayeli, wakondya kucibena cilagane cako na wene. Wavibenanga vikumbicilo vyako, wawawulaga ne wawizi wako, nane nasigala nili wene du. Nawo lu, wakunondola nane wambulaje!”

11 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela yakatya, “Alu Eliya wubite wukeme hali'ne mucanyha ye litunda.” Na langa ta, Muwaha Mulungu yakapuluta, mbeho mbaha ye vilungo yikatenjelemula matunda, yikapandula ne matalawe, ninga Muwaha Mulungu si yali yali mumo mu mbeho. Nayo mbeho yali yeza likaza litulo, ninga Muwaha Mulungu si yali yali mumo mwi'tulo.
12 Na wukaza moto, ninga Muwaha Mulungu si yali yali mumo mu moto. Na hanyuma yo moto, likaza lizi lyo wupwepwe wo wutule.

13 Nayo Eliya yali yeza kuhulika, yakefunika wusu wakwe mu vivwalo vyakwe, yakalawila, yakema ha mulango we mhango. Hanyuma lizi likamutya, “Alu Eliya wukunoza cici baha?”

14 Nayo Eliya yakapitula yakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu we mihanga, ane nhondo zose nali kukwitumamila gwegwe du wuli wene. Ninga i wanhu wa Isilayeli, wakondya kucibena cilagane cako na wene. Wavibenanga vikumbicilo vyako, wawawulaga ne wawizi wako, nane nasigala nili wene du. Nawo lu, wakunondola nane wambulaje.”

15 Muwaha Mulungu nayo yakamutya, “Alu, wubite ku nyika behi na Damesiki, winjile mwi'kaya, wumwitile mafuta Hazayeli yawe mutemi we yisi ya Siliya.
16 Kanyhi wumwitile mafuta Yehu u mwana we cilume wa Nimushi, yawe mutemi wa Isilayeli. Hanyuma kanyhi wumwitile mafuta Elisha u mwana wa Shafati, munya Abeli Mehola, yahalile u mulimo wako wo wuwizi.
17 Ho yiwe mono kopona yekale si yawulagwe na Hazayeli, ale kowulagwa na Yehu. Na mono kopona yekale si yawulagwe na Yehu, ale, kowulagwa na Elisha.
18 Hamba nhavivyo, nane nosigaza wanhu elufu mupunghati mu Isilayeli, wono wanyogopile nene, kanyhi wono si wamuhembaga Baali, hamba kucinonela ne cihwani cayo.”

U lucemo lwa Elisha

19 Holeka Eliya yakawa kutuluka, yakamwagana Elisha yena ne zing'ombe zakwe zitunjile nyejete nyejete zikulima. Nazo zali zitunjize nyejete nyejete kali kumi na nyejete, na zose zilongoleye ku mwande yakwe, mwene yakulima na azilya zono zali zityatizye mu nyuma ye zinzawo zose. Hobaho Eliya yakavula civwalo cakwe yakamuvwika Elisha.
20 Nayo Elisha yakazileka zing'ombe zakwe, yakamumanyhilila Eliya, yakamulonjela yakatya, “Alu Eliya, ane nghukunena wundece namule mbite nghawanonele baba na yaya, niwelaje, hanyuma ho nize cibitane.”

Nayo Eliya yakamutya, “Ale heya wubite, ane si nghukulemeza yaye!”

21 Elisha yakazibitila zi zilya zing'ombe zakwe, yakaziwulaga zose, yaketumamila mibano yazo zikawa zo ngwagu, yakayiteleka nyama yazo. Yakawa kuwagawila wanhu nyama yiyo wakayilya yose. Hanyuma nhondo ho yakawa kubita kumutyatya Eliya, yakawa mutazi wa Eliya.


1 Watemi 20

U wihomi na Siliya

1 U mutemi Benihadadi wa Siliya, yakagakongholela hamonga mapuga gakwe gose ge wasilikale wo wulugu, yakawa hamonga ne watemi wanji waficile makumi madatu na wejete, wena ne matuka gawo go wulugu, hamonga ne zifwalasi zawo. Yakabita, yakalipilima likaya lya Samaliya yakalihoma.
2 Yakatuma wanhu wabite ku kaya yo mutemi Ahabu, wakamulonjele watye, “U mutemi Benihadadi yakukusaka
3 wilavye kuli mwene, hamonga ne zifweza zako zose, ne zizahabu zako, hamonga ne wagolece wako ne wanago.”

4 Nayo Ahabu mutemi wa Isilayeli yakapitula yakatya, “Mubite mukamulonjele muwaha wangu, mutemi Benihadadi mumutye vyono ane numbile du, kosuma kusola nene no mweneco hamonga ne vinhu vyose vyono nina navyo.”

5 Hanyuma kanyhi wanhu wakaza kuli Ahabu wena nghani yinji kanyhi kulawa kuli Benihadadi, wakamulonjela Ahabu wakatya, “Mutemi Benihadadi katya nhavi, alu vyono wuvumiye kwiyilavya go mweneco kuli 'ne, hamonga ne zifweza zako, ne zizahabu zako, ne wagolece wako hamonga ne wanago.
6 Alu notuma wetumami wangu waze walondole mwi'kulu lyako, hamonga na mu zikaya ze wetumami wako wose, wasole cinhu co cose cono wowona cina cambuzi. Nowatuma wokuza kuko i mitondo kwinha vivi.”

7 Nayo mutemi Ahabu yakawacema i walongola wose we yisi, yakawatya, “Alu mulanje, mukuwona vyono munhu ayu yakusaka yacongomeze. Yanhumila wanhu waze wanonjele vyono mwene Benihadadi yakusaka nimupele wagolece zangu ne wanangu, ne zifweza hamonga ne zizahabu, nane nghavumila.”

8 Nawo walongola hamonga ne wanhu wakapitula wakatya, “Wulecele lukulu kumuhulicila hamba wulece kumuvumila.”

9 Hodu Ahabu yakawa kuwalonjela wanhu wabite wakamulonjele Benihadadi watye, “Gwe go mutemi Benihadadi go muwaha wangu, ane nalumba du mu cisakwa cako cilongozi, ninga mu cisakwa cako ce kejete, ane si nghuvumila yaye.”

I wanhu wa walya wono watumwa wakabita kuli Benihadadi. Ninga kanyhi wakaza kuli Ahabu na nghani yinji kanyhi, kulawa kuli Benihadadi, wakatya,
10 “Mutemi Benihadadi yakutya, Notuma wanhu wenji waze walyongomeze likaya lyako lilino, wagazole ne malowanjila galyo mu mawoko gawo. Nane hono nikale si nizitumile zizi, hodu milungu yimbulagaje!”

11 Nayo mutemi Ahabu yakawapitula yakatya, “Ale mubite mukamulonjele Benihadadi nha vyono, u musilikale citung'ati, kekumyaga lono wulugu wapuluta, ninga si kekumyaga yaye lono wulugu wukali.”

12 Benihadadi yakatuga lipitulo lya Ahabu mwi'pango lono wang'wezaga wali mu mahema gawo, mwene hamonga ne watemi wayagwe wono vyali vilunzize. Hobaho Benihadadi yakalajiliza wanhu wakwe wewikanize wabite wakalihome likaya. Nawo wanhu wakawa kutuluka hamonga wakawa kubita.

13 Ninga kono nyuma, u muwizi yakamubitila mutemi Ahabu, yakamulonjela yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, wulece kulyogopa yaye lipuga liwaha lilyo lye wasilikale! Ane nokupela wusume zuwalili du kuli wene, nagwe ho komanya vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”

14 Nayo Ahabu yakatya. “Alu ma, mono kowulongoza wulugu wuwo kowa nani?” U muwizi yakamutya, “Muwaha Mulungu yakutya vyono i wasilikale wazelelo wono wakulawa mu matumbi, wawo wo wokwituma mulimo wuwo, akuno wakulanjililwa ne wasilikale wawaha wa mu zisi zose ze Waisilayeli.”

Mutemi Ahabu yakawuza yakatya, “Alu hamba nhavivyo, ma mono kocema wulugu wuwo kowa nani?” Muwizi yakamutya, “Kowa gwegwe.”

15 Hobaho mutemi Ahabu, yakawa kuwakonghola i wasilikale we cizelelo wose, wono wali wali mu wulanjilizi we wawaha wose, magana mejete na makumi madatu na mejete we wasilikale. Hanyuma kanyhi yakawakonghola ne wasilikale we Waisilayeli waficile wanhu elufu mupunghati.

16 Hobaho wulugu wukawa kwanza mwi'pango lya mu misi mugati, lono Benihadadi hamonga ne watemi makumi madatu na wejete, wono wali welunzize nayo, wali wakali wali mu wung'wi mu mahema gawo.
17 I wasilikale wazelelo wo wakawa walongoleye kumwande ye mapuga. Nayo Benihadadi yali yawicile walanjilizi, wono hanyuma wakaza kumulonjela nha vyono wawona lipuga lye wasilikale likuza likulawa ku Samaliya.
18 Nayo Benihadadi yakalajiliza yakatya, “Ale mubite mukawalete womi, hambi nha si nghani yaye hono wawe wakuza ku wihomi, hamba wakuza kulomba mulamu.”

19 Iwasilikale wazelelo wo wakalongola kwihoma ne walugu, hanyuma lipuga lye wasilikale wa Isilayeli, nawo ho wakatyatya.
20 Wakawahoma muno, wakawawulaga munhu na mulugu wakwe. Sunga ne wanhu wa Siliya wakasumwa wakatiza, nawo Waisilayeli wakawalondelela du. Ninga Benihadadi yakapanda fwalasi yakatiza, ne wasilikale wanji wapanda fwalasi.
21 Mutemi Ahabu yakayisola migunda ne zifwalasi, hamonga ne matuka go wulugu, yakawahoma muno muno wanhu wa Siliya no kuwasuma.

22 Hanyuma kanyhi u muwizi yakamubitila mutemi Ahabu yakamutya, “Alu, wubite wukawawikanize viswanu wasilikale wako wo wulugu, hamonga na gwegwe, hambi mutemi wa Siliya nha kokuza mwihome nayo kanyhi ku mwaka.”

Wihomi lwe kejete na Siliya

23 I wetumami wo mutemi Benihadadi wakamulonjela Benihadadi wakatya, “Mwene wumanyile nha vyono milungu ye Waisilayeli yo milungu ya mu matunda, na yiyi yo soko holeka Waisilayeli wakacisuma. Ninga mbeka cidahile kuwasuma, hono cihomele nawo mu yisi yo wuhihi.
24 Holeka aluno wuwawusaje wawano watemi makumi madatu na wejete wawuce mu wulanjilizi wawo, wuwawice walanjilizi wanji wahomele hanhu hawo.
25 Hanyuma wukonghole wasilikale wenji muno muno wo wulugu, wawe wenji nha wa walya wono wakatiza, hamonga no winji wuwo we zifwalasi ne matuka go wulugu. Nase cokwihoma ne Waisilayeli mu yisi yo wuhihi. Nhondo muhinzo wuwu nase cowasuma du.”

Mutemi Benihadadi nayo yakavumila, yakanoza nha vyono wamulonjela.
26 Na mu mwaka wono watyatya, yakawakonghola wanhu wakwe, yakabita nawo sunga kwi'kaya lya Afeki, yakawahome Waisilayeli.
27 Ninga Waisilayeli nawo wali wakondya kwiwikaniziza lukulu. Nawo wakabita wakegawa mapuga mejete, wakasita nghambi zawo zili hejete, wakawa vidomile ne wanhu wa Siliya. Ninga i Waisilayeli, wejelaga nha vitewa vidodo vya mhene ha mwande ye wanhu wa Siliya, wono wali weyagaye du mu yisi yose.

28 U muwizi yakamubitila kanyhi mutemi Ahabu yakamutya, “Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi: ‘Alu vyono wanhu wa Siliya wakutya ane nili mulungu wa mu matunda, hamba si nili wa mu yisi yo wuhihi, ale, nane nokupela wusume wuwaha kuli mapuga gawo mawaha ge wasilikale, nagwe hamonga ne wanhu wako, ho momanya vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.’ ”

29 Waisilayeli ne Wasiliya, wakekala vilo mupunghati mu zinghambi zawo vyo wedomile du munhu yamulanjile muyagwe. Na mwi'zuwa lyo mupunghati nhondo ho wulugu wukawa kwanza. Ne Waisilayeli wawawulaga wanhu wa Siliya waficile elufu yigana limonga.
30 Na wono wasigala wakatiziza kwi'kaya lya Afeki, ninga nawo majinji ge likaya gakawagwila, wakafwa wanhu waficile elufu makumi mejete na mupunghati.

Ninga mwene Benihadadi, yakatiziza mwi'kaya, yakehundika mu cinyumba ca kumhilimo ye kaya.
31 I wetumami wakwe wakamubitila wakamulonjela wakatya, “Ase cikuhulika nha vyono watemi we Waisilayeli, wo wanhu wono wali lusungu. Holeka cikukulomba wucikombole cimubitile mutemi wa Isilayeli, ciwe citunjile mu viwino magunila akuno cidajile milejehe mu zisingo zetu, lunji kokuleka si kokuwulaga.”
32 Hobaho wakawa kwitunga magunila mu viwino vyawo, wakadaga ne milejehe mu zisingo zawo, wakawa kumubitila Ahabu wakamutya, “U mwitumami wako Benihadadi yakukulomba wulece kumuwulaga.”

33 Nayo Ahabu yakawawuza yakatya, “Alu kumbe yakali mumi hongo? Ale viswanu! Ayo mwene nha ndugu yangu.” Hodu wakawacilila kamonga wakatya. “Heya nha vyono wulonjile, Benihadadi mbeka nduguyo!”

Nayo Ahabu yakawatya, “Heya! Alu mubite mukamulete hono hangu.” Nayo Benihadadi yaza kuwa yaza, Ahabu yakamubocela viswanu muno, yakamupandiza mwi'tuka lyakwe lyo wulugu.
34 Benihadadi yakamulonjela Ahabu yakatya, “Alu wayiko, ane nokugomolela makaya gose gono baba yagasola kuli sogo, nagwe wulinoze likaya lya Damesiki liwe likaya lyako lyo wusuluza, nha vyono baba yalinoza likaya lya Samaliya.”

Ahabu yakamupitula yakatya, “Alu ku soko ya zizi zono wulonjile, hodu nane nokulecela.” Ahabu yakanoza cilagane na Benihadadi, hanyuma yakamuleka yabite.

U muwizi yakumuhosa Ahabu

35 U munhu yunji wa muli wana we wawizi, ku so lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, yakamulonjela muwizi muyagwe yakutya, “Wunhowaje” ninga yuyo muyagwe yakalema kumutowa.
36 Nayo yakamutya, “Alu vyono walema kunhowa, wabena lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, na ku soko yiyo, vyono wukuwuka bahano du, simba yokuwulaga.” Nayo mbeka yali yeza kuwuka baho du, simba yikamwagana yikamuwulaga.

37 Hanyuma yayo, muwizi yuyo du, yakabita kanyhi kuli munhu yunji yakamutya, “Nhowaje.” U munhu yuyo yakamutowa, yamutowa hawaha yakamutamisa muno.
38 Muwizi yakewopa wusu wakwe ne citema co mwenda, ho yekawanize yunji yalece kumumanya. Hanyuma yakabita yakema mu nzila, yalindiye kolo mutemi yakupuluta.
39 Na lono mutemi yapulutaga, u muwizi yakamucema yakutya, “Gwe go mutemi, ane nali nili mu wulugu, hanyuma musilikale muyangu kandetela mulugu mateka, kanonjela nha vyono, ‘Wumudime munhu yuyu, na hono wumuleceleze yatije, ale agwe go mweneco kowulagwa, hamba kolipa fweza elufu yidatu.’
40 Ninga nane niwa kucelajila sunga munhu yuyo kawa kutiza.”

U mutemi Ahabu yakamutya, “Alu nha wakondya kwilonjela go mweneco vyono koling'aniza, holeka yikunojeye gwegwe wulipe yiyo fayini.”

41 Nayo muwizi hodu yakawa kuciwusa citema co mwenda mu wusu wakwe. Nayo mutemi yakawa kumumanya vyono yo muyagwe ne wawizi.
42 Hanyuma muwizi yakamulonjela mutemi yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, ‘Alu vyono kumuleka Benihadadi kawa kutiga, u munhu mono nali nghulajilize wumuwulaje, alu gwegwe yo kofwa wuhalile wufwe wakwe, kanyhi ne mapuga gako gose ge wasilikale wako wo wulugu, gokongomezwa, ku soko vyono kugaleka mapuga ge wasilikale wo wulugu ga Benihadadi gatiga.’ ”

43 Hobaho mutemi Ahabu yakawa kuwuya ku kaya yakwe ku Samaliya akuno yakalaye, yakoligwe ne likuwo.


1 Watemi 21

U mugunda wa zabibu wa Naboti

1 Hanyuma ya zizo, yikawa, behi ne likulu lyo mutemi Ahabu kuko ku Yezeleli, hali ho wuli mugunda wa zabibu, wono yawulimaga munhu yunji mono yacemwaga Naboti.
2 Ale, mwi'zuwa linji, Ahabu yakamutya Gwe, Naboti, “Naboti, nghukulomba wundecele wuwo mugunda wako niwitumamile nene, hambi nha wuli behi muno muno ne likulu lyangu. Ane nghuhila nime busitani ya mboga. Ninga ane nokupela mugunda wunji wono muswanu kuciza awu, wono kosuma kulima zabibu. Hamba hono wende, ane nosuma kuwugula mugunda wako ko wuguzi wuswanu.”

3 Ninga Naboti yakapitula yakatya, “U mugunda wu wuno, wo wuhazi wangu, ane nawuhala ku wulawa kuli walezi wangu, no Muwaha Mulungu yo muhomelezi wangu. Holeka siyosumika yaye nghupele wuhazi we walezi wangu!”

4 Hodu Ahabu yakawuya ku kaya yakwe, akuno yena ne nhunduji, kanyhi yakoligwe muno ne likuwo kuli zono yalonjeywe na Naboti. Yaza kufika ha kaya, yakatambalala mu citanda cakwe akuno vidomile ne lijinji. Hamba si yasaka no kulya cinji yaye.
5 U mugolece wakwe Yezebeli, yakamubitila yakamuwuza yakutya, “Alu nhawule wukalaye muno muno, sunga weza si wukusaka no kulya ndigwa?”

6 Nayo Ahabu yakamupitula yakatya, “Heya, nhumbula yangu nzito ku soko ya zono yanonjeye Naboti. Hambi ane nha nali nalumba niwugule mugunda wakwe we zabibu, hamba lunji hono yawe yendile, nimupele mugunda wunji cikawane no mugunda wakwe wuwo. Ninga mwene kawa kunyitya vyono si yosumika yaye!”

7 Nayo Yezebeli mugolece wakwe yakamupitula yakamutya, “Alu, si yo gwegwe mutemi wa Isilayeli, hamba ng'o? Ale wisindamice, winuce wuwuce mu citanda, wulye ndigwa. Ane nokupela wuwo mugunda wa Naboti!”

8 Hanyuma ya zizi, Yezebeli yakawa kuzandika baluwa nyinji, yakazitajila sayini mwi'tagwa lya Ahabu, yakazitajila mu zibahasha, yakazidinda, yakawicila ne citanjilo cakwe. Hanyuma yakawategulila wetumami wose hamonga ne walongola wose wono wekaye mwi'kaya lyakwe.
9 Ne zibaluwa zizo zikandikwa nhavi, “Mwanze lizuwa lyo wuleka kulya, muwakongholele wanhu wose hamonga, mumuwice Naboti mwi'goda litambule lya hamwande.
10 Hanyuma muwice wanhu wejete we caka caka wono wosuma kumuhomelela watye, ‘Naboti kamutovula Mulungu hamonga no mutemi.’ Hobaho nanye muwe kumusola mumutegule kunze ye likaya, mumutowe ne mabwe kuko sunga yafwe.”

11 Nawo wetumami hamonga ne walongola wono wekaye mwi'kaya lya Yezebeli, wakanoza nha vyono Yezebeli yawalajiliza.
12 Wakalyanza lizuwa lyo wuleka kulya, wakawakongholela wanhu hamonga, wakamuwika Naboti mwi'goda litambule lya hamwande.
13 Ne wanhu wejete wakamuhomelela calinze nha vyono kamutovula Mulungu hamonga no mutemi. Hodu hobaho wakamutegula kunze ye likaya, wakawa kumutowanga ne mabwe sunga yakafwa.
14 Hobaho, wakabita kumulonjela Yezebeli, nha vyono Naboti yakondya kuwulagwa.

15 Nayo Yezebeli yali yeza kuhulika nha vyono Naboti kakondya kufwa, hodu yakabita kamonga kumulonjela Ahabu yakutya, “U muyago Naboti, kakondya kufwa. Alu wubite wukawusole mugunda wuwe wako, wono mwene yalema kukupela.”
16 Hobaho nayo Ahabu yakawa kubita kuwusola mugunda wa Naboti, wukawa wakwe.

17 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela muwizi Eliya, munya Tishibi yakamutya,
18 “Ale wubite kuli Ahabu mutemi wa Samaliya, komwagana yali mu mugunda wa Naboti yeza kuwusola wuwe wakwe.
19 Wukamulonjele wutye nhavi, ane no Muwaha Mulungu nghumutya nhavi, ‘Agwe Ahabu kumuwulaga Naboti, kanyhi wukusola sunga ne sawo yakwe hongo?’ Kanyhi wumulonjele wutye, Muwaha Mulungu yakukutya, ‘I hanhu hono mabwa gayinamhila sakami ya Naboti, nagwe hanhu baho du ho mabwa goyinamhila ne sakami yako!’ ”

20 Nayo Ahabu yali yeza kumuwona Eliya yakuza, yakamutya, “Alu langa izuwali, wanyibata go mulugu wangu.”

Nayo Eliya yakamutya, “Heya nakwibata. Hambi nha kwiyendela gwegwe go mweneco kwituma zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu.
21 Holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakukutya, ‘Nokuletela magayo mawaha. Nokuwusiza lukulu, hamonga ne walume wose wa mu mhuga yako, i wazelelo hamonga ne wakombi nhavivyo.
22 Nawo we nyumba yako, wokwihwana na we nyumba yo mutemi Yelobowamu u mwana wa Naboti. Kanyhi kokwihwana na we nyumba yo mutemi Baasha u mwana wa Ahiya. Hambi nha kunghoza likuwo ku soko yo kuwanoza Waisilayeli wetume wubi.’
23 Na kuli za Yezebeli, u Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, nayo Yezebeli, i zimbwa zowulya muwili wakwe mwi'kaya lya Yezebeli.
24 Ne wanduguzo wose wono wofwila mwi'kaya, miwili yawo yoligwa ne mabwa, na mono kofwila mwi'hala, miwili wakwe woligwa ne zi'ndi.”

25 (Hali hasina munhu yunji wo wose, mono yeyendela mweneco kwituma zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu, kotya Ahabu, mono Yezebeli mugolece wakwe, yamufwanghanaga.
26 Ahabu yetuma nghani ya cevu muno mono yo kuhemba vihwani nha vyono wanozaga Waamoli, wono Muwaha Mulungu yawawinga mu yisi ha mwande ye wanhu wa Isilayeli.)

27 Eliya yaza kukondya kuzilonga zizo, hodu Ahabu yakawa kuvidemulanga vivwalo vyakwe, yakavitaga, yakavwala magunila. Yakalema kulya ndigwa, yakawa kugona mu magunila, yakawa yakujenda yena wusungu no wukalale mu nhumbula yakwe.

28 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Eliya yakatya,
29 “Ale, wawona, vyono Ahabu yenenenyheza hali'ne? Alu vyono yakondya kwinenenyheza hali'ne, hodu nane sinowuleta wongomezwe mu cipango cakwe lono yakali mumi, langa wowa mu cipango co wikale we wanagwe, nane baho ho nolowuletela wongomezwe mu nyumba ya Ahabu.”


1 Watemi 22

U muwizi Mikaya yakumukaga Ahabu

(2Zin 18:2-27)

1 I Waisilayeli, ne wanhu wa Siliya, wakekala welamuye sunga yikapuluta myaka myejete.
2 Ninga mu mwaka we kadatu, u mutemi Yehoshafati we yisi ya Yuda, yakatuluka, yakabita yakamuwone Ahabu u mutemi wa Isilayeli.

3 Ahabu yakawawuza wetumami wakwe yakawatya, “Alu mwene sese, ho cilindiye cici, hono cikali nayigomola Lamoti ya mu yisi ya Gileyadi ku wulawa kuli mutemi we yisi ya Siliya, nayo kumbe Lamoti yo yetu?”
4 Hanyuma Ahabu yakamuwuza Yehoshafati yakatya, “Wule, agwe Yehoshafati, kosuma kubitana nane cikayihome Lamoti?”

Yehoshafati yakapitula yakatya, “Ane hamonga ne wasilikale wangu, wose cikondizye kwiwikaniza, hamonga ne wasilikale wose i wapanda fwalasi. Ase cikulindiye gwegwe du, nagwe wiwikanize.
5 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, camule cimuwuze Muwaha Mulungu.”

6 Hodu Ahabu yakawakonghola wawizi waficile magana mane, yakawawuza yakutya, “Ati, wulenyi, mbite nghayihome Lamoti, hamba ng'o?”

Nawo wawizi wakamutya, “Heya wubite, hambi u Muwaha Mulungu nha kokupela wusume.”

7 Ninga Yehoshafati yakawuza yakutya, “Alu, mwene, hasina kanyhi hamba muwizi yunji mono nayo cosuma kumuwuza zono Muwaha Mulungu kocilonjela?”

8 Nayo Ahabu yakapitula yakatya, “Ho yuli muwizi yunji mono yakucemwa Mikaya u mwana wa Imula. Ninga u munhu yuyo, ane nimusulile muno muno, vyono mwene yakali nambilila nghani swanu, hamba kamonga bwete, langa mwene kambililaga nghani 'mbi du nhondo zose.”

Yehoshafati yakamutya, “Wulecele lukulu kulonga nhavivyo.”

9 Hodu Ahabu yakawatuma wetumami wakwe wabite wakamuceme Mikaya yaze mbela mbela.

10 Nawo watemi wawa vyono wejete, nha cinji Ahabu u mutemi wa Isilayeli, hamonga na Yehoshafati u mutemi wa Yuda, wose wali wekaye mu magoda gawo ge citemi-temi ha cuga, hanze yo mulango muwaha we likaya lya Samaliya. Ne wawizi wose magana mane, wawililaga ha mwande yawo.
11 Muwizi monga wa muli wawizi wawo, litagwa lyakwe Zedekiya u mwana wa Cenana, yakenozeza mahembe ga cuma, yakamulonjela Ahabu yakutya, “Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, ‘Mu mahembe gaga, kosuma kuwahoma wanhu wa Siliya, nagwe kowasumila lukulu.’ ”
12 Ne wawizi wanji wose wakalonjela kumonga wakatya, “Agwe wubite du wukayihome Lamoti, no Muwaha Mulungu kokupela wusume wuwaha.”

13 I wetumami wono wakabita kumucema Mikaya, wakamulonjela Mikaya wakatya, “I wawizi wanji wose wamuwilila mutemi nha vyono kosuma du. Holeka nagwe wubite wukalonje nha vivyo.”

14 Ninga Mikaya yakawapitula yakatya, “Ane nghwihuwiza mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu mumi, nha vyono ane nolonga du zono mweneco Muwaha Mulungu kononjela nizilonje!”

15 Nayo Mikaya yali yeza kufika hali mutemi Ahabu, mutemi Ahabu yakamuwuza yakutya, “Ati Mikaya mutemi Yehoshafati hamonga nane cosuma kubita cikayihome Lamoti, hamba ng'o?”

Mikaya yakamutya, “Heya wubite nane nitumbiye vyono Muwaha Mulungu kokupela wusume.”

16 Ninga Ahabu yakamupitula Mikaya yakamutya, “Gwe Mikaya, hono wuwe wukulonganila nane mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu, yikunojeye wunonjele ze mbeka! Ne nghani yiyi nha nghulonjeye kali kenji muno?”

17 Mikaya yakapitula yakatya, “Ane nghusuma kuwawona Waisilayeli wose weyagaye mu matunda wenha zingholo zono zisina mudimi. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, ‘I wanhu awa wasina mulongola, holeka muwalece wawuye ku zikaya zawo wapolile.’ ”

18 Nayo Ahabu yakamulonjela Yehoshafati yakatya, “Ale, wawona! Zizi zo nghulonjeye, nha vyono, ayu Mikaya ane si kambililaga ziswanu ng'o, langa kambililaga zimbi du nhondo zose!”

19 Nayo Mikaya yakajendelela kulonga yakutya, “Alu Ahabu, wuhulicize zono Muwaha Mulungu yakuzilonga! Nane namuwona Muwaha Mulungu yekaye mwi'goda lyakwe ku wulanga, hamonga ne wamalayika wakwe wamupilime.
20 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakuwawuza yakutya, ‘Alo nani mono komukoca Ahabu ho yabite ku Lamoti yakawulagwe?’ Nawo wamalayika wakegawa hejete, wanji wakalonga nhavi, na wanji nhavi.
21 Nhondo likenuka licisi, likema hali Muwaha Mulungu, likatya, ‘Ane nobita nghamukoce.’
22 Muwaha Mulungu yakaliwuza yakatya, ‘Alo komukoca wuwe kutya nhawule?’ Nalyo licisi likapitula likatya, ‘Ane nobita nghawanoze wawizi wose wa Ahabu, walonje zo wulambi.’ Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakalitya licisi, ‘Ale wubite wukamukoca, nitumbiye vyono komusuma.’ ”

23 Kanyhi Mikaya yakonjeza yakatya, “Ne nghani yiyi, vyo yiweye. Muwaha Mulungu kawanoza wawizi wako wakulonjele zo wulambi. Ninga mwene Muwaha Mulungu yo yalonga nha vyono wongomele wokwagana!”

24 Hobaho muwizi Zedekiya yakamuzumhila Mikaya yakamutowa kofi mu wusu wakwe, akuno yakumutya, “Alu, 'gwe Mikaya, ku wulawa na nili muhe wo Muwaha Mulungu lono wukandeka, ho wulongane nagwe?”

25 Mikaya yakamupitula Zedekiya yakatya, “Agwe kowona, kolo weza kubita kwihundika mu cinyumba cimonga, ca mu vyumba vya ku mhilimo.”

26 Hobaho mutemi Ahabu yakalajiliza yumonga wa muli wetumami wakwe, yakatya, “Musolaje Mikaya, wumutegule kuli Amoni u muwaha we likaya, na kuli Yowashi u mwana wo mutemi.
27 Wuwalonjele wamudindile mu jela, wawe kumulisa mukate mukavu na malenga, sunga kono nilawuyila mholile.”

28 Nayo Mikaya yakamupitula yakatya, “Hono wuze wu wuye wupolile, Muwaha Mulungu yeze si yalongane nane! Holeka nyo wanhu wose, muhulice zono nonjile!”

U wufwe wa Ahabu

(2Zin 18:28-34)

29 Ale mutemi Ahabu wa Isilayeli hamonga no mutemi Yehoshafati wa Yuda, wakabita kuyihoma Lamoti ya Gileyadi.
30 Ahabu yakamulonjela Yehoshafati, yakamutya, “Alu lono ceza kubita ku wulugu, ane nokwipitula ninoze ndece kumanyika, ninga agwe wuvwale vivwalo vyako vyo wutemi.” Hodu Ahabu mutemi we Waisilayeli yakabita ku wulugu yepituye.

31 Nayo mutemi we yisi ya Siliya, yakawalajiliza wawaha we matuka go wulugu makumi madatu na mejete, nha vyono walece kumuhoma munhu yunji wo wose, langa wamuhomaje Ahabu du mutemi we Waisilayeli.
32 Ninga wene wali weza kumuwona Yehoshafati u mutemi wa Yuda, wakehwesa yo mutemi Ahabu, holeka wakanza kumuhoma. Ninga nayo Yehoshafati yali yeza kuzoza,
33 nhondo nawo ho wakamumanya vyono yo si mutemi wa Isilayeli, hodu wakawa kumuleka.
34 Ninga u musilikale yunji we wanhu wa Siliya, yakayitawika songa yakwe mu wupinde, yali yeza kuwukwega no kuyilecela songa kotya nha yakujela, na kumbe songa, yikamuguma Ahabu mutemi wa Isilayeli ha wulunzizo we vivwalo vyakwe vye cuma. Ahabu yakatya, “Wuwi, natamiswa!” Yakawa kumucema mono kawujendyaga mutuka wakwe wo wulugu, yakumutya, “Alu wusolaje, ciwuye ne nyuma ciwuce bahano ha ciligati yo wulugu, hambi nha natuga nghomele mbaha!”

35 No wihomi wukawa wuwaha muno mwi'zuwa lilyo. Ninga mutemi Ahabu wakamwegamika mu mutuka wakwe wo wulugu, akuno yawalanjile wanhu wa Siliya. Sakami nyinji yikalawila mu nghomele yakwe, sunga yikaligubika litende lyo mutuka wakwe wo wulugu, ne lizuwa lyali lyeza kufika cinyamihe, nayo yakawa kufwa.
36 Na lono lizuwa lyagwilaga, Waisilayeli wose, wakalonjelwa nha vyono, “Alenyi wanhu wose ciwuye ku zikwetu, munhu na kaya yakwe ne yisi yakwe!”

37 Hobaho, mutemi Ahabu yakawa yakondya kufwa. Muwili wakwe wakawudinghula, wakabita kuwuzika kwi'kaya lya Samaliya.
38 No mutuka wakwe wo wulugu, wukakwemwa ha cilamba ca Samaliya ne mabwa gakayinamha baho sakami yakwe, sunga ne wasenyha wehovujilaga baho, nha vyono yinha nghani yo Muwaha Mulungu yono yayilonga.

39 Ne zinghani zose zono mutemi Ahabu yetuma, hamonga ne cifo co wuzenji we likulu, lyono yakalihamba ne zimbali ze zinhembo, hamonga ne makaya gose gono yazenga, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.
40 Nayo Ahabu yaza kufwa, u mwanagwe Ahaziya, yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Yehoshafati u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 20:31–21:1)

41 Na mu mwaka we kane wo mutemi Ahabu wa Isilayeli, nayo Yehoshafati u mwana wa Asa, ho yamula kutema mu Yuda.

42 Yehoshafati yali yaficile myaka makumi madatu na mihano lono yakutema. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi, myaka makumi mejete na mihano. U nyina wa Yehoshafati, yacemwaga Azuba, u mwana we cicekulu wa Shilihi.
43 Nayo Yehoshafati, yaketuma ze ling'ani hali Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono sogwe Asa yali yenha. Ninga si yasuma kuhamwagula hanhu hose ho wukumbicilo we milugu yo wulambi mu vigongo, holeka wanhu wajendelela kulavya makumbiko gawo mumo, hamonga no kufusa wuvumba.
44 Mutemi Yehoshafati, yakawa yelamuye no mutemi wa Isilayeli.

45 Ne zinghani zose zono mutemi Yehoshafati yetuma, hamonga ne vilungo vyakwe, hamonga no wulugu wono yehoma, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Yuda.
46 Yakawawulaga wanhu walume wose wono wagonaga ne walume wayawo, hamonga ne wacekulu wasenyha, wono wetumaga mu vikumbicilo vye milungu yo wulambi, wono wali wakali wasigaye ku wulawa mu vilo lono sogwe Asa yali yakali yatemile.

47 Nayo yisi ya Edomu lulo yali yisina mutemi, langa yali yitemigwe no mutazi wo mutemi, mono yali muwikwe no mutemi wa Yuda.

48 Mutemi Yehoshafati, yakanoza ngalawa, ho zibite ku yisi ya Ofili kusola zahabu, ninga zingalawa zizo zikabenecela ha Eziyoni-gebeli, hamba si zafika na ku Ofili yaye.
49 Nayo mutemi Ahaziya yakelavya, ho yalavye wanhu wakwe, wabitane mu zingalawa zakwe, hamonga ne wanhu wa Yehoshafati. Ninga Yehoshafati si yayilumba yaye nghani yiyo.

50 Mutemi Yehoshafati, yakafwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vyo wutemi, mu kaya ya Dawudi. Na Yeholamu u mwanagwe yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Ahaziya u mutemi wa Isilayeli

51 Na mu mwaka we kumi na mupunghati wo wutemi wa Yehoshafati u mutemi wa Yuda, baho nayo Ahaziya u mwana wa Ahabu, ho yatemela, yakawa mutemi wa Isilayeli. Yakatema myaka myejete ku Samaliya.
52 Ninga yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, yakatyatya musambo mubi wo sogwe Ahabu, hamonga no nyina Yezebeli, no mutemi Yelobowamu, wono wawanoza Isilayeli wetume wubi.
53 Mwene nayo Ahaziya, yakamuhemba no kumwitumamila Baali. Yakamukoza likuwo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, nha vyono sogwe Ahabu yanoza.
