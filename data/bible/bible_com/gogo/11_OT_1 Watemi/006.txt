1 Watemi 6

Solomona yakuyizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu

1 Mutemi Solomona yakanza mulimo wo kuyizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu, lono yali yakondya kupuluta myaka magana mane na makumi munana, ku wulawa wanhu wa Isilayeli, lono wawucila ku Misili, mu mwaka we kane wo wutemi wakwe muli Isilayeli, mu mwezi we kejete, mwezi wa Zivu.
2 Ne Nyumba yono Solomona yamuzenjela Muwaha Mulungu, yali yina wutali wayo wa mugati mita makumi mejete na mupunghati, wugazi mita yicenda, no wutali wayo wo wubita kucanyha wali mita yikumi ne 'datu na nusu.
3 Ne likumbo lyali no wutali wayo wa mugati mita yine na nusu, no wugazi mita yicenda nho wugazi we hanhu hono helile.
4 Ne majinji ge Nyumba yo Mulungu, gali gena madilisha go kwinjiza wuzelu.
5 Na kunze ye majinji gose ge Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakazenga vinyumba, vipilime sunga na ku mhilimo yayo, ne viyumba vyose vyali vina wutali wo kubita kucanyha, mita nyejete na lobo.
6 Ne vinyumba vya hasi navyo, vyali vina mita nyejete na lobo wugazi wayo. Ne cinyumba ca ha ciligati, cali cina mita nyejete na lobo yidatu wugazi wayo, ne cinyumba ca hacanyha, mita yidatu wugazi wayo. Ne majinji ge Nyumba yo Mulungu yakagacepula wugazi kucanyha mbavu zose, vinyumba ho vikale mwi'jinji visina micici yayo.

7 Ne mabwe gose gono wazenjela Nyumba yo Mulungu, gaseseswaga kono wagahimbulaga du, ho halece kuwa ne zozo lya nyondo hamba nhemo, hamba citumamilwa co cose ca cuma, lono Nyumba yo Mulungu yazengwaga.

8 U mulango we cinyumba ca kusi, wukawikwa ku lubavu lwa kutakama ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, zikazengwa ne zi ngazi zo kubitila ku vinyumba vya kucanyha.
9 Mutemi Solomona, yakayizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu yakayikondya, yakayifunika ne mibici ne zimbawo zo muhagata.
10 Yakazenga vinyumba vili hadatu mu mhelo ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, ne vinyumba vyose vyali no wutali wa mita nyejete na lobo, navyo vikalunzwa hamonga ne Nyumba yo Mulungu kwe micici ye mibici yo muhagata.

11 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Solomona yakatya,
12 “Hono wibate wulajilize wangu hamonga ne malajilizo gangu gose, ale, nane nokunozeza zose nha vyono namwihuwiza sogo Dawudi.
13 Nane nokwikala hamonga ne wanhu wangu Waisilayeli, mumu mono Nyumba yangu yono wukunzenjela, nane si nowaleka kanyhi yaye.”

14 Solomona yakayizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu yakayikondeleza.

Vitumamilwa vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu

(2Zin 3:8-14)

15 Mugati ye majinji, yakagafunika na mbawo zo muhagata, ku wulawilila hasi ye nyumba, sunga ko kwi'jinji lya kucanyha. Na hasi yayo yakafunika na mbawo za mibeloshi.
16 Ne cinyumba ca hasi, cono cacemwa i Hanhu Hono Helile, helile muno, cikazengwa kumhilimo ye Nyumba yo Mulungu. Naco cali no wutali wa mita yicenda, cali citemanuywe ne libupa lya mbawo zo muhagata, zilawiliye hasi, sunga ko kucanyha.
17 Ne cinyumba ca kono mwande ye Hanhu Hono Helile, helile muno, cali no wutali wa mita yikumi na munana.
18 Nazo zizo mbawo zo muhagata, zali zihambigwe no wukakate we zimboga ne maluwa. Na mugati mose, mukahambwa ne zimbawo zo muhagata, ho mabwe galece kwijela.

19 Yakanoza cinyumba ca kugati mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, ho yawice mumo Lisanduku lye Cilagane.
20 Ne cinyumba cico, cali cina wutali wa mita yicenda, no wugazi wa mita yicenda, no wutali wo kubita kucanyha nawo mita yicenda, yakacihiliwa zahabu nengu. Naco cikumbicilo cali cifunicizwe na mbawo zo muhagata.
21 Na mugati ye Nyumba yo Mulungu mwali muhiliwe zahabu, mose, ne minyololo ya zahabu yikawikwa yikuninyina yidumulashe mulango we cinyumba cico ca kugati, cono naco cali cihiliwe zahabu.
22 Ne Nyumba yo Mulungu yose, mugati yali hiliwe zahabu, hamonga ne cikumbicilo ce Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno.

23 Yakanoza vihwani vya makelubi mejete ko mubici wo muzeyituni, yakagawika Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno, u wutali wayo limonga mita yine na nusu.
24 Ne vihwani vye makelubi gose, gakawa gehwanile wuwaha no wutali.
25 Limonga lina mabawa mejete, libawa limonga lina wutali wa mita nyejete na lobo,
26 no wutali wa ku wulawa libawa limonga sunga kwi'bawa liyagwe, wukawa mita yine na nusu.
27 Navyo vihwani vivyo vye makelubi vikawikwa akuno na akuno mugati ye Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno, ho mabawa gayo mejete gasume kwipamiza hagati ye cinyumba, ne mabawa ganji mejete gakapamiza lijinji.
28 Nago makelubi, gakahiliwa zahabu gose.

29 Ne lijinji lye cinyumba ciwaha, na lye cinyumba ca kugati, gose gakahambwa no wukakate we vihwani vye makelubi, ne mitende, hamonga ne maluwa kunze na ku gati.
30 Sunga na hasi du mu sakafu, mose mwali muhiliwe zahabu.

31 Wukanozwa mulango, wumonga we milango myejete ko mubici wo muzeyituni, wukawicilwa ha mulango we Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno, hamonga ne zimbawo nyejete zo wima ze zifulemu, na ye cipinga ya hacanyha yayo.
32 Ne milango nayo yikahambwa ko wukakate we vihwani vye makelubi, ne mitende, hamonga ne maluwa. Nawo mulango weneco, hamonga ne vihwani vye makelubi, ne mitende, vyose vikahiliwa zahabu.
33 Yakanoza kanyhi fulemu ko mubici wo muzeyituni nha vyono fulemu nongozi, yakanoza ku so mulango muwaha we nyumba mbaha yo wihangulizize.
34 Hakawa na milango yinji myejete yo kugodya, minozwe no mubici wo mubeloshi,
35 nayo nhavivyo yikahambwa no wukakate we vihwani vye makelubi, hamonga ne mitende, ne maluwa, yakayihiliwa zahabu mu wukakate.

36 Ne likumbo lya kugati, lyali lizenjigwe ha mwande ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, yipilimywe majinji gono gali mazenje micilili midatu, ya mabwe, na mucanyha yo mucilili monga monga we mabwe, wakajeleka mucilili wumonga wa mbawo zo muhagata sunga micilili yose vyono midatu ye mabwe.

37 No musinji we Nyumba yo Mulungu, wukasimywa mu mwezi we kejete, mwezi wa Zivu, mu mwaka we kane wo wutemi wa Solomona.
38 Na mu mwezi wo munana, mwezi wa Buli, wa mu mwaka we kumi na monga wo wutemi wa Solomona, ho Nyumba yo Mulungu yakondezya lukulu kuzengwa, nha vyono yasakwaga yiwe. Ale, Solomona yayizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu myaka mupunghati, nayo yikawa yakondya.
