1 Watemi 9

Mulungu yakwilajila kanyhi kuli Solomona

(2Zin 7:11-22)

1 Mutemi Solomona, yali yeza kukondya kuyizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne likulu, sunga na vyose vyono yasakaga yazenje,
2 Muwaha Mulungu yakelajila kuli Solomona lwe kejete, nha vyono yelajila kuli mwene, lono yali yali ku Gibeyoni.
3 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela yakatya, “Nakondya kuwuhulika wulombe wako, kanyhi nakondya kuyisiwanula Nyumba yangu yiyi yono wanzenjela, yiwe ho hanhu hono nowa kuhembelwa cibitila. Nane noyilanjilila no kuyidima vilo vyose.
4 Nagwe hono wunyitumamile kwe cogopo na ko wihuwilwa, nha vyono sogo Dawudi yanoza, na hono wuwukamise wulajilize wangu no kwituma zose nha vyono nakulajiliza,
5 nane nowufisiliza wihuwizo wangu wono namwihuwiziza sogo Dawudi, lono namulonjela vyono, Isilayeli wowa kutemwa ne wanagwe cibitilila.
6 Ninga gwegwe hambe wanago, hono mulece kunhyatya, na hono mulece kuwa na cihuliko kuli wulajilize wangu, hamonga ne malajilizo gangu gono nakupela, hanyuma muhembe milungu yinji,
7 ale, nane nowahamya wanhu wa Isilayeli niwawuse mu yisi yono nawapela. Noyileka sunga ne Nyumba yangu yiyi yono nakondya kuyisiwanula hawe hanhu hangu ho kuhembelwa. Ne wanhu wose hohose wowaseka Isilayeli no kuwatendela ko wubedegazi.
8 Sunga ne Nyumba yiyi yo Mulungu, yopituka yiwe yilundo lya matongo, ne wanhu wose wono wowa kupuluta baho, wowa kwiwuza wakutya, ‘Nhawule Muwaha Mulungu kayinoza nhavivi yisi yiyi ne Nyumba yiyi?’
9 Nawo wanhu wowa kupitula wakutya, ‘Ku soko vyono wamuleka Muwaha Mulungu u Mulungu wawo, mono yawawusa walezi wawo ku yisi ya Misili. Wakayipitucila milungu yinji no kuyitamamila no kuyihemba. Na yiyi yo soko yono yikuleka Muwaha Mulungu kawaletela wongomele wuwu.’ ”

Wilumbanize wa Solomona na Hilamu

(2Zin 8:1-2)

10 Mutemi Solomona yayizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu hamonga ne likulu myaka makumi mejete.
11 Nayo Mutemi Hilamu mutemi we yisi ya Tulo yo yamupela mibici yose yo muhagata, hamonga ne zahabu yose yono yasakaga so mulimo wo wuzenji. Ninga wuzenji wali weza kukondya, mutemi Solomona yakamupela Hilamu makaya gono gali gali mu yisi ya Galilaya.
12 Nayo Hilamu yakabita kugalanga makaya gago, ninga si yagenda yaye.
13 Hodu yakawa kumubitila Solomona yakamutya, “Alu ndugu yangu, aga go makaya mucici gono wukumhela!” Na ku soko yiyo, holeka hanhu baho hakali hakucemwa yisi ya Kabuli sunga nalu.
14 Hilamu yali yakondya kumutegulila Solomona zahabu ya wuzito wono wucizize kilo elufu yine.

Milimo yinji miwaha yono yetuma Solomona

(2Zin 8:3-18)

15 Mutemi Solomona, yewetumya kwe vilungo i wetuma milimo, mu wuzenji we Nyumba yo Mulungu hamonga ne likulu, no kumemya muhangha makombo gono gali gali kucilima ye likaya, hamonga no kuzenga magomawakali ge likaya. Kanyhi yakawetumamila so wuzenga makaya ga Hazoli, Megido hamonga na Gezeli.
16 Ninga mutemi wa Misili, yali yakondya kuyihoma Gezeli no kuyisola, yakawawulaga Wakanani wose i wekazi wa mwi'kaya, yakalipembela likaya no moto, hanyuma, yakawa kumupela mwanagwe we cicekulu likaya lilyo, nha cipegwa ce halusi lono yatolelwa na Solomona.
17 Nayo Solomona ho yakawa kulizenga kanyhi likaya lilyo lya Gezeli. Yaketumamila wanhu wakwe. Solomona yo yazenga ne Beti Holoni yono yili kusika
18 na Balati, na Tamali, mu nyika ya Yuda,
19 i makaya gono yagetumamilaga so wuwika vitumamilwa vyakwe. Go makaya gono yawikaga zifwalasi zakwe, ne matuka go wulugu, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyose vyono yasakaga so wuzenjela mu Yelusalemi, hamba mu Lebanoni, ne hanhu hanji hose du mu wutemi wakwe.
20 I wanhu wono wasigala, i Wakanani, wono Waisilayeli si wawawulaga lono wayisolela yisi yawo,
21 hamonga ne Waamoli, ne Wahiti, ne Wapelizi, ne Wahivi, ne Wayebusi wono wasigala, wakajendelela du kuwa wetumami sunga nalu. Ale wawo wo Solomona yawetumamilaga.
22 Ninga Solomona si yawanoza Waisilayeli wawe wetumami. Langa wene wetumaga mulimo wo wusilikale no kuwa walongola, ne wawaha we wasilikale, ne walongola we matuka go wulugu, hamonga ne wajendela zifwalasi.

23 Hali ho wali wawaha walanjilizi we milimo ya Solomona yu wuzenji, wali waficile magana mahano na makumi mahano, wono wemilizaga milimo hayo.

24 Solomona yakamemya muhangha, na mu makombo gono gali gali kucilima ye likaya, lono mugolece wakwe, u mwana we cicekulu wo mutemi wa Misili, yali yakondya kuhama mu kaya ya Dawudi, no kwinjila mwi'kulu lyono Solomona yamuzenjela. Hobaho ho yayizenjela na Milo.

25 Nayo Solomona yalavyaga nghanghulo yidatu mu mwaka, vilavywa vinyanywe, hamonga ne vilavywa vyo mulamu, mu cikumbicilo cono yamuzenjela Muwaha Mulungu. Yakawa yakufusa no wuvumba ku so Muwaha Mulungu. Nayo yakawa yakondya kuyizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu.

26 Mutemi Solomona, kanyhi yakanoza ngalawa ku Eziyoni-gebeli, yono yili behi na Eloti ha mhwani ya Agaba, mu yisi ya Edomu.
27 Mutemi Hilamu, yakawategula wanhu wakwe kuli Solomona, wono wali wazoweye kujendya ngalawa mwi'lenga, ho waketaze ne wanhu wa Solomona.
28 Wakajenda sunga wakafika ku Ofili, na kuko, wakasola zahabu yono yali yicizize wuzito wa kilo elufu yikumi na yine, wakamuletela Solomona.
