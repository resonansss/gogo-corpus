Luka 8

I Wacekulu wono wetyatya na Yesu

1 Hanyuma ya zizo, Yesu yakakola mu makaya na mu vitumbi, yakulonga no kuganula Luganuzi Luswanu lo wutemi wo Mulungu. Na awalya we likumi na wejete wali wali hamonga nayo.
2 Hamonga ne wacekulu wanji wono Yesu yawaponya macisi ne zinhamwa zinji, nawo wali wali hamonga nayo. Wawo wo wa Malyamu mono (yacemwaga Magadalena,) muli yuyo galawa macisi mupunghati.
3 Na Yowana u mugolece wa Cuza u mwitumami mulongola wa Helode, na Susana, na wanji wenji. Wacekulu wawo wawetumamilaga Yesu ne wejizwa wakwe mu sawo yawo weneco.

I Cihwanicizo co Mumiza mbeyu

(Mat 13:1-9; Mlk 4:1-9)

4 Lipuga liwaha lye wanhu, wekongholaga, na wanhu wenji wakawa kumuziza Yesu wakulawa mu matumbi gose. Nayo yakawalonjela cihwanicizo cici,
5 “U mumiza mbeyu yalawila kumiza mbeyu yakwe, na lono yamizaga, zinji zikalagala mu nzila; wakola nzila wakazikanyanjila, zindeje zikazilya.
6 Zinji zikalagala hono hogali mabwe, zikota ninga zikanyala, vyono zali zisina malenga.
7 Zinji zikalagala mu mabici ge mamivya, zikawilukana hamonga ne mamivya, gakazititiliza.
8 Zimbeyu zinji zikalagala mu muhangha muswanu, zikawiluka, zikalela mhece yigana.” Yaza kukondya kulonga zizo, yakalonga ne yizi yiwaha, yakatya “Holeka muhulice, hono mwina makutu.”

I Soko yo kwijiziza mu vihwanicizo

(Mat 13:10-17; Mlk 4:10-12)

9 Ne wejizwa wakwe wakamuwuza Yesu nhunizo ye cihwanicizo cico.
10 Yakawatya, “Anye mupegwa kumanya zono zihundicigwe zo wutemi wo Mulungu, ninga kuli wanji, vyo si yinha. Langa wene walonjelwaga mu vihwanicizo, hono wakuwona, ho walece kuwona, na hono wakuhulika, ho walece kwizuka.”

Yesu yakulolosa nhunizo ye cihwanicizo co Mumiza mbeyu

(Mat 13:18-23; Mlk 4:13-20)

11 Ale, ne nhunizo ye cihwanicizo cici nha yoyi. I mbeyu yo nghani yo Mulungu.
12 Zono zalagala mu nzila, zikulajila iwanhu wono wakuyihulika nghani, hanyuma Setani yakuza yakuyiwusa yiyo nghani mu zinhumbula zawo, ho walece kwihuwila no kupona.
13 Azilya zono zalagala hono hogali mabwe, zikulajila iwanhu wono wakuyihulika nghani no kuyibocela na nyemo. Ninga nha vyono zinha zimbeyu zizo, iwanhu wawo wakuwa wasina milela, wono wehuwilaga kwa cidaha du, na kolo wakujelwa, wakuwuya ne nyuma.
14 Na zono zalagala mu mabici ge mamivya, co cihwanicizo ce wanhu wono wakuyihulika nghani, ninga mu wujendelele wawo, wakuzinjilizwa ne mahwele ne zisawo, no wiyendeze we mikalile yiyi, nawo siwakulela mikalo yono yikangaye.
15 Nazo zono zalagala mu muhangha muswanu wo wanhu wono wakuyihulika nghani, no kuyikamisa kwa nhumbula swanu na ye cihuliko. Nawo wakulela mikalo mu wukangalile.

I Tala yono yigubicizwe mu nhoto

(Mlk 4:21-25)

16 “Wanhu si wakolelezaga tala no kuyigubiciza, hamba kuyiwika mwi'vungu lye citanda; langa wayiwikaga ha wuwiko wayo, wanhu wono wakwinjila mugati, wawone wuzelu wayo.
17 Nghani yo yose yono hundikwe yolokwanzalikwa, na ze cinyele zolomanyika, no kulawila kuzelu.
18 Holeka mwiyihenje vyono mukuhulika. Hambi mono yena, nha kopegwa; na mono yasina, kosolelwa sunga na cono yakutya yena naco.”

U Nyina ne wanduguze wa Yesu

(Mat 12:46-50; Mlk 3:31-35)

19 Baho, u nyina ne wanduguze wa Yesu wakamuziza, ninga si wasuma kwiwona nayo so winji we wanhu.
20 Yesu yakalonjelwa, vyono u nyina ne wanduguze wali kunze, wakusaka kumuwona.
21 Ninga Yesu yakawalonjela wanhu wose, “Yaya ne wandugu zangu, wo walya wono wakuyihulika nghani yo Mulungu no kuyitumama.”

Yesu yakuyitacila mbeho ne lilenga vinyamale

(Mat 8:23-27; Mlk 4:35-41)

22 Kukawa mwizuwa linji, Yesu yakenjila mu ngalawa mwene ne Wejizwa wakwe; yakawatya, “Cilowoce ku nhumbi nzagwe ye lilamba.” Wakatuluka.
23 Ninga lono wajendelelaga mu ngalawa, mwene Yesu yakebatwa na nhongo, yakagona. Likatima likukunzo yiwaha lye mbeho mwilamba: malenga gakawa kwanza kwinjila mu ngalawa, wakawa wali mu mbali mbaha.
24 Wejizwa wawalya wakamubitila Yesu, wakamusinhusa wakutya “Muwaha, Muwaha, ceza kufwa!” Yesu yakasinhuka, yakavitacila imbeho ne mawingo mawaha ge malenga, gakatula, hakanyamala twii.
25 Yakawatya i wejizwa wakwe “Wihuwilo wenyu wuli hayi 'mbi? Ninga wene wakezina no kogopa, wakeyitya, Alo munhuci yuyu, sunga i mbeho ne malenga yakuvilonjela, vinyamale, navyo vikumuhulika!”

Yesu yakumuponya munhu mono yali yena macisi

(Mat 8:28-34; Mlk 5:1-20)

26 Yesu ne wejizwa wakwe wakajendelela no muhinzo wakafika mu yisi ya Wagelasene, yono vidomile na Galilaya ku nhumbi ye lilamba.
27 Nayo Yesu yaza kutima mu'si viketang'anila na munhu monga mono yali yena macisi, yakulawa mwi'kaya, u munhu yuyo mu vilo vinji si yavwalaga myenda, hamba si yekalaga mu kaya, langa yekalaga mu vitenjelo.
28 Yaza kumuwona Yesu, yakazoza, yakagwa hali mwene, yakabalaluka na lizi yiwaha yakatya, “Alo wina nghanici nase, Yesu go Mwana wo Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye Muli Vyose? Nghukunena wulece kungaza.”
29 Hambi nha yalilajiliza licisi lilawanize muli munhu. Hambi nha lyamwibataga kenji. Hamba yiwe wanhu wamuwikaga mugati no kumuwopa ne vyuma; ninga yavidumulaga viwopelo kali kenji, ne licisi lilyo lyamuwinjilaga ku nyika.
30 Yesu yakamuwuza, litagwa lyako gwegwe nani? Yakatya litagwa lyangu nene “Mhuga,” vyono macisi menji gali gamwinjiye munhu yuyo.
31 Gago macisi gakamunena Yesu yalece kugalajiliza gawuce no kwajilila mwikombo lyono lisina mudumo.

32 Kwali ne 'tewa lya ngubi nyinji, likudima ha mwejelele we litunda. Ale gago macisi gakamunena Yesu yagakombole genjile muli zene. Nayo Yesu yakagakombola.
33 Imacisi gakalawa muli munhu, gakenjila mu zingubi, nazo zingubi zikavulumucila mwilamba, zikafwila mumo mu malenga.
34 I wadimi waza kuziwona zono ziweye, wakatiza, wakabita kuwalonjela wanhu wa mwi'kaya na mu'si.
35 Wanhu wakaza wazilanje zono ziweye; wakafika kuli Yesu, wakamuwona munhu mono yali yalavywa macisi, yavwalile myenda, yena ne mahala gakwe; yekaye ha magulu ga Yesu, wakogopa muno.
36 Nawo wono waziwona zizo, wakawaloloseza wanhu wawo vyono munhu yuyo yaponyigwe.
37 Ne wanhu wose we yisi ya Wagelasene wakebatwa no woga wuwaha, holeka wakamunena Yesu yawuce, yabite. Nayo Yesu yakenjila mu ngalawa, yakawuka.
38 Ninga u munhu mono yalavywa macisi, yakamunena viwezane nayo, ninga Yesu si yamulumba, langa yakamutya.
39 “Wuwuye kwenyu, wukalolose zimbaha zono Mulungu yakutendeye.” Yakabita, yakaganula hanhu hose mwi'kaya lilyo, zimbaha zono yatendelwa na Yesu.

Mwana we cicekulu wa Yayilo no mucekulu yunji, wakuponywa

(Mat 9:18-26; Mlk 5:21-43)

40 Nayo Yesu yaza kuwuya ku nhumbi nzagwe ye lilamba, wanhu wenji wakamubocela na nyemo, hambi nha wamulindilaga wose.
41 Baho yakaza munhu, litagwa lyakwe Yayilo, nayo yali muwaha we nhang'anilo yo wuhembelo, yakagwa ha magulu ga Yesu, yakamunena yaze kwakwe;
42 hambi nha yali yena mwana wa cicekulu we ciwene, kotya wa myaka yikumi na myejete, nayo yali yeza behi no kufwa. Ninga Yesu lono yabitaga wanhu wenji wakamumemela.

43 Na mumo mwi'puga lye wanhu, mwali na mucekulu yunji mono yali yena wutamwa wo wuzaze we sakami myaka yikumi na myejete, [nayo hamba yawe yali yetumamila sawo yakwe yose mu waganga,] ninga hasina hamba yumonga mono yasuma kumuponya.
44 U mucekulu yuyo, yakaziza ku mugongo wa Yesu, yakapamiza limhembo lye civwalo cakwe, na hobaho du, u wuzaze wakwe we sakami wukaduma kamonga.
45 Yesu yakawuza, “Alo nani mono yamhamize?” Wakakana wose. Nayo Petelo yakatya, “Muwaha, wanhu wenji wakupilime no kukonyeceza.”
46 Ninga Yesu yakatya, “Ho yuli munhu mono yamhamize, hambi nha manyile vilungo vilawa muli nene.”
47 No mucekulu yaza wona vyono si kosuma kwihundika, yakelajila akuno yakucicima so woga, yakagwa hali Yesu, yakalolosa hali wanhu wose cono cilecile yakumupamiza Yesu, na vyono yakondya kuponywa kamonga.

48 Yesu yakamutya, “Go muhinza, wihuwilo wako wukuponya, wubite wupolile.”
49 Na lono Yesu yali yakali kulonga, Yayilo yakaletelwa nghani kulawa ku kaya kwakwe, vyono, “U mwanago yakondya kufwa, wulece kumugaza Mwijizi.”
50 Ninga Yesu yaza kuzihulika zizo, yakamulonjela Yayilo yakatya, “Wulece kogopa. Wihuwile du, nayo mwanago kopona.”
51 Yaza kufika kukaya ya Yayilo si yamukombola yunji wenjilane nayo, langa Petelo, na Yohana, na Yakobo, ne walezi wo muhinza yuyo du.
52 Wanhu wose, walilaga, ku soko yo mwana yuyo. Ninga Yesu yakawatya, “Mulece kulila, hambi mwana nha siyafwiye, langa yagonile nhongo du.”
53 Nawo wakamuseka, hambi nha wali wamanyile vyono yafwiye.
54 Ninga Yesu yakamwibata muwoko, yakacema yakutya “Go muhinza winuce.”
55 Muhe wo muhinza wukamuwuyila, yakenuka kamonga, Yesu yakalajiliza wamupele ndigwa.
56 Walezi wakwe wakezina, ninga Yesu yakawalajiliza, walece kumulonjela munhu kuli zizo zono zijeye.
