Yelemiya 39

U wugwe wa Yelusalemi

1 Na mu mwezi we likumi, wa mu mwaka we licenda, lono Zedekiya yali yatemile mu Yuda, Nebukadineza u mutemi wa Babiloni, yakawa kuza ku Yelusalemi, hamonga ne lipuga lyakwe lyose lye wasilikale, wakawa kuyihoma Yelusalemi.
2 Na mwi'zuwa lye licenda, lya mu mwezi we kane, wa mu mwaka we likumi na monga, wa mu wutemi wa Zedekiya, baho ho majinji ge likaya gakumulanjilwa.

3 (Na lono Yelusalemi yali yakondya kutekwa, i wetumami wawaha wose wo mutemi wa Babiloni, wakawa kubita, wakawa kutandala ha mulango wa haciligati. Ne wetumami wawo, wali wali hamonga ne wanhu awano. Neligali Salezeli, na Samugali Nebo u muwaha we wapaswe na Selisekimu, hamonga na Neligali Salezeli yunji kanyhi.)

4 Nayo mutemi Zedekiya hamonga ne wasilikale wakwe wose, wali weza kuziwona zono zikuwaziza, hobaho wakajela kwiponya, watize mwi'kaya, mwi'pango lye cilo. Hodu wakawa kutuluka cilo cose, wakakolela ku nzila ye busitani yo wutemi, wakawa kubita, wakakolela ku mulango wono wali wina majinji mejete, wakatiziza ku lubavu lwa kwi'lolo lya Yoludani.
5 Ninga nawo i wasilikale we Cibabiloni, wakawatyatya du, sunga wakawagana behi na Yeliko. Wakawa kumusola u mutemi Zedekiya, wakawa kumutegula kuli mutemi Nebukadineza, kwi 'kaya lya Libula, mu yisi ya Hamati, kono yali mwi'pango lilyo. Nayo Nebukadineza, yakawa kumuling'aniza Zedekiya yawulagwe.
6 Ninga Nebukadineza, yakamula kuwawulaga i wana wa Zedekiya, baho ha Libula, yakawawulajila ha meso du ge sogo yawo. Yakawawulaga hamonga ne wetumami wawaha wose wa Yuda, mwi'pango lilyo.
7 Hanyuma ya zizo, yakawa kumusola Zedekiya, yakawa kumukopozola meso gakwe gose, yakawa kumuwopa ne manyolo, yakawa kumutegula ku Babiloni.
8 Nawo i wasilikale we Cibabiloni, kono nyuma, wakawa kulikongoga likulu, hamonga ne zikaya ze wanhu wose, wakawa kugakumulanga ne majinji ge likaya lya Yelusalemi.
9 Hanyuma ya zizo nayo Nebuzaladani, mono yali yo musilikale muwaha, nayo yakawa kuwasola i watekwa, mu wanhu wono wali wakali wasigaye mwi'kaya, yakawategula ku Babiloni, hamonga ne wanhu wono wali watizizi, wono wali wasigaye wono welunza nayo.
10 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, yawasigaza wanji mumo mu yisi ya Yuda, ko muno i wanhu wono wali waciwa muno, wono wali wasina hamba cinji, na mwi'zuwa lilyo du yakawa kuwagawila i migunda ye mizabibu ne migunda yo kulima.

Yelemiya yakulecelwa

11 Lulo, u mutemi Nebukadineza, yakamulajiliza Nebuzaladani, u muwaha we wasilikale, yakamutya,
12 “Alu, wubite wukamusole Yelemiya, wumukamise viswanu, wulece hamba kumunozeza nghani 'mbi yo yose, langa wumunozezaje du nha vyono mweneco kowa kusaka wumunozeze.”
13 Ale baho, yuyo Nebuzaladani, hamonga ne wetumami wayagwe wanji, nha cinji wa Nebushazibani, na Neligali Salezeli, na wetumami wanji wo mutemi wa Babiloni,
14 wakawa kuza kusola 'ne, mwi'kumbo lya mwi'kulu, wakawa kumbika mu wulanjilizi wa Gedaliya, u mwana wa Ahikamu, kanyhi yo mwizukulu wa Shafani, mono yandima sunga yakafisa ko ku kaya mholile, nghawa kwikala hamonga ne wanhu wangu.

U witumbilo wa Ebedimeleki

15 Nane Yelemiya, lono nali nghali ndindiywe mwi'kumbo lya mwi'kulu, u Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela
16 vyono mbite nghamulonjele Ebedimeleki u Muetiyopiya, nha vyono yalonjile u Muwaha Mulungu, we mihanga, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli. Nghawa kumubitila Ebedimeleki nghawa kumulonjela nghatya vyono, u Muwaha Mulungu katya nhavi, “Lanje! Ane nolilipiciza likaya lili nha vyono nalonga, hamba si nolimotya yaye. Na kolo zizi zikwijela, nagwe go mweneco koloziwona kwe meso gako.
17 Ninga ane, no Muwaha Mulungu nolokuponya, hamba si kolotajilwa na mu miwoko ye wanhu wono wukuwogopa yaye.
18 Nolokukamisa, nagwe hamba si kolowulagwa yaye. Kolokwiponya wumi wako, vyono wali wunyitumbiye nene. Vyo nghutya ane, no Muwaha Mulungu.”
