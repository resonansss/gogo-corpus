Yelemiya 27

Yelemiya yakuvwala lubano

1 Mu mwamulizo wo wuteme wo mutemi Zedekiya u mutemi wa Yuda, u mwana wa Yosiya, u Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela,
2 niyinozeze no mweneco lubano lwa viwopelo vya mikowa, hamonga ne mabici gayo, nivwice mu singo yangu.
3 Hanyuma kanyhi, yakanonjela mbite nghawalonjele zono yanhumile, i watemi wa Edomu, na Mowabu, na Amoni, na Tulo, hamonga na Sidoni. Niwalonjele mu nzila ya wono watumwa, wono wazaga ku Yelusalemi kuli mutemi Zedekiya.
4 U Muwaha Mulungu, we mihanga u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, yanonjela nha vyono, niwalajilize wawo wabite wakawalonjele i watemi wawo, zono Muwaha Mulungu yalonjile nhavi,
5 “Mumanye vyono yo nene mono nayiwumba yinze kwe vilungo vyangu, hamonga no munhu, ne vilimu vyose vyono vikaye mono'nze. Nane nimupelaga munhu wo wose nha vyono nyendile nene no mweneco.
6 Kanyhi yo nene mono nagawika makungugo gaga gose mu miwoko yo mwitumami wangu Nebukadineza, u mutemi wa Babiloni. Nghavinoza ne vilimu vyose vya mu mbago vimwitumamile mwene.
7 Sunga ne makungugo gose golomwitumamila mweneco. Golomwitumamila sunga no mwanagwe, sunga no mwizukuluwe. Sunga kono vilaficila i vilo vyo wugwe we likungugo lyakwe, nalyo likungugo lyakwe ho lyolowetumamila i makungugo gono gali vilungo hamonga ne watemi wawaha.

8 “Ninga hono haze hejele likungugo linji lyono si lyolosaka litemwe no mwene Nebukadineza, nane nololilipiciza likungugo lilyo no wulugu, ne nzala, hamonga ne matamwa, sunga kolo mutemi Nebukadineza yawe yakondya kulyongomezeza lukulu.
9 Holeka 'nye wayangu, mulecele lukulu kuwahuliciza wawo wawizi wenyu, hamba kuli munhu wo wose mono yakwiyitya nha vyono kosuma kuwila za ku mwande, mu nzila ye zindoto, hamba mu nzila yo wucemeleza myuhe ye wanhu wono wafwiye, hamba mu nzila yo wuhawi. Wawe kumulonjelenyi nha vyono mulece kwiyilavya kuli mutemi Nebukadineza u mutemi wa Babiloni.
10 Holeka mumanye nha vyono i wanhu wawo, wakumukonjenyi du bwete, ho wamwambuzenyi muze musolwe mu yisi yenyu, no kutegulwa kutali. Nane nize nimuwinjenyi mu yisi yenyu, mubite mukongomele.
11 Langa likungugo lyo lyose, lyono lyolokwilavya mu miwoko ya Nebukadineza u mutemi wa Babiloni, no kumwitumamila, nane nololileka likungugo lilyo likale mu yisi yalyo lyeneco, liyilime no kwikala mumo. Vyo nghutya, ane no Muwaha Mulungu.”

12 Nane Yelemiya, nghamulonjela Zedekiya u mutemi we yisi ya Yuda, zinghani zizo zose nha vyono zinha, nghamutya nhavi, “Alu 'gwe go mutemi Zedekiya, wiwikaje mu miwoko ya Nebukadineza, u mutemi wa Babiloni. Wumwitumamile hamonga ne wanhu wakwe, holeka nagwe kowumipa.
13 Alo socici mukusaka mwiyambuze nyo weneco, hamonga ne wanhu wako mufwile mu wulugu, hamba mu nzala, hamba mu matamwa? Hambi nha vyo yateye u Muwaha Mulungu, vyono zizo zo zolyagana likungugo lyo lyose, lyono lyolema kwiwika mu miwoko ya Nebukadineza, u mutemi wa Babiloni.
14 Holeka 'gwe mutemi, wulecele lukulu kuwahulicila, wawo wawizi wono wakukutya vyono, wulece kwiyilavya kuli Nebukadineza u mutemi wa Babiloni. Hambi nha wakukukonga du bwete.
15 Hambi u Muwaha Mulungu mweneco, nha yalonga nha vyono, mwene si yawatumile yaye. Langa wakuwila zo wulambi du mwi'tagwa lyakwe. Ho yamuwinjilenyi kutali, mubite mukawulaganwe, hamonga ne wawizi wawo wono wakumulonjelenyi zinghani zo wulambi.”

16 Hanyuma nghawa kuwalonjela nawo i wakohani hamonga ne wanhu wose, zono Muwaha Mulungu yalonga, nghawalonjela nawo nghatya nhavi, “Ale, nanye mulecele lukulu kuwahuliciza i wawizi wono wakumutyenyi nha vyono, i viya vye Nyumba yo Mulungu, vyologomolwa kanyhi ku wulawa ku Babiloni. Wakumukonjenyi bwete.
17 Holeka mulece kuwahulicila! Anye langa mwiyilavye mu miwoko yo mutemi wa Babiloni du, ho muwumipe! Alo socici lo, mulyambuze likaya lili, liwe malundo ga matongo?
18 Nawo hono wawe wawizi mbeka, na hono nghani yangu yiwe yili muli wene, ale, walombe kuli'ne, ane no Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga ndece kuvikombola i viya vyono vyasigala mu Nyumba yangu, na mu nyumba yo mutemi wa Yuda, na mwi'kaya lya Yelusalemi, vilece kusolwa no kutegulwa ku Babiloni.”

19 (Vyono mu vilo vyono mutemi Nebukadineza, yamusolela Yehoyakimu u mutemi we yisi ya Yuda, u mwana wa Yehoyakimu, no kumutegula ku Babiloni, hamonga ne wawaha wose wa Yuda na wa Yelusalemi.
20 Siyasoleleza yaye ne masumbili, ne litanji lye shaba lye malenga, ne mikokoteni, hamonga ne viya vinji vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.)

21 “Ale, lu muhulicize, ane no Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga ane no Mulungu wa Isilayeli, zono nghutya, kuli ze viya vye Nyumba yo Mulungu vyono vyasigala mu Nyumba yo Mulungu na mu nyumba ya mutemi wa Yuda, na mwi'kaya lya Yelusalemi.
22 Navyo vyolokwikala kuko du, sunga mwi'zuwa lyono nene no mweneco nilavikumbucilila. Ale baho nhondo, ho nolovigomelela kanyhi, no kuviwika hanhu bahano. Vyo nghutya, ane no Muwaha Mulungu.”
