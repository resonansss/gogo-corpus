Yelemiya 45

U wihuwizo wo Mulungu kuli Baluku

1 Na mu mwaka we kane wo wutemi wa Yehoyakimu u mwana wa Yosiya, lono yali yatemile mu yisi ya Yuda. Baluku yakawa kuzandika zinghani zose zono namulonjelezaga. Nghamulonjeleza,
2 zose zono u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, yazilonga yakutya, “Gwe Baluku,
3 agwe wukutya ti, Woyi he! Hambi u Muwaha Mulungu nha yandeka! Nalu kanyonjelezeza wukalale ne magayo. Sunga nane luno neza kusakala so wugule wangu, hamba si nghuwona wuswanu yaye.”

4 “Ninga ane no Muwaha Mulungu, nghuzimwagula zono nazizenga, kanyhi nghuzikulanga na zono nazihadika. Nane nozinoza zizi, mono yisi yose.
5 Alu agwe Baluku, wukwisacila 'gwe zinghani zimbaha? Ng'o ye, wulece kunoza nhavivyo. Hambi 'ne nha noleta wongomezwe wuwaha muli wanhu wose, langa agwe go mweneco wumi wako wopona, ho hose hono kobita. Vyo nghutya, ane no Muwaha Mulungu.”
