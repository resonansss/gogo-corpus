Yelemiya 40

Yelemiya yakwikalana na Gedaliya

1 U Muwaha Mulungu yakalongana nane kanyhi mwi'pango lono Nebuzaladani u muwaha we wasilikale, yali yakondya kundecela kuko ku Lama. Mu madaha gono nali nasolwa nategulwa kuko ku Lama, akuno mbopigwe ne manyolo, hamonga ne wanhu wanji wa ku Yelusalemi na Yuda, wono nawo wasolwa nha wafungwa, no kutegulwa ku Babiloni.
2 Yuyo u muwaha we wasilikale, yakasola yakanhegula ha cibembo, yakanonjela yakatya nhavi, “U Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako, yakondya kuyogofya yisi yiyi ne mabi gaga.
3 Nalu yakondya kwituma no kufisiliza zose zono yalonga. Na zizi zose ziwagana, ku soko i wanhu wako wetuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, no kumubedegala mwene.
4 Ninga ane luno, nghukuwopolela zuwali i manyolo gono wuwopigwe mu ciwino cako, nghulece wuli mwelecele. Nagwe wuhagule go mweneco, hono wuwe kusaka cibitane nane ku Babiloni, ale cibite, nane nokukamisa viswanu. Na hono wuwe si wendile cibitane nane, ale hodu, hambi yisi yose yiyi, nha yili mu mawoko gako, nagwe kosuma kuhagula no kubita wukekale hanhu ho hose hono kokwenda gwegwe go mweneco.”

5 Na lono nali nghali si namupitula, mwene Nebuzaladani yakanyitya kanyhi, “Alu wubite kuli Gedaliya u mwana wa Ahikamu, kanyhi yo mwizukulu wa Shafani, u munhu mono u mutemi wa ku Babiloni, yamuwika yawe yo mulongola mu makaya ga Yuda. Wubite wukekalane nayo, muwe hamonga ne wanhu. Hamba hono wende gwegwe, kosuma kuhagula hanhu ho hose du, hono kosaka gwegwe kwikala.” Hanyuma ya zizo, hodu yakawa kumhela zindigwa, hamonga ne vipegwa, yakawa kunghombola, nane nghawa kubita.
6 Nane nghawa kubita kuli Gedaliya ku Mizipa, nghawa kwikalana nayo, cikawa hamonga ne wanhu, wono wasigala mumo mu'si.

Gedaliya mulongola we yisi ya Yuda

(2Wat 25:22-24)

7 Ninga hali ho wali wetumami wanji, hamonga ne wasilikale wanji we Ciyuda, wono si wali wendile kwiyilavya kuli Wababiloni. Holeka wali weza kuhulika nha vyono u mutemi wa Babiloni yamunozize Gedaliya yawe yo mulongola we yisi ya Yuda, kanyhi nha vyono kamunoza yawe yo mulanjilizi wa muli wanhu waciwa wose wono wasigaye, wono si wasolwa no kutegulwa ku Babiloni.
8 Hobaho, Isimayeli mwana wa Netaniya, na Yohanani u mwana wa Kaleya, na Selaya u mwana wa Tanhumeti, ne wana wa Efayi, munya Netofa, na Yezaniya munya Maka, hamonga wose, wakawa kumubitila Gedaliya ku Mizipa, hamonga ne wanhu wawo.
9 Nayo Gedaliya yakawalonjela yakatya, “Ane nghumwilahizenyi nha vyono, anye musina soko yo yose yo kogopa kwilavya no kuwetumamila Wababiloni. Anye, mwikalaje du mu yisi yiyi, mujendelele du kumwitumamila u mutemi wa Babiloni, na zose zowa swanu du muli'nye.
10 Langa ane nojendelela kwikala bahano du, ha Mizipa, ho niwe mulonji wenyu, kolo Wababiloni wakuciziza. Ninga anye, mosuma kunoza divayi nyinji no kuyiwika ciwutwa, hamonga ne mikalo, ne mafuta. Mujendelele kwikala du mu vitumbi vyenyu vyono mwikaye.”
11 Nawo i Waisilayeli wose wono wekalaga ku Mowabu, na ku Amoni, wali weza kuhulika nha vyono u mutemi wa Babiloni kakondya kuwakombola Waisilayeli wanji wasigale, wekale mu yisi ya Yuda, kanyhi na vyono kamunoza Gedaliya yawe yo mulongola wawo.
12 Hodu, wose wakawa kwihanguliza, mu wulawa mu ma'si gono wali weyagaliye wakawa kuwuya ku yisi ya Yuda. Wakawa kumuziza Gedaliya, kuko ku Mizipa. Wakakonghola divayi nyinji muno hamonga ne mikalo ye libahu minji muno muno.

Gedaliya yakuwulagwa

(2Wat 25:25-26)

13 Na hanyuma ya zizo, Yohanani, hamonga ne wawaha wose we wasilikale, wono si wavumila kwilavya no kuwetumamila Wababiloni, hobaho wakawa kumuziza Gedaliya kuko ku Mizipa,
14 wakawa kumutya, “Alu mwene agwe Gedaliya, hongo si wumanyile nha vyono, u mutemi Balisi we yisi ya Amoni, kamutuma Isimayeli yaze yakuwulaje?” Ninga mwene Gedaliya hamba si yehuwila ng'o.
15 Hamonga na zizo, Yohanani yakamulonjela cinyele yakamutya, “Gwe Gedaliya nghukulomba wunghombole, mbite nghamuwulaje Isimayeli, kanyhi hasina hamba munhu monga mono komanya vyono nani mono yamuwulaje. Alu sokoci yene yono yoleka mwene Isimayeli yakombolwe yaze yakuwulaje 'gwe? Ku soko hono nghani yiyi yitumwe, yowambuza i Wayahudi wose wono wakondya kwikongholela hali'gwe, weyagale kanyhi ako na ako. Ne nghani yiyo, yowambuza i wanhu wose wono wasigaye mu yisi ya Yuda, watuje magayo mawaha muno.”

16 Ninga mwene Gedaliya yakapitula yakatya, “Agwe Yohanani, wulecele lukulu kwituma nghani yiyo! Hambi zose zono wukumulonga Isimayeli, nha si za mbeka yaye.”
