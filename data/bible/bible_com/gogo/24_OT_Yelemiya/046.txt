Yelemiya 46

Wusumwe we yisi ya Misili, ha Kalikemishi

1 U Muwaha Mulungu, yakanonjela za we makungugo,
2 yakanziza yisi ya Misili. Zinghani ze lipuga lye wasilikale wa Neko, u mutemi wa Misili, wono Nebukadineza u mutemi wa Babiloni, yawasuma ha Kalikemishi, behi ne likolongo lya Efulata, mu mwaka we kane wa Yehoyakimu lono yali mutemi we yisi ya Yuda.

3 “I wawaha we Wamisili wakazoza wakutya,

‘Ale, mwiwikanizaje ne zingula zenyu,

mubite ku wulugu!

4 Mutandice zifwalasi zenyu Mwime micilili,

muvwale ne zikofiya zenyu ze cuma!

Munole ne migoha yenyu!

Musoleleze hamonga ne vitwalo vyenyu!’ ”

5 Kanyhi u Muwaha Mulungu yakuwuza yakutya,

“Alu, yo nghanici kanyhi yiyi yono nghuyiwona?”

“Lanje ta! Weza kuwuya ne nyuma so woga.

Ne wasilikale wawo weza kuwingwa,

wakondya kusumwa so woga,

nalu wakutiza wamanyhile muno muno.

Hamba si wakwilenga kanyhi kunyuma.

6 Ninga wawo wono wakutiza, hamba si wotiga yaye,

ne wasilikale hamba si wokwiponya yaye.

Nako ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, he kolongo lya Efulata,

wokobala, wogwa.

7 Hamba nani lo mono kokwinyasula nhe likolongo lya Nile,

hamba kotya nhe likolongo lyono lilapuce malenga galyo?

8 Yo yiyo yisi ya Misili,

yo yikwinyasuliza hacanyha nhe likolongo lya Nile,

nhe likolongo lyono lilapuce malenga galyo ko ku nyenje.

Nayo yisi ya Misili yikalonga yikatya,

‘Ane nokwiyinula sunga niyigubice yinze yose.

Nogongomeza makaya gose,

hamonga ne wanhu wayo wono wekaye mu makaya gago.

9 Ale, muzilonjele zifwalasi zituluce,

nago i magali go wulugu gakwegwe!

Muwategulaje wasilikale,

ne wanhu wa Kushi na Libiya,

wadinghuye ne zingula zawo,

nawo i wanhu wanghula ku wuguma no 'pinde,

wakulawilila ku Lidiya.’ ”

10 I zuwali, lyo lizuwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu,

we mihanga, lyono kokwilipiciziza.

Izuwali, kokwilipiciza lilipicizo mu walugu wakwe.

No mumaje wakwe wowalya sunga wigute,

woying'wa ne sakami yawo, sunga wuce.

Hambi izuwali, u Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga,

nha yakuwalavya walugu wakwe makumbiko,

he kolongo lya Efulata, ku lubavu lwa kusumuka.

11 Nawo i wanhu wa Misili,

wakubita ku yisi ya Gileyadi kulondola miti!

Kumbe ne miti yenyu yiyo,

hamba si yikwiliye cinji hamba si yosuma kumuponyenyi yaye.

12 Nago makungugo nha gazihulika ze cevu cenyu,

ne wanhu wose wakondya kuhulika vyono mukulila.

Ne wasilikale wakwikobaza munhu na muyagwe,

nawo wawa kugwizana hasi wose.

U wuze wa Nebukadineza

13 Nayo Nebukadineza u mutemi wa Babiloni,

lono yaziza ho yayihome Misili,

u Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya,

14 “Ale, muyanzaje nghani yiyi, mu makaya gose ga Misili,

na mu Migidodi, na mu Mempisi sunga na mu Tapenesi,

‘Muwalonjele mutye wewikanize, weyidime weneco.

Hambi i vinhu vyenyu vyose,

nha vyokongomezezwa mu wulugu.

15 Alo nhawulenyi kanyhi u mulungu wenyu Apisi we vilungo,

yakondya kugwa?

U Muwaha Mulungu yo yamubamiliza hasi!’

16 Sunga ne wasilikale wenyu,

nha wakondya kwikobala, nalu wagwa hasi,

nalu wakulongana weneco wose wakwiyitya,

‘Nye, alenyi musunhe!

Alenyi ciwuye ku zikaya ku wanhu wetu,

ho ciwutize u mumaje wo mulugu!’

17 “Ale, mumupele Falawo u mutemi wa Misili litagwa yipya,

Yacemwe ‘Nghabuso,

Mono yakondya kucitaga cilejeho cakwe.’

18 Ane yo nene u Muwaha Mulungu, we mihanga,

yo nene mutemi kanyhi yo nene Mulungu mumi.

Nha vyono yinha minala ye litunda lya Taboli,

yigasulutiye matunda kucanyha,

na vyono litunda lya Kalimeli,

nalyo lijeye mucanyha ye lilenga,

Ale, ne vilungo vya mono yakukuhoma,

vyo vyowa vinha.

19 Holeka mwiwikanizaje ku wusolwe nha wafungwa,

anye nyo wanhu wa Misili!

Hambi Mempisi nha yowa nha nyika, howa matongo,

howa hanhu hono hamba yunji si kusuma kwikala mumo.

20 Misili yihwanile ndama swanu muno,

yono yakondya kuhomwa no wuhodanji

wono wukulawa ku lubavu lwa kusumuka.

21 Sunga ne wasilikale wakwe wo kulipwa, watimbile du,

wenha ndama si wakusuma kwina no kwihoma,

langa wose wawuya ne nyuma no kutiza.

Hambi lizuwa lyawo lyo wongomezwe,

nha lyakondya kuza, lidaha lyo wuhodanjice wawo.

22 Alu i Wamisili weza kumangha caka,

wakulila nha nzoka, lono i walugu wakuwasejelela,

wakuwatemanga ne zinhemo,

nha vyono wanhu watemangaga mabici,

23 no kumala mbago mbaha.

Ne wanhu wawo, ho wenji wi wakusumika na ku wuwalwe,

ne wasilikale wawo wenji nhe zinzije.

24 Ninga i wanhu wa Misili, wakozwa cevu,

hambi nha wakondya kusumwa ne wanhu

wa ku lubavu lwa kusumuka.

Vyo nghutya, ane no Muwaha Mulungu.”

25 U Muwaha Mulungu, we mihanga, u Mulungu wa isilayeli, yakutya nhavi, “Noyilipiciza yisi ya Amoni, u mulungu wa Tabesi, ne yisi ya Misili, hamonga ne milungu yayo, ne watemi wayo. Nomusola u mutemi wa Misili, hamonga ne wanhu wose wono wamwitumbiye,
26 niwawice mu miwoko ya wono wakuhila kuwawulaga, nha cinji, kuli Nebukadineza, u mutemi wa Babiloni, ne wasilikale wakwe. Ninga hanyuma yayo, i wanhu wolowuya wekale mu yisi ya Misili kanyhi, nha vyono wanoza mu vilo vyono vipulute. Vyo nghutya, ane no Muwaha Mulungu.”

U Muwaha Mulungu kolowaponya wanhu wakwe

27 “Nye nyo wanhu wangu, mulece kogopa ye,

anye nyo wanhu wa Isilayeli, mulece kogofywa.

Hambi 'ne, nha nomuponyenyi ku wulawa kuko ku yisi yo kutali,

ku wulawa ku yisi yiyo yono mwikaye nha muli wafungwa.

Molowuya du ku yisi yenyu mukekale mupolile,

nanye molowa mucehile du,

holowa hasina yunji wo wose mono kolomogofyenyi kanyhi yaye.

28 Ane nolomuzizenyi nimuponyenyi.

Nane nologongomeza i makungugo gose,

ku wulawa hanhu hose, hono namwiyagazizenyi.

Ninga anye, si nolomongomezenyi yaye.

Hamba nhavivyo,

si nolomulecenyi du ndece kumulipicizenyi yaye,

langa nolomulipicizenyi ko wulipicizwe we ling'ani vyo nghutya,

ane no Muwaha Mulungu.”
