Yelemiya 35

Yelemiya ne Walekabi

1 Mu vilo vyono Yehoyakimu u mwana wa Yosiya, yali yo mutemi wa Yuda, u Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya,
2 “Ale, wubite ku wanhu wo mulango we Walekabi, wukalonje nawo. Hanyuma wuwalete, wuwenjize mugati ye cinyumba cimonga ca mu Nyumba yangu, hanyuma wuwapele divayi wang'we.”
3 Lulo nghamusola Yazaniya u mwana wa Yelemiya, mwana Habaziniya, hamonga ne wanduguze, ne wanagwe wose, ne nyumba yose ye Walekabi,
4 nghawa kuwaleta ku Nyumba yo Mulungu. Nghawa kuwenjiza mu cinyumba ce wana wa Hanani, mwana wa Igidaliya, u munhu wo Mulungu. Ne cinyumba cico, cali cili mucanyha ye cinyumba ca Maseya u mwana wa Shalumu, u mwitumami mulongola wa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, behi ne vinyumba vye wetumami wanji.
5 Ale, nghawa kuwawicila vikombe ha mwande yawo wene Walekabi, hamonga ne zibakuli zimemile divayi, nghawa kuwatya, “Ale mung'we divayi yiyi.”

6 Ninga wene wakamhitula wakatya, “Ase si cing'wezaga divayi yaye. Hambi u sogo yetu Yonadabu u mwana wa Lekabi, nha yacilonjela yakatya vyono, cilecele lukulu kung'wa divayi, sese sunga ne wana wetu cibitilila.
7 Kanyhi yacilonjela nha vyono, cilecele lukulu kuzenga kaya, hamba kulima migunda, hamba cilece kuhadika zabibu hamba kuzigula yaye. Kanyhi yacilajiliza vyono, ase cinojeye ciwe kwikala mu mahema du vilo vyose, ho cisume kwikala mono yisi yiyi nha cili wajenzi du.
8 Holeka nase cigebatiye lukulu i mejizo gono yacijiza Yonadabu. Na sese co weneco si cing'wezaga yaye divayi, hamba wagolece zetu, ne wana wetu we cilume na we cicekulu.
9 Holeka si cizengaga kaya zo kwikala yaye, langa cikalaga mu mahema du. Hamba cisina migunda ya zabibu, hamba migunda yo kulima no kuhadika wuhemba.
10 Langa cigakamisize lukulu i malajilizo gono u sogo yetu Yonadabu yacilajiliza.
11 Ninga lono mutemi Nebukadineza yayisolela yisi, baho nase ho calamulila cize ku Yelusalemi, ciwe kutali ne Babiloni hamonga ne walugu we Cisiliya. Na yiyi yo soko yono yikuleka ase cili baha cikaye mu Yelusalemi.”

12 Hanyuma kanyhi u Muwaha Mulungu u Mudaha vyose u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, yakanhuma
13 mbite kuli wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, nghawalonjele nitye nhavi, “Ane no Muwaha Mulungu, nghumuwuzenyi anye nyo wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, nhawulenyi si mukusaka kuhulika 'ne hamba kugebata ne mafundo gangu.
14 Ninga mulanje vyono i wana wa Yonadabu wamuhulika u sogo yawo, lono yawalonjela vyono walecele lukulu kung'wa divayi. Na mbeka si wang'wezaga divayi, sunga nalu. Ninga ane nikalaga nghulonga nanye du nhondo zose, nanye hamba si mukuhulika yaye.
15 Nghamutumilenyi ne wetumami wangu wawizi kenji na kenji, nawo wakamulonjelenyi vyono mupituce, mugalece metumo genyu mabi, mwitume ze ling'ani. Kanyhi wakamusugulenyi mulece kuyihemba milungu yinji, hamba kuyitumamila, ho musume kujendelela kwikala mu yisi yono namupelenyi nyenye, hamonga ne walezi wenyu. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, anye si mwasaka kuhulika yaye, hamba kuzicelelela zono nghulonga.
16 Ninga mulanje, i wana wa Yonadabu, vyono walibata no kulikamisa lilajilizo lyono sogo yawo yawalajiliza. Ninga anye nyo wanhu nye, hamba musina cihuliko kuli 'ne ng'o.
17 Holeka ku soko yiyo, alu mowona! Ane no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, nomuletelenyi anye nyo wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, i magazo gose gono nehuwiza. Nomutendelenyi nhavivyo, vyono anye si mukusaka kuwa ne cihuliko kuli 'ne, nghamucemenyi, nanye hamba kumhitula yaye.”

18 Hanyuma, nghawa kuwalonjela wo mulango we Walekabi, vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mudaha Vyose u Mulungu wa Isilayeli yateye nhavi, “Anye nyo wo mulango we Walekabi, vyono mwagebata malajilizo gono u sogo yenyu Yonadabu yamulajilizenyi, no kugatyatya mafundo gakwe, hamonga no kwituma zose, nha vyono sogo yenyu yamulajilizenyi.
19 Holeka luno, ane nane no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mudaha vyose, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, ku soko yiyo, nghwihuwiza nha vyono Yonadabu u mwana wa Lekabu, vilo vyose kowa na mwana mu mhuga yakwe, mono kowa kunyitumamila.”
