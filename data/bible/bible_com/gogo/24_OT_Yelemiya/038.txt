Yelemiya 38

Yelemiya yakutajilwa mwi'kombo lyono lisina malenga

1 Nayo Shefatiya, u mwana wa Matani, na Gedaliya u mwana wa Pashuli, na Yehukali u mwana wa Shelemiya, hamonga na Pashuli, u mwana wa Malikiya, wahulika zono nawalonjelaga wanhu,
2 zono Muwaha Mulungu yazilonga yakutya, “U munhu wo wose mono kolosigala mwi'kaya lili, kolofwila mu wulugu, hamba mu nzala, hamba mu matamwa. Ninga munhu wo wose mono kolowuka mwi'kaya lili, yetegule mweneco kuli Wababiloni, ayo langa si kolowulagwa yaye, langa koloponya wumi wakwe.”
3 Nawalonjela kanyhi nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalonga yakatya, “Nolitajila likaya lili mu miwoko ye wasilikale we Cibabiloni nawo wolisola.”

4 Ale, nawo i wetumami wakawa kubita kumulonjela mutemi wakutya, “Gwe mutemi, u munhu yuyu, ciba yawulagwe ye. Ku soko u wulonje wakwe wuwu, wukuwanyenyula vilungo i wasilikale wa mwi'kaya, sunga ne wanhu wose wono wasigaye mwi'kaya. Ne zinghani zakwe zizi, si zikuwataza cinji wanhu, langa mwene yakusaka yawatamise du.”

5 Nayo mutemi Zedekiya yakawapitula yakawatya, “Ale, viswanu, mumutendelaje nha vyono mwendile nyenye nyo weneco. Ane si nosuma kumwimilenyi yaye.”
6 Ale, hobaho wakawa kuza kusola, wakawa kunhimya ne milejehe mwi'kombo lya Malikiya u mwana wo mutemi, lyono lyali lili mwi'kumbo lya mwi'kulu. Ninga lyali lisina malenga yaye, langa lyali lina nhope du. Nane nghawa kudodomela mu nhope yiyo.

7 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, Ebedimeleki u Muetiyopiya mupaswe, mono yetumaga mwi'kulu, yahulika nha vyono wanhajila mwi'kombo. Na mwi'daha lilyo u mutemi yali yekaye ha mulango wa Benyamini.
8 Nayo Ebedimeleki yakamubitila mutemi bahalya, yakamulonjela yakatya, “Gwe mutemi, zinghani zono wetumile i wanhu wawa, si nghani swanu yaye. Hambi nha wamutajila Yelemiya mwi'kombo, na ho hanhu mbeka hono kofwila ne nzala.
9 Kanyhi wumanyile nha vyono, sunga na mono 'kaya, musina ndigwa yaye.”
10 Ale, nayo mutemi yakawa kumulajiliza Ebedimeleki yawasole wanhu wadatu waze wambuse mwi'kombo lono nghali sinafwa.
11 Ale, Ebedimeleki yakawa kuwasola wanhu wawo, yakawa kubita nawo sunga ku ku cinyumba ce viwutwa ca mwi'kulu, yakasola masambi-sambi yakagawapa no mulejehe yakagasunja ne milejehe yakawa kumheleza mumo mwi'kombo.
12 Hanyuma yakanonjela yakatya mbicile masambi gago mu zinghwapa, milejehe ho yilece kunghubula. Nane nghanoza nhavivyo,
13 nawo wakawa kunghwega no kumbusa mwi'kombo. Na ku wulawa baho, nhondo wakawa kumbika mwi'kumbo.

Zedekiya yakulondola wulanjizwe kuli Yelemiya

14 Hanyuma kanyhi u mutemi Zedekiya, yakatuma wanhu waze wanyheme mbite kwakwe, lono yali yali ha mulango we kadatu we Nyumba yo Mulungu. Yakanonjela yakutya nhavi, “Alu Yelemiya, ane nghusaka nghuwuze nghani, nane nghukusaka wunonjele ze mbeka zose.”

15 Nane nghamupitula nghamutya, “Ninga mutemi, hono nghulonjele ze mbeka, agwe kombulaga. Na hono nghulajile wulanjize wo wose, agwe si kowucelelela yaye.”
16 Ninga mwene mutemi Zedekiya, yakanyihuwiza cinyele yakutya, “Ane nghwilahiza kuli Mulungu mumi, u Mulungu mono kaciwumifyaga, vyono ane si nokuwulaga yaye. Hamba si nokutajila yaye mu miwoko ye wanhu wono wakukulondola wakuwulaje.”

17 Hodu, nane nghawa kumulonjela zono yalonjile u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mudaha vyose, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, nghamutya, “Hono witegule go mweneco kuli wetumami wo mutemi wa Babiloni, koponya wumi wako, sunga ne likaya lili hamba si lyoticimizwa no moto yaye. Agwe hamonga na we mhuga yako wose mopona.
18 Ninga hono wijelele du wulece kwitegula go mweneco, ale, wumanye nha vyono likaya lili, lyotajilwa mu miwoko ye Wababiloni, nawo woliticimiza no moto, nagwe go mweneco, hamba si kopona yaye.”

19 Ninga mutemi yakamhitula yakatya, “Gwe Yelemiya, ane nghuwogopa i wanyawusi wayetu, wono wakondya kwilunza ne Wababiloni. Lunji yisumice ninjizwe mu miwoko yawo, nawo ho wamhele magazo.”
20 Ninga nane nghamutya, “Ng'o mutemi, si kosuma kwinjizwa mu miwoko yawo yaye. Ninga ane nghukunena wuyihulice nghani yo Muwaha Mulungu. Na zose zowa swanu du hali 'gwe, kokwiponya no wumi wako.
21 Ninga ane ku lubavu lwangu u Muwaha Mulungu kakondya kundajila zono zokwijelela, hono wuleme kwilavya no kubita go mweneco.
22 Na mu wugubulilwe wuwo, ane niwawona wacekulu wanji wono walekwa mwi'kulu lya mu yisi ya Yuda, wakutegulwa kuli wetumami wo mutemi wa Babiloni. Alu wuhulicize nghulonjele zono wali kulonga lono wali mu nzila, wali kutya nhavi,

‘I wendece muno wo mutemi,

alu wamukonga, nawo kanyhi wamusuma.

Ne migulu yakwe luno yakopela mu nhope,

ne wendece wakwe wakondya kumuleka.’ ”

23 Hanyuma kanyhi nghajendelela nghatya, “I wagolece zako wose, hamonga ne wana, wose wolosolwa wategulwe ku Babiloni, nagwe go mweneco hamba si kolokwiponya yaye hali wene. Nagwe kolosolwa no mutemi wa Babiloni wuli muwopwe. Ne likaya lili lyoloticimizwa no moto.”

24 Nayo Zedekiya yakapitula yakatya, “Nane nghukunena, 'gwe Yelemiya, wulece kumumanyiza munhu yunji zinghani zizi. Hambi nha kowa weyambuza wumi wako go mweneco.
25 Na hono i wetumami wahulice du, nha vyono nali kuduwana nagwe, ale, wokuza du wakuwuze cono cali kuduwana cicici. Nawo wokwihuwiza nha vyono si wokuwulaga yaye, hono wuwalonjele zinghani zose.
26 Holeka nagwe nhondo wuwalonjele nha vyono, agwe wali kuza kulomba kuli'ne, ho ndece kukugomoleza kanyhi ku jela ho wukafwile kuko.”
27 Na mbeka i wetumami wose wakawa kuza no kumbuza, ninga nane nghawalonjela zizilya du, zono mutemi yanonjela vyono zo nizilonje. Na ku wulawa baho, nhondo wakawa wasina kanyhi yinji yono wonoza. Vyono hali hasina hamba monga mono yazihulika zono caduwana no mutemi.
28 Nane nghawa kujendelela kwikala du mwi'kumbo lya mwi'kulu, sunga mwi'zuwa lyono Yelusalemi yasolelwa.
