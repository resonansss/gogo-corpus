Yelemiya 47

I nghani yo Muwaha Mulungu kuli ze yisi ye Wafilisiti

1 Na lono u mutemi wa Misili yali yakali si nayihoma Gaza, u Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela zinghani za Gaza
2 yakatya,

“Lanje, malenga gakuza gakulawa ku lubavu lwa kusumuka,

nago gowa yikolongo lyono limemile,

nago golomema no kuyigubika yisi,

na vyose vyono vili mumo mu makaya,

hamonga ne wanhu wono wekaye mumo.

Ne wanhu wololondola wutazi,

ne wanhu wose wa mono'nze, wololila ko wusungu wuwaha.

3 Wolohulika nyhinyinho zo wukanyaje we mabangho ge zifwalasi,

hamonga ne misindo yo lumangho lwe magali go wulugu,

no wulindime we maguludumu gayo.

Mwi'zuwa lilyo, i wasogo zo munhu,

hamba si wolowuya ne nyuma wawasole wana wawo ng'o,

hambi i miwoko yawo nha yolowa yidejepe.

4 Ninga lipango lyakondya kuza lyo kuyongomeza yisi ye Wafilisiti,

kuyiwusiza lukulu u wutazi wose wono wusigaye,

wono wukulawa ku yisi ya Tulo na Sidoni.

Ane no Muwaha Mulungu,

nowongomezeza lukulu Wafilisiti wose wono wekaye mu zimhwani za Kilete.

5 Wukalale wuwaha wakondya kuwaziza i wanhu wa Gaza,

ninga i wanhu wa Ashikeloni, wanyamaye twii.

Alo i wanhu wono wasigaye mu yisi ye Wafilisiti,

wojendelela kulila sunga nili lo?

6 Mukulila mukutya, ‘Woyi hee!

U mumaje wo Muwaha Mulungu!

Alu kojendelela kutemanga sunga nili?

Wuwuye mu njasulu yako, wikale mumo wucehe!’

7 Alu u mumaje ho woceha nhawule,

kumbe nane ho niwupelile mulimo wo kwituma?

Hambi nha nakondya kuwulajiliza wuyihome Ashikeloni,

hamonga ne wanhu wose wono wekaye mu mhwani ze lilenga.”
