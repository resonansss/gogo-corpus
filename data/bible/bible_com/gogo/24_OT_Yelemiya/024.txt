Yelemiya 24

Nhoto nyejete zono zina nghuyu

1 U Muwaha Mulungu yakandajila nhoto nyejete zono zali zina nghuyu, zono zali ziwicigwe hamwande ye Nyumba yo Mulungu. (Na zizi zejela lono Nebukadineza u mutemi wa Babiloni, yali yakondya kumusola ku Yelusalemi u mwana wa Yehoyakimu, mono yacemwaga Yehoyakimu, u mutemi wa Yuda, no kumutegula nha mufungwa ku Babiloni, hamonga ne walongola wose we yisi ya Yuda, ne wanghula mu wuzenji, hamonga ne watyani we vyuma.)
2 Ne nhoto monga ya muli zinhoto zizo, yali yimemile nghuyu swanu muno, zono zali zipiye lulo baho. Nhoto nyagwe, nayo yali yimemile nghuyu zono 'mbi muno, zono sizisumice hamba ku wulye kose.
3 Ale, baho u Muwaha Mulungu yakambuza yakutya, “Gwe Yelemiya, alu wukuwona cinhuci?”

Nane nghamupitula nghamutya, “Nghuwona nghuyu. Nghuyu ziswanu muno, ne zinghuyu zimbi muno muno, zono si zisumice hamba ku wuligwe kose.”

4 U Muwaha Mulungu, yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya,
5 “Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, mono nghuwehweseza muno muno, i wanhu wangu wono wasolwa no kutegulwa ku Babiloni, hambi nha wehwanile ne zinghuyu zizo ziswanu muno. Holeka nane nowatendela ko wuswanu muno.
6 Nowadima sunga nize niwagomole, wawuye mono yisi yiyi, kanyhi nowakamisiza mu ziswanu zose, hamba si nowaleka yaye. Nowazenga, hamba si nowamwagula yaye. Kanyhi nowahadika, hamba si nowakula yaye.
7 Nane nowasikamya, ho wamanye ane, vyono yo nene u Muwaha Mulungu. Nawo wowa wanhu wangu, nane nowa Mulungu wawo. Hambi lulo, nha wolombuyila 'ne, ko mutima wawo wose.”

8 “Na kuli za Zedekiya, u mutemi we yisi ya Yuda ne wawaha wakwe, hamonga ne wanhu wa Yelusalemi, wono wasigala mu yisi yiyi, hamonga na wono wabita ku Misili, ane no Muwaha Mulungu, nowatendela wawo wose, nha vyono zinha zinghuyu zizi zono mbi muno muno hamba ku wulye kose.
9 Nane noloweyagaziza mu zinghugugo zose za kono'nze, ho watuje wongomezwe wubi. Ne wanhu wose wolowezwajiza no kuwahosa hamonga no kuwasekanga, no kuwalijita, ho hose hono noloweyagaziza.
10 Nane nowaletela wulugu, ne nzala, hamonga ne matamwa, sunga kolo hakuwa hasina hamba munhu monga muli wene, mono kolosigala mu yisi yono nawapela wene, hamonga ne walezi wawo.”
