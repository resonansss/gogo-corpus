Yelemiya 20

Wisumanize wa Yelemiya no mukohani Pashuli

1 Nayo Pashuli mukohani, u mwana wa Imeli, mono yali yo mulindilizi muwaha we Nyumba yo Mulungu, yali yeza kuhulika vyono ane nghuziganula zizi,
2 hobaho yakawa kulajiliza nhowe, mbopwe ne manyolo behi no mulango wa Benyamini wa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
3 Ne mitondo yaco, lono Pashuli yali yakali nambopolela mu manyolo, ane nghamulonjela nghatya, “U Muwaha Mulungu si yakucemela litagwa lilyo lya Pashuli, langa lya ‘Nghakamo Hanhu Hose.’
4 Nayo u Muwaha Mulungu, mweneco yo yalonga nha vyono, ‘Nokunoza wuwe munhu wa nghakamo gwegwe mweneco, na kuli wayago wanji. Nagwe kolowawona wose wakuwulagwa ko mumaje we walugu wawo. Nane nowatajila i wanhu wose wa Yuda, mu miwoko yo mutemi wa Babiloni. Nayo kowasola wanji nha wafungwa, yawategule kutali ku yisi yakwe, na wanji nawo kowawulaga.
5 Kanyhi nowaleka i walugu wawo, waze wayisole ne sawo yose ya mwi'kaya lili, wavisole ne vinhu vyayo vyose, hamonga ne viwutwa vye watemi wose wa Yuda, wavitegule ku Babiloni.
6 Sunga na gwegwe Pashuli, hamonga na we mhuga yako, wose molotekwa mutegulwe ku Babiloni. Nagwe Pashuli, kolofwila kuko, wakuzice kuko, hamonga ne wayago wono wali wuwendile, wono wawalonjelaga nghani nyinji zo wulambi.’ ”

Yelemiya yakumung'ung'ulicila Muwaha Mulungu

7 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, kunghonga'ne,

nane niwa kukongwa-kongwa.

Hambi agwe nha wina vilungo wunyhizize'ne

kumbusiza sunga ne vilungo vyangu,

nalu wanhu wose wakusekanga,

wakunzwajiza misi yose.

8 Na ho hose lono nghulonga, nghulila du nzozize, nghutya,

“Wukali! No Wongomele.”

Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, alu'ne nha nghuhong'ongwa,

hamonga no kusowa misi yose.

Ku soko'ne vyono nghuyiganula nghani yako.

9 Na kolo nghutya,

“Alu hodu ciba nimusemwe Muwaha Mulungu

ndece no kulitambula ne litagwa lyakwe.”

Hobaho nghuyiwona nghani yako yinha moto

yikunyanya mu nhumbula yangu.

Nane nghujela muno muno kukangalila,

ninga hamba si nghusuma kwiyimila yaye.

10 Nane nghuwahulika wanhu wose wakupwepwa wakutya,

“Nghakamo yili hanhu hose!

Holeka, alenyi le, cikawalonjele wono wena wulajilize.”

Sunga ne weyendi wayangu, wose walindiye du,

wawone wugwe wangu, akuno wakutya,

“Na lunji kosuma kukocwa-kocwa,

nase baho ho cosumila kumwibata, cilipicize.”

11 Ninga 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nha wuli hamonga nane,

yo gwegwe wuli vilungo vyangu,

hamonga no wusume wangu.

Nawo wono wakungaza,

wose wogwa citawima,

hamba si wosuma yaye,

langa wokozwa cevu cibitilila.

Ne cevu cawo hamba si colosemigwa yaye.

12 Ninga agwe go Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga

kuwajelaga wanhu we ling'ani.

Nagwe wuzimanyile zose zono zili mu zinhumbula zawo,

na mu mahweso gawo.

Holeka ane nghulindiye mbone wulipicizwe

wono kowalipiciza i walugu wangu,

hambi 'ne nha nakulecela gwegwe zinghani zangu.

13 Ale, mwimbilenyi u Muwaha Mulungu!

Mukumyenyi Muwaha Mulungu!

Hambi nha kamuponya mono yali kwiwonelwa,

mu miwoko ye wanhu wabi.

14 Lilijitwe lizuwa lyono nalelelwa,

Lilece kulugutizwa yaye,

lizuwa lilyo lyono yaya yandelela.

15 Yalijitwe munhu yuyo mono yamupela baba nyemo,

mono yamulonjela zinghani yakutya,

“Kulelelwa mwana we cilume.”

16 Yimunojeye munhu yuyo, yawe nhe makaya gago,

gono u Muwaha Mulungu yagongomeza yasina lusungu.

Yahulice cililo co wusungu ca hakali mitondo

hamonga ne lizozo lyo wulugu,

lya mu mapango ge misi.

17 Hambi nha si yambulaga'ne

lono nali nghali nalelwa,

ne 'nda ya yaya, yali yiwe co citenjelo cangu.

18 Alu mwene nye! Nhawule'ne nghalelwa?

Alo mwene nalelwa ho nhuje magaziko no wukalale hongo?

Ne vilo vyangu vimalicile mu cevu du?
