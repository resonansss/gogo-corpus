Yelemiya 29

Baluwa ya Yelemiya, kuli Wayahudi wa ku Babiloni

1 Hanyuma nghawa kwandika baluwa kuli wakohani, ne wawizi, ne walongola we wanhu, na kuli wanhu wose wono wasolwa na Nebukadineza, ku wulawa ku Yelusalemi, no kutegulwa ku Babiloni nha wafungwa.
2 Baluwa yiyi, nghayandika lono u mutemi Yehoyakimu hamonga no nyina, ne wetumami wose wa mwi'kulu, ne walongola wose wa Yuda na wa Yelusalemi, ne wanghula ku wuzenji, hamonga ne watyani, wali wakondya kusolwa no kutegulwa ku wuhamywe ku Babiloni.
3 Nane nali neza kuyandika baluwa yiyo, nghawa kumupeleza Elasa mwana wa Shafani, na Gemaliya mwana wa Hilikiya, wono u mutemi Zedekiya yawatumaga wabite kuli Nebukadineza u mutemi wa Babiloni. Ne baluwa yiyo yali yandicigwe zinghani azi.

4 “U Muwaha Mulungu, we mihanga u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, yakumulonjelenyi anye nyo wanhu wose, mono mwene Mulungu, yamukombola Nebukadineza, ho yamusolenyi ku wulawa ku Yelusalemi, yamutegulenyi ku Babiloni nha muli wafungwa,
5 yakutya nhavi, ‘Alu, muzenje zikaya, mwikale mumo, muhadice mu migunda, mulye mikalo yayo.
6 Mutole wacekulu, mulele wana we cilume na we cicekulu. Muwatoze ne wana wenyu we cilume na we cicekulu, nawo walele wana we cilume na we cicekulu nhavivyo, ho musume konjelezeka kuko, hamba mulece kucepuka yaye.
7 Mwitumaje ziswanu za mu makaya gago, kuko kono namunozenyi ho muwe wafungwa. Mugalombele kuli Muwaha Mulungu i makaya gago. Ku soko, hono gatuje wumoteswe, nanye nhavivyo mowa mwamoteswa.
8 Ninga ane no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, nghumusugulenyi nha vyono, mwilanjililaje! Mulecele lukulu kukongwa ne wawizi wono mwikalane hamonga, hamba ne wanhu wanji wo wose, wono wakwiyitya nha vyono ati, wosuma kuwila za mu vilo vyono vikuza. Mulecele lukulu hamba kuzihulicila zindoto zawo.
9 Hambi nha wowa kumulonjelenyi zinghani zo wulambi du, wakuzilonjela mwi'tagwa lyangu. Ninga ane hamba si niwatumile yaye. Vyo nghutya, ane no Muwaha Mulungu.’

10 “U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, ‘Kolo myaka makumi mupunghati yikufisilizwa mu Babiloni, nane baho ho nolomuzizenyi, no kucifisiliza cilagane cangu, nimugomolenyi kanyhi ku yisi yenyu.
11 Hambi 'ne, nha nigamanyile mahweso gangu gono nghumwihwesezenyi anye. Mahweso go kumuletelenyi wumoteswe, hamba si wongomele yaye. Mahweso go kumupelenyi 'nye zo witumbilo wa mu vilo vyono vikuza.
12 Nanye molonyhemeleza. Kanyhi molonyiziza, mololomba, nane nolomupitulenyi.
13 Nanye molonondola, molombona, hambi nha molonondola kwe mitima yenyu yose.
14 Heya, mbeka molombona, nane nolomugomolenyi kanyhi ku yisi yenyu. Nolomukongholezenyi ku wulawa mu ma'si gose, ne hanhu hose hono namwiyagazizenyi. Nolomugomolenyi kanyhi sunga mu yisi yenyu, yono namulecenyi musolwe no kutegulwa kutali ku wuhamywe. Vyo nghutya, ane no Muwaha Mulungu.’ ”

15 “Hambi nha mukutya nha vyono, u Muwaha Mulungu kacipela wawizi mu Babiloni.
16 Ninga muhulicize zono u Muwaha Mulungu yakulonga kuli zo mutemi mono yatemile, mu wutemi wono yali yatemile Dawudi, hamonga na kuli ze wanhu wa mwi'kaya lili. Nha cinji, i wandugu zenyu wono wasigala, wono si wasolelezwa hamonga nanye.
17 U Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga, yakutya nhavi, ‘Lanje, ane nomuletelenyi wulugu, ne nzala, hamonga ne matamwa, nimunozenyi muwe nhe zinghuyu zimbi zono si zisumice hamba ku wulye kose.
18 Nane nomutyatililenyi no wulugu, ne nzala, hamonga ne matamwa. Ne makungugo gose ga mono'nze, golokwinjilwa no wizine wuwaha muno. Ne hanhu hose hono nowa niweyagazize, i wanhu wa mumo wolotinuswa no kogofywa muno muno ku soko ya zizo zono zolomwijelelenyi Ne wanhu wose wolomusekanjenyi, no kulitumamila litagwa lyenyu nha cinhu cilijitwe.
19 Na zizi zose zolomuzizenyi, vyono si mwasaka kuzihulika zinghani zangu, zono namulonjelajenyi kenji na kenji, mu nzila ye wetumami wangu i wawizi, nanye hamba kuzihulika yaye.
20 Holeka muhulice nghani yo Muwaha Mulungu, anye wose mono namulecelenyi musolwe ku wulawa ku Yelusalemi, mutegulwe ku Babiloni ku wuhamywe.’

21 “U Muwaha Mulungu, we mihanga, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, kuli zinghani za Ahabu mwana wa Kolaya, na Zedekiya mwana wa Maseya, wono wakumulonjelenyi zinghani zo wulambi mwi'tagwa lyakwe, yakutya nhavi, Nowatajila mu miwoko ya Nebukadineza u mutemi wa Babiloni, nayo yawawulajile ha meso genyu.
22 Nawo i wanhu wono wasolwa nha wafungwa ku Yelusalemi, no kutegulwa ku Babiloni, hono wawe kusaka kumulijita munhu, wosuma kutya, ‘U Muwaha Mulungu yakutendele nha vyono yamutendela Zedekiya na Ahabu, wono u mutemi wa Babiloni, yawakasika mu moto wali nyumi!’
23 Na zizi zo zowagana na weneco, hambi nha wetuma wubi muno mu Isilayeli. Kanyhi wakasenyha ne wacekulu we wandugu zawo no kulonga zo wulambi, wakwitumamila litagwa lyangu. Ne zinghani zizi zikawa yiwifyo kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Nayo yazimanyile zose zono wakwituma, kanyhi mwene yo muhomelezi muli wene. Vyo yakutya, u Muwaha Mulungu.”

Baluwa ya Shemaya

24 U Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, yakanonjela nghani,
25 ho nimulonjele Shemaya munya Nehelamu, mono yandika baluwa mwi 'tagwa lyakwe mweneco, kuli wanhu wose wa mu Yelusalemi, na kuli Zefaniya mukohani, u mwana wa Maseya, sunga na kuli wakohani wanji wose. Na mu baluwa yiyo, Shemaya yamwandicila Zefaniya yakutya nhavi.

26 “U Muwaha Mulungu yakunoza agwe Zefaniya wuwe mukohani, wuhalile i hanhu ha Yehoyada. Sunga nalu yo gwegwe mulongola muwaha wa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Holeka yikunojeye wuwalanjilile i wanhu wo wose, u munhu mono kowa yena likwale, no kwinoza nha muwizi, munhu yuyo wumwibate, wumuwope ne manyolo.
27 Hamba nha vivyo, nhawule lu si wazinoza zizi kuli Yelemiya munya Anatoti, mono yekalaga yakulonga nha muwizi kuli wanhu?
28 Holeka yuyo yimunojeye yemilwe muli zizo. Hambi nha yawalonjela wanhu ku Babiloni, nha vyono wokwikala wafunjigwe kuko vilo vinji. Holeka yiwanojeye wazenje zikaya, wekale, wahadice migunda, walye mikalo yayo.”

29 Nayo Zefaniya yakasomela baluwa yiyo,
30 nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakanonjela ane Yelemiya yakatya,
31 vyono niwamanyize nghani yiyi ya Shemaya, wanhu wose i wafungwa, wono wali ku Babiloni, niwalonjele nitye nhavi, “U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya vyono,
32 ‘Ane no Muwaha Mulungu, nomulipiciza Shemaya munya Nehelami hamonga no lulelo lwakwe. Kanyhi howa hasina hamba munhu monga wa muli wanhu wakwe mono kokwikala mumi ho yaziwone ziswanu, zono nowanozeza wanhu wangu. Hambi mwene nha kawasagahaza wanhu wangu ho waneme 'ne. Vyo yakutya, u Muwaha Mulungu.’ ”
