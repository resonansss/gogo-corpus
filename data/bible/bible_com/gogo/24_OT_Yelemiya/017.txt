Yelemiya 17

U wubi no wulipicizwe wa Yuda

1 U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, “Anye nyo wanhu wa Yuda, u wubi wenyu wandicigwe na kalamu ya cuma, wukakatwe mu zinhumbula zenyu ne cisonje ca alimasi, na mu zinhuzi ze vikumbicilo vyenyu.
2 Hambi i wanhu wako nha wahembaga ha vikumbicilo hamonga ne vihwani vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, behi ne mabici gono gena mahamha madece mu matunda matali,
3 sunga na mu matunda gono gali mu'si mwi'hala. Alu nane nowanoza i walugu wenyu waze wayisole sawo yenyu yose, hamonga no wugoli wenyu, ku soko yo wubi wono mwetuma mu yisi yenyu yose.
4 Nanye yolowa yimunojelenyi muyilece yisi yono namupelenyi, nane nomunozenyi muwe wetumami we walugu wenyu, mu yisi yono si muyimanyile yaye. Hambi likuwo lyangu nha linha moto, nalyo lyowa kwaka cibitilila.”

I zinghani ze hayo hayo

5 U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi,

“Nane nomulipiciza munhu wo wose mono yakundeka 'ne,

no kumwitumbila munhu muyagwe,

kawa kumunoza munhu muyagwe yiwa yo nyhito yakwe.

6 U munhu we liwala lilyo,

yehwanile nhe cititika cono cili mu nyika,

cono cikuwilucila mu nyika nghavu,

mu muhangha wo mwino,

i hanhu hono hasina cinji co cose cono ciwilukaga yaye.

Kanyhi hasina hamba nghani swanu,

yono yosuma kumuziza ng'o.

7 Ane langa, nomumotya u munhu

mono yanyitumbiye nene du.

8 Hambi u munhu yuyo,

nha kowa yehwanile no mubici wono wukuwilucila behi ne cikolongo,

wono wuhimbize milela yayo mu malenga.

No mubici wuwo,

si wogopaga lizuwa hamba likalipe muno.

Hambi i mahamha gayo, nha gekalaga gadedewaye du.

Hamba mvula yilece kuwutonyela,

wene wikalaga wusina mahwele yaye,

langa wene wujendelelaga kulela mikalo du.

9 “Alo mwene, nani 'mbi mono kosuma kuyimanya

nhumbula yo munhu?

Hambi nhumbula yo munhu nha yo nghoci muno

kuciza vinhu vyose,

kanyhi yina wutamwa wono si wosuma kuponywa yaye.

10 Ninga ane no Muwaha Mulungu,

yo nene du mono nigajelaga mahala,

no kuzisunjilila zinhumbula ze wanhu,

ho nimutendele munhu mu nghango ye zinzila zakwe,

mu nghango ye mikalo ye metumo gakwe.”

11 No munhu mono ketujilaga sawo mu zinzila zimbi,

yehwanile ndeje yono yigonelaga magangha

gono si yitajile yeneco.

Nayo koyajiliza sawo yakwe mu wuzelelo wakwe,

na ku ndumo yayo, kowa mulele du.

12 Ne Nyumba yo Mulungu wetu, yihwanile ligoda lye nhojo,

lyono lyasimywa mucanyha ye litunda ku wulawa na katali.

13 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

agwe yo gwegwe go witumbilo wa Isilayeli,

no munhu wo wose mono kokuleka 'gwe,

kokwikoza cevu mweneco.

Hambi nha wolokwajilila wose,

nhe litagwa lyono lyandicigwe mu manghundi.

Ku soko vyono wakuleka gwegwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

go muvwilo we malenga momi.

Yelemiya yakulomba wutazi kuli Muwaha Mulungu

14 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nghukulomba wumhonye,

nane noponela lukulu, wunhaze,

nane nokwikala mholile.

Hambi nha yo gwegwe du wuli wene,

mono nghukumyaga!

15 I wanhu nawo, wakumbuza wakutya,

“Nghani yo Muwaha Mulungu yili hayi lo?

Ale lu, wuwasolaje wuwawuse.”

16 Ninga agwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

agwe go mweneco, wumanyile nha vyono,

ane si calongana nagwe,

ho wuwaletele wongomezwe wanhu wawa.

Hamba si nakulomba ho wuwaletele lizuwa lye magazo yaye.

Ninga agwe wuzimanyile zose zono nalonga.

17 Holeka, nghukunena wulece kunyogofya 'ne,

hambi agwe,

nha yo gwegwe wutizizo wangu mwipango lyo wugaye.

18 Holeka 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wuwakoze cevu, wose wono wakunyiwonela'ne.

Ninga 'ne nghukunena wundece 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu.

Wuwogofye wene muno muno,

ninga ane, wulece kunyogofya.

Wuwaletele wongomezwe, wuwabenanjize.

U wusiwanulwe we lizuwa lye Sabato

19 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya, “Gwe Yelemiya, alu wubite wukeme ha mulango we wanhu, u mulango wono u mutemi wa Yuda kawitumamilaga lono yakwinjila no kulawila mwi'kaya. Wuwalonjele wanhu nghani yangu. Hanyuma ho wuwe kubita na mu milango yose ye likaya lya Yelusalemi.
20 Na mumo mose, wuwalonjele watemi, hamonga ne wanhu wose wa Yuda, sunga na wose wono wekaye mu Yelusalemi, no kwinjila mu milango yiyi, vyono yiwanojeye wose wazihulice zono ane nghulonga.
21 Wuwalonjele nha vyono, hono wawe wakali wendile kuwumipa, yiwanojeye walece kudinghula mitegulo yo yose mwi'zuwa lye Sabato. Hamba kuheleza cinhu cinji co cose mu milango ya Yelusalemi.
22 Hamba kulavyaniza cinhu cinji co cose, kunze ye zikaya zawo mwizuwa lilyo lye Sabato. Kanyhi walece hamba kwituma mulimo wunji wo wose mwizuwa lye Sabato. Langa iwanojeye wayinoze nhe lizuwa lisiwanule kuli wene, nha vyono nakondya kuwalajiliza i walezi wawo.
23 Ninga i walezi wawo, hamba si wahulika 'ne yaye, hamba kuzitajila mu zinhumbula zawo ng'o. Langa wene wakacila kuwa wasina cihuliko du kuli'ne, hamba si wasaka kugabocela ne mafundo gangu yaye.

24 “Holeka wuwalonjele wanhu wawa, nha vyono yiwanojeye wagakamise malajilizo gangu. Walecele lukulu kudinghula mutegulo wo wose, no kuwukoza mu milango ye likaya lili lya Yelusalemi, mwizuwa lye Sabato. Langa yiwanojeye wayibate muno Sabato, wayinoze nhe 'zuwa lisiwanule kuli wene. Holeka yiwanojeye walece kwituma mulimo wunji wo wose mwizuwa lilyo.
25 Holeka ne watemi wawo, hamonga ne wawaha wawo, wolosuma kwinjila mu milango ya Yelusalemi, wekale mwi'goda lyo wutemi lya Dawudi, ne wanhu wose wa Yuda na wa Yelusalemi, wose wolopanda mu mituka yo wulugu na mu zifwalasi. Ne likaya lya Yelusalemi, lyolowa likaye limemile wanhu nhondo zose.
26 Ne wanhu wose wolowa kwikongholela baho, wakulawa mu makaya gose ga Yuda hamonga na mu vitumbi vyono vipilime Yelusalemi. Na wanji wolowa kulawa mu yisi ya Benyamini, mucanyha ye matunda, na mu mwalambalo we vigongo, sunga na ku lubavu lwa kutakama yose ye yisi ya Yuda. Wolowa kuza baho mu Nyumba yangu, wakundetela vilavywa vinyanywe, ne makumbiko, ne vilavywa vyo wusaje, no wuvumba, hamonga ne vilavywa vyo wulumbize.
27 Ninga yiwanojeye wahulicize 'ne, no kuyikamisa Sabato, i lizuwa lisiwanulwe. Kanyhi walecele lukulu kudinghula mutegulo wo wose, no kuwukoza mu milango ya Yelusalemi mwizuwa lilyo. Na hono wejelele du wanoze nhavivyo, nane noyipembela moto milango yose ya Yelusalemi. No moto wuwo wologakongoga i makaya gose go wutemi ga mu Yelusalemi. Na holowa hasina hamba munhu monga mono kolosuma kuwuzimya moto wuwo.”
