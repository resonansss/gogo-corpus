Yelemiya 28

Yelemiya no muwizi Hananiya

1 Na mu mwaka wuwo du kanyhi, mu mwezi we 'hano, wa mu mwaka we kane, wo wuteme wa Zedekiya. Hananiya u muwizi, u mwana wa Azuli, munya cikaya ca Gibeyoni, lono nali nili mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, ha mwande ye wakohani, hamonga ne wanhu, Hananiya yanonjela
2 nha vyono, u Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga u Mulungu wa Isilayeli yakutya, “Nakondya kuvibenanga vilungo vyose vyo mutemi wa Babiloni.
3 Na mwi'pango lya myaka myejete, ane nowa nakondya kuvigomola hanhu baha, i viya vyose vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, vyono u mutemi Nebukadineza yavisola, yakavitegula ku Babiloni.
4 Kanyhi nomugomola na Yekoniya u mwana wa Yehoyakimu u mutemi wa Yuda, hamonga ne wanhu wose wa Yuda wono wahamywa wakategulwa ku Babiloni. Na mbeka, novibenanga vilungo vyo mutemi wa Babiloni. Vyo nghutya, ane no Muwaha Mulungu.”

5 Nane Yelemiya, nghamupitula Hananiya, ha mwande ye wakohani, hamonga ne wanhu wose wono wali wemile mu Nyumba yo Mulngu, nghamutya,
6 “Heya viswanu! Nane nitumbiye vyono, u Muwaha Mulungu kokwituma zizi! Nane nitumbiye vyono, kowunoza u wuwile wako, wuwe wa mbeka, yavigomole baha i viya vyose vye Nyumba yo Mulungu, ku wulawa ku Babiloni, hamonga ne wanhu wose wono wali wasolwa nha wafungwa.
7 Ninga luno, ta wuhulicize zono nane nghukulonjela, hamonga ne wanhu wose wono wali baha.
8 I wawizi wono wacilongolela katali na katali, lono agwe na ane cikali si nawa, wawila nha vyono, wulugu, ne nzala, hamonga ne matamwa, golowaziza we makungugo menji, gono gena wutemi we vilungo viwaha.
9 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, u muwizi mono yakuwila nghani zo mulamu, ne nghani yo muwizi yuyo kolo yeza kwijela, baho langa, u muwizi yuyo, ho komanyika vyono yuyo yo muwizi mbeka, mono Muwaha Mulungu yamutumile.”

10 Hanyuma ya zizo, Hananiya yakawa kuluwusa lubano mu singo yangu, yakawa kulubenanjiza,
11 yakawa kulonga ha mwande ye wanhu wose yakutya, “U Muwaha Mulungu katya nha vyono, nhavivi vyo kolubenanjiza lubano, lono u mutemi Nebukadineza, yali yagavwicile mu zisingo makungugo gose. Nayo kozituma zizi mu myaka yiyi myejete yono yikuza.” Hodu nane Yelemiya, nghawa kuwuka.

12 Hanyuma ya zizi, vilo vyali kupuluta hadodo, u Muwaha Mulungu yakanhuma kanyhi ane Yelemiya mbite,
13 cikalongane na Hananiya nghamutye nhavi, “U Muwaha Mulungu kalonga nha vyono, agwe Hananiya, heya kosuma kulubenanjiza lubano lwe mabici, ninga mwene konoza lubano lunji lwa cuma.
14 U Muwaha Mulungu, we mihanga u Mulungu wa Isilayeli, kalonga nha vyono, kogavwika lubano lwa cuma i makungugo gaga gose, nago ho gajendelele kumwitumamila Nebukadineza, u mutemi wa Babiloni. Kanyhi u Muwaha Mulungu yalonga yakatya, nha vyono koganoza sunga ne malimu ga mu mbago gose, gamwitumamile Nebukadineza.”

15 Namulonjela Hananiya zizi, ninga kanyhi nghonjeleza nghamutya, “Alu Hananiya! Wuhulicize ta! U Muwaha Mulungu hamba si yakutumile yaye, langa agwe wukuwanoza i wanhu wawa wawihuwile u wulambi wako.
16 Na ku soko yiyo, nayo u Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nha vyono, kokuwusa mono'nze. Holeka agwe kofwa mwakawu na lono mwaka wuwu wukali namalika. Ku soko vyono kuwanoza i wanhu wamubeze Muwaha Mulungu.”
17 U muwizi Hananiya, yakafwa mu mwaka wuwo mu mwezi wo mupunghati.
