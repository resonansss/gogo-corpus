Yelemiya 26

Yelemiya vikwitang'anila ne vimizi

1 Ha mwamulizo wo wutemi wa Yehoyakimu u mutemi wa Yuda u mwana wa Yosiya,
2 u Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya, “Ale, wime he wanza lye Nyumba yo Mulungu, wuziganule kuli wanhu wose wono wakulawa mu makaya ga Yuda, wakuza kuhemba bahano. Wuwalonjele zinghani zose zono nghulajilize kuli wene. Ninga wulanjilile, wulece kusigaza hamba nghani monga yaye.
3 Na lunji wanhu wohulika, wazilece zinzila zawo zimbi. Na hono wanoze nhavivyo, nane, si nowongomeza kanyhi, nha vyono nali nakondya kwihwesa niwongomeze, ku soko yo wubi wawo.”

4 Nayo u Muwaha Mulungu, yakanonjela nha vyono, niwalonjele wanhu nitye nhavi, “Ane no Muwaha Mulungu nalonga nha vyono, anye munojeye muwe na cihuliko kuli 'ne, mugebate i mejizo gangu gono namwijizenyi.
5 Kanyhi na vyono, munojeye muzicelelele zinghani ze wetumami wangu i wawizi, wono nikalaga nghumuletelenyi ku wulawa na katali. Ninga anye si mwazicelelela yaye zono wamulonjelajenyi.
6 Ninga luno mwilanjililaje muno muno, vyono hono mujendelele du kuwa musina cihuliko, nane noyinozeza i Nyumba yangu yiyi, nha vyono nayinozeza Shilo. Ne makungugo gose ge yinze, wolitumamila litagwa lye likaya lili nhe 'lijito.”

7 Ale, du, i wakohani ne wawizi, hamonga ne wanhu wose, wakahulika lono nazilongaga zinghani zizi baho ha Nyumba yo Mulungu.
8 Nane nali neza kukondya kuzilonga zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yali yandajilize, hodu, hobahobaho du wakawa kunyibata, wakawa kuzoza wakutya, “Agwe Yelemiya, mbeka kowulagwa, ku soko ye zinghani zako zizi!
9 Alu nhawule wukulonjela mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono Nyumba yiyi yo Mulungu, yolokwihwana Shilo, kanyhi na vyono, likaya lili lyolokongomezwa, na vyono kanyhi, holowa hasina hamba munhu monga mono kolokwikala mumo?” Hodu, hobaho i wanhu wenji wakawa kumhilima mbavu zose.
10 Ale, nawo i wawaha wa mu yisi ya Yuda, wali weza kuzihulika zono zijeye, hobaho, nawo wakawa kuza musunho wakulawilila mwi'kulu, wakawa kwima hanhu hawo, ha mulango mupya.
11 Hanyuma i wakohani hamonga ne wawizi, wakawalonjela i wawaha wawo, hamonga ne wanhu wose, wakutya, “U munhu yuyu, mbeka yanojeye yaling'anizwe yafwe. Hambi nha kalonga nghani 'mbi kuli likaya lyetu. Nanye mumuhulika nyo weneco kwe makutu genyu.”

12 Nane nghapitula nghatya, “Nye wayangu, u Muwaha Mulungu, yo yanhumile 'ne, nonje zose zizi zono muzihulice nghuzilonga, za kuli Nyumba yo Mulungu, na za mwi'kaya lili.
13 Holeka anye munojeye du, mukawanize i mikalile yenyu mibi yono mwikalilaga, hamonga ne zinghani zenyu zimbi zono mwitumaga, kanyhi munojeye mumuhulice u Muwaha Mulungu u Mulungu wenyu. Nanye hono mwitume nhavivyo, nayo kowupitula wuhile wakwe wo kumongomezenyi, nha vyono yali yakondya kwihwesa.
14 Ninga ku lwangu, ane nili mu miwoko yenyu du! Holeka mosuma kunhendela nghani yo yose yono mukuyiwona yo swanu, kanyhi yili ling'ani.
15 Ninga hamba nhavivyo mweze mumanyiye lukulu, nha vyono, hono mumbulaje, nyenye hamonga ne wanhu wose wa mwi'kaya lili, mowa mwenjila mu masutya so kumuwulaga munhu, mono yasina lisutya lyo lyose. Hambi 'ne nha Muwaha Mulungu du mweneco, yo yanhumile nize nimusugulenyi muli zizi.”

16 Ale, i walongola hamonga ne wanhu, wakawa kuwalonjela i wakohani hamonga ne wawizi wakatya, “Nye, u munhu yuyu, kacilonjela zizo zose mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, holeka hamba si yanojeye yawulagwe yaye.”
17 Hanyuma ya zizo, i walongola wanji wakema ha mwande ye lipuga lye wanhu wose wono wali wekongholeye baho, wakawalonjela wakatya,
18 “Nye wayetu, mu vilo lono Hezekiya yali mutemi we yisi ya Yuda, yakaza muwizi yunji, mono yacemwaga Mikaya, munya Molesheti, u muwizi yuyo yawalonjela wanhu nha vyono, u Muwaha Mulungu, we mihanga yalonga yakutya,

‘Agwe go Sayuni kololimwa nha mugunda,

Nagwe Yelusalemi kolowa yilundo lye matongo,

Ne litunda lye Nyumba yo Mulungu, lyolowa mbago.’

19 Ninga u mutemi Hezekiya, hamonga ne wanhu wa Yuda, hamba si wamuwulaga Mikaya yaye. Langa Hezekiya yakalajila cogopo du hali Muwaha Mulungu, yakamunena yawawonele lusungu. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakawupitula wuhile wakwe, hamba si yawaletela kanyhi wongomezwe, wono yali yehwesa nha vyono kowaletela. Holeka nase cilanjilile, hambi nha cikusaka ciyiletele magazo mawaha sese co weneco.”

20 (Na kanyhi, hali na munhu yunji litagwa lyakwe Uliya, u mwana wa Shemaya, munya Kiliyati Yeyalimu, mono nayo yalonjela mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu kuli ze likaya lili, hamonga ne likungugo, nha vyono yanozize Yelemiya.
21 Ninga u mutemi Yehoyakimu ne wasilikale wakwe, hamonga ne wetumami wakwe, wali weza kuzihulika zizi zono yazilonga Uliya, hobaho u mutemi yakasaka Uliya yawulagwe. Ninga Uliya yali yeza kuzihulika zizo, yakawa kutiziza ku Misili so woga.
22 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, u mutemi Yehoyakimu, yakatuma Elinatani u mwana wa Akiboli, hamonga ne wanhu wanji, wakabita ku Misili wakamulondole Uliya wamulete.
23 Nawo wakabita, wakamuleta Uliya kuli mutemi Yehoyakimu, nayo mutemi Yehoyakimu yakawa kumuwulaga, yakawa kuwuzika muwili wakwe mu vitenjelo vye wanhu wose.)
24 Ninga Yelemiya, vyono yali yali mu wutazi wa Ahikamu u mwana wa Shafani, holeka siwamukombola mu miwoko ye wanhu ho yawulagwe.
