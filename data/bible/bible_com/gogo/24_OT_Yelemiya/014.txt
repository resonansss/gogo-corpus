Yelemiya 14

Wu wula mvula kwa vilo vinji

1 Ku soko yo wu wula mvula mu'si kwa vilo vinji,

u Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya,

2 “Alu Yuda yeza yili mu cililo,

hambi i makaya gayo nha geza kufwa,

ne wanhu wayo, wagonile hasi du so wukalale,

nayo Yelusalemi, yikulilila wutazi.

3 I wagoli nawo, wakuwatuma i wetumami wawo ku lulenga,

wetumami wawa kubita ku visima,

hamba kutuga malenga yaye.

Hodu wawa kuwuya wali bwete wasina malenga.

Akuno wakoligwe ne zisoni, wakuwona cevu,

wakwihundika sunga no wusu wawo.

4 Hambi nha heza hasina mvula,

no muhangha wababala,

ne walimi wose, watuga wusungu mu zinhumbula zawo

nawo weza kwihundika sunga no wusu wawo.

5 Na mu mbago namo, sunga ne sawada nayo,

yikuyileka nyanagwe caka du

hambi nha mweza musina mahanze.

6 Nazo zinyhenje, zimile mu vizuli vye matunda,

zikutwetucila kotya maminzi,

sunga ne meso gazo, hamba si gakuwona viswanu yaye.

Hambi nha heza hasina mahanze.

7 Nawo i wanhu wangu, wakundilila wakutya,

‘Hamba wuwe u wubi wetu wukucihomelela,

ninga 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu wucitazaje du,

nha vyono gwegwe wehuwiza.

Na mbeka, ase cikulecile kali kenji du,

na kanyhi cituma wubi hali'gwe.

8 Ninga agwe yo gwegwe du wuli wene

mono wo witumbilo wa Isilayeli.

Kanyhi yo gwegwe du mono kuciponyaga mu magayo.

Alu nhawule winha munhu mujenzi mu yisi yetu,

hamba nho munhu mono yali mu muhinzo,

mono yakugona lizuwa limonga du?

9 Hamba nhawule winha munhu mono yatinuswe,

nhe citung'ati mono si kosuma kuponya?

Ninga mbeka, agwe go Muwaha Mulungu wuli hamonga nase!

Nase cili wanhu wako du,

holeka dedede, wulece kucileka.’ ”

10 Kanyhi u Muwaha Mulungu,

yakulonga kuli ze wanhu wawa, yakutya nhavi,

“Ninga wene wendile kunhiza'ne,

akuno hamba si wakusuma no kwiyimila kose.

Holeka nane si nghwendezwa nawo.

Alu nane nogakumbucila i makani gawo mabi,

niwalipicize ku soko yo wubi wawo.”

11 U Muwaha Mulungu, yakanonjela yakatya nhavi, “Alu, wulece kuwalombela kuli'ne wanhu wawa, ho niwaponye.
12 Nawo hamba walece kulya, ninga 'ne hamba si nowahuliciziza lukulu cililo cawo ho niwaponye, ng'o. Hamba wandavizye ne cilavywa cinyanywe, hamonga ne cilavywa co wusaje, ane si nokwendezwa nawo yaye. Langa 'ne nowawulajila wose ku wulugu na mu nzala, na mu matamwa.”

13 Ninga nane, ane Yelemiya, nghamupitula u Muwaha Mulungu nghamutya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, agwe wumanyile manyi manyi, nha vyono i wawizi wakuwalonjela wanhu, vyono holowa hasina kanyhi wulugu, hamba nzala, hambi nha vyo wehuwiza gwegwe, nha vyono, holowa mulamu du mu yisi yetu.”

14 U Muwaha Mulungu nayo yakapitula yakatya, “I wawizi walonjelaga mwi'tagwa lyangu, zinghani zo wulambi. Ninga 'ne hamba si niwatumile yaye, hamba kuwalajiliza nghani yinji yaye, hamba kuwalonjela nghani monga du. Holeka wene wakulonga zo wugubulilwe wono si wulawile kuli'ne yaye. Ku soko yiyo, u wuwile wawo, mbeka wusina cambuzi hamba cidodo bwete, hambi gago nha mahweso gawo du, gono wehwesize weneco.
15 Ninga ane no Muwaha Mulungu, nokulonjela luno zono nowanoza, i wawizi wawa wono wakwisolela zinghani weneco, wono ane si niwatumile yaye, wawa kulonjela mwi'tagwa lyangu, wakutya vyono ati wulugu ne nzala si vyosuma kwinjila mu yisi yiyi. Ane nowawulaga i wawizi wawa ko wulugu ne nzala.
16 Ne wanhu wose wono wali kulonjelwa zizo, nawo wowulajilwa mu nzila yiyo du. Ne mivimba yawo yogumilwa caka caka mu zinzila za mwi'kaya lya Yelusalemi. Na holowa hasina munhu wo wose mono koloyizika i mivimba yiyo. Ne nghani yiyi, yolowaziza wene wose, hamonga ne wagolece zawo, ne wana wawo we cilume na we cicekulu. Nane nolowalipiciza mu nghango ye metumo gawo mabi vyono genha.”

17 U Muwaha Mulungu yakandajiza niwalonjele wanhu

zo wukalale wangu niwatye nhavi,

“I meso gangu gasuluze mahozi misi ne cilo,

galece kuduma yaye,

hambi i wanhu wangu nha watuga nghomele

kanyhi watamiswe muno muno.

18 Nane lono nghubita mu mawimbi,

nghuyiwona i miwili ye wanhu wono wawulagwe mu wulugu,

na kolo nghubita mu makaya,

namo nghuwawona i wanhu wono wakugaya no kufwa so nzala.

Ninga nawo i wawizi hamonga ne wakohani,

wene wajendeleye du ne milimo yawo,

hamba kumanya yaye na cono wakwituma.”

I wanhu wakumuneneleza u Muwaha Mulungu

19 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

alu mwene hodu wamulemela lukulu Yuda?

Alo wawasulila lukulu i wanhu wa ku Sayuni lo?

Alu nha wacisuliliza muno,

sunga ceza si cikusuma hamba no kuponywa yaye?

Ase cali cilanjiliye kutuga mulamu,

ninga hamba kuziwona ziswanu yaye.

Cali citumbiye nha vyono coponywa,

ninga cikatuga wogofywe wuwaha muno.

20 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, ase cetuma wubi muno hali 'gwe,

holeka aluno, cikuwuvumila wubi wetu,

hamonga no wubi we walezi wetu.

21 Ninga wukumbucile wihuwizo wako,

holeka wulece kucisuliliza,

hamba kulikoza cevu likaya lyako Yelusalemi,

i hanhu he ligoda lyako lyo wutemi, lye nhojo.

Kanyhi wulece kucibena cilagane cono walagana nase.

22 Hambi mu makungugo gose, nha musina cihwani co cose cono

cosuma kutonyeza mvula, sunga ne mavunde,

nha sigosuma geneco du kunyunyutya mvula.

Holeka ase, witumbilo wetu wose,

wuli kuli 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu go Mulungu wetu.

Hambi nha yo gwegwe du mono kwitumaga zizi zose.
