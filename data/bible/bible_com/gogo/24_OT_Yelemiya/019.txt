Yelemiya 19

Nyungu yono yitulice

1 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela mbite nghagule nyungu ya malenga ko muwumbi, niwasolaje ne wanyamhala wanji we wanhu, hamonga ne wakohani wanji,
2 hanyuma ngholele ku mulango wa Potishedi, mbite sunga ko kwi'lolo lyo mwana wa Hinomu. Nghaganule kuko zinghani zono kononjela.
3 Hanyuma yakanonjela mbite nghatye nhavi, “Anye nyo watemi wa Yuda, hamonga ne wanyawusi wa Yelusalemi, ale muhulicize ta, zono ane no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli nghusaka kuzilonga. Ane nohaletela wongomele wuwaha muno i hanhu baha, u wongomele wono munhu wo wose kolo yakuzihulika, zinghani zizi, mbeka kocelewala.
4 Nane nozituma zizi, vyono i wanhu wangu wakondya kundeka, no kuhavufya i hanhu baha, so wulavya makumbiko kuli milungu yinji, i milungu yono weneco, hamba i walezi wawo, hamba i watemi wa Yuda, wali si wayimanyile yaye. Nawo wakondya kuhamemya hanhu bahano, ne sakami ye wanhu wono wasina masutya.
5 Kanyhi wakondya kumuzenjela Baali vikumbicilo vinji, vyono wowa kuwanyanyila i wana wawo mu moto, wawe likumbiko linyanywe kuli Baali. Ne nghani yiyi, yo nghani yono ane si nawalajiliza yaye, yo nghani yono nghali nehwesa na mu nhumbula yangu.
6 Holeka ku soko yiyo, i vilo vikuza, lono i hanhu bahano, si holocemwa kanyhi Tofeti, hamba Lilolo lyo mwana wa Hinomu. Langa lyolomanyika nhe Lilolo lyo Wuwulaji.
7 Ne hanhu bahano, ho nologewajiziza i mahweso ge wanhu wa Yuda, hamonga ne wanyawusi wa Yelusalemi. Nolowaleta i walugu wawo, waze wawasume, no kuwawulajila ku wulugu. Ne mivimba yawo, nolozipela zindeje ziyilye, hamonga ne malimu ga mu mbago.
8 Nane nololeta wogofywe, hamonga no wuhodanjice mumu mwi'kaya lilino, sunga ne wanhu wose wono wowa kukola behi nalyo, wowa kuyisinjisa ne mitwe yawo ko wizine wuwaha.
9 Ne walugu nawo wololipilima likaya, wawawulaje i wanhu wose wono wali mumo. No wupilimwe wuwo we likaya, wolowa wa wogofye muno. Hambi i wanhu wono wolowa wali mugati, nha wolowa kwiyilya weneco wose, wolowa kulya sunga ne wana wawo.”

10 Hanyuma ya zizo, u Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela niyitule nyungu yiyilya, niyitulile ha mwande ye wanhu wawalya wono nabitana nawo.
11 Niwalonjele nha vyono, u Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, “Ane nowabenanjiza i wanhu wawa, hamonga ne likaya lili, nawo wokwihwana ne nyungu yiyi ye lilongo, yono si yosumika kuyilunza hamonga kanyhi mazaye gayo. Nawo i wanhu, wolowa kuyizika mivimba ye wandugu zawo, sunga na kuko ku Tofeti, hambi nha holowa hasina kanyhi hanhu hanji ho hose ho kuwazika.
12 Nane nehuwiza nha vyono nolinozeza likaya lili, hamonga ne wekazi walyo wose, nha vyono nayinoza Tofeti.
13 Ne zikaya zose za Yelusalemi, ne zikaya zose ze watemi wa Yuda, du ne zikaya zose, zikaya zono zafusiswaga wuvumba kuli zinyelezi, hamonga ne hanhu hono halavizwaga i vilavywa vye divayi, ku soko yo kuyilavizya milungu yinji. Wawo wose wokwihwana ne Tofeti i nyavufywe.”

14 Hanyuma ya zizo, hodu, nghawa kuwuka kuko ku Tofeti, kono u Muwaha Mulungu yanhuma, mbite nghayiganule nghani yakwe. Nghawa kubita kwima ha mahakama ge Nyumba yo Mulungu, nghawa kuwalonjela wanhu,
15 nha vyono u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli katya nhavi, “Alu nane noliletela likaya lili, wulipicizwe wuwaha muno, hamonga ne vikaya vye behi behi, u wulipicizwe wono nali nawulonga. Hambi anye, nha muli wawanji muno, musina cihuliko, hamba si mosuma yaye kuzihulika zono 'ne nghuzilonga.”
