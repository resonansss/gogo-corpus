Malaki 1

1 Ayi yo nghani yono Muwaha Mulungu yamupela Malaki, yawalonjele wanhu wa Isilayeli.

Wendo wo Muwaha Mulungu kuli Isilayeli

2 Muwaha Mulungu yakuwalonjela wanhu wakwe yakutya, “Ane nimwendilenyi nhondo zose.” Ninga wene wakutya, “Wukuwulajila wule wendo wako kuli'se?” Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakupitula yakutya, “Alu wule! Esawu si yali nduguye wa Yakobo? Ninga nghamwenda Yakobo no lulelo lwakwe,
3 no kumusula Esawu no lulelo lwakwe. Ne yisi yakwe ye matunda, nghayinoza yiwe wambuce, nghayileka yiwe wikalo we macewe ga mu mbago.”
4 Na hono Waedomu u lulelo lwa Esawu watye, “Imakaya getu, heya gahodangwa, ninga cogazenga kanyhi.” Nayo Muwaha Mulungu kopitula yatye, “Heya, lece wagazenje kanyhi, ninga nane nogamwagulanga kanyhi. Ne wanhu wowacema watye, ‘Yisi yo wubi’ kanyhi ‘Lyo likungugo lyono Muwaha Mulungu yalikoleywe likuwo cibitilila.’ ”
5 Ne wanhu wa Isilayeli woloziwona zizi kwe meso gawo weneco, nawo wololonga vyono “Muwaha Mulungu yo we vilungo sunga kunze ye mipaka ye yisi ya Isilayeli.”

Muwaha Mulungu yakuwahosa Wakohani

6 Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga yakuwatya wakohani, “Mwana kamogopaga sogwe, no mutumwa nayo kamogopaga u muwaha wakwe. Ane nili sogo yenyu, alu nhawule si mukunyogopa? Nane ho nili mutwa wenyu, alu nhawule nha si munyogopile, langa mukunyihepa? Akuno kanyhi mukuwuza ti mukutya, Cikukwihepa wule?
7 Mukunyihepa mu nzila yo kundavizya vilavywa vye ndigwa nyavu mu cikumbicilo cangu, akuno kanyhi ho mukuwuza ti mukutya, calemwa nhawule kukupela cogopo? Alu nane nomulonjelenyi vyono mukucibedegala cikumbicilo cangu.
8 Alu mwene lono mukundetela cilavywa co mutugo wono wuli cibofu, hamba wono wutamigwe, hamba we cilema, muwa kundavizya likumbiko, mukwihwesa hongo vyono kusina yisutya mu nghani yiyo? Mujele ta kumupela u mulongola we'si, mitugo yono yinha nhavivyo! Wule, mukwihwesa kokwendezwa nanye no kumulumbenyi?”
9 Alu nanye nyo wakohani, mono mukumulomba Mulungu yawe muswanu kuli'se. Mwene sikowupitula wulombe wenyu, na lili lyowa yisutya lyenyu.
10 U Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga yakutya, “Yali yiwe yili ciba yunji wa muli nyenye, yadinde milango yo wuhembelo, ho muze mulece kwasa moto wono wusina cambuzi mu cikumbicilo cangu. Ane singhwendezwa nanye hamba hadodo, hamba sinovibocelela lukulu ne vilavywa vyono mukundavizya.
11 Wanhu ku wulawa ku mhelo ye yinze, sunga ko ku mhelo nzagwe, wose wanyogopile nene. Hanhu ho hose wakufusiza wuvumba, no kulavya vilavywa vyono vikwiliye. Na wose wawo, wanyogopile nene!”
12 Ninga anye si munyogopile 'ne, vyono mukuciwona cikumbicilo cangu si cinji yaye, lono mukulavya mucanyha yaco ndigwa nyavu, yono yisina cambuzi.
13 Akuno mukutya, “Ka! Lanje ta nghani ayi vyono yacikataza! Akuno mukunyile ne zimhula zenyu ku soko yangu. Na kuno mwandetela vilavywa vyenyu vyo mutugo wono muhize, kanyhi wina cilema na kanyhi wutamigwe, hambi nha vyo vilavywa vyono mukundetela. Alu mukwihwesa vyono 'ne, hongo nosuma kumubocelenyi vilavywa vye liwala lilyo mbi?
14 Wulijitwe wuli muli mukoci, mono yakundavizya vye mitugo yono yisoceye, na kuno, ho yena ne mitugo miswanu mwi'tewa lyakwe, yono yelahiza vyono komhela 'ne! Hambi 'ne nha nili mutemi Muwaha, ne makungugo gose, wanyogopile nene.”


Malaki 2

1 U Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga yakuwalonjela wakohani yakutya, “Lilajilizo lyenyu lyo lino. Munojeye munyogope nene muli zose zono mukwituma.”
2 Ninga hono mulece kuzihuliciza zono nghulonga, nane nomuletelenyi wulijitwe. Novilijita sunga na vyose vyono mukuvibocela so vitumamilwa vyenyu. Heya, nakondya kuvilijita, ku soko vyono si mukwibata lilajilizo lyangu musimbiliye.
3 Nane nowalipiciza wana wenyu, niwubuluje wusu wenyu ne matope ge mitugo zenyu zono mukulavya. Nanye mosolwa mutagwe ku cugulu hamonga ne matope gago.
4 Nanye baho ho molomanyila vyono, yo nene mono namupelenyi lilajilizo lili, ne cilagane cangu mu nzila ye wakohani wo lulelo lwa Lawi, naco hamba si colobenecela lukulu yaye.

5 “Na mu cilagane cangu cico, nawehuwiza wumi no mulamu, na zizi zo nawapela wene, ho wawe ne cogopo kuli'ne. Na mu vilo vivyo wakahulika no kunyogopa nene.
6 Wakejiza zono zali zili ling'ani, hamba si zimbi yaye. Wakawa wali hamonga nane, kanyhi hamba si wetuma ze ling'ani weneco du, langa kanyhi wakawataza sunga ne wanhu wanji walece kwituma zo wubi.
7 Yinojeye wakohani wejize mafundo ge mbeka go Mulungu. Wanhu ho wabite kuli wene, wakelanjize wuhile wangu, hambi wene nha wetumami wo Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga.

8 “Ninga alu, anye nyo wakohani, mwakondya kuwuka mu zinzila ze ling'ani, no wijize wenyu, wukuwatonga wenji ku wituma zimbi. Mwabena icilagane cangu cono calagana nanye.
9 Holeka nane, nowanoza i wanhu wa Isilayeli, wamusekanjenyi muno, vyono si mukuwutyatya wuhile wangu. Na lono mukuwejiza wanhu wangu, hamba si mukuwanozeza wose muling'ano.”

Wu wula wihuwilwa we wanhu kuli Mulungu

10 Ma ase wose, si cina sogo yetu du yumonga? Si yo Mulungu yuyo du, mono yaciwumba wose? Alu nhawule cikwiyibenela wihuwizo wetu sese wose, hamba nhawule cikucibedegala cilagane cono Mulungu yacinoza ne walezi wetu?
11 I wanhu wa Yuda, wakondya kuwubena wihuwizo wawo hali Mulungu, no kwituma zo wubi mu Yelusalemi, na mu yisi yose. Wayihodanga Nyumba yo Mulungu yono Muwaha Mulungu yayendile. Wanhu watola iwacekulu wono wakuhemba imilungu mijenzi.
12 Muwaha Mulungu yawawuse mu mhuga ya Isilayeli, wawo wono wakwituma zizi, hamba yalece kuwaleka kanyhi, wahanje mu wulavya vilavywa, vyono likungugo likumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga.
13 Ayi yo nghani yinji kanyhi yono mukwituma, mukucigubika cikumbicilo co Muwaha Mulungu kwe mahozi ge cililo cenyu, vyono mwene siyakuvibocela vilavywa vyono mukumulavizya.
14 Mukuwuza mukutya, nhawule siyakuvibocela, Heya siyakuvibocela, hambi nha yamanyile vyono agwe wabena wihuwizo wako kuli mugolece, mono wamutola lono wali wukali muzelelo. Mwene yali yo muyago, ninga agwe wabena wihuwizo wako kuli mwene, hamba nha yiwe wehuwiza ha mwande yo Mulungu, nha vyono kowa mwihuwilwa kuli mwene.
15 Hamba Mulungu siyamunozenyi 'nye muwe muwili monga no muhe monga? Mukwihwesa wuhile wakwe cali cicici muli zizi? Wuhile wakwe wali, ho mutuje wana wono wolowa wanhu we mbeka wo Mulungu. Holeka mwinozeleze, halece kuwa na yunji muli nyenye mono yakubena wihuwizo wakwe kuli mugolece wakwe.
16 Muwaha Mulungu we Waisilayeli yakutya nhavi, “Ane si niyendile nghani yiyi yo wilece. Ane siniyendeye lukulu nghani yiyi, lono muyenyu yunji yakwituma nghani 'mbi yono yinha yiyo kuli mugolece wakwe. Holeka wiyihenje go mweneco, wulecele lukulu kubena cilagane co kuwa mwihuwilwa kuli mugolece wako.”

Lizuwa lyo wuling'anizwe lili behi

17 Mwamukataza u Muwaha Mulungu ne zinghani zenyu zono mukulonga. Ninga kanyhi mukuwuza mukutya, “Camukataza nhawule?” Mwamukataza no wulonje wenyu wono mukutya, “Muwaha Mulungu kehwesaga i wetuma vibi wose waswanu, kanyhi yakuwenda.” Hamba lono mukutya, “Yali hayi Mulungu mono koling'aniza?”


Malaki 3

1 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakupitula yakutya, “Ane, nomutuma mutumwa wangu yanozelezeze nzila. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu mono mukumulanjilila, kolokuza nhinicizo mu Wuhembelo wakwe. No mutumwa mono mukumulanjilila, kolokuza no kuganula icilagane cangu.”
2 Nani 'mbi mono kolosuma kukangalila mwi'zuwa lilyo lyo wuze wakwe? Hamba nani mono kolosuma kwima kolo yakwijela mwene? Hambi mwene nha kolowa nha sabuni yono yikukanza myenda, kanyhi kolowa nha moto wono wukukwema fweza.
3 Kolokuza yaling'anize kotya mono yakukwema fweza no kuyeza. Kotya mono yakwituma mulimo wo kukwema fweza na zahabu. Nayo mutumwa wo Muwaha Mulungu, koloweza wakohani, ho wasume kutegula kuli Muwaha Mulungu, i vilavywa vyono vikwilaniciye.
4 Holeka vilavywa vyono wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, wolovileta kuli Muwaha Mulungu, vyolomwendeza, nha vyono vyamwendezaga ha mwamulizo.

5 Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga yakutya, “Ane nolomuzizenyi so wuling'anizi, nane nowa muhomelezi mupelu kuli wono wendile kwituma zo wuhawi, na muli wasenyha, na muli wono walavyaga wuhomelezi wo wulambi, na muli wono wawapujilaga i wetumami wawo musahala wawo, na muli wono wawewonelaga i wafwile, ne wana wono wasina walezi wawo, ne wajenzi. Sunga na muli wose wono si wanyogopile 'ne.”

U wulavye we zaka

6 “Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, mono sina cipitupitu yaye. Holeka nanye nyo lulelo lwa Yakobo, mukali si najililila lukulu ng'o.
7 Anye, mwihwanile ne walezi wenyu walongola, wagabedegala malajilizo gangu, hamba kugebata yaye. Ale mbuyilenyi nene, nane nomuwuyilenyi nanye. Ninga hono mutye, ‘Alu citende wule ho cisume kukuwuyila?’
8 Ale nane nghumuwuzenyi nghutya, wulenyi, yili viswanu munhu yamukonje Mulungu? Ng'o, si yowa viswanu. Ninga hamba nhavivyo anye mukunghonga 'ne. Kanyhi mukuwuza mukutya, ‘Alu cikumukonga nhawule?’ Mukunghogela mu nzila yo wulavya zaka ne vilavywa.
9 Holeka wulijitwe wuli muli nyenye wose, hambi likungugo lyose nha likunghonga nene.
10 Holeka letenyi ku Nyumba yo Mulungu zaka yenyu yono yikwilaniciye, ho hawe na ndigwa nyinji. Ta munjelaje, nanye mowona, nodigula madilisha ga ku wulanga, nimumwajilenyi ko winji muno muno i vinhu vyose i viswanu.
11 Hamba sinoleceleza mang'onyo gahodanje nhwanga yenyu hamba hadodo, sunga ne mizabibu yenyu, yolela zabibu nyinji muno muno.
12 Sunga ne wanhu we makungugo gose womucemenyi, ‘Muli wamoti’ hambi yisi yenyu nha yolowa hanhu haswanu muno ku wikale.”

Wihuwizo wo Mulungu wo lusungu

13 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Anye mwalonga nghani zo wogofye muno kuli'ne. Ninga kanyhi mukuwuza ti, mukutya, ‘Alo 'se cakulonga nghanici?’
14 Mwalonga mukatya, ‘Kumwitumamila Mulungu yo nghani ya bwete du. Hamba ho cili cambuzici mu wituma zono mwene yakulonga, hamba zo kujela kumulajila Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga nha vyono cikuzikalalila zono cetuma?
15 Ninga nha vyono ase cikuwona, i wanhu wevuni wo wawo wono wekalaga wena nyemo. Ninga i wanhu wabi, si vyono wakumota du, langa kanyhi wakuwa kumujela Mulungu wukangalizi wakwe mu metumo gawo mabi, nawo wakupona.’ ”
16 Ne wanhu wono wamogopile Muwaha Mulungu, walonganaga weneco wose, nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakawahuliciza, yakahulika zono walonganaga. Hali Mulungu, hakawa na citabu cono candikwe matagwa ga wose wono wamogopaga Muwaha Mulungu no kumuhulika.
17 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga yakutya, “Wawo wo wowa wanhu wangu. Na mwi'zuwa lyono nolofisiliziza wihuwizo wangu, i wene wolowa wanhu wangu. Nane nowawonela lusungu, nha vyono sogo yo munhu kamuwonelaga lusungu, mwana mono yakumwitumamila.
18 Kanyhi i wanhu wangu, ho wolowuwonela wisimajile wono wolowa, kuli we ling'ani na kuli i wanhu wabi, na kuli munhu mono yakunyitumamila, na kuli yulya mono si yakunyitumamila'ne.”


Malaki 4

Lizuwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu likuza

1 U Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga yakutya, “Lizuwa likuza lyono i wanhu we cibede, hamonga ne wanhu wabi, wolotakamizwa nhe mahanze. Mwi'zuwa lilyo, wene wolotakamizwa. Hamba si wolosigazizwa na cinji co cose.
2 Ninga kuli'nye mono muhulicile 'ne, i vilungo vyangu vye mhonelo, vyolomulawilenyi nhe'zuwa, no kumuletelenyi mhono, nhe nyhenjezi ye lizuwa. Mowa muli welecele, akuno musangalaye, kotya nyehe ndigulile mu wulawa mwi'kumbo.
3 Na mwi'zuwa lyono nolokuziza kuwufisiliza wihuwizo wangu, anye molowasuma i wabi, nawo wolokwihwana manghundi hasi ye magulu genyu.

4 “Ninga mukumbucile mejizo go mwitumami wangu Musa, hamonga no wulajilize, ne malajilizo gono namupelela kwi'tunda lya Sinayi, anye wanhu wose wa Isilayeli, mugebate.

5 “Ninga lono likali si naza lizuwa lilyo liwaha lyo wogofye lyo Muwaha Mulungu, ane nomutumilenyi muwizi Eliya.
6 Nayo kolo yakuza, kozipitula zinhumbula ze wasogo zo munhu zilanje wana wawo, ne zinhumbula ze wana, nazo, zilanje wasogo zawo, ho nize ndece kuyihodanga yisi yenyu.”
