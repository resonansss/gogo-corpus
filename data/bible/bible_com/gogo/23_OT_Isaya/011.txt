Isaya 11

Wutemi wo Mulamu

1 U wityatye we ndelo ye citemi mu mhuga ya Dawudi,

wihwanile no mubici wono wadumulwa,

ninga lisina lyayo, likutula kanyhi manhambi ganji.

Holeka mutemi mupya kololelwa muli wawo i wana wa Dawudi.

2 Nayo muhe wo Muwaha Mulungu kolomupela mahala,

hamonga no wizuce no wunghula

wo kuwatemela wanhu wakwe.

Nayo kolowumanya wuhile wo Muwaha Mulungu

kanyhi kowa ne cogopo kuli mwene.

3 Nayo kowona wendezwe muno mu cihuliko ca muli mwene.

Hamba no wuling'anizi wakwe,

sikoling'aniza so zinghani zono kolowona ne meso,

hamba zono kolohulika kwe makutu gakwe.

4 Langa kolowaling'aniza wasocele ko wiling'ano,

no kulamula nghani yo muciwa kwe ling'ani.

Nawo wanhu wolowa kulipicizizwa mu wulajilize wakwe,

ne wanhu wetuma zimbi wolowulagwa.

5 No wiling'ano wolowa musipi wa mu ciwino cakwe,

no wihuwilwa wo musipi wo kwiwopela.

6 Nago maminzi ne zingholo,

vyolokwikalana hamonga ko mulamu,

nago masuwi, golowa gagonile hamonga ne zinyana ze zimhene,

nazo zindama ne mana ge simba, zolodima hamonga.

Ne wana wadodo, wo wolowa wakuzidima.

7 Zing'ombe nazo, ne madubu, zolodima hamonga,

nazo zinyehe ne mana ge dubu,

zolowa zikugona hamonga ko mulamu.

Nazo simba zololya mahanze nha ng'ombe.

8 Hamba mwana mugalika,

yedawale behi ne lizoka, lyono lina sumu nghali,

ninga si lyolomuluma yaye.

9 Na mwi'tunda lyo Mulungu lyono lyelile lya Sayuni,

molowa musina citamisi co cose hamba cibi co cose.

Hambi yisi nha yolomema wumanye wo Muwaha Mulungu

nha vyono lilenga limemile malenga kose.

I wanhu wono wahamywa wolowuya

10 Ne lizuwa lyolokuza, lono mutemi mupya, mono kololawa mu wityatye we mhuga ye citemi ya Dawudi, kolowa citanjilo kuli makungugo. Makungugo golokwihanguliziza hamonga kwi'kaya lyakwe lyo wutemi, no kumupela lilumbo.
11 Na yolowa mwizuwa lilyo, U Muwaha Mulungu kolokwitumamila kanyhi vilungo vyakwe, yawagomole wanhu wakwe wono wowa wakali wasigaye ku Ashuli, na ku Misili, na ku Patilosi, na ku Kushi, na ku Elamu, na ku Sinali, na ku Hamati, ne zi'si zose za ku zimhwani.

12 Muwaha Mulungu kolowenulila bendela makungugo,

ho yagalajile nha vyono, aluno nhondo,

yeza kuwakongholeza kanyhi hamonga,

i wanhu wa Isilayeli na Yuda,

wono wali weyagaye ako na ako,

yawagomole ku wulawa ku zimhelo ze yinze zose vyono yine.

13 No wutemi we Waisilayeli si wolowuwonela wivu

kanyhi u wutemi wa Yuda.

No wutemi wa Yuda nawo si wolowa mulugu

kanyhi wo wutemi wa Isilayeli.

14 Langa wene wolokwilunza hamonga,

wabite wakayihome yisi ye Wafilisiti wa ku lubavu lwa kumwezi,

wawatece ne wanhu wono wekaye ku cilima.

Kanyhi wolowasuma i wanhu wa ku Edomu, na Mowabu.

Nawo i wanhu wono wekaye mu yisi ya Amoni,

wolowa wena cihuliko kuli wene.

15 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu,

kololinyaza lilenga lya Shamu,

kololeta mbeho yono yili moto,

yalinyaze likolongo lya Efulata,

ninga kololeka vikolongo vidodo-dodo vili mupunghati,

munhu wo wose ho yasume kulowoka hakavu.

16 Kanyhi holowa na nzila mbaha yono yilawiliye ku Ashuli,

yono wolowa kukola i wanhu wakwe Waisilayeli

wono wolowa wakali wasigaye kuko ku Misili.

Nha vyono yali yinha, lono i walezi wawo wawucila ku Misili.
