Isaya 12

Lwimbo lo Wulumbe

1 Ne lizuwa lyolokuza lono wanhu wolokwimba wawe kutya,

“Nghukulumba 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu!

Hamba wuwe wali wungholeywe likuwo,

ninga aluno wusindamice,

wulece kungholelwa kanyhi likuwo.

2 Mulungu yo mhonelo yangu nomwitumbila mwene,

hamba si nokogopa cinji yaye.

Hambi Muwaha Mulungu,

nha yo kamhelaga 'ne vilungo no wusume,

kanyhi yo muponyi wangu.

3 Ne wanhu wo Mulungu wasangalalaga lono yawaponya,

nha vyono malenga ge mbeho gamusangalazaga

mono yena nyhilu mwizuwa likali.”

4 Ne lizuwa lyolokuza lono wanhu wolokwimba wawe kutya,

“Mulumbenyi Muwaha Mulungu!

Mucemelezenyi yamutazenyi!

Mugalonjele makungugo gose ze metumo gakwe!

Muwalonjele zo wuwaha wakwe!

5 Mwimbilenyi Muwaha Mulungu

so metumo gakwe mawaha gono yetumile.

Ne wanhu wose wazihulice zo lukumyo lwakwe.

6 Muwalekaje wose wono wekaye mu Sayuni,

wembe ne mazi mawaha wawe kutya,

Mulungu mono yelile we Waisilayeli, yo muwaha,

kanyhi yekalane ne wanhu wakwe.”
