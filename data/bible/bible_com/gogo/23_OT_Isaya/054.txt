Isaya 54

U wendo wo Muwaha Mulungu kuli Isilayeli

1 Agwe Yelusalemi, wali wihwanile mucekulu mugumba

mono si kalelaga,

ninga luno kokwimba muno ko wusangalale.

Hambi nha kowa wina wana wenji muno

wucizize no mucekulu mono yena no mulume wakwe.

2 Holeka luno wonjeleze wugazi we lihema lyako lyono wikaye,

wuyitalimhye ne milejehe yayo,

wuzisikamye ne zimambo zayo!

3 Kanyhi koyigazimhya ne mipaka yako mbavu zose,

hambi wanhu wako wono webatwe ne

makungugo ganji, nha wolowuya wose.

Ne makaya gono galekwa, gose gomema wanhu.

4 Holeka wulece kogopa,

hambi nha si kokozwa cevu hamba kupuvywa yaye kanyhi.

No wu wula wihuwilwa wako,

kowusemwa nho mucekulu mono yakali mudodo,

no wumondwe wo wufwile wako

hamba si kowukumbucila kanyhi yaye.

5 Hambi u muwumbi wako, nha yo mulume wako,

ne litagwa lyakwe yakucemwa Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga.

U Muwaha Mulungu mono yelile wa Isilayeli yo kolokuponya,

hambi mwene nha yo mutemi we yinze yose.

6 Agwe Isilayeli,

wehwana no mucekulu mono yakali mudodo,

mono yalecigwe no mulume wakwe,

mono yeza yali mu wusungu wuwaha.

Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakukucemeleza

wumuwuyile, yakutya nhavi,

7 “Ane nali nghulecile cipango cidodo du

ninga so wendo wangu wuwaha kuli'gwe

holeka nokukongholeza nghugomole kanyhi.

8 Na mwi'kuwo lyangu,

holeka nghehundika wusu wangu hali'gwe, cipango cidodo,

ninga nokulajila zo wendo wangu cibitilila.”

Nhavivi vyo yakutya Muwaha Mulungu Mukombozi wako.

9 “Nane mu cipango ca Noha,

nehuwiza nha vyono si nololetela lukulu

kanyhi liwazi mono'nze.

Naluno kanyhi nghwihuwiza

nha vyono si nolokukolelwa likuwo kanyhi yaye,

hamba kukutacila, hamba kukulipiciza ng'o.

10 Matunda ne vigongo, vyolotenjelemulwa,

ninga wendo wangu kuli'gwe si wolomalika yaye.

Nane nowukamisa sunga cibitilila wihuwizo wangu

wo mulamu kuli'gwe.”

Nhavivi vyo yakutya Muwaha Mulungu

mono yakukuwonela lusungu.

Yelusalemi vyono yolowa hanyuma

11 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi,

“Gwe Yelusalemi, mbeka wagazika muno,

'gwe kaya lyono lisina wutazi,

ninga 'ne noyizenga kanyhi misinji yayo

ne mabwe go wuguzi wukamu.”

12 Noyizenga minala yako ne vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu,

milango yako nayo ne mabwe gono gakung'alala nha moto.

Aga nago majinji gose gono gakupilime

nogazenga nhavivyo ne vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu.

13 Nane no mweneco nowejiza wanhu wako,

kanyhi nowamotya wekale wapolile.

14 Nagwe kosikamywa mu wiling'ano na mu ling'ani,

nagwe hamba si kokwiwonelwa kanyhi

hamba kuwa no woga yaye.

15 Na hono yunji yaze yakuhome,

yuyo kowa kuzinoza zizo mweneco du

ninga ane si nimukomboye yaye.

Kanyhi munhu wo wose mono kokwisumaniza nagwe,

munhu yoyo kolemwa mweneco du.

16 Ane nha yo nene mono namuwumba mutyani,

mono kafugutaga moto we makala,

kawa kutyana vitwalo,

kanyhi yo nene mono namuwumba musilikale,

mono kavitumamilaga no kuwulajila vitwalo vivyo.

17 “Ninga hamba nhavivyo,

howa hasina citwalo co cose

cono cosuma kukutamisa yaye.

Nagwe kowa wina yo kumupitula munhu

wo wose mono yakukusitaka.

Ane nowadima wetumami wangu

nowapela no wusume.”

Vyo yakutya Muwaha Mulungu.
