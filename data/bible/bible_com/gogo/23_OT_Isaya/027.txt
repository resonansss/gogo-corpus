Isaya 27

1 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo, Muwaha Mulungu kolokwitumamila mumaje we lifwa, kwe vilungo vyakwe, kololilipiciza lilimu lyono likucemwa Lewiyatani, lizoka lyono libinzulaga, liwa kwinyongola, no kuliwulaga lizoka lyono likalaga mwi'lenga.

2 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo,

u Muwaha Mulungu kololonga

zo mugunda wakwe muswanu we mizabibu, yawe kutya,

3 “Nene no Muwaha Mulungu mono niwukamisaga,

kanyhi niwumwajilaga ne malenga nhondo zose.

Yo nene mono niwulindilizaga ku cilo ne misi,

ho yunji yalece kuwuhodanga.

4 Hamba nghali si nawukolelwa likuwo yaye u mugunda wuwu.

Na hono gawe mogali mamivya ne mabijili,

gono gosaka gawutitilize,

ane nigalimaga niwa kuganyanya.

5 Ninga nawo i walugu we wanhu wangu,

hono wasace niwadime,

yiwanojeye wamule welamule nane.

Heya mbeka, yiwanojeye sunga welamule nane.”

6 Na mu vilo vivyo vyono vikuza,

i wanhu wa Isilayeli, wo lulelo lwa Yakobo,

wolocindika nha mubici,

wolosanula no kupasicila,

no kuyimemya yinze yose mikalo.

7 Hambi Waisilayeli nha si watuga wulipicizwe wuwaha

nha vyono walipicizwa i walugu wawo,

ne wanhu wa Isilayeli,

hamba si wafwa wenji,

nha vyono wafwa walugu wawo.

8 Langa Muwaha Mulungu,

yawalipiciziza wanhu wakwe,

mu nzila yo kuwahamya no kuwategula ku yisi yo wujenzi.

Lono yawahwajiliza no mukukunzo we mbeho nghali ya kucilima.

9 Ninga wubi we Waisilayeli, wolonenelwa du,

kolo wene wakondya kugatulanjiziza lukulu i mabwe

ge vikumbicilo vye wanhu wono si wamumanyile Muwaha Mulungu,

hawe hasina kanyhi vikumbicilo vyo kufusiza wuvumba,

hamba vihwani vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela,

vyono vikali visigaliye.

10 Woyi hee!

I magomawakali ge makaya, gaweza 'lu matongo.

Gakondya kulekwa gasina wanhu,

geza nha mbago yono yisina cinji.

Alu haweza wudimilo we zing'ombe,

zikudima no kuceha mumo.

11 Sunga ne manhambi ge mibici gakunyala,

gakubeneka, ne wacekulu nawo wakutenya mumo ne zingwagu.

Hambi i wanhu nha siwakutanga yaye,

holeka nayo Mulungu u Muwumbi wawo,

si kowawonela lusungu, hamba si kowanenela yaye.

12 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo, u Muwaha Mulungu kolowakongholeza wanhu wakwe yumonga yumonga nha vyono munhu kelaga wuhemba mu mawaje ku wulawa Efulata, sunga ko ku mupaka we yisi ye Misili.
13 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo, ndulele yololila, yiwacemeleze Waisilayeli wose wono wali ku wuhamywe, ku yisi ya Ashuli, na ku yisi ya Misili, wose wawuye. Nawo wolowuya, wamuhembe Muwaha Mulungu mwi'tunda lyakwe lyono lyelile lya mu Yelusalemi.
