Isaya 44

Muwaha Mulungu yo Mulungu yali wene du

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya,

“Wuhulicize 'gwe go nghungugo ya Isilayeli

go mwitumami wangu,

anye nyo wanhu wangu wahagulwa

nyo wana wa Yakobo.

2 Ane yo nene no Muwaha Mulungu mono

namuwumbenyi wulawa wulelwe wenyu.

Holeka mulece kogopa, anye nha muli

wetumami wangu.

Kanyhi muli wanhu wangu, wendece wangu.

3 Nane noyipela malenga yisi yono yina nyhilu,

kanyhi notula makolongo gahulume mu yisi nghavu.

Nane nowajidila zo muhe wangu i wana wenyu.

Kanyhi nowalugutiza we zindelo zenyu.

4 Nawo wocindika viswanu nhe mahanze mamwajile

viswanu malenga, wowa nho mubici wono wucindice

ha mivwilo ye malenga.”

5 “Nawo wanhu wolonga yumonga yumonga wawe kutya,

‘Ane nili wo Muwaha Mulungu.’

Nawo wolokuza wehangulize hamonga

ne wanhu wa Isilayeli.

Nawo wose munhu na lwakwe

kolokwiyandika litagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu

mu mhukumhuku yakwe,

no kwiyitya vyono yaweza yumonga

ne wanhu wo Mulungu.”

6 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu mono yatemile, kanyhi

mono kawadimaga Waisilayeli

u Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga,

yakutya nhavi,

“Ane yo nene mulongozi, na wo mundumo,

ane mono nili Mulungu nili wene,

na hasina kanyhi mulungu yunji langa nene du.

7 Wulenyi, hambi ho yuli yunji kanyhi

mono kusuma kuzituma zono ane netuma?

Hamba nani mbi mono yali yasume

kuwila zose nha vyono zolokwijela

ku wulawa katali ha mwamulizo, sunga

za ku vilo vya ku ndumo?

8 Holeka anye wanhu wangu, mulece kogopa!

Hambi nha mumanyile nha vyono ku wulawilila

mu vilo vye katali sunga nalu,

ane nali nakondya kuwila, nha vyono zizi zolokwijela,

kanyhi yo nyenye wahomelezi wangu.

Wulenyi, hambi ho yuli mulungu yunji kanyhi?

Hamba ho yuli mulungu yunji mono nayo yali vilungo

mono ane nghali namuhulika?”

Wuhemba vihwani wukubedegalwa

9 Nawo wanhu wono wakunoza vihwani viseswe, wose wapuwile, sunga ne vihwani vyawo vye milungu vyono wakuviguza ko wuguzi wukamu, navyo visina hamba cambuzi cidodo bwete. Nawo wanhu wawo wono wakuyihemba milungu ye vihwani viseswe, mbeka wabofu, kanyhi wasina wizuce hamba hadodo, kanyhi wokolwa cevu.
10 Kanyhi hamba si yinojeye munhu yenozeze cihwani cenjedulwe, yacinoze ciwe yo mulungu yacihembe!
11 Hambi nha munhu wo wose mono kocihemba, nha kokolwa soni. Nawo wanhu wono wakusesa vivyo vihwani, nha wanhu du, wono wasina cinji co cose. Holeka anye muwalekaje du, hambi nha wose wolokwima ha wuling'anizi, nawo wolokogofywa muno no kukolwa cevu.

12 Nayo u mutyani, nha yakuwa kusola cuma cidodo du, kawa kucinyanya mu moto. Hanyuma yakucinoza cuma cico mu hwani yono yakuyisaka mweneco. Ninga na lono yakutyana, katugaga nzala, ne nyhilu ye malenga, hamonga no mukatalo.
13 Nayo muselemala kapimaga lubawo ne cipimo cakwe. Kawa kuwicila citanjilo ne kalamu. Hanyuma kawa kuyisesa ne visesezo vyakwe, kawa kuyinoza yiwa yina hwani yo munhu ne futa yono yikwendeza, kawa kuciwika cihwani cico mu kaya yakwe.
14 Hanji yadahile kuyitemanga mibici yo muhagata, ho yayitumamile, hamba lunji mibici ye minghola, hamba mubici wo wose, wa mu mbago. Hamba lunji kohadika mubici wo muhagata, kawa kulindila mvula yiwutonyele yiwuwiluse.
15 Na hanyuma kawa kusola libici linji lyo mubici wuwo, so ngwagu, na linji kawa kusesa cihwani, Ne mabici ganji go mubici wuwo kawa kugetumamila nha ngwagu zono kokota ho zimupele civuce, no kutelecela ndigwa zakwe. Ne libici linji lyo mubici wuwo du, kawa kusesa mulungu, kawa kumuhemba.
16 Kanyhi ngwagu zinji, yakupembela moto, kawa kuwitumamila so kukasika nyama, no kuyilya, nayo kawa kwiguta. Hanyuma kawa kwiyoteza ha moto akuno yakutya, “Kaa! Nye civuce ciswanu si! Nye moto wanoga si!”
17 Ne mabici ganji gono gasigaye go mubici wuwo du, kawa kusesa cihwani, na hanyuma yayo ati, kawa kucinamila no kucihemba. Kawa kucilomba yakutya, “Agwe yo gwegwe mulungu wangu, nghukulomba wumhonye!”

18 Na mbeka wanhu we liwala lilyo, wapuwile, hamba si wakuzimanya bwete na zono wakwituma yaye. Hambi meso gawo hamonga ne mahala gawo, nha wafufunize, ho walece kuyimanya i mbeka.
19 Nayo yuyo mono kasesaga vivyo vihwani, yasina hamba lihweso lyo kutya, “Alu mwene mabici ganji ge libici lili, nha nigapembela. No moto wayo nhelecela ndigwa yangu, kanyhi nghasicila ne nyama yangu, niwa kuyilya. Hanyuma libici lyono lisigaye niwa kusesa cihwani. Naluno nicifugamiye cihwani ciseswe co mubici wu wulya!”
20 Ne nghani yiyi, yo nghani yo wulele, yihwanile wubwiga mavu. Hambi i mahweso gakwe go wupufi, nha gakondya kumwajiliza nzila, ho yalece kuyiwona nzila yono yina wutazi. Hamba si yakusuma kumanya, nha vyono cico cihwani cono yebate mu muwoko wakwe, si mulungu yaye.

Muwaha Mulungu yo Muwumbi, kanyhi yo Muponyi

21 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, nhavi,

“Anye nyo nghungugo ya Isilayeli,

mukumbucile ta nghani ayi,

nha vyono anye muli wetumami wangu.

Ane yo nene mono namuwumbenyi muwe wetumami wangu.

Holeka nane hamba si nomusemwenyi yaye.

22 Nakondya kuwupatila lukulu wubi wenyu nhe livunde.

Holeka mumbuyilaje.

Hambi nha yo nene du mono nghumuponyenyi.”

23 Mwimbe ne nyemo 'nye nyo wulanga!

Nanye nyo kusi ye 'si, mwimbe mwihindile muno!

Nanye nyo matunda hamonga ne mabici gose ga mu mbago,

mwimbe ne nyemo kwe mazi mawaha.

Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha kawulajila wuwaha wakwe,

mu nzila yo kuwaponya wanhu wa Isilayeli.

24 “Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, u muponyi wenyu.

Ane yo nene mono namuwumbenyi.

Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu,

u muwumbi we vinhu vyose

yo nene mono nawutandaza wulanga na lono nayiwumbile yinze,

hasina hamba monga mono yanhaziliza.

25 Yo nene mono nivimilaga vitanjilo vye watowa visangu,

no kuwapela lukwale i waganga,

yo nene mono niwalambizaga we mahala,

no kulajila vyono mahala gawo yo nghani ya wulele du.

26 Ninga kolo mwitumami wangu yakuwila,

nane kolo nghumutuma mwitumami wangu

ho yakalajile ze mahweso gangu,

nane niganozaga mahweso gago

hamonga no wuwile wuwo viwa vya mbeka.

Nane nghuyilonjela Yelusalemi,

nha vyono wanhu wowuya wekale mumo kanyhi,

na vyono ne makaya ga Yuda gozengwa kanyhi.

Ne makaya gago, i matongo gayo gokwinulwa.

27 Ane nongaga nghani du, niwa kulinyaza lilenga.

28 Nghumulonjela Kulo, nghutya,

‘Agwe yo gwegwe mono kowufisiliza wuhile wangu,

nagwe konoza zono nghukulajiliza

kololajiliza Yelusalemi yizengwe kanyhi

hamonga no musinji we Nyumba yo Mulungu.’ ”
