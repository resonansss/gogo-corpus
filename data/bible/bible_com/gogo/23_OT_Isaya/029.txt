Isaya 29

Zimbali zono zoyagana Yelusalemi

1 Woyi hee, kuli likaya lya Yelusalemi,

lyono co cikumbicilo co Mulungu!

Woyi hee 'likaya lyono Dawudi yasita nghambi!

Ale, mulece mwaka monga,

hamba myejete yipulute,

nalyo liwe lijendeleye ne zisikukuu zalyo.

2 Ninga hanyuma Mulungu koliletela

wongomele likaya lilyo lyono lyacemwaga,

“I cikumbicilo co Mulungu.”

Na mu mapango gago, colowa cililo no wukalale,

ne likaya lyose lyolokwihwana cikumbicilo

cono cihiliwe sakami cose.

3 Nayo Mulungu kololihoma likaya,

yalipilime, yalizinjilize mbavu zose.

4 Nalyo likaya lya Yelusalemi,

lyolowa kutakanya hasi nho munhu mono yena mhepo,

no wulonje walyo,

wolowa wa wupwepwe wono wukulawilila baho hasi mu manghundi.

5 Ninga nago mapuga ge wajenzi i walugu wako,

golopemheluswa ne mbeho nhe manghundi.

Ne mapuga gawo ge wasilikale gono gakogofya,

golopemheluswa nhinicizo nhe mawaje.

6 Ninga u Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga,

kolomuponyenyi anye mu mbali ze madidimo,

hamonga na ze matulo ge'si.

Mulungu kololeta libeho lyo mukukunzo,

hamonga no moto we malambi mawaha,

7 no moto wuwo wolowongomeza wasilikale

wose we makungugo gose

gono gakulihoma likaya lya Yelusalemi,

likaya lye cikumbicilo co Mulungu.

Wolokongomezwa hamonga ne vitwalo vyawo vyose vyo wulugu,

hamonga ne vinhu vyawo vyose vyono wena navyo.

Vyose vyolokwajilila lukulu nha ndoto,

vyowa nhe vinhu vyono vyali kwihweswa du ne cilo.

8 Ne makungugo gose gono golowa gekongholeye

hamonga ho galewane ne likaya lya Yelusalemi,

golokwihwana no munhu mono yadabuce,

alu yakulota yakulya ndigwa,

ninga kumbe kolo yakusinhuka mu zinhongo,

yakwagana nzala vyo yikutama du.

Hamba lunji nho munhu mono yena nyhilu muno muno ye malenga,

alu yakulota yakung'wa malenga,

ninga kumbe kolo yakusinhuka mu zinhongo,

yakwagana nyhilu ye malenga vyo yimwibate du.

Wu wula kucelelela kuli zo wusugulwe

9 Na hono mwipele ku wulele nyo weneco,

na mbeka mocila kuwa walele!

Na hono mwipele ku wubofu nyo weneco,

na mbeka mojendelela kuwa wabofu!

Na mokwijela mugalile,

ninga kumbe ho si muwung'weye no 'jimbi yaye.

Kanyhi mokwijela mukupebesuka-pebesuka,

ninga kumbe ho si muwujezize bwete no wujimbi.

10 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu

nha kamupelenyi nhongo ze lifwefwe,

ho mugone hawaha.

Kanyhi yali yiwanojeye wawizi wawe go meso ge wanhu,

ninga Mulungu yakondya kuwabofuza meso gawo.

11 Ne zinhunizo zo wugubulilwe wose wo wuwizi, wowa muhundikwe kuli'nye. Wowa nhe zinghani ze citabu cono cigubikwe kanyhi cinamatizwe ne cinamatizo.
12 Na hono mumupele munhu mono si yamanyile kusoma, mumutye yamusomelenyi, nayo ko mupitulenyi nha vyono mwene hamba si yamanyile yaye kusoma.

13 Ninga nayo Muwaha Mulungu yalonga yakatya,

“I wanhu wawa, wenozaga nha wakuhemba nene,

ninga kumbe ne zinghani zawo ho zisina nhunizo yo yose,

ne zinhumbula zawo zili kunji caka.

Ne cogopo cawo ca kuli'ne nghani ya bwete du,

hambi wene nha wakutyatya wulajilize we wanhu,

hamonga ne myoto yono wayizoweye

nha vyono wakondya kwiyijiza.

14 Ninga nane nowatowa nhinicizo kali kenji.

Na muli wanhu wawo we mahala,

wolelepa, nawo wasugu,

no wusugu wawo wowa wa bwete du.”

Witumbilo wa mu vilo vyono vikuza

15 Ninga woyi hee,

kuli wanhu wono wakujela kumuhundika Muwaha Mulungu mahweso gawo!

Wakuzinoza zinghani zawo cinyele akuno wakwihwesa

nha vyono hasina yunji mono yakuwawona yaye,

hamba kuzimanya zono wakwituma ng'o.

16 Wakuzipitulanya zinghani.

Alu mwene u muwumbi we nyungu ne lilongo,

ca hayi ciwaha?

Hamba cinhuci cono munhu yacinozize cono kanyhi comutya,

“Agwe yo si gwegwe mono nozize?”

Hamba lunji cimutye,

“Agwe hamba si wumanyile yaye na cono wukwituma?”

17 Na zizo lono zowa zikali zijendeleye kulongwa,

cidaha cidodo du, mbago mbaha yogaluka mugunda,

nawo mugunda kanyhi wowa kwijela nha mbago mbaha.

18 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo kolo lyaza,

i we ciziwi wolohulika kolo citabu cikusomwa kwe'zi yiwaha.

Nawo wono wali cibofu,

wono wekalaga mu wutitu,

meso gawo golonugula wasume kuwona.

19 Nawo i waciwa, hamonga ne wanhu wenenenyhezi,

wolotuga kamonga nyemo yono Muwaha Mulungu,

u Mulungu mono yelile we Waisilayeli kalavyaga.

20 Na mwizuwa lilyo lyolowa lyo lizuwa

lyo mudumo kuli wanhu wono wendile kuwewonela wayawo,

no kumubedegala Mulungu.

Ne wanyo wubi wose, wolokongomezezwa lukulu.

21 Mulungu kolowongomeza wanhu wose

wono wendile kuwasonjeza-sonjeza wayawo.

Hamonga na wono wendile kuwahijila i wabeno lajilize,

na wono wendile kulonga zo wulambi

ho wawapamanjize iwanhu

we ling'ani walece kunozezwa i ling'ani yawo.

22 Holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu,

u Mulungu wa Isilayeli,

mono yamuponya Abulahamu mu magayo,

yakutya, nhavi, “Alu anye nyo wanhu wangu,

hamba si molokozwa cevu kanyhi,

sunga no wusu wenyu si wolosininghalila lukulu kanyhi so cevu.

23 Nanye kolo mwawawona wana wenyu wono nolomupelenyi,

nhondo ho mulamanyila vyono,

ane yo nene Mulungu mono nelile,

u Mulungu wa Isilayeli.

Nanye baho ho mulamhelela lilumbo no kwikala mu cogopo cangu.

24 Ne wanhu walele nawo wolotuga wizuce.

Ne wanhu wono wekalaga wakung'ung'ulika nhondo zose,

nawo wolokwenda kulanjizwa.”
