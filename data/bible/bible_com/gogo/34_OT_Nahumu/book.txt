Nahumu 1

1 Ayi yo nghani kuli Ninawi,

nha vyono yagubulilwa Nahumu

mono yali munya Elikoshi.

Likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu kuli Ninawi

2 U Muwaha Mulungu sikowakangalila

wono wakwisumaniza nayo,

langa kawalipicizaga wose

wono wakumupinga-pinga,

na mwi'kuwo lyakwe,

mwene mo kawalipicizizaga.

3 Langa mwene Muwaha Mulungu,

sikakolwaga ne likuwo mbela,

ninga yali vilungo,

hamba sikalekaga kuwulipiciza wubi.

Ho hose hono Muwaha Mulungu yakukola,

cijelaga civulavumbi.

Ne mavunde,

go manghundi gono galawaga mu magulu gakwe.

4 Nayo kalilajilizaga lilenga,

nalyo liwa kunyala,

kanyhi kanyazaga ne makolongo.

Sunga ne migunda

yono yili ku Bashani yiwa kunyala,

ne litunda lya Kalimeli nalyo,

liwa ne liwala nha lye mahanze gono ganyalile,

ne maluwa ga ku Lebanoni gawa kuwomba.

5 Ne matunda gawa kucicima

ha mwande yo Muwaha Mulungu,

ne vigongo vyayo viwa kwenjeduka

ha mwande yakwe.

Nayo Muwaha Mulungu,

kolo yakwilajila,

yisi yiwa kusinjisika,

yinze hamonga ne wanhu wose wawa kucicima.

6 Nani mwene mono kosuma kupona,

kolo mwene yakukolwa likuwo?

Hamba nani mono kolosuma kusigala

mu wogofye we likuwo lyakwe?

Yakwita lilambi lye likuwo lyakwe,

ne matalawe gakubiduka

mu manghundi ha mwande yakwe.

7 U Muwaha Mulungu muswanu,

kawadimaga wanhu wakwe

mu cipango co wugaye;

kawakamisaga wose wono wakumuwuyila mwene.

8 Langa mwene kawongomezezaga lukulu walugu wakwe,

nhe 'hwazi lyono likuhuluma.

Nawo wono wakwisumaniza nayo,

kawafisaga sunga ko ku wufwe wawo.

9 Alu mukwihwesa cici muli Muwaha Mulungu?

Mwene komuhodanjenyi.

Hamba hasina yunji kanyhi mono kokwidoma nayo.

10 Nanye mo wagalo 'jimbi,

molotakamizwa nhe mamivya

gono gedawilize ne mahanze makavu.

11 Ku wulawa kuli'gwe Ninawi, yakaza munhu mono yali yamemile wuhile wo wubi, mono yakwihwesa vibi kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
12 Azi zo yakulonga u Muwaha Mulungu kuli wanhu wakwe Waisilayeli yakutya, “Hamba yiwe Waashuli wali vilungo kanyhi wenji, ninga wene wohodangwa no kuwusizwa lukulu. Heya nyo wanhu wangu, nghamunozenyi mugaye, ninga sinomutendelenyi kanyhi nhavivi.
13 Nane aluno novidumya i vilungo vye Waashuli kuli'nye, kanyhi noyidumula ne minyolo yono muwopeywe.”
14 Azi zo Muwaha Mulungu yalamuye kuli Waashuli, “Si wolowa na lulelo kanyhi lono lolocemwa mwi'tagwa lyawo. Novihodanga vihwani vyono vili mu wuhembelo we milungu yawo. Kanyhi nghuwikaniza citenjelo ku soko ye Waashuli, hambi nha si yiwanojeye wekale womi.”

15 Lanje, u muganuzi yakuza yakulawa mucanyha ye matunda, hamonga no luganuzi Luswanu, mono yakwanza zo mulamu. Mwene yali mu nzila yakwe ho yanze zo wusume. Nanye nyo wanhu wa Yuda, mwibate zi sikukuu, kanyhi mumufisilizize Mulungu vyono mwamwihuwiziza. Hambi i wabi, nha si wolokwinjila kanyhi no kuyihoma yisi yenyu. Hambi nha wakondya kuwusizwa lukulu!


Nahumu 2

U wugwe wa Ninawi

1 Gwe Ninawi, alu weza wuli mu wuhomwe!

Ne vilungo vyono vyokubenanjiza vyakondya kuza.

Holeka 'lu wulidimaje likaya lyako!

Wuzilanjilile ne zinzila zako!

Wiwikanize so wihomi!

Wime wukamile!

2 (U Muwaha Mulungu kolugomola lukumyo lwa Isilayeli nha vyono lwali, lono walugu wali wakali nawateka.)

3 I wasilikale walugu, wadinghuye ngula ndunghu,

kanyhi wavwalile myenda midunghu,

wakwiwikaniza wamuhomenyi!

Ne mituka yawo yo wulugu,

yikulipuka nha moto.

Ne zifwalasi zawo, zikuzumha-zumha muno.

4 Ne mituka yawo yo wulugu,

yikumangha mangha musunho mu zinzila,

yikumangha ako na ako mu zinhuzi ze likaya.

Kanyhi yikwaka kotya toci,

no kwijela musunho kotya mamuli.

5 Yakuwakumbucila

wanhu wakwe we lilumbo,

wakwikobala mu njenda yawo

lono wakwiyoha.

Nawo wanya kuwahoma,

wakumanyhila musunho mu majinji,

no kuwikaniza zi ngula,

so kwicita no wulugu.

6 Ne milango ya kwi'kolongo, yadiguka,

nalyo likaya lyo wutemi limemile wogofye.

7 Likaya lyekala lili bwete,

i wanhu wayo wakondya kusolwa mateka,

ne wetumami wakwe we cicekulu wakulila kotya

mazi ga nghunda,

no kwitowa-towa mu zimhambaga so wusungu.

8 Wanhu wakumangha musunho

ku wulawa Ninawi nhe malenga

gono gakulawa mwi'lamba lyono lyaponghola.

Ne lizi likucemeleza likutya,

“Mwime! Mwime!”

Ninga hasina hamba yunji

mono yakwilenga ku nyuma.

9 Musole ngaluzi ze zifweza!

Solenyi ngaluzi ze zahabu!

Hambi likaya nha limemile viwutwa!

10 Ninawi yahodangwa nha vyono yali yinojeye,

alu yekala nyhiwa.

Ne zinhumbula ze wanhu wayo,

zenjilwa no woga,

ne mafugamilo gawo gakucicima,

ne vilungo vyeza musina,

no wusu wawo wawomba so woga.

11 Alu lili hayi likaya,

lyono lyali linha wikalo wa simba,

ne hanhu hono wana we simba wakuliswa,

i hanhu hono simba nume

ne simba nyhekulu zobita,

hamba zinyana zayo hono zowa zikaye zipolile?

12 Simba yawulaga mapelembo gayo,

yalupulanga mhonje mhonje

so wagolece wakwe na so wanagwe.

Yamemya nyama ye mapelembo gayo mu wikalo wayo.

13 Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga yakutya, “Ane nili mulugu wako, nane nogakongoga matuka gako go wulugu. Ne wasilikale wako wolowulagwa ku wulugu, nane novisola vinhu vyose vyono wavisola ku wulawa kuli wanhu wanji. Ne mazi ge wetumami wako, hamba sigolohulikwa kanyhi yaye.”


Nahumu 3

1 Woyi hee likaya lyono limemile wuwulaji,

limemile wulambi no wugoli

wono lisawiye mu nzila yo wuhizi!

2 Muhulice ta,

mazozo ge mililo ye mizipo,

ne mazozo ge maguludumu,

hamonga ne zifwalasi

zono zikubita zikumangha muno muno,

hamonga ne nyhinyinho ye matuka go wulugu!

3 Iwajendela zifwalasi wakubita lumangho,

ne mimaje yawo ne migoha yawo yikung'alala!

Mivimba yilundile malundo mawaha,

ne miwili ya wono wafwiye yisina hamba yiwalo,

wanhu wakwikobala mucanyha ye mivimba!

4 Ninawi u musenyha yakondya kulipicizwa.

Hambi nha yakwendezwa no wuwulaji,

mono yakugakwega makungugo

no kugatajila mu wuwanda.

5 U Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga yakutya,

“Ane nokwisumaniza nagwe go Ninawi!

Kanyhi nokuvula ne myenda wikale cipwi,

ne makungugo gakuwone vyono wuli cipwi,

ne matemi gakuwonele mu cevu cako.

6 Nane nokunoza wuwe cinhu

cono cibedegaywe,

kanyhi wuwe wuli cinhu cavu,

ne wanhu wokulanga ko woga.

7 Na wose wono wokuwona,

wotiza, akuno wakutya,

‘Ninawi yili mu wuhodangwe!

Alu nani 'mbi mono koyiwonela lusungu?

Hamba nani lo mono kosuma kuyisindamika?’ ”

8 Ninawi, alo agwe wuli mutelevu kuciza Tebesi likaya liwaha lya Misili? Lyono lipilimwe ne makolongo ho galicitile nhe 'jinji. Ne likolongo lya Nile, likawa yo ngula yalyo.
9 Yakazitema Kushi na Misili, hakawa hasina mupaka mu wutemi wakwe. Na Libiya nayo vikawa vihulice.
10 Ninga wanhu wa No-amoni, wategulwa kutali ku wuwanda. Na mu zinhuzi zose ze zinzila, wana wawo watowangwaga sunga wafwa. Ne walongola wawo wose wasolwa, akuno wawopwe ne manyolo, wakawa kugawana wanya kuwateka.
11 Ninawi, nagwe kogala sunga mahala gajilile! Kanyhi kojela kwiponya ku wulawa kuli walugu wako.
12 Ne magomawakali gako gose, gowa nha mikuyu yono yina mana gono gapiye. Agwe wuyisinjisaje mibici du, kowona mana gakugwila mu mulomo wako!
13 Hambi wasilikale wako wo wulugu, nha weza nha wacekulu, ne yisi yako yeza yisina mulindilizi ha mwande ye walugu wako. No moto wogahodanga macuma gono wugapinjice mu malango ge likaya lyako.
14 Holeka muwikanize malenga menji, mwiwikanize so wulugu wono wukuza. Kanyhi musikamye ne magomawakali genyu! Mutiwe lilongo, muwumbe matofali, ho muwe ne matofali gono mawumbile lukulu.
15 Ninga, hamba wunoze vyono konoza, vyo kokongogwa du mu moto sunga wufwe, hamba kowulagwa ku wulugu. Nagwe kowusizwa lukulu nhe vilimwa vyono vilijigwe ne zinzije.

Mwali monjelezeka mweza muli wenji nhe zinzije!
16 Wukawonjeleza ne wakwawi wako wawe wenji, kuciza zinyelezi za ku wulanga! Ninga 'lu, wakondya kubita, nhe zinzije zono zitandakulaga mabawa no kuguluka.
17 I walongola wako wenha matewa ge zinzije zono zicehile mu majinji mwi'zuwa lye mbeho nghali. Ninga lizuwa kolo likuwalika, zikuguluka no kubita, hamba hasina yunji mono yakumanya na kono zibitile!
18 Gwe go mutemi wa Ashuli, walanjilizi wako wakondya kufwa, ne wawaha wako we lilumbo wagona cibitilila! Wanhu wako weyagaye mu matunda, hasina hamba munhu mono kowagomola kanyhi ku zi kwawo.
19 Kusina kanyhi hamba miti yono yowupoza wusungu wako, ne zinghomele zako sizikusumika hamba kuwunoje yaye. Na wose wono wohulika zo wuhodangwe wako, mbeka wotowa maganza gawo so nyemo. Hambi nani mono yelega no wukali wako wono wusina ndumilizo?
