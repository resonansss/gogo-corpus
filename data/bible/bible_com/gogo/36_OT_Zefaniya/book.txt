Zefaniya 1

1 Ayi yo nghani yono Muwaha Mulungu yamupela Zefaniya, u mwana wa Kushi, mwana wa Gedaliya, mwana wa Amaliya, mwana wa Hezekiya, mu vilo vya Yosiya Mwana wa Amoni mutemi wa Yuda.

Lizuwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu lyo wuling'anize

2 Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Ane novihodanga vinhu vyose vyono vili mono'nze,
3 nowongomeza wanhu wose hamonga ne zinimu, ne zindeje ne zisomba. Ne wabi nowongomezeza lukulu. Kanyhi nowongomeza wanhu wose, hamba hasina cinhu cumi co cose cono colosigala. Ane no Muwaha Mulungu vyo nghutya.
4 Nowalipiciza wanhu wa Yelusalemi, sunga na wose wono wali mu yisi ya Yuda. Nowuhodanga wityatye wono wupulute wo kuhemba Baali, hamba hasina yunji kanyhi, mono kolowakumbucila wakohani wa Baali wono wamwitumamilaga.
5 Kanyhi nomuwusiza lukulu munhu wo wose, mono kabitaga mwi'tembe mucanyha, kawa kuvihemba lizuwa no mwezi sunga ne zinyelezi. Kanyhi nowawusiza lukulu wose wono wahembaga nene, no kwilahiza vyono wena cihuliko kuli'ne, na kuno kanyhi, ho wakwilahiza kuli mulungu Moleki.
6 Kanyhi nowawusiza lukulu na wawo wono wawuya ne nyuma hamba si wakunhyatya'ne kanyhi. Wawo wono si wakuza kuli'ne hamba si wendile kuwulondola wutonji wangu niwatonje.”

7 Lizuwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu lili behi, lizuwa lyono Mulungu kolokwikala no kulavya wuling'anize. Holeka wikalaje wutule hali Muwaha Mulungu. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, nha yakwiwikaniza yawalavye yikumbiko wanhu wakwe, nayo yakondya kuwahagula wono yawalalice.
8 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo lyo wuwulagwe, nolowalipiciza i wawaha we lilumbo, hamonga ne wana we watemi, sunga na wose wono wakwibata myoto ye cijenzi.
9 Kanyhi nolowalipiciza wose wono wakuhemba nhe wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu, hamonga na wono wahizaga no kuwulaga, ho wazimemye ngaluzi zinyumba ze wawaha wawo.
10 Mwi'zuwa lilyo yakutya Muwaha Mulungu, “Molohulika mazi ge cililo mu Yelusalemi, ha Mulango we Somba. Kanyhi molohulika cililo ciwaha mwi'tumbi lye kejete lye likaya, hamonga ne lizozo yiwaha mu matunda.”
11 Mulile mwikunguze kolo mukuzihulika zizi, anye mono mwikaye mu mhelo ye likaya, hambi wasuluza wose nha wowa wakondya kufwa!

12 “Mu cipango cico nolosola tala niyimulice Yelusalemi, niyiwone vyono yinha. Kanyhi nolowapezya iwanhu wewoni, wono weyitumbilaga weneco, wono wakwiyitya, ‘U Muwaha Mulungu, si konoza ziswanu hamba si konoza zimbi.’
13 U wugoli wawo wolosolwa, ne zikaya zawo zolohodangwa. Hamba si wolosumila lukulu kanyhi, kwikala mu zikaya zawo zono wakuzenga, hamba kung'wa divayi mu wulawa mu migunda yawo ye zabibu yono walimile.”
14 Lizuwa liwaha lyo Muwaha Mulungu, lili behi kuza, likuza mbela mbela! Ne lizuwa lilyo lyolowa yizuwa lyo wusungu, hamba ne wasilikale we vilungo wololila so wuwula witumbilo!
15 Lyolowa yizuwa lyo wutamiswe, yizuwa lyo wugaye no wambuce, yizuwa lyo wongomezwe, yizuwa lye citi na lye nghungulyosi, wutitu ne mavunde,
16 lizuwa lyono limemile mazi ge zindulele zo wulugu, ne cililo co wihome we wasilikale, wakwinjila mu makaya na mu minala mitali muno.
17 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Ane noleta wugaye muli wanhu, ne wanhu wose wowa kujenda nha wali cibofu. Hambi nha wose wasutizye kuli'ne. Holeka sakami yawo yokwitika nha malenga, ne mivimba ye miwili yawo yolowa minji mu'si.”
18 Na mwi'zuwa lyono Muwaha Mulungu kololajila likuwo lyakwe, fweza ne zahabu zawo zose, hamba si zolowaponya yaye, hambi i yinze yose, nha yolowa yakondya kutakamizwa no moto we likuwo lyakwe. Hambi nha kolowadumya wanhu wose wono wekaye mono'nze, ko mudumyo wo wogofye wuwaha.


Zefaniya 2

Wulombe so wunene

1 Anye nyo likungugo lyono lisina cevu, ale mwikongholelaje hamonga,
2 lono mukali nawinjilwa kutali, kotya mawaje gono gapemheluswe ne mbeho, na lono likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, lyono lihwanile moto likali si namuzizenyi, lizuwa lyono kololajila wukali wakwe.
3 Mumupitucilaje Muwaha Mulungu anye wose nyo wanhu wenenenyhezi wa mu yisi, anye mono muwukamise wulajilize wakwe. Mwitumaje ze ling'ani, kanyhi mwinenenyheze nyo weneco hali Muwaha Mulungu, nha lunji mopona mwi'zuwa lyono Muwaha Mulungu, yalalajila likuwo lyakwe.

Wongomezwe we makungugo gono gayipilime Isilayeli

4 Hasina yunji mono kolosigala mwi'kaya lya Gaza. Na Ashikeloni yolowa wambuce. Ne wanhu wa Ashidodi wolowingwa mu mapango ge misi, ne wanhu wa Ekiloni wolowingwa ku wulawa mwi'kaya lyawo.
5 Anye nanye nyo Wafilisiti, woyi hee kuli'nye, anye nyo wanhu mono mwikaye mu mhwani. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, nha yakondya kudumula nghani muli'nye. Mwene komongomezenyi wose, hamba hasina yunji muli'nye mono kolosigala.
6 Yisi yenyu ku wulawa ku mhwani, yolowa migunda yono yili mwazi, yolowa yina vibanda vye wadimi, hamonga ne mabululu ge zingholo.
7 Ne wanhu wa Yuda, wono wasigala, woloyisola yisi yenyu. Wolodima zimitugo zawo kuko no kugona mu zikaya za Ashikeloni. U Muwaha Mulungu wawo, kolowa hamonga nawo, na kanyhi kowamotesa.
8 Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga yakutya “Nawahulika wanhu wa Mowabu na Amoni wakuwaliga no kuwezwajiza wanhu wangu, no kwitapa vyono woyisola yisi yawo.
9 Ninga mbeka, nha vyono ninha ane no Mulungu mumi, ane no Mulungu wa Isilayeli, nghwilahiza nha vyono, Mowabu na Amoni zolokongomezwa nha vyono Sodoma na Gomola zongomezwa. No mwino wologwa hanhu hose, no kongomezezwa lukulu cibitilila, ne mabalwe go golocindika mumo. Ne wanhu wangu wono wasigaye, woloyipumha, no kuyisola yisi yawo.”

10 Na vyo wolowa wanhu wa Mowabu na Amoni, wololipicizwa ku soko yo wiwoni wawo, hamonga ne zinyono zawo, na ku soko yo kuwaliga wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga.
11 U Muwaha Mulungu, kolowogofya muno. Hambi nha koloyimala milungu yose ya mono yinze, ne makungugo gose golomuhemba mweneco du, wanhu na yisi yawo weneco.
12 Muwaha Mulungu kanyhi, kolowawulaga nhavivyo ne wanhu wa Kushi.
13 Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu, kolokwitumamila vilungo vyakwe yayihodanje Ashuli. Mwene kololinoza likaya lya Ninawi liwe nyika ye 'tongo, kanyhi yiwe nyika yono yisina malenga.
14 Holowa hanhu hono matewa ge zimitugo, ne mawala gose ge zinimu, wowa wo wikalo wayo. Ne zinhuwi zolokwikala mu matongo gago, akuno zikulila ku ulawilila mu madilisha ge matongo. Ne mahowe nago, golowa kulila ha milango. Ne mabici go muhagata gono wazenjeye, yolowa nghani ya bwete du.
15 Azi zo zololijelela likaya lyono lina wiwoni wo kwiyitumbila vilungo vyalyo lyeneco, no kwihwesa vyono likaye lipolile. Ne wanhu wayo wakwihwesa vyono likaya lyawo lye 'waha kuciza makaya gose mono yinze yose. Na kumbe wo wambuce wa bwete, hambi nha holopituka hawe wikalo we zinimu za mu mbago! No munhu wo wose mono kokolela baho, kololihoweleza no kusinjisa mutwe wakwe.


Zefaniya 3

Wuling'anizwe wa Yelusalemi

1 Woyi hee! Yelusalemi! Hambi nha yokongomezwa. Hambi nha lye 'kaya lyono limemile wibavye. Ne walongola wawo, wo wanhu wabi, wakuwewonela wanhu wawo.
2 Lye 'kaya lyono si likumuhulika Muwaha Mulungu, hamba kubocela ze mejizo gakwe. Likali namwitumbila Muwaha Mulungu, hamba kulomba wutazi kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
3 Ne wawaha wayo wabuguci, wehwanile zisimba zono zikukoloma, ne Wahakimu wayo, nawo wehwanile maminzi ge camihe gono gadabuce, gono si gasigazaga hamba cinji, lono wela hakali mitondo.
4 Ne wawizi wayo si wehuwilwa, kanyhi wakonji. Wakohani nawo, wahatajila wavu i hanhu hono helile, kanyhi wakulipitula ne lilajilizo lyo Mulungu ku soko ye cambuzi cawo weneco.
5 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu, yali mwi'kaya, yakwituma ze ling'ani, si yakwituma zimbi ng'o. Nayo kawaletelaga wanhu wakwe ze ling'ani, nhondo zose. Ninga i wanhu wono wasina ling'ani, vyo wajendeleye du ku wituma zimbi, hamba kuwona cevu yaye.

6 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Nagawusiza lukulu makungugo gose. Nagagwisanga makaya gawo, no kugaleka majinji gawo hamonga ne minala yawo vihodanjice.
7 Ne makaya gawo geza gali bwete, na mu zinzila zayo, mweza musina cinji, hasina hamba yunji mono yasigaye. Nane nghehwesa vyono, wanhu wangu wowa wena cogopo kuli'ne no kubocela mejizo gangu, no kuleka kusemwa, mejizo gono nawejiza. Ninga wakacenya musunho, no kwituma zimbi mu mikalile yawo.”
8 Ane nghutya, “Wunindilaje.” Vyo yakutya Muwaha Mulungu, “Wulilindile lizuwa lyono nokwinuka ho niwasitace makungugo. Nane nakondya kutemya vyono, nigakonghole i makungugo, hamonga no wutemi wose, ho wasume kuviwona i vilungo vye likuwo lyangu. Ne yinze yose yotakamizwa no moto we likuwo lyangu.

9 “Lulo nolowapitula i wanhu we makungugo niwapele nonga swanu, nawo wolohemba nene du nili wene, hamba si woloyihemba kanyhi milungu yinji. Langa wolohulika nene du.
10 Hamba kuwe kutali ku wulawa mu makolongo ga Kushi, ninga iwanhu wangu wono weyagaye ako na ako, wose wolondavizya vilavywa nene du.
11 Na mwi'pango lilyo, anye nyo wanhu wangu, molowa musina soko yo kuwona cevu, vyono mwali mwambedegala. Hambi 'ne nha nowawusa i wewoni wose, hamonga ne wevuni wose. Nanye lulo, si molombedegalila lukulu kanyhi mwi'tunda lyangu lyono lyelile.
12 Nane nolowaleka baho i wanhu wenenenyhezi ne waciwa, wono wokuza kulondola wutazi kuli'ne.
13 Ne wanhu wa Isilayeli wono wasigaye, si wolokwitendela vibi kanyhi munhu na muyagwe, hamba kwilonjela zo wulambi, hamba kuwa wakonji yaye. Wolowa walugutwe kanyhi wapolile, na holowa hasina yunji mono kolowogofya kanyhi.”

Lwimbo lwe nyemo

14 Ale, mwimbe ne nyemo kwe mazi mawaha 'nye

nyo wanhu wa Isilayeli.

Nagwe go Yelusalemi,

wusangalale kwe nhumbula yako yose.

15 Muwaha Mulungu kakondya kuwudumya

u wuling'anizwe wako,

kanyhi kakondya kuwawusa

ne walugu wako wose.

U Muwaha Mulungu,

u Mutemi wa Isilayeli,

yali hamonga nanye.

Holeka kusina soko kanyhi

yono yoleka muwe no woga.

16 Cipango cikuza,

lono woloyilonjela Yelusalemi wawe kutya,

“Ale, wulece kogopa 'gwe go likaya lya Sayuni!

Ne miwoko yako yilece kudejepa!”

17 Muwaha Mulungu wako,

yali hamonga nagwe,

ne vilungo vyakwe, vyokupela wusume.

Muwaha Mulungu,

kotuga wendezwe kuli'gwe,

na mu wendo wakwe,

kokupela wumi.

Kokwimbila no kukusangalalila

18 ne nyemo, yono yinha nyemo

ye wanhu wono wali mu sikukuu.

Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu yakutya,

“Nakondya kuwuwusa u wogofye wo wongomezwe,

naciwusiza lukulu ne cevu cawo.”

19 Icipango cikuza!

Lono nolowalipiciza

wono wakumwiwonelenyi,

nolowaponya we vilema wose,

kanyhi nolowagomola

ku zi kwawo wose wono wali wasolwa

mu yisi yawo no kutegulwa kutali.

Nolopitula ne cevu cawo ciwe lukumyo,

ne yinze yose yolowakumya wene.

20 “Icipango cikuza!

Lono nolowagomola wanhu wenyu

wono weyagazwa,

wawuye ku zikaya,

nolomunozenyi lukumyo

no kumanyika muli yinze yose,

no kumunozenyi kanyhi mumoteswe

nha vyono ha mwamulizo.”

Vyo yakutya Muwaha Mulungu.
