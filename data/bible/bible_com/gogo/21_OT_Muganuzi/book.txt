Muganuzi 1

I mikalile ya mono'nze, yo nghani ya bwete du.

1 Azi zo nghani zo muganuzi, u mwana wa Dawudi, mono yali mutemi ku Yelusalemi.

2 Yo nghani ya bwete du, yo nghani ya bwete du,

nghukulonjela mbeka ane no muganuzi!

Vinhu vyose, yo nghani ya bwete du!

3 Munhu kamotaga ci cici mu wipiganze wakwe wose wa mono'nze?

4 Ndelo na ndelo zipulutaga,

na ndelo zinji kanyhi zikuza,

ninga yinze, yene yikalaga cibitilila.

5 Lizuwa nalyo lisosomokaga,

kanyhi lizuwa liswezaga,

likubita musunho muno hanhu hono lilawaga.

6 Nayo mbeho yiputaga wulawilila lubavu lwe takama,

yiwa kuputa kanyhi no lubavu lwe sumuka,

yiputaga kali kenji du,

akuno yikupilima-pilima no kuwuyajila kukolela

mu wukolo wayo we nhondo zose.

7 Ne makolongo gose nago gahulumililaga kwi'lenga,

ninga 'lanje, lilenga si limemaga yaye hamba hadodo,

nago makolongo, kuko kono gahulumililaga,

kanyhi kuko ko galawililaga.

8 Ne zinghani zose, ziletaga mukatalo du,

mukatalo muwaha si wakulonga.

Meso getu nago, si gegutaga yaye no kulanga,

ne makutu getu nago,

hamba si gegutaga yaye no kuhuliciza.

9 Na zono zejela katali,

zo zizo du zono zokwijela kanyhi na lulu,

na zono zetumwa katali,

zo zizo du zono zokwitumwa kanyhi na lulu,

mono'nze musina nghani mhya, hamba monga.

10 I wanhu watezaga, “Lanje, ayi nghani mhya,”

kumbe nghani yiyo,

yali yili baho du ku wulawa katali,

na lono cali cikali nalelwa.

11 Na mbeka, hasina munhu mono kozikumbucila ze katali,

hamba mono kozikumbucila zono zikuza.

Ku wulawa lulu na mu vilo vyono vikuza.

Zono yaziwona Muganuzi

12 Ane no Muganuzi, nali nili mutemi we Waisilayeli, kuko ku Yelusalemi.
13 Nane nghapiligala muno kuzilondola-londola kwe mahala, no kuzisunjilila muno muno zose zono zikwitumwa mono'nze yiyi. Nawo wuwu wo mulimo muwaha, wono Mulungu yawapela wanhu.
14 Nane nghukulonjela, naziwona zose zono zikwitumwa mono'nze yiyi vyono, yo nghani ya bwete du, kanyhi wo wigaze du wono wusina cambuzi.

15 Naco cono cipetizye,

si cosuma kugoloswa,

naco cono cili hasina,

si cosuma kuwalwa.

16 Holeka mu wihwese wangu nghawa kutya, “Netujila mahala menji muno, kuciza wose wono wanongolela kuwa watemi mu Yelusalemi. Heya, alu nakondya kumanya nhunizo ye mahala co cici.”
17 Nghapiligala muno muno ho sume kumanya wisimajile we mahala, no lukwale, no wulele. Ninga nghamanya vyono, wunoza nhavivyo, wo wigaze we bwete du, wono wusina cambuzi co cose.

18 Hambi nha mu nghango yono kocila kuwa na mahala menji,

yo nghango yiyo du kanyhi,

yono kocila kwipela u wu wula mulamu mu nhumbula yako,

na mu nghango yono wukucila kuwa no wumanye winji,

ale yo nhavivyo kanyhi vyo kocila kwiyonjelezeza wukalale mu nhumbula yako.


Muganuzi 2

1 Holeka nghehwesa mu nhumbula yangu nghatya, “Leka ta niyinjize muli ze cinyemo-nyemo, ho sume kwisangalaza.” Ninga hamba nhavivyo, nghamanya vyono yiyo nayo, yo nghani ya bwete du.
2 Baho ho namanyila vyono, luseko nha lukwale du, na vyono nazo ze cinyemo-nyemo, kumbe zisina cambuzi co cose.
3 Nane naza kuwa nehwesa muno, hodu nghalamula nisangalaze mahala gangu ne divayi, ninga akuno ho nghali nghudindimizwa no wuhile wangu wo wutuga mahala. Kanyhi nghalamula vyono ciba cityatye ne wanhu walele, ho sume kuwona hono yiyo yo nzila swanu ye wanhu, ya mu mikalile yawo micefu ya baha hono'nze.

4 Netuma metumo mawaha, hambi nha nezenjela makaya, kanyhi nghelimila ne migunda ya zabibu.
5 Nghenozeza sunga ne busitani, hamonga ne viwanza, nghahadika mumo mibici ye mikalo ye mawala gose.
6 Nghahimba sunga ne malamba ge malenga, so wumwajilila malenga mibici yiyo.
7 Nghegulila sunga ne wawanda we cilume na we cicekulu, na wanji wakalelelwa mu kaya yangu. Nali nina sawo nyinji muno muno, matewa ge zing'ombe sunga ne zingholo nyinji muno, kuciza munhu wo wose mono yanongolela kwikala no kutema mu Yelusalemi.
8 Nane nghewutila mapesa menji muno, hamonga na zahabu nyinji, mu wulawa mu viwutwa vyo wutemi, vya mu zisi zono nali nizitemile. Sunga walume ne wacekulu, wose wakasangalaza so nyimbo zawo, nane nghetujila wacekulu wose wajitulwa waswanu, wono mulume wo wose, yali yawasulumile, wono nene no mweneco nasulumila kuwa nawo.
9 Nghawa mutambule muno muno kuciza wose wono wanongolela kwikala mu Yelusalemi, sunga ne mahala gangu nago, gakanojela muno muno.
10 Na co cose cono nacisaka, nacituga musunho muno. Hamba si neyimila muli ze nyemo yo yose yilya. Nane nghavisangalalila vyose vyono netuma. Nayo yiyo, yikawa wo musahala wangu so mulimo wono netuma.
11 Hanyuma nghawa kwamula kwihwesa muli zose zono nali netuma, na vyono nazituma ko wuhidimbazi wuwaha muno muno. Nghamanya vyono zose zizo kumbe zali nghani ya bwete, kotya wisumanize ne mbeho, musina cambuzi co cose mono'nze.

12 Hobaho nghawa kwihwesa nhunizo yo kuwa munhu we mahala, hamba kuwa wuli munhu mono si yaceleleye, hamba kuwa wuli mulele. Hambi mutemi nha kosuma kunoza du zono yanozaga mutemi mono yamulongolela.
13 Baho nghamanya vyono, kumbe kuwa na mahala, kuswanu si nho wulele, nha vyono winha wuzelu wuswanu wucizize citi.
14 Hambi munhu we mahala nha yena meso, nayo yamanyile na kono yakubita, ninga munhu mulele, kajendelaga mu wutitu du, hamba kumanya na kono yakubita. Ninga hamba nhavivyo kanyhi, nghamanya vyono kumbe, u munhu we mahala, hamonga no munhu mulele, wose mudumo wawo wo wumonga du.
15 Hobaho nghehwesa nghatya,

“Kumbe, zono zomwagana u munhu mulele, zo zizo du zono zonyagana na nene no mweneco. Alu co ca hayi 'mbi, co kuwa ne mahala gose gagano?” Ale, nghehwesa kanyhi ngheyipitula nili wene nghatya, “Ayi nayo mbeka, yo nghani ya bwete du.”
16 Hambi mbeka nha hasina munhu wo wose mono kamukumbucilaga we mahala, hamba mono kamukumbucilaga mulele. Hambi mu vilo vyono vikuza, wose nha wosemelwa lukulu. Hambi u munhu we mahala nha kafwizaga, sunga na ayulya munhu mulele, nayo kafwizaga!
17 Baho, hodu nghawa kuyisula i mikalile ya mono'nze, vyono zose zono zikwitumwa mono'nze, mbeka zamhela magaziko mawaha. Nazo zose zo nghani za bwete du, kumbe wo wigaze we bwete du, wono wusina cambuzi co cose.

18 Nane mbeka nghayisula ne milimo yangu yose yono netumaga baha mono'nze, hambi nha namanya vyono, nane, yowa yinojeye nimulecele munhu mono konhyatya mu wutemi.
19 Hambi nani kanyhi, mono komanya vyono yuyo mono kotema kowa munhu we mahala, hamba kowa mulele? Ninga hamba yiwe nhavivyo, mwene kotema muli zose zono netuma mu mahala gangu baha mono'nze. Ninga hamba yiwe nhavivyo, azo zose nha nghani ya bwete du.
20 Ale, naza kwihwesa kanyhi muli zose zono netuma mononze, mbeka nghawa kudobanya du.
21 Hambi munhu, nha ketumaga milimo, yakwitumila mahala, ko wizuce hamonga no wunghula wakwe wose, ninga kali kenji kamulecelaga munhu yunji mono yakutyatya, yuyo yo kawa kuzimota ze mana go mulimo, wono si yawulavizya nhuguti yaye. Yiyo nayo, mbeka yo nghani ya bwete du, kanyhi yo nghani yono 'mbi muno muno.
22 Mwene munhu kamotaga ci cici ca mu wuhidimbazi wo wipiganze wakwe wose mono'nze, hamonga ne mawine, no mukatalo vyono yatuga?
23 Hambi mikalile yakwe yose, nha yimemile magayo du, sunga no witume wakwe wukuwa nha nghani ya bwete, hambi nha sunga na mu cilo mose, mahala gakwe si gatulaga yaye! Zizo nazo go muyangu, yo nghani ya bwete du.

24 Gwe, hasina yinji swanu yono yimukwiliye munhu, langa hono si kulya no kung'wa, hamonga no kuwusangalalila mulimo wakwe. Yiyi nayo nghuwona vyono yilawaga kuli Mulungu,
25 vyono hono wulece kupegwa no Mulungu, si kosuma kuwa na ndigwa hamba si kosuma kusangalala yaye.

26 Hambi Mulungu nha kamupelaga mahala munhu mono yamwendile mweneco, hamonga no wizuce ne nyemo. Ninga u munhu mubi, Mulungu kamupelaga mulimo wo kududula no kulundilila du, hanyuma Mulungu kawa kumupela munhu yunji mono yakumwendeza. Ninga 'gwe, zizo zose nazo, nha nghani ya bwete du, kanyhi wo wigaze we bwete du, wono wusina cambuzi co cose.


Muganuzi 3

Mulungu yavipanjiye vyose, cinhu na cipango cayo

1 Nghani zose zono zikwijela mono'nze,

Mulungu yazipanjiye vipango vyayo.

2 Mulungu yapanjile cipango co kulelwa ne cipango co kufwa,

cipango co kuhadika, ne cipango co kududula,

3 cipango co kuwulaga, ne cipango co kuponya,

cipango co kumwagula, ne cipango co kuzenga,

4 cipango co kulila, ne cipango co kuseka,

cipango co kukalala, ne cipango co kuvina,

5 cipango co kwiyagaza mabwe,

ne cipango co kugakongholeza,

cipango co kwikumbatila,

ne cipango co kuleka kwikumbatila,

6 cipango co kulondola, ne cipango co kwajiliza,

cipango co kukamisa, ne cipango co kutaga,

7 cipango co kudemula, ne cipango co kuhona,

cipango co kunyamala, ne cipango co kulonga,

8 cipango co kwenda, ne cipango co kusula,

cipango co wulugu, ne cipango co mulamu.

9 Alu munhu kamotaga cici ca mu wihidimbaze wakwe?
10 Ane nawuwona vyono mulimo mukamu muno, wono Mulungu yamupela munhu.
11 Hambi Mulungu nha yavinoza vyose viwe viswanu, cinhu ne cipango cayo. Mulungu yamupelile munhu wuhile wo kumanyila za ku mwande, ninga si yamupela wuwe wo kusuma kumanya i metumo gose go Mulungu.
12 Nane manyile vyono ku lubavu lo munhu, koyili nghani monga du yono yomukwila, nha cinji, kusangalala, no kwituma ziswanu mu vilo vyose vyo wikale wakwe.
13 Ase wose, yicinojeye cilye no kung'wa, cisangalalile ziswanu ze milimo yetu. Hambi aci nha cipegwa co Mulungu.

14 Nane manyile vyono nghani yo yose yono Mulungu kayinozaga yikalaga cibitilila. No munhu si kosumila lukulu kuyonjeleza cinji hamba kuyicepula cinji yaye, hambi Mulungu nha yazinoza zose ziwe nha vyono zinha, i wanhu ho wamogope mwene.
15 Naco cono cikwijela kwi zuwali, nha cali cili baho du ku wulawa katali, na cono cokwijela hanyuma, nha cali cili baho du na katali. Nayo Mulungu kanozaga cinhu cijele mu vidaha hayo hayo, ninga cene cinhu co ci cilya du.

U wu wula wiling'ano mono'nze

16 Hamonga na zizo, nakondya kucimanya cinhu cinji kanyhi mononze, vyono i hanhu hono wulanjiliye vyono kowona i mbeka ne ling'ani, kumbe 'lu wavu wo wutemile.
17 Nane mu nhumbula yangu nghawa kutya, “Ale hodu, si nghani yaye! Hambi Mulungu nha yo kowaling'aniza we ling'ani sunga ne wabi nha vivyo, hambi nha yakondya kuwikaniza cipango cono cinojeye kuli vinhu vyose, na kuli vyose vyono vikwijela.”
18 Holeka nghawa kutya mu nhumbula yangu, “Mulungu yo kawajelaga wanhu wose, ho yawalajile nha vyono wene, wehwanile malimu du.”
19 Hambi u mudumo wo munhu no mudumo we malimu, nha wo wumonga du. Hambi munhu nha kafwizaga nha vyono lilimu nalyo lifwizaga. Na vyose vyehelaga nhavivyo. Holeka munhu si yalicizize lilimu cinji co cose yaye. Vyose kuli munhu na kwi'limu, yo nghani ya bwete du.
20 Wose wafwizaga no kuwuya kono walawa. Wose walawa mu manghundi, na wose wowuya kanyhi mu manghundi.
21 Hambi nani mono yamanyile vyono mbeka umuhe wo munhu wubitaga kucanyha, no muhe we lilimu wudodomelaga kusi yo muhangha?
22 Baho ho namanyila vyono, hasina nghani yono swanu kuciza yono munhu kosuma kuyinoza, langa hono si kusangalalila ziswanu ze milimo yakwe. Nayo Mulungu vyo yamuwiciye munhu nhavivyo. Alu nani 'mbi mono kosuma kumumanyiza munhu, zono zomutuga kumwande kolo yafwa?


Muganuzi 4

1 Hanyuma nghawona wiwonelwe wose wono wukwitumwa kono'nze. Ne wanhu wono wakwiwonelwa, wakulila mahozi, nawo wasina hamba yunji mono yakuwatuza, wasina munhu hamba yumonga mono kowataza, vyono wono wakuwewonela nha wali vilungo wawacizize wene.
2 Holeka nghawa kuwawonela wivu i wafu wono wakondya kubita i hanhu hono si walawuya akuno kanyhi, hambi wene nha wali ciba si nha wono wakali womi.
3 Ninga awalya wono wakali nalelwa no kuziwona zimbi zose zono zikwitumwa mono'nze, wawo nawo wali ciba si nha wono wafwa, hamba wono wakali womi.

4 Kanyhi ngheyijiza vyono kumbe wuhidimbazi wose wo munhu, hamonga no wunghula wakwe wose wa mu witume wakwe, vilawililaga mu wiwonela wivu munhu na muyagwe. Ninga yiyo nayo yo nghani ya bwete du, hambi nha yinha wikataze we bwete du.

5 Kanyhi yo nghani ya wulele du,

yo munhu kwikala hamba si wukwituma cinji,

hanyuma kuwa kufwa ne nzala.

6 Co ciba wuwe na cinhu cidodo du,

akuno wina no mulamu mu nhumbula,

si nha kwigaza na nghani nyinji,

hambi nha yinha wikataze we bwete du.

7 Kanyhi nghawona nghani yinji mono'nze yono yili bwete du.
8 Namuwona munhu yunji mono yasina mwana hamba nduguye, hamba nhavivyo, mwene si kalekaga kwituma milimo, kanyhi mwene hamba si yakuwa kuca ne sawo yakwe, hamba si ketuzaga akuno yakwiwuza yakutya, “Ale mwene, nghumwitumamila nani mwene mono yakunyambuza nghwikapisa sunga ndece kusangalala mu ziswanu?” Ayo mbeka yo nghani ya bwete du kanyhi yo nzila'mbi ye nyikala.

9 Kanyhi yili ciba hono muwe wejete, si nha kuwa wuli wene. Hambi i wejete nha wotuga cihumbilizwa ca mu mulimo wawo.
10 Na hono yunji yagwe, ale, yuyo muyagwe komwinula. Ninga woyi he kuli mono yali wene kolo yeza kugwa! Hambi yuyo nha kowa yasina munhu mono komwinula!
11 Vyo nhavivyo kanyhi, hono wejete wagone hamonga, wosuma kwipela civuce, ninga munhu hono yawe yali wene, alu ho kosuma nhawule kwipela civuce yali wene?
12 Na kanyhi, munhu hono yawe yali wene, nha kusumwa no mulugu, ninga hono wawe wanhu wejete, womwimila mulugu no kumusuma. Kanyhi wumanye vyono, hono milejehe midatu yibotelezwe hamonga, si kupelu yaye kuyidumula lwa kamonga.

13 Yali ciba muzelelo muciwa we mahala, yuyo yamucizize mutemi mukombi kanyhi mulele, mono si yendile kubocela mafundo maswanu.
14 Ku soko kuli mwene hamba yawe yali yalawiliye mu jela, nalu yatema, hamba yawe yali yaleleywe mu wuciwa, nalu yawa mutemi, ayo si nghani yaye.
15 Nane nghehwesa kuli ze wanhu wose wono wekaye kono'nze, nghamanya vyono muli wene, moyuli muzelelo mono kosuma kutema hanhu ho mutemi mukombi.

16 Liwalo lye wanhu lyali lisina mudumo, ninga mwene yakawatema wose. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, awalya wono wakulelwa, si wosuma kumwenda yaye. Ninga mbeka yiyo nayo, yo nghani ya bwete du, kanyhi wo wigaze we bwete du, wono wusina hamba cambuzi co cose.


Muganuzi 5

Wulece kwihuwiza musunho

1 Ale wiyilanjililaje, wulece kwinjila caka mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Yowa ciba kuli'gwe hono winjile mu Nyumba yo Mulungu ho wuhulicize viswanu no kwiyijiza, si nha kulavya cilavywa nha vyono wanozaga wanhu walele, wanhu wono si wamanyile kuzitemanula ziswanu ne zimbi.
2 Kanyhi weze kwamula kwihwesa lono wukali si nalonga cinji, hamba wulece kuwa wuli mupelu ku wihuwiza co cose kuli Mulungu, hambi Mulungu nha yali ku wulanga nagwe wuli mono'nze. Holeka wulece kulonga nghani nyinji nha vyono yikunojeye.

3 Hambi nha munghango ye mahwele gako,

vyo nhavivyo vyo kolota ne zindoto zimbi,

na vyo yinha kanyhi,

munghango ya mu wulonji wako we zinghani zi nyinji,

yikuwa kupelu ku wulonga mumo ne zinghani zo wulele.

4 Holeka hono wihuwize kuli Mulungu, ale, wuwufisilizaje mbela wihuwizo wako. Hambi mwene Mulungu nha si kendezwaga ne wanhu walele. Holeka yikunojeye wufisilize cono wihuwize.
5 Hambi nha yowa ciba kuli'gwe hono wulece kwihuwiza cinji, si nha kwihuwiza, hamba kufisiliza yaye wihuwizo wako.
6 Kanyhi wulece kuzileceleza zinghani zako zikutegule mu wusutye, hanyuma kanyhi wuwe kumulonjela u mwitumami wo Mulungu wukutya, “Wayiko, si nozize ne nyono yaye.” Alu ho socici wukusaka kumunoza Mulungu yakukolelwe likuwo? Hamba socici wumunoze Mulungu yayihodanje milimo yako?
7 Ninga wumanye vyono, mu wulota ndoto nyinji, ayo si nghani yaye. Langa mu wulonga nghani nyinji, amo langa mo wuli wajilile. Ninga nghani mbaha, wumogope Mulungu wako.

Mikalile ya mono'nze yo nghani ya bwete du

8 Na kanyhi wulece kwizina, hono wuwone mu yisi, wanhu waciwa wakwiwonelwa, hamba si wakunozezwa ling'ani ne walongola we yisi, kuli zono zinojeye watendelwe. Ninga wumanye vyono, munhu wo wose we lilumbo liwaha mu yisi, mono yakuwewonela i wasocele, yuyo nayo yali hasi yo muwaha yunji, na muli wawo wose vyono wejete, ho yuli muwaha yunji kanyhi mono yawacizize wene.
9 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, yowa ciba hono mutemi yepiganzize mu mulimo wo kulima, hambi nha co cambuzi ce wanyawusi wose.

10 Kanyhi wumanye vyono, mono yendile mapesa, yuyo hamba si kokwigushwa na mapesa yaye hamba yatye, hambi yiyo nayo, nha nghani ya bwete du.
11 Na munghango yo wonjelezece we sawo, ale, yo nhavivyo na wanya kulya nawo, vyo wonjelezekaga, nayo munya sawo, wukwihwesa yakuwa kukwizwa na cici, mu sawo yiyo? Mwene langa mulimo wakwe kuyilanga du sawo yakwe.
12 Mbeka nghukulonjela vyono, zinhongo zo mwituma mulimo, hamba yalye yegute, hamba yekale si yegute, ninga ku cilo kagonaga nhongo swanu muno. Ninga mugoli, ayo kekalaga yehweleye du nhondo zose, hambi winjipe we sawo yakwe nha wumugazaga du cilo cose.

13 Kanyhi nakondya kuyiwona nghani yinji 'mbi muno muno mono'nze, yono munhu keyiwicilaga, sawo yono hanyuma yayo, hamba si yomutaza cinji co cose.
14 Na hanji zimbali zijelaga, nayo kawa kuyajiliza sawo yiyilya yose, hamba kusigalilwa na cinhu cinji, cono na lunji, yali yamupele hamba mwanagwe.
15 Na vyo yinha, hambi munhu nha vyono yaza mononze yali cipwi yasina cinji co cose, mu wulawa mu'nda yo nyina, ale, vyo nhavivyo vyo kolowuya kanyhi yali cipwi kuko kono yalawilila. Hamba si kosuma kusoleleza na cinji co cose ca mu nhuguti yakwe.
16 Nayo nghani yiyi yo 'mbi du muno muno. Ati du kowuya nha vyono yaza. Na mbeka yegazaga du na nghani ya bwete.
17 Nayo ho yakekala mu citi no wukalale, na mu wugazice, hamonga na mu matamwa ne likuwo mu mikalile yakwe yose ya mono'nze.

18 Alu azi ane zo nakondya kuziwona, vyono kumbe, nghani swanu yono yomukwila munhu yoyi, kulya, kung'wa hamonga no kusangalalila mana gono yetujiye mu nhuguti yakwe mu milimo yono yakwituma bahano hono'nze, mu vilo vivyo vicefu du vya mu wikale wakwe, vyono Mulungu yamupeye, hambi nha vyo yawicilwa no Mulungu.
19 Holeka hono yawe Mulungu yamupeye munhu wugoli ne sawo, kanyhi yawe yamupelile ne cilejeho co kuvitumamila no kuvisangalalila vivyo, ale, yimunojeye munhu yuyo yalumbe, kanyhi yavimotaje vivyo vyono yetujiye mu nhuguti yakwe mweneco. Hambi avyo nha cipegwa du cilawa kuli Mulungu.
20 Na vyono Mulungu yo yamukomboye yawe no wusangalale, holeka munhu si kosuma kuwa na mahwele mu mikalile yakwe yiyi ye vilo vicefu.


Muganuzi 6

1 Nayiwona kanyhi nghani yinji 'mbi mono yinze, kanyhi yo nghani nzito muno kuli wanhu.
2 Mulungu kamupelaga munhu wugoli, sawo, hamonga ne lilumbo, ho yalece kwanjila cinji co cose cono yakucisaka, ninga si kamupelaga wusume wo kuvisangalalila vinhu vivyo, langa mujenzi yo kovilya. Yiyi nayo yo nghani ya bwete du, kanyhi yo nghani 'mbi.
3 Munhu yadahile kuwa na wana yigana limonga, hamonga no wikale mononze myaka minji. Ninga hono munhu yuyo yalece kuyisangalalila mikalile yakwe, hamba kuzikwa mu citenjelo kwe lilumbo kose, mbeka, u mwana mono yaleligwe yafwiye, kowa yali ciba, si nha munhu yuyo.
4 Hambi u mwana mono yafwiye, nha si kamotaga cinji co cose so wulelwe wakwe, langa mwene kajililaga du mu citi, hodu, wawa kumusemwa.
5 Hamonga na zizo, u mwana we liwala lilyo, si yawuwene no wuzelu we lizuwa yaye, hamba kusuma kuwumanya wumi vyono winha.
6 Ninga hamba nhavivyo u mwana yuyo, yakutuga wucehe wono ayulya munhu si kowutujila lukulu, hamba yekale sunga yamale myaka elufu nyejete. Hambi wose nha wobita hanhu hamonga du.

7 Hambi munhu nha katugutilaga so wusaka ndigwa du, ninga lu, hamba si kegutaga yaye.
8 Alu, wule, wukwihwesa vyono u munhu we mahala, kowa yali ciba hongo, si nha yulya u munhu mulele? Ma, u munhu muciwa, katugaga cambuzici, hono yawe yamanyile vyono yimunojeye yekale, mu mikalile yakwe?
9 Mbeka yiyo yo nghani ya bwete du, kanyhi wo wigaze we bwete wono wusina cambuzi co cose. Ciba weze wuceye na cono wina naco, si nha kugazika nhondo zose, ne masulumizi go wusulumila kutuga vinhu vinji.

10 Ninga wumanye vyono, vyose vyono vikwijela, vyawikwa ku wulawa katali na katali, u wuwe wo munhu nha wumanyice, kanyhi cimanyiye lukulu vyono u musocele, si kosuma kwisumaniza no munhu we vilungo.
11 Kanyhi wumanye vyono, u wuwanji, wusina cambuzi hamba hadodo, hambi nha si wusumaga kumunyasuliza munhu.
12 Hambi nani lo mono yazimanyile zono zomukwila munhu, mu mikalile yiyi micefu, yono yisina hamba cambuzi co cose, i mikalile yono yipulutaga du nha mwenzenze? Hamba nani lo mono yali mono'nze yiyi, mono yazimanyile zono zomwagana munhu, kolo yakondya kufwa?


Muganuzi 7

I mafundo ga mu mikalile

1 Lilumbo liswanu lili ciba,

si nhe mafuta gono gena mhunjile swanu go wuguzi wukamu.

Kanyhi lizuwa lyo kufwa lili ciba si nhe lizuwa lyo kulelelwa.

2 Ciba kubita ku cililo, si nho kubita ku sikukuu,

kanyhi i womi yiwanojeye wakumbucile vyono,

lifwa licilindiye sese wose.

3 Kanyhi wukalale wuli ciba, si nho luseko,

hambi nhumbula nha yitugaga cambuzi ku soko yo wukalale wa ha wusu.

4 I nhumbula ya we mahala yizitununhicilaga zinyumba za wono wafwiliywe.

Ninga nhumbula yo munhu mulele,

yene yihwesaga zo wumote du.

5 Yo nghani swanu kuhuliciza mafundo ga we mahala,

si nha kuzihuliciza zinyimbo ze wanhu walele.

6 Hambi luseko lo munhu mulele,

nha lwihwanile no mulilo we mamivya,

lono gakutakamila mu moto.

Yiyo nayo yo nghani ya bwete du.

7 Nayo munhu we mahala,

hono yamusolele munhu yunji cinhu cakwe ko kumwiwonela,

yakwituma nghani ya wulele.

Nawo wubocela mafupo,

wubofuzaga mahala.

8 No mudumilizo we nghani, wuli ciba,

si nha ku mwamulizo wayo,

nayo munhu mukangalizi,

yuyo yo muswanu, si nho munhu mwidunhumuzi.

9 Holeka wulece kuwa mupelu ku wukolwa likuwo,

hambi likuwo nha wo wuwe we wanhu walele.

10 Kanyhi, wulece kuwuza wuwe kutya,

“Alo nhawule i vilo vye katali vyali viswanu,

si nhe vilo vya lulu?”

Wumanye vyono liwuzo lyono linha lilino,

si lilawaga mu mahala yaye.

11 Nago mahala, makamu muno gehwanile no wuhazi,

holeka yo nghani mbaha yono yinojeye kuli wanhu wose wono wakali womi.

12 Holeka, wudimwa ne mahala,

wihwanile no wudimwa ne mapesa, hambi mahala,

nha gamukamisaga mono yena nago.

13 Wihwesaje ta ze metumo go Mulungu,

wukwihwesa nani 'mbi mono kosuma kugolosa cono yacipetizye Mulungu?

14 Holeka mu vilo vyono wukali watuga vinji, ale, weze wina wusangalale, na mu vilo vyo wugaye navyo, ale wihwesaje nghani yiyi, vyono, u wutuga vinji, no wugaye, vyose vilawaga kuli Mulungu, ninga munhu si kosuma kumanya za hanyuma zono zikuza.

15 Na mu mikalile yangu yo wuciwa, nakondya kuziwona nghani zose, vyono kumbe u munhu we ling'ani, nayo kafwizaga du hamba yawe yali ling'ani, ninga u munhu mubi kajendelelaga kuwumipa du hamba yawe mubi.
16 Holeka, wulece kuciziliza kuwa we ling'ani, hamba wulece kuwa we mahala muno! Alo socici wiyongomeze go mweneco?
17 Ninga kanyhi, wulece kuwa wuli munhu mubi kuciza, hamba wulece kuwa wuli mulele! Socici lo wufwe lono vilo vyako vikali?
18 Holeka yikunojeye wulece kuciziliza muli vivyo vinhu vyejete. Hambi mono yamogopile Mulungu, nha si kosuma kugwizwa na nghani yo yose muli zizo.

19 Nago mahala gamupelaga vilungo munhu we mahala, kuciza wanhu yikumi, wono watemile mwi'kaya.
20 Na mbeka, hasina munhu wo wose mono'nze mono yali ling'ani, mono ketumaga ziswanu du nhondo zose, hamba kwituma wubi yaye.

21 Kanyhi wulece kuwa kwihwesa vyono, nghani zose za mbeka zono walongaga wanhu, hambi nha kosuma kuhulika vyono mwitumami wako kakuliga,
22 nagwe komanya go mweneco vyono nagwe kuwaligaga wanhu kali kenji.

Kulomba mahala

23 Nane nghazipima kwe mahala zizo zose, hanyuma nghawa kwihwesa mu nhumbula yangu nghatya, “Alu mwene nghusaka niwe munhu we mahala, hongo!” Ninga lu mahala, nha gali kutali muno nane.
24 Alu mwene, munhu kosuma kumanya nhawule nhunizo yo wikala mumi? Mbeka nghani yiyi nzito, kanyhi nghamu muno muno kuli'se.
25 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, nghahidimbala muno mu wumanye, no kulanga-langa, sunga no kulondola muno mahala, hamonga ne nhunizo ya zono zili baha, kanyhi ho sume kumanya u wubi wo wulele vyono winha, u wulele wo wu wula mahala.

26 Kanyhi ho yili nghani yinji yono nghayiwona, nghani yono 'mbi muno muno kuciza lifwa, yo nghani yo mucekulu, mono nhumbula yakwe, yikuwa kugwiza nha mutego hamba lwavu, ne miwoko yakwe yihwanile manyolo. Ninga munhu mono yamogopile Mulungu, yakuwa kumwilega u mucekulu we liwala lilyo. Ninga u munhu mubi, ayo ketegulizaga du ho yagwizwe nayo.
27 Azi zo nghukulonjela ane no Muganuzi, vyono zo naziwona lono nazilondelelaga hadodo hadodo.
28 Nane nghahidimbala muno kali kenji ho nizimanye zizo, ninga hamba si nasuma yaye. Mu wanhu walume elufu monga, nghasuma kumuwona mulume monga du mono yanojeye lilumbo, ninga muli wacekulu wose we liwalo nha lilyo, si nasuma kumuwona mucekulu hamba monga mono yanojeye lilumbo.
29 Alu kanyhi ko yili nghani yinji monga, yono nakondya kuyimanya, nayo yoyi, vyono Mulungu yaciwumba cili waswanu kanyhi cili wedodofyi, ninga sese co weneco, ciketajila mu mahodango.


Muganuzi 8

1 Alo nani mbi mono yehwanile na we mahala?

Hamba nani mono yadahile kuwulolosa wuwe weneco,

we vinhu vyono vinha?

Hambi mahala go munhu, nha gang'alazaga wusu wakwe,

no kugawusa manghunya-nghunya ga mu wusu wakwe.

Weze ne cihuliko kuli mutemi

2 Nane nghukulonjela nghutya, Weze wuli cihuliko kuli zose zono mutemi yakuzilajiliza, ku soko yo wilahize wako wa kuli Mulungu.
3 Wulece kuwuka musunho hali mutemi, hamba wulece kujendelela kwituma zono si zikwendeza, hambi mutemi, nha kanozaga nha vyono yendile mweneco.
4 Nalyo lilajilizo lyo mutemi, nha yo nghani ye ndumilizo, nani kanyhi 'mbi mono kosuma kumuwuza yawe kutya, alu wukunoza nhawule.
5 Nayo mono kakamisaga malajilizo, yuyo kekalaga yapolile du, nayo munhu we mahala, kamanyaga vyono ko lili lizuwa lyo wulipicizwe.
6 Heya, hambi nha ho wuli wuling'anizwe we vinhu vyose, holeka munhu, mbeka yena mahodango mawaha,
7 hambi nha si yacimanyile cicici cono cokwijela hanyuma yayo. Alo nani 'mbi mono kosuma kumumanyiza zono zolokwijela ha nyuma?
8 Hambi nha hasina munhu wo wose mono kosuma kuyibatiliza nhumbula yakwe ho yalece kufwa, hamba hasina munhu wo wose mono kosuma kwiyimila ho yalece kufwa. Wumanye vyono, mwipango lyo wulugu, hasina mono kosuma kulekwa, ale, nhavivyo no wubi nawo, si womuponya u munhu mubi.

I wabi, na we ling'ani

9 Na mu wulondole-londole wangu, nghaziwona zizi zose zono zikwitumwa bahano hono yinze, vyono, hono munhu yawe yena vilungo, hodu kovitumamila vilungo vivyo ko kumwiwonela no kumutamisa munhu muyagwe.
10 Na kanyhi niwawene ne wanhu wabi vyono wakuzikwa, i wanhu wono wali wazoweye kwinjila no kulawila mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga no kukumywa muno mwi'kaya mono wetumilaga zo wavu wawo. Ninga yiyo nayo nghani ya bwete du.
11 Alu na vyono wubi si wulipicizwaga musunho, holeka zinhumbula ze wanhu, zihidimbalilaga mu wituma zimbi du.
12 Wuwalangaje ta wanyo wubi, vyono wetumaga zimbi kali cigana, ninga hamba nhavivyo, vyo wajendeleye du kuwumipa. Ninga ane manyiye lukulu vyono, i nyemo yiwalindiye wono wamogopile Mulungu, ku soko ye cogopo cawo,
13 ninga kuli wabi, ako kusina nyemo hamba hadodo. Wawo wene, wopuluta nha mwenzenze, si wosuma kuwuponya wumi wawo, hambi nha si wamogopile Mulungu yaye.
14 Kanyhi hoyili nghani yinji 'mbi yono nakondya kuyiwona baha hono yinze, vyono i wanhu waswanu, wakuwa kutendelwa zimbi, zono ziwanojeye i wanhu wabi wo watendelwe, nawo i wabi wakutendelwa ziswanu, zono ziwanojeye i i wanhu waswanu, wo watendelwe. Nane nghatya, ayi mbeka yo nghani ya bwete du.

15 Ninga ane nihuwiye vyono, u munhu yanojeye yawe yali mu wumote muno, vyono ahano hono yinze, hasina cinji ciswanu cono cicizize kulya no kung'wa, hamonga no kwiyendeza. Azi zo munhu kosuma kuzituma mu wipiganze wakwe we milimo, mu vilo vyo wikale wakwe wa baha hono yinze, vyono Mulungu komupela.
16 Na lono najelaga kuhidimbala muno, ho sume kumanya mahala, hamonga no kwiyijiza zono zikwitumwa mono'nze, nghatanga vyono kosuma kugona meso bwete,
17 hamba kusuma kuzimanya zo Mulungu. Hamba wuhidimbale muno muno gwee, si kosumila lukulu kuzimanya yaye. Wanhu we mahala, wadahile kwiyitya wene wazimanyile, ninga i mbeka kumbe, ho si wamanyile cinji yaye.


Muganuzi 9

1 Nane nghehwesa muli zizo zose, nghawona vyono, kumbe Mulungu du yali wene, yo katongaga ze metumo gose ga we mahala hamonga na ga we ling'ani, hamba wuwe wendo hamba wuwe lusulo, wanhu si wamanyile yaye. Hamba hasina munhu wo wose mono kosuma kuzimanya zono zomwijelela hanyuma,
2 hambi i we ling'ani ne wabi, waswanu ne wavu, watelevu na wono wetipize, wono wakumbikaga na wono si wakumbikaga, wose wawo mudumo wawo monga du. Hambi u muswanu, nha vihwanile du no mubi, nayo mono yakwilahiza, si visimajiye yaye na mono si yakwilahiza.
3 Nayo ayi yo nghani yono 'mbi muno muli zose zono zili mononze, vyono, wose wena mudumo monga du. Kanyhi na vyono wanhu mu mikalile yawo yose, wekalaga wamemile wavu no wulele mu zinhumbula zawo, na hanyuma yayo wawa kufwa.
4 Ninga kuli mono yakali vili hamonga ne womi, ayo yakali yena witumbilo, hambi nha wo wutya, “Ilibwa lyono lili nyumi nha lili ciba, si nha lisimba lyono lifwiye.”
5 Hambi wono wakali womi, nha wamanyaga vyono wofwa, ninga wono wafwiye, si wamanyaga cinji co cose. Hambi nha wasina cipegwa cinji kanyhi, langa wene wakondya du kusemelwa lukulu.
6 U wendo wawo no lusulo lwawo, sunga ne masulumizi gawo, vyose vivyo vyajililana hamonga nawo, na hodu du, hamba si wowa ne'fungu lyo lyose kanyhi mono'nze.

7 Holeka, wubite wukalye ndigwa yako ko wusangalale, wukang'we ne divayi yako ne nhumbula yono wusangalaye, hambi Mulungu nha yakondya kuyilumbiliza milimo yako.
8 Ale wivwikaje viswanu, wibace ne mafuta mu mutwe wako.
9 Wuyisangalalile mikalile yako hamonga no mugolece wako mono wumwendile muno, mu vilo vyose vyo wikale wako wono wusina cambuzi, wono Mulungu yakupelile baha hono'nze. Hambi yiyino, nha yo wapegwa mu wikale wako wa mu milimo yako ya bahano hono'nze.
10 Nghani yo yose yono wendile kuyituma, wuyitumaje kwe vilungo vyako vyose, hambi ku yisi ye wafu kono kobita, nha kusina milimo, hamba lihweso lyo lyose, hamba wumanye, hamba mahala bwete.

Zimbali zizaga nhinicizo

11 Kanyhi nakondya kuciwona cinhu cinji mono'nze, vyono i wanhu wamanyhi muno, hamba si wawasumaga wayawo mu midawalo yo kwitiga lumangho. Nawo we vilungo, hamba si wasumaga wulugu, nawo we mahala, hamba si watugaga ndigwa. Nawo wono wena wizuce, hamba si wagolipaga yaye, nawo wanghula, hamba si wapegwaga lilumbo lya hacanyha yaye. Ne lyuwa liwazizaga wose du.
12 Hamba yunji si yamanyile lizuwa lono lipango lyakwe lyomuficila. Nha vyono zisomba zikuwa kwibatwa no lwavu wula kumanya, nha vyono kanyhi zindeje nazo, zikuwa kugwila mu mitego nhinicizo, ale, ne wanhu nawo, vyo wenjilaga mu zimbali nhavivyo lono si walanjiliye.

13 Ho yili nghani yinji kanyhi yono nayiwona, yono yikulajila viswanu vyono, kumbe mahala mono'nze, gakwiliye muno muno.
14 Kwali na cikaya cinji cidodo vivi, cono cina wekazi waco wacefu. Ale yakaza mutemi yunji we vilungo, yakacipilima cikaya cico, yakewikaniza yacihome.
15 Na mu cikaya cico, mwali moyuli yunji, munhu we mahala, ninga muciwa muno, mono ku so mahala gakwe, yasuma kuciponya cikaya cico. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, hasina hamba munhu monga mono hanyuma yasuma kumukumbucila u muciwa yuyo.
16 Ku soko yiyo, holeka nane nghutya nhavi, “Mbeka kumbe mahala gacizize vilungo, hamba yiwe mahala go muciwa si gatondowozwa, ne zinghani zakwe hamba kuhulicizwa yaye.

17 Ninga yili ciba wuzihulicize zinghani zo wutule za we mahala,

si nha kuhuliciza mazozo go mutemi mu wihangulize we wanhu walele.

18 Holeka ku wuwa ne mahala, kuswanu muno,

kucizize u witumbila vitwalo.

Ninga hamba nhavivyo,

u munhu mubi yumonga,

kahodangaga nyinji ziswanu.”


Muganuzi 10

1 Mahazi gono gafwiye,

gatajilaga munungho mubi mu mafuta ge mhunjile swanu,

ale, no wulele wudodo nawo,

wuhodangaga mahala ne lilumbo.

2 U munhu we mahala,

nhumbula yakwe yimutongaga ku wituma zo mugoloka,

ninga u mulele, nhumbula yakwe yimwajilizaga.

3 Nayo mulele, hamba yawe yakolile mu nzila,

yamanyice du, hambi nha kalonjelaga wanhu wose,

vyono mwene mulele.

4 Holeka hono mulongola yakukolelwe likuwo,

wulece kwizila, weze manya vyono,

masutya mawaha gapatilwaga mu wutuvu.

5 Kanyhi ho wuli wubi wunji wono nakondya kuwuwona mono'nze, u wubi wono wukuletwa ne walongola,
6 Vyono ati milimo yo wulongola wakupegwa i wanhu walele, nawo i wagoli, wakusola vilejeho vya hasi.
7 Kanyhi niwawene wetumami wakujendela mu zifwalasi, ninga i wawaha we lilumbo, wakujenda kwa migulu nha wetumami.

8 Na hono wuhimbe yikweme,

kogwila go mweneco mumo,

na hono wumwagule lijinji,

ale, lizoka lyokuluma.

9 Na lono wukuhimbula mabwe,

wumanye vyono gokutamisa,

na lono wukutenya ngwagu,

naho wumanye vyono zimbali nazo zokwagana.

10 Hono witumile nhemo nduje yono si nole,

yosaka witumile vilungo muno,

ninga mu witumamila mahala,

munhu katugaga cambuzi.

11 Ne lizoka hono lilume lono likali nakulugwa,

hodu u mukulujizi hamba si kosakwa kanyhi.

12 Izinghani za we mahala,

zimupelaga cambuzi mono yakuzilonga,

ninga u munhu mulele,

keyongomezaga mweneco ko wulonje wakwe.

13 Hambi u mulele, nha kelonganjicilaga nghani zo wulele,

no wulonje wakwe,

kawumalililaga mu ciwula mahala wakwe.

14 U mulele, kalonganjikaga nghani nyinji muno muno.

Ninga hasina munhu mono yazimanyile zono zolowa hanyuma,

hamba hasina mono kosuma kucimanyiza,

zono zolowa hanyuma kolo cafwiza.

15 Umulele yakuwa kukatazwa no mulimo wakwe mweneco,

sunga kawa kwajililwa ne nzila yo kuwuyila ku kaya yakwe.

16 Woyi, hee, kuli'gwe, go yisi,

hono mulongola wako yawe yakali muzelelo,

ne walongola wako hono wanoze sikukuu hakali mitondo!

17 Agwe go yisi, kowa wuli ciba,

hono mulongola wako yawe yena wuwe wono wukamile.

Na hono walongola wako wanoze sikukuu mwi'pango lyono linojeye,

ho wetujile vilungo,

ninga si ko wugale yaye.

18 Nalyo litembe, lidodomelaga ku soko yo wudoba wo munhu,

ne nyumba nayo,

yisululaga ku soko yo wu wula kutondowozwa.

19 Ndigwa nyinji, yiletaga wusangalale, nayo divayi,

yisangalazaga mikalile.

Nago mapesa go gaziletaga zizo zose.

20 Wulecele lukulu kumulijita mono yatemile hamba mu nhumbula yako,

wulece kumulijita mugoli,

hamba mu cinyumba cako co kugona,

ku soko sunga ne zindeje nha zolihulika lizi lyako,

naco ciwumbwa cono cigulukaga,

nha cozanza zinghani zako.


Muganuzi 11

Vyono kanozaga u munhu we mahala

1 Wujele lyuwa lyako, wunoze wusuluza,

wugumile ndigwa yako mu malenga,

nagwe kotuga cambuzi mu vilo vicefu!

2 Wagawile wanhu zisawo zako,

hambi nha si wumanyile yimbi yono yokwagana mono'nze.

3 Mavunde kolo gamema malenga,

mvula yitonyaga, ne libici nalyo,

hono ligwile kutakama hamba kusumuka,

baho hono lyogwila, ho lyokwikala du.

4 Nayo mono kalindilaga sunga mbeho yitule ho yahadice,

yuyo hamba si kohadika yaye,

nayo mono kalindilaga sunga mavunde gawuce,

yuyo si kodudula cinji.

5 Nagwe si wumanyile na vyono mida yinjilaga, hamba no wumi vyono wukuwa kwanza mumo mu'nda yo mucekulu mono yena muda. Holeka yo nhavivyo kanyhi vyo si kosuma kugamanya metumo go Mulungu, mono kazinozaga zose.
6 Holeka lono wukuhadika mbeyu zako, wuhadice mwi'pango lye hakali mitondo na mwi'pango lye cinyamihe nhavivilya, hambi nha si wumanyile za hayi zono zokota, hono za hakali mitondo, hamba ze cinyamihe, hamba lunji zose.

7 Heya, wuzelu wuswanu muno, no ku wulanga lizuwa nako, kwendezaga meso.
8 Holeka, munhu hono yawe yena myaka minji yo wikale wakwe, yimunojeye yayisangalalile myaka yiyo yose, akuno yakukumbucila vyono, i vilo vibi, navyo vinji nhavivyo, na kanyhi, vyono zose zono zokwijela zowa za bwete du.

Mafundo kuli wazelelo

9 Gwe go muzelelo wusangalalile wudodo wako, nhumbula yako yiwusangalalile wuzelelo wako, witume nghani yo yose yono wendile kwituma, wutyatye wuhile we nhumbula yako, ninga akuno wukumbucile vyono Mulungu kokuling'aniza, mu nghango ya zizo zose.
10 Wulece kuwa na mahwele, langa wutuzaje du mutima wako, weze wuli mwiyimizi, ho wulece kwinjila mu zimbali. Weze kukumbucila vyono si kojendelela kuwa wuli mudodo du vilo vyose.


Muganuzi 12

1 Wumukumbucilaje Muwumbi wako mu vilo vyo wuzelelo wako, lono i vilo vibi vikali si vinaza yaye, hamba lono yikali si yinaza yono kolotya nhavi, “Aa, vinhu avi vyose hamba si vikunyendeza kanyhi yaye.”

2 Holeka wuzitumaje zizino lono lizuwa likali natukuwazwa,

hamonga no wuzelu, no mwezi,

sunga ne zinyelezi zose,

na lono mavunde gakali sinagubika kanyhi,

lono mvula yikali yawuka.

3 Mwi'pango lono i miwoko yako yono yakukamisaga, yolocicima, ne migulu yako yono yili vilungo, nayo yolowa yisina vilungo. Ne meno gako, nago golowa gasina vilungo vyo kutafunyila ndigwa yako, ne meso gako nago golowa si gakuwona viswanu.
4 Nago i makutu gako si gakuhulika i mazozo ga mu zinzila. Hamba si kolohulika bwete ne lizozo lye masine yo kusaga, hamba mulilo we masumbi yaye, hambe mazozo ge zindeje, si golokusinhusa yaye mu zinhongo.
5 Mwi'pango lono wanhu wowa wena woga, no wogofywe wotagalila mu zinzila zose. Sunga no wujendanje wose ako na ako wowa wukamu muno muno. Mwi'pango lono kowa wakombipa, watuga 'mvwi zose, lono kolowa kweza kukamu muno kuli'gwe kujenda, lono no wusulumizi wako wose wowa wakondya kuwuka.

Wumanye nha vyono cikubita ku wikalo we cibitilila, na holowa cililo ciwaha mu zinzila.
6 Baho langa, u luputi lwe fweza ho lolodumucila, ne tala ye zahabu nayo, baho ho yilatulicilaa. Ne liguludumu nalyo lyo kukwejela malenga mwi'sima lyolobeneka, ne njilo ye malenga yilatulika.
7 Ale, mwi'pango lilyo, nagwe go munhu mono wuli manghundi, muwili wako nagwe, ho wulawuyila mu manghundi kono walawilila, no muhe wako nawo, womuwuyila Mulungu mono yacipela.
8 Yo nghani ya bwete du, yo nghani ya bwete du, ane nghukulonjela mbeka no Muganuzi! Vinhu vyose vyono wukuviwona, mbeka yo nghani ya bwete du.

Ze ndumilizo

9 Nayo Muganuzi ku soko ye mahala gakwe, yakajendelela kuwejiza wanhu zo wumanye wakwe. Yakazihwesa muno mu nhumbula yakwe, yakazilondola-londola no kuyimanya imbeka yazo. Hanyuma yakawa kuziloloseza mu wumanye wakwe.
10 Nayo Muganuzi yakehidimbaza muno yatuje zinghani zono zikwendeza, nayo yakawa kuzandika mu nghango yo wunghula wakwe, zinghani zose zono zili mbeka.
11 Nazo zinghani ze wanhu we mahala, zihwanile zinghome zono zina visonje, zono wadimi wazitumamilaga kuzitonga zingholo. Ne zimbazi zono zikonghoywe no kuwikwa hamonga, zizo zikalaga zikamile kotya zimambo zono nghomanhilwe hasi. Hambi nha zalawa kuli Mulungu u mudimi yumonga wa sese wose.

12 Ninga hamonga na zizo, 'gwe mwanangu, nghukunena wilanjililaje! Weze kumanya vyono u wandici we vitabu, wusina ndumo, no wusoma muno nawo, wuletaga wukatale wuwaha wo muwili.
13 Hamonga na zizo zose, ho yili nghani monga kanyhi ye ndumilizo, nayo yoyi, “Mogopaje Mulungu, kanyhi wibate ne malajilizo gakwe.” Yiyi yo nghani mbaha muno, yo yaleka munhu yakawumbwa ho yayitume.
14 Hambi Mulungu nha koloyiling'aniza milimo yetu yose, hamonga ne metumo getu gose, gono cetumilaga mu cinyele, hono gawe maswanu hamba gawe mabi.
