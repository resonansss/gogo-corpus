Esita 10

U wuwaha wa Ahasuwelo hamonga na Moledekayi

1 Mutemi Ahasuwelo, yakawatoweza kodi i wanhu wono wekalaga mu zisi zose zo wutemi wakwe, hamonga na wose wono wekalaga mu vizingwa vya mwi'lenga.
2 Ne zinghani zakwe zose zimbaha na zo wizine zono yazituma, hamonga sunga no wulolose wa vyono yamupela wuwaha Moledekayi yawe muwaha we lilumbo lya hacanyha, zose zizo zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze watemi we Wamedi na Pelisiya.
3 Hambi Moledekayi u Muyahudi, nha yo yali we kejete mu wulongola ku wulawilila ko mutemi Ahasuwelo. Nayo Moledekayi yakawa yena lilumbo yiwaha muno, kanyhi yakawa yendezwe mwipuga lyose lye Wayahudi wayagwe. Nayo yakawatendela viswanu muno wanhu wakwe, no kulonga zo mulamu kuli zo lulelo lwawo lwose.
