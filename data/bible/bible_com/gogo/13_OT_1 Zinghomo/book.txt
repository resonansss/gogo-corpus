1 Zinghomo 1

Kuwulawilila Adamu sunga kuli Abulahamu

(Mwa 5:1-32; 10:1-32; 11:10-26)

1 Adamu yali yo sogwe wa Seti, nayo Seti yo sogwe wa Enosi, nayo Enosi yo sogwe wa Kenani,
2 Nayo Kenani yo sogwe wa Mahalaleli, nayo Mahalaleli yo sogwe wa Yaledi.
3 Yaledi yo sogwe wa Enoci, nayo Enoci yo sogwe wa Metusela, nayo Metusela yo sogwe wa Lameki.
4 Nayo Lameki yo sogwe wa Noha. Noha yakalela wana wadatu, Shemu, Hamu na Yafeti.

5 Iwana wa Yafeti wali awano, Gomeli, na Magogi, na Madayi, na Yavani, na Tubali, na Mesheki na Tilasi. Wawano wali wo walezi we wanhu wono wasola matagwa gawo.
6 I wana wa Gomeli wakawa wo wanhu wono wekalaga ku Ashikenazi na Lifati, na ku Togalima.
7 Nawo i wana wa Yavani, wali awa: Elisha, Talishishi na Citimu, na Walodani.

8 Nawo i wana wa wa Hamu, wali awano. Kushi, Misili, Putu, na Kanani. Nawo wawano wali wo walezi we wanhu wono wasola matagwa gawo.
9 Nawo i wana wa Kushi, wakawa wo wanhu wono wekalaga ku Seba, Havila, Sabuta, Lama na Sabuteka. Nawo i wana wa Lama, wakawa wo wanhu wono wekalaga ku Sheba, na Dedani.
10 (Nayo Kushi yakamulela mwana litagwa lyakwe Nimulodi. Yuyu Nimulodi yakawa yo munhu mulongozi we vilungo mono yinze.)
11 Nawo i wana wa Misili, wali wo Waludi, Waanamu, Walehabi ne Wanafutuhi.
12 Patulusi, Kasilu na Kilete. (Na muli mwene mo walawa i Wafilisiti.)
13 Nawo i wana wa Kanani, wali awano, Sidoni, yo mwana mulongozi kulelwa, na Heti. Nawo wawa wo wali walezi we wanhu wono wasola matagwa gawo.
14 Nayo Kanani yali yo mulezi we Wayebusi, ne Waamoli, ne Wagiligashi,
15 Wahivi ne Waaliki, ne Wasini,
16 ne Waalivadi, ne Wazemali, hamonga ne Wahamati.

17 Nawo i wana wa Shemu, wali awano, Elamu, Ashuli, Alipakishadi, Ludi, Alamu, Uzi, Huli, Geteli, na Mesheki. Nawo wali wo walezi we wanhu wono wasola matagwa gawo.
18 Nayo Alipakishadi yali yo sogwe wa Shela, nayo Shela yali yo sogwe wa Ebeli.
19 Nayo Ebeli yali yena wana wejete. Yumonga yacemwaga litagwa lyakwe Pelegi, vyono mu vilo vyakwe, i wanhu ho wagawanucila mono yinze. U muyagwe nayo yacemwaga Yokitani.
20 Nawo i wana wa Yokitani, wali wo wanhu wono wekalaga ku Alimodadi, Shelefu, Hazalimaweti, na Yela.
21 Hadolamu, Uzali, Dikila,
22 Ebali, Abimayeli na Sheba.
23 Ofili, Havila, na Yobabu.

24 U wityatye we mhuga ku wulawilila kwa Shemu sunga ko kwa Abulamu wuli nhavi, Shemu, Alipakishadi, Shela,
25 Ebeli, Pelegi, Lewu,
26 Selugi, Naholi, Tela,
27 na Abulamu, (mono yamanyice kwe 'tagwa lya Abulahamu.)

Wo lulelo lwa Ishumayeli

(Mwa 25:12-16)

28 Abulahamu, yali yena wana wejete, Isaka na Ishumayeli.
29 I wana wa Ishumayeli, wakawa wo walongola we zimhuga yikumi na nyejete, nha cinji, Nebayoti, wo we mhuga yono litagwa lyali lyo mwana we Ishumayeli, mwana u mulongozi kulelwa. Hanyuma, Kedali, Abudiyeli, Mibisamu.
30 Mishuma, Duma, Masa, Hadadi, Tema,
31 Yetuli, Nafishi, na Kedema.

32 Kanyhi Abulahamu, yakawa yena mujitulwa litagwa lyakwe Ketula. Ketula yakamulelela wana mutandatu, nawo wo awano, Zimulani, Yokishani Medani, Midiyani, Ishibaki, Shuwa. Nayo Yokishani, yali yena wana wejete Sheba na Dedani.
33 Nayo Midiyani yali yena wana wahano, Efa, Efeli, Hanoki, Abida, na Elida.

Wo lulelo lwa Esawu

(Mwa 36:1-19)

34 Nayo Isaka u mwana wa Abulahamu, yali yena wana wejete, Esawu na Yakobo.
35 I wana wa Esawu wali wo awa, Elifazi, Lewuyeli, Yewushi, Yalamu na Kola.
36 Nayo Elifazi, yakawa yo sogwe wa Temani, Omali, Zefo, Getamu, Kenezi, Timuna, na Amaleki.
37 Nayo Lewuyeli, yakawa yo sogwe wa Nahati, Zela, Shama, na Miza.

Iwekazi, wanghalaco we yisi ya Edomu

(Mwa 36:20-30)

38 I wekazi wanghalaco we yisi ya Edomu, walawilila mu lulelo lwe wana wa Seyili nawo wo awano.

39 Lotani, mono yali yo sogwe we zimhuga za Holi na Homani. (Nayo Lotani yakawa yena lumbulye litagwa lyakwe Timina.)
40 Nayo Shoba, yali yo sogwe we zimhuga za Alivani, Manahati, Ebali, Shefi na Onamu.

Nayo Zibeyoni, yakawa na wana wejete, Ayiya na Ana.
41 Nayo yuyu Ana, yali yo sogwe wa Dishoni. Nayo Dishoni, yali yo sogwe we zimhuga za Hamulani, Eshiba, Itilani na Celani.
42 Nayo Ezeli, yali yo sogwe we zimhuga za Bilihani, Zawayani, na Yawakani.

Nayo Dishani, yali yo sogwe we zimhuga za Uzi na Alani.

I watemi we yisi ya Edomu

(Mwa 36:31-43)

43 Awa wono wetyatizye, wali wo watemi we yisi ye Waedomu wono welecelaga wutemi, lono Isilayeli yali yikali sinatuga mutemi wayo wo wose.

44 Bela, u mwana wa Beyoli munya Dinihaba

45 Yohabu, u mwana wa Zela munya Bozila.

46 Hushamu, munya yisi ya Temani.

Hadadi, u mwana wa Bedadi munya Aviti.

(Nayo yuyu yawasuma Wamidiyani mu wulugu,

mu yisi ya Mowabu).

47 Samula, munya Masileka

48 Shawuli, munya Lehoboti mwikolongo.

49 Baali Hanani, u mwana wa Akiboli.

50 Hadadi, munya Pawu.

(U mugolece wakwe yacemwaga Mehetabeli u mwana

we cicekulu wa Matuledi, kanyhi mwizukulu wa Mezahabu.)

51 Nawo i wanhu wono wekalaga ku Edomu, wakagawanuka zikawa nghungugo nyinji, nha vyono nhavi: We nghungugo ya Timuna, ya Aluva, ya Yeteti,
52 ya Oholibama, ya Ela, ya Pinoni,
53 ya Kenazi, ya Temani, ya Mibusali,
54 ya Magidiyeli, na ya Ilamu.


1 Zinghomo 2

Wo lulelo lwa Yuda

1 Yakobo yali yena wana yikumi na wejete, nawo wo awa: Lubeni, Simiyoni, Lawi, Yuda, Isakali, Zabuloni,
2 Dani, Yusufu, Benyamini, Nafutali, Gadi, na Asheli.

3 Yuda, yali yena wana wahano. Na kuli mugolece wakwe we Cikanani Batishuwa, nako yakatuga wana wadatu, Eli, Onani, na Shela. Ninga Eli, u mwanagwe mulongozi kulelwa, yali munhu mubi muno muno, na ku soko yiyo, holeka Muwaha Mulungu yakamuwulaga.
4 Nayo mukwewe Tamali, yakamulelela wana wejete, Pelezi na Zela.

5 Nayo Pelezi, yali yena wana wejete, Hezeloni na Hamuli.
6 U mwanakwawe Zela, yali yena wana wahano, Zimuli, Etani, Hemani, Kalikoli, na Dalida.
7 Akani mwana wa Kalimi, wa muli wana wa Zela, yaleta wongomezwe wuwaha kuli Waisilayeli, vyono yavisola vinhu vyo mwiko, vyono vyali vilavywe kuli Mulungu.

8 Nayo Etani yali yena mwana monga, litagwa lyakwe Azaliya.

Wityatye we mhuga yo mutemi Dawudi

9 Hezeloni, yali yena wana wadatu, Yelameli, Lamu na Kalebi.

10 Wityatye we mhuga yiyo, ku wulawilila kwa Lamu sunga kuli Yese, wali wuli nhavi: Lamu yakamulela Amuminadabu, nayo Amuminadabu yakamulela Nahashoni, (yuyu Nahashoni yali yo munhu muwaha we lilumbo mu we mhuga ya Yuda.) Nayo Nahashoni yakamulela Salemoni,
11 Salemoni yakamulela Bowazi, Bowazi yakamulela Obedi,
12 Obedi yakamulela Yese.

13 Nayo Yese yali yena wana mupunghati, na mu ndelwa yawo wali wetyatizye nhavi, Mwana mulongozi kulelwa yali Eliyabi, hanyuma wakatyatya Abinadabu, Shama,
14 Nataneli, Ladayi
15 Ozemu na Dawudi.
16 Ninga wakawa ne walumbu zawo wejete nhavivyo, nawo wo awano, Zeluya na Abijeli.

Nayo Zeluya u mwana we cicekulu wa Yese, yali yena wana wadatu, nawo wo awano, Abishayi, Yowabu, na Asaheli.
17 U mwanagwe we cicekulu Abijeli, nayo yakatolwa na Yefa wa mu lulelo lwa Ishumayeli. Nawo wakalela mwana yumonga litagwa lyakwe Amasa.

Wo lulelo lwa Hezeloni

18 Kalebi u mwana wa Hezeloni, yakamutola Azuba, nawo wakalela mwana yumonga we cicekulu, litagwa lyakwe Yeliyoti. Kanyhi yakalela na wana wadatu we cilume, nawo wo awano: Yesha, Shoba, na Alidoni.
19 Azuba yaza kufwa, Kalebi yakatola mugolece yunji, litagwa lyakwe Efulata. Wakalelana nayo mwana monga, litagwa lyakwe Huli.
20 Nayo Huli yakalela mwana yumonga, litagwa lyakwe Uli. Nayo Uli yakamulela Bezaleli.

21 Nayo Hezeloni, lono yali yaficila myaka makumi mutandatu, yakamutola mwana we cicekulu wa Macili, u lumbulye lya Gileyadi, nawo wakalela mwana yumonga, litagwa lyakwe Segubu.
22 Nayo Segubu, yakalela mwana monga, litagwa lyakwe Yayili. Nayo yuyu Yayili, yakatema makaya makumi mejete na madatu ga mu yisi ya Gileyadi.
23 Ninga wutemi wa Geshuli na Alamu, wukagasuma kuko makaya makumi mutandatu, hamonga ne vitumbi vya Yayili na Kenati, ne vikaya vyose vyono vyali vili behi behi. Ne wanhu wose wono wekalaga mumo, wali wo lulelo lwa Macili u sogwe wa Gileyadi.
24 Na lono Hezeloni yali yeza kufwa, u mwanagwe Kalebi, yakamutola Efulata, u mucekulu mono yali wo sogwe Hezeloni. Wakalelana nayo mwana yumonga, litagwa lyakwe Ashihuli, mono yalizenga likaya lya Tekowa.

Wo lulelo lwa Yelameli

25 Nayo Yelameli, mono yali yo mwana mulongozi kulelwa kwa Hezeloni, yali yena wana wahano, nawo wo awano. Lamu, u mwana u mulongozi kulelwa, hanyuma ho Buna, Oleni, Ozemu, na Ahiya.
26 Nayo Lamu yali yena wana wadatu, nawo wo awano; Mayazi, Yamini, na Ekeli.
27 Ninga yuyu Yelameli, yakawa yena mugolece yunji kanyhi we kejete, litagwa lyakwe Atala. Wakalelana nayo mwana monga, litagwa lyakwe Onamu.
28 Nayo Onamu yali yena wana wejete, nawo wo awano, Shamayi na Yada. Nayo Shamayi yali yena wana wejete, Nadabu na Abishuli.

29 Abishuli yakamutola mucekulu, litagwa lyakwe Abihayili, wakalelana wana wejete, Ahibani na Molidi.
30 Nayo Nadabu u muwahawe wa Abishuli, yali yena wana wejete, Saledi na Apayimu. Ninga Saledi yakafwa si yaleka hamba wana.
31 Nayo yuyu Apayimu, yali yo sogwe wa Ishi. Nayo Ishi, yali yo sogwe wa Ahilayi.

32 Nayo Yada u mwanakwawe wa Shamayi, yali yena wana wejete, Yeta, na Yonazani. Ninga Yeta yakafwa si yaleka hamba wana.
33 Yonazani yali yena wana wejete, Peleti na Zaza. Na wawano wose wali wo wana wa Yelameli.

34 Nayo Sheshani yali yasina wana we cilume, yali yena wana wa cicekulu wose. Yakawa yena mwitumami we Cimisili, litagwa lyakwe Yeliha.
35 Sheshani yakamukombolela Yeliha mwanagwe monga we cicekulu vitole nayo. Nawo wakalelana mwana yumonga, litagwa lyakwe Atayi.
36 No wityatye we mhuga yawo, ku wulawilila Atayi sunga ko kwa Elishama, wali wuli nhavi: Atayi, yakamulela Natani, Natani yakamulela Zabadi,
37 nayo Zabadi yakamulela Efilali, nayo Efilali yakamulela Obedi.
38 Nayo Obedi yakamulela Yehu, na Yehu yakamulela Azaliya.
39 Nayo Azaliya yakamulela Helesi, nayo Helesi yakamulela Eleyasa,
40 nayo Eleyasa yakamulela Sisimayi, nayo Sisimayi yakamulela Shalumu.
41 Nayo Shalumu yakamulela Elishama.

Wo lulelo wanji kanyhi wa Kalebi

42 Nayo Mesha mwana wa Kalebi, u mwana mulongozi kulelwa, yuyu yali yo sogwe wa Zifu, nayo Zifu yali yo sogwe wa Melesha, nayo Melesha yali yo sogwa wa Hebuloni.
43 Nayo yuyu Hebuloni, yali yena wana wane, Kola, Tepuwa, Lekemu na Shema.
44 Shema yali yo sogwe wa Lahamu, kanyhi yali yo kukuye wa Yolekeyamu. Nayo Lekemu u muwahawe wa Shema, yali yo sogwe wa Shamayi,
45 nayo shamayi yali yo sogwe wa Mawoni, nayo Mawoni yali yo sogwe wa Betizuli.

46 Nayo Kalebi kanyhi, yali yena mujitulwa wakwe, litagwa lyakwe Efa. Kalebi vikalelana na yuyo mujitulwa wakwe wana wanji wadatu, Halani, Moza na Gazezi. Nayo Halani yali yena mwana yumonga, litagwa lyakwe Gazezi.

47 (Hakawa ho yuli munhu yunji, litagwa Yahidayi. Munhu yuyu, yali yena wana mutandatu, nawo wo awano; Legemu, Yotamu, Geshani, Peleti, Efa, na Shayafu.)

48 Kanyhi Kalebi, yakawa na mujitulwa yunji kanyhi, litagwa lyakwe Mayaka, vikalelana nayo wana wejete, Sheba na Tilihana.
49 Hanyuma yayo kanyhi, yakatuga wana wejete, Shayafu mono yalizenga likaya lya Madimana. Nayo Shava yo yagazenga i makaya ga Makibena na Gibeya. Hamonga na zizo, Kalebi kanyhi yali yena mwana monga wa cicekulu, litagwa lyakwe Akisa.

50 Na wawano wono wetyatizye, nawo wali wana wa Kalebi nhavivyo.

Huli, mono yali yo mwana u mulongozi kulelwa kwa Kalebi, no mugolece wakwe litagwa lyakwe Efulata. Nayo Shobali u mwana wa Huli, yuyo yo yalizenga likaya lya Kiliyati Yeyalimu.
51 Nayo Salema u mwanagwe we kejete, yo yalizenga likaya lya Betelehemu. No mwanagwe we kadatu, mono yacemwaga Helefu, nayo yo yalizenga likaya lya Betigada.

52 Yuyu nayo Shobali mono yayizenga Kiliyati Yeyalimu, yali yo sogo we wanhu wa ku Halowe, yono yali yimemile nusu ye wanhu wa ku Menuhoti.
53 Hamonga ne wanhu we zimhuga azino zono zekalaga kuko ku Kiliyati Yeyalimu, nha cinji Wayitili, Waputi, Washumati, Wamishilayi. (Ne wanhu wono wekalaga mu makaya ga Zola na Eshitayoli, wose walawa mu zimhuga zizi.)

54 Nayo Salema mono yalizenga likaya lya Betelehemu, yali yo sogwe we wanhu wono wekalaga ku Netofati, na ku Atiloti-beti-yowabu, na wono wekalaga ku Zoli, wono wali wo wayagwe ne zimhuga zinyejete za ku Manahati.

55 (Ne zimhuga zizi zono zityatizye, zali zo mhuga ze wanghula wo kwandika mandiko, wali wekaye ku cikaya ca Yabezi, Watilati, ne Washimeyati, ne Wasukati. Wawo wo Wakeni, wono wetolanilaga ne Walekabi.)


1 Zinghomo 3

Wo lulelo lo mutemi Dawudi

1 Awa nawo wono watyatizye mu ndelwa yawo, wo wana wa Dawudi, wono walelelwa ku Hebuloni, lono yali yali kuko. Amunoni, u nyina yali Ahinowamu munya Yezeleli, Danyeli, u nyina yali Abijeli munya Kalimeli.

2 Abusalomu, u nyina yali Maka, u mwana we cicekulu wa

Talimayi u mutemi wa Geshuli.

Adoniya, u nyina yali Hajiti

3 Shefatiya, u nyina yali Abitali

Itileyamu, u nyina yali Egila.

4 Na wawa wose vyono wali mutandatu, walelelwa ku Hebuloni, mu myaka yi yilya mupunghati na nusu, yono Dawudi yali yatemile kuko ku Hebuloni. Dawudi yatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi madatu na midatu.
5 Ne wanagwe wenji walelelwa kuko.

Nayo Betisheba u mugolece wakwe, u mwana we cicekulu wa Amiyeli, yakamulelela wana wane, Shimeya, Shobabu, Natani na Solomona.

6 Kanyhi yali yena wana wanji yicenda, Ibiha, Elishuwa, Elipeleti,
7 Noga, Yefegi, Yefiya,
8 Elishama, Eliyada na Elifeleti.
9 Ninga hamonga ne wana wawa wose, Dawudi yali yena wana wanji kanyhi wono vyali vilelane ne wajitulwa wakwe. Yali yena sunga no mwana we cicekulu, litagwa lyakwe Tamali, nayo yali yo lumbu lyawo.

Wo lulelo lo mutemi Solomona

10 Awu nawo wo wityatye we liwalo lye wana wa Solomona: Solomona yakamulela Lehobowamu, nayo Lehobowamu yakamulela Abiya, nayo Abiya yakamulela Asa, nayo Asa yakamulela Yehoshafati,
11 Nayo Yehoshafati yakamulela Yeholamu, nayo Yeholamu yakamulela Ahaziya, nayo Ahaziya yakamulela Yowashi,
12 nayo Yowashi yakamulela Amaziya, nayo Amaziya yakamulela Uziya, nayo Uziya yakamulela Yotamu.
13 Nayo Yotamu yakamulela Ahazi, nayo Ahazi yakamulela Hezekiya, nayo Hezekiya yakamulela Manase.
14 Nayo Manase yakamulela Amoni, nayo Amoni yakamulela Yosiya.
15 Nayo Yosiya yali yena wana wane, Yohanani, Yehoyakimu, Zedekiya na Salumu.
16 Nayo Yehoyakimu yali yena wana wejete, Yehoyacini na Zedekiya.

Wo lulelo lo mutemi Yekoniya

17 Awa nawo wo wana wo mutemi Yekoniya, mono yasolwa mufungwa, yakategulwa ku Babiloni. Yekoniya yali yena wana mupunghati, Shelitiyeli,
18 Malicilamu, Pedaya, Shenaza, Yekamiya, Hoshama, na Nedabiya.
19 Yuyu Pedayiya, yali yena wana wejete, Zelubabeli, na Shimeyi. Nayo Zelubabeli, yali yena wana wejete, Meshulamu na Hananiya, hamonga no mwama monga we cicekulu mono yacemwaga Shelomiti.
20 Kanyhi, yali yena wana wanji wahano, Hashuba, Oheli, Belekiya, Hasadiya na Yushabu-hesedi.

21 Nayo Hananiya yali yena wana wejete, Pelatiya na Yeshaya. Yeshaya yakamulela Lefaya, nayo Lefaya yakamulela Alenani, nayo Alenani yakamulela Obadiya, nayo Obadiya yakamulela Shekaniya.
22 Yuyu nayo Shekaniya yali yena mwana monga, Shemaya, hamonga ne wezukulu wahano, Hatushi, Igali, Baliya, Neyali na Shafati.
23 Nayo Neyali, yali yena wana wadatu, Eliyowenayi, Hizikiya na Azilikamu.
24 Nayo Eliyowenayi, yali yena wana mupunghati, Hadaviya, Eliyashibu, Pelaya, Akubu, Yohanani, Delaya, na Anani.


1 Zinghomo 4

Wo lulelo lwa Yuda

1 Awano wo wana wanji wa Yuda: Pelezi, Hezeloni, Kalimi, Huli na Shobali.
2 Shobali yali yo sogwe wa Lewaya, nayo Lewaya yakamulela Yahati, nayo Yahati yakamulela Ahumayi na Lahadi. Wawa wo wali wo walezi we wanhu wono wekalaga ku Zola.

3 Huli, yali yo mwana u mulongozi kulelwa kuli Efulata mugolece wo sogwe Kalebi. Ne wanagwe wo walizenga likaya lya Betelehemu. Huli, yali yena wana wadatu, Etamu, Panuweli, na Ezeli. Etamu nayo yali yena wana wadatu, Yezeleli, Ishima, na Idibashi, hamonga no mwana monga we cicekulu litagwa lyakwe Hazeleliponi.
4 Na yuyu Penuweli, yo yalizenga likaya lya Gedoli. Nayo Ezeli, yakayizenga Husha.

5 Nayo Ashuli mono yayizenga Tekowa, yali yatolile wacekulu wejete, Hela na Nala.
6 Nala, yakamulelela wana wane, Ahuzamu, Hefa, Temeni, na Hayahashitali.
7 Ashuli na Hela, wakalelana wana wadatu, Zeleti, Izihali, na Etinani.

8 Nayo Kozi, yali yo sogwe wa Anubu, na Zobeba. Kanyhi yali yo sogwe we zimhuga zono zalawilila muli Ahaliheli u mwana wa Halumu.

9 Hali ho yuli munhu litagwa lyakwe Yabezi, munhu yuyu yali yo munhu we lilumbo yiwaha si nha we mhuga yakwe wose. Nayo u nyina, yo yamucemela litagwa lili lya Yebezi, vyono u nyina yatuga wusungu wuwaha muno mu nghunga ha wusawe.
10 Ninga Yebezi yakamulomba Mulungu we Waisilayeli yakutya nhavi, “Gwe go Mulungu, wundugutize, wumhele yisi ngazi. Weze wuli hamonga nane, wunghamise muli zimbi zose zono zosuma kumhela wusungu.” Nayo Mulungu yakamuhulika wulombe wakwe, yakamupela vinhu vyono yalombaga.

Wityatye we mhuga yinji kanyhi

11 Nayo Kalebi u muwahawe wa Shuha, yali yena mwana, litagwa lyakwe Mehili. Nayo Mehili yakamulela Eshitoni.
12 Nayo Eshitoni yali yena wana wadatu, Betalafa, Paseya, na Tehina. Nayo Tehina yo yalizenga likaya lya Nahashi. Ne wana wose we wanhu wawa, wekalaga ku Leka.

13 Nayo Kenazi yali yena wana wejete, Otiniyeli na Selaya. Otiniyeli nayo yali yena wana wejete, Hatali, na Mewonotayi.
14 Mewonotayi yali yo sogwe ya Ofila.

Selaya yali yo sogwe wa Yowabu, mono yalongola kuliwona lilolo lye wasesi we vinhu vinhu, i hanhu hono wanhu wose wali wanghula mu mulimo wo wusesi.

15 Nayo Kalebi u mwana wa Yefune, yali yena wana wadatu, Iluna, Ela, na Namu. Nayo Ela, yali yo sogwe wa Kenazi.

16 Yahaleleli yali yena wana wane, Zifu, Zifa, Tiliya, na Asaleli.

17 Nayo Ezila yali yena wana wane, Yeta, Meledi, Efa, Yaloni. Yuyu Meledi, yakamutola Bitiya u mwana we cicekulu wo mutemi wa Misili. Wakalelana mwana litagwa lyakwe Milyamu, hamonga ne wana wejete, Shamayi, na Ishiba. Nayo yuyu Ishiba yo yalizenga likaya lya Eshitemowa.
18 Nayo Meledi nhavivyo, yakatola mucekulu wo mulango wa Yuda, wakalelana wana wadatu, Yaledi mono yo yalizenga likaya lya Gedoli. Nayo Heba, yo yalizenga likaya lya Soko. Nayo Yekutiyeli yo yalizenga likaya lya Zanowa.

19 Hodiya, yakamutola u lumbulye wa Nahamu. Ne wana wawo, wo mwamulizo ya we mhuga ya Galimu, wono wekalaga mwi'kaya lya Keyila. Hamonga na we mhuga ya Maka, wono wekalaga mwi'kaya lya Eshitemowa.

20 Nayo Shimoni yali yena wana wane, Amoni, Lina, Benihanani, na Tiloni.

Ishi, yali yena wana wejete, Zoheti na Benizoheti.

Wo lulelo lwa Shela

21 Shela yali yo muyagwe ne wana wa Yuda. Ne wanagwe wawo hamonga na Eli, mono yazenga likaya lya Leka. Nayo Lada yo yalizenga likaya lya Malesha, lya we mhuga ye wahona myenda ye citani ciswanu, wono wekalaga mu cikaya ca Beti-ashibeya.
22 Hamonga na Yokimu, ne wanhu wono wekalaga mu cikaya ca Kozeba, hamonga na Yowashi na Selafu mono yamutola u mucekulu we Cimowabu, na hanyuma yayo, wakekala mu Betelehemu. (No moto wuwu wali wo moto we katali muno muno).
23 Nawo wakawa wo wawumbi, wakawa wekaye mu makaya ga Netayimu na Gedela akuno wakumwitumamila mutemi.

Wo lulelo lwa Simiyoni

24 Nayo Simiyoni yali yena wana wahano, Nemuyeli, Yamini, Yalibu, Zela, na Shawuli.
25 Nayo Shawuli yakawa yena mwana yumonga, litagwa lyakwe Shalumu, no mwizukuluwe Mibisamu, hamonga no mwizukulu civu wakwe, Mishima.
26 Na hanyuma yayo, mhuga yiyo yikajendelela sunga ko kuli Hamuyeli, na kuli Zakuli, sunga kuli Shimeyi.
27 Nayo Shimeyi yali yena wana yikumi na mutandatu we cilume, hamonga ne wana we cicekulu wali mutandatu. Ninga i wanduguze, wali wena wana wacefu du. Hamonga na zizo, wo mulango wa Simiyoni si wonjezeka yaye wawe wenji, nha vyono wali wenha wo mulango wa Yuda.

28 Sunga mu vipango vyo mutemi Dawudi, wawa wo mulango wa Simiyoni, wali wekaye mu makaya ga Belisheba, Molada, Hazali-shuwali,
29 Biliha, Ezemu, Toladi,
30 Betuyeli, Holima, Zikilaji,
31 Beti, Malikaboti, Hazali-susimu, Betibili Shalayimu.
32 Kanyhi na wanji wakekala mu makaya ganji mahano, Etamu, Ayini, Limoni, Tokeni, Ashani,
33 hamonga na mu vitumbi vyayo vyono vipilime ku lubavu lwa kutakama ye mwezi, sunga ko ku cikaya ca Balati. Na zizi zo zinghomo zono zandikwa ze zimhuga zawo, hamonga ne hanhu hono wekalaga.

34 Ne wanhu wawa wono wetyatizye, wali wo walongola we zimhuga zawo.

Meshoba na Yamuleki, na Yosha u mwana wa Amaziya, hamonga na Yoweli.

35 Yehu (u mwana wa Yoshibiya, u mwana wa Selaya, u mwana wa Asiyeli.)

36 Eliyowenayi, na Yakoba, na Yeshohaya na Asaya, na Adiyeli, na Yesimiyeli, na Benaya.
37 Ziza (u mwana wa Shifi, u mwana wa Aloni, we ndelo ya Yedaya, na Shimuli, na Shemaya.)

Na vyono zimhuga zawo wakacila kwinjipa muno,
38 na vyo wakacila kumema mu lubavu lwa kumwezi, ko muno ku Galali.
39 Wakawa wakuzidimila zingholo zawo kucilima ye lilolo, kono likaya lyali lizenjigwe.
40 Nawo wakatuga wudimilo wuswanu muno kuko, mu yisi ngazi, kanyhi yo wuhihi. Yisi yono yali yitulile muno kanyhi yipolile. I wanhu wono wali wekaye mumo katali, wali wowo lulelo lwa Hamu.

41 Na mu vilo vyo wutemi wa Hezekiya u mutemi wa Yuda, lulo i wanhu wawo wono matagwa gawo gandicigwe baho hacanyha, wabita kuko ku Gelali, wakagongomeza mahema, hamonga ne vibanda vye wanhu wawo wono wekalaga mumo. Wakawawinga wanhu wayo wose, i wene wakenjila wakekala mumo, sunga nalu. Hambi nha hali howuli wudimilo wuswanu we zingholo zawo.
42 Ninga wanji wo mulango wa Simiyoni waficile wanhu magana mahano, wawo wakabita ku lubavu lwa kucilima ku Edomu. Wakabitana hamonga ne walongola wawo, Pelatiya, Neyaliya, Lefaya, na Uziyeli, i wana wa Ishi.
43 Nawo wawo, wakawagana Waamaleki wono wali wasigaye mumo, wakawawulaga wose, i wene wakenjila wakekala mumo sunga nalu.


1 Zinghomo 5

Wo lulelo lwa Lubeni

1 Lubeni u mwana wa Yakobo, u mulongozi kulewa, yagona no mujitulwa wo sogwe. Na ku soko yiyo, siyapegwa lilumbo lyono lyali limunojeye u mwana mulongozi kulelwa. Ne lilumbo lilyo yakapegwa Yusufu.
2 (Ninga hamba nhavivyo, mumo mu mulango wa Yuda mo mwalawa mutemi).

3 Nayo Lubeni mwana, u mulongozi kulelwa kuli Yakobo, yali yena wana wane, Hanoki, Palu, Hezeloni, na Kalimi.

4 Nawo awano wali wo wana wa Yoweli, ku wulawa mu lulelo na lulelo, nha cinji Shemaya, Gogu, Shimeyi,
5 Mika, Leyaya, Baali, na Bela.
6 Nayo Tigilati Pilesa u mutemi wa Ashuli, yakamuteka Bela, mono yali yo mulongola we Walubeni, yakamuhamya.

7 Ne liwalo lye walongola wawo mu zimhuga zawo, wa mu zimhuga za Lubeni, wali wo awa: Yeyiyeli, Zekaliya,
8 na Bela, wa mu mhuga ya Yoweli. Ne mhuga yiyi, wekalaga ku Aloweli, na mu yisi yo wulawilila baho sunga ko ku lubavu lwa kusumuka ku Nebo, na Baali-meyoni.
9 Nawo wali wena matewa mawaha ge mitugo mu yisi ya Gileyadi. Na ku soko yiyo, wakayisola yisi yose ya Gileyadi, sunga ko ku lubavu lwa kucilima ku nyika yono yali yitambalaye sunga ko kwi'kolongo lya Efulata.

10 Na mu vilo vyo mutemi Sawuli, wawa wo mulango wa Lubeni wakawahoma Wahajili, wakawawulaga Wahajili wose mu wulugu, wakayisola yisi yawo ku lubavu lwa kucilima ya Gileyadi wakekala mumo.

Wo lulelo lwa Gadi

11 Nawo wo mulango wa Gadi wakekala ku lubavu lwa kusumuka ya we mhuga ya Lubeni, mu yisi ya Baashayi, ku lubavu lwa kucilima sunga ko ku Saleka.
12 Yoweli yali yo mulongola mulongozi wa mu mhuga yawo, na we kejete yali Shafamu. Nayo Yanayi na Shafati, wali wo walongola walongozi we zimhuga zinji ku Bashani.
13 I wanji nawo wono wali we mhuga yawo, wali wali mupunghati, nha cinji, Mikayeli, na Meshulamu, na Sheba, na Yolayi, na Yakani, na Ziya, na ya Ebeli.
14 Wawa wose wali wo wo lulelo lwa Abihayili, u mwana wa Huli. Ne walezi wawo ku wulawilila ku nyuma wali awa: Abihayili, u mwana wa Huli, u mwana wa Yaloya, u mwana wa Gileyadi, u mwana wa Mikayeli, u mwana wa Yeshishayi, Yahido, u mwana wa Buzi.
15 Nayo Ahi u mwana wa Abudiyeli, kanyhi mwizukulu wa Guni, yuyo yali yo mulongola we zimhuga zizo.
16 Nawo wekalaga mu yisi ya Bashani, na ya Gileyadi, mu vikaya vyayo, sunga na mu wudimilo wose wa mu mawimbi gose ge yisi ya Shaloni.
17 (Ne liwalo lilino lyawalwa mu vilo vya Yotamu u mutemi wa Yuda na Yelobowamu we kejete, u mutemi wa Isilayeli.)

I mapuga ge wasilikale ga we milango ya wono wekalaga ku lubavu lwa kucilima

18 Mu mulango wa Lubeni na wa Gadi, hamonga na we nusu ya wo mulango wa Manase, wono wali wekaye ku lubavu lwa kucilima, mwali mowali wasilikale wo wulugu waficile elufu makumi mane na yine, na magana mupunghati na makumi mutandatu, wono wali wejizwa hawaha mu witumamila ngula, ne mimaje, hamonga no wupinde.
19 Wakabita kuwahoma we zimbeyu ze Wahajili wono wekalaga ku Yetuli, na ku Nafishi, na ku Nodabu.
20 Akuno wali wamwitumbiye Mulungu, wakamulomba yawapele wutazi. Nayo Mulungu yakawahulika wulombe wawo, yakawapela wusume wuwaha kuli Wahajili, hamonga ne wekalani wayawo wono vyali vilunzize nawo.
21 Wakawasuma walugu wawo, wakateka ngamiya zificile elufu makumi mahano, ngholo elufu magana mejete na makumi mahano, ndogowe zificile elufu nyejete, wakawateka wanhu mu wulugu waficile wanhu elufu yigana limonga.
22 Wakawawulaga walugu wenji, hambi wulugu wuwo, nha wali wuli mu wuhile wo Mulungu. Wakajendelela kwikala mu yisi yiyo, sunga mwi'zuwa lyawo lyo wuhamywe wa mu yisi yawo.

I wanhu wa Manase ya kucilima

23 I wanhu wa Manase ya kucilima, wakekala mu yisi ya Bashani, sunga na mu zisi zo lubavu lwa kusumuka za Baali, na Helimoni na Senili, sunga ko kwi'tunda lya Helimoni. Ne liwalo lyawo likacila konjelezeka muno.
24 Ne walongola we zimhuga zawo wali awano: Efa, Ishi, Eliyeli, Yelemiya, Hadoviya, na Yahidiyeli. Na wawa wose wali wasilikale vitung'ati wo wulugu, kanyhi wali wo walongola wono wali wamanyice muno muno mu zimhuga zawo.

Zimhuga za ku lubavu lwa kucilima, wakuhamywa

25 Ninga wanhu wakawa si wehuwilwa kuli Mulungu we walezi wawo, wakamubedegala no kuhemba milungu ye wanhu wono wekalaga muno, wono Mulungu yali yakondya kuwawinga mu yisi.
26 Holeka nayo Mulungu yakamuleka Pulu u mutemi wa Ashuli, mono hanji yacemwaga Tigilati Pilesa yayisole yisi yawo. Yakawahamya wo mulango wa Lubeni, na wa Gadi, hamonga na we nusu ya wo mulango wa Wamanase wono wekalaga ku lubavu lwa kucilima, yakawategula wekale ku Hala, na Haboli, na Hala, sunga ko kwi'kolongo lya Gozani. Nawo wakekala kuko sunga nalu.


1 Zinghomo 6

Wityatye we zimhuga ze Wakohani wawaha

1 Lawi yali yena wana wadatu, Gelishomu, Kohati, na Melali.
2 Kohati yali yena wana wane, Amulamu, Izikali, Hebuloni, na Uziyeli.
3 Amulamu yali yena wana wejete, Aloni, na Musa, no mwana monga we cicekulu mono yacemwaga Milyamu.

Nayo Aloni yali yena wana wane, Nadabu, Abihu, Eliyazali, na Itamali.

4 Nawo i wana wa Eliyazali ku wulawa ndelo na ndelo, wali wali nhavi: Eliyazali yakamulela Finehasi, Finehasi yakamulela na Abishuwa, Abishuwa yakamulela Buki,
5 Buki yakamulela Uzi.
6 Nayo Uzi yakamulela Zelahiya, Zelahiya yakamulela Melayoti.
7 Nayo Melayoti yakamulela Amaliya, Amaliya yakamulela Ahitubu,
8 Ahitubu yakamulela Zadoki, Zadoki yakamulela Amaziya,
9 Amaziya yakamulela Azaliya, Azaliya yakamulela Yohana.
10 Nayo Yohana yakamulela Azaliya, (Azaliya mono yetumaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu yono mutemi Solomona yayizenga ku Yelusalemi.)
11 Azaliya yakamulela Amaliya, Amaliya yakamulela Ahitubu,
12 Ahitubu yakamulela Zadoki, Zadoki yakamulela Meshulamu,
13 Meshulamu yakamulela Hilikiya, Hilikiya yakamulela Azaliya,
14 Azaliya yakamulela Selaya, Selaya yakamulela Yehozadaki.
15 Nayo Yehozadaki, u mutemi Nebukadineza, yakamuhamya hamonga ne wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, wono Muwaha Mulungu yasaka mutemi Nebukadineza yawahamye.

Wanji wo lulelo lwa Lawi

16 Nayo Lawi, yali yena wana wadatu, Gelishoni, Kohati, na Melali.
17 Ne wana wawano wose, wali wena wana. Gelishoni, yakamulela Libuni, na Shimeyi.
18 Nayo Kohati yakamulela Amulamu, Iziha, Hebuloni, na Uziyeli.
19 Nayo Melali, yakamulela Mahili na Mushi.

20 Nawo i wana wa Gelishoni ku wulawa ndelo na ndelo, wali wali nhavi, Libuni yakamulela Yahati, Yahati yakamulela Zima.
21 Zima nayo yakamulela Yowa, Yowa yakamulela Ido, nayo Ido yakamulela Zela, Zela yakamulela Yeyatelayi.

22 Awa nawo wo wana wa Kohati, ku wulawa ndelo na ndelo, Abinadabu yakamulela Kola, nayo Kola yakamulela Asili,
23 Asili nayo yakamulela Elikana, nayo Elikana yakamulela Abiyasafu, nayo Abiyasafu yakamulela Asili,
24 Asili yakamulela Tahati, nayo Tahati yakamulela Uliyeli, nayo Uliyeli yakamulela Uziya, nayo Uziya yakamulela Shawuli.

25 Elikana nayo yali yena wana wejete, Amasayi na Ahimoti.
26 Na wawa wo wana wa Ahimoti ku wulawa mu ndelo zawo. Elikana yakamulela Zofayi, nayo Zofayi yakamulela Nahati,
27 nayo Nahati yakamulela Eliyabi, Eliyabi yakamulela Yelohamu, Yelohamu yakamulela Elikana.

28 Samwili yali yena wana wejete, Yoweli mwana u mulongozi kulelwa, na Abiya u mwanakwawe.

29 Awa nawo wo wana wa Melali ku wulawa mu zindelo zawo, Mahili yakamulela Libuni, nayo Libuni yakamulela Shimeyi, nayo Shimeyi yakamulela Uza,
30 nayo Uza yakamulela Shimeya, Shimeya yakamulela Hagiya, nayo Hagiya yakamulela Asaya.

I wembi wa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu

31 Awa wo wanhu wono mutemi Dawudi yawawika, wawe wo watonji wo wimbi, mu vipango vyo wuhembi, ku Yelusalemi; lono Lisanduku lye cilagane lyali lyakondya kutegulwa kuko.
32 Wene wawo wo welecelaga mu milimo ya mwi'Hema lyono Lyelile, sunga mu mapango lono mutemi Solomona yali yakondya kuyizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu.
33 Ne liwalo lye zimhuga za wawo wono wahagulwa ho webate mulimo wuwo, wali awano:

Mu mhuga ya Kohati, yali Hemani, u mutonji mulongozi wo wimbi, nayo yali yo mwana wa Yoweli. Ne ndelwa ye mhuga yakwe, yilawiliye kuli Yakobo nha vyono nhavi, Hemani mwana wa Yoweli, nayo Yoweli mwana wa Samwili.
34 Nayo Samwili mwana wa Elikana, nayo Elikana mwana wa Yelohamu, nayo Yelohamu mwana wa Eliyeli, nayo Eliyeli mwana wa Towa.
35 Nayo Towa mwana wa Zufu, nayo Zufu mwana wa Elikana, nayo Elikana mwana wa Mahati, nayo Mahati mwana wa Amasayi.
36 Amasayi mwana wa Elikana, nayo Elikana mwana wa Yoweli, nayo Yoweli mwana wa Azaliya, nayo Azaliya mwana wa Zefaniya.
37 Zefaniya mwana wa Tahati, nayo Tahati, mwana wa Asili, nayo Asili mwana wa Abiyasafu, nayo Abiyasafu mwana wa Kola.
38 Nayo Kola mwana wa Izihali, nayo Izihali mwana wa Kohati, nayo Kohati mwana wa Lawi, nayo Lawi mwana wa Yakobo.

39 Asafu yali yo mulongoza wimbi we cipuga ce kejete. Ne ndelwa ye mhuga yakwe, yilawiliye ku wulawa kuli Lawi nha vyono nhavi; Asafu mwana wa Belekiya, nayo Belekiya wa mwana Shimeya.
40 Nayo Shimeya mwana wa Mikayeli, nayo Mikayeli mwana wa Baseya, nayo Baseya mwana wa Malikiya.
41 Malikiya mwana wa Etini, nayo Etini mwana wa Zela, nayo Zela mwana wa Adaya,
42 Adaya mwana wa Etani, nayo Etani mwana wa Zima, nayo Zima mwana wa Shimeyi.
43 Shimeyi mwana wa Yahati, nayo Yahati mwana wa Gelishoni, nayo Gelishoni, mwana wa Lawi.

44 Nayo Etani we mhuga ya Melali, yakawa yo mutonji we cipuga ce kadatu ce wembi. Nayo i ndelwa ye mhuga yakwe yilawiliye ku wulawilila kuli Lawi, nha vyono nhavi: Etani mwana wa Kishi, nayo Kishi mwana wa Abudi, nayo Abudi mwana wa Maluki,
45 Nayo Maluki mwana wa Hashabiya, nayo Hashabiya mwana wa Amaziya, nayo Amaziya mwana wa Hilikiya,
46 nayo Hilikiya mwana wa Amuzi, nayo Amuzi mwana wa Bani, nayo Bani mwana wa Shemeli.
47 Nayo Shemeli mwana wa Mahili, nayo Mahili mwana wa Mushi, nayo Mushi mwana wa Melali, nayo Melali mwana wa Lawi.

48 Ninga i Walawi, wose wakapegwa milimo yose yo wulanjilizi we hanhu ho Wuhembelo.

Wo lulelo lwa Haluni

49 Haluni hamonga na wo lulelo lwakwe, mulimo wawo wali kulavya vilavywa vyo wuvumba, hamonga ne vilavywa vinyanywe mu cikumbicilo. Kanyhi wali wo mulimo wawo wo wuhembi wose we Hanhu Hono helile, helile muno. Ho wawanozeze Waisilayeli wilamule. Nawo wasumaga kuzituma zizino zose, wakutyatya mejizo nha vyono wejizwa na Musa, u mwitumami wo Mulungu.
50 No lulelo lwe wana wa Haluni wuli nhavi: Eliyazali yakamulela Finehasi, nayo Finehasi yakamulela Abishuwa,
51 nayo Abishuwa yakamulela Buki, nayo Buki yakamulela Uzi, nayo Uzi yakamulela Zelahiya,
52 Zelahiya yakamulela Melayoti, nayo Melayoti yakamulela Amaliya, nayo Amaliya yakamulela Ahitubu.
53 Nayo Ahitubu yakamulela Zadoki, nayo Zadoki yakamulela Ahimazi.

I hanhu hono wekalaga i Walawi

54 Ayi yo yisi yono wakapegwa i wana wa Haluni we mhuga ye Wakohati. Wakapegwa ligawo lilongozi lya mu yisi yono wapegwa Walawi.
55 Hamonga na Hebuloni mu yisi ya Yuda, hamonga no wudimilo we mitugo, wono wali wuyipilime.
56 Langa mawimbi hamonga ne vitumbi, vyono vyali vipilime likaya, avyo yakapegwa Kalebi u mwana wa Yefune.
57 Ne makaya gaga gono getyatizye go wapegwa i wana wa
58 Haluni: Hebuloni, likaya lyo wutizizo, na Yatili, hamonga ne vikaya vya Libuna, na Eshitemowa, na Holeni, na Debili,
59 na Ashani, na Bete-shemeshi, hamonga ne hanhu ho wudimilo we mitugo.
60 Na mu yisi yo mulango wa Benyamini, wakapegwa makaya, hamonga ne hanhu ho wudimilo we mitugo zawo, nha cinji Geba, na Alemeti, na Anatoti. Gakawa hamonga makaya yikumi na madatu, gono wapegwa ho wekale mumo zimhuga zawo wose.
61 Nago makaya yikumi gono gali gali mu yisi ya ku lubavu lwa kumwezi lye yisi ya Manase, wakapegwa we zimhuga ze Wakohati mhuga na mhuga, mu nzila yo kutowela kula.

62 Nawo we mhuga ya Gelishomu, wakapegwa makaya yikumi na madatu, ga mu yisi ya Isaka, na Asheli, na Nafutali, hamonga na ga ku lubavu lwe Manase ya kucilima, mu yisi ya Bashani.
63 Na mu nzila yiyo du, i makaya ganji nago yikumi na mejete ga mu yisi ya Lubeni, na Gadi, na Zabuloni, wakapegwa we mhuga ya Melali, mhuga na mhuga.
64 Na mu nzila yiyo yo wutowela kula, yo nzila yono i wanhu wa Isilayeli wetumamila ku wugawa makaya kuli Walawi, wekale mumo, hamonga no wudimo we mitugo zawo, wono wugapilime makaya gawo.
65 (Sunga ne makaya gono gali mu yisi ya Yuda, na Simiyoni, na Benyamini, gono gatambuligwe hacanyha, gose gakagawa mu nzila yo kutowa kula.)

66 Na wanji wa mu mhuga ye Wakohati, wapegwa makaya ga mu yisi ya Efulayimu, hamonga no wudimo we mitugo zawo.
67 Na Shekemu likaya lyo wutizizo, lyono lili mu matunda ga Efulayimu, nha cinji, likaya lya Gezeli,
68 na Yokimeyamu, na Bete-holoni,
69 na Ayiloni, hamonga na Gati-limoni.
70 Na wa mu yisi ya Manase ya kumwezi, wakapegwa makaya ga Aneli, na Bileyamu, hamonga ne hanhu ho wudimilo we mitugo zawo.

71 Nawo we mhuga ya Gelishoni, wakapegwa makaya agano; hamonga no wudimo we mitugo zawo.

Mu yisi ya Manase wa kucilima, wakapegwa Golani ya mu Bashani, hamonga na Ashitaloti.

72 Na mu yisi ya Isakali, wakapegwa Kadeshi, na Dabelati,
73 na Lamoti, hamonga na Anemu.

74 Na wa mu yisi ya Asheli, wakapegwa Mashali, na Abudoni,
75 na Hukoki, hamonga na Lehobu.

76 Na wa mu yisi ya Nafutali, wakapegwa Kadeshi, ya mu Galilaya, na Hamoni, hamonga na Kiliyatayimu.

77 Ne zimhuga zono zasigala ze mhuga ya Melali, wakapegwa makaya, hamonga no wudimo we mitugo zawo, mu yisi ya Zabuloni, na Limoni hamonga na Taboli.

78 Na wa mu yisi ya Lubeni, ku lubavu lwa kucilima ye likolongo lya Yoludani, ku nhumbi ya Yeliko, nako wakapegwa Bezeli mu yisi yono yinuce, hamonga na Yahiza,
79 na Kedemoti, hamonga na Mefati.

80 Na wa mu yisi ya Gadi, namo wakapegwa Lamoti ya mu Gileyadi, na Mahanayimu,
81 na Heshiboni, hamonga na Yazeli.


1 Zinghomo 7

Wo lulelo lwa Isakali

1 Isakali yali yena wana wane, Tola, Puwa, Yashubu, na Shimuloni.
2 Tola yali yena wana mutandatu Uzi, Lefaya, Yeliyeli, Yahimayi, Ibusamu, na Shemuyeli. Nawo wakawa wo walongola we zimhuga za we mhuga ya Tola, kanyhi wali wali vitung'ati mu milewo. Na mu vilo vyo mutemi Dawudi, wene wali waficile wanhu elufu makumi mejete na nyejete, na magana mutandatu.

3 Nayo Uzi, yali yena mwana yumonga, litagwa lyakwe Izilahiya. Nayo Izilahiya yali yena wana wane, Mikayeli, Obadiya, Yoweli, na Ishiya. Nawo wakawa wo walongola wa mu zimhuga zawo.
4 Nawo wali wena wacekulu wenji hamonga ne wana, holeka i wana wawo wono welavya mu mulimo wo wusilikale, wakawa waficile elufu makumi madatu na mutandatu.

5 Ne wanhu wose, wono wali wakwiliye mu mulimo wo wusilikale, wono walawa mu zimhuga za wo mulango wa Isakali, wali waficile wanhu elufu makumi munana na mupunghati liwalo lyawo.

Wo lulelo lwa Benyamini na Dani

6 Benyamini yali yena wana wadatu, Bela Beka, na Yediyayeli.

Ne wana wawo wono wali wakwiliye mu mulimo wo wusilikale, wali waficile wanhu elufu makumi mejete na nyejete, na makumi madatu na wane, liwalo lyawo, mu wulawa mu zimhuga zawo zose.

Wo lulelo lwa Nafutali

Wo lulelo lwa Manase

16 Nayo Maaka u mugolece wa Macili, yakamulelela Macili wana wejete, wono wacemwaga Peleshi, na Sheleshi. Yuyo Peleshi, yali yena wana wejete, Ulamu, na Lakemu.
17 Nayo yuyo Ulamu, yali yena mwana yumonga, litagwa lyakwe Bedani. Na wawa wo wo lulelo lwa Gileyadi u mwana wa Macili, u mwizukulu wa Manase.

18 Nayo Hamoleketi u lumbulye wa Gileyadi, yali yena wana wadatu, Ishodi, Abiyezeli, na Mahila.
19 (Shemida nayo yali yena wana wane, Ahiyani, Shekemu, Likihi, na Aniyamu.)

Wo lulelo lwa Efulayimu

24 Efulayimu yali yena mwana yumonga wa cicekulu, litagwa lyakwe Shela. Nayo yuyu Shela yo yazenga makaya ga Bete-holoni ya hasi na ya kucanyha, hamonga na Uzeni Shela.

25 Efulayimu kanyhi, yali yena mwana yumonga, litagwa lyakwe Lefa, ne wana wa Lefa wali awa: Lefa, yakamulela Leshefu, nayo Leshefu yakamulela Tela, nayo Tela yakamulela Tahani, nayo Tahani yakamulela Ladani,
26 nayo Ladani yakamulela Amihudi, nayo Amihudi yakamulela Elishama,
27 nayo Elishama yakamulela Nuni, nayo Nuni yakamulela Yoshuwa.

28 Ne yisi yono wayisola wakekala mumo, yali yisi yono yali yili hamonga na Beteli, ne vikaya vyono vyali vipilime, sunga ko ku lubavu lwa kucilima ku Nala, sunga ko kulubavu lwa kumwezi ku Gezeli hamonga ne vikaya vyono vyali vipilime. Ne makaya ga Shekemu na Aya, ne vikaya vyono vyali vipilime.

29 Nawo i wana wa Manase, wakebata no kugatema makaya ga Bete-shani, Tanaki, Megido, na Doli, hamonga ne vikaya vyono vyali vipilime.

Ne hanhu baha hose, ho hanhu hono yekalaga Yusufu u mwana wa Yakobo.

Wo lulelo lwa Asheli

30 Awa nawo wo wana wa Asheli: Mwene Asheli yali yena wana wane, Imuna, Ishiva, Ishivi, na Beliya, hamonga no mwana monga wa cicekulu, litagwa lyakwe Sela.

31 Nayo Beliya, yali yena wana wejete, Hebeli na Milikiyeli. (Yuyo Milikiyeli yo yazenga likaya lya Bilizayiti.)

32 Nayo Hebeli, yali yena wana wadatu, Yafuleti, Shomeli, na Hatamu, na mwana monga wa cicekulu, litagwa lyakwe Shuwa.

33 Yafuleti, yali yena wana wadatu, Pasaki, Bimuha, na Ashivati.

34 Nayo Shomeli, u mwanakwawe yali yena wana wadatu, Lohiga, Yehuba, na Alamu.

35 Nayo Hotamu, u mwanakwawe kanyhi, yali yena wana wane, Zofa, Imuna, Sheleshi, na Amali.

36 I wana wa Sofa wali awa, Suwa, Halinefe, Shuwali, Beli, Imula.
37 Bezeli, Hodu, Shama, Shilisha, Itilani, na Bela.

38 Nawo i wana wa Yefeli, wali awa, Yefune, Pisipa, na Ala.
39 Ne wana wa Ula nawo wali awa, Ala, Haniyeli, na Liziya.

40 Ne wana wawo wose, wali wana wa Asheli. Wali wo walongola we zimhuga zawo, kanyhi wali wo wanhu wono wali citung'ati mu wulugu, wawaha mu zindeco ze wasogo zawo kanyhi wali vilungo. I wo lulelo lwa Asheli, wali waficile wanhu elufu makumi mejete na mutandatu, wono wali wakwiliye mu mulimo wo wusilikale wo wulugu.

1 Zinghomo 9

I wanhu wono wawuya ku wulawa ku wuhamywe

1 Ne wanhu wose wa Isilayeli wakandikwa liwalo lyawo, mu nghango ye zimhuga zawo vyono zali zinha. No lwandiko lulo lukandikwa mu citabu ce Zinghomo ce watemi wa Isilayeli.

Nawo wanhu wa Yuda, wakahamywa nhavivyo, wakategulwa ku Babiloni, ku soko yo wubi wawo.
2 Ne wanhu wono walongola kuwuya mu yisi yawo mu makaya gawo, wali Waisilayeli, i wanyawusi, hamonga ne wakohani ne Walawi, ne wetumami wono wetumamaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
3 Ne wanhu wo mulango wa Yuda na wa Benyamini, na wa Efulayimu, hamonga na wa Manase, awa wakabita wakekala ku Yelusalemi.

4 Zali zificile mhuga za Yuda magana mutandatu na makumi yicenda, wono wali wekaye mu Yelusalemi. Nayo yali yili nhavi: Yuda yakamulela Pelezi, nayo Pelezi, yakamulela Bani, nayo Bani yakamulela Imuli, nayo Imuli yakamulela Omuli, nayo Omuli yakamulela Amihudi, nayo Amihudi yakamulela Utayi.
5 Iwana wa Shela u mwana wa Yuda, yali yena mwana litagwa lyakwe Asaya, yuyu Asaya, yakawa yo mulongola wa mu mhuga yakwe.
6 Nayo Zela u mwana wa Yuda, yakawa yena mwana, litagwa lyakwe Yeyuweli, nayo yakawa yo mulongola wawo.

7 Awa nawo: Salu, u mwana wa Meshulamu, mono yali mwana wa Hodaviya u mwana wa Hasenuwa,
8 na Ibineya u mwana wa Yeholamu, na Ela u mwana wa Uzi, kanyhi mwizukulu wa Makili. Na Meshulamu u mwana wa Shefatiya, mono yali mwana wa Lewuyeli u mwana wa Ibiniya. Wawa wose, wali wo wo mulango wa Benyamini, wono wekala mu Yelusalemi.

9 Nawo hamonga mhuga yose, wali waficile wanhu magana yicenda na makumi mahano na mutandatu wo mulango wuwo wa Benyamini, wono wekala kuko ku Yelusalemi. Na wawo walume wose wono matagwa gawo gatambuligwe, wose wali walongola wa mu zimhuga zawo.

I Wakohani wono wali wekaye mu Yelusalemi

10 Awa nawo wo wakohani wono wekalaga mu Yelusalemi: Yedaya, Yehoyalibu, na Yakini.
11 Azaliya u mwana wa Hilikiya, (mono yali yo mulongola mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.) Mono walezi wakwe wali Meshulamu, Zadoki, Melayoti, na Ahitubu.
12 Adaya u mwana wa Yelohamu, mono ne walezi wakwe wali Pashuli, na Malikiya. Masayi u mwana wa Adiyeli mono ne walezi wakwe wali Yahizela, na Meshulamu, na Meshilemiti, na Imeli.

13 Ne wakohani wono wali wo walongola wa mu zimhuga zawo, wali waficile wakohani, elufu monga na magana mupunghati na makumi mutandatu, liwalo lyawo. Nawo wali wanghula we milimo yose yono yetumwaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.

I Walawi wono wali wekaye mu Yelusalemi

14 Awa nawo wo Walawi wono wekalaga mu Yelusalemi.

Shemaya, u mwana wa Hashubu, mono ne walezi wakwe wali, Azilikamu na Hashabiya we mhuga ye Wamelali.

15 Bakibakali, Heleshi, na Galali, Mataniya u mwana wa Mika, mono ne walezi wakwe wali, Zikili, na Asafu.
16 Obadiya, u mwana wa Shemaya, mono ne walezi wakwe wali, Galali, na Yedutuni. Belekiya, u mwana wa Asa, kanyhi mwizukulu wa Elikana, mono yekalaga mu yisi yono yali yisi ya mwi'kaya lya Netofa.

I walindilizi we Nyumba yo Mulungu, wono wekalaga mu Yelusalemi

17 Awa wono wali walindilizi we Nyumba yo Mulungu, wono wekalaga mu Yelusalemi. Shalumu, Akubu, Talimoni, hamonga na Ahimani. Nayo Shalumu yali yo mulongola wawo.
18 Ninga mu cipango cico lono cali cikali, i wanhu we mhuga yawo wose, walindilizaga ku mulango wo lubavu lwa kucilima wono yenjililaga mutemi. Ha mwamulizo, wene wali wo walindilizi we milango ye zinghambi ze Walawi.

19 Ninga Shalumu u mwana wa Kole, kanyhi mwizukulu wa Ebiyasafu, hamonga ne wayagwe we mhuga ya Kola, wali wo walanjilizi wo wulindilizi wo mulango we Lihema lyono Lyelile, nha vyono walezi wawo wetumaga lono wali walanjilizi we nghambi yo Muwaha Mulungu.
20 Nayo Finehasi u mwana wa Eleyezali, Muwaha Mulungu yali yali hamonga nayo! Hambi nha yo yabitaga kuwalingula mu madaha ganji.

21 Na Zekaliya nayo, u mwana wa Meshelemiya, yali mulindilizi wa ha mulango we Lihema lyono Lyelile.

22 Wanhu magana mejete ne kumi limonga na wejete, wo wahagulwa wawe wo walindilizi mu milango. Nawo wakawalwa mu nghango ye vitumbi vyawo vyono wekalaga. U mutemi Dawudi hamonga no muwizi Samwili, wo wawawika walezi wawo wawe walanjilizi wo mulimo wuwu.
23 Holeka wawo walezi, hamonga ne wana wawo, wakajendelela kuwa walindilizi we milango ye Nyumba yo Mulungu.
24 Ne milango yali yipilime mbavu zose, kusumuka, na kutakama, kucilima na kumwezi. Ne milango yose yali yina mulanjilizi mulongola.
25 Ne walindilizi wawa, nawo watazilizwaga ne wandugu zawo, wono wekalaga mu vitumbi, wono wali wanojeye welecele mu mulimo wuwo wo wulindilizi, mwidaha lya vilo mupunghati, mupunghati.
26 Na muli wawo walanjilizi walongola, i walongola wane, wali Walawi, kanyhi wali wo wena nghani ye ndumilizo. Kanyhi wali wo walanjilizi we vinyumba vyose vye Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose vyono vyali viwicigwe mumo.
27 Nawo wekalaga du behi ne Nyumba yo Mulungu, vyono wali wo mulimo wawo kuyilindiliza, no kuyidigula milango yayo hakali mitondo nhondo zose.

I Walawi wanji kanyhi

28 I Walawi wanji kanyhi, wali wena mulimo wo wulindilizi we viya vyono vyetumamilwaga mu wuhembi. Waviwalaga mu mapango gose, lono vikusolwa no kwitumamilwa, na lono vikugomolwa mu wuwiko wayo lono vyakondya kwitumamilwa.
29 Na wanji nawo kanyhi wakawa wena mulimo wo wulanjilizi we viya vyose vya mugati, vye Hanhu Hono Helile, hamonga no wusaje, divayi, mafuta, wuvumba, hamonga na vye mhunjile swanu.
30 Langa mulimo wo kuhanzahanza vye mhunjile swanu, wali mulimo we wakohani.

31 Hakawa ho yuli mulawi yunji, litagwa lyakwe Matitiya, u mwana mulongozi kulewa wa Shalumu, we mhuga ya Kola. Yuyu mwene yali yo mulanjilizi wo wuteleci we mikate ye vilavywa.
32 Nawo we mhuga ya Kola, wali wo walanjilizi wo kuteleka mikate yono yelile ku so Nyumba yo Mulungu mu Sabato zose.

33 Ne zimhuga zinji ze Walawi, wakawa wo walanjilizi we wembi wa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Ne walongola we zimhuga zizino, wanji wekalaga mu vinyumba vinji vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Nawo wakalekwa wetume mulimo wuwo du. Hambi nha wetumaga misi ne cilo.

34 Na wawo wose wakawa wo walongola wa mu zimhuga ze Walawi, mu nghango ye zimhuga zawo mu walezi wawo. Nawo wakawa walongola, wekalaga ku Yelusalemi.

I wakuku zo munhu hamonga na wo lulelo lo mutemi Sawuli

(1Zin 8:29-38)

35 Yayiyeli yo yalizenga likaya lya Gibeyoni, nayo yakekala mumo. U mugolece wakwe litagwa lyakwe yali Maaka.
36 No mwanagwe mulongozi kulelwa, litagwa lyakwe yali Abudoni, ne wanagwe wanji wali awa, Zuli, Kishi, Baali, Neli, Nadabu,
37 Gedoli, Ahiyo, na Zekaliya, hamonga na Mikiloti,
38 u sogwe wa Shimeya. I wana wawo, wekalaga ku Yelusalemi behi behi ne zimhuga ze wandugu zawo.

39 Neli yakamulela Kishi, nayo Kishi yakamulela Sawuli, nayo Sawuli yakalela wana wane, Yonazani, Malikishuwa, Abinadabu, na Eshibali.
40 Yonazani yakamulela Melibali, nayo Melibali yakamulela Mika.
41 Nayo Mika yali yena wana wane, Pitoni, Meleki, Talesi, na Ahazi.
42 Nayo Ahazi yakamulela Yala, nayo Yala yakalela wana wadatu, Alemeti, Azimaveti, na Zimuli. Nayo Zimuli yakamulela Moza,
43 Nayo Moza yakamulela Bineya, nayo Bineya yakamulela Lefaya, nayo Lefaya yakamulela Eleyasa, nayo Eleyasa yakamulela Aseli.

44 Aseli yali yena wana wali mutandatu, wowa, Azilikamu Bocelu, Ishimayeli, Sheyaliya, Obadiya, na Hanani.


1 Zinghomo 10

Wufwe wo mutemi Sawuli

(1Sa 31:1-13)

1 I wafilisiti wakawa kwihoma ne Waisilayeli, he 'tunda lya Gibowa. Ne Waisilayeli wenji wakawulagwa, na wono wasigala, wakatiza wose hamonga no mutemi Sawuli ne wanagwe wadatu.
2 Ninga Wafilisiti wakawatyatilila sunga wakawebata, wakawawulaga i wana wa Sawuli wadatu, Yonazani, Abinadabu, na Milikishuwa.
3 Na lulo wulugu wukawa wukali muno muli Sawuli. I wagumi we zisonga, wakamuguma Sawuli, wakamupela nghomele 'mbi muno.
4 Nayo yali yeza kuwona vyono yakondya kutamiswa muno, hobaho yakawa kumucema muzelelo mono yali yadinghuye vitwalo vyakwe, yakamutya, “Wusomole mumaje wako mu njasulu, wumbulaje, walece kuza wawo wanhu i Wafilisiti wono si wamumanyile Mulungu wanyidawalile.” Ninga yu yulya muzelelo yakogopa muno kuyituma nghani yiyi. Hodu Sawuli yakawusomola mumaje wakwe mu njasulu, yakawugwila, yakehomeleza mweneco.
5 Nayo muzelelo yuyo yali yeza kuwona vyono Sawuli yakondya kufwa, hobaho nayo yakawa kwigumila, yakawugwila mumaje wakwe yakawa nayo kwiwulaga.
6 Hodu, Sawuli hamonga ne wanagwe wadatu, wakawa wose wakondya kufwa. Na hakawa hasina kanyhi hamba monga wa muli wanagwe, mono yatema.
7 Nawo Waisilayeli wono wekalaga mwilolo lya Yezeleli, wali weza kuhulika nha vyono, Wasilikale wawo watiza hali Wafilisiti, na vyono Sawuli hamonga ne wanagwe wose wakondya kufwa, hobaho nawo, wakawa kugaleka makaya gawo wakatiza. Nawo Wafilisiti wakaza wakagasola.

8 Na mwi'zuwa lye kejete, Wafilisiti wakabita wakapumhe vinhu vya wono wafwiye, na kuko wakawuwona muwili wa Sawuli hamonga ne miwili ye wanagwe, yigwiye hasi he'tunda lya Gibowa.
9 Hobaho wakawudumula mutwe wa Sawuli, wakavisola ne vitwalo vyakwe. Wakatuma wanhu wavidinghule wavipilimye mu yisi yose ye Wafilisiti, no kuwalonjela wanhu wawo, hamonga ne milungu yawo, ze nghani yiyo swanu.
10 Wakavitumbika vitwalo vyakwe mu nyumba monga ya mu zinyumba zo wuhembelo we milungu yawo. No mutwe wakwe nawo, wakawutumbika mu nyumba yo wuhembelo yo mulungu wawo Dagoni.
11 Nawo wanhu wa ku Yabeshi ya Gileyadi, wali weza kuzihulika zono Wafilisiti wetumile kuli Sawuli,
12 hobaho i wanhu we vilungo, wakabita wakawusola muwili wa Sawuli hamonga ne miwili ye wanagwe wadatu, wakayitegula ku Yabeshi. Wakayizika kuko hasi yo mubici wo munghola. Wakaleka kulya vilo mupunghati.

13 Nayo Sawuli yafwa, vyono si yali mwihuwilwa kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Si yasaka kumuhulika Muwaha Mulungu, hamba kugebata malajilizo gakwe. Langa mwene yali yendile kwilaguliza kuli wono wena macisi, no kusaka wutonji kuli myuhe ye wanhu wono wafwa,
14 hamba si yendaga kuwuza kuli Muwaha Mulungu yaye. Na ku soko yiyo, Muwaha Mulungu yakamuwulaga, yakawa kumupela wutemi Dawudi, mwana wa Yese.


1 Zinghomo 11

Dawudi yawa mutemi wa Isilayeli na Yuda

(2Sa 5:1-10)

1 Hobaho Waisilayeli wose wakamwikongholela Dawudi kuko ku Hebuloni, wakamutya, “Lanje ta, ase cili muwili wako ne sakami yako.
2 Ikatali, na lono Sawuli yali yakali yacitemile, agwe yo gwegwe mono wawalongoza Waisilayeli ku wulugu. No Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako, yakwihuwiza nha vyono yo gwegwe mono kowatonga wanhu, kanyhi kowatema.”
3 Hobaho i walongola wose we Waisilayeli, wakamuziza mutemi Dawudi kuko ku Hebuloni. Nayo Dawudi yakanoza cilagane nawo. Nawo wakamwitila mafuta, wakamunoza yawe mutemi we Waisilayeli, zikawa nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamwihuwiziza muli Samwili.

4 Mutemi Dawudi hamonga ne Waisilayeli wose, wakabita kulihoma likaya lya Yelusalemi. Ninga lulo lyali limanyice mwi'tagwa lya Yebusi. Sunga ne Wayebusi wanyawusi wali wakali wali muno.
5 Ninga Wayebusi wakamulonjela Dawudi nha vyono, si kosuma kwinjila mwi'kaya. Ninga Dawudi yakalisola ligomawakali lyawo lya ku Sayuni, nalyo likamanyika nha vyono “Likaya lya Dawudi.”
6 Dawudi yakatya, “U munhu wo wose mono kowa mulongozi kuwahoma Wayebusi, yuyo yo kowa muwaha we lipuga lye wasilikale wo wulugu!” Ale, Yowabu munghalo nyina mono yacemwaga Zeluyiya, yo yakawa mulongozi kwinjila mu milewo, nayo yakawa yo muwaha.
7 Alu vyono Dawudi yakabita kwikala mwi'gomawakali, holeka likacemwa “Likaya lya Dawudi.”
8 Yakalizenga kanyhi likaya mbavu zose. Yakanziza kono kwamemywa muhangha, ku lubavu lwa kucilima ye litunda. Nayo Yowabu yakanozeleza likaya lyono lyasigala.
9 Dawudi yakajendelela kuwa yali vilungo muno, hambi u Muwaha Mulungu, nha yali yali hamonga nayo.

I wasilikale we lilumbo wa Dawudi

(2Sa 23:8-39)

10 I wasilikale we lilumbo kanyhi vitung'ati wa Dawudi, hamonga ne Waisilayeli wono wali wasigaye, wose wakamutaza Dawudi ho wamutemye, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yehuwiza. Nawo wo wawukangaza no kuwupela vilungo wutemi wakwe.

11 Ne wasilikale we lilumbo kanyhi vitung'ati, wono Dawudi yali yena nawo, wo awa: U mulongozi yali Yashobeyamu we mhuga ye Wahacimoni, u mulongola we “Wadatu.” Yuyu yakawahoma no mugoha wakwe, wanhu waficile magana madatu, yakawawulaga nghanghulo monga mu wulugu.
12 Nayo mono yakutyatya muli wawo we citung'ati wadatu, yali Eleyezali u mwana wa Dodo, we mhuga ye Waahohi.
13 Yuyo nayo yali yali hamonga na Dawudi, lono wawahomaga Wafilisiti ku wulugu wa ku Pasi-damimu, mwi'lolo lyono limemile shayili. Na lono Waisilayeli, watizaga ha mwande ye Wafilisiti,
14 ninga mwene Dawudi na Eleyezali hamonga ne wanhu wakwe, wakema haciligati ye lilolo lilyo lya shayili, yakawahomanga Wafilisiti. No Muwaha Mulungu yakamupela wusume wuwaha muno.

15 Na mwi'zuwa linji, i walongola wadatu, wa muli walongola we makumi madatu, wakamubitila Dawudi behi ne mhango ye'bwe ya Adulamu, kono yali yekaye. Na mwi'pango lilyo, liwumbi lye Wafilisiti wali wapanjile nghambi, mwi'lolo lya Lefayimu.
16 Nayo Dawudi mu madaha gago, yali yali mwi'gomawakali mwi'tunda, i cipuga ce Wafilisiti nawo, wali wakondya kuyisola Betelehemu.
17 Baho Dawudi yakatuga cisukwa muno, yakatya, “Ciba munhu yali yamhele ning'we malenga ga mu cisima ca ku Betelehemu, cono cili behi no mulango, hambi nha nagakalucila muno!”
18 Baho wa walya wasilikale wakwe wadatu vitung'ati, wakenyeleleka mwi'wumbi lye Wafilisiti, wakanega malenga wulawa mu cisima cico ca Betelehemu, cono cali cili behi no mulango, wakamuletela Dawudi. Ninga Dawudi si yenda kugang'wa yaye, langa mwene yakagajida gawe cilavywa kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
19 Yakatya, “Ane hamba si nogang'wela lukulu malenga gaga! Hono nigang'we, nowa nha nghung'wa sakami ye wanhu wawa wono wesuzize wumi wawo. Hambi nha wesuza wumi wawo, holeka wasuma kugaleta!” Na ku soko yiyo, holeka hamba si yenda kugang'wa yaye. Azi zo wetuma wawalya wasilikale vitung'ati wadatu.

20 Nayo Abishayi u nduguye wa Yowabu, yali yo mulongola wa wawo vitung'ati we “Makumi madatu.” Yali yo munhu mono yawahomanga no mugoha wakwe, wanhu waficile magana madatu, yakawawulaga, yaketujila lilumbo lyo kuwa hamonga na wa walya i vitung'ati “Wadatu.”
21 Yali yo we lilumbo liwaha muno muli wawo we “Makumi madatu.” Nayo yakawa yo mulongola wawo. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, lilumbo lyakwe si lyali linha na lya awalya citung'ati “Wadatu.”

23 Kanyhi yamuwulaga Mumisili mono yali munhu mono yena yiwili yiwaha muno, kanyhi mutali, yena wutali wa behi na mita nyejete, mono yali yebate mugoha wono wali wina yidete yiwaha, kotya mucici wa kaya. Ninga mwene Benaya, yakamubitila yakamutowa ne nhumbi, yakawusukula mugoha mu muwoko wo Mumisili, yakamuhoma no mugoha wuwo, yakamuwulaga.
24 Na gago gali go metumo mawaha ge vilungo, gono yetuma Benaya mwana wa Yehoyada, yakawa ne lilumbo muli awalya vitung'ati “Wadatu.”
25 Nayo yakatuga lilumbo yiwaha muli walya we “Makumi madatu.” Ninga hamba nhavivyo, lilumbo lyakwe si lyali linha lya awalya vitung'ati “Wadatu.” Nayo Dawudi yakamuwika Benaya yawe yo mulanjilizi we walindilizi wakwe.

1 Zinghomo 12

Iwatyatyi walongozi wa Dawudi, wo mulango wa Benyamini

1 Ale, mu madaha gago Dawudi yali yehundice ku Zikilagi, yamutizaga mutemi Sawuli. Ne wasilikale wenji we vilungo, kanyhi wono wali vitung'ati mu wulugu,
2 i wanhu wo mulango wa Benyamini, wono wali wali ku lubavu lwa Sawuli, wono wali wezoweleze muno kuguma songa no kugumila nhago, kwe miwoko yawo yose, muwoko wo kulume hamba wo kucekulu wakamutyatya kuko.
3 Nawo wakawa hasi yo wulanjilizi wa Ahiyezeli na Yowashi, i wana wa Shemaya munya Gibeya. Ne wasilikale wawo, wali wo awa:

Yeziyeli na Peleti i wana wa Azimaveti,

Belaka na Yehu wanya Anatoti.

4 Ishimaya munya Gibeyoni, u musilikale citung'ati,

muli awalya we makumi madatu kanyhi yali yo mulongola

wawo, hamonga na Yelemiya na Yahaziyeli na Yohanani,

na Yozabadi wanya Gedela.

5 Eluzayi, na Yelimoti, na Beyaliya,

Shemaliya na Shefatiya, wanya Halifu.

6 Elikana na Ishiya, na Azaleli, na Yoyezeli,

na Yoshobeyamu we mhuga ya Kola.

7 Yoyela na Zebadiya, i wana

wa Yelohamu munya Gedoli.

I watyatyi wa Dawudi wo mulango wa Gadi

8 Aga hano, go matagwa ge vitung'ati. I wasilikale wo mulango wa Gadi wono wali wazoweye muno mulimo wo wusilikale, wono wakabita kwihanza ne mapuga ge wasilikale wa Dawudi, lono yali yali mu nyika. Nawo wawa, wakawa wanghula muno mu wicita ne zingula, hamonga no wuguma migoha, wena meso ga kogofya genha ga simba, kanyhi wapelupelu ku lumangho, wehwanile nzimba za mu matunda.
9 Nawo wali wawicigwe mu wityatye wo wulongola wawo: Yezeli yali yo muwaha mulongola mono yamulecela Obadiya, Obadiya yakamulecela Eliyabi.
10 Nayo Eliyabi yakamulecela Mishimana, nayo Mishimana yakamulecela Yelemiya, nayo Yelemiya yakamulecela Atayi.
11 Nayo Atayi yakamulecela Eliyeli.
12 Nayo Eliyeli yakamulecela Yohana, nayo Yohana yakamulecela Elizabadi.
13 Nayo Elizabadi yakamulecela Yelemiya, nayo Yelemiya yakamulecela Mekebanayi.

14 Na wanji wa muli wanhu wawa wo mulango wa Gadi, wali wo walongola wawaha we wanhu elufu monga monga, na wanji wa wanhu yigana limonga limonga.
15 Na mu mwezi mulongozi wa mu mwaka, mu mapango gono likolongo lya Yoludani lyamemaga malenga gayo ko ku nhumbi wene walowokaga likolongo, wawa kuweyagaza wanhu wono wekalaga mu malolo, mu mbavu zose, kucilima na kumwezi ye likolongo.

I watyatyi we milango ya Benyamini na Yuda

16 Mwizuwa linji, lipuga lye wanhu we milango ya Benyamini na Yuda, wakabita kwi'gomawakali, kono yekalaga Dawudi.
17 Nayo Dawudi yakawahinjiliza, ninga yakawatya, “Anye! Hono muwe mukuza no mulamu, ho munhazilize, ale muze! Ninga hono muwe mukuza ho mungaluce kuli walugu wangu, hamba yiwe ane sina wuhile wo kumutamisenyi 'nye, ninga u Mulungu we walezi wetu, yo kozimanya zizo, kanyhi komulipicizenyi.”

18 U muhe wo Mulungu yakamuziza Abishayi vyono nayo yo mulongola we wanhu wawo “Makumi madatu.” Yakalonga yazozize, yakatya,

“Gwe Dawudi go mwana wa Yese,

ase cili wako!

Mulamu weze kuli'gwe,

na kuli wose wono wakukutaziliza!

U Mulungu yali ku lubavu lwako.”

Hodu, nayo Dawudi nhondo ho yakawa kuwabocela, yakawa kuwawika wawe wawaha we lipuga lyakwe lye wasilikale.

I watyatyi wo mulango wa Manase

19 Wasilikale wanji wo mulango wa Manase, nawo wakelunza na Dawudi, lono yabitaga hamonga ne Wafilisiti kwihoma na Sawuli. Ninga i mbeka, yali yasina wuhile wo kuwataziliza i Wafilisiti, hambi nha yogopa vyono lunji i watemi, wawo wokwihwesa nha vyono mwene Dawudi, yakuhila yawagwizize kwa Sawuli u muwaha wakwe we katali. Holeka wakamugomola Dawudi ku Zikilagi.
20 Nawo awa wo wasilikale wo mulango wa Manase, wono wamubitila Dawudi no kwilunza nayo, lono yali yakondya kuwuya ku Zikilagi. Adina, Yozabadi, Yediyayeli, Mikayeli, Yozabadi, Elihu, hamonga na Ziletayi. Na mumo mu Wamanase, wali wakondya kwiwikaniza vipuga vipuga, vya wanhu elufu monga monga.
21 Nawo wakamwitumamila Dawudi mu vipuga vyakwe vye wasilikale, hambi wose nha wali wasilikale vitung'ati. Na hanyuma yayo, wakawa wetumami mwi'puga lye wasilikale we Waisilayeli.
22 Hambi nha wazaga izuwa ne zuwa, wanhu wono wamutazaga Dawudi, sunga ne wasilikale wakwe wakenjipa muno muno.

I liwalo lye wasilikale wa Dawudi

23 Nayo Dawudi lono yali yali ku Hebuloni, wasilikale wenji wakaza kwilunza ne mapuga gakwe ge wasilikale, ho wamutazilize, wamunoze yawe mutemi, yahalile ho mutemi Sawuli, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yehuwiza. Ne liwalo lye wasilikale wawa lyali nhavi:

24 Wa muli wana wa Yuda, wono wali wena ngula ne migoha,

wali waficile wanhu elufu mutandatu na magana munana.

25 We wana wa Simiyoni, wali wali elufu

mupunghati ne 'gana limonga.

26 We wana wa Lawi,

elufu yine na magana mutandatu.

27 Nawo i watyatyi wa Yehoyada u mwana wa Haluni,

wali waficile wanhu elufu yidatu,

na magana mupunghati.

28 Nawo we ndugu ya Zadoki, mono yali muzelelo we

vilungo, wali waficile wanhu

makumi mejete na wejete.

29 Nawo i wana wa Benyamini u nduguye wa Sawuli,

wali waficile wanhu elufu yidatu.

(Ninga hamba nhavivyo, wanhu wanji wo mulango

wa Benyamini, wasigala ku lubavu lwa Sawuli.)

30 Ne wana wa Efulayimu, wono wali wena lilumbo

mu mhuga zawo, wali waficile wanhu

elufu makumi mejete na magana munana.

31 Ne nusu ya wo mulango wa Manase wa kumwezi,

wali waficile wanhu elufu yikumi na munana

cititila, wono wahagulwa wabite wakamutemye

Dawudi yawe mutemi.

32 Na muli wana wa Isakali, i wanhu wono wali

wena mahala go wumanya madaha, na zono

zowanojela Waisilayeli wazitume, nawo

wali waficile wanhu magana mejete.

33 Ne Wazebuloni, wono wali wali cihuliko, na

kanyhi wali wena nhumbula yono yisina woga,

na kanyhi wewikanize ku milewo, weyijize sunga no

kwitumamila mawala gose ge vitwalo, nawo

wali waficile wanhu elufu makumi mahano ho wamutaze Dawudi.

34 Na muli wana wa Nafutali, mwali na walongola elufu

monga. Nawo iwasilikale wono wali wena

zingula ne migoha, wali waficile wanhu elufu

makumi madatu na mupunghati.

35 Nawo wana wa Dani, wali waficile wanhu elufu

makumi mejete na munana,

na magana mutandatu.

36 Nawo wana wa Asheli, wanhu wono wewikanize

so wulugu, wali waficile wanhu elufu

makumi mane cititila.

37 Nawo wa ku nhumbi ya kucilima ye

likolongo lya Yoludani, wali wana wa Lubeni,

na wa Gadi, hamonga ne Wamanase wa kucilima, wali waficile

wanhu elufu yigana limonga na makumi mejete

cititila. Nawo wali wo wanhu

wono wali wejizwe muno ku witumamila

vitwalo vye mawala gose.

38 Ne wasilikale wawano wono wali wewikanize so wulugu, wose wakabita ku Hebuloni. Na wose wakabita no wuhile wumonga du, ho wamutemye Dawudi yawe mutemi muli Waisilayeli wose. Ne Waisilayeli wono wali wasigaye ku zikaya, wose wali wena wuhile wumonga du, wo kumutemya Dawudi.
39 Nawo wakaswa baho vilo vidatu wali hamonga na Dawudi, wakulya no kung'wa, vyono wanyawusi wa baho wali wakondya kuwawikaniziza.
40 Ne wayawo wo kutali ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, we milango Isakali na ya Zabuloni, na ya Nafutali, wanhu wakaza wakaleta ndigwa, no wusaje, ne zinghuyu, na zabibu nghavu, ne divayi, hamonga ne mafuta. Ne vinhu vyose vivino, wavileta wapacize mu zindogowe, na mu zingamiya, na mu zinyumbu, na mu zing'ombe. Waleta hamonga ne zing'ombe ne zingholo, nyinji du, wakaziwulaga wakazilya. Na zizi zose zikanozwa so nyemo yono wali wayituga mu yisi yose.


1 Zinghomo 13

Lisanduku lye Cilagane, likuhamywa ku Kiliyati Yeyalimu

(2Sa 6:1-11)

1 Mutemi Dawudi, yamula kuduwana ne wetumami wakwe i walongola wawaha, we vipuga vye wasilikale elufu monga monga, ne walongola we vipuga vye wasilikale yigana yigana.
2 Hanyuma, ho yakawa kuwalonjela wanhu wose wa Isilayeli yakawatya, “Hono yiwe viswanu kuli'nye, na hono wuwe wo wuhile wo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, ale, citume wanhu, wabite mu matumbi gose, kuli wandugu zetu wono wekaye mu yisi yose ya Isilayeli, wono muli wene, mo wali ne Wakohani, ne Walawi, wawalonjele wekongholele kono kwetu.
3 Hanyuma ho cibite cikalisole Lisanduku lye Cilagane, lyono si walicelelela yaye lono Sawuli yali yakali yatemile.”
4 Nawo wanhu wose wakendezwa muno ne lihweso lilyo, wakalivumila.

5 Hobaho, Dawudi yakawakonghola hamonga wanhu wose wa Isilayeli ku wulawa mu yisi yose. Ku wulawa mu mipaka ye yisi ya Misili kutakama, sunga ko ku Hamati Pasi, lubavu lwe sumuka, ho yasume kuligomola Lisanduku lye Cilagane ku wulawa Kiliyati Yeyalimu, walitegule ku Yelusalemi.
6 Hanyuma, Dawudi, hamonga ne wanhu, wakabita kwi'kaya lya Bala nha cinji ku Kiliyati Yeyalimu, ku yisi ya Yuda, wakalisole Lisanduku lye Cilagane lyo Mulungu, u Mulungu mono yekaye mucanyha ye makelubi.
7 Wakalisola lilyo Lisanduku lye Cilagane mu kaya ya Abinadabu, wakalijeleka mucanyha yo mukokoteni mupya. Uza na Ahiyo wo wakapegwa mulimo wo kulibidusa.
8 Mwene Dawudi hamonga ne wanhu wose, wakawa wakuvina kwe vilungo vyawo vyose, wakumulumba Mulungu. Wakawa wakwimba akuno wakuvina, wakutowa ne zifilimbi, ne zindonondo, ne zing'oma, ne zimbilili, ne zindulele.

9 Nawo wali weza kufika ha cuga ca Gidiyoni, zing'ombe zikekobala, nayo Uza yakagolola muwoko wakwe, yakalibata Lisanduku lye Cilagane ho lilece kugwa.
10 Hobaho baho Muwaha Mulungu yakamukolelwa likuwo Uza, yakamuwulaga, vyono Uza yalipamiza Lisanduku lye Cilagane. Yakafwila baho du hali Mulungu.
11 Na ku soko yiyo, ku wulawilila lulo, i hanhu baho hakacemwa Pelezi-uza, sunga nalu. Nayo Dawudi yakakolwa muno likuwo vyono Muwaha Mulungu, kamulipiciziza Uza mwi'kuwo.

12 Na ku wulawa baho, Dawudi yakamogopa Mulungu, yakatya, “Alu ho nolitegula wule Lisanduku lye Cilagane kwangu?”
13 Na ku soko yiyo, Dawudi si yasaka kanyhi yalitegule Lisanduku ku Yelusalemi, langa yakalileka mu kaya ya munhu litagwa Obedi Edomu, munyawusi we likaya lya Gati.
14 Nalyo Lisanduku, likekala baho myezi midatu. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakamulugutiza Obedi, mhuga ya Edomu, hamonga na vyose vyono yali yena navyo.


1 Zinghomo 14

I milimo ya Dawudi, yono yetuma mu Yelusalemi

(2Sa 5:11-16)

1 Nayo Hilamu u mutemi wa Tulo, yakatuma wanhu kuli Dawudi, yakamutegulila ne mabici go muhagata, hamonga ne wazenji wanghula wo wuzenjela mabwe, ne waselemala, ho wamuzenjele Dawudi kaya ye likulu.
2 Na ku soko yiyo, nhondo Dawudi ho yakamanya nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu, yakondya kumusikamya, ho yateme muli Waisilayeli, kanyhi na vyono wutemi wakwe wokumywa so wanhu wakwe Waisilayeli.

3 Na mumo mu Yelusalemi, Dawudi yakatola wacekulu wenji, na ku soko yiyo, yakatuga wana we cilume wenji, hamonga ne wana we cicekulu.
4 Ne wana awa, wo walelelwa ku Yelusalemi: Shama, Shobabu, Natani, Solomona,
5 Ibuhali, Elishuwa, Elipeleti,
6 Noga, Nefeji, Yafiya,
7 Elishama, Beliyada na Elifeleti.

Wafilisiti wakusumwa

(2Sa 5:17-25)

8 Nawo Wafilisiti, wali weza kuhulika nha vyono, Dawudi yo yeza mutemi muli yisi yose ye Waisilayeli, hobaho lipuga lyawo lye wasilikale likawa kwikonghola, likawa kubita kumulondola Dawudi. Nayo Dawudi yali yeza kuzihulika zizo, nayo yakatuluka yabite viketang'anile nawo.
9 Wafilisiti wakafika mwi'lolo lya Lefayimu, wakawa kwanza kuliteka.
10 Ninga Dawudi yakamuwuza Mulungu yakatya, “Wule 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, niwahomaje Wafilisiti wawa, hamba ng'o? Wule, komhela vilungo vyo kuwasumila?”

Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamupitula yakamutya, “Heya wubite wukawahome, nane nokupela vilungo vyo kuwasumila wene!”

15 Nagwe kolo wukulihulika lizi lilawiliye ku visonje vye mibici, kotya wanhu wakujenda, hobaho wuwahungucilaje Wafilisiti wuwahome, hambi ane nha nowa nghulongoleye kumwande yako, niwasume lipuga lye wasilikale we Wafilisiti.”
16 Nayo Dawudi yakanoza nha vyono Mulungu yamulajiliza. Na mu nzila yiyo, yakawawinga Wafilisiti, ku wulawa ku Gibeyoni, sunga ko ku Gezeli.

1 Zinghomo 15

Wiwikanize wo kuliwusa Lisanduku lye Cilagane

2 Hanyuma yayo yakatya, “Alu langa i Walawi du wali wene, wo wolidinghula Lisanduku lye Cilagane, hambi nha wo weneco du wono wahagulwa no Muwaha Mulungu, wo wawe wadinghuzi walyo, kanyhi wajendelele kumwitumamila mwene cibitilila.”
3 Hobaho Dawudi yakawakongholela wanhu wose ku Yelusalemi, ho wabite wakalilete Lisanduku lye Cilagane, waliwice i hanhu hono mwene yali yakondya kuhawikaniza.
4 Hanyuma kanyhi, Dawudi yakawakonghola hamonga i wana wa Haluni ne Walawi.
5 Mu wulawa mu Walawi we wana wa Kola, yakaza Uliyeli, u mulanjilizi we wanhu yigana limonga na makumi mejete, we mhuga yakwe.
6 Na mu wulawa mu wana wa Melali, yakaza Asaya, mulanjilizi we wanhu magana mejete na makumi mejete.
7 Na mu wulawa mu wana wa Gelishoni, yakaza Yoweli, u mulanjilizi we wanhu yigana limonga na makumi madatu.
8 Mu wulawa mu wana wa Elizafani, yakaza Shemaya, u mulanjilizi we wanhu magana mejete.
9 Na mu wulawa mu wana wa Hebuloni, yakaza Eliyeli, u mulanjilizi we wanhu makumi munana.
10 Na mu wulawa mu wana wa Uziyeli, yakaza Aminadabu, u mulanjilizi we wanhu yigana limonga ne kumi limonga na wejete.

11 Hanyuma kanyhi, Dawudi yakawacema i wakohani, Zadoki na Abiyatali, hamonga ne Walawi, wali mutandatu liwalo lyawo, wo wutya Uliyeli, Asaya, Yoweli, Shemaya, Eliyeli, na Aminadabu.

13 Hambi anye nha mwali muli hasina ha lulongozi, ho mulidinghule nyenye, lono ase calidinghulila. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, yakacilipiciza, vyono si camuhemba mwene nha vyono yali yicinojeye cimuhembe.”

14 Ale, baho, i Wakohani, hamonga ne Walawi, wakawa kwiyeza weneco, ho wasume kulidinghula Lisanduku lye Cilagane lyo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli.
15 Wakawa kulidinghula Lisanduku lye Cilagane mu mawega gawo walisopece mu mabici, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalajiliza muli Musa.

16 Dawudi yakawalajiliza i walongola we Walawi, wawawice Walawi wanji mu mulimo wo kwimba, hamonga no kutowa zing'oma ne masumbi ne zindonondo ne zimbilili na nyemo mbaha.
17 Na mu wulawa mu zimhuga zono zina cipegwa co wimbi, wakahagulwa walume wono wowa kutowa zimbilili ze shaba, nawo wo awa.
18 Hemani u mwana wa Yoweli, no nduguye Asafu u mwana wa Belekiya, hamonga na Etani, u mwana wa Kushaya, we mhuga ya Melali.
19 Wakawahagula ne Walawi wono wowa kuwataza ku wutowa zindonondo, ze mazi ga kucanyha, nawo wo awa:
20 Zekaliya, Yaziyeli, Shemilamoti, Yehiyeli, Uni, Eliyabi, Maseya, na Benaya.

Wakahagulwa wanji kanyhi, wono wowa kutowa zindonondo ze mazi ga hasi, nawo wo Walawi awa.
21 Matitiya, Elifelehu, Mikineya, na Azaziya, hamonga ne walindilizi we Nyumba yo Mulungu, Obedi Edomu, na Yayiyeli.

22 Nayo Kenaniya, yakahagulwa yawe mulanjilizi we Walawi i wembi, ku soko yo wunghula wakwe wo wimbi.
23 Nayo Belekiya, Elikana, Obedi Edomu, na Yehiya, wawa wose wahagulwa wawe wo walindilizi we Lisanduku lye Cilagane.
24 Nawo i Wakohani: Shebaniya, Yoshafati, Netaneli, Amasayi, Zekaliya, Benaya, na Eleyezali, wawa wose wahagulwa wawe wo watowa zindulele ha mwande ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane.

Lisanduku lye Cilagane, likugomolwa ku Yelusalemi

(2Sa 6:12-22)

25 Ale, mutemi Dawudi, ne walongola we Waisilayeli, hamonga ne wawaha we wasilikale, wose wakabita ku kaya ya Obedi Edomu, wakalisola Lisanduku lye Cilagane. Nawo wakanoza sikukuu mbaha muno.
26 Wakalavya ng'ombe mupunghati, makumbiko, hamonga na ngholo mupunghati, ho Mulungu yawataze Walawi wono walidinghula Lisanduku lye Cilagane,
27 nayo Dawudi lulo, yali yavwalile musweko wa bafuta swanu muno. Na hakawa ne wembi, Kenaniya, u mutonji wawo, ne Walawi wono wali walidinghuye Lisanduku lye Cilagane. Dawudi yakavwala hamonga ne efodi ya bafuta swanu.
28 Nhavivyo Waisilayeli wose, wakawa wali hamonga, wakalitegula Lisanduku lye Cilagane sunga ko ku Yelusalemi, wena nyemo mbaha, hamonga ne mazi ge zindulele, ne misesele, wakwimba ne mazi mawaha ne zimbilili hamonga ne zindonondo.

29 Na lono Lisanduku lye Cilagane lyenjizwaga mu kaya, Mikali u mwana we cicekulu wa Sawuli, yali yemile he 'dilisha yasunjiliye kudeha. Nayo yakamuwona mutemi Dawudi vyono yavinaga, akuno yakuzumha zumha so nyemo, mwene Mikali yakamubedegala Dawudi mu nhumbula yakwe.


1 Zinghomo 16

1 Wakalitegula Lisanduku lye Cilagane, wakalinjiza mwihema lyono Dawudi yali yakondya kuliwikaniza so mulimo wuwo. Hanyuma ya zizo, wakalavya makumbiko, hamonga ne vilavywa vyo mulamu kuli Mulungu.
2 Nayo Dawudi, yali yeza kukondya kulavya makumbiko, yakawalugutiza wanhu mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu,
3 hanyuma yakawa kuwagawila wanhu wose Waisilayeli, walume ne wacekulu, munhu mukate we cilagane ne ciponje ca nyama, hamonga no mukate we zabibu nghavu.

4 Hanyuma Dawudi, yakawahagula Walawi wanji, wawe walongoza wuhembi wo kumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli, ha mwande ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane, mu nzila yo kwimba, hamonga no kumukumya Muwaha Mulungu we Waisilayeli.
5 Asafu yakawikwa yawe yo muwaha wawo, nayo Zekaliya, yakawa yo mutazi wakwe. Nayo Yeyiyeli, na Shemilamoti, Yehiyeli, Matitiya, Eliyabi, Benaya, na Obedi Edomu, hamonga na Yeyiyeli, awa wali wo watowa zindonondo.

7 Lulo mwizuwa lilyo, wali wo mwamulizo Dawudi ho yamulajiliziza Asafu ne wanduguze Walawi, wawe wo wembi wo kumwimbila Muwaha Mulungu nyimbo zo kumulumba.

Lwimbo lo wulumbe

(Zab 105:1-15; 96:1-13; 106:1, 47-48)

8 Mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu,

mwanze zo wuwaha wakwe.

Mugalonjele makungugo zinghani zono yetumile

9 Mwimbilenyi Muwaha zinyimbo zo lukumyo,

mwanze zinghani zo wizine zono yetumile,

10 Cisangalale vyono ase cili wakwe.

Wasangalale wose wono wamuhembaga mwene.

11 Mubitilenyi Muwaha Mulungu mutuje wutazi

kanyhi muwe kumuhemba nhondo zose.

12 Nanye mo wana wa Yakobo, mo wetumami wakwe,

mo wana wa Isilayeli mono mwahagulwa no Mulungu,

13 Mweze kukumbucila ze mapoto gono Mulungu yaganoza

hamonga ne ning'aniza yono yayilavya.

14 U Muwaha Mulungu, yo Mulungu wetu,

ne malajilizo gakwe go ge yinze yose.

15 Holeka mulece kucisemwa cilagane co Mulungu

cono yacinoza cikale cibitilila.

16 I cilagane cono yacinoza na Abulahamu

no wihuwizo wono yamwihuwiza Isaka.

17 Muwaha Mulungu yanoza cilagane na Yakobo

ciwe cilagane cono cokwikala cibitilila.

18 “Yakatya, nokupela yisi ya Kanani.”

“Nayo yowa yako gwegwe.”

19 I wanhu wo Mulungu, wali wacefu ku wuwale,

kanyhi wali wajenzi, mu yisi ya Kanani.

20 Wakapilima pilima ku wulawa yisi na yisi

ku wulawa mu wutemi awu, sunga ko ku wutemi wuyagwe.

21 Ninga Mulungu si yawaleceleza wewonelwe na cinji,

langa yawasugula watemi, ho yawacitile wene, yakawatya,

22 “Mulece kuwatamisa wetumami wangu wahagulwa,

mulece kuwapamiza i wawizi wangu.”

23 Mwimbilenyi Muwaha Mulungu nye nyo yinze yose!

Nhondo zose mweze kwanza ze mhonelo yakwe

yono yaciponya.

ze metumo gakwe mawaha kuli wanhu wose.

25 Muwaha Mulungu nha yo yalawiliciye,

yo munya lilumbo liwaha,

kanyhi yo we cogopo kuciza milungu yose.

26 Ne milungu ye zinghungugo zose, nha vihwani du,

ninga u Muwaha Mulungu, yo yawumba wulanga.

27 Lilumbo no lukumyo, zimupilime mwene,

Vilungo ne nyemo, vimemile mu Nyumba yakwe.

28 Mulumbenyi Muwaha Mulungu, anye nyo we yinze wose,

wulumbenyi lukumyo lwakwe ne vilungo vyakwe.

29 Mumupele Muwaha Mulungu lukumyo

lwe litagwa lyakwe,

mulete vilavywa, mwinjile mu Nyumba yakwe,

mumuhembe Muwaha Mulungu ko wuswanu no wele.

Heya, yinze yawikwa ho yilece kusinjiswa.

31 Musecelele nyo yisi yose no wulanga!

Mugalonjele makungugo gose,

vyono Muwaha Mulungu yo mutemi.

32 Ale, go lilenga wutunhume, hamonga na vyose

vyono vili muli'gwe.

Nayo migunda yisanghuce, hamonga na vyose

vyono vili mumo.

33 Holeka mibici yose ya mu mbago,

yimbe ne nyemo, lono Muwaha Mulungu

yakuza kuyitema yinze.

34 Mulumbenyi Muwaha Mulungu,

hambi nha yali 'hewa

hambi lusungu lwakwe nha lwa cibitilila.

Wucikonghole no kucilavyaniza muli we makungugo,

ho cikulumbe no kulikumya litagwa lyako lyono lyelile.”

Yalumbizwe ku wulawa lulu, sunga cibitilila!

wakamulumbiza Muwaha Mulungu.

Wuhembi wa ku Yelusalemi, na ku Gibeyoni

37 Mutemi Dawudi yakawawika Asafu hamonga ne Walawi wayagwe, wawe wo walanjilizi we nhondo zose, wo wuhembi wono walongozwaga, i hanhu hono lyali yiwice Lisanduku lye Cilagane. Nawo welecelaga yizuwa ne 'zuwa mu mulimo wawo.
38 Nayo Obedi Edomu, u mwana wa Yedutuni, hamonga ne wayagwe, waficile wanhu makumi mutandatu na munana, we mhuga yakwe, wali wo watazi wawo. Hosa na Obedi Edomu, wali wo walindilizi we milango.

39 Nayo Zadoki u mukohani, hamonga ne wanduguze wakohani, wene wali wo walanjilizi wo wuhembi wo Muwaha Mulungu, ku wuhembelo wono wali wuli ku Gibeyoni.
40 Nawo wawezaga no mulimo wo kulavywa vilavywa vinyanywe mu cikumbicilo, nhondo zose hakali mitondo na ha cinyamihe, nha vyono zandicigwe mu Wulajilize.
41 Hamonga na wana, hakawa ho yuli Hemani ne Yedutuni hamonga ne wayawo wanji wono wahagulwa so mulimo wo kwimba du, ho wamukumye Muwaha Mulungu so lusungu lwakwe lwe cibitilila.
42 Na muli wene, Hemani na Yedutuni, wo wali walanjilizi we watowa zindulele ne zimbilili, hamonga ne viya vinji vyono vyatowaga lono zinyimbo zo lukumyo zikwimbwa. Nawo we mhuga ya Yedutuni, wali wo walindilizi we milango.

43 Hanyuma ya zose, wose wakawuya ku zikaya zawo, nayo Dawudi yakabita ku kaya yakwe vilo vicefu, yakekalana ne mhuga yakwe.


1 Zinghomo 17

Nghani ya Natani kuli Dawudi

(2Sa 7:1-17)

1 Na lono Dawudi yali yekala mu kaya yakwe, mwizuwa linji yakamucema muwizi Natani, yakamulonjela yakutya, “Lanje ta! Ane nikaye mu kaya yono nzenje ne mibici yo muhagata, ninga Lisanduku lye Cilagane lyo Muwaha Mulugu, yiwice mwi'hema!”

2 Nayo Natani yakamupitula yakatya, “Zose zono zili mu nhumbula yako, wuzitumaje, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yali hamonga nagwe.”

3 Ninga mu cilo cico, Mulungu yakamulonjela Natani yakatya,
4 “Wubite wukamulonjele mwitumami wangu Dawudi wutye, ‘Agwe yo si gwegwe mono konzenjela Nyumba yangu yono nokwikala mumo.
5 Hambi nha ku wulawa lono nawawusiza wanhu wa Isilayeli ku Misili, nghali sinekala mu Nyumba yo wuhembelo sunga nalu. Langa nhondo zose, ane nekalaga mu mahema du, nghuhamywa ku wulawa hanhu aha, nghutegulwa hanhu aho.
6 Hamba nhavivyo, mu mihinzo yangu yose yono najendana ne Waisilayeli, hamba lizuwa linji sinawawuza i walongola wono nawahagula niwe kutya, nhawulenyi lu si mukunzenjela Nyumba yono nzengwe kwe mibici yo muhagata.’

7 “Holeka wumulonjele mwitumami wangu Dawudi wutye, ‘Ane, yo nene no Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga. Ane nakusola wali wuli mudima ngholo mu mawimbi, nghakunoza wuwe mulongola muli wanhu wangu Waisilayeli.
8 Nane nghawa nili hamonga nagwe ko kose kono wabitaga, kanyhi nghakusumila ne walugu wako hali'gwe. Holeka nane nokunoza wuwe ne lilumbo yiwaha, kanyhi wuwe mulongola mono kowa wukumucile mono yinze yose.
9 Hambi nha nahahagula i hanhu hono nowawika wanhu wangu Waisilayeli, nane lu nha nakondya kuwenjiza mumo, nawo wakondya kuzowela, i hanhu hono wokwikala wasina kanyhi wiwonelwe wowose.
10 Na kanyhi ku wulawa lono wenjilila mono yisi yiyi, kenji na kenji wali kuhomwa ne wanhu wono wasina lusungu. Ninga alu zizo zose si zokwijela kanyhi ng'o. Nane nha nakondya kwihuwiza nha vyono nowasuma walugu wako wose, kanyhi nokupela wana.

12 Ale, yuyo langa yo kolonzenjela ne Nyumba yangu. Nane nolowunoza wutemi wakwe wukame cibitilila.
13 Nane nolowa sogwe, nayo kolowa mwanangu. Nane hamba si nolomulecela lukulu, nha vyono namuleka Sawuli mono namuwusa mu wutemi, agwe ho wuteme.

(2Sa 7:18-29)

18 Alu ane kanyhi ho nolonga cici kuli'gwe! Agwe nha wumanyile zangu zose, ane no mwitumami wako.
19 Hambi nha wo wuhile wako gwegwe ku wituma zizi kuli'ne, ho wundajile zono zili ku mwande yangu, zo wukumuce wangu.
20 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, hasina yunji kanyhi mono yehwanile gwegwe. Nase cimanyile vyono, yo gwegwe Mulungu du wuli wene.
21 Kanyhi bahano hono'nze, hasina hamba nghungugo yinji yo yose yono yihwanile nghungugo ye Waisilayeli, wono wawaponya mu wulawa mu wuwanda, wukawa kuwanoza wawe wanhu wako go mweneco. 23 “Holeka aluno go Muwaha Mulungu, nghukulomba wufisilize mapango gose, u wihuwizo wako wono wanyihuwiziza, ane hamonga no lulelo lwangu, kanyhi witumaje nha vyono walonga.

1 Zinghomo 18

Wusume wa Dawudi ku wulugu

(2Sa 8:1-18)

1 Hanyuma mutemi Dawudi, yakawahoma kanyhi Wafilisiti yakawasuma. Yakawawusiza lukulu mwi'kaya lya Gati.
2 Hanyuma kanyhi, yakawasuma Wamowabi, wakawa wetumami wa Dawudi, wakawa wakumulipa sunga ne kodi.

3 Hanyuma kanyhi Dawudi, yakamuhoma Hadadezeli u mutemi wa Siliya ya Zoba, behi ne yisi ya Hamati. Hambi mwene Hadadezeli nha yasakaga yetujile yisi ya Efulata ya kucanyha.
4 Lulo Dawudi yakamuboka Hadadezeli matuka go wulugu gaficile elufu monga, hamonga ne wapanda fwalasi elufu mupunghati, ne wasilikale wajenda ne magulu, nawo elufu makumi mejete. Yakasola fwalasi zificile liwalo lyono lyali likwiliye ku wukwega matuka go wulugu, yigana limonga. Ne zifwalasi zose zono zasigala, yakazidumulanga matimba gazo.

5 Nawo Washami wanya Siliya ya ku Damesiki, wakategula wasilikale wakamutazilize mutemi Hadadezeli, ninga Dawudi yakawahoma Washami, yakawawulaga wanhu waficile elufu makumi mejete na nyejete.
6 Hanyuma yakawa kusita ne zinghambi ze wasilikale wakwe mu yisi yawo. Washami wakawa wetumami wakwe, wakumulipa sunga ne kodi. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakamupela Dawudi wusume wuwaha muno hanhu hose.
7 Dawudi yakaziteka zingula zose ze zahabu zono zali zidinghuywe ne wetumami wa Hadadezeli, yakazitegula zose ku Yelusalemi.
8 Yakasoleleza hamonga ne shaba nyinji muno, yono yali yili ku Tibuhati na Kuni, i makaya ga Hadadezeli. (Na hanyuma yayo, Solomona yakayitumamila shaba yiyo mu wuzenjela litanji lye malenga, hamonga ne masumbili, ne viya vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.)

9 Nayo Towi u mutemi wa Hamati, yakahulika nha vyono mutemi Dawudi yagasumiza mapuga gose ge wasilikale wa Hadadezeli.
10 Hodu yakawa kumutuma Yolamu u mwanagwe yabite yakamulamuse mutemi Dawudi, no kumuhonjeza so wusume wakwe wono yakondya kumusuma Hadadezeli, mono vyekalaga vikwihoma na Towi kenji na kenji. Nayo Yolamu yakawa kumutegulila mutemi Dawudi vipegwa vye zahabu, ne fweza, hamonga ne shaba.
11 Nayo mutemi Dawudi yakavisiwanula, ho viwe kwitumamilwa mu wuhembi, hamonga ne zifweza, ne zizahabu zono yali yakondya kuzisola ku wulawa mu makungugo gono yagasuma, ge Waedomu, Wamowabi, Waamoli, Wafilisiti, hamonga ne Waamaleki.

12 Nayo Abishayi munghalo nyina mono yacemwaga Zeluya, yakawasuma Waedomu mwi'lolo lyo Mwino, yakawawulaga wanhu waficile elufu yikumi na munana liwalo lyawo.
13 Nayo yakasita nghambi nyinji ze wasilikale mu yisi yose ye Waedomu. Ne wanhu wayo, wakawa wetumami wo mutemi Dawudi. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamupela Dawudi wusume wuwaha hanhu hose.

15 Nayo Yowabu u nduguye wa Abishayi, yali yo muwaha we wasilikale. Ayu nayo Yehoshafati u mwana wa Ahiludi, yali yo mulanjilizi we liwalo lye vilo.

1 Zinghomo 19

Dawudi yakuwasuma Waamoni ne Wasiliya

(2Sa 10:1-19)

1 Hanyuma Nahashi u mutemi we Waamoni yakawa yafwiza, nayo Hanuni u mwanagwe, yakawusola wutemi.
2 U mutemi Dawudi yakatya, “Ane nghuwona vyowa viswanu nimunozeze wuswanu Hanuni, nha vyono sogwe Nahashi yanozeza 'ne.” Hobaho Dawudi yakatuma wanhu, wabite wakamusindamice Hanuni.

Nawo wali weza kwinjila mu yisi ye Waamoni, no kusaka wamuwone Hanuni,
3 i walongola we Waamoni wakamulonjela mutemi Hanuni wakatya, “Gwe mutemi wukwihwesa wuswanu wo sogo Nahashi, wo wulecile Dawudi yawatume wanhu wawa waze wakusindamice? Aa ng'o, hamba hadodo! Langa mwene kawatuma ho waze walitandile likaya, ho wacisume!”

4 Hodu nayo mutemi Hanuni yakawa kuwebata wawalya wanhu, yakawaceta zindevu lubavu lumonga, lwe videvu vyawo, yakayisapa myenda yawo sunga ha madako, hodu yakawa kuwawinga.
5 Nawo wakawa kuwona cevu ku wu wuya ku zikwawo. Nayo mutemi Dawudi yali yeza kuzihulika zono ziwejeleye, hobaho yakawa kuwatumila munhu, yawalonjele vyono yiwanojeye wekale ha Yeliko, walece kuwuya, sunga kolo zindevu zawo zakondya kusanga no kutalimha kanyhi, ho wolowuya.

6 Nawo Waamoni, wakamanya du, vyono litumo lilyo, lyakondya kuwaletela wulugu no mutemi Dawudi. Holeka wakawa kulipa fweza zo wuzito wa kilo elufu makumi madatu na yine, ho wasume kwazima matuka go wulugu, hamonga na wono wogajendya matuka gago ku wulawa Mesopotamiya, na ku wulawa ku Wasiliya, ku yisi ya Alamu Maka, na Zoba.
7 Nago matuka go wulugu gali gali elufu makumi madatu na nyejete gono wali wazime, hamonga ne lipuga lye Wasilikale lyo mutemi wa Maka, nalyo likaza, likasita nghambi yalyo behi na Medeba. Nawo Waamoni wakaza nhavivyo ku wulawa mu makaya gawo gose, wakawa kwiwikaniza so wihomi.

8 Nayo mutemi Dawudi yali yeza kuzihulika zizo zono zejelaga, hobaho yakamutuma Yowabu, hamonga ne lipuga lyose lye wasilikale.
9 Waamoni wakatandala ha mulango we likaya lyawo lya Laba. Nawo watemi wono wazaga kuwataziliza, wakatandala mu yisi yo wuhihi.

10 Ninga Yowabu yakamanya nha vyono i mapuga ge walugu, gosuma kuwahoma ku wulawa ku mwande na ku nyuma yawo. Holeka yakawahagula wasilikale vitung'ati wa muli Waisilayeli, yakawawika watandale vidomile ne Wasiliya.
11 Ne wasilikale wono wasigala, awo yakawa kuwawika mu miwoko ya Abishayi u mwanakwawe. Nayo Abishayi yakawatandalika wakawa wedomile ne Waamoni.
12 Yowabu yakamulonjela Abishayi, yakamutya, “Ale, hono wuwone Wasiliya weza kusumiliza, wuze mbela mbela wunhaze. Nane hono mbone Waamoni weza kukusumiliza, ale, nane nokuza mbela mbela nghutazilize.
13 Ninga weze ne vilungo no mutima wono wusikame! Hambi nha yicinojeye ase cihome kwe vilungo ku soko ye wanhu wetu, na ku soko ye makaya go Mulungu wetu. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yetume zono yakuwona swanu ha meso gakwe.”

14 Yowabu ne wanhu wakwe, wakalongola kuwahoma Wasiliya. Nawo Wasiliya wakatiza.
15 Nawo Waamoni wali weza kuwona vyono Wasiliya wakutiza, hodu nawo wakawa kutiza ha mwande ya Abishayi, wakenjila mwi'kaya. Hodu nayo Yowabu yakawa kuleka kuwahoma Waamoni, yakawa kuwuya ku Yelusalemi.

16 Nawo Wasiliya wakamanya nha vyono wakondya kusumwa ne Waisilayeli. Holeka wakagakonghola mapuga ge wasilikale ku wulawa Siliya, ku lubavu lwa kucilima ye likolongo lya Efulata, wakagawika mu miwoko ya Shobaki u muwaha we wasilikale wo mutemi Hadadezeli wa Zoba.
17 Nayo Dawudi yali yeza kuzihulika zizo, yakawa kugakonghola mapuga ge wasilikale we Waisilayeli, yakawa kulowoka likolongo lya Yoludani. Yakawatandalika vidomile ne Wasiliya, hobaho wulugu wukawa kwanza,
18 ne Waisilayeli, wakawawinga Wasiliya wakatiza. Nayo Dawudi ne wanhu wakwe, wakawawulaga Wasiliya waficile elufu mupunghati i wajendya matuka go wulugu, hamonga ne Wasilikale wajenda ne migulu waficile elufu makumi mane. Wakamuwulaga no mulongola wawo muwaha mono yakucemwa Shobaki.
19 Nawo watemi wono wamutazilizaga Hadadezeli, wali weza kuwona vyono wakondya kusumwa ne Waisilayeli, hodu wakanoza mulamu na Dawudi, wawe wetumami wakwe. Nawo Wasiliya hamba si wejelela kanyhi wawataze Waamoni.


1 Zinghomo 20

Dawudi yakuliteka likaya lya Laba

(2Sa 12:26-31)

1 Na lono wali mwamulizo wo mwaka mupya, mu madaha ge watemi ga ku wubita ku milewo, Yowabu yakawa yo mutonji we vitung'ati we milewo, yakayihoma no kuyisola yisi ye Waamoni. Ninga mutemi Dawudi yekala ku Yelusalemi. Nayo Yowabu yakayihoma yisi ya Laba, yakayongomeza.
2 Naco cihwani ce Waamoni, cono cikucemwa, Moleki, cali civwicigwe mukumbo wa zahabu, wono wali wina wuzito wa kilo makumi madatu na yine. Na mugati yayo mwali movili vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu. Dawudi yakavisola yakaviwicila mu mukumbo wakwe mweneco. Kanyhi yakasola liwalo yiwaha lye mateka mwi'kaya.
3 Yakawasola wanhu wa mwi'kaya, yakawetumya milimo yo wuselemala, no wutyana masili, hamonga ne zimheno. Kanyhi yakawanoza nhavivyo ne wanhu wa mu makaya ganji ge Waamoni. Na hanyuma, mwene hamonga ne wanhu wakwe, wakawuya ku Yelusalemi.

Wulugu ne manhu ge Wafilisiti

(2Sa 21:15-22)

4 Wulawa lulo, kukejela kanyhi wulugu ne Wafilisiti ku Gezeli. Hobaho lono Sibekayi munya Husha, yamuwulajila Sipayi ne Wafilisiti wakawa kusumwa.

5 Kanyhi wukenuka wulugu wunji ne Wafilisiti. Nayo Elihanani u mwana wa Yayili, yakamuwulaga Lahimi u nduguye wa Goliyati u Mugiti, mono u mugoha wakwe, wali wina yidete yikangazi nha mucici wa kaya.

6 Kanyhi wulugu wunji wukenuka ku Gati. Na kuko kwali na munhu mutali muno, mono yali yena vidole mutandatu mutandatu mu miwoko yakwe yose, na kanyhi vidole mutandatu mutandatu mu migulu yakwe yose. Munhu yuyu yali we ndelwa ye manhu mawaha kanyhi matali muno ge katali.
7 Munhu yuyo yakawaliganga Waisilayeli. Ninga Yonazani u mwana wa Shama, u nduguye wa Dawudi, yakamuwulaga.

8 Ne wanhu wawano wadatu, wono Dawudi yawawulaga wakwitaziliza ne wanhu wakwe, wali wo we ndelwa ye manhu mawaha kanyhi matali muno ga Gati.


1 Zinghomo 21

Dawudi yakusola liwalo lye wanhu

(2Sa 24:1-25)

1 Kanyhi Setani, yakawa mulugu kuli Waisilayeli, yakamubabula Dawudi, yakawawale Waisilayeli.
2 Nayo Dawudi yakawa kumulajiliza Yowabu, u muwaha we wasilikale wakwe, yakamutya, “Alu, wubite gwegwe, hamonga ne wetumami wako, muyipilime yisi yose, mukawawale i Waisilayeli, ku wulawilila ku lubavu lumonga lwe yisi, sunga ko ku lubavu luyagwe. Nghusaka manye liwalo lyawo.”

3 Ninga Yowabu yakamupitula yakatya, “Gwe Mutemi, u Mulungu wako yakawonjeleze Waisilayeli kali cigana, wawe wenji kuciza nha vyono wenha aluno! Gwe go Mutemi, wanhu wose nha wetumami wako du. Alu nhawule kanyhi wukuhila witume nghani yono yinha yiyo, wuliletele likungugo masutya?”
4 Ninga mutemi yakamudindimiza Yowabu sunga yamuhulice. Hodu Yowabu yakatuluka, yakayipilimila yisi yose ya Isilayeli, hanyuma yakawuya ku Yelusalemi.
5 Yakawa kumuloloseza mutemi Dawudi, liwalo lye walume wose wono wakwiliye mu mulimo wo wusilikale, nha vyono mu yisi ya Isilayeli waficile walume milyoni monga na elufu yigana limonga liwalo lyawo. Nawo wa mu yisi ya Yuda waficile elufu magana mane na makumi mupunghati cititila.
6 Ninga Yowabu vyono nghani yo mutemi si yamwendeza yaye, holeka nayo hamba si yawawala yaye, i wanhu wo mulango wa Lawi na wa Benyamini.

7 Nayo Mulungu, nghani yiyo yo wuwala wanhu, yikamukalaza muno, holeka yakawalipiciza Waisilayeli.
8 Hobaho Dawudi yakamulonjela Mulungu yakatya, “Ane netuma wubi so wituma zizi! Holeka nghukulomba wunenele. Hambi nha nitumila wulele du.”

9 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Gadi, u muwizi wa Dawudi, yakamutya,
10 “Wubite wukamulonjele Dawudi nha vyono nghumupela nghani yidatu, zono nghumusaka yahagule mweneco nghani monga du muli zizo yono kosaka nimutendele. Nane nomutendela nha vyono mwene kohagula.”

11 Ale, Gadi yakawa kumubitila Dawudi yakawa kumuloloseza zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yateye. Yakamuwuza yakatya, “Alu wukwihaga nghanici?
12 Yiwe nzala mu myaka midatu? Hamba wuwe kutiza hali walugu wako sunga myezi midatu? Hamba vilo vidatu, Muwaha Mulungu yakutowe ne lihuhuji mu yisi yako, yamutume malayika wakwe, yawawulaje mu yisi yose ya Isilayeli? Alu wumhelaje lipitulo lyono nomutegulila Muwaha Mulungu.”

13 Dawudi yakamupitula Gadi, yakatya, “Ane nenjilwa na mahwele mawaha muno muno! Kanyhi hamba si nendile ndipicizwe ne wanhu. Holeka yili ciba hono Muwaha Mulungu yo yali yandipicize mweneco, hambi mwene nha yali lusungu.”

14 Holeka Muwaha Mulungu yakawaletela lihuhuji Waisilayeli. Wakafwa wanhu waficile elufu makumi mupunghati.
15 Hanyuma kanyhi, yakatuma malayika yayongomeze Yelusalemi. Ninga kanyhi yakawupitula wuhile wakwe wuwo, yakamutya, malayika, “Hodu wulece! Zizo du zakondya!” Nayo malayika, lulo, yali yemile ha cuga ca Olinani u Muyebusi.

16 Nayo Dawudi yakamuwona malayika yemile, akuno yanyasuye no mumaje mu muwoko wakwe, yewikanize ho yayongomeze Yelusalemi. Hobaho Dawudi, hamonga ne walongola wose we wanhu, wono wali wavwalile vivwalo vye magunila, wakenama, no wusu wawo wukapamiza ho hasi.
17 Dawudi yakalomba yakutya, “Gwe go Mulungu, ane yo nene mono sutizye. Ane yo nene mono ndajilize wanhu wawalwe. Alu wanhu wawa nawo ho wasutya nghanici? Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wangu, nghukunena wundipicizaje nene du, hamonga na we mhuga yangu, wuwalece wanhu wako.”

18 Malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu, yakamulonjela Gadi, yamulajilize Dawudi, yabite yakamuzenjele cikumbicilo Muwaha Mulungu, ha cuga ca Olinani Muyebusi.
19 Dawudi yakalihulika lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, yakabita nha vyono yalonjelwa na Gadi.
20 Naho baho ha cuga ca Olinani, yali yali baho Oliani hamonga ne wanagwe wane watowaga wuhemba mu cuga. Nawo wali weza muwona malayika, wa walya wana wakatiza, wakehundika.
21 Nayo Olinani yali yeza muwona mutemi Dawudi yakuza, mwene yakatela ha mhelo hadodo ye cuga, yakenama no wusu wakwe wukapamiza ho hasi.
22 Dawudi yakamutya Olinani, “Nghukulomba wunguzize cuga cako, nimuzenjele baho cikumbicilo Muwaha Mulungu, ho yaliwuse lihuhuji. Nane nocigula cuga cako ko wuguzi wono wuling'aniciye.”

23 Ninga Olinani yakamutya, “Gwe Mutemi! Wucisolaje du, wucitumamile gwegwe nha vyono wendile. Ne zing'ombe azino, wuzitumamile so cilavywa cinyanywe mu cikumbicilo. Kanyhi wusole ne matuli aga go kutwanjila wuhemba, wugetumamile nha ngwagu. Wusole no wuhemba, nawo wuwulavye cilavywa. Ane nakukombolela vyose du.”

24 Ninga mutemi nayo yakamupitula yakatya, “Aa, ng'o! Ane langa, novigula vinhu vivi vyose kuli'gwe ko wuguzi wono wuling'aniciye. Hambi nha si nosuma kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu cinhu cono cako, i cinhu cono si nicilavizye nhuguti yaye.”
25 Hobaho Dawudi yakawa kumupela Olinani fweza magana mutandatu cifo ce cuga cakwe.
26 Yakawa kumuzenjela Muwaha Mulungu cikumbicilo baho, yakamulavizya ne vilavywa vinyanywe, hamonga ne vilavywa vyo mulamu. Hanyuma yakalomba, nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamuhulika, yakaleta moto wulawa ku wulanga, wukavinyanya vilavywa vyono vyali vili mucanyha ye cikumbicilo.

27 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela malayika yawugomoleze mumaje wakwe mu njasulu yayo. Nayo malayika yakanoza nhavivyo.
28 Nayo Dawudi, mu nzila yiyo, yakamanya nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yakondya kuwupitula wulombe wakwe. Holeka nhondo, nayo yakawa kulavya makumbiko mu cikumbicilo, ha cuga ca Olinani.
29 Lihema lyono Lyelile lyono yalinoza Musa ku nyika, hamonga ne cikumbicilo cayo cono canyanyilwaga makumbiko, vyose vivyo mu cipango cico, vyali vikali vili hanhu ho wuhembelo kuko ku Gibeyoni.
30 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, Dawudi si yasumaga kubita no kumuhemba Mulungu kuko ku Gibeyoni. Hambi nha yogopaga mumaje wa Malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu.


1 Zinghomo 22

1 Lulo Dawudi yakatya, “Ahano ho hanojeye Nyumba yo Mulungu yizengwe. Hamonga ne cikumbicilo cono wanhu wa Isilayeli wowa kulavizya vilavywa vinyanywe.”

Wuwikanize wo kuyizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu

2 Mutemi Dawudi yakalajiliza i wajenzi wose wono wekaye mu yisi ya Isilayeli, wehangulize hamonga. Nayo yakawapela munhu na mulimo wakwe. Wanji mulimo wo kusesa mabwe, go kuzenjela Nyumba yo Mulungu.
3 Dawudi yakalavya viwutwa vinji vye cuma, ho watyane misumali hamonga ne viwopelo so milango ye zimbawo. Hamonga ne shaba nyinji muno, yono yisina cipimo.
4 Yakawawikaniza wanhu wa Tulo na Sidoni, wamuletele mibici minji ye mihagata.
5 Dawudi yakehwesa yakatya, “Nyumba yo Mulungu yono mwanangu Solomona koyizenga, yowa yinojeye yiwe swanu muno, kanyhi yiwe ye lilumbo yiwaha mono yinze yose. Ninga lu mwanangu Solomona, nha yakali mudodo, kanyhi yakali namanya nyinji. Holeka yinojeye nimuwikanizize vitumamilwa vyose.” Holeka Dawudi yakawikaniza vinhu vinji muno, lono yali yakali si nafwa.

6 Dawudi yakamutumila Solomona munhu, yakawa kumulajiliza nha vyono yanojeye yamuzenjele Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli, Nyumba yakwe.
7 Dawudi yakamutya, mwanagwe, “Gwe mwanangu, ane nali nina wuhile wo kumuzenjela Nyumba, Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wangu.
8 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya nha vyono, ane mbulaje wanhu wenji, kanyhi ndewile milewo minji. Holeka ku soko ye sakami nyinji yono nayimwaga, nayo si konghombola ane nimuzenjele Nyumba yakwe.
9 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, yanyihuwiza yakatya, ‘Agwe Dawudi kotuga mwana mono kolotema ko mulamu. Hambi nha nomunoza yawe no mulamu kuli walugu wakwe wose. Ne litagwa lyakwe kolocemwa Solomona. Vyono mu cipango co wutemi wakwe, ane nolowapela Waisilayeli mulamu hamonga no wucitilwe.
10 Nayo yo kolonzejela Nyumba yangu. Nayo kolowa mwanangu, nane nolowa nili sogwe. Na wo lulelo lwakwe, wolowa watemi we Waisilayeli cibitilila.’ ”

11 Hanyuma kanyhi yakajendelela kulonga yakutya, “Holeka 'gwe mwanangu, u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako, yawezane nagwe, kanyhi yawufisilize no wihuwizo wakwe, yakupele wusume wo kumuzenjela Nyumba yakwe.
12 Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako, yakupele wizuce ne mahala, ho wuwatemele Waisilayeli mu wibate Lilajilizo lyakwe.
13 Na hono wibate wulajilize wose wono Muwaha Mulungu yawapelela Waisilayeli muli Musa, nagwe komoteswa. Holeka weze ne vilungo ne nhumbula ye citung'ati, wulece kogofywa na nghani yinji.
14 Nane ko wuhidimbazi wangu, nakondya kuyiwicila ciwutwa i Nyumba yo Mulungu, ciwutwa ca zahabu, tani elufu yidatu na magana mane, hamonga na fweza, tani elufu makumi madatu na yine cititila. Zitumamilwe mu wuzenji we Nyumba yo Mulungu. Hamonga na zizo, ho cili ciwutwa ciwaha ca shaba na cuma. Kanyhi mbikanize ne mabici hamonga ne mabwe, vyose vikondizye, ninga hamba nhavivyo yinojeye nagwe wonjeleze vinji.
15 Nha wina wetumami wenji muno. Ho wali wanhu wono wosuma kwituma mulimo wo kusesa mabwe, ho wali wazenji, hamonga ne waselemala. Hamonga ne wanghula we milimo yose,
16 mu milimo ye zahabu, na ye fweza, na ye shaba, hamonga na ye cuma. Holeka alu, wuwanzaje mulimo wo wuzenji, nayo Muwaha Mulungu yawezane hamonga nagwe.”

17 Dawudi yakawalajiliza i walongola wose we Waisilayeli, wamutazilize Solomona.
18 Yakawatya, “U Muwaha Mulungu u Mulungu wenyu, yali yali hamonga nanye no kumupelenyi mulamu mu mbavu zose. Yakamhela vilungo vyo kuwasumila wanhu wose wono wekalaga mono yisi yiyi, sunga nalu, weza wo wetumami wenyu, wakumwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu.
19 Holeka alu, mumwitumamile Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kwe nhumbula yenyu yose na ko mutima wenyu wose. Mwanze mulimo wo wuzenji we Nyumba yo Mulungu, ho musume kulinjiza mugati Lisanduku lye Cilagane lyo Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga ne viya vinji vyose vyono vyelile, vyono vikwitumamilwa mu wuhembi wakwe.”


1 Zinghomo 23

1 Nayo Dawudi lono yali yakombipa muno, hodu yakamutemya Solomona u mwanagwe, yawe mutemi we Waisilayeli.

U mulimo we Walawi

2 Mutemi Dawudi yakawakongholela hamonga i walongola wose we Waisilayeli, hamonga ne wakohani ne Walawi.
3 Yakawawala i Walawi wose walume, wono waficile myaka makumi madatu hamba kuciza. Ne liwalo lyawo, wakawa waficile wanhu elufu makumi madatu na munana.
4 Na muli wawo, yakawawika wanji elufu makumi mejete na yine, wo wali walanjilizi we milimo ya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Na wanji elufu mutandatu, wakawa wo walanjilizi we vikumbucizo vye zinghani, kanyhi walamuzi.
5 Ne wanhu elufu yine, wakawa wo walindilizi. Na wanhu elufu yine wakawa wo wo mulimo wo kumukumya Muwaha Mulungu kwe viya vye masumbi, vyono mutemi yavilavya so mulimo wuwo.

6 Dawudi yakawatemanula i Walawi, wakalawa vipuga vidatu, nha vyono zali zinha zimhuga zawo, Wagelishoni, Wakohati, ne Wamelali.

7 Yuyu Gelishoni, yali yena wana wejete, Ladani na Shimeyi.
8 Ladani yali yena wana wadatu, Yeliyeli, Zetamu, na Yoweli.
9 Nawo wawo, wo wakawa walongola wa mu zimhuga ze wana wa Ladani. (Nayo Shimeyi yali yena wana wadatu, Shelomoti, Haziyeli, na Halani.)
10 Shimeyi yali yena wana wane, Yahati, Zina, Yewushi, na Beliya.
11 Ninga Yewushi na Beliya, wali wasina wana wenji, na ku soko yiyo, wakatowelelwa wakawa mhuga monga.

12 Kohati yali yena wana wane, Amulamu, Iziha, Hebuloni, na Uziyeli.
13 Nayo Amulamu u mwana muwaha wa Kohati, yali yo sogwe wa Haluni na Musa. (Nayo Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wakatemanulwa, ho wawe walanjilizi cibitilila, we vinhu vyono vikwitumamilwa mu wuhembi, kufusa wuvumba mu wuhembi wo Muwaha Mulungu, no kumwitumamila, hamonga no kuwalugutiza wanhu mwi'tagwa lyakwe.
14 Ninga i wana wa Musa, u munhu wo Mulungu, wene wakahanzilizwa hamonga ne Walawi.)
15 Musa yali yena wana wejete, Gelishomu na Eleyezali.
16 U mulongola wa muli wana wa Gelishomu, yali Shebuweli.
17 Ninga Eleyezali yali yena mwana monga du, litagwa Lehabiya. Ninga yuyu Lehabiya, yali yena wana wenji muno.

18 Izihali, u mwana we kejete, kuli Kohati, nayo yali yena mwana monga du, Shelomiti, nayo yakawa yo mulongola mu mhuga yawo.
19 Hebuloni mwana we kadatu wa Kohati, nayo yali yena wana wane, Yeliya, Amaliya, Yehaziyeli, na Yekamiyamu.

1 Zinghomo 26

I walindilizi we Nyumba yo Mulungu

1 Aga nago go magawo ge milimo yono wapegwa Walawi wono wetumaga mulimo wo wulindilizi we Nyumba yo Mulungu. Wa mu mhuga ya Kola, kwali na Meshelemiya u mwana wa Kole, we mhuga ya Asafu.
2 Yuyo Meshelemiya, yali yena wana mupunghati, na mu ndelwa yawo, wali wetyatyizye nhavi: Zekaliya, Yediyayeli, Zebadiya, Yatiniyeli,
3 Elamu, Yehohanani, na Eliyehonayi.

4 Hakawa ho yuli nayo Obedi Edomu, mono nayo, Mulungu yamumotya, yakamupela wana munana. Nawo mu wilecele we ndelwa yawo, wali nhavi: Shemaya yali yo mulongozi kulelwa, yakamulecela Yehozabadi, hanyuma Yoya, Sakali, Netaneli,
5 Amiyeli, Isakali, no mwanakwawawo Peyuletayi.

6 Nayo Shemaya, u mwana mulongozi kulelwa wa Obedi Edomu, yali yena wana mutandatu, Oteyi, Lafayeli, Obedi, Elizabadi, Elihu, na Semakiya.
7 Nawo wali wanhu vitung'ati mu mhuga yawo, ku soko ye vilungo vyawo, na ko muno, wawa wana wejete we ndumilizo, wali wena cipegwa ciwaha.

8 Wawa wose wali wo wana wa Obedi Edomu, nawo wakawa wena wana, hamonga ne wandugu zawo. Nawo mwi'walo lyawo, wali waficile wanhu makumi mutandatu na wejete, wono wakwiliye mu mulimo wuwu.

9 Mu mhuga ya Meshelemiya, mwali mowali wanhu yikumi na munana, wono wali wakwilile mu mulimo wuwu.

10 Namo mu mhuga ya Melali, kwali ko yuli Hosa. Yuyu Hosa, yali yena wana wane, Shimuli, (yuyu Shimuli, u sogwe yakamuwika yawe yo mulongola, hamba yawe yali yo si mwana mulongozi kulelwa.)
11 Hilikiya, Tobaliya, na Zekaliya. Nawo wakawa waficile wanhu yikumi na wadatu, wose hamonga mwi'walo lyawo, wa mu mhuga ya Hosa, wono wali wo walindilizi we Nyumba yo Mulungu.

12 Nawo wawa i walindilizi we Nyumba yo Mulungu, wakegawa mu vipuga vipuga mu nghango ye zimhuga zawo. Nawo ho wakawa kuwikwa mu mulimo wo wulindilizi we Nyumba yo Mulungu, nha vyono wetumaga i Walawi.
13 Nazo mhuga zose zikatowelwa kula, ho yimanyice mhuga no mulango wa hayi wono wowulindiliza.
14 Ne zi kula zizo zali zeza kutowa, hobaho Shelemiya yakapegwa mulango wa kucilima. Nayo Zekaliya u mwanagwe, u munhu mono nhondo zose yena nonga yakwe ya mahala, mwene yakapegwa yalindilize mulango wa kusumuka.
15 Nayo Obedi Edomu yakapegwa yalindilize mulango wa kutakama. Nawo wanagwe, wakapegwa walindilize nyumba ye viwutwa.
16 Shupimu hamonga na Hosa, wakapegwa walindilize mulango wa ku mwezi, hamonga no mulango wa Shaleketi, wa mu balabala yono yibitile ku cinanuko. No wulindilizi wuwo wono wali wagawiywe, welecelaga munhu na cipango cakwe.
17 Ku lubavu lwa kucilima, wekalaga walindilizi wali mutandatu nhondo zose. Ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, wane, na ku lubavu lwa kutakama, nako wane. Kanyhi na mu zinyumba ze viwutwa, wakawikwa nako wanhu wane wane vilo vyose, wejete, wejete, mu zinyumba zose.
18 Na kono ko yili Palebali, ku lubavu lwa kumwezi, nako kwali kowali walindilizi wane, walindilizaga ku balabala, ne wejete nawo, kono ko yili Palebali.
19 Na wuwu wali wo wugawe we milimo yo wulindilizi, kuli we mhuga ya Kola, na kuli we mhuga ya Melali.

I milimo yinji kanyhi ya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu

20 Na wanji kanyhi wa muli Walawi, wakapegwa mulimo wo wulanjilizi we zisawo za mu Nyumba yo Mulungu na ya mu zinyumba ze viwutwa, vye vipegwa vilavye kuli Mulungu.
21 Nayo Ladani muyagwe ne wana wa Gelishoni, yali yo mulezi we zimhuga zinji, hamonga ne mhuga ya Yahiyeli mwanagwe.
22 Zetamu na Yoweli, i wana wa Ladani, wali wo walanjilizi we sawo ya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne zinyumba ze viwutwa.

23 Ne milimo yinji kanyhi wakapegwa i wana wa Amulamu, na Izihali, na Hebuloni, na Uziyeli.

24 Nayo Shebuweli we mhuga ya Gelishoni, u mwana wa Musa, yuyo nayo, yali yo mwitumami muwaha, mono yo yali yena wulajilize muli sawo yose ya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
25 Na muli Eleyezali u nduguye wa Gelishoni, mwene Shebuweli wali wali ndugu na Shelomiti. Mwene Eleyezali yali yo sogwe wa Lehabiya, nayo Lehabiya, yakamulela Yeshiya, nayo Yeshiya yakamulela Yolamu, nayo Yolamu yakamulela Zikili, nayo Zikili yakamulela Shelomiti.
26 Nayo Shelomiti hamonga na wa mu mhuga yakwe, wali wo walanjilizi we vipegwa vyose vyono yavyeza mutemi Dawudi, ne wawaha wose we zimhuga, ne walongola wose we zimhuga, mu vipuga vyawo, hamonga ne wetumami wawaha we wasilikale.
27 Wakavisola ne vinhu vinji vyono waviteka ku wulugu, navyo wakavyeza, ho vitumamilwe mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
28 Nayo Shelomiti, hamonga na we mhuga yakwe, wali wo walanjilizi we vinhu vyose vyono vikezwa, ho vitumamilwe mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne vipegwa vyono vyaletwa no muwizi Samwili, no mutemi Sawuli, ko muwoko wa Abineli u mwana wa Neli, hamonga na Yowabu u mwana wa Zeluya.

I milimo yinji ye Walawi

29 Na mu wana wa Izihali, Kenaniya hamonga ne wanagwe, wo wapegwa mulimo wo kuwika vikumbucizo vye zinghani, hamonga no wuling'anizi muli Waisilayeli.

30 Na mu wana wa Hebuloni, Hashabiya hamonga ne wanduguze, waficile wanhu elufu yikumi na mupunghati, wose wono wali wasigaye, wakapegwa mulimo wo wululicizi we milimo yose yo Muwaha Mulungu, na ku so witumami wo mutemi, mu Isilayeli yo lubavu lwa kumwezi ku nhumbi ye likolongo lya Yoludani.
31 Nayo Yeliya, yali yo mulongola we wana wa Hebuloni. Na mu mwaka we makumi mane, lono Dawudi yali yakali yatemile, i wana wose wa mu mhuga ya Hebuloni, wakalondolwa, na yikejela vyono kowali wanji wasilikale we mhuga yiyo, wono wasigala wali wekaye ku Yazeli, mu yisi ya Gileyadi.
32 Dawudi yakawahagula wanhu elufu nyejete na magana mupunghati, wono wali wasigaye, i wawaha we zimhuga ze wanduguze za Yeliya, yakawawika wawe walulicizi we milimo yose yo Mulungu, na ku so witumami wo mutemi, mu Isilayeli yo lubavu lwa kucilima ku nhumbi ye likolongo lya Yoludani, mu yisi ya Lubeni, na Gadi, hamonga na ya Manase ya kucilima.


1 Zinghomo 27

Wityatye we milimo yo wusilikale wo wulugu, na we milimo ye Selekali

Na mu myezi yose ya mu mwaka mututu, welecelaga vipuga hayo hayo, vipuga vya wanhu elufu makumi mejete na yine, hasi yo wulanjilizi wo mulanjilizi monga, wa mu mwezi wuwo.
2 Na awa hano wo wali walanjilizi wawaha wa mu myezi yose.
3 Mwezi mulongozi Yashobeyamu u mwana wa Zabudiyeli. (Nayo yali wa mhuga ya Pelezi, ninga kunji kanyhi yali wo mulango wa Yuda.)
4 Mwezi we kejete, Dodayi u mwana we Ahohi. (Nayo Mikiloti yali yo mutyatyi wakwe mu wulongola.)
5 Mwezi we kadatu, Benaya u mwana wa Yehoyoda u mukohani. Kanyhi yali yo muwaha we cipuga ce wanhu makumi madatu.
6 (No mwanagwe Amizabadi yo yakahala wulongola we cipuga cico.)

8 Mwezi we hano: Shamuhuti, u mwana wa Izihali.
9 Mwezi wo mutandatu Ila, u mwana wa Ikeshi, munya Tekowa.
10 Mwezi wo mupunghati: Helezi mu Efulayimu, munya Peloni.
11 Mwezi wo munana: Sibekayi, munya Husha. (Nayo yali we mhuga ya Zela, kunji kanyhi yakawa wo mulango wa Yuda.)
12 Mwezi we licenda: Abiyezeli, munya Anatoti, mu yisi ya wo mulango wa Benyamini.
13 Mwezi we likumi: Mahalayi, munya Netofa. (Nayo yali we mhuga ya Zela.)
14 Mwezi we likumi na monga: Benaya, munya Pilatoni, mu yisi ya wo mulango wa Efulayimu.
15 Mwezi we likumi na nyejete: Helidayi, munya Netofa, (Nayo yali mwana wa Otiniyeli.)

Wululicizi we zimhuga ze Waisilayeli

16 Ali ahano lyo liwalo lye walulicizi we zimhuga ze Waisilayeli: Wo mulango wa Lubeni, mululicizi Eliyazali u mwana wa Zikili: Wo mulango wa Simiyoni, mululicizi Shefatiya u mwana wa Maka.

19 Wo mulango wa Zabuloni, mululicizi, Ishimaya u mwana wa Obadiya. Wo mulango wa Nafutali, mululicizi, Yelemoti u mwana wa Aziliyeli.
20 Wo mulango wa Efulayimu, mululicizi, Hosheya u mwana wa Azaziya. Wa Manase wa kumwezi, mululicizi, Yoweli u mwana wa Pedaya.
21 Wa Manase wa kucilima, mululicizi Ido u mwana wa Zekaliya. Wo mulango wa Benyamini, mululicizi, Yasiyeli u mwana wa Abineli.
22 Wo mulango wa Dani, mululicizi Azaleli u mwana wa Yelohamu.

23 Ninga mutemi Dawudi si yawawala i wanhu wono wali si waficile myaka makumi mejete yaye, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yehuwiza nha vyono kowanoza Waisilayeli, wonjelezece wawe wenji muno nhe zinyelezi za ku wulanga.
24 Ninga Yowabu munghalo nyina mono yacemwaga Zeluyiya, yali yakondya kwanza kusola liwalo lye wanhu, ninga hamba nhavivyo, si yasuma kukondeleza yaye. Na ku soko yiyo, Mulungu yakawalipiciza Waisilayeli ku soko yo wuwale we wanhu. Na holeka liwalo lye ndumilizo lye wanhu, si lyasumika ku wumanyice yaye. Na holeka si lyandikwa yaye na mu vitabu vyo wutemi wo mutemi Dawudi.

30 Zingamiya, mululicizi, Obilimu Ishumayeli;
31 Zindogowe, mululicizi, Yehidaya munya Melonoti. Zingholo ne Zimhene, mululicizi Yazizi Muhajili.

I watazi we mahweso wo mutemi Dawudi

32 Yonazani u mwihwawe wo mutemi Dawudi, yali munhu we mahala kanyhi yo yamutazaga mahweso mutemi Dawudi kanyhi yali musomi. Mwene Yonazani, hamonga na Yehiyeli u mwana wa Hakimoni, wali wo walanjilizi we wana wo mutemi.
33 Nayo Ahitofeli yali yo mutazi we mahweso wo mutemi. Nayo Hushayi Mwaliki, yali yo mwendece wo mutemi, kanyhi yali yo muling'anizi.
34 Ninga Ahitofeli yali yeza kufwa, hodu Abiyatali na Yehoyada u mwana wa Benaya, wakawa wo watazi we mahweso wo mutemi Dawudi. Nayo Yowabu yali yo muwaha we wasilikale wo mutemi, i wasilikale wose wo wulugu.


1 Zinghomo 28

Malajilizo go mutemi Dawudi, kuli ze Nyumba yo Mulungu

1 Mutemi Dawudi, yakawacema wawaha wose we Waisilayeli, wehangulizize ku Yelusalemi. Holeka wawaha wose wa mu milango ye Waisilayeli, ne wawaha walulicizi wo mulimo wo wutemi, ne walongola wose wa mu zimhuga, ne walanjilizi we sawo ne mitugo zo mutemi, ne wanagwe, ne wetumami wa mwi'kulu, ne wawaha we wasilikale, hamonga ne wanhu wawaha, wose wawo wakehanguliza ku Yelusalemi.

2 Dawudi yakema ha mwande yawo, yakawalonjela yakatya, “Ale, muhulicize anye mo wandugu zangu na mo wanhu wangu. Ane nali nina wuhile wo kuyizenga nyumba yono Lisanduku lye Cilagane lyo Muwaha Mulungu, ne ligoda lyo wuwiko we migulu yakwe. Sunga nalu nali nakondya kuviwikaniziza lukulu vinhu vyose vyo kuyizenjela yiyo Nyumba yo Mulungu.
3 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yanemeza ane ho ndece kuyizenga, vyono ane, nili musilikale, mbulaje no kumwaga sakami ya wanhu wenji.
4 Holeka Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli, yahagula ane hamonga no lulelo lwangu ciyiteme Isilayeli cibitilila. Yakawuhagula mulango wa Yuda, na mumo mu mulango wa Yuda, yakayihagula mhuga yo sogo yangu. Na ku wulawa mu mhuga yiyo, Muwaha Mulungu yakendezwa yasole ane, yanoze niwe mutemi we Waisilayeli wose. Kanyhi yakamhela wana wenji.
5 Na muli wanangu wawo, Muwaha Mulungu yakamuhagula mwanangu Solomona, yateme muli Waisilayeli, i wanhu wo Mulungu.”
6 Muwaha Mulungu yakanyitya, “U mwanago Solomona yo konzenjela Nyumba yangu. Hambi nha nimuhagula yawe mwanangu, nane nowa nili sogwe.
7 Nane nowukangaza wutemi wakwe, wikale cibitilila, hono mwene nayo yajendelele kugebata malajilizo gangu, hamonga no wulajilize wayo nha vyono yakunoza lulu.”

8 “Holeka aluno anye mo wanhu wangu, mono muli baha ha mwande yo Mulungu wetu, hamonga no wihangulize wuwu we Waisilayeli wose we wanhu wo Mulungu. Ane nghumulajilizenyi, mwibatile lukulu zose zono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu yacilajiliza, ho mujendelele kuyihala yisi yiyi swanu yono mwikaye, ho musume nanye kuyihaza kuli zindelo zenyu zono zikuza, cibitilila.”

9 Na ku lubavu lwa Solomona, yakamulonjela yakatya, “Gwe mwanangu Solomona, nghukulajiliza nagwe nhavivyo, wumumanyile lukulu Mulungu wo sogo, kanyhi wumwitumamile kwe nhumbula yako yose, hamonga no mutima muzelu. Hambi mwene nha yagamanyile mahweso getu gose, hamonga ne visakwa vyetu. Holeka hono wumubitile, mwene kokubocela, ninga hono wijelele wumulece, mwene nhondo, kukulecela lukulu cibitilila.
10 Kanyhi yikunojeye wumanye, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu kakuhagula gwegwe, ho wumuzenjele Nyumba yakwe yono Yelile. Holeka luno nghukunena wuyizenje, kanyhi wuyizengaje ko wuhidimbazi.”

11 Hanyuma Dawudi yakawa kumupela Solomona, mahweso gose ne vihwani vyose vye lizengo lyose lye Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne vihwani vye zinyumba ze viwutwa, ne zinyumba zinji zose, hamonga na vye i Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno, i hanhu hono wubi wukuwa kunenelelwa.
12 Hanyuma kanyhi, yakamuloloseza ze mahweso gakwe vyono yali yehwesize, vyono wozengwa wuduwilo we Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne vinyumba vyose vyono viyipilime, ne vinyumba vyo kuwicila viwutwa vye vitumamilwa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne vipegwa vyono vyakondya kwezwa no Muwaha Mulungu.
13 Kanyhi Dawudi, yakamupela Solomona zo wityatye wose we milimo ye wakohani, hamonga ne Walawi, mu wilecele we milimo yawo. I milimo ya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga no wulanjilizi we vitumamilwa vyose vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.

14 Yakamulanjiza nha vyono yinojeye fweza na zahabu, zitumamilwe mu wunoza vitumamilwa.
15 Hamonga ne zitala ne ciwicilo ce tala.
16 Hamonga ne zimeza za fweza, ne zimeza zose ze zahabu, zono zali njelece mikate milavywe kuli Mulungu.
17 Yakamulajiliza kanyhi kuli ze nghango ye zahabu nengu, yono yowa yinojeye yitumamilwe mu wunoza vicinyilo, ne zibakuli, hamonga ne vidumu, ne nghango ye fweza yono yowa yinojeye yitumamilwe so wunoza zisahani,
18 ne nghango ye zahabu nengu, yono yokwila kucinoza cikumbicilo co kufusiza wuvumba wono wafusaga, hamonga no wunoza mukokoteni, so wudinghulila vihwani vye makelubi, gono gagoloye mabawa gayo mucanyha ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane lyo Muwaha Mulungu.
19 Hanyuma yayo, mutemi Dawudi, kanyhi yakatya, “Ninga zizi zose zili mu vihwani vyono vyandicigwe nha vyono winha wulajilize wono mweneco Muwaha Mulugu yandajila yakamhela.”

20 Dawudi kanyhi yakamulonjela mwanagwe Solomona yakatya, “Solomona, weze citung'ati, kanyhi wuli muhidimbazi. Aluno wanze mulimo, ninga wulece kuleka nghani yinji yo yose yikwimile mu mulimo wako. Hambi u Muwaha Mulungu mono nghumwitumamila, nha kowa yali hamonga nagwe. Nayo hamba si kokulecela lukulu, langa kowa yali hamonga nagwe, sunga kono kolokondezya milimo yose yono yikusakwa yitumwe mu Nyumba yakwe.
21 Ninga nha vyono wumanyile, i wakohani hamonga ne Walawi, awo wakondya kupegwa milimo yawo yono yiwanojeye wetume, mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Nawo i wetumami wose wono wena wunghula hayo hayo we milimo, awo yiwanojeye wakutaze. Kanyhi i wanhu wose hamonga ne walongola wawo, wawa wose wali mu wulajilize wako gwegwe.”


1 Zinghomo 29

Vipegwa so wuzenjela Nyumba yo Mulungu

1 Hanyuma mutemi Dawudi yakawa kanyhi kuwalonjela wanhu wose wono wali wehangulize baho, yakawatya, “Mwanangu Solomona, yo yuyu mono Mulungu yamuhaguye. Ninga 'lu yakali mudodo, kanyhi yakali yasina wuzowele wo mulimo wuwu. No mulimo wuwu nawo ho muwaha muno, hambi nyumba yiyi yo mutemi, nha si nyumba ya munhu, langa yo Nyumba yo Mulungu.
2 Ninga nane nakondya kwituma zose zono nali sumile kwituma, hambi nha nakondya kuviwikaniza vinhu vyose vyono vyosakwa kuwuzenjela Nyumba yo Mulungu, nha cinji, zahabu, fweza, shaba, cuma, mbawo, hamonga ne mabwe go wuguzi wukamu, ne vibwe vye mawala hayo hayo, ne mabwe ge malumalu, hamonga ne mabwe ge mihilimo yose.
3 Hamonga ne vinhu vivyo vyose vyono nakondya kulavya, ninga nane no mweneco nakondya kulavya fweza, na zahabu ku wulawa mu sawo yangu no mweneco, ku soko yo wendo wangu kuli Nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu.
4 Kanyhi hamba nhavivyo, nakondya kulavya kanyhi tani yigana limonga lya zahabu nengu, kanyhi hamonga na tani zono zicizize magana mejete na makumi mane za fweza, so mulimo wo kuhamba majinji ge Nyumba yo Mulungu,
5 hamonga ne vinhu vyose vyono vinojeye vinozwe ne walula. Alu nani mono yakwilavya na mutima muzelu, ho yamulavizye cilavywa Muwaha Mulungu?”

6 Hobaho i wawaha wa mu zimhuga, ne walongola we milango, ne wawaha we wasilikale wo wulugu, hamonga ne walulicizi we sawo yo mutemi, wakawa kwilavya weneco, wamulavizye Mulungu
7 vinhu avi, so mulimo wa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Holeka wakalavya vinhu avi: Zahabu, kuciza tani yigana limonga, na makumi mupunghati. Fweza, kuciza tani magana madatu na makumi mane. Shaba, kuciza tani magana mutandatu na makumi mejete. Cuma, kuciza tani elufu yidatu, na magana mane.
8 Nawo wono wali wena mabwe go wuguzi wukamu, wakagalavya, wakawika mu ciwutwa ce Nyumba yo Mulungu. No mululicizi wayo, yali Yahiyeli Mulawi we mhuga ya Gelishoni.
9 Ne wanhu wose wakamulavizya Muwaha Mulungu ko mutima muzelu, akuno wakawa wasangalaye muno so vinhu vinji muno vyono vikalavywa. Sunga no mutemi Dawudi, nayo yakasangalala muno muno.

Dawudi yakumukumya Mulungu

10 Mutemi Dawudi yakawa kumukumya Muwaha Mulungu ha mwande ye wanhu wose wono wali wehangulize baho, yakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wo mulezi wetu Yakobo, Agwe yo gwegwe mono wunojeye lukumyo cibitilila ne cibitilila!
11 Agwe yo gwegwe mono wuwaha wo wako, kanyhi ne vilungo, no lukumyo, no wusume, no wutemi. Ne vinhu vyose vya ku wulanga na vyono vili mono yinze, vyose nha vyako, kanyhi agwe wutemile vyose, kanyhi wuli muwaha muli vyose.
12 Wugoli wose, hamonga ne lilumbo, vyose vilawaga kwako. Nagwe wutemile muli vyose kwe vilungo vyako no wusume wako. Kanyhi yo gwegwe du mono kumunozaga munhu kawa ne lilumbo, ne vilungo.
13 Holeka alu Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, cikukulumba kanyhi cikulilumba litagwa lyako lyono lyelile.

14 “Ninga hamba nhavivyo, nene no mweneco, hamonga ne wanhu wangu, si cosuma kutya nase cikupela cinji, hambi vinhu vyose nha gwegwe mono kucipelaga'se, alu nase nha cikukugomolela du vyono vyako na katali.
15 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, agwe nha wumanyiye lukulu, nha vyono ase cakolela mu mikalile yo wuhamywe, hamonga no wujenzi, nha vyono wali i walezi wetu. Ne vilo vyetu, vinha mwenzenze du wono wupulutaga, nase hamba si cosuma kwiponya ne lifwa.
16 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wetu, ase wose hamonga, cikuletela sawo yiyi yose, so wuzenjela Nyumba yako, ho cilikumye litagwa lyako lyono lyelile. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, i vinhu vivi vyose nha vyako du gwegwe, kanyhi vilawaga kwako.
17 Nane manyile nha vyono agwe wukuwa kuzijela nhumbula, nagwe wukuwa kwendezwa ne wanhu wono wena nhumbula zono zikogopile gwegwe. Alu nane, ku soko ye cogopo cono nina naco kuli'gwe, holeka ndavya vinhu vivino vyose, so wendezwe wangu nene no mweneco. Kanyhi nghondya no kuyiwona ne nyemo yono yili muli wanhu wako wawa, wono wehangulize baha so vinhu vyono wakulavizye gwegwe.
18 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu u Mulungu we walezi wetu Abulahamu na Isaka, na Yakobo, wukangaze nghani yiyi cibitilila mu zinhumbula ze wanhu wako. Kanyhi wuwakamise, nhondo zose wekale wehuwilwa kuli'gwe.
19 Wumupelaje mwanangu Solomona nhumbula nhuvu, yebate wulajilize wako, yawitumame, kanyhi yayizenje ne Nyumba yako yiyi, ye nhojo, yono nakondya kuyiwicila ne vinhu vyose vyo kuyizenjela.”

20 Hanyuma Dawudi yakawalajiliza wanhu yakatya, “Ale, mukumyenyi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu.” Ne lipuga lyose lye wanhu, wakamukumya Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo, wakenama, wakamupela lilumbo Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga no mutemi.

21 Na mwi'zuwa lye kejete, wakawulaga mitugo, wakalavya makumbiko, wakeyeza so Muwaha Mulungu, na hanyuma wakawapela wanhu walye. Hamonga na zizo, wakalavya makumbiko, ng'ombe elufu monga, na nduwilu elufu monga, na nyana za ngholo nazo elufu monga. Zono wazinyanya mu moto, mucanyha ye cikumbicilo zili muvilima. Kanyhi wakaleta ne vilavywa vye divayi.
22 Wakalya, wakang'wa hali Muwaha Mulungu, wakawa wena nyemo mbaha muno mwi'zuwa lilyo.

Wakamutemya lwe kejete Solomona, wakamwitila mafuta mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu, yawe mutemi wawo, nayo Zadoki yawe yo mukohani.
23 Hobaho Solomona yakahala wutemi wo sogwe Dawudi, yakekala mwi'goda lye nhojo, lyono Muwaha Mulungu yaliwikaniza. Solomona yakamoteswa muno, ne Waisilayeli wose wamuhulikaga.
24 Hobaho, i wetumami wose, hamonga ne wasilikale wose, sunga ne wana wanji wa Dawudi, wawo wose, wakehuwiza nha vyono womuhulika mutemi Solomona.
25 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamukumya Solomona, ha meso ge Waisilayeli wose, yakamupela nhojo yono yilawiliciye muli watemi wose we Waisilayeli wono wamulongolela kutema muli Waisilayeli.

Wulolose we ndumula wa zo wutemi wa Dawudi

26 Dawudi mwana wa Yese, yatema myaka
27 makumi mane mu Isilayeli yose. Yakatema myaka mupunghati mu Hebuloni, na myaka makumi madatu na midatu, mu Yelusalemi.
28 Yakafwa yena myaka minji, yena ne sawo nyinji, hamonga ne lilumbo. No mwanagwe Solomona, yakahala wutemi wakwe.
29 Ne zinghomo so mutemi Dawudi, ku wulawilila ku mwamulizo, sunga ko ku mudumo wakwe, zose zandicigwe mu vilo vye wawizi wadatu, nha cinji Samwili, Natani, na Gadi.
30 Nazo zikulolosa nha vyono yali yatemile, na vyono yali munhu we vilungo mu wutemi wakwe, hamonga ne zinghani zose zono zamwijelela, kuli Waisilayeli, sunga na kuli watemi wose wa mu zi'si zono zali zimupilime.
