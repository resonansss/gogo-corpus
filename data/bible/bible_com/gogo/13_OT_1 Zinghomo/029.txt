1 Zinghomo 29

Vipegwa so wuzenjela Nyumba yo Mulungu

1 Hanyuma mutemi Dawudi yakawa kanyhi kuwalonjela wanhu wose wono wali wehangulize baho, yakawatya, “Mwanangu Solomona, yo yuyu mono Mulungu yamuhaguye. Ninga 'lu yakali mudodo, kanyhi yakali yasina wuzowele wo mulimo wuwu. No mulimo wuwu nawo ho muwaha muno, hambi nyumba yiyi yo mutemi, nha si nyumba ya munhu, langa yo Nyumba yo Mulungu.
2 Ninga nane nakondya kwituma zose zono nali sumile kwituma, hambi nha nakondya kuviwikaniza vinhu vyose vyono vyosakwa kuwuzenjela Nyumba yo Mulungu, nha cinji, zahabu, fweza, shaba, cuma, mbawo, hamonga ne mabwe go wuguzi wukamu, ne vibwe vye mawala hayo hayo, ne mabwe ge malumalu, hamonga ne mabwe ge mihilimo yose.
3 Hamonga ne vinhu vivyo vyose vyono nakondya kulavya, ninga nane no mweneco nakondya kulavya fweza, na zahabu ku wulawa mu sawo yangu no mweneco, ku soko yo wendo wangu kuli Nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu.
4 Kanyhi hamba nhavivyo, nakondya kulavya kanyhi tani yigana limonga lya zahabu nengu, kanyhi hamonga na tani zono zicizize magana mejete na makumi mane za fweza, so mulimo wo kuhamba majinji ge Nyumba yo Mulungu,
5 hamonga ne vinhu vyose vyono vinojeye vinozwe ne walula. Alu nani mono yakwilavya na mutima muzelu, ho yamulavizye cilavywa Muwaha Mulungu?”

6 Hobaho i wawaha wa mu zimhuga, ne walongola we milango, ne wawaha we wasilikale wo wulugu, hamonga ne walulicizi we sawo yo mutemi, wakawa kwilavya weneco, wamulavizye Mulungu
7 vinhu avi, so mulimo wa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Holeka wakalavya vinhu avi: Zahabu, kuciza tani yigana limonga, na makumi mupunghati. Fweza, kuciza tani magana madatu na makumi mane. Shaba, kuciza tani magana mutandatu na makumi mejete. Cuma, kuciza tani elufu yidatu, na magana mane.
8 Nawo wono wali wena mabwe go wuguzi wukamu, wakagalavya, wakawika mu ciwutwa ce Nyumba yo Mulungu. No mululicizi wayo, yali Yahiyeli Mulawi we mhuga ya Gelishoni.
9 Ne wanhu wose wakamulavizya Muwaha Mulungu ko mutima muzelu, akuno wakawa wasangalaye muno so vinhu vinji muno vyono vikalavywa. Sunga no mutemi Dawudi, nayo yakasangalala muno muno.

Dawudi yakumukumya Mulungu

10 Mutemi Dawudi yakawa kumukumya Muwaha Mulungu ha mwande ye wanhu wose wono wali wehangulize baho, yakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wo mulezi wetu Yakobo, Agwe yo gwegwe mono wunojeye lukumyo cibitilila ne cibitilila!
11 Agwe yo gwegwe mono wuwaha wo wako, kanyhi ne vilungo, no lukumyo, no wusume, no wutemi. Ne vinhu vyose vya ku wulanga na vyono vili mono yinze, vyose nha vyako, kanyhi agwe wutemile vyose, kanyhi wuli muwaha muli vyose.
12 Wugoli wose, hamonga ne lilumbo, vyose vilawaga kwako. Nagwe wutemile muli vyose kwe vilungo vyako no wusume wako. Kanyhi yo gwegwe du mono kumunozaga munhu kawa ne lilumbo, ne vilungo.
13 Holeka alu Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, cikukulumba kanyhi cikulilumba litagwa lyako lyono lyelile.

14 “Ninga hamba nhavivyo, nene no mweneco, hamonga ne wanhu wangu, si cosuma kutya nase cikupela cinji, hambi vinhu vyose nha gwegwe mono kucipelaga'se, alu nase nha cikukugomolela du vyono vyako na katali.
15 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, agwe nha wumanyiye lukulu, nha vyono ase cakolela mu mikalile yo wuhamywe, hamonga no wujenzi, nha vyono wali i walezi wetu. Ne vilo vyetu, vinha mwenzenze du wono wupulutaga, nase hamba si cosuma kwiponya ne lifwa.
16 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wetu, ase wose hamonga, cikuletela sawo yiyi yose, so wuzenjela Nyumba yako, ho cilikumye litagwa lyako lyono lyelile. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, i vinhu vivi vyose nha vyako du gwegwe, kanyhi vilawaga kwako.
17 Nane manyile nha vyono agwe wukuwa kuzijela nhumbula, nagwe wukuwa kwendezwa ne wanhu wono wena nhumbula zono zikogopile gwegwe. Alu nane, ku soko ye cogopo cono nina naco kuli'gwe, holeka ndavya vinhu vivino vyose, so wendezwe wangu nene no mweneco. Kanyhi nghondya no kuyiwona ne nyemo yono yili muli wanhu wako wawa, wono wehangulize baha so vinhu vyono wakulavizye gwegwe.
18 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu u Mulungu we walezi wetu Abulahamu na Isaka, na Yakobo, wukangaze nghani yiyi cibitilila mu zinhumbula ze wanhu wako. Kanyhi wuwakamise, nhondo zose wekale wehuwilwa kuli'gwe.
19 Wumupelaje mwanangu Solomona nhumbula nhuvu, yebate wulajilize wako, yawitumame, kanyhi yayizenje ne Nyumba yako yiyi, ye nhojo, yono nakondya kuyiwicila ne vinhu vyose vyo kuyizenjela.”

20 Hanyuma Dawudi yakawalajiliza wanhu yakatya, “Ale, mukumyenyi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu.” Ne lipuga lyose lye wanhu, wakamukumya Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo, wakenama, wakamupela lilumbo Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga no mutemi.

21 Na mwi'zuwa lye kejete, wakawulaga mitugo, wakalavya makumbiko, wakeyeza so Muwaha Mulungu, na hanyuma wakawapela wanhu walye. Hamonga na zizo, wakalavya makumbiko, ng'ombe elufu monga, na nduwilu elufu monga, na nyana za ngholo nazo elufu monga. Zono wazinyanya mu moto, mucanyha ye cikumbicilo zili muvilima. Kanyhi wakaleta ne vilavywa vye divayi.
22 Wakalya, wakang'wa hali Muwaha Mulungu, wakawa wena nyemo mbaha muno mwi'zuwa lilyo.

Wakamutemya lwe kejete Solomona, wakamwitila mafuta mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu, yawe mutemi wawo, nayo Zadoki yawe yo mukohani.
23 Hobaho Solomona yakahala wutemi wo sogwe Dawudi, yakekala mwi'goda lye nhojo, lyono Muwaha Mulungu yaliwikaniza. Solomona yakamoteswa muno, ne Waisilayeli wose wamuhulikaga.
24 Hobaho, i wetumami wose, hamonga ne wasilikale wose, sunga ne wana wanji wa Dawudi, wawo wose, wakehuwiza nha vyono womuhulika mutemi Solomona.
25 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamukumya Solomona, ha meso ge Waisilayeli wose, yakamupela nhojo yono yilawiliciye muli watemi wose we Waisilayeli wono wamulongolela kutema muli Waisilayeli.

Wulolose we ndumula wa zo wutemi wa Dawudi

26 Dawudi mwana wa Yese, yatema myaka
27 makumi mane mu Isilayeli yose. Yakatema myaka mupunghati mu Hebuloni, na myaka makumi madatu na midatu, mu Yelusalemi.
28 Yakafwa yena myaka minji, yena ne sawo nyinji, hamonga ne lilumbo. No mwanagwe Solomona, yakahala wutemi wakwe.
29 Ne zinghomo so mutemi Dawudi, ku wulawilila ku mwamulizo, sunga ko ku mudumo wakwe, zose zandicigwe mu vilo vye wawizi wadatu, nha cinji Samwili, Natani, na Gadi.
30 Nazo zikulolosa nha vyono yali yatemile, na vyono yali munhu we vilungo mu wutemi wakwe, hamonga ne zinghani zose zono zamwijelela, kuli Waisilayeli, sunga na kuli watemi wose wa mu zi'si zono zali zimupilime.
