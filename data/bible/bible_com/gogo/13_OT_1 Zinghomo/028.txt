1 Zinghomo 28

Malajilizo go mutemi Dawudi, kuli ze Nyumba yo Mulungu

1 Mutemi Dawudi, yakawacema wawaha wose we Waisilayeli, wehangulizize ku Yelusalemi. Holeka wawaha wose wa mu milango ye Waisilayeli, ne wawaha walulicizi wo mulimo wo wutemi, ne walongola wose wa mu zimhuga, ne walanjilizi we sawo ne mitugo zo mutemi, ne wanagwe, ne wetumami wa mwi'kulu, ne wawaha we wasilikale, hamonga ne wanhu wawaha, wose wawo wakehanguliza ku Yelusalemi.

2 Dawudi yakema ha mwande yawo, yakawalonjela yakatya, “Ale, muhulicize anye mo wandugu zangu na mo wanhu wangu. Ane nali nina wuhile wo kuyizenga nyumba yono Lisanduku lye Cilagane lyo Muwaha Mulungu, ne ligoda lyo wuwiko we migulu yakwe. Sunga nalu nali nakondya kuviwikaniziza lukulu vinhu vyose vyo kuyizenjela yiyo Nyumba yo Mulungu.
3 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yanemeza ane ho ndece kuyizenga, vyono ane, nili musilikale, mbulaje no kumwaga sakami ya wanhu wenji.
4 Holeka Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli, yahagula ane hamonga no lulelo lwangu ciyiteme Isilayeli cibitilila. Yakawuhagula mulango wa Yuda, na mumo mu mulango wa Yuda, yakayihagula mhuga yo sogo yangu. Na ku wulawa mu mhuga yiyo, Muwaha Mulungu yakendezwa yasole ane, yanoze niwe mutemi we Waisilayeli wose. Kanyhi yakamhela wana wenji.
5 Na muli wanangu wawo, Muwaha Mulungu yakamuhagula mwanangu Solomona, yateme muli Waisilayeli, i wanhu wo Mulungu.”
6 Muwaha Mulungu yakanyitya, “U mwanago Solomona yo konzenjela Nyumba yangu. Hambi nha nimuhagula yawe mwanangu, nane nowa nili sogwe.
7 Nane nowukangaza wutemi wakwe, wikale cibitilila, hono mwene nayo yajendelele kugebata malajilizo gangu, hamonga no wulajilize wayo nha vyono yakunoza lulu.”

8 “Holeka aluno anye mo wanhu wangu, mono muli baha ha mwande yo Mulungu wetu, hamonga no wihangulize wuwu we Waisilayeli wose we wanhu wo Mulungu. Ane nghumulajilizenyi, mwibatile lukulu zose zono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu yacilajiliza, ho mujendelele kuyihala yisi yiyi swanu yono mwikaye, ho musume nanye kuyihaza kuli zindelo zenyu zono zikuza, cibitilila.”

9 Na ku lubavu lwa Solomona, yakamulonjela yakatya, “Gwe mwanangu Solomona, nghukulajiliza nagwe nhavivyo, wumumanyile lukulu Mulungu wo sogo, kanyhi wumwitumamile kwe nhumbula yako yose, hamonga no mutima muzelu. Hambi mwene nha yagamanyile mahweso getu gose, hamonga ne visakwa vyetu. Holeka hono wumubitile, mwene kokubocela, ninga hono wijelele wumulece, mwene nhondo, kukulecela lukulu cibitilila.
10 Kanyhi yikunojeye wumanye, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu kakuhagula gwegwe, ho wumuzenjele Nyumba yakwe yono Yelile. Holeka luno nghukunena wuyizenje, kanyhi wuyizengaje ko wuhidimbazi.”

11 Hanyuma Dawudi yakawa kumupela Solomona, mahweso gose ne vihwani vyose vye lizengo lyose lye Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne vihwani vye zinyumba ze viwutwa, ne zinyumba zinji zose, hamonga na vye i Hanhu Hono Helile, Helile muno, i hanhu hono wubi wukuwa kunenelelwa.
12 Hanyuma kanyhi, yakamuloloseza ze mahweso gakwe vyono yali yehwesize, vyono wozengwa wuduwilo we Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne vinyumba vyose vyono viyipilime, ne vinyumba vyo kuwicila viwutwa vye vitumamilwa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne vipegwa vyono vyakondya kwezwa no Muwaha Mulungu.
13 Kanyhi Dawudi, yakamupela Solomona zo wityatye wose we milimo ye wakohani, hamonga ne Walawi, mu wilecele we milimo yawo. I milimo ya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga no wulanjilizi we vitumamilwa vyose vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.

14 Yakamulanjiza nha vyono yinojeye fweza na zahabu, zitumamilwe mu wunoza vitumamilwa.
15 Hamonga ne zitala ne ciwicilo ce tala.
16 Hamonga ne zimeza za fweza, ne zimeza zose ze zahabu, zono zali njelece mikate milavywe kuli Mulungu.
17 Yakamulajiliza kanyhi kuli ze nghango ye zahabu nengu, yono yowa yinojeye yitumamilwe mu wunoza vicinyilo, ne zibakuli, hamonga ne vidumu, ne nghango ye fweza yono yowa yinojeye yitumamilwe so wunoza zisahani,
18 ne nghango ye zahabu nengu, yono yokwila kucinoza cikumbicilo co kufusiza wuvumba wono wafusaga, hamonga no wunoza mukokoteni, so wudinghulila vihwani vye makelubi, gono gagoloye mabawa gayo mucanyha ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane lyo Muwaha Mulungu.
19 Hanyuma yayo, mutemi Dawudi, kanyhi yakatya, “Ninga zizi zose zili mu vihwani vyono vyandicigwe nha vyono winha wulajilize wono mweneco Muwaha Mulugu yandajila yakamhela.”

20 Dawudi kanyhi yakamulonjela mwanagwe Solomona yakatya, “Solomona, weze citung'ati, kanyhi wuli muhidimbazi. Aluno wanze mulimo, ninga wulece kuleka nghani yinji yo yose yikwimile mu mulimo wako. Hambi u Muwaha Mulungu mono nghumwitumamila, nha kowa yali hamonga nagwe. Nayo hamba si kokulecela lukulu, langa kowa yali hamonga nagwe, sunga kono kolokondezya milimo yose yono yikusakwa yitumwe mu Nyumba yakwe.
21 Ninga nha vyono wumanyile, i wakohani hamonga ne Walawi, awo wakondya kupegwa milimo yawo yono yiwanojeye wetume, mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Nawo i wetumami wose wono wena wunghula hayo hayo we milimo, awo yiwanojeye wakutaze. Kanyhi i wanhu wose hamonga ne walongola wawo, wawa wose wali mu wulajilize wako gwegwe.”
