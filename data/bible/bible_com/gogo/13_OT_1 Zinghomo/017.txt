1 Zinghomo 17

Nghani ya Natani kuli Dawudi

(2Sa 7:1-17)

1 Na lono Dawudi yali yekala mu kaya yakwe, mwizuwa linji yakamucema muwizi Natani, yakamulonjela yakutya, “Lanje ta! Ane nikaye mu kaya yono nzenje ne mibici yo muhagata, ninga Lisanduku lye Cilagane lyo Muwaha Mulugu, yiwice mwi'hema!”

2 Nayo Natani yakamupitula yakatya, “Zose zono zili mu nhumbula yako, wuzitumaje, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yali hamonga nagwe.”

3 Ninga mu cilo cico, Mulungu yakamulonjela Natani yakatya,
4 “Wubite wukamulonjele mwitumami wangu Dawudi wutye, ‘Agwe yo si gwegwe mono konzenjela Nyumba yangu yono nokwikala mumo.
5 Hambi nha ku wulawa lono nawawusiza wanhu wa Isilayeli ku Misili, nghali sinekala mu Nyumba yo wuhembelo sunga nalu. Langa nhondo zose, ane nekalaga mu mahema du, nghuhamywa ku wulawa hanhu aha, nghutegulwa hanhu aho.
6 Hamba nhavivyo, mu mihinzo yangu yose yono najendana ne Waisilayeli, hamba lizuwa linji sinawawuza i walongola wono nawahagula niwe kutya, nhawulenyi lu si mukunzenjela Nyumba yono nzengwe kwe mibici yo muhagata.’

7 “Holeka wumulonjele mwitumami wangu Dawudi wutye, ‘Ane, yo nene no Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga. Ane nakusola wali wuli mudima ngholo mu mawimbi, nghakunoza wuwe mulongola muli wanhu wangu Waisilayeli.
8 Nane nghawa nili hamonga nagwe ko kose kono wabitaga, kanyhi nghakusumila ne walugu wako hali'gwe. Holeka nane nokunoza wuwe ne lilumbo yiwaha, kanyhi wuwe mulongola mono kowa wukumucile mono yinze yose.
9 Hambi nha nahahagula i hanhu hono nowawika wanhu wangu Waisilayeli, nane lu nha nakondya kuwenjiza mumo, nawo wakondya kuzowela, i hanhu hono wokwikala wasina kanyhi wiwonelwe wowose.
10 Na kanyhi ku wulawa lono wenjilila mono yisi yiyi, kenji na kenji wali kuhomwa ne wanhu wono wasina lusungu. Ninga alu zizo zose si zokwijela kanyhi ng'o. Nane nha nakondya kwihuwiza nha vyono nowasuma walugu wako wose, kanyhi nokupela wana.
11 Nagwe kolo wakondya kufwa no kuzikwa, hamonga ne walezi wako, nane nolomunoza yunji yumonga wa muli wanago, yawe mutemi, nowukamya no wutemi wakwe.
12 Ale, yuyo langa yo kolonzenjela ne Nyumba yangu. Nane nolowunoza wutemi wakwe wukame cibitilila.
13 Nane nolowa sogwe, nayo kolowa mwanangu. Nane hamba si nolomulecela lukulu, nha vyono namuleka Sawuli mono namuwusa mu wutemi, agwe ho wuteme.
14 Nane nolomuwika yawe yo mulanjilizi we wanhu wangu, hamonga no wutemi wangu, cibitilila. No wutemi wakwe si woloduma yaye.’ ”

15 Nayo Natani yakabita, yakamulonjela Dawudi zose, nha vyono Mulungu yamulonjela.

Lilombo lya Dawudi lyo kumulumba Mulungu

(2Sa 7:18-29)

16 Hanyuma, Dawudi yakenjila mwi 'Hema lyono Lyelile yakekala hasi, yakawa kumulomba Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, ane si nghwiliye hamba hadodo kuli zono wakondya kunyinhumila, nene hamonga na we mhuga yangu.
17 Kumbe sunga nalu wukali wujendeleye du kunyinhumila na zinji kanyhi, hambi nha wehuwiza kuli lulelo lwangu za ku myaka yono yikuza. Alu yo nhavivi kumbe vyo kuwatendelaga wanhu!
18 Alu ane kanyhi ho nolonga cici kuli'gwe! Agwe nha wumanyile zangu zose, ane no mwitumami wako.
19 Hambi nha wo wuhile wako gwegwe ku wituma zizi kuli'ne, ho wundajile zono zili ku mwande yangu, zo wukumuce wangu.
20 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, hasina yunji kanyhi mono yehwanile gwegwe. Nase cimanyile vyono, yo gwegwe Mulungu du wuli wene.
21 Kanyhi bahano hono'nze, hasina hamba nghungugo yinji yo yose yono yihwanile nghungugo ye Waisilayeli, wono wawaponya mu wulawa mu wuwanda, wukawa kuwanoza wawe wanhu wako go mweneco. Ne zimbaha kanyhi zo wogofye zono wetuma so wanhu wako Waisilayeli, zizo zo zawumanyiza u wuwaha wako, mono yinze yose. Wukawakomboza wanhu wako kulawa ku Misili, wukawawinga makungugo ganji, wanhu wako ho wekale.
22 Wukawanoza Waisilayeli wawe wanhu wako cibitilila; nagwe wukawa Mulungu wawo.

23 “Holeka aluno go Muwaha Mulungu, nghukulomba wufisilize mapango gose, u wihuwizo wako wono wanyihuwiziza, ane hamonga no lulelo lwangu, kanyhi witumaje nha vyono walonga.
24 No lukumyo lwako locila kuwa luwaha muno, sunga ne wanhu wowa kulonga cibitilila wakutya, ‘Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga, mbeka yo Mulungu we Waisilayeli.’ Nagwe baho ho kojendelela kuwakumbucila wo lulelo lwangu, vilo vyose.
25 Nane nina wuluwuce wo kukulomba lilombo lili kuli'gwe, go Muwaha Mulugu wangu. Hambi nha yo gwegwe mono wungubuliye zizino zose no mwitumami wako. Kanyhi wanonjela nha vyono kowanoza wo lulelo lwangu wawe watemi.
26 Holeka aluno go Muwaha Mulungu, agwe yo gwegwe Mulungu, nagwe wakondya kunyihuwiziza wihuwizo wuwu wo wizine.
27 Nane nghukulomba, wuwalugutize wose wo lulelo lwangu, ho wajendelele cibitilila. No wulugutwe wako, wokwikala mu lulelo lwangu cibitilila, ku soko cono agwe wacilugutiza, nha ciwezaga cakondya kulugutizwa cibitilila.”
