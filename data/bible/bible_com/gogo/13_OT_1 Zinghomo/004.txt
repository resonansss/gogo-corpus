1 Zinghomo 4

Wo lulelo lwa Yuda

1 Awano wo wana wanji wa Yuda: Pelezi, Hezeloni, Kalimi, Huli na Shobali.
2 Shobali yali yo sogwe wa Lewaya, nayo Lewaya yakamulela Yahati, nayo Yahati yakamulela Ahumayi na Lahadi. Wawa wo wali wo walezi we wanhu wono wekalaga ku Zola.

3 Huli, yali yo mwana u mulongozi kulelwa kuli Efulata mugolece wo sogwe Kalebi. Ne wanagwe wo walizenga likaya lya Betelehemu. Huli, yali yena wana wadatu, Etamu, Panuweli, na Ezeli. Etamu nayo yali yena wana wadatu, Yezeleli, Ishima, na Idibashi, hamonga no mwana monga we cicekulu litagwa lyakwe Hazeleliponi.
4 Na yuyu Penuweli, yo yalizenga likaya lya Gedoli. Nayo Ezeli, yakayizenga Husha.

5 Nayo Ashuli mono yayizenga Tekowa, yali yatolile wacekulu wejete, Hela na Nala.
6 Nala, yakamulelela wana wane, Ahuzamu, Hefa, Temeni, na Hayahashitali.
7 Ashuli na Hela, wakalelana wana wadatu, Zeleti, Izihali, na Etinani.

8 Nayo Kozi, yali yo sogwe wa Anubu, na Zobeba. Kanyhi yali yo sogwe we zimhuga zono zalawilila muli Ahaliheli u mwana wa Halumu.

9 Hali ho yuli munhu litagwa lyakwe Yabezi, munhu yuyu yali yo munhu we lilumbo yiwaha si nha we mhuga yakwe wose. Nayo u nyina, yo yamucemela litagwa lili lya Yebezi, vyono u nyina yatuga wusungu wuwaha muno mu nghunga ha wusawe.
10 Ninga Yebezi yakamulomba Mulungu we Waisilayeli yakutya nhavi, “Gwe go Mulungu, wundugutize, wumhele yisi ngazi. Weze wuli hamonga nane, wunghamise muli zimbi zose zono zosuma kumhela wusungu.” Nayo Mulungu yakamuhulika wulombe wakwe, yakamupela vinhu vyono yalombaga.

Wityatye we mhuga yinji kanyhi

11 Nayo Kalebi u muwahawe wa Shuha, yali yena mwana, litagwa lyakwe Mehili. Nayo Mehili yakamulela Eshitoni.
12 Nayo Eshitoni yali yena wana wadatu, Betalafa, Paseya, na Tehina. Nayo Tehina yo yalizenga likaya lya Nahashi. Ne wana wose we wanhu wawa, wekalaga ku Leka.

13 Nayo Kenazi yali yena wana wejete, Otiniyeli na Selaya. Otiniyeli nayo yali yena wana wejete, Hatali, na Mewonotayi.
14 Mewonotayi yali yo sogwe ya Ofila.

Selaya yali yo sogwe wa Yowabu, mono yalongola kuliwona lilolo lye wasesi we vinhu vinhu, i hanhu hono wanhu wose wali wanghula mu mulimo wo wusesi.

15 Nayo Kalebi u mwana wa Yefune, yali yena wana wadatu, Iluna, Ela, na Namu. Nayo Ela, yali yo sogwe wa Kenazi.

16 Yahaleleli yali yena wana wane, Zifu, Zifa, Tiliya, na Asaleli.

17 Nayo Ezila yali yena wana wane, Yeta, Meledi, Efa, Yaloni. Yuyu Meledi, yakamutola Bitiya u mwana we cicekulu wo mutemi wa Misili. Wakalelana mwana litagwa lyakwe Milyamu, hamonga ne wana wejete, Shamayi, na Ishiba. Nayo yuyu Ishiba yo yalizenga likaya lya Eshitemowa.
18 Nayo Meledi nhavivyo, yakatola mucekulu wo mulango wa Yuda, wakalelana wana wadatu, Yaledi mono yo yalizenga likaya lya Gedoli. Nayo Heba, yo yalizenga likaya lya Soko. Nayo Yekutiyeli yo yalizenga likaya lya Zanowa.

19 Hodiya, yakamutola u lumbulye wa Nahamu. Ne wana wawo, wo mwamulizo ya we mhuga ya Galimu, wono wekalaga mwi'kaya lya Keyila. Hamonga na we mhuga ya Maka, wono wekalaga mwi'kaya lya Eshitemowa.

20 Nayo Shimoni yali yena wana wane, Amoni, Lina, Benihanani, na Tiloni.

Ishi, yali yena wana wejete, Zoheti na Benizoheti.

Wo lulelo lwa Shela

21 Shela yali yo muyagwe ne wana wa Yuda. Ne wanagwe wawo hamonga na Eli, mono yazenga likaya lya Leka. Nayo Lada yo yalizenga likaya lya Malesha, lya we mhuga ye wahona myenda ye citani ciswanu, wono wekalaga mu cikaya ca Beti-ashibeya.
22 Hamonga na Yokimu, ne wanhu wono wekalaga mu cikaya ca Kozeba, hamonga na Yowashi na Selafu mono yamutola u mucekulu we Cimowabu, na hanyuma yayo, wakekala mu Betelehemu. (No moto wuwu wali wo moto we katali muno muno).
23 Nawo wakawa wo wawumbi, wakawa wekaye mu makaya ga Netayimu na Gedela akuno wakumwitumamila mutemi.

Wo lulelo lwa Simiyoni

24 Nayo Simiyoni yali yena wana wahano, Nemuyeli, Yamini, Yalibu, Zela, na Shawuli.
25 Nayo Shawuli yakawa yena mwana yumonga, litagwa lyakwe Shalumu, no mwizukuluwe Mibisamu, hamonga no mwizukulu civu wakwe, Mishima.
26 Na hanyuma yayo, mhuga yiyo yikajendelela sunga ko kuli Hamuyeli, na kuli Zakuli, sunga kuli Shimeyi.
27 Nayo Shimeyi yali yena wana yikumi na mutandatu we cilume, hamonga ne wana we cicekulu wali mutandatu. Ninga i wanduguze, wali wena wana wacefu du. Hamonga na zizo, wo mulango wa Simiyoni si wonjezeka yaye wawe wenji, nha vyono wali wenha wo mulango wa Yuda.

28 Sunga mu vipango vyo mutemi Dawudi, wawa wo mulango wa Simiyoni, wali wekaye mu makaya ga Belisheba, Molada, Hazali-shuwali,
29 Biliha, Ezemu, Toladi,
30 Betuyeli, Holima, Zikilaji,
31 Beti, Malikaboti, Hazali-susimu, Betibili Shalayimu.
32 Kanyhi na wanji wakekala mu makaya ganji mahano, Etamu, Ayini, Limoni, Tokeni, Ashani,
33 hamonga na mu vitumbi vyayo vyono vipilime ku lubavu lwa kutakama ye mwezi, sunga ko ku cikaya ca Balati. Na zizi zo zinghomo zono zandikwa ze zimhuga zawo, hamonga ne hanhu hono wekalaga.

34 Ne wanhu wawa wono wetyatizye, wali wo walongola we zimhuga zawo.

Meshoba na Yamuleki, na Yosha u mwana wa Amaziya, hamonga na Yoweli.

35 Yehu (u mwana wa Yoshibiya, u mwana wa Selaya, u mwana wa Asiyeli.)

36 Eliyowenayi, na Yakoba, na Yeshohaya na Asaya, na Adiyeli, na Yesimiyeli, na Benaya.
37 Ziza (u mwana wa Shifi, u mwana wa Aloni, we ndelo ya Yedaya, na Shimuli, na Shemaya.)

Na vyono zimhuga zawo wakacila kwinjipa muno,
38 na vyo wakacila kumema mu lubavu lwa kumwezi, ko muno ku Galali.
39 Wakawa wakuzidimila zingholo zawo kucilima ye lilolo, kono likaya lyali lizenjigwe.
40 Nawo wakatuga wudimilo wuswanu muno kuko, mu yisi ngazi, kanyhi yo wuhihi. Yisi yono yali yitulile muno kanyhi yipolile. I wanhu wono wali wekaye mumo katali, wali wowo lulelo lwa Hamu.

41 Na mu vilo vyo wutemi wa Hezekiya u mutemi wa Yuda, lulo i wanhu wawo wono matagwa gawo gandicigwe baho hacanyha, wabita kuko ku Gelali, wakagongomeza mahema, hamonga ne vibanda vye wanhu wawo wono wekalaga mumo. Wakawawinga wanhu wayo wose, i wene wakenjila wakekala mumo, sunga nalu. Hambi nha hali howuli wudimilo wuswanu we zingholo zawo.
42 Ninga wanji wo mulango wa Simiyoni waficile wanhu magana mahano, wawo wakabita ku lubavu lwa kucilima ku Edomu. Wakabitana hamonga ne walongola wawo, Pelatiya, Neyaliya, Lefaya, na Uziyeli, i wana wa Ishi.
43 Nawo wawo, wakawagana Waamaleki wono wali wasigaye mumo, wakawawulaga wose, i wene wakenjila wakekala mumo sunga nalu.
