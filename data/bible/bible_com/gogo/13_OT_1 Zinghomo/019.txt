1 Zinghomo 19

Dawudi yakuwasuma Waamoni ne Wasiliya

(2Sa 10:1-19)

1 Hanyuma Nahashi u mutemi we Waamoni yakawa yafwiza, nayo Hanuni u mwanagwe, yakawusola wutemi.
2 U mutemi Dawudi yakatya, “Ane nghuwona vyowa viswanu nimunozeze wuswanu Hanuni, nha vyono sogwe Nahashi yanozeza 'ne.” Hobaho Dawudi yakatuma wanhu, wabite wakamusindamice Hanuni.

Nawo wali weza kwinjila mu yisi ye Waamoni, no kusaka wamuwone Hanuni,
3 i walongola we Waamoni wakamulonjela mutemi Hanuni wakatya, “Gwe mutemi wukwihwesa wuswanu wo sogo Nahashi, wo wulecile Dawudi yawatume wanhu wawa waze wakusindamice? Aa ng'o, hamba hadodo! Langa mwene kawatuma ho waze walitandile likaya, ho wacisume!”

4 Hodu nayo mutemi Hanuni yakawa kuwebata wawalya wanhu, yakawaceta zindevu lubavu lumonga, lwe videvu vyawo, yakayisapa myenda yawo sunga ha madako, hodu yakawa kuwawinga.
5 Nawo wakawa kuwona cevu ku wu wuya ku zikwawo. Nayo mutemi Dawudi yali yeza kuzihulika zono ziwejeleye, hobaho yakawa kuwatumila munhu, yawalonjele vyono yiwanojeye wekale ha Yeliko, walece kuwuya, sunga kolo zindevu zawo zakondya kusanga no kutalimha kanyhi, ho wolowuya.

6 Nawo Waamoni, wakamanya du, vyono litumo lilyo, lyakondya kuwaletela wulugu no mutemi Dawudi. Holeka wakawa kulipa fweza zo wuzito wa kilo elufu makumi madatu na yine, ho wasume kwazima matuka go wulugu, hamonga na wono wogajendya matuka gago ku wulawa Mesopotamiya, na ku wulawa ku Wasiliya, ku yisi ya Alamu Maka, na Zoba.
7 Nago matuka go wulugu gali gali elufu makumi madatu na nyejete gono wali wazime, hamonga ne lipuga lye Wasilikale lyo mutemi wa Maka, nalyo likaza, likasita nghambi yalyo behi na Medeba. Nawo Waamoni wakaza nhavivyo ku wulawa mu makaya gawo gose, wakawa kwiwikaniza so wihomi.

8 Nayo mutemi Dawudi yali yeza kuzihulika zizo zono zejelaga, hobaho yakamutuma Yowabu, hamonga ne lipuga lyose lye wasilikale.
9 Waamoni wakatandala ha mulango we likaya lyawo lya Laba. Nawo watemi wono wazaga kuwataziliza, wakatandala mu yisi yo wuhihi.

10 Ninga Yowabu yakamanya nha vyono i mapuga ge walugu, gosuma kuwahoma ku wulawa ku mwande na ku nyuma yawo. Holeka yakawahagula wasilikale vitung'ati wa muli Waisilayeli, yakawawika watandale vidomile ne Wasiliya.
11 Ne wasilikale wono wasigala, awo yakawa kuwawika mu miwoko ya Abishayi u mwanakwawe. Nayo Abishayi yakawatandalika wakawa wedomile ne Waamoni.
12 Yowabu yakamulonjela Abishayi, yakamutya, “Ale, hono wuwone Wasiliya weza kusumiliza, wuze mbela mbela wunhaze. Nane hono mbone Waamoni weza kukusumiliza, ale, nane nokuza mbela mbela nghutazilize.
13 Ninga weze ne vilungo no mutima wono wusikame! Hambi nha yicinojeye ase cihome kwe vilungo ku soko ye wanhu wetu, na ku soko ye makaya go Mulungu wetu. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yetume zono yakuwona swanu ha meso gakwe.”

14 Yowabu ne wanhu wakwe, wakalongola kuwahoma Wasiliya. Nawo Wasiliya wakatiza.
15 Nawo Waamoni wali weza kuwona vyono Wasiliya wakutiza, hodu nawo wakawa kutiza ha mwande ya Abishayi, wakenjila mwi'kaya. Hodu nayo Yowabu yakawa kuleka kuwahoma Waamoni, yakawa kuwuya ku Yelusalemi.

16 Nawo Wasiliya wakamanya nha vyono wakondya kusumwa ne Waisilayeli. Holeka wakagakonghola mapuga ge wasilikale ku wulawa Siliya, ku lubavu lwa kucilima ye likolongo lya Efulata, wakagawika mu miwoko ya Shobaki u muwaha we wasilikale wo mutemi Hadadezeli wa Zoba.
17 Nayo Dawudi yali yeza kuzihulika zizo, yakawa kugakonghola mapuga ge wasilikale we Waisilayeli, yakawa kulowoka likolongo lya Yoludani. Yakawatandalika vidomile ne Wasiliya, hobaho wulugu wukawa kwanza,
18 ne Waisilayeli, wakawawinga Wasiliya wakatiza. Nayo Dawudi ne wanhu wakwe, wakawawulaga Wasiliya waficile elufu mupunghati i wajendya matuka go wulugu, hamonga ne Wasilikale wajenda ne migulu waficile elufu makumi mane. Wakamuwulaga no mulongola wawo muwaha mono yakucemwa Shobaki.
19 Nawo watemi wono wamutazilizaga Hadadezeli, wali weza kuwona vyono wakondya kusumwa ne Waisilayeli, hodu wakanoza mulamu na Dawudi, wawe wetumami wakwe. Nawo Wasiliya hamba si wejelela kanyhi wawataze Waamoni.
