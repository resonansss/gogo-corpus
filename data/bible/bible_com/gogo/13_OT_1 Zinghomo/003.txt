1 Zinghomo 3

Wo lulelo lo mutemi Dawudi

1 Awa nawo wono watyatizye mu ndelwa yawo, wo wana wa Dawudi, wono walelelwa ku Hebuloni, lono yali yali kuko. Amunoni, u nyina yali Ahinowamu munya Yezeleli, Danyeli, u nyina yali Abijeli munya Kalimeli.

2 Abusalomu, u nyina yali Maka, u mwana we cicekulu wa

Talimayi u mutemi wa Geshuli.

Adoniya, u nyina yali Hajiti

3 Shefatiya, u nyina yali Abitali

Itileyamu, u nyina yali Egila.

4 Na wawa wose vyono wali mutandatu, walelelwa ku Hebuloni, mu myaka yi yilya mupunghati na nusu, yono Dawudi yali yatemile kuko ku Hebuloni. Dawudi yatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi madatu na midatu.
5 Ne wanagwe wenji walelelwa kuko.

Nayo Betisheba u mugolece wakwe, u mwana we cicekulu wa Amiyeli, yakamulelela wana wane, Shimeya, Shobabu, Natani na Solomona.

6 Kanyhi yali yena wana wanji yicenda, Ibiha, Elishuwa, Elipeleti,
7 Noga, Yefegi, Yefiya,
8 Elishama, Eliyada na Elifeleti.
9 Ninga hamonga ne wana wawa wose, Dawudi yali yena wana wanji kanyhi wono vyali vilelane ne wajitulwa wakwe. Yali yena sunga no mwana we cicekulu, litagwa lyakwe Tamali, nayo yali yo lumbu lyawo.

Wo lulelo lo mutemi Solomona

10 Awu nawo wo wityatye we liwalo lye wana wa Solomona: Solomona yakamulela Lehobowamu, nayo Lehobowamu yakamulela Abiya, nayo Abiya yakamulela Asa, nayo Asa yakamulela Yehoshafati,
11 Nayo Yehoshafati yakamulela Yeholamu, nayo Yeholamu yakamulela Ahaziya, nayo Ahaziya yakamulela Yowashi,
12 nayo Yowashi yakamulela Amaziya, nayo Amaziya yakamulela Uziya, nayo Uziya yakamulela Yotamu.
13 Nayo Yotamu yakamulela Ahazi, nayo Ahazi yakamulela Hezekiya, nayo Hezekiya yakamulela Manase.
14 Nayo Manase yakamulela Amoni, nayo Amoni yakamulela Yosiya.
15 Nayo Yosiya yali yena wana wane, Yohanani, Yehoyakimu, Zedekiya na Salumu.
16 Nayo Yehoyakimu yali yena wana wejete, Yehoyacini na Zedekiya.

Wo lulelo lo mutemi Yekoniya

17 Awa nawo wo wana wo mutemi Yekoniya, mono yasolwa mufungwa, yakategulwa ku Babiloni. Yekoniya yali yena wana mupunghati, Shelitiyeli,
18 Malicilamu, Pedaya, Shenaza, Yekamiya, Hoshama, na Nedabiya.
19 Yuyu Pedayiya, yali yena wana wejete, Zelubabeli, na Shimeyi. Nayo Zelubabeli, yali yena wana wejete, Meshulamu na Hananiya, hamonga no mwama monga we cicekulu mono yacemwaga Shelomiti.
20 Kanyhi, yali yena wana wanji wahano, Hashuba, Oheli, Belekiya, Hasadiya na Yushabu-hesedi.

21 Nayo Hananiya yali yena wana wejete, Pelatiya na Yeshaya. Yeshaya yakamulela Lefaya, nayo Lefaya yakamulela Alenani, nayo Alenani yakamulela Obadiya, nayo Obadiya yakamulela Shekaniya.
22 Yuyu nayo Shekaniya yali yena mwana monga, Shemaya, hamonga ne wezukulu wahano, Hatushi, Igali, Baliya, Neyali na Shafati.
23 Nayo Neyali, yali yena wana wadatu, Eliyowenayi, Hizikiya na Azilikamu.
24 Nayo Eliyowenayi, yali yena wana mupunghati, Hadaviya, Eliyashibu, Pelaya, Akubu, Yohanani, Delaya, na Anani.
