1 Zinghomo 21

Dawudi yakusola liwalo lye wanhu

(2Sa 24:1-25)

1 Kanyhi Setani, yakawa mulugu kuli Waisilayeli, yakamubabula Dawudi, yakawawale Waisilayeli.
2 Nayo Dawudi yakawa kumulajiliza Yowabu, u muwaha we wasilikale wakwe, yakamutya, “Alu, wubite gwegwe, hamonga ne wetumami wako, muyipilime yisi yose, mukawawale i Waisilayeli, ku wulawilila ku lubavu lumonga lwe yisi, sunga ko ku lubavu luyagwe. Nghusaka manye liwalo lyawo.”

3 Ninga Yowabu yakamupitula yakatya, “Gwe Mutemi, u Mulungu wako yakawonjeleze Waisilayeli kali cigana, wawe wenji kuciza nha vyono wenha aluno! Gwe go Mutemi, wanhu wose nha wetumami wako du. Alu nhawule kanyhi wukuhila witume nghani yono yinha yiyo, wuliletele likungugo masutya?”
4 Ninga mutemi yakamudindimiza Yowabu sunga yamuhulice. Hodu Yowabu yakatuluka, yakayipilimila yisi yose ya Isilayeli, hanyuma yakawuya ku Yelusalemi.
5 Yakawa kumuloloseza mutemi Dawudi, liwalo lye walume wose wono wakwiliye mu mulimo wo wusilikale, nha vyono mu yisi ya Isilayeli waficile walume milyoni monga na elufu yigana limonga liwalo lyawo. Nawo wa mu yisi ya Yuda waficile elufu magana mane na makumi mupunghati cititila.
6 Ninga Yowabu vyono nghani yo mutemi si yamwendeza yaye, holeka nayo hamba si yawawala yaye, i wanhu wo mulango wa Lawi na wa Benyamini.

7 Nayo Mulungu, nghani yiyo yo wuwala wanhu, yikamukalaza muno, holeka yakawalipiciza Waisilayeli.
8 Hobaho Dawudi yakamulonjela Mulungu yakatya, “Ane netuma wubi so wituma zizi! Holeka nghukulomba wunenele. Hambi nha nitumila wulele du.”

9 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Gadi, u muwizi wa Dawudi, yakamutya,
10 “Wubite wukamulonjele Dawudi nha vyono nghumupela nghani yidatu, zono nghumusaka yahagule mweneco nghani monga du muli zizo yono kosaka nimutendele. Nane nomutendela nha vyono mwene kohagula.”

11 Ale, Gadi yakawa kumubitila Dawudi yakawa kumuloloseza zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yateye. Yakamuwuza yakatya, “Alu wukwihaga nghanici?
12 Yiwe nzala mu myaka midatu? Hamba wuwe kutiza hali walugu wako sunga myezi midatu? Hamba vilo vidatu, Muwaha Mulungu yakutowe ne lihuhuji mu yisi yako, yamutume malayika wakwe, yawawulaje mu yisi yose ya Isilayeli? Alu wumhelaje lipitulo lyono nomutegulila Muwaha Mulungu.”

13 Dawudi yakamupitula Gadi, yakatya, “Ane nenjilwa na mahwele mawaha muno muno! Kanyhi hamba si nendile ndipicizwe ne wanhu. Holeka yili ciba hono Muwaha Mulungu yo yali yandipicize mweneco, hambi mwene nha yali lusungu.”

14 Holeka Muwaha Mulungu yakawaletela lihuhuji Waisilayeli. Wakafwa wanhu waficile elufu makumi mupunghati.
15 Hanyuma kanyhi, yakatuma malayika yayongomeze Yelusalemi. Ninga kanyhi yakawupitula wuhile wakwe wuwo, yakamutya, malayika, “Hodu wulece! Zizo du zakondya!” Nayo malayika, lulo, yali yemile ha cuga ca Olinani u Muyebusi.

16 Nayo Dawudi yakamuwona malayika yemile, akuno yanyasuye no mumaje mu muwoko wakwe, yewikanize ho yayongomeze Yelusalemi. Hobaho Dawudi, hamonga ne walongola wose we wanhu, wono wali wavwalile vivwalo vye magunila, wakenama, no wusu wawo wukapamiza ho hasi.
17 Dawudi yakalomba yakutya, “Gwe go Mulungu, ane yo nene mono sutizye. Ane yo nene mono ndajilize wanhu wawalwe. Alu wanhu wawa nawo ho wasutya nghanici? Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wangu, nghukunena wundipicizaje nene du, hamonga na we mhuga yangu, wuwalece wanhu wako.”

18 Malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu, yakamulonjela Gadi, yamulajilize Dawudi, yabite yakamuzenjele cikumbicilo Muwaha Mulungu, ha cuga ca Olinani Muyebusi.
19 Dawudi yakalihulika lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, yakabita nha vyono yalonjelwa na Gadi.
20 Naho baho ha cuga ca Olinani, yali yali baho Oliani hamonga ne wanagwe wane watowaga wuhemba mu cuga. Nawo wali weza muwona malayika, wa walya wana wakatiza, wakehundika.
21 Nayo Olinani yali yeza muwona mutemi Dawudi yakuza, mwene yakatela ha mhelo hadodo ye cuga, yakenama no wusu wakwe wukapamiza ho hasi.
22 Dawudi yakamutya Olinani, “Nghukulomba wunguzize cuga cako, nimuzenjele baho cikumbicilo Muwaha Mulungu, ho yaliwuse lihuhuji. Nane nocigula cuga cako ko wuguzi wono wuling'aniciye.”

23 Ninga Olinani yakamutya, “Gwe Mutemi! Wucisolaje du, wucitumamile gwegwe nha vyono wendile. Ne zing'ombe azino, wuzitumamile so cilavywa cinyanywe mu cikumbicilo. Kanyhi wusole ne matuli aga go kutwanjila wuhemba, wugetumamile nha ngwagu. Wusole no wuhemba, nawo wuwulavye cilavywa. Ane nakukombolela vyose du.”

24 Ninga mutemi nayo yakamupitula yakatya, “Aa, ng'o! Ane langa, novigula vinhu vivi vyose kuli'gwe ko wuguzi wono wuling'aniciye. Hambi nha si nosuma kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu cinhu cono cako, i cinhu cono si nicilavizye nhuguti yaye.”
25 Hobaho Dawudi yakawa kumupela Olinani fweza magana mutandatu cifo ce cuga cakwe.
26 Yakawa kumuzenjela Muwaha Mulungu cikumbicilo baho, yakamulavizya ne vilavywa vinyanywe, hamonga ne vilavywa vyo mulamu. Hanyuma yakalomba, nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamuhulika, yakaleta moto wulawa ku wulanga, wukavinyanya vilavywa vyono vyali vili mucanyha ye cikumbicilo.

27 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela malayika yawugomoleze mumaje wakwe mu njasulu yayo. Nayo malayika yakanoza nhavivyo.
28 Nayo Dawudi, mu nzila yiyo, yakamanya nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yakondya kuwupitula wulombe wakwe. Holeka nhondo, nayo yakawa kulavya makumbiko mu cikumbicilo, ha cuga ca Olinani.
29 Lihema lyono Lyelile lyono yalinoza Musa ku nyika, hamonga ne cikumbicilo cayo cono canyanyilwaga makumbiko, vyose vivyo mu cipango cico, vyali vikali vili hanhu ho wuhembelo kuko ku Gibeyoni.
30 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, Dawudi si yasumaga kubita no kumuhemba Mulungu kuko ku Gibeyoni. Hambi nha yogopaga mumaje wa Malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu.
