1 Zinghomo 23

1 Nayo Dawudi lono yali yakombipa muno, hodu yakamutemya Solomona u mwanagwe, yawe mutemi we Waisilayeli.

U mulimo we Walawi

2 Mutemi Dawudi yakawakongholela hamonga i walongola wose we Waisilayeli, hamonga ne wakohani ne Walawi.
3 Yakawawala i Walawi wose walume, wono waficile myaka makumi madatu hamba kuciza. Ne liwalo lyawo, wakawa waficile wanhu elufu makumi madatu na munana.
4 Na muli wawo, yakawawika wanji elufu makumi mejete na yine, wo wali walanjilizi we milimo ya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Na wanji elufu mutandatu, wakawa wo walanjilizi we vikumbucizo vye zinghani, kanyhi walamuzi.
5 Ne wanhu elufu yine, wakawa wo walindilizi. Na wanhu elufu yine wakawa wo wo mulimo wo kumukumya Muwaha Mulungu kwe viya vye masumbi, vyono mutemi yavilavya so mulimo wuwo.

6 Dawudi yakawatemanula i Walawi, wakalawa vipuga vidatu, nha vyono zali zinha zimhuga zawo, Wagelishoni, Wakohati, ne Wamelali.

7 Yuyu Gelishoni, yali yena wana wejete, Ladani na Shimeyi.
8 Ladani yali yena wana wadatu, Yeliyeli, Zetamu, na Yoweli.
9 Nawo wawo, wo wakawa walongola wa mu zimhuga ze wana wa Ladani. (Nayo Shimeyi yali yena wana wadatu, Shelomoti, Haziyeli, na Halani.)
10 Shimeyi yali yena wana wane, Yahati, Zina, Yewushi, na Beliya.
11 Ninga Yewushi na Beliya, wali wasina wana wenji, na ku soko yiyo, wakatowelelwa wakawa mhuga monga.

12 Kohati yali yena wana wane, Amulamu, Iziha, Hebuloni, na Uziyeli.
13 Nayo Amulamu u mwana muwaha wa Kohati, yali yo sogwe wa Haluni na Musa. (Nayo Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wakatemanulwa, ho wawe walanjilizi cibitilila, we vinhu vyono vikwitumamilwa mu wuhembi, kufusa wuvumba mu wuhembi wo Muwaha Mulungu, no kumwitumamila, hamonga no kuwalugutiza wanhu mwi'tagwa lyakwe.
14 Ninga i wana wa Musa, u munhu wo Mulungu, wene wakahanzilizwa hamonga ne Walawi.)
15 Musa yali yena wana wejete, Gelishomu na Eleyezali.
16 U mulongola wa muli wana wa Gelishomu, yali Shebuweli.
17 Ninga Eleyezali yali yena mwana monga du, litagwa Lehabiya. Ninga yuyu Lehabiya, yali yena wana wenji muno.

18 Izihali, u mwana we kejete, kuli Kohati, nayo yali yena mwana monga du, Shelomiti, nayo yakawa yo mulongola mu mhuga yawo.
19 Hebuloni mwana we kadatu wa Kohati, nayo yali yena wana wane, Yeliya, Amaliya, Yehaziyeli, na Yekamiyamu.
20 Uziyeli mwana we kane wa Kohati, nayo yali yena wana wejete, Mika na Ishiya.

21 Melali yali yena wana wejete, Mahili na Mushi. Nayo Mahili yali yena wana wejete, Eleyezali na Kishi.
22 Ninga Eleyezali yafwa yasina wana we cilume, langa wa cicekulu du. Ne wanagwe wose we cicekulu, wakatolwa ne wandugu zawo, i wana wa Kishi.
23 Mushi, u mwana we kejete wa Melali, yali yena wana wadatu, Mahili, Edeli, na Yelemoti.

24 Na wawa wose wali wo wana wa Lawi, mu mhuga zawo, ne zimhuga zawo. Na wose wawo wakandikwa munhu ne'tagwa lyakwe. Ne wanagwe wose, wono wali waficile myaka makumi mejete no kuciza, wose wakenjizwa mu mulimo wo kuyizenga Nyumba yo Mulungu.

25 Nayo Dawudi yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu we Waisilayeli yakondya lu kuwapela mulamu wanhu wakwe, nayo lu kokwikala mu Yelusalemi cibitilila.
26 Holeka aluno, hasina soko kanyhi, yo kuwawika Walawi wawe kulidinghula Lihema lyono Lyelile, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose vyono vitumamilwaga mu wuhembi.”
27 No wulajilize wa Dawudi we ndumilizo, yalajiliza nha vyono i Walawi, weze kuwalwa no kwinjizwa mu milimo, lono wakondya kuficila myaka makumi mejete.
28 Nawo wakapegwa milimo ayi, kuwalindiliza wana wa Haluni, lono wakwituma mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, lono wali mu wuhembi, kulanjilila wuduwilo, hamonga ne zinyumba, no kuvikwema vyono vyelile vyose.
29 Wawe walanjilizi we mikate milavywe, kuli Mulungu, no wusaje wono wukwitumamilwa mu vilavywa, hamonga ne mafuta.
30 Hamonga no kumukumya no kumulumba Muwaha Mulungu, nhondo zose, hakali mitondo ne cinyamihe.
31 Na mu mapango go gose lono vilavywa vinyanywe vikulavywa kuli Muwaha Mulungu, mu mazuwa ge Sabato, na mu zisikukuu ze ndawo yo mwezi, sunga na mu zisikukuu zinji zinji zose. No wulajilize wuwu, wukawikwa mu nghango ye liwalo lye Walawi wono wawikwa mu mulimo wuwo vyono lyali linha mu mapango gose. Wene Walawi, wali wawicigwe mu mulimo wo kumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu mapango gose.
32 Wali wapejigwe wulanjilizi we Lihema lyono Lyelile hamonga ne Nyumba yo Mulungu, no kuwataza i wandugu zawo, hamonga ne wana wa Haluni lono wali mu wuhembi mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
