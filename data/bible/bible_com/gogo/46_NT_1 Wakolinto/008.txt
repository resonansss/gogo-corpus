1 Wakolinto 8

I zindigwa zono zikumbiciywe i vihwani

1 Aluno kuli i zindigwa zono zikumbiciywe vihwani, cimanyile nha vyono ase wose cina uwutanje mu mahala, ninga kanyhi u wutanje wuwo, wuwapelaga wanhu wawo no widunhumuzi, ninga u wendo, wuzengaga.
2 Ale, holeka mono yakwihwesa nha yamanyile cinji, i mbeka, yuyo si yamanyile cinji co cose nha vyono yimunojeye kumanya.
3 Ninga u munhu mono yamwendile u Mulungu, yuyo langa yo yamanyice kuli mwene.

4 Holeka kuli i zindigwa zono zikumbiciywe vihwani, ase cimanyile, vyono vihwani si cinji mono yinze, ase cimanyile vyono u Mulungu yo yumonga du.
5 Hamba viwe ho vili vinhu vyono vicemwaga milungu, mu muno mono'nze, hamba ku wulanga, na hamba hawe hoyili milungu mu muno, ne wawaha wenji,
6 hamba nhavivyo, kuli'se koyuli Mulungu yumonga du, Baba, u Muwumbi we vinhu vyose, mono sunga na sese nase, cili baha ku soko yakwe. Na kanyhi, koyuli u Muwaha yumonga du, yo Yesu Kilisito, mono nayo mu nzila yakwe, vinhu vyose vikawumbwa, na mu nzila yakwe na sese nase mo cikuwumipila.
7 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, si wose wono wena u wumanye wuwuno. Hambi nha howali wanhu wanji wono wavizowela i vihwani, wanhu wono sunga nalu, lono wakulya zindigwa, wakuziwona kotya nha ndigwa zono zikumbiciywe vihwani. Na vyono viselinji vyawo visoceye, wakutipizwa mu viselinji vyawo.
8 Ninga ndigwa siyosuma kucitegula behi no Mulungu. Na hamba hono cilece kuyilya, si cocepucilwa na cinji, na hamba hono cilye, si cokonjelezezwa na cinji.

9 Ninga mwilanjililaje, u welecele wenyu wuwu, wulece kuwanoza wono wena u wihuwilo wono wusoceye, wagwile mu wubi.
10 Hambi u munhu mono i ciselinji cakwe cisoceye, hono yakuwone agwe mono wina wizuce wukulya zindigwa zizo mugati ye cikumbicilo ce vihwani, wule, si kowa wamupela vilungo vyo kulya zindigwa zono zikumbiciywe vihwani?
11 Na hobaho yuyo u nduguyo u musocele, mono Kilisito yafwa ku soko yakwe, kokwajilila ku soko yo wumanye wako.
12 Na hono muwasutizye wandugu zenyu nhavivyo, no kuvitajila makomele viselinji vyawo, vyono visoceye, mowa mwamusutizya Kilisito.
13 Holeka hono ndigwa yiwe yikuwa kumuletela ndugu yangu wugwe, hodu sinolila lukulu nyama, ho ndece kumugwisiza ndugu yangu mu wubi.
