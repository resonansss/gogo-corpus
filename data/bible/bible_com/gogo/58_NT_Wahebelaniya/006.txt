Wahebelaniya 6

1 Holeka cijendelele kumwande kuli zono zikangaye, kanyhi cilece kwikala mu mejizo ge myamulizo ge Cikilisito. Si yicinojeye ciwice u musinji mu wuwuyajila kanyhi mu mafundo gagalya go mwamulizo, nha vyono kotya i metumo mafu, kumwihuwila u Mulungu,
2 i mafundo go wubaputizo, no wuwicilwe we miwoko, wuzuce we wafu, no wuling'anizwe we cibitilila.
3 Ase cijendelele kumwande! Na yiyo yo conoza, hono u Mulungu yendile.

4 Hambi alu kuli wanhu wono wakondya kuwubeza u wihuwilo wawo, yosumika nhawule kuwagomola ho wanene kanyhi? Ku soko i wanhu wawo wali wakondya kuwa mu wuzelu wo Mulungu, wali wakondya kucijeza ne cipegwa ciswanu ca ku wulanga, kanyhi no kuhanjizwa mu Muhe Mono Yelile,
5 wali wakondya kuwujeza no wuswanu we nghani yo Mulungu, ne vilungo vya mu yinze yono yikuza,
6 hanyuma kanyhi wakawa kuwubena u wihuwilo wawo! Si yosumika kuwagomola ho wanene kanyhi, vyono wakumutumbika kanyhi u Mwana wo Mulungu kejete, kanyhi wakumupela cevu ca cagalanze.
7 U Mulungu kawulugutizaga u muhangha wono wuyibocelaga i mvula yono yikuwutonyela kenji na kenji, no kucindya i zimbeyu zono zina cambuzi kuli wono walimilwa.
8 Ninga hono u muhangha wuwo wucindye mabici ge mamiya, na ganji caka, wukuwa wusina cambuzi, wukuwa wuli behi no wulijitwa no Mulungu, no mudumo wayo wukuwa wutakamizwe mu moto.

9 Ninga hamba ciwe cikulonga nhavivi, mo wendece wetu, cina witumbilo wuwaha muli zo wuwe wenyu, cimanyile vyono mwabocela u wulugutizwe muli azilya i ziswanu ze mhonelo yenyu.
10 Hambi u Mulungu nha si kawezaga yasina ling'ani, u mwene si kowusemwa mulimo wenyu wono mwetuma, hamba u wendo wono mwawulajila ku soko ye litagwa lyakwe, mu witumami wenyu wono mwawetumamila, na vyono sunga na lulu mukali mukuwetumamila i wanhu wakwe.
11 Hambi u wuhile wetu kuli nyenye nha wowu, cikumusacenyi wose muli nyenye, munhu yalajile u wuhijilile wuwo du, sunga ko ku mudumo wo wumemelecele wo witumbilo wenyu.
12 Mulece kuwa wadoba, langa muweze nha awalya wono wehuwiye, na wono wena u wukangalizi, ho wazibocele zono u Mulungu yazihuwiza.

U wihuwizo wo Mulungu wo we mbeka

13 U Mulungu lono yamupelela Abulahamu u wihuwizo, yelahiziza mwitagwa lyakwe mweneco, hambi nha hasina yunji muwaha mono yamucizize u Mulungu, mono u Mulungu yali yelahize.
14 U Mulungu yatya, “Mbeka nghukwihuwiza vyono nokumotya, kanyhi no lulelo lwako lokonjelezeka.”
15 Abulahamu yalindiliza ko wukangalizi, holeka yakacibocela cono u Mulungu yamwihuwiziza.
16 I wanhu lono wakwilahiza, welahizizaga kuli muwaha mono yawacizize, no wilahize wuwo wumalaga u wuwanji wose.

17 Nayo u Mulungu, yawukangaziza u wihuwizo wakwe mu wilahize, na muli zizo, yakawalajila calinze, wawo wono yawehuwiziza, vyono si kowupitula kanyhi u wuhile wakwe wuwo.
18 Holeka ho vili vinhu vi vyejete, u wihuwizo no wilahize, vyono si vyosuma kukawanika, na hamba muli zizo, u Mulungu si kosuma kulonga wulambi. Holeka ase mono camanyhilila ku wukamiswe wakwe, cikupegwa mu mitima yo wukamisa muno u witumbilo wono wuli ha mwande yetu.
19 No witumbilo wuwo wukuwa nha cimizo mu mikalile yetu. No witumbilo wuwo wukamile kanyhi wa mbeka mbeka, kanyhi wukwinjila sunga ko kwi'bupa lye Nyumba yo Mulungu ya ku wulanga, sunga ko kugati ya hono helile hayo.
20 Nayo Yesu yo yalongola kwinjila mumo ku soko yetu, nayo yawa Mukohani Muwaha cibitilila, mu wutyatya u wityatye wo wukohani wa Melicizedeki.
