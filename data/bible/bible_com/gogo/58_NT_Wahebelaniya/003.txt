Wahebelaniya 3

Yesu yo Muwaha yamucizize Musa

1 Ale, anye wandugu zangu, mo wanhu wo Mulungu, mono mwacemwa no Mulungu, mwihwesaje za Yesu mono u Mulungu yamutuma yawe Mukohani Muwaha wo wihuwilo wetu.
2 U mwene yali mwihuwilwa kuli Mulungu mono yamuhagula ho yetume u mulimo wakwe, nha vyono Musa nayo yali mwihuwilwa mu nyumba yose yo Mulungu.
3 Nha vyono u muzenji we kaya yo katugaga i lilumbo muno si nhe kaya yeneco. Nayo Yesu nhavivyo yo yanojeye yatuje lilumbo yiwaha si nha Musa.
4 Hambi i zikaya zose, nha zina u muzenji wayo, ninga u Mulungu yo muzenji we vinhu vyose.
5 Musa yali mwihuwilwa mu nyumba yose yo Muwaha nho mwitumami, nayo yakazilonga i zinghani zono u Mulungu yali yazilonje hanyuma.
6 Langa Kilisito yo mwihuwilwa nho mwana mono yena wulajilize mu nyumba yo Mulungu. Ne nyumba yakwe yo sese, hono cibatilile lukulu zo wuluwuce wetu wa muli cono citumbiye.

U wuceho we wanhu wo Mulungu

7 Holeka nha vyono yakulonga u Muhe Mono Yelile, yakutya:

“Holeka i zuwali hono muhulice lizi lyo Mulungu,

8 Mulece kuzikamufya zinhumbula zenyu,

nha vyono wali i wasogo zenyu,

lono wamubezeza u Mulungu,

nha vyono wanoza ku nyika mwizuwa lililya,

lono wamujelela u Mulungu.

9 Kuko i wasogo zenyu wasagala,

wakanjela yakutya u Muwaha,

hamba wawe wali waziwona

zono nali nakondya kwituma

mu myaka makumi mane.”

10 Ku soko yiyo nane nghawakolelwa likuwo

i wanhu wawa, nghawa kutya,

“I mahweso ge wanhu wawa gajiliye,

wakali si nazimanya zinzila zangu.”

11 Holeka nghakolwa ne likuwo,

nghawa kwilahiza nghutya,

“Si wokwinjila mu wuceho wono nali niwawiciye.”

12 Holeka wandugu zangu, mwilanjililaje, halece kuwa na yunji muli nyenye, mono yena i nhumbula yimbi nhavivyo, mono si yehuwiye no kumuleka u Mulungu mumi.
13 Ninga mweze kwiyilanjiza nhondo zose, lono hakali kutegwa, “I zuwali,” muyenyu yunji yalece kukamufywa no wukoci wo wubi no kuwa ne cibede.
14 Hambi ase nha ceza cili hamonga na Kilisito, soko ciwe ciwukamise muno muno u witumbilo wetu wono cali cina nawo hamwamulizo.
15 I Mandiko gakutya nhavi:

“I zuwali hono muhulice lizi lyo Mulungu

mulece kuzikamufya zinhumbula zenyu

nha vyono wanoza wasogo zenyu

lono wamubezeza u Mulungu.”

16 Hambi wa nani wono walihulika lizi lyo Mulungu ninga wakamubeza? Wo wawo wose wono watongwa na Musa ku wulawa ku Misili.
17 Kanyhi wa nani wono u Mulungu yawakolelwa likuwo mu myaka makumi mane kuko ku nyika?
18 U Mulungu lono yelahiza yakutya, “Si wokwinjila mu wuceho wono nali niwawiciye.” Yawalongaga wa nani? Yalongaga muli za wawo i wabezi.
19 Holeka cikuwona vyono si wasuma kwinjila kuko, vyono wali si wehuwiye.
