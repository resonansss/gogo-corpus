Waefeso 3

U mulimo wa Pawulo kuli wono si Wayahudi

1 Ku soko yiyo holeka ane no Pawulo no muwopwa wa Kilisito Yesu ku soko ya nyenye mono si muli Wayahudi, nghumulomba u Mulungu.
2 Nitumbiye mwakondya kuhulika vyono u Mulungu, so lihewa lyakwe, yamhela u mulimo wuwu, niwitume so cambuzi cenyu.
3 Ane namanyizwa zizi mu nzila yo wugubulilwe wo wuhile wakwe wono wali wuhundicigwe. Nizandice kwa cifupi, izinghani zizi,
4 nanye kolo mukuzisoma izinghani zangu, mosuma kuwutanga u wumanye wangu wa muli zono zihundicigwe za muli Kilisito.
5 Zono katali wanhu si wamanyizwa, ninga alu u Mulungu yakondya kuwamanyiza iwatumwa, ne wawizi wakwe wono welile mu nzila yo Muhe.
6 Ne nghani yiyo yono yihundicigwe nha yoyi, vyono mu nzila yo Luganuzi Luswanu, iwanhu wono si Wayahudi mo wakutujila ilifungu lyawo hamonga ne Wayahudi muli zo wulugutwe wo Mulungu, wene nawo nha wali vilunzwa vyo muwili wuwulya, kanyhi wahanji wo wihuwizo wuwulya wono u Mulungu yawunoza mu nzila ya Kilisito Yesu.

7 Ane nanozwa ho niwe mwitumami wo Luganuzi Luswanu, ha nghango ye cipegwa ce lihewa lilya lyo Mulungu, lyono napegwa ha nghango yo witume we vilungo vyakwe.
8 Ane nili mudodo si nhe wanhu wo Mulungu wose, ninga napegwa ilihewa lyakwe, ho niwaganulile iwanhu wono si Wayahudi u wugoli wa Kilisito, wono wusina cipimo,
9 kanyhi ho niwalajile iwanhu wose wawone vyono wukufisilizwa u wuhile wo Mulungu wono wali wuhundicigwe. U Mulungu mono yaviwumba vinhu vyose, yayihundika inghani yakwe yiyo kulawa katali,
10 alu i wawaha ne vilungo vya ku wulanga ho vimanyizizwe mu nzila ye Likanisa, ho wasume kugamanya i mahala go Mulungu gono gena mihilimo minji.
11 U Mulungu yayituma inghani yiyo nha vyono wali winha u wutemye wakwe we cibitilila, wono yawunozeza muli Kilisito u Muwaha wetu.
12 Holeka mu wilunza na Kilisito, na mu nzila yo wihuwilo muli Kilisito, ase cikwijelela kumusejelela u Mulungu cisina woga.
13 Holeka nghumunenenyi, mulece kuyitukuwika i mitima yenyu, ku soko ye magayo gono nghugatuga ku soko yenyu, hambi gago nha gali ku soko yo lukumyo lwenyu.

U wendo wa Kilisito

14 Ku soko yiyo, holeka nane nghumufugamila u Sogo yo Munhu,
15 mono wo mwamulizo wa vyose vyono vili mono'nze na vya ku wulanga.
16 Nghumulomba u Mulungu, mu nghango yo wugoli wo lukumyo lwakwe, yamupelenyi u wusume wo Muhe wakwe, i vilungo vyo kuwa musikame mu wunhu wa mugati,
17 nayo Kilisito yekalaje mu zinhumbula zenyu ko wihuwilo. Nghumulomba muwe mukamile mu musinji wo wendo,
18 ho musume kumanya hamonga ne wanhu wo Mulungu wose, nha vyono winha u wendo wa Kilisito, vyono wutandalaga mu wugazi no wutali, no wima na hasi yayo.
19 Kanyhi muwumanye no wendo wa Kilisito, wono wucizize wumanye wose, ho mumemywe u wukwilanicile wose wo Mulungu.
20 Ale kuli yuyo mono kwe vilungo vyakwe vyono vikwituma mugati yetu, yasumile kwituma zimbaha zono zicizize kuli zono cidahile kulomba hamba kwihwesa,
21 kuli yuyo lweze lukumyo mu Kanisa na muli Kilisito Yesu, mu mapango gose sunga ku myaka ne cibitilila. Heya.
