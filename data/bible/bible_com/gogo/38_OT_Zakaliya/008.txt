Zakaliya 8

Muwaha Mulungu yakwihuwiza nha vyono koyizenga kanyhi Yelusalemi

1 Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga, yakamulonjela Zekaliya yakatya,
2 “Alu, nendezwa kulitaza no kulizenga kanyhi likaya lya Yelusalemi, ku soko yo wendo wangu wuwaha kuli wanhu wayo, u wendo wono wamhela likuwo, ku soko ye walugu walyo.
3 Alu nowuya 'ne ku Yelusalemi, likaya lyangu lyono lyelile, niwe kwikala mumo. Likaya lyocemwa likaya lye mbeka. Ne litunda lyo Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga, nalyo lyocemwa litunda lyono lyelile.
4 Kanyhi i wakombi we cilume na we cicekulu wono wakwikomajiza ne zinghome lono wakujenda, wolowa kuceha mu viwanza vye likaya.
5 Ne zinzila za mwi'kaya zolomema wana we cilume na we cicekulu, wolowa kwidawala mumo.”

6 Ne nghani yiyi kuli wanhu we zinghungugo zono zisigaye, wolowona kotya nha siyosumika, ninga kuli'ne yosumika du.
7 Ane nowawusa wanhu wangu ku wulawa ku yisi ya ku'cilima na ku yisi ya kumwezi.
8 Niwagomole wose wa ku wulawilila ku cilima sunga ko ku mwezi wawuye wekale mu Yelusalemi. Nawo wowa wanhu wangu, nane nowa Mulungu wawo, mono nowadimila mu mbeka na mu ling'ani.
9 U Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga yakutya, “Mwime mukamile mweze kuli vilungo! Hambi sunga nalu, nha mukuzihulika zinghani zono i wawizi wazilonga, lu lulya, lono wawicilwa u musinji so wuzenji kanyhi we Nyumba yangu.
10 Na mbeka hono zizi zali zikali nejela, hasina yunji mono yasuma kumuhumbiliza muyagwe hamba mutugo, kanyhi hali hasina mono yali yena mulamu kuli walugu wakwe. Vyono ane nghawaleka ho wagombane munhu na muyagwe.
11 Ninga 'lu nghuwanozeza zono zisimile wono wasigaye we nghungugo yiyi.
12 Wolohadika zimbeyu zawo ko mulamu. Ne mizabibu yawo yolela mikalo viswanu, nawo muhangha we yisi wololavya wuhemba, ne mvula yowa kutonya muno. No wulugutizwe wuwu nowapela i wanhu wa mu nghungugo yangu wono wasigaye.
13 Nye, nyo wanhu wa Yuda na Isilayeli! Ha mwamulizo, wajenzi welijitaga weneco wose wakatya, ‘Wongomezwe wono wawutuga wanhu wa Yuda na Isilayeli, ale, wukutuje na gwegwe.’ Ninga ane nomuponyenyi, sunga ne wajenzi wapitule ne mahweso gawo kanyhi, walongane munhu na muyagwe wawe kutya, ‘Ale, wubocele wulugutizwe, nha vyono wawubocela wanhu wa Yuda na Isilayeli. Holeka mwime mukamile kanyhi mulece kogopa.’ ”

14 U Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga yakutya, “Lono walezi wenyu wanghalazaga, nawawikaniziza wongomezwe, hamba sinagakawaniza mahweso gangu yaye, langa najendelela du ne mahweso gago.
15 Ninga 'lu, nghwiwikaniza niwapele wulugutizwe wanhu wa Yelusalemi na Yuda. Holeka mulece kogopa.
16 Alu azi zo mokwituma. Mweze kulonga ze mbeka munhu na muyagwe. Namo mu mahakama, mweze kuling'aniza kwe ling'ani mu nzila yono yikuleta mulamu.
17 Mulece kwihwesa zimbi zo kwiyitamisa nyenye wose. Hamba kulavya wuhomelezi wo wulambi ko wilahize, hambi 'ne nha nizisulile zinghani zo wulambi na zo wukali, mu nzila yo kuwewonela wanhu.”

18 U Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga, yo yamupela zinghani zizi u muwizi Zekaliya.
19 Wikapise wo wuleka kulya, wa mu mwezi we kane na we 'hano na wo mupunghati, na we likumi zowa zo sikukuu ze nyemo mbaha no wusangalale kuli wanhu wa Yuda. Holeka mwende mbeka no mulamu.
20 Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga yakutya, “Vilo vyokuza, vyono i wanhu wololawa mu makaya ganji, nawo wokuza ku Yelusalemi.
21 Nawo wanhu we likaya linji, wolongana na we likaya linji, wotya, ‘Ase cikubita kumuhemba Muwaha Mulngu we mihanga, ho yacipela wulugutwe no wendezwe wakwe. Cibitenyi nanye cikamuhembe hamonga!’
22 Hobaho wanhu wenji hamonga ne zinghungugo zono zina vilungo zolokuza ku Yelusalemi kumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga, wamulombe yawapele wulugutizwe wakwe.
23 Na mu vilo vivyo, wanhu yikumi we cijenzi, wolomuziza muyahudi monga wamutye, cikulomba ciwe hamonga nanye, hambi nha cihulika vyono u Mulungu yali hamonga nanye.”
