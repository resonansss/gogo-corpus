Zakaliya 1

Muwaha Mulungu yakuwacema wanhu wakwe wamuwuyile

1 Mu mwezi wo munana wo mwaka we kejete lono mutemi Daliyo yali yatemile Pelisiya. Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela muwizi Zekaliya, u mwana we cilume wa Belekiya kanyhi u mwizukulu kwa Ido yabite yakawalonjele wanhu yatye,
2 “Ane no Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, nali niwakoleywe yikuwo yiwaha muno i wasogo zenyu.
3 Ninga 'lu nghumutyenyi, mbuyilenyi, nane nomuwuyilenyi nye.”
4 Mulece kwihwana nhe wasogo zenyu wono katali i wawizi wawapela zinghani zangu ho wagalece metumo gawo mabi, hamonga no wubi wawo. Ninga wene siwahulika, hamba kumvumila 'ne yaye.
5 Alu i wasogo zenyu, hamonga ne wawizi wawalya wose, wakondya kufwa.
6 Nane nghawapela i wasogo zenyu suguzi hamonga no wulajiize, mu nzila ye wetumami wangu wawizi. Ninga wene wakalema kuzityatya, hodu nane nghawa kuwaleka, wakatuga wugaye wuwaha. Ninga kanyhi wakanena no kumanya vyono, Ane no Muwaha Mulungu, we Mihanga, nakondya kuwalipiciza nha vyono yiwanojeye, nha vyono nali nalamula niwatendela.

Wulajilwe wulongozi: Fwalasi

7 Ale, mu mwaka we kejete, wa mu wuteme wakwe mutemi Daliyo. Mwi'zuwa lye makumi mejete na yine, lya mu mwezi we likumi na monga ne cilo, (Mwezi wono wacemwaga Shebati) Muwaha yakangubulila.
8 Nghamuwona malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu, yali yali mucanyha ye fwalasi ndunghu, yali yemile behi ne mibici ye mihadasi mwi'lolo. Mu nyuma yakwe hali hozili fwalasi zinji ze liwala lidunghu, na ze liwala lye 'hali ne zinzelu.
9 Nghamuwuza nghatya, “Gwe Muwaha wangu, zifwalasi azi zikulajila nghanici?
10 Muwaha Mulungu kawatuma awa, ho waze wayilanje yinze.”

11 Nawo wakamulonjela malayika wakatya, “Cayipilima yinze yose, kanyhi cawona vyono yinze yose yitulile kanyhi yisina wutazi.”
12 Hobaho malayika yulya yakatya, “Gwe Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga, wayikolelwa yikuwo Yelusalemi ne matumbi gose ga Yuda, sunga alu yafika myaka makumi mupunghati. Alu ho kojendelela sunga nili kuwawonela yikuwo, ho wuze wuwalajile lihewa lyako?”
13 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamupitula malayika mu zinghani zono zina wutuzwe.
14 Hanyuma malayika yakanonjela niwalonjele wanhu vyono azi zo Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga yakutya, “Nghuyiwonela lusungu no wendo Yelusalemi, likaya lyangu lyono lyelile.
15 Kanyhi nghuziwonela yikuwo zinghungugo zono zicehile kanyhi zina mulamu. Ku soko lono likuwo lyangu lyali lyapola so wanhu wangu, zinghungugo zizo, ho zikacila muno kuwagaza wanhu wangu no kuwetumya vibi.
16 Alu nawuya kanyhi ha Yelusalemi, ho ndajile zo lusungu lwangu mwi 'kaya. Ne Nyumba yangu yonozelezwa, ne likaya nalyo, lyozengwa kanyhi.”
17 Kanyhi malayika yakanonjela yakatya, nyanze nitye, “Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga yakutya, makaya gakwe gose, gotuga wulugutwe, kowuyajila kanyhi kuyitaza Yelusalemi, no kuyiwala vyono lyo likaya lyakwe.”

Wulajilwe we kejete: Mahembe

18 Na mu wulajilwe we kejete, nghawona mahembe mane ga nghongolo.
19 Nghamuwuza kanyhi Malayika yu yulya mono calonganaga nayo nghamutya, “Mahembe gaga nhunizo yayo nyhici.”

Yakamhitula yakanyitya, “Aga gemile gakulajila i vilungo vye yinze, vyono vyaweyagaza wanhu wa ku Yuda, na Isilayeli, na Yelusalemi.”
20 Hanyuma Muwaha Mulungu yakandajila wetumami wane, wono wali webate ne zinyondo zawo.
21 Nghamuwuza kanyhi nghatya, “Alo wanhu awano, waza kunozaci?” Yakamhitula yakanyitya, “Waza ho wazitajile woga no kuzigwisa zinghugugo zono zayikandamiza yisi ya Yuda, sunga zikaweyagaziza wanhu wose wa Yuda ako na ako.”


Zakaliya 2

Wulajilwe we kadatu: Mulejehe wo kupimila

1 Kanyhi mu wulajilwe wangu wunji, nghawona munhu mono yali yebate mulejehe wo kupimila.
2 Nghamuwuza nghamutya, “Alo 'gwe wukubita hayi?” Yakamhitula yakanyitya, “Nghubita kuwupima Yelusalemi, ho manye uwutali no wugazi wayo.”
3 Hanyuma nghamuwona malayika yulya mono calonganaga nayo yakutela ha mwande, nayo malayika yunji yakaza viketang'anila nayo.
4 Malayika ayulya umulongozi, yakamulonjela muyagwe yakamutya, “Wumanyhe, wukamulonjele u muzelelo ayulya mono yena mulejehe wo kupimila, vyono ku Yelusalemi kolowa ko wali wanhu wenji, hamonga ne mitugo minji. Holeka ne likaya lyowiluka liwe yiwaha sunga lifute no kuwuzenjela majinji.
5 Muwaha Mulungu mweneco, yelahiza vyono, u mwene yo kolowa majinji go moto gono gololipilima likaya no, kulidima kanyhi mwene kolojendelela kwikala baho nhondo zose, hamonga no lukumyo lwakwe lose.”

I wahamywa wakucemwa wawuye ku Yelusalemi

6 Muwaha Mulungu yakalonga ne wanhu wakwe yakatya, “Naweyagaza anye wose mu malanga gose.
7 Ninga alu nyo wahamywa, muwuce lu ku Babiloni, muwuye ku Yelusalemi.
8 No munhu wo wose mono komusagalenyi anye, yamanye vyono yakusagala cinhu co wuguzi wukamu kuli'ne.” Holeka u Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga kandeta nizilolose zizi kuli makungugo gono gawahamya i wanhu wakwe, nigatye nha vyono,
9 Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga mweneco kolokwihoma na nyenye. Nanye molosolwa ne wanhu wono baho halulongozi, wali wo wetumami wenyu.

Na kolo zizi zakondya kwijela, nawo wose ho wolomanya vyono yo gwegwe Muwaha Mulungu mono wanhuma.
10 Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Ale mwimbe musangalale 'nye nyo wanhu wa Yelusalemi! Hambi nha nghuza kwikalana nanye!”
11 Na mu vilo vivyo makungugo menji golokuza kuli Muwaha Mulungu, nawo wowa wanhu wakwe. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu kolokwikala hamonga na nyenye. Nanye ho molomanya vyono mwene yo yanhuma kuli'nye.
12 Kanyhi Yuda yolowa yo sawo yakwe siwanulwe yo Muwaha Mulungu mu yisi yakwe yono yelile, nayo Yelusalemi yolowa lyo likaya lyono yalyendile muno.
13 Ale munyamale wose twiii, hamwande yo Muwaha Mulungu. Hambi nha yakuza yakulawilila mu wikalo wakwe zono welile.


Zakaliya 3

Wulajilwe we kane: Mukohani Muwaha Yoshuwa

1 Ale, mu wulajilwe wunji kanyhi, Muwaha Mulungu yakandajila Mukohani Muwaha Yoshuwa yali yemile ha mwande ya malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu. Baho behi na Yoshuwa, yakema Setani mono yali yewikanize ho wesumanize na Yoshuwa.
2 Malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu yakamutya Setani. “Ale Muwaha Mulungu yakutacile 'gwe Setani! Muwaha Mulungu mono yayendile Yelusalemi, kokulipiciza. Hambi mwene Yoshuwa na yenha cizinga cono cawuswa mu moto.”
3 Ale, Yoshuwa yali yemile baho ha mwande ya malayika yavwalile myenda yono yitipiye.
4 Malayika yakawalonjela wetumami wakwe wa ku wulanga, wono wali wemile ha mwande yakwe yakawatya, “Mumuwopolaje munhu ayu myenda yiyo yono yitipiye, yono yavwalile.” Hanyuma yakamulonjela Yoshuwa yakamutya, “Ale, nakondya kukuwuziza wubi wako, nokupela ne myenda mipya yo wuvwale.”

5 Yakawalajiliza wetumami wamuvwalize Yoshuwa ne mukumbo muswanu mu mutwe wakwe, nawo wakamuvwika. Hanyuma wakasola ne myenda mipya nayo wakamuvwaliza. Lulo malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu yali yemie baho yalanjiliye.
6 Hanyuma malayika yakamulonjela Yoshuwa nha vyono
7 Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga yakutya, “Hono wuvumile no kwibata malajilizo gangu, no kwituma milimo yono nakupela witume, holeka kojendelela kuwa mulongola wa mu Nyumba yangu, hamonga ne mihali yayo. Nane nghwulika wulombe wako, nha vyono nghuwa kuwahuliciza i wamalayika wono wemile ha mwande yangu.
8 Wuhulicize 'gwe Yoshuwa, agwe yo gwegwe mono weza wuli Mukohani Muwaha. Nanye nyo wakohani wayagwe, i watyatyi wakwe, muhulicize, anye yo nyenye mono muli citanjilo ce ziswanu zose zono cikuzilanjilila. Nomulajila calinze u mwitumami wangu, mono kacemwaga Inhambi!
9 Nane nghuwika ha mwande ya Yoshuwa yibwe limonga, lyono lina wusu wuli mupunghati. Nokakata mandiko mucanyha yalyo, na mwi'zuwa limonga, nolowusiza lukulu u wubi wa mu yisi yiyi.
10 Ne lizuwa lilyo kolo lyeza kuza, nyenye wose molowalalika wekalani wayenyu waze muhanje nawo mu nyemo yo mulamu no wutule, i hanhu hono howa hapilimwe ne mizabibu yenyu hamonga ne mikuyu.”


Zakaliya 4

Wulajilwe we hano: Ciwicilo ce zitala

1 Malayika yuyulya mono calonganaga nayo yakaza kanyhi yakanyinusa kotya nha nali ngonile.
2 Yakambuza yakutya, “Alu wukuwona cici?” Nane nghamupitula nghamutya, “Nghuwona ciwicilo ce ziala cinozwe na zahabu. Kanyhi mucanyha, mo yili bakuli yono yina mafuta, a mu ciwicila ce zitala, mozili tala mupunghati, zono zina vitajililo vye mafuta mu zitala zizo.
3 Kanyhi ho yili ne mizeyituni myejete yili mu mhelo ye ciwicilo ce zitala, muzeyituni monga ku lubavu lumonga no muyagwe nawo ku lubavu luyagwe.”
4 Hanyuma nghamuwuza malayika yu yulya nghatya, “Alu i vinhu avi so cici vili baha”
5 Yakanyha, “Alu 'gwe siwuyimanyile nhunizo ye vinhu vivi?” Nane nghamutya, “Aa! Ng'o ye, simanyile muwaha.”

Wihuwizo wo Mulungu kuli Zelubabeli

6 Malayika yakanonjela nghamutegulile Zelubabeli nghani yono yikulawa kuli Muwaha Mulungu, yono yikutya, “Si kwe vilungo, hamba si ko wusume wako go mweneco, aa, ng'o! Langa ko muhe wangu.
7 Yakajendelela kulonga yakutya, ‘Wule, litunda lili lyokwimila? Aa ng'o! Agwe Zelubabeli, kolitenjelemula. Nagwe kokwanza wuzenji wupya we Nyumba yo Mulungu kolo wukulijeleka mwi'jinji libwe lye ndumilizo, wanhu wose wolozoza wakutya, 'ulungu wulilugutize! Mulungu wulilugutize!’ ”
8 Kanyhi yinghani yo Muwaha Mulungu yikanyiziza,
9 yikutya, “Zelubabeli yakondya kuwika musinji we Nyumba yo Mulungu, na mbeka koyikondeleza yikaya yiyi. Na kolo zizi zakondya kwijela ale, baho wanhu wose ho wokwihuwila vyono yo nene mono nakutegula kuli wene.
10 Aluno wakukalala lono wakuwona luzengo lukubita luli mhola mhola. Ninga luno wowona vyono zelubabeli yakulujendeleza luzengo lwe Nyumba yo Mulungu, nawo wosangalala muno muno.”

Malayika yakamhitula yakatya, “Zitala mupunghati, go meso mupunghati go Muwaha Mulungu gono gakusuma kuyiwona yinze yose.”
11 Hobaho nghamuwuza kanyhi nghatya, “Mibici myejete ye mizeyituni yono yili mu lubavu lwe ciwicilo ce zitala nhunizo yayo co cici.
12 Kanyhi i nhunizo ye manhambi mejete go mezeyituni gono gali mu lubavu lo kulume no kucekulu lwe vimilo vye zitala vye zahabu hamonga ne vibomba vyono vikutajila mafuta mu zitala?”
13 Ale, nayo yakambuza yakatya, “Alu 'gwe siwuzimanyile? Nghamupitula nghamutya, ng'o ye, simanyile 'gwe Muwaha.
14 Hanyuma ho yakanonjela yakatya, wo wanhu i wejete wono Mulungu yawahagula no kuwetila mafuta ho wamwitumamile Muwaha Mulungu we yinze yose.”


Zakaliya 5

Wulajilwe wo mutandatu Citabu cono ciguluce

1 Hobaho kanyhi nghenula meso, nghawa kuwona citabu ciguluce mucanyha.
2 Malayika yakawa kumbuza, alu wukuwona cici? Nghamupitula nghatya, nghuwona citabu ciguluce mucanyha, cono cina wutali wa mita yicenda no wugazi wayo mita yine na nusu.
3 Baho yakawa kunonjela yakatya, “Mu citabu cici mo wandicigwe wulijitwe wono wobita mu yisi yose, no lubavu luyagwe lwe citabu, lukutya vyono, i wahizi wose wa mu yisi yiyi, wowuswa mu yisi. No lubavu luyagwe nalo, lukutya vyono, na kuli munhu wo wose mono kokwilahiza ko wulambi, nayo kowingwa.
4 Zizi zo yakulonga u Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga yakutya vyono, kowuleta wulijitwe, nawo wokwinjila mu zikaya ze wahizi wose, na mu zikaya za wose wono wakwilahiza ko wulambi ale wulijitwe wowa mu zikaya zawo no kuwaleka mu matongo.”

Wulajilwe wo mupunghati: Mucekulu, mono yali yali mu nhoto

5 Hobaho malayika yu yulya mono calonganaga, yakejela hamwande yangu kanyhi yakanyitya, “Wulanje! Ko cili cinhu cinji kanyhi cono cikuza!”
6 Nane nghamuwuza nghatya, “Cicici cene?” Nayo yakamhitula yakatya. Ayo nhoto yo yikulajila wubi we yisi yose.
7 Langa ta ne nhoto yiyo yali yina cigubiko we liwala lye buli. Nane lono nali nghali niyilanjile, hobaho cigubiko cayo cikenulwa na mugati yayo, mo yali munhu wa cicekulu yekaye.
8 Malayika yu yulya yakatya, “U mucekulu ayu yakulajila u wubi.” Hanyuma yakamugomoleza kanyhi mu nhoto, yakawa kuyigubika ne cigubiko cayo.
9 Nghenula meso nghalanga kucanyha, nghawawona we cicekulu wejete waguluce wakuza kono nali nimile wena mabawa gono gali vilungo muno kotya ga Yobwa. Wakayinula nhoto yiyilya wakawa kuguluka nayo.
10 Nghawa kumuwuza malayika nghatya, “Alu wawa wakuyitegula nhoto yiyino hayi?”
11 Nayo yakapitula yakatya, wakuyitegula ku Babiloni wakayizenjela nyumba kuko. Na kolo nyumba yiyo yikukondya, i nhoto yiyo ho yowikwa baho, i hanhu hono wowa kuyihembela.


Zakaliya 6

U wulajiliwe wo munana: Mikokoteni ye zifwalasi

1 Kanyhi nghenula meso gangu, nghawona magali mane go wulugu gakuza gakulawilila haciligati ye matunda mejete ga shaba.
2 Ligali lilongozi lyakwegwaga ne zifwalasi zindunghu, na lye kejete nalyo wakwegwaga ne zifwalasi zinhitu,
3 lye kadatu nalyo ne zifwalasi zinzelu, na lye kane, nalyo ne zifwalasi ze buli.
4 Hobaho nane nghamuwuza malayika yu yulya nghatya, “Gwe muwaha, magali gaga gena nhunizoci?”
5 Nayo yakamhitula yakatya, “I magali gaga zo mbeho yine za ku wulanga, nazo zizi zikulawa kuli Muwaha Mulungu yinze yi yino yose.”

6 Ligali lyono likukwegwa ne zifwalasi zi nhitu, likubita ku lubavu lwe sumuka ku Babiloni, nazo zi nzelu zidomile ku lubavu lwa ku mwezi, nazo ze buli zikubita ku yisi ya ku lubavu lwa kutakama.
7 Hobaho zaza kulawila ze liwala lidunghu, zikasaka kubita ako na ako zikayilingule yinze yose. Nayo malayika yakazilonjela yakatya, “Ale, mubite ako na ako, mukayilingule yinze yose!” Nazo zikabita kuyilingula.
8 Hobaho malayika yakanyhema, yakanonjela yakatya, “Zifwalasi zono zibitile ku lubavu lwe sumuka, sunga ku Babiloni, zakondya kulipoza likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu.”

Lilajilizo lyo kumuvwika Yoshuwa mukumbo

9 Baho u Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya,
10 Ale, wusole i vipegwa vyono wokupela awalya wono walawa ku wuhamywe. Helidayi, na Tobiya, na Yedaya. Hanyuma wubite mbela mbela ku kaya ya Yosiya u mwana wa Zefaniya. Ne wanhu wawa wose, wo wakondya kuwuya ku wulawa ku wuhamywe ku Babiloni.
11 Wunoze mukumbo mu wulawa mu fweza ne zahabu, zono wakupelile, wumuvwice mu mutwe u mukohani muwaha Yoshuwa u mwana wa Yehozadaki.
12 Wumulonjele wutye, u Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga yakutya, “U munhu mono yakucemwa yinhambi yo kocindika baho kanyhi yo kozenga ne nyumba yo Mulungu no kumugomolela Muwaha lilumbo lyakwe.
13 U mwene yo kolizenga no kubocela lilumbo lyo mutemi, nayo kuwatema i wanhu wakwe. Nayo mukohani muwaha kolokwima he'goda lyakwe lyo wutemi. Nawo wokwitumana mulimo viswanu kwa mulamu, kanyhi wowa wehulicile.
14 No mukumbo wuwo, wolowa co cikumbucizo mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, so kuwapela lilumbo Helidayi, na Tobiya, na Yedaya na Yosiya.”
15 Ne wanhu wono wekaye kutali, wose wolokuza ho mwitaze nawo, ku wuzenga kanyhi Nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu. Nayo Nyumba kolo yakondya, baho ho molomanya vyono u Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga yo yanhuma kuli 'nye. Na zizi zose zoficilicila, hono anye wose mugavumile no kugatyatya i malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu.


Zakaliya 7

Muwaha Mulungu si yakwendezwa no wuleka kulya wo wukoci

1 Kukawa mu mwaka we kane, wa mu vilo vyo mutemi Daliyo, mwi'zuwa lye kane, na mu mwezi we licenda, mwezi wono wucemwaga ciselevu u Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela ane Zekaliya yakatya.
2 Wanhu wa ku Beteli wa wawatuma Shelezeleli na Legemumeleke, wanhu wawo, ku Nyumba yo Muwaha Mulugu we mihanga walombe wulugutizwe wo Muwaha Mulungu,
3 kanyhi wawuze kuli wakohani ne wawizi i liwuzo ali, watye, “Alu cijendelele kulila mu nzila yo kuleka kulya, mu mwezi we hano kusoko yo wongomezwe we Nyumba yo Mulungu, nha vyono cetuma mu myaka minji yono yipulute?”
4 Ayi yo nghani yo Muwaha Mulungu yono yanyiziza.
5 Yakanonjela kanyhi yakatya wuwalonjele wanhu wa mu yisi hamonga ne wakohani vyono, lono walekaga kulya, akuno wa kulila mu mwezi we hano na wo mupunghati mu myaka yi yilya yose makumi mupunghati, wule, si yali nzila yo kulajila cogopo ca kuli'ne.
6 Na lono walizaga wo kung'wa wali so wiyendeze wawo weneco du.
7 Zizi zo yalonda Muwaha Mulungu, mu milomo ye wawizi walongozi mu vipango vyono na Yelusalemi, likaya lyono lyelile, lyali likali lina no lukumyo, kanyhi limemile ne wanhu. Na lono wanhu wali wenji, wono wekalaga mu makaya, gono gali galipilime likaya, sunga na mu zi'si za kutakama, na mu mwalambalo wa mu matunda ga ku lubavu lwa ku mwezi.

Wu wula cihuliko, yo soko yo wuhamywe wuhamywe

8 Hobaho Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Zekaliya yakatya,
9 “Ane nawapela i wanhu wangu malajilizo gaga, ku wulawa katali, nghawatya, ‘Ale, mulanjilile mwitume ze ling'ani, kanyhi mweze mwina wendo no lusungu, kuli munhu no muyagwe.
10 Mulece kuwawonela wafwile, hamba wana wono wasina walezi, hamba wajenzi wono mwikalane nawo, hamba munhu wo wose mono yali mu wanjile. Kanyhi mulece kuleta mahweso mabi go witamise munhu na muyagwe.’
11 Ninga i wanhu wangu wangu wakawa wali cibede siwakasaka hamba no kuhuliciza, wakadinda ne makutu gawo ho walece kuhulika,
12 wakakamufya ne zinhumbula zawo zikawa nghamu ku wuhulice. Na vyono si wasaka no kugahuliciza ne mafundo, gono nawapelela muli wawizi we katali, hodu, nane nghawakolelwa muno likuwo.
13 Alu vyono siwahuliciza zangu, holeka nane sinawapitula no wulombe wawo.
14 Na ku soko yiyo holeka nghawafwagula, no kuwawinjila ku zi'si zo wujenzi. Ne yisi ayi swanu, yikekala nyhiwa musina munhu mono yasigala yekale mumu.”


Zakaliya 8

Muwaha Mulungu yakwihuwiza nha vyono koyizenga kanyhi Yelusalemi

1 Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga, yakamulonjela Zekaliya yakatya,
2 “Alu, nendezwa kulitaza no kulizenga kanyhi likaya lya Yelusalemi, ku soko yo wendo wangu wuwaha kuli wanhu wayo, u wendo wono wamhela likuwo, ku soko ye walugu walyo.
3 Alu nowuya 'ne ku Yelusalemi, likaya lyangu lyono lyelile, niwe kwikala mumo. Likaya lyocemwa likaya lye mbeka. Ne litunda lyo Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga, nalyo lyocemwa litunda lyono lyelile.
4 Kanyhi i wakombi we cilume na we cicekulu wono wakwikomajiza ne zinghome lono wakujenda, wolowa kuceha mu viwanza vye likaya.
5 Ne zinzila za mwi'kaya zolomema wana we cilume na we cicekulu, wolowa kwidawala mumo.”

6 Ne nghani yiyi kuli wanhu we zinghungugo zono zisigaye, wolowona kotya nha siyosumika, ninga kuli'ne yosumika du.
7 Ane nowawusa wanhu wangu ku wulawa ku yisi ya ku'cilima na ku yisi ya kumwezi.
8 Niwagomole wose wa ku wulawilila ku cilima sunga ko ku mwezi wawuye wekale mu Yelusalemi. Nawo wowa wanhu wangu, nane nowa Mulungu wawo, mono nowadimila mu mbeka na mu ling'ani.
9 U Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga yakutya, “Mwime mukamile mweze kuli vilungo! Hambi sunga nalu, nha mukuzihulika zinghani zono i wawizi wazilonga, lu lulya, lono wawicilwa u musinji so wuzenji kanyhi we Nyumba yangu.
10 Na mbeka hono zizi zali zikali nejela, hasina yunji mono yasuma kumuhumbiliza muyagwe hamba mutugo, kanyhi hali hasina mono yali yena mulamu kuli walugu wakwe. Vyono ane nghawaleka ho wagombane munhu na muyagwe.
11 Ninga 'lu nghuwanozeza zono zisimile wono wasigaye we nghungugo yiyi.
12 Wolohadika zimbeyu zawo ko mulamu. Ne mizabibu yawo yolela mikalo viswanu, nawo muhangha we yisi wololavya wuhemba, ne mvula yowa kutonya muno. No wulugutizwe wuwu nowapela i wanhu wa mu nghungugo yangu wono wasigaye.
13 Nye, nyo wanhu wa Yuda na Isilayeli! Ha mwamulizo, wajenzi welijitaga weneco wose wakatya, ‘Wongomezwe wono wawutuga wanhu wa Yuda na Isilayeli, ale, wukutuje na gwegwe.’ Ninga ane nomuponyenyi, sunga ne wajenzi wapitule ne mahweso gawo kanyhi, walongane munhu na muyagwe wawe kutya, ‘Ale, wubocele wulugutizwe, nha vyono wawubocela wanhu wa Yuda na Isilayeli. Holeka mwime mukamile kanyhi mulece kogopa.’ ”

14 U Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga yakutya, “Lono walezi wenyu wanghalazaga, nawawikaniziza wongomezwe, hamba sinagakawaniza mahweso gangu yaye, langa najendelela du ne mahweso gago.
15 Ninga 'lu, nghwiwikaniza niwapele wulugutizwe wanhu wa Yelusalemi na Yuda. Holeka mulece kogopa.
16 Alu azi zo mokwituma. Mweze kulonga ze mbeka munhu na muyagwe. Namo mu mahakama, mweze kuling'aniza kwe ling'ani mu nzila yono yikuleta mulamu.
17 Mulece kwihwesa zimbi zo kwiyitamisa nyenye wose. Hamba kulavya wuhomelezi wo wulambi ko wilahize, hambi 'ne nha nizisulile zinghani zo wulambi na zo wukali, mu nzila yo kuwewonela wanhu.”

18 U Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga, yo yamupela zinghani zizi u muwizi Zekaliya.
19 Wikapise wo wuleka kulya, wa mu mwezi we kane na we 'hano na wo mupunghati, na we likumi zowa zo sikukuu ze nyemo mbaha no wusangalale kuli wanhu wa Yuda. Holeka mwende mbeka no mulamu.
20 Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga yakutya, “Vilo vyokuza, vyono i wanhu wololawa mu makaya ganji, nawo wokuza ku Yelusalemi.
21 Nawo wanhu we likaya linji, wolongana na we likaya linji, wotya, ‘Ase cikubita kumuhemba Muwaha Mulngu we mihanga, ho yacipela wulugutwe no wendezwe wakwe. Cibitenyi nanye cikamuhembe hamonga!’
22 Hobaho wanhu wenji hamonga ne zinghungugo zono zina vilungo zolokuza ku Yelusalemi kumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga, wamulombe yawapele wulugutizwe wakwe.
23 Na mu vilo vivyo, wanhu yikumi we cijenzi, wolomuziza muyahudi monga wamutye, cikulomba ciwe hamonga nanye, hambi nha cihulika vyono u Mulungu yali hamonga nanye.”


Zakaliya 9

U wuling'anizwe we zinghungugo zono zipilime Yelusalemi

1 Ayi yo nghani yono yikulawa kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Muwaha Mulungu yakalavya lilajilizo lyo wulipicizwe we yisi ya Hadelaki, ne likaya lya Damesiki. Ninga wulipicizwe wuwo si wowa so zimhuga ze Waisilayeli du, langa hamonga ne likaya lya Siliya, likaya lyo Muwaha Mulungu.
2 Na Hamati yono yili behi na Hedelaki, hamonga ne makaya ga Tulo na Sidoni, hamba gawe gena mahala menji.
3 Nayo Tulo, yezenjela magomawakali gono galipilime likaya, no kukonghola fweza nyinji, hamonga na zahabu nyinji, kotya matope ga mu nzila.
4 Ninga lanje ta! U Muwaha Mulungu komuwusiza lukulu vivyo vyose. Nayo kolozigumila zi sawo zakwe mwi'lenga, ne likaya nalyo, lyoloticimizizwa lukulu.

5 Ne likaya lya Ashikeloni lyoloziwona zizi nalyo lyolokogopa. Nhavivyo ne likaya lya Gaza, lyoloziwona zizi, nalyo lyolowona wusungu wuwaha muno. Nayo Ekiloni hamonga no witumbilo wayo wose, nayo yolobenanjizwa. Nayo Gaza yolowula mutemi, Ashikeloni yololekwa mu wuciwa wuwaha muno.
6 Ne wanhu macotala, wolokwikala hamonga ku Ashidodi. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Nowanyetya wawa i Wafilisiti wewoni.
7 Siwoloyilila lukulu kanyhi nyama yono yina ne sakami yayo, hamba kulya ndigwa yo yose yono yili mwiko kuyilya. Nawo wose wono wolosigala, wolojendelela kuwa wanhu wangu, nawo nolowehwaniciza ne mhuga ya mu lulelo lwe nyumba ya Yuda. Nayo Ekiloni yose, wolowa wanhu wangu kotya Wayebusi.
8 Nane noyilanjilila yisi yiyi, kanyhi nowika ne wasilikale wono woyidima ho wanji walece kukola mumo. Kanyhi hamba sinowakombola walugu hamonga ne wewonezi, ho wawewonele wanhu wangu. Hambi nha nakondya kuwona vyono, i wanhu wangu wakugazwa muno.”

U mutemi wo mulamu mono cikumulanjilila

9 Musecelele, musecelele nye nyo

wanhu wa ku Sayuni!

Muzoze muno muno so nyemo,

anye nyo wanhu a Yelusalemi!

Mulanje ta!

Mutemi wenyu yakumuzizenyi 'nye!

Yakuza yena nyemo mbaha ne mhonelo,

kanyhi mupolo,

yakuza yali mucanyha ye ndogowe,

nyana ye ndogowe.

10 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya,

“Nogawusa ne matuka go wulugu,

gono gakulawa ku Isilayeli,

niziwuse ne zifwalasi

zono zolawa ku Yelusalemi,

hamonga no wupinde

wono wukwitumamilwa mu wulugu,

vyose vyokongomezwa.

No mutemi wenyu,

koleta mulamu kuli zinghungugo zose.

Kanyhi kotema ku wulawilila kwi'lenga sunga

ko kwi'lenga na ku wulawilila kwi 'kolongo lya Efulata,

sunga ko kundumo ye yisi.”

Kuwagomola kanyhi wanhu wo Mulungu

11 U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya,

“Kusoko ye cilagane cangu na nyenye,

cono cawikwa

mu nzila ye sakami ye makumbiko.

Holeka nane nowawopolela wafungwa

no kuwawusa mwi'kombo

lyono lisina malenga,

likombo lyo wuhamywe.

12 Ale muwuye anye wose nyo wahamywa

mono alu mweza mwina witumbilo,

muwuye ku wikalo wenyu wono wina mulamu.

Alu nghumulonjelenyi vyono,

nomupelenyi wulugutwe kali kejete

ku soko magaziko genyu.

13 Nomwitumamila Yuda yawe kotya

wo wupinde wo musilikale,

na Isilayeli yawe kotya zo songa.

Nowetumamila wanhu wa

ku Sayuni wawe kotya wo mumaje,

wehome ne wanhu wo Wugiliki.”

14 Nayo Muwaha ho kokwilajila

mu wanhu wakwe koguma zi songa zakwe,

nazo zobita kotya limuli.

U Muwaha Mulungu yo kotowa ne ndulele.

Nayo kojenda ne mbeho

ye civulavumbi kuwulawilila ku takama ye yisi.

15 Kanyhi u Muwaha Mulungu

we mihanga yo kowakamisa ne wanhu wakwe,

nawo wowongomeza ne walugu wawo.

Kanyhi wolowa kuzoza kuko

ku wulugu kotya nhe wagalo 'jimbi

kanyhi wolopegwa vilungo

vyo kuyimwaga sakami ye walugu wawo;

sakami yiyo yolohuluma kotya sakami

ya ha wukumbicilo,

yono yimwajigwe ku wulawa

mu bakuli ha cikumbicilo.

16 Hobaho lizuwa lilyo lilaza kuwa lyafika,

u Muwaha Mulungu

ho kolowaponya wanhu wakwe,

nha vyono umudimi kaponyaga litewa lyakwe,

lono lyamanyhilwa ne lilimu.

Nawo wolong'alala mu yisi yakwe,

kotya nha vibwe vyo wihambo,

vya mu mukumbo wo wutemi.

17 Lanjenyi ta,

yisi yiyo yolowa swanu wule?

I wahinza ne wazelelo,

wolowa kuwiluka wali vilungo,

so wuhemba ne divayi vyono vili mumo.


Zakaliya 10

U Muwaha Mulungu yakuwehuwizia wukombozi

1 Ale mumulombaje Muwaha i mvula mu vilo vye cifuku. Hambi mwene nha yo Muwaha Mulungu mono kaciletelaga mvula ne mavunde, hamonga ne mahalavu, kanyhi yo kayinozaga ne migunda yetu yiwa yili dedede.
2 Hambi i wanhu wono wawuzaga kuli milungu yinji ye vihwani, no kwitoweleza cisangu, ne lipitulo lyono wakuwa kutuga nha lya wulambi, kanyhi lya wulele du. Wanji wakuwa kuzilolosa zi ndoto no kuzinoza kotya nha cinhu ca mbeka, kumbe wakumukonjenyi bwete du. No wutuzwe wono wakuwa kumulonjelenyi wa bwete du. Holeka hobaho, i wanhu wakupilima-pilima du, kotya nhe zingholo zono zajiliye. Wali mu magayo, hambi nha wasina mudimi.
3 U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Nghuwawonela yikuwo, i wajenzi wose wono wawatemile wanhu wangu. Holeka nane nowalipiciza wose, hambi i wanhu wa Yuda, nha wangu. Nane no Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga, nowadima wanhu wangu. Kanyhi nowanoza wawe nha fwalasi zangu zo wulugu.
4 Na muli weneco, nowahagulila wono wowalongoza, i walongola hamonga ne vitung'ati wono wowatema i wanhu wangu.
5 Ne wanhu wa Yuda wowa no wusume, kotya nha wasilikale wono wakuwakanyanjila i walugu wawo, mu matope ga mu zi nzila za muwulugu. Kanyhi wowahoma, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yali hamonga nawo, kanyhi wowasumiza sunga walugu wawo i wajendela zifwalasi.

6 “Nowanoza wanhu wa Yuda wawe

ne vilungo kanyhi nowaponya wanhu wa Isilayeli.

Kanyhi nowawonela lusungu

no kuwagomola wose kono kaya yawo.

Wowa kotya nha wono si nawalema

ha mwamulizo yaye.

Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu,

Mulungu wawo, nowapitula no wulombe wawo.

7 Wanhu wa Isilayeli wowa

ne vilungo kotya wasilikale.

Kanyhi wowa wena nyemo,

kotya wanhu wono wang'weye divayi.

Ne wana wawo,

wolokumbucila zo wusume wuwu,

nawo wolowa wena nyemo

ku soko ya zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yazitumile.

8 Nowacema wanhu wangu

no kuwakongholela hamonga.

Nowakomboza kanyhi nowanoza wonjelezece

ho wawe wenji nha

vyono wali wenha ha mwamulizo.

9 Hambi nha naweyagaziza wose

mu makungugo ganji.

Hamba wawe nha wali kutali muno,

ninga wonghumbucila du.

I wene hamonga ne wana wawo,

wosigala no kuwuya wose hamonga.

10 Nolowagomola kono kaya,

ku wulawilila ku Misili na Ashuli,

no kuwawika wacehe mu yisi yawo,

kanyhi nolowawika

mu Gileyadi na mu Lebanoni.

Ne yisi yose yolowa yimemile wanhu.

11 Nawo kolo weza kukola mwi'lenga lyo wugaye.

Ane no Muwaha Mulungu,

nologatowa i mawingo ge malenga.

Ne visolo vye likolongo

lya Nile vyolonyala.

No wivuni we yisi ya Ashuli wolonyetywa.

Ne yisi ya Misili yono yili vilungo,

yolowula vilungo.

12 Nolowanoza i wanhu wangu wawe ne vilungo,

nawo wolowa kuhemba 'ne,

no kunyogopa nene.”

Vyo yakutya u Muwaha Mulungu.


Zakaliya 11

U wugwe wa wono wawewonelaga wayawo

1 Wudigule milango yako 'gwe!

Lebanoni,

u moto ho wutakamize mibici yako ye mikambala!

2 Wuzoze no kulila 'gwe go musonobali,

hambi u muhagata nha wagwiza,

i mibici yono yikamile

nha yakondya malika!

Muzoze kanyhi mulile 'nye

nyo mibici ye minghola ya Bashani,

hambi libago liwaha na lye vilungo,

nha lyatemangwa.

3 I watemi wako wakulila

ko wusungu wuwaha no kukalala,

hambi u lukumyo lwawo nha lwaduma,

wuhulicize ne mikolomo ye zisimba hambi

i mbago yono zikalaga ya

mu nhumbi ya Yoludani,

nha yahondanjika.

I wadimi wejete

4 U Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wangu, yakanonjela yakatya, “Wudime litewa lye zingholo zono zowulagwa.
5 Hambi i wanya zingholo, nha wakuwa kuziwulaga du, kanyhi wasina wulipicizwe, langa wakuwa kuziguza nyama no kutya, ‘Yalumbizwe Muwaha Mulungu. Hambi ase nha cili wagoli!’ Hamba ne wadimi wawo we zingholo zizi, wakuwa wasina lusungu ne zingholo zizi.”
6 U Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Sinomuwonela lusungu kanyhi munhu wo wose mu yisi yiyi. Nene no Muwaha Mulungu, nowawika wanhu wose mu miwoko ye walongola wawo, ne walongola wawo wayihome yisi yiyi, nane hamba sinowaponya mu miwoko yawo yaye.”

7 Hobaho awalya wono wagulaga no kuguza zi ngholo wakunondola no kusola, ho niwe mudima zingholo zilya zono zategulwaga ho zikawulagwe. Baho nghetujila nghome monga, nghayicemela yitagwa “Ihewa” na ye kejete nghayicemela “Malunza.” Hobaho nghajendelela kulidima litewa lili.
8 Nghawa sina witumbilo kuli wadimi wadatu wa ha mwamulizo, wono wakasula muno ho baho nane nghawa kuwawusa wose mu mwezi monga.
9 Hanyuma nghalilonjela litewa nghatya, “Alu ne sinowa mudimi wenyu kanyhi, holeka lece zono zikufwa zifwe du, na zono zikongomezwa, zongomezwe du, na zono zisigaye nazo zokwiyilya zeneco zose.”
10 Hobaho nghawa kuyisola nghome yangu yono yikucemwa “Ihewa,” nghayibenanga ho mbene cilagane co Muwaha Mulungu kuli makungugo gose.
11 Hobaho cilagane cici cikawa cabeneka mwi'zuwa lilyo. Awalya wono wagulaga no kuguza zingholo, wali wananjiliye, nawo wakamanya vyono u Muwaha Mulungu yalongaga nawo mu nzila ya zizo zono nazitumaga.
12 Hobaho nghawatya, “Hono muwe mwendezwa no mulimo wangu, ale, mumhelaje musahala wangu. Ninga hono muwe si mwendezwe, hodu, mulece kumhela.” Hobaho nawo wakawa kumhela u musahala wangu vipande vye zifweza makumi madatu.

13 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya, uviwikaje vivyo vipande vye fweza mu vinyumba vyo kuwicila viwutwa vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Hobaho nghawa kuvisola vivilya vipande vye fweza, liwalo lililya du lyono wehwesaga vyono lyonitumbiye, nghawa kuviwika mu vinyumba vyo kuwicila viwutwa vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
14 Hanyuma nghayibena ne nghome ye kejete yono yacemwaga “Malunza.” Hobaho u wilunze wa Yuda na Isilayeli, wukawa kutemanuka.
15 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya, “Wusole kanyhi viya, wuwe nha wuli mudimi mulele.
16 Hambi nha nakondya kumuwika u mudimi we litewa lyangu. Ninga mwene hamba si yakusuma kuzitaza zingholo zono zibenece migulu, hamba kuzilondola yaye zono zajiliye, hamba kuziwopela zono zifyonghoye, hamba kuzilisa zono zina miwili miswanu. Langa mwene, kolya du nyama ye zingholo zono cijinile, no kugapandulanga ne mabangho gazo.
17 Ninga woyi hee, kuli mudimi yuyu mulele, mono si mwihuwilwa, Hambi nha yazileka zingholo. Holeka wulugu womutijinha vilungo vyakwe. U muwoko wakwe wonyala, ne liso lyakwe lyo kulume lyobofula.”


Zakaliya 12

U wukombozwe wono wukuza wa Yelusalemi

1 Azi zo nghani zono yakuzilonga Muwaha Mulungu kuli wanhu wa Isilayeli. U Muwaha Mulungu mono yawutandaza wulanga, yakayiwumba ne yinze, yakamupela no munhu wumi, yakutya,
2 “Lanjenyi ta, noyinoza Yelusalemi yiwe cikombe ce divayi, ne zinghungugo zono ziyipilime, zong'wa no kupebesuka-pebesuka kotya i wanhu wono wagalile wujimbi. Na kolo walipilima likaya lya Yelusalemi, na matumbi gose ga Yuda nago gohomwa.
3 Ninga vipango vivyo kolo vyafika, ane noyinoza Yelusalemi yiwe kotya yibwe yizito. Ne nghungugo yo yose yono yojela kuyinula Yelusalemi, yolotamiswa muno. Ne zinghungugo zose za mono yinze, zolokwilunza ho ziyihome Yelusalemi.
4 Nane mu vipango vivyo, nolozogofya zifwalasi zawo, no kuwanoza i wajendela zifwalasi wawo, wawe wena lukwale. Nowalanjilila viswanu wanhu wa Yuda, kanyhi nolozinoza zifwalasi ze walugu wawo zibofule.
5 Ne zindelo zose za Yuda wololongana weneco wose wawe kutya, ‘U Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga, yo yacipeye vilungo co wanhu wakwe, mono cikaye ha Yelusalemi.’
6 Mu vilo vivyo, nozinoza zindelo zose za Yuda ziwe kotya moto wono wukwaka mu mbago, hamba moto wono wukwaka mu mugunda wo wuhemba wono wupiye. Nawo wozongomeza zinghungugo zose zono ziwapilime. Ne wanhu wa he 'kaya lya yelusalemi, wolokwikala wapolile du mwi'kaya.

7 “Nane no Muwaha Mulungu, nolowapela wusume i wanhu wa Yuda, hanyuma ho lilumbo lyono we ndelo ya Dawudi ne wanu wa Yelusalemi wotuga, lilece kuwa licizize si nha lilumbo lye wanhu wa Yuda wono wasigaye.
8 Mu vilo vivyo, u Muwaha Mulungu kolowadima i wanhu wose wono wekaye mu Yelusalemi, sunga na wono wasoceye muli wene, nawo wolotuga vilungo kotya vyono yali yenha Dawudi. Na we ndelo ya Dawudi wolowalongoza kotya nha malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu, kanyhi kotya Mulungu mweneco.

9 “Na mwi'pango lilyo noloyongomezeza lukulu nghungugo yo yose yono yolohila kuyihoma Yelusalemi.
10 Kanyhi nowamemya Muhe wo lusungu no Muhe wo wulombe, wose we ndelo ya Dawudi, hamonga ne wanhu wose wa Yelusalemi. Nawo womulanga no kumulilila u munhu mono wamuwulaga, kanyhi womulilila cililo kotya nha wakumukalila u mwana we ciwene. Wolila ko wusungu wuwaha wowa kotya nha wono wamwajilize mwana wawo we ciwene hamba u mwana wawo umulongozi kulelwa.
11 Na mwi'pango lilyo cililo colowa ciwaha mu Yelusalemi, kotya cililo cha Hadalilimoni mwi 'lolo lya Megido.
12 Ne zimhuga zose za mu yisi, zolokwililila zeneco, nha cinji zimhuga ze ndelo ya Dawudi, ne zimhuga ze ndelo ya Natani,
13 ne zimhuga ze ndelo ya Lawi, ne zimhuga ze ndelo ya Shimeyi hamonga ne zimhuga zinji zose.
14 Zimhuga zose zololila wanhu na hawo. Nawo wolowa kulila wewaguye we cilume lwawo, nawo we cicekulu lwawo wali wene.


Zakaliya 13

1 “Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga yakutya, vilo vivyo kolo vyafika, mumvilo wolodigulwa, wuweze wanhu we ndelo ya Dawudi, ne wanhu wa ha Yelusalemi, u wubi wawo hamonga no wuhemba milungu yinji.
2 Kanyhi mu vilo vivyo, nolowusa ne matagwa ge milungu ye vihwani yono yili mu yisi, na holowa hasina yunji wo wose mono kolosuma kujikumbucila kanyhi. Nolowawinga wose wono wakwinoza wawizi wo wulambi, no kuwawusiza wuhile wuwo i wahemba vihwani.
3 No munhu wo wose mono kolojendelela kulavya wuwizi ale, u sogwe no nyina, wolomulonjela vyono yanojeye yawulagwe. Hambi nha yakwinoza vyono yakulonga zinghani zo Muwaha Mulungu, kumbe ho yakulonga zo wulambi du. Holeka lono yakuwila, u sogwe no nyina womuwulaga.
4 Ne vilo vivyo kolo vikuza, holowa hasina muwizi wo wose mono kolokwidunhumulila zo wugubulilwe wakwe hamba kwituma zo wuwizi, hamba kuvwala i kanzo ye ciwizi-wizi, ho yawakonje wanhu.
5 Langa mwene kolotya, ‘Ane si nili muwizi yaye, ane mulimo wangu kulima du, wo mulimo wangu wulawa wudodo wangu.’
6 Na lono yunji yamuwuze yatye, ‘Alu zinghomele zizo zono zili mu mhambaga yako, wawa cici?’ Nayo komupitula yatye, ‘Nazituga mu nyumba yo mwiyendi muyangu.’ ”

I lilajilizo lyo kumuwulaga u mudimi wo Mulungu

7 U Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga yakutya, “Winuce 'gwe go mumaje, wumuhungucile mudimi wangu, mono yakunyitumamila. Wumuwulagaje, ne zingholo zokwiyagala, Nane nowatowanga wanhu wangu.
8 Na mu yisi yiyi yose mafungu mejete ge wanhu wofwa.
9 Nane ho nowalanga wa walya we lifungu lye kadatu wono wasigaye, nane noweza nha fweza yono yezigwe no moto kanyhi nowajela nha vyono zahabu yikuwa kujelwa. Hobaho wonyhema, nane nowapitula, ‘Nane nowalonjela vyono wene wo wanhu wangu, awo wovumila vyono ane yo nene Mulungu wawo.’ ”


Zakaliya 14

Yelusalemi ne zinghugugo zinji

1 Lanjeta! Lizuwa lyono Muwaha Mulungu kolokwikala mwi'goda lyakwe lyo wuling'anizi, nha lili behi. Baho Yelusalemi yolotekwa. Ne mateka wose wologawilwa ha mwande ye meso genyu.
2 U Muwaha Mulungu kolozileta zinghungugo zose hamonga, zihome na Yelusalemi. Likaya lyolosolwa, ne zikaya zolotekwa. Ne we cicekulu wolokwibatwa kwa vilungo ne walugu, wagone nawo. Ne nusu ye wanhu wolobita mu mateka ninga wanji wa muli wene, siwolosolwa yaye ho wawuce mwi'kaya.
3 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu ho kololawila yehome ne zinghungugo zizo, nha vyono yawahoma mu vilo vyono vipulute.
4 Baho ho kolokwinuka no kwima mwi'tunda lye Mizeyituni ku lubavu lwa kucilima ya Yelusalemi. Baho litunda lye Mizeyituni, ho lyolokwigawa hejete, ku wulawilila kucilima ko ku mwezi. Ne lilolo yiwaha lyolowa hagati yayo. Ne nusu ye litunda, lyolowa likwiwila lidomile ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, ne nusu yayo nayo yolowa kwiwila yidomile lubavu lwa kutakama.
5 Nanye molotiza mukolele mwi'lolo lilyo lyono lilitemanuye litunda hejete. Mulamanghaga nha vyono i wasogo zenyu wanozaga, mu vipango vye litulo lyono lyejela, mu vilo vyo mutemi Uziya wa Yuda. U Muwaha Mulungu wangu, mwene kolokuza hamonga ne wamalayia wakwe.

6 Vilo vivyo kolo vyaza, kolowa kusina mbeho, hamba mbeho ye cinyuli,
7 Hamba wutitu wo wose. Langa kolowa no wuzelu du nhondo zose, sunga na ku cilo kose. No Muwaha Mulungu du yali wene, yo yamanyile lizuwa lyono zilejelela zizi.
8 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo, malenga maswanu golohuluma gakulawilila ku Yelusalemi. Nusu yago, golokwinjila mwi'lenga lyo mwino, ne nusu nzagwe ye kejete, golokwinjila mwi'lenga lya Meditelaneya. Lyolohuluma vilo vyose vya mu mwaka wose. Mu vipango vye cifuku ne cibahu.
9 Ale baho, u Muwaha Mulungu ho kolowa yo mutemi we yinze yose. Baho wose wolomuhemba mwene vyono yo Mulungu, kanyhi wolomumanya mwene vyono lilyo lyo litagwa lyakwe.

10 Ne yisi yose, ku wulawilila Geba lubavu lwe sumuka, sunga ko ku lubavu lwe takama ku Limoni, yolotelemezwa ho yiwe ya wuhihi. Nayo Yelusalemi, yolozengwa mu cizuli ce yisi yono yipilime. Ne likaya lyololawilila kwi 'lango lya Benyamini, sunga ko kwi'lango lya kumhelo, hono hali ho wuli mulango mulongozi. Na ku wulawilila ha munala wa Hananeli, sunga kono kogali makobonjelo ge zizabibu go mutemi.
11 Ne wanhu wolokwikala baho wapolile, kolowa kusina wogofywe, hamba wongomezwe yaye.
12 U Muwaha Mulungu kololeta nhamwa 'mbi yo kogofya mu zinghungugo zose zono zoloyihoma Yelusalemi. Ne nyama ye miwili yawo, yolowola lono wakali womi, ne meso gawo hamonga ne zinimi zawo, navyo vyolowola.

13 Mu vilo vivyo, u Muwaha Mulungu kolowewajiza wose mahala gawo, no kuwamemya woga mu zinhumbula zawo, ho wasume kwiyihoma weneco wose munhu no munhu muyagwe mono yali behi nayo.
14 Nawo i wanhu wa Yuda, wolokwinjila mumo mu wulugu wakuyitaziza Yelusalemi. Kanyhi woloziteka ne zisawo ze zinghungugo zinji, zahabu, fweza, ne myenda hamonga ne vinhu vinji muno.
15 Kanyhi litamwa na yibi lyolozigwila fwalasi, ne zingamiya, ne zinyumbu ne zindogowe, sunga na mu mitugo yose yowa yili mu zinghambi ze walugu.
16 Hobaho wose wono wasigaye wa mu zinghungugo zono zayihomaga Yelusalemi, zolowa kubita mwaka kwa mwaka kumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga u mutemi, no kusangalalila sikukuu ye vibanda.
17 Na hono nghungugo yiyi yileme kubita kumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga u mutemi, ale ne mvula nayo siyotonya mu yisi yawo.
18 Nawo wa Mumisili, hono waleme kubita kusangalalila sikukuu ye vibanda, ale, nawo wokwibatwa ne litamwa lilyo libi, lyono Muwaha Mulungu kowaletela zinghungugo zose zono zolema kubita kumuhemba.
19 Awu wowa wo wulipicizwe wono woyigwila Misili, hamonga ne zinghungugo zinji zono zi zobita kuyisangalalila sikukuu yiyo ye vibanda.

20 Kanyhi hamba nhavivyo, na mwi'zuwa lilyo sunga ne madodolo ge zifwalasi golokwandikwa zinghani azi, “Welile kuli Muwaha” ne vyungu zose zo kutelecela mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, zowa zezigwe nhe zibakuli zono zili ha cikumbicilo.
21 Ne zinyungu zose zo kutelecela za mu Yelusalemi, na za mu Yuda yose, zolowa zasiwanulwa, ho zitumamilwe mu wuhembi wo Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga. Ne wanhu wono wowa kulavya makumbiko gawo, wozitumamila so wutelecela zinyama zawo ze makumbiko. Ne lizuwa lilyo kolo lyafika, kolowa kusina kanyhi musuluza mu Nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga.
