Zakaliya 11

U wugwe wa wono wawewonelaga wayawo

1 Wudigule milango yako 'gwe!

Lebanoni,

u moto ho wutakamize mibici yako ye mikambala!

2 Wuzoze no kulila 'gwe go musonobali,

hambi u muhagata nha wagwiza,

i mibici yono yikamile

nha yakondya malika!

Muzoze kanyhi mulile 'nye

nyo mibici ye minghola ya Bashani,

hambi libago liwaha na lye vilungo,

nha lyatemangwa.

3 I watemi wako wakulila

ko wusungu wuwaha no kukalala,

hambi u lukumyo lwawo nha lwaduma,

wuhulicize ne mikolomo ye zisimba hambi

i mbago yono zikalaga ya

mu nhumbi ya Yoludani,

nha yahondanjika.

I wadimi wejete

4 U Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wangu, yakanonjela yakatya, “Wudime litewa lye zingholo zono zowulagwa.
5 Hambi i wanya zingholo, nha wakuwa kuziwulaga du, kanyhi wasina wulipicizwe, langa wakuwa kuziguza nyama no kutya, ‘Yalumbizwe Muwaha Mulungu. Hambi ase nha cili wagoli!’ Hamba ne wadimi wawo we zingholo zizi, wakuwa wasina lusungu ne zingholo zizi.”
6 U Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Sinomuwonela lusungu kanyhi munhu wo wose mu yisi yiyi. Nene no Muwaha Mulungu, nowawika wanhu wose mu miwoko ye walongola wawo, ne walongola wawo wayihome yisi yiyi, nane hamba sinowaponya mu miwoko yawo yaye.”

7 Hobaho awalya wono wagulaga no kuguza zi ngholo wakunondola no kusola, ho niwe mudima zingholo zilya zono zategulwaga ho zikawulagwe. Baho nghetujila nghome monga, nghayicemela yitagwa “Ihewa” na ye kejete nghayicemela “Malunza.” Hobaho nghajendelela kulidima litewa lili.
8 Nghawa sina witumbilo kuli wadimi wadatu wa ha mwamulizo, wono wakasula muno ho baho nane nghawa kuwawusa wose mu mwezi monga.
9 Hanyuma nghalilonjela litewa nghatya, “Alu ne sinowa mudimi wenyu kanyhi, holeka lece zono zikufwa zifwe du, na zono zikongomezwa, zongomezwe du, na zono zisigaye nazo zokwiyilya zeneco zose.”
10 Hobaho nghawa kuyisola nghome yangu yono yikucemwa “Ihewa,” nghayibenanga ho mbene cilagane co Muwaha Mulungu kuli makungugo gose.
11 Hobaho cilagane cici cikawa cabeneka mwi'zuwa lilyo. Awalya wono wagulaga no kuguza zingholo, wali wananjiliye, nawo wakamanya vyono u Muwaha Mulungu yalongaga nawo mu nzila ya zizo zono nazitumaga.
12 Hobaho nghawatya, “Hono muwe mwendezwa no mulimo wangu, ale, mumhelaje musahala wangu. Ninga hono muwe si mwendezwe, hodu, mulece kumhela.” Hobaho nawo wakawa kumhela u musahala wangu vipande vye zifweza makumi madatu.

13 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya, uviwikaje vivyo vipande vye fweza mu vinyumba vyo kuwicila viwutwa vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Hobaho nghawa kuvisola vivilya vipande vye fweza, liwalo lililya du lyono wehwesaga vyono lyonitumbiye, nghawa kuviwika mu vinyumba vyo kuwicila viwutwa vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
14 Hanyuma nghayibena ne nghome ye kejete yono yacemwaga “Malunza.” Hobaho u wilunze wa Yuda na Isilayeli, wukawa kutemanuka.
15 U Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya, “Wusole kanyhi viya, wuwe nha wuli mudimi mulele.
16 Hambi nha nakondya kumuwika u mudimi we litewa lyangu. Ninga mwene hamba si yakusuma kuzitaza zingholo zono zibenece migulu, hamba kuzilondola yaye zono zajiliye, hamba kuziwopela zono zifyonghoye, hamba kuzilisa zono zina miwili miswanu. Langa mwene, kolya du nyama ye zingholo zono cijinile, no kugapandulanga ne mabangho gazo.
17 Ninga woyi hee, kuli mudimi yuyu mulele, mono si mwihuwilwa, Hambi nha yazileka zingholo. Holeka wulugu womutijinha vilungo vyakwe. U muwoko wakwe wonyala, ne liso lyakwe lyo kulume lyobofula.”
