2 Samwili 22

U lwimbo lwa Dawudi lwo wusume

(Zab 18)

1 Na lono Muwaha Mulungu yali yakondya kumusumila Dawudi kuli Sawuli na kuli walugu wakwe wanji. Hodu Dawudi yakawa kumwimbila Muwaha Mulungu lwimbo alu yakatya,

2 “Muwaha Mulungu wo wutizizo wangu,

lyo ligomawakali lyangu lye vilungo.

3 Mulungu wangu wo wutizizo wangu,

nane nikaye mholile muli mwene.

Yakuwa kunyhitila kotya ngula,

kandimaga no kumbika mholile.

Kanyhi yo mukombozi wangu,

kanyhitilaga no kumhonya mu zimbali.

4 Nimulililaga Muwaha Mulungu,

nayo kamhonyaga muli walugu wangu.

Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe!

5 “Hambi i mawingo ge lifwa, nha gali gamhilime,

ne mahwazi go wongomezwe, gakunghola mucanyha yangu.

6 Ne zimbali ze lifwa zali zimhilime,

ne mitego ye lifwa yali yinhejeze nene.

7 Na mu wugaye wangu nghamucemeleza Muwaha Mulungu,

nghamucemeleza Mulungu yamhele wutazi.

Na mu Nyumba yakwe yakalihulika lizi lyangu,

yakacihulika cililo cangu co kulondola wutazi.

8 “Yisi nayo yikasinjisika, yikacicima,

ne misinji ya ku wulanga yikatinuka,

yikasinjisika vyono Mulungu yali yena likuwo!

9 Lyosi likakomoka mu mhula zakwe,

malambi go wutakamize, hamonga no moto we makala,

vikalawa mu mulomo wakwe.

10 Yakawudemula wulanga, yakatima hasi

hamonga ne citi ciwaha ce mavunde ha magulu gakwe.

11 Yakagulucila mwi'Kelubi

yakajendela mu mabawa ge mbeho.

12 Yakegubika no wutitu,

mavunde mazito, gamemile malenga gamupilime.

13 Makala go moto gakaka,

ha wuzelu wa ha mwande yakwe.

14 Hanyuma u Muwaha Mulungu yatowa lididimo

ku wulawa ku wulanga,

ne lizi lyo Mulungu mono Yalawiliciye, likahulikwa.

15 Yakazigumila zisonga zakwe, yakaweyagaza

walugu wakwe, yakawawinga ko wumwekule we limuli.

16 Lilenga likanyazwa, hakawa hakavu,

no musinji we yinze wukawa wuli mwazi,

lono yawakalipila walugu wakwe,

no kuwakolomela kwe likuwo lyakwe.

17 “Muwaha Mulungu yakatima hono hasi

ku wulawa kucanyha, yakanyibatiliza,

yakamhonya mu wulawa mu malenga menji.

18 Yamhonya mu miwoko ye walugu wangu we vilungo,

na kuli wose wono wasulile.

19 Na lono nali nili mu wugaye, walugu wangu wakanyiziza,

ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakamhonya.

20 Yakamhonya ku wulawa mu mbali,

yamhonya vyono yali yendezwe nane.

21 “Muwaha Mulungu kahumbilizaga,

vyono nane nitumaga zo wiling'ano,

kandugutizaga, hambi nha sina masutya.

22 Hambi nane nha nilikamisaga lilajilizo

lyo Muwaha Mulungu

nghali hamba sinamugaluka Mulungu wangu.

23 Nane niwukamisaga wulajilize wakwe,

nghali nagabeza ne malajilizo gakwe.

24 Kanyhi yamanyile vyono ane sina masutya,

ngheyimila ho ndece kusutya.

25 Muwaha Mulungu kahumbilizaga,

vyono nane nitumaga zo wiling'ano,

hambi nha yamanyile vyono ane sina masutya.

26 “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, agwe wuli mwihuwilwa

kuli wono wehuwilwa kuli gwe,

kanyhi wuli muswanu muno kuli wose

wono wali mugoloka.

27 Agwe welile kuli wono welile,

ninga wuwasulile i wanhu wabi.

28 Agwe kuwaponyaga i wenenenyhezi,

ninga kuwanyetyaga wono wakwikumya.

29 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

yo gwegwe go wuzelu wangu,

kumbusizaga wutitu hali'ne.

30 Kumhelaga vilungo vyo kwihomela ne walugu wangu,

kanyhi kumhelaga wusume wo kugazumha majinji gawo.

31 Nzila zo Mulungu, za mugoloka

ne zinghani zakwe za mbeka.

Mwene wo wutazi kuli wono wakutiziza kuli mwene.

32 “Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yo Mulungu du yali wene,

Mulungu du yali wene yo mudimi wetu.

33 Mulungu yuyu lyo ligomawakali lyangu,

yo kanhongaga mu nzila yo mugoloka.

34 Kaganozaga magulu gangu gawa nha ga nhemanyika,

kanghamisaga mholile mu matunda.

35 Kanyijizaga zinzila zo wihomi mu wulugu,

ho sume kuwitumamila wupinde wono wukamile.

36 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wunhaze kanyhi wumhonye,

hambi wutazi wako nha wamhela lukumyo.

37 Wanhaza lono nali nhecigwe

hamba nghali nagwa hasi yaye.

38 Nghawalonda i walugu wangu nghawongomeza,

nane sinolecela lukulu, sunga kolo nawongomezeza lukulu.

39 Nghawagwisa hasi, hamba si wosuma kwinuka kanyhi,

heya, wagwa hasi ye magulu gangu.

40 Kumhelaga vilungo mu milewo,

hamonga no wusume kuli walugu wangu.

41 Wukawanoza walugu wangu watize ha mwande yangu,

niwongomezeze lukulu wono wasulile.

42 Wakalondola wutazi,

ninga hamba si wamutuga mono kowaponya.

Wakamucemeleza Muwaha Mulungu,

ninga nayo hamba si yawapitula yaye.

43 Nane nghawapondanga, wakawa nhe manghundi,

nghawakanyanjila nha nhope ya mu nzila.

44 “Agwe wanhaza mu miwoko ye wanhu wangu wagaluci,

wukanghamisa niwe muwaha mu makungugo,

ne wanhu wono nali si niwamanyile,

alu woweza wetumami wangu.

45 Ne wanhu wajenzi, wakunyinamila,

na lono nghulonga, wakuhulika.

46 Nawo vilungo vyawo vyakondya kumalika,

alu wakuza wakucicima, wakulawa mu magomawakali gawo.

47 “Muwaha Mulungu yo mumi!

Yalumbwe u mudimi wangu!

Ale, mwanze zo wuwaha wakwe,

u Mulungu mono yali vilungo mono kamhonyaga!

48 Hambi nha kamhelaga wusume muli walugu wangu,

kawacepulaga vilungo makungugo ha mwande yangu,

49 kanyhi kamhonyaga kuli walugu wangu.

Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, kumhelaga

wusume kuli walugu wangu,

kanyhi kunyitilaga ne wanhu wabi.

50 “Holeka nane nghukulumbila mu makungugo,

kanyhi nolimbila ne litagwa lyako.

51 Mulungu kamupelaga wusume mutemi wakwe,

kalajilaga lusungu lwakwe luwaha kuli mono yamuhagula,

kuli Dawudi hamonga ne wanagwe cibitilila.”
