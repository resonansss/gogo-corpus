Yoweli 1

1 Ayi yo nghani yo Muwaha Mulungu yono yamuziza Yoweli Mwana wa Petuweli.

Wanhu wakulila so wuhodangwe we migunda

2 Hulicizenyi ta, anye nyo wakombi,

sunga nanye wose mono mwi'kaye mu'si ya Yuda,

muhulicize.

Wulenyi hono mukulanga,

zono zinha zizi zowa zejela 'mbi mu vilo vyenyu

hamba lunji mu vilo vye walezi wenyu?

3 Ale, muwalonjelaje zizi, wana wenyu,

nawo wana wenyu wawalonjele wana wawo,

i wana wawo nawo,

wawalonjele lulelo lono lukuza.

4 Matewa na matewa

ge zinzije genjila mu migunda yetu,

galya vilimwa vyetu,

na cono litewa lye zinzije,

lisigaze, litewa lye mbilazi likulya,

na cono litewa lye mbilazi lisigaze,

litewa lye mang'ungu likulya,

na cono litewa lye mang'ungu lisigaze,

litewa lye mapanzi likulya.

5 Holeka nanye nyo wang'wela,

mwenzeluce mulile muno muno,

mulile ku soko ye divayi mhya,

anye wose mono mung'wezaga divayi.

Hambi zabibu zono muyinozezaga divayi mhya,

nha zakondya kuhodangwa ne zinzije.

6 Hambi litewa lye zinzije nha linjila mu'si yetu,

kanyhi lili vilungo,

nzije nyinji muno sizikusumika

hamba kuwuwalwe.

Zina meno makali kotya meno ga simba

ne majego gono genha ga simba nyhekulu.

7 Zahodanga sunga ne mizabibu yetu,

zakondya kulya ne mikuyu yetu,

zayikubulanga ne mabada gayo gose,

ne manhambi gayo geza mazelu swee.

8 Anye nyo wanhu,

mulile nho mwana munyacipale,

mono yafwilwa no munhu mono yali hila kutolwa nayo.

9 Hambi vilavywa vyo wusaje hamba vye divayi,

vyo kulavya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu,

nha vyeza vili hasina lu.

Iwakohani nawo wakulila ku soko

i vilavywa vyo kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu,

alu vyeza vili hasina.

10 Hambi migunda nha yeza yisina cinji.

Yisi nayo, yikulila ku soko wuhemba nha wahodangwa,

sunga ne divayi yawula,

ne mafuta geza sigakwijela.

11 Ale, mukolwe cevu anye nyo walima migunda,

nanye nyo walindilizi we migunda ye mizabibu,

mulile ku soko yo wuhemba ne shayili,

heya, hambi vilimwa vyose nha vyahodangwa.

12 Hambi mizabibu nayo sunga

ne mikuyu nha yakondya kunyala.

Sunga ne mibici yose

ye mikalo yakondya kunyala.

Sunga ne wanhu wose nhondo,

weza wasina hamba nyemo bwete.

13 Ale, muvwale magunila, mulile,

anye nyo wakohani

mono mwitumaga ha cikumbicilo!

Mubite mu Nyumba

yo Mulungu mukalile cilo cose!

Hambi nha heza hasina vilavywa

vyo wuhemba ne divayi,

vyono momulavizya Mulungu wenyu

mu Nyumba yakwe.

14 Mwiyezeze mu nzila yo wuleka kulya,

mucemeleze wanhu waze ku wuhembi,

muwakongholaje walongola,

hamonga ne wanhu wose we yisi ya Yuda,

wenjile mu Nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu wenyu,

mumulilile Mulungu.

15 Woyi he nye!

Hambi lizuwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu,

nha lyasejelela,

lizuwa lyono Muwaha Mulungu

u Mudaha Vyose kololeta wongomele!

16 Alu, ceza cisina witumbilo kanyhi,

vyono vilimwa vyetu vyose vyakondya kuhodangwa.

Sunga ne nyemo yose yeza yili musina

mu Nyumba yo Mulungu wetu.

17 Hambi imbeyu nayo

nha yahodanjicila mu muhangha mukavu,

imadongha nago galekwa gasina hamba cinji,

zinyumba zo wuwiko we madongha,

nazo zamwaguka.

Hambi wuhemba nha wanyala.

18 Ne zing'ombe nazo zikulila ku soko yiyo,

hambi nha kweza kusina wudimo wazo,

ne matewa ge zingholo nago gakugaya du.

19 Ane nghukulilila agwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

ku soko yo wudimo hamonga ne mibici,

vyono vyanyala kotya nha vitakamizwe no moto.

20 Sunga ne zinimu za mu mbago nazo nha zikukulilila,

ku soko ye malamba ne makolongo gono ganyala.


Yoweli 2

Nzije zikuwa co citanjilo ce suguzi co wuze we Lizuwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu

1 Towenyi ndulele ye citanjilo mu Sayuni,

mwi'tunda lyono lyelile lyo Muwaha Mulungu.

Wanhu wose mono mwikaye mu Yuda, mucicime.

Hambi lizuwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu nha likuza,

kanyhi lyasejelela.

2 Nalyo kanyhi lyolowa yizuwa lya citi no wukalale,

yizuwa lye mavunde no wutitu tuku.

Hambi litewa yiwaha lye zinzije,

nha lyologubika matunda kotya citi.

Cikali hamba si nejela cinhu cono cinha vivi,

mu vilo vyono vyapuluta,

hamba mu vilo vyono vikuza si cilejela yaye.

3 Litewa lili lye zinzije zikulya vilimwa kotya

moto wukutakamiza mahanze,

ku mwande yazo,

yisi yihwanile Busitani ya Adeni,

ninga ku nyuma yazo,

yihwanile kotya nyika yono yihodanjice.

Hasina hamba cinji cono gasigaze.

4 Wijele we zinzije zizo,

zikwijela zihwanile Fwalasi,

kanyhi zikumangha kotya Fwalasi zo wulugu.

5 Na lono zikuguluka musunho mu vizuli vye matunda,

cihumbi cazo cihwanile mituka yono yikubita ku wulugu,

misindo yazo no mulilo we cihumbi cazo,

kotya mahanze makavu

gono gakutakamila mu moto.

Zipanjile kotya wasilikale

wono wewikanize kubita ku wulugu.

6 Na vyono zikucila kusejelela behi,

wusu we wanhu wose wukusininghala so woga.

7 Zikwinjila kotya wasilikale wo wulugu,

zikutambala mu majinji kotya we citung'ati,

zose zidomile kumwande,

hamba sizikuleka wulunji wazo hamba hadodo.

8 Zikukola mu nzila yono nyagwe yikolile,

sunga na mu magomawakali gawo,

zene zikubita zikupuluta du,

hamba hasina cinji cono cosuma kuzimila yaye.

9 Zikwinjila musunho muno mwi'kaya,

zikumangha mu majinji gawo

sunga na mu matembe ge zikaya,

kanyhi zikwinjilila mu madilisha nha wahizi.

10 Sunga ne yisi nayo yikucicima,

no wulanga nawo wukusinjisika,

ne lizuwa nalyo no mwezi vyatukuwala,

sunga ne zinyelezi, hamba sizikusana yaye.

11 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu

nha yakuwalajiliza lipuga

lye wasilikale wakwe kwe'zi yiwaha,

nalyo lipuga lyono limuhulicile,

wo wenji muno, kanyhi wali vilungo.

Wugayeci wono wolowa,

mwi'zuwa lilyo lyo Muwaha Mulungu!

Nani 'mbi mono kolokangalila?

Lucemo lo wunene

12 Alu Muwaha Mulungu yakutya,

“Munene mu zinhumbula zenyu mumhitucile nene,

ko wuleka kulya, no wulila muno.

13 Kanyhi muwulajilaje winenenyhezi wa

mu zinhumbula zenyu ko wukalale wenyu.

Hambi wudemulanga vivwalo vyenyu,

nha si wukwiliye cinji yaye.”

Mumupitucile Muwaha Mulungu,

hambi mwene nha yo yena nheto no lusungu.

Sikakolagwa ne likuwo mbela,

mwene nhondo zose,

yewikanize kunenela du,

hamba si kulipiciza yaye.

14 Na lunji Muwaha Mulungu wenyu,

komukumbucilenyi no kukawaniza lihweso lyakwe,

no kumulugutizenyi,

hamonga no kumonjelezezenyi vilimwa vyenyu.

Nanye ho mosuma kumulavivya vilavywa

vyo wusaje ne divayi.

15 Holeka mutowe ndulele mwi'tunda lya Sayuni,

muwalajilize wanhu walece kulya cinji,

wekongholele wose hamonga!

16 Muwakongholaje wanhu hamonga,

muwawikanize so wihangulize wono welile.

Muwaletaje ne wakombi,

hamonga ne wana,

ne wana wagalika wose,

hamonga na bwana halusi

na bibi halusi walece nyumba yawo,

waze wose.

17 Nawo wakohani,

hamonga ne wetumami

wono wakumwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu,

haciligati ye cikumbicilo

no mulango we Nyumba yo Mulungu,

yiwanojeye walile no kulomba muno muno, watye,

“Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

weze no lusungu kuli wanhu wako,

wulece kugaleceleza makungugo ganji gacibedegale,

hamba kucisekanga kose, wawe kutya,

‘Alu yali hayi Mulungu wenyu?’ ”

Mulungu koyigomolela yisi lulelo

18 Hobaho Muwaha Mulungu

yakayiwonela nghalazi yisi yakwe,

no kuwawonela lusungu wanhu wakwe.

19 Muwaha Mulungu yakawapitula yakawatya,

“Alu nomupelenyi wuhemba,

ne divayi, ne mafuta,

nanye momoteswa,

sunga ne makungugo

ganji sigomubedegalenyi kanyhi yaye.

20 Noliwusa litewa lye zinzije

lyono lyaza ku wulawa kusumuka.

Noziwinjila ku nyika,

nago matewa ga ku mwande,

nogategula ko kwi'lenga lyo mwino,

nago ga mu nyuma ko kwi'lenga lya Meditelaneya.

Nazo zofwila mumo, ne miwili yazo,

yolavya munungho mumo mwi'lenga.

Nozihodanga ku soko ya zose

zono zamutendelenyi.

21 “Nanye mo migunda,

mulece kogopa langa musangalale,

kanyhi mweze ne nyemo,

so zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yamutendelenyi.

22 Nyo vilimu nanye nhavivyo,

mulece kogopa,

hambi wudimo wenyu nha wasipuka,

ne mibici nayo yikulela mikalo yayo,

hamonga ne mikuyu,

ne mizabibu yose, alu vyenjipa muno.

23 “Holeka muweze ne nyemo

anye wanhu wa Sayuni,

musangalalile

zono Muwaha Mulungu wenyu yamutendelenyi.

Hambi mwene nha kamupelenyi mvula

ye lifuku yono yikwiliye,

kanyhi komutonyezezenyi

ne mvula ye mhinga

na ye mafuso ku soko yenyu,

nhe vilo vyono vipulute.

24 Ne vyuga vyenyu vyomema wuhemba,

ne matuli go kukobonjela,

gokwiguta divayi ne mafuta.

25 Nomugomolelenyi

kanyhi vilimwa vyenyu,

vyono vyajilila mu myaka yono zinzije zalya.

Hambi nha yo nene

mono nalituma litewa lye zinzije kuli'nye.

26 Holeka nanye mowa na vinji vyo kulya,

kanyhi mokwiguta.

Nanye momukumya Muwaha Mulungu,

u Mulungu wenyu,

mono yetuma mapoto ku soko yenyu,

nanye nyo wanhu wangu

hamba simobedegalilwa lukulu kanyhi.

27 Holeka Isilayeli,

momanya vyono ane nili hamonga nanye,

kanyhi vyono yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu,

hasina Mulungu yunji kanyhi.

Nanye nyo wanhu wangu,

hamba simobedegalilwa lukulu kanyhi.

Lizuwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu

28 “Yolowa hanyuma yayo,

nolokwita zo muhe wangu muli wanhu wose,

wana wenyu we cilume

na we cicekulu wolowila ze nghani yangu,

ne wakombi wenyu wololota ndoto,

ne wazelelo wenyu nawo wolowona wugubulilwe.

29 Mu vilo vivyo,

nolokwita zo muhe wangu,

mu wetumami wangu we cilume na we cicekulu.

30 “Nane nololajila citanjilo

ce lizuwa lilyo ku wulanga,

na kono'nze kolowa sakami

no moto ne mavunde ge nghungulyosi.

31 Lizuwa yolotukuwala,

no mwezi nawo wolopituka wuwe mudunghu nha sakami,

lono likali sinafika lizuwa

lyo kogofya lyo Muwaha Mulungu.

32 Kanyhi munhu wo wose

mono kololomba wutazi kuli Muwaha Mulungu,

yuyo yo kolopona.

Nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalonga yakatya,

Wenji wa mu Yelusalemi wolopona,

na wono nawahagula wolokwikala wapolile.”


Yoweli 3

Mulungu kologaling'aniza makungugo

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya,

“Mu vilo vivyo,

nolowugomola wumoteswe wa Yuda na Yelusalemi.

2 Nane nologakonghola makungugo gose,

nigategule sunga ko kwi'lolo lyono likucemwa,

“U Muwaha Mulungu Yakuling'aniza.

Nane nolowaling'aniza

i makungugo gago ku soko ye metumo gawo gose

gono wawatendela wanhu wangu Isilayeli.

Ku soko waweyagaziza Isilayeli mu ma'si go wujenzi,

no kuyigawa Isilayeli yisi yangu.

3 Nawo wakawagawa

wanhu wangu mu nzila yo kuwatowela kula,

ho yejele mono kosuma kuwasola wafungwa.

Wakawaguza wana we cilume

na we cicekulu ku wuwanda

ho wetujile sawo yo kuwapela wasenyha,

hamonga no kugula divayi wang'we.

4 “Alo nanye nyo Tulo na Sidoni, hamonga ne zi'si zose ze Wafilisiti, mukusaka mwitume nghanici kuli'ne? Hono muwe mukusaka kwituma nghani yinji yo yose kuli'ne, ale, ko kwenyu, ninga nane nomugomolelenyi nanye musunho zo wulipicizwe wenyu.
5 Hambi nha mwesolela nyo weneco fweza ne zahabu, ne vinhu vyangu viswanu, no kuvinjiza mu zinyumba zo wuhembelo wenyu.
6 Mukawasola wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, mukawategula kutali ne yisi yawo, mukawaguza ku Wagiliki.
7 Alu nane neza kubita kuwasola kuko kono anye mwawaguza, nane nomutendelenyi zose, nha vyono nanye mwawatendela wene.
8 Nane nowaleka wana wenyu we cilume na we cicekulu, waguzwe kuli wanhu wa Yuda, ne wanhu wa Yuda nawo wowaguza kutali ku wanhu wa Sheba, hambi ane no Muwaha Mulungu nha vyo nghutya.

9 “Muwalonjelaje makungugo gose mutye,

‘Mwiwikanizaje so wulugu,

muwacemaje we citung'ati wenyu,

mukonghole wasilikale wose wabite ku wulugu!

10 Mutyane masili genyu gawe mimaje,

ne zihengo zenyu ziwe migoha,

sunga na wono wasoceye

nawo wose wabite ku wulugu.

11 Ale musunhe muze,

anye nyo makungugo gose

mono muli mu mbavu zose,

mwihangulizize mwi'lolo.’ ”

Gwe Muwaha Mulungu,

wutegule vitung'ati wako vikehome.

12 “Makungugo gewikanizaje,

gaze mwi'lolo lyono likucemwa,

U Muwaha Mulungu Yakuling'aniza,

ane no Muwaha Mulungu kuko

ko nogaling'aniza makungugo

ga mu zimbavu zose.

13 Hambi wene nha wabi muno,

holeka wuwatemangaje wuwagwise hasi

nha wuhemba mwi'pango lye ndudula,

wuwakobonje nha zabibu

vyono zikuwa kukobongwa mwi'tuli lyokukobonjela,

sunga ne divayi yiwa kulawila.”

14 Mapuga na mapuga wali mwi'lolo

lyo wuling'anizizwe.

Na vyo yolowa mwi'zuwa lilyo

lyo Muwaha Mulungu,

nalyo likuza hamba si lyocela yaye.

15 Mwi'zuwa lilyo,

mwezi ne lizuwa vyolotukuwala,

ne zinyelezi si zolong'alala yaye.

Mulungu kowalugutiza wanhu wakwe

16 Muwaha Mulungu yakulonga

ne lizi yiwaha ku wulawa mwi'tunda lya Sayuni,

ne lizi lyakwe likudidima

ku wulawa mu Yelusalemi,

sunga ne yisi hamonga

no wulanga navyo vikucicima,

ninga mwene Muwaha Mulungu yo kowadima.

17 “Nagwe go Isilayeli baho ho kolomanyila

vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wako.

Nikaye mu Sayuni mwi'tunda lyangu lyono lyelile.

Nayo Yelusalemi yowa likaya lyangu lyono lyelile,

hamba wajenzi si wolisuma yaye.

18 “Mu vilo vivyo,

matunda golomema mizabibu yose,

ne zing'ombe nazo,

zolowa ziyagaye mu matunda gose,

kanyhi kolowa na malenga menji

ku soko ye vikolongo vya Yuda.

Ne cikolongo colohuluma ku wulawilila

mu kaya yo Muwaha Mulungu,

sunga ne lilolo lya Shitimu nalyo,

lyolowa lina malenga menji.

19 “Misili yolowa nyika nghavu,

na Edomu yolohodangwa no wongomezwe,

vyono wayihoma yisi ya Yuda,

no kuwawulaga wanhu wono wasina masutya.

20 Holeka nane nowalipiciza wawo wono wawawulaga,

hamba sinowawonela lusungu yaye,

ku soko ye lisutya lyawo lilyo.

Ninga Yuda na Yelusalemi wokwikala cibitilila.

21 Nane nowalipiciza wono wawawulaga

i wanhu wa Yuda hamba

si nowalecela yaye i wasutyi.

Nane ane no Muwaha Mulungu,

nikaye mu Sayuni.”
