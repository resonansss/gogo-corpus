Walumi 9

U Mulungu ne nghungugo yakwe i wahagulwa

1 Nghulonga mbeka, nakondya kwilunza na Kilisito, nane si nghulonga wulambi. Iciselinji lono cikutongwa no Muhe Mono Yelile, cikunghangaziza inghani yiyi nhavivyo.
2 Nghusaka kutya nhavi. Ninga wukalale wuwaha, no wusungu si wakulonga, mu nhumbula yangu, ku soko ye wanhu wangu, wono cili sakami monga na nene!
3 Hono yali yiwe so cambuzi cawo, nali nyende niwe mulijitwe, no kusiwanulwa na Kilisito.
4 Wawo wo wanhu wa Isilayeli, wono u Mulungu yawasiwanula wawe wanagwe, nayo yakawapela no lukumyo lwakwe, vikalagana nawo, yakawapela lilajilizo lyakwe, wihuwilo we mbeka no wihuwizo wakwe.
5 Wene wo wezukulu we wakoko, nayo Kilisito ku lubavu lo wunhu wakwe, yalawilila mu mbeyu yawo. U Mulungu mono yatemile muli vyose, yalumbwe cibitilila, Heya.
6 Ninga si vyono nghutya, uwihuwizo wo Mulungu wuwelile, hambi nha si wanhu wose wa mu Waisilayeli, wono wo wahagulwa wo Mulungu.
7 Hamba si wose we mbeyu ya Abulahamu, wanagwe we mbeka. Langa nha vyono gakulonga i Mandiko Gono Gelile. “Muli Isaka mo yolocemelwa imbeyu yako.”
8 Wo wutya, si wana wo muwili wo wana wo Mulungu aa, langa wawalya du, wono waleleywe mu cilagane co Mulungu, wo wocemwa wanagwe.
9 Hambi icilagane ceneco nha coci. “Nolokuziza kwinha nhavivi, nayo Sala kolosawa mwana wa cilume.”

10 Ninga si yiyo du, langa na Labeka nayo, yatuga wana we metundu kuli sogo yawo yumonga, yo Isaka u Sogo yetu.
11 Ninga ho Mulungu yejele vyono yena welecele wo kuhagula, na lono wawalya iwana we metundu wakali si nalelwa, na lono wakali si namanya kuhagula iziswanu ne zimbi.
12 Labeka yalonjelwa vyono, u mwana mulongozi komwitumamila ayulya mono kotyatya mu nyuma. Holeka u wuhaguzi wo Mulungu, wuwezaga nha vyono yakuhila mweneco, hamba si metumo ga munhu.
13 Nha vyono gakulonga i Mandiko Gono Gelile, “Namwenda Yakobo, ninga Esawu namusula.”

14 Hambi cilonje ya hayi? Alu u Mulungu yasina iling'ani? Aa ng'o!
15 Hambi nha yamulonjela Musa, “Nomuwonela lusungu munhu wo wose mono nghusaka kumuwonela lusungu, kanyhi nomunenela munhu wo wose mono nghusaka kumunenela.”
16 Holeka zose zili mu lusungu lo Mulungu du, hamba si zili mu wuhidimbale wo munhu, hamba si zili mu wuhile wo munhu.
17 Hambi i Mandiko Gono Gelile nha gakulonga nhavi kuli Falawo, “Nakunoza wuwe mutemi, ho vilungo vyangu vimanyicile muli gwegwe, ne litagwa lyangu limanyice kononze kose.”
18 Holeka yili calinze, vyono u Mulungu kamuwonelaga lusungu munhu wo wose mono mweneco du yakusaka kumuwonela lusungu, na hono mweneco yasace kumunoza munhu yawe yali cibede, kanozaga nhavivyo.

I likuwo no lusungu vyo Mulungu

19 Lunji kombuza wutye, “Alu hono zili nhavivyo, u Mulungu kosuma nhawule kumuhosa munhu? Alu nani 'mbi mono kosuma kuwimila u wuhile wakwe?”
20 Alu agwe go munhu, wuli nani gwegwe ho wijelele kumupitula u Mulungu? Wule, nyungu nayo ma, yosuma kumuwuza cinji mono yayiwumba yikutya, “Nhawule wukamumba nhavivi?”
21 U muwumbi kalitumamilaga ililongo nha vyono yakusaka mweneco, yadahile kuwumba nyungu nyejete mwi'bilingwa limonga, yimonga so witumilwe we lilumbo, ne nyagwe nayo so witumilwe wono si we lilumbo.

22 Nhavivyo vyo yinha kuli Mulungu. Yakasaka yalilajile ilikuwo lyakwe, no kuvimanyiza ivilungo vyakwe. Ninga yakawa mukangalizi muno, yakuwakangalila walya wono wali yo soko ye likuwo lyakwe, wono wali wanojeye wongomezwe.
23 Yakasaka kanyhi kuwulajila u winji wo lukumyo lwakwe, lono yacijidila ase mono cali yo soko yo lusungu lwakwe, ase mono yali yakondya kuciwikaniza ho cilubocele u lukumyo lwakwe.
24 Vyono ase yo sese mono cacemwa nayo, si vyono kulawa muli Wayahudi du, langa sunga ku wulawa mu wono si Wayahudi yaye.
25 Hambi nha vyo yakulonga mu citabu ca Hoseya:

“Nolowacema, ‘wanhu wangu,’ Wono si wali wanhu wangu.

Nayo ‘Mwendece,’ mono si yali mwendece!

26 Na hono hatezagwa ‘Si muli wanhu wangu!’

Baho wocemwa I Wana wo Mulungu mumi!”

27 Nayo u muwizi Isaya, yakumulonjela Isilayeli yazozize, yakutya, “Iliwalo lye wanhu wa Isilayeli, hamba liwe kotya musawawa wa mwi'lenga, ninga wacefu du wono woloponywa,
28 hambi u Muwaha nha si koleka kuyileta mbela i ning'aniza yakwe yono yikwilaniciye kuli yinze yose.”
29 Nha vyono Isaya yalonga ha mwamulizo, “Hono u Muwaha mono yali vilungo, yali yalece kucisigaziza wanji wa muli wanhu wa Isilayeli, mhali cakondya kuwa nha Sodoma, mhali cawa kotya Gomola.”

Waisilayeli no Luganuzi Luswanu

30 Hambi colongaci kanyhi? Yili nhavi: iwanhu wono si Wayahudi wono si wepiganza kuwulondola u wuvumilwe hali Mulungu, wanozwa wavumilwe hali Mulungu mu nzila yo wihuwilo.
31 Ninga i wanhu wa Isilayeli wono walondolaga u wulajilize, wono wuletaga u wuvumilwe hali Mulungu, si wawutuga.
32 Ku soko ya hayi? Ku soko i wene wali wetumbiye metumo gawo, siwetumbila u wihuwilo. Wakekobala mu libwe lyo wikobale,
33 nha vyono gakutya i Mandiko Gono Gelile:

“Langa ta, nghuwika mu Sayuni, libwe lyo wikobale,

Ne litalawe lyono lyowagwisa!

No munhu mono komwihuwila yuyo mono lyo libwe lilyo,

yuyo si kolokozwa soni.”
