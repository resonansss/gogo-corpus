Maluko 14

U wikanize wo kumuwulaga Yesu

(Mat 26:1-5; Luk 22:1-2; Yoh 11:45-53)

1 Na lono vyali vikali vilo vyejete du, ho yiwe sikukuu ya Pasaka na ye mikate yono siyisasize. Iwakohani wawaha ne wejizi wo Wulajilize, walondolaga nzila yo kumwibata Yesu ko wusugu ho wamuwulaje.
2 Ninga hamba nhavivyo wakatya, “Cilece kumwibata mwizuwa lye sikukuu, ho yilece kuwa wuma muli wanhu.”

Yesu yakubakwa mafuta ku Betaniya

(Mat 26:6-13; Yoh 12:1-8)

3 Nayo Yesu lono yali yali ku Betaniya, mu kaya ya Simoni Mukoma. Na lono yalizaga zindigwa ha meza, yakaza mucekulu yunji mono yali yena supa ye mafuta go wulenzi go wuguzi wukamu, yakayitula supa yiyi, yakamujidila Yesu mafuta gago mu mutwe wakwe.
4 Na wanji wa muli wanhu wono wali wali baho, wakasimba wakawa kulongana weneco wose wakutya, “Alu so cici mafuta gaga gajilizwe caka caka nha vivi?
5 Hambi nha gali gaguzwe pesa zono zificile dinali magana madatu, ho wapegwe waciwa!” Hodu wakamutacila u mucekulu yuyo.
6 Ninga Yesu yakawatya, “Mulecenyi, alu nhawule mukumugaza? Kanyitumila mulimo muswanu.
7 Hambi iwaciwa nha muwezanaga nawo nhondo zose, holeka mosuma kuwataza mwipango lyo lyose lyono mwendile. Ninga ane, si mosuma kuwezana nane nhondo zose.
8 U Mwene kanoza nha vyono yadahile, kawubaka umuwili wangu mafuta so wuwikanizwe wo wuzikwe wangu mu citenjelo.
9 Nane nghumulonjelenyi mbeka, vyono, ho hose mono yinze yose, hono loganulwa u Luganuzi Luswanu, ilitumo lili lyono yetumile u mucekulu yuyu, lyowa kulongwa so cikumbucizo cakwe.”

Yuda yakuvumila kumugaluka Yesu

(Mat 26:14-16; Luk 22:3-6)

10 Ale, Yuda Musikaliyota, muyagwe na wawalya we likumi na wejete, yakabita kuli wakohani wawaha ho yamukombole Yesu kuli wene.
11 Nawo Wakohani wawaha weza kuhulika inghani yiyo, wakasecelela muno, wakalagana nayo vyono womupela zipesa. Hodu nayo Yuda yakawa kwanza kulondola cilejeho co kumukombola Yesu kuli wene.

Yesu yakulya ndigwa ye Pasaka hamonga ne wejizwa wakwe

(Mat 26:17-25; Luk 22:7-14, 21-23; Yoh 13:21-30)

12 Na mwizuwa lilongozi lye sikukuu ye mikate yono siyisasize, mwipango lyono wawulajilaga nyana ye ngholo we Pasaka, iwejizwa wakwe wakamuwuza, wakutya, “Wukusaka cikuwikanizize hayi ndigwa ye Pasaka?”
13 Ale, Yesu yakawatuma wejete wa muli wejizwa wakwe yakuwatya, “Bitenyi kwi'kaya, na kuko mokwitang'anila na munhu yunji yadinghuye njilo ye malenga. Ale, mumutyatyaje yuyo,
14 sunga ko ku kaya yono kokwinjila, mumutye munya kaya, ‘Umwijizi yakutya, cili hayi cumba cangu cono nolila Pasaka hamonga ne wejizwa wangu?’
15 Nayo komulajilenyi cumba ciwaha ca ku golofa cono ciwikanizizwe lukulu, kanyhi cihambigwe. Ale mucitenjelaje mu cumba cico.”

16 Iwejizwa wakatuluka, wakabita kwi'kaya, nawo wakagana vyose viwikanizwe nha vyono Yesu yali yawalonjeye. Wakawa kuyitenga i ndigwa ye Pasaka.
17 Yaza kuwa cinyamihe. Yesu yakaza hamonga ne wejizwa wakwe we likumi na wejete.
18 Na lono walizaga zindigwa ha meza, Yesu yakatya, “Mbeka nghumulonjelenyi, muyenyu yunji mono cikulizana hamonga nane, kongaluka.”
19 Baho iwejizwa wakwe wakanza kukalala, hanyuma wakawa kwanza kumuwuza Yesu yumonga yumonga, wakutya, “Ma yo nene?”
20 Yesu yakawatya, “Muyenyu yumonga wa muli nyenye yikumi na wejete, mono cikulilizana i nhonje hamonga nane mu bakuli.
21 Mbeka u Mwana wo Munhu nha kofwa nha vyono i Mandiko Gono Gelile gakulonga zo mwene, ninga, woyi he, kuli munhu yuyo mono yakumugaluka Mwana wo Munhu! Yali yiwe yili ciba kuli munhu yuyo, yalece kulelwa!”

I ndigwa yo Muwaha

(Mat 26:26-30; Luk 22:14-20; 1Kol 11:23-25)

22 Na lono walizaga, Yesu yakasola mukate, yakalumba, yakawubegula no kuwapela wejizwa wakwe akuno yakutya, “Solenyi awu wo muwili wangu.”
23 Hanyuma yayo yakasola ne cikombe naco, yakamulumba Mulungu, yakawapela, nawo wose wakacing'wela i cikombe cico.
24 Yesu yakawatya, “Ayi yo sakami yangu yono yikukangaza i cilagane co Mulungu, isakami yono yikwitika so wanhu wenji.
25 Mbeka nghumulonjelenyi, sinong'wa kanyhi divayi ye zabibu, sunga mwizuwa alilya lono noloying'wa divayi yimhya, mu wutemi wo Mulungu.”
26 Ale baho wakemba lwimbo, nawo waza kondya, wakatuluka, wakabita kwi'tunda lye Mizeyituni.

Yesu yakuwila vyono Petelo komukana

(Mat 26:31-35; Luk 22:31-34; Yoh 13:36-38)

27 Yesu yakawatya wejizwa wakwe, Anye wose motiza no kundeka nili wene, hambi i Mandiko Gono Gelile nha gakutya, “Nolomutowa u mudimi, ne zingholo nazo zolokwiyagala ako na ako.”
28 Ninga kolo nakondya kuzuka, nomulongolelenyi ku Galilaya.
29 Nayo Petelo yakamutya, “Hamba wose watize wakulece wuli wene, ninga ane si nokulecela lukulu!”
30 Nayo Yesu yakamutya, “Mbeka nghukulonjela Petelo, vyono i cilo cici lono i nzogolo yowa yikali nalila kali kejete, agwe kowa wakondya kunghana kali kadatu.”
31 Ninga Petelo yakehinda muno kulonga yakutya, “Hamba hono yiwe yinojeye cifwizane hamonga nagwe, ane sinokulecela lukulu hamba hadodo.” Ne wejizwa wose nawo wakalonga nhavivyo.

Yesu yakulomba mu busitani ya Getisemane

(Mat 26:36-46; Luk 22:39-46)

32 Ale, wakaza ku cigundu cono cacemwaga Getisemane. Yesu yakawatya iwejizwa wakwe, “Ikalenyi baha, ane lono nghali kulomba.”
33 Ale, yakawa kuwasola wa Petelo, na Yakobo na Yohana, yakawa kwanza kugazika no kuwona wusungu mu nhumbula yakwe.
34 Yakawatya, “I nhumbula yangu yina wukalale wuwaha wono wunhuliye muno muno. Ale, mwikalaje bahano akuno mwilanjiliye.”
35 Yakatela ha mwande hadodo, yakagwa hasi, yakalomba vyono, hono yiwe yisumice, yalece kucagana cipango cico ce magazo.
36 Yakatya, “Gwe Baba, kuli'gwe zose nha zisumice. Ale wumbusizaje icikombe cicino ce magazo. Ninga si vyono nyendile nene, langa yiwe nha vyono wendile gwegwe.”

37 Hanyuma yayo, yakaza kuli wejizwa wawalya wadatu, yakawagana wagonile. Ale, yakamuwuza Petelo, yakutya, “Simoni, alu, wagona? Si wusumile kwikala meso hamba saa monga du?
38 Yakawa kuwatya, mwikalaje meso, mulombe ho mulece kwinjila mu wujelwe. Mbeka nhumbula yendile, ninga muwili wusoceye.”
39 Yakabita kulomba kanyhi yakuwuyajila kulonga zinghani zizilya du.
40 Ale, yakaza kanyhi kuli wejizwa wakwe, yakawagana wagonile. Nhondo hamba si wasuma bwete no kunugula ne meso gawo. Hamba siwamanya na yono wopitula.
41 Nayo yali yeza kuwaziza lwe kadatu yakawatya, “Alu mukali mugonile mucehile? Ninga alu hodu zakwila! Icipango nha cakondya kuza! U Mwana wo Munhu yakukombolwa kuli wanhu wabi.
42 Ale, yinucenyi, cibite. Lanjenyi ta, ayo u munhu mono yakungaluka yaza!”

Yesu yakwibatwa

(Mat 26:47-56; Luk 22:47-53; Yoh 18:3-12)

43 Na lono Yesu yali yakali yakulonga, lanje, Yuda yuyulya u muyagwe na wawalya iwejizwa we likumi na wejete, yakawa kuza, hamonga ne lipuga lye wanhu, wena ne mimaje ne zishale. Iwanhu wawo wali watumigwe ne Wakohani wawaha, ne wejizi wo Wulajilize hamonga ne Wanyamhala.
44 Nayo Yuda u mugaluci, yali yakondya kuwapela citanjilo yakutya, “Yuyo mono nomunonela, ale, yo yuyo mono mukumusaka, nanye mumwibataje mumutegule hasi yo wulindilizi.”
45 Nayo Yuda yaza kuza du, yakawa kumubitila Yesu, yakawa kumutya, “Mwijizi!” Hanyuma yakawa kumunonela.
46 Ale, iwanhu wawo wakamwibata Yesu.
47 Ninga yunji muyagwe na wawalya wono wali wemile hamonga na Yesu, yakawukwega u mumaje wakwe, yakamutowa muwanda wo Mukuhani muwaha, yakamubuha likutu.
48 Nayo Yesu yakawatya, “Alu muza mwina ne mimaje hamonga ne zishale ho munyibate nha kotya nili muhizi?
49 Na kumbe nhondo zose nawezanaga nanye nghwijiza mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, ninga simwanyibata ng'o. Ninga alu yinojeye i Mandiko Gono Gelile gafisilizwe.”
50 Ale baho iwejizwa wakwe wose wakamuleka, wakatiza.
51 Baho hali hoyuli muzelelo yunji mono yamutyatyaga Yesu, akuno yekumbile mwenda we bafuta. Nawo wakajela kumwibata.
52 Ninga mwene yakawesukula, yakawuleka u mwenda wuwulya we bafuta, yakatiza yali cipwi.

Yesu hamwande ye libalaza lye wanyamhala

(Mat 26:57-68; Luk 22:54-55, 63-71; Yoh 18:13-14, 19-24)

53 Ale, wakamutegula Yesu kuli Mukohani Muwaha, kono Iwakohani wawaha wose, ne wanyamhala ne wejizi wo wulajilize wali wekongholeye.
54 Nayo Petelo yakawa yakumutyatya Yesu, yakujendela kutali, Petelo yakenjila mwi'kumbo lyo Mukohani Muwaha, yakekala hamonga ne walindilizi, yakawa yakota moto.
55 Iwakohani wawaha ne Libalaza lyose, wakalondola wuhomelezi wo kumusitacila Yesu ho wamuwulaje, ninga hamba si wawutuga yaye.
56 Ne wanhu wenji wakahomelela zo wulambi kuli Yesu, no wuhomelezi wawo wali wusutane.
57 Ale, na wanji nawo wakema, nawo wakahomelela zo wulambi kuli Yesu, wakutya,
58 “Ase camuhulika umunhu yuyu yakutya, Ane noyimwagula i Nyumba yiyino yo Mulungu yono yizenjigwe ne miwoko, na mu vilo vidatu, ane noyizenga yinji yono siyizenjigwe ne miwoko.”
59 Ninga, hamba nhavivyo, u wuhomelezi wawo nawo, wali wusutane.

60 Ale, Mukohani Muwaha yakema haciligati yawo, yakamuwuza Yesu, yakutya, “Alu, siwukupitula cinji? Nghanici yono wakukuhomelela i wanhu wawa?”
61 Ninga u mwene yakanyamala twii, hamba kulonga cinji. Mukohani Muwaha yakamuwuza kanyhi yakutya, “Ma, alu yo gwegwe Kilisito. U mwana wo Mulungu Mulugutizwe?”
62 Nayo Yesu yakapitula, yakutya, “Heya yo nene. Na kanyhi, molomuwona u Mwana wo Munhu yekaye ku lubavu lo kulume lwa Mono yena Vilungo, yakuziza mu mavunde ga ku wulanga.”
63 Baho Mukohani Muwaha yakavidemulanga i vivwalo vyakwe vye cikohani, akuno yakutya, “Hambi alu kanyhi cikusaka wuhomelezi wa cici?
64 Hambi nha muwuhulika nyo weneco u wotovule wakwe!” Alu 'nye mukuwona wule? Wose wakatemya vyono yimunojeye yawulagwe.
65 Ale ho baho baho du, wanji wa muli wene, wakanza kumufunyila mate, wakamugubika wusu, wakamutowanga makofi no kumutya, “Ale wutambule ta, nani mono yakutowile!” Sunga ne wetumami wakamusola, nawo wakamutowanga makofi.

Petelo yakumukana Yesu

(Mat 26:69-75; Luk 22:56-62; Yoh 18:15-18, 25-27)

66 Na lono Petelo yali yakali he kumbo, yunji wa muli wetumami we cicekulu wo Mukohani Muwaha yakaza.
67 Yaza kumuwona Petelo yakota moto, yakamulanga, yakamutya, “Hamba na gwegwe wali hamonga na Yesu wa Nazaleti.”
68 Ninga mwene Petelo yakakana, yakutya, “Si manyile hamba singhwizuka yono wukulonga!” Hanyuma yayo Petelo yakatuluka yakalawila kunze. Baho inzogolo nayo yikawa kulila.
69 No muhinza yuyulya yaza kumuwona kanyhi Petelo, yakanza kanyhi kuwalonjela i wanhu wono wali wemile baho, yakutya, “U Munhu ayu nayo mama, muyawo.”
70 Nayo Petelo yakakana kanyhi. Na hanyuma yayo hadodo, iwanhu wono wali wemile baho wakamutya Petelo, “Mbeka nagwe uli muyawo, hambi nagwe ha wuli Mugalilaya.”
71 Ninga Petelo yakanza kwilijita no kwilaha yakutya, “Ane si nimumanyile munhu ayu mono mukumulonga.”
72 Baho nayo inzogolo yikawa kulila lwe kejete. Hobaho Petelo yakakumbucila vyono Yesu yali yamulonjeye, vyono “Lono inzogolo yowa yikali siyinalila kali kejete, agwe kowa wakondya kunghana kali kadatu.” Hodu Petelo yakalila muno muno tobo ne mahozi.
