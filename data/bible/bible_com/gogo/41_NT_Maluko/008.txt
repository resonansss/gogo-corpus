Maluko 8

Yesu yakuwapela ndigwa wanhu elufu yine

(Mat 15:32-39)

1 Mu vilo vivyo lipuga yiwaha lye wanhu likekonghola kanyhi, nawo wali wasina ndigwa. Ale, Yesu yakawacema iwejizwa wakwe, yakawatya,
2 “Nghuwawonela lusungu wanhu wawa, hambi nha cekalana nane vilo vidatu, nawo wasina cinji co kulya.
3 Nane hono niwakombole wabite kwawo akuno wadabuce, wozilika mu nzila, hambi wayawo wanji nha walawa kutali.”
4 Nawo wejizwa wakwe wakamuwuza, wakutya, “Alu ahano nha nyika, ho yolawa hayi mikate yono yosuma kuwegutya i wanhu wawa wose?”
5 Nayo Yesu wakawawuza, yakutya, “Alu 'nye mwina mikate milinga?” Nawo wakamutya, “Mupunghati.”
6 Ale, yakawalajiliza wanhu wekale hasi. Nayo yakayisola mikate yiyilya vyono yili mupunghati, yakamulumba Mulungu, yakayibegula, yakawapela iwejizwa wakwe wawagawile wanhu, nawo wakawagawila.
7 Kanyhi wali wena ne visomba vicefu. Nayo Yesu yakavilugutiza, yakalajiliza wavigawe kuli wanhu nhavivyo.
8 Iwanhu wakalya, wakeguta. Wakatondoleza ivibegulwa vyono vyasigala wakamemya nhoto mupunghati.
9 Nawo wono walya wali waficile nha wanhu elufu yine. Yesu yakawakombola,
10 lo lulolulo yakenjila mu ngalawa hamonga ne wejizwa wakwe, yakabita ku yisi ya Dalamanuta.

Iwafalisayo wakusaka citanjilo

(Mat 16:1-4)

11 Iwafalisayo wakaza, wakanza kwiwuza na Yesu. Nawo wakawa kumujela, wakamusaka yawanozeze citanjilo cono cikulajila vyono mwene yena wukombolwe kulawa ku wulanga.
12 Yesu yakakalala muno mu nhumbula yakwe, yakatya, “Alu so cici u lulelo lulu lukusaka citanjilo? Mbeka nghumulonjelenyi lulelo lulu silopegwa citanjilo co cose.”
13 Hodu yakawaleka, yakenjila kanyhi mu ngalawa, yakalowoka ku nhumbi nzagwe ye lilamba.

I cisase ce Wafalisayo na ca Helode

(Mat 16:5-12)

14 Iwejizwa wakasemwa kusoleleza ne mikate, nawo wali wena mukate monga du mu ngalawa.
15 Yesu yakawakong'onheza, yakutya, “Lanjenyi ta! Mweze musujile kuli cisase ce Wafalisayo ne cisase ca Helode.”
16 Iwejizwa wakanza kulongana weneco wose, wakutya, “Alu yakulonga nha vivyo vyono cisina mikate.”
17 Nayo Yesu yakamanya cono walongaga, yakawatya, “Alu so cici mukulongana nyo weneco wose vyono musina mukate? Alo mukali sinezuka du, hamba kumanya ng'o? Alo i zinhumbula zenyu zikamupe?
18 Alu nha mwina ne meso, simukuwona? Nha mwina ne makutu, simukuhulika? Alu simukukumbucila,
19 i cidaha cilya lono nayibegula mikate yiyilya mihano, no kuwapela wanhu elufu yihano? Wulenyi, mwatondola nhoto yilinga zono zali zimemile vibegulwa vyono vyasigala?” Wakamutya, “Ikumi na nyejete.”
20 “Na lono nayibegula mikate yiyilya mupunghati no kuwapela wanhu elufu yine, ma, mukatondola nhoto yilinga zono zali zimemile vibegulwa vyono vyasigala?” Wakamutya “Mupunghati.”
21 Nayo yakawatya, “Alu sunga nalu mukali si nezuka du?”

Yesu yakumuponya we cibofu wa ku Betesayida

22 Yesu yakafika ku Betesayida hamonga ne wejizwa wakwe. Na kuko, wanhu wakamuletela munhu yunji we cibofu, wakamunena yamupamize.
23 Nayo Yesu yakamwibata muwoko munhu yuyo we cibofu, yakamutegula kunze ye cikaya ca Betesayida. Yakamubaka mate mu meso, yakamuwicila miwoko yakwe, yakamuwuza, yakutya, “Wule, wukusuma kuwona cinji.”
24 Nayo u munhu yuyo we cibofu yakalanga, yakatya, “Nghuwawona wanhu wakwijela kotya mabici gakujenda.”
25 Hanyuma yayo Yesu yakamuwicila kanyhi miwoko yakwe mu meso gakwe, nayo yakanugula muno, ne vilungo vyakwe vyo kuwona vikamuwuyila yakaviwona vyose viswanu nha vyono vinha.
26 Yesu yakamutya yabite ku kaya kwawo, yakamukong'onheza, yakutya, “Wulece hamba kwinjila mu cikaya!”

Petelo yakumuvumila Yesu vyono yo Kilisito

(Mat 16:13-20; Luk 9:18-21)

27 Hanyuma Yesu ne wejizwa wakwe wakabita ku vikaya vya Kayisaliya Filipi. Nawo lono wali wali mu nzila, Yesu yakawawuza iwejizwa wakwe, yakutya, “Alu wanhu wanhezaga nene nani?”
28 Wakamutya, “Wanhu wanji watezaga gwegwe yo Yohana u Mubaputizi, na wanji Eliya, ninga wanji watezaga wuli yumonga muyagwe ne wawizi.”
29 Nayo yakawawuza, yakutya, “Ninga anye nanye ma? Munhezaga nene nani?” Petelo yakamutya, “Agwe yo gwegwe Kilisito.”
30 Hanyuma Yesu yakawakong'onheza walece kumulonjela munhu wo wose zinghani zakwe.

Yesu yakulonga zo wugazwe no wufwe no wuzuce wakwe

(Mat 16:21-28; Luk 9:22-27)

31 Yesu yakanza kuwejiza iwejizwa wakwe, yakutya, “Yimunojeye u Mwana wo Munhu yatuje magazo menji no kulemwa ne wanyamhala, ne wakohani wawaha ne wejizi wo Wulajilize. Kowulagwa na hanyuma ye vilo vidatu, kozuka.”
32 Yesu yakawalonjela nghani yiyo calinze. Holeka Petelo yakamuteza Yesu ha mhelo, yakanza kumutacila.
33 Ninga Yesu yakapituka, yakawalanga iwejizwa wakwe, yakamutacila Petelo yakutya, “Wuce hali'ne Setani! Imahweso gako si mahweso go Mulungu, langa ga cimunhu-munhu.”
34 Hanyuma Yesu yakalicema i lipuga lye wanhu, hamonga ne wejizwa wakwe, yakawatya, “Hono munhu wo wose yasace yawe mutyatyi wangu, yimunojeye yekapise mweneco, yawudinghule umusalaba wakwe.
35 Hambi munhu mono yakuhila kuwuponya wumi wakwe mweneco, nha kowajiliza, langa munhu mono kowajiliza u wumi wakwe, ku soko yangu na ku soko yo Luganuzi Luswanu, yuyo, langa kowuponya u wumi wakwe.
36 Alu ma, u munhu kocituga cambuzici, hono yayituje yinze yose kono yalila, ninga yawajilize u wumi wakwe?
37 Hamba munhu kolavyaci, cihomele wumi wakwe?
38 Umunhu wo wose wa mu lulelo luluno lubi, lono si lukumucelelela Mulungu, hono yambonele soni nene, hamonga ne mejizo gangu, Umwana wo Munhu nayo, kolomuwonela soni u munhu yuyo, mwipango lyono kokuziza mu nhojo yo Sogwe, hamonga ne wamalayika wono welile.”
