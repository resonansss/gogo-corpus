Maluko 5

Yesu yakumuponya munhu we licisi lyavu

(Mat 8:28-34; Luk 8:26-39)

1 Ale, wakafika ku nhumbi nzagwe ye lilamba, mu'si ye Wagelasene.
2 Yesu yaza kutima mu ngalawa, hobaho viketang'anila na munhu yunji mono yali yena licisi lyavu, yakulawa ku vitenjelo.
3 U munhu yuyo yekalaga mu vitenjelo, hamba hasina munhu mono yasuma kanyhi kumuwopa ne manyolo.
4 Hambi nha kali kenji wamuwopaga ne zipingu hamonga ne manyolo, ninga mapango gose yasumaga kugadumula manyolo gago, no kuzibenanga zipingu zizo, hamba hasina munhu mono yasuma kumwimila.
5 Na mu misi ne cilo, yekalaga mu vitenjelo, na mu matunda, yakuzoza no kwidemulanga ne mabwe.

6 Nayo yaza kumuwonela Yesu kutali, yakamumanyhilila, yakamuhemba,
7 akuno yakulonga kwe'zi 'waha yakutya, “Gwe, Yesu, go Mwana wo Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye, alu wina nghanici nane? Mwi'tagwa lyo Mulungu, nghulomba wulece kungaza!”
8 (Nayo yalonga nhavivyo vyono Yesu yali yamulonjela yatya, “Go licisi lyavu, wulawile muli munhu ayu.”)
9 Holeka Yesu yakamuwuza, yakutya, “Alu litagwa lyako gwegwe nani?” Nayo yakapitula, yakutya, “Litagwa lyangu nene ‘Mhuga’ hambi ase nha cili wenji.”
10 U munhu yuyo yakamunena Yesu, yalece kugawinga gago macisi mavu mu'si yiyilya.
11 Ale kwali ne'tewa yiwaha lye zingubi likudima ha mwejelele we litunda.
12 Ale, imacisi gago gakamunena Yesu, gakutya, “Wucitegulaje ku zingubi zizo, cikenjile muli zene.”
13 Nayo yakagakombola. Ale, gago macisi mavu gakalawila, gakenjila mu zingubi zizilya. Ne litewa lyose lye zingubi elufu nyejete, likavulumucila he weta zikenjila mwi'lamba, no kufwila mumo.

14 Nawo wadimi we zingubi zizo wakatiza, wakayiganula i nghani yiyo kwi'kaya na mu migunda. Iwanhu wakaza ho wawone nyhici yono yiweye.
15 Wakamuziza Yesu, wakamuwona no munhu yuyulya mono yali yena litewa lye macisi yekaye hasi, yavwalile ne myenda, yena ne mahala gakwe, yatulile, nawo wakogopa muno.
16 Ne wanhu wono waziwona zizo, wakawaloloseza wanhu wanji vyono yimuweye u munhu mono yali yena macisi, na ze zingubi zizilya.
17 Nawo, wakanza kumunena yawuce mu'si yawo.
18 Na lono Yesu yenjilaga mu ngalawa, u munhu yuyulya mono yali yena imacisi, yakamunena yawezane nayo.
19 Ninga Yesu siyamuvumila. Langa yakamutya, “Wubite ku kaya yako, ku wanduguzo, wukawalonjele zimbaha zose zono u Muwaha yakutendeye na vyono yakuwoneye lusungu.”
20 Ale, munhu yuyo yakabita, yakanza kuganula kuko ku Dekapoli i zimbaha zose zono Yesu yamutendela, ne wanhu wose wakezina muno muno.

U muhinza wa Yayilo no mucekulu yunji, wakuponywa

(Mat 9:18-26; Luk 8:40-56)

21 Nayo Yesu yakalowocela kanyhi mu ngalawa ku nhumbi nzagwe ye lilamba. Ne lipuga yiwaha lye wanhu likekongholela ha mwande yakwe, nayo yakawa yemile ha nhumbi ye lilamba.
22 Baho yakaza yunji wa muli wawaha we nhang'anilo yo wuhembelo, litagwa Iyakwe Yayilo. Nayo yaza kumuwona Yesu, yakagwa hamwande ye magulu gakwe,
23 yakamunena muno yakutya, “Umuhinza wangu mudodo yatamigwe, yali behi no kufwa. Cibite nghukunena, wukamuwicile miwoko yako, ho yapone no kuwumipa!”
24 Ale, Yesu yakabitana nayo. Ne liwuta yiwaha lye wanhu likamutyatya, likawa limufinhile mbavu zose.
25 Na muli wene mwali moyuli mucekulu yunji mono yali yena wutamwa wo wuzaze we sakami myaka yikumi na myejete.
26 Umucekulu yuyo yali yagazika muno ne waganga wenji. Na hamba yawe yali yakondya kwitumamila sawo yakwe yose so wulagulwe, ninga siyatuga ciba hamba hadodo, langa wutamwa wakwe ho wukacila kuwa wubi muno muno.
27 Yali yakondya kuzihulika zinghani za Yesu, holeka yakaziza ku mugongo wa Yesu, yakekokomeza mu mwinji we wanhu wono wali wumufinhile Yesu mbavu zose,
28 akuno yakwiyitya, “Hono mhamize civwalo cakwe du, nopona.”
29 Hodu, yakawa kucipamiza i civwalo ca Yesu, no wuzaze wakwe we sakami, wukawa kuduma lwa kamonga, nayo yakehulika mu muwili wakwe nha vyono yaponywa wutamwa wakwe.
30 Nayo Yesu yakamanya kamonga vyono vilungo vya muli mwene vyalawa. Hobaho, yakalipitucila lipuga lye wanhu, yakawuza, yakutya, “Alo nani mwene mono yamhamize i civwalo cangu?”
31 Iwejizwa wakwe wakamutya, “Wukuwona vyono wanhu wenji wakufinhile mbavu zose, alu nhawule wukuwuza wukutya nani mwene mono yakupamize?”

32 Ninga Yesu yakajendelela kulanga langa kose kose ho yamuwone yuyo mono yanozize nhavivyo.
33 Baho umucekulu yuyo, vyono yali yamanyile zono zimuweye, yakaza akuno yakucicima so woga, yakagwa hamwande ya Yesu no kulonga ze mbeka zose.
34 Nayo Yesu yakamutya, “Go muhinza, wihuwilo wako wukuponya. Wubite wupolile, no wutamwa wako wuponele lukulu.”
35 Na lono Yesu yali yakali yakulonga, iwanhu wakaza kulawa ku kaya yo muwaha we nhang'anilo yo wuhembelo, wakamutya, “Umuhinza wako yakondya kufwa. Alu nhawule wukali wukumugaza Mwijizi?”
36 Ninga mwene Yesu hamba siyacelelela cono walonga, langa yakamutya muwaha we nhang'anilo yo wuhembelo, “Wulece kogopa, wihuwile du.”
37 Hamba siyaleka munhu yunji wo wose vibitane nayo, langa Petelo du, na Yakobo na Yohana u mwanakwawe wa Yakobo.

38 Wakafika ku kaya yo muwaha we nhang'anilo yo wuhembelo, nayo Yesu yakahulika lizozo, ne cililo.
39 Yakenjila ku gati, yakawatya, “Alu nhawule mukuzoza no kulila? U muhinza nha siyafwiye yaye, langa yagonile du.”
40 Ninga wene wakamuseka. Nayo, yakawalavyaniza wose kunze, yakawasola usogwe no nyina wo muhinza yuyo, hamonga na wawalya iwejizwa wakwe, wakenjila kugati kono kwali kowuli u muvimba wo muhinza yuyo.
41 Yesu yakamwibata muwoko, yakamutya, “Talita kumi.” Nhunizo yayo, “Go muhinza nghukulonjela, winuce!”
42 Lo lulolulo umuhinza yakenuka, yakanza kujenda. (Yali yena myaka ikumi na myejete.) Baho, iwanhu wakezina muno muno kuciza.
43 Nayo Yesu yakawakong'onheza muno, walece kuyitandaza inghani yiyo kuli wanhu. Hanyuma yakawa kuwalonjela wamupele muhinza zindigwa.
