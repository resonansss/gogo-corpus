Lwimbo Luswanu muno 8

1 Nane nali nyende, mhali wali wuwe wuli lumbu lyangu,

mono wongha matombo ga yaya.

Nha cinji, kolo cikwitang'anila mu nzila,

ho sume kukunonela ko welecele,

hamba munhu yunji kucelelela yaye.

2 Nane nali nghusole nghutegule sunga ko ku kaya ya yaya,

kuko ko wali wunyijizize wendo vyono winha.

Nane nali nghung'wese divayi yono yikoleye,

divayi yo mukomamanga wangu.

3 No muwoko wako wo kucekulu wuwe hasi yo mutwe wangu,

no muwoko wako wo kulume wuwe wunghumbatiye.

4 Holeka anye nyo wacekulu wa mu Yelusalemi,

nha vyono zinha nzimba hamba sawada za mu mbago,

mulece kuwusagahaza hamba kuwusinhusa wendo,

sunga kolo wukuwona viswanu weneco.

Lwimbo lo Mutandatu

Zinghani za we cicekulu wenji

5 Alo yuyo nani mono yakuza yakulawilila ku nyika,

yakujenda weyibate miwoko no mwendece wakwe?

Zinghani za we cicekulu

Nha nakusinhusa he'sina lyo muyembe,

i hanhu hono walelelwa.

6 Wumbice nha mutozo mu nhumbula yako,

nha citanjilo mu muwoko wako.

Hambi wendo nha wina vilungo kotya lifwa,

no wivu nawo, wukali kotya citenjelo.

No wukolele wayo, winha wukolele wo moto,

wunyanyaga nho moto mukali.

7 Malenga menji, si gosuma kuwuzimya wendo,

hamba makolongo, si gosuma kuwudodomeza yaye.

Holeka, hamba munhu yatye yalavye sawo yakwe yose

yawugule wendo, mbeka, kokwipela wubedegalwe bwete.

Zinghani za we cilume kuli lumbu lyawo

8 Ase cina lumbu lyetu mono yakali mudodo,

yakali hamba si nota ne vitombo yaye.

Alu, cimunozezeci yuyo u lumbu lyetu,

hono muzelelo yaze yasace kumupogoza?

9 Mwene yali yawe lyo yijinji,

cali cimuzenjele, cimupilimye no munala wa fweza.

Na hono yali yawe wo mulango,

cali cimucitile ne zimbawo zo muhagata.

Zinghani za we cicekulu

10 Ane nha nili 'jinji ne vitombo vyangu, yo minala yalyo.

No mwendece wangu,

yamanyile manyi manyi nha vyono,

hono nili hamonga nayo,

nghutuga wucehe hamonga no mulamu.

Zinghani za we cilume

11 Solomona yali na mugunda wakwe we mizabibu kuko ku Baali-hamoni.

Hakawa ne walimi,

wono wakawazima mugunda wuwo kulawa kuli mwene,

ho wawe kulavya munhu fweza elufu monga so mikalo yayo.

12 Mugunda we mizabibu, wo mugunda wangu nene.

Agwe Solomona, wusole fweza elufu monga,

nawo walimi, wasole magana mejete lye 'gawo lyawo.

13 Leka ta, nilihulicize lizi lyako go mwendece wangu ku wulawa mu busitani,

nawo wayangu wakuhila walihulice lizi lyako lono wukulonga.

Zinghani za we cicekulu

14 Gwe, wuze si, go mwendece wangu,

mono wihwanile nzimba hamba sawada,

zono zili mu matunda kono mibici

ye mhunjile swanu yilimwaga.
