Lwimbo Luswanu muno 1

1 Awu wo Lwimbo luswanu muno lwa Solomona.

Lwimbo lulongozi

Zinghani za we cicekulu

2 Kunonela, kunonela no mulomo wako,

no wikumbatile wako, mbeka,

wuswanu wucizize divayi.

3 Ne manukato gako, gakununyhila viswanu,

ne litagwa lyako, yiswanu linha manukato.

Holeka ne wacekulu wakukwenda.

4 Wusolaje, nase cimanyhe cibitane nagwe,

weze mutemi wangu, wunyinjize mu nyumba yako.

Cosangalalana hamonga,

cing'we muno divayi, no kuzwila mu wikumbatile.

Heya yo ling'ani wacekulu wose wakwende!

5 Nye wacekulu wa Yelusalemi, ane no muyenyu heya,

nili mutitu, kanyhi nili muswanu,

nili mutitu, nihwanile ne mahema ga Kedali,

kanyhi, nili muswanu nhe mabupa ge myenda,

ga mwi'kulu lya Solomona.

6 Ninga mulece kuhenahena vyono nili mutitu tuku,

hambi lizuwa nha lya nyanya.

Nawo wandugu zangu wasimbila,

wambika niwe mulindilizi we migunda ye mizabibu,

nghawa sina hamba cilejeho co kuwulanjilila no

mugunda wangu no mweneco.

7 Gwe go mwendece wangu, wunonjelaje ta,

alo hayi lo, hono kodimila litewa lyako?

Na hayi hono zosuma ne misi?

Alu nhawule niswe nghukulondola mu matewa

ge wadimi wanji?

Zinghani za we cilume

8 Hono wuwe si wuhamanyile hanhu,

agwe go muswanu muli wacekulu wayago,

ale wubite wulityatye litewa,

wuhawone i hanhu ha wudimilo we zimhene zako,

behi ne zinghambi ze wadimi.

9 Gwe go mwendece wangu,

nghukwihwananiza ne fwalasi zinume za mu magali ga Falawo.

10 Sunga ne zimvwili zako zikwijela swanu muno,

zinojile mucanyha ye zinzagasa zako,

lono zikuninyina mo mu singo,

nha vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu.

11 Ninga ase, cokunozeza mukufu wa zahabu,

wono wina wihambo wa fweza.

Zinghani za we cicekulu

12 Mutemi wangu yali yagonile mwi'goda lyakwe, ne mbeho,

yikamemywa ne mhunjile ye manukato gangu.

13 Mwendece wangu yenha mufuko wa manemane,

lono yanyegamiye mu mhambaga mu vitombo vyangu.

14 Mwendece wangu yenha viluwa vya mu mbago,

vyono vibonholaga mu mizabibu ya Engedi.

Zinghani za we cilume

15 Gwe mwendece wangu, nye wuli muswanu si,

ne meso gako gakulembula nha meso ge nghunda, so wendo!

Zinghani za we cicekulu

16 Lanje, mwendece wangu vyono wuli muswanu,

kanyhi wunyendeze muno.

Na mu mahanze madece, wowa wo wugono wetu.

17 Ne mibici ye mihagata, yowa yo micici ye kaya yetu,

ne mabici ganji, gowa go mahapa getu.


Lwimbo Luswanu muno 2

1 Nane nili 'luwa lya mu yisi ya Shaloni,

nili 'luwa lya mu malolo.

Zinghani za we cilume

2 Nha vyono linha liluwa lyono lili mu mamivya,

ale nhavivyo no mwendece wangu mu ciligati ye wacekulu.

Zinghani za we cicekulu

3 Nha vyono winha mubici wo muyembe,

wono wuli ha ciligati ye mabici ga mu mbago,

Nhavivyo no mwendece wangu nayo, vyo yenha,

mu ciligati ye walume wanji.

Nane nali nyende niwe nikaye ha mubeho wayo,

ne mikalo yayo, yili muzinzi lono nghuyijeza.

4 Yakandeta, sunga ko ku nyumba yakwe yo kulila zindigwa.

Yakanyinulila cipepela co wendo.

5 Yakangomolela kanyhi vilungo vyangu,

mu wulya zabibu nghavu,

yakamhela vilungo so kulya mikalo,

hambi nha naganda muno, ku soko yo wusungu,

6 No muwoko wakwe wo kucekulu yawuwicile

hasi yo mutwe wangu.

Yanghumbatiye no muwoko wakwe wo kulume.

7 Holeka anye nyo wacekulu wa mu Yelusalemi,

nha vyono zinha nzimba hamba sawada, za mu mbago,

mulece kuwusagahaza hamba kuwusinhusa wendo

sunga kolo wukuwona viswanu wenezo.

Lwimbo lwe Kejete

Zinghani za we cicekulu

8 Nane nghulihulika lizi lyo mwendece wangu,

yakunyiziza yakumangha, yakolile mu matunda,

no kuzumhazumha mu vigongo.

9 Nayo mwendece wangu,

yehwanile nzimba, yenha sawada.

Lanje, yemile ku mhilimo ye lijinji,

yasunjiliye mwi'dilisha,

yakulanjila mu nghalama.

10 Hanyuma mwendece wangu, yakunonjela yakutya,

Zinghani za we cilume

Ale winuce wuze, 'gwe go mwendece wangu,

go muswanu wangu, wuze cibite.

11 Alu wulanje, cinyuli capuluta,

ne mvula nayo yawuka.

12 Ne maluwa geza kubonhola mwi'hala.

Holeka aci co cidaha co kwimba,

ne zinyimbo ze zinziwa, cikuzihulika mwi'hala.

13 Lanje, ne zinghuyu nazo zeza kupya,

nayo mizabibu yikulavya maluwa.

Holeka wuze 'gwe go mwendece wangu,

go muswanu wangu, wuze cibite.

14 Hambi nha wihwanile ngungulima, yono

yihundikaga mu mapalika ga mu mabwe mawaha.

Ta wundekaje nghulanje wusu wako vyono wunojeze,

nilihulice ne lizi lyako,

lyono likunhamisa mu nhumbula yangu.

15 Wuzibataje zinyhewe,

ko muno zinyhewe zono zikali ndodo,

lono zikali sinahodanga migunda yetu ye mizabibu,

lono mizabibu yanza kulavya maluwa.

Zinghani za we cicekulu

16 Mwendece wangu, yo wangu, nane nili wakwe.

Kalilisaga litewa lyakwe mu maluwa.

17 Sunga kolo wela hakali mitondo lono cibeho cikuputa,

no wutitu wukuwuka.

Holeka wumhitucilaje 'gwe go muswanu wangu

wuwe nha nzimba hamba sawada za mu

matunda ga Beteli.


Lwimbo Luswanu muno 3

1 Na mu vilo vyose ne cilo,

lono ngonile mu citanda cangu.

Nimulotaga du yuyo mono nimwendile.

Nghawa kumulondola muno,

ninga hamba si nghumuwona yaye,

2 Nghawa kutuluka,

nghabita kujendajenda mu zinzila mwi'kaya.

Hodu nghawa kutya, “Alu nokwinuka mhilime mwi'kaya,

nghole mu zinzila zayo zose, sunga na mu vibalangu,

nimulondole mwendece wangu, muswanu wangu.

Ninga nghamulondola hamba kumuwona ng'o.”

3 Nawo i walindilizi wono wapilimaga mwi'kaya,

kumbe wakambona.

Nane nghawawuza nghawatya, “Nye wayangu,

mbonelenyi baha ta mwendece wangu?”

4 Ninga nali neza kwileka nawo du,

lo lulolulo nghawa kumuwona.

Hodu, nghawa kumwibatilila,

hamba si namulecela kanyhi,

nghawa kumutegula sunga ko ku kaya kwa yaya,

kugati ye cumba cono nalelelwa.

5 Holeka anye nyo wacekulu wa mu Yelusalemi,

nha vyono zinha nzimba hamba sawada za mu mbago.

Mulece kuwusaga haza

hamba kuwusinhusa wendo sunga kolo wukuwona viswanu weneco.

Lwimbo lwe Kadatu

Zinghani za we cicekulu

6 Alo cicici cono cikuza cikulawilila ku nyika,

cihwanile lisumbili lye lyosi,

cono cina mhunjile swanu yo wuvumba,

hamonga ne manemane,

no wuvumba wono wukuwa kuguzwa ne wasuluza?

7 Lanje, Solomona yakuza,

wamudinghuliye mwi'goda lyakwe lyo wutemi,

ne wasilikale vitung'ati wa mu Isilayeli,

wono waficile makumi mutandatu liwalo lyawo,

wamupilime wakumudima.

8 Nawo wose webate mimaje mu miwoko yawo,

wewikanize ku wulewane.

9 Nayo mutemi Solomona,

wamudinghuliye mwi'goda lyakwe lyo wutemi,

linozigwe no mubici muswanu.

10 Ne masumbili gayo, gali ga fweza,

na mucanyha yalyo, lyali yigubice no mwenda,

wono wali mubakwe wihambo wa zahabu.

I mito yayo nayo,

yali yina folonya ze citambala ce liwala lyo lugandu,

yono yahonwa ko wendezwe we wacekulu wa Yelusalemi.

11 Nye nyo wacekulu wa mu Sayuni,

muze ta, mumuwone mutemi Solomona.

Mwene yavwalile mukumbo,

wono nyina yamuvwika mu mutwe wakwe,

mwi'zuwa lye halusi yakwe.

Mwi'zuwa lyakwe lye nyemo no wusangalale.


Lwimbo Luswanu muno 4

Zinghani za we cilume

1 Gwe mwendece wangu, ku wulangwe,

nye wuli muswanu si!

Lanje ne meso gako vyono gakulembula nha ga nziwa,

kusi ye citambala cako, cono wigubice mu wusu.

Ne zimvwili zako zinha litewa lya mhene,

zono zigonaga mu mwalambalo we litunda lya Gileyadi.

2 Ne meno gako mazelu swee, kotya litewa lya ngholo,

zono zakondya kucenyenhwa no kuhovugwa,

kanyhi zono hasina hamba monga yono yajiliye,

zikujenda zitunjile nyejete nyejete.

3 Milomo yako yinha luputi ludunghu,

nayo yendezaga viswanu lono wukulonga.

Zinghatu zako, zikung'alala kusi ye citambala,

cono wigubice mu wusu wako.

4 Nayo singo yako,

yihwanile nho munala wa Dawudi yinyolocile kanyhi yiteleye,

yina no mukufu,

wono wehwana nha ngula elufu monga, nhumbice ziyipilime.

5 Ne vitombo vyako vyejete, vinha kotya nyana nyejete

za nzimba, zono zikudima mu maluwa.

6 Ale, nane nokwikala mumo du mwi'tunda

lyono lina manemane, mwi'tunda lyo wuvumba,

sunga wele, mbeho yipute ya hakali mitondo,

no wutitu wuwe wawuka.

7 Gwe mwendece wangu, nye wuli muswanu si!

Musina hamba nghani 'mbi muli'gwe.

8 Ale, cibitane nane ku wulawa mu matunda ga Lebanoni,

'gwe go bibi halusi wangu,

cibitane wose ku wulawa Lebanoni.

Wutime wuwuce kuko mwi'tunda lya Amana,

ku wulawa mwi'tunda lya Senili na mwi'tunda lya Helimoni,

mono zikalaga zisimba ne zisuwi.

9 Wulanje we meso gako, 'gwe go mwendece wangu,

go bibi halusi wangu,

hamonga no mukufu wono wuvwalile mu singo,

ane no muyago, wakondya kuyihiza nhumbula yangu.

10 Gwe go mwendece wangu, go bibi halusi wangu,

u wendo wako, wukusangalaza muno.

I mbeka, wendo wako, wucizize wuswanu we divayi.

Ne mhunjile ye mafuta gako,

mbeka yicizize wuvumba we mawala gose.

11 No muzinzi wo wuci, wuli mu milomo yako

'gwe go mwendece wangu,

no lulimi lwako lwaweza mele no wuci kuli'ne.

Ne vivwalo vyako,

vina mhunjile yose nho wuvumba wa ku Lebanoni.

12 Mwendece wangu, bibi halusi wangu, yo busitani hundikwe.

Busitani yono sitilwe no mululu,

co cikolongo ce ciwene.

13 Ne mibici baho yikutula.

Yikuwiluka nhe mibici ye mikomamanga,

yono yilelaga mikalo miswanu.

Nayo yina ne mafuta go wulenzi,

go wuguzi wukamu, si vimalikaga kuko.

14 Vye lanji ye manjano, no mucayicayi,

no mudalasini, hamba wuvumba we mawala gose.

Wugutika no wudi, vyose vicindikaga kuko,

hamonga ne mibici yose ye mhunjile swanu.

15 Visima viyimwajilaga busitani malenga,

hamonga ne mivwilo ye malenga.

Ne vikolongo vya mu matunda ga Lebanoni,

vyono vikuhuluma musunho.

Zinghani za we cicekulu

16 Ale, wusinhuce go mbeho ya kusumuka,

nagwe go mbeho ya kutakama,

wuze wupute mu busitani yangu, mhunjile yayo yipemuce.

No mwendece wangu, ho yaze mu busitani,

yalye mikalo miswanu.


Lwimbo Luswanu muno 5

Zinghani za we cilume

1 Nenjila mu busitani yangu, 'gwe go mwendece wangu,

go bibi halusi wangu.

Nghuhepa wugutika hamonga ne mhunjile ye manemane.

Nghulya no wuci wangu, hamonga ne lilala lyo wuci.

Nghung'wa ne divayi yangu, hamonga ne mele.

Zinghani za we cicekulu wenji

Aa! Nye nyo wendece wetu, heya mulye, mung'we,

sunga nyenye weneco nhondo muwe mwagala wendo!

Lwimbo lwe Kane

Zinghani za we cicekulu

2 Lono nali ngonile, nhumbula yangu yali yili meso.

Na mumo mu nhongo,

nali kumulota mwendece wangu yakukong'onha ha mulango.

Zinghani za we cilume

Ale, wundigulile ninjile, 'gwe go mwendece wangu,

agwe go wuswanu we nhumbula yangu, go ngungulima yangu.

Hambi mutwe wangu nha wadodowekwa ne buhe,

ne zimvwili zangu zitonyelwa ne mahalavu ge cilo.

Zinghani za we cicekulu

3 Alu mwendece wangu,

ane nha nakondya kwiwopola ne myenda yangu,

alu ho ninuce kanyhi niyivwale?

Ane nali nakondya kwizuguza ne magulu gangu,

alu ho ninuce niyitipize kanyhi?

4 Ninga mwene mwendece wangu, yakawibata mulango,

nane nhumbula yangu yikatamika muno so mwene,

vyono yali yasejelela ha mulango.

5 Hobaho, nane nghawa kwiwikaniza ho nghamudigulile

mulango yenjile.

Miwoko yangu lulo yikawa yina mhunjile swanu yo wugutika,

ne vidole vyangu vyasululaga mhunjile swanu yo 'gutika,

lono nebataga cidindilo co mulango.

6 Hobaho, nghawa kumudigulila mulango mwendece wangu,

ninga kumbe yali yakondya kuwuka, yabita.

Hee! Mwene! Nane ho nali nyendile kulihulika lizi lyakwe.

Hodu, nghawa kwanza kumulondola,

ninga hamba si namuwona yaye.

Nghamucemeleza, ninga hamba si yedika yaye.

7 Nawo walindilizi wono wapilimaga mwi'kaya,

kumbe wakambona,

wakanhowanga no kumhela zinghomele.

Nawo walindilizi wono wagalanjililaga majinji ge likaya,

wakayidemulanga kofiya yangu.

8 Ale, ta, munonjelaje anye nyo wacekulu wa mu Yelusalemi,

hono muwe mumuwona mwendece wangu,

mumulonjele nha vyono, ane nghuzilika so wendo.

Zinghani za we cicekulu wenji

9 Gwe go mucekulu muswanu muli wacekulu,

alu mulume yuyo, yena cicici co wizine,

holeka nase yiwe yicinojeye cikulonjele?

Zinghani za we cicekulu

10 Nye wayangu,

u mwendece wangu yena wusu wono wukwendeza,

kanyhi yali vilungo, ne vilungo vyakwe,

vyokwinjila wanhu elufu yikumi.

11 No wusu wakwe winha shaba,

kanyhi wutelemeye muno,

yena mvwili za cilende zitambalaye,

kanyhi nhitu tuku nhe lihowe.

12 Ne meso gakwe maswanu,

genha ga nziwa zono zili ha cikolongo cono cikuhuluma.

Kotya nha nziwa zono zakondya kwihovuga mu mele,

alu zimile ha cikolongo.

13 Zinghatu zakwe nazo, zendeze muno,

zinhe busitani swanu muno

yono yimemile mboga hamonga ne hati yayo.

Nayo milomo yakwe,

yihwanile maluwa mamwajilwe mhunjile swanu.

14 Ne miwoko yakwe, ta miswanu 'nye,

kavwalaga nyamwidole ze vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu.

U muwili wakwe nhondo,

wutelemeye winhe zimbali ze zinhembo,

zono zina ne vibwe vyo wulembo wo wuguzi wukamu.

15 Ayi migulu yakwe,

yinha zisumbili ze'bwe lyono likung'alala viswanu

zono ziwicigwe mucanyha ye misinji ye zi zahabu.

Yo munhu mutambule, yenhe matunda ga Lebanoni,

hamonga ne mibici yayo ye mihagata.

16 No mulomo wakwe, lono wukuwunonela, nye muswanu si!

Ne zinghani zakwe zose, zinyendezaga muno.

Holeka anye nyo wacekulu wa mu Yelusalemi,

nhavivi vyo yenha u mwendece wangu.


Lwimbo Luswanu muno 6

Zinghani za we cicekulu wenji

1 Gwe go mucekulu muswanu muli wacekulu,

alo mwendece wako kowa kabita hayi?

Wucilonjelaje ta, nzila yono yakolile yuyo mwendece wako,

nase ho cikutaze kumulondola.

Zinghani za we cicekulu

2 Mwendece wangu kabita ku busitani yakwe,

hono yicindice mibici ye manukato.

Yakulilisa litewa lyakwe mu busitani,

akuno yakuhepa ne maluwa.

3 Mwendece wangu, yo wangu, nane nili wakwe,

kalilisaga litewa lyakwe mu maluwa.

Lwimbo lwe Hano

Zinghani za we cilume

4 Gwe mwendece wangu,

mbeka wuli muswanu winha Yelusalemi,

wunojeze nhe likaya lya Tiliza,

wina lilumbo nhe makaya gaga mawaha.

5 Wupituse meso gako, wulece kunanga,

hambi nha wukunghwega muno.

Ne zimvwili zako swanu, zinha litewa lya mhene,

zono zigonile mu mwalambalo we litunda lya Gileyadi.

6 Ne meno gako mazelu swee,

kotya litewa lya ngholo zono zakondya kuhovugwa.

Kanyhi zono hasina hamba monga yono yajiliye.

Zikujenda zitunjile nyejete nyejete.

7 Ne zinghatu zako,

zikung'alala kusi ye citambala cono wigubice mu wusu wako.

8 Mulece mutemi yawe ne wagolece makumi mutandatu,

hamonga ne wajitulwa makumi munana,

ne wahinza si wakuwala!

9 Ninga ane, nyendile mucekulu monga du,

nayo mbeka yanojeze yenha nziwa.

Nayo yo mwana we cicekulu yumonga du kuli nyina,

kanyhi yo mwana mwendece muno kuli nyina.

Ne wacekulu wose kolo wakumulanga, wakumukumya,

nawo wagolece zo mutemi hamonga ne wajitulwa,

wose wakumwimbila zinyimbo zo lukumyo.

10 Alo yo nani yuyu mono yakulanga,

wulanje wakwe winha hakali mitondo.

Muswanu muno, kanyhi yena wusu wono wusangalaye,

yakung'alala nhe lizuwa hamba mwezi.

11 Nane nghabita mu busitani ye milozi,

ho nanje wusipuce wa mwi'lolo

ho mbone hono mizabibu yakondya kutula mahamha,

ne mikomamanga nayo hono yilavizye maluwa.

12 Alu'ne, nhondo nghucicima,

hambi nha wamhela cisukwa ciwaha muno mu nhumbula yangu,

so wendo, nha vyono kawezaga u mujendya lituka lyo wulugu,

haciligati yo wulugu.

Zinghani za we cicekulu wenji

13 Ale, wuvine! Wuvine 'gwe yaya,

go muhinza wa ku Shulamu.

Nase ho ciwe cikulanjiliye vyono wukuvina.

Zinghani za we cicekulu

Alu nye, nhawule mukusaka muwe munanjiliye,

lono'ne nghuvina, haciligati ye micilili

ya wono wananjiliye?


Lwimbo Luswanu muno 7

Zinghani za we cilume

1 Agwe mbeka, wuli muhinza wa mbuko!

Lanje, ta, migulu yako vyono miswanu,

lono wuvwalile malatu.

No wipalinje we vima vyako,

wihwanile vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu,

mulimo wo munhu mulula.

2 Ne ciduwo cako, cinha bakuli

yono si yanjizaga divayi, yono yina mhunjile swanu.

Ne'nda yako nayo, yihwanile ne lilundo lyo wuhemba

lyono lipilimwe ne maluwa.

3 Navyo vitombo vyako, vihwanile nzimba nyejete

ze ma mhasa, kanyhi nha sawada nyejete.

4 Nayo singo yako,

yihwanile nho munala we zimbali ze zinhembo.

Meso gako nago,

gehwanile vilamba vya mwi'kaya lya Heshiboni,

vyono vili behi no mulango we likaya lilyo.

Nayo mhula yako vyono yikwendeza!

Nayo yihwanile no munala wa Lebanoni,

wono vidomile na Damesiki.

5 Nawo mutwe wako wuli ha ka canyha nhe litunda lya Kalimeli.

No wusuce we zimvwili zako,

wukung'alala kotya mwenda wa halili.

No wuswanu wayo,

mbeka wosuma kumukwega sunga no mutemi.

6 Gwe, mwene wuli muswanu ta, na mbeka ku wulangwe,

wuli muswanu muno, kanyhi wukwendeza muno,

no wendo wako, wukusangalaza muno.

7 Wuswanu wako wihwanile no mubici wo mutende.

Ne vitombo vyako vimile kotya vivunda vya nhende.

8 Nane nobita kucanyha yo mutende

nghahepe mikalo yayo.

Ne vitombo vyako kuli'ne, vihwanile

nhe vivunda vya zabibu,

no mwehelo wako nawo, wina mhunjile swanu,

nhe mhunjile ye mikalo.

9 No wunonele wako, winha divayi swanu muno.

Zinghani za we cicekulu

Ale hono nhavivyo, wuyilekaje le divayi

yimuhulumile mwendece wangu,

yimuhulumile mhola mhola mu mulomo wakwe na mu meno gakwe.

10 Ane nili wakwe mwendece wangu,

nayo kanyilowaga muno.

11 Ale, wuze 'gwe mwendece wangu, cibite kwi'wimbi,

cikagone kuko mu vitumbi.

12 Nase, nhondo cilawe hakali mitondo muno muno,

cikayilanje mizabibu,

ciyiwone hono yiwe yawiluka,

na hono maluwa gawe gabonhola,

na hono mikomamanga nayo yiwe yalavya maluwa.

Kuko langa ko cokwikumbatilila.

13 Nagwe ho koyiwonela mhunjile swanu yo mubici we mhunjile,

ne mikalo yose yono yikwendeza,

yili behi no mulango wetu.

Gwe go mwendece wangu,

ane nghuwiciye mikalo mipya ne mihuma,

na ye mawala gose.


Lwimbo Luswanu muno 8

1 Nane nali nyende, mhali wali wuwe wuli lumbu lyangu,

mono wongha matombo ga yaya.

Nha cinji, kolo cikwitang'anila mu nzila,

ho sume kukunonela ko welecele,

hamba munhu yunji kucelelela yaye.

2 Nane nali nghusole nghutegule sunga ko ku kaya ya yaya,

kuko ko wali wunyijizize wendo vyono winha.

Nane nali nghung'wese divayi yono yikoleye,

divayi yo mukomamanga wangu.

3 No muwoko wako wo kucekulu wuwe hasi yo mutwe wangu,

no muwoko wako wo kulume wuwe wunghumbatiye.

4 Holeka anye nyo wacekulu wa mu Yelusalemi,

nha vyono zinha nzimba hamba sawada za mu mbago,

mulece kuwusagahaza hamba kuwusinhusa wendo,

sunga kolo wukuwona viswanu weneco.

Lwimbo lo Mutandatu

Zinghani za we cicekulu wenji

5 Alo yuyo nani mono yakuza yakulawilila ku nyika,

yakujenda weyibate miwoko no mwendece wakwe?

Zinghani za we cicekulu

Nha nakusinhusa he'sina lyo muyembe,

i hanhu hono walelelwa.

6 Wumbice nha mutozo mu nhumbula yako,

nha citanjilo mu muwoko wako.

Hambi wendo nha wina vilungo kotya lifwa,

no wivu nawo, wukali kotya citenjelo.

No wukolele wayo, winha wukolele wo moto,

wunyanyaga nho moto mukali.

7 Malenga menji, si gosuma kuwuzimya wendo,

hamba makolongo, si gosuma kuwudodomeza yaye.

Holeka, hamba munhu yatye yalavye sawo yakwe yose

yawugule wendo, mbeka, kokwipela wubedegalwe bwete.

Zinghani za we cilume kuli lumbu lyawo

8 Ase cina lumbu lyetu mono yakali mudodo,

yakali hamba si nota ne vitombo yaye.

Alu, cimunozezeci yuyo u lumbu lyetu,

hono muzelelo yaze yasace kumupogoza?

9 Mwene yali yawe lyo yijinji,

cali cimuzenjele, cimupilimye no munala wa fweza.

Na hono yali yawe wo mulango,

cali cimucitile ne zimbawo zo muhagata.

Zinghani za we cicekulu

10 Ane nha nili 'jinji ne vitombo vyangu, yo minala yalyo.

No mwendece wangu,

yamanyile manyi manyi nha vyono,

hono nili hamonga nayo,

nghutuga wucehe hamonga no mulamu.

Zinghani za we cilume

11 Solomona yali na mugunda wakwe we mizabibu kuko ku Baali-hamoni.

Hakawa ne walimi,

wono wakawazima mugunda wuwo kulawa kuli mwene,

ho wawe kulavya munhu fweza elufu monga so mikalo yayo.

12 Mugunda we mizabibu, wo mugunda wangu nene.

Agwe Solomona, wusole fweza elufu monga,

nawo walimi, wasole magana mejete lye 'gawo lyawo.

13 Leka ta, nilihulicize lizi lyako go mwendece wangu ku wulawa mu busitani,

nawo wayangu wakuhila walihulice lizi lyako lono wukulonga.

Zinghani za we cicekulu

14 Gwe, wuze si, go mwendece wangu,

mono wihwanile nzimba hamba sawada,

zono zili mu matunda kono mibici

ye mhunjile swanu yilimwaga.
