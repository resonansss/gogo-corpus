Lwimbo Luswanu muno 7

Zinghani za we cilume

1 Agwe mbeka, wuli muhinza wa mbuko!

Lanje, ta, migulu yako vyono miswanu,

lono wuvwalile malatu.

No wipalinje we vima vyako,

wihwanile vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu,

mulimo wo munhu mulula.

2 Ne ciduwo cako, cinha bakuli

yono si yanjizaga divayi, yono yina mhunjile swanu.

Ne'nda yako nayo, yihwanile ne lilundo lyo wuhemba

lyono lipilimwe ne maluwa.

3 Navyo vitombo vyako, vihwanile nzimba nyejete

ze ma mhasa, kanyhi nha sawada nyejete.

4 Nayo singo yako,

yihwanile nho munala we zimbali ze zinhembo.

Meso gako nago,

gehwanile vilamba vya mwi'kaya lya Heshiboni,

vyono vili behi no mulango we likaya lilyo.

Nayo mhula yako vyono yikwendeza!

Nayo yihwanile no munala wa Lebanoni,

wono vidomile na Damesiki.

5 Nawo mutwe wako wuli ha ka canyha nhe litunda lya Kalimeli.

No wusuce we zimvwili zako,

wukung'alala kotya mwenda wa halili.

No wuswanu wayo,

mbeka wosuma kumukwega sunga no mutemi.

6 Gwe, mwene wuli muswanu ta, na mbeka ku wulangwe,

wuli muswanu muno, kanyhi wukwendeza muno,

no wendo wako, wukusangalaza muno.

7 Wuswanu wako wihwanile no mubici wo mutende.

Ne vitombo vyako vimile kotya vivunda vya nhende.

8 Nane nobita kucanyha yo mutende

nghahepe mikalo yayo.

Ne vitombo vyako kuli'ne, vihwanile

nhe vivunda vya zabibu,

no mwehelo wako nawo, wina mhunjile swanu,

nhe mhunjile ye mikalo.

9 No wunonele wako, winha divayi swanu muno.

Zinghani za we cicekulu

Ale hono nhavivyo, wuyilekaje le divayi

yimuhulumile mwendece wangu,

yimuhulumile mhola mhola mu mulomo wakwe na mu meno gakwe.

10 Ane nili wakwe mwendece wangu,

nayo kanyilowaga muno.

11 Ale, wuze 'gwe mwendece wangu, cibite kwi'wimbi,

cikagone kuko mu vitumbi.

12 Nase, nhondo cilawe hakali mitondo muno muno,

cikayilanje mizabibu,

ciyiwone hono yiwe yawiluka,

na hono maluwa gawe gabonhola,

na hono mikomamanga nayo yiwe yalavya maluwa.

Kuko langa ko cokwikumbatilila.

13 Nagwe ho koyiwonela mhunjile swanu yo mubici we mhunjile,

ne mikalo yose yono yikwendeza,

yili behi no mulango wetu.

Gwe go mwendece wangu,

ane nghuwiciye mikalo mipya ne mihuma,

na ye mawala gose.
