Jobu 24

1 “Alu socici Mulungu yalihagula lipango lyo wuling'anizi,

lizuwa lyo wuling'anizwe, kuli wose wono wakumwitumamila?”

2 I Wanhu wendile kuteza mabwe ge mipaka,

ho weyonjelezeze yisi,

wakuhiza zingholo, wawa kuzijihila mu matewa gawo.

3 Wakusola zindogowe ze wana wono wasina walezi wawo,

no kusola nghongolo yo mucekulu mufwile, no kuyidindila,

sunga yaze yalipe mugawa wono yena nawo.

4 Wakuwemila waciwa, ho walece kutuga ling'ani yawo,

no kuwawinjiliza wono wena wanjile, wamanyhe wehundice.

5 Nawo waciwa, wawa wehwanile ndogowe za mu mbago,

wesacizaga ndigwa mu nyika.

Ninga hasina hanji hono wosuma kutuga ndigwa,

ku soko ye wana wawo.

6 Hanyuma wawa kududula mu migunda yono si yawo,

no kuhalaliza zabibu mu migunda ye wanhu wabi.

7 Na mu cilo, wagonaga wali cipwi wasina myenda,

wasina cinhu co kwifunika mu mbeho.

8 Wakutonyelwa ne mvula ya mu matunda,

wawa kwihundika mu mabwe ho wecite,

walece kutonyelwa ne mvula.

9 I wanhu wabi wakuwa kusola i wana wono wasina walezi,

wawa kuwanoza wawanda wawo,

wakuwasola ne wana wo munhu muciwa,

wakuwetumya milimo yawo, ninga hamba si

kuwalipa musahala wawo hamba kamonga yaye.

10 Na ku soko yiyo, wanhu waciwa wojenda

wasina myenda yo kuvwala,

nawo wakuwa wali nzala du na

mu mapango lono wakududula wuhemba.

11 Wakobongaga mizeyituni ho watuje mafuta,

kanyhi ne zi zabibu ho watuje divayi

ninga wene wakuwa vyo wali ne nyhilu.

12 Na mu makaya mukuwa mo wali wono wena nghomele

hamonga na wono wakulila wakufwa,

ninga Mulungu hamba si kawutondowozaga wulombe wawo.

13 “Ho wali wanhu wanji wono si wawendile

wuzelu hamba hadodo,

kono wukumulika.

14 Nayo munhu muwulaji,

yendile kwilawa hakali mitondo,

kawa kubita kumuwulaga muciwa,

na mu cilo kahizaga.

15 Nayo musenyha, kalindilaga sunga cinyamihe,

kolo wutitu-titu wenjila.

Kegubicizaga wusu wakwe,

ho yunji yalece kumumanya.

16 Nawo wahizi, wabenaga mu zinyumba ne cilo,

ninga kolo wela, wehundikaga wakogopa wuzelu.

17 Wawogopaga wuzelu ku misi,

ninga cilo si wacogopaga yaye.

18 “Ninga munhu mubi, kasolelezwaga ne lihwazi lye malenga,

no mugunda wakwe, wuwa wuli mu wulijitwe wo Mulungu,

nayo hamba si kosuma kanyhi kuwulima zabibu mugunda wuwo.

19 Nha vyono seluji yenjedukaga mwi'zuwa,

na mwi'pango lye cibahu yiwa kwajilila,

ale, nhavivyo no munhu mubi vyo kajililaga

mu yisi ye womi.

20 Na mwi'daha lilyo, hamba nyina si komukumbucila yaye.

Hambi nha kowa yaligwa ne mang'onyo,

yakondya kuhodanjika nho mubici wono wugwiye.

21 “Na zizo zomwijelela vyono kawatendelaga vibi

i wacekulu wafwile.

Hamba kuwawonela lusungu i wacekulu wagumba.

22 Ninga Mulungu nayo mu vilungo vyakwe,

kawongomezaga we vilungo.

Hambi Mulungu nha yo ketumaga,

no munhu mubi kawa kufwa.

23 Mulungu kamupelaga yekale yapolile,

ninga meso gakwe, galangaga zinzila zakwe.

24 Hambi u munhu mubi, nha kamotaga cidaha cidodo du,

ninga hanyuma yakusakala nhe mahanze,

yakuwa nhe liso lyo wuhemba lyono lyadudulwa.

25 Wule, ho yuli munhu mono kozitya zizi, si za mbeka?

Hamba ho yuli mono kosuma kutya vyono,

nghani zangu si za mbeka?
