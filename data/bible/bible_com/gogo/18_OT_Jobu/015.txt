Jobu 15

Zinghani ze kejete za Elifazi

(15:1–21:34)

1 “Elifazi Mutemani yakamupitula Jobu yakamutya,

2 Gwe Jobu, hambi u munhu we mahala kalongaga nghani zo wulele,

Wule, u munhu yuyo, kowa yamemile nghani za bwete du?

3 Hambi u munhu we mahala, kosuma kwitetela mu wulonga nghani zono

si zinojeye, hamba ne zinghani zono zisina cambuzi yaye.

4 Kanyhi yikwijela nha vyono, agwe hamba si wukuwucelelela u wogopo

Mulungu, kanyhi wukuwimila u wihwesa zo Mulungu.

5 Ku soko wubi wako, wukwijelela mu wulonje wako,

nagwe wukwihinda kuwugubiciza no wulonje wako wo wusugu.

6 Ninga ane Elifazi, sina wuhile wo kukuling'aniza,

hambi nha kuling'anizizwa mu zinghani zako zose

zono wuzilonjile go mweneco.

7 Alu wukwihwesa nha vyono yo gwegwe munhu

mulongozi kulelwa?

Hamba wali wakondya kulelwa lono Mulungu

yagawumbila matunda?

8 Wule! Walihulika ne lihala lye cinyele lyo Mulungu?

Hamba mahala go munhu gali muli'gwe du wuli wene?

9 Hamba ho zili zinji zo zose zono agwe wuzimanyile,

zono ase zicanjiye.

10 Ase co wayago, ceyijiza mahala ku wulawa

kuli wanhu wono wacindice zimvwi,

i wanhu wono walelwa, na lono sogo yali yakali nalelwa.

11 Mulungu yakukupela wusindamice, alu nhawule

agwe si wukuwubocela?

Ne nghani yono cikulongana nagwe ne mazi mapolo

hongo si cinji kuli'gwe.

12 Ninga agwe wukukomala no kucinugulila kwe 'kuwo.

13 Ninga wumanye, vyono wukumukolelwa likuwo Mulungu,

kanyhi no kumuhosa.

14 Alo munhu mbeka kosuma kuwa yelile hongo?

Hamba kuwa ling'ani hali Mulungu?

15 Alu nhawule Mulungu hamba si kawetumbilaga

sunga ne wamalayika wakwe,

vyono nawo nhavivyo, si welile hali mwene.

16 No munhu kang'wezaga wubi nhe malenga

na mbeka munhu iwifyo, kanyhi musocele.

17 “Holeka alu Jobu, wuhulicizaje ta zono nghukulonjela.

18 Iwanhu we mahala, wanyijiza no muyago ze zi mbeka

zono wene nawo, weyijiza kuli walezi wawo

nawo si wazihundika yaye hamba monga.

19 I wajenzi wose wakawingwa wawuce mu yisi yawo.

Hakawa hasina yunji kanyhi mono yali yawapatuse

no kuwawika kutali no Mulungu.

20 No munhu mubi mono yawewonelaga wanji,

yuyo kowa mu wusungu mu mikalile yakwe yose.

21 Ne lizozo lyo woga, lyohulikwa mu makutu gakwe,

ne wahizi womuziza mwi'pango lyono mwene

yakwiwona yapolile.

22 Kanyhi kowa yasina witumbilo vyono kowuya

ku wulawa mu wutitu.

Hambi nawo mumaje, nha wowa wumulindiye

ho wumuwulaje,

23 ne zindi nazo, zowa zilindiye

ho ziwulye muvimba wakwe.

Nayo yamanyile du, vyono citi cimulindiye.

24 Nawo wusungu, wono vihwanile no mutemi we vilungo,

wowa wumulindiye ho wumuhawanje.

25 Yiyo yo nghani yono yimulindiye munhu,

mono yakumukunjila funzi Mulungu

no kumwavufya Mono yali vilungo.

26 No munhu we liwala lilyo, yo mwiwoni kanyhi mufyuzi.

27 Kanyhi yenuye ngula yakwe kucanyha

ko wuwula cihuliko,

yakumangha vikehome no Mulungu.

28 Yuyo yo munhu mono kagatekaga makaya

no kwibata zikaya zono wanya zikaya wakondya kutiza.

Ninga wulugu, wogongomeza makaya gago

hamonga ne zikaya zayo.

29 Nayo hamba si kojendelela no wugoli wakwe cibitilila,

hasina hamba cinji cono yena naco,

cono colokwikala cibitilila.

Sunga no mwenzenze wakwe mweneco wolokwajilila.

30 Nayo hamba si kolokwilega ne citi,

kolokwihwana no mubici,

wono manhambi gayo ganyanywa no moto,

ne mahamha gayo, gawa kupemheluswa ne mbeho.

31 Na hono yawe mulele, yawe yetumbiye zimbi,

ale, i zimbi zizo, zo colowa co cipegwa cakwe.

32 Na lono cipango cakwe cowa cikali nafika,

ninga mwene kowa yakondya kunyala,

kowa yanyala nhe linhambi, hamba si kotula kanyhi.

33 Kolokwihwana nho mubici wo muzabibu, wono

wakondya kuwuswa zabibu zayo,

kanyhi nho muzeyituni, wono wukukung'unhika mahamha gayo.

34 Ne wanhu wose wono si wamogopile Mulungu,

wawo hamba si wotuga wana yaye

ne zikaya zawo zono wazizenjela mu nzila ye

mafupo, zose zolokongogwa no moto.

35 Wawo wo wanhu wono wekalaga wakuleta magaziko,

no kwituma zimbi.

Ne zinhumbula zawo zikalaga zimemile nghani zo wungwele.”
