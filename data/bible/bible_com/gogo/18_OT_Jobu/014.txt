Jobu 14

1 “Na wose cileligwe cili wasocele,

kanyhi cisina wutazi,

wose cikalaga vilo vicefu du,

kanyhi cimemile wugaye mu mikalile yetu.

2 Cikuwa kuwiluka no kunyala mbela mbela

nhe maluwa, cikupuluta nho mwenzenze.

3 Gwe go Mulungu, alu hongo kosuma kunanga,

wusitace, no kuning'aniza?

4 Hambi nha hasina ciswanu co cose

cono cosuma kulawa muli cono si ciswanu,

nha vyono munhu yenha.

5 Hambi wuwale we vilo vyakwe, nha watemezezwa lukulu,

ne myezi yo wikale wakwe.

Agwe wakondya kuviwaga, hamba si vyopitulwa yaye.

6 Wupituce, wulece kumulanga, ho yacehe,

mulekaje yayisangalalile mikalile yakwe ye magayo.

7 U mubici wono wadumulwa,

wukuwa wina witumbilo vyono wotula,

no wumi wayo wuwuwuyile kanyhi.

8 Hamba milela yayo yikale mu muhangha vilo vinji,

ne lisina lyayo liwe lyanyala,

9 ninga kolo likumwajilwa malenga,

lyotula du no kutuga mahamha.

10 Ninga munhu kolo yakondya kufwa,

hodu, yiyo yikuwa yo ndumilizo yakwe.

Na kolo yafwa, yakuwa yali kwihi kwene?

11 “Na vyono malenga ga mu makolongo galekaga kuhuluma,

ne malamba ganyalaga,

12 Ale, nhavivyo ne wanhu vyo wafwizaga

hamba kwinuka kanyhi.

Nawo hamba si wolokwinucila lukulu,

sunga wulanga wuwe hasina.

Hamba si wosuma kusinhuka yaye mu zinhongo zawo.

13 “Nane nali nende wuhundice mu yisi ye wafu,

nihundice kuko, sunga likuwo lyako liselete,

hanyuma wunhemezezye cipango co kunghumbucila.

14 Alu mwene hono munhu yawe yakondya kufwa,

ho kowumipa kanyhi?

Ninga ane nolindila sunga kono cokuziza

cipango cono cinojeye,

nolindila sunga cipango cici ce magayo cipulute.

15 Hanyuma go Mulungu ho konyhema, nane nokwidika

nagwe ho kowa watuga wendezwe nane,

ane no ciwumbwa cako.

16 Hanyuma nagwe ho kosuma kuyilanga mikalile yangu,

hamba si kosuma kanyhi kuning'aniza

ku soko yo wubi wangu.

17 Kowusemwa wubi wangu, no kuwutaga kutali,

kanyhi, konghwemela lukulu ne masutya gangu

gose gono gapulute.

18 I cipango cikuza cono matunda gologwa,

ne mabwe mawaha golovulumuka.

19 I malenga gologasakaza mabwe,

nayo mvula mbaha yolowuhulumya muhangha we'si,

nagwe go Mulungu, kolowuhodanga

witumbilo wo wumi wo munhu.

20 Wukumusumila lukulu munhu, no kumuwinjila lukulu,

wukumulanzya wusu wakwe kwi 'fwa.

21 Ne wanagwe wawa wena lilumbo, ninga mwene sikomanya,

na kolo wakuwusizwa lilumbo, mwene sikomanya yaye.

22 Baho mwene, kowa yena wusungu du wo muwili wakwe,

no wukalale wa mu nhumbula yakwe.”
