Jobu 30

1 “Ninga lu, wono wali wadodo wangu, wakusekanga,

wono wasogo zawo nawabedegalaga, nghawa kuwawona

wehwanile ne mabwa gono gadimaga litewa lyangu.

2 Wali wenha nhe wanhu wono wakondya kusakala,

wasocele muno, wono si wadahile kunhaza mulimo wo wose,

3 Wali waciwa kanyhi wali nzala, wono wagugudaga milela

yono yinyalile ku cilo, mu mbago, hanhu ha ciwene.

4 Wakulaga zimboga za mu nyika wawa kulya.

Sunga ne milela yo muletemu, yono yisina ndaso.

5 Ne wanhu wose wawazozezaga wawa kuwawinjiliza,

nha vyono wanhu wawacenjelaga wahizi.

6 Wekalaga mu mhango, mu mhalika za mu mabwe mawaha.

7 Walilaga nhe malimu mu mbago,

wekongholelaga hamonga mu vititika.

8 Wo wana we walele, heya wo wana we

wanhu wono wasina litagwa,

wono wawingwa wulawa mu'si.

9 “Alu, wakuza wakusekanga, naweza yidawalilo lyawo.

10 Wakunhendela kwe'kuwo,

wene wakuwona nha wakunhendela viswanu,

wakuza wakufunyila mate mu wusu wangu.

11 Vyono Mulungu yakondya kumhela wusocele, sina wutazi,

holeka wakunyiziza ko wukali.

12 Wanhu awa, wakuhoma cidomile nawo,

kanyhi wakunghwenghweleza,

no kwinozeleza, ho wahungucile.

13 Wayihodanga nzila yangu, wakusaka wanyongomeze

na hasina munhu mono kosuma kuwemila.

14 Wakunyiziza nha wanhu wono wakukola mwi'dilisha ligazi,

wakuhungucila 'ne ho wanyongomeze.

15 Nane luno woga wuwaha wakondya kungwila,

no lukumyo lwangu lwapuluta, nha lupemheluswe ne mbeho.

No wumoteswe wangu wawembuka nhe mavunde.

16 “Alu nhondo neza kufwa, hambi wusungu wangu nha

si wukucepuka.

17 Mafupa gangu gose, nha gakutama muno ku cilo,

no wusungu wono wukubwanghula mu mafupa,

hamba si wukucepuka yaye.

18 Mulungu yanyonyile mu singo, ne kola yo musweko wangu.

Yakuyivuwanjiza ne myenda yangu, yiwuka na ku wuswanu.

19 Yanhaga kunze mu nhope, neza ninhe manghundi.

20 Gwe go Mulungu, ane nghukucemeleza, ninga hamba

si wukumhitula,

na lono nghukulomba, hamba si wukuhuliciza yaye.

21 Wukunhendela ko wukali, kanyhi wukuminjiliza

kwe vilungo vyako vyose.

22 Wukundeka, mbeho yimhemhelusize kutali,

wukunyinula no kumbika mucanyha yayo,

kuwa kungalagaza mwi'hwazi lye libeho.

23 Nane manyile nha vyono weza kundeta kwi'fwa lyangu.

I hanhu hono watemezezwa wanhu wose i womi.

24 “Alu socici wukumutowa munhu mono yakondya kuciwapa,

u munhu mono yasina co kunoza, langa kulomba lusungu du?

25 Gwe go Mulungu, wule, ane si nali kulilana na wono

wali mu wugaye,

no kuwakalalila wono wali mu wanjile?

26 Ane nalanjililaga nyemo no wuzelu, ninga nghazizwa

ne magayo no wutitu.

27 Mahwele no wusungu vyanhamisa, hambi nha nili mu

magazo vilo vyose.

28 Alu nawa njendelaga mu wutitu,

musina wuzelu we lizuwa.

Nghwima calinze mu mapuga ge wanhu,

nghulondola wutazi.

29 Ne lizi lyangu lyeza lya wukalale, kanyhi lya cinyhiwa

nhe cililo ce macewe ne nyacihema.

30 Ne nghuli yangu yatukuwala tuku, ku soko

ye mhungo yono nghutamwa

31 I katali nahulikaga mazi ge masumbi,

ninga aluno, nghuhulika mazi ga wono wakulila cililo.
