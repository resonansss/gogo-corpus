Jobu 18

Wulonje we kejete wa Bilidadi

1 “Bilidadi Mushuhi, yakamupitula Jobu yakamutya,

2 Alu mwene Jobu, nhawule wuli zeze,

hamba si wukwenda no kunyamala?

Ta wali wunyamale, nase cilongane nagwe.

3 Alu mwene cono coleka agwe

wuciganicize ase nha cili walele

kotya ng'ombe, cicici?

4 Hambi nha weza kwitamisa wuli wene so likuwo lyako,

wukwihwesa yisi yolekwa yili wene so likuwo lyako?

Hamba wukwihwesa vyono Mulungu kogahamya matunda

ho yakwendeze gwe?

5 “Heya, limuli lye wanhu wabi, lyozimywa,

no wuzelu walyo, hamba si wong'alala kanyhi yaye.

6 Ne tala yono yili mwi'hema lyayo, nayo yotukuwikwa.

7 Ne zinghanzo zakwe ze vilungo, zohogoha,

ne lihala lyakwe mweneco, lyomugwisa.

8 Yakujendela mu lwavu,

ne migulu yakwe yikwibatwa.

9 Kokwibatwa mu cising'anilo no mutego.

10 No mutego, muhundikwe hasi wumutejile,

mutego mutegwe mu wukolo wakwe.

11 “No wogofye wumupilime mbavu zose.

Nawo wumutyatizye kose kono yakubita.

12 Yali mugoli muno, ninga luno yali ne nzala,

magayo gamulindiye ha mhelo yakwe.

13 “Ne litamwa libi lyakondya kutandala mu

muwili wakwe wose,

lyakondya kumuhodanga miwoko ne migulu yakwe.

14 Kakondya kuwuswa na mwi'hema lyono

yekalaga yapolile.

Nalu yakukomhoswa mwi'hema lyono yetumbiye,

yategulwe vikedome no Mutemi we nghakamo,

mono yakucemwa Mutemi ‘Lifwa.’

15 Naluno wose wokwinjila mw'ihema lyakwe,

ne cibiliti cokwiyagazwa mwi'hema lyakwe.

16 Milela yakwe hamonga ne manhambi gayo, vyonyala.

17 Ne lilumbo lyakwe, nalyo lyowuka mu kaya yakwe,

sunga na mu yinze yose.

Hamba howa hasina yunji kanyhi mono kolomukumbucila.

18 Nayo kolowusizwa lukulu mu yisi ye womi,

kolowuswa mu wuzelu, yenjizwe mu wutitu.

19 Nayo si kowa na wana hamba wezukulu yaye.

20 Ne wanhu wose, ku wulawa ku lubavu lwa kumwezi,

sunga ku lubavu lwa kucilima,

wono wozihulika zizo,

wokwizina muno no kutinuswa.

21 Ale, zizo zo zowatuga i wanhu wabi.

Naho baho ho hanhu he wanhu wono si wamumanyile Mulungu.”
