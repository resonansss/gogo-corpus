Jobu 33

1 “Alu Jobu, nghukunena, wuhulicizaje viswanu,

muli zose zono nghusaka nghulonjele.

2 Ane lu nghusaka nonje zono zili mu nhumbula yangu.

3 Ne zinghani zangu zose nozilonga na nhumbula nzelu,

nane nghulonga nghani za mbeka.

4 U muhe wo Mulungu wo wamumba no kumhela wumi.

5 Alu wumhitulaje hono kosuma, hono wina

zo kulongana nane.

6 Hambi ase wose nha cili wamonga du ha

mwande yo Mulungu,

hambi ase wose nha cawumbilwa mwi'longo.

7 Holeka wulece kunyogopa, ane si nokunoza cinji yaye.

8 Nane mu wulonje wako, nghuhulika vyono wali kulonga,

9 wukutya, ‘Ane nili ling'ani, sina wusutye,

si nahodanga cinji, hamba wubi muli 'ne musina.

10 Langa Mulungu yo kawonaga zi soko zo kuhosa'ne

yakunhendela nha nili mulugu wakwe.

11 Kagawopaga magulu gangu ne manyolo,

kazigozaga zinzila zangu zose.’

12 “Ninga'ne nghukulonjela Jobu vyono, agwe wuli musutyi.

Soko Mulungu muwaha, yacizize munhu wo wose.

13 Holeka lu, socici 'gwe wukwisumaniza nayo?

Mwene nha si kapitulaga hamba hadodo,

mang'ung'uliko go munhu.

14 Hamba yiwe Mulungu kalongaga kenji na kenji

ne wanhu wakwe,

ninga hasina hamba monga,

mono yakumuhulika zono yakulonga.

15 Na mu cilo lono wanhu wagona nhongo,

mwene kalongaga nawo mu nzila ye zindoto no wugubulilwe.

16 Kawanozaga ho wazihulice zono yakulonga,

ninga wene wogofywaga ne zinghani zakwe.

17 Mulungu kalongaga nawo, ho yawawusize wanhu wuhile wawo

wubi, no kuwawusa mu wusisi wawo.

18 Si kowaleceleza wongomezwe, langa kawaponyaga mwi'fwa.

19 Mulungu kamufundilaga munhu mu nzila ye matamwa,

no wisumanize wo wusungu wa mu muwili wakwe.

20 Nayo munhu mutamwa, nzala yakwe yifwizaga,

no mutima wakwe si wusakaga ndigwa,

hamba yiwe swanu muno.

21 No muwili wakwe wukuganda, kosuma

kuwona ne mafupa gakwe,

22 nayo kawa yali behi no kubita ku yisi ye wafu.

23 Na yumonga wa muli wamalayika maelufu wo Mulungu,

lunji kokuza yamutaze, mono kawakumbucizaga wanhu

zono ziwanojeye wetume.

24 Nayo malayika kolonga ko lusungu yatye,

‘Mumulekaje ayu!

Hambi nha si yimunojeye mwene yabite ku yisi ye wafu.

Awu wo wukombozi wo kumukomboza.’

25 No muwili wakwe wowuya ku wudodo, no

kuwa ne vilungo kanyhi.

26 Nayo nhondo kolo yakumulomba Mulungu,

nayo Mulungu komupitula, nayo komuhemba Mulungu na nyemo.

Mulungu nayo kozinoza zakwe zose ziwe mugoloka.

27 Nayo ho kolonjela mwi'puga lye wanhu yawe kutya,

‘Ane nili mubi, ninga Mulungu kambonela lusungu du,

hambe ane si netuma ze ling'ani yaye.

28 Yakondya kunghomboza, ho ndece kubita ku yisi ye wafu,

nalu nghali nili mumi.’

29 “Mulungu ketumaga zizi vilo vyose,

30 kaponyaga wumi wo munhu,

no kumupela ne nyemo mu mikalile yakwe.

31 Holeka Jobu, alu wuhulicizaje zono nghulonga,

alu wituzaje wuhulicize.

32 Ninga hono wuwe wina yinji yo kulonga,

ale lonje, nane nokuhuliciza, ku soko nyendile

kukuwala wuli ling'ani.

33 Na hono wuwe wusina yo kulonga,

ale wunyamale twii wuhulicize nane nokwijiza ze Mahala.”
