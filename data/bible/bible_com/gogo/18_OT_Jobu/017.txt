Jobu 17

1 “No mudumo we vilo vyangu, nha wasejelela,

no wehele wangu wacepuka,

alu hasina cinji kanyhi cono cisigaliye,

langa kwinjila mu citenjelo du.

2 Nane nghuwalanga ko wusungu wuwaha muno,

wose wono wakusekanga.

3 Gwe go Mulungu, ane nili mwinenenyhezi.

Holeka wubocele zinghani zangu.

Hambi nha hasina kanyhi yunji

mono yadahile kunhaza muli zono nghulonga.

4 Hambi nha kuwahundika zinhumbula zawo,

ho walece kwizuka,

holeka wulece kuwaleka wasume'ne.

5 Cikuwona mu zimbazi ze katali, nha vyono

munhu yakuwagaluka wendece wakwe

ku soko ya pesa,

ne wanagwe wawa wali mu wugaye.

6 “Naluno nghuwona vyono, wanhu

wakwitumamila mbazi yiyi kuli'ne,

wakuza, wakufunyila mate mu wusu wangu.

7 No wusungu wangu nhondo wambofuza,

ne miwoko yangu, hamonga ne magulu gangu,

vyasisilika vyawa nha muzizi.

8 Ne wanhu wo mugoloka, wakwizina so nghani yiyi

hanyuma wakuhosa, nha kotya ane nane nihwanile ne wanhu

wono so wamogopile Mulungu.

9 Ninga we ling'ani, wocila kuwa we ling'ani.

10 Hamba waze wose, weme ha mwande yangu,

ninga si nosuma kumutuga hamba monga we mahala.

11 “Vilo vyangu vyakondya kupuluta, no wuhile wangu

wakondya kudumanjila, no witumbilo wawuka.

12 Ninga wendece wangu, watezaga cilo yo misi,

wakutya vyono wusiku wuli behi kwela,

ane ninga manyile vyono nghali nili mu wutitu.

13 No witumbilo wangu, wuli mu yisi du ye wafu,

i hanhu hono nogona mu wutitu,

14 Nane nocicema citenjelo yo baba,

ne mang'onyo gono gowa kunyilya,

nogacema yo yaya ne walumbu zangu.

15 Ale 'lu, witumbilo wangu wanda wuli hayi?

No witumbilo wangu, nani mono kowuwona?

16 Nane hamba si cobitana hamonga no witumbilo,

kolo neza kubita kuko ku yisi ye wafu.”
