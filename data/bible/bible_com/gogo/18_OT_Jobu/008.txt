Jobu 8

1 Nayo Bilidadi Mushubi yakamupitula Jobu yakatya,

2 “Alu mwene, zinghani zako zizo kozilonga sunga nili?

Zojendelela sunga nili kuwa nha mbeho mbaha?

3 Mulungu si kahodangaga wiling'ano,

hamba si yakuwa kulemwa kwituma ziswanu.

4 Ne wanago nawo, kowa wamusutizya Mulungu,

holeka nayo kawa kuwalipiciza.

5 Ninga aluno wunene kuli Mulungu mono yali vilungo.

6 Hono wuwe mbeka welile, kanyhi wuli ling'ani,

nayo Mulungu kokuza yakupele wutazi,

no kuyipela nyumba yako wumoteswe.

7 Ne sawo yako yose yono yakondya kwajilila,

yowa si cinji yaye,

hambi Mulungu nha kokupela vinhu vinji muno

kuciza na vyono wali wakondya kuvyajiliza.

8 Alu, wukumbucile mahala ga we zindelo ze katali.

Wihwese ne zimbeka zono walezi wetu wazilondolaga.

9 Kanyhi wumanye vyono mikalile yetu, ya vilo vicefu du

hamba si cimanyile cinji co cose,

cipulutaga mono'nze nho muzizi.

10 Holeka wulece wawaha we katali wakwijize

nagwe wuwahulicize azi zono wakuzilonga.

11 ‘Alu wumanye vyono, madete-lulenga,

sigotaga hono hasina malenga,

hamba hono hasina nhope.’

12 Na hono malenga ganyale,

lono gene gakali gadedewaye.

Hodu gakuwomba mbela,

si nhe mahanze ganji.

13 Nhavivyo vyo wenha i wanhu wose

wono wakumusemwa Mulungu

no witumbilo wawo wukuwa wa bwete,

vyono wakondya kumusemwa Mulungu.

14 Hambi nha wetumbiye mhuti ze nhandabuli.

15 Na hono wawe wetumbiye mhuti ze nhandabuli

wule, mbeka zosuma kuwebatiliza?

Na hono wawe wetumbiye luputi,

alu mbeka losuma kuwasikamya?

16 I wanhu wabi, wakuwa kutula nhe maselegwa mwi'zuwa,

wenha maselegwa gono gamemaga mu mugunda wose.

17 Ne milela yago, yigazingaga mabwe

yiwa kwibata zii mu mabwe gose.

18 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, kolo wagakula,

hodu, hakuwa hasina mono komanya

vyono gali gacindice mumo.

Wanji nawo wawa kuhadika mumo vyono wakusaka weneco.

19 Ale ne nyemo ye wanhu wabi nayo,

nhavivyo vyo yinha.

20 “Ninga Mulungu hamba si komuleka yaye

u munhu wakwe mwihuwilwa.

Hamba si kowapela wutazi i wanhu wabi.

21 Nayo kokumemya luseko hamonga ne lizi lye nyemo.

22 Kanyhi kowaletela cevu ciwaha muno

wose wono wakusulile.

Ne zikaya ze wanhu wabi,

si zokwijela kanyhi.”
