Jobu 29

1 Jobu yakanza kulonga kanyhi yakatya,

2 “Woyi he! Nanda niwe nha mu myezi ye katali,

yiwe nha mu vilo vyono Mulungu yangolangolaga.

3 Lono Mulungu yali yali hamonga nane vilo vyose

yakamhela no wuzelu wakwe wumulicile mu wutitu,

4 Na mu vilo vivyo, vyali vyo vilo vyono nali motile,

no wendezwe wo Mulungu, wayidimaga kaya yangu.

5 Lulo Mulungu mono yali vilungo,

yali yali hamonga nane,

nane nali mhilimwe ne wanangu wose.

6 Zing'ombe zangu hamonga ne zimhene,

zamhelaga mele menji muno,

ne mibici yangu ye mizeyituni, yali yicindice

mu muhangha wono wina mabwe mabwe.

7 Na lono wawaha we likaya wehangulizaga,

nane nehangulizaga hamonga nawo.

8 Nawo i wazelelo lono wambonaga,

wehundikaga ha meso gangu.

Nawo wakombi wanyinucilaga kolo wakumbona,

wakulajila cogopo cawo kuli'ne.

9 Ne walongola we wanhu,

wanyamalaga kulonga kolo wambona.

10 Sunga ne wanhu wose watambule, wanyamalaga twii.

11 Ne wanhu wose wono wali wamanyile,

hamba wono wali wahulice zangu.

Wali wena nghani nyinji swanu zo kulonga,

za muli zono nene nitumile.

12 Hambi i waciwa lono wali wali mu wugaye,

nha nawatazaga,

Nawatazaga sunga ne wana wono wasina walezi wawo,

hamba ku wubito.

13 Ne wanhu wono wali wali mu wusungu wukali mu nhumbula

zawo, nawo wandugutiza.

Nghawapela wutazi wacekulu wafwile, mu wanjile wawo.

14 Netumaga ziswanu na ze ling'ani vilo vyose.

15 Nali nha meso kuli wabofu, ne magulu kuli we citewe.

16 Kuli waciwa nghawa nha nili sogo yawo

ne wajenzi nawo, nghawataza mu zinghani zawo.

17 Navibenanga vilungo vya wono si wali ling'ani,

nghawaponya na wono wagazwaga nawo.

18 “Nane nhondo zose,

nghawa nina witumbilo wo kwikala vilo vinji,

no kufwila mu kaya yangu mholile.

19 Nali ninhwanile mubici,

wono wikalaga wina malenga mu milela yayo.

Ne manhambi gayo madece so buhe.

20 Ne wanhu wose wanghumyaga,

ne vilungo vyangu hamba si vyacepuka yaye.

21 Na lono nawalonjelaga wanhu zinghani zangu,

wose wawezaga wahulicize muno,

kuli zono nghuwalonjela,

22 Nawo wawezaga wasina hamba nghani yinji,

kanyhi yo konjeleza zinghani zangu,

zawenjilaga muno mu zinhumbula zawo,

kotya mabanhi ga mvula.

23 Na wose wambocelaga nha vyono mulimi

kayibocelaga mvula mwi'fuku.

24 Nawalangaga na nyemo,

lono wali wasina witumbilo,

no wusangalavu wo wusu wangu,

wawapelaga witumbilo.

25 Nawahagulilaga nzila yawo,

nghawa muwaha wawo, nghawalongoza,

nha vyono mutemi kalongozaga wasilikale wakwe,

no kuwasindamika lono wakutuga wukatale.
