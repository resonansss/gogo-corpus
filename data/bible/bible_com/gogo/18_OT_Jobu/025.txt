Jobu 25

Zinghani za Bilidadi

1 “Nayo Bilidadi Mushuhi yakapitula yakatya,

2 Mulungu yo we vilungo,

holeka yinojeye wose wamogope mwene.

Yo mulamuzi wa mu wutemi wa ku wulanga.

3 Wule, ho yuli munhu wo wose, mono kosuma kuwawala

wamalayika wose, wono wamwitumamilaga?

Hamba ho hali hanhu ho hose hono wuzelu

wo Mulungu si wumulikaga?

4 Hamba ho yuli yunji mono kowa ling'ani, hali Mulungu,

hamba mono yelile ha meso gakwe?

5 Wumanye nha vyono ha meso gakwe, sunga no mwezi

wose wusina wung'alale,

ne zinyelezi nazo si zelile hali mwene.

6 Alu waco, holeka munhu, mono yenha linyongololo, ling'onyo lilya?

Ho kowa yena cambuzici ha meso go Mulungu?”
