Jobu 12

Mapitulo ga Jobu

1 Nayo Jobu yakapitula yakatya,

2 “Heya, hamba muwe anye yo muli we mahala,

ninga mumanye vyono kolo mukufwa, mofwa hamonga

ne mahala genyu.

3 Ninga mumanye vyono nane nina mahala nhe genyu,

hamba si munyhizize cinji yaye.

No munhu wo wose du, yazimanyile zose zono mulonjile.

4 Sunga naluno i wendece wangu wakuseka 'ne,

ninga wakuseka bwete no muyawo,

nane ho nili ling'ani, kanyhi sina hamba wuhoswe,

ninga ho cili cipango cono namulomba Mulungu,

nayo yakamhitula wulombe wangu.

5 Anye mukuwona musina magayo,

holeka mukusekanga.

Mukumutowanga munhu mono

yali behi no kugwa.

6 Ninga i wahizi hamonga ne wanhu wono

wasina mulungu, awo wekalaga wapolile.

Hamba wawe mulungu wawo yali mu

vilungo vyawo weneco.

7 Hamba nhavivyo, zindeje hamonga ne vilimu,

zina nyinji zo kumwijizenyi,

8 holeka munojeye muyiwuze yinze ne lilenga

mahala gavyo.

9 Hambi vyose nha vimanyile,

vyono Muwaha Mulungu yo yaviwumba.

10 Kanyhi na vyono Mulungu yo yebate wumi

we viwumbwa vyose,

no wumi we wanhu wose wuli mu miwoko yakwe.

11 Alu nha vyono zinimi zenyu zisangalalaga

lono zikujeza ndigwa,

Ale nhavivyo ne makutu genyu

nago, gakuwa kusangalala lono gakuhulika nghani.

12 “Mahala gali mu wakombi,

ninga Mulungu yo yena mahala ne vilungo.

13 I wakombi wena wizuce

ninga Mulungu yo yena wizuce, hamonga

ne vilungo vyo kwitumila.

14 Na hono Mulungu yamwagule,

nani kanyhi mono kosuma kuzenga,

hamba nani mono kosuma kumuwopolela

mu jela, u munhu mono Mulungu yamudindiye?

15 Mazala, gazaga lono Mulungu si yatonyeze mvula,

nago mahwazi, gazaga lono Mulungu yalecile

mvula yitonye ko winji.

16 Mulungu yo yali vilungo, kanyhi yo we cisumisumi,

wose wali mu miwoko yakwe.

17 Mwene kawawusizaga mahala, iwatemi,

kawanozaga walongola wawa walele.

18 Kawawusaga watemi mu wutemi wawo

kawa kuwanoza wakuwa wafungwa.

19 Kawanenenyhezaga i wakohani

hamonga ne wanhu we vilungo.

20 Kawanyamazaga i wanhu, wono wanhu wawetumbiye,

kanyhi kagawusaga ne mahala i wakombi.

21 Kawakozaga cevu i wanhu we lilumbo,

no kuwawusiza vilungo vyawo i walongola.

22 Kamulikaga hono hatitu nhe lifwa.

23 Kagapelaga makungugo vilungo no wuwaha.

Ninga kanyhi ho kagasuma no kugongomeza.

24 Kawanozaga walongola wawo walelepa

kawa kuwaleka wewajizwe, wayegayeje wajilile.

25 Wakwalabatya kwalabatya mu citi,

wakujenda wakupebesuka nhe wagalo 'jimbi.”
