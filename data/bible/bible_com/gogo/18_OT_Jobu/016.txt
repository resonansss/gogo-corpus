Jobu 16

I mapitulo ga Jobu

1 Jobu yakapitula yakatya.

2 “Nye wayangu, zinghani

ze liwala lilyo, ane nakondya kuzihulika katali.

Wusindamice wono mukusindamika,

ane nghuwona mukumhela wusungu du.

3 Alu mojendelela kulonga sunga nili?

Ninga yinojeye nhondo zose muwe ne nghani ye ndumilizo.

4 Na hono anye mwali muwe yo ane, nane niwe

yo nyenye, nali nonje zose zono mulonjile.

Nali sinjise mutwe wangu kwe mahala,

nimusumenyi ne zinghani zono zinha lihwazi lye malenga.

5 Nali nimusikamyenyi ko wulonje wangu,

njendelele kumusindamicenyi.

6 “Ninga lu, hamba nali nonje, wukalale wangu si wali

wutuzwe yaye,

na hono nyamale, si nghutuga wutuzwe

mu wusungu wangu.”

7 Alu wakondya kusuma go Mulungu,

hambi nha kuwaleka we mhuga yangu wose wawulagwa.

8 Nane wambopa, weza wuli mulugu wangu.

Ane nili nghuli ne mafupa.

Ne wanhu nawo so nghani yiyi,

wakondya kwihuwila vyono ane nili musutyi.

9 Yawusalangulanga muwili wangu

kanyhi yanuguliye kwe'kuwo

10 Ne wanhu wakusekanga,

wehangulize hamonga, wakunhowa makofi

mu wusu wangu.

11 Nayo Mulungu kakondya kumbika

mu miwoko ye wanhu wabi.

12 Ninga 'ne ho nekalaga mholile,

Mulungu yakanyibata mu singo yangu,

yakambenanga no kumhondanga vidodo vidodo.

Mulungu yakambika ho niwe wisumanizo wo wugume.

13 Ne wagumi nawo, wakunguma ne zisonga mbavu zose.

Zisonga zono zikuhoma no kumhela nghomele,

ninga hamba nhavivyo, mwene si yakumbonela

lusungu yaye.

14 Nayo yakumhela nghomele nyinji du,

yakuhoma nho musilikale mono yena lukwale lo lusulo.

15 “Nghalila, nghavwala magunila,

nghawa kwivwagamika mu manghundi, nalemwa.

16 Nane nalila, sunga no wusu wangu nhondo wadunghuwala,

ne meso gangu gavimba, gawicila no mubeho,

17 ninga'ne ho sina masutya go wugazi yaye,

no wulombe wangu kuli Mulungu, welile.

18 “Gwe go yinze, wulece kuhundicila i zimbi

zono netuma!

19 Ninga kuko ku wulanga, ko ko yuli u muhomelezi wangu,

mono yali ku lubavu lwangu.

20 Wendece wangu, wakumbedegala,

ninga meso gangu gakumulavizya mahozi Mulungu.

21 Holeka nghumusaka yunji yanombele kuli Mulungu,

nha vyono munhu kamulombelaga u mwendece wakwe.

22 Myaka yangu yikupuluta musunho,

nane nhyatizye nzila yono si noloyiwuyila kanyhi.”
