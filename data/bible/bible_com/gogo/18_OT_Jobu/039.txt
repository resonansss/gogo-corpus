Jobu 39

1 “Ma, wucimanyile cipango co kupapila zimhene zinghali za mu matalawe?

Hamba wuyiwene nzimba lono yikulela?

2 Wule, kosuma kuziwalila myezi yazo,

hamba cipango cazo co kulelela?

3 Hamba wucimanyile cipango cono zikwinama ziwa kulela,

ziwa kuleta nyana zazo mononze?

4 Ne zinyana zayo zina futa swanu,

ziwilukaga viswanu mu mawimbi magazi,

zitulukaga ziwa kubita hamba si zi kuwuya kanyhi.

5 “Nani mono yazipela zi nyhenje welecele wo kwidimya

zili wene, hamba nani mono yaziwopolela,

ziwe kuyumba-yumba mu mbago?

6 Ane yo nene mono nazipela nyika yiwe yo kaya yazo,

nghazikombola ziwe kwikala mu yisi yono yina mwino.

7 Zikalaga kutali ne mazozo ga mu makaya,

hamba hasina mono kosuma kuzifuga no kuzitumya milimo.

8 Namo mu matunda wo wudimilo wazo mono zidimilaga,

mumo mo zikuwa kutuga mahanze madece go kulya.

9 “Wule, wukwihwesa mbogo yokwenda kukwitumamila?

Hamba kwinjila yikale mwi'bululu lyako?

10 Wule, kosuma kuyiwopa mbogo monga,

no mulejehe yikulimile?

Hamba kutelemeza malolo,

ne lisili mu migunda yako?

11 Kovitumbila vilungo vyayo viwaha,

no kwihwesa vyono yosuma kwituma milimo yako mizito?

12 Kosuma kwihuwila vyono yogomola ndudula yako ku kaya,

no kukungha wuhemba wako mu wulawa mu cuga?

13 “Mabawa ge nyacihema, gapumunhaga mbela mbela,

ninga hasina nyacihema,

hamba monga yono yigulukaga nha sangalujembe.

14 Hambi nyacihema nha yilekaga,

magangha gayo mu muhangha,

ho gatuje civuce cono cili mu muhangha.

15 Hamba kwihwesa vyono magulu gosuma kugatula.

Hamba nimu kugakanyaga gatulice.

16 Yiganozezaga magangha gayo,

kotya si magangha gayo yeneco,

hamba kwihwesa vyono,

no mulimo wayo wowa wa bwete du.

17 Hambi nha yo nene mono nayiwumba yiwe ndele ndele,

hamba si nayipela ne mahala yaye.

18 Ninga kolo yikwanza kumangha, yiyisekaga fwalasi,

hamonga na mono yayipandile mucanyha yayo.

19 Mwene 'gwe Jobu,

yo gwegwe mono wazipela vilungo zifwalasi,

wukazivwika na mu singo zayo,

wukunzeje wono wukusinjisika?

20 Hamba yo gwegwe mono wazinoza ziwe kuzumha nha

mhanzi, kanyhi ziwe kuwogofya wanhu ne cililo cazo?

21 Zipalilaga palilaga mwi'lolo,

no kusangalalila vilungo vyazo,

nazo zimanyhilaga ku wulugu kwe vilungo vyazo vyose.

22 Zene hamba si zimanyaga nhunizo yo kogopa yaye,

hasina hamba mumaje wono wosuma kuzigomoleza ne nyuma.

23 Ne vitwalo vyono wavidinghuye wono wazipandile,

vikung'alala muno mwi'zuwa.

24 “Wakucicima ko wujilimuce wuwaha,

lono zifwalasi zikumangha zidomile ku mwande.

Na hamba ndulele yilile,

zene vyo zimanyhile du hamba si zikwima yaye.

25 Na kolo ndulele yikulila, zene zikukoloma,

hambi nha zikusuma kuwuhemvya wulugu na lono zikali

kutali, kanyhi zikugahulika ne mazi ge wawaha lono

wakulavya wulajilize wawo.

26 “Limwewe ma, lyefundila kwako kuguluka,

lono likutandakula mabawa galyo, lilanjile kutakama?

27 'Ndi nayo ma, yilindilaga sunga wuyilonjeleze,

yizenje linhuli lyayo kucanyha mu matunda?

28 Nayo yizengaga linhuli lyayo mu matalawe gono gali

kucanyha muno muno, ne visonje vikali vye mabwe

viwa go magomawakali gayo.

29 Na kuko ko yigozezaga mhelembo zayo,

hambi meso gayo nha gakuyiwona ku wulawilila kutali.

30 Nazo zi'ndi, zendile kwikongholela hono ho wuli muvimba

ne zi'ndi zindodo, zing'wezaga sakami.”
