Jobu 20

Wulonje we kadatu wa Zofali

1 Nayo Zofali Munamati yakapitula yakatya,

2 “Gwe Jobu, ninga ane wukunghalaza ye.

Holeka nane nopitula musunho.

3 Hambi zinghani zako zose zono wulonjile,

nha kulonjela mu cibede cako du,

ninga nane manyile vyono nokupitula.

4 Jobu, wumanyile ku wulawa katali,

vyono, lono munhu yawicilwa mono'nze,

5 u munhu mubi yali yena nyemo ya cidaha cidodo du.

6 Hamba munhu mubi yawiluce muno, no widunhumuzi wakwe wufice

ko ku wulanga, no mutwe wakwe wufice ko ku mavunde,

7 Ninga vyo koputwa nhe manghundi du,

na wose wono wali wamumanyile,

wolokwizina kolo si wakumuwona, wawe kutya, ‘Yali hayi?’

8 Kolokwajilila nhe ndoto, kotya wugubulilwe we cilo,

hamba si kolokwijelela lukulu kanyhi.

9 Kolokwajililila lukulu hamba kwijela kanyhi

i hanhu hono yekalaga,

10 i wanagwe wolozigomola zisawo

ze waciwa zono yahizaga.

11 No muwili wakwe wono wali wa wuzelelo ne vilungo,

ninga cidaha cidodo du wowa manghundi.

12 “Hamba wubi wuwe wuli muzinzi mu mulomo wakwe,

13 hamba yawe yawuhundice hasi yo lulimi lwakwe.

14 Ninga mu'nda yakwe, ndigwa yikuwa nghali muno

yihwanile vyono sumu yiwezaga.

15 No munhu mubi, kayidekaga sawo yose yono yayihiza.

Mulungu kayigomolezaga kwakwe, sunga na

yono yili mu'nda yakwe.

16 Na cono munhu mubi kamelaga, nha cihwanile sumu,

nayo yomuwulaga nha mono yalumigwe ne lizoka.

17 Hamba si kowumipa kanyhi, ho yagawone

makolongo ge mafuta,

hamba vikolongo vyono vikuhuluma mele no wuci.

18 Nayo kovileka vyose vyono yavitugutilila,

hamba si kotuga kanyhi cilejeho, co

kusangalalila sawo yakwe,

19 vyono yawewonelaga waciwa no kuwabedegala,

kanyhi yakawaboka wanji zikaya zono wali wezenjeye.

20 “Hamba nhavivyo, wusulumizi wakwe,

si wokwigushwa yaye.

21 Na lono yalizaga, hasina cono yasigazaga,

ninga aluno wumoteswe wakwe wamalika.

22 Magayo gomuziza haciligati yo winji

we vinhu vyono yena.

23 Holeka mulekaje yalye vyose vyono yakusaka mwene.

Ninga Mulungu yo kolomulipiciza

ko wukali ne likuwo lyakwe.

24 Nayo kolo yakujela kwiponya ku mumaje we cuma,

ninga kogumwa no wupinde we shaba.

25 Ne songa yowa yipuguze mu muwili wakwe,

no wung'alale we cisonje ce songa, cowa cikwiliye

sakami yakwe, no woga wowa mu nhumbula yakwe.

26 Ne vinhu vyose vyono kowa yewiciye, vyokongomezwa,

nayo mwene hamonga na we mhuga yakwe wose,

wotakamizwa no moto wono wowa si mukoleleze

ne miwoko yo munhu.

27 Wulanga wugubulaga wubi wo munhu,

nayo yisi yikuhomelela.

28 Ne sawo yakwe yose yokongomezezwa mwi

'hwazi lye likuwo lyo Mulungu.

29 Ali lye'fungu lye wanhu wabi

lye' fungu lyono Mulungu yawawiciye.”
