Jobu 36

1 Kanyhi Elihu yakajendelela kulonga yakatya,

2 “Alu Jobu, wukangalile, wujendelele kuhuliciza hadodo,

hambi nha nghali nina zo kulonga ku soko

yakwe Mulungu.

3 Hambi wumanye wangu nha wuficile kutali,

holeka nolonga zono manyile

ho sume kulajila vyono Mulungu muwumbi wangu yali ling'ani,

4 Ne zinghani zangu hamba si za wulambi yaye.

Nayo mono yakwilaniciye mu wumanye, yali hamonga nagwe.

5 “Langa ta, Mulungu yo we vilungo! Hamba si

kamubedegalaga munhu wo wose.

Kanyhi hasina hamba cinji co cose cono si yacimanyile.

6 Mwene si kawukamisaga wumi we wabi,

nayo vilo vyose kawatendelaga ling'ani i waciwa.

7 Kawakamisaga we ling'ani, kanyhi kawatemyaga

hamonga ne watemi,

kawa kuwapela ne lilumbo cibitilila.

8 Na hono wanhu wawe wawopigwe ne manyolo,

wakugazika ku soko ye metumo gawo,

9 Mulungu kawalajilaga wubi wawo,

hamonga no widunhumuzi wawo.

10 Kawanozaga wamuhulicize mwene, wapituce no

kuleka wubi wawo.

11 Na hono wamuhulice Mulungu no kumwitumamila,

wokwikala mu mikalile yawo wapolile,

hamonga no wumoteswe.

12 Ninga hono walece kumuhulika, wofwila mu wulele wawo,

wenjile mu yisi ye wafu.

13 “Na wose wono wamusina Mulungu mu mikalile yawo,

wekalaga wena makuwo.

Hamba watuje wulipicizwe, wene si walombaga

wutazi yaye.

14 Kanyhi wafwizaga wakali wadodo du,

wakusakalila mu mikalile ye cevu.

15 Ninga Mulungu, kawejizizaga wanhu mu magayo,

kanyhi ketumamilaga wugaye, ho yawanuguze meso gawo.

16 Mulungu yakuwusa mu wugaye, yakakuleka wikale wupolile,

ne meza yako yekalaga yimemile ndigwa.

17 “Ninga aluno, watuga wulipicizwe wono wukunojeye,

18 Holeka alu wulanjilile, mafupo galece kukukoca-koca

hamba wugoli wulece kukwajiliza.

19 Nghani yiyo yokunoza, hamba wulile,

si kotuga wutazi yaye.

Sunga ne vilungo vyako kwaluno, hamba si vyosuma

kukutaza cinji yaye.

20 Wulece kusulumila cilo cize,

cipango cono makungugo gokwajilila.

21 Holeka wilanjilile, wulece kuwuya ku zimbi kanyhi,

hambi magayo gaga nha gakuziza ho gakutazilize

wulece kuziwuyila zimbi kanyhi.

22 Wukumbucile wuwaha we vilungo vyo Mulungu vyono winha,

mwene yo mwijizi muwaha wa wose.

23 Hasina hamba monga mono kosuma kumulonjeleza Mulungu

cono yetume,

hamba kumuhosa vyono ketuma zono si zili ling'ani.

24 “Hambi mwene, nha katugaga lukumyo du, muli zose

zono ketumaga

holeka nagwe wunojeye wumupele lukumyo.

25 Ne wanhu wose wakondya kuziwona zono yetumile,

ninga hamba nhavivyo, cikuzilanjila kutali.

26 Hamba nhavivyo, si cosuma kuwumanyila lukulu

wuwaha wakwe,

hamba kuyiwala myaka yo wikale wakwe.

27 Yo Mulungu mono kasolaga malenga mu wulawa mu'si

hanyuma kanyhi kagapitulanya, gawa mabanhi ga mvula.

28 Nayo kayilekaga mvula yitonye ku wulawa mu mavunde,

yiwatonyele wanhu wose.

29 Kanyhi hasina mono yamanyile vyono mavunde gajendaga,

hamba vyono mabalalusi gadidimaga mu wulanga,

kono yekaye Mulungu.

30 Kaletaga mamuli mu wulanga wose, ninga kuvisolo

vye lilenga, kwikalaga vyo kutitu du.

31 Nayo kawalisizaga wanhu mu nzila yiyo,

no kuwapela ndigwa nyinji.

32 Kaligubikaga limuli ne miwoko yakwe,

kalilajilizaga lilece kuwupulutiza mupaka.

33 Mabalalusi galajilaga nha vyono lididimo likuza,

sunga ne zing'ombe zikumanya vyono lyalawila.”
