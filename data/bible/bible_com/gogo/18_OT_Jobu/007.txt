Jobu 7

1 “Mikalile yo munhu, yihwanile mulimo we

wasilikale ku wulugu.

Yinha mikalile yo munhu mono

yakwituma milimo kwe vilungo.

2 Yihwanile muwanda mono yakusulumila mubeho,

kotya mwitumami mono yalindiye musahala wakwe.

3 Myezi na myezi, nikaye du sina ndigwa

cilo na cilo vilo vyose, nikalaga nili mu wukalale du.

4 Na kolo nghugona mu citanda,

mapango hamba si gakupuluta mbela,

hodu, niwa kwipituciza-pituciza du sunga wusiku wela.

5 No muwili wangu, wumemile masomi gose,

weza wina mapele-pele gose du,

no wufila wukulawila mu zinghomele zangu.

6 Ne vilo vyangu vikupuluta visina witumbilo,

navyo vikubita mbela mbela, kuciza no munhu

muhona luheneko.

7 “Gwe go Mulungu, wukumbucile nha vyono wumi

wangu, wihwanile mbeho du.

Ne nyemo yangu nha yakondya kuduma.

8 Naluno wukusuma kumbona, na si kombonela lukulu kanyhi.

Na kolo wukunondola, ane nowa nakondya kufwa.

9 Nha vyono livunde lipemhelukaga no kwajilila,

ale nhavivyo, no munhu vyo kafwizaga,

hamba kuwuya kono'nze kanyhi.

10 Na wose wono wali wamumanyile, hodu,

wawa kumusemwela lukulu.

11 “Holeka ane hamba si nonyamala ndece kulonga yaye,

hambi nha nili mu wusungu, kanyhi ndabuce,

holeka yinojeye nonje du.

12 Alu, nhawule wukunyimila?

Hamba wukwihwesa vyono ane nili 'limu lya mwi'lenga lo?

13 Ane ngonile hasi ho nyhehe,

ho nhuje ciba mu wusungu wangu.

14 Ninga agwe wukunyogofya ne zindoto,

wukundetela wugubulilwe, hamonga no wilimucilwe

15 sunga niwa kuwona vyono ciba nali nifwe,

si nha kwikala mu muwili wuwu we magayo.

16 Nawukatala no wumi wangu,

si nghusaka hamba no kuwumipa yaye.

Holeka wundekaje, hambi vilo vyangu nha vya bwete du.

17 Alu munhu ci cici, ho wumukumye?

Ho waci waco, sunga wumwihwese mu nhumbula yako?

18 Agwe kumulanjililaga zakwe nhondo zose hakali mitondo,

hamonga no kumujela mapango gose.

19 Alu ma, sikoleka kunanga yaye cipango cidodo

nane ho sume kumela mate gangu lo?

20 Hamba nitume wubi, cono cikukutamisa ci cici,

'gwe go musepi we wanhu?

So cici lo wukusepa ho wunhowe?

Hamba'ne neza mutegulo kuli'gwe?

21 So cici si wukunenela wubi wangu,

no kumbusiza masutya gangu gono sutyaga?

Vyono alu nofwa no kuzikwa mu citenjelo

nagwe konondola ko wupiligazi,

ninga nowa nakondya kuwa nili hasina.”
