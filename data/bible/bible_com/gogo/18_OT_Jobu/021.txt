Jobu 21

Mapitulo ga Jobu

1 Jobu yakapitula yakatya,

2 “Ale ta muhulicize nane nimulonjelenyi,

nawo wuwu wowa wo wutuzwe du wono nghuwulomba

kuli 'nye.

3 Alu, mumhelaje cilejeho nane nonje,

na lono nghali kulonga nanye, hono musace mosuma kuseka.

4 Ane nghani yangu si yili kuli wanhu yaye,

holeka nina soko yo kuwa no wukangalizi.

5 Ale, munangaje ta vyono ninha, wulenyi, yiyi si yosuma

kumunozenyi mwizine no kunyamala twii hongo?”

6 Ku soko nane kolo nghwihwesa zizi

zono zakondya kunyijelela,

mbeka nghugazika, nghucicima,

kanyhi nghwinjilwa no woga.

7 Alu nhawule mwene, Mulungu yakuwaleka i wanhu wabi

wekale womi, sunga wakombipe hamonga no wumoteswe?

8 Wakuwa wena ne wana, hamonga ne wezukulu,

wekaye wakuwadima, sunga wawiluka.

9 Hamba Mulungu si kawaletelaga magayo mu zikaya zawo,

wekalaga du wapolile, wasina woga wo wose.

10 Heya, ne zing'ombe zawo zilelaga

viswanu, zisina magaziko.

11 Ne wana wawo, nawo wakumangha-mangha no kwidawala

nhe zinyana ze zingholo.

12 Wakuvina wena ne zindonondo, hamonga ne zifulembe.

13 Wekalaga vilo vyose mu wumoti,

ninga wofwa lwa kamonga wasina wugazice.

14 I wabi wakumulonjela Mulungu yawalece wali wene,

hambi nha si wendile wawumanye wuhile wo Mulungu

mu mikalile yawo.

15 Wakwihwesa nha vyono, hasina soko yo kumwitumamila

Mulungu, hamba cambuzi co kumulomba.

16 Wene wawonaga nha vyono, vyose vyono wena navyo,

wavitugaga kwe vilungo vyawo weneco.

Ninga ane ku lwangu, hamba si nghugavumila yaye

mahweso gawo gago.

17 “Kali kalinga limuli lye wabi likuzimywa?

Hamba no wongomezwe wuwazizaga 'mbi?

Nayo Mulungu wule, kawalipicizizaga i wabi mwi'kuwo

18 lyakwe, no kuwapemhelusiza kutali nhe mawaje

mu mbeho, hamba kotya manghundi gono gakukunzwa

no mukukunzo?

19 “Anye mukutya vyono Mulungu kamulipicizaga mwana

so wubi wo sogwe.

Ninga vyo si nhavivyo, mulece Mulungu yawalipicize

weneco du wono wetumile wubi,

mulece yawalajile nha vyono yakuzinoza zizo

ku soko yo wubi wawo.

20 Muwalekaje wabi, wedinghulile weneco wulipicizwe

wawo, mulece walijeze likuwo lyo Mulungu

mono yali vilungo.

21 Nalo munhu kolo yafwa, hambi kehwesaga kanyhi

ze nyemo ye wanagwe?

22 Hambi munhu kosuma kumwijiza Mulungu mono

kaling'anizaga, sunga na wose wono wena lilumbo liwaha?

23 “Howali wanhu wanji, wono mu mikalile yawo yose,

wekalaga miwili yawo miswanu du,

yisina wunyoganyoga sunga wolofwa,

24 nawo wafwizaga wapolile wasina wugazice

ne miwili yawo miliswe viswanu.

25 Ninga wanji wawezaga wasina nyemo hamba nhavi,

wekalaga sunga wafwila mu wusungu mu zinhumbula zawo.

26 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, wose vyo wehwanile du,

wafwizaga no kuzikwa, wawa kuligwa ne mang'onyo.

27 Mulanje! Nane manyile mahweso genyu mabi

gono mukunyihweseza.

28 Hambi nha mukutya, ‘Alu kaya yakwe yili hayi yo

munhu yuyu muwaha, u munhu mono kedawalaga no wubi?’

29 Wulenyi, anye mukali naduwana ne wanhu wajenda

mihinzo? Hamba si muzimanyile zono

wazilolosaga kolo wawuya?

30 Na mwizuwa kolo Mulungu yakolwa likuwo no kulipiciza,

i wanhu wabi, wo wakuwa kulekwa nhondo zose.

31 Hasina munhu mono komuhosa u munhu mubi,

hamba kumulipiciza muli zose zono yasutizye.

32 Ninga kolo yafwa, yeza kutegulwa kuzikwa mu citenjelo,

33 wanhu wenji muno wokwilunza mu maziko gakwe,

womukanyiciza no muhangha mhola mhola

mu muwili wakwe.

34 Alu nanye yonye! Mukujela kunhuza na

nghani za bwete du!

Hambi mu mapitulo genyu, nha mumemile nghani za

wulambi du zose!”
