Jobu 32

Zinghani za Elihu

(32:1–37:24)

1 Alu vyono Jobu yali yali ling'ani ha meso gakwe mweneco, ku soko yiyo, wawalya wanhu wadatu Elifazi, Bilidadi na Zofali, nawo siwamupitula Jobu kanyhi yaye.
2 Ninga munhu yunji mono yali yali baho litagwa lyakwe Elihu, u mwana wa Balakeli u mwana wa Buzi, we mhuga ya Lamu, yakakolwa likuwo, vyono Jobu yewonaga yali ling'ani mweneco, no kumuhosa Mulungu.
3 Kanyhi yakawakolelwa likuwo sunga na wa walya wendece wa Jobu wadatu. Vyono si wasuma kutuga nzila yo kumutaza Jobu, ne nghani yiyo, yikalajila nha vyono kotya u Mulungu yo musutyi.
4 Alu vyono Elihu yali yo mudodo muli wene wose, holeka yalindila du sunga wene wose wamale wulonje wawo.
5 Nayo yali yeza kuwona nha vyono wa walya wanhu wadatu, weza wasina kanyhi nghani zo kumulonjela Jobu, mwene yakakolwa ne likuwo muno.
6 Yakawa kwanza nayo kulonga yakatya,

“Ane heya nili mwana mudodo,

nanye muli wanhu mututu,

na ku soko yiyo, holeka nane nali kogopa

kumulonjelenyi ze mahweso gangu.

7 Niwa kwihwesa vyono, anye yo nyenye mono

munojeye mulonje, vyono nyenye mo wanhu mututu,

munojeye mwitaze mahweso genyu.

8 Ninga mumanye, vyono, wo muhe wo Mulungu du mono yali

vilungo, yo kawazizaga wanhu no kuwapela mahala.

9 Ninga si wukombi yaye, wo wuwapelaga wanhu mahala.

Hamba kuwapela wumanye wo kumanya vyono wali ling'ani.

10 Holeka luno nghumulombenyi,

muhulicize zono nghuzilonga,

mulece nimulonjelenyi nane ze mahweso gangu.

11 Ane nali hulicize du ko wukangalizi zose zono

mwali kulonga, nali nindiye du,

lono mwali kutikula-tikula zono molonga.

12 Nane nimuhulicizenyi viswanu, ninga mbona vyono, wose

mwalemwa kumupitula Jobu kuli zono yalonjile.

13 Alu ho mosuma nhawule mutye vyono mahala mwagawona?

Hambi Mulungu nha yo yali yanojeye yamupitule Jobu,

hambi anye nha mwakondya kulemwa.

14 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, Jobu yali kulongana nanye,

ninga si yali kulongana na nene yaye,

holeka sinomupitula nha vyono

anye mumupituye.

15 “Alu Jobu, wayago zinghani zawalemela,

wasina kanyhi hamba yo kukupitula.

16 Hambi nane lu, nojendelela kunyamala,

lono wene wakali wanyamaye?

Hambi nha wanyamala wasina kanyhi yono wolonga.

17 Aa, ng'o, ane langa aluno nokulonjela zono nghwihwesa,

ho nghupele lipitulo lyangu nane.

18 Hambi nha si nosuma kulindila kulonga

vyono si nosuma kuzigomoleza zinghani zangu yaye.

19 Na hono ndece kutuga cilejeho co kulonga,

mbeka nobahuka nho mufuko we nyhingo,

wono wumemile divayi mhya.

20 Nane hamba si nosuma kukangalila,

langa sunga nonje du.

21 Nane mu zinghani zizi,

hamba si nosuma kwima ku lubavu lwa yunji yaye,

hamba kwiyendeza kuli munhu wo wose.

22 Ane wiyendezi si niwumanyile yaye

na mhali nili nhavivyo u Mulungu yali yanyongomeze.
