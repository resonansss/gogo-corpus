Jobu 1

Setani yakumujela Jobu

1 Kwali na munhu litagwa lyakwe Jobu, munhu yuyu, yekalaga mu yisi ya Uzi. Munhu yuyu yali muhembi muno, kanyhi mwihuwilwa kuli Mulungu. Kanyhi yali munhu muswanu, mono si yali yendile kwituma nghani yo yose 'mbi.
2 Nayo yali yena wana mupunghati wa cilume, hamonga na wana we cicekulu wadatu.
3 Kanyhi yali yasawile ngholo zificile elufu mupunghati, na ngamiya elufu yidatu, ne litewa lye zing'ombe zificile elufu monga, hamonga na ndogowe magana mahano. Kanyhi Jobu, yali yena wetumami wenji muno, kanyhi yali yo mugoli muno yali wene ku lubavu lwa kucilima.

4 Ne wanagwe we cilume, wali wazoweye kubita kulya ndigwa mu zikaya zawo mu wilecele, munhu ne zuwa lyakwe, yakucemela wayagwe. Nawo wetang'anilaga hamonga, wawacemelaga ne walumbu zawo vyono wadatu, wawa kuhanga zindigwa hamonga nawo.
5 Na nhondo zose lono wela hakali mitondo, lono walawa mu wulye wuwo, Jobu yelawaga nhondo zose hakali mitondo, kawa kuwalavizya makumbiko wanagwe wose monga monga, ho yaweze. Nayo yakawa yakunoza nhavivyo nhondo zose, hambi nha yehwesaga vyono, lunji yumonga wa muli wanagwe, kolowa ketuma wubi mu nzila yo kumutovula Mulungu wula kumanya.

6 Ne lizuwa lyali lyeza kufika, lyono lyali linojeye i viwumbwa vya ku wulanga, vilajile hali Muwaha Mulungu, kumbe nayo Setani yali yali mu ciligati yawo.
7 Baho Mulungu yakamuwuza Setani, yakamutya, “Alu 'gwe Setani, wina mulimoci bahano?”

Nayo Setani yakamupitula yakatya, “Ane nili mu wupilime-pilime du, ako na ako mono'nze.”

8 Muwaha Mulungu yakamuwuza Setani yakamutya, “Ninga wumuwene mwitumami wangu Jobu? Hambi nha hasina munhu yunji mononze yiyi, mwihuwilwa, kanyhi munhu muswanu, mono yehwanile yuyu Jobu. Hambi Jobu nha kahembaga nene, kanyhi kelanjililaga muno, ho yalece kwituma nghani yo yose 'mbi.”

9 Nayo Setani yakapitula yakatya, “Wukwihwesa yuyo Jobu yali yasume kukuhemba, mhali yawe yasina cinji mugati yayo?
10 Hambi agwe nha kumudimaga nhondo zose, hamonga ne mhuga yakwe, hamonga na vyose vyono yena navyo. Kuyilugutizaga sunga ne milimo yakwe, kanyhi wumupelile na ng'ombe nyinji zimemile yisi yose.
11 Ninga luno, hono wuwe nha wukumuwusiza vyose vyono yena navyo, nayo hodu kokukana calinze!”

12 Muwaha Mulungu yakamutya Setani, “Ale viswanu, ne vinhu vyose vyono yena navyo, nha vili mu muwoko wako. Langa nghukulonjela vyono, wulecele lukulu kumutamisa Jobu mweneco.” Hodu hobaho Setani yakawa kuwuka hali Mulungu.

I wana wa Jobu hamonga ne sawo yakwe, vikongomezwa

13 Kukawa mwi'zuwa linji, lono wanagwe we cilume na we cicekulu, walizaga no kung'wa divayi mukaya yo muwaha wawo,
14 mwitumami yunji yakamangha, yakamuziza Jobu, yakamulonjela yakatya, “Ase cali cili mu mugunda cikulima ne zing'ombe, nazo zindogowe zikudima baho behi ne zing'ombe.
15 Waseba wawa kuza nhinicizo, wawa kuzisola zing'ombe hamonga ne zindogowe zose. Kanyhi wawa kuwawulaga ne wetumami wako wose, na yo nene du mono sigaye. Yonene du mono mhonile, ho nize nghulonjele zizo.”

16 Nayo lono yali yakali yakulolosa zizo, yakaza kanyhi mwitumami yunji, nayo yakamulonjela Jobu yakatya, “Limuli lizitowa zingholo liziwulaga zose, hamonga ne wadimi wayo. Nane yo nene du mono mhonile, ho nize nghulonjele zizo.”

17 Nayo lono yali yakali yakulolosa zizo, yakaza kanyhi mwitumami yunji, nayo yakamulonjela Jobu yakatya, “Wakalidayo waza mapuga madatu, wacihoma, wasola ne zingamiya zose, kanyhi wawawulaga ne wetumami wako wose. Nane yo nene du mono mhonile, ho nize nghulonjele zizo.”

18 Nayo yuyo lono yali yakali yakulolosa zizo, yakaza kanyhi mwitumami yunji, nayo yakamulonjela Jobu yakatya, “I wanago wose, we cilume na we cicekulu, lono wali kulya no kung'wa divayi mukaya yo muwaha wawo,
19 liza libeho yiwaha lyo mukukunzo, likulawilila ku nyika, liyikundujiza kaya, yiwagwila no kuwawulaga wose. Nane yo nene du nili wene mono mhonile, ho nize nghulonjele zizo.”

20 Hodu nhondo, Jobu yakawa kwinuka, yakawa kuwudemulanga musweko wakwe ko wusungu muno. Yakawa kuwuceta zimvili mutwe wakwe, yakawa kwigumila hasi wugubama.
21 Yakawa kutya,

“Ane nalelwa sina cinji,

kanyhi nolofwa sina cinji.

Muwaha Mulungu yo yalavya,

kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu yo yawusize,

Litagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu lilumbwe!”

22 Na muli zizo zose zono zamwijelela, Jobu hamba si yamuhosa Mulungu yaye.


Jobu 2

Jobu yakujelwa kanyhi na Setani

1 Kukawa mwi'zuwa linji kanyhi, lono viwumbwa vya ku wulanga vyazaga ho vilajile hali Muwaha Mulungu, na Setani nayo yakaza hamonga navyo.
2 Muwaha Mulungu yakamuwuza Setani yakatya, “Gwe Setani, alu wali wuli hayi?”

Nayo Setani yakamupitula yakatya, “Ane nili mu wupilime-pilime du, ako na ako mono'nze.”

3 Muwaha Mulungu yakamuwuza Setani yakamutya, “Ninga wumuwene u mwitumami wangu Jobu? Hambi nha hasina munhu yunji mononze yiyi, mwihuwilwa, kanyhi munhu muswanu, mono yehwanile yuyu Jobu. Hambi Jobu nha kahembaga nene, kanyhi kelanjililaga muno, ho yalece kwituma nghani yo yose 'mbi. Ninga agwe wukasaka nghukombole wubite wukamutamise mbula soko. Ninga lu wulanje! Jobu yakali vyo mwihuwilwa du kuli'ne nha vyono kawezaga vilo vyose.”

4 Setani nayo yakamupitula Muwaha Mulungu yakamutya, “Heya, munhu kolavya vyose vyono yena navyo, vihomele wumi wakwe.
5 Ninga aluno, hono wumupele magazo mu muwili wakwe, manyile vyono kokukana calinze.”

6 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamutya Setani, “Ale viswanu, Jobu yali mu miwoko yako, ninga langa du, wulece kumuwulaga.”

7 Hobaho Setani yakawa kuwuka hali Muwaha Mulungu, yakawa kumupela Jobu mapu mawaha muwili wose, wulawilila mu magulu sunga ko kwi ndosi.
8 Nayo Jobu yakawa kubita kwikala mu cugulu, yakasola cizaye, yakawa yakwisuwila mu mapu gakwe.
9 Ninga mugolece wakwe yakamutya, “Alu mwene agwe Jobu, hongo wukali wuli mwihuwilwa du nha vyono kuwezaga vilo vyose? Alu si yali yiwe ciba wali wumutovule Mulungu, hodu du wuwe kufwa?”

10 Ninga Jobu yakamupitula mugolece wakwe, yakamutya, “Gwe mugolece wangu, mbeka wukulonga nghani za caka du! Alu kumbe, hono Mulungu yakuciletela nghani swanu, ayo langa ciyibocelaje na nyemo. Ninga kolo yakuciletela wugaye, hongo yicinojeye cimung'ung'ulicile?” Na muli zizo zose ze magayo zono zamwijelela Jobu, ninga mwene hamba si yalonga nghani yo yose 'mbi kuli Mulungu yaye.

I wendece wa Jobu, wakumuziza

11 Nayo Jobu yali yena wendece wakwe wadatu, Elifazi munya kwi'kaya lya Temani, na Bilidadi mulawa ku yisi ya Shuwa, hamonga na Zofali, nayo munyawusi we yisi ya Nama. I wanhu wawa wali weza kuzihulika, nha vyono Jobu yali mu magazo mawaha, hodu wakawa kulagana wabite wose hamonga wakamusindamice.
12 Nawo lono wabitaga, na lono wali wakali wali cidaha, wakamuwona Jobu kwa kutali, ninga hamba si wasuma kumumanya vyono yo mweneco Jobu. Ninga wali weza kumumanya vyono yo mweneco Jobu, hobaho wakawa kwanza kulila wazozize muno, akuno wakuyidemulanga ne myenda yawo ko wusungu wuwaha muno, akuno wakwesa ne manghundi mucanyha, no kwikupila ganji mu mitwe yawo.
13 Hanyuma wakawa kubita hono hoyuli Jobu, wakawa kwikala baho hasi hamonga nayo, vilo mupunghati, cilo na misi, hamba kulongana nayo nghani yo yose, hambi nha wali wakondya kuwona vyono mbeka yali mu wusungu wuwaha muno.


Jobu 3

Jobu yakumulilila Mulungu

1 Nhondo Jobu yakawa kwanza kulonga, yakalilijita lizuwa lyono yalelelwa.
2 Jobu yakalonga yakatya,
3 Gwe go Mulungu, wulilijite lizuwa lyono nalelelwa,

hamonga ne cilo ci cilya cono nadinghulilwa 'nda!

4 Wulinoze lizuwa lilyo ciwe citi 'gwe go Mulungu.

Hamba wulecele lukulu kulikumbucila kanyhi

lizuwa lilyo, hamba wuzelu wulece kulimulicila kanyhi.

5 Wulinoze lizuwa lilyo wuwe wutitu, liwe wutitu tuku.

Wuligubice ne mavunde, lizuwa lilece kulimulika.

6 Wuciwuse cilo cico mu vilo vyo mwaka

ho cilece kuwalilwa lukulu kanyhi

7 I cilo cico cikale cili wene,

ciwe cilo cono cisina nyemo.

8 Walonjele wono walilijitaga lizuwa,

walilijite lizuwa lilyo,

wawo wono wamanyile kulitonga ne lilimu

lyono licemwaga Lewiyatani.

9 Wuzimile zinyelezi za mwi'jingu lyo wusiku,

zilece kulajila wuzelu wayo,

cilo cico cilece kuwuwona wele we hakali mitondo.

10 Wucilijite cilo cico, cono caleka ane nghalelwa

no kumbika mu wusungu ne magayo.

11 Ciba le nali fwile mu'nda ya yaya,

hamba kuwuzilila kamonga du.

12 Alu, socici nayo yaya yakamhagata?

Hamba nhawule lu yakanyosa?

13 Ninga hono baho nali nifwe,

alu baha mhali nyhehile.

14 Mhali ngonile du, kotya watemi,

hamonga ne walongola wono wagazenga

makaya ge katali go wutemi.

15 Kanyhi mhali ngonile kotya i wana we watemi,

wono wali wamemizye zikaya zawo zahabu na fweza.

16 Hamba mhali ngonile nhongo nho mwana

mono yaleligwe mwezi si wakwe.

Nha mono yalelwa i vimba, hamba kuwuwona wuzelu.

17 Namo mu vitenjelo, i wanhu wabi,

hamba si wajendelelaga kwituma nghani

zawo zimbi, na wono wakataye

so milimo yawo wakuwa kuceha.

18 Sunga ne wafungwa wose, nawo watugaga mulamu,

hamba si wahulikaga kanyhi mazi go wukali.

19 Ne wanhu wose wali baho, we lilumbo,

hamonga na wose wono si wamanyice,

ne wawanda, nhondo wakuwa welecele.

20 Alu socici wukuwaleka wanhu wawe kwikala

wali mu wugaye

Hamba socici wukuwapela wuzelu

i wanhu wono wali mu wusungu?

21 Wene walindilaga lifwa, nalyo lifwa hamba kuwaziza yaye.

Wakusulumila kufwa, si nha ciwutwa cihundice.

22 Nawo wekalaga wasina nyemo,

sunga kolo wafwa no kuzikwa.

23 Mulungu yawahundiciye zawo za ku mwande,

yawapilime mbavu zose.

24 Alu wugule wangu, wo wukuhomela wulye we zindigwa,

nane hamba si nosuma kuleka kukoloma yaye.

25 Ne nghani yono niyogopaga hamonga na yono

yinyogofyaga, vyose vikunyiziza.

26 Alu neza sina mulamu, hamba wucehe bwete,

no wugaye wangu si wukuduma yaye.


Jobu 4

Zinghani za Elifazi

(4:1–14:22)

1 Hanyuma Elifazi Mutemani yakamupitula Jobu yakatya,

Ati Jobu, hono nonje nokukalaza?

2 Hambi nha nghuwona vyono si nosuma kukangalila

niwe nyamaye twii.

3 Agwe Jobu, wuwejize wanhu wenji,

nagwe wuwatajiye vilungo we miwoko yono yitimbile.

4 Na lono munhu yawe yekobaye mu nghani yinji,

hamba yawe yatimba kanyhi yakatala,

zinghani zako zimupelaga vilungo, kawa kwima kanyhi.

5 Alu lweza lwako lo kutuga wugaye,

nagwe lu, wazilika.

6 Agwe wali wuli muhemba Mulungu,

sunga ne mikalile yako yose,

yali yisina yilema lyo lyose.

Holeka yali yikunojeye wuwe no witumbilo

hamonga ne nhumbula yono yikamile.

7 Alu, wukumbucile ta, hono kosuma kumutuga munhu

we ling'ani, mono yatuga magayo nha gaga.

8 Nha vyono mbwene nene, wanhu wono wakulima zimbi,

no kuhadika zimbi nha mbeyu,

nawo wodudula zizo zimbi du.

9 Ninga Mulungu, kawongomezezaga mwi'kuwo lyakwe

lyo mukukunzo.

10 Ne wanhu wabi webulyaga, no kukoloma

nha simba, ninga Mulungu kazinyamazaga,

no kugabena meno gazo.

11 Nawo wakuwa nha simba zono zisina mhelembo,

zifwizaga, ne mana gazo gawa kwiyagala.

12 “Nghani yikanyiziza lwa kamonga cinyele,

nane nghayihulika wupwepwe wayo.

13 Yikawa nha wilimucilwe,

yikanyaza ne zinhongo zangu.

14 Nghacicima ko woga muno,

no muwili wangu wose,

wukenjilwa na woga wuwaha.

15 Baho cibeho cikamhuta mu wusu wangu,

ne zimvwili zangu zikanyanyamuka so woga.

16 Nghawona cinhu cinji cimile ha mwande yangu,

nane nghacinugulila muno,

ninga hamba si nasuma kucimanya cali cinhuci.

Hanyuma nghahulika lizi likulawa mu

wunyamale wuwo likutya,

17 “Hambi munhu wo wose kosuma kuwa ling'ani

hali Mulungu, hamba yawe yelile hali Muwumbi wakwe?

18 Hambi, Mulungu, nha si kawetumbilaga

sunga ne wetumami wakwe wa ku wulanga,

nha kawawonaga sunga ne wamalayika wakwe

vyono wasutyi.

19 Alu agwe, wukwihwesa nha vyono

kosuma kucitumbila ciwumbwa ce lilongo,

cinhu ce manghundi, cono cosuma kusajinhwa,

nhe lipulaputya?

20 U munhu kosuma kuwa mumi hakali mitondo,

ninga kanyhi kosuma kufwa, lono yunji si yalanjiliye

lono lizuwa likali hamba si naswa yaye.

21 Ne vinhu vyakwe vyose vyono yali yena

navyo, vikusolwa na wanji,

yakufwa, yasina mahala.


Jobu 5

1 “Gwe Jobu, ale ta wuceme, wuwone hono yunji kokwidika.

Hamba ho yuli malayika wo wose mono agwe komupitucila?

2 Holeka wumanye, vyono mahwele gawulagaga

wanhu ko wusungu,

na yowa nghani ya wulele, kanyhi cowa cinhu

ca wulele ku witumwe.

3 Nane niwawene i walele, wono wehwesaga wapolile,

ninga nane nghazilijita, musunho ko wukalale, zikaya zawo.

4 I wana wawo we cilume, si woyiwonela lukulu mhonelo,

hamba hasina munhu mono kowataza mu mahakama.

5 Nawo wono wali nzala, wolya mhwanga yo mulele

hamba wuwe wuhemba wukuwilucila mu mamivya,

nawo wanhu wono wena nyhilu,

woloyiwonela wivu sawo yakwe.

6 I zimbi si zikuwa kucindika mu manghundi,

hamba wugaye nawo, si wucindikaga mu muhangha.

7 Na yiyi yo nghani ya mbeka! U munhu keletelaga

wugaye mweneco du,

nha vyono vinyesela vyo moto,

vilawaga mu moto viwa kuzumha kucanyha.

8 “Holeka, hono ane, nali niwe yo gwegwe,

nali nimuwuyile Mulungu, nimuloloseze zangu zose.

9 Hambi ase nha si cosuma kuzimanya zimbaha

zono ketumaga

ne mapoto gakwe nha gasina ndumo.

10 Mwene katonyezaga mvula mu'si

kayitonyezaga mu mawimbi.

11 Heya, yo Mulungu du mono kawenulaga i wenenenyhezi,

no kuwasangalaza wono wakulila.

12 Kazimilaga zinzila ze wasugu,

ho walece kutuga cambuzi mu milimo yawo.

13 Kawagwizizaga we mahala mu mahweso gawo.

Ho walece kutuga cambuzi muli zose zono,

wowa kwituma.

14 Sunga na hono misi, wene wakwalabata-kwalabata

kotya nha kutitu.

15 Ninga Mulungu kawaponyaga waciwa mwifwa.

Kawatazaga mu wiwonelwe wono wena wanjile.

16 Kawapelaga witumbilo waciwa,

ninga kawanyamazaga i wabi.

17 “Langa ta, yo mumoti munhu mono Mulungu

yakumutacila. Holeka wulece kubedegala

lono yakukutacila.

18 Hambi Mulungu nha katamisaga, kanyhi no kulagula,

miwoko yakwe yikupelaga nghomele, ninga kanyhi

i miwoko yakwe yo yikukuponya.

19 Nayo vilo vyose kokuponya kali kenji mu magayo gako gose,

na mumbali ye ndumilizo, u wubi si wokupamiza.

20 Na hono lizala lize, mwene kokukamisa wikale mumi,

na mu wulugu namo, kokucitila ne lifwa.

21 Mulungu kokuponya na mu wuvilwe mose,

nayo kokuponya sunga na mu wongomezwe kolo waza.

22 Nagwe kolobedegala, no kuseka mu vipango vyo

wongomezwe hamonga ne lizala.

Hamba si kologogopa i malimu ga mu mbago.

23 Na mu mawimbi mono kowa kulima,

molowa musina mabwe,

hamba malimu ga mu mbago,

si golokugaza yaye.

24 Nagwe lulo, kolokwikala wupolile mwi'hema lyako.

Nagwe kolo wukulilanga litewa lyako lye zingholo,

koloziwona zipolile du.

25 Kolotuga wana wenji muno, wolowa wadedewaye

nhe mahanze ga mwi'dimo.

26 Kolofwa lono weza wuli mukombi kokolo,

nha kotya liso lyo wuhemba lyono ligwiye.

27 Alu 'gwe Jobu, cakondya kuyilondola-londola

nghani yiyi hamonga no kuyisunjilila muno muno.

Nayo yo nghani ya mbeka. Holeka aluno

nghukunena wuyibocele.”


Jobu 6

1 Jobu yakamupitula Elifazi yakatya,

2 yali yiwe ciba, wugazice wangu hamonga no wutamice wangu

hono vyali vipimwe mu muzani

3 vyali viwe vizito muno kuciza wuzito wo

musawawa wa mwi'lenga.

Holeka ne zinghani zangu, zilece kukupela wizine.

4 Mulungu mono yali vilungo, yakondya kunguma ne

zisonga zakwe

ne sumu yazo, yakondya kutandala mu muwili

wangu mose,

Mulungu yanhandaliciye nene wogofye wakwe.

5 Nyhenje si yililaga hono yiwe yikulya mahanze,

ne nghambaku nayo yiwezaga yinyamaye twii,

lono yikulya mahanze.

6 Hamba nani mono kasumaga kulya ndigwa

yili cisusuli yisina mwino?

Wule mu wuzelu we ligangha,

mo yili ndaso?

7 Nane hamba si niyidabuciye ndigwa ye liwala lilyo,

na vyose vyono nghuvilya, vikuhesuza mutima wangu.

8 “Alu nayo Mulungu, hamba nhawule si yakumhela

yono nghumulomba?

Hamba si yakusaka kumhitula no wulombe wangu.

9 Yali yiwe yili ciba le, hono yali yambulaje du lwa kamonga!

10 Na hono nali manye, nha vyono kosuma kumbulaga lwa kamonga,

nane nali nzumhe zumhe na nyemo.

No wusungu wangu, hamba si nali niwuhulice yaye.

Manyile vyono Mulungu Yelile,

nane nghali si namubeza muli zono kalajilizaga zose.

11 Alu nowa nina vilungo ci kanyhi,

vyono vyoleka njendelele kuwa mumi?

Alu socici lo njendelele kuwumipa

na lono neza sina witumbilo.

12 Hambi 'ne ninha yibwe? No muwili wangu nawo

hambi winha shaba?

13 Nalu, nha neza sina ne vilungo vyo kwitaziza,

hamba hasina kanyhi hanji hono notiziza

ho nhuje wutazi.

14 “Na mu wugaye wono winha wuwu,

mbeka nghusaka wendece, wono

wonhendela viswanu, hono niwe nakondya

kumuleka Mulungu hamba ng'o.

15 Ninga lu anye nyo wendece wangu,

mukunghonga-nghonga cinye vikolongo

vyono vinyalaga mwibahu

16 Vyono malenga gavyo matibulangwe,

gakutukuwazwa ne seluji.

17 Ninga seluji kolo yikutuga civuce hodu yiwa kwenjeduka,

ne vikolongo viwa kunyala.

18 Wanhu wakwajilila so wulondola malenga,

wakuyumbayumba sunga wafwila mu nyika.

19 Wanhu wokulawa Sheba na Tema wavilondola,

20 ninga witumbilo wawo wukamalicila mu

vikolongo vyono vinyalile.

21 Holeka nanye, ane nghumuwonenyi nha

vyono mwihwanile ne vikolongo vivyo kuli'ne,

mwakondya kuwuwona wugaye wono nina nawo,

nanye lu mukogopa.

22 Mwene ane nimulombenyi mumhele cinhu co cose,

hamba nimulombenyi nitya mubite mukamupele

yunji mafupo ku soko yangu,

23 hamba nimulombenyi nitya munhaze

kuli mulugu wangu, hamba kuli mono yakumbonela?

24 “Ale viswanu! Alu munyijizaje, mundajile masutya gangu

Nane nowa nyamaye du nghumuhulicizenyi.

25 Ninga mumanye vyono, zinghani zo mugoloka zo zitazaga,

ninga lu anye mukulonga nghani za wulele du.

26 Anye mukwihwesa nghani zangu za bwete kotya mbeho du.

Alu nhawule kanyhi mukumhitula zinghani zangu

ane mono nawa sina witumbilo?

27 Heya, mukuwatowela kula i wana wono wasina walezi wawo,

no kwigolifizya mu wendece wenyu!

28 Muwulangaje ta wusu wangu,

ane si nili mulambi yaye.

29 Zizo zono mulonjile,

hodu zakondya, mulece kuwa wakonji.

Kanyhi mulece kuhosa, hambi 'ne nha nili mu mbeka.

30 Ninga anye mukwihwesa ane nghulonga za wulambi,

kanyhi mukwihwesa vyono,

sinosuma kuzihosa zinghani zimbi.”


Jobu 7

1 “Mikalile yo munhu, yihwanile mulimo we

wasilikale ku wulugu.

Yinha mikalile yo munhu mono

yakwituma milimo kwe vilungo.

2 Yihwanile muwanda mono yakusulumila mubeho,

kotya mwitumami mono yalindiye musahala wakwe.

3 Myezi na myezi, nikaye du sina ndigwa

cilo na cilo vilo vyose, nikalaga nili mu wukalale du.

4 Na kolo nghugona mu citanda,

mapango hamba si gakupuluta mbela,

hodu, niwa kwipituciza-pituciza du sunga wusiku wela.

5 No muwili wangu, wumemile masomi gose,

weza wina mapele-pele gose du,

no wufila wukulawila mu zinghomele zangu.

6 Ne vilo vyangu vikupuluta visina witumbilo,

navyo vikubita mbela mbela, kuciza no munhu

muhona luheneko.

7 “Gwe go Mulungu, wukumbucile nha vyono wumi

wangu, wihwanile mbeho du.

Ne nyemo yangu nha yakondya kuduma.

8 Naluno wukusuma kumbona, na si kombonela lukulu kanyhi.

Na kolo wukunondola, ane nowa nakondya kufwa.

9 Nha vyono livunde lipemhelukaga no kwajilila,

ale nhavivyo, no munhu vyo kafwizaga,

hamba kuwuya kono'nze kanyhi.

10 Na wose wono wali wamumanyile, hodu,

wawa kumusemwela lukulu.

11 “Holeka ane hamba si nonyamala ndece kulonga yaye,

hambi nha nili mu wusungu, kanyhi ndabuce,

holeka yinojeye nonje du.

12 Alu, nhawule wukunyimila?

Hamba wukwihwesa vyono ane nili 'limu lya mwi'lenga lo?

13 Ane ngonile hasi ho nyhehe,

ho nhuje ciba mu wusungu wangu.

14 Ninga agwe wukunyogofya ne zindoto,

wukundetela wugubulilwe, hamonga no wilimucilwe

15 sunga niwa kuwona vyono ciba nali nifwe,

si nha kwikala mu muwili wuwu we magayo.

16 Nawukatala no wumi wangu,

si nghusaka hamba no kuwumipa yaye.

Holeka wundekaje, hambi vilo vyangu nha vya bwete du.

17 Alu munhu ci cici, ho wumukumye?

Ho waci waco, sunga wumwihwese mu nhumbula yako?

18 Agwe kumulanjililaga zakwe nhondo zose hakali mitondo,

hamonga no kumujela mapango gose.

19 Alu ma, sikoleka kunanga yaye cipango cidodo

nane ho sume kumela mate gangu lo?

20 Hamba nitume wubi, cono cikukutamisa ci cici,

'gwe go musepi we wanhu?

So cici lo wukusepa ho wunhowe?

Hamba'ne neza mutegulo kuli'gwe?

21 So cici si wukunenela wubi wangu,

no kumbusiza masutya gangu gono sutyaga?

Vyono alu nofwa no kuzikwa mu citenjelo

nagwe konondola ko wupiligazi,

ninga nowa nakondya kuwa nili hasina.”


Jobu 8

1 Nayo Bilidadi Mushubi yakamupitula Jobu yakatya,

2 “Alu mwene, zinghani zako zizo kozilonga sunga nili?

Zojendelela sunga nili kuwa nha mbeho mbaha?

3 Mulungu si kahodangaga wiling'ano,

hamba si yakuwa kulemwa kwituma ziswanu.

4 Ne wanago nawo, kowa wamusutizya Mulungu,

holeka nayo kawa kuwalipiciza.

5 Ninga aluno wunene kuli Mulungu mono yali vilungo.

6 Hono wuwe mbeka welile, kanyhi wuli ling'ani,

nayo Mulungu kokuza yakupele wutazi,

no kuyipela nyumba yako wumoteswe.

7 Ne sawo yako yose yono yakondya kwajilila,

yowa si cinji yaye,

hambi Mulungu nha kokupela vinhu vinji muno

kuciza na vyono wali wakondya kuvyajiliza.

8 Alu, wukumbucile mahala ga we zindelo ze katali.

Wihwese ne zimbeka zono walezi wetu wazilondolaga.

9 Kanyhi wumanye vyono mikalile yetu, ya vilo vicefu du

hamba si cimanyile cinji co cose,

cipulutaga mono'nze nho muzizi.

10 Holeka wulece wawaha we katali wakwijize

nagwe wuwahulicize azi zono wakuzilonga.

11 ‘Alu wumanye vyono, madete-lulenga,

sigotaga hono hasina malenga,

hamba hono hasina nhope.’

12 Na hono malenga ganyale,

lono gene gakali gadedewaye.

Hodu gakuwomba mbela,

si nhe mahanze ganji.

13 Nhavivyo vyo wenha i wanhu wose

wono wakumusemwa Mulungu

no witumbilo wawo wukuwa wa bwete,

vyono wakondya kumusemwa Mulungu.

14 Hambi nha wetumbiye mhuti ze nhandabuli.

15 Na hono wawe wetumbiye mhuti ze nhandabuli

wule, mbeka zosuma kuwebatiliza?

Na hono wawe wetumbiye luputi,

alu mbeka losuma kuwasikamya?

16 I wanhu wabi, wakuwa kutula nhe maselegwa mwi'zuwa,

wenha maselegwa gono gamemaga mu mugunda wose.

17 Ne milela yago, yigazingaga mabwe

yiwa kwibata zii mu mabwe gose.

18 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, kolo wagakula,

hodu, hakuwa hasina mono komanya

vyono gali gacindice mumo.

Wanji nawo wawa kuhadika mumo vyono wakusaka weneco.

19 Ale ne nyemo ye wanhu wabi nayo,

nhavivyo vyo yinha.

20 “Ninga Mulungu hamba si komuleka yaye

u munhu wakwe mwihuwilwa.

Hamba si kowapela wutazi i wanhu wabi.

21 Nayo kokumemya luseko hamonga ne lizi lye nyemo.

22 Kanyhi kowaletela cevu ciwaha muno

wose wono wakusulile.

Ne zikaya ze wanhu wabi,

si zokwijela kanyhi.”


Jobu 9

Lipitulo lya Jobu

1 Nayo Jobu yakapitula yakatya,

2 “Heya! Nane manyile vyono zose zizi zono

wukuzilonga za mbeka.

Ninga munhu kosuma wule kuwa

yali ling'ani hali Mulungu?

3 Hamba munhuci mono kosuma kwisumaniza nayo?

Hambi Mulungu, nha kosuma kuwuza mawuzo

gaficile elufu monga, gono hasina hamba

munhu monga, mono kosuma kugapitula.

4 Hambi Mulungu nha yo we mahala gose kanyhi yali vilungo,

hasina munhu hamba monga, mono kosuma

kwima ho visumanize nayo.

5 Mwene kagawusaga matunda si yagamanyize,

na mwi'kuwo lyakwe, kagongomezaga.

6 Mulungu kaletaga litulo, kawa kuyisinjisa yinze,

ne masumbili gayo, gawa kucicima.

7 Kosuma kulimila lizuwa lilece kusosomoka,

ne zinyelezi zilece kung'alala ku cilo.

8 Hasina munhu wo wose mono yamutaziliza Mulungu

kuwutandaza wulanga

no kulikanyanjila i mawimbi ge lilenga.

9 Mulungu kaziwikaga zinyelezi mu mwanya,

ne Dubu, ne Oliyoni, ne Cilimila,

hamonga ne zinyelezi za kutakama.

10 Nase hamba si cosuma kuzimanya zimbaha

zono kazitumaga,

hamonga ne mapoto gakwe menji,

gono gasina liwalo.

11 Mulungu yakukola, ninga hamba si nghumuwona yaye.

Yakupuluta ha mwande, nane hamba kumanya yaye.

12 Nayo kasolaga cono mweneco yendile,

na hasina munhu wo wose mono kosuma kumwimila yaye,

hasina hamba monga, mono kokwijelela

kuwuza nghani, yamutye, ‘Alo wukunoza cici?’

13 “Mulungu si koliwusa likuwo lyakwe,

Mwene kawongomezaga i walugu wakwe

wono wamutaza Lahabu, hamonga ne watazi wakwe.

14 Alu ane ma, nosuma nhawule kumupitula Mulungu?

15 Hamba niwe sina wusutye, ninga nghumulomba Mulungu

u muling'anizi wangu, yambonele lusungu.

16 Hamba mhali yandece nongane nayo,

si nihuwiye vyono yali yasume kuhuliciza.

17 Mwene yakundetela mukukunzo no kumhondanjiza,

no konjeleza zinghomele zangu mbula soko.

18 Hamba si yakusaka ho nehele yaye,

langa yayimemizye mikalile yangu no wusungu.

19 Alu wule, nitumamilaje vilungo hongo?

Njele kumudindimiza Mulungu?

Hamba nimutegule mu mahakama?

Ninga nani lu mono kosuma kumwibata, no

kumutegula kuko mu mahakama?

20 Ane heya sina wusutye, kanyhi nili mwihuwilwa,

ninga zinghani zangu zohosa,

sunga na zose zono nghuzilonga,

nha zikwijela vyono zikuhosa nene.

21 Hamba niwe sina wusutye,

ninga si nghwilanga nhumbula yangu,

Hambi mikalile yangu nha yitamigwe. Holeka kuli'ne

si cinji yaye, hono niwe sina wusutye, hamba

22 niwe nili musutyi, hambi Mulungu

nha kocongomeza du sese wose.

23 Na hono munhu mono yasina masutya yafwe lwa kamonga,

Mulungu yakuwa kuseka.

24 Hambi Mulungu nha yawakombolela yinze i wabi,

Kanyhi yakawabofuza meso i mahakimu

Alu, hono yawe Mulungu yo si yazituma zizo,

alu ho kowa nani kanyhi mono yazituma?

25 “Hambi vilo vyangu nha vikupuluta mbela,

hasina hamba lizuwa limonga lyono nowona ziswanu.

26 Vilo vyangu vikupuluta nha ngalawa

zono zijendaga musunho.

Vikumangha mbela nha 'ndi yono yikuswekula sungula.

27 Hamba sece, no kujela kuwusemwa wusungu wangu,

28 ninga wusungu wangu vyo wukunyiziza du

no kunhamisa.

Nane baho ho nghumanyila vyono, Mulungu

yakumbona nili musutyi.

29 Alu lono nakondya kwijela vyono nili musutyi,

ho noswa nghwigazizaci kanyhi?

30 Hambi nha hasina kanyhi sabuni ye liwala lyo lyose,

yono yosuma kunghwema wubi wangu.

31 Mulungu kongumila mu nhope

ne myenda yangu yonghoza cevu.

32 Nye! Mulungu yali yawe munhu,

nane nali nimupitule.

Cali cibitane nayo mu mahakama cikaling'anizwe

wugombane wetu.

33 Ninga lu, nha hasina munhu wo wose mono

kosuma kwima hagati yetu.

Hasina mono kosuma kuciling'aniza ane no Mulungu.

34 Gwe go Mulungu, wulece kujendelela kundipiciza!

Wumbusize wogofye wako!

35 Ane hamba si nghogopa kulonga yaye, langa nolonga du,

hambi nha niyimanyile nhumbula yangu no mweneco.”


Jobu 10

I nghani ya Jobu ye ndumilizo

1 “Hambi mikalile yiyi nha yanghataza muno.

Holeka aluno wuhulicize wung'ung'ulice wangu

wono wumemile wusungu.

2 Holeka go Mulungu, wulece kuning'aniza yaye,

Langa wundajile nghanici yono wina kuli'ne?

3 Hambi mbeka yikunojeye wuwe wusina lusungu?

Wukuwubedegala sunga no mulimo wono

wawituma gwegwe mweneco?

Hanyuma kanyhi kuwa kuwuseka wuhile we wanhu wabi?

4 Wule, wina meso ge cikulikuli?

Ma, wukuwona nha vyono wanhu wawonaga?

5 Hambi 'gwe wina wumi wa vilo vicefu

nha vyono winha wumi wetu?

Hamba myaka yako yinha myaka ye wanhu?

6 Alu nhawule wukuwuza-wuza zo wubi wangu,

no kulondola-londola zo wusutye wangu?

7 Agwe nha wumanyile vyono 'ne si nili musutyi,

kanyhi na vyono hasina munhu wo wose

mono konhaziza kuli'gwe.

8 Miwoko yako yo yamumba, no kumhela hwani yangu.

Kanyhi alu, miwoko yo yiyo yose yo yikunyongomeza.

9 Ninga wukumbucile nha vyono, wamumba ku wulawa mwi'longo,

alu ho weza kungomoleza kanyhi mu manghundi?

10 Wamupela baba wusume wo kundela'ne,

wukambilusiza munda ya yaya.

11 Wamumba muwili wangu hamonga ne mafupa,

hamonga ne matimba,

hanyuma wukamvwika nghuli ne nyama.

12 Wamhela wumi no lusungu lwako,

kanyhi wukundima no wumi wangu.

13 Ninga aluno nakondya kumanya nha vyono

kumbe mu madaha gago gose, wali wina

mahweso go kunhamisa nene.

14 Nagwe wukawa wulanjiliye, ho wuwone

hono nokwinjila mu wubi,

nagwe ho wuleme kunenela.

15 Na hono sutye du, hodu du kuligwe,

neyinjiza mu magayo, na hono nitume ziswanu,

si nghwambulila cinji yaye.

Nane memile cevu, nghuwuwona wugaye wangu du.

16 Na hono ninule mutwe wangu du,

agwe komhelemba nha simba ho wunhamise,

wukulajila metumo gako go wizine.

17 Hambi nhondo zose nha wina wuhomelezi muli'ne

ne likuwo lyako kuli'ne licilaga konjelezeka

nhondo zose, nagwe nhondo zose,

kwihwesaga nzila mhya yo kunhowela.

18 “Alu 'gwe go Mulungu, ho nhawule

wukandeka nghalelwa?

Yali yiwe ciba le, hono nali nifwe

lono munhu wo wose yakali sinambona.

19 Nali mhulutize du mu wulawa munda ya yaya,

sunga ko ku citenjelo.

Yiyo langa yali yiwe yo nghani swanu,

hambi nha yali yijele nha kotya sinawumbwa yaye.

20 Ne vilo vyangu ma, si visigaye vicefu?

Holeka nghukunena wundece.

Wundekaje nivisangalalile hadodo

i vilo vyangu vyono visigaye.

21 Hambi nha nobita mbela

hamba si nowuya kanyhi,

nghubita ku yisi yo wutitu no wukalale.

22 Yisi yo wutitu tuku, yo mubeho we lifwa,

yisi yono yili cakacaka du,

i hanhu hono wuzelu wayo, winha wutitu tuku.”


Jobu 11

Mapitulo ga Zofali

1 Hanyuma Zofali Munamati, yakamupitula Jobu yakatya,

2 “Alu wukwihwesa hasina munhu mono kusuma

kuzipitula zinghani zako zizo zo wulele?

Agwe wukwihwesa wulonga nghani nyinji,

wumunozaga munhu kawa yali ling'ani?

3 Alu mwene 'gwe Jobu, wukwihwesa nha vyono,

ase si cosuma kukupitula hongo?

Wukwihwesa zinghani zako zizo ze zinzwaji,

zocidinda milomo cilece kulonga hongo?

4 Agwe wukwiyitya vyono, zose zono wulonjile za mbeka.

Na kanyhi wukwiyitya vyono, agwe

wuli ling'ani hali Mulungu

5 Alu nane nali nombe Mulungu yakupitule!

6 Nayo yali yakulajile zo lubanzo lwe mahala,

hambi nha hozili zinji zono zicizize wumanye.

Alu Mulungu yakukulipiciza hadodo du,

si nha vyono wali wunojeye wulipicizwe.

7 “Alu 'mbi kosuma kumanya ze mabanzo go Mulungu,

hamonga no wuwaha wakwe ne vilungo vyakwe?

8 No wulanga hamba si wosuma kumwimila Mulungu yaye,

ninga kumbe wulanga ho wuli kucanyha muno kono agwe

si kosuma kuficila.

Kanyhi Mulungu yayimanyile ne yisi ye wafu

ninga agwe waco, hamba si wuyimanyile yaye.

9 U wuwaha wo Mulungu, wutandaye muno

sinhe yinze.

Kanyhi wugazi muno si nhe lilenga.

10 Na hono Mulungu yakwibate, yakusitace,

nani kanyhi mono kosuma kumwimila?

11 Mwene Mulungu yawamanyile wanhu wono wasina cambuzi,

kanyhi yakuziwona ne zimbi zono wakwituma.

12 “Ne wanhu walelelele, hamba si wotuga wizuce

hambi ndogowe ya mu mbago, nha ndogowe ya mu mbago du.

13 Holeka agwe Jobu, wuyiwikaje

nhumbula yako mu ling'ani,

wumubitile Mulungu.

14 Wuwusize lukulu wubi wose no wuwaya wose wa

mu kaya yako.

15 Hanyuma ho kosuma kwidoma ne yinze wukamile,

wusina nhumbula nyoga.

16 Ne magayo gako gose gowuka mu mahweso gako,

gokwihwana mahwazi ge malenga

gono gapulutaga hamba kukumbucilwa kanyhi yaye.

17 Ne mikalile yako nhondo yowa miswanu,

kuciza mu misi mugati

ne mikalile ye madaha go wutitu,

nhondo yong'alala nha hakali mitondo.

18 Nagwe kokwikala wupolile,

kanyhi wina no witumbilo.

Mulungu kokudima no kukupela wucehe.

19 Hamba si kowogopa kanyhi walugu wako yaye,

ne wanhu wenji wokuza walondole wutazi kuli'gwe.

20 Ninga i wanhu wabi, wowa kunasa ako na ako

kwe mahwele

nawo hamba si wotuga nzila

yo kwiponezya yaye.

Langa witumbilo wawo, wowa wufwe du.”


Jobu 12

Mapitulo ga Jobu

1 Nayo Jobu yakapitula yakatya,

2 “Heya, hamba muwe anye yo muli we mahala,

ninga mumanye vyono kolo mukufwa, mofwa hamonga

ne mahala genyu.

3 Ninga mumanye vyono nane nina mahala nhe genyu,

hamba si munyhizize cinji yaye.

No munhu wo wose du, yazimanyile zose zono mulonjile.

4 Sunga naluno i wendece wangu wakuseka 'ne,

ninga wakuseka bwete no muyawo,

nane ho nili ling'ani, kanyhi sina hamba wuhoswe,

ninga ho cili cipango cono namulomba Mulungu,

nayo yakamhitula wulombe wangu.

5 Anye mukuwona musina magayo,

holeka mukusekanga.

Mukumutowanga munhu mono

yali behi no kugwa.

6 Ninga i wahizi hamonga ne wanhu wono

wasina mulungu, awo wekalaga wapolile.

Hamba wawe mulungu wawo yali mu

vilungo vyawo weneco.

7 Hamba nhavivyo, zindeje hamonga ne vilimu,

zina nyinji zo kumwijizenyi,

8 holeka munojeye muyiwuze yinze ne lilenga

mahala gavyo.

9 Hambi vyose nha vimanyile,

vyono Muwaha Mulungu yo yaviwumba.

10 Kanyhi na vyono Mulungu yo yebate wumi

we viwumbwa vyose,

no wumi we wanhu wose wuli mu miwoko yakwe.

11 Alu nha vyono zinimi zenyu zisangalalaga

lono zikujeza ndigwa,

Ale nhavivyo ne makutu genyu

nago, gakuwa kusangalala lono gakuhulika nghani.

12 “Mahala gali mu wakombi,

ninga Mulungu yo yena mahala ne vilungo.

13 I wakombi wena wizuce

ninga Mulungu yo yena wizuce, hamonga

ne vilungo vyo kwitumila.

14 Na hono Mulungu yamwagule,

nani kanyhi mono kosuma kuzenga,

hamba nani mono kosuma kumuwopolela

mu jela, u munhu mono Mulungu yamudindiye?

15 Mazala, gazaga lono Mulungu si yatonyeze mvula,

nago mahwazi, gazaga lono Mulungu yalecile

mvula yitonye ko winji.

16 Mulungu yo yali vilungo, kanyhi yo we cisumisumi,

wose wali mu miwoko yakwe.

17 Mwene kawawusizaga mahala, iwatemi,

kawanozaga walongola wawa walele.

18 Kawawusaga watemi mu wutemi wawo

kawa kuwanoza wakuwa wafungwa.

19 Kawanenenyhezaga i wakohani

hamonga ne wanhu we vilungo.

20 Kawanyamazaga i wanhu, wono wanhu wawetumbiye,

kanyhi kagawusaga ne mahala i wakombi.

21 Kawakozaga cevu i wanhu we lilumbo,

no kuwawusiza vilungo vyawo i walongola.

22 Kamulikaga hono hatitu nhe lifwa.

23 Kagapelaga makungugo vilungo no wuwaha.

Ninga kanyhi ho kagasuma no kugongomeza.

24 Kawanozaga walongola wawo walelepa

kawa kuwaleka wewajizwe, wayegayeje wajilile.

25 Wakwalabatya kwalabatya mu citi,

wakujenda wakupebesuka nhe wagalo 'jimbi.”


Jobu 13

Lipitulo lya Jobu likujendelela

1 “Na zose zono mulonjile ane nali nakondya

kuzihulika katali.

2 Na kanyhi nizimanyile zose,

nizimanyile nha vyono muzimanyile nyenye.

Kanyhi ane si nili mudodo wenyu yaye.

3 Ane yinojeye cilongane no Mulungu,

ninga si nyenye yaye.

Nghuhila nimulonjele Mulungu zangu zose.

4 Anye lu mukusaka muwugubicize

wulele wenyu, mu nzila ye zinghani zenyu zo wulambi.

Mwehwana waganga wono si walagulaga yunji yanoje.

5 Ciba mwali munyamalile lukulu,

holeka yunji yali yehwese vyono mwina mahala!

6 Alu le, muhulicize nimulonjelenyi zangu nane.

7 Alu nhawulenyi muli walambi nhavi?

Mukwihwesa wulambi wenyu wina

cambuzi hali Mulungu?

8 Hamba mukujela kumuhijila mbejele Mulungu?

Hamba momutetela Mulungu mu mahakama?

9 Mwene nha baha hono Mulungu, yamusunjililenyi

mu zinhumbula zenyu,

mbeka kowona nghani yo yose swanu muli'nye?

Anye mukwihwesa vyono mosuma kumukonga

Mulungu, nha vyono muwakongaga wanhu?

10 Hamba muwe mwihundice mumo mu wukoci wenyu,

ninga Mulungu komutacilenyi.

11 No wuwe wakwe we vilungo, womogofyenyi.

12 Ne zimbazi zenyu za bwete, zinha mavu,

ne zinghani zenyu zose, mvuluje nhe lilongo.

13 “Alu munyamale du twii, mumhele cilejeho nane nonje,

mundekaje cinyagane co cose cono conyagana.

14 Mundekaje niyambuze wumi wangu no mweneco.

15 Hambi witumbilo wangu wose nha wawuka,

holeka hamba Mulungu yambulaje, ninaci?

Nane nobita du nghamulonjele zangu.

16 Na yiyo lunji yali yimhele wutazi,

ninga lu nha hasina munhu mubi mono

kosuma kumuwona Mulungu.

17 Alu muhulicize zono nghusaka kuzilolosa.

18 Mundekaje niyambuze no mweneco

ne zinghani zangu.

Hambi ane nha manyile no mweneco

vyono nili mu ling'ani.

19 “Alu go Mulungu, kokuza wuning'anize?

Na hono wunoze nhavivyo, ane nowa nyamaye du

twii, nindiye kufwa.

20 Ninga nghulomba nghuwuze nghani nyejete,

nagwe wunhendele, nane ho ndece

kwihundika hali'gwe, nazo zozi,

21 wulece kundipiciza hamba kunyogofya

ko woga wako.

22 “Gwe go Mulungu, wunyhemaje, nane nokwidika,

hamba wundece ane nonje, nagwe wumhitule.

23 Alu wubi wangu wo wa hayi?

Hamba zimbi za hayi zono nitumile?

Hamba wulajilizeci wono niwubenile,

wono wukuleka ane nghusitakwa?

24 Gwe go Mulungu, socici wukwihundika hali'ne?

Hamba nhawule wukunhendela nha nili mulugu?

yuyo kowa mu wusungu mu mikalile yakwe yose.

Hambi nawo mumaje, nha wowa wumulindiye

ko wuwula cihuliko,

yakumangha vikehome no Mulungu.

Ninga wulugu, wogongomeza makaya gago

kanyhi nho muzeyituni, wono wukukung'unhika mahamha gayo.

Na hono yawuhundice wusu wakwe, wanhu wowula wutazi.

yakwe Mulungu.

holeka nolonga zono manyile

Nayo mono yakwilaniciye mu wumanye, yali hamonga nagwe.

kamubedegalaga munhu wo wose.

Kanyhi hasina hamba cinji co cose cono si yacimanyile.

kawa kuwapela ne lilumbo cibitilila.

wakugazika ku soko ye metumo gawo,

9 Mulungu kawalajilaga wubi wawo,

hamonga no widunhumuzi wawo.

kuleka wubi wawo.

wenjile mu yisi ye wafu.

zono ketumaga

holeka nagwe wunojeye wumupele lukumyo.

25 Ne wanhu wose wakondya kuziwona zono yetumile,

ninga hamba nhavivyo, cikuzilanjila kutali.

yiwatonyele wanhu wose.

29 Kanyhi hasina mono yamanyile vyono mavunde gajendaga,

hamba vyono mabalalusi gadidimaga mu wulanga,

kono yekaye Mulungu.

30 Kaletaga mamuli mu wulanga wose, ninga kuvisolo

vye lilenga, kwikalaga vyo kutitu du.

31 Nayo kawalisizaga wanhu mu nzila yiyo,

no kuwapela ndigwa nyinji.

32 Kaligubikaga limuli ne miwoko yakwe,

kalilajilizaga lilece kuwupulutiza mupaka.

33 Mabalalusi galajilaga nha vyono lididimo likuza,

sunga ne zing'ombe zikumanya vyono lyalawila.”

wakulavya wulajilize wawo.

26 “Limwewe ma, lyefundila kwako kuguluka,

lono likutandakula mabawa galyo, lilanjile kutakama?

27 'Ndi nayo ma, yilindilaga sunga wuyilonjeleze,

yizenje linhuli lyayo kucanyha mu matunda?

28 Nayo yizengaga linhuli lyayo mu matalawe gono gali

kucanyha muno muno, ne visonje vikali vye mabwe

viwa go magomawakali gayo.

29 Na kuko ko yigozezaga mhelembo zayo,

hambi meso gayo nha gakuyiwona ku wulawilila kutali.

30 Nazo zi'ndi, zendile kwikongholela hono ho wuli muvimba

ne zi'ndi zindodo, zing'wezaga sakami.”


Jobu 40

1 U Muwaha Mulungu yakajendelela kumutya Jobu,

2 “Agwe Jobu wukwisumaniza no Mulungu mono yali vilungo

alu wule, kozileka zizo zo wisumanizi wako,

hamba komhitula?

3 Nayo Jobu yakamupitula Muwaha Mulungu yakatya,

‘Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, ane nalonga ciwula mahala du,

holeka alu nokupitula niwe kutyaci?’

4 Ane si nosuma kwijelela kulonga hamba nghani

yinji yaye,

5 Nha nakondya kulonga nghani nyinji muno, kuciza

na vyono yali yinojeye nonje.

6 Muwaha Mulungu yakamupitulila Jobu mu mukukunzo

kanyhi, yakamutya,

7 Alu witungaje nha wuli munhu, wupitule mawuzo

gono nokuwuza,

8 Alu ma, kojendelela kuwuwembula wuling'anizi wangu,

wunyitye ane nili musutyi, agwe langa si wuli musutyi?

9 Alu hono wukulanga, agwe wina vilungo wunyhizize ne?

Ne lizi lyako lyosuma kubalaluka nhe lizi lyangu?

10 “Na hono yiwe mbeka vyo yinha, ale winuce wivwice

wuswanu ne lilumbo,

wuvwale lukumyo ne lilumbo lye citemi-temi.

11 Wuwalanje wedunhumuzi, wujide likuwo lyako,

wuwanyetye.

12 Heya wuwalanje, wuwagwise hasi, no kuwakanyanjila

iwabi kono wekaye.

13 Wuwazice wose mu muhangha, wuwawope mu yisi ye wafu.

14 Nane nowa yo nene munhu mulongozi kukutogoza,

baho nane nhondo, ho novumila vyono kwitujila go

mweneco wusume.

15 “Alu, wuyilangaje ta nhomondo, yono nayiwumba,

nghakuwumba nagwe. Yene yilizaga mahanze cinya zing'ombe,

16 ninga, wulanje vyono yili vilungo muno mu muwili,

sunga na mu matimba gayo!

17 Mucila wayo wikalaga winuce kucanyha nho mubici wo

muhagata ne matimba ga mu magulu gayo,

gali vilungo muno.

18 Ne mafupa gayo gali vilungo genha cuma ca shaba,

sunga ne magulu gayo genha makongho ge macuma.

19 “Ne nghani yiyo yo nghani nongozi yo wizine,

kuli anye mo viwumbwa vyangu!

Nene no muwumbi wayo du yo noyisuma.

20 Ne mahanze gono yilizaga, nha gacindikaga mu matunda

mono malimu ga mu mbago gedawalaga.

21 Yene nhomondo, yigonaga hasi mu vititika vye mamivya,

yiwa kwihundika mu madete lulenga ga mu matetema.

22 Navyo vititika vye mamivya hamonga ne mibici ye

mijiha, yiyifunikaga ne mibeho yayo.

23 Yene, si yogopaga hamba ne likolongo lyono

likuhuluma yaye, yina witumbilo, hamba yiwe Yoludani

yimemile sunga malenga galapuce.

24 Nayo nani mono kosuma kuyibofuza meso ho yayibate?

Hamba nani mono kosuma kuyibata mu mhula yayo ne ndobogo?”


Jobu 41

1 “Alu wule, kosuma kulibata ne ndowano, lilimu lyono

likucemwa Lewiyatani, hamba kuliwopa lulimi lwayo

no mulejehe?

2 Wule, kosuma 'mbi kuwukoza mulejehe mu mhula yayo,

hamba kukoza ndowano mu luzagasa lwayo?

3 Hambi lyokulomba ho wulilecele?

Hamba kulongana nagwe zinghani ze mhola mhola?

4 Hamba mbeka mokwilumbaniza nalyo,

likwihuwize nha vyono lyokwitumamila cibitilila?

5 Wule, kosuma kuliwopa nha ndeje, ciwe nha cinhu cono

cowa kuwasangalaza i wetumami wako we cicekulu lo?

6 Ne mhuga ye wanimula zisomba, wule, wolikwawila nalyo?

Hamba i wambazi, wolitemasha wakaliguze?

7 Wule, kosuma kulihoma ne zisonga ze cuma,

hamba kulihoma mu mutwe walyo ne migoha yo kuhomela zisomba?

8 Ta wulipamizaje kamonga, nitumbiye vyono si kowuyajila

kanyhi kulipamiza yaye, nagwe hamba si koyisemwela lukulu milewo yiyo.

9 “No munhu wowose, mono koliwona lilimu lyono licemwaga

Lewiyatani, kotimba so woga, yagwe ho hasi.

10 Na hono wulisagahize, yikali muno muno,

hasina munhu mono kokwijelela kulisejelela

11 Hamba nani mono kosuma kulihoma, yapone,

hasina mono'nze hamba monga mono kosuma kwituma zizo.

12 Lece ta, nghulonjele vyono genha magulu ge Lewiyatani,

nghuloloseze wuwaha walyo, hamonga ne vilungo vyalyo.

13 Nani mono kosuma kulihinza Lewiyatani, hamba kulihoma

ne citwalo cono kowa yena naco.

14 Hamba nani mono kosuma kulyahamula zinzagasa zalyo,

hambi meno galyo nha gakogofya muno?

15 Ne makaka galyo, gena vilungo vyo wizine,

malunzwe hamonga, kanyhi gakamile nhe'bwe.

16 Nago gose, malunze limonga ne liyagwe, gakamile muno,

hamba mbeho si yosuma kukola haciligati yago.

17 Gose malunzwe hamonga, ho galece kutemanulilwa

lukulu na cinhu co cose.

18 Nalyo lifunghaga moto mu mhula zalyo, lono likwehela,

ne meso galyo, gakumelemeta kotya lono lizuwa

likusosomoka.

19 Ne malambi go moto, galawaga mwi'lomo lyalyo.

Ne vinyesela vizumhaga vikulawa mumo.

20 Sunga ne malyosi, galawilaga mu zimhula zalyo,

kotya nhe malyosi gono gakufungha mu mahanze, gono

gakunyanywa mu nyungu.

21 No wehele walyo, wosuma kukoleleza moto mu makala,

hambi malambi go moto nha gazumhaga gakulawa

mwi'lomo lyalyo.

22 Lina singo yikamile muno muno,

na wose wono vikwitang'anila nalyo, webatwaga

no woga wuwaha.

23 Na mu mukunza walyo, musina hamba cipele cimonga,

kanyhi wukangaye winha cuma.

24 Ne nhumbula yalyo yisina woga, yihwanile libwe,

nghamu, yinho luzala.

25 Na lono likwinuka, sunga na we vilungo wose,

wakwinjilwa no woga, wawa siwamanyile na vyono wonoza.

26 Hasina mumaje wo wose wono wusuma kulipela nghomele,

hamba mugoha, hamba songa, hamba ndundo, vyono

vyosuma kulitamisa.

27 Ku lubavu lwayo, cuma cihwanile mabeleje,

ne shaba, yihwanile libici lyono lidodile.

28 Hasina songa yo yose yono yolitinusa ho litize,

hamba wulitowanje ne mabwe, lyene likuwona nha

wukulitowa na mabeleje du.

29 Ne nhumbi nayo, ku lubavu lwalyo, likuwona

nhe hanze du, kanyhi hono munhu yaligumilile

migoha, lyene likuseka du.

30 Na hasi ye'nda yalyo, linha mazaye makali,

ne makaka ga mucanyha ye'nda yalyo, gakukwala

mu matope, nhe ciya co kutwanjila wuhemba mu cuga.

31 Litibulangaga lilenga,

lihwana nhe nyungu yono yikuhemula,

liwa kulavya mafulo, nha nyungu ya mafuta.

32 Nzila yalyo mwi'lenga, ying'alalaga mu nyuma yalyo

likulinoza lilenga, liwa lina mafulo mazelu swee.

33 Na mononze, musina cinji cono cosuma kwihwananizwa

nalyo, co ciwumbwa cono cisina woga hamba hadodo.

34 Lyene livilanjililaga vilimu vyose i vidunhumuzi,

lyene lyo litemile muli vilimu vyose vya mu mbago.”


Jobu 42

1 “Nayo Jobu nhondo ho yakawa kumupitula Muwaha

Mulungu yakatya,

2 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, ane manyile vyono yo

gwegwe du mono wuli vilungo,

na kanyhi vyono wudahile kwituma nghani yo yose

nha vyono wendile gwegwe du.

3 Agwe kumbuza nha vyono nhawule lo nghwijelela

kugawuza mahala gako,

ninga nane ho simanyile cinji.

Nali kulonga du nghani zono si nizimanyile yaye.

Nghani zo wizine, kanyhi nghamu kuli 'ne kuzimanya.

4 Nagwe kunonjela nha vyono nyamale, hulicize lono

wukulonga, hanyuma ho mhitule mawuzo gako.

5 Katali, nali manyile nha vyono

nali nonjeywe na wanji.

Ninga aluno, nghuwona nene no mweneco ne meso gangu.

6 Holeka aluno, nghuziwonela cevu zose zono nalonga,

nane nghunena mu manghundi ne mavu.”

Ze ndumilizo

7 Muwaha Mulungu yali yeza kukondya kulongana na Jobu, yakawa kumutya Elifazi, “Agwe Elifazi, ane ngholigwe ne likuwo ku soko yako, hamonga ne weyendi wayago wawo wejete. Vyono anye si mulonjile ze mbeka za kuli'ne, nha vyono mwitumami wangu Jobu yalonjile.
8 Holeka aluno, wusole kuli Jobu, nghongolo mupunghati, hamonga na nduwilu nazo mupunghati. Muzilavye likumbiko linyanywe ku soko yenyu. Nayo Jobu komulombelenyi nyenye wose, nane nowupitula wulombe wakwe, hamba si nomukozenyi cevu nha vyono mwali munojeye. Hambi nha si mulonjile ze mbeka ya kuli'nye nha vyono muyenyu Jobu yalonjile.”

9 Elifazi, na Bilidadi, hamonga na Zafali, wakanoza nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yawalonjela wetume. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawubocela wulombe wa Jobu.

10 Nayo Jobu yali yeza kukondya kuwalombela wendece wakwe wadatu, u Muwaha Mulungu yakamulugutiza kanyhi Jobu, yakamupela vinhu vinji kali kejete kuciza na vyono yali yena navyo ha mwamulizo.
11 Ne wanduguze za Jobu, hamonga ne walumbuze, hamonga ne walimi wayagwe, wose wakamuziza kumuwona no kulya zindigwa hamonga nayo mu kaya yakwe. Wakamuwonela lusungu no kumusindamika so magayo gose gono Muwaha Mulungu yali yamugumilila. Nawo wose, wakamupela mapesa hamonga ne nyamwidole ya zahabu.

12 Muwaha Mulungu yakamumotya muno muno Jobu, mu vilo vyakwe vya munyuma si nhe vilongozi. Jobu yakawa yena ngholo elufu yikumi na yine, na ngamiya elufu mutandatu, ng'ombe elufu nyejete, na ndogowe elufu monga.
13 Yali yena wana we cilume mupunghati, hamonga ne wana we cicekulu wadatu.
14 Ne wahinza wawa, u mulongozi kulelwa yacemwaga Yemima, we kejete Keziya, na we kadatu u mudodo, yacemwaga Keleni Hapuku.
15 Na mu vilo vivyo, mu yinze yose mwali musina wacekulu waswanu ku mumbwa yawo, nha vyono wali wawa wana wa Jobu we cicekulu. Nayo sogo yawo Jobu, yakawagawila wose wuhazi wawo hamonga ne walumbu zawo.

16 Jobu yekala myaka yigana limonga na makumi mane, lono zali zakondya kupuluta zizi, yakasuma no kuwawona ne wezukulu hamonga ne wezukulucivu.
17 Hanyuma nhondo, ho yakawa kufwa, lono yali yakondya kukombipa muno muno.
