Jobu 34

1 Elihu yakajendelela kulonga yakatya,

2 “Hambi anye nyo wanhu nha muli we mahala no wumanye,

Holeka luno muhulicize nane zono nghulonga,

3 Hambi likutu nha lijelaga zinghani,

Ndigwa swanu yimanyikaga lono wukuyijeza.

4 Ninga yo nghani yetu co weneco, kuhagula cono cili ling'ani.

5 Mwene Jobu yakwiyitya vyono yali ling'ani,

ninga Mulungu, yakulema kumuwala vyono yali ling'ani.”

6 Kanyhi yakutya, “Alu ho nosuma nhawule kulonga zo

wulambi, niwe kutya ane nili musutyi?

Heya natuga nghomele, ninga si nahodanga cinji yaye.”

7 Ati wayangu, muwene munhu yunji mono yenha yuyu Jobu?

Hambi nha si yakulajila cogopo hali Mulungu.

8 Yehwanile ne wetuma zimbi, kajendanaga ne wabi.

9 Hambi mwene nha yakutya, vyono hasina cambuzi co cose

mu wituma wuhile wo Mulungu.

10 “Holeka muhulicize anye nyo wanhu wo wizuce!

Mukwihwesa Mulungu mono yali vilungo, kokwituma zo wubi?

11 Mwene kawatendelaga wanhu mu nghango ye metumo gawo,

no kuwalipa mu nghango nha vyono wanojeye.

12 Na mbeka, Mulungu mono yali vilungo hamba

sikahodangaga yaye,

no wuling'anizi wakwe, wo we ling'ani kuli wanhu wose.

13 Hambi Mulungu kabocelaga vilungo ku wulawa kuli

munhu yunji wo wose 'mbi?

Hamba hoyuli yunji mono yamupela Mulungu mulimo wo

kuyidima yinze lo?

14 Na hono Mulungu yali yawuse muhe wo mwehelo,

15 Wanhu wose wali wafwe wawuye kanyhi mu mangundi.

16 “Alu muhulicizaje, hono muwe mwina mahala.

17 Alu anye hongo, mukumuhosa Mulungu we ling'ani?

Mukwihwesa vyono mwene si yendile ling'ani?

18 Mwene Mulungu kawaling'anizaga watemi

hamonga ne walongola,

lono weza wasina cambuzi, kanyhi wakwituma zo wubi.

19 Mwene si kawahijilaga mbejele i walongola,

hamba si yakuwawona wagoli wawe wo waswanu

si nhe waciwa, hambi mwene nha yo yawawumba wose.

20 No munhu kosuma kufwa nhinicizo ne cilo.

Hambi Mulungu nha kawatowaga wanhu wajilila

na we vilungo kawawulagaga.

21 “Hambi meso gakwe nha galangaga zinzila ze wanhu

22 Hasina wutitu tuku, wono wosuma kumuhundiciza

munya wubi hali Mulungu.

23 Mulungu yasina soko, yo kuwawicila wanhu lizuwa

lyo kuwaling'aniziza.

24 Kanyhi yasina soko yo kuwasunjilila walongola,

no kuwawusa, no kuwawika wanji wahalile hanhu hawo.

25 Hambi nha yazimanyile zose zono wakwituma,

nayo yakuwawusa mu wulongola wawo, no

kuwapondanga ne cilo.

26 Kawalipicizizaga i wabi calinze, lono wose wakuwona.

27 Vyono waleka kumutyatya mwene, wakagabedegala ne

malajilizo gakwe.

28 Wakuwanoza waciwa sunga wamulilile Mulungu

nayo Mulungu yakucihulika cililo cawo co

kulondola wutazi.

29 Na hono Mulungu yawe si yakusaka kwituma nghani yinji,

alo nani mono kosuma kumuhosa.

Na hono yawuhundice wusu wakwe, wanhu wowula wutazi.

30 Na howa hasina nghani yo yose, yono makungugo gosuma

kwituma, hamba si gosuma kuwawika i walongola, wono si

wamumanyile Mulungu, wo wawe walongola wawo.

31 Alu 'gwe Jobu, wakondya kunena wubi wako hali Mulungu,

no kwihuwiza nha vyono si kokwituma wubi kanyhi?

32 Wule wakondya kumulomba Mulungu yakulajile masutya

gako, no kuvumila nha vyono walecela lukulu kwituma

zinghani zimbi?

33 “Alu hono wukuzilema zono Mulungu ketumaga,

nha cinji wukusaka Mulungu yetume zono gwegwe wendile du?

Ninga wulamuzi, wina gwegwe go mweneco, si wangu yaye,

holeka aluno wucilonjele zono wukwihwesa.

34 No munhu wo wose we mahala, mbeka kovumila,

no munhu wo wose wo wizuce mono yakuhulika, kosuma kutya

35 vyono Jobu yakulonjela mu wu wula wumanye,

na zose zono yakulonga, zisina cambuzi.

36 Alu nye mwihwesaje ta, muli zono Jobu yakulonga,

mowona nha vyono yakulonga nha munhu mwituma zimbi.

37 Hambi nha yakonjeleza wufyule wakwe mu wubi wakwe,

kanyhi yakumwizwajiza Mulungu ha mwande yetu.”
