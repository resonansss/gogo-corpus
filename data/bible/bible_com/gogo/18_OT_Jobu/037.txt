Jobu 37

1 “Lididimo liyinozaga nhumbula yangu yiwa kucicima.

2 Ale lu anye wose muhulicize lizi lyo Mulungu,

mwi'balalusi lyono likulawa mu mulomo wakwe.

3 Mwene kaletaga mamuli gakolile mu mavunde,

ku wulawilila ku lubavu aluno lwe yisi,

sunga ko ku lubavu luyagwe.

4 Hanyuma ngholomo ye lizi lyakwe, ho yiwa kuhulikwa.

Kadidimaga kwe lizi lyo wutemi wakwe,

hanyuma limuli nalyo liwa kumulika.

5 Na mu wulajilize wo Mulungu, vinhu vinji vyo wizine

vijelaga vyono hamba si cosuma kuvimanya.

6 Kalajilizaga seluji yigwa mono'si

kanyhi kaletaga mahalavu, ne mabanhi ge mvula.

7 Kayinozaga i milimo ye wanhu yiwa si yikwitumwa,

yakuwalajila wanhu wayimanye i milimo yakwe.

8 Malimu gabitaga mu mamwina gayo,

9 Libeho lyo mukukunzo, lizaga ku wulawa kutakama,

ne mbeho nghali nayo yilawaga kusumuka.

10 Malenga gajimaga so wehele wo Mulungu,

gapituka gawa balafu.

11 Mamuli gamulikaga mu mavunde,

12 nago gamulikaga so wendezwe wo Mulungu

ganozaga zose mu yinze yose, nha vyono Mulungu yakugalajiliza.

13 Mulungu kaletaga mvula kayitonyeza yisi,

ninga, yadahile yayilete ho yiwalipicize wanhu,

hamba kuwalajila zo lusungu lwakwe.

14 “Alu, wuceheze ta hadodo Jobu, wuhulicize,

wihwese ze zinghani zo wizine, zono Mulungu ketumaga.

15 Wule, wumanyile Mulungu vyono kalavyaga wulajilize

wakwe, na vyono kaganozaga mamuli gamulice

wulawa mu mavunde?

16 Wule, wumanyile 'mbi mavunde vyono gelelaga mu

wulanga, mulimo wo wizine wo Mulungu mono yakwilaniciye

mu wumanye?

17 Agwe wugole, ninga kowona wusungu mwi'vuce,

kolo mbeho ya kutakama yikuyinyamaza yisi.

18 Alu, wule, kosuma kumutaza Mulungu, mu wutandaze wo

wulanga, no kuwunoza wuwe wung'alaye,

nhe cilole ce cuma cono cikung'alala?

19 Ale, ta, wucilonjelaje zono cimulonjele Mulungu,

hambi 'se nha cili citu, cisina yo kulonga.

20 Ane si nghusaka nhumwe, ho nongane no Mulungu,

alo socici nimupele cilejeho co kunyongomezeza?

21 “Nalu wuzelu wono wuli ku wulanga, wukung'alala muno,

wung'alale wono ase, hamba si cidahile kuwukomalila,

no wulanga wakondya kukwemwa ne mbeho.

22 Zahabu yono yikung'alala yikwijela ku lubavu lwa

kusumuka, no lukumyo lo Mulungu, lukucogofya.

23 I vilungo vyo Mulungu viwaha, hamba si cosuma

kumusejelela, mwene muswanu, kanyhi yali linga'ni

mu wikalana ne wanhu.

24 Kanyhi si nghani yo wizine yaye, lono wanhu wose

wakumogopa mwene.

Na kanyhi vyono yakuwabedegala wose wono

wakwiyitya, wena mahala.”
