Jobu 19

Mapitulo ga Jobu

1 “Nayo Jobu yakapitula yakatya,

2 Alu mwene, nha mwanhimvya muno ne zinghani zenyu?

3 Nha mwasola katali nhondo mukunhovulanga du,

sunga zafika nghanghulo yikumi.

Hamba si mukuwona cevu yaye mu nzila

yenyu yono mukuhoseza'ne.

4 Alu ninga hamba ane niwe nina masutya,

anye cono cikumutamisenyi cicici?”

5 Anye mukwihwesa vyono muli waswanu si nha ane,

na holeka mukuwusola wugaye wangu

muwitumamile, ciwe co cihomelezi cangu

co kundajila vyono ane nili musutyi.

6 Alu 'nye, hongo si mukuwona vyono

zizi zose, Mulungu yo yazitumile?

Mulungu kambicila mutego ho yangwize.

7 Langa ta, nghucenga, hamba kuhulikwa,

nghulilila wutazi, ninga hasina wuling'anizwe.

8 Mulungu kakondya kuyidinda nzila,

nane hamba si nosuma yaye kupuluta.

Kawuhundika wukolo wangu mu wutitu.

9 Kakondya kumbusiza wugoli wangu wose,

no kumbusiza lilumbo lyangu.

10 Yambenanga ku wulawa mbavu zose

kawukula no witumbilo wangu nhe libici.

11 Mulungu yangholeywe likuwo,

kanyhi yena nhunduji kuli'ne

holeka yakunhendela kotya nha nili mulugu wakwe.

12 Yakundetela lipuga lye wasilikale wahome,

wahimba likweme lipilime mwi'hema lyangu.

13 Mulungu kawawika wandugu zangu wawe kutali nane,

alu neza mujenzi kuli wono wali wamanyile.

14 Iwandugu zangu hamonga ne wendece wangu,

wose wakondya kusemwa.

15 Na wose wono wali wazoweye kuza kunamusa

ha kaya yangu, wakondya kusemwa.

Sunga no mwitumami wangu we cicekulu,

aluno yakunhendela nha nili mujenzi,

Neza nha munhu mono si wamumanyile yaye.

16 Na lono nghumucema mwitumami wangu,

mwene hamba si yakwidika yaye,

sunga na lono nghumulomba du yamhele wutazi.

17 No mugolece wangu nayo, hamba si yakusuma

kuwukangalila no wehele wangu,

sunga ne wandugu zangu, nawo hamba si wakusejelela.

18 Sunga wana wadodo wakumbedegala, no

kusekanga, lono wakumbona.

19 “Ne wendece wangu muno, nawo wakunanga ko wizine,

na wose wono wali wanyendile muno, nawo wandeka.

20 Ne nghuli yangu vyetemanuka ne mafupa gangu,

nane mhona no wumi wangu du.

21 Nye nyo wendece wangu! Ta mumbonelaje lusungu ye!

Hambi muwoko wo Mulungu, nha wakondya kunhowa.

22 Ale, nhawulenyi nanye mumhele magazo,

nha vyono Mulungu yakunozeza?

Alo wusungu wuwu wono mwakondya kumhela,

ho si wukumukwilenyi yaye?

23 “Na mbeka, nali nyende yumonga wa muli nyenye,

yakumbucile zinghani zangu zose zono nonjile,

yazandice mu citabu!

24 Hamba zali ziseswe mwi'talawe,

zandikwe ziwe cikumbucizo, zikale mumo cibitilila.

25 Ninga manyile vyono Mukombozi wangu yo mumi,

Kanyhi vyono kolokwima mu manghundi gago mwi'zuwa lye ndumilizo.

26 Hamba yiwe nghuli yangu yakondya kongomezwa nhavivi,

ninga nokwima kunze ye nghuli yangu.

27 Nomuwona Mulungu nene no mweneco ne meso gangu.

Nayo hamba si kowa mujenzi hali'ne yaye.

28 Anye mukutya, ‘Ase lu cosuma kumutyatilila wule?’

29 Ho coyituga wule soko yo kumutamisiza?

Ninga nane luno nghumutyenyi, mwilanjililaje no wulipicizwe,

No lusulo lwenyu losuma kumuletelenyi lifwa!

Munojeye mumanye vyono, U Mulungu yali wene du, yo hakimu.”
