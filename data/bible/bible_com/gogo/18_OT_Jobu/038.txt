Jobu 38

Muwaha Mulungu yakumupitula Jobu

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamupitulila Jobu mu

mukukunzo, yakamutya,

2 “Alu gwegwe nani mono wukuwuza ze mahala, mu nghani

zako ze bwete zono zisina nhunizo?

3 Witungaje nha wuli munhu,

wupitule mawuzo gono nokuwuza.

4 “Wule, wali wuli baho, lono nayiwumbila yinze?

Ale ta wulonje hono wuzimanyile.

5 Ma, mono yehwesa zo wuwaha we yinze vyono wuwe, nani?

Hamba nani mono yayijela no mulejehe?

Wule, wugamanyile mapitulo ge mawuzo gago gose?

6 Ma, wusimyo wayo wawikwa mwihi?

Hamba nani mono yaliwika libwe lyo musinji?

7 Hakali mitondo muno ya mwizuwa lilyo,

zinyelezi zemba hamonga,

ne viwumbwa vyose vya ku wulanga,

vikemba na mazi mawaha ne nyemo.

8 Mwene nani mono yayidinda milango ye lilenga,

lono lyabahucila mu'nda ye yisi?

9 Nha yo nene no Mulungu,

mono naligubika lilenga ne mavunde,

no kulipalinjiza no wutitu.

10 Nghaliwicila mipaka lilenga,

nghaliwika ku lubavu luyagwe.

11 Nghalilonjela nghatya,

‘Kowa kuza sunga ahano,

ninga si kopuluta!

Ne mawingo gako mawaha,

geze kudumila bahano.’

12 “Wule, Jobu ho lili lizuwa linji lya mu mikalile yako,

lono walajiliza wele hakali mitondo?

13 Wule, holili lizuwa linji,

lono walajiliza hakali

mitondo yiyibate yinze,

yiwakung'unhe i wabi ku wulawa mono wehundice?

14 Wuzelu we misi,

wo wunozaga matunda ne malolo gejela,

vinhu vyose viwikwe kotya nhe civwalo.

Yinze, yikawanizwaga yituje hwani nhe'longo,

hasi yo mutozo.

15 No wuzelu we misi, si wuwendezaga i wanhu wabi,

hambi nha wuwemilaga mu wituma i metumo gawo go wuwulaji.”

16 “Wule, winjiye ku mivwilo ya mu visolo vye lilenga?

Hamba kujendela mu mhango ze cisolo cayo?

17 Hamba hoyuli mono yakondizye kukulajila milango,

yo kwinjilila ku yisi yo wutitu ye wafu?

18 Wule, wuwumanyile wugazi we yinze vyono winha?

Ale ta mhitulaje hono wuzimanyile zizo.

19 Wule, wuhamanyile i hanhu hono wuzelu wulawililaga?

Hamba lisina lyo wutitu cicici?

20 Wule, kosuma kuvilajilila kono

vibite no kuvigomola kanyhi?

21 Nane nitumbiye vyono kosuma kunoza nhavivyo,

hambi nha wakombipa,

kanyhi wali wuli baho lono yinze yawumbilwa!

22 “Wule, winjiye no kuziwona mugati zinyumba ze viwutwa,

mono mbikaga seluji hamonga ne mvula ye mabwe?

23 Nane niviwikanize so mapango go wugaye,

mu vilo vyo wihomi no wulugu.

24 Wule, wuficile i hanhu hono lizuwa lisosomocelaga?

Hamba i hanhu hono mbeho ya kucilima yilawililaga?

25 Mwene nani mono yatula likolongo lye malenga ge mvula,

yakawika no wukolo we mabalalusi?

26 Na mono katonyezaga mvula i hanhu hono hasina

wanhu, nani mbi?

27 Nani mono kamwajilaga malenga i hakavu,

na mu yisi yono yinyalile,

no kucindya mumo mahanze?

28 Wule, mvula ne buhe, vina sogo yawo?

29 Hamba nani nyina ye balafu ne seluji,

30 yono yiganozaga malenga gajima gawa nha mabwe,

no wusu we cisolo wukuwa nhe talawe?

31 “Wule! Alu wugalangaje mapuga ge zinyelezi,

kosuma kuyiwopa mhuga ye cilimila,

hamba kuvihogosa viwopelo vya Oliyoni?

32 Kosuma kuzitonga zinyelezi mu vipango vyazo

no kulilongoza li dubu hamonga ne managwe?

33 Wule, wuwumanyile wulajilize wono wuwutongaga wulanga,

hamba kosuma kuwusimya wutemi wayo mu'si.

34 Wule, kosuma kwinula lizi lyako ko ku mavunde,

ho winji we malenga wukufunice?

35 Na hono wulajilize mamuli gamulice,

nago gokuziza gatye,

cili baha cikwitumamile?

36 Nani 'mbi mono kavilonjelaga viwokowoko vye likolongo,

lono likolongo lye

Nile lyomema malenga ko ku nyenje,

hamba mono kayilonjelaga nzogolo vyono mvula yikuza.

37 Nani mono kosuma kuwala mavunde kwe mahala gakwe,

hamba kugenamya ho gatonye mvula,

38 yono yigajimyaga manghundi gawa nhope?

39 “Wule, kosuma kuyipelembela simba mhelembo yayo,

no kugegusha mana gayo,

40 lono gehundika mu mhango zago,

hamba lono gagonile mu mhalika zago?

41 Nani mono kagalisaga mahowe ndigwa yago,

lono gakwanga gali ne nzala,

lono mana gayo nago gakundilila gakusaka ndigwa?
