Jobu 27

Mapitulo ga Jobu

1 Jobu yakapitula yakatya,

2 “Nane nghwilahiza mwi'tagwa lyo Mulungu, mono yali vilungo,

mono yambusiza ling'ani yangu,

no kumhela wusungu mu nhumbula yangu,

3 vyono, ane lono nghali Mulungu yamhelile wehele,

4 milomo yangu hamba si yolonga nghani yo yose 'mbi,

kanyhi no lulimi lwangu hamba si lolonga zo wulambi.

5 Nane hamba si nolonjela lukulu niwe kutya,

vyono anye mukuhosa kwe ling'ani, aa ng'o.

Langa ane nokangaza ze ling'ani yangu du, sunga

mwi'zuwa lyono nolofwila.

6 Nane nowibatiliza muno muno wiling'ano wangu

hamba si nowuleka ng'o hamba hadodo

ne ciselinji cangu hamba si cikuhosa yaye.

7 “Nawo wose wono wakuhoma no kwisumaniza nane,

yiwanojeye walipicizwe nhe wanhu wabi wono

si wali ling'ani.

8 Alu wawo wanhu wono si wamogopile Mulungu,

ho wena witumbiloci, kolo Mulungu yakuwusaka wumi wawo?

9 Na hono magayo gawagane, wukwihwesa 'mbi Mulungu

kowahulika cililo cawo?

10 Holeka yali yiwanojeye wawe wena nyhilu ye nyemo

yono Mulungu kalavyaga.

Kanyhi yali yiwanojeye wamulombe mapango gose.

11 Alu mulece nimwijizenyi zo wuwaha we

vilungo vyo Mulungu

nimulolosezenyi sunga ne mahweso go Mulungu

mono yali vilungo vyono genha.

12 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, si nghani yaye,

hambi nha mwakondya kwiwonelela nyo weneco.

Alu nhawule mukulonga nghani zo wulele?”

13 “Ale, nhavivi vyo Mulungu kawalipicizaga

i wanhu wabi, kanyhi wawulaji.

14 Wosuma kuwa na wana wenji we cilume

ninga wose wofwila ku wulugu.

Ne wana wawo walece kutuga ndigwa yono yikwiliye.

15 Na wono wosigala si wafwiye, ninga nawo wofwa ne

matamwa, sunga ne wagolece zawo wono wolekwa,

nawo hamba si wowalilila i wafu wawo.

16 Ne wanhu wabi, wadahile kuwa na fweza nyinji muno,

hamonga ne myenda minji si nhe wanhu wose.

17 Ninga munhu yunji we ling'ani,

yo koloyivwala myenda yiyo.

No munhu yunji u mwinenenyhezi,

yo koloyisola fweza yiyo.

18 I wanhu wabi, wazengaga zikaya zihwanile lipulaputya

hamba kotya cilingo co muwanda, co kwamilila migunda.

19 Na ha mudumo wawo, wogona nhe wagoli,

ninga kolo wakwinuka, wokwagana sawo yose hasina.

20 No woga wowaziza nhinicizo nhe lihwazi lye malenga.

Ne libeho lye cilo, lyowapemhelusiza kutali.

21 Nalyo libeho lya kucilima, lyowafwaguliziza

lukulu mu zikaya zawo.

22 Libeho lyolowapemhelusa lisina lusungu,

mwi'pango lyono wowa kujela kwiponya.

23 Ne libeho lilyo lyowapemhelusa, na lono wakumangha

wakutiza, lyowogofya muno ne vilungo vyalyo vyo wuwulaji.”
