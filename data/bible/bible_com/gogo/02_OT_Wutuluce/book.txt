Wutuluce 1

Wudumyanzwe, wutulilwe, hamonga no wuhinyizwe we Waisilayeli ku Misili

1 Nawo wana wa Isilayeli, wono wenjila mu yisi ye Misili hamonga no sogo yawo Yakobo, munhu ne mhuga yakwe, wali awa,
2 Lubeni, Simiyoni, Lawi, na Yuda,
3 Isakali, Zabuloni, Benyamini,
4 Dani, Nafutali, Gadi na Asheli.
5 Liwalo lyawo, wali waficile wanhu makumi mupunghati, nawo wali wo wana wa Yakobo. Ninga mwanagwe Yusufu, yali yakondya kulongola kuko ku Misili.
6 Na hanyuma yayo, yuyo Yusufu hamonga no lulelo lululya lose, wakawa wafwiza wose.
7 Ne wana wa Isilayeli wali welelile muno, wakacila konjelezeka, wakawa ne vilungo, kanyhi wakawa wenji muno muno, sunga ne yisi yiyilya ye Misili, yikamema weneco wose.

8 Hanyuma yayo, yakenuka mutemi mupya, mono yali si yamumanyile Yusufu hamba nhavi, yakawa mutemi mu yisi yiyo ya Misili.
9 Ale, yuyo yakawalonjela wanhu wakwe yakatya, “Ta mulanje, wanhu wawa Waisilayeli weza wenji, kanyhi wali vilungo wacicizize na sese.
10 Holeka ciwanozezaje mahala, ho walece kujendelela kwilela no konjelezeka, yilece kuwa hono wijele wulugu, lunji welunze ne walugu wetu wacihome ase, wawe kuwuka na mu yisi yetu. Holeka ciwalondolelaje nzila yo kuwemila, ho walece kucila kwilela, no konjelezeka no kuwa wenji muno.”
11 Hobaho Wamisili, wakawa kuwawika wemilizi, wono wowetumya wanhu wa Isilayeli, no kuwadindimiza kwituma milimo mizito muno. Ale, Waisilayeli, wakawa kumuzenjela mutemi Falawo makaya ga Pitomu, na Lamesesi, so wuwicila viwutwa vyo mutemi.
12 Ninga nha vyono Wamisili wacila kuwagaza Waisilayeli, nawo Waisilayeli, vyo wakacila kwilela no konjelezeka no kuwa wenji, wakatandala mu yisi yose. Nawo Wamisili wakenjilwa muno no woga ku soko ye Waisilayeli.
13 Ne nghani yiyo yikawanoza Wamisili wawadumyanze muno muno Waisilayeli, no kuwahinyiza muno muno kwe milimo mizito.
14 Wakawapela mikalile yo wusungu muno muno so wituma milimo mikamu kanyhi mizito, yo wunyanya cokaa no wufyatula matofali, hamonga na yo wuzenga makaya gawo, no kulima migunda yawo, hamba kuwawonela lusungu yaye.

15 Hodu u mutemi wa Misili, yakawa kulongana ne wadala wanghunga we Cihebelaniya, yunji yacemwaga litagwa lyakwe Shifula, u muyagwe nayo Puwa, yakawatya,
16 “Kolo mukwinjila mu mulimo wenyu wo kuwataziliza wacekulu we Cihebelaniya lono wakusawa, muwawulagaje wana wose we cilume wono wakulelwa, langa wana we cicekulu, mulece kuwawulaga yaye.”
17 Ninga wadala wawa, wali wamogopile Mulungu, holeka siwaketuma vyono wali walajilizwe no mutemi. Ninga wene, wakawaleka wana wose we cilume womi.
18 Nayo mutemi wa Misili yakawacema wawalya wadala, yakawa kuwawuza yakutya, “Alu nhawulenyi kanyhi mukunoza nha vivi? Nhawule mukuwaleka i wana we cilume womi?”

19 Nawo wakawa kumupitula wakatya, “Gwe mutemi, i wacekulu we Cihebelaniya si wehwanile nhe wacekulu we Cimisili yaye, Wene wakusawa mbela mbela, lono ase cikali hamba sinawaficila, wene wakuwa wakondya kusawa.”
20 Na vyono wadala wamogopaga Mulungu, nayo Mulungu yakawatendela viswanu wa walya wadala, ne wanhu wa Isilayeli wakenjipa.
21 Nawo wana wa Isilayeli wakajendelela kwilela no konjelezeka, no kucila kuwa ne vilungo muno muno.
22 Nayo Falawo yakawalajiliza wanhu wakwe yakawatya, “Mwana we cilume wowose we Cihebelaniya mono kolelwa, mumutagaje mwi'kolongo lya Nile, langa i wana we cicekulu, muwalekaje mulece kuwawulaga.”


Wutuluce 2

U wulelwe wa Musa

1 Na mu madaha gago, munhu yunji wa mu mhuga ya Lawi, yakatola mucekulu we mhuga yiyo ya Lawi,
2 Nayo yakamulelela mwana we cilume. Yali yeza kumuwona mwana yuyo vyono muswanu muno, yakamuhundika myezi midatu.
3 Ninga yali yeza kuwona vyono si kosuma kujendelela kumuhundika kanyhi, yakasola litenga lyono lyali yihonwe ne madetelulenga, yakalihiliwa na lami hamonga ne ngholeko, yakamuwika yuyo mwana mugati, yakalisosomeka mu madete lulenga ha mwejelele we likolongo lya Nile.
4 Nayo lumbulye lyo mwana yuyo, yakawa yamulindilize yemile cidaha, yalanjiliye cono comwijelela yuyo mwana.

5 Ale baho, yakawa kuza u mwana we cicekulu wa Falawo ho yehovuje mumo mwikolongo, lono wetumami wakwe we cicekulu, wajendajendaga ha mwejelele we likolongo. Nayo yakaliwona kwa nhinicizo litenga yisosomece mugati ye madete lulenga. Yakamutuma mwitumami wakwe we cicekulu yakalisole yamuletele.
6 Nayo yuyo mwana we cicekulu wa Falawo, yali yeza kuligubula litenga lilyo, yakamuwona mwana mugalika wa cilume mugati, yakulila. Yakamuwonela lusungu muno, yakatya, “Ayu yo yumonga we wana we Wahebelaniya.”
7 Nayo lumbulye yo mwana yuyo yakamuwuza yakatya, “Wule, kokwenda nghakulondolele mucekulu we Cihebelaniya mono kosuma kukukoceza mwana yuyo?”

8 Nayo yakapitula yakatya, “Heya, dedede, bite kanondolele.” Nayo muhinza yuyo yakamangha mbela mbela, yakamuleta u nyina yo mwana yuyo mugalika.
9 Nayo mwana we cicekulu wa Falawo yakamutya yuyo mucekulu, “Gwe yaya, musolaje mwana yuyuno wukanyoseze, nane nokulipa.” Mucekulu yuyo yakamusola mwana no kumosa.
10 Mwana yuyo yali yeza kuwa yawiluka, u mucekulu yuyo yakamutegula kuli mwana we cicekulu wa Falawo, nayo yakamubocela yakawa mwanagwe. Yakawa kumucemela litagwa lyakwe Musa, yakutya, “Vyono namunimula mu malenga.”

Musa yakutiziza ku Midiyani

11 Nayo Musa lono yali yawiluka yawa munhu mututu, mwi'zuwa linji yakabita kuwalamusa wanduguze Wahebelaniya. Yakawona vyono Wamisili wawetumyaga no kuwadindimiza kwituma cifugwa fugwa. Kanyhi yakamuwona Mumisili yunji yakumutowa Muhebelaniya yunji muyagwe ne wanduguze za Musa.
12 Nayo Musa yali yeza kulanga ako na ako, hamba kuwona munhu mono yamulanjiliye, hodu yakawa kumuzumhila yu yulya Mumisili yakawa kumuwulaga, yakawufuciza muwili wakwe mu musawawa.
13 Ale, na mwi'zuwa lye kejete Musa yakabita kanyhi kuli wanduguze, na kuko yakawawona Wahebelaniya wejete wakwitowa. Nayo yakamulonjela yumonga wawo mono yali yo musutyi, yakamutya, “Alu nye wazelelo, nhawulenyi mukwitowa, nanye kumbe ho muli Wahebelaniya wose?”

14 Munhu yuyo nayo yakamupitula yakatya, “Alu mwene 'gwe, mono yakuwicile nani wuciteme, na kanyhi wuwe mulamuzi muli sese? Hamba weza kusaka wumbulaje nane, nha vyono wukamuwulaga ayulya Mumisili lo?” Baho, Musa yakenjilwa na woga muno, yakatya, “Nye! Kumbe wanhu wakondya kuzimanya zono netuma.”
15 Nayo Falawo yali yeza kuzihulika zizo zono Musa yetuma, hodu yakawa kwanza kumulondola Musa yamuwulaje.

Ninga Musa yakatiza yakabita kwikala ku yisi ya Midiyani.
16 Ninga mwi'zuwa linji lono Musa yali yekaye he'sima, baho i wana mupunghati we cicekulu wa Lewuyeli mono kacemwaga Yetelo, u mukohani wa Midiyani, wakaza kunega malenga he'sima lilyo, wakamemya mapugu, ho wang'wese mitugo yo sogo yawo, zingholo ne zimhene.
17 Wadimi wanji wakawawinga wawo wahinza. Ninga Musa yaza kuziwona zizo, yakabita kuwataziza, yakazing'wesa ne zimitugo zawo.
18 Nawo wali weza kuwuya kuli Yetelo sogo yawo. Sogo yawo yakawawuza yakatya, “Alu nhawulenyi zuwali nha mwiyandila muno kuwuya?”
19 Nawo wahinza wakamupitula sogo yawo wakatya, “Mumisili yunji kaciponya mu miwoko ye wadimi, kacinejela ne malenga, kazing'wesa ne zimitugo zetu.”
20 Nayo Yetelo yakawawuza kanyhi wanagwe yakatya, “Alu munhu yuyo yali hayi mwene? Hamba nhawule kanyhi mumuleka kuko? Alu mubite mukamulete cilizane nayo zindigwa.”
21 Musa yakavumila ku wikala baho, nayo Yetelo yakawa kumukombolela mwanagwe we cicekulu mono yacemwaga Zipola yamutole.
22 Zipola yakamulelela Musa mwana wa cilume. Musa yakamucemela yuyo mwana litagwa lya Gelishomu, yakutuniliza yakutya, “Ane nili mutizizi mono yisi yiyi.”

23 Na yali yeza kupuluta myaka minji, yuyo Falawo mutemi wa Misili yakafwa, ninga Waisilayeli wali wakali wajendeleye mu wuwanda, akuno wakulila, wakulondola wutazi. Cililo cawo cikamuficila Mulungu,
24 nayo Mulungu yakacihulika cililo cawo, yakacikumbucila nhavivilya ne cilagane cakwe muli Abulahamu, na Isaka, na Yakobo.
25 Yakawuwona no wuwanda we Waisilayeli, Mulungu yakawakamisa.


Wutuluce 3

Mulungu yakumucema Musa

1 Mwi'zuwa linji lono Musa yali yali mu wudimi we zimitugo zo mukwewe Yetelo u mukohani wa Midiyani, yazitegula zimitugo ku nhumbi nzagwe ye linyika, yakafika sunga ho he 'tunda lyo Mulungu lya Holebu lyono likucemwa Sinayi.
2 Malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu yakejelela mu cititika kuli Musa, yehwanile nhe 'lambi lyo moto. Nayo Musa yalanje nhavi, yakawona vyono cititika cacile moto, ninga hamba si cikuticimila yaye.
3 Musa yakehwesa yakatya, “Kaa nye, nghani yiyi njenzi ayi. Alu si kotya cititika cikwaka moto? Lekata sejelele behi ta, nghalanje.”
4 Muwaha Mulungu yali yeza kumuwona Musa yakusejelela, baho baho yakawa kumucema ku wulawilila mugati ye cititika, yakutya, “Musa gwe! Musa!” Nayo Musa yakapitula yakatya, “Ane nili baha.”

5 Mulungu yakamutya, “Ale, wulanjilile, wulece kusejelela behi muno muno na baha. Wuvule malatu gako mu magulu gako, hambi bahano hono wimile, nha hanhu hono helile.
6 Ane nane yo nene mono nili Mulungu we walezi wako, nili Mulungu wa Abulahamu, no Mulungu wa Isaka no Mulungu wa Yakobo.” Musa yakawa kwigubiciza ku wusu wakwe, hambi nha yogopa kwidoma no Mulungu.

7 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kumutya, “Gwe Musa, ane nakondya kuwuwona wugaye we wanhu wangu wa ku Misili, nicihulika ne cililo cawo, vyono wakusaka waponywe ku wulawa mu miwoko ye wemilizi wawo, wono wakuwetumya ciwanda wanda. Nane nizimanyile zose zo wugazwe wawo.
8 Holeka aluno nhima ho niwaponye ku wulawa mu miwoko ye Wamisili. Niwawuse mu yisi ya Misili, niwategule sunga ko ku yisi swanu. Yisi yono yina moso, hamonga no wumemelecele wo wuswanu wose, yisi yono aluno wekaye wa Kanani, ne Wahiti, ne Waamoli, ne Wapelizi, ne Wahivi, ne Wayebusi.
9 Heya mbeka, nakondya kucihulika cililo ce wanhu wangu, nakondya kuwona sunga na vyono Wamisili wakuwagaza muno muno.
10 Holeka aluno nghukutuma kuli Falawo mutemi we Misili, ho wukawawuse wanhu wangu Waisilayeli mu yisi yiyo.”

11 Ninga Musa yakamupitula Mulungu yakatya, “Alu ane waco nili nani. Ho nosuma nhawule kumubitila mutemi Falawo, niwawuse wana wa Isilayeli mu yisi yiyo ya Misili?”
12 Ninga Mulungu yakamutya Musa, “Ane nowa nili hamonga nagwe, na kolo wakondya kuwawusa wanhu ku wulawa ku Misili, waze wahembe nene mwi'tunda alino, cico cowa co citanjilo cono colajila vyono, ane yo nene mono nghutumile.”
13 Ninga Musa yakapitula yakatya, “Kolo nghubita kuli Waisilayeli no kuwatya, ‘U Mulungu we walezi wenyu yo yanhumile kuli'nye,’ nawo wombuza watye, ‘Alu litagwa lyakwe nani?’ Nane niwapitule niwe kutya nhawule?”

14 Mulungu nayo yakamutya, Musa, “ANE YO NENE, MONO YO NENE,” wubite wukawalonjele wutye, “ANE YO NENE yo yanhumile kuli'nye.”
15 Wuwalonjele wawo Waisilayeli wutye, Ane, no Muwaha Mulungu, no Mulungu we walezi wawo, u Mulungu wa Abulahamu, no Mulungu wa Isaka, no Mulungu wa Yakobo, yo yanhumile kuli'nye. Lilino lye 'tagwa lyangu cibitilila, na lilino lye 'tagwa lyangu lyono sunga na we zindelo zono zikuza wanojeye wanyheme.
16 Holeka aluno wubite wukawakonghole hamonga i walongola wose we Waisilayeli, wuwalonjele vyono Ane, no Muwaha Mulungu, no Mulungu we walezi wawo, u Mulungu wa Abulahamu, no Mulungu wa Isaka, no Mulungu wa Yakobo, nilajila kuli'gwe. Wuwalonjele nha vyono, ane niza ku soko yawo, na vyono nakondya kuziwona zose zono Wamisili wakuwatendela.
17 Holeka nane nadumula nghani vyono, niwawuse mu yisi yiyo ya Misili, hanhu hono wakuwatendela ko wukali, niwategule ku yisi yono yina moso, hamonga no wumemelecele wo wuswanu wose, yisi ye Wakanani ne Wahiti, ne Waamoli, ne Wapelizi, ne Wahivi, hamonga ne Wayebusi.

18 “Nawo wanhu wangu wokuhulika du muli zose zono kowalonjela. Nagwe yowa yikunojeye wubite hamonga ne walongola we Waisilayeli, kuli mutemi we Misili, mukamulonjele mutye nhavi, ‘U Muwaha Mulungu we Wahebelaniya, kelajila kuli'se. Holeka aluno wucikombole vilo vidatu, cibite ku nyika, cikamulavizye makumbiko u Muwaha Mulungu wetu.’
19 Ninga manyile vyono mwene mutemi we Misili si kokwenda kumukombolenyi yaye, sunga langa kolo yakunozezwa vilungo, ho yamukombolenyi.
20 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, nokwitumamila vilungo vyangu, nane noyilipiciza yisi ya Misili kwe mezino mawaha gono nogetuma muli yene. Na hanyuma ya zizo, nayo komukombolenyi mubite.”

21 “Nane nowanoza Wamisili wakupele cogopo ciwaha muno, na kolo wanhu wangu weza kutuluka no kuwuka kuko, walece kutuluka wali mawoko.
22 Langa wacekulu wose we Ciisilayeli, wowabitila we cimanyi wayawo we Cimisili, sunga na kuli mucekulu wowose we Cimisili munhu na kaya yakwe, nawo wawalombe wawapele vivwalo no wihambo we zahabu, ne fweza. Nawo Waisilayeli wawavwice i wana wawo we cilume na we cicekulu, kanyhi wasoleleze na cocose du ca mu zisawo ze Wamisili.”


Wutuluce 4

1 Nayo Musa yakamupitula Mulungu yakumutya, “Alu ma, yisolaga lunji Waisilayeli walece kunyihuwila, hamba kuhuliciza kuli zono nghuzilonga. Alu 'ne ho nonoza wule, hono watye vyono agwe go Muwaha Mulungu si wilajiye kuli'ne?”
2 Muwaha Mulungu nayo yakamuwuza Musa yakatya, “Alu mwene mu muwoko wako wibate cici?” Nayo Musa yakatya, “Nibate nghome.”

3 Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Ale wuyigumilaje nghome yiyo hasi.” Musa yali yeza kuyigumila nghome yiyo hasi, nghome yikahunduka nzoka, nayo Musa yakawa kumangha yakuyitiza.
4 Mulungu yakawa kumutya Musa, “Musa, winamaje wuyibate nzoka ku mucila wayo, wuyinule kucanyha.” Musa yakenama, yakayibata nzoka mu mucila wayo, nayo yikapituka yikawa nghome kanyhi.
5 Hodu Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kutya, “Ale, wubite kuli Waisilayeli wukanoze nhavivyo, ho wehuwile vyono u Muwaha Mulungu we walezi wawo, u Mulungu wa Abulahamu no Mulungu wa Isaka no Mulungu wa Yakobo, mbeka kakulawila.”

6 Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa kanyhi yakatya, “Ale lu, winjize muwoko wako mugati ye kanzo yako wuwujelece ha mhambaga yako.” Musa yakamuhulika Mulungu, yakawa kunoza nhavivyo. Nayo yali yeza kuwulavyaniza muwoko wakwe, kumbe muwoko wose wukawa watuga wutamwa wo wukoma, wukawa weza muzelu swee nha cokaa.
7 Mulungu yakamutya kanyhi, “Ale, wuwinjizaje kanyhi le muwoko wako mugati ye kanzo yako, wuwujelece ha mhambaga yako.” Nayo Musa yakanoza nhavivyo, ninga muhinzo wuwuno yali yeza kuwulavyaniza muwoko wakwe, kumbe wukawa weza muswanu du telele, winha muwili wakwe wose.
8 Mulungu yakatya, “Ale, hono walece kukwihuwila hamba kukuhulika so lipoto lilongozi, ninga lipoto alino lyowanoza wakuhulice kamonga.
9 Ninga yiwezaga lunji so mapoto gagano gose vyono mejete, walece hamba kukwihuwila, na hono walemele lukulu kuzihulika zono wukuwalonjela, ale, wuneje malenga madodo ga mwi'kolongo lya Nile, wugamwaje hasi. Nago malenga gago, gohunduka kamonga baho hasi gawe sakami.”

10 Ninga Musa yakatya, “Ng'o Muwaha Mulungu, nghukunena wulece kunhuma nene, hambi waco'ne nha si nili mulonji, hamba nghali si nawa mulonji ku wulawa lono wamulila kulongana nane. Hambi ane nha nili muzito ku wulonje, nongaga nili mhola mhola, sunga no lulimi lwangu nalo luzito.”
11 Muwaha Mulungu yakamutya Musa, “Gwe Musa, mwene mono kamupelaga munhu mulomo, nani? Hamba nani mono kamunozaga munhu kawa yali ciziwi, hamba kawa yali cibwibwi? Hamba nani mono kamupelaga munhu wuwone, hamba mono kamunozaga kawa yali cibofu? Alu wumanye vyono yo nene no Muwaha Mulungu mono nizinozaga zizo zose.
12 Ale, holeka alu, wubite! Ane nokutaza mu wulonje wako, kanyhi yo nene mono nokupela zinghani zono kozilonga.”
13 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, mwene Musa yakapitula yakatya, “Gwe Muwaha Mulungu ng'o ye, dedede, nghukunena wutume munhu yunji.”

14 Na ku soko ya zizo nhondo, Mulungu yakamukolelwa Musa likuwo, yakawa kutya, “Alu mwene ma u muwahawo Haluni u Mulawi? Ane manyile vyono mwene mulonji, mono kosuma kulonga. Lanje nalu mbeka ayo yakuza yakuwone, nayo kosangalala muno kolo yakukuwona.
15 Holeka kosuma kulongana nayo no kumulonjela zose zono yanojeye yazilonje. Nane nowa nili hamonga nanye mu wulonje wenyu wose vyono muli wejete, nane nomwijizenyi zose zono mokwituma wose vyono muli wejete.
16 Nayo Haluni yo kowa mulonji wako, nayo yo kowa kulongana ne wanhu, kowa wo mulomo wako. Nagwe kowa nho Mulungu, mono kowa kumulonjela Haluni zono nayo kowa kuzilonga.
17 Holeka wusoleleze ne nghome yiyino, hambi nha yo yokupela wusume wo kunoza mapoto.”

Musa yakuwuya kanyhi ku Misili

18 Nayo Musa yakawa kubita kuli mukwewe Yetelo, yakawa kumulonjela yakutya, “Nghukunena wunghombole mbuye ku Misili ku wandugu zangu, mbite nghawalanje hono wakali womi hamba ng'o.” Nayo Yetelo mukwewe hamba si yamulemeza, langa yakamukombola no kumwilaga yabite.

19 Nayo Musa lono yali yakali mu Midiyani, baho Muwaha Mulungu yakamulawila yakamutya, “Ale Musa, wuwuye ku Misili, hambi wawalya wose wono wahilaga kukuwulaga nha wakondya kufwa.”
20 Musa yakawa kuwasola mugolece wakwe, hamonga ne wanagwe, yakawapaciza mu ndogowe, yakawa kutuluka hamonga nawo, sunga ko ku Misili, yakayisola ne nghome yiyilya yono Mulungu yamulajiliza yayisole.

21 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa kanyhi yakatya, “Gwe Musa alu vyono weza kuwuya ku Misili, wugalajilaje hali mutemi mapoto gose gono nghupeleye vilungo vyo kwitumila, ninga wulanjilile, wulece kuleka hamba lipoto limonga yaye. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, ane nomunoza yawe yasina cihuliko. Nayo hamba si kosuma kuwakombola wanhu ho wawuce mu yisi ya Misili.
22 Baho nagwe wumulonjele Falawo wutye, ‘Ane no Muwaha Mulungu, yo nene mono nghutya nhavi, Isilayeli yo mwanangu we cilume u mwanangu mulongozi kulelwa.
23 Nane nghukulonjela vyono, wumukombole mwanangu yabite, ho yasume kuhemba 'ne, ninga nagwe wukulema. Ale hono yili nhavivyo, alu nane nomuwulaga u mwanago mulongozi kulelwa.’ ”

24 Na lono Musa yali yapanjile mu nghambi, mu nzila, lono yabitaga ku Misili, u Muwaha Mulungu yakamwagana Musa baho ha nghambi, yakasaka kumuwulaga.
25 Nayo Zipola u mugolece wakwe, yakasola libwe yikali, yakamwalamya u mwanagwe we cilume, yakayisola yiyo nzunga yono yayitema, yakamupamiza Musa mu magulu gakwe ne nzunga yiyo.
26 Na ku soko ye litumo lilyo lye calamo co mwanagwe, Zipola yakamutya Musa, “Agwe wuli mulume wangu so sakami yo walame.” Holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu si yasaka kanyhi kumuwulaga Musa.

27 Hanyuma Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Haluni yakumutya, “Ale, wubite wuketang'anile na Musa ku nyika.” Nayo Haluni yakawa kubita, wakawa kwitang'anila na Musa he 'tunda lyono lyelile, nayo Haluni wali weza kwiwona na Musa, yakamunonela muno.
28 Nayo Musa yakawa kumuloloseza Haluni zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yamulonjela, lono yamulonjelela vyono yawuye ku Misili, yakamuloloseza sunga na ze mapoto gono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza yaganoze kuko ku Misili.
29 Hodu Musa na Haluni wakawa kubita ku Misili, wakawa kuwakongholela hamonga i walongola wose we Waisilayeli.
30 Nayo Haluni yakawaloloseza zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yamulonjela Musa. Nayo Musa yakawa kuganoza mapoto gose ha meso ge wanhu wose.
31 Nawo wanhu wakehuwila. Nawo wali weza kuhulika vyono Muwaha Mulungu kawaziza, kawona na vyono wakugazwa muno, hodu, wakenamya mitwe yawo hasi, wakahemba.


Wutuluce 5

Musa na Haluni wakumubitila mutemi wa Misili

1 Baho, Musa na Haluni wakawa kumubitila mutemi we Misili, wakamutya, “U Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli yakutya nhavi, ‘Wuwakombole wanhu wangu wabite, ho wasume kunozeza lizuwa lye nyemo mu nyika so cogopo ca kuli'ne.’ ”
2 Nayo mutemi yakawawuza yakatya, “Alo yuyo Muwaha Mulungu yo nani nayo? Hamba nosuma nhawule kumuhulika ho niwakombole Waisilayeli wabite? Ane si nghumumanya yaye yuyo Muwaha Mulungu, hamba si nosuma kuwalecela lukulu Waisilayeli wabite.”

3 Nawo Musa na Haluni wakapitula wakatya, “U Mulungu we Wahebelaniya kelajila kuli'se. Holeka wucikombole vilo vidatu, cibite ku nyika cikamulavizye makumbiko u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu. Vyono hono cilece kwituma nhavivi, mwene kociwulaga ase wose so matamwa hamba ko wulugu.”
4 Nayo mutemi yakawatya Musa na Haluni, “Alu mwene nye, alo co cicici, mukuwanoza wanhu walece kwituma milimo yawo? Walecenyi wawo wetumami wajendelele ne milimo yawo!
5 Alu, ta mulanje, vyono anye mweza muli wenji muno kuciza sunga ne Wamisili, alu kanyhi mukusaka mulece kwituma milimo!”

6 Na mwizuwa lilyo, mutemi Falawo yakawalajiliza wemilizi we Cimisili, hamonga ne wanyamhala we Ciisilayeli, yakawatya,
7 “Mulecele lukulu kuwapela kanyhi wanhu, mahanze go kuhanzahanza ne lilongo lyo kufyatula matofali, nha vyono mwali muzoweye kuwapela. Langa muwalekaje wabite wakelondolele weneco.
8 Ninga anye, musace wanoze liwalo lililya du lilongozi lye matofali, lilece kucepuka hamba litofali limonga bwete. Hambi nha wasina milimo yo kwituma, na yo soko yono yikuleka wakusaka niwakombole wabite wakamulavizye makumbiko Mulungu wawo!
9 Holeka muwetumyaje kwa vilungo muno, wawe mu mulimo mu mapango gose, ho walece kutuga kanyhi cilejeho co kuhuliciziza zinghani zo wulambi.”

10 Nawo i wemilizi, hamonga ne wanyamhala we Ciisilayeli, wakabita, wakawalonjela Waisilayeli wakatya, “Mutemi katya vyono, si kosuma kanyhi kumupelenyi mahanze go kunozeza matofali.
11 Langa katya, vyono munojeye nyenye nyo weneco, mukelondolele gago mahanze ho hose hono mosuma kugatuga. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, mojendelela kunoza matofali ge liwalo lililya du.”
12 Holeka wanhu wose wakawa kwiyagala ako na ako mu yisi yose ya Misili, mu wulondola gago mahanze.
13 Nawo i wemilizi wakeyoha kuwaswaswaniza, ho wasume kunoza nhondo zose liwalo lililya du lye matofali, nha vyono wanozaga lono wali wakali wakupegwa mahanze.
14 Nawo iwemilizi we Cimisili, wakawatowanga i wanyamhala we Ciisilayeli wono wawawika ho wawe walanjilizi we milimo, wakuwatya, “Alo nhawulenyi lu, si mukunoza liwalo lililya lye matofali lyono mwali kunoza halulongozi?”

15 Wanyamhala we Cisilayeli wakamubitila mutemi Falawo wakamung'ung'ulicila wakutya, “Alu mutemi, nhawule wukucitendela nhavivi?
16 Ase si cikupegwa yaye, mahanze go kunozeza matofali ninga hamba nhavivyo cikali kulonjelwa vyono cinoze matofali! Kunji kanyhi cikutowa. Ninga akuno kunji i wanhu wako weneco wo wali mu masutya.”

17 Nayo mutemi Falawo yakawapitula yakatya, “Lanjenyi ta, muli wadoba nye, hamba si mukusaka kwituma milimo yaye, na yiyi yo soko yono yikuleka mukusaka nimukombolenyi mukamulavizye makumbiko Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.
18 Alu hodu mubite mbela muketume milimo! Kanyhi hamba si mopelelwa lukulu mahanze go kunozeza matofali, ninga mukali munojeye munoze liwalo lililya lilongozi du lye matofali.”
19 Nawo wanyamhala wali weza kulonjelwa vyono, yiwanojeye wanoze matofali nhondo zose, ge liwalo lililya du, nhe liwalo lyono wanozaga ha lulongozi, hodu wakamanya nha vyono alu langa, weza wali mu wugaye wuwaha.

20 Nawo wanyamhala lono wawukaga ha mwande yo mutemi Falawo, wakawagana Musa na Haluni wawalindiye.
21 Wakawalonjela Musa na Haluni, wakawatya, “U Muwaha Mulungu kaziwona zose zono mwitumile, holeka nayo komulipicizenyi, vyono mumunozize mutemi hamonga ne walongola wakwe wacisule 'se. Hambi nha mwawapela soko yo kuciwulajila 'se.”

Musa yakumung'ung'ulicila Mulungu

22 Musa yakawuya kuli Muwaha Mulungu, yakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, alu nhawule wukuwaletela magazo wanhu wako? Hamba socici wukanhuma ho nize kukuno?
23 Na ku wulawa nali neza kubita kuli mutemi Falawo, no kumuloloseza zono wanhuma, hodu nayo yakawa kwanza kuwatendela vibi. Nagwe hamba si wanoza cinji ho wuwakomboze!”


Wutuluce 6

1 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamutya Musa, “Alu langa Musa, ho koziwona zono nozituma kuli yuyo mutemi Falawo. Ane nomudindimiza yawakombole wanhu wangu wabite. Nane mbeka nomudindimiza yawawinje wawuce mu yisi yakwe.”

Mulungu yakumucema Musa

2 Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.
3 Mono nawalawila Abulahamu, na Isaka, na Yakobo nha Mulungu Mudaha Vyose, ninga hamba sinemanyiza kuli wene kwe litagwa lyangu lyono lyelile, lyo Muwaha Mulungu.
4 Hamba nhavivyo, nghanoza cilagane cangu nawo, kanyhi nghawehuwiza vyono nowapela yisi ya Kanani, yisi yono wekala katali nha wajenzi.
5 Alu nakondya kucihulika cililo ce Waisilayeli, vyono Wamisili wakuwetumya milimo mizito, nane alu nacikumbucila cico cilagane cangu.
6 Ale, wuwalonjele Waisilayeli vyono ane nghuwatya nhavi, ‘Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, nane nomuponyenyi, kanyhi nomuwicenyi welecele, mu wulawa mu wuwanda we Wamisili. Nowinula muwoko wangu we vilungo, niwaletele wulipicizwe wo kogofya, nane nomuponyenyi.
7 Nane nomunozenyi muwe wanhu wangu, nane nowa Mulungu wenyu. Nanye ho momanya vyono yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kolo nakondya kumuwusenyi mu wuwanda mu yisi ya Misili.
8 Nane nomutegulenyi sunga ko ku yisi yono nakondya kuyiwagula, ku so wihuwizo wono namwihuwiziza Abulahamu, na Isaka, na Yakobo, vyono nowapela. Holeka nane nomupelenyi nyenye yiwe sawo yenyu, ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.’ ”
9 Nayo Musa yakawa kubita kuwalonjela zizi zose Waisilayeli, ninga wene hamba si wamuhulika yaye, vyono zinhumbula zawo zali zakondya kutulilwa no wusungu ku soko yo wuwanda wawo muzito.

10 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
11 “Ale, wubite wukamulonjele Falawo mutemi we Misili wutye nhavi, ‘Yimunojeye yawakombole Waisilayeli wawuce mu yisi yakwe.’ ”
12 Ninga nayo Musa yakapitula yakatya, “Gwe Muwaha Mulungu, Waisilayeli weneco du hamba si wakuhulika yaye, alu mutemi Falawo yo kohulika hongo? Nane nha vyono ninha, si nili mulonji yaye.”
13 Muwaha Mulungu yakawalajiliza Musa na Haluni yakatya, “Muwalonjele Waisilayeli hamonga no mutemi Falawo, vyono yo nene mono nimulajilizenyi muwawuse Waisilayeli wawuce mu Misili.”

Liwalo lye zimhuga za Musa na Haluni

14 Lubeni u mwana mulongozi wa Yakobo, yakawa yena wana we cilume wane, nawo wali awa. Hanoci, Palu, Hezeloni, na Kalimu, wali wo walezi we zimhuga zono zikalawilila mu matagwa gawo.
15 Nayo Simiyoni yali yena wanagwe wali mutandatu, nawo wali awa, Yemuyeli, Yamini, Ohadi, Yacini, Zohali, na Shawuli, u mwana we cilume mono yali mulelwe no mucekulu we Cikanani, wali wo walezi we zimhuga zono zikalawilila mu matagwa gawo.
16 Lawi nayo yali yena wana we cilume wadatu, nawo wali awano, Gelishoni, na Kohati, na Melali, wali wo walezi we zimhuga zono zikalawilila mu matagwa gawo. Lawi yekala myaka yigana limonga na makumi madatu na mupunghati ya mu wikale wakwe.
17 Gelishoni nayo yakawa na wana we cilume wejete, nawo wali awano, Libuni, na Shimeyi, nawo wakawa na wana wenji muno.
18 Nayo Kohati yakawa yena wana we cilume wane, nawo wali awano, Amulamu, Izihali, Hebuloni, na Uziyeli. Kohati yekala myaka yigana limonga na makumi madatu na midatu.
19 Nayo Melali yakawa na wana we cilume wejete, nawo wali awano, Mali, na Mushi. Wawa wo we mhuga ya Lawi hamonga ne wana wawo.

20 Amulamu yakamutola Yokebedi u lumbulye lyo sogwe Kohati, nayo yakamulelela wana we cilume wejete, Haluni na Musa. Nayo Amulamu yekala myaka yigana limonga na makumi madatu na mupunghati.
21 Izihali nayo yali yena wana we cilume wadatu, nawo wali awano, Kola, Nefeli, na Zicili.
22 Nayo Uziyeli, yali yena wana we cilume wadatu, nawo wali awano, Mishayeli, Elizafani, na Sitili.

23 Nayo Haluni yakamutola Eliseba, u mwana we cicekulu wa Aminadabu u lumbulye wa Nasoni, nayo yakamulelela wana we cilume awano, Nadabu, Abihu, Eleyezali, na Itamali.
24 Nayo Kola yakawa na wana we cilume wadatu, nawo awano, Asili, Elikana, na Abiyasafu, wali wo walezi wo wugawanuce we nghungugo ye Wakola.
25 Nayo Eliyeza u mwana we cilume wa Haluni, yakamutola yumonga muyagwe ne wana we cicekulu wa Putiyeli, mono yamulelela u mwana we cilume, litagwa Finehasi. Ale wawa wo wali wawaha we zimhuga zo mulango wa Lawi.

26 Wawa Haluni na Musa, wo wawa wono Muwaha Mulungu yalongana nawo yakutya, “Ale, muwawusaje we nghungugo ya Isilayeli, wawuce mu yisi ya Misili.”
27 Kanyhi wo wanhu wawo wono wamulonjela mutemi Falawo, yawakombole Waisilayeli.

Lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu kuli Musa na Haluni

28 Muwaha Mulungu lono yalonga na Musa kuko ku yisi ya Misili,
29 yamulonjela yakatya, “Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu. Holeka wumulonjelaje Falawo mutemi wa Misili zose zono nghukulonjela.”
30 Ninga mwene Musa yapitula yakatya, “Gwe Muwaha Mulungu, nha wumanyile 'ne vyono ninha, si nili mulonji yaye, alu yuyo Falawo ho kohulika 'ne hongo?”


Wutuluce 7

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamutya Musa, “Ane nokunoza wuwe nha wuli Mulungu hali Falawo, nayo Haluni u muwahawo, kowa muwizi wako kuli Falawo.
2 Nagwe wumulolosezaje Haluni zose zono nghukulajiliza, nayo yamulonjele mutemi Falawo yawalece Waisilayeli wawuce mumo mu yisi yakwe.
3 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, ane nomunoza Falawo yawe yasina cihuliko, nayo hamba si kohulika 'ne yaye, hamba noze vitanjilo ne mezino menji mu yisi ya Misili.
4 Nane noyiletela Misili wusungu wukali muno muno wo wulipicizwe, niwawuse ne nghungugo ye wanhu wangu Waisilayeli mu yisi yiyo.
5 Nane kolo nawenulila muwoko wangu, no kuwawusa Waisilayeli mumo mu yisi yawo, nawo Wamisili ho womanya vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”
6 Nawo Musa na Haluni wakanoza nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yawalajiliza.
7 Nayo Musa yali yaficila myaka makumi munana, nayo Haluni myaka makumi munana na midatu, baho lono walonganila no mutemi Falawo.

Nghome ya Haluni

8 Muwaha Mulungu yakawalonjela Musa na Haluni yakatya,
9 “Hono mutemi Falawo yasace kuwona mapoto ho yamwihuwilenyi, ale, wumutyaje Haluni yayigumile nghome yakwe ha mwande yo mutemi, nayo yohunduka nzoka.”
10 Musa na Haluni wakawa kubita ko mutemi Falawo, waketuma nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yawalajiliza. Haluni yakayigumila nghome yakwe, ha mwande yo Mutemi Falawo hamonga ne wetumami wakwe, nayo nghome yikahunduka nzoka.
11 Nayo mutemi yakawa kuwacema wanhu we mahala, ne wahawi, hamonga ne waganga. Nawo ko wuganga wawo, wakanoza nhavivyo.
12 Wakazigumila hasi zinghome zawo, nazo zikahunduka nzoka. Ninga nghome ya Haluni yikazimela zose.
13 Hamba nhavivyo, Falawo vyo yakacila kuwa yasina cihuliko, yikawa nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalonga yakatya, mutemi Falawo si kowahulika Musa na Haluni.

MAGAZO YIKUMI

Ligazo lilongozi

Sakami

14 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Gwe Musa, ayu mutemi Falawo mukamu muno ku wuhulice, kanyhi vyo yakali du yakulema kuwakombola wanhu wabite.
15 Holeka alu, wubite witang'anile nayo hakali mitondo, lono yakubita kwi'kolongo lye Nile. Ninga weze wina ne nghome yiyilya yono yahunduka nzoka. Nagwe wumulindilile ha nyenje ye likolongo.
16 Hanyuma wumulonjele wutye, ‘U Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Wahebelaniya, kanhuma nize nghulonjele, wuwakombole wanhu wakwe wabite ho wamuhembe kuko ku nyika. Ninga agwe sunga nalu wukali si wukuhulika ng'o.
17 Holeka alu wulanje, ane nogatowa malenga ga mwi'kolongo lya Nile ne nghome yono yili mu muwoko wangu, ne malenga gohunduka sakami.
18 Ne zisomba zono zili mwikolongo zose zofwa. Nalyo likolongo lyonungha vibi, sunga ne Wamisili si wosuma kung'wa hamba ne malenga galyo. Na muli zizi nagwe mo komanyila vyono ane nili Muwaha Mulungu.’ ”

19 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Ale, wumulonjelaje Haluni yasole nghome yakwe, yagolole muwoko wakwe, yayinule nghome mucanyha ye makolongo gose, na mu vikolongo, sunga na mu vilamba, na mu malamba, mu yisi yose ya Misili. Nago malenga gono gali mumo, gose gohunduka sakami. Nayo sakami yowa mu yisi yose ye Misili, na mu viya vye mabici, sunga na mu viya vye mabwe.”

20 Musa na Haluni wakanoza nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yawalajiliza. Haluni yakenula nghome yakwe ha meso go mutemi Falawo, na ha meso ge wetumami wakwe, yakagatowa malenga ga mwi'kolongo, nago malenga gose gono gali gali mwi'kolongo gakahunduka sakami.
21 Ne zisomba zono zali zili mwi'kolongo, zose zikafwa. Ne likolongo likanungha vibi, ne Wamisili hamba si wasuma kanyhi kung'wa ne malenga galyo. Nayo sakami yikawa hanhu hose mu yisi yose ya Misili.
22 I waganga wa mu Misili nawo, wakanoza nha vivyo no wuganga wawo. Na ku soko yiyo, nhondo mutemi Falawo ho yakacila muno muno kuwa mukamu ku wuhulice. Yikawa nhavivilya du vyono Muwaha Mulungu yali yalonga. U mutemi yakalemela lukulu kuwahulika Musa na Haluni.
23 Mwene Falawo yakawa kubita ku kaya yakwe yo wutemi, hamba kutinuswa yaye ne nghani yiyo.
24 Nawo Wamisili wakawa kuhimba ha nyenje ye likolongo lya Nile, ho wetujile malenga go kung'wa, hambi nha si wasuma kung'wa malenga ga mwi'kolongo.
25 Vikapuluta vilo mupunghati, ku wulawa mwi'zuwa lyono Muwaha Mulungu yagatowela malenga ga mwi'kolongo.


Wutuluce 8

Ligazo lye kejete

Madelu

1 U Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakamutya, “Alu, wubite kuli mutemi Falawo wukamulonjele wumutye, Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, ‘Wuwakombole wanhu wangu wabite wakahembe nene.
2 Na hono wutye du wuleme kuwakombola, ale, noyilipiciza yisi yako, nogagumila madelu mu yisi yako yose.
3 Ne likolongo lye Nile lyomema madelu gose, nago gomatuka genjile mu kaya yako, na mu nyumba yako yono kugonaga, na mu citanda cako, na mu zinyumba ze wetumami wako, sunga na mu wanhu wako wose, na ha mafigwa hako, na mu viya vyako vyose vyo kutiwila wusaje.
4 Kanyhi gokutambala sunga na mu muwili wako gwegwe, ne wanhu wako, sunga ne wetumami wako wose.’ ”

5 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Wumulonjele Haluni yasole nghome yakwe, yayinule mucanyha ye makolongo na mu vikolongo na mu malamba, gaze madelu gameme mu yisi yose ya Misili.”
6 Haluni yakayinula yiyo nghome mucanyha ye malenga gose ge Misili, ne madelu gakalawila menji muno, gakamema mu yisi yose.
7 Ninga nawo waganga wakanoza nhavivyo no wuganga wawo, nawo wakagalavya madelu menji, nago gakamema mu yisi yose.

8 Mutemi Falawo yakawacema Musa na Haluni, yakawatya, “Ale, mumulombe Muwaha Mulungu yagawuse gaga mabula, nane nowakombola wanhu wenyu, wabite wakamulavizye Muwaha Mulungu makumbiko.”
9 Musa yakamupitula Falawo yakamutya, “Ane nosangalala muno nghulombele. Agwe wunonjele du lidaha lyono kosaka ane nghulombele gwegwe, ne wetumami wako, hamonga ne wanhu wako. Ne madelu gose gowuka mu yisi yako, hasina hamba linji lyono lyosigala, langa mwi'kolongo lya Nile du mo gosigala.”

10 Nayo mutemi Falawo yakamupitula yakatya, “Ale, nhondo wunombelaje mitondo.” Nayo Musa yakatya, “Ale viswanu, nokwituma nha vyono wulombile. Nagwe nhondo, baho ho komanyila vyono, hasina mulungu yunji wo wose mono yehwanile no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu.
11 Nagwe hamonga ne wetumami wako, hamonga ne wanhu wako wose, madelu gomuwucilenyi, hasina linji lyono lyosigala, langa mwi'kolongo lya Nile du mo gosigala.”
12 Nawo Musa na Haluni wakawa kuwuka hali mutemi Falawo. Nayo Musa yakalomba kuli Muwaha Mulungu, ho yagawuse madelu gono yali yamugumilila Falawo.
13 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakanoza nha vyono Musa yalomba, ne madelu gono gali gali mu zikaya, na gono gali gali mwi'hala na mu migunda, gakafwa gose.

14 Nawo Wamisili wakagafwaguliza, wakagalundika malundo malundo mawaha muno, sunga ne yisi yose yikawa yina munungho mubi ku soko ye madelu.
15 Nayo mutemi Falawo, yali yeza kuwona vyono mabula gose gakondya kufwa, hodu yakawa kanyhi yasina cihuliko, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalonga du yakutya, “Falawo hamba si kowahulika Musa na Haluni.”

Ligazo lye kadatu

Mhani

16 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Wumulonjele Haluni yagatowe manghundi ge yisi ne nghome yakwe, nago manghundi ga mu yisi yose ya Misili, gohunduka mhani.”
17 Haluni yakagatowa manghundi ne nghome yakwe, nago manghundi gose ge yisi yose ya Misili, gakahunduka mhani, gakenjila muli wanhu sunga na mu zi nimu mose.
18 Nawo waganga wakajela kunoza wuganga wawo ho mhani zijele, ninga wakalemegwa. Nazo zi mhani zikatagalila hanhu hose, na mu wanhu na mu zi nimu.
19 Nawo waganga nhondo wakamutya Falawo, “Gwe mutemi Falawo, azi zinozwa no Mulungu du!” Ninga Falawo yakacila kuwa yasina cihuliko, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalonga du yakutya, “Falawo hamba si kowahulika Musa na Haluni.”

Ligazo lye kane

Mahazi

20 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Ale, wilawaje mitondo hakali mitondo muno muno, wuketang'anile no mutemi Falawo lono yakubita kwi' kolongo. Nagwe wumulonjele wutye, Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, ‘Wuwakombole wanhu wangu wabite wakahembe.
21 Nane nghukusugula nghutya, hono wuleme du, ale, nokuletela gwegwe mahazi, mu wetumami wako, na mu wanhu wako sunga na mu zikaya zako. Nazo zikaya zose ze Wamisili, zomema mahazi gose, sunga ne yisi yose yomema mahazi gose.
22 Ninga ku yisi ya Gosheni kono wekaye wanhu wangu, ako langa mahazi si gokwinjila yaye. Nane nozinoza zizi, agwe ho wumanye vyono yo nene no Muwaha Mulungu, mono nghuzituma zizi mono yisi yiyi.
23 Nane nowika wisimajile kuli wanhu wangu na kuli wanhu wako. Ne lipoto lilino lyokwitumwa mitondo.’ ”
24 Hodu, Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kugaleta mawumbi mawaha muno ga mahazi, gakenjila mu wikalo wo mutemi Falawo, hamonga na mu zinyumba ze wetumami wakwe. Yisi yose ya Misili yikahodanjika ku soko ye mawumbi ge mahazi.

25 Hanyuma nhondo, mutemi Falawo yakawacema Musa na Haluni yakawalonjela yakatya, “Ale mubite mukamulavizye makumbiko Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, ninga yiweze bahano du mono yisi yiyino, mulece kubita kunji.”
26 Nayo Musa yakamupitula Falawo yakatya, “Si yowa viswanu hamba hadodo hono citume nhavivyo. Hambi Wamisili nha si wokwendezwa yaye ne makumbiko getu, ge zimitugo zono comulavizya Muwaha Mulungu wetu. Na hono cizitumamile zimitugo zizi ciwakalaze Wamisili, so makumbiko gono cokumbicila hanhu hono wosuma kuciwona vyono cikunoza, mbeka wocitowanga ne mabwe sunga waciwulaje.
27 Holeka langa, yicinojeye cibite ku nyika muhinzo wa vilo vidatu, kuko ko cikamulavizye makumbiko u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, nha vyono mwene yacilajiliza.”

28 Nayo mutemi Falawo yakatya, “Ale, viswanu, nomukombolenyi mubite mukamulavizye makumbiko u Muwaha Mulungu wenyu kuko ku nyika, ninga mulece kubita kutali muno muno. Ale munombelaje na nene.”
29 Musa yakapitula yakatya, “Ale viswanu, nane nonoza mbela mbela, nimulombe Muwaha Mulungu, i mitondo mahazi gawuce mu wikalo wako, hamonga na mu wetumami wako, sunga na muli wanhu wako. Ninga wulanjilile, wulece kucikonga, kanyhi wuwalemeze wanhu walece kubita kumulavizya makumbiko Muwaha Mulungu.”

30 Musa yakawuka hali mutemi Falawo, yakamulomba Muwaha Mulungu.
31 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakanoza nha vyono Musa yalomba. Mahazi gakawuka gose mu kaya yo mutemi Falawo, na kuli wetumami wakwe, sunga na kuli wanhu wakwe, si lyasigala hamba yihazi limonga yaye.
32 Ninga hamba no muhinzo wuwu, mutemi Falawo yakawa yasina cihuliko, hamba si yawakombola wanhu wabite yaye.


Wutuluce 9

Ligazo lye hano

Wufwe we mitugo

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Alu, wubite kuli mutemi Falawo wukamulonjele wutye, ‘Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Wahebelaniya, yakutya nhavi, wuwakombole wanhu wangu, wabite wakahembe.
2 Na hono wuleme kanyhi kuwakombola, ale,
3 nane nokuletela wulipicizwe wa nhamwa 'mbi muno muno, mu mitugo zako, na mu zindogowe zako, na mu zifwalasi zako, na mu zingamiya, na mu zing'ombe, na mu zingholo, sunga na mu zimhene.
4 Ninga nowika wisimajile muli mitugo ye Waisilayeli na muli mitugo ye Wamisili, hasina mutugo hamba wumonga we Waisilayeli wono wofwa.
5 Nane, nene no Muwaha Mulungu, nozifisiliza zizi mitondo mu yisi yose.’ ”

6 Ne mitondo yaco, Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kuzinoza nha vyono yali yalonjile, ne mitugo zose ze Wamisili zikafwa, ninga hasina mutugo hamba wumonga we Waisilayeli wono wafwa.
7 Nayo mutemi Falawo yakawuza ho yamanye zono ziweye, nayo yakalonjelwa nha vyono, hasina mutugo hamba monga we Waisilayeli wono wufwiye. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, Falawo yikacila kuwa yisina cihuliko, hamba si yawakombola wanhu wabite yaye.

Ligazo lyo mutandatu

Mapu

8 Hanyuma kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu yakawalonjela Musa na Haluni yakawatya, “Musole mavu ga mwi'kozo lyo moto mu maganza genyu, hanyuma Musa yagagumile mucanyha, ha meso go mutemi Falawo.
9 Nago mavu gago gokwiyagala mumo mucanyha, gowa nhe 'nghundi lipelupelu muno mu yisi yose ya Misili. Nago gowa mapu hanhu hose, gono govimba muno no kuhehemuka. Nago gowa muli wanhu sunga na muli mitugo zose.”
10 Nawo Musa na Haluni wakasola mavu, wakema hali mutemi Falawo. Musa yakagagumila mucanyha, nago gakawa mapu gono gavimba muno muli wanhu, sunga na muli vilimu vyose.
11 Ne waganga hamba si wasuma kanyhi yaye kwima hali Musa, hambi wose nha wali wavimbile mapu gose, nha vyono wali wavimbile Wamisili wose.
12 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, Muwaha Mulungu yakamunoza mutemi Falawo yawe yasina cihuliko, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yali yalonga yakutya, vyono mutemi Falawo, si kowahulika Musa na Haluni.

Ligazo lyo mupunghati

Mvula ye mabwe

13 Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Ale wilawaje mitondo hakali mitondo muno muno, wubite kuli mutemi Falawo wukamulonjele wutye, u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Wahebelaniya, yakutya nhavi, ‘Wakombole wanhu wangu wabite wakahembe.
14 Kanyhi wumanye nhondo vyono muhinzo wuwuno, hamba si nowalipiciza wetumami wako wali wene du, langa nokulipiciza sunga na gwegwe go mweneco, nagwe ho wumanye nha vyono hasina hamba monga mono yinze yiyino yose, mono yehwanile nene no Muwaha Mulungu.
15 Na hono nali nitumamile vilungo vyangu vyose, nghutowe gwegwe, hamonga ne wanhu wako, mu nzila yo kukuletela nhamwa, mbeka wali wongomelele lukulu.
16 Ninga lu ho nghulajile vilungo vyangu, holeka nghuwicile sunga nalu wuli mumi, u lukumyo lwangu ho lusume kwijela mono'nze mose.
17 Ninga sunga nalu agwe wukali du wusina cihuliko, hamba si wukusaka no kuwakombola wanhu wangu wabite ng'o.
18 Alu nhondo mu muhinzo wu wuno, i mitondo, noleta mvula ya mabwe, mvula yono yikali si yinawa hamba kamonga mono yisi ya Misili ku wulawa wulawo sunga nalu.
19 Holeka, alu wulajilize mitugo zako, hamonga ne vinhu vyako vyono vili mwi'hala, vyose vigomolwe mono kaya. Hambi mvula ye mabwe, nha yowatonyela wanhu hamonga ne mitugo, na muli vyose vyono vyowa kunze mwi'hala, navyo vyose vyofwa.’ ”
20 Nawo wanji wa muli wetumami wakwe, wakenjilwa na woga wuwaha muno ku soko ye zinghani zizi zono Muwaha Mulungu yalonjile. Holeka wakawatuma wetumami wawo wazigomole mitugo zawo wazinjize mu zikaya.
21 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, wanji hamba si wazicelelela yaye zizo zono Muwaha Mulungu yawasugula. Wakazileka mitugo zawo hamonga ne wetumami wawo kuko kwi'hala.

22 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Ale wugolole muwoko wako kucanyha, ne mvula ye mabwe yotonya mu yisi yose ya Misili, nayo yowatonyela wanhu, ne mitugo, sunga na mu vilimwa vyose vya mu migunda.”
23 Musa yakagolola nghome yakwe kucanyha, nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakaleta mabalalusi, hamonga ne mvula ye mabwe, ne mamuli gakawa kukoma ho hasi.
24 U Muwaha Mulungu yakatonyeza mvula mbaha muno ya mabwe, hamonga ne mamuli gono gamulikaga ako na ako. Ne mvula yiyo yikawa mbaha muno mu yisi yose, kuciza nghango yono yikali hamba si natonya mu yisi ya Misili ku wulawa wulawo.
25 Ne mvula yiyo ye mabwe, yikavihodanga vinhu vyose vyono vyali vili mwi'hala caka, na mu migunda mu yisi yose ya Misili, hamonga ne wanhu ne mitugo zose. Kanyhi yikavibenanjiza ne vilimwa vyose vya mu migunda, sunga ne mibici yose.
26 Ninga ku Gosheni ku yisi yono wekalaga Waisilayeli, ako mvula yiyo ye mabwe, hamba si yatonya yaye kwali kukavu du.

27 Mutemi Falawo yakatuma munhu yakawacema Musa na Haluni, yakawatya, “Aluno ane nakondya kwituma wubi, u Muwaha Mulungu yo we ling'ani yali wene, ninga ane ne wanhu wangu, wose cili wabi.
28 Holeka mumulombe Muwaha Mulungu! Hambi mvula ye mabwe hamonga ne mabalalusi gayo, nha vyacila muno! Nane nghwihuwiza vyono, nomukombolenyi mubite, hamba si mojendelela kanyhi kwikala bahano.”

29 Nayo Musa yakamutya Falawo, “Lono nghulawila kunze ye likaya du, nonyasula kucanyha miwoko yangu nimulombe Muwaha Mulungu. Nago mabalalusi gowuka lwa kamonga, sunga ne mvula ye mabwe howa hasina kanyhi, nagwe baho ho komanyila vyono yinze yose yo sawo yo Muwaha Mulungu.
30 Ninga manyiye lukulu vyono gwegwe go mweneco, hamonga ne wetumami wako, wose nyenye mukali musina cogopo co Muwaha Mulungu muli nyenye.”
31 Citani hamonga ne shayili, vyose vikabenanjizwa ne mvula ye mabwe, hambi shayili nha yali yakondya kutuga maso. Ne citani naco cali cina maluwa.
32 Langa wuhemba wo si wabenanjizwa yaye ne mvula yiyi ye mabwe, hambi cipango cayo co kupila nha cali cikali.

33 Hodu Musa yakawa kuwuka hali Falawo, yakalawila kunze ye kaya, yakenula miwoko yakwe kucanyha, yakamulomba Muwaha Mulungu. Hobaho mabalalusi, hamonga ne mvula ye mabwe vikawuka lwa kamonga.
34 Nayo Falawo yali yeza kuwona vyono mabalalusi hamonga ne mvula vyakondya kuwuka, hodu yakaliwuyila lizeze lyakwe kanyhi. Yakacila kuhodanga no kuyikamufya nhumbula yakwe. Mwene hamonga ne wetumami wakwe wose, wakawa wasina cihuliko hamba hadodo, kotya ha mwamulizo.
35 Nayo yikawa nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yali yalonga kuli Musa, vyono mutemi Falawo si kosuma kuwakombola Waisilayeli wabite.


Wutuluce 10

Ligazo lyo munana

Nzije

1 Muwaha Mulungu, yakamulonjela Musa kanyhi yakatya, “Ale, wubite mukewone na Falawo. Hambi nha namunoza mwene hamonga ne wetumami wakwe wawe wasina cihuliko, nane ho sume kuganoza mapoto gaga gose hali wene.
2 Nagwe ho wuwe na co kuwalonjela wanago hamonga ne wezukuluwo vyono nawanoza Wamisili lono nanozeza mapoto gangu muli wene. Nanye wose ho momanya vyono ane yo nene nili Muwaha Mulungu.”

3 Nawo Musa na Haluni wakamubitila mutemi Falawo wakamulonjela wakatya, “U Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Wahebelaniya, yakutya nhavi, ‘Alu kojendelela kuwa wusina cihuliko kuli'ne sunga nili? Wuwakombole wanhu wangu wabite, ho wakahembe nene.
4 Na hono wujendelele kuwalemeza, ale, nane nokuletela nzije mitondo mu yisi yako.
5 Nazo nzije zizo zowa nyinji muno muno, zotandala mu yisi yose, zolya vinhu vyose vyono vyasigala vyono si vyahodangwa ne mvula ye mabwe, hamonga ne mibici yose yono yisigaye.
6 Nazo nzije zizo, zomema mu zikaya zako, hamonga na mu zikaya zose ze wetumami wako, na mu zikaya zose ze wanhu wako, mu yisi yose ya Misili. Nazo nzije zizo, zowa cinhu cibi muno cono ne walezi wako, hamba wakukuzo, wali wakali naciwona ku wulawa katali sunga nalu.’ ” Hodu Musa yakawa kuwuka hali Falawo.

7 Nawo wetumami wa Falawo wakamulonjela Falawo wakutya, “Alu mwene u munhu yuyu kojendelela kucipela magazo sunga nili ta? Ale wuwakombole Waisilayeli wabite le, ho wamuhembe Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo. Alo mwene si wukuwona lu vyono yisi ya Misili yeza kongomela?”
8 Holeka Musa na Haluni wakaletwa kanyhi kuli mutemi Falawo. Nayo Falawo yakawalonjela yakatya, “Ale mosuma kubita mukamuhembe Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu. Ninga lu wa nani 'mbi wono wosuma kubita?”
9 Nayo Musa yakapitula yakatya, “Wanhu wose cobita hamonga ne wana wetu, hamonga ne wakombi. Nase cosoleleza hamonga ne wana wetu we cilume na we cicekulu, hamonga ne zingholo ne zimhene, ne zing'ombe zetu. Hambi nha yicinojeye cimunozeze Muwaha Mulungu lizuwa yiwaha lyo kumukumya.”

10 Nayo mutemi Falawo yakapitula mu nzila yo kuwabedula yakatya, “Nane nghwilahiza hali Muwaha Mulungu, vyono si nosuma kumukombolenyi mubite hamonga ne wacekulu ne wana! Hambi nha yili calinze vyono, anye mwina wuhile wubi mu zinhumbula zenyu.
11 Holeka si yosumika yaye! Langa wabite walume du wo wakamuhembe Muwaha Mulungu, hono wuwo wo wuhile wenyu.” Na ku soko yiyo, Musa na Haluni wakawingwa wawuce hali Falawo.

12 Hanyuma kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Wugolole muwoko wako mu yisi ya Misili, wulete nzije. Nazo zokuza zilye vilimwa vyose vyono vikuwiluka, vilimwa vyose vyono vyasigala, vyono mvula ye mabwe si yavihodanga yaye.”
13 Nayo Musa yakanyasula nghome yakwe, nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakaleta mbeho mbaha muno muno ku wulawilila ku cilima, yikajendelela kuputa mu yisi yose, mwi'zuwa lililya lyose sunga na mu cilo cose. Nawo wali weza kwela hakali mitondo, yikawa yaleta nzije nyinji.
14 Nzije zikaza mawumbi na mawumbi, nazo zikawa kwiyagala no kumata mu yisi yose ya Misili. Nalyo lyali lye 'wumbi liwaha muno muno lya nzije lyono lyali likali nejela hamba hadodo ku wulawa katali na katali. Hamba si lyolokwijela kanyhi na mu vilo vyono vikuza.
15 Nazo zikayigubika yisi yose, sunga ne yisi yose yikatukuwikwa ku soko yo winji we zinzije. Zikalya vinhu vyose vyono vyasigala, vyono si vyahodangwa ne mvula ye mabwe, hamonga ne mikalo yose yono yili mu mibici. Hamba si zasigaza cinji co cose cono cili dedede ca mu mibici mucanyha, hamba vihadikwa vyovyose vya mu migunda vya mu yisi yose ya Misili.

16 Ale baho, mutemi Falawo yakawa kuwacema mbela mbela Musa na Haluni, yakawa kuwalonjela yakutya, “Ane nimusutizya Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, sunga na kuli nyenye nhavivyo.
17 Holeka nghunena, alu munenele wubi wangu wuwu, kanyhi mumulombe Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yambusize wulipicizwe wu wuno wuwaha muno.”
18 Nayo Musa yakawuka hali Falawo, yakamulomba Muwaha Mulungu.
19 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakayipitula mbeho ya kucilima, yakaleta mbeho mbaha muno muno yono yikulawa kumwezi. Mbeho yiyo yikazipemhelusiza zinzije zose mwi'lenga lya Shamu. Siyasigala nzije hamba monga mu yisi yose ya Misili.
20 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, Muwaha Mulungu yakamunoza Falawo yawe yisina cihuliko. Holeka hamba si yawakombola Waisilayeli wabite ng'o.

Ligazo lye licenda

Citi

21 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa kanyhi yakatya, “Alu, wugolole muwoko wako kucanyha, naco citi cokuza, coyigubika yisi yose ya Misili.”
22 Musa yakawugolola muwoko wakwe kucanyha. Cikawa kuza citi ciwaha muno muno mu yisi yose ya Misili, cikawa citi kwa vilo vidatu.
23 Sunga ne Wamisili hamba si wasumaga kwiwona munhu na muyagwe. Na mwipango lilyo lye citi, hasina munhu hamba monga mono yasuma kuwuka mu nyumba yakwe. Ninga kono wekalaga Waisilayeli, ako kwali kowuli du wuzelu.

24 Mutemi Falawo yakamucema Musa yakamulonjela yakatya, “Alu langa, mosuma kubita hamonga ne wacekulu ne wana wenyu, mukamuhembe Muwaha Mulungu. Ninga langa mulece kusoleleza hamonga ne zingholo, na zimhene, sunga ne zing'ombe zenyu, azo zosigala bahano du.”

25 Nayo Musa yakamupitula yakatya, “Ninga hamba nhavivyo, yikunojeye wucipele mitugo so makumbiko hamonga ne vilavywa vinyanywe, cikamulavizye Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu.
26 Holeka ase cosoleleza hamonga ne mitugo zetu, si yosigala hamba monga kono nyuma. Kanyhi ase yo sese mono cinojeye ciyisiwanulile co weneco, mitugo yono yinojeye ciyitumamile, mu wuhemba Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu. Kanyhi alu, hamba si cosuma kumanya mutugo wa hayi wono comulavizya Muwaha Mulungu langa sunga kolo cafika kuko.”

27 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakamunoza Falawo, yakawa yasina cihuliko. Nayo yakalemela lukulu kuwakombola Waisilayeli wabite.
28 Falawo yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Gwe Musa, wuce lulu baha ha mwande yangu! Si nghusacila lukulu kukuwona kanyhi ha meso gangu! Na hono nize nghuwone kanyhi ha meso gangu, hodu kofwa!”
29 Nayo Musa yakamupitula yakatya, “Ale viswanu, nane hamba si nolokuwonela lukulu kanyhi wusu wako.”


Wutuluce 11

Musa yakulonga zo wufwe wo Mwana we cilume mulongozi kulelwa

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Alu lisigaye lipoto limonga du, lyono nomuletela mutemi Falawo wa Misili hamonga ne wanhu wakwe. Na mwi'poto lilyo, Falawo komukombolenyi mubite. Na mbeka komuwinjilenyi lukulu wose muwuce mu yisi yakwe.
2 Alu, wuwalonjelaje wana wa Isilayeli, wawalonjele we cikalani wayawo, walete wihambo wa fweza no wihambo we zahabu.”
3 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawapela Waisilayeli wendezwe kuli Wamisili. Nayo yili mbeka, vyono wetumami wo mutemi, hamonga ne wanhu wose wa Misili, wamuwonaga Musa nha vyono munhu muwaha mu yisi ya Misili.

4 Musa yakamulonjela mutemi Falawo yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, ‘Mu cilo mugati, ane nokola mu yisi yose ya Misili.
5 Ne wana we cilume walongozi kulelwa, wose wofwa. Ku wulawilila mwana we cilume wa Falawo, u mwana mono kowa yo muhazi wakwe wo wutemi, sunga ko mwana we cilume mulongozi kulelwa wo mwitumami we cicekulu mono yakusaga wuhemba. Sunga ne zinyana zose zinongozi kulelwa ze mitugo, nazo nhavivilya zofwa.
6 Na mowa na cililo ciwaha muno muno mu yisi yose ya Misili, cililo cono cikali si nejela hamba nhavi ku wulawa katali, hamba si cilejela kanyhi yaye.
7 Ninga hasina hamba libwa lyono lyowafwemela Waisilayeli, hamba mitugo zawo. Nagwe baho ho komanyila vyono, Ane no Muwaha Mulungu, yo nene mono nozize wisimajile hagati ye Wamisili ne Waisilayeli.’ ”
8 Nayo Musa yakamalila wulonje wakwe yakatya, “Gwe Falawo, wetumami wako wose wonyiziza wafugamile, akuno wakunena niwasole wanhu wangu mbite nawo. Hanyuma ya zizo, nane ho notuluka.” Hodu Musa yakawa kuwuka hali Falawo akuno yakoligwe ne likuwo yiwaha.

9 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu, yali yakondya kumulonjela Musa, vyono, Falawo kojendelela kuwa yasina cihuliko, nane ho sume kunoza mapoto gangu mu yisi ya Misili.
10 Nawo Musa na Haluni wakaganoza mapoto gago gose hali Falawo. Ninga Muwaha Mulungu, yakamunoza Falawo yawe yasina cihuliko. Holeka Falawo hamba si yawakombola Waisilayeli wawuce mu yisi yakwe.


Wutuluce 12

Pasaka

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakawalonjela Musa na Haluni lono wali wakali kuko ku Misili yakatya,
2 “Ale, u mwezi wu wuno, wo wowa wo mwezi wenyu mulongozi mu mwaka.
3 Holeka muwalonjele we mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli, vyono mwi'zuwa lye likumi lyo mwezi wuwu, yinojeye munhu wo wose muli'nye, yasole nyana ye ngholo hamba nyana ye mhene so we mhuga yakwe.
4 Na hono mhuga yiyo yiwe yina wanhu wacefu, mu nghango yo kufutigwa kulya no kuwumala mutugo wuwo wose, ale wehanzaje mhuga nyejete hamonga, wagawane mutugo monga na wose walye mu nghango ye liwalo lyawo.
5 Wudahile kusola ngholo hamba mhene, ninga yiweze nume yono yificile mwaka monga du, kanyhi yiweze yono yisina cilema co cose.
6 Nanye, kolo lyeza kufika lizuwa lye likumi na yine lyo mwezi wuwu, ale, zimhuga zose ze Waisilayeli waziwulagaje mitugo zizo zose, mwipango lye cinyamihe mwi'zuwa lilyo.
7 Hanyuma wanhu wasole sakami ye mitugo yiyi wayibace mu fulemu ze milango, mu zimbawo zinyejete zo wima, ne mbawo ye cipinga ya hacanyha, mu milango ye zinyumba mono wolila mitugo yiyo.
8 Na mu cilo cico, woyikasika nyama yiyo mu moto, wayilye hamonga ne mikate yono yisina cisase, hamonga ne mboga ye mahanze yono yili cisungu.
9 Mulece kuyilya nduma hamba nhelece, langa yose yeze nghasice, hamonga no mutwe wayo ne migulu yayo yose, sunga no wutumbu wayo wose.
10 Kanyhi mulece kusigaza nyama yinji yo yose yigone sunga hakali mitondo. Na hono lunji yinji yisigale sunga hakali mitondo, muyikongogaje mu moto.
11 Kanyhi lono mukuyilya, muyilizaje ko musunho, akuno muvwalile ne myenda yenyu hamonga ne malatu genyu mu migulu, akuno mwina ne zinghome zenyu, mu miwoko yenyu. Yiyo yo sikukuu ye Pasaka yono yikulajila cogopo cenyu kuli'ne no Muwaha Mulungu.”

12 “Nane mu cilo cico nokola mu yisi yose ya Misili, niwawulaje wana walume wose, walongozi kulelwa. I wana we wanhu, hamonga ne zinyana ze mitugo. Kanyhi noyilipiciza milungu yose yo wulambi ya mu yisi ya Misili. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.
13 Nayo sakami yono yili mu fulemu ze milango, cowa co citanjilo cono cikulajila vyono zo kaya zenyu zono mwikaye. Nane kolo nghuyiwona yiyo sakami, hodu nopuluta, hamba si nomutamisenyi yaye, lono neza kuwalipiciza Wamisili.
14 Nanye molisiwanula lizuwa lilino, liwe sikukuu yo wuhembi, yono yojendelela kumukumbucizenyi zono nizitumile ane no Muwaha Mulungu. Holeka mojendelela kuyibata sikukuu yiyi mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza.”

Sikukuu ye mikate yono yisina cisase

15 Muwaha Mulungu yakatya “Mu vilo mupunghati, molecela lukulu kulya mikate yono yina cisase. Na mwizuwa lilongozi, wose muwuse cisase cose cono cili mu zikaya zenyu. Na hono yejele yunji muli nyenye mu vilo vivyo vyo mupunghati, yakulya mukate wono wina cisase, munhu yuyo si kosuma kanyhi kuwalilizwa hamonga ne wanhu wangu.
16 Holeka mwi'zuwa lilongozi na mwi'zuwa lyo mupunghati, munojeye muwe kwihanguliza mu wuhembi. Mulece kwituma mulimo wowose mu vilo vivyo, langa munojeye muwikanize ndigwa.
17 Mweze kuyikumbucila sikukuu yiyi, ye mikate yono yisina cisase. Hambi nha lyo lizuwa lyono namuwusenyi nyenye mu yisi ya Misili. Holeka mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza, munojeye mulinozeze sikukuu lizuwa lili.
18 Kanyhi ku wulawilila cinyamihe, ce lizuwa lye likumi na yine lyo mwezi mulongozi, sunga cinyamihe, ce lizuwa lye makumi mejete na monga, mulecele lukulu kulya mikate yono yina cisase.
19 Hambi mu vilo mupunghati, nha yinojeye cisase cilecele lukulu kwijela mu zikaya zenyu.
20 Vyono munhu wo wose, hamba yawe munyawusi muyenyu, hamba yawe mujenzi, hono yalye du mukate wono wina cisase, hodu munhu yuyo, si kosuma kanyhi kuwalilizwa hamonga ne wanhu wangu.”

Pasaka nongozi

21 Hobaho Musa yakawacema walongola wose we Waisilayeli, yakawalonjela yakatya, “Alu, nyenye wose munhu na mhuga yakwe, muhagule ngholo hamba mhene yono yikali ndodo, muyiwulaje, muyitumamile mu sikukuu ye Pasaka.
22 Nanye musole linhambi lya ezobu, mukalinimice mu sakami yono yili mu bakuli. Muyibace mu fulemu ze milango mu zimbawo zo wima, ne mbawo ya hacanyha ye cipinga, mu zinyumba zenyu. Kanyhi yunji wa muli nyenye, yalece kuwuka mu nyumba yakwe sunga kolo wukwela mitondo yaco.
23 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu kolo yeza kukola mu yisi ya Misili, ho yawawulaje Wamisili, koyiwona yiyo sakami ya mu fulemu ze milango yenyu, nayo si komuleka malayika we lifwa, yenjile mu zi nyumba zenyu yamuwulajenyi.
24 Nanye hamonga ne wana wenyu, munojeye muwukamise wulajilize wuwuno sunga cibitilila.
25 Na kolo mukwinjila mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu yamwihuwizenyi vyono komupelenyi, munojeye muwukamise wityatye wu wuno wo wuhembi.
26 Na kolo wana wenyu wakumuwuzenyi wakutya, ‘Alu wityatye wuwu wo wuhembi wina nhunizoci?’
27 Nanye mowapitula muwe kutya, ‘Alino lyo likumbiko lye Pasaka, so kumupela cogopo Muwaha Mulungu mono yaziwagula zikaya ze Waisilayeli ku Misili. Yakawa kuwawulaga Wamisili, ninga ase yakacileka.’ ”

Nawo Waisilayeli wakafugama hasi, wakahemba.
28 Wakawa kubita kwituma nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yawalajiliza Musa na Haluni.

Wuwulagwe wo mwana mulelwa mulongozi we Wamisili

29 Yali yeza kufika mu cilo mugati, Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kuwawulaga i wana wose walelwa walongozi wa mu yisi ya Misili, ku wulawilila u mwana we cilume wa Falawo mono yali yo muhazi we ligoda lyo wutemi, sunga ko kuli mwana wa mono yali mufungwa mu jela, sunga kuli ne zinyana zinongozi kulelwa ze mitugo, zose zikawulagwa.
30 Na mu cilo cico, u mutemi Falawo hamonga ne wetumami wakwe wose, sunga ne Wamisili wose, wakasinhuswa mu nhongo. Kukawa na cililo ciwaha muno muno mu yisi yose ya Misili, hambi nha hali hasina kaya hamba monga, yono yali yisina mwana mono yafwiye.
31 Na mu cilo cico, mutemi Falawo yakawacema Musa na Haluni yakawalonjela yakatya, “Ale muwuce wose lulu baha, hamonga ne Waisilayeli wenyu! Muwuce mono yisi yangu, mubite mukamuhembe Muwaha Mulungu wenyu nha vyono mwasakaga.
32 Musole hamonga ne zingholo zenyu, ne zimhene, hamonga sunga ne zing'ombe zenyu, muwuce mubite. Ninga nane munombele wulugutizwe.”

33 Wamisili wakawasunhaniza muno muno Waisilayeli wawuce mbela mbela mu yisi yawo, akuno wakutya, “Hambi ase wose nha cofwa cimalice, hono anye mulece kuwuka.”
34 Hodu Waisilayeli wakawa kuwusola wusaje wawo wono wali wakondya kuwutiwa, ninga wali wukali nasasa yaye, wakasola ne viya vyawo vyo kuwutiwila wusaje, wakaviwopa mu myenda yawo, wakawegala mu mawega gawo, wakatuluka.
35 Waisilayeli wakanoza nha vyono Musa yawalonjela. Kanyhi wakawasaka Wamisili wawapele no wihambo wa fweza, no wihambo we zahabu, hamonga ne myenda.
36 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawanoza Waisilayeli wawe wendece ha meso ge Wamisili. Nawo Wamisili wakawakombolela Waisilayeli vinhu vivyo vyono wavisakaga. Na mu nzila yiyo, Waisilayeli wakasola sawo ye Wamisili nyinji muno muno.

Wuwuce we Waisilayeli mu yisi ya Misili

37 Waisilayeli wakatuluka wakujenda ne magulu ku wulawa ku Lamesesi wakabita ku Sukoti, nawo wali waficile wanhu we cilume elufu magana mutandatu liwalo lyawo, ninga i wacekulu hamonga ne wana, awo si wawalilize mwi'walo lili.
38 Ne wanhu wanji wenji muno, vikabitana nawo hamonga ne matewa mawaha ge zingholo, zimhene ne zing'ombe.
39 Wakasola wusaje wuwulya wutiwe wono waza nawo ku wulawa ku Misili, wakateleka mikate yono yisina cisase. Hambi nha wawuka musunho musunho kuko ku Misili, hamba si watuga cilejeho co kuwikaniza ndigwa zawo, langa hono si wusaje wuwo du, wono wali wusina cisase wono wawuka nawo ku Misili. Wakawusola wuwo wakateleka mikate yono yisina cisase.

40 Wawa Waisilayeli wekala ku Misili myaka magana mane na makumi madatu.
41 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo lye ndumilizo lya mu myaka yiyo ye magana mane na makumi madatu, lyo lizuwa lyono mhuga yose ye wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu wawucila kuko ku Misili.
42 Cali cilo, lono Muwaha Mulungu yawawusa mu yisi ya Misili. Ne cilo ce lizuwa lilyo, cikasiwanulwa ciwe kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Na mu myaka yose yono yikuza, ciwe cilo cono cinojeye ciwe kukumbucilwa ne Waisilayeli wose.

Wulajilize we Pasaka

43 Muwaha Mulungu yakawalonjela Musa na Haluni yakatya, “Awu wo wulajilize we Pasaka, hasina munhu mujenzi wo wose mono kosuma kuyilya ndigwa ye Pasaka.
44 Langa mwitumami wo wose mono wumugulile na fweza, ayo yadahile kuyilya, ninga nayo, yeze wakondya kumutegula ku calamo.
45 Ninga hasina mwikazi hamba mwitumami wo kulipwa musahala, mono kosuma kuyilya.
46 Ne nyama yose, yinojeye yiligwe, no kumalicila mu nyumba mono yiwikanizizwe, yilece kutegulwa kunze hamba hadodo. Kanyhi wulanjilile, wulecele lukulu kugabenanga mafupa go mutugo wuwo.
47 Nazo zimhuga zose ze Waisilayeli, wanojeye wawe kuyinoza sikukuu yiyi.
48 Ninga munhu mono si yalame, yuyo si yanojeye kuyilya. Na hono hawe ho yuli munhu mujenzi, mono yakondya kulowela mu yisi yenyu, hamba si muhami kanyhi, na hono yasace nayo kuyinoza sikukuu yiyi ya Pasaka so Muwaha Mulungu, kosuma kuyinoza, ninga yamule yalame, mwene hamonga na we cilume wa mu nyumba yakwe wose. Ale baho langa, ho kolangwa nha Mwiisilayeli mulelelwa mu'si, nayo ho kosuma no kuyilya yiyo Pasaka.
49 Ninga wumanye vyono, wulajilize we Pasaka, wuwanojeye Waisilayeli wose i walelelwa mu'si, sunga na kuli wajenzi walowezi wa mu yisi yenyu.”
50 Nawo Waisilayeli wose, wakawa wali cihuliko, wakanoza nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yawalajiliza Musa na Haluni.
51 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo u Muwaha Mulungu yakawawusa we nghungugo yose ye Waisilayeli ku wulawa mu yisi ya Misili.


Wutuluce 13

Wusiwanulwe we wana walongozi kulelwa

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
2 “Wusiwanulilaje i wana we cilume wose i walongozi kulelwa, hambi i wana wose we cilume we Waisilayeli, i walongozi kulelwa, hamonga ne zinyana zinume ze mitugo zinongozi kulelwa, nha vyangu nene.”

Sikukuu ye mikate yono yisina cisase

3 Nayo Musa yakawalonjela wanhu yakatya, “Ale, mulikumbucilaje lizuwa lili, lizuwa lyono mwawucila ku Misili, mu yisi yono mwali wawanda. Ali lyo lizuwa lyono Muwaha Mulungu yamuwusenyi kwe vilungo vyakwe viwaha. Holeka mulece kulya mukate wono wina cisase.
4 Nanye mukuwuka mu yisi ya Misili mwi'zuwa lili, mu mwezi mulongozi, wono wukucemwa mwezi wa Abibu.
5 Muwaha Mulungu yelahiza kuli walezi wenyu, vyono komupelenyi yisi ye Wakanani, na ye Wahiti, na ye Waamoli, na ye Wahivi, na ye Wayebusi. Nayo kolo yakumwinjizenyi mu yisi yiyo yono yina moso muwaha muno muno, ale, monoza sikukuu yiyi mu mwezi mulongozi wa mu mwaka.
6 Holeka mu vilo mutandatu, molya mikate yono yisina cisase, na mwi'zuwa lyo mupunghati, lyowa lyo lizuwa lye sikukuu yo Muwaha Mulungu.
7 Mu vilo mupunghati, munojeye mulecele lukulu kulya mukate wono wina cisase. Kanyhi mu yisi yenyu, hanhu ho hose, halece kuwa na cisase hamba mukate wono wina cisase.
8 Ne sikukuu yiyi kolo yanza, muwalolosezaje wana wenyu, vyono mukuzinoza zizi, ku soko ya zono Muwaha Mulungu yetuma ku soko yenyu, lono mwawucila ku yisi ya Misili.
9 Ne citanjilo cici, cowa cikumbucizo, kanyhi cowa nha cinhu cono mwibate mu miwoko yenyu, hamba cono cijeye ha meso genyu. Naco comukumbucizenyi ho mujendelele kuwukamisa no kuwusoma wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yo yamuwusenyi anye mu yisi ya Misili kwe vilungo vyakwe viwaha.
10 Mweze kuyinoza sikukuu yiyi mu myaka yose mu cipango cici cono cakondya kuwagwa so nghani yiyo.”

Mwana mulongozi kulelwa

11 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu kolo yakumwinjizenyi mu yisi ye Wakanani, yono yamwihuwizenyi nyenye hamonga ne walezi wenyu. Na kolo yakumupelenyi yisi yiyo,
12 yowa yimunojelenyi nanye mumulavizye Muwaha Mulungu u mwana mulongozi kulelwa. Ne nyana nume ze mitugo zinongozi kulelwa, zizo yo sawo yo Mulungu.
13 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, hono yiwe nyana nume ya ndogowe, ayo langa yikunojeye wuyikomboze ko kulavya nyana ye ngholo yo yihomele hanhu hayo. Na hono wuwe si wendile kuyikomboza yiyo nyana ye ndogowe, ale hodu, ninga langa wuyibenaje singo yayo. Ninga kanyhi kosuma kuwakomboza i wana wako we cilume iwalongozi kulelwa.
14 Na mu vilo vyono vikuza, hono mwanago yakuwuze yawe kutya alu citanjilo cici cina nhunizoci, nagwe komupitula wuwe kutya, “Muwaha Mulungu yaciwusa ase mu yisi ya Misili kwe vilungo vyakwe viwaha, i hanhu hono ase cali cili wawanda.
15 Na lono mutemi Falawo yali yeza kuwa mukamu kuwuhulice no kulema kucikombola ase cibite, ‘Muwaha Mulungu nayo yakawawulaga i wana we cilume wose iwalongozi kulelwa wa mu yisi yose ya Misili, i wana we wanhu sunga ne zinyana ze mitugo nhavivilya. Nayo ayi, yo soko yono yikuleka cikumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu nyana zose zinume ze mitugo zinongozi kulelwa, langa i wana wetu we cilume, awo cikuwakomboza.
16 Ne citanjilo cici, cowa cikumbucizo, kanyhi cowa nha cinhu cono cibate mu miwoko yetu hamba cono cijeye ha meso getu. Naco cowa kucikumbuciza vyono, u Muwaha Mulungu, yo yaciwusa ase ku yisi ya Misili, kwe vilungo vyakwe viwaha.’ ”

Lisumbili lye livunde ne lisumbili lyo moto

17 Mutemi Falawo yaza kuwakombola wanhu wabite, Mulungu si yawatonga wakole mu nzila yono yali yikoleye mu yisi ye Wafilisiti, hamba yiwe yo yali yo nzila ye ndumula. Hambi Mulungu nha yehwesa yakatya, “Singhusaka wanhu wacenye no kuwuya kanyhi ku Misili kolo wakuwona vyono kowuli wulugu ku mwande yawo.”
18 Holeka ku soko yiyo, Muwaha Mulungu yakawatonga wakole nzila nhali yono yipilime, yono yikolile mu nyika ye lilenga lya Shamu. Nawo Waisilayeli wakatuluka ku wulawa Misili akuno wavwalile ne vitwalo vyo wulugu.
19 Musa yakasoleleza ne mafupa ga Yusufu, wakabita nago, nha vyono Yusufu mweneco yali yawelahiza Waisilayeli wanoze nhavivyo. Hambi Yusufu lono yali yakali mumi, nha yali yawalonjela yatya, “Kolo Mulungu yakumuponyenyi, munojeye nhondo musoleleze ne mafupa gangu mubite nago.”

20 Waisilayeli wakatuluka ku wulawa ku Sukoti, wakasita nghambi yawo mu Etamu, ku mhelo ye nyika.
21 Na mu misi, Mulungu yakawalongolela mwi'vunde lititu lyono lipalinjile nhe lisumbili, ho yawatonje mu nzila. Na mu cilo namo, yakawalongolela mwi'vunde lyo moto, lyono lipalinjile nhe lisumbili, ho yawamulicile nzila, ho wasume kujenda muhinzo wawo misi ne cilo.
22 Ne livunde lyono lipalinjile nhe lisumbili, vilo vyose lyawalongolelaga ku mwande yawo mwi'pango lye misi. Nalyo livunde lyo moto, lyono lipalinjile nhe lisumbili, lyawamulicilaga nzila ku cilo. Hamba si yawukaga ha mwande ye wanhu.


Wutuluce 14

Wulowoce we lilenga lya Shamu

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela kanyhi Musa yakatya,
2 “Walonjele Waisilayeli wawuye ne nyuma, mukasite nghambi yenyu, muwe mwidomile na Pi-hahiloti, hagati ya Migidodi ne lilenga lya Shamu, behi na Baali-zefoni.
3 Nayo mutemi Falawo kokwihwesa vyono Waisilayeli wajililwa nzila, alu weza wakuyumbayumba du mu'si, nyika yawadindila.
4 Nane nomunoza Falawo yawe yasina cihuliko, nayo komulondenyi, na kolo yakumutyatyenyi, nane nomongomeza hamonga ne lipuga lyakwe lyose. Nayo Falawo ne wanhu wakwe ho womanya vyono ane nili vilungo. Ne Wamisili ho womanya vyono, ‘Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.’ ” Nawo Waisilayeli wakanoza nha vyono walonjelwa.

5 Nayo Falawo mutemi wa Misili yali yeza kulonjelwa nha vyono wanhu wakondya kutiza, mwene hamonga ne wetumami wakwe, mahweso gawo kanyhi gakapituka wakawa kutya, “Alu mwene 'nye, nghanici yiyi yono citumile? Cawaleceleza Waisilayeli watiza, nase cakondya kuwajiliza lu wawanda wetu!”
6 Hodu mutemi Falawo yakawa kuwikaniza mutuka wakwe wo wulugu, hamonga ne lipuga lyakwe.
7 Yakawa kutuluka hamonga ne mituka yakwe yose yo wulugu, hamonga ne mituka yinji yo wulugu magana mutandatu i miswanu, yali yo ye wetumami wakwe.
8 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamunoza mutemi Falawo yawe yasina cihuliko, yakawalonda Waisilayeli, hambi nha wawuka kwa cisumisumi mu yisi ya Misili.
9 Nalyo lipuga lye Wamisili, hamonga ne zifwalasi, ne mituka yo wulugu ne wadeleva wose, wakawa kuwalonda mu nyuma Waisilayeli. Wakawagana hanhu hono wali wasitile nghambi yawo ha mwande ye lilenga lya Shamu behi na Pi-hahiloti na Baali-zefoni.

10 Nawo Waisilayeli wali weza kumuwona mutemi Falawo hamonga ne lipuga lyakwe wakuwaziza, wakenjilwa na woga wuwaha muno muno, wakawa kumulilila Muwaha Mulungu yawataze.
11 Wakamulonjela Musa wakatya, “Mwene 'gwe Musa, kuko ku Misili kwali kusina vitenjelo? Hamba kucisola wucilete ahano ho cifwile mono nyika? Alu wulanje ta, yo ya hayi lu yono witumile, yo kuciwusiza ku Misili!
12 Wule, ase si cakulonjela lono cikali hamba sinatuluka ku Misili, vyono zizi si zoleka kwijela? Kanyhi ase cakulonjela vyono, wucilekaje cijendelele kuwetumamila Wamisili. Yali yiwe yili ciba cijendelele kuwetumamila Wamisili, si nho kufwila mono nyika.”

13 Nayo Musa yakawapitula yakawatya, “Nye, wayangu, mulece kogopa ye! Mwime mukamile, nanye mowona vyono Muwaha Mulungu komuponyenyi izuwali. Nanye hamba si mowawonela lukulu kanyhi Wamisili wawa wono mukuwawona izuwali.
14 Muwaha Mulungu mweneco yo komulewelenyi, nanye mowa munyamaye twii, hamba kunoza cinji yaye.”

15 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Alo nhawule wukundilila 'ne, wukulondola wutazi? Walonjele wanhu wajendelele ku mwande.
16 Ale, winule nghome yako mucanyha ye lilenga, nago malenga gokwitemanula hejete, nawo Waisilayeli wosuma kukola hakavu, ha ciligati ye lilenga.
17 Nane nowanoza Wamisili wawe wasina cihuliko. Nawo wowatyatya Waisilayeli mu nyuma yawo mwi'lenga, nane nogagomoleza malenga, niwagubize Wamisili. Baho ho nokwitujila lilumbo yiwaha muno, kolo nakondya kuwongomeza Falawo, ne lipuga lyakwe, ne matuka gakwe go wulugu, hamonga ne wajendela mu zifwalasi wakwe.
18 Nane kolo nawasuma Wamisili, nawo ho womanya vyono, ‘Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.’ ”

19 Nayo malayika wo Mulungu mono yawatongaga lipuga lye Waisilayeli, yakawuka ku mwande yawo, yakabita mu nyuma yawo. Nalyo livunde lyono lipalinjile kotya yisumbili, likawuka ku mwande yawo, likabita ku nyuma,
20 likawa hagati ye Wamisili ne Waisilayeli. Nalyo livunde likawapela citi Wamisili, ninga Waisilayeli, likawamulicila ne cilo. Holeka mapuga gago mejete lipuga lya Falawo ne lipuga lye Waisilayeli, si gasuma kwisejelela cilo cose sunga wusiku wukela.

21 Musa yakawugolola muwoko wakwe mucanyha ye lilenga, nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakalikunya lilenga cilo cose ne mbeho mbaha ya kucilima. Mbeho yiyo yakukunza cilo cose sunga wukela, yikalitemanula lilenga hejete, ne yisi nghavu yikawa kwijela.
22 Nawo Waisilayeli wakakola hakavu hagati ye lilenga, ne majinji ge malenga gakawa gemile ku lubavu lwawo lo kulume no lubavu lo kucekulu.
23 Nawo Wamisili wakawatyatya Waisilayeli mu nyuma yawo, wakenjila nhavivilya mwi'lenga hamonga ne zifwalasi zawo, ne mituka yawo yo wulugu, hamonga ne wajendela mu zifwalasi wawo wose.
24 Wusiku wali weza kuwa wuli behi no kwelelecela, Muwaha Mulungu yakalilanga lipuga lye Wamisili ku wulawilila mwi'vunde lyo moto lyono lipalinjile kotya nhe lisumbili, yakawatajila Wamisili woga wuwaha muno muno.
25 Muwaha Mulungu yakagadodomeza mu nhope matuka gawo go wulugu, na ku soko yiyo wakajenda ko wugaye wuwaha. Sunga nhondo wakawa kutya, “Nye, alenyi citize ha mwande ye wanhu wawa Waisilayeli, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yali ku lubavu lwawo yakuwalewela.”

26 Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Wugolole muwoko wako mwi'lenga, nago malenga gowuya hamonga gawagubize Wamisili, hamonga ne matuka gawo go wulugu, hamonga ne wajendela mu zifwalasi wawo.”
27 Musa yakagolola muwoko wakwe mucanyha ye lilenga, na lono wali weza kwelelecela, malenga gakawa kuwuya hamonga. Nawo Wamisili wakajela muno muno wagatize malenga, ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakawongomezeza wose mwi'lenga mugati.
28 Nago malenga gakawuya hamonga, gakayigubiza mituka yo wulugu, ne wajendela mu zifwalasi, hamonga ne lipuga lyose lya Falawo lyono lyawatyatyaga Waisilayeli, na wose wakafwila mugati mwi'lenga. Si yapona hamba monga bwete.
29 Ninga Waisilayeli wapuluta hakavu hagati ye lilenga, lono malenga gali gemile kotya majinji ku lubavu lwawo lo kulume no lubavu lo kucekulu.

30 Nhavivi vyo Muwaha Mulungu yawaponya Waisilayeli mwizuwa lilyo, mu miwoko ye Wamisili. Waisilayeli wakawawona Wamisili walambalaye mu nhumbi ye lilenga wafwiye.
31 Nawo Waisilayeli wali weza kuliwona litumo lilyo lyo wizine, lyono yetumile Muwaha Mulungu kuli Wamisili, nhondo nawo wakawa na cogopo kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Wakamwihuwila Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga na Musa mwitumami wakwe.


Wutuluce 15

Lwimbo lwa Musa

1 Nayo Musa hamonga ne Waisilayeli, baho wakamwimbila Muwaha Mulungu lwimbo aluno:

“Nomwimbila Muwaha Mulungu,

hambi nha yasuma ko lukumyo,

zifwalasi hamonga ne wajendela mu zifwalasi wayo,

kawongomezeza wose mwi'lenga.

2 Muwaha Mulungu vyo vilungo vyangu,

kanyhi lo lwimbo lwangu,

nayo mweneco yo mhonelo yangu.

Nayo yo Mulungu wangu,

nane nomulumba,

yo Mulungu wo sogo yangu, nane nomukumya.

3 Muwaha Mulungu yo we vilungo mu milewo,

Muwaha Mulungu, lye 'tagwa lyakwe.

4 “Mituka yo wulugu, hamonga ne

lipuga lyose lya Falawo,

Muwaha Mulungu kavyongomezeza vyose mwi'lenga.

Ne wetumami wakwe watambule,

wafwila wose mwi'lenga lya Shamu.

5 Visolo vye malenga viwagubiza,

wavulumucila mwi'lenga kotya yibwe.

6 “Muwoko wako wo kulume 'gwe Muwaha Mulungu,

wo we nhojo mu vilungo.

No muwoko wako wo kulume 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wukuwa kuwatijinha walugu.

7 Nagwe ko wuwaha wako wo wizine,

kuwongomezaga wose wono wakukupingapinga,

kuwaputilaga wukali wako,

wuwa kuwaticimiza kotya mabeleje makavu.

8 Na ku soko yo wehele wa mu mhula yako,

malenga gakelundika hamonga.

Nago mawingo gakema wima kotya yijinji,

ne visolo navyo vikajima mwi'lenga mugati.

9 Nayo mulugu yakeyitya, ‘Nowalonda nghawebate,

nane nozigawa zisawo zawo,

kanyhi nosola mu nghango yono nghuyisaka nene.

Nane nowukwega mumaje wangu,

na ko muwoko wangu, nowongomeza wose.’

10 Ninga 'gwe, wukaputa ne mbeho yako

nalyo lilenga likawagubiza wose.

Nawo wakadodomela mu malenga kotya lisasi.

11 “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

Alo nani 'mbi mono yehwanile gwegwe

mu milungu yose?

Hamba nani mono yehwanile

gwegwe go Muwaha Mulungu mono Welile,

mono wukogofya so mapoto gako,

ne milimo yako ye vilungo no wizine?

12 Nagwe wagolola muwoko wako wo kulume,

nayo yisi yikawamela walugu.

13 “Kanyhi na ko wihuwilwa wako wuwaha,

wukawatonga wanhu wono wawakomboza,

na kwe vilungo vyako,

wukawatonga ku wikalo wako wono welile.

14 I wanhu wakahulika,

wakacicima ko woga wuwaha,

nawo Wafilisiti wakwibatwa no woga wuwaha.

15 Ne wawaha wa ku Edomu,

nawo wakwinjilwa no woga wuwaha.

Nawo walongola wa ku Mowabu

wakwibatwa no woga wuwaha,

hamonga no wucicime, ne wekala ku Kanani

wose vilungo vyawo vyamalika.

16 Wogofye no woga, vyawagana,

ku soko yo wuwaha we vilungo vyako,

nawo wanyamala twii kotya yibwe,

sunga wanhu wako kolo wapuluta

wakondya, 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

heya, sunga wanhu wako wono wawakomboza,

wakondya kupuluta.

17 Agwe kowaleta wanhu wako,

wuwahadice mwi'tunda lyako,

i hanhu hono wehagulila,

go mweneco go Muwaha Mulungu,

ho hawe wo wikalo wako.

Bahalya hono helile hono wezenjela

kwe miwoko yako 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu.

18 Gwegwe, agwe go Muwaha Mulungu

kotema cibitilila ne cibitilila.”

Lwimbo lwa Milyamu

19 Waisilayeli wakalowoka lilenga hakavu, ninga mituka yo wulugu ye Wamisili ne zifwalasi zawo, hamonga ne wajendela mu zifwalasi, wali weza kwinjila mwi'lenga, u Muwaha Mulungu yakagagomoleza malenga hamonga, nago malenga gakawagubiza wose.
20 Nayo Milyamu u muwizi we cicekulu, u lumbulye wa Haluni, yakawa kusola ng'oma. Ne wacekulu wanji wose, nawo wakawa kumutyatya wena ne zing'oma zawo akuno wakuvina.
21 Nayo Milyamu yakawatonga no lwimbo alu yakutya,

“Ale mwimbilenyi Muwaha Mulungu,

hambi nha kasuma ko lukumyo luwaha,

fwalasi hamonga no mujendela mu fwalasi wayo,

yakawagwiliza mwi'lenga.”

Malenga makali

22 Hanyuma Musa yakawatonga Waisilayeli ku wulawa kwi 'lenga lya Shamu, wakabita sunga ko ku nyika ya Shuli. Nawo wajenda vilo vidatu mu nyika hamba kuwona malenga yaye.
23 Wali weza kufika hanhu hono hakucemwa Mala, hamba si wasuma kugang'wa malenga ga baho ha Mala, hambi nha gali makali. Na ku soko yiyo i hanhu baho hakacemwa Mala.
24 Ne wanhu wose wakamung'ung'ulicila Musa wakutya, “Alu Musa, cong'wa cici?”
25 Nayo Musa yakamulomba ko winenenyhezi Muwaha Mulungu, nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajila Musa cibici. Musa yakacigwiliza mu malenga, nago malenga gakawa maswanu ku wung'we.

Nayo Muwaha Mulungu kuko yakawapela Waisilayeli lilajilizo hamonga no wulajilize, yakawajela ho yamanye wusikame wawo.
26 Yakawalonjela yakatya, “Hono mbeka mulihulice lizi lyangu ane no Muwaha Mulungu, no Mulungu wenyu, mwitume zono zili ling'ani hali'ne, na kanyhi hono muwukamise no wulajilize wangu, hamonga ne malajilizo gangu, ale, nane si nomugumililenyi matamwa gagalya gono nawagumilila Wamisili yaye. Hambi ane nha yo nene Muwaha Mulungu mono nghumuponyenyi.”
27 Hanyuma Waisilayeli wakafika ku Elimu, hanhu hono hali ho yili mivwilo yikumi na myejete ye malenga, hamonga ne mitende makumi mupunghati. Nawo wakasita nghambi yawo baho behi ne malenga.


Wutuluce 16

Ndigwa yono yikucemwa Mana hamonga na Nghwale

1 Imhuga yose ye Waisilayeli, wakatuluka wakafika mu nyika ya Sini, hagati ya Elimu na Sinayi. Ali lyali lizuwa lye likumi ne hano lya mu mwezi we kejete, wulawa lono wali wawuka mu yisi ya Misili.
2 Baho mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli wakamung'ung'ulicila Musa na Haluni.
3 Wakatya, “Si ciba le Muwaha Mulungu yali yaciwulajile ku yisi ya Misili, kono cali cikaye ne masefuliya gamemile nyama ne mikate, nase calizaga nha vyono casakaga sunga ciguta. Alu mulanje ta, anye mwacileta amuno mono nyika, ho muciwulaje ne nzala.”

4 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Alu, ane nomulagazizenyi mikate kulawa ku wulanga. Nawo wanhu wolawila no kutondola nhondo zose ndigwa yono yikwiliye lizuwa lilyo limonga du. Na mu nzila yiyi, nane ho nosuma kuwona hono wowutyatya wulajilize wangu.
5 Langa mwi'zuwa lyo mutandatu, wotondola ndigwa nyinji kali kejete ya mu zindigwa ze nhondo yose.”

6 Nawo Musa na Haluni wakawalonjela Waisilayeli wakatya, “Zuwalili cinyamihe ho momanyila vyono, Muwaha Mulungu yo mwene mono yamuwusenyi mu yisi ya Misili.
7 Kanyhi mitondo hakali mitondo moluwona lukumyo lo Muwaha Mulungu, hambi nha kakondya kuwuhulika wung'ung'ulice wenyu wono mukumung'ung'ulicila. Ase 'mbi cili wanani ho mucing'ung'ulicile? Ase cikutyatya zono mwene yakucilajiliza du.”
8 Musa yakatya kanyhi, “Muwaha Mulungu, i cinyamihe, komupelenyi nyama, na hakali mitondo komupelenyi mikate ho mulye mwigute, hambi mwene Muwaha Mulungu nha kakondya kuwuhulika wung'ung'ulice wenyu, wono mukumung'ung'ulicila. Ase 'mbi cili wanani ho mucing'ung'ulicile? Hono mukucing'ung'ulicila ase, mumanye vyono mukumung'ung'ulicila Muwaha Mulungu.”

9 Musa yakawa kumulonjela Haluni yakamutya, “Gwe Haluni, wuwatezaje mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli, wekongholele hali Muwaha Mulungu, hambi nha kawuhulika wung'ung'ulice wenyu wono mukumung'ung'ulicila.”
10 Nayo Haluni lono yalongaga ne mhuga ye Waisilayeli, wakalanga wose ku lubavu lwa ku nyika, hobaho lukumyo lo Muwaha Mulungu, lukejela mu mavunde.
11 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakamutya,
12 “Nghondya kuwuhulika wung'ung'ulice we Waisilayeli. Alu wulongane nawo wuwatye vyono, mu madaha ge cinyamihe, wolya nyama, na hakali mitondo wolya mukate. Nawo ho womanya vyono, Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu.”

13 Nayo yikawa mu madaha ge cinyamihe, zikaza nghwale nyinji, zikamema mu nghambi yose ye Waisilayeli. Na hakali mitondo, buhe yikagwa yikatandala mu behi behi ye nghambi yawo.
14 Ne buhe yiyo, lono yawukaga kuko ku nyika, hasi cikasigala cinhu cono cali cinha mukate mupelupelu.
15 Nawo Waisilayeli wali weza kuwona wakezina muno muno hambi nha si wamanya vyono cali cinhuci, holeka wakawa kwiwuza wakutya, “Alu nye, cicici cene ci?”

Nayo Musa yakawalonjela yakatya, “Ayino yo ndigwa yono mupejigwe no Muwaha Mulungu ho mulye.
16 No Muwaha Mulungu kalajiliza mutondole ndigwa ayi, ha nghango du yono munhu kosuma kulya. Mutondole mu nghango ye cipimo ca munhu likopo limonga limonga kuli wanhu wose wono wekaye mwi'hema lyenyu.”

17 Nawo Waisilayeli wakanoza nhavivyo. Na yikawa lono watondolaga, wanji wakatondola nyinji, na wanji nyhefu.
18 Ninga wali weza kuyipima wose mwi'kopo, wakagana vyono mono yatondola nyinji si yasigaza yaye, nayo mono yatondola nyhefu hamba si yanjiza ng'o. Nawo wose wakatondola munhu ha nghango yo wulye wakwe.
19 Musa yakawalonjela kanyhi Waisilayeli yakatya, “Munhu wo wose yalece kusigaza ndigwa yigone sunga hakali mitondo.”

20 Ninga wanhu hamba si wamuhulika Musa. Wanji wa muli wene wasigaza ndigwa sunga hakali mitondo. Na hakali mitondo, i ndigwa yiyo yikawa yakondya kuwola no kuwicila mang'onyo. Nayo Musa yakawakolelwa likuwo muno muno wanhu.
21 Nhondo ku wulawa baho, yikawa nhondo zose hakali mitondo, watondolaga du ndigwa yono munhu yasuma kuyilya no kuyimala. Ne lizuwa lyali lyeza kuwalika, no kukalipa, hodu ndigwa yiyilya yikenjeduka.

22 Na mwizuwa lyo mutandatu, Waisilayeli watondola ndigwa yiyo nyinji ya nghanghulo nyejete, watondola ndigwa ya munhu monga makopo mane. Ne wanyamhala wose wa mu mhuga ye Waisilayeli, wamubitila Musa, wakamulonjela nghani yiyi.
23 Nayo Musa yakawalonjela yakatya, “Awu wo wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu. Imitondo lyo lizuwa lyono lyelile lyo kuceha. Ne mitondo lyo lizuwa lye Sabato yo Muwaha Mulungu. Holeka mubite mukatelece, hamba muhemuse ndigwa yiyo mu nghango yono mukuyisaka izuwali, ne ndigwa yono yosigala, muyiwice sunga mitondo.”
24 Nawo wakanoza nha vyono Musa yawalonjela, wakasigaza ndigwa yinji so mitondo yaco. Nawo wali weza kwela hakali mitondo, wakayiwona ndigwa yiyilya vyo swanu du, si yawola hamba kwinjilwa na mang'onyo yaye.
25 Nayo Musa yakawalonjela yakatya, “Ale mulize ndigwa yiyo yono yisigaye, hambi izuwali nha yo Sabato yo Muwaha Mulungu. Hambi izuwali nha si moyiwona ndigwa mu nyika.
26 Hamby nha munojeye mutondole ndigwa mu vilo mutandatu du, ninga mwizuwa lyo mupunghati, mwizuwa lyo wucehe, ndigwa yowa hasina.”

27 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo lyo mupunghati, wanhu wanji wakabita ku wulondola ndigwa, ninga hamba si wayituga yaye.
28 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Alo sunga nili, mojendelela kulema kugebata malajilizo no wulajilize wangu?
29 Ninga mumanye vyono, yo nene Muwaha Mulungu mono nimupelenyi lizuwa lilyo lye Sabato, na yo soko yono yikuleka nomupelenyi ndigwa ye vilo vyejete mwi'zuwa lyo mutandatu. No munhu wo wose, yimunojeye yekale baho du hono ho yuli, mwi'zuwa lilyo lye Sabato, yalece kuwuka mu kaya yakwe.”
30 Holeka mwizuwa lyo mupunghati, wanhu si wetuma mulimo wunji, langa waceha du.

31 Nawo Waisilayeli wakayicema ndigwa yiyo “Mana.” Nayo yali yihwanile kotya mbeyu yo wuhemba wo lugugu, no muzinzi wayo wali winha nha wo mukate mupelupelu mubace wuci.
32 Nayo Musa yakawalonjela Waisilayeli yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu kalajiliza katya, cisigaze Mana yinji, cipimo ce kopo limonga, ciyiwice, waze wayiwone we zindelo zetu zose. Nazo zindelo zetu waze wawone ndigwa yono Mulungu yacilisa mu nyika, lono yaciwusiza mu yisi ya Misili.”

33 Musa yakamulonjela Haluni yakatya, “Ale, sole ciya wutajile Mana yikopo limonga, wuyiwice hali Muwaha Mulungu, ho yiwe so zindelo zetu zono zikuza.”
34 Nayo Haluni yakayiwika yiyo Mana, Hanhu hono helile, mugati ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane, yikale mumo.
35 Waisilayeli wayilya yiyo mana mu myaka makumi mane, sunga wakafika mu yisi ya Kanani.
36 (Likopo limonga likwinjila lita makumi mejete.)


Wutuluce 17

Malenga gakulawila mwi'talawe

(Wuw 20:1-13)

1 Ne mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli, wakatuluka wulawa mu nyika ya Sini, wakajenda ku wulawa hanhu, sunga hanhu hanji, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalajilizaga. Wakasita nghambi kuko ku Lefidimu. Ninga kuko kwali kusina malenga go kung'wa yaye.
2 Holeka wanhu wakamung'ung'ulicila Musa wakamutya, “Gwe Musa, wucipelaje le malenga cing'we.”

Nayo Musa yakawawuza yakawatya, “Alu nye, nhawulenyi mukung'ung'ulicila? Hamba nhawule mukumujela Muwaha Mulungu?”

3 Ninga wanhu wose vyono wali wena nyhilu ye malenga, wakajendelela kumung'ung'ulicila Musa du wakumutya, “Alu mwene, wukaciwusa akulya ku Misili ho wuciwulaje ne nyhilu ye malenga, hamonga ne wana wetu ne mitugo zetu?”
4 Hodu, Musa yakamulomba Muwaha Mulungu yakamutya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, alu niwanoze nhawule wanhu wawa? Alu nha wali behi no kunhowanga ne mabwe!”

5 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamupitula Musa yakamutya, “Alu agwe, wulongolele ku mwande ye Waisilayeli, weze wuli hamonga na wanji wacefu wa muli wanyamhala wawo. Ninga weze wudinghuye ne nghome yi yilya yono walitowela likolongo lye Nile.
6 Wulanje, nane nokwima ha mwande yako mwi'talawe, kuko he'tunda lya Holebu lyono likucemwa Sinayi. Nagwe wutowe litalawe, ne nghome yako, nago malenga golawila mumo mwi'talawe nawo wanhu wong'wa.” Ale, nayo Musa yakanoza nhavivyo ha meso ge wanyamhala we Waisilayeli.

7 Ne hanhu baho, Musa yakahacema Masa na Meliba, hambi nha ho hanhu hono Waisilayeli wang'ung'ulicila no kumujela Muwaha Mulungu wakutya, “Aa! Hongo mbeka Muwaha Mulungu yali hamonga nase hamba ng'o?”

Wihome we Waisilayeli ne Waamaleki

8 Hobaho wakaza Waamaleki, wakehoma ne Waisilayeli kuko ku Lefidimu.
9 Musa yakamulonjela Yoshuwa yakamutya, “Ale, wuhagule wanhu we cilume, wubite wukehome ne Waamaleki. Nane mitondo, nokwima mwi'tunda kucanyha, akuno nowa nina ne nghome yo Mulungu niyibate mu muwoko wangu.”
10 Nayo Yoshuwa yakanoza nha vyono Musa yamulonjela. Yakabita kwihoma ne Waamaleki. Nawo Musa na Haluni hamonga na Huli, wakabita wakema mwi'tunda kucanyha.
11 Yikawa, Musa lono yenulaga miwoko yakwe kucanyha, Waisilayeli wakasuma, ninga lono Musa yayitimyaga miwoko yakwe hasi, Waamaleki wakasuma.
12 Nhondo hanyuma miwoko ya Musa yikasuluka. Holeka Haluni na Huli wakasola yibwe wakamuwicila Musa yekalile, nawo wakayibatiliza miwoko yakwe, yumonga muwoko wo kulume, na yunji muwoko wo kucekulu. Nhondo miwoko ya Musa hamba si yasuluka kanyhi yaye, yikawa vyo minulwe du kucanyha, sunga lizuwa likaswa.
13 Na mu nzila yiyi, Yoshuwa yakawasumila lukulu Waamaleki.

14 Baho Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakamutya, “Ale, wuyandikaje nghani yiyino mu citabu, yiwe cikumbucizo. Kanyhi mulonjele na Yoshuwa vyono nowapatila lukulu Waamaleki mono yinze.”
15 Nayo Musa yakazenga cikumbicilo, yakacicemela litagwa cikumbicilo cico yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu yo bendela yangu.”
16 Kanyhi yakatya, “Mwinule kucanyha bendela yo Muwaha Mulungu! Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha kojendelela kuwahoma Waamaleki cibitilila!”


Wutuluce 18

Yetelo yakubita kumuwona Musa

1 Nayo Yetelo u mukohani wa Midiyani, u mukwewe wa Musa, yakazihulika zinghani zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yawatendela Musa hamonga ne Waisilayeli, lono yawawusiza mu yisi ya Misili.
2 Yetelo yakamubitila Musa yakamuteguliza na Zipola mugolece wa Musa, vyono yali yakali yasigaye ku nyuma ko sogwe Yetelo.
3 Nayo Zipola yakawa yena hamonga ne wanagwe wejete, u mulongozi yacemwaga litagwa lyakwe Gelishomu. Hambi Musa nha yamucemela litagwa lili yakutuniliza yakutya, “Nali nili mutizizi mu yisi njenzi.”
4 Ne litagwa lyo mwanagwe we kejete, yacemwaga Eliyeza, hambi nha yatunilizaga yakutya, “Mulungu wa baba, yo yamhonizye, ho ndece kuwulagwa na Falawo.”
5 Nayo Yetelo mukwewe wa Musa, yakaza kuli Musa yena na Zipola mwanagwe we cicekulu, u mugolece wa Musa, hamonga ne wanagwe. Yakamwagana Musa kuko ku nyika kono yali yasitile nghambi he'tunda lyono lyelile.

6 Nayo Musa yali yeza kulonjelwa nha vyono mukwewo, no mugolece wako, hamonga ne wanago wejete wakuza,
7 hobaho yakawa kubita kumuhinjiliza mukwewe. Yakenama yakamunonela. Wakawa kwilamusa no kwitambuza, hanyuma wose wakawa kwinjila mwi'hema.
8 Nayo Musa yakawa kumuloloseza mukwewe zinghani zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yamunoza Falawo, hamonga ne Wamisili, ho yawaponye Waisilayeli. Yakamuloloseza na zose zono zawagana, zo wugazice wa mu muhinzo, na vyono Muwaha Mulungu yawaponya.
9 Nayo Yetelo yakendezwa muno so zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yetuma ku soko ye Waisilayeli, na vyono yawaponya mu miwoko ye Wamisili.
10 Baho Yetelo yakatya, “Yalumbwe Muwaha Mulungu, mono yamuponyenyi mu miwoko ya Falawo, no kumuwusenyi mu miwoko ye Wamisili!
11 Alu nane namanya nha vyono, u Muwaha Mulungu, yo Yalawiliciye kuciza milungu yinji yose. Hambi nha yawakomboza Waisilayeli, mu miwoko ye Wamisili wono wali kuwatendela vibi.”
12 Baho Yetelo mukwewe wa Musa, yakamulavizya Mulungu cilavywa cinyanywe hamonga ne vilavywa vinji. Nayo Haluni yakaza hamonga ne wanyamhala wose we Waisilayeli, ho wahanje ndigwa hamonga na Yetelo, hali Muwaha Mulungu.

Wuhagulwe we Walamuzi

(Kum 1:9-18)

13 Imitondo yaco, Musa yakekala no kuwalamula Waisilayeli. Nawo wakema wamupilime wulawa hakali mitondo sunga camihe.
14 Nayo Yetelo mukwewe wa Musa yali yeza kuziwona zizo, zono Musa yawanozezaga Waisilayeli, yakamuwuza yakatya, “Musa, yo nghanici yiyi waco yono wukuwanozeza wanhu? Alu nhawule wukwikala wuli wene, akuno wanhu wakupilime wulawa mitondo sunga cinyamihe?”

15 Nayo Musa yakamupitula mukwewe yakatya, “Alu wanhu nha wakunyiziza no kuwuza zo wuhile wo Mulungu.
16 Na hono hawe ho wali wanhu wejete wono wowa wena wugomvi wo wose, wakuza kwangu, nane niwa kuwalamula munhu no muyagwe, kanyhi niwa kuwejiza zo wulajilize wo Mulungu hamonga no wutemye wakwe.”

17 Baho nayo Yetelo yakamulonjela Musa yakamutya, “Ninga Musa, zono wukuzinoza, si wukunoza viswanu ng'o.
18 Hambi nha kokwitamisa so mukatalo gwegwe go mweneco hamonga ne wanhu wawa. Ku soko mulimo awu wono wukwituma nha mukamu, hamba si kosuma yaye kwituma gwegwe du wuli wene.
19 Alu ane nokupela lihala, no Mulungu yawe hamonga nagwe. Agwe wime hagati ye wanhu no Mulungu, wumuletele Mulungu zinghani zawo.
20 Na kanyhi yikunojeye wuwejize u wulajilize wo Mulungu hamonga no wutemye wakwe. Kanyhi wuwalajile nha vyono yiwanojeye mu mikalile yawo, na vyono yiwanojeye wetume.
21 Ninga hamonga na zizo, ko muno, wuhagule wanhu wono wakwilaniciye mu Waisilayeli, wanhu wono wali cogopo hali Mulungu, kanyhi wehuwilwa, wono si wendezwaga ne mafupo. Nagwe wuwapele wawo wulajilize, wawe wawaha mu wanhu, wawaha we maelufu, na we magana, na we makumi mahano, na we wanhu yikumi yikumi.
22 Wuwapele wulajilize, ho wawalamule wanhu mu zinghani zawo. Ne zinghani zinghamu, azo langa zo wakuletele gwegwe, ninga azilya i zindodo ndodo, azo wazilamule weneco du kuko. Ne nghani yiyi yokucepulila winji we milimo, nawo wokutaziliza mu mulimo wuwu mukamu.
23 Nagwe hono wunoze nhavivyo nha vyono Mulungu yendile, si ko kwisakaza musunho, ne wanhu wose nawo wusuma kuwuya kwawo wapolile.”

24 Nayo Musa yakazivumila zose zono yalonjelwa no mukwewe, yakanoza nha vyono yalonjelwa.
25 Yakawa kuwahagula wanhu we vilungo mu Waisilayeli, yakawanoza wawe watonji mu zimhuga ze wanhu, mu maelufu, mu magana, mu makumi mahano na we wanhu yikumi yikumi.
26 Nawo wawo wo wawalamulaga wanhu nhondo zose, langa zinghani zinghamu du, zo wakamuletela Musa, ninga zindodo, wakazilamula weneco kuko du.

27 Hodu, Musa yakawa kwilaga no mukwewe. Nayo Yetelo yakawa kuwuya ku yisi yakwe.


Wutuluce 19

Waisilayeli he 'tunda lya Sinayi

1 Na mwizuwa lilongozi lya mu mwezi we kadatu, ku wulawa lono wawucila ku Misili, Waisilayeli wakafika mu nyika ya Sinayi.
2 Nawo wali weza kuwuka ku Lefidimu no kufika mu nyika ya Sinayi, wakasita nghambi yawo mu nyika vidomile ne litunda lya Sinayi.
3 Musa yakabita kwi'tunda kucanyha ho vilongane no Mulungu.

Muwaha Mulungu yakalongana na Musa ku wulawilila mumo mwi'tunda, yakamulonjela yawalonjele wawo Waisilayeli wana wa Yakobo yatye nhavi,
4 “Nyenye nyo weneco mwewonelela vyono nawanoza Wamisili, kanyhi na vyono nghamudinghulenyi nha vyono 'ndi yidinghulaga vyanagwe mu mabawa gayo, nghamuletenyi sunga hono hangu.
5 Holeka aluno, hono mulihulice lizi lyangu, kanyhi mwibate ne cilagane cangu, mowa muli wanhu wangu wahagulwa muli makungugo gose, hambi yinze yose nha yangu nene.
6 Nanye kuli'ne mowa wutemi we wakohani, kanyhi nghungugo yono yelile.” Nazo zizi zo nghani zono kowalonjela Waisilayeli.

7 Musa yakawa kuwuya kuli wanhu, yakawakonghola hamonga i wanyamhala we Waisilayeli, yakawa kuwalonjela zinghani zizo zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza.
8 Nawo wanhu wakazivumila wose hamonga wakatya, “Zizo zose zono Muwaha Mulungu, yazilonjile, ase cozituma.” Nayo Musa yakamumanyiza Muwaha Mulungu lipitulo lilyo lye wanhu.
9 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela kanyhi Musa yakatya, “Ale, ane lu nokuziza mwi'vunde lizito, Waisilayeli ho wasume kuhulika 'ne lono cikulongana nagwe, nawo ho wasume kukwihuwila agwe cibitilila.” Nayo Musa yakamulonjela Muwaha Mulungu zono wanhu walonjile.

10 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela kanyhi Musa, yakatya, “Alu, wubite wukaweze wanhu izuwali ne mitondo. Nawo wose wanojeye wakanze vivwalo vyawo,
11 ne cilawu, wose mweze mwikondelezeze lukulu. Na mwi'zuwa lilyo lye kadatu, nane no Muwaha Mulungu, notima mucanyha ye litunda lya Sinayi ha meso ge wanhu wose.
12 Ninga wuwawicile wanhu mupaka, wupilimye litunda lyose. Kanyhi wuwasugule vyono, walecele lukulu kubita kwi'tunda kucanyha, hamba kupamiza mupaka wayo. No munhu wo wose mono kokwijelela du yalipamize litunda, ale munhu yuyo kowulagwa.
13 Kanyhi wulanjilile, munhu yunji muli nyenye yalece kumupamiza munhu yuyo lono yakuwulagwa, langa munhu yuyo yawulagwe mu nzila yo kutowangwa ne mabwe, hamba kugumwa na songa, hono yawe munhu hamba yiwe mutugo. Kolo ndulele yalila cidaha, ale wanhu wose wekongholelaje hamonga behi ne litunda.”

14 Nayo Musa yakawa kutima ku wulawa mwi'tunda mucanyha, yakawa kuwabitila wanhu, yakaweza, nawo wose wakavikanza vivwalo vyawo.
15 Hanyuma yakawalonjela yakatya, “Alu, icilawu, wose mweze mwekondelezeza lukulu, munhu wo wose yalece kumwejelela mucekulu.”

16 Ale, wali weza kwela mwizuwa lye kadatu hakali mitondo, likaza livunde yizito hacanyha ye litunda, hamonga ne mabalalusi ne mamuli. Kanyhi likahulikwa lizi yiwaha lya ndulele, lyono lizozize muno muno, no wuzoze wayo, wukawogofya wanhu wose wa mu nghambi, sunga wose wakacicima muno.
17 Baho Musa yakawa kuwatonga wanhu wose mu wulawa mu nghambi, wakawa kubita ho waketang'anile no Mulungu. Nawo wose wakabita wakekongholela hasi ye litunda.

18 Nalyo litunda lya Sinayi likawa yigubikwe ne malyosi, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yatima mucanyha yalyo ko moto. Ne lyosi lyo moto wuwo likabita kucanyha muno nha lyosi lye 'kozo yiwaha muno. Sunga ne litunda lyose likacicima muno muno.
19 Nalyo lizi lye ndulele likacila kuzoza muno, nayo Musa yakawa kuduwana no Mulungu. Nayo Mulungu yakamupitulila Musa mumo mwi'balalusi.

20 Muwaha Mulungu yakatima mucanyha ye litunda lya Sinayi, yakamucema Musa ku wulawa kuko kucanyha, nayo Musa yakawa kubita kuko kwi'tunda kucanyha.
21 Hanyuma Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Alu, wutime hasi ye litunda, wukawasugule wanhu wose vyono walecele lukulu kunanga, na hono wananje du wenji wofwa.
22 Nawo wakohani wono wasejelelaga behi nane, nawo yiwanojeye weyeze, hono si nhavivyo, ane nowalipiciza.”
23 Nayo Musa yakamupitula Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Wanhu awa, hamba si wosuma kubita kwi'tunda lya Sinayi kucanyha, hambi nha gwegwe go mweneco mono wacilajiliza wukatya, ‘Muwicile mupaka wono wolipilima litunda lyose kanyhi mulyeze.’ ”
24 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamutya Musa, “Ale, wutime hasi ye litunda, wubite wukamulete Haluni, ninga langa wulanjilile, wulece kuwakombola wakohani, hamba wanhu wanji, walece kwijelela kuza kono kwangu, hono si nhavivyo, ane nowalipiciza.”
25 Nayo Musa yakawa kutima mwi'tunda, yakawa kuwaloloseza wanhu zinghani zose zono Mulungu yali yamulajilize.


Wutuluce 20

Malajilizo yikumi

(Kum 5:1-21)

1 Mulungu yalonga zinghani zizi yakatya,
2 “Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako, mono nakuwusa mu yisi ya Misili, kono wali wuli mu wuwanda.
3 Wulece kuwa na milungu yinji, langa nene du.
4 Wulece kwinozeza cihwani ciseswe, hamba cihwani cinji co cose cono cili ku wulanga kucanyha, hamba cono cili mono yinze, hamba cono cili mu malenga kusi ye yisi.
5 Wulece kuvihemba hamba kuvitumamila, hambi ane no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako, nha nili Mulungu wo wivu, Nane nghuwalipiciza wana wubi we walezi wawo, sunga ku lulelo lwe kadatu na lwe kane lwa wono wasulile.”
6 Ninga nghuwawonela lusungu maelufu na maelufu, wawalya wono webate malajilizo gangu.

7 “Wulece kulitumamila litagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu wako mu zinzila zimbi. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha komulipiciza munhu wowose mono yakulitumamila litagwa lyakwe mu zinzila zimbi.
8 Weze kulikumbucila lizuwa lye Sabato wulisiwanule.
9 Mu vilo mutandatu weze kwituma no kukondelezeza lukulu milimo yako yose.
10 Ninga lizuwa lyo mupunghati, yo Sabato yo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako. Holeka mwi'zuwa lilyo wulece kwituma mulimo wunji, gwegwe, hamba mwanago we cilume, hamba we cicekulu, hamba wetumami wako we cilume hamba we cicekulu, hamba mitugo zako, hamba mujenzi wako mono yali hako.
11 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, nha yawumba wulanga ne yinze, hamonga ne lilenga na vyose vyono vili mugati, kwa vilo mutandatu. Na mwi'zuwa lyo mupunghati yakawa kuceha. Holeka Muwaha Mulungu yakalilugutiza lizuwa lyo mupunghati yakalyeza.

12 “Wogopaje sogo no nyina yako, vilo vyo wikale wako ho viwe vinji mu yisi yono yakukupela Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako.

13 “Wulece kuwulaga munhu.

14 “Wulece kusenyha.

15 “Wulece kuhiza.

16 “Wulece kulavya wuhomelezi wo wulambi kuli muyago.

17 “Wulece kuyisulumila nyumba yo muyago. Wulece kumusulumila mucekulu wo muyago, hamba mwitumami wakwe we cilume hamba we cicekulu, hamba ng'ombe yakwe, hamba ndogowe yakwe, hamba cinji co cose co muyago.”

Woga we wanhu

(Kum 5:22-33)

18 Nawo Waisilayeli wali weza kulihulika libalalusi, no kugawona ne mamuli gakoleye, hamonga ne lizi yiwaha lye ndulele mumo mwi'tunda mucanyha, sunga ne lyosi lyono lyafunghaga, hodu wose wakenjilwa no woga, wakawa kucicima muno, ninga wose wali wemile kutali.
19 Wakamulonjela Musa wakatya, “Gwe Musa, agwe yo gwegwe du mono weze kulongana nase, nase cokuhulika du, ninga Muwaha Mulungu yalece kulongana nase, ho cilece kufwa.”
20 Nayo Musa yakawatya, “Ale mulece kogopa, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha kamuzizenyi ho yamujelenyi, ho yamunozenyi muwe ne cogopo kuli mwene nhondo zose, ho mulece kwituma zo wubi.”

21 Ne wanhu wose, wali wemile kutali, lono Musa yalisejelelaga livunde lililya lizito, kono kwali ko yuli Mulungu.

Wulajilize we cikumbicilo

22 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Wuwalonjele Waisilayeli wutye nhavi, ‘Alu mwakondya kwiwonelela nyenye nyo weneco vyono nongana nanye ku wulawa ku wulanga.
23 Holeka mulecele lukulu kwinozeza vihwani vye milungu ya fweza cihanje nane, hamba milungu ye zahabu.
24 Anye langa munzenjele cikumbicilo ce malongo. Nanye mondavizya mumo i vilavywa vinyanywe, hamonga ne vilavywa vyo mulamu, nha cinji zo ngholo zenyu, hamonga ne zing'ombe zenyu. Nane hanhu ho hose hono nohahagula nene, baho ho mukalombele mwi'tagwa lyangu, nane nomuzizenyi, kanyhi nomulugutizenyi.
25 Na hono munzenjele cikumbicilo ce mabwe, mulece kwitumamila mabwe masese. Hambi lono mukwitumamila vitwalo vyo kuseseza mabwe gago, nha mowa mwakondya kucitajila wavufywe cikumbicilo cico.
26 Kanyhi mulece kwitumamila ngazi lono mukubita mucanyha ye cikumbicilo, ho mulece kucikalila cipwi cikumbicilo cangu.’ ”


Wutuluce 21

Vyono yiwanojeye wawanda watendelwe

(Kum 15:12-18)

1 “Aga go malajilizo gono kowapela Waisilayeli.
2 Hono wumugule muwanda we Cihebelaniya, yuyo kokwitumamila myaka mutandatu du. Ninga mu mwaka wo mupunghati, hodu, wumukombole yabite, hamba wulece kumusaka yakulipe cinji cocose.
3 Na hono wuwe wamugula lono yakali si natola, kowuka du yali wene. Ninga hono yawe yaza yena no mugolece wakwe, kowuka yabite hamonga no mugolece wakwe.
4 Ninga langa hono mono yamugula yawe yamutoza mucekulu, nayo yakamulelela wana we cilume na we cicekulu, yuyo mucekulu hamonga ne wana, wowa sawo yakwe yuyo mono yamugula, ninga mwene mwitumami kowuka yali wene du.
5 Ninga mwitumami yuyo, hono yalonje calinze vyono yakali yamwendile yuyo mono yamugula, hamonga ne wanagwe, no mugolece wakwe, holeka si kokwenda kuwuka,
6 hodu, yuyo mono yamugula komuleta hali Mulungu. Na hanyuma kanyhi yamutegule ha cipimbila-mulango, yamutobole likutu no muhonelo. No mwitumami yuyo, komwitumamila mono yamugula mu mikalile yakwe yose.

7 “Ninga hono munhu yamuguze mwanagwe we cicekulu, ayo si kosuma kuwuka nha vyono wakuwuka wetumami we cilume yaye.
8 Na hono yuyo mwitumami we cicekulu yawe yagulwa no munhu mono yehwesaga vyono komutola, alu kanyhi yeza si yakumusaka, hodu, kosuma kumuleka yakombozwe no mulezi wakwe. No mulume yuyo kowa yasina ling'ani yo kumuguza yuyo muhinza kuli wanhu we makungugo ganji, hambi nha kamukonga bwete hamba kumutola yaye.”
9 Na hono yunji yamugulile mwanagwe we cilume mwitumami we cicekulu, yimunojeye yamudime viswanu nha vyono kamudimaga mwanagwe wakwe we cicekulu.
10 Na hono munhu yatole mucekulu we kejete, ale yimunojeye yajendelele kumudima mugolece wakwe mulongozi viswanu, yawe kumupela ndigwa, hamonga ne vivwalo vyono vikwiliye, kanyhi yamutendele viswanu muli zo wutole nha vyono kamutendelaga nhondo zose.
11 Na hono yawone vyono si kosuma kumutendela zizi zose vyono yidatu, hodu, yimunojeye yamukombole yabite kono yakusaka mweneco, kanyhi yalece kumulifya hamba cinhu co cose.

Wulajilize wa kuli zo wukali

12 “Mono komutowa munhu sunga yamuwulaje, yuyo nayo kowulagwa.
13 Ninga hono yawe yali si yamupinjiye na katali, langa Mulungu yo yamukomboye mu miwoko yakwe, ale, ane nomusiwanulilenyi hanhu hono yuyo muwulaji kosuma kutiziza.
14 Ninga hono munhu yakolwe likuwo, yamumanyhilile muyagwe ne nyono, no kumuwulaga mbula soko, u munhu yuyo hamba yatizize ku cikumbicilo, anye momuwusa kuko ku cikumbicilo cangu, nayo mumuwulaje.

15 “Munhu mono komutowa sogwe hamba nyina, yuyo yawulagwe.

16 “Munhu mono komuhiza munhu yamuguze, hamba kumunoza yawe mwitumami wakwe, munhu yuyo yawulagwe.

17 “Munhu mono komulijita sogwe hamba nyina, yuyo yawulagwe.

18 “Nawo wanhu wejete hono wawe kugombana, hanyuma yumonga muli wene, yamutowe muyagwe ne libwe lunji na funzi, ninga si yamuwulaje, langa kamutamisa mu nghango yo kumunoza yawe mutamwa, sunga yagone mu citanda, munhu yuyo si kolipicizwa yaye.
19 Nayo mono yatowigwe kolo yatuga ciba, no kusuma kujenda yakwisimatiza ne nghome, hodu, ayulya mono yamutamise konenelwa. Ninga langa komulipa madaha gono yu yulya mutamwa yagajilize, kanyhi komutamya sunga yanojele lukulu.

20 “Na hono munhu yamutowe mwitumami wakwe we cilume, hamba we cicekulu, ne nghome, no kumuwulaga, munhu yuyo yanojeye yalipicizwe.
21 Ninga mwitumami yuyo hono yekale lizuwa limonga hamba vilo vyejete yawe yanoga, hodu, si kolipicizwa yaye, vyono yuyo mwitumami nayo nha sawo yakwe mweneco.

22 “Nawo walume wanji hono wawe wakwitowa, hanyuma wamutamise mucekulu mono yali wimo wunji, ne 'nda yakwe yilawe, ninga yawe yasina mawine ganji, ale yuyo mulume mono yamutamise, kolipa malipo nha vyono kotemya u munya mugolece yuyo, hamonga ne walamuzi.
23 Ninga langa hono yejele yeza yena wunyoga-nyoga wunji, ale, yomuwela mulume yuyo mono yamutamise, yalipe wumi kwa wumi,
24 liso kwa liso, lino kwa lino, muwoko kwa muwoko, mugulu kwa mugulu,
25 wunyanywe kwa wunyanywe, nghomele kwa nghomele, ne lidali kwe 'dali.

26 “Na hono munhu yamutowe liso mwitumami wakwe we cilume hamba we cicekulu, sunga yalivize liso lyakwe, hodu, komukombola yuyo mwitumami yabite, nayo gowa go malipo gakwe ku soko ye liso lyakwe.
27 Na kanyhi yo nhavivyo na hono yamutowe mu lino mwitumami wakwe we cilume hamba we cicekulu, sunga lino lyakwe libenece, hodu, komukombola yabite ku soko ye lino lyakwe.”

Vyono yiwanojeye wanya zisawo

28 “Hono ng'ombe yimutowe munhu ne lihembe sunga yimuwulaje, ng'ombe yiyo nayo yiwulagwe mu nzila yo kutowa ne mabwe, ne nyama yayo yilece kuligwa yaye. Ninga munya ng'ombe yiyo, yalece kulipicizwa yaye.
29 Langa hono ng'ombe yiyo yiwe yali yizowelile kutowa wanhu ne mahembe, na munya ng'ombe nayo yawe yalonjeywe zizo, nayo hamba kuyiwopa yaye, holeka hono yiwulaje munhu, ng'ombe yiyo yotowangwa ne mabwe sunga yifwe, na munya ng'ombe nayo yawulagwe nhavivilya.
30 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, yuyu munya ng'ombe, hono yavumilwe yalipe malipo no kuponywa wumi wakwe, ale yalipe yiyo nghango yono womusaka yalipe.
31 Nayo ng'ombe hono yimutowe mwana wa munhu yunji, ale nghani yiyi nayo yolamulilwa mu nzila yiyo du.
32 Ne ng'ombe hono yimutowe ne mahembe mwitumami we cilume hamba we cicekulu, munya ng'ombe yiyo, komulipa munya mwitumami yuyo fweza makumi madatu. Ne ng'ombe yiyo nayo moyitowanga ne mabwe sunga yifwe.

33 “Na munhu hono yawuse ligubicizo lye likombo, no kulileka lili mwazi, hamba lunji hono yahimbe likombo yalilece mwazi hamba kuligubiciza, na hanyuma ng'ombe lunji ndogowe yigwile mwi'kombo lilyo,
34 yuyo munya likombo lilyo, kowulipa mutugo wuwo. Yimunojeye yamulipe mapesa munya mutugo, kanyhi yasole no mutugo wuwo wono wufwiye wuwe wakwe.
35 Na hono ng'ombe ya yunji yiwulaje ng'ombe ya yunji, wanhu wawo, wayisole ng'ombe yiyo yono yili nyumi wayiguze, wagawane cico cifo cayo. Kanyhi wagawane sunga ne zinyama ze ng'ombe yono yifwiye.
36 Ninga langa hono yiwe ng'ombe yiyo yali yizowelile kuzitowa zinyagwe ne mahembe, nayo munya ng'ombe hamba kuyiwopa ng'o, kolipa ng'ombe kwa ng'ombe, na ayilya yono yifwiye yowa yakwe.”


Wutuluce 22

Wulajilize kuli ze malipo

1 “Hono munhu yahize ng'ombe lunji ngholo, no kuyiwulaga hamba kuyiguza, munhu yuyo kolipa ng'ombe yihano mu ng'ombe monga, na ngholo yine mu ngholo monga.
2 Muhizi hono yebatwe yakubena nyumba, nayo ho yatowe sunga yafwe, yuyo mono yamuwulaje hamba si kowa ne lisutya yaye lyo wuwulaji.
3 Ninga muhizi yuyo hono yebatwe lono lizuwa lyasosomoka hakali mitondo, nayo ho yawulagwe, ale yuyo mono yamuwulaje langa kowa musutyi. Hambi muhizi yuyo nha yali yimunojeye yalipe du. Na hono yawe yasina cinhu co kulipa, hodu, umuhizi yuyo yinojeye yaguzwe mweneco ho yalipile wuhizi wakwe.
4 Na hono mutugo wuwo wono wali muhize wijele ko muhizi yuyo wukali wuli nyumi, ale, yuyo muhizi yimunojeye yalavye malipo kali kejete, hono yiwe ng'ombe, hamba ndogowe hamba ngholo.

5 “Na hono munhu yalise mitugo yakwe mu mugunda we mizabibu, hamba hono lunji yayileceleze mitugo yakwe yidime mugunda wa munhu, ale, yuyo munya mitugo komulipa munya mugunda wambuce wuwo. Komulipa vilimwa viswanu vyono vikwilaniciye vya mu mugunda wakwe, hamba viswanu vya mu mugunda wakwe we mizabibu.

6 “Hono munhu yakoleleze moto mu mugunda wakwe, nawo moto wuwo wutandale, sunga wufice ku mugunda wo muyagwe yunji, wutakamize malundo ge zindigwa, hamba vilimwa vyose vyo muyagwe, vyono vili mumo mu mugunda vikali sinawuswa, hamba lunji mugunda wose wutakamila, yu yulya mono yakoleleze moto wuwo, yimunojeye yawulipe wambuce wuwo wose.

7 “Na hono munhu yamulecele muyagwe pesa yamuwicile, hamba sawo yoyose du yilya, na hanyuma sawo yiyo yihizwe mu nyumba yakwe, no muhizi hono yejele, muhizi yalipe cifo cayo cico kali kejete.
8 Ninga hono muhizi yalece kwijela, yuyo munya kaya yimunojeye yelahize hali Mulungu, yakangaze vyono mwene yali musina mumo mu wuhizi we zisawo zo muyagwe.

9 “Na hono hawe howuli wiwanganile wa mu ng'ombe, lunji ndogowe hamba ngholo, lunji myenda, hamba cinhu cinji cocose cono cajiliye, cono wanhu wejete wakwisoka, ayu yakutya cangu, no muyagwe nayo yakutya cangu, ale, wanhu wawo vyono wejete waletwe hali Mulungu. Na mono Mulungu kolamula vyono ayu yo musutyi, ale yuyo komulipa muyagwe kali kejete.

10 “Na hono munhu yamukoze muyagwe ndogowe, hamba ng'ombe hamba ngholo, hamba mutugo wo wose wulya yamudimile, no mutugo wuwo wufwe, lunji wutamiswe, lunji wusolelezwa hamba yunji kuwona.
11 Ale, wilahize wa hali Muwaha Mulungu wuwo du wo wolamula hali wene, no kulajila vyono yuyo mukozwa si yayihizize yiyo sawo yo muyagwe. Nayo munya sawo yimunojeye yawubocele wilahize wuwo. No mukozwa nayo, hodu si kolipa cinji yaye.
12 Ninga langa hono sawo yiyo yiwe yahizizwa ko mukozwa, ale, yimunojeye yuyo mukozwa yamulipe munya sawo.
13 Na hono yiwe mutugo wuwo wulumwa ne malimu ga mu mbago, ale, munhu yoyo mono yali yakozigwe, yimunojeye yawulete wuwo mutugo so wuhomelezi. Na vyono mutugo wulumwa ne malimu ga mu mbago, hodu mwene si kolipa yaye.

14 “Na hono munhu yamwazime muyagwe mutugo, na hanyuma mutugo wuwo wutamiswe, hamba lunji wufwe, na hono mwene munya mutugo yawe yali hasina, ale yu yulya mono yawazime, yimunojeye yalipe vyono yinojeye.
15 Ninga langa hono munya mutugo yawe yali baho, hodu yu yulya mono yamwazime, si kolipa cinji yaye. Na hono mutugo wuwo wuwe wusoligwe ho witume mulimo wa malipo, ale, munya mutugo kobocela du malipo go mulimo nha vyono wali walagane.”

Wulajilize wo wuhembi hamonga ne mikalile miswanu

16 “Hono we cilume yamukonjekonje, yamubabule muhinza mono yakali namanya walume yaye, mono yakali si mubanywe, ho yagone nayo, ale munhu yuyo kolavya cigumo no kumutola yuyo muhinza.
17 Na hono sogwe wo muhinza yalemele lukulu kumutoza muhinza wakwe ko mulume yuyo, ale munhu yuyo kolipa mu nghango ye cigumo cono cinojeye kuli muhinza mono yakali si namumanya mulume yaye.

18 “Wulece kumuleka muhawi we cicekulu yekale mumi.
19 Munhu wo wose mono kogona no mutugo, yimunojeye yawulagwe.
20 No munhu wo wose mono komulavizya makumbiko mulungu wo wulambi, yalece kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu du yali wene, munhu yuyo yongomezezwe lukulu.
21 Mulece kumwiwonela hamba kumukunhulanga munhu wo wose mujenzi, mweze kukumbucila vyono nanye mwali muli wajenzi mu yisi ya Misili.
22 Kanyhi mulece kumwiwonela mufwile, hamba mwana mono yasina walezi.
23 Na hono muwewonele, nawo wandilile, nane mbeka nocihulika cililo cawo.
24 Ne likuwo lyangu lyokwaka, nane nomuwulajenyi ko mumaje, ne wagolece zenyu wowa wafwile, ne wana wenyu nawo, wowa wasina walezi.

25 “Na hono wumukopeshe munhu pesa mu wanhu wangu iwaciwa, ale, wulece kuwa kuli mwene nha munhu mono wukumudayadaya, hamba wulece kumulifya ne ngomola yayo.
26 Na hono wusole mwenda wo muyago ciwe nha cipingo, ale yikunojeye wumugomolele lono lizuwa likali si nagwila.
27 Hambi wuwo nha wo mwenda wakwe du wono kefunikaga. Holeka hono wikale si wumugomoleye, alu ho kokwifunika cici? Na hono yandilile, nane nomuhuliciza, hambi ane nha nili lusungu.

28 “Wulece kumutovula Mulungu, hamba kumulijitiza mulongola we wanhu wako.

29 “Wulece kucela kundavizya vilavywa vyangu vya mu vilimwa vyako vyose, na vye divayi yako, na vye mafuta gako mu madaha gayo.

“Mumhele ne wana wenyu we cilume, i walongozi kulelwa.
30 Kanyhi mumhele ne zinyana zinongozi kulelwa ze zing'ombe zenyu na ze zingholo zenyu. Ninga mwamule muyilece yiyo nyana yiwe hamonga ne nyina kwa vilo mupunghati, na mwi'zuwa lyo munana, ale, ho mundavizye.
31 Anye muli wanhu wangu, mono mwasiwanulwa ku soko yangu. Holeka mulecele lukulu kulya nyama yo mutugo wo wose, wono gawulaje malimu ga mu mbago. Langa muzigumililaje zimbwa zenyu zo ziyilye nyama yiyo.”


Wutuluce 23

Ling'ani no wiling'ano

1 “Wulece kuzitandaza zinghani zo wulambi. Kanyhi wulece kwihanza no munhu mubi ho wuwe muhomelezi wakwe wo wulambi.
2 Wulece kutyatya lipuga lye wanhu lono wakwituma i zimbi, hamba wulece kwihanza ne lipuga lye wanhu, no kulavya wuhomelezi kuli munhu we ling'ani, ho yajilize ling'ani yakwe.
3 Kanyhi wulece kumuhijila mbejele u muciwa mu calo cakwe.

4 “Na hono wuyagane ng'ombe hamba ndogowe yo mulugu wako yajiliye, ale, wumugomolelaje munya ng'ombe hamba ndogowe yakwe.
5 Na hono wuyiwone ndogowe yo munhu mono yakusulile yigwiye hasi, yina no mutegulo wayo, ale, wumutazilizaje munhu yuyo kuyinula yiyo ndogowe yakwe, wulece kumuleka yaye wumupulute vyono yakusulile.

6 “Wulece kuyiwajiza ling'ani yono yimunojeye u munhu muciwa mu calo cakwe.
7 Kanyhi wulecele lukulu kulavya wuhomelezi wo wulambi, hamba kumwambuza munhu mono yasina lisutya, kawa kufwa bwete.
8 Wulece kubocela mafupo, hambi mafupo nha gabofuzaga wawaha no kwajiliza zinghani za we ling'ani.
9 Wulece kumwiwonela mujenzi, hambi nanye nha mumanyile wuwe wo wujenzi vyono winha. Nanye nha mwali muli wajenzi mu yisi ya Misili.”

Mwaka wo mupunghati ne lizuwa lyo mupunghati

10 “Mu myaka mutandatu, weze kuyihadika migunda yako hamonga no kugomola vilimwa vyayo.
11 Ninga mu mwaka wo mupunghati, wuyilekaje migunda yako yiceheze, hamba wulece kududula cinhu co cose cono cowa cisigaye mumo. Iwaciwa nawo ho walye vyono vyowa visigaye mumo, hamonga ne vilimu vya mu mbago navyo vilye. Sunga na mu mugunda wako we mizabibu, namo konoza nhavivyo, hamonga no mugunda we mizeyituni.

12 “Na mu vilo mutandatu, kokwituma milimo yako, ninga lizuwa lyo mupunghati, wulece kwituma mulimo wunji, nawo wanago, hamonga ne wetumami wako, ne wajenzi wono wakukwitumamila, yiwanojeye wose wacehe. Sunga ne zing'ombe zako ne zindogowe zako, zose zoceha.
13 Ale, wuzibataje no kuzikamisa zose zono ane no Mulungu nimulonjelenyi. Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kuyihemba milungu yinji yose yo wulambi, hamba kuyitambula kose mu milomo yenyu.”

Zi sikukuu zidatu zimbaha

(Wut 34:18-26; Kum 16:1-17)

14 “Munoze sikukuu yidatu mu mwaka, ku soko ye lilumbo lyangu.
15 Mu mwezi wa Abibu, mwezi wono mwawucila mu yisi ya Misili, monoza sikukuu ye mikate yono yisina cisase, nha vyono namulajilizenyi. Holeka mulecele lukulu kulya mukate wono wina cisase, mu vilo vivyo vyo mupunghati vya mu sikukuu yiyi. Kanyhi munhu wo wose yalece kuza ku wuhembi yali bwete yasina cilavywa.

16 “Muyinoze sikukuu ye nhwanga, lono mukwanza du kududula vilimwa vyenyu. Kanyhi munoze sikukuu ye vibanda, mu ndumilizo yo mwaka, lono mukuhepa mikalo ye zabibu ne mizeyituni yenyu.
17 Ne walume wose weze kwikongholela hali'ne mu mwaka kadatu, ane no Muwaha Mulungu.

18 “Na lono mukundavizya cilavywa, mulece kundavizya sakami ye cilavywa, hamonga no mukate wono wina cisase, hamba mulece kugaleka i mafuta ge sikukuu yangu, gasigale sunga mitondo yaco.
19 Ne nhwanga nongozi ye vilimwa vyenyu, mweze kuyileta myaka yose mu Nyumba yangu nene no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu. Mulece kuyitelecela nyana ye ngholo hamba nyana ye mhene mu mele ge nyina.”

Wihuwize hamonga no wulanjizwe

20 “Lanjenyi, ane nomutuma malayika yamulongolelenyi ku mwande yenyu, mu muhinzo wenyu. Ho yamudimenyi mu nzila, sunga yamufisenyi mu yisi yono niyiwikanize.
21 Holeka mumuhulikaje, kanyhi mweze ne cihuliko kuli zose zono komulonjelenyi. Mulece kumubedegala, hambi nha yo nene mono nimutumile. Nayo hamba si kosuma kumunenelenyi hono mumubedegale.
22 Ninga hono mumuhulice kwe mitima yenyu yose, no kwituma zose zono nghumulonjelenyi, nane baho langa ho nokwihoma ne walugu wenyu wose.
23 Nayo malayika wangu komulongolelenyi ku mwande yenyu, sunga yamufisenyi mu yisi ye Waamoli, ye Wahiti, ye Wapelizi, ye Wakanani, ye Wahivi, na ye Wayebusi. Nane nowongomeza wawo wose.
24 Ninga mulecele lukulu kuyinamila hamba kuyihemba milungu yawo yo wulambi, hamba kuyitumamila kose, hamba kwiga mu zinghani zawo. Langa muyongomezaje milungu yawo yiyo, kanyhi mugabenanje hamonga ne masumbili gawo ge mabwe gono wagasiwanuye so wuhembi wawo.
25 Nanye hono muhembe nene, no kunyitumamila nene no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nane nomulugutizenyi mu ndigwa zenyu ne malenga genyu, nogawusa ne matamwa muli'nye.
26 Kanyhi howa hasina mucekulu wo wose mono kohodanjika muda wakwe, hamba howa hasina yunji mono si yakulela mu yisi yenyu. Nane nomonjelezenyi vilo vyenyu vyo wikale wenyu mono yinze.

27 “Nowanoza wanhu wose i walugu wenyu, wanyogope nene. Kanyhi nowaletela ciwajiza wawo wanhu wono mukwihoma nawo. Nane nowanoza walugu wenyu wapituce no kutiza ha mwande yenyu.
28 Nowaletela woga nhinicizo, nowawinga Wahivi, Wakanani ne Wahiti.
29 Ninga si nowawinga wose mu mwaka monga ng'o. Hambi hono noze nhavivyo, yisi nha yopituka yiwe nyika, sunga ne malimu ga mu mbago gokwinjipa muno muli'nye.
30 Ninga langa, nowawinga hadodo hadodo, sunga kolo mukonjelezeka no kuyitema yisi.
31 Ne mipaka ye yisi yenyu yowa ku wulawa mwi'lenga lya Shamu, sunga kwi'lenga lya Meditelaneya, na ku wulawilila ku nyika, sunga ko kwi'kolongo lya Efulata. Nane nomupelenyi vilungo vyo kuwasumila i wekazi wa mu yisi yiyo, nanye mowawinga, nawo womuwucilenyi.
32 Mulecele lukulu kwilumbaniza nawo hamba ne milungu yawo yo wulambi.
33 Hamba mulece kuwakombola wekale nanye mu yisi yenyu. Na hono munoze nhavivyo, wene womunozenyi mwitume wubi kuli'ne. Na hono muyihembe du milungu yawo yo wulambi, hodu, wowa weza mutego kuli'nye wo kumugwizenyi.”


Wutuluce 24

Cilagane cikukangazwa

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Gwe Musa, muze akuno kono kwangu kwi'tunda kucanyha, gwegwe hamonga na Haluni, na Nadabu na Abihu, muze hamonga ne wanyamhala makumi mupunghati we Waisilayeli. Na kolo mweza muli behi no kufika kono kwangu, na lono mukali cidaha hadodo, nhondo mwiname hasi muhembe.
2 Langa agwe Musa, yo gwegwe du wuli wene mono kosuma kusejelela, ninga wanji wose, walece kusejelela behi. Nawo Waisilayeli, walece kuza akuno kwi'tunda kucanyha.”

3 Nayo Musa yakawa kubita no kuwalonjela wanhu wose, zinghani zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yalajilize. Nawo wanhu wakapitula wose hamonga wakatya, “Zinghani zose zono yalonjile Muwaha Mulungu, ase cozituma.”
4 Nayo Musa yakawa kuwandika wulajilize wose wo Muwaha Mulungu. Hanyuma ya zizo, Musa yakelawa kwinuka hakali mitondo muno muno. Yakazenga cikumbicilo ha mwejelele we litunda, yakawicila masumbili yikumi na mejete, nha vyono linha liwalo lye milango yose yikumi na myejete ye Waisilayeli.
5 Hayuma yakawa kuwatuma wazelelo we Ciisilayeli, wakamulavizya Muwaha Mulungu vilavywa vinyanywe, hamonga ne vilavywa vyo mulamu.
6 Nayo Musa yakasola sakami ndodo ye mitugo yiyo, yakayitajila mu zi sahani. Ne sakami yono yasigala, yakayimiza mu cikumbicilo.
7 Hanyuma yayo, Musa yakacisola citabu cono cali candicigwe Cilagane co Muwaha Mulungu, yakacisoma hali wanhu, nawo wanhu wakatya, “Azi zose zono yalonjile Muwaha Mulungu, ase cozituma, kanyhi cowa ne cihuliko muli zose zono yalajilize.”

8 Nayo Musa yakawa kuyisola sakami, yakawa kuwamiziza wanhu akuno yakutya, “Ayino yo sakami yono yikucikangaza cilagane cono Muwaha Mulungu yacinozize nanye, lono yamupelelenyi gaga malajilizo gakwe.”
9 Nawo Musa na Haluni na Nadabu na Abihu, wakawa kubita kwi'tunda kucanyha, hamonga ne wanyamhala makumi mupunghati we Waisilayeli.
10 Wakamuwona Mulungu we Waisilayeli. Na hasi ye magulu gakwe, kwali kocili cinhu cono cinha sakafu ya mabwe gono gakung'alala, maswanu kotya wulanga wono wukulengalenga.
11 Nayo Mulungu hamba si yawatamisa wawo walongola we Waisilayeli. Wakamuwona Mulungu, wakalya, wakang'wa.

Musa mwi'tunda lya Sinayi

12 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Musa, wuze kono kwangu mwi'tunda kucanyha, na lono wuli ku kuno, ane nokupela mbawo nyejete za mabwe, zono zandicigwe malajilizo gangu gose gono nagandika, ho niwejize Waisilayeli.”
13 Musa hamonga na Yoshuwa mwitumami wakwe, wakawa kwiwikaniza. Nayo Musa yakawa kubita kucanyha kwi'tunda lyono lyelile.
14 Musa yakawalonjela Wanyamhala yakawatya, “Ale, anye mucilindilaje bahano sunga kono cowuyila. Haluni na Huli wowa wali hamonga nanye. Na muli wose mono kowa yena nghani yono yikumugaza, ale, yawawonaje wene.”

15 Hodu, Musa yakawa kubita mwi'tunda lya Sinayi kucanyha. Ne livunde likawa kuligubika litunda.
16 Ulukumyo lo Muwaha Mulungu lukatima mwi'tunda lya Sinayi mucanyha, livunde nalyo likaligubika litunda lya Sinayi vilo mutandatu. Na mwizuwa lyo mupunghati, Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kumucema Musa mu wulawa muciligati ye livunde lilyo.
17 No lukumyo lulo lo Muwaha Mulungu, lukejela ha meso ge Waisilayeli, kotya moto wono wukutakamiza mwi'tunda mucanyha.
18 Musa yakenjila mwi'vunde lilyo mucanyha ye litunda, yakekala kuko mwi'tunda vilo makumi mane misi ne cilo.


Wutuluce 25

Vilavywa so Lihema lyono lyelile

(Wut 35:4-9)

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
2 “Ale, wuwalonjele Waisilayeli wandavizye vilavywa. Nagwe wuvibocelaje vilavywa, mu cilavywa co cose cono munhu kowa yendile mweneco kulavya.
3 Navyo vilavywa vyono kovibocela vyovino, Zahabu, fweza, shaba,
4 bafuta swanu, ne zimhuti ze liwala lye buluu, na ze liwala lyo lugandu, na ze liwala lidunghu, hamonga no mwenda muhonwe no wukunzeje we zimhene.
5 Ne zinguwo ze zinduwilu zono nhajilwe lanji ndunghu, hamonga ne zinguwo ziswanu, hamonga ne zimbawo zo mubici wo mukambala.
6 Ne mafuta ge tala, na vye mhunjile swanu, ku soko ye mafuta go witile, no wuvumba wono wina mhunjile swanu.
7 Vibwe vya Salidoniki, ne vibwe vinji so wihambo we efodi yo mukohani muwaha, hamonga ne cifuko cakwe ca ha mhambaga.
8 Nawo Waisilayeli nhavivilya, yiwanojeye wanozeze Lihema lyono lyelile, nane ho sume kwikala muli wene.
9 Nagwe wulinoze Lihema lilyo, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo, wukwiga wihwananizwe wono ane nokulajila.”

Lisanduku lye Cilagane

(Wut 37:1-9)

10 “Ale, wunoze Lisanduku, wulinoze kwe zimbawo zo mubici wo mukambala, Lisanduku lilyo liwe no wutali wa sentimita yigana limonga ne kumi, no wutali walyo wuwe sentimita makumi mutandatu na mutandatu, no wugazi walyo wuwe sentimita makumi mutandatu na mutandatu nhavivilya.
11 Nagwe wulihiliwe zahabu swanu mugati yalyo na kunze, kanyhi, wulinozeze mugo wa zahabu mbavu zose.
12 Hanyuma, wunoze nyamwidole yine zo kulidinghulila, wuziwicile mu magulu galyo, wuwicile mugulu nyamwidole monga.
13 Hanyuma kanyhi, wunoze ne mahapa matali go kulidinghulila, nago wuganoze ko mubici wo mukambala nhavivilya, wugahiliwe zahabu.
14 Nago mahapa gago, wugakoze gakole mumo mu zizo zi nyamwidole, zono zili mu mbavu nyejete ze Lisanduku, ho wuwe wo wudinghulilo walyo.
15 Ne mabici gago gekalaje masopeceze mu zinyamwidole zizo nhondo zose, galece kuwusizwa lukulu.
16 Na mugati ye Lisanduku lilyo, wuwice zizilya zimbawo ze mabwe zinyejete ze Cilagane zono nokupela, zono zandicigwe malajilizo.

17 “Hanyuma wunoze ligoda lyo lusungu lya zahabu nengu, wutali walyo wuwe sentimita yigana limonga ne kumi, no wugazi walyo wuwe sentimita makumi mutandatu na mutandatu.
18 Kanyhi wunoze ne makelubi mejete ga zahabu, wugajelece mucanyha ye ligoda lilyo lyo lusungu mu mhelo yalyo.
19 Wugawice makelubi gago, limonga ku mhelo ayino, ne liyagwe nalyo ku mhelo nzagwe. Wugawikaje makelubi gago ku zimhelo ze ligoda lilyo no kugalunza hamonga ne ligoda lilyo.
20 Ne makelubi gago, wugawikaje gawe gedomile, ne mabawa gazo nago geze gagoloywe galigubice ligoda lyo lusungu, no wusu wago weze wulilanjile ligoda.
21 Na mugati ye Lisanduku, wuwice zimbawo ze mabwe zono zandicigwe malajilizo, ne ligoda lyo lusungu nalyo, wulijelece mucanyha ye Lisanduku.
22 Nane cokwitang'anila nagwe baho. Nane nokupela malajilizo gangu gose kuli Waisilayeli, ku wulawilila baho hacanyha ye ligoda lilyo, haciligati ya gago makelubi mejete gono gali hacanyha ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane.”

Meza ye mikate yono milavye kuli Mulungu

(Wut 37:10-16)

23 “Kanyhi, wunoze meza kulawa mu mubici wo mukambala, meza yo wutali wa sentimita makumi munana na munana, no wugazi wayo sentimita makumi mane na yine, no wima wayo sentimita makumi mutandatu na mutandatu.
24 Ne meza yiyo, wuyihiliwe zahabu nengu, wuyinozeze no mugo wayo wa zahabu.
25 Hanyuma wupilimye ciciwizizo co wugazi wa sentimita makumi mutandatu na mutandatu.
26 Ne meza yiyo nayo, wuyinozezaje nyamwidole yine za zahabu, nazo wuziwicile ha lubavu mu magulu ge meza mu zimbavu zose vyono yine.
27 Ne zinyamwidole zizo, zo zokozwa mabici go kuyidinghulila meza yiyo, holeka wuziwicilaje behi no mugo wayo, so wudinghulila meza yiyo.
28 Kanyhi wusese mahapa mejete matali go mubici wo mukambala, nago wugahiliwe zahabu, so wudinghulila meza yiyo.
29 Na hanyuma kanyhi, wunoze zisahani hamonga ne visahani vyo kuwicila wuvumba baho mu meza, wunoze hamonga ne zi bilawuli ne vikombe, hamonga ne vidumu vyo kujidila vilavywa vye divayi. Ne viya vivyo vyose vyeze vya zahabu nengu.
30 Ne meza yiyo, wuyiwikaje ha mwande ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane, na mucanyha ye meza yiyo, mo kowa kuyiwika nhondo zose mikate yono yelile i milavywe kuli'ne.”

Wuwiko we zitala

(Wut 37:17-24)

31 “Wunoze wuwiko we zitala wa zahabu nengu. No wuwiko wuwo we zitala, nha cinji vikombe vyayo ne zimhimbili zayo, hamonga ne maluwa gayo, vyose wuvinoze kwa zahabu, kanyhi vyeze vilunzwe vyose hamonga ne litende lyayo.
32 Wuwiko wuwo weze wina manhambi mutandatu, gono galawiliye mwi'tende lyayo, gawe manhambi madatu lubavu aluno, ne manhambi madatu nago gawe ku lubavu luyagwe.
33 Na mu manhambi gose, linhambi limonga liwe na vikombe vidatu, vyono vihwanile kotya nhe liluwa lyo mulozi, cikombe cimonga na mhimbili yayo ne 'luwa lyayo.
34 Na mwi'bici lyayo lya haciligati yayo, heze na vikombe vine vyono vihwanile kotya nhe liluwa lyo mulozi, hamonga ne zimhimbili zayo ne maluwa gayo.
35 Ne hanhu hono getang'aniliye i manhambi gago go mutandatu baho he tende lyayo, wuwicile vipimbili cimonga cimonga.
36 Vipimbili vivyo hamonga ne manhambi gago, vyeze vilunze hamonga no wuwiko wuwo we zitala. Wunozaje wuwiko wuwo wose kwa zahabu nengu.
37 Kanyhi, wunoze tala mupunghati ku soko yo wuwiko wuwo we zitala. Wuzijelece mucanyha yayo, ho zisume kuleta wuzelu ku mwande yayo.
38 Kanyhi, wunoze sunga ne mibato yayo, hamonga ne visahani vyayo, na vyose wuvinozaje kwa zahabu nengu.
39 Ninga witumilaje kilo makumi madatu ne 'hano du za zahabu nengu, mu wunoze wa wuwo wuwiko we zitala, hamonga ne viya viya vyayo.
40 Ninga, wulanjilile muno, ho wuvinoze vivyo vyose, mu hwani yono walajilwa akulya kwi'tunda.”


Wutuluce 26

Lihema lyono lyelile lyo Muwaha Mulungu

(Wut 36:8-38)

1 “Wunoze Lihema lyono lyelile, wulinozaje kwa mapaziya yikumi gono mahone ne bafuta swanu yono mbote, hamonga ne zimhuti ze liwala lye buluu, na ze liwala lyo lugandu, na ze liwala lidunghu. Mapaziya gago, wugahonaje wugawicile hamonga ne cihwani ce makelubi ko wunghula.
2 Mapaziya gose geze gena wutali wa mita yikumi na nyejete, wugazi wayo weze wa mita nyejete nyejete. Kanyhi gose gawe na cipimo cimonga du.
3 Wugalunzaje mapaziya mahano hamonga, mwi'fungu lilongozi, ne mahano mayagwe nago ge lifungu lye kejete, wugalunze hamonga.
4 Hanyuma, wunoze vinanicizo vye liwala lye buluu, wuvinanicize mwi'pindo lye lipaziya lya kumhelo ye lifungu lye kejete.
5 Kanyhi wuwicilile vinanicizo makumi mahano mwi'paziya lilongozi lye lifungu lilongozi, na vinji kanyhi makumi mahano, navyo wuviwicile mwi'paziya lya ku mhelo mwi'fungu lye kejete, navyo vinanicizo vyose vyeze vidomile.
6 Hanyuma yayo kanyhi, wunoze vidobogo makumi mahano vya zahabu, wuvitumamile ku wulunza gagalya mafungu mejete ge mapaziya, no kulinoza Lihema liwe lyo limonga.

7 “Hanyuma kanyhi wunoze cigubiko ce Lihema ca mucanyha yalyo. Wunoze cigubiko cico kwa mapaziya yikumi na limonga go wukunzeje we zimhene.
8 Ne mapaziya gose limonga limonga, geze gena wutali wa mita yikumi ne 'datu, no wugazi wa mita nyejete nyejete. Ne mapaziya gose gago, geze na cipimo cimonga du.
9 Hanyuma, wugalunze mapaziya mahano hamonga, ne mayagwe nago go mutandatu hamonga. Ne lipaziya lilyo lyo mutandatu, wulipetyaje kali kejete ha mwande ye lihema.
10 Wunoze vinanicizo vinji kanyhi makumi mahano, wuviwicile mwi'pindo lye lipaziya lya kumhelo ye lifungu lilongozi, na vinji kanyhi makumi mahano, navyo wuviwicile mwi'pindo lye lipaziya lya kumhelo ye lifungu lye kejete.
11 Wunoze kanyhi vidobogo vinji, makumi mahano vya shaba, navyo vivyo, wuvinjize mu vinanicizo vye makumi mahano, ho wuvitumamile ku wulunza gagalya mafungu mejete ge mapaziya, no kulinoza lipaziya lye Lihema liwe lyo limonga.
12 Ne lipaziya lyono lyosulutila lyono lyosigala, wulitumbice ku lubavu lwa kumhilimo ye Lihema.
13 Na azilya nazo zi sentimita ze makumi mahano mahano, zono zowa zisigaye mu mbavu zose zo wutali we Lihema, azo wuzitumbice mu zimbavu ze Lihema ho ziligubice Lihema.

14 “Kanyhi, wunoze ne cigubiko cinji ce Lihema, ceze ca nguwo ya nduwilu, cono cina liwala lidunghu, na mucanyha yayo, ceze cina cigubiko ca nguwo yono yidejepe viswanu.
15 Wusese mabici go mubici wo mukambala go kuzenjela Lihema.
16 Ne mabici gose, geze gena wutali wa mita yine yine, no wugazi wago Sentimita makumi mutandatu na mutandatu.
17 Ne mabici gose, wugasesaje gawe gena manghwasa mejete mejete go wulunzizo. Mabici gose limonga limonga, geze gena manghwasa.
18 Nagwe koganoza nhavi gago mabici ge Lihema, wusese mabici makumi mejete ku so lubavu lwa kutakama,
19 hamonga ne matende gayo makumi mane ga fweza, mono kogasimya mabici gago makumi mejete. Mu mabici gose, libici limonga liwe na matende mejete, i manghwasa gayo mejete ho gasume kusimywa mumo.
20 No lubavu lwe kejete lwe Lihema, nha cinji ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, ako nako kokuseseza mabici makumi mejete,
21 hamonga ne matende gayo makumi mane ga fweza nhavivilya, nago gowa matende mejete mejete mu mabici gose limonga limonga.
22 No lubavu lwa ku mhilimo ya kumwezi ye Lihema, ako koseseza mabici mutandatu du.
23 Kanyhi wunoze hamonga ne mabici ganji mejete gono kogasimya mu zinhuzi zi nyejete kuko ku mhilimo ye Lihema.
24 Ne mabici gago ga mu mhelo yayo, wugalunzaje hamonga ku wulawilila hasi sunga ko kucanyha ku nyamwidole nongozi. Ne mabici ga mu mhelo yayo nago, wuganoze nhavivyo.
25 Na mu nzila yiyo, gowa mabici munana hamonga, hamonga ne matende gayo yikumi na mutandatu ga fweza. Matende mejete mejete, mwibici limonga limonga.

26 “Kanyhi wusese ne mahapa yikumi na mahano go mubici wo mukambala, geze mahapa mahano so mabici go lubavu lumonga lwe Lihema,
27 na mahapa mahano so lubavu lwe kejete lwe Lihema. Kanyhi mahapa ganji mahano so mabici gono gali ku mhilimo ye Lihema, nha cinji ku lubavu lwe mwezi.
28 Langa mucici wa hagati ye Lihema, kowujeleka weze wulawiye mhelo zose ze Lihema, ku wulawilila ku mhelo ayino sunga ko ku mhelo nzagwe.
29 Mabici gose wugahiliwe zahabu, kanyhi wuganozeze gose nyamwidole za zahabu, zono zokwibata mahapa. Ne mahapa nago, wugahiliwaje zahabu nhavivilya.
30 Nagwe kolizenga Lihema lilyo mu cihwani cono nakulajila akulya kwi 'tunda.

31 “Kanyhi wunoze lipaziya lyono yihone na bafuta swanu, mbote, hamonga ne zimhuti ze liwala lye buluu, na ze liwala lyo lugandu, na ze liwala lidunghu. Ne lipaziya lilyo, lihonwe liwicilwe ne cihwani ce makelubi ko wunghula muno muno.
32 Nagwe wulitumbice lilyo lipaziya mu mabici mane go mukambala gono mahiliwe zahabu, gono gena ne zinyamwidole za zahabu, hamonga ne matende ga fweza.
33 Lipaziya, lilyo wulitumbikaje mu vidobogo. Hanyuma yayo, wuwe kulisola Lisanduku lye Cilagane, lyono lina zimbawo zinyejete zono zandicigwe malajilizo, wulinjize mumo mugati, ku mhilimo ye lipaziya lilyo. Nalyo lipaziya lilyo, lyowa nhe 'bupa lyono lyohatemanula Hanhu Hono Helile ne hanhu hono helile Helile muno.
34 Na baho Hanhu Hono Helile Helile muno, wujelece lililya ligoda lyo lusungu mucanyha ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane, lyono lina zimbawo zi nyejete ze malajilizo mugati yalyo.
35 Ne meza yiyilya yo mubici wo mukambala, nayo wuyiwice hanhu hono helile, wuyiwice kono sumuka yayo, nawo wuwiko we zitala, wuwuwice ha katakama yayo, viwe vidomile ne meza yiyo. Ninga meza yiweze kono sumuka yayo.

36 “Kanyhi, wunoze lipaziya linji likangazi, lyo kucitiza mulango we Lihema. Wulinozaje na mhuti ze liwala lye buluu, ne liwala lyo lugandu, ne liwala lidunghu, hamonga ne bafuta swanu, mbote, kanyhi lyeze yihonwe ko wunghula muno.
37 Wunoze kanyhi mabici mahano go mubici wo mukambala, nago wugahiliwe zahabu. Mabici gose, gawe gena vidobogo vya zahabu, kanyhi gose, limonga limonga, gawe ne litende lya shaba.”


Wutuluce 27

Cikumbicilo

(Wut 38:1-7)

1 “Wunoze cikumbicilo ko mubici wo mukambala, naco cikumbicilo cico, ceze ciling'aniye mbavu zose wutali no wugazi wayo. Wutali wayo wuwe mita nyejete na lobo, no wugazi wayo nawo mita nyejete na lobo. No wima wayo wuwe mita monga na lobo.
2 Hanyuma, wuciwicile ne viwoko vyo kucidinghulila, wuviwicile mu zinhuzi zose vyono yine ze cikumbicilo, navyo vyeze vityanilizwe hamonga ne cikumbicilo ceneco. Kanyhi wucihiliwe shaba cikumbicilo cico.
3 Wunoze ne viya vyayo vyose, viya vyo kuzolela mavu, koleyo, mabeseni, ne vicinyilo, ne visahani vyo kupalila moto. Ne vivya vivino vyose, wuvinozaje kwa shaba.
4 Kanyhi, wunoze lwavu lwa shaba, lono lowa lwina nyamwidole yine za shaba, mu zinhuzi zayo zose vyono yine.
5 No lwavu lulo, wulusopece mugati mu cikumbicilo, luwe ha ciligati ciligati co wima we cikumbicilo.
6 Wusese hamonga ne mahapa go kucipicila cikumbicilo, wugasese mu mubici wo mukambala, nago wugahiliwe shaba.
7 Hanyuma mahapa gako, wugakoze mu zi nyamwidole mu zimbavu zose ze cikumbicilo lono mukucidinghula.
8 Wunoze cikumbicilo ca mabici, ceze cikombe mugati cinye matuli, nhe cihwani cono nakulajila akulya kwi'tunda.”

Luwa ye Lihema lyono Lyelile

(Wut 38:9-20)

9 “Kanyhi, wunoze vyandaluwa kwa bafuta swanu, mbote, so Lihema. Ku lubavu lwa kutakama, vyandaluwa vyayo, vyeze vyo wutali wa mita makumi mane na yine.”
10 Ne vyandaluwa vivyo, vyeze vibatilizwe ne masumbili makumi mejete ga shaba, gono gena matende ga shaba, nhavivilya. Langa zindobogo ze masumbili gago, hamonga ne vinanicizo vyayo, viweze vya fweza.
11 Ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, nako wunoze nha vyono wunozize ku lubavu lwa kutakama.
12 No lubavu lwa kumwezi we luwa yiyo, lweze ne lipaziya lyo wutali wa mita makumi mejete na nyejete, ne masumbili gayo yikumi, hamonga ne matende gayo yikumi.
13 Nako ku lubavu lwa kucilima kono kowuli mulango we Lihema, luwa yayo, yiwe no wugazi wa mita makumi mejete na nyejete.
14 Nalyo lipaziya lyayo liwe no wugazi wa mita mutandatu na nusu, hamonga ne masumbili madatu, ne matende gayo madatu.
15 No lubavu luyagwe lo mulango, nako nhavivyo, kuwe ne lipaziya lyo wugazi wa mita mutandatu na nusu, hamonga ne masumbili madatu, ne matende gayo madatu.
16 Naho ha mulango we luwa, wuwice lipaziya lyono likangaye, lyono lina wutali wa mita yicenda, lyono yihonwe na mhuti ze liwala lye buluu, ne liwala lyo lugandu, ne liwala lidunghu, hamonga ne bafuta swanu, mbote. Kanyhi, lyeze yihonwe ko wunghula muno. Nalyo lipaziya lilyo, libatizwaje mu masumbili mane gono gena ne matende gayo mane.
17 Ne masumbili gose gono gapilime luwa yose, wugawopelezaje na sito za fweza, ne zindobogo zayo zose ziwe za fweza, langa matende gayo gawe ga shaba.
18 No wutali we luwa yiyo, wuwe mita makumi mane na yine kose kose, wugazi wayo wuwe mita makumi mejete na nyejete, no wima wayo wuwe mita nyejete na lobo. Candaluwa cayo, wucinoze kwa bafuta swanu, langa matende gayo hamonga ne zimambo zayo, ne zimambo ze luwa, avyo viwe vya shaba.
19 Vyombologoto vyose vyono kokwitumamila mwi'hema, ne zimambo ku so Lihema hamonga ne luwa yayo, vyose viwe vya shaba du.

Wulanjilizi we zi Tala

(Lawi 24:1-4)

20 “Hanyuma, wuwalonjele Waisilayeli wakuletele mafuta malengu so kwasiza tala, ho tala yiwe kwaka nhondo zose du.
21 Nayo Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wayiwice tala yiyo mugati ye Lihema lilyo lyono lyelile, kunze ye lipaziya lyono licitize Lisanduku lye Cilagane. Ne tala yiyo, yeze kwikala yacile ha mwande yangu vilo vyose cilo na misi. Ne lilajilizo lili, linojeye Waisilayeli walikamise mu zindelo zawo zose cibitilila.”


Wutuluce 28

Vivwalo vye Wakohani

(Wut 39:1-7)

1 “Nagwe wumulete muwahawo Haluni hali 'ne, hamonga ne wanagwe, Nadabu, Abihu, Eleyezali, na Itamali ku wulawa mu Waisilayeli, ho wanyitumamile mu mulimo wo wukohani.
2 Ninga nagwe wumuhonelaje muwahawo Haluni, vivwalo vyo wukohani, ho yejele mu nhojo no wuswanu.
3 Nagwe wuwalonjele wanhu wose wono wena wumanye, i wanhu wono niwapelile wizuce, ho wamuhonele muwahawo Haluni vivwalo vivyo vyo wukohani, nayo ho yasiwanulwe so mulimo wangu wo wukohani.
4 Wuwalonjele wamunozeze vinhu avino, cifuko cono kovwala ha mhambaga, efodi, kanzo, no musweko we mawalawala, no mukumbo, hamonga no musipi. Nawo wahone vinhu vivyo vyo wukohani, wamuhonele muwahawo Haluni, hamonga ne wanagwe, ho wanyitumamile mu mulimo wo wukohani.
5 Nawo wahoni wawo, lono wakuhona myenda yiyo, wetumamilaje liwala lye buluu ne liwala lyo lugandu hamonga ne liwala lidunghu, ne zimhuti za zahabu, ne bafuta swanu, mbote.

6 “Nayo efodi wayihonelaje zimhuti za zahabu ne bafuta ye liwala lye buluu, ne liwala lyo lugandu ne liwala lidunghu, hamonga ne bafuta swanu, mbote, kanyhi yono honwe viswanu muno ko wunghula.
7 Wayihonelezezaje ne vikowa vyayo mu mamhembo gayo, ho yisume kusuhikwa viswanu mu mawega.
8 No musipi wo kuyiwopela yiyi efodi, weze musipi wono muhonwe ko wunghula muno, muno. Kanyhi weze muhonelwe vinhu vivilya vyono vihonelwe mu efodi, nha cinji, zimhuti za zahabu, bafuta ye liwala lye buluu, ne liwala lyo lugandu, hamonga ne liwala lidunghu, mu bafuta swanu, mbote.

9 “Hanyuma kanyhi, wusole mabwe mejete ge Salidoniki, wandice matagwa ge wana yikumi na wejete wa Yakobo mucanyha yago.
10 Wandice matagwa mutandatu mwi'bwe lilongozi, ne matagwa ganji mutandatu, mwi'bwe lye kejete. Matagwa gago wugandice nha vyono wetyatizye mu ndelwa zawo.
11 Matagwa gago wugandice mucanyha ye mabwe gago, nha vyono mutyana vyuma kanozaga muhuli, kanyhi wugahiliwe ne zahabu.
12 Hanyuma wugatumbice mu mulya mu vikowa vye efodi vyono vibate mu mawega akuno na akuno, ho gawe nha citanjilo ce milango yikumi na myejete ye Waisilayeli. Nayo Haluni kogavwala matagwa gago mu mawega gakwe, gawe nha cikumbucizo hali'ne no Muwaha Mulungu kuli wanhu wangu.
13 Kanyhi wuganozezaje ne vibatilizo vyejete vya zahabu,
14 hamonga ne mikufu myejete ya zahabu nengu, yeze mibotwe kotya milejehe. Wuyikoze mikufu yiyo mumo mu vibatilizizo.”

Cifuko ca ha mhambaga

(Wut 39:8-21)

15 “Kanyhi, wuhone cifuko ca ha mhambaga ce ning'aniza, naco wucihonaje ko wunghula muno muno nha vyono wuyihonile efodi, wucihonaje ko wuputi wa zahabu, ne bafuta ye liwala lye buluu, ne liwala lyo lugandu, hamonga ne liwala lidunghu, wucihonaje kwe bafuta swanu, mbote.
16 Ne cifuko cico ceze ciling'aniye wutali kose kose, no wutali wayo ne cipinga cayo, ceze ca sentimita makumi mejete mejete na nyejete kose kose, cono cosuma kupetyeka.
17 Hanyuma, wucihambe ne vibwe vyo wuguzi wukamu micilili mine. Mucilili mulongozi, weze wa akiki, topazi, ne alimasi ye liwala lidunghu,
18 no mucilili we kejete, weze wa zamalidi, yakuti samawi, na alimasi,
19 mucilili we kadatu, weze wa yasinito, akiki ndunghu, na amesisito,
20 no mucilili we kane, weze wa zabalijadi, salidoniki na yasipi, ne vibwe vyose vyeze vinamatizizwe na zahabu.
21 Na ho baho, howa na vibwe yikumi na vyejete, vyono vyandicigwe matagwa yikumi na mejete, ge wana wa Yakobo, citanjilo ce milango yikumi na myejete ye Waisilayeli.
22 Nagwe wunoze mikufu ya zahabu nengu, yono yibotigwe kotya mulejehe, ku soko ye cifuko cico.
23 Kanyhi wunoze nyamwidole nyejete za zahabu, wuzihoneleze mu mamhembo mejete ga kucanyha ge cifuko cico,
24 wusole mikufu yiyo ye zahabu, nayo wuyiwopeleze mu zizo zinyamwidole.
25 Hanyuma, wusole visonje vyo mukufu we zahabu, wuviwopeleze mu vibatilizizo vyejete vye cifuko, ho visume kwiyilunza na acilya ce efodi, cono cibate mu mawega ku lubavu lwa kono mwande.
26 Kanyhi wunoze nyamwidole nyejete za zahabu, nazo wuzihoneleze mu mamhembo mejete ga kusika, ku lubavu lwa mugati ye cifuko ca ha mhambaga, ciwe cili behi na efodi.
27 Wunoze kanyhi nyamwidole nyejete zinji za zahabu, zizo nazo wuzihoneleze mu mamhembo ga kusika ge efodi, hono vilunzize efodi no musipi wono wuhonigwe ko wunghula.
28 Naco cifuko wuciwopeleze ko mulejehe we liwala lye buluu hamonga ne efodi, mu nzila ye zinyamwidole zayo. Na mu nzila yiyo, u musipi wuwo wono muhone ko wunghula, hamba si wosuma kuhogoha yaye.

29 “Nayo Haluni lono yakwinjila i hanhu hono helile, kocivwala cifuko cico ca ha mhambaga cono candicigwe matagwa ge milango ye wanhu wa Isilayeli, nane no Muwaha Mulungu, ho sume kuwakumbucila i wanhu wangu nhondo zose.
30 Nayo Haluni, mu cifuko cico, yatajile Ulimu na Tumimu, yavidinghule ha mhambaga yakwe lono yakunyiziza ha mwande yangu. Na mu mapango gago, yimunojeye nhondo zose yeze yacivwalile cifuko cico, ho yasume kuwumanya wuhile wangu wa kuli wanhu wa Isilayeli.”

Vivwalo vinji kanyhi vye Cikohani

(Wut 39:22-31)

31 “Kanyhi wuhone kanzo ye liwala lye buluu, yono Haluni kowa kuyivwala kusi ye efodi.
32 Nayo yeze yina no wukozo wo mutwe haciligati yayo. No wukozo wuwo wo mutwe, wuwuhonelaje lipindo ho kanzo yilece kudemuka.
33 Na kusika yayo lipindo lyayo, wulihonelaje wulipilimye wihambo we makomamanga ge liwala lye buluu, ne liwala lyo lugandu hamonga ne liwala lidunghu, wuwicile ne vinyanjili hagati ya gago makomamanga,
34 wuwicile yikomamanga, hanyuma cinyanjili, yikomamanga hanyuma cinyanjili, sunga wuyipilimye kanzo yose, vyose vyeze vya zahabu.
35 Nayo Haluni kowa kuyivwala kanzo yiyo lono yakwitumama mulimo wo wukohani. Na mwi'pango lono yakwinjila i hanhu hono helile hali'ne, na mwi'daha lono yakulawila, mulilo we vinyanjili wowa kuhulikwa, nayo sikosuma kuwulagwa yaye.

36 “Kanyhi wunoze cibati ca zahabu nengu, naco wucandice mucanyha yaco nha vyono mu muhuli, wucandice nhavi, ‘Welile kuli Muwaha.’
37 Ne cibati cico naco, wuciwopeleze no mukumbo ko musipi we liwala lye buluu.
38 Nayo Haluni kowa kucivwala he handa lyo wusu wakwe, nayo kowudinghula wuhoswe wose, wono wanhu wa Isilayeli wowa wawunoza, lono wakulavya likumbiko lyono lyelile. Nane nogavumila makumbiko gawo.
39 Wumuhonelaje Haluni cisweko ca bafuta swanu, kanyhi wumuhonele no mukumbo wa bafuta swanu, hamonga no musipi wono muhonwe viswanu muno.

40 “Nawo i wana wa Haluni, wuwahonelaje kanzo, ne misipi, hamonga ne zikofiya, nawo ho wejele mu nhojo no wuswanu.
41 Nagwe wumuvwice vivwalo vivyo Haluni, hamonga ne wanagwe, wuwetile mafuta, wuwawicile miwoko wuwasiwanule, no kuweza, ho wanyitumamile mu mulimo wo wukohani.
42 Kanyhi wuwahonele ne zi kaputula ze bafuta zono zowa zilawiliye mu wulawa ha ciwino sunga ku vima, ho zihundice vipwi vyawo.
43 Nayo Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wowa kuzivwala kaputula zizo nhondo zose lono wakwinjila mwi 'Hema lyono Lyelile, na lono wakusejelela ha cikumbicilo, ho wetumame witumami wo wukohani i hanhu hono helile, ho walece kuwulagwa so wikala cipwi wawo. No wutemye wuwuno, wowa wutemye we cibitilila kuli Haluni na kuli zindelo zakwe zose.”


Wutuluce 29

Wulajilize wo kumusiwanulila Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, mu mulimo wo Wukohani

(Lawi 8:1-36)

1 “Azi nazo zo wuwanozezaje Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, ho wuwasiwanule wanyitumamile mu mulimo wo wukohani. Sole nzeku monga, hamonga na nduwilu nyejete zono zisina cilema,
2 hamonga ne mikate yono yisina cisase, ne maandazi gono gasina cisase, gono gatiwigwe hamonga ne mafuta, hamonga ne capati zono mbakwe mafuta. Na vivi vyose wuvinozaje ko wusaje we ngano wono wunamuce muno muno.
3 Hanyuma wuyitajile mikate yiyo mu nhoto, wundavizye vyose hamonga, lono wukundavizya cilavywa ce nzeku, hamonga ne zinduwilu zizo zi nyejete.

4 “Na hanyuma yayo, wumusole Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wuwalete ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile, wuwazuguze migulu yawo.
5 Wusole vivwalo vivilya vye cikohani wumuvwice Haluni, nha cinji, shati, kanzo, efodi, ne cifuko ca ha mhambaga, wumuwope no musipi wuwulya wono wuhonigwe ko wunghula muno.
6 Wumuvwice ne kofiya mu mutwe wakwe. Ne cibati cicilya cono candicigwe ‘Welile kuli Muwaha’ wuciwopeleze naco mucanyha ye kofiya he handa lyo musu wakwe.
7 Na kolo wakondya kuzinoza zizo zose, wusole mafuta gagalya go kusiwanulila, i mafuta go witile, wumujidile Haluni mu mutwe wakwe, no kumubaka mafuta so wusiwanulwe wakwe.

8 “Hanyuma wuwasole nawo wanagwe, nawo wuwavwice zi shati.
9 Wuwawope ne misipi mu viwino vyawo, no kuwavwika ne zi kofiya zawo. Mu nzila yiyi yo kowasiwanulila Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, no wukohani wowa muli wene hamonga ne zindelo zawo ko wutemye we cibitilila.

10 “Na kolo zakondya zizo, ale wuyilete nzeku yiyilya he Hema lyono lyelile. Nayo Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wayijelece miwoko yawo mu mutwe,
11 hanyuma nagwe wuwe kuyiwulajila hali Muwaha Mulungu, baho ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile.
12 Wuwe kusola sakami ndodo no kuyibaka mu zinhuzi ze cikumbicilo kwe cidole cako, ne sakami yose yono yosigala, wuyimwagaje baho hasi ye cikumbicilo.
13 Kanyhi wusole mhama yose yono yifunicize wutumbu, ne nhandamala yono yili mwi'toga, hamonga ne zifigo zi nyejete, hamonga ne mhama yayo. Na vivyo vyose wuviticimizize mucanyha ye cikumbicilo.
14 Ninga i nyama ye nzeku yiyo, ne nyhingo yayo, no wutope wayo, wuvisolaje wukaviticimizize mu moto kunze ye nghambi yenyu. Na yiyo, co cilavywa co wubi.

15 “Kanyhi wusole nduwilu monga ya muli zinduwilu zizilya zi nyejete, wumulonjele Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wayijelece miwoko yawo mu mutwe.
16 Nagwe wuyiwulaje, wusole sakami yayo, wuyimize mu cikumbicilo cose mbavu zose.
17 Wusole nyama yayo wuyitemase temase, wusole wutumbu wayo wuwukweme ne malenga hamonga ne migulu yayo, hanyuma wuvilundice vinhu vivyo vyose hamonga no mutwe wayo.
18 Wuyiticimize nduwilu yose mu moto mucanyha ye cikumbicilo, wundavizye cilavywa cinyanywe. Ne mhunjile ye cilavywa cicino, yinyendezaga muno.

19 “Hanyuma, wuyisole yi yilya nduwilu nzagwe nayo, wumulonjele Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wayijelece miwoko yawo mu mutwe.
20 Nagwe wuyiwulaje, wusole sakami yayo ndodo, wumubace Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, mu makutu gawo go lubavu lo kulume, hamonga ne vidole kululu vya mu miwoko yawo yo kulume, hamonga ne vidole kululu vye migulu yawo yo kulume. Ne sakami yono yosigala, wuyimize mu cikumbicilo cose mbavu zose.
21 Kanyhi wusole sakami ndodo yono yili mu cikumbicilo, wusole ne mafuta go kusiwanulila, wumumizize Haluni hamonga ne vivwalo vyakwe, i wanagwe, nawo nhavivyo, hamonga ne vivwalo vyawo. Nayo Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, hamonga ne vivwalo vyawo vyose, wowa wakondya kusiwanulilwa kuli'ne mu nzila yiyo.

22 “Kanyhi wuwe kusola mhama ye nduwilu yiyo, hamonga no mucila wayo, ne mhama yose yono yifunicize wutumbu, ne nhandamala yono yili mwi'toga, hamonga ne zifigo zi nyejete, ne mhama yayo, wusole ne cima co mugulu wo kulume. Hambi nduwilu yiyo nha yo yo mulimo wo kusiwanula.
23 Na mu nhoto yiyilya yono yili hali'ne, ane no Muwaha Mulungu, yono yina mikate yono yisina cisase, wusole mumo namo mukate monga, ne lindazi limonga lyono litiwigwe hamonga ne mafuta, wusole na capati monga.
24 Na vyose vivyo, wuviwice mu miwoko ya Haluni na mu miwoko ye wanagwe, nawo woviyumvya ciwe ellavywa ciyumvywe hali'ne ane no Muwaha Mulungu.
25 Hanyuma, wuvisole kanyhi ku wulawa mu miwoko yawo, wuviticimize no moto mucanyha ye cikumbicilo, hamonga ne cilavywa cinyanywe, viwe mhunjile swanu yono yonyendeza nene no Muwaha Mulungu. Naco cico co cilavywa cono cikunozwa no moto.

26 “Hanyuma wusole ne nyama ye nghwa ye nduwilu yiyo, yono yitumamiywe mulimo wo kumusiwanula Haluni, nagwe wuyiyumvye, ciwe cilavywa ciyumvywe hali 'ne no Muwaha Mulungu. Nayo yowa lyo ligawo lyako.
27 Na lono mukohani yakusiwanulwa, nyama ye 'nghwa hamonga ne nyama ye cima ce nduwilu vyono vyokwitumamilwa so mulimo wo wusiwanuzi, avyo vyamule viletwe visiwanulwe ku soko yangu, ho viwe co citanjilo co kundavizya 'ne. Navyo vyowa lyo ligawo lya Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe,
28 na vivyo vyowa lyo ligawo lyono kowa kupegwa nhondo zose Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, ku wulawa kuli wanhu wa Isilayeli. Hambi nha lyo ligawo lye posho ye wakohani yono yowa kulavywa ne wanhu wa Isilayeli, lono wakulavya vilavywa vyo mulamu. Nawo co cilavywa cawo kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

29 “Nayo Haluni kolo yafwiza, vivwalo vyakwe vyo wukohani, wapegwe wanagwe, wavivwale, ho wawicilwe miwoko wawe wakohani.
30 Nayo mwana wa Haluni, mono kowa yanozwa yawe mukohani ho yahalile hanhu ho sogwe, yavwale vivwalo vivyo. Yenjile mwi 'Hema lyono lyelile, yetumame hanhu hono helile kwa vilo mupunghati.

31 “Nyama ye nduwilu yiyo yono yokwitumamilwa mulimo wuwo wo wusiwanuzi, wuyisole wuyihemusize baho i hanhu hono helile.
32 Wuwapele Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe nyama yiyo, nawo wayilile ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile, hamonga ne mikate yiyilya yono yisigaye mu nhoto.
33 Nawo walye weneco du vinhu vivyo vyono vitumamiywe nhe kumbiko, na so wilamule, ha wityatye wo wuhembi wo wuwicilwe miwoko ho wawe wakohani. Ninga wulecele lukulu kumukombola munhu yunji wowose yavilye vinhu vivyo, hambi avi nha vinhu vyono vyelile.
34 Ne nyama yo yose, hamba mikate yo yose muli vivyo, hono visigale sunga hakali mitondo, hodu, vitakamizwe mu moto, vilece kuligwa na yunji, hambi nha vyelile.

35 “Ale, nhavivyo vyo wuwatendelaje Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe muli zose, nha vyono nghulajilize. Wuwasiwanule mu vilo mupunghati.
36 Nagwe mu vilo vivyo, nhondo zose, weze kulavya nzeku yiwe cilavywa co wubi, ku so wilamule. Na mu wunoza nhavivyo, kowa wakondya kuceza cikumbicilo. Hanyuma wucijidile mafuta ho wucisiwanule.
37 Nagwe mu vilo mupunghati, kocinozeza wilamule cikumbicilo no kuceza. Kolo weza wazikondya zizo, ale nhondo, cikumbicilo cowa cakondya kwelela lukulu, na muli cinhu co cose cono copamiza cikumbicilo, hodu naco cowa celile.

Vilavywa vye nhondo zose

(Wuw 28:1-8)

38 “Na mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza, weze kulavya vilavywa mu cikumbicilo vinhu avi, nyana nyejete za ngholo zono zificile mwaka monga.
39 Nyana nongozi wuyiwulaje hakali mitondo, na ye kejete nayo wuyiwulaje mwi'pango lye cinyamihe.
40 Nagwe wuyilavye ngholo yiyo yinongozi, hamonga na kilo monga yo wusaje, wono wunamuce muno, wono wuhanzahanze hamonga na lita monga ya mafuta malengu, hamonga na lita monga ya divayi, viwe cilavywa ce cing'wegwa.
41 Ne ngholo nzagwe nayo wuyiwulaje, wuyilavye hamonga ne cilavywa co wusaje na ce cing'wegwa, nha vyono wunozize hakali mitondo. Ne mhunjile ye cilavywa cico yono yikunozwa no moto, yonyendeza muno ane no Muwaha Mulungu.
42 Ne cilavywa cici cinyanywe, cowa kulavizywa nhondo zose ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile, mu zindelo zenyu zose zono zikuza. Nane ho nowa kwitang'anila nanye baho, no kuduwana nanye.
43 Nokwitang'anila ne wanhu wa Isilayeli, no lukumyo lwangu lohanoza baho hawe hanhu hono helile.
44 Nane nolisiwanula Lihema lyono lyelile hamonga ne cikumbicilo, viwe vyelile. Nayo Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, nowasiwanula ho wanyitumamile mu mulimo wo wukohani.
45 Nane nokwikala muli wana wa Isilayeli, nowa Mulungu wawo.
46 Nawo baho ho womanyila vyono ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo, mono nawawusa wawuce mu yisi ya Misili, ho nikale muli wene. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo.”


Wutuluce 30

I cikumbicilo co mulimo wo kufusiza wuvumba

(Wut 37:25-28)

1 “Wunoze ne cikumbicilo co kufusiza wuvumba, ninga wucinoze ko mubici wo mukambala.
2 Ne cikumbicilo cico, wucinozaje ciwe ciling'aniye wutali no wugazi wayo, wutali no wugazi wayo weze sentimita makumi mane ne 'hano kose kose, no wima wayo weze sentimita makumi 'cenda. Ne mahembe gayo geze malunze hamonga ne cikumbicilo ceneco.
3 Naco cikumbicilo cico wucihiliwe ne zahabu nengu, mucanyha yayo, na mu zimbavu zayo zose mu mhelo yayo, sunga na mu mahembe gayo. Kanyhi wucinozeze no mugo wayo wa zahabu.
4 Wunoze nyamwidole nyejete za zahabu, wuzinamatize ha kasika yo mugo wayo, ninga wuziwicile akuno na akuno ziwe zidomile. Ne zinyamwidole zizo, zosuma kusopekwa libici lyo kucidinghulila cikumbicilo lono cikudinghulwa.
5 Nago mabici gago go kucidinghulila, wuganoze ko mubici wo mukambala, wugahiliwe ne zahabu.
6 Ne cikumbicilo cico wuciwice kunze ye lipaziya lyo mwenda, lyono litemanuye hono holili Lisanduku lye Cilagane, hanhu hono ane nowa kwitang'anila nagwe.
7 Nayo Haluni nhondo zose lono yakuhelela mugati ha wunozeleza zitala zono zili baho, kosuma kufusa wuvumba mucanyha ye cikumbicilo cico, wono wina mhunjile swanu.

8 “Hanyuma kanyhi, kofusa wuvumba mu cipango ce cinyamihe lono yakukoleleza zitala. Ne cilavywa cicino co wuvumba, cowa kulavywa nhondo zose hamba kulecelela yaye, mu zindelo zenyu zose.
9 Kanyhi, mulecele lukulu kufusa wuvumba wono si welile mu cikumbicilo cico, hamba kulavya cilavywa cinyanywe, hamba cilavywa co wusaje, hamba kujidila mucanyha yayo cilavywa ce cing'wegwa.
10 Nayo Haluni yimunojeye yawe kunozeza wilamule mu mahembe gaco, mu mwaka kamonga. Nayo konozeza wilamule mu nzila ye sakami ye cilavywa co wubi, mu mwaka kamonga, mu zindelo zenyu zose. Ne cikumbicilo cico kuli'ne, cowa celile muno muno, ane no Muwaha Mulungu.”

Kodi so Lihema lyono lyelile

11 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakamutya,
12 “Mu madaha gogose lono wakuwawala wanhu wa Isilayeli, yiwanojeye munhu wowose yawe kulavya kuli'ne yikombo so wumi wakwe mweneco. Ho yalece kugwilwa ne litamwa libi mwi'pango lono wakuwalwa.
13 No munhu wo wose mono kowalwa, yimunojeye yalavye mu nghango ye mapesa gono gosakwa, mu mapesa gono gakwitumamilwa mwi Hema lyono lyelile. Na cico co cilavywa cakwe cono kondavizya.
14 No munhu wo wose mono yanojeye yawalwe, yeze mono yaficile myaka makumi mejete no kuciza, kanyhi yo yanojeye yandavizye ne cilavywa cico.
15 Nawo lono wakulavya cilavywa co wilamule ku soko yo wumi wawo, u munhu mugoli yalece kulavya yacize, hamba munhu muciwa nayo yalece kulavya zicepuce si nha liwalo lyono likusakwa.
16 Nagwe wubocele zipesa zizo zo wilamule ku wulawa kuli wanhu wa Isilayeli. Wuzitumamile so milimo ya mwi 'Hema lyono lyelile. Ne mapesa gago, gonoza nane niwakumbucile no kuwadima.”

Ciya ca shaba co wizuguzizo

17 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela kanyhi Musa yakatya,
18 “Ale, wunoze ciya ca shaba cono cowa cina ne litende lyayo lya shaba, so wizuguzize. Wuciwice haciligati ye Lihema lyono lyelile ne cikumbicilo. Wucitajile ne malenga.
19 Nayo Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wogetumamila malenga gago so wizuguze mu miwoko ne migulu yawo.
20 Na lono wakali si nenjila mwi 'Hema lyono lyelile, na lono weza kusejelela ha cikumbicilo ho walavye cilavywa cinyanywe, yiwanojeye wezuguze mu malenga gago ho walece kufwa.
21 Yiwanojeye wezuguze miwoko ne migulu yawo ho walece kufwa. No wutemye wuwu, yiwanojeye wawukamise mu zindelo zawo zose, sunga cibitilila.”

Mafuta go witile

22 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
23 “Ale, wulondole vihatilwa avino vye mhunjile swanu muno muno: Manemane ge malenga gawe kilo mutandatu, mudalasini nawo we mhunjile swanu, wuwe kilo yidatu, magaji ge mhunjile swanu, gawe kilo yidatu.
24 No mudalasini we liwala linji, nawo wuwe kilo mutandatu. Ne vipimo vivyo vyose, viwe nha vyono vinha vipimo vya mwi Hema lyono lyelile. Wusole kanyhi na lita yine za mafuta go muzeyituni.
25 Na mu wulawa mu vinhu vivyo, wunoze mafuta gono gelile go witile, so mulimo wo wusiwanule. Wuvihanzahanze vivyo vyose hamonga kotya manukato.
26 Hanyuma, wulijidile Lihema lyono lyelile mafuta gago, hamonga ne Lisanduku lye Cilagane,
27 ne meza hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo, ne cikumbicilo co kufusiza wuvumba,
28 ne cikumbicilo ce vilavywa vinyanywe, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose, ne ciya co wizuguzizo hamonga ne litende lyayo.
29 Na vivino vyose wuvisiwanule, navyo vyowa vyelile muno muno. Ne cinhu co cose cono covipamiza vitumamilwa vivyo, naco cowa cezwa.
30 Hanyuma, wumwitile mafuta Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wuwasiwanule ho wanyitumamile mu witumami wo wukohani.
31 Wuwalonjele wanhu wa Isilayeli wutye nhavi, ‘Ago gowa go mafuta gangu gono gelile go witile, so wusiwanule mu zindelo zenyu zose.
32 Ne mafuta gaga, galece kujidilwa kuli munhu yunji wo wose mono si mukohani, hamba galece kunozwa mafuta ganji gehwananizwe na gagano. Vyono mafuta gaga gono mahanzahanzwe, go mafuta gono gelile.
33 No munhu wo wose mono kokwiga, yanoze mafuta gono genha gaga, hamba hono mafuta gaga yagetile muli munhu mono si yanojeye, munhu yuyo kotemanulilwa lukulu muli wanhu wakwe.’ ”

Wuvumba

34 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela kanyhi Musa yakamutya, “Ale, wusole vipimo vyono viling'aniye, vye hati ye mhunjile swanu, nha vyono nhavi, natafi, na shekeleti, na kelibena, hamonga no wuvumba we mhunjile swanu.
35 Nagwe witumamilaje vinhu vivyo, wunoze wuvumba wuhanzahanze nhe manukato, wuwutajile no mwino ho wuwe wuswanu kanyhi welile.
36 Hanyuma yayo, wusagaje wunji wuwe wusaje wono wutibuce, wuwinjize mwi Hema lyono lyelile, wuwuwice he Lisanduku lye Cilagane, i hanhu hono nowa kwitang'anila nagwe. No wuvumba wuwo wowa welile muno kuli'nye.
37 Nanye, mulecele lukulu kunoza wuvumba wunji, wono wihwanile wuvumba wuwu, so vitumamilwa vyenyu nyo weneco. Hambi wuvumba wuwu nha wowa welile hali'ne.
38 No munhu wo wose mono kokwijelela yanoze wuvumba wono winha wuwu, yawitumamile kotya manukato kwe visakwa vyakwe mweneco, munhu yuyo kotemanulilwa lukulu muli wanhu wakwe.”


Wutuluce 31

Wanghula wo kulinozeleza Lihema lyono lyelile

(Wut 35:30–36:1)

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela kanyhi Musa yakatya,
2 “Langa ta, Ane nimuhagula Bezaleli u mwana wa Uli, mwizukulu wa Huli wo mulango wa Yuda.
3 Nayo nimumemya no Muhe wangu. Kanyhi nimupela wumanye no wizuce, mahala no wunghula.
4 Ho yasume kutula milimo yo wunghula, ho yasume kwituma milimo yo kutyana zahabu, fweza, na shaba.
5 Kanyhi nimupela wizuce wo wukakata mabwe go kuhamba, hamonga no wusesi we zimbawo, hamonga no wizuce we mawala gose go wunghula.
6 Kanyhi nimuhagula Oholiyabu u mwana wa Ahisamaki, wo mulango wa Dani, nayo wetumane milimo hamonga na Bezaleli. Hamonga na zizo, niwapela wizuce wuwaha wanhu we hayo hayo, ku so milimo ye hayo hayo. Ho wasume kunoza vitumamilwa vyose vyono ndajilize vinozwe.
7 Wo wutya, Lihema lyono lyelile, Lisanduku lye Cilagane ne ligoda lyo Lusungu, lyono lili mucanyha ye Lisanduku, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose.
8 Kanyhi, ne meza, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo, no wuwiko we zitala, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo, ne cikumbicilo co kufusiza wuvumba.
9 Kanyhi, hamonga ne cikumbicilo ce cilavywa cinyanywe, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo. Ne ciya co wizuguzizo hamonga ne litende lyayo.
10 Hamonga ne vivwalo vyono vyelile vyo mukohani Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wono wokwituma mulimo wo wukohani.
11 Kanyhi, ne mafuta go witile, no wuvumba we mhunjile swanu, so hanhu hono helile. Nawo wavinoze vinhu vivyo vyose nha vyono nakulajiliza.”

Sabato lizuwa lyo wucehe

12 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakamutya,
13 “Wuwalonjele wanhu wa Isilayeli wuwatye nhavi, ‘Mweze kuzikamisa Sabato zangu mu zindelo zenyu zose, nha cinji i vilo vyo kuceha.’ Hambi zi Sabato zizo, nha co citanjilo hagati yenyu na nene. Vyono yo nene no mweneco du, mono nosuma kumwezenyi.
14 Holeka muyikamise Sabato, hambi nha lyo lizuwa lyono lyelile kuli'nye. No munhu wo wose mono koyiviza, yimunojeye yawulagwe. No munhu wo wose mono kokwituma mulimo mwizuwa lilyo, kotemanulilwa lukulu kuli wanhu wakwe.
15 Munojeye mwitume milimo yenyu mu vilo mutandatu, ninga lizuwa lyo mupunghati, lyo lizuwa lye ciwene lyo kuceha, lyo lizuwa lyangu lyono lyelile. No munhu wo wose mono kokwituma mulimo mwizuwa lilyo, yimunojeye yawulagwe.
16 Nawo wanhu wa Isilayeli walikamise lizuwa lye Sabato mu zindelo zawo zose, ciwe citanjilo ce cilagane ce cibitilila.
17 Ne nghani yiyi, cowa citanjilo ca nhondo zose muli wanhu wa Isilayeli na nene no mweneco. Hambi ane no Muwaha Mulungu, nha nawuwumba wulanga ne yinze mu vilo mutandatu, ninga lizuwa lyo mupunghati, si netuma mulimo wunji wo wose, langa nghaceha du.”

18 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yali yeza kukondya kulongana na Musa mwi'tunda lya Sinayi, Muwaha Mulungu yakamupela Musa vibawo vyejete vye mabwe, vyono mweneco Muwaha Mulungu yavyandika malajilizo mucanyha yavyo kwe cidole cakwe mweneco.


Wutuluce 32

Ndama ya zahabu

(Kum 9:6-29)

1 Nawo wanhu wali weza kuwona vyono Musa yacela muno kuko mwi'tunda, hamba si yakuwuya mbela ku wulawa mwi'tunda, hodu, wakawa kwikongholela hali Haluni, wakawa kumutya, “Gwe Haluni! Ale wucinozeze milungu yono yocitonga, hambi nha si cimanyile yono yimutujile yuyo Musa mono yaciwusa ku yisi ya Misili.”

2 Nayo Haluni yakawapitula yakawatya, “Ale, musole wihambo we zahabu wono wavwalile mu makutu wagolece zenyu, ne wana wenyu, we cilume na we cicekulu, mundetele.”
3 Hobaho wanhu wose, wakawa kuwopola wihambo wawo we zahabu mu makutu gawo, wakawa kumuletela Haluni.
4 Nayo Haluni yali yeza kuvibocela, yakawa kuvyenjedula, yakanoza ndama nyenjedulwe. Nawo wanhu wali weza kuyiwona, wakawa kuzoza wakutya, “Gwe Isilayeli, ayu yo mulungu wetu mono yaciwusa mu yisi ya Misili!”

5 Nayo Haluni yakayizenjela ndama yiyo cikumbicilo, hanyuma, yakawa kuwalonjela wanhu yakatya, “I mitondo, howa na sikukuu ku soko yo Muwaha Mulungu.”
6 I mitondo yaco, wanhu wakelawa kwinuka hakali mitondo muno, wakalavya mitugo so vilavywa vinyanywe, hamonga ne vilavywa vyo mulamu. Wanhu wakekala hasi, wakalya no kung'wa ko wubugu, sunga wakagala no kwinjila mu wusenyha.

7 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Ale, wutime hasi wulanje, hambi wanhu wako wono wawawusa mu yisi ya Misili, nha weyihodanga.
8 Hambi nha wapatuka, wayileka lwa kamonga i nzila yono nawalajiliza. Alu wene wenozeza ndama nyenjedulwe, wakuyihemba no kuyilavizya zahabu, akuno wakulonga wakutya, ‘Gwe Isilayeli, ayu yo mulungu wetu mono yaciwusa mu yisi ya Misili!
9 Nane niwawene wanhu wawa, vyono wasina cihuliko.
10 Holeka Musa, wulece kunyimila. Ndekaje niwongomeze kwe likuwo lyangu liwaha. Ninga hanyuma nokunoza gwegwe hamonga ne wanago, wuwe yikungugo yiwaha.’ ”

11 Ninga Musa yakamunena Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe, yakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, alo nhawule likuwo lyako, likwaka muno kuli wanhu wako, wono wawawusa gwegwe mu yisi ya Misili, kwe vilungo viwaha, hamonga no wusume wuwaha?
12 Alo socici lo wuwapele Wamisili cilejeho, watye wawawusa wanhu wako ku wulawa ku yisi ya Misili, ho wuwawulajile mu matunda no kuwongomezeza lukulu? Nghukunena, wulece kukolwa ne likuwo, dedede wupitule wuhile wako, wulece kuwuleta wongomezwe wuwu muli wanhu wako.
13 Gwe Muwaha Mulungu, wukumbucile za Abulahamu na Isaka na Yakobo i wetumami wako, wono gwegwe go mweneco wawelahiza wukutya, ‘Nokwinjifya zindelo zenyu nhe zinyelezi za ku wulanga cibitilila.’ ”
14 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu hodu yakawa kuleka, si yawuleta wongomezwe wuwo muli wanhu wakwe.

15 Musa yakawa kutima ku wulawa mwi'tunda, akuno yebate ne vibawo vyejete, vyono vyandicigwe i malajilizo mbavu zose vyono nyejete.
16 Mulungu mweneco yo yavinoza vibawo vivyo, yakawa kwandika ne malajilizo mucanyha ye vibawo vivyo.
17 Nayo Yoshuwa yali yeza kulihulika lizozo lye wanhu, yakamutya Musa, “Gwe Musa, lizozo lilyo likulajila nhe zozo lya wulugu.”
18 Ninga Musa yakamupitula yakatya, “Gwe Yoshuwa, lizozo lilyo, si yizozo lya wusume hamba si yizozo lya wusumwe, langa lizozo lilyo lyono ane nane nghulihulika, nghuwona nhe zozo lye wanhu wono wakwimba.”
19 Nawo wali weza kuyisejelela nghambi, wakayiwona yiyilya ndama, ne wanhu nawo wakuvina. Hobaho likuwo lya Musa likawa kwaka nha moto, yakawa kuvigumila hasi ye litunda vibawo vivilya, yakavitula.
20 Yakawa kuyisola ndama yiyilya, yakayitakamiza mu moto, hanyuma yakawa kuyitindya yikawa wusaje. Yakawusola wusaje wuwo we ndama, yakawukologa mu malenga. Yakawa kuwadindimiza wanhu wa Isilayeli wang'we.

21 Musa yakamuwuza Haluni yakamutya, “Alu gwe Haluni, wanhu wawa wakunoza nhawule, sunga nagwe kuwa kuwaletela wubi wono winha wuwu?”
22 Nayo Haluni yakapitula yakatya, “Gwe Musa, nghukulomba wulece kungholelwa likuwo, ane no mwitumami wako. Hambi nha wuwamanyile wanhu wawa vyono wali dindimila ku wituma zo wubi.
23 Wo weneco wono wanonjeye wakutya, ‘Haluni, wucinozeze milungu yono yocitonga, hambi nha si cimanyile yono yimwagane yuyo Musa mono yaciwusa ku yisi ya Misili.
24 Holeka nane niwa kuwalonjela vyono, munhu wo wose mono yena wihambo wa zahabu yalavye. Nawo wawa kundetela, nane niwa kuvyenjedula mu moto, ne ndama yiyi vyo yinozigwe.’ ”

25 Musa yakawona vyono Haluni yo yawasutizye wanhu, holeka wetuma zono wakusaka no kwiletela cevu hali walugu wawo.
26 Hodu Musa yakawa kwima ha mulango we nghambi yakawuza yakutya, “Ati, nye wayangu, nani mono yali ku lubavu lo Mulungu? Ale yuyo mono yali ku lubavu lo Mulungu, yaze yeme akuno kono kwangu.” Nawo wana wose wa Lawi wakekongholela hali Musa.
27 Nayo Musa yakawalonjela wawo wana wa Lawi yakatya, “U Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli yakutya nhavi, ‘Wanhu wose mwiwopaje mimaje yenyu mu viwino, nanye munhu na lwakwe, yapilime-pilime mono nghambi, ku wulawilila kono mulango awuno sunga ko ku mulango muyagwe, nayo mu wupilime wuwo, yawulaje nduguye we cilume, no mwendece wakwe we cilume, hamonga no mwikalani muyagwe we cilume.’ ”
28 Nawo i wana wa Lawi waketuma nha vyono walajilizwa na Musa. Na mwi'zuwa lilyo, wakawulaga wanhu nha kotya elufu yidatu.
29 Nayo Musa yakawalonjela Walawi yakawatya, “Alu langa izuwali, mwiyisiwanulila kuli Mulungu nyo weneco, ko witumami wo kumwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu, mu mulimo wo wukohani, mu nzila yo kuwawulaga wana wenyu we cilume hamonga ne wandugu zenyu we cilume, u Mulungu nayo kamupelenyi wumoteswe izuwali.”

Musa yakuwalombela wanhu wa Isilayeli

30 Wali weza kwela mitondo yaco, Musa yakawalonjela wanhu yakatya, “Anye, mwituma wubi muno. Holeka alu nomubitila kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu kuko kwi'tunda, lunji nosuma kumunozezenyi wilamule ku soko yo wubi wenyu.”
31 Ale, Musa yakawa kumubitila kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu, yakawa kumutya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, ane nghukalala muno muno, vyono wanhu awa wetuma wubi muno, hambi nha wenozeza weneco milungu yo wulambi ya zahabu.
32 Ninga aluno go Muwaha Mulungu, ane nghukunena, wuwanenele wubi wawo. Na hono wulece kuwanenela, ale, nghukunena ciba nane wumhate mu citabu cako cono wuwandice wanhu wako.”
33 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakamutya Musa, “Ane mu citabu cangu, nomupata munhu mweneco du mono yasutizye nene.
34 Ninga aluno, hodu wubite wukawatonje wanhu sunga hanhu hono nghulonjeye. Ninga lanje, malayika wangu kokulongolela. Hamba nhavivyo, nane mwi'zuwa lyono nolomuzizenyi, ale, nolowalipiciza ku soko yo wubi wawo.”
35 Muwaha Mulungu yakawagumilila matamwa mabi muno muno, ku soko vyono wamudindimiza Haluni, yawanozeze yiyilya ndama ya zahabu.


Wutuluce 33

Muwaha Mulungu yakuwalajiliza Waisilayeli wawuce he'tunda lya Sinayi

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Wuwuce baha, mujendelele ku mwande, gwegwe hamonga ne wanhu wono wawawusa mu yisi ya Misili. Sunga mufice ku yisi yono namwilahila Abulahamu, na Isaka na Yakobo, yono natya vyono, ‘Nolowapela zindelo zako yisi yiyi.’
2 Nane notuma malayika yamulongolelenyi. Nowawinga Wakanani, ne Waamoli, ne Wahiti, ne Wapelizi, ne Wahivi, sunga ne Wayebusi.
3 Mubite ku yisi yiyo swanu, yono yina moso mwinji. Ninga lu, vyono nanye muli wanhu mono musina cihuliko, holeka nane hamba si cojendana nanye ho ndece kumongomezezenyi mu nzila.”

4 Nawo wanhu wali weza kuzihulika zinghani zizo, wakalila muno muno. Hasina kanyhi hamba munhu mono yasulumila kuvwala wihambo wakwe.
5 Nawo wanoza nhavivyo, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yali yakondya kumulonjela Musa vyono, “Walonjele wanhu wa Isilayeli wutye nhavi, ‘Anye muli wanhu mono musina cihuliko, nane hono nitye cijendane nanye, hamba mu cidaha cidodo du, ane nomongomezenyi. Holeka alu, hambulenyi wihambo wenyu, nane ho manye yono nomutendelenyi.’ ”
6 Nawo wanhu wa Isilayeli, wakehambulila wihambo wawo, he 'tunda lya Holebu lyono likucemwa Sinayi, hamba si wehamba kanyhi.

Lihema lyono lyelile

7 Nayo Musa, lono wanhu wa Isilayeli, ho hose hono wayisitaga nghambi, yali yena moto wo kulidinghula Lihema lililya no kulikoma kunze kutali hadodo ne nghambi. Ne Lihema lilyo, yakalipela litagwa yakalitya. “Lihema lyono lyelile.” No munhu wo wose mono kosaka wutonji kuli Muwaha Mulungu, yabitaga kuko kwi Hema lyono lyelile kunze ye nghambi.
8 Nayo Musa nhondo zose lono yenjilaga kwi 'Hema, wanhu wose, munhu he 'hema lyakwe, wenukaga wakwima wima mu milango ye mahema gawo, wawa wamulanjiliye Musa lono yakubita kwinjila kwi 'Hema, sunga kolo yenjila mwi 'Hema.
9 Nayo Musa kolo yakwinjila mwi 'Hema, livunde lyatimaga lipalinjile nhe lisumbili, liwa kwima wima ha mulango we Lihema. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu viwa kuduwana na Musa.
10 Nawo wanhu wose, lono waliwonaga lisumbili lye livunde limile ha mulango we Lihema, nawo wemaga no kuhemba, munhu ha mulango we lihema lyakwe.
11 Na mu nzila yiyo, vyo Muwaha Mulungu yasuma kuduwana na Musa wedomile, nha vyono wenha munhu na mwendece wakwe. Nayo Musa lono yawukaga mwi 'Hema, yakuwuya kwi hema lyakwe, muzelelo Yoshuwa u mwana wa Nuni, mono yali yo mutazi wakwe, ayo langa si yawuka mumo mwi 'Hema.

U Muwaha Mulungu, yakwihuwiza vyono kowa hamonga ne wanhu wakwe

12 Musa yakamulonjela Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, yo gwegwe mono wanonjela wukatya, ‘Wuwatongaje wanhu wawa.’ Ninga wukali si nanonjela munhu mono wukumutuma mu mulimo wuwu, mono kowa yali hamonga nane. Hamba nhavivyo, agwe wukutya vyono ane wumanyile, kanyhi na vyono wendezwe nane.
13 Holeka nane luno, nghukunena nghutya, vyono, hono yiwe ane nendezwe nagwe, ale wundajile zinzila zako. Nane ho nyhile kukumanya gwegwe go Muwaha Mulungu wangu. Ho nhuje wendezwe hali 'gwe. Kanyhi wukumbucile nha vyono likungugo lili, wo wanhu wako.”
14 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa, yakatya, “Lanje Musa, ane no mweneco nowa nili hamonga nagwe, kanyhi nokusindamika.”
15 Musa nayo yakapitula yakatya, “Hono agwe wuwe si kosuma kuwezana nase, hodu wulece kuciwusa bahano.
16 Hambi ho yomanyika wule vyono nina wendezwe hali'gwe, ane hamonga ne wanhu wako? Ninga hono wuwe wuli hamonga nane, hamonga ne wanhu wako, ale, baho langa, ho yomanyicila vyono, ase cili wanhu mono cisimajiye muli wanhu wose wa mono'nze.”

17 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Alu vyono wakondya kutuga wendezwe hali'ne, na vyono nane nghumanyile ne litagwa lyako, holeka nghani yiyi yono wuyilonjile, ale, nane noyituma.”
18 Nayo Musa yakapitula yakatya, “Ale, nghukunena wundajile lukumyo lwako.”
19 Muwaha Mulungu yakamupitula Musa yakamutya, “Ane, nokola ha mwande yako, no wuswanu wangu wose, kanyhi nolyanza litagwa lyangu hali'gwe, litagwa lyo ‘Muwaha Mulungu.’ Nane nghuwa kumuwonela lihewa mono nene nyendile kumuwonela lihewa, nomuwonela lusungu mono nene nyendile kumuwonela lusungu.
20 Ninga agwe si kosuma kumbonela lukulu wusu wangu, hambi nha hasina munhu wo wose, mono kosuma kuwuwona wusu wangu yekale mumi.”
21 Hanyuma kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Ahano behi nane, ho hali hanhu, hono agwe kokwima mucanyha ye libwe.
22 No lukumyo lwangu kolo lweza lukupuluta baho, ane nokwinjiza agwe mumo mwi'zatu lye libwe lilyo, nghugubice ko muwoko wangu, sunga kolo nakondya kupuluta.
23 Hanyuma ho nowuwusa muwoko wangu, nagwe kosuma kumbona ku mugongo wangu du, ninga wusu wangu si kuwuwona yaye.”


Wutuluce 34

Vibawo vinji kanyhi vipya vye Cilagane

(Kum 10:1-5)

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Wusese kanyhi vibawo vyejete vya mabwe, viwe vinha nhe vibawo vilongozi, nane nokwandika nghani zizilya, zono zali zandicigwe mu vibawo vilongozi vyono wavitula.
2 Holeka wiwikanizaje, mitondo hakali mitondo, nhondo wuze ciwone nane mwi'tunda lya Sinayi.
3 Ninga wulanjilile, wulece kuzana na munhu wo wose, hamba munhu wo wose yalecele lukulu kwijela mwi'tunda. Sunga ne zingholo ne zing'ombe zose, zilece kudimila he'tunda lilyo.”
4 Nayo Musa yakawa kuvisesa vibawo vyejete vya mabwe vinha ivilongozi. Yakawa kwilawa hakali mitondo kubita kwi'tunda lya Sinayi, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza, yena ne vibawo vivilya vyejete vye mabwe mu muwoko wakwe.

5 Muwaha Mulungu yakatima mwi'vunde, yakema na Musa, yakanza litagwa lyakwe, “Muwaha Mulungu.”
6 Muwaha Mulungu yakapuluta ha mwande ya Musa, akuno yakwanza yakutya, “Ane no Muwaha Mulungu, yo nene no Muwaha Mulungu mono nina nheto no lusungu, kanyhi singholwaga ne likuwo mbela, kanyhi nina wendo wuwaha no wihuwilwa.
7 Nane nghuwawonela lusungu wanhu maelufu, no kuwanenela wavu wawo no wusutye wawo, hamonga no wubi wawo. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, nowalipiciza wana hamonga ne wezukulu, sunga ko ku lulelo lwe kadatu na lwe kane, ku soko yo wubi we walezi wawo.”

8 Nayo Musa ho baho yakenama no kumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu.
9 Hanyuma Musa yakawa kutya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, alu vyono wakondya kumhela lihewa lyako, nane lu nghukunena, wujendane hamonga nase. Hambi wanhu wawa nha wasina cihuliko. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, wucinenele wavu wetu hamonga no wubi wetu. Nagwe wucibocele du nha wanhu wako go mweneco.”

I Cilagane cikunozwa wupya

(Wut 23:14-19; Kum 7:1-5; 16:1-17)

10 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Alu, nghunoza cilagane hamonga ne wanhu wa Isilayeli. Nane nokwituma mapoto hali wene, mapoto gono gakali hamba sinetumwa mono'nze yose, hamba mwi'kungugo lyo lyose. Ne wanhu wose wono kowezana nawo, wogawona metumo gangu nene no Muwaha Mulungu. Hambi nha nonoza nghani yo wizine, nayo noyinozeza muli gwegwe.

11 “Holeka mugakamisaje malajilizo gono nghumupelenyi izuwali. Nane nghuwawinga hali'nye, Waamoli, Wakanani, Wahiti, Wapelizi, Wahivi, sunga ne Wayebusi.
12 Ninga langa, mwilanjilile, mulece kwilumbaniza ne wanhu wono mowagana, i wekazi wa mu yisi yiyo, yono nanye mukubita. Hambi nghani yiyo, nha yowa nha mutego kuli'nye.
13 Langa kolo mwafika kuko, muvimwagulaje vikumbicilo vyawo, hamonga ne masumbili gawo, ne vihwani vyawo vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela.
14 Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kuhemba mulungu yunji, hambi ane no Muwaha Mulungu, nha nyhemwaga, ‘Mono nili wivu,’ nili Mulungu wo wivu.
15 Kanyhi mulece kwilumbaniza ne wekazi wa mu yisi yiyo mu nghani yo yose, ho walece kumukwejelezenyi mu wuhembi wawo wo wusenyha, ho yilece kuwa, lono wakuyihemba milungu yawo yo wulambi no kuyikumbika, wamulalicenyi nanye sunga mulye vye makumbiko gawo,
16 no kuwatoza wana wenyu we cilume kuli wana wawo we cicekulu. Hambi nha wowajiliza no kuwanoza wawe si wehuwilwa kanyhi kuli'ne, wawe kuhemba ne milungu yawo yo wulambi.

17 “Mulece kwinozeza hamba kuyihemba milungu myenjedulwe.
18 Muyikamisaje sikukuu ye mikate yono yisina cisase. Na mu vilo mupunghati, mwi'daha lyono lyawikwa, mu mwezi wa Abihu, mweze kulya mikate yono yisina cisase, nha vyono namulajilizenyi. Hambi mu mwezi wuwo, nha wo mwezi wono mwawucila mu yisi ya Misili.

19 “Holeka mwana we cilume mulelwa mulongozi, ayo kowa wangu nene, hamonga ne nyana zindelwa zinongozi ze mitugo zako zose, nyana ze zing'ombe ne nyana ze zingholo.
20 Langa nyana ye ndogowe, ayo koyikomboza ko kundavizya ngholo. Na hono wulece kusuma kuyikomboza, hodu wuyibenaje singo yayo. Sunga ne wana wenyu we cilume wose i walelwa walongozi, nawo nhavivyo mowa kuwakomboza.

“Ninga munhu wo wose yalece kunyiziza yali mawoko yasina cilavywa.
21 Ale, mwina vilo mutandatu vyo kwitumila milimo yenyu, ninga mwizuwa lyo mupunghati munojeye mucehe. Hamba yiwe mu vilo vye lilima, hamba vye mhwanga.
22 Muyikamisaje sikukuu ye malimbuko, lono mweza kwanza kududula wuhemba wa mu migunda yenyu, kanyhi, muyikamisaje ne sikukuu ye ndudula, lono mwakondya, ha ndumilizo yo mwaka.
23 Na we cilume wose, yiwanojeye kadatu mu mwaka, wekongholele hali'ne no Muwaha Mulungu, mono nili Mulungu wa Isilayeli.
24 Nane nogawinga makungugo ganji hali'nye, kanyhi noyigazimhya ne mipaka ye yisi yenyu. Na hasina munhu wo wose, mono koyisulumila no kuyizumhila yisi yenyu, mu mapango lono anye mwekongholela hali'ne, muli zizo zinghanghulo yidatu ho muhembe, ane no Muwaha Mulungu mono nili Mulungu wenyu.

25 “Na lono mweza kundavizya likumbiko, mulece kuhanzahanza sakami ye likumbiko lyangu hamonga no mukate wono wina cisase. Nalyo likumbiko lya mwi'zuwa lye Pasaka, lilece kulekwa ligone sunga wele mitondo yaco.
26 Ne malimbuko ga mu'si yenyu, mugaletaje mu kaya yangu ane no Muwaha Mulungu, mono nili Mulungu wenyu. ‘Mulecele lukulu kuyitelecela nyhinda hamba nyana ye ngholo mu mele ge nyina.’ ”

27 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Ale, wuzandikaje zinghani zizi, hambi mu zinghani zizi, nha mo nghunozeza cilagane na gwegwe, hamonga ne wanhu wa Isilayeli.”
28 Nayo Musa yakawa kuko kwi 'tunda hamonga no Muwaha Mulungu, vilo makumi mane misi ne cilo, hamba si yalizaga cinji hamba kung'wa malenga. Nayo yakazandika zinghani zose ze cilagane, hamonga na gagalya malajilizo yikumi.

Musa yakutima ku wulawa mwi'tunda lya Sinayi

29 Nayo Musa lono yatimaga yakulawa mwi'tunda, yena ne vibawo vyejete mu mawoko gakwe, vyono vyandicigwe zinghani ze Cilagane, hamba si yamanya vyono wusu wakwe wang'alalaga ku soko yo wuduwane no Mulungu.
30 Nayo Haluni hamonga ne wanhu wose wa Isilayeli, wali weza kumuwona Musa, wakogopa hamba no kumusejelela behi, hambi wusu wakwe nha wang'alalaga muno.
31 Ninga mwene Musa yakamucema Haluni, hamonga ne walongola wose we wanhu wa Isilayeli, wamubitile, nayo yakaduwana nawo.
32 Na hanyuma yayo, wanhu wose wa Isilayeli wakamusejelela behi, nayo yakawalajiliza zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yamulonjela mwi'tunda lya Sinayi.
33 Nayo Musa yali yeza kukondya kulongana ne wanhu, yakawugubika wusu wakwe ne citambala.
34 Ninga yikawa lono Musa yabitaga kulongana no Muwaha Mulungu mwi 'Hema lyono lyelile, yaciwusaga cigubiko mu wusu wakwe, sunga kolo yakulawila kunze. Na lono yalawilaga kunze, yawalonjelaga wanhu wa Isilayeli, zose zono yalajilizwa no Mulungu.
35 Nawo wanhu wa Isilayeli wawuwonaga wusu wakwe wukung'alala. Nayo Musa kawa kuwugubiciza wusu wakwe kwe citambala, sunga mwi'daha lono yabitaga kuduwana no Muwaha Mulungu kanyhi.


Wutuluce 35

Wulajilize we Sabato

1 Musa yakawakonghola hamonga mhuga yose ye wanhu wa Isilayeli, yakawalonjela yakatya, “Azi zo nghani zono Muwaha Mulungu yakumulajilizenyi mwitume.
2 Mu vilo mutandatu, mowa kwituma milimo yenyu. Ninga lizuwa lyo mupunghati, lyo lizuwa lyono lyelile kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Holeka munhu wo wose mono kokwituma mulimo mwi'zuwa lilyo, yimunojeye yuyo yawulagwe.
3 Mulece hamba kupemba moto mu wikalo wenyu mwi'zuwa lye Sabato.”

Vilavywa ku so Lihema lyono Lyelile

(Wut 25:1-9)

4 Nayo Musa yakawalonjela wose we mhuga ye wanhu wa Isilayeli, yakatya, “Ayi yo nghani yono Muwaha Mulungu yalajilize muyitume.
5 Mumulavizye Muwaha Mulungu vinhu vyono vikulawa mu zisawo zenyu. No munhu wowose mono yena nhumbula nzelu, yamulavizye Muwaha Mulungu zahabu, fweza, shaba.
6 Bafuta ye liwala lye buluu, na ye liwala lyo lugandu, na ye liwala lidunghu, hamonga ne bafuta swanu, mbote, hamonga no wukunzeje we zimhene.
7 Ne zinguwo ze zinduwilu zono zowa nhajilwe lanji ndunghu, kanyhi ne zinguwo ze zimhene, hamonga ne mabici go mukambala.
8 Hamonga ne mafuta ge zitala. Kanyhi hamonga ne vinhu vyo kuhanziliza mu mafuta go witile, na ku so wuvumba we mhunjile swanu.
9 Kanyhi ne vibwe vya salidoniki hamonga ne vibwe vinji vyo kuwicila wihambo mu efodi hamonga ne cifuko cayo.”

Vitumamilwa vya mwi 'Hema lyono lyelile

(Wut 39:32-43)

10 “No munhu wo wose wa muli nyenye, mono kowa yamanyile kuwituma mulimo wo wose, ale, yaze ho yanoze vinhu vyose vyono yavilajilize Muwaha Mulungu.
11 Nha cinji, kunoza Lihema lyono lyelile, ne ligubiko lyayo, ne lipaziya lyayo, ne vidobogo vyayo, ne mahapa gayo, hamonga ne matende gayo.
12 Ne Lisanduku lye Cilagane, hamonga ne mabici go kulidinghulila. Kanyhi ne ligoda lyo lusungu, ne lipaziya lyono lyotemanula i Hanhu hono helile muno.
13 Hamonga ne meza, ne mabici gayo go kuyidinghulila, ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose. Ne mikate milavywe kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
14 Kanyhi no wuwiko we zitala, ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose, hamonga ne zitala zayo, ne mafuta gayo.
15 Ne cifusizo co wuvumba hamonga ne mabici go kucidinghulila. Ne mafuta go witile no wuvumba we mhunjile swanu muno, ne lipaziya lya ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile.
16 Kanyhi ne cikumbicilo ce vilavywa vinyanywe no lwavu lwa shaba, ne mabici go kucidinghulila, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose. Ne ciya co wizuguzizo hamonga ne litende lyayo.
17 Kanyhi ne vyandaluwa vya mu luwa ne masumbili gayo hamonga ne matende gayo, ne lipaziya lyo mulango we luwa.
18 Ne zimambo zo kukomela Lihema lyono lyelile, hamonga no luwa, ne milejehe yayo.
19 Kanyhi ne vivwalo vyono vihonwe viswanu muno ku so witumami we Hanhu hono helile, nha cinji vivwalo vyono vyelile vya Haluni mukohani hamonga ne vivwalo vye wanagwe, ku soko yo witumami wawo wo wukohani.”

Wanhu wakuleta vilavywa vyawo

20 Ale, mhuga yose ye wanhu wa Isilayeli, wakawa kuwuka hali Musa.
21 Nawo, muli wose mono yali yendile, wakawa kuza yumonga yumonga no kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu vinhu ku soko ye Lihema lyono lyelile, hamonga ne visakwa vyayo vyose, ne vivwalo vyayo vyono vyelile.
22 Nayo vyo yikawa, wanhu wose we nhumbula nzelu, walume hamonga ne wacekulu, wose wakaleta wihambo we vipini, hamonga ne zi heleni, ne zinyamwidole ze citanjilo, ne mikufu, hamonga ne mawala gose ge viya vye zahabu.
23 Ne wanhu wose munhu na lwakwe, yakaleta cinhu co cose du cono yali yena naco, nha vyono lunji bafuta ye liwala lye buluu, hamba ye liwala lyo lugandu, hamba ye liwala lidunghu, hamba bafuta swanu, hamba wukunzeje we zimhene, hamba nguwo ya ngholo yono yitajiywe lanji ndunghu.
24 No munhu wo wose mono yasuma kuleta fweza hamba shaba, yamuletela Muwaha Mulungu, cikawa co cilavywa cakwe kuli Mulungu. No munhu wo wose mono yali yena mabici go mukambala, gono gali gakwiliye mulimo wo wose wa mu mulimo wuwo, nayo yakagaleta nhavivyo.
25 Ne wacekulu wose wono wali wena wumanye wo kuhona, nawo wakaleta vinhu vyono wahonaga vye bafuta ye liwala lye buluu, na ye liwala lyo lugandu, na ye liwala lidunghu, hamonga ne bafuta swanu, mbote.
26 Nawo wono wali wamanyile kubota wukunzeje we zimhene, wakabota wakaleta.
27 Walongola nawo, wakaleta vibwe vya salidoniki hamonga ne vibwe vinji vyo kuwicila mu efodi, hamonga na mu cifuko ca ha mhambaga.
28 Wakaleta kanyhi sunga ne vinhu vyo kuhanziliza, hamonga ne mafuta ge zitala, hamonga ne mafuta go witile, no wuvumba we mhunjile swanu.
29 Ne wanhu wose wa Isilayeli, walume ne wacekulu, wono zinhumbula zawo, zikawa zendile kulavya cinhu co cose, ku soko yo mulimo wo Muwaha Mulungu. Mulimo wono Mulungu yali yamulajiliza Musa witumwe. Nawo wavileta vinhu vivyo kwa nhumbula nzelu, wakavileta viwe vyo vilavywa vyawo kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

Wanghula wo kulizenga Lihema lyono lyelile

(Wut 31:1-11)

30 Musa yakawalonjela wanhu wa Isilayeli yakawatya, “Lanjenyi ta, Muwaha Mulungu kamuhagula Bezaleli mwana wa Uli, mwizukulu wa Huli, wo mulango wa Yuda.
31 Kanyhi kamumemya no Muhe wakwe, kamupela no wizuce ne mahala no wumanye hamonga no wunghula.
32 Ho yasume kutula milimo yo wunghula, yawe munghula wo kwituma mulimo wo kutyana zahabu, fweza na shaba.
33 Kanyhi yakakate ne mabwe go wihambo, hamonga ne mabici, gono gokwitumamilwa mu milimo yinji yose yo wunghula.
34 Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu kamuwika mwene hamonga na Oholiyabu, mwana wa Ahisamaki wo mulango wa Dani, ho wawejize na wanji mulimo wo wunghula.
35 Kawapela no wizuce wo kwituma milimo yose yo wunghula hamba yo wukakati, na yo wunghula wo kuhona mu bafuta ye liwala lye buluu, hamba liwala lyo lugandu, ne liwala lidunghu, hamonga ne bafuta swanu, mbote, mu nzila yo wunghula du we liwala lyo lyose.


Wutuluce 36

1 “Nayo Bezaleli na Oholiyabu, hamonga ne wanhu wose we cilume, wono Muwaha Mulungu yawapelile mahala no wizuce, wo kumanya kwituma mulimo wose wo wuzenji we Lihema lyono lyelile, yiwanojeye wetume mulimo wuwo wuwe winha nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalajiliza.”

Wanhu wakuleta vilavywa ko winji muno

2 Musa yakawacema Bezaleli na Oholiyabu, hamonga ne wanhu wose wono wapejigwe wizuce no Muwaha Mulungu, wono wali wendile weneco mu nhumbula zawo kuwituma mulimo wuwo.
3 Nawo wakavibocelela kulawa kuli Musa i vinhu vyose vyono vyali vyalavywa ne wanhu wa Isilayeli na nhumbula nzelu, ku soko yo mulimo we Lihema lyono lyelile. Ne wanhu wa Isilayeli, wakajendelela nhondo zose hakali mitondo, kumuletela Musa vilavywa vyawo na nhumbula nzelu.
4 Ne wanhu wose wono wali wena wizuce, wono wetumaga milimo ye hayo hayo, yo kuzenga Lihema lyono lyelile,
5 wakamubitila Musa, wakamulonjela wakutya, “Alu wanhu nha wa kuleta vinhu vinji muno, kuciza nghango yono yikusakwa so mulimo wono yacilajiliza Muwaha Mulungu ciwitume.”
6 Nayo Musa yakalajiliza yakatya, “Aluno munhu wo wose hodu yalece kujendelela kuleta vilavywa kanyhi, ku soko ye Lihema lyono lyelile.” Hodu, wanhu wakawa kuleka kujendelela kuleta vinhu kanyhi.
7 Hambi i vinhu vivyo vyono wali wakondya kuvileta, nha vyali vikondizye kuwumala mulimo wose no kusigala.

Wunoze we Lihema lyono lyelile

(Wut 26:1-37)

8 Walume wose wono wali wena wizuce, wakalinoza Lihema lyono lyelile kwe mapaziya yikumi, gono gali mahonwe kwe bafuta yono mbote, na kwe bafuta ye liwala lye buluu, na ye liwala lyo lugandu, na ye liwala lidunghu, yina ne cihwani ce makelubi.
9 No wutali we mapaziya, limonga lyali lina wutali wa mita yikumi na nyejete, no wugazi wa mita nyejete. Ne mapaziya gose gakawa gena cipimo cimonga du.

10 Wakagalunza hamonga mapaziya mahano mahano.
11 Kanyhi wakanoza ne vinanicizo vye liwala lye buluu, mwi'pindo lye lipaziya lya ku mhelo, lya mwi'fungu lilongozi. Ne vinanicizo vinji navyo, wakaviwicila mwi'fungu lye kejete.
12 Wakawicila vinanicizo makumi mahano mwi 'paziya lilongozi lye lifungu lilongozi, na vinanicizo vinji kanyhi mwi 'paziya lye kejete lye lifungu lye kejete, ne vinanicizo vyose vikawa viyidomile.
13 Hanyuma wakanoza vidobogo makumi mahano vya zahabu, ho visume kugalunza hamonga gagalya mafungu mejete ge mapaziya. Na mu nzila yiyo, Lihema likawa lyakondya kuwa lyo limonga.

14 Kanyhi wakanoza mapaziya yikumi na limonga, go wukunzeje we zimhene, gono gokwitumamilwa kugubika mucanyha ye Lihema, liwe lye'tembe lyalyo.
15 No wutali we mapaziya, limonga likawa no wutali wa mita yikumi na yidatu, no wugazi wa mita nyejete. Ne mapaziya gose yikumi na limonga, gakawa gose gena cipimo cimonga du.
16 Wakagalunza hamonga mapaziya mahano. Na agalya go mutandatu nago, wakagalunza hamonga.
17 Hanyuma wakawa kuviwicila vinanicizo makumi mahano mwi 'mhembo lye lipaziya lya ku mhelo, mwi'paziya lye lifungu lilongozi, na vinanicizo vinji kanyhi makumi mahano, navyo wakaviwicila mwi 'mhembo lye lifungu lye kejete.
18 Hanyuma kanyhi, wakanoza vidobogo makumi mahano vya shaba, ho wagalunze gagalya mafungu mejete ge mapaziya, ho walinoze liwe lipaziya limonga du so Lihema.
19 Wakanoza ne cigubiko ca nguwo za nduwilu, na mucanyha yayo kanyhi, wakanoza cigubiko ca nguwo swanu.

20 Hanyuma, yakanoza mabici go mukambala, go kusimya wima ku soko ye Lihema.
21 Ne mabici gose gakawa, limonga lina wutali wa mita yine, no wugazi wa sentimita makumi mutandatu na mutandatu.
22 Mabici gose, limonga likawa lina manghwasa go kulunziza. Ne mabici gose ge Lihema, yaganoza gena manghwasa.
23 Mabici gago ge Lihema, yaganoza nhavi, mabici makumi mejete, gakawa ku lubavu lwa kutakama,
24 hamonga ne matende gayo makumi mane, gono gakawikwa hasi ya gago mabici makumi mejete ge manghwasa. Yikawa matende mejete ne 'bici lyayo, i manghwasa ga galya mejete, ho gajelekwe mumo mu matende gago mejete.
25 Na ku lubavu lwa kusumuka lye Lihema, nako yakakunozeza mabici makumi mejete nhavivyo.
26 Hamonga ne matende gayo makumi mane ga fweza, matende mejete hasi ye libici limonga.
27 Na ku mhilimo yayo, nha cinji kumwezi ye Lihema, ako yakakunozeza mabici mutandatu.
28 Yakanoza na mabici mejete kanyhi, go kuwika mu zinhuzi ze Lihema, ku mhilimo yayo.
29 Ne mabici gago ga mu mhelo yayo, gali malunzwe hamonga ku wulawilila hasi sunga ko kucanyha. Ne mabici ga galya nago i mejete mejete, gono gawicilwa mu zinhuzi zayo zinyejete, nago gali malunzwe nhavivyo.
30 Na mu nzila yiyo, holeka kukawa na mabici munana, hamonga ne matende gayo ga fweza, nago gali gali yikumi na mutandatu, nha cinji matende mejete hasi ye 'bici limonga.

31 Yakanoza ne mahapa go mukambala. Mahapa mahano ku so mabici go lubavu lumonga lwe Lihema.
32 Ne mahapa mahano ganji ku so lubavu luyagwe nalo lwe Lihema. Ne mahapa mahano ganji kanyhi, ku so mabici gono gali ku lubavu lwa ku mhilimo ye lihema, nha cinji kumwezi yayo.
33 Yakawunoza mucici wa mugati, wukole haciligati ye zimbawo, wulawe ku nhuzi monga sunga ko ku nhuzi nzagwe.
34 Ne mabici gose, yakagahiliwa zahabu. Kanyhi, yakanoza ne zinyamwidole za zahabu zono zokwibatiliza mahapa gago, nazo zinyamwidole, yakazihiliwa zahabu.

35 Yakanoza ne lipaziya lye zimhuti ze liwala lye buluu, ne liwala lyo lugandu, ne liwala lidunghu, hamonga ne bafuta swanu; mbote. Ne lipaziya lilyo, likahonelwa cihwani ce makelubi mucanyha yayo, ko wunghula wuwaha muno.
36 Yakanoza kanyhi masumbili mane go mubici wo mukambala, yakagahiliwa zahabu, yakawicila ne vidobogo vyayo vya zahabu. Yakaganozeza ne matende gayo mane ga fweza.
37 Kanyhi yakanoza ne lipaziya lyono likangaye muno, kwa mhuti ze liwala lye buluu, na ze liwala lyo lugandu, na ze liwala lidunghu. Yakalinoza kwa bafuta swanu, mbote, ku so yo mulango we Lihema. Nalyo lipaziya likawa yihonwe viswanu muno muno.
38 Ne masumbili mahano ge lipaziya, nago yakagawicila vidobogo vihiliwe zahabu. Yakanoza hamonga ne matende gayo mahano ga shaba, ku soko ye masumbili gago.


Wutuluce 37

Wunozi we Lisanduku lye Cilagane

(Wut 25:10-22)

1 Bezaleli yakalinoza Lisanduku lye Cilagane ko mubici wo mukambala, wutali walyo wukawa sentimita yigana limonga ne kumi, ne cipinga calyo, sentimita makumi mutandatu na mutandatu, no wima walyo sentimita makumi mutandatu na mutandatu.
2 Yakalihiliwa zahabu nengu, kunze yalyo na mugati yalyo, kanyhi yakaliwicila no mugo wa zahabu mbavu zose.
3 Yakalinozeza kanyhi ne zinyamwidole yine za zahabu zo kulidinghulila, yakaziwicila monga monga mu zinhuzi zalyo.
4 Yakalinozeza ne mabici go mukambala, go kulidinghulila Lisanduku, yakagahiliwa nago zahabu.
5 Yakagatunjila mu zinyamwidole zono zili mu zimhelo ze Lisanduku, so kulidinghulila.
6 Yakanoza ne ligoda lyo lusungu lya zahabu nengu, no wutali walyo, sentimita yigana limonga ne 'kumi, ne cipinga calyo, sentimita makumi mutandatu na mutandatu.
7 Yakanoza kanyhi ne cihwani ce makelubi mejete ga zahabu nhyane.
8 Yakagawicila mucanyha ye ligubiko lye Lisanduku, limonga akuno, ne liyagwe ku lubavu luyagwe, gakawa malunzizwe lukulu hamonga ne ligubiko lye Lisanduku.
9 Nago makelubi gago, yakagawicila gedomile, gagoloye ne mabawa gayo, galigubice ligoda lyo lusungu.

Meza ye mikate yono milavywe kuli Mulungu

(Wut 25:23-30)

10 Yakanoza kanyhi meza ko mubici wo mukambala, wutali wayo, sentimita makumi munana na munana, no wugazi wayo, sentimita makumi mane na yine, no wima wayo, sentimita makumi mutandatu na mutandatu.
11 Nayo yakayihiliwa zahabu nengu, yakayiwicila no mugo wayo wa zahabu.
12 Hanyuma, yakawicila ciciwizizo ce cibawo, co wugazi wa milimita makumi mupunghati ne 'hano, cipilime mucanyha ye meza, yakaciwicila no mugo wa zahabu.
13 Yakanoza ne zinyamwidole yine za zahabu, yakaziwicila monga monga mu migulu ye meza ha kacanyha yayo.
14 Zinyamwidole zo kugatunjila mabici go kuyidinghulila meza yiyo, yakaziwicila ha kacanyha behi ne ciciwizo ce meza.
15 Hanyuma, yakanoza ne mabici mejete go mukambala, go kuyidinghula meza yiyo, nago yakagahiliwa zahabu.
16 Yakanoza ne viya vya zahabu nengu, vyo kwitumamila baho ha meza yiyo. Yakanoza sahani zayo hamonga ne zi sahani zi ndodo ndodo so wuvumba, hamonga ne vikombe, ne vidumu vyayo, so cilavywa ce cing'wegwa.

Wunoze wo wuwiko we zitala

(Wut 25:31-40)

17 Yakanoza no Wuwiko we zitala wa zahabu nengu. Litende lyayo, no wibato wayo wa haciligati yayo, ne vikombe vyayo ne zimhimbili zayo hamonga, kwa cuma ca zahabu, cikawa co cinhu cimonga du.
18 Manhambi mutandatu gakawa galawiye mu mbavu zo wuwiko we zitala, manhambi madatu ku lubavu aluno, ne manhambi madatu nago, ku lubavu luyagwe.
19 Na mu manhambi gose, linhambi limonga, lyali lina vikombe vidatu, vyono vihwanile kotya nhe liluwa lyo mulozi, cikombe ne zimhimbili zayo ne liluwa lyayo.
20 Na mwi'bici lyayo lya haciligati yayo, hakawa na vikombe vine vyono vihwanile kotya nhe liluwa lyo mulozi, hamonga ne zimhimbili zayo ne maluwa gayo.
21 Ne hanhu hono getang'aniliye i manhambi gayo go mutandatu, hali hawiciywe linhambi na hundo yayo.
22 Ne zihundo zizo, hamonga ne manhambi gayo, vyose vikawa vilunzwe hamonga no wuwiko wuwo we zitala. Navyo vyose, vikawa vityanwe na zahabu nengu.
23 Yakanoza zitala zayo mupunghati, hamonga ne mibato yayo, hamonga ne viwicilo vyayo vya zahabu nengu.
24 Yakawunozeza wuwo wuwiko we zitala vitumamilwa vyayo vyose kwa kilo makumi madatu na yihano za zahabu.

Wunoze we cikumbicilo co kufusiza wuvumba

(Wut 30:1-5)

25 Yakanoza cikumbicilo co kufusiza wuvumba ko mubici wo mukambala. Ne cikumbicilo cico, cikawa ciling'aniye mbavu zose, u wutali wayo sentimita makumi mane ne'hano, u wugazi wayo, sentimita makumi yicenda. Ne mahembe ge cikumbicilo cico, gakawa malunzizwe lukulu hamonga ne cikumbicilo.

26 Yakacihiliwa cose zahabu nengu, mucanyha yayo sunga na mu zimbavu zayo zose, mu mhelo yayo, sunga na mu mahembe gayo. Yakacinozeza no mugo wayo wa zahabu.
27 Yakanoza nyamwidole nyejete za zahabu, yakazinamatiza ha kasika yo mugo wayo, mu mbavu zose vyono nyejete, yakaziwicila zidomile, nazo ziketumamilwa so kutunjila mabici go kucidinghulila cikumbicilo.
28 Yakanoza mabici mejete go mubici wo mukambala, go kucidinghulila cikumbicilo, yakagahiliwa zahabu.

Wunoze we Mafuta go witile, hamonga no wuvumba

(Wut 30:22-38)

29 Yakanoza ne mafuta gono gelile go witile, hamonga no wuvumba, wono wina mhunjile swanu muno muno, wono wuhanzahanze kotya manukato.


Wutuluce 38

Wunoze we cikumbicilo ce vilavywa vinyanywe

(Wut 27:1-8)

1 Yakanoza kanyhi ne cikumbicilo co mubici wo mukambala ce cilavywa cinyanywe. Naco cikumbicilo cikawa ciling'aniye mbavu zose. Wutali wayo, mita nyejete na lobo, no wugazi wayo nawo, mita nyejete na lobo. Wima wayo, mita monga na lobo.
2 Yakawicila ne viwoko vyayo vyo kucidinghulila, yakavityaniliza mu zinhuzi zose ze cikumbicilo vyono yine. Yakacihiliwa shaba cose.
3 Yakanoza ne vitumamilwa vyose vye cikumbicilo, nha cinji, vyungu, koleyo, mabeseni, vicinyilo, hamonga ne visahani vyo kupalila moto. Nayo yakavinoza vinhu vivi vyose vya shaba.
4 Yakanoza no lwavu lwa shaba, yakulusopeceza haciligati ye cikumbicilo.
5 Hanyuma, yakanoza ne zinyamwidole yine, yakaziwicila mu zinhuzi zose ze cikumbicilo vyono yine, ku soko yo kucidinghulila.
6 Yakanoza ne mabici go mukambala, go kucidinghulila cikumbicilo, yakagahiliwa shaba.
7 Yakagatunjila mabici ga galya, mu zinyamwidole zizilya zono zali mbicile mu zimbavu ze cikumbicilo, ho cisume kudinghulwa. Yakacinoza cikumbicilo cico kwe mabici, naco cikawa cikombwe mugati yayo cinye matuli.

Wunoze we ciya ca Shaba co wizuguzizo

(Wut 30:18)

8 Hanyuma yayo, yakanoza ciya ca shaba, hamonga ne litende lyayo lya shaba, ku wulawa mu vilole vye shaba vyo kwilanjila, vye wetumami we cicekulu, wono wetumamilaga ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile.

Luwa ye Lihema lyono lyelile

(Wut 27:9-19)

9 Hanyuma kanyhi, yakalinozeza luwa, Lihema lyono lyelile. Luwa yiyo, yayinoza kwa candaluwa ca bafuta swanu muno, mbote. Vyandaluwa vyo lubavu lwa kutakama, vyali na wutali wa mita makumi mane na yine.
10 Navyo vikawa vibatilizwe mu masumbili makumi mejete ga shaba, gono gena ne matende gayo makumi mejete nago ga shaba. Ninga zinyamwidole ze masumbili gago, hamonga ne vinanicizo vyayo, avyo langa vyali vya fweza.
11 Nalo lubavu lwa kusumuka, candaluwa cayo, cali na wutali wa mita makumi mane na yine, hamonga ne masumbili gayo makumi mejete ga shaba. Ninga zinyamwidole ze masumbili gago, hamonga ne vinanicizo vyayo, vyali vya fweza.
12 No lubavu lwa kumwezi nako, kwali na candaluwa co wutali wa mita makumi mejete na nyejete, ne masumbili yikumi, hamonga ne matende gayo yikumi. Zinyamwidole za mu masumbili hamonga ne vinanicizo vyayo, vyose vivyo vikawa vya fweza.
13 No lubavu lwa kucilima kono kwali ko wuli mulango we Lihema, kwali no wugazi wa mita makumi mejete na nyejete.

14 Ne vyandaluwa i vyejete, vyono vyali vitumbikwe ku lubavu kono kwali kowuli no mulango we Lihema, cimonga cali na wutali wa mita mutandatu na nusu. Navyo vikatumbikwa cimonga ku mhelo ayino yo mulango, ne ciyagwe naco ku mhelo nzagwe. Hamonga ne masumbili gayo madatu ne matende gayo nago madatu.
15 Ne candaluwa co lubavu lulo luyagwe, naco cali cina wutali wa mita mutandatu na nusu, hamonga ne masumbili gayo madatu, ne matende gayo nago madatu.
16 Ne vyandaluwa vyose vyono vyali vipilimywe luwa, vyali vinoze na bafuta swanu, mbote.
17 Ne matende gose ge masumbili, gali ga shaba, ninga zinyamwidole ze masumbili gayo, hamonga ne vinanicizo vyayo, avyo vyali vya fweza. Ne masumbili gayo gose gali makoweleze na sito za fweza.
18 Langa lipaziya lya ha mulango we luwa, alyo likanozwa kwa bafuta ye liwala lye buluu, ne liwala lyo lugandu, ne liwala lidunghu. Lyanozezwa bafuta swanu, mbote, kanyhi yihonelwe viswanu ne maluwa mucanyha yalyo. Nalyo likawa na wutali wa mita yicenda, no wugazi wa mita nyejete, nha vyono vinha vyandaluwa vye luwa yose.
19 Nalyo likawa litumbikwe mu masumbili mane, ne matende gayo mane ga shaba. Langa zindobogo zayo, ne mitwe ye masumbili, hamonga ne vinanicizo, vyose vyali vya fweza.
20 Sunga ne zimambo zose ze Lihema, ne zimambo zose ze luwa mu mhelo ye Lihema, zose zali za shaba.

Vitumamilwa vye Lihema lyono lyelile

21 Nalyo ali, lyo liwalo lye viya vyono vyetumamilwa mwi 'Hema lyono lyelile, nha cinji, Lihema lye Cilagane. Ne liwalo lili, lyanozwa ko wutonji wa Itamali mwana wo mukohani Haluni, nha vyono Musa yalajiliza.

22 Nayo Bezaleli mwana wa Uli, u mwizukulu wa Huli, wo mulango wa Yuda, yuyo yo yovinoza vinhu vyose nha vyono Musa yalajilizwa no Muwaha Mulungu.
23 Nayo Oholiyabu mwana wa Ahisamaki wo mulango wa Dani, mono yamutazaga Bezaleli, yuyo yali yo munghula muno, wo mulimo wo wukakati no wucolaji, hamonga no mulimo wo wuhoneleza maluwa, ne zimhuti ze bafuta ye liwala lye buluu, ne liwala lyo lugandu, ne liwala lidunghu ne bafuta swanu.

24 Ne zahabu yose yono wamulavizya Muwaha Mulungu ku soko yo wuzenji we Lihema lyono lyelile, yali yificile wuzito wa kilo magana munana na makumi mupunghati na mupunghati na gulamu magana madatu, nha vyono vinha vipimo vye Lihema lyono lyelile.
25 Ne fweza yono yakongholwa, mu nghango ye liwalo lye wanhu we mhuga zose, mu wuwalwe wawo, yali yificile wuzito wa kilo elufu yidatu na yikumi na mupunghati na gulamu magana mupunghati na makumi mahano, nha vyono vinha vipimo vye Lihema lyono lyelile.
26 Ne liwalo lilino, wo wutowelele we fweza yose yono yalavywa ne wanhu wose, wono wali wakondya kwandikwa, mwi'pango lyo wuwale we wanhu. Nawo wakawa kulavya mu nghango yono yasakwaga. Nayo yikapimwa kwe vipimo vye zifweza ze Lihema lyono lyelile. Kwali na walume, elufu magana mutandatu ne 'datu, na magana mahano, na makumi mahano. Wono wali waficile myaka makumi mejete no kuciza, wono wali wakondya kwandikwa mwi 'walo lilyo lye wanhu walavyaga munhu monga fweza gulamu yihano.
27 Na mu fweza yiyo, kilo elufu yidatu, zetumamilwa mu wunoza matende yigana limonga ge Lihema lyono lyelile, hamonga ne lipaziya. Na mu matende gose, limonga liketumamila kilo makumi madatu za fweza.
28 Na azilya zi kilo ze likumi na mupunghati na gulamu makumi mupunghati ne 'hano za fweza zono zali zisigaye, Bezaleli yakanoza vidobogo vye masumbili, na mu wuhiliwe we mitwe ye masumbili no kunoza vinanicizo.
29 Nayo shaba yono wamulavizya Muwaha Mulungu, yali yificile wuzito wa kilo elufu nyejete ne 'gana limonga na makumi mejete na yine.
30 Nayo Bezaleli, yakayitumamila shaba yiyo, mu wunozeza matende go mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile, ne cikumbicilo ce shaba, hamonga no lwavu lwayo lwa shaba, ne vitumamilwa vyose vye cikumbicilo.
31 Hamonga ne matende gose ge luwa, yono yali yipilime Lihema lyono lyelile, hamonga ne matende go mulango we luwa yiyo. Ne zimambo zose ze Lihema lyono lyelile, hamonga ne zimambo ze luwa yose.


Wutuluce 39

Wunoze we vivwalo so Wakohani

(Wut 28:1-14)

1 Wakahona vivwalo vyo wulenzi, vyo kwitumamilwa ne wakohani, lono wakwitumama i hanhu hono helile. Na mu wuhone wayo, waketumamila bafuta ye liwala lye buluu, ne liwala lyo lugandu, ne liwala lidunghu. Wakamuhonela Haluni vivwalo vyono vyelile, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.

2 Ne cifuko ca ha mhambaga naco, wakacihona ne bafuta swanu, mbote.
3 Wakayityana zahabu, wakayihebenha muno muno, wakayisapa yikawa mhuti sisili, wakazitumamila zimhuti zizo, kuzihoneleza hamonga ne bafuta ye liwala lye buluu, ne liwala lyo lugandu, ne liwala lidunghu, hamonga ne bafuta swanu, mbote, ko wunghula muno.
4 Nayo efodi, wakayihoneleza hamonga ne misipi yayo myejete, ya mu mawega, yono milunzwe ku zimhelo zayo zinyejete.
5 U musipi wono wali muhonwe kwa zahabu ne bafuta ye liwala lye buluu, ne liwala lyo lugandu, ne liwala lidunghu, hamonga ne bafuta swanu, mbote, nawo wakawulunza hamonga ne efodi, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.

6 Hanyuma wakasola vibwe vya salidoniki, vyono vyali vikakate nha muhuli, i matagwa yikumi na mejete ge wana wa Yakobo. Wakayisola zahabu wakayijidila mu mulya mu wukakate we matagwa ge wana yikumi na wejete wa Yakobo.
7 Wakaviwopeleza vibwe vivyo mu misipi ya mu mawega, yono yibate efodi, viwe vibwe vyo wukumbucize we milango yikumi na myejete ye Waisilayeli kuli Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.

Wunoze we cifuko ca ha mhambaga

(Wut 28:15-30)

8 Wakanoza ne cifuko ca ha mhambaga ko wunghula muno muno, wakacinoza nha vyono wayinoza efodi. Wakacinoza kwa zahabu, ne bafuta ye liwala lye buluu, ne liwala lyo lugandu, ne liwala lidunghu, hamonga ne bafuta swanu, mbote.
9 Ne cifuko cico cikawa ciling'aniye kose kose wutali no wugazi waco, cono casumikaga kupetyeka. Wutali waco, wukawa sentimita makumi mejete na nyejete, no wugazi wayo nawo, sentimita makumi mejete na nyejete.
10 Naco cikawa cihambwe micilili mine ye mabwe go wuguzi wukamu. Mucilili mulongozi wali wa mabwe ga akiki, topazi, na alimasi ye liwala lidunghu.
11 No mucilili we kejete nawo, wali wa mabwe ga zumalidi, yakuti samawi, na alimasi.
12 Mucilili we kadatu, wali na mabwe ga yasinito, akiki ndunghu, hamonga na amesisito.
13 No mucilili we kane, nawo wukawa ne vibwe vya zamalajadi, salidoniki na yasipi. Ne vibwe vivino vyose, vikazubujizwa mu mulya mono wali wahimbile vikombo vye matagwa, wakawa kuvinamatiza kwa zahabu.
14 Holeka hakawa na vibwe yikumi na vyejete, vyono vyali vikakatigwe litagwa limonga limonga, mu matagwa yikumi na mejete ge wana we cilume wa Yakobo, nha vyono yinha milango yose ye wana wa Isilayeli.
15 Wakacinozeza mikufu miswanu muno, i cifuko cico ca ha mhambaga.
16 Kanyhi wakanoza ne viwicilo vyejete vya zahabu nengu, hamonga na nyamwidole nyejete za zahabu. Nazo wakaziwicila mu mamhembo gayo vyono mejete, ga kucanyha ye cifuko cico.
17 Ne mikufu yiyo ye zahabu, wakayinaniciza mu zinyamwidole zizo.
18 Ne visonje vyejete vye mikufu yiyo ya zahabu, wakaviwicilila vivilya viwicilo vyejete. Na mu nzila yiyo, wakatuga nzila yo kuvibatiza viwicilo vivyo, mu misipi ye efodi ya mu mawega, ku lubavu lwa kono mwande.
19 Wakanoza kanyhi nyamwidole zinji nyejete za zahabu. Nazo wakaziwicila mu mamhembo mejete ga kono sika, ge cifuko ca mu mhambaga, na kusi yayo, vikawa vili behi ne efodi.
20 Wakanoza kanyhi nyamwidole zinji nyejete za zahabu, nazo wakaziwicila kono mwande, mu mamhembo mejete ga kusika ge efodi, hanhu hono musipi wu wulya wono muhone ko wunghula muno, vyali vilunzize ne efodi.
21 Wakawa kuviwopeleza hamonga, i cifuko ca ha mhambaga hamonga ne efodi, mu nzila yo kuyihanza hamonga, ko mulejehe we liwala lye buluu, zinyamwidole za mu efodi ne zinyamwidole za mu cifuko ca ha mhambaga. I cifuko ho cikame nzii, hacanyha yo musipi wuwulya muhone ko wunghula, cilece hamba kuhogoha, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.

Wunoze we Vivwalo vinji kanyhi vye Cikohani

(Wut 28:31-43)

22 Yakanoza ne kanzo ye liwala lye buluu, yo kuvwala kusi ye efodi.
23 Nayo kanzo yiyo, yali yina no wukozo wo mutwe haciligati yayo. Nayo yakayihonela ne lipindo lyayo wuwo wukozo wo mutwe, ho kanzo yilece kudemuka.
24 Ne lipindo lya hasi lye kanzo, wakalihoneleza wihambo we makomamanga ge bafuta ye liwala lye buluu, ne liwala lyo lugandu, ne liwala lidunghu, ne bafuta swanu, mbote.
25 Hanyuma, wakanoza vinyanjili vya zahabu, navyo wakaviwicila mwi 'pindo lya kusika ye kanzo. Wakawa wakuwicila yikomamanga hanyuma cinyanjili, hanyuma yikomamanga hanyuma cinyanjili.
26 Na vyo yali, nha vivyo, viketyatya yikomamanga hanyuma cinyanjili, sunga lipindo lyose lye kanzo wakalipilimya makomamanga hamonga ne vinyanjili, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.

27 Hanyuma kanyhi, wakawahonela Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, shati za bafuta swanu.
28 Hamonga no mukumbo wa bafuta swanu, ne zikofiya ze bafuta swanu, hamonga ne zikaputula ze bafuta swanu, mbote.
29 Hamonga ne misipi ye bafuta swanu, mbote, ne bafuta ye liwala lye buluu, ne liwala lyo lugandu ne liwala lidunghu. Kanyhi zikawa honwe viswanu muno, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.

30 Wakanoza cibati ca zahabu swanu, ku so mukumbo wono welile, naco wakacandika mucanyha yayo kotya muhuli, nghani zono zikutya “Welile kuli Muwaha.”
31 Hanyuma, wakawa kuciwopeleza mu mukumbo, he handa lyo wusu, ko mulejehe we liwala lye buluu, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.

Wumalile wo Mulimo

(Wut 35:10-19)

32 Mulimo wo wunozi we Lihema lyono lyelile, nhondo wukawa wamalika. Wanhu wa Isilayeli, wakanoza zose nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.
33 Wakawa kumuletela Musa Lihema lyono lyelile, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose, nha cinji, zinyamwidole zayo, ne mabici gayo, ne mahapa gayo, ne masumbili gayo, ne matende gayo.
34 Ligubiko lye zinguwo ze zinduwilu ne zimhene, yitajilwe lanji ndunghu. Ne lipaziya lyo kuwicila i Hanhu hono helile helile muno.
35 Ne Lisanduku lye Cilagane, ne mabici gayo go kulidinghulila, ne Ligoda lyo Lusungu.
36 Ne meza, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose, nha cinji, mikate yono milavywe kuli Mulungu.
37 Hamonga no wuwiko we zitala, wunoze ne zahabu nengu, ne zitala zayo, ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose, hamonga ne mafuta go kwasiza zitala zizo.
38 Ne cikumbicilo ca zahabu, ne mafuta go witile, no wuvumba we mhunjile swanu, hamonga ne lipaziya lya ha mulango we Lihema.
39 Ne cikumbicilo ce shaba, hamonga no lwavu lwayo lwe shaba, ne mabici gayo go kucidinghulila cikumbicilo, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose. Bakuli, ne ciya co wizuguzizo, hamonga ne litende lyayo.
40 Ne myenda misimye ye luwa, ne masumbili gayo gose, hamonga ne matende gayo. Ne lipaziya lya ha mulango we luwa, ne milejehe yayo, ne zimambo zayo, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose, ho vitumamilwe mu mulimo wo wunoza Lihema lyono lyelile.
41 Ne vivwalo vyose vyono vyali vihonwe viswanu, so witumamilwe wa mu mulimo we hanhu hono helile, nha cinji vivwalo vyono vyelile vya Haluni mukohani, hamonga ne wanagwe, so mulimo wo wukohani.
42 Wanhu wa Isilayeli wakavinoza vinhu vyose, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.
43 Nayo Musa yakavilanga vinhu vyose, yakawona vyono wavinozize viswanu, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yali yalajilize. Nayo Musa yakawalugutiza.


Wutuluce 40

Wusimywe we Lihema lyono lyelile hamonga no wusiwanulwe walyo

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
2 “Gwe Musa, mwizuwa lilongozi lyo mwezi mulongozi, nhondo wulisimye Lihema lyono lyelile.
3 Na mugati ye Lihema lilyo, wuwice ne Lisanduku lye Cilagane, hanyuma wuwice ne lipaziya, wulicitize lilyo Lisanduku.
4 Hanyuma wuyinjize ne meza, wujelece ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose mucanyha yayo. Winjize no wuwiko we zitala, wujelece ne zitala zayo, wuwe kuzikoleleza.
5 Naco cikumbicilo ce zahabu co kufusiza wuvumba, wuciwice mu wulunji we Lisanduku lye Cilagane, Wuwe kulitumbika ne lipaziya lya ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile.
6 Hanyuma yayo, wusole cikumbicilo ce cilavywa cinyanywe, naco wuciwice behi no mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile.
7 Wucisole ne ciya co kwizuguziza, wucimemye ne malenga naco wuciwice haciligati ye Lihema lyono lyelile ne cikumbicilo cico.
8 Hanyuma, wuwe kulipilimya Lihema ne luwa yayo, wuwe kulitumbika ne lipaziya ha mulango we luwa.

9 “Hanyuma, wuwe kulisiwanula no kulyeza Lihema lyono lyelile, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose, mu nzila yo kuvibaka vyose mafuta go witile. Nalyo lyowa lyelile.
10 Kanyhi wucisiwanule no kuceza cikumbicilo ce cilavywa cinyanywe, mu nzila yiyo yo kucibaka mafuta go witile, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vyayo vyose, naco cowa celile muno.
11 Sunga ne ciya co kwizuguziza, naco wucisiwanule no kuceza mu nzila yiyo du.

12 “Hanyuma ya zizo, ale wumuceme Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, waze weme ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile, wuwe kuwazuguza.
13 Wuwe kumuvwika Haluni vivilya vivwalo vyono vyelile, wuwe kumwitila mafuta go witile, wuwe kumusiwanula, ho yasume kunyitumamila mu mulimo wo wukohani.
14 Wuwaceme ne wanagwe, wuwavwice nawo zi shati.
15 Hanyuma wuwetile mafuta go witile nha vyono wumunozize Haluni u sogo yawo, nawo nhavivyo ho wanyitumamile mu mulimo wo wukohani. Na mu witilwe we mafuta gago, wonozwa wenjile mu witumami we cibitilila wo wukohani mu zindelo zawo zose.”

16 Nayo Musa yakazinoza zizo zose viswanu nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza.
17 Holeka, lyali lyeza kufika lizuwa lilongozi, lyo mwezi mulongozi, wa mu mwaka we kejete, lono wali wakondya kuwuka mu yisi ya Misili, Lihema lyono lyelile, likawa kusimywa.
18 Musa yakagawicila matende gayo, yakasimya ne zimbawo zayo, yakazilunza zimbawo hamonga ne mahapa gayo, no kusimya ne masumbili gayo.
19 Yakawa kulitandalika lipaziya mucanyha ye Lihema lyono lyelile, yakawa kuligubika ne ligubiko lyalyo, nha vyono yalajilizwa no Muwaha Mulungu.
20 Hanyuma, yakawa kuvisola vivilya vibawo vyejete vye mabwe, navyo yakavitajila mugati ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane. Yakagatunjila ne mabici go kulidinghulila Lisanduku lye Cilagane mu zi nyamwidole zalyo, yakawa kulijeleka ligoda lyo Lusungu mucanyha ye Lisanduku.
21 Hanyuma, ho yakawa kulinjiza Lisanduku lye Cilagane mugati ye Lihema, yakawa kutumbika ne lipaziya. Na mu nzila yiyo, yakalicitiza Lisanduku lye Cilagane, nha vyono yalajilizwa no Muwaha Mulungu.

22 Yakayiwika nayo meza mugati ye Lihema lyono lyelile, yakayiwika ku lubavu lwe sumuka, kunze ye lipaziya.
23 Na mucanyha yayo, yakawa kuyijeleka viswanu mikate yono milavywe kuli Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono yalajilizwa no Muwaha Mulungu.
24 Yakawinjiza wuwiko we zitala mugati ye Lihema lyono lyelile, yakawuwika ku lubavu lwa kutakama, vidomile ne meza.
25 Yakawa kuzikoleleza zitala zayo hali Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono yalajilizwa no Muwaha Mulungu.
26 Yakaciwika ne cikumbicilo ce zahabu mugati ye Lihema, behi ne lipaziya.
27 Yakawa kuwufusa mumo wuvumba we mhunjile swanu, nha vyono yalajilizwa no Muwaha Mulungu.
28 Yakawicila lipaziya ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile.
29 Na ha mwande ye lipaziya lilyo, yakaciwika cikumbicilo ce cilavywa cinyanywe, yakalavya mumo cilavywa cinyanywe ne cilavywa co wusaje, nha vyono yalajilizwa no Muwaha Mulungu.
30 Hanyuma, yakaciwika ciya co kwizuguziza haciligati ye Lihema lyono lyelile ne cikumbicilo. Yakagatajila ne malenga mu ciya co kwizuguziza.
31 Nayo Musa na Haluni hamonga ne wana wa Haluni, wose wakezuguza miwoko yawo hamonga ne migulu yawo mumo.
32 Na mu mapango gose, lono wenjilaga Mwi'hema, hamba lono wasejelelaga ha cikumbicilo, wamulaga wezuguza, hanyuma ho wenjila, nha vyono Musa yalajilizwa no Muwaha Mulungu.
33 Na ha ndumilizo, nhondo Musa yakawa kuyinoza luwa, yakalipilimya Lihema lyono lyelile hamonga ne cikumbicilo, yakawa litumbika ne lipaziya ha mulango we luwa. Nhondo, baho Musa ho yakawa yayikondeleza milimo yose.

Livunde, hacanyha ye Lihema lyono lyelile

(Wuw 9:15-23)

34 Hanyuma, livunde likaligubika Lihema lyono lyelile, ne nhojo yo Muwaha Mulungu, yikalimemya Lihema.
35 Sunga na Musa hamba si yasuma yaye ku winjila mwi 'Hema lyono lyelile, hambi livunde lilyo, nha lyali likaye hacanyha ye Lihema, ne nhojo yo Muwaha Mulungu, yali yimemile mumo.
36 Nawo wanhu wa Isilayeli watyatyaga livunde lilyo. Nalyo kolo likuwuka hacanyha ye Lihema, nawo wawukaga mu nghambi yiyo, wawa kutuluka no kujendelela mu muhinzo wawo. Nawo wakawa wakunoza nhavivyo du mu mihinzo yawo yose.
37 Na mu mapango gose, lono livunde lilyo lyowa likali lili hacanyha ye Lihema, nawo hamba si wawukaga mu nghambi yiyo.
38 Na mu mihinzo yawo yose i wanhu wa Isilayeli, wasumaga kuliwona livunde lilyo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, hacanyha ye Lihema lyono lyelile ku misi. Na ku cilo, moto wakaga mwi'vunde lilyo.
