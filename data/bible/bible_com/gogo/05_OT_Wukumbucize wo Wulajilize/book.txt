Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 1

1 Mu citabu cici, mo mozili zinghani zono Musa yawalonjela wanhu wa Isilayeli lono wali wali mu nyika, kucilima ye likolongo lya Yoludani. Lulo, wali wali mwi'lolo lya Yoludani behi na Sufi, haciligati ye likaya lya Palani. Makaya ga Tofeli, Labani, Hazeloti, na Dizahabu, gali gali ku lubavu luyagwe.
2 (Nawo wali muhinzo wa vilo yikumi na cimonga, ku wulawa he 'tunda lya Holebu lyono licemwaga Sinayi, sunga ku Kadeshi Balineya, mu nzila yono yikolile mu Edomu mu yisi ye matunda.)
3 Na mwi'zuwa lilongozi lya mu mwezi we likumi na monga, lya mu mwaka we makumi mane, lono wali wawuka ku Misili, Musa yawaloloseza wanhu, zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza yawalonjele.
4 Na zizi zikejela lono Muwaha Mulungu yali yakondya kumusuma Sihoni, u mutemi we Waamoli, mono yali yatemile mu makaya ga Heshiboni. Na Ogu, u mutemi wa Bashani, mono nayo, yali yatemile mu makaya ga Ashitaloti ku Edileyi.
5 Lono wanhu wali wali ku lubavu lwa kucilima ya Yoludani, mu yisi ya Mowabu, mwizuwa lilyo, Musa ho yakawa kwanza kulolosa zo wulajilize wo Mulungu hamonga ne mejizo.

Yakawa kutya,
6 “Lono cali cili he 'tunda lya Holebu lyono licemwaga Sinayi, u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, yacilonjela yakatya, ‘Mwekala vyeza vilo vinji bahano he 'tunda lili.
7 Alu mwinukaje muwuce baha, mujendelele no muhinzo wenyu. Mubite sunga ko ku yisi ye Waamoli, yisi ye matunda, na mu zi'si zose zono zipilime, ko kwi'lolo lya Yoludani, na ku yisi ye matunda, ne zi'si za mu wuhihi, sunga na ku zi'si zono zili kutakama, mu mwalambalo we lilenga lya Meditelaneya. Mujendelele mubite ko ku yisi ya Kanani, ko ku mwande ye matunda ga Lebanoni, ko kwi' kolongo liwaha lya Efulata.
8 Na mumo mose, yo yisi yono nehuwiza ane no Muwaha Mulungu, vyono nowapela walezi wenyu, Abulahamu na Isaka, na Yakobo, sunga ne zindelo zawo. Holeka mubite mukayihale yiwe yenyu.’ ”

Musa yakuwawika wahakimu

(Wut 18:13-27)

9 Musa yakawalonjela wanhu yakatya, “Lono cali cikali he 'tunda lya Sinayi, namulonjelenyi nghatya nhavi, ‘Mulimo wo wulongola, wono ane nghuwituma, wo kumulongozenyi anye, alu weza mulimo mukamu muno kuli'ne. Hamba si nosuma kujendelela kuwituma nili wene yaye.
10 Muwaha Mulungu kamwinjifyenyi muwa wenji muno muno, nhe zinyelezi za ku wulanga.
11 Alu, Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wenyu, yamwinjifyenyi kali elufu monga kuciza, kanyhi kamumotyenyi, nha vyono yehuwiza!
12 Ninga alu ane, ho nosuma nhawule nili wene, kuwituma mulimo muzito wuwu we litunya lyenyu, no mutegulo wenyu, hamonga no wugombane wenyu?
13 Holeka nghamutyenyi vyono, ciba muhagule wanhu walume we mahala no wizuce, wono wena no wuzowele wo kulongoza, walawe mu milango yenyu yose, nane nowawika wawe walanjilizi wenyu.’
14 Nanye mukavumila vyono, nghani yiyi yowa swanu hono yitumwe.
15 Holeka nane nghasola i wanhu we mahala, kanyhi wono wena wuzowele wo kulongoza wanhu, nanye nyo weneco, yo nyenye mono mwawahagula mu wulawa mu milango yenyu. Nane nghawa kuwawika wawe walanjilizi wenyu. Wanji wakawa walanjilizi we wanhu elufu monga monga, na wanji wakawa we ligana limonga limonga, na wanji wakawa we makumi mahano mahano, na wanji wakawa we wanhu yikumi yikumi. Hamba nhavivyo, kanyhi nghawawika ne walongola wanji wa mu wulawa mu milango yenyu yose.

16 “Lulo, nghawalajiliza walongola wenyu wawo nghatya, ‘Ale, mweze kuvihuliciza vyalo vye wanhu wenyu. Ninga mweze kulamula ling'ani, hamba ciwe calo cili muli wanhu wenyu nyenye, hamba ciwe calo cili mu wajenzi wono mwikalane nawo.
17 Mulanjilile mulece kulamula ko wuhijilambejele, langa mweze kuwalamula wanhu wose ko muling'ano, mulece kulanga vyono ayu nani, hamba ayo nani. Mulece kumogopa munhu wowose, hambi u wulamuzi wono mowa kuwunoza, nha wukulawa kuli Mulungu. Ne nghani yoyose yono moyiwona nghamu kuli'nye, mundetelaje nene, nane no mweneco noyilamula.’
18 Nane lulo nghamulajilizenyi zose zono zali zinojeye anye muzitume.”

Watandizi wakutumwa ku wulawa Kadeshi-balineya

(Wuw 13:1-33)

19 “Nase ciketuma nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, yali yacilajilize. Cikawuka he 'tunda lya Holebu lyono likucemwa Sinayi, cikajendelela no muhinzo wetu, cikakolela mu nyika yiyilya mbaha yo kogofya, cikajendelela, sunga cikafika mu yisi ye matunda yisi ye Waamoli. Nase cali ceza kufika ha Kadeshi-balineya, nghamulonjelenyi nghatya,
20 ‘Alu mwakondya kwinjila mu yisi ye matunda, yisi ye Waamoli, yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, u Mulungu we walezi zetu yakucipela.
21 Alu mulanje! Nayo yisi yo ayilya ahalya! Alu, mwinjile mukayihale, hambi nha vyo yalajiliza Muwaha Mulungu. Holeka mulece kogopa, hamba kuwa na wukangamyanje yaye.’

22 “Ninga anye mwanyiziza, mukanonjela mukatya, ‘Alu, wucilekaje camule citume wanhu, alongole wakayitandile yisi. Nawo kolo wakuwuya wacilonjele nzila swanu yono yowa yinojeye ciyikolele, kanyhi wacimanyize no wuwe we makaya ga kuko.’

23 “Ne nghani yiyo, yikejela vyono yowa swanu ku witume. Holeka nane nghawa kuwahagula wanhu yikumi na wejete, munhu monga monga mu wulawa mu milango yenyu yose.
24 Cikawa kuwatuma, wakawa kubita sunga ko ku yisi ye matunda, wakafika ko kwi'lolo lya Eshikoli, wakayitandila yisi mumo mose.
25 Sunga na lono wawuyaga, wakaciletela ne mikalo yono wayisola kuko. Kanyhi wakaciloloseza vyono, yisi yiyo yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu yacipelaga, yali yina moso muno muno.

26 “Ninga hamba nhavivyo, anye mukabeza lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, na ku soko yiyo holeka si mwasuma kwinjila mu yisi yiyo.
27 Kanyhi mukawa kwing'ung'ulicila nyenye weneco wose mukutya, ‘Muwaha Mulungu yacisula muno. Holeka yaciwusa ku Misili, yacilete mu miwoko ye wawa Waamoli, ho waciwulaje.
28 Alu cosuma wule kanyhi kubita kuko? Aa! Ase langa cikogopa. Hambi i wanhu wono cali ciwatumile kuko, nha wacilonjela vyono, i wanhu wono wali kuko, wali vilungo muno, kanyhi watali muno kuciza ase, hamonga na zizo, wekaye mu makaya mawaha, gono gapilimwe ne majinji matali, gono gaficile ko ku wulanga. Kanyhi wawawona ne Waanaki wali kuko!’

29 “Ninga nane nghatya, ‘Mulece kuwogopa wanhu wawo.
30 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha yo komutonjenyi, kanyhi yo komulewelenyi, nha vyono mwaziwona zono yazinoza ku Misili,
31 sunga na mu nyika. Kanyhi na vyono yamujendyenyi mu nzila yose mupolile, sunga yakamufisenyi hanhu baha, yakamudinghulenyi nha vyono sogo yo munhu kadinghulaga u mwanagwe we cilume.’
32 Ninga hamba niwe nazilonga zizo zose, anye hamba si mwamwihuwila Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.
33 Hamba yawe yo mweneco, mono yamulongolelenyi ku mwande mu nzila, sunga no kumulajililenyi i hanhu, hono mwali munojeye musite nghambi yenyu. Yakamulajilenyi nzila, mu nzila ye lisumbili lyo moto, lyono lyali lilongoye ku mwande yenyu ku cilo na kanyhi mu nzila ye lisumbili lye livunde ku misi.”

U Muwaha Mulungu yakuwalipiciza Waisilayeli

(Wuw 14:20-45)

34 “Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakawuhulika wing'ung'unze wenyu, yakakolwa likuwo, yakawa kutya,
35 ‘Alu hodu, hasina hamba monga wa muli nyenye nyo lulelo lubi, mono kosuma kwinjila mumo mu yisi yo moso, yono nehuwiza vyono nowapela i walezi wenyu.
36 Langa hono si Kalebi du u mwana wa Yefune, yuyo yo kokwinjila. Hambi nha yo mweneco yali wene du, mono yasigaye mwihuwilwa kuli'ne. Holeka nane nomupela yisi yiyi yono yayitandila, u mwene ne zindelo zakwe.’
37 Na ku soko yenyu, holeka Muwaha Mulungu, yakangholelwa likuwo sunga na nene, yakawa kutya, ‘Hamba na gwegwe Musa, si kokwinjilila lukulu mu yisi yiyo.
38 Holeka wumusindamikaje mutima Yoshuwa u mwana wa Nuni, hambi nha yo kolowahaza Waisilayeli yisi yiyo.’

39 “Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu yacilonjela yakatya, ‘Ne wana wenyu wono wakali wadodo, wono wakali namanya ku wuhagula zimbi ne ziswanu, awo langa wo wolokwinjila mu yisi yiyo, i wana wono anye mwatya vyono, wowa mateka we walugu wenyu. Nane lu wawo, wo nowahaza yisi yiyo.
40 Ninga anye, luno, mupituce, muwuye ne nyuma, mubite sunga ko ku nyika, mukolele balabala yono yibitile sunga kwi'lenga lya Shamu.’

41 “Ninga nanye mwamhitula mukanyitya, ‘Gwe Musa, ase camusutizya Muwaha Mulungu, holeka alu cobita cihome, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, yacilajilize.’ Na mbeka wose muli nyenye mukewikaniza, akuno mukwihwesa nha vyono, kowa kupelu ku wusola yisi ye matunda.

42 “Ninga nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakanyitya, ‘Wuwalonjele walece kwijelela kubita ku wihomi, hambi ane nha si nowa nili hamonga nawo. Na ku soko yiyo, walugu wawo wowasuma.’
43 Nane nghamulonjelenyi zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yazilonga, ninga nanye hamba si mwazihulika yaye, langa mukamubedegala. Na so wiwoni wenyu, mukawa kubita ku yisi ye matunda.
44 Na mbeka, i Waamoli wono wekalaga mumo mu matunda, wakamuzizenyi ko winji muno, kotya nzuci, wakamuwinjenyi, wakamufisenyi sunga ko ku Holima, wakamusumilizenyi mumo mu yisi ye matunda ya Edomu.
45 Nanye mukamulilila Muwaha Mulungu yamupelenyi wutazi. Ninga nayo, hamba si yakamuhulicenyi ng'o, hamba kumucelelelenyi kose.”

Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 2

1 “Hanyuma nhondo ho cikawa kupituka, cikawa kubita ku nyika, cikakolela balabala yono yibitile sunga ko kwi'lenga lya Shamu, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yali yalajilize. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, ciketumamila vilo vinji muno muno cikupilima pilima mu yisi ye matunda ya Edomu.

2 “Hanyuma Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela vyono,
3 cipango lu camalika co wupilime pilime wa mu matunda gago, na vyono aluno, yicinojeye cijendelele no muhinzo wetu, ciwe cidomile ku lubavu lwe sumuka.
4 Nayo yakandajiliza nimulonjelenyi nha vyono, mokolela mu yisi ye matunda ya Edomu, yisi ye wandugu zenyu, i wana wa Esawu, nawo womogopenyi muno.
5 Ninga anye mwilanjililaje, mulecele lukulu kwihoma nawo. Hambi ane, nha si nomupelenyi yaye, hamba hanji ho hose mu yisi yawo, hambi yisi ya Edomu nha niwapeye we ndelo ya Esawu.
6 Ninga anye mosuma kugula ndigwa hamba malenga kuli wene.

7 “Ninga mukumbucile vyono Muwaha Mulungu u Mulungu wenyu yamulugutizenyi mu milimo yenyu yose, yono mwakondya kwituma. Kanyhi na vyono yamukamisenyi lono mwapilimagapilimaga mu nyika yiyi. Na vyono yali yali hamonga nanye mu myaka yiyi makumi mane, kanyhi yakamupelenyi na vyose vyono mwasakaga.

8 “Nase cikajendelela no muhinzo wetu, cikayileka balabala yono yibitile mu makaya ga Elati na Eziyoni-gebeli sunga ko kwi'lenga lyo mwino. Cikawa kubita ku lubavu lwe sumuka ye cilima, cilanjile ku Mowabu.
9 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakutya, ‘Mulece kuwagaza i wanhu wa Mowabu, i wana wa Loti, hamba mulece kwihoma nawo. Hambi wene nha nakondya kuwapela likaya lya Ali liwe lyawo. Nanye hamba si nomupelenyi wuhazi wo wose mu yisi yawo yiyo.’ ”

10 (Katali i Wemimu, wanhu watali we cangamo, wenha Waanaki, wawo wekalaga mumo mu Ali.
11 Wawo hanji kanyhi, watezagwa Walefayi, wenha Waanaki. Nawo Wamowabi wawacemaga Wemimu.
12 Katali, i Waholi nawo, wekalaga mu yisi ye Waedomu, ninga i wana wa Esawu wakawasuma, wakawawinga mu yisi yiyo, wakalyongomezeza lukulu ne likungugo lyawo, wene wakawa kwikala mumo, yikawa nha vyono, Waisilayeli wawasuma no kuwawinga walugu wawo, mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu yawapela.)

13 “Hanyuma, cikawa kulilowoka likolongo lya Zeledi, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yacilajiliza.
14 Na mwi'pango lili, yali yakondya kuficila myaka makumi madatu na munana, ku wulawa lono cawucila ku Kadeshi-balineya. Ne vitung'ati wo wulugu wo lulelo lulo, wose wali wakondya kufwa, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalonga, vyono wose wolowa wafwa.
15 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakajendelela kwisumaniza nawo du, sunga yakawongomezeza lukulu wose.

16 “Nawo wali weza kuwa wakondya kufwa wose,
17 baho Muwaha Mulungu yakacilonjela ase yakatya,
18 ‘Izuwali, lyo lizuwa lyono linojeye mukolele mu yisi ya Mowabu, ninga mukole mu nzila yono yibitile ku Ali.
19 Holeka si mowa muli kutali ne yisi ye Waamoni, i wana wa Loti. Ninga anye mwilanjililaje, mulecele lukulu kwihoma nawo. Hambi ane nha si nomupelenyi yaye hamba hanji hadodo mu yisi yawo, yisi yono nakondya kuwapela wene.’ ”

20 (Ne yisi yiyo yikawa yimanyice kanyhi, nha vyono yo yisi ye Walefayi, i litagwa lye wanhu wono wekalaga mumo katali. Ninga Waamoni wawacemaga Wazamuzumimu.
21 Nawo wali watali wehwanile Waanaki. Wali wenji muno, kanyhi watali, wali cangamo, kanyhi lyali yikungugo lyono lina vilungo muno. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, Muwaha Mulungu yakawongomeza wose, i Waamoni ho wayisole yisi yawo no kwikala mumo.
22 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakawanozeza nhavivyo sunga ne Waedomu i wana wa Esawu, wono wekaye mu yisi ye matunda ya Edomu. Muwaha Mulungu yakawongomeza Waholi, i Waedomu ho wayisole yisi yawo no kwikala mumo, nawo wakali wekaye mumo sunga nalu.
23 Nayo yisi yono yali yalambaye lilenga lya Meditelaneya, i wanhu wono walawa ku cizingwa ca Kupulo wo wakekala mumo. Wawo wo wawongomeza i Wavi, i wekazi wa mumo ku wulawa na katali, wakayisola yisi yawo ku wulawilila kutakama, sunga ko kwi' kaya lya Gaza.)

24 “Nase cali ceza kukondya kupuluta mu yisi ya Mowabu Muwaha Mulungu yakacilonjela yakutya, ‘Mwinukaje, mulowoce likolongo lya Alinoni. Nane nghumupelenyi Sihoni, u mutemi we Waamoli, mu miwoko yenyu, ne yisi yakwe yose. Holeka mumubitilaje mumuhome muyisole ne yisi yakwe.
25 Nane ku wulawilila izuwali, nowanoza wanhu, hanhu hose, wamogopenyi nyenye. Kanyhi wose wocicima ko woga wuwaha muno, kolo wakulihulika litagwa lyenyu likutambulwa.’ ”

Waisilayeli wakumusumiza u mutemi Sihoni

(Wuw 21:21-30)

26 “Nane ku wulawilila ku nyika ya Kedemoti, nghatuma wanhu kuli mutemi Sihoni, wakamulonjele watye,
27 ‘Cikukulomba wucikombole cikolele mu yisi yako. Nase cowa cityatizye balabala du, hamba si cotemanuka ciyilece balabala yaye.
28 Nase cozilipila zindigwa zono colya, ne malenga gono cong'wa. Ase cikukulomba nghani monga hodu, nha cinji cikusaka cikolele mu yisi yako du,
29 sunga cilowoce likolongo lya Yoludani, cinjile mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu yakucipela. I wana wa Esawu wono wekaye mu yisi ya Edomu, ne Wamowabi wono wekaye mu yisi ya Ali, wene wacikombola cikolele mu zi'si zawo.’

30 “Ninga, u mutemi Sihoni hamba si yavumila yaye yacikombole cikolele mu yisi yakwe. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha yali yamunoza yawe yasina cihuliko, kanyhi yawe mufyuzi, ase ho cimusume, ciyisole ne yisi yakwe yono cikali ciyikaye sunga nalu.

31 “Hanyuma, Muwaha Mulungu kanyhi yakanyitya, ‘Alu wulanje! Nakondya kumunoza mutemi Sihoni ne yisi yakwe, yawe yasina vilungo hali'gwe. Holeka winjile wuyisole yisi yakwe, yiwe yisi yako.’
32 Nayo mutemi Sihoni ne wanhu wakwe, wakekonghola ho wehome nase behi ne likaya lya Yahazi.
33 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, yakacikombolela Sihoni mu miwoko yetu, nase cikamuwulaga, ne wanagwe we cilume wose, hamonga ne wanhu wakwe wose.
34 Na mwi'pango lilyo du, cikagateka no kugongomeza makaya gose, kanyhi cikawawulaga wanhu wose i walume ne wacekulu, ne wana wawo wose. Nase si camusigaza munhu hamba monga mono yekala mumi.
35 Cikazisola mitugo zose, kanyhi cikagateka ne makaya gose.
36 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, yakacipela wusume, cikagateka makaya gose ku wulawilila ku Aloweli, mu mwejelele we lilolo lya Alinoni, sunga ko kwi 'kaya lyono lyali lili haciligati ye lilolo, sunga ko ku Gileyadi. Kanyhi hakawa hasina yikaya lyo lyose, lyono likawa na majinji gono gakamile muno, gono gali gacizize vilungo vyetu, lyono lyacifutila.
37 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, si casuma kubita behi ne yisi ye Waamoni, hamba ku nyenje ye likolongo lya Yaboki, hamba mu makaya ga mu matunda, hamba hanhu hanji ho hose, hono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, yacilajiliza cilece kubita.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 3

Waisilayeli wakumusuma Mutemi Ogu

(Wuw 21:31-35)

1 “Hanyuma kanyhi, cikawa kutuluka cilanjile ku lubavu lwe sumuka, mu nzila yono yibitile ku Bashani. Nayo Mutemi Ogu yakawa kuza mwene ne wanhu wakwe wose, ho wacihome behi ne likaya lya Edileyi.
2 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu, yakanonjela yakatya, ‘Wulecele lukulu kumogopa mutemi Ogu, hambi ane nha nokukombolela mwene mu miwoko yako, hamonga ne wanhu wakwe wose, ne yisi yakwe. Nagwe wumutendelaje nha vyono wamutendela mutemi Sihoni u mutemi we Waamoli mono yali yayitemile Heshiboni.’

3 “Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakacikombolela Ogu mu miwoko yetu, mwene ne wanhu wakwe wose, nase cikawawulaga wose wose.
4 Na mwi'pango lilyo, cikagateka makaya gakwe gose, hakawa hasina hamba likaya limonga lyono si caliteka. Na muli makaya gose gono cagasola, makaya makumi mutandatu, gali ga mwi'tumbi lya Aligobu, hono yali yatemile Ogu u mutemi wa Bashani.
5 Ne makaya gago gose gali mazenjele majinji matali muno ge magomawakali, gono gali gena ne malango gayo, ne makomeyo go kudindila malango. Ninga kanyhi, hakawa ne vikaya vinji vyono vyali visina majinji matali ge magomawakali.
6 Ninga nase cikagongomeza makaya gose gose, kanyhi cikawawulaga ne wanhu wose walume, hamonga ne wacekulu, ne wana wawo. Cikanoza nha vyono caganozeza makaya gono yali yagatemile mutemi Sihoni wa ku Heshiboni.
7 Cikazisola mitugo zose, cikagateka ne makaya gose.

8 “Na mwi'pango lilyo du, cikayisola yisi ya ku lubavu lwa kucilima ye likolongo lya Yoludani, mu wulawa mu wawo watemi wejete we Waamoli, ku wulawilila likolongo lya Alinoni sunga ko kwi'tunda lya Helemoni.
9 (Litunda lye Helemoni, i wanhu wa Sidoni, walitezaga Siliyoni. Nawo Waamoli walitezaga Senili.)
10 Cikayisola yisi yose ya Ogu mutemi wa Bashani. I makaya gono gali gali mu yisi yo wuhihi ya mu matunda, ne matumbi ga Gileyadi na ga Bashani, sunga ko ku cilima mu makaya ga Saleka na Edileyi.”

11 (U mutemi Ogu, yali yo mutemi we ndumilizo yali wene muli Walefayi. Ne citanda cakwe cono wamuzicila, cali ca cuma. No wutali waco wali mita yine, no wugazi waco mita nyejete. Naco sunga nalu, cidahile kwijela kuko kwi' kaya lye Waamoni lyono likucemwa Laba.)

We milango wono wekala ku cilima ye likolongo lya Yoludani

(Wuw 32:1-42)

12 “Nase cali ceza kukondya kuvisola vinhu vyose vya mu yisi, nghawa kuwagawila wo mulango wa Lubeni na wa Gadi, yisi yo lubavu lwa kusumuka, likaya lya Aloweli behi ne likolongo lya Alinoni, ne nusu ye yisi ye matunda ya Gileyadi hamonga ne vitumbi vyayo.
13 Nawo wo mulango wa Manase, nghawapela nusu yono yali yisigaye, ye yisi ya Gileyadi na Bashani, i hanhu hono yali yatemile u mutemi Ogu, nha cinji litumbi lyose lya Aligobu. (Nayo Bashani, yali yimanyice nha yisi ye Walefayi.
14 Nayo Yayili, u munhu wo mulango wa Manase, yakalisola litumbi lilyo lya Aligobu, sunga ne Bashani yose, sunga ko ku mupaka wa Geshuli na Maka. Mwene Yayili, yakavicemela vitumbi vyose litagwa lyakwe mweneco. Navyo sunga nalu, vikali vikucemwa vitumbi vya Yayili.)

15 “Nghayisola Gileyadi nayo, nghawapela wanhu we mhuga ya Macili wo mulango wa Manase.
16 Nawo i wanhu wo mulango wa Lubeni na wa Gadi, awo nghawapela yisi yo ku wulawilila ku Gileyadi, sunga ko kwi' kolongo lya Alinoni. Na haciligati ye likolongo, wali wo mupaka wawo ku lubavu lwa kutakama. Na ku lubavu lwa ku sumuka nako, mupaka wukawa likolongo lya Yaboki. Kanyhi wali wo mupaka we Waamoni.
17 Na ku lubavu lwe mwezi, yisi yawo, yali yibitile sunga ko kwi'kolongo lya Yoludani, ku wulawa kwi 'lamba lya Galilaya, ku lubavu lwe sumuka, sunga ko kwi'lenga lyo Mwino, ku lubavu lwa kutakama, sunga mo mu mwalambalo we litunda lya Pisiga ku lubavu lwe cilima ye litunda.

18 “Na mwi'pango lilyo du, nghawa kuwapela wulanjize nghawatya, ‘Alu, mulanje ta! U Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, yakondya kumupelenyi anye yisi yiyi, ya kono lubavu lwa kucilima ye likolongo lya Yoludani, yiwe yenyu nyenye. Aluno muwawikanizaje vitung'ati wenyu wo wulugu, mulowoce likolongo lya Yoludani, mulongole ku mwande ye wandugu zenyu Waisilayeli ho muwatazilize nawo wayisole yisi yawo.
19 Ninga i wagolece zenyu, ne wana wenyu, awo wosigala du mu makaya genyu gono nakondya kumupelenyi, hamonga ne mitugo zenyu zose.
20 Nanye muwatazilize Waisilayeli wayenyu, sunga wayisole yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu yakuwapela, yisi yo lubavu lwa ku mwezi ye likolongo lya Yoludani. Sunga kolo Muwaha Mulungu yakondya kuwapela mulamu mu wikalo wawo, nha vyono yakondya kumunozezenyi nyenye ahano. Hanyuma ya zizo zose, nanye ho mowuya mono yisi yenyu yiyi, yono nakondya kumupelenyi.’

21 “Hanyuma nghawa kumupela Yoshuwa nayo wulanjize awuno, nghamutya, ‘Nagwe Yoshuwa, si kotya wakondya kuziwona zose zono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, yawatendela i watemi wawalya wejete, Sihoni na Ogu. Holeka Mulungu vyo kowatendela nhavivyo wanhu wose we zi'si zose zono mokwinjila.
22 Holeka mulecele lukulu kuwogopa, hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha yo komulewelenyi.’

Musa si yakukombolwa kwinjila mu yisi ya Kanani

23 “Nane mwipango lililya, nghawa kumulomba Muwaha Mulungu ko winenenyhezi nghatya,
24 ‘Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu mono wutemile vyose, ane manyile manyi manyi, nha vyono wakondya kundajila u mwamulizo we zimbaha na zo wizine, zono kozituma. Hambi mbeka nha hasina mulungu yunji wo wose hono yinze hamba ku wulanga, mono kosuma kwituma metumo gago ge vilungo viwaha gono wagetuma gwegwe!
25 Holeka nghukunena, wundekaje le sunga ndowoce likolongo lya Yoludani, Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, ho sume kuyiwona yisi yono yina moso, kuko ku nhumbi nzagwe, yisi yiyo swanu muno ye matunda, hamonga ne Matunda ga Lebanoni.’

26 “Ninga ku soko ya anye nyo wanhu, u Muwaha Mulungu yakangholelwa likuwo, hamba si yenda ho yahulicize 'ne kanyhi. Ninga yamhitula yakatya, ‘Hodu, zizo zakondya! Wulecele lukulu kuzitambula zizo kanyhi!
27 Langa aluno, wubite kucanyha ye litunda lya Pisiga sunga ko ku cizuli cayo. Na ku wulawilila kuko ku cizuli, ale, wutegule meso gako wulanje ku lubavu lwe sumuka, ne takama, ne cilima, sunga no lubavu lwe mwezi. Nagwe wuzilanjilaje lukulu zose zono koziwona, vyono agwe si kolowocela lukulu likolongo lya Yoludani.
28 Holeka wumulajilize Yoshuwa, kanyhi wumusindamice no kumusikamya, hambi mwene nha yo kowalongoza wanhu walowoce likolongo, wabite wakayisole yisi yiyo yono koyiwona.’
29 Ale, cikawa kwikala mwi'lolo lyono vidomile ne likaya lya Betipeyoli.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 4

Musa yakuwasaka Waisilayeli wawe ne Cihuliko

1 Hanyuma Musa yakawalonjela wanhu yakatya, “Mweze muli cihuliko kuli zo wulajilize wono nghumwijizenyi, nanye holeka mowumipa, sunga muyisole ne yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wenyu, yakumupelenyi.
2 Ninga mulece konjeleza hamba kucepula muli zose zono nghumulajilizenyi. Langa mweze ne cihuliko du kuli malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, gono nakondya kumulonjelenyi.
3 Hambi nha mwewonelela nyenye nyo weneco, zono Muwaha Mulungu yazituma he 'tunda lya Peyoli. Hambi nha yawongomezeza lukulu wose wono wahembaga Baali.
4 Ninga awalya wono wali wehuwilwa kuli Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, awo wakali wali cawumi sunga nalu.

5 “Nane nha nakondya kumwijizenyi ze malajilizo gose, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wangu yanonjela nitume. Holeka mweze ne cihuliko kuli malajilizo gago, mumo mu yisi yono mokwinjila no kuyisola yiwe yenyu.
6 Mugakamisaje ko wihuwilwa. Na mu nzila yiyo, i wanhu we makungugo ganji, mumo mo wowonela vyono anye muli wanhu we mahala no wizuce. Nawo kolo wakuzihulika zose ze malajilizo gaga, wotya, ‘Mbeka nghungugo yiyi mbaha, wo wanhu wono wena mahala no wizuce!’

7 “Na hasina likungugo linji lyolyose, hamba liwe yikungugo yiwaha nha vyono lyowa linha, lyono lyowa lina mulungu mono yali behi nawo, lono wakuhila cinji co cose kuli mwene. Ninga ase, Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, yali hamonga nase. Kanyhi kacipitulaga wulombe wetu lono cikumulomba wutazi wakwe.
8 Na kanyhi, hasina likungugo linji lyo lyose, hamba liwe yikungugo yiwaha nha vyono lyowa linha, lyono lyowa lina wulajilize we ling'ani, wono wihwanile no wulajilize wono nakondya kumwijizenyi anye izuwali.
9 Holeka mwiyihenje nyo weneco! Kanyhi mwilanjilile mulece kuzisemwa zose zono muziwene ne meso genyu, mu vilo vyenyu vyose vyo wikale wenyu. Kanyhi munojeye muwalonjele zizi, wana wenyu, ne wezukulu zenyu,
10 ze lizuwa lyono anye mwema ha mwande yo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, he'tunda lya Sinayi, lono yanonjelela ane Musa, yakunyitya, ‘Wakongholelaje wanhu hamonga, hambi nha nghusaka wahulice zono nghuhila kulonga, ho weyijize kuwa ne cihuliko kuli'ne, mu vilo vyawo vyose vyo wikale wawo. Kanyhi ho wawejize ne wana wawo, nawo ho watyatye luzu lwawo.’

11 “Kanyhi muwalonjele wana wenyu na vyono mukabita mukema ha mwalambalo we litunda, lyono lyali yigubicizwe ne mavunde matitu tuku, gono galawaga lyosi yititu tuku, hamonga ne malambi go moto, gaficile ko ku wulanga.
12 Kanyhi muwalonjele na vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalonganila nanye mu moto, na vyono nanye mwalihulika lizi lyakwe du, ninga si wamuwona yaye mumbwa yakwe vyono yenha.
13 Kanyhi na vyono yamulonjelenyi nha vyono munojeye mucikamise cilagane cakwe cono yacinoza nanye. Na vyono munojeye mugakamise i malajilizo gakwe yikumi, gono yagandika mweneco mucanyha ye vibawo vyejete vye mabwe.
14 Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu, yanonjela vyono yinojeye nimwijizenyi wulajilize no wuling'anizi, wono munojeye muwukamise, lono muli mu yisi yono mokwinjila no kuyisola yiwe yenyu.”

Wusugulwe kuli zo wukumbika vihwani

15 “Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, lono yalonganila nanye mu wulawa mu moto he'tunda lya Sinayi, nanye hamba si mwawona mumbwa yakwe hamba futa yo yose.
16 Holeka mwiyihenje nyo weneco, kanyhi mulecele lukulu kwituma wubi so winozeza nyo weneco cihwani co cose ciseswe, ca mu futa ye cihwani co munhu mulume, hamba we cicekulu,
17 hamba cihwani ce nimu, hamba ce ndeje,
18 hamba cihwani ca cinji co cose cono citambalaga hasi, hamba somba.
19 Kanyhi mwilanjilile, mulece kukwegwa na vya ku wulanga, lizuwa, mwezi, ne zinyelezi, ho muvitumamile no kuvihemba. Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yawaleceye wanhu wanji vivyo vyose ho wavihembe.
20 Ninga anye muli wanhu mono mwawuswa ku Misili, mwi kongojelo lyono likwaka moto mukali. Yakamuletenyi, ho yamunozenyi muwe wanhu wakwe, nha vyono mwinha izuwali.
21 Na ku soko yenyu, Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakangholelwa likuwo. Yakawa kwilaha no kulonga, vyono ane, si nolowocela lukulu likolongo lya Yoludani, ninjile mu yisi yono yina moso, yisi yono yakumupelenyi 'nye.
22 Ane nofwila mono yisi yiyi du, hamba si nolowocela lukulu likolongo. Ninga anye molowoka muyisole yisi yiyo yono yina moso.
23 Holeka mwilanjilile, mulece kusemwa cilagane cono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu yacinoza nanye. Kanyhi mweze ne cihuliko kuli ze lilajilizo lyakwe, mulecele lukulu kwinozeza nyo weneco cihwani co cose ciseswe.
24 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha moto wono wuticimizaga, yo Mulungu wo wivu.

25 “Nanye kolo mwekala mu yisi vilo vinji muno, no kutuga wana ne wezukulu, mulecele lukulu kumusutizya Mulungu, so winozeza nyo weneco cihwani co cose ciseswe ca mu futa ye cihwani cinji. Hambi nghani yiyi, nha si yomunojeza Muwaha Mulungu, kanyhi yomukoza likuwo muno muno.
26 Nane nghuwutambula wulanga ne yisi, viwe wuhomelezi kuli 'nye izuwali, nha vyono, hono mwikale si muhulice 'ne, hodu, mokwajililila lukulu ku wulawa mu yisi yiyi, hamba si molokwijela kanyhi yaye. Kanyhi hamba si mosuma kwikala vilo vinji, mu yisi yiyo yono yili ku nhumbi ya Yoludani yono moyisola.
27 Muwaha Mulungu kolomwiyagazenyi, mwiyagalile mu makungugo ganji, na kuko, wayenyu wacefu du wo wolosigala wekale womi.
28 Na kuko kono molokwiyagazizwa, molowa kuhemba milungu yono minozwe ne miwoko ye wanhu, i milungu ye mabici na ye mabwe, milungu yono si yosuma kuwona hamba kuhulika hamba kulya, hamba kunusa kose yaye.
29 Na kuko nanye, molowa kumulondola Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu. Na hono muze mumulondole kwe nhumbula yose na ko mutima wose, molomuwona.
30 Nanye kolo mwenjila mu magayo, ne zinghani zizo zose zakondya kumwaganenyi, nhondo, ho molomuwuyila Muwaha Mulungu, kanyhi ho molomuhulika.
31 Mwene Muwaha Mulungu, yo Mulungu wo lusungu. Hamba si kosuma kumulecenyi yaye, hamba kumongomezenyi, hamba kucisemwa cilagane cakwe cono yacinoza mweneco ne walezi wenyu.

32 “Mujele ta kuzilanga, ze vilo vyono vyapuluta, ze vilo vyono mwali mukali nalelwa. Zinghani zono zejela katali muno muno, lono Mulungu yamuwumbila munhu mononze. Kanyhi mulanje no wuwaha we yinze vyono winha. Wulenyi! Yowa yejela nghani yinji mbaha yono yinha yiyi? Hamba ho yuli yunji muyenyu, mono kowa yayihulika nghani yono yihwanile yiyi?
33 Hamba ho hali hanji hono wanhu wahulika mulungu yakulonganila nawo mu moto, nha vyono nyenye?
34 Hamba ho yuli mulungu yunji wo wose, mono yejelela yabite yakawawuse wanhu ku wulawa mwikungugo linji, kanyhi yawanoze wawe wanhu wakwe, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu yamunozezenyi nyenye ku yisi ya Misili? Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yazinozeza zose ha meso genyu, yetumamila vilungo vyakwe viwaha hamonga no wusume. Yaleta magazo, no wulugu, yakanoza vitanjilo, ne mezino, yakaleta nghani zo wogofye wuwaha muno muno.
35 Muwaha Mulungu yazinoza zizi zose, ho yamulajilenyi vyono, hasina mulungu yunji kanyhi, langa yo mweneco du yo Mulungu yali wene.
36 Kanyhi yakamunozenyi mulihulice lizi lyakwe ku wulawa ku wulanga, ho yamwijizenyi. Kanyhi yakamunozenyi ho muwuwone moto wakwe wono welile bahano hono hasi, nayo yakawa kulongana nanye mu wulawa mu moto wuwo.
37 Nayo vyono yali yawendile i walezi wenyu, holeka yakamuhagulenyi nanye, na kwe vilungo vyakwe viwaha, yakamuwusenyi mu wulawa mu yisi ya Misili.
38 Hanyuma yakagawinga ku mwande yenyu i makungugo gono gali mawaha, kanyhi gali gali vilungo muno kumucizenyi nyenye. Ho yasume kumwinjizenyi no kumupelenyi yisi yawo, yisi yono alu yeza yenyu.
39 Holeka izuwali, mukumbucile, mulece kusemwa. Vyono Muwaha Mulungu, yo Mulungu mono yali ku wulanga sunga na mononze. Hasina mulungu yunji kanyhi wowose.
40 Holeka mukamise malajilizo gakwe gono nghumulajilizenyi izuwali. Na mu nzila yiyo, holeka zenyu zose zojendelela viswanu, hamonga ne wana wenyu. Kanyhi holeka mojendelela kwikala mononze vilo vinji, vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumupelenyi viwe vyenyu cibitilila.”

Makaya go wutizizo, ku cilima ya Yoludani

41 Hobaho Musa yakagasiwanula makaya madatu ku nhumbi ye cilima ye likolongo lya Yoludani.
42 I makaya gono munhu kosuma kutiziza mumo no kuwa yapolile, hono yawulaje munhu mhalanyuka hamba si yali yamusulile yaye. Munhu yuyo kosuma kwiponezya mwi'kaya limonga lya mu makaya gago, hamba si kowulagwa yaye.
43 Likaya lya Bezeli lyono lyali lili mu yisi yo wuhihi, likawa so wo mulango wa Lubeni. Likaya lya Lamoti lyono lyali lili mu yisi ya Gileyadi, likawa so wo mulango wa Gadi. Nalyo likaya lya Golani, lyono lyali lili mu yisi ya Bashani, likawa so wo mulango wa Manase.

U wulolosezwe we soko ye malajilizo go Mulungu

44 Musa yakawaloloseza i wanhu Waisilayeli ze malajilizo go Mulungu, hamonga ne mejizo gayo.
45 Na zizi ziketumwa lono wali wakondya kuwuka ku Misili,
46 lono wali wali mwi'lolo, ku cilima ye likolongo lya Yoludani, lyono vidomile ne cikaya ca Betipeyoli. Baho ho yawalolosezeza malajilizo gago. Mu yisi yo mutemi Sihoni, u mutemi we Waamoli, mono yali yatemile mu cikaya ca Heshiboni. Ninga Musa ne wanhu Waisilayeli wakamusuma lono wawucila ku Misili.
47 Wakayisola yisi yiyo, hamonga ne yisi yo mutemi Ogu wa Bashani, u mutemi yunji muyagwe no mutemi Sihoni, mono nayo, yali mutemi we Waamoli mono yekalaga ku lubavu lwa ku cilima ye likolongo lya Yoludani.
48 Ne yisi yiyi, yali ngazi muno, yikawa yilawiliye ku cikaya ca Aloweli ha mwejelele we likolongo lya Alinoni, yikawa yibitile sunga ko kwi'tunda lya Siliyoni, wo wutya, litunda lya Helemoni.
49 Nayo yikawa yibitile du, sunga mu yisi yose yo lubavu lwa ku cilima ye likolongo lye Yoludani, sunga ko kwi'lenga lyo Mwino kutakama, sunga ko ku cilima mu mwejelele we litunda lya Pisiga.


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 5

Malajilizo yikumi

(Wut 20:1-17)

1 Musa yakawakonghola hamonga i Waisilayeli, yakawalonjela yakatya, “Anye nyo Waisilayeli, alu nghumunenenyi, muhulicize malajilizo gono nghumulajilizenyi izuwali. Mugeyijize, sunga mumanyile lukulu nha vyono mwakondya kugakamisa gose.
2 Kanyhi mumanyile nha vyono, Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, yanoza cilagane ne walezi wetu he 'tunda lya Sinayi,
3 ninga si yacinoza cilagane cico ne walezi wetu du, langa sunga na sese wose mono cili womi izuwali.
4 Kanyhi mukumbuciye na vyono Muwaha Mulungu, yalonganila nanye ciwonelela, ku wulawilila mwi'tunda, mu wulawa muciligati yo moto.
5 Na vyono mwi'pango lilyo, ane nghema haciligati yenyu no Muwaha Mulungu, ho nimulonjelenyi zono mwene yazilongaga. Hambi anye nha mwawogopaga moto, kanyhi hamba si mwali musume kubita kwi'tunda kucanyha. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakatya,
6 ‘Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako, mono nakuwusa mu yisi ya Misili, kono wali wuli mu wuwanda.

7 Wulece kuwa na milungu yinji,

langa nene du.’

8 “Wulece kwinozeza cihwanicizo ciseswe, hamba cihwani ce cinhu co cose cono cili ku wulanga, hamba cono cili mono yisi, hamba cono cili mu malenga kusi ye yisi.
9 Wulece kuvitumamila vihwani vye milungu vye liwala lyo lyose, hamba wulece kuvihemba. Hambi ane no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako, nha nili Mulungu wo wivu, mono si nosuma kwisumaniza na yunji wo wose. Nane nghuwalipiciza wana wubi we malezi wawo, sunga ko ku lulelo lwe kadatu na lwe kane lwa wono wasulile.
10 Ninga nghuwawonela lusungu maelufu na maelufu wono wanyendile, kanyhi wagebate ne malajilizo gangu.

11 “Wulece kulitumamila litagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu wako mu zi nzila zimbi, hambi Muwaha Mulungu, nha komulipiciza munhu wo wose mono ya kulitumila litagwa lyakwe mu zinzila zimbi.
12 Weze kulikumbucila lizuwa lye Sabato wulisiwanule.
13 Mu vilo mutandatu weze kwituma no kukondelezeza lukulu milimo yako yose.
14 Ninga lizuwa lyo mupunghati yo Sabato yo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako. Holeka mwi'zuwa lilyo, wulece kwituma mulimo wunji, gwegwe, hamba mwanago we cilume hamba we cicekulu, hamba wetumami wako we cilume hamba we cicekulu, hamba mitugo yako, hamba mujenzi wako mono yali hako. I wetumami wacehe mwi'zuwa lilyo, nha vyono nagwe kowa wucehile.
15 Hambi nha yikunojeye weze kukumbucila vyono nagwe wali wuli muwanda mu yisi ya Misili, kanyhi na vyono, ane no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako, nakukombozeza mu nzila ye vilungo vyangu viwaha hamonga no wusume. Na yiyi yo yikuleka ane nghukulajiliza wulikamise lizuwa lye Sabato.

16 “Wuwogopaje sogo no nyina yako, nha vyono nghukulajiliza, ane no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako, ho wusume kutuga wumoteswe, na kanyhi ho wusume kwikala vilo vinji mono yisi yono nghukupela.

17 “ ‘Wulece kuwulaga munhu.

18 “ ‘Wulece kusenyha.

19 “ ‘Wulece kuhiza.

20 “ ‘Wulece kulavya wuhomelezi wo wulambi kuli muyago.

21 “ ‘Wulece kumusulumila mugolece wo muyago, hamba kaya yakwe, hamba mugunda wakwe, hamba mwitumami wakwe, hamba ng'ombe yakwe, hamba ndogowe yakwe, hamba cinhu co cose co muyago.’

22 “Aga go malajilizo gono yamulajilizenyi nyenye wose lono mwali mwikongholeye he'tunda. Lono yalonjela nanye kwe lizi lye vilungo mu wulawilila mu moto, na mu mavunde matitu tuku. Nayo yakamulajilizenyi malajilizo gaga du, hamba si yonjeleza kanyhi nghani zinji yaye. Hanyuma yakawa kugandika malajilizo gago mu mbawo nyejete ze mabwe, yakawa kumhela nene.”

Woga we wanhu

(Wut 20:18-21)

23 “Nanye mwali mweza kulihulika lizi likulawilila mu wutitu, lono litunda lyakaga moto, hobaho i walongola wenyu hamonga ne wawaha wenyu we milango yenyu wose, wose wakanyiziza,
24 wakanyitya, ‘Cakondya kuwona vyono, Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, kelajila u Wuwaha wakwe hamonga no lukumyo lwakwe, lono cimuhuliciye yakulonga mu wulawa mu moto! Holeka izuwali, cakondya kumanya vyono, munhu wo wose kojendelela du kuwa mumi, hamba yiwe Mulungu kakondya kulongana nayo.
25 Alu kanyhi nhawule cijendelele kwiyinjiza mwi'fwa? Hambi moto wuwo wo kogofya nha wocongomeza. Hambi nha citumbiye vyono cofwa du, hono cimuhulice Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu yakulonga nase kanyhi.
26 Alu mwene, mbeka ho yuli munhu wo wose we nghuli, mono yekala mumi mono yahulika Mulungu mumi yakulonjela mu wulawa mu moto?
27 Holeka gwe Musa, gwegwe du yo weze kumuhuliciza u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, muli zose zono kolonga. Hanyuma nagwe ho kuwa kuza kucilonjela zono mwene yakulonjeye. Nase cozihuliciza du no kuzituma.’

28 “Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yali yeza kuzihulika zizo zono mukazilonga, nayo yakanonjela yakatya, ‘Ane nazihulika zose zono wanhu wawa wakuzilonga, nane nghuwona vyono wakulonga mbeka.
29 Na hono mbeka nhondo zose wawe kwihwesa nhavivi! Na hono mbeka wawe wanyogopile, no kuwa ne cihuliko kuli'ne nhondo zose, muli ze malajilizo gangu, nawo wojendelela kuwa no wumoteswe muli zose, ne wana wawo cibitilila.
30 Holeka alu wubite wukawalonjele wawuye mu mahema gawo.
31 Ninga agwe Musa, weze wuli baha du hamonga nane, nane nokulajiliza wulajilize wangu wose, hamonga ne malajilizo. Nagwe wubite wukawejize wawo wanhu, wagebate no kugakamisa mumo mu yisi yono nghuwapela.’

32 “Holeka anye nyo Waisilayeli, mumanyile lukulu vyono munojeye muzikamise no kwituma zose zono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu yamulajilizenyi. Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kuwubedegala wulajilize wakwe.
33 Mweze mwina cihuliko muli zose, ho musume kutuga wumoteswe, na kanyhi ho musume kwikala vilo vinji, mu yisi yono moyisola yiwe.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 6

I lilajilizo liwaha

1 “Na gagano go malajilizo gose gono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yandajilize nimwijizenyi. Holeka mweze na cihuliko, kanyhi mugakamise muno, mumo mu yisi yono aluno mweza kwinjila no kuyisola yiwe yenyu.
2 Nanye hamonga ne wana wenyu mweze muli cogopo kuli Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, muwukamise wutemye wakwe malajilizo gakwe gono nghumulajilizenyi izuwali, mu vilo vyose vyo wikale wenyu, ho musume kwikala vilo vinji mumo mu yisi yiyo.
3 Holeka mweze ne cihuliko anye nyo Waisilayeli, mugakamise muno malajilizo gakwe! Holeka mowa no wulugutwe, kanyhi mowa nghungugo ye vilungo muno muno. Nanye ho mosuma kwikala mu yisi yono yina moso, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wenyu yehuwiza.

4 “Anye nyo Isilayeli, ta mukumbucile! Muwaha Mulungu, yo Muwaha Mulungu yumonga du, yo Muwaha Mulungu wetu.
5 Holeka mumwendaje Muwaha Mulungu wetu, kwe zinhumbula zenyu zose, na kwe mitima yenyu yose, na kwe vilungo vyenyu vyose.
6 Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kugasemwa malajilizo gaga gono nghumulajilizenyi izuwali.
7 Nanye mupiligale kuwejiza wana wenyu. Kanyhi mweze mupiligaye kuwejiza lono muli ha kaya, na lono muli mu mihinzo, na lono mwi'kaye mucehile, sunga na lono muli mu milimo yenyu.
8 Kanyhi mugawope mu zimuhukumhuku ze miwoko yenyu, na haciligati ye meso, mu vihanda vyo wusu wenyu viwe nha citanjilo co kumukumbucizenyi nhondo zose.
9 Kanyhi mugandice na mu fulemu ze milango ye zikaya zenyu, na mu malango ge ziluwa zenyu.”

Wusugulwe kuli zo wu wula cihuliko

10 “Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, komupelenyi yisi yono yina makaya mawaha, kanyhi maswanu muno, gono si mwagazenga nyenye nyo weneco, nha vyono yawehuwiza walezi wenyu, Abulahamu, na Isaka, na Yakobo.
11 Ne zikaya zolowa zimemile vinhu viswanu vyono si mwavisawa nyenye. Kanyhi ne masima gono si mwagahimba nyenye nyo weneco, ne migunda ya zabibu na ye mizeyituni yono si mwayihadika nyenye nyo weneco. Na kolo Muwaha Mulungu yakondya kumwinjizenyi mu yisi yiyi, nanye muwe mwakondya kuvituga vinhu vyose vyono mowa kuhila kulya,
12 mulanjilile, mulecele lukulu kumusemwa Muwaha Mulungu, mono yamuwusenyi ku wulawa ku Misili, kono mwali muli wawanda.
13 Munojeye mweze ne cogopo kuli Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, mumuhembe mweneco du yali wene, sunga no wihuwizo wenyu wose, mweze kuwunozeza mwi'tagwa lyakwe mweneco du.
14 Mulecele lukulu kuhemba milungu yinji yoyose, ya muli milungu ye wanhu wono wamupilimenyi yono wakuyihemba.
15 Na hono mwijelele du muyihembe milungu yinji, ale, likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu lyomuzizenyi nha moto mukali, nalyo lyomongomezezenyi lukulu. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu mono yali haciligati yenyu, nha Mulungu mono si kosuma kwisumaniza na yunji wo wose.

16 “Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kumujela u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha vyono mwanoza ha Masa.
17 Holeka mwilanjililaje, mweze ne cihuliko, kanyhi mwibate wulajilize wakwe wose wono yamulajilizenyi.
18 Kanyhi mweze kwituma zono Muwaha Mulungu yakuzilajiza, iziswanu na zo mugoloka. Na holeka mosuma kumoteswa muli zenyu zose, no kuyisola yisi yiwe yenyu, i yisi yono yina moso, yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu yawehuwiza i walezi wenyu.
19 Kanyhi kowawinjila kutali i walugu wenyu, nha vyono yehuwiza.

20 “Na mu vilo vyono vikuza, i wana wenyu hono wamuwuzenyi wawe kutya, ‘Alu ku sokoci Muwaha Mulungu yacilajiliza ciwukamise wulajilize wuwu wose?’
21 Nanye muwapitule mutye, ‘Ase cali cili wawanda wo mutemi Falawo ku yisi ya Misili, nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakaciwusa kuko kwe vilungo vyakwe viwaha.
22 Nase cewonelela co weneco ne meso getu vyono yetuma mapoto, ne zinghani zo wogofye kuli Wamisili, no mutemi wawo Falawo, na kuli walongola wakwe wose.
23 Muwaha Mulungu yakaciwusa kuko ku Misili, yakacileta sunga ho bahano, yakacipela ne yisi yiyi, nha vyono yawehuwiza walezi wetu vyono kocipela.
24 Hanyuma ho Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, yakawa kucilajiliza vyono, yinojeye ciwukamise wulajilize wose, kanyhi cimupele mwene cogopo. Na hono citume nhavivyo, nayo koyidima nghungugo yetu, no kuyimotya.
25 Na hono cizibate ko wihuwilwa zose zono Muwaha Mulungu yacilajilize, nayo kokwendezwa nase.’ ”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 7

I wanhu wo Mulungu

(Wut 34:11-16)

1 “Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, komutegulenyi sunga ko ku yisi yono moyisola yiwe yenyu, nayo kogawinga makungugo menji gono gali mu yisi yiyo gawuce. Hamonga ne makungugo mupunghati mawaha, gono gali vilungo kuciza anye, likungugo lye Wahiti, ne Wagiligashi, ne Waamoli, ne Wakanani, ne Wapelizi, ne Wahivi, ne Wayebusi.
2 Na kolo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumukombolelenyi wanhu wawo mu miwoko yenyu, nanye kolo mwakondya kuwasuma, munojeye muwongomezeze lukulu. Mulecele lukulu kwilumbaniza nawo, hamba mulece kuwawonela lusungu.
3 Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kwitolanila nawo.
4 Hambi hono munoze nhavivyo, i wana wenyu nha wokwejelezwa na wene, sunga wamusemwe Muwaha Mulungu, hanyuma wawe kuhemba milungu yinji. Na hono zizo zijele kuli'nye, Muwaha Mulungu komukolelwenyi yikuwo, sunga yamongomezenyi nyenye wose lwa kamonga.
5 Holeka mugamwagulaje makumbicilo gawo, mugabenanjize ne masumbili gawo ge mabwe, kanyhi mugatemanje ne Maashela gawo, muvikongojeze ne vihwani vye milungu yawo viseswe.
6 Muzitumaje zizi, vyono anye muli wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yakamuhagulenyi anye mu wulawa mu wanhu wa mono'nze yose, muwe nghungugo yono yili wene so mweneco.

7 “Ninga Muwaha Mulungu si yakamuhagulenyi anye vyono mwali muli wenji mucizize i makungugo ganji gose, aa, ng'o, hambi nha mwali muli nghungugo ndodo du muno, mono yinze.
8 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakamwendenyi du, kanyhi yakasaka yafisilize wihuwizo wakwe, wono yawehuwiza i walezi wenyu. Na yiyi yo yaleka yakamuwusenyi ku yisi ya Misili, kwe vilungo vyakwe viwaha, yakamunozenyi muwe welecele, mu wulawa mu wuwanda wa ku Misili, wa kuli mutemi Falawo.
9 Kanyhi mweze kukumbucila vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yo Mulungu monga du, kanyhi mwihuwilwa. Mono kosuma kukamisa cilagane cakwe, no kulajila lusungu lwakwe luwaha kuli ndelo maelufu na maelufu ga wono wamwendile, kanyhi webate ne malajilizo gakwe.
10 Ninga hamba si kokangamyanga yaye kuwalipiciza wose wono si wamwendile.
11 Holeka muzikamisaje zose zono mwakondya kwijizwa. Kanyhi mugebate ne malajilizo gose gono nimulonjelenyi izuwali.”

Wulugutwe wa kuli ze cihuliko

(Kum 28:1-14)

12 “Na hono mugahulice malajilizo gaga mugakamise no kugetuma ko wihuwilwa, u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nayo kojendelela kucikamisa cilagane cakwe cono yacinoza nanye, kanyhi komulajilenyi no lusungu lwakwe luwaha, nha vyono yawehuwiza walezi wenyu.
13 Nayo Mulungu komwendenyi, kanyhi komulugutizenyi, ho monjelezece no kuwa na wana wenji. Kanyhi koyilugutiza ne migunda yenyu, ho mutuje wuhemba winji ne divayi, ne mafuta. Kanyhi komulugutizenyi yamupelenyi mitugo minji ne zingholo. Komupelenyi wulugute wuwu wose, mumo mu yisi yono yawehuwiza walezi wenyu nha vyono komupelenyi.
14 Na howa hasina kanyhi munhu wo wose mono yinze yiyi, mono kowa no wulugute nha nyenye. Kanyhi muli nyenye, howa hasina mulume hamba mucekulu mono si kolela wana, hamba mutugo wono si wukulela yaye.
15 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yo komucitilenyi muli matamwa ge mawala gose. Kanyhi hamba si koleka matamwa mabi gamuzizenyi, gono gamutujenyi lono mwali muli ku Misili, langa kogaleka gawatuje i walugu wenyu.
16 Ninga langa, mugongomezezaje lukulu makungugo gose gono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumukombolelenyi, langa mulecele lukulu kuwawonela lusungu hamba hadodo. Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kuyihemba milungu yawo, hambi nha yowa mutego kuli'nye.

17 “Kanyhi mu madaha go gose, mulecele lukulu kuwogopa, hamba kuwehwesa wanhu wawa vyono wenji muno si nha nyenye, holeka lunji simosuma kuwasuma no kuwawinga wawuce.
18 Mulece kuwogopela lukulu, ninga mukumbucile zono yetuma Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kuli Falawo mutemi wa Misili, sunga na kuli wanhu wakwe wose.
19 Kanyhi mukumbucile ze litamwa lyo kogofya lyo wukoma, lyono mwaliwona nyenye nyo weneco ne meso genyu, ne mapoto, ne mezino mawaha, no wusume, ne vilungo, vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yetumila yakamuwusenyi mu yisi ya Misili. Na mu nzila yiyo du, yakawongomeza Wamisili. Holeka kowongomeza ne wanhu wawa wose, wono aluno anye mukuwogopa.
20 Hambi nha kowaletela woga wuwaha, sunga muwongomezeze lukulu wose wono wotiza no kwihundika.
21 Holeka anye mulecele lukulu kuwa no woga ku so wanhu wawa, hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha yali hamonga nanye. Mwene nha yo Muwaha Mulungu mono Yalawiliciye, kanyhi yo mweneco du mono yanojeye wanhu wamogope.
22 U Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kogawinga hadodo hadodo makungugo gago hali'nye mu nghango vyono mowa kucila kwinjila mu'si. Nanye si mosuma kogongomeza lwa kamonga yaye. Ku soko hono munoze nhavivyo, i malimu ga mu mbago nha gokwinjipa mu yisi yenyu, no kumogofyenyi.
23 Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu komukombolelenyi i walugu, no kuwogofya, sunga kolo mwawongomezeza wose lukulu.
24 Muwaha Mulungu komukombolelenyi watemi wawo wose. Nanye muwawulaje, nawo wawe kusemwelwa lukulu. Hasina hamba yunji wowose mono kosuma kumwimilenyi, langa sunga kolo mwawongomezeza wose lukulu.
25 Muviticimizaje mu moto, i vihwani vye milungu yawo viseswe. Hamba mulecele lukulu kusulumila fweza, hamba zahabu, vyono vili muli vyene, mulecele lukulu kuvisola viwe vyenyu. Hono munoze nhavivyo, wowa mutego kuli'nye, hambi Muwaha Mulungu, nha siyawendile u wuhemba vihwani viseswe.
26 Kanyhi, mulecele lukulu kuvisola vivyo vihwani vye milungu viseswe, no kuvileta mu zikaya zenyu. Hono munoze nhavivyo, u wulijitwe wono wuli muli vyene, womuwuyilenyi nyenye. Holeka, munojeye muyikomale no kuyinyalaha, milungu yiyi ye vihwani viseswe, hambi nha vili mu wulijitwe wo Muwaha Mulungu.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 8

Wuteme we yisi swanu

1 “Munojeye mupiligale kugebata ko wihuwilwa, malajilizo gaga gose, gono nakondya kumupelenyi izuwali, ho muwumipe, kanyhi ho monjelezece muwe wenji, no kuyitema yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu yawehuwiziza walezi wenyu.
2 Kanyhi mukumbucile vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yamutonjenyi mu muhinzo mutali, wa mu nyika mu myaka yiyi makumi mane yono yipulute, yakamukozenyi mu makamu menji, ho yamujelenyi, ho yamanye zono zili mu zinhumbula zenyu, na hono mbeka mwendile kugebata malajilizo gakwe, hamba ng'o.
3 Yakamulecenyi ho mudabuce muno, hanyuma yakamulisenyi ndigwa yono yikucemwa Mana. Ndigwa yono ku wulawa katali si mwali muyimanyile nyenye, hamba walezi wenyu, mwali mukali hamba nayilya yaye. Muwaha Mulungu yakazinoza zizi, ho yamwijizenyi mumanye vyono munhu si kekalaga mumi so mukate du, langa vyono, munhu kekalaga mumi ku so zinghani zose zono kazilongaga Muwaha Mulungu.
4 Na mu myaka yiyi yose makumi mane, myenda yenyu hamba si yasakala yaye, hamba migulu yenyu si yavimba yaye.
5 Ninga mukumbucile vyono, Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakuwa kumufundenyi cihuliko, nha vyono i wana wakuwa kufundwa cihuliko ne walezi wawo.
6 Holeka munojeye mwitume nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajilizenyi, kanyhi mujendele muli ze malajilizo gakwe, akuno mumogopile mwene.
7 Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha yakumutegulenyi mu yisi yono yina moso, yisi yono yina makolongo ne mivwilo ye malenga, sunga ne vikolongo vye malenga, vikuvwila mu malolo na mu vigongo mucanyha.
8 Yisi yono yikulela ngano, na shayili, na zabibu, ne makomamanga, yisi ye mafuta, no wuci.
9 Na mu yisi yiyo, si molodabucila lukulu, hamba kuwa na wanjile wa cinji co cose. Yisi yono mabwe gayo gena cuma, na mu vigongo vyayo mosuma kuhimba shaba.
10 Nanye mosuma kulya co cose cono mosaka kulya, ninga mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, so yisi yiyo swanu yono yamupelenyi.”

Suguzi ku soko yo kumusemwa Muwaha Mulungu

11 “Mulanjilile, mulecele lukulu kumusemwa Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, mugebate malajilizo gakwe gose gono nghumupelenyi izuwali.
12 Na kolo mwatuga visakwa vyenyu vyose vyo kulya, na kolo mwazenga kaya swanu no kwikala mumo,
13 na kolo ng'ombe zenyu ne zingholo zenyu, ne fweza zenyu ne zahabu zenyu, vyakondya konjelezeka, hamonga ne sawo yenyu yose,
14 mulanjilile, mulecele lukulu kwikumya, muwe kumusemwa no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, mono yamuwusenyi ku wulawa ku yisi ya Misili, kono mwali muli wawanda.
15 Mono yamutonjenyi sunga mukalowoka nyika yo kogofya, mono mwali mogali mazoka gono gena sumu nghali, hamonga ne zinzije. Mu yisi nghavu yono yisina malenga, ninga mwene yakamung'wesenyi malenga mu wulawa mwibwe.
16 Na mumo mu nyika, yakamupelenyi ndigwa yono yikucemwa Mana, ho mulye, i ndigwa yono ku wulawa katali, walezi wenyu si wali wayimanyile hamba kuyilya. Yakamukozenyi mu makamu menji, ho yamujelenyi, hanyuma ho yamulugutizenyi kwe vinhu viswanu.
17 Holeka mulecele lukulu kwihwesa nha vyono, i vinhu vivyo vyose, mwavisawa kwe vilungo vyenyu ne mahala genyu nyo weneco.
18 Langa mweze kukumbucila vyono, Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yo yakumupelenyi wusume wo kugolipa. Nayo yakwituma zizi vyono mwene kekalaga mwihuwilwa sunga izuwali, ku cilagane cono yacinoza ne walezi wenyu.
19 Holeka mulecele lukulu kumusemwa Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, hamba kuyityatya milungu yinji no kuyihemba no kuyitumamila. Na hono mwijelele munoze nhavivyo, nghumusugulenyi izuwali vyono mbeka wose mokongomezwa.
20 Na hono mulece kumuhulika Muwaha Mulungu, ale mokongomezwa nhe makungugo gago gono Mulungu kogongomezeza hali'nye.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 9

U wu wula cihuliko we wanhu

1 “Muhulicize! Anye nyo wanhu Waisilayeli! Alu izuwali mwasejelela ku wulowoka likolongo lya Yoludani, mwinjile mu yisi yono yikaye makungugo mawaha, kanyhi gali vilungo kumucizenyi. Ne makaya gawo mawaha, gazenjeywe majinji matali muno gono gaficile ko ku mavunde.
2 Ne wanhu weneco watali muno kanyhi wali vilungo, wakucemwa Waanaki, nghwihwesa mukondizye kuhulika yikulongwa vyono, hasina munhu mono kosuma kwisumaniza na wene.
3 Ninga anye alu, mowona nyenye nyo weneco vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kowa yamulongolelenyi kotya moto wono wuticimizaga. Mwene kowasumiza ha mwande yenyu, nanye ho musume kuwawinga no kuwongomeza mbela mbela, nha vyono Mwene yehuwiza.

4 “Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kolo yakondya kuwawinga ha mwande yenyu, mulece kwiyitya vyono Muwaha Mulungu kamwinjizenyi ho muyisole yisi yiyi ko wiling'ano wenyu. Aa, ng'o, u Muwaha Mulungu kowawinga wanhu wawa, ku soko yo wubi wawo.
5 Kanyhi si vyono anye muli we ling'ani, mukwituma ze ling'ani, holeka yiwe Muwaha Mulungu yakumupelenyi yisi yawo. Langa mwene kowawinga ku soko yo wubi wawo. Yakuhila yajendelele kukamisa cilagane cakwe cono yacinoza kuli walezi wenyu, Abulahamu, Isaka na Yakobo.
6 Holeka yinojeye mumanyile lukulu nha vyono, Muwaha Mulungu si yakumupelenyi yisi yiyi yo moso ku soko yo wiling'ano wenyu. Ng'o yaye! Hambi nha muli wanhu mono musina cihuliko.

7 “Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kusemwa vyono mwamukoza likuwo Muwaha Mulungu kuko ku nyika. Ku wulawilila mwizuwa lyono mwawucila ku Misili, sunga mwizuwa lyono mwaficila bahano, mwamubeza Muwaha Mulungu.
8 Sunga na he'tunda lya Sinayi, naho nhavivyo mwamukoza Muwaha Mulungu likuwo, yakakolwa likuwo yakasaka yamongomezenyi wose.
9 Ane nghabita kwi'tunda kucanyha ho mbocele vibawo vye'bwe vyono vyali vyandicigwe cilagane cono Muwaha Mulungu yacinoza nanye. Nghekala kuko vilo makumi mane misi ne cilo, hamba kulya cinji hamba kung'wa malenga yaye, mu vilo vivyo vyose.
10 Hanyuma ho Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kumhela vibawo vyejete vya mabwe, vyono vyali vyandicigwe no muwoko wakwe mweneco, zinghani zono yalonganila nase wose he'tunda.
11 Heya vyali vyeza kupuluta vilo vivyo makumi mane misi ne cilo, Muwaha Mulungu ho yakawa kumhela vivyo vibawo vyejete vye mabwe, vyono vyali vyandicigwe i cilagane.

12 “Hanyuma Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya, wutime hasi ye litunda musunho, hambi i wanhu wono wawawusa ku yisi ya Misili, nha wakondya kwiyihodanga weneco, no kwiyinjiza mu zinghani zimbi. Wakondya kuzileka zose zono nawalajiliza wetume, alu wenozeza milungu ye vihwani viseswe.

13 “Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya, ‘Manyile vyono wanhu wawa, wena nhumbula yono yisina cihuliko.
14 Holeka lu 'gwe wulecele lukulu kunyimila. Ane neza kusaka kuwongomezeza lukulu wose, sunga hawe hasina kanyhi munhu mono kowakumbucila yaye. Nane nokunoza agwe wuwe sogo we likungugo yiwaha kanyhi lye vilungo kuciza vyono wenha wene.’

15 “Hodu, nane nghawa kutima hasi ye litunda, akuno ndinghuye ne vibawo vi vilya vyejete vye mabwe mu mawoko gangu, vyono vyali vyandicigwe i cilagane. Ne malambi go moto gakaga mu wulawa mwi'tunda.
16 Nane nghawona vyono mwali mwakondya kucibena cilagane cono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yali yamunozezajenyi. Na kanyhi vyono mwali mwamusutizya Muwaha Mulungu, so winozeza nyo weneco cihwani ce ndama nyenjedulwe.
17 Hodu nane nghawa kuvigumila hasi ha mwande yenyu, i vibawo vyejete vye mabwe, navyo vikawa kutulanjika no kuzaganjika.
18 Ho baho nane nghagwa funama hali Muwaha Mulungu mu vilo makumi mane kanyhi misi ne cilo, hamba si nalizaga cinji, hamba kung'wa malenga, mu vilo vyose vivyo. Nane nazinoza zizi ku soko mwali mwakondya kumusutizya Muwaha Mulungu, mwamukoza likuwo.
19 Nghawa kogopa likuwo liwaha lyo Muwaha Mulungu, hambi nha yali yakalipe muno yasakaga ho yamongomezenyi nyenye wose. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, Muwaha Mulungu yakahulika kanyhi wulombe wangu.
20 Muwaha Mulungu yakamukolelwa likuwo Haluni, yakasaka yamuwulaje. Nane nghamulombela na ku soko ya Haluni mwi'daha lilyo.
21 Nghawusola wubi wenyu, nha cinji yiyilya ndama nyenjedulwe yono mwali mwayinoza, nghawa kuyigumila mu moto. Nghayitakamiza, nghawa kuyitindya, yikawa nha manghundi, hanyuma, nghawa kugagumila manghundi gago mu cikolongo, cono cahulumaga cilawiliye mwi'tunda.

22 “Kanyhi mwamukoza likuwo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, lono mwali muli ha Tabela, na ha Masa, na ha Cibiloti-hatava.
23 Na lono yamuletenyi ku wulawa ku Kadeshi-balineya, lono yamulajilizenyi mubite mukayisole yisi yono yali yamupelajenyi, ninga 'nye mukamubeza Muwaha Mulungu, hamba si mwamwitumbila, hamba kumuhulika kose.
24 Holeka nane ku wulawa mwi'zuwa lilyo, nghamumanyenyi vyono musina cihuliko kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

25 “Holeka nane nghawa kugwa funama hali Muwaha Mulungu, vilo vivyo makumi mane misi ne cilo, hambi nha nali nakondya kumanya vyono komongomezenyi wose.
26 Nane nghawa kulomba nghatya, ‘Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wulece kuwongomeza wanhu wako, i wanhu wono wawaponya no kuwawusa ku yisi ya Misili kwe vilungo viwaha, na ko wusume wuwaha.
27 Kanyhi nghukulomba wuwakumbucilaje i wetumami wako, Abulahamu, na Isaka na Yakobo. Holeka go Muwaha Mulungu, wulece kuwulanga wubedegazi wawo, no wubi wawo ne masutya ge wanhu wawa.
28 Yilece kuwapela Wamisili cilejeho co kulonjela, wawe kutya, vyono siwasuma kuwenjiza wanhu wako, mu yisi yono wawehuwiziza. Kanyhi wotya, vyono wawasola wanhu wako wuwategule mu nyika ho wukawawulajile mumo, vyono wali wuwasulile.
29 Hamonga na zizo, awano wo wanhu wono wawahagula ho wawe wako go mweneco, nagwe wukawawusa ku Misili kwe vilungo vyako viwaha na ko wusume wuwaha.’ ”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 10

Musa yakubocela kanyhi Malajilizo yikumi

(Wut 34:1-10)

1 “Baho nayo Muwaha Mulungu yamhitula yakatya, ‘Ale wusese kanyhi vibawo vyejete vya mabwe, viwe vinha nhe vibawo vilongozi, kanyhi wusese ne lisanduku lya mbawo, lyono vyowikwa mugati yalyo. Hanyuma wuze navyo, wunyagane akuno kwi'tunda kucanyha,
2 nane novyandika vibawo vivyo navyo, zinghani zono nali nizandice mu vibawo vi vilya vyono wavitula. Hanyuma wuwe kuviwika mwi'sanduku mugati.’

3 “Nane nghawa kulisesa lisanduku, nghetumamila zimbawo zo mubici wo mukambala, hanyuma nghawa kusesa ne vibawo vyejete kanyhi vye mabwe, nha vyono vyali vinha vilongozi. Nghawa kuvitegula kuko kwi'tunda kucanyha.
4 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakawa kuvyandika mucanyha yavyo zinghani zi zilya du zono yali yazandice halulongozi, nha cinji i Malajilizo yikumi gono yamupelenyi lono yalonganila nanye muciligati yo moto, mwizuwa lililya lyono mwali mwihangulize ha mwejelele we litunda. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakawa kumhela vibawo vivyo,
5 nane nghawa kupituka, nghawa kutima hasi ku wulawa mwi'tunda mucanyha. Hanyuma nghawa kuviwika mwi'sanduku mugati, mwisanduku lyono nalisesa nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalajiliza. Navyo vibawo vivyo, vikawa vikalaga mumo du sunga nalu.”

6 (Waisilayeli wakawa kutuluka, wulawa ha masima ge malenga gono gali ge wanhu wa Yakani, wakawa kubita sunga wakafika ku Mosela. Baho Haluni ho yafwila, yakazikwa baho. Nayo mwanagwe we cilume Eleyezali, yakawa kuhalila u mulimo wo sogwe wo wukohani.
7 Na ha wulawilila baho, wakajendelela, wakawa kubita hanhu hono hakucemwa Gudigoda. Na ku wulawa Gudigoda, wakabita Jotibata hanhu hono ho vili vikolongo vye malenga.
8 Na baho he 'tunda, Muwaha Mulungu yakawahagula wanhu wo mulango wa Lawi, wo wawe walanjilizi we Lisanduku lye Cilagane. Wamwitumamile nha wakohani. Kanyhi wawe kulavizya wulugutize mwitagwa lyakwe. Na wuwo wo mulimo wawo sunga nalu.
9 Na yiyo yo soko yono yaleka wo mulango wa Lawi wasina ligawo lye yisi, nha vyono wenha we milango yinji yose. Ninga wene wabocela lihewa lyo kuwa wakohani wo Mulungu, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yehuwiza.)

10 “Nane nekala vilo makumi mane misi ne cilo kuko mwi'tunda, nha vyono nekala ha lulongozi. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakahulika wulombe wangu, yakavumila yalece kumongomezenyi.
11 Hanyuma yakawa kunonjela nize nimutonjenyi, ho musume kwinjila no kuyitema yisi yono yawehuwiza i walezi wenyu.”

Cono Mulungu yakucisaka

12 “Alu 'nye nyo Waisilayeli, muhulicize ta cono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakucisaka kuli'nye, u Muwaha Mulungu yakumusacenyi, Mumuhembe Muwaha Mulungu, mwitume zose zono yakumulajilizenyi mwitume. Mumwende, mumwitumamile kwe zinhumbula zenyu zose na kwe mitima yenyu yose,
13 kanyhi mugebate ne malajilizo gakwe gose, gono nghumulajilizenyi i zuwali, ho mutuje wumoteswe.
14 Hambi u Muwaha Mulungu, nha yo munghala wulanga wose, wono wulawiliciye, sunga ne yinze yose nha yakwe, sunga ne vinhu vyose vyono vili mugati yayo.
15 Hambi u wendo wakwe kuli walezi wenyu, nha wali wuwaha, holeka yakamuhagulenyi anye si nhe wanhu wanji, sunga nalu, mukali muli wanhu wakwe wahagulwa.
16 Holeka alu, ku wulawilila zuwali, mweze muli cihuliko kuli Muwaha Mulungu, mulecele lukulu kuwa muli cibede.
17 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha yo Mulungu we milungu yose, kanyhi yo munghala vilungo vyose. Mwene yo Mulungu mono Yalawiliciye kanyhi yo we vilungo. Holeka yanojeye yogopwe mweneco du. Hambi mwene nha yasina wuwaguzi, hamba si kabocelaga mafupo.
18 Mwene yendile kuwona i wana wono wasina walezi, hamonga ne wacekulu wafwile, wose wakutendelwa ling'ani. Kanyhi yawendile sunga ne wajenzi, wono wekalane hamonga ne wanhu wetu. Mwene kawapelaga zindigwa ne vivwalo.
19 Holeka nanye munojeye muwende wawo wajenzi, hambi nanye nha mwali wajenzi mu yisi ya Misili.
20 Holeka munojeye mweze ne cihuliko kuli Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, muwe kumuhemba mweneco yali wene du. Kanyhi mweze muli wehuwilwa kuli mwene, kanyhi mweze kwilahiziza mwitagwa lyakwe mweneco du.
21 Hambi mwene nha yo nhojo yenyu, kanyhi mwene yo Mulungu wenyu, mono yetuma mezino mawaha, na go wogofye ku soko yenyu, i metumo gono sunga na nyenye nyo mweneco mwagawona ne meso genyu.
22 Mukumbucile vyono, lono walezi wenyu wabitila ku Misili, wali waficile wanhu walume makumi mupunghati du. Ninga aluno, mulanje vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yetumile kuli'nye, kamunozenyi muwa muli wenji muno, mwihwana no winji we zinyelezi za ku wulanga.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 11

U wuwaha wo Muwaha Mulungu

1 “Mumwendaje Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kanyhi mweze mugebate ne malajilizo gakwe nhondo zose.
2 Kanyhi izuwali mukumbucile zo Muwaha Mulungu zono mweyijiza mu mikalile yenyu. Kanyhi yo nyenye nyo weneco, hamba si wana wenyu yaye, wono waziwona zizo. Anye mwawuwona u wuwaha wo Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga ne vilungo vyakwe, no wusume wakwe,
3 ne mapoto gakwe. Kanyhi mwawona na vyono yanoza kuli Falawo mutemi we Misili, na kuli yisi yakwe yose.
4 Kanyhi mwawona na vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalyongomezeza lukulu lipuga lye wasilikale wa Misili, hamonga ne zifwalasi zawo ne matuka gawo go wulugu, lono yagagubiciziza mwi'lenga lya Shamu, lono wamutyatyajenyi mu nyuma yenyu.
5 Kanyhi mumanyile manyi manyi, zono Muwaha Mulungu yamutendelenyi, lono mwali mukali mu nyika, lono mukali nafika bahano.
6 Na zono yawatendela ha mwande ye wanhu wose, Datani na Abilamu, i wana we cilume wa Eliyabi wo mulango wa Lubeni. Wono yisi yahama, yikawamela, hamonga ne wagolece zawo ne wana wawo, ne mahema gawo, ne wetumami wawo, ne zimitugo zawo.
7 Heya, nanye yo nyenye mono mwaziwona zizi zose zi mbaha, zono Muwaha Mulungu yazituma.”

U wumoteswe wa mu yisi yono wehuwizwa

8 “Holeka mugebataje malajilizo gose gono nakondya kumulonjelenyi izuwali. Ho mosuma kulilowoka likolongo no kuyisola yisi yono muyilindiye kuyitema.
9 Kanyhi ho mosuma kwikala vilo vinji mu yisi yo moso, yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu yawehuwiza walezi wenyu hamonga ne wana wawo, vyono kowapela.
10 Ne yisi yiyo yono moyisola no kwikala mumo, si yinha yisi ya Misili yaye, yono mwekalaga katali, yisi yono mwahadikaga zimbeyu, hanyuma kanyhi muwa kutuga mulimo muwaha wo kuzimwajilila malenga mu migunda yenyu.
11 Ninga yisi yono mokwinjila muyiteme, yo yisi ye vigongo ne malolo, yo yisi yono yitonyelwaga malenga ge mvula ya ku wulanga.
12 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu u Mulungu wenyu, yo kayilanjililaga nhondo zose, ku wulawilila mwamulizo wo mwaka, sunga ko ku ndumo kanyhi yo mwaka.

13 “Holeka munojeye mugebate muno muno i malajilizo gono nakondya kumulonjelenyi izuwali. Mumwende Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, mumwitumamile kwe zinhumbula zenyu zose.
14 Na hono mwitume nhavivyo, nayo komutonyezezenyi mvula mu yisi yenyu, mu cidaha cayo, mvula ye mhinga ne mvula ye mafuso, ho mududule wuhemba wenyu, ne divayi yenyu, hamonga ne mafuta ge mizeyituni yenyu,
15 ne mahanze gono mozilisa mitugo zenyu. Nanye mowa mwina ndigwa nyinji muno, mu nghango nha vyono mosaka nyenye.
16 Holeka mwiyilanjililaje muno muno ho mulece kumuleka Muwaha Mulungu, no kuhemba milungu yinji, no kuyitumamila.
17 Na hono mwijelele mumulece Muwaha Mulungu, ale mumanye, vyono Muwaha Mulungu nayo, komukolelwenyi likuwo muno muno. Koyiwusa mvula, ne yisi yenyu yonyala yiwe nghavu, yono si yosuma kanyhi kulela zindigwa. Hamba yiwe yisi yeneco swanu muno, yono Muwaha Mulungu yakumupelenyi. Nanye mofwila mumo.

18 “Holeka mugakamise malajilizo gaga mu zinhumbula zenyu na mu mitima yenyu. Mugawope mu zimhukumhuku ze miwoko yenyu, na ha ciligati ye meso mu vihanda vyo wusu wenyu, viwe nha citanjilo co kumukumbucizenyi nhondo zose.
19 Kanyhi muwejize ne wana wenyu. Mweze kuziduwila ze malajilizo gaga, lono muli mu zikaya zenyu, hamba lono muli hanhu ho hose, hamba lono mucehile, hamba lono muli mu milimo yenyu.
20 Mugandice sunga na mu zi fulemu ze milango ye zikaya zenyu, sunga na mu malango genyu ge zi luwa zenyu.
21 Holeka anye, ne wana wenyu, mokwikala vilo vinji, mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yawehuwiziza i walezi wenyu, nha vyono kowapela. Na mbeka, mokwikala mu yisi yiyo vilo vinji du muno, nha vyono vinha vilo vyo wulanga wono wuli mucanyha ye yinze.

22 “Holeka, mugebate ko wihuwilwa, i malajilizo gose gono nakondya kumulonjelenyi. Mumwende Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kanyhi mwitume zose zono yakumulajilizenyi mwitume. Kanyhi mweze wehuwilwa kuli mwene.
23 Holeka nayo kogawinga makungugo gago gose hali'nye. Nanye moyitema yisi yiyo ye makungugo gago mawaha, kanyhi gali vilungo si nha nyenye.
24 Ne hanhu ho hose hono luzayo lwenyu lohakanyaga, baho howa heza henyu nyenye. Ne mipaka ye yisi yenyu, yowa yilawiliye ku wulawa ku nyika, ku lubavu lwa kutakama, sunga mu matunda ga Lebanoni ku lubavu lwa kusumuka. Kanyhi ku wulawilila he kolongo lya Efulata, ku lubavu lwa kucilima, sunga ko kwi'lenga lya Meditelaneya ku lubavu lwa kumwezi.
25 Ne hanhu ho hose hono mowa kukolela mu yisi yiyo, u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kowanoza wanhu wose wamogopenyi, nha vyono yehuwiza. Kanyhi howa hasina hamba munhu mono kosuma kwijelela hali'nye, yamwimilenyi.

26 “Alu nane izuwali, nghumulecenyi mwihagulile nyo weneco, hono mokwenda wulugute, hamba mokwenda wulijitwe.
27 Hono mugebate malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, gono nghumulonjelenyi izuwali, ale, molugutwa.
28 Ninga hono mulece kugebata malajilizo gaga, kanyhi muwuye ne nyuma muwe kuyihemba milungu yo wulambi, yono si mwayihembaga katali, ale mumanye, vyono molijitwa.

29 “Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, kolo yakondya kumwinjizenyi mu yisi yono moyisola yiwe yenyu, mowa munojeye muziganule zo wulugutwe, mu wulawa mwi'tunda lya Gelizimu. Nazo zo wulijitwe, muziganule mu wulawa mwi'tunda lya Ebali.
30 (Ne matunda gagano mejete, gali ku lubavu lwa ku mwezi ye likolongo lya Yoludani, mu yisi ye Wakanani, wono wekaye mwi'lolo lye likolongo lya Yoludani. Matunda gago, gali ku lubavu lwa kumwezi, hamba si gali kutali yaye ne mibici ya Mole.)
31 Nanye molowoka likolongo lya Yoludani, no kuyisola yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumupelenyi. Na kolo mwayisola, mwekala mumo,
32 mugebataje no kugakamisiza lukulu i malajilizo gono nghumulonjelenyi izuwali.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 12

Hanhu hamonga du ha Wuhembelo

1 “Alu agano go malajilizo gono ganojeye mugebate no kugakamisa, mu vilo vyose vyo wikale wenyu, mu yisi yono u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wenyu, yakumupelenyi yiwe yenyu. Holeka alu muhulicize!
2 Kolo mweza kwinjila mu yisi yiyo yono mukuyisola, muhamwagulaje hanhu hose hono i wanhu wayihembelaga milungu yawo, mu matunda matali, na mu vigongo, sunga na hasi ye mibici yose yono yidedewaye.
3 Mugamwagule makumbicilo gawo, mugatulanjize ne masumbili gawo ge mabwe. Muviticimize vihwani vyose vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, muvibenanjize ne vihwani vyawo viseswe, ho walecele lukulu kuhemba kanyhi hanhu baho.

4 “Kanyhi, mulecele lukulu kumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, mu nzila yono wanhu wawa wali kuyihembela milungu yawo.
5 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu, kohagula hanhu hamonga du mu yisi yose, i hanhu hono i wanhu wose we milango yenyu yose, mowa kumubitila no kumuhemba.
6 Ne hanhu baho ho mowa kuvilavizya vilavywa vyenyu vinyanywe, ne makumbiko genyu ganji ganji, ne zaka zenyu, ne vilavywa, ne vipegwa vyono muvihuwize nyenye nyo weneco kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Hamonga ne vilavywa vye lihewa. Ne zinyana zinongozi kulelwa, ze zing'ombe zenyu ne zingholo zenyu.
7 Nanye molya no kung'wa ko wusangalale baho hali Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, mono yamulugutizenyi, no kumupelenyi vinhu viswanu ku walawa mwamulizo wenyu, nyenye hamonga ne wagolece zenyu ne wana wenyu.

8 “Ne lipango lilyo, kolo lyeza kufika, nanye hamba si mokwituma kanyhi nha vyono mukwituma izuwali, hambi izuwali, nha mukwituma munhu na zakwe du nha vyono yendile mweneco. Hambi nha mukali mukuhemba nha vyono nyenye weneco mwendile du,
9 vyono mukali si nenjila mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumupelenyi yiwe yenyu, i hanhu hono mokwikala nhondo mupolile.
10 Nanye kolo mwakondya kulowoka likolongo lya Yoludani, nayo Muwaha Mulungu, nhondo ho komulecenyi muyisole no kuyitema yisi yiyo wuwe wo wikalo wenyu. Nayo komucitilenyi kuli walugu wenyu wose, nanye mokwikala mupolile du.
11 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, kokwihagulila mweneco, i hanhu hono mosuma kumuhembela. Na kanyhi hanhu baho, ho mowa kumulavizya vilavywa vyenyu vyose, nha vyono nghamulajilizenyi. I vilavywa vyenyu vinyanywe, ne makumbiko ganji ganji, ne zaka zenyu, hamonga ne vilavywa vinulwe vyono nyenye nyo weneco mwehuwiza nha vyono momulavizya Muwaha Mulungu.
12 Mumusangalalile Muwaha Mulungu, nyenye ne wana wenyu, ne wetumami wenyu, ne Walawi wono mwikalane nawo mu makaya genyu, ninga mweze kukumbucila vyono Walawi, wowa wasina yigawo, hamba wuhazi we yisi.
13 Holeka mulecele lukulu kulavizya makumbiko genyu hanhu hohose du, hono muhaguye nyenye nyo weneco.
14 Langa yinojeye wose mulavizye hanhu hamonga du, i hanhu hono Muwaha Mulungu kohahagula, mu wulawa mu yisi monga ya muli zi'si ze milango yenyu. Ne hanhu baho, ho mowa kulavizya makumbiko genyu ge vilavywa vinyanywe, no kwitumila zinji zose zono namulajilizenyi.

15 “Langa zi mitugo zono mosaka kulya nyenye du nyo weneco, azo mosuma kuziwulajila hanhu ho hose hono mwikaye. Nanye mosuma kulya nyama nyinji nha vyono mukusaka, mu nghango nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yakumupelenyi. Ne nyama yiyo, wosuma kuyilya wanhu wose, i wavufywe na wono si wavufywe, nha vyono mulizaga du nyama ye nzela na ye haluzi.
16 Ninga langa, mulecele lukulu kulya sakami yazo, langa mweze kuyita hasi nha malenga.
17 Langa cinhu cocose cono momulavizya Muwaha Mulungu, mulecele lukulu kucilila mu zikaya zenyu, hamba yiwe zaka yo wuhemba, hamba divayi, hamba mafuta, hamba zinyana zinongozi kulelwa ze zing'ombe, hamba ze zingholo, hamba vilavywa vyenyu vye lihewa, hamba vilavywa vinji vinji vye liwala lyolyose lilya.
18 Ne vilavywa vivyo vyose, munojeye muvilile hali Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu. Muvilile i hanhu bahalya du hono yahahaguye u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu. Muvilye nyenye, ne wana wenyu, hamonga ne wetumami wenyu, hamonga ne Walawi wono mwikalane nawo mu makaya genyu. Nanye muzisangalalile zose zono mwitumile.
19 Ninga mulanjilile, mulecele lukulu kuwataga Walawi mu vilo vyenyu vyose, vyo wikale wenyu wa mu yisi yenyu.

20 “Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kolo yayigazimhya mipaka ye yisi yenyu, nha vyono yehuwiza, baho langa ho mosuma kulya nyama nha vyono mwendile.
21 Na hono i hanhu ho kuhembela hawe hali kutali muno muno na hono wikaye, kosuma kuwulaga ya muli ng'ombe zako, hamba muli ngholo zako mu mapango gogose gono wendile, mu nghango ya vyono Muwaha Mulungu yakupelile. Nanye mosuma kuyilya nyama yiyo mu zikaya zenyu, nha vyono nghamulonjelenyi.
22 Kanyhi no munhu wowose u mwavufywe, hamba mono si mwavufywe, wose wosuma kuyilya nyama yiyo, nha vyono walizaga nyama ye nzela hamba ye haluzi.
23 Langa du, mulanjilile, mulecele lukulu kulya nyama yono yikali yina ne sakami yayo, vyono u wumi wuli mu sakami. Holeka si viswanu kulya nyama hamonga no wumi wayo.
24 Kanyhi mulece kuyinoza sakami yiwe yo ndigwa. Langa mweze kuyita hasi nha malenga.
25 Na hono mulibate lilajilizo lili, mbeka Muwaha Mulungu kokwendezwa nanye, na zose zono mowa kwituma, zowa moteswe kuli'nye na kuli wana wenyu.
26 Vilavywa vyose, ne vipegwa vyose, nha vyono mwamwihuwiziza Muwaha Mulungu, mweze kuvitegula baho i hanhu ho wuhembelo.
27 Mweze kulavizya baho ha cikumbicilo co Muwaha Mulungu, i makumbiko ge vilavywa vinyanywe, ne makumbiko gose, gono anye moyilya nyama yayo no kuyita sakami yayo mucanyha ye cikumbicilo.
28 Muzibataje ko wihuwilwa, zose zono nakondya kumulajilizenyi, na zose zono mowa kwituma, zowa moteswe kuli'nye, na kuli wana wenyu cibitilila, hambi nha mowa kwituma zono zili ling'ani, na zono zikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu u Mulungu wenyu.”

Suguzi kuli zo wuhemba vihwani

29 “Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kogongomeza makungugo, nanye muwe kuyisola yisi yawo, no kwikala mumo.
30 Ninga mulanjilile, kolo Muwaha Mulungu yakondya kugongomeza makungugo, mulecele lukulu kuwutyatya wuhembi wawo, hambi kuli'nye nha wowa mutego. Hamba mulece kuwawuza wuza ho mumanye vyono wayihembaga milungu yawo, nanye ho mwije, muhembe nha vyono wene wahembaga.
31 Mulece kumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, mu nzila yono wene wayihembaga milungu yawo yo wulambi. Hambi mu wuhemba milungu yawo, nha wetumaga nghani ze cikomaza zono Muwaha Mulungu si yazendile yaye. Sunga wakulavya ne wana wawo we cilume na we cicekulu wawa kuwakongoga mu moto mu vikumbicilo vyawo.
32 Holeka muzitumaje zose zono nakondya kumulajilizenyi, mulece konjeleza hamba kucepula nghani yo yose muli zizi.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 13

1 “Na hono yejele muli nyenye muwizi, hamba mulolosa ndoto, mono kolajila citanjilo hamba mezino, yawe kutya,
2 ‘Alenyi cihembe no kuyitumamila milungu yinji, yono nanye mukali hamba si nayimanya.’
3 Anye mulecele lukulu kuzihulicila zinghani zakwe munhu yuyo. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, nha yakumujelenyi, ho yamanye hono mbeka mumwendile mwene kwe zi nhumbula zenyu zose, na kwe mitima yenyu yose.
4 Ninga anye mumutyatye du Muwaha Mulungu, mumupele cogopo, kanyhi mumuhulice mwene, mwibate ne malajilizo gakwe, mumuhembe mweneco du yali wene, kanyhi mweze muli wehuwilwa kuli mwene.
5 No muwizi yuyo hamonga no mulolosi we zindoto, awo wanojeye wawulagwe, vyono wakujela kumunozenyi ho mumubedegale Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, mono yamuwusenyi ku wulawa ku Misili, yakamukombozenyi mu wulawa mu wuwanda. Kanyhi wakujela kumukwejenyi ho muyilece nzila yo wumi, yono Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, yamulajilizenyi muyityatye. Holeka wanhu we liwala lilyo, mbeka wanojeye wawulagwe. Na mu nzila yiyo, mo mowuwusiza u wubi muli'nye.

6 “Hamba yawe nduguyo, u mwana wo nyina yako, hamba mwanago we cilume hamba we cicekulu, hamba yawe mugolece wako u mwendece wako, hamba mwiyendi muyago mono mwiyendile nayo muno muno, hono yejele vyono yakusaka yakukwejeleze cinyele ho wuyihembe milungu yinji, i milungu yono nyenye nyo weneco, hamba walezi wenyu si mwayihemba hamba kamonga,
7 i milungu ye wanhu wono mwikalane behi nawo, hamba milungu ye wanhu wono wekaye kutali nanye.
8 Ninga anye mulecele lukulu kumuhuliciza munhu we liwala lilyo, hamba yawe vyono yenha. Kanyhi munhu we liwala lilyo, mulece kumuwonela lusungu hamba kumutetela hamba kumuhundika yaye.
9 Mbeka munhu yuyo yawulagwe! Kanyhi agwe go mweneco yo weze wuli mulongozi wo kumutowanga ne mabwe, hanyuma wanji nawo ho watyatye, wamutowanje ne mabwe.
10 Mumutowanje ne mabwe sunga yafwe. Hambi nha yali kusaka kumupatusenyi, ho muyilece nzila yo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, mono yamuwusenyi ku wulawa ku Misili, yakamukombozenyi mu wulawa mu wawanda.
11 Ne wanhu wose Waisilayeli, wozihulika zono zijeye, nawo wokwinjilwa no woga wuwaha. Na ku soko yiyo, hasina kanyhi hamba monga mono kokwijelela kwituma zimbi zono zinha zizo.

12 “Na kolo mweza kuwa mwikaye mu makaya gono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumupelenyi, mumo mudahile kuletelwa nghani zo wihehemeze, zono zikutya,
13 ‘Wenuka wanhu wanji we cakacaka muli nyenye, wono wakuwakwejeleza wanhu wa mu makaya gawo, wabite wayihembe milungu yono mukali si nayihemba ku wulawa katali.’
14 Holeka hono wuhulice wihehemeze wono winha wuwo, wupiligale kuwuzawuza. Na hono zijele vyono nghani zizo zimbi zili mbeka,
15 ale, wawulagaje wanhu wose wa mwi'kaya lilyo hamonga ne mitugo zawo zose. Wulyongomezezaje lukulu likaya lilyo.
16 Hanyuma wukonghole sawo yawo yose ya mwi'kaya lilyo, wuyilundice yose hamonga mu nzila, hanyuma wuyikongoje no moto hamonga ne likaya lyayo, viwe cilavywa kuli Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu. Nalyo likaya lilyo likale vye 'tongo cibitilila, na hamba si lyolozengwa kanyhi.
17 Nanye mulece kusola cinhu co cose ca muli vinhu vivyo vyono vili mwiko kusola, holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu si komukolelwenyi likuwo yaye, langa komuwonelenyi lusungu, no kumwinjifyenyi muwe wenji, nha vyono yawehuwiza walezi wenyu,
18 hono mugebate malajilizo, nha vyono nimulonjelenyi izuwali, kanyhi mwitume nha vyono Mulungu yakuhila mwitume.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 14

Wulemezwe we makani gono si ganojeye ga mu vililo

1 “Kanyhi anye muli wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu. Holeka lono muli mu vililo ku soko ya wono wakondya kufwa, mulece kwisalanga masale mu miwili yenyu, hamba kwisilula mu vihanda vyo wusu wenyu, nha vyono wanhu wanji wanozaga.
2 Anye muli wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yamuhagulenyi muwe wanhu wakwe mu wulawa mu wanhu wose wono wekaye mono yinze.”

I malimu mavufywe na gono si mavufywe

3 “Mulece kulya cinhu cocose cono Muwaha Mulungu yakondya kucilemeza, vyono cinhu cico cavufywe.
4 Langa malimu agano ahano go mosuma kulya. Ng'ombe, ngholo, mhene,
5 haluzi, nzela, nyonganyonga, mhene ya mu mbago, ne dishoni, ne nhongolo, ne limuhanga.
6 Ne nimu yoyose yono yikwasanuce mabangho gayo kanyhi yono yikusindula.
7 Langa malimu aga, ngamiya, sungula ne mhimbi, hamba ziwe zina mabangho gakwasanuce kanyhi zikusindula, mulece kuzilya, hambi nha nyavufywe.
8 Nayo ngubi hamba yiwe yikwasanuce mabangho gayo, ninga si yisindulaga yaye, holeka nyama yayo mulece kuyilya, hambi nayo, nha nyavufywe ku soko yiyo, hamba no mudunhu wayo mulece kuwupamiza.

9 “Mawala gose ge zisomba za mwi'lenga, zono zina malebeto na zono zina makaka, azo mosuma kuzilya. Langa zono zisina malebeto hamba makaka, azo mulece kuzilya hambi nha nyavufywe ku soko yiyo.
10 Mawala gose ge zindeje ziswanu, mosuma kuzilya.

11 “Langa zindeje azi.
12 Nyaciko, na mwacikwanghwala, ne fulugobe,
13 Laha ne citema, ne limwewe ku hwani yavyo.
14 Ne mahowe ku hwani yavyo.
15 Ne nyacihema, ne maluwatu, ne lidudumizi ne mwewe ku hwani yavyo.
16 Ne lituwi lidodo, ne lituwi liwaha, ne lituwi lye mahembe.
17 Ne zinyamwala, ne zi'ndi, ne nyhinyinha.
18 No mung'wa, ne mhuje, ku hwani yavyo, ne limukutwa, ne libudibudi.
19 Hamonga na vyono vikutambala, na vye mabawa. Avi vyose mulece kuyilya, hambi nha vyavufywe.
20 Ninga zindeje i ziswanu, azo mosuma kuzilya.
21 Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kulya nyama ya nimu yoyose yono yifwiliye yeneco. Langa mosuma kuwaleka i wajenzi wono mwikalane nawo wo wayilye, hamba mosuma kuyiguza kuli wanya makungugo. Hambi anye nha muli wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, muli wanhu wakwe. Kanyhi mulece kusola nyama ye nyana ye ngholo, hamba ye nyana ye mhene no kuyitelecela mu mele ge nyina yayo.”

U wulajilize ku so zaka

22 “Mweze kukumbucila kuwika ha mhelo zaka zenyu, nha cinji monga kwe 'kumi ya mu vilimwa vyose vyono mukutuga mwaka kwa mwaka mu migunda yenyu.
23 Muwe kuvitegula i hanhu hono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yahahagula hawe ho hanhu ho kumuhembela mwene. Nanye baho ho mweze kulila hali mwene, zi zaka zenyu zo wuhemba, na ze divayi, ne mafuta. Hamonga ne zinyana zinongozi kulelwa ze zing'ombe zenyu na ze zingholo zenyu. Mweze kwituma nhavivi, ho musume kwiyijiza vyono yinojeye, mumupele cogopo nhondo zose Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu.
24 Na hono yiwe i hanhu ho wuhembelo hali kutali muno, ku wulawa ha zikaya zenyu hono mwikaye, mu nghango yono si mosuma kuvidinghula vye zaka ku wulawa mu vilimwa vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu kamumotyenyi.

25 “Ale mosuma kuviguza vyose, muzitegule zipesa ze cifo cazo ku hanhu ho wuhembelo.
26 Nanye kolo mweza kufika kuko, mosuma kugula mu zipesa zizo, cocose cono mowa mwendile nyenye, hono ng'ombe, hamba ngholo, hamba divayi hamba cocose cono wendile gwegwe. Nagwe kosuma kulya hali Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako, nagwe kosangalala, gwegwe hamonga na we mhuga yako yose.
27 Ninga mulanjilile, mulecele lukulu kuwasemwa i Walawi wono mwikalane nawo mu makaya genyu, hambi nha wasina ligawo lyawo lyolyose.
28 Na mu ndumilizo yo mwaka we kadatu wa mu myaka yose, mweze kumbucila mumulavizye Mulungu zaka ya mu vilimwa vyenyu vyose, muvitajile mu madongha gono mowa mugawicile mu makaya genyu gose.
29 Ne ndigwa yiyo, yowa so Walawi. Hambi wene nha wasina sawo yinji yo yose. Kanyhi ndigwa yiyo yiwe so wajenzi, ne wana wono wasina walezi wawo, ne wacekulu wafwile, wawo wose wono mwikalane nawo mu makaya genyu. Kolo wakumuzizenyi, nanye muwapele visakwa vyawo. Mweze kunoza nhavivi, nayo Muwaha Mulungu komumotyenyi mu milimo yenyu yose.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 15

Mwaka wo Mupunghati

(Lawi 25:1-7)

1 “Mu mwaka wo mupunghati wa mu myaka yose, weze kuli'nye wo mwaka wo kuwalecela wanhu no kuyinenela migawa yose kuli wose wono anye mukuwadaya mapesa.
2 Na yiyi yo munojeye mwitume nhavivyo. Munhu wo wose mono kowa yamukopeshe Mwiisilayeli muyagwe, hodu yalece kanyhi kumudaya, hamba yalece kwijelela kumudaya, hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha yo yatemizye zizo.
3 Langa mosuma kuwadaya wajenzi vyono muwakopeshe. Ninga mulece kuwadaya vyono muwakopeshe wandugu zenyu Waisilayeli wayenyu.

4 “Nayo Muwaha Mulungu komumotyenyi mu yisi yono yakumupelenyi. Na muli nyenye wose, hasina hamba monga mono kowa muciwa,
5 hono mumuhulice mwene no kwibata zose zono nghumulajilizenyi izuwali.
6 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, komumotyenyi muno, nha vyono yehuwiza. Anye yo mowa kuwakopesha we makungugo ganji menji, ninga hamba si motuga wanjile, wono womunozenyi nanye sunga mubite mu makungugo ganji ho mukakope kuli wene. Kanyhi mowatema we makungugo menji, ninga hasina hamba yikungugo limonga, lyono lyomutemenyi 'nye.

7 “Na hono hawe hoyuli yunji muciwa muli wandugu zenyu Waisilayeli, mwi'kaya lyolyose lya mu yisi yenyu, yono yakumupelenyi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, ale, mulanjilile, mulece kuwa muli wakamu, hamba mulece kulema kumupela wutazi muyenyu yuyo.
8 Langa munojeye mweze no lusungu, mumukopeshe mu nghango ye visakwa vyakwe.
9 Kanyhi mulece kulema kumukopesha cono yakusaka, lunji mukutya vyono mwaka wo mupunghati wo kunenela wanhu migawa nha wuli behi kuza. Mulecele lukulu kuleceleza mahweso gago mabi gamuzizenyi mu zinhumbula zenyu. Na hono wuleme kumukopesha, nayo munhu yuyo komulilila Muwaha Mulungu ku soko yako, nagwe kowa wuli musutyi.
10 Holeka viswanu muwapele ko mutima muzelu, nayo Muwaha Mulungu komumotyenyi mu milimo yenyu yose.
11 Kanyhi mweze kumanya vyono, si moleka kuwa na wa muli wandugu zenyu Waisilayeli, wono wowa waciwa na wono wali mu wanjile. Holeka nghumulajilizenyi vyono, mweze no lusungu kuli wanhu we liwala lilyo.”

Vyono munojeye mwikale ne wetumami

(Wut 21:1-11)

12 “Na hono nduguyo Mwiisilayeli we cilume hamba we cicekulu, yeguze mweneco kuli'gwe, yawe mwitumami wako, yikunojeye wumukombole, yalaza kuwa yafisa myaka mutandatu yo kukwitumamila. Kolo wafika mwaka wo mupunghati, hodu, wumukombole yabite.
13 Na hono wukumukombola, wulece kumukombola yabite yali mawoko.
14 Langa yikunojeye wumupele kulawa muli vyono Muwaha Mulungu yakumotizye, muli ze zingholo, no wuhemba, ne divayi.
15 Mweze kukumbucila vyono, nanye mwali muli wawanda ku Misili, ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakamukombozenyi no kumuwicenyi welecele. Na yiyi yo soko yono yikuleka nane nghumulajilizenyi lilajilizo lili.
16 Ninga hono mwitumami wako yalece kwenda kuwuka baho hako, vyono yakwendile gwegwe, hamonga ne mhuga yako, nayo yawe yakuhila kujendelela kwikalana nagwe.
17 Ale, wumusole wumuwice ha cipimbilamulango ce kaya yako, wumutobole likulu no muhonelo. Nayo alu, kowa mwitumami wako sunga ko ku wufwe wakwe. Ne wetumami we cicekulu, muwatendele nawo nhavivyo.
18 Hamba wulece kuwona vikamu kumukombola mwitumami, hambi nha yakondya kukwitumamila myaka mutandatu, nusu ye cifo cono wali wumulipe mwitumami wo musahala. Mweze kunoza nhavivi, nayo Muwaha Mulungu komumotyenyi mu milimo yenyu yose.”

Zinyana zinongozi kulelwa ze zing'ombe na ze zingholo

19 “Mweze kukumbucila kumuwicila Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, zinyana zinume zinongozi kulelwa ze zing'ombe na ze zingholo zenyu. Mulece kuzitumamila ko mulimo wo wose zi mitugo zizi. Hamba mulece kuzicenyenha zingholo wukunzeje wazo.
20 Nanye hamonga ne zimhuga zenyu, mweze kuzilila hali Muwaha Mulungu mwaka na mwaka, mweze kuzilila i hanhu ha wuhembelo.
21 Ninga hono mutugo wunji wutuje cilema, lunji yiwe yikusenzegula, hamba yabofula, hamba lunji yiwe yina cilema co cose. Hodu mutugo wuwo, mulece kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu.
22 U mutugo wuwo kosuma kuwulya gwegwe go mweneco baho ha kaya yako. Ne nyama yayo munhu wo wose du kosuma kuyilya, u mwavufywe na mono si mwavufywe, nha vyono wosuma kulya nyama ya nzela na ye haluzi.
23 Ninga wulece kwitumamila sakami yazo nha ndigwa, langa wuyimwaje hasi du kotya malenga.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 16

Pasaka

(Wut 12:1-20)

1 “Mweze wukumbucila mwezi wa Abibu, muwe kumunozeza Pasaka Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, mu mwezi wuwo. Hambi mu mwezi wuwo wa Abibu ne cilo, Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha ho yamukombozezenyi ku wulawa ku Misili.
2 Holeka mu mwezi wuwo, munojeye mumuwulajile Pasaka Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu. Mumuwulajile ngholo na ng'ombe mu hanhu ho wuhembelo, ho mumupele cogopo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu.
3 Na lono mukulya ndigwa yiyo, mulece kulya no mukate wono wina cisase. Nanye mojendelela kulya mukate wono wusina cisase vilo mupunghati, nha vyono mwanoza mwizuwa lilya, lono mwawucila musunho ku Misili. Ale muwulya wuwo mukate, nawo wocemwa mukate wo wugaye. Ho mutuje wukumbucile mu vilo vyose vyo wikale wenyu, we lizuwa lilyo lyono mwawucila ku Misili, i hanhu ho wugaye.
4 Kanyhi wukumbucile vyono yunji wo wose wa muli nyenye, mu yisi yose, yalece kuwa na cisase co cose mu kaya yakwe, sunga vipulute vilo mupunghati. Ne nyama nayo yo mutugo, wono muwuwulaje mu cilo ce lizuwa lilongozi, nyama yiyo yiligwe mu cilo cico du, yilece kusigazwa.
5 Mitugo yose ye Pasaka, muyiwulajilaje ha wuhembelo du. Mulece kuyiwulajila caka du, hanhu ho hose mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumupelenyi.
6 Kanyhi mweze kuziwulaga mwi'pango lono lizuwa likugwila, i cipango ce lizuwa lyono mwawucila ku Misili.
7 Nyama yiyo, muyitelece, muyilile i hanhu ho wuhembelo, wose hamonga. Ne mitondo yaco hakali mitondo, muwe kuwuya ku zikaya zenyu.
8 Na ku wulawa baho, mujendelele kulya mukate wono wusina cisase, sunga vipulute vilo mutandatu. Na mwi'zuwa lyo mupunghati, mwihangulize wose hamonga, mumuhembe Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu. Na mwizuwa lilyo, lyowa yizuwa lyo wucehe, mulece kwituma mulimo wunji wo wose.”

Sikukuu ye nhwanga

(Wut 34:22; Lawi 23:15-21)

9 “Mweze kuwala sunga gapulute majuma mupunghati, ku wulawa mwi'zuwa lyono mweza kwanza kududula,
10 hanyuma ho muwe kunoza sikukuu ye nhwanga, mumupele cogopo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu. Na mwi'zuwa lilyo mumulavizye Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, vilavywa vye lihewa, vyono vyolawa mu vinhu vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kowa yamumotyajenyi.
11 Musangalalile hali Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, mwi'zuwa lilyo, hamonga ne wana wenyu we cilume na we cicekulu, ne wetumami wenyu, ne Walawi, ne Wajenzi, hamonga ne wana wono wasina walezi, hamonga ne wacekulu wafwile, wono mwikalane nawo mu makaya genyu. Na zizi zose mweze kuzinozeza i hanhu ha wuhembelo du.
12 Mweze kukumbucila vyono nanye mwali muli wawanda ku Misili, holeka munojeye mugebatile lukulu malajilizo gaga.”

Sikukuu ye Vibanda

(Lawi 23:33-43; Wuw 29:12-39)

13 “Nanye kolo mwakondya kuwutowa wuhemba wenyu, mwakongholeza vyose vya mu matuli, na vya mu vyuga vyenyu, no kuziminya zabibu zenyu, mukumbucile, munoze sikukuu ye vibanda mu vilo mupunghati.
14 Nanye musangalale ne wana wenyu we cilume na we cicekulu, ne wetumami wenyu, ne Walawi, ne Wajenzi, hamonga ne wana wono wasina walezi, hamonga ne wacekulu wafwile wono mwikalane nawo mu makaya genyu.
15 Mumupele Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, cogopo, mumunozeze sikukuu yiyi mu vilo mupunghati. Muyinozeze i hanhu ho wuhembelo du. Munojeye musangalale, hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha kavilugutiza vilimwa vyenyu, hamonga ne milimo yenyu yose.

16 “Wanhu we cilume wa mwi'kungugo lyenyu, yiwanojeye wose, waze ha wuhembelo wamuhembe Muwaha Mulungu nghanghulo yidatu mu mwaka, mu mazuwa ge sikukuu ye Pasaka, sikukuu ye nhwanga ne sikukuu ye vibanda. No munhu wowose mono kokuza ku wuhembi, yimunojeye yaleteleze ne cipegwa,
17 mu nghango vyono yakusuma, mu nghango nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamupelile no kumumotya.”

Kuli zo mulimo we Walongola

18 “Kanyhi muwawikaje walongola hamonga ne watazi wawo, mu makaya gose gono Muwaha Mulungu u Mulungu wenyu yakumupelenyi. Nawo wanhu wawa, u mulimo wawo, wowa kuwalamula wanhu ko wuling'anizi we ling'ani.
19 Walece kuwa no wuwaguzi hamba kuwewonela wanhu. Kanyhi walecele lukulu kubocela mafupo. Hambi mafupo hamonga ne vipegwa, nha vibofuzaga meso ge wanhu we mahala, ne wanhu wenenenyhezi. Sunga viwa kuwanoza wawa kulavya wulamuzi wono si wuli ling'ani.
20 Holeka munojeye muyityatye no kuyibata ling'ani, ho musume kwikala no kuyitema yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumupelenyi, kanyhi ho musume kujendelela kwikala mu yisi yiyo.

21 “Na lono mweza kumuzenjela Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, cikumbicilo, mulece kuwika kanyhi ne cihwani ce libici co mulungu we cicekulu Ashela ha mhelo ye cikumbicilo.
22 Kanyhi mulece hamba kwiyinulila lisumbili lye libwe, ho mulihembe. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha siyakwendezwa nazo.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 17

1 “Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, mutugo wowose, ng'ombe hamba ngholo, yono yina cilema, mwene si yakwendezwa nazo.
2 Na lunji muhulika vyono, linji lya mu makaya genyu, koyuli munhu monga we cilume hamba we cicekulu, mono yetumile wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu,
3 kabena cilagane, yakuhemba milungu yinji no kuyitumamila, lunji lizuwa, hamba mwezi, hamba nyelezi, si nhe lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu.
4 Na hono muzihulice zono zinha zizo, muziwuzilizaje mu yisi yose. Na hono yijele vyono nghani yiyi mbeka vyo yinha mu Waisilayeli.
5 Hodu mumusolaje munhu yuyo, mumuwice kunze ye likaya, mumutowe ne mabwe sunga yafwe.
6 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, yalece kuwulagwa, sunga hawe howali wahomelezi wejete hamba kuciza, wono womuhomelela. Ninga yalece kuwulagwa hono hawe ho yuli muhomelezi monga du.
7 Ne wahomelezi weze wo walongozi wo kumugumilila mabwe, hanyuma wanji nawo ho watyatye, wamugumilile munhu yuyo mabwe. Na mu nzila yiyi, mo kowuwusiza wubi wuwu.

8 “Hanji, zidahile kwijela nghani zono nghamu muno, ku wulamulwe na wawo walongola. Holeka nghani ze liwala lilyo, nha vyono lunji wuhazi wa sawo, hamba witula mazeya, hamba lunji nghani zo wiwulaji. Muli zizi kolo zejela, munojeye mubite ha wuhembelo, i hanhu hono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu yahahagula.
9 Muyilolose nghani yenyu kuli Wakohani Walawi na kuli mulongola mono kowa yali baho mu vilo vivyo, muwalece wene wayilamule nghani yiyo.
10 Nawo womulonjelenyi nha vyono wayilamuye, nanye mobita muketume nha vyono mulonjeywe.
11 Nanye munojeye muwubocele wulamuzi wawo, mutyatye zose nha vyono womulolosezenyi.
12 Na mono kokwijelela yabedegale zono yalonjeywe no mulongola, hamba no mukohani mono yali baho yakumwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu, munhu yuyo yawulagwe. Na mu nzila yiyi, yowa yo nzila yo kuyiwusiza nghani yiyo 'mbi, muli Isilayeli.
13 Nawo wose kolo weza kuzihulika zizi, holeka wokogopa. Na howa hasina kanyhi hamba monga, mono kokwijelela kwituma kanyhi zono zinha zizi.”

Kuli zono zimunojeye mutemi

14 “Nanye kolo mwakondya kuyisola yisi, yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumupelenyi, na kolo mwekala mu yisi yiyo, lunji mosaka muwe no mutemi wenyu nanye, nha vyono genha makungugo ganji gono gamupilimenyi.
15 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, munojeye mulanjilile muno, u munhu mono momuhagula yawe mutemi, yeze munhu mono Muwaha Mulungu mweneco yamuhaguye. Kanyhi yinojeye yalawe muli nyenye nyo weneco. Mulecele lukulu kumuhagula munhu mujenzi yawe mutemi wenyu.
16 Kanyhi mumanye vyono, munhu kuwa mutemi, si vyono ho yawe na fwalasi nyinji so wasilikale wakwe wo wulugu, hamba si vyono ho yawategule wanhu ku Misili wakagule zifwalasi. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yalonga vyono, i wanhu wakwe si wolowuyila lukulu kanyhi ku Misili.
17 Kanyhi munhu kuwa mutemi, si vyono ho yawe ne wagolece wenji, hambi zizo nha zomunoza yamulece Muwaha Mulungu. Na kanyhi hamba si yinojeye mwene ho yawe mugoli wa fweza na zahabu.
18 Ninga munhu kolo yawa mutemi, munhu yuyo, yimunojeye yapegwe citabu cono candicigwe i malajilizo go Mulungu, hamonga ne mejizo, vyono visoleywe mu wulawa mu citabu cono cikalaga ne wakohani Walawi.
19 Nayo yimunojeye yaciwice citabu cico behi no mwene, yawe kucisoma nhondo zose, mu vilo vyakwe vyose vyo wikale wakwe, ho yasume kwiyijiza vyono yimunojeye yamogope Muwaha Mulungu, yazibate ko wihuwilwa, zose zono zandicigwe mu citabu cico yazitume.
20 Na yiyi yo yomunoza yalece kwihwesa nha vyono, mwene yo munhu muswanu sinha wanji i Waisilayeli wayagwe. Kanyhi yomutaza ho yalece kubedegala hamba hadodo i malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu. Nayo holeka kotema myaka minji, ne wanagwe nawo wolitema likungugo lye Waisilayeli mu ndelo nyinji.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 18

I ligawo lye Wakohani

1 “I wakohani wo mulango wa Lawi, wene wasina ligawo lyolyose lye yisi, hamba wutemi, mu Isilayeli, ligawo lyawo, wene, wowa kulya du vyono vikulawa mu vilavywa, hamonga ne makumbiko ganji ganji gono gowa kuletwa so kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu.
2 Wene wowa wasina wuhazi we yisi, nha vyono wenha i wandugu zawo we milango yinji. Langa ligawo lyawo, lyowa cico cilejeho cono wena naco co kuwa wakohani wo Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yehuwiza.
3 Na mu mapango gogose lono ng'ombe hamba ngholo yikulavywa yiwe yikumbiko, wakohani wapegwe ciwoko, ne mazagasa vyono mejete, hamonga ne lilomofu.
4 Kanyhi yiwanojeye wakohani wapegwe malimbuko go wuhemba, ne divayi, hamonga ne mafuta, no wukunzeje wulongozi we zingholo zenyu.
5 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yawuhagula mulango wa Lawi mu wulawa mu milango yenyu yose, wo wumwitumamile mu mulimo wo wukohani cibitilila.

6 “No Mulawi wo wose, kosuma kubita hanhu hohose ha wuhembelo, mu yisi yose ya Isilayeli,
7 yalongoze wuhembi mu wuhembelo wuwo nha mukohani wo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe, yetume mulimo nha vyono wanduguze wose Walawi wono wakwituma baho.
8 Ninga yowa yinojeye wamupele nayo ligawo lye zindigwa lyono viling'aniye ne magawo ge wakohani wayagwe. Ninga kanyhi ho yahanze ne ligawo lyono mhuga yakwe wowa wamuleteye.”

Wusugulwe so wutyatya metumo ga we makungugo ganji

9 “Nanye kolo mweza kwinjila mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumupelenyi. Mwilanjilile ho mulece kuzityatya zinzila zawo ne metumo mabi ga we makungugo gono gali mumo.
10 Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kuwalavya i wana wenyu we cilume hamba we cicekulu wawe kuticimizwa mu moto mu makumbiko genyu, hamba kuwaleceleza wanhu wenyu weyinjize mu wutowa mawizi, hamba mu wutowa cisangu, hamba mu witumamila wuhawi, hamba mu wuvwala mhiji.
11 Kanyhi mulece kuwaleceleza ho weyinjize mu wuhimbuzi, hamba mu wu wuza we macisi, hamba mu wunoza masoce, hamba mu wuwuza myahe ye wafu.
12 Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yawasulile wanhu wono wakwituma zimbi ze liwala lilyo. Co cikuleka yakugawinga gago makungugo, gawuce mu yisi, so cambuzi cenyu.
13 Holeka mweze mukwilaniciye muno muno hali Muwaha Mulungu.”

Wihuwizo wo Kumutuma Muwizi

14 Hanyuma Musa yakawalonjela yakatya, “Mu yisi yono mweza kubita kwinjila no kuyisola, i wanyawusi wa mumo, wendile kuwahulika wono wakutowa mawizi, na wono wakutowa cisangu. Ninga anye mwilanjilile, hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha si yakumukombolenyi nanye mwitume zono zinha zizo.
15 Langa mwene komuletelenyi muwizi, mono kolawa muli nyenye nyo weneco, mono kowa yenha nene. Nanye mumuhulice yuyo.
16 Kanyhi mukumbucile mwizuwa lilyo, lono mwali mwihangulize he 'tunda lya Sinayi, mwatya vyono si mosaka mwidome no Muwaha Mulungu yalongane nanye kanyhi, hamba si mwasaka muwuwone no wusu wakwe, vyono mogopaga mulece kufwa.
17 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakanonjela yakatya, ‘Heya wanoza wulamule wuswanu we mahala.
18 Holeka alu nowaletela muwizi mono yehwanile gwegwe, mono kolawa muli weneco. Nane nomulonjela zono kolonga, nayo kowalonjela i wanhu zinghani zose zono nowa kulajiliza.
19 No muwizi yuyo kowa kulonjela mwitagwa lyangu. Nane nomulipiciza munhu wo wose, mono si komuhulika.
20 Na hono yejele muwizi wo wose, mono kokwijelela yalitumamila litagwa lyangu, mu zinghani zono ane si nimutumile yazilonje, muwizi yuyo yimunojeye yafwe so nghani yiyo. Na yowa nhavivyo, sunga na kuli wawizi wanji wono wowa kulonjela mwi'tagwa lye milungu yinji.’

21 “Nagwe hono wulonjele mu nhumbula yako wuwe kutya, ‘Alu, ho coyimanya nhawule nghani yiyo, hono yiwe si yilawile kuli Muwaha Mulungu?’
22 Ninga moyimanya nhavi, ‘Hono muwizi yalonjele nghani mwi'tagwa lyangu, na zono yalonjile ho zilece kufisilizwa, baho ho momanyila vyono nghani yiyi si yilawile kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Muwizi yuyo kalonga zakwe mweneco du. Holeka nanye mulece kumogopa yaye.’ ”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 19

Makaya go wutizizo

(Wuw 35:9-28; Yos 20:1-9)

1 “Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kolo yakondya kuwongomeza, i wanhu wono wekaye mu yisi yono yakumupelenyi nyenye, nanye kolo mwakondya kugasola makaya gawo, hamonga ne zikaya zawo, no kwikala mumo,
2 nhondo muyigawe yisi yilawe hadatu, na mumo mose, mwisiwanulile likaya limonga limonga, lyono lyowa kupelu kulificila mbela mbela.
3 Ho yiwe, hono munhu yawulaje muyagwe, yasume kutiziza mwi 'kaya limonga lya mu makaya gaga madatu, ho yeponye.
4 Na hono munhu yamuwulaje muyagwe mhalanyuka, mono si mulugu wakwe, kosuma kutiziza mwa linji lya muli makaya gaga madatu, ho yeponye.
5 Lunji citye, wanhu wejete wabitana mu mbago mu wutenya zingwagu, na muli wene, yunji kawa yakutema libici yaligwise, na mu wunoza nhavivyo, nhemo yakwe yiwa kukongoloka mu caka cayo, yiwa kumuwulaga muyagwe, ale u munhu yuyo, kosuma kutiziza mwa linji lya muli makaya gaga madatu ho yeponye.
6 Holeka yinojeye hawe na makaya madatu, hambi hono hawe ne 'kaya limonga du, lyowa lili kutali muno ku wufika mbela, u munhu mono yakusaka kwiponya. Vyono yuyulya munya nduguye mono yawulagwe, kosuma kumuwinga no kumwibatila mu nzila no kumuwulaga kwe 'kuwo, mbula soko, so wilipicize we lifwa lyo nduguye. Nayo kumbe ho kamuwulaga mhalanyuka du, hamba si yali mulugu wakwe yaye.
7 Na yiyi yo soko yono yikuleka nghumulajilizenyi mwisiwanulile makaya madatu.

8 “Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kolo yakondya kuyonjeleza mipaka ye yisi yenyu, nha vyono yawehuwiziza walezi wenyu, no kumupelenyi yisi yose,
9 baho mosiwanula makaya ganji kanyhi madatu. (Nayo komupelenyi yisi yiyi hono mwibate zose zono nghumulajilizenyi izuwali, na hono mumwende Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, na hono mujendele mu zinzila zakwe.)
10 Holeka musiwanule makaya gago, ho mulece kuwambuza iwanhu wono wasina masutya wawulagwe mbula soko, nanye kanyhi nhavivyo, ho mulece kuwalilwa masutya bwete so wuleceleza i wanhu we liwala lilyo, wawulajilwe mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu yakumupelenyi.

11 “Ninga munhu hono yawe yena simbili no muyagwe, ho yamusepe yamuwulaje, hanyuma yatizize mwa linji lya muli makaya gaga madatu ho yeponye.
12 Mu nghani yono yinha yiyo, i walongola we likaya lyono yakulawilila munhu yuyo, wamutyatye kuko, wakamusole, wamuwice mu miwoko ye wanya ndugu wa mono yawulagwe. Nawo wanya ndugu, wamuwulaje yuyo muwulaji wo ndugu yawo, welipicize lifwa lyo ndugu yawo.
13 Mulecele lukulu hamba kumuwonela lusungu munhu we liwala lilyo. Nanye muwuwusizaje lukulu u wubi wuwu wo wuwulaji mu Isilayeli, nanye ho mutuje wumoteswe.”

Kuli ze mipaka yono yawikwa katali

14 “Mulece kuwusa vitanjilo vye mipaka ye wayenyu vyono wawika we katali, mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumupelenyi yiwe yisi yenyu.”

Kuli zo Wuhomelezi

15 “Wuhomelezi wo munhu monga, si wokwila yaye kumuhomelela munhu vyono yena masutya, langa sunga hawe na wuhomelezi wa wanhu wejete hamba wadatu.
16 Na hono munhu yejelele yasace yamutamise muyagwe yunji, ko wuhomelezi wo wulambi.
17 Wanhu wawo, u musitakwa hamonga na mono yakumusitaka muyagwe, wose vyono wejete, wategulwe i hanhu ho wuhembelo, ho walamulwe ne wakohani, hamonga ne wanyamhala wono wowa wali baho mu vilo vivyo.
18 Nawo wanyamhala woziwuza wuza, na hono zijele vyono, u munhu yuyo kamuhomelela, no kumusitaka Mwiisilayeli muyagwe ko wulambi,
19 munhu yuyo yapegwe wulipicizwe wu wulya wono yali yalipicizwe yuyulya mono yali yasitakwa. Na mu nzila yiyo, yo kowuwusiza u wubi we liwala lilyo.
20 Nawo wanhu wose wozihulika zizo, nawo wokogofywa. Na howa hasina hamba monga kanyhi, mono kokwituma zimbi zono zinha zizo.
21 Na mu vyalo vye liwala lilyo, mulece kuwana lusungu. Ne ndipiciza yayo, yowa yili nhavi: Wumi kwa wumi, liso kwa liso, lino kwa lino, muwoko kwa muwoko, mugulu kwa mugulu.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 20

Kuli zo wulugu

1 “Na lono mukubita ku wulugu kwihoma ne walugu wenyu, na kolo mukuwona kuko matuka go wulugu, hamonga ne zifwalasi, ne wasilikale wo wulugu wenji wacizize liwalo lyenyu, anye mulece kuwogopa hamba hadodo. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, mono yakamuponyenyi ku wulawa mu yisi ye Misili, nha kowa yali hamonga nanye.
2 Na lono mowa mukali nanza kwihoma ne walugu wenyu, yinojeye mukohani yabite yeme ha mwande yenyu, yawe kutya,
3 ‘Muhulicize 'nye nyo Waisilayeli! Izuwali mweza kubita ku wihomi ku wulugu, ninga nghumutyenyi, mulece kuwogopa walugu wenyu, hamba nhumbula zenyu zilece kuzilika, hamba kucicima yaye.
4 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha yali hamonga nanye, yo komulewelenyi no kumupelenyi wusume.’

5 “I walongola nawo, wawalonjele wanhu, watye nhavi, ‘Wulenyi, ho yuli munhu bahano, mono yazenga kaya ninga yali yakali nayisiwanula? Hono yawe yali baha, ale yawuye ku kaya. Yisolaga lunji kawulagwa kuko ku wulugu. Hanyuma ne kaya yakwe yiwe kusiwanulwa na munhu yunji.
6 Hamba wulenyi, ho yuli munhu bahano mono yalimile mugunda wa zabibu, nayo yali yakali natuga cilejeho co kuzidudula? Ale yawuye ku kaya. Yisolaga lunji, kawulagwa ku wulugu. Hanyuma ne mikalo yakwe yiwe kumotwa no munhu yunji.
7 Hamba wulenyi, ho yuli munhu bahano, mono yabanyile mucekulu, ho yatole? Ale yawuye ku kaya. Yisolaga lunji kawulagwa kuko ku wulugu. Hanyuma munhu yunji yamutole mucekulu mono vyali vitole nayo.’
8 Nawo walongola wawalonjele kanyhi wanhu watye nhavi, ‘Wulenyi, ho yuli munhu bahano, mono yena woga, yakucicima mu nhumbula? Ale yawuye ku kaya, ho yalece kuwogofya ne wayagwe.’
9 Na kolo walongola wakondya kulongana ne wasilikale wo wulugu, wahagulwe walongola wono wosuma kuvilongoza vipuga vipuga vye wasilikale.

10 “Na lono mweza kulihoma likaya, nghani nongozi, mweze kuwalecela wanhu wa mwi'kaya cilejeho, wamule weyikombole weneco kuli'nye.
11 Na hono wamudigulilenyi milango ye likaya, kanyhi wekombole kuli'nye, ale, wanhu wose wono wali mumo mwi'kaya, wowa weza wetumami wenyu.
12 Ninga hono wanhu wa mwi'kaya lilyo, waleme kunoza mulamu nanye, wawe wahagula weneco wehome nanye, ale, wasilikale wenyu walizinjilizaje likaya lilyo.
13 Na kolo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakondya kumupelenyi likaya lilyo mwaliteka, ale, muwawulagaje wanhu walume wose wono wali mumo.
14 Langa i wacekulu ne wana, ne zimitugo, hamonga ne vinhu vyose vyono vyowa vili mumo mwi'kaya lilyo, avyo vyose muvisolaje viwe vyenyu. Ne vinhu vyose vye walugu wenyu vyono movisola, avyo mudahile kuvitumamila. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha kamupelenyi viwe vyenyu.
15 Nhavivyo vyo mugatendelaje makaya gago, gono gali kutali ne yisi yono mokwikala.

16 “Ninga langa lono mukugahoma no kugateka makaya gono gali mu yisi yono Mulungu yakumupelenyi, ale, muwawulagaje wanhu wose wono wali mumo, mulece kusigaza hamba monga bwete.
17 Muwongomezezaje lukulu wanhu wose, i Wahiti, ne Waamoli, ne Wakanani, ne Wapelizi, ne Wahivi, ne Wayebusi, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajilizenyi mwitume.
18 Muwawulajilaje lukulu wose, ho waze walece kumunozenyi, mumusutizye Muwaha Mulungu, wamwijizenyi mwitume mawifyo gawo gono wakwituma, go wuhemba milungu yawo.

19 “Nanye lono mweza kulihoma no kuliteka likaya, mulece kuyitemanga mibici ye mikalo, ho mulece kuhahodanga hanhu ha wikalo wenyu. Langa, anye mulye mikalo yayo du, ninga mulece kuyitemanga mibici. Hambi i mibici, nha musina wulugu nayo.
20 Langa mosuma kugatemanga mabici ganji gono si ge mikalo, mugetumamile mabici gayo so wuzenjela ngazi zo kupandila mucanyha ye majinji ge likaya, lyono mukwihoma nalyo, sunga kolo mwalisuma no kuliteka.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 21

Za kuli munhu mono siyakumanyika na mono yamuwulaje

1 “Na hono yejele munhu yafwiye mu mawimbi genyu, mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumupelenyi, na yijele vyono u munhu mono yamuwulaje si yakumanyika.
2 Ale, i Walongola wenyu, hamonga ne wanyamhala wenyu, wabite wakapime wutali wo wulawa hanhu baho hono yuyo munhu yafwiliye, sunga ku cikaya ne vikaya vyono vipilime behi ne hanhu baho.
3 Hanyuma i walongola we cikaya cono cokwijela co cili behi ne hanhu baho, hono munhu yuyo yafwiliye, ale, wasole nzeku mwitewa lilyo lya ha cikaya cico, nzeku yono yikali netumamilwa hamba mulimo wo wose.
4 Wayisole nzeku yiyo, wayitegule sunga ha cikolongo cono si cinyalaga malenga yaye, cono cili mwi'lolo lyono si likuwa kulimwa, hamba kuhadikwa yaye. Nawo kolo wayifisa baho nzeku yiyo, wayibene singo.
5 Nawo wakohani Walawi wanojeye weze wali baho. Hambi nha wowanojeye walamule zinghani zo wuwulaji. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu nha yawahagula wamwitumamile no kulavizya wulugute mwi'tagwa lyakwe.
6 Nawo walongola wono wakulawa mu cikaya cono cili behi ne hanhu hono yafwiliye munhu yuyo, wowa wanojeye wayizuguzize miwoko yawo mucanyha ye nzeku yiyo,
7 akuno wakulonga wakutya, ‘Ase si cimuwulaje munhu yuyu, kanyhi hamba si cimumanyile na mono yamuwulaje.
8 Holeka go Muwaha Mulungu, wuwanenele wanhu wako Waisilayeli, wono wawakomboza ku wulawa ku Misili. Wucinenele, kanyhi wulece kuciwalila wubi wo wuwulaji wo munhu yuyu mono yasina wusutye.’
9 Na mu wituma zizo zono Muwaha Mulungu yakuhila, nanye ho si mowalilizwa mwisutya lili lyo wuwulaji.”

Kuli ze wacekulu wono wakutekwa ku wulugu

10 “Nanye kolo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yamupelajenyi wusume mu wulugu, nanye kolo mwatuga mateka wenji,
11 na mu mhuga yiyo ye mateka wenyu, muwe mowali ne wacekulu waswanu, wono mowasulumila no kusaka kuwatola.
12 Mudahile kuwasola muwategule ku zikaya zenyu. Nayo mucekulu yuyo yanojeye yacete zimvwili zakwe, kanyhi yadumule ne makombazala gakwe,
13 yakawanize ne myenda yakwe. Kanyhi yimunojeye yekale mu nyumba yako, yawalilile walezi wakwe mu mwezi monga. Hanyuma ho wuwe kumutola yawe mugolece wako.
14 Na hanyuma hono wuwe wamukatala, hamba si wukumusaka kanyhi yaye. Hodu wumulece yabite. Na vyono wali wamutendela kwe vilungo no kugona nayo, holeka si kosuma kanyhi kumwitumamila nha muwanda, hamba si kosuma kumuguza kuli munhu yunji yaye.”

Kuli zo wuhazi wono wuwanojeye i wana we cilume i walongozi kulelwa

15 “Na hono munhu yawe yena wacekulu wejete, na wose walele wana we cilume. Ninga u mwana mulongozi kulelwa yawe si yaleligwe no mucekulu mono yuyo munhu yamwendile.
16 Lizuwa kolo lyeza kufika lyo kuwagawila wuhazi, wawo i wanagwe vyono wejete, si kosuma kumulongoza u mwana mudodo mono yaleligwe no mucekulu mono yamwendile, yamupele ligawo yiwaha, lyono lyali limunojeye u mwana mulongozi kulelwa yo yapegwe.
17 Langa yowa yimunojeye yamupele ligawo liwaha yuyulya du u mwana mulongozi kulelwa, hamba yawe siyaleligwe no mucekulu u mwendece wakwe. U munhu yuyo yanojeye yamanye vyono, u mwanagwe mulongozi kulelwa, yo yanojeye du yapegwe ligawo liwaha, lyono limunojeye mu ling'ani yakwe yo mulelwa mulongozi.”

Za kuli mwana we cilume mono yali cibede

18 “Na lunji munhu kowa yena mwana mono yali cibede, yasina cihuliko hamba hadodo kuli walezi wakwe, hamba watowe muno, ninga vyo bwete du.
19 Ale, u mwana we cilume we liwala lilyo, walezi wakwe wamusole, wamutegule kuli walongola we cikaya cono wekaye, wakamuling'anize.
20 Wabite wawalonjele walongola wawo watye, ‘Ayu mwana wetu yali cibede kanyhi yasina cihuliko hamba hadodo, hamba si yakucihulika yaye. Mulimo wakwe kugala wujimbi du no kwajiliza mapesa mu zinzila zono zinojeye.’
21 Nawo i wanhu we cilume wa mwi'kaya, wasole mabwe wamutowanje sunga yafwe. Nanye holeka mosuma kuwuwusa wubi wuwu. Ne wanhu wose i Waisilayeli, wozihulika zizi zono zijeye, holeka nawo wotuga woga.”

Imalajilizo ganji ganji

22 “Na hono munhu yawulagwe so yisutya, no muwili wakwe wutumbikwe mwibici,
23 muwili wuwo wulece kwikala cilo cose mumo mucanyha mwibici, langa wunojeye wuzikwe mwi'zuwa lilyo du. Hambi mono yakutumbikwa, nhe lijito lyo Mulungu. Holeka yinojeye muwuzice muwili wuwo ho wulece kuyavufya yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumupelenyi.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 22

1 “Na hono muyiwone ng'ombe hamba ngholo yo Mwiisilayeli muyenyu yajiliye, mulece kuyileceleza, langa wuyisole mu mugomolele munya mutugo.
2 Na hono yiwe munya mutugo yekaye kutali, hamba lunji kowa si wumumanyile munya mutugo, ale wuwutegulaje mutugo wuwo ku kaya yako. Na hono munya mutugo yaze yawe kuwulondola, ale, wumupelaje.
3 Mweze kunoza nhavivyo kuli vinhu vyose vyono mowa kuviwona, hono yiwe ndogowe, hamba citema co mwenda, hamba ciwe cinhu cocose du cono Mwiisilayeli muyago kowa yajilize.

4 “Na hono muyiwone ndogowe hamba ng'ombe yo Mwiisilayeli muyenyu yigwiye hasi, mulece kuyileceleza, langa mumutazaje, muyinule, muyimye viswanu.

5 “Iwacekulu, walece kuvwala myenda ye cilumelume, hamba nawo i walume, walece kuvwala myenda ye cicekulu. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha si yakwendezwa ne wanhu wono wakwituma zono zinha zizi.

6 “Na hono wuwe wukujenda mu nzila, wuwone linhuli lya ndeje mu mubici, hamba hasi, lina ne ndeje nyina yayo, yina magangha, hamba yina mana gayo, wulece kuyisola nyina yayo hamonga ne mana.
7 Langa kosuma kusola mana, ninga nyina yayo wuyilece yibite. Nagwe i vilo vyako vyo wikale wako ho viwe vinji, hamonga no wumoteswe.

8 “Na lono wukuzenga kaya yako mhya, wuyipilimyaje litembe lyayo ne cicitizo. Ho wulece kwiletela wuhoswe go mweneco, hono munhu yagwe ku wulawa mwi'tembe, yafwe.

9 “Mulece kuhadika mbeyu ye liwala linji muyihanzahanze hamonga mu migunda yenyu ya zabibu. Na hono munoze nhavivyo, hamba si motuga cambuzi co cose, mbeyu yono muhadice hamba si yolela, ne zabibu zeneco nazo hamba si zolela.

10 “Mulece kuzilimya ndogowe ne ng'ombe hamonga.

11 “Mulece kuwa mukuvwala myenda yono yitowece sufu ne bafuta.

12 “Mu myenda yenyu yo kwifunika, mweze kuyihonela masune mu mamhembo gose vyono mane.

Malajilizo ku so wahinza wono wakali namanya walume

13 “Na hono munhu yatole muhinza, nayo yalaza kuwa yagona nayo, u mulume kanyhi, ho yawe kumulema muhinza no kumulonga makani mabi no kulihodanga litagwa lyo muhinza yakutya,
14 ‘Ane namutola muhinza ayuno, ninga lono neza kugona nayo, hamba si mbwene citanjilo cono cikulajila vyono yali yakali namanya walume.’
15 Holeka hono ze liwala lili zijele, walezi wo muhinza yuyo, wasole mwenda we halusi, wono wali wawutandice mu citanda mu cilo cico, wono wina ne citanjilo ce sakami, yono yikulajila vyono si yali yamanyile walume, wawulajile kuli wanyamhala, ha mahakama. Wuwe wuhomelezi vyono muhinza yuyo yali yakali namanya walume.
16 Nayo sogwe yo muhinza yawalonjele wanyamhala yatye, ‘Ane namukombolela munhu ayu u mwanangu ho wetole, ninga aluno yeza si yakumusaka kanyhi yaye.
17 Na kanyhi kanoza nghani 'mbi muno muno kuli mwanangu, vyono mwene yakutya, mwanangu yali yakondya kumanya walume, lono wali kwitola nayo. Ninga alu, awuno wo wuhomelezi wayo, wono wukulajila vyono mwanangu yali yakali si namanya walume. Lanjenyi, ayi yo sakami mu mwenda we halusi, wono wali wawutandice mu citanda ne cilo!’
18 Hanyuma wanyamhala wamusole mulume yuyo, wamukunanje.
19 Kanyhi wamutoweze ne fayini ya fweza yigana limonga, fweza yiyo wamupele u sogwe yo muhinza. Hambi u munhu yuyo nha kamuletela cevu ciwaha muhinza we Ciisilayeli. Kanyhi hamba nhavivyo, mulume yuyo yajendelele kwitola du no muhinza yuyo. Hamba sikomulecela lukulu kanyhi sunga ko ku wufwe.

20 “Ninga langa hono nghani yiyo yo muhinza yuyo yijele vyono mbeka yali yakondya kumanya walume,
21 ale, wamusole muhinza yuyo wamuwice ha mulango, hanze ye kaya yo sogwe. Ne walume wa mwi'kaya lilyo, wamutowanje ne mabwe sunga yafwe. Hambi nha kanoza nghani ya cevu ciwaha mu wanhu wetu, so wumanya walume lono yakali natolwa, kanyhi yakali yekaye mu nyumba ye walezi wakwe. Na mu nzila yiyi vyo kowuwusa u wubi muli nyenye.

22 “Na hono mulume yebatwe yagonile no mucekulu wo muyagwe, wose vyono wejete wawulagwe. Na mu nzila yiyi, vyo kowuwusa wubi muli Isilayeli.

23 “Na hono mulume yebatwe mwi'kaya yagonile no muhinza mono yabanyigwe no muyagwe yunji,
24 ale, muwasolaje wose vyono wejete, muwategule kunze ye likaya, muwatowanje ne mabwe sunga wafwe. Muhinza yanojeye yawulagwe, vyono si yacenjile yaye ho yatuje wutazi. Na kumbe yali yali mwi'kaya, i hanhu hono i wanhu wali wamuhulice vyono yakucenga. Nayo mulume yuyo yawulagwe, vyono kagona no muhinza mubanywe. Na mu nzila yiyi, vyo kowuwusa u wubi muli nyenye.

25 “Na lunji yisolaga, mulume kamwagana mu mawimbi muhinza mono mubanywe na yunji, nayo ho yamunozeze vilungo no kugona nayo. U mulume yuyo mweneco du yo yanojeye yawulagwe.
26 Ninga muhinza yalece kunozwa cinji yaye, vyono mwene si yetumile wubi wono wumunojeye yawulagwe. Hambi nghani yono yinha yiyi, nha yihwanile ne nghani yo munhu mono yakumuhungucila muyagwe no kumuwulaga.
27 Vyono munhu yuyo nha kamwagana mu mawimbi u muhinza mubanywe, kawa kumuhungucila no kugona nayo kwe vilungo. Holeka hamba muhinza yawe kacenga, nha kutali, nani mono yali yamuhulice no kumutaza.

28 “Na lunji yisolaga mulume kamwibata kwe vilungo u muhinza mono yakali nabanywa na yunji, no kugona nayo.
29 Ale mulume yuyo yamupelaje sogwe yo muhinza yuyo, fweza makumi mahano. Kanyhi mulume yuyo yimunojeye yamutole muhinza yuyo. Hambi nha mweneco yamuhunguciye no kugona nayo kwe vilungo. Kanyhi hamba si komulecela lukulu sunga ko ku wufwe.
30 Kanyhi yili mwiko kuli mwana we cilume yagone no mucekulu yunji wa muli wacekulu wo sogwe, hambi nha nghani ya cevu kuli sogwe.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 23

Wusiwanulwe we wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu

1 “Munhu wo wose we cilume mono mupaswe, hamba mono yadumuywe mapinde gakwe, munhu yuyo yalece kuwalilizwa hamonga ne wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu.

2 “No mwana mutumulwa, nayo nhavivyo yalece kuwalilizwa hamonga ne wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu. Sunga ko ku lulelo lwakwe lwe likumi, yalece kuwalilizwa hamonga ne wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu.

3 “Hamba hasina Mwamoni, hamba lunji Mumowabu wo wose, hamba munhu wo wose wa muli lulelo lwawo, sunga ko ku lulelo lwe likumi, mono kosuma kuwalilizwa hamonga ne wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu.
4 Hambi wene nha walema kumupelenyi ndigwa ne malenga lono mwali muli mu muhinzo, mukulawilila ku Misili. Kanyhi wo wawo wono wamusola Balamu u mwana wa Beyoli, ku wulawa mwi'kaya lya Petoli ya ku Mesopotamiya, wakamulipa, ho yaze yamulijitenyi.
5 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, hamba si yendezwa ne nghani yiyo, holeka hamba si yamuhuliciza Balamu. Muwaha Mulungu yakaliwaniza lilijito lilyo, wukawa wulugutwe kuli'nye, vyono mwene yamwendilenyi 'nye.
6 Holeka nanye mulecele lukulu kuwapela wutazi wowose, hamba kuwanozeza ziswanu mu vilo vyenyu vyose vyo wikale wenyu.

7 “Ninga langa mulece kuwasula i Waedomu, hambi awo nha wandugu zenyu. Ne Wamisili nawo mulece kuwasula, hambi nanye nha mwekala nha muli wajenzi mu yisi yawo.
8 Ne wana wawo wo lulelo lwe kadatu wono wolelwa, awo wosuma kuwalilizwa hamonga ne wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu.”

Kuli zo wezwe we nghambi ku wulugu

9 “Nanye lono mwenjila mu nghambi ku wulugu, mu madaha go wihomi, ale, munojeye mwilanjilile, mulece kwituma nghani yinji yo yose yono yidahile kumwavufyenyi.
10 Na hono hawe ho yuli mono yeloteye ne cilo no kulavya mbeyu yo lulelo, ale munhu yuyo kowa yakondya kwiyavufya. Holeka munhu yuyo yimunojeye yawuce mu nghambi yakekale kunze.
11 Sunga kolo gafika mapango ge misi, ale ho yehovuje malenga. Ne lizuwa kolo lyeza kugwila, ho yawuye mu nghambi kanyhi.

12 “Kanyhi munojeye mweze na hanhu hanji kunze ye nghambi yenyu, i hanhu hono mohetumamila nha coloni.
13 Na lono weza kubita coo, wudinghulize ne lisili lidodo, nagwe kolo wasunzumala, wuhimbehimbe baho hasi cikombo, nagwe kolo wakondya, ale wufucilize ne lisili lyako vyono vikulawa muli'gwe.
14 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha kowa kupilimapilima baho mu nghambi yenyu, ho yamuponyenyi kanyhi yamupelenyi wusume kuli walugu wenyu. Ku soko yiyo, holeka nghambi yenyu, yinojeye yeze yelile, ho Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yalece kuwona muli'nye cinhu cinji cocose cavu, yamulecenyi.”

I malajilizo ge hayo hayo

15 “Na hono mwitumami wa munhu yunji yeheme ko munya mwitumami, yatizize kwako, hodu wulece nagwe kumugomola.
16 Hambi nha kosuma kwikala hanhu ho hose mu makaya genyu, nha vyono kokwenda kuhagula mweneco. Ninga nanye mulanjilile mulece kumwiwonela.

17 “Kanyhi halece kuwa ne wamalaya we cidini, muli wanhu Waisilayeli, muli we cilume, hamba muli we cicekulu.
18 Kanyhi ne zipesa zozose zono zikulawa mumo mu wumalaya, zilecele lukulu kulavywa mu Nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, ziwe so wilahize wawo. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, nha si kendezwaga no wumalaya.

19 “Na hono wumukopeshe nduguyo Mwiisilayeli ndigwa hamba mapesa, wulece kudaya ngomola.
20 Langa mosuma kudaya ngomola kuli wajenzi, muli vinhu vyono mowakopesha, ninga si muli vyono muwakopeshe wandugu zenyu Waisilayeli. Nane nghumunenenyi mwibataje lilajilizo lili, holeka Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nayo koyilugutiza milimo yenyu yose mu yisi yono moyisola yiwe yenyu.
21 Na lono mukunoza wilahize wowose kuli Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, mulece kwitendya, langa musimbilile sunga muwufisilize wihuwize wenyu, hambi Muwaha Mulungu wenyu nha kowa yalindiye du wufisilize wo wilahize wako kuli mwene. Na wowa wubi kuli'gwe, hono wulece kufisiliza wilahize wako.
22 Hamba si wubi yaye hono wulece kwilahiza kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
23 Nazo zono walonga ko mulomo wako, zo kumwihuwiza Muwaha Mulungu wako, wunojeye wuzifisilize, hambi nha cilavywa ce lihewa, cono wehuwiza gwegwe go mweneco ko mulomo wako.

24 “Na lono wuwe wukolile mu nzila yono yikoleye mu mugunda wa zabibu wo muyago, hono wende, kosuma kuhepa zabibu no kulya mu nghango nha vyono wukusaka go mweneco. Langa wulecele lukulu kusola zabibu zinji, no kuzitajila mu ciya no kubita nazo.
25 Nhavivilya kanyhi, hono wuwe wukolile mu nzila, yono yikoleye mu mugunda wo wuhemba wa yunji. Kosuma kubena ne miwoko yako, i maso go wuhemba no kuwufijiha no kuwulya, mu nghango nha vyono wukusaka go mweneco. Langa wulecele lukulu kududula ne nzolo wuhemba wo muyago no kubita nawo.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 24

Kuli ze Talaka, no wutola mucekulu yunji kanyhi

1 “Na hono munhu we cilume yatole mucekulu, na hanyuma kanyhi ho yalece kwendezwa nayo, vyono lunji kawona nghani za cevu muli mucekulu yuyo. Na hanyuma yamupele talaka no kumuleka.
2 No mucekulu yuyo ho yatolwe na mulume yunji kanyhi,
3 no mulume yuyo nayo we kejete yekale si yendezwe no mucekulu yuyo, nayo yamupele talaka.
4 Na lunji u mulume yuyu we kejete yafwe. Na hono yiwe nhavivyo, u mulume wakwe mulongozi, si kosuma kanyhi kumutola yaye, hambi mucekulu yuyo, nha yakondya kwiyavufya. Holeka hono yamutole kanyhi, lyowa yiwifyo hali Muwaha Mulungu. Holeka mulecele lukulu kwituma wubi wono winha wuwo, mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumupelenyi.”

I malajilizo ge hayo hayo

5 “Nayo munhu we cilume, hono yawe ho yakutola mucekulu, yalece kwinjizwa baho baho mu mulimo wo wusilikale wo wulugu, hamba mu mulimo wunji wowose. Langa wamulekaje ha kaya yakwe mwi'daha lya mwaka monga, ho yekale hamonga no mugolece wakwe, yamusangalaze.

6 “Na hono wumukopeshe muyago cinhu cinji, wulece kusaka yapinjice kuli'gwe luzala hamba sago yayo. Ku soko hono wunoze nhavivyo, nha wukuwawusiza citumamilwa cono cikuwataza, ku wonozeleza ndigwa yawo yo kulya ho wawumipe.

7 “Munhu wo wose mono komuhiza nduguye Mwiisilayeli, no kumunoza yawe mwitumami wakwe, hamba kumuguza ku wuwanda, munhu yuyo yanojeye yawulagwe. Na mu nzila yiyo, vyo kowuwusa wubi we liwala lilino.

8 “Na lono watuga wutamwa wo wukoma, wulanjilile witume nha vyono Wakohani Walawi wowa kukulonjela. Weze kwituma zose nha vyono nawalonjela.
9 Akuno wukukumbucila zono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako, yamunozeza Miliyamu mu nzila, lono mwalawila ku Misili.
10 Na hono wumukopeshe munhu cinhu cinji, wulece kubita no kwinjila mu kaya yakwe, no kuwusola mwenda wakwe wono yakukupela wuwe nha cipingo.
11 Langa wulindile hadeha du, sunga mweneco yenjile mu kaya yakwe, yasole yakupele.
12 Na hono yawe muciwa, wulece kwikala no mwenda wuwo mu mapango ge cilo.
13 Langa weze kumugomolela nhondo zose mu mapango ge cinyamihe, ho yefunice ne cilo. Nayo mbeka kokulumbiliza muno muno. No Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nayo kokwendezwa nagwe muno muno.

14 “Mulece kuwewonela i wetumami waciwa, hamba i waghwenzele, wa muli wandugu zenyu Waisilayeli, hamba wa muli wajenzi wono wekaye nanye mu makaya genyu ganji.
15 Langa munojeye mweze kuwalipa nhondo zose cinyamihe, u musahala wawo wa mwi'zuwa lilyo. Hambi nawo nha wewikanize wazituje. Holeka hono mulece kuwalipa, nawo womulilila Muwaha Mulungu ku soko yenyu, nanye mowa muli wasutyi so nghani yiyo.
16 Nawo walezi we wana, si wosuma kuwulagwa ku soko yo wubi wono wetumile wana wawo. Hamba wana nawo, si wosuma kuwulagwa ku soko yo wubi wono wetumile walezi wawo. Langa munhu wowose kosuma kuwulagwa so wubi wono yetumile mweneco.
17 Mulece kuhodanga ling'ani ye wajenzi, hamba ye wana wono wasina walezi, hamba mulece kusola myenda ye wacekulu wafwile, yiwe nha cipingo so wukope wono yakopile.
18 Mweze kukumbucila, vyono nanye mwali muli wawanda ku Misili, na vyono, Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakamukombozenyi, holeka nane nghumukong'onhezenyi mwitume lilajilizo lilino.

19 “Na lono muwe mukududula wuhemba, na lono mukuwukunyhila ku zikaya, na kumbe mulece kusuma kuwukungha wose, na wunji wuwe wukali wusigaye ku mugunda, wono mwali mwawududula, hodu mulece kuwuyajila ku mugunda mukawukunyhe. Awo wowa wawasigalila i wajenzi, ne wana wono wasina walezi wawo, hamonga ne wacekulu wafwile. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, koyilugutiza i milimo yenyu yose yono mowa kwituma.
20 Sunga na kolo wakondya kukong'onha kamonga mizeyituni yako mu mibici yako, hodu wulece kuwuyajila kanyhi wukakong'onhe zono zali zikali zisigaliye mu mibici. Azo zono zowa zisigaye, zowa zawasigalila i wajenzi, ne wana wono wasina walezi wawo, hamonga ne wacekulu wafwile.
21 Nagwe kolo wakondya kuhepa kamonga zabibu zako, wulece kuwuyajila kanyhi, ho wukahepe zono zisigaliye. Azo zono zowa zikali zisigaye, zowa zawasigalila i wajenzi, ne wana wono wasina walezi wawo, hamonga ne wacekulu wafwile.
22 Kanyhi mulece kusemwa vyono nanye mwali muli wawanda ku Misili. Holeka nane nghumukong'onhezenyi mwitume lilajilizo lilino.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 25

1 “Na hono Waisilayeli wejete wawe wakwisoka nghani, nawo kolo wakubita mu mahakama, na kuko yunji yejele yena masutya, no muyagwe yejele vyono yasina masutya.
2 Na hono musutyi yapegwe wulipicizwe wo kukunwa viboko, ale u mulamuzi yamusole, yamuwice yagone wugubama, hanyuma wamukune viboko. No winji we viboko vyono kokunwa, vyowa mu nghango ye lisutya lyono yetumile.
3 Ne viboko vyono womukuna, vilece kuwa vinji muno kuciza viboko makumi mane. Hambi hono vicize vivyo, nha vyomukoza cevu u nduguyo hali wose.
4 Mulece kuziwicila lubano mu mulomo, zing'ombe zono zikutwanga wuhemba.”

Zono zinojeye kuli we ndugu, mono yakondya kufwa

5 “Na hono wanhu wejete wono wali ndugu, wawe wekaye hamonga, na hono monga muli wene yafwe, yalece yasina hamba mwana wa cilume. Nayo mucekulu wakwe mulekwa, nha vyono yinha, si kosuma kutolwa, na wono si walawile mu mhuga yo mulume wakwe mono yakondya kufwa, holeka yowa yimunojeye yuyo nduguye yo mulume wakwe mono yafwiye, yamutole u mucekulu yuyo.
6 Na kolo wakulelana u mwana mulongozi we cilume, ayo kowa mwana wo mulume mono yafwa, womucemela ne litagwa lyakwe. Ho litagwa lilece kwajilila mu Waisilayeli ho yetume mulimo wono ndugu yali kwituma.
7 Ninga hono u nduguye wo mulume mono yafwa, yaleme kumutola mucekulu yuyo, ale, mucekulu yuyo yabite kuli wanyamhala wa mwi'kaya yawalonjele yatye, ‘U nduguye wo mulume wangu mono yafwa, si yakusaka kunhola, yamulelele nduguye wana, ho yalijendeleze litagwa lyo mulume wangu mu Isilayeli.’
8 Nawo wanyamhala wa mwi'kaya lilyo, wamuceme mulume yuyo walongane nayo. Nayo hono yajendelele kulema kumutola mucekulu yuyo,
9 ale, mucekulu yuyo yamubitile yuyo mulamuwe, yamuwopoze lilatu limonga ha mwande ye wanyamhala, hanyuma yamufunyile mate mu wusu, akuno yakutya, ‘Nhavivi vyo yakunozezwa u munhu mono yakulema kumulelela nduguye wana, ho yalijendeleze litagwa lyo nduguye mu Isilayeli.’
10 Holeka sunga ne mhuga yakwe yomanyika mu Isilayeli mose, woyicema, mhuga yo munhu mono yawopozwa lilatu lyakwe limonga.”

Malajilizo ganji

11 “Na hono hawe ho wali wanhu wejete wakwitowa, hanyuma mugolece wa yumonga, muli wawa wono wakwitowa, yaze yamutazize mulume wakwe, na baho ha wutazize, yagolole muwoko wakwe yebate mapinde go mulume mono vikwitowa no mulume wakwe,
12 wumudumulaje muwoko wakwe u mucekulu yuyo, wulece kumuwonela lusungu hamba hadodo.
13 Wulece kuwa na vipimo vili hejete mu mufuko wako vyono visimajiye, ciwaha ne cidodo.
14 Kanyhi wulece kuwa na vipimo vili hejete mu kaya yako, vyono visimajiye, ciwaha ne cidodo.
15 Munojeye mwitumamile vipimo vye ling'ani na vye mbeka, ho mwikale vilo vinji mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumupelenyi yiwe yenyu.
16 Muwaha Mulungu si yakwendezwa ne wanhu wakonji.”

I lilajilizo lyo kuwawulaga i Waamaleki

17 “Ale, mukumbucile zono wamutendelenyi Waamaleki, lono mwali muli mu nzila mukulawa ku Misili.
18 Wene wali wasina hamba wogopa Mulungu kose. Holeka wakamuhungucilenyi, wakawatowanga wanhu wenyu wono wali wasigaye mu nyuma, na wono wali wasoceye, na wono wali wakataye, wakawawulaga wose.
19 Holeka luno, kolo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakondya kumupelenyi yisi, no kumuwicenyi mupolile mu wulawa muli walugu wenyu wose, wono wekaye mbavu zose ze yisi yenyu, ale, muwawulagaje Waamaleki wose, muwongomezeze lukulu, ho walece kukumbucililwa lukulu kanyhi na munhu wowose. Mulece kuyisemwa nghani yiyi!”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 26

Vilavywa vye nhwanga

1 “Nanye kolo mwakondya kwinjila mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumupelenyi no kwikala mumo,
2 ale, wose muli nyenye, mweze kusola mikalo milongozi kulelwa, muli vye vilimwa vyenyu vyose vyono movituga wulawa mu migunda yenyu. Muvitajile mu zinhoto, muvitegule i hanhu ho wuhembelo.
3 Hanyuma mumubitile mukohani mono kowa yali baho mu vilo vivyo, mumulonjele muwe kutya, ‘Alu nhondo ho camanya vyono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, yakondya mbeka kucinjiza mu yisi yono yawehuwiziza i walezi wetu vyono kocipela sese.’ ”

4 “Nayo mukohani kocibocela citoto cico wulawa mu miwoko yako, yaciwice ha mwande ye cikumbicilo co Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu.
5 Nagwe wuwe kulonga hali Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wako, wuwe kutya nhavi; ‘U mulezi wangu yali Mwalamu, mono yawasola we mhuga yakwe wose, wakahamila ku Misili. Nawo wali wacefu muno lono wabitaga kuko, ninga hanyuma yayo, wakonjelezeka muno, kanyhi wakawa yikungugo yiwaha, kanyhi wali vilungo muno.
6 Nawo Wamisili wakacigaza muno, wakacitumya milimo ciwandawanda.
7 Nase cikamulilila Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wetu, yacipele wutazi, nayo yakacihulika, yakawona na vyono cikugazwa muno, na vyono cikwitumywa muno, na vyono cikwiwonelwa ne Wamisili.
8 Nayo yakaciponya kuko ku Misili kwe vilungo vyakwe viwaha. Yetuma mapoto mawaha, na nghani zo wizine muno, hamonga no wogofye wuwaha muno muno.
9 Yakacileta sunga bahano mono yisi yiyi yono yina moso mwinji.
10 Holeka nane luno, nghumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu nhwanga yiyi ye vinhu vilongozi kulelwa, vyono yamhelile.’

“Hanyuma wuciwice cico citoto hali Muwaha Mulungu no kuhemba.
11 Munojeye mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, so vinhu vinji viswanu vyono yamupelenyi, hamonga na we zimhuga zenyu wose. Holeka muwaceme Walawi, hamonga ne wajenzi wono mwikalane nawo, ho waze muhanje nawo mu sikukuu yiyo.
12 Na ha ndumilizo yo mwaka we kadatu wa mu myaka yose, mweze kuwapela zaka ya mu vilimwa vyenyu vyose, i Walawi, ne wajenzi, ne wana wono wasina walezi wawo, hamonga ne wacekulu wafwile. Ho mhuga zose, wawe wena ndigwa yono yowakwila. Nanye kolo mwakondya kuzituma zizi zose,
13 ale, ho mumulonjele Muwaha Mulungu muwe kutya, ‘Hasina hamba zaka yoyose kanyhi yono yisigaye mu kaya yangu, hambi nha nakondya kuwagawila vyose i Walawi, ne wajenzi, ne wana wono wasina walezi wawo, hamonga ne wacekulu wafwile, nha vyono wacilajiliza citume. Si mbedegaye, hamba kugasemwa malajilizo gako ga muli ze zaka.
14 Hamba si niluvizye niyilye yiyo zaka yaye, lono nili mu cililo. Hamba si niyilavizye kunze ye kaya yangu, lono nali nili mu wavufywe yaye hamba si niyilavizye liwe yikumbiko kuli wafu ng'o. Langa nghakuhulika gwegwe du go Muwaha Mulungu, nane nakondya kwituma nha vyono wandajiliza nitume, kuli zo wulavya zaka.
15 Holeka go Muwaha Mulungu, alu wulanje wulawa hanhu hako hono helile kuko ku wulanga, wuwalugutize wanhu wako Waisilayeli, hamonga ne yisi yiyi swanu muno yono yina no moso mwinji, yono wacipela, nha vyono wawehuwiza walezi wetu.’ ”

I wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu

16 “Alu izuwali Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumukong'onhezenyi mugebate malajilizo gakwe gose. Holeka mugebate, kwe zinhumbula zenyu zose na kwe mitima yenyu yose.
17 Nalu izuwali mwakondya kumumanya Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, sunga luno, mwehuwiza nha vyono momuhulika mwene, no kwibata malajilizo gakwe gose, hamonga no kwituma zose zono yakuzilajiliza.
18 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, izuwali kakondya kumubocelenyi, mweza muli wanhu wakwe, nha vyono yamwihuwizenyi. Holeka nayo aluno yakumukong'onhezenyi muno, mugebate malajilizo gakwe gose.
19 Nayo komunozenyi muwe yikungugo yiwaha muno, kuciza makungugo ganji gose gono yagawumba, nanye molikumya no kulitogoza litagwa lyakwe. Kanyhi mowa wanhu wakwe nha vyono yehuwiza.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 27

I malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu, mandikwe mu mabwe

1 Hanyuma kanyhi Musa hamonga ne walongola we wanhu wa Isilayeli, wakawalonjela Waisilayeli wakatya, “Alu mugebataje mejizo gono nghumupelenyi izuwali,
2 Mwi'zuwa lyono molowa mweza kusaka kulilowoka likolongo lya Yoludani, no kwinjila mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakumupelenyi, ale, mwi'zuwa lilyo, nhondo musimye mabwe mawaha, mugakandilize ne lilongo,
3 hanyuma mwandice mu mhelo yago zinghani zose ze malajilizo ne mejizo gaga. Na kolo mwakondya kulilowoka likolongo no kwinjila mu yisi yiyo swanu, kanyhi yono yina moso mwinji, yono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wenyu, yakumupelenyi.
4 Na kolo mwakondya kulowoka ku nhumbi nzagwe ye likolongo lya Yoludani, ale, mugasimye mabwe gago mwi'tunda lya Ebali, nha vyono nghumwijizenyi izuwali, mugakandilize ne lilongo.
5 Hanyuma muzenje cikumbicilo baho ca mabwe, ninga mabwe gago, mulece kugasesa na citwalo co cose ca cuma.
6 Hambi nha munojeye mumuzenjele Muwaha Mulungu cikumbicilo kwe mabwe gono si masese yaye. Na mu cikumbicilo cico, mumulavizye Muwaha Mulungu makumbiko, ge cilavywa cinyanywe.
7 Na baho kanyhi mulavye ne vilavywa vyenyu vyo mulamu, muvilile baho musangalaye, akuno mwina ne lilumbo hali Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu.
8 Mabwe gago gono mowa mwakondya kugakandiliza ne lilongo liswanu, ale, mugandice calinze mu mhelo yayo, zinghani zose zo wulajilize wuwu wo Muwaha Mulungu.”

9 Nayo Musa hamonga ne wakohani Walawi, wakawalonjela wanhu wose Waisilayeli wakatya, “Ta hulicizenyi 'nye nyo Waisilayeli, muteje makutu muhulice. Izuwali anye mweza wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu.
10 Holeka munojeye mumuhulice kanyhi mwibate ne malajilizo gakwe gono nghumulonjelenyi izuwali.”

Wulijitwe kuli wono wasina cihuliko

11 Kanyhi Musa yakawalonjela Waisilayeli yakawatya,
12 “Kolo mwakondya kulilowoka likolongo lya Yoludani, ale we milango ayi yono yityatizye, wokwima he 'tunda lya Gelizimu, mu cipango lono wulugutwe wukulavywa kuli wanhu, nha cinji wo mulango wa Simiyoni, na wa Lawi, na wa Yuda, na wa Isaka, na wa Yusufu, na wa Benyamini.
13 Nawo we milango ayi yono yityatizye, wawe wemile mwi'tunda lya Ebali, lono wulijitwe wukulavywa, nawo wowa, mulango wa Rubeni, na wa Gadi, na wa Asheli, na wa Zabuloni, na wa Dani, na wa Nafutali.
14 Nawo Walawi baho, wolonga zinghani azi kwe mazi mawaha, wawe kutya,

15 “ ‘Malijito go Mulungu, geze muli munhu wo wose mono kosesa cihwani ce libwe hamba ce libici, hamba ca cuma, yawe kucihemba cinyele. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, nha si yazendile zizo.’

“Nawo wanhu wapitule wose hamonga watye, ‘Heya!’

16 “Malijito go Mulungu, geze muli munhu wo wose mono yasina cihuliko kuli sogwe hamba kuli nyina.

“Nawo wanhu wapitulile wose hamonga watye, ‘Heya.’

17 “Malijito go Mulungu, geze muli munhu wo wose mono yakuteza citanjilo ce mimbi yo muyagwe.

“Nawo wanhu wapitulile wose hamonga watye, ‘Heya.’

18 “Malijito go Mulungu, geze muli munhu wo wose mono yakumutonga munhu mubofu, kawa kumwajiliza nzila ne nyono.

“Nawo wanhu wapitule wose hamonga watye, ‘Heya.’

19 “Malijito go Mulungu, geze muli munhu wo wose mono yakuyiweyanga ling'ani ye wajenzi, hamba ye wana wono wasina walezi, hamba ye wacekulu wafwile.

“Nawo wanhu wapitule wose hamonga watye, ‘Heya.’

20 “Malijito go Mulungu, geze muli munhu wo wose mono yakumukoza sogwe cevu, so kugona na yumonga wa muli wagolece wo sogwe.

“Nawo wanhu wapitule wose hamonga watye, ‘Heya.’

21 “Malijito go Mulungu, geze muli munhu wo wose mono kogona ne lilimu.

“Nawo wanhu wapitule wose hamonga watye, ‘Heya!’

22 “Malijito go Mulungu, geze muli munhu wo wose mono kogona no lumbulye mono vihanjile sogwe no nyina, hamba lumbulye mono vihanjile sogwe du, hamba nyina du.

“Nawo wanhu wapitule wose hamonga watye, ‘Heya.’

23 “Malijito go Mulungu, geze muli munhu wo wose mono kogona no mukwewe we cicekulu.

“Nawo wanhu wapitule wose hamonga watye, ‘Heya!’

24 “Malijito go Mulungu, geze muli munhu wo wose mono komuwulaga muyagwe cizilago.

“Nawo wanhu wapitule wose hamonga watye, ‘Heya!’

25 “Malijito go Mulungu, geze muli munhu wo wose mono yakubocela mafupo, ho yamuwulaje munhu mono yasina masutya.

“Nawo wanhu wapitule wose hamonga watye, ‘Heya!’

26 “Malijito go Mulungu, geze muli munhu wo wose mono si yakugebata malajilizo go Mulungu hamonga ne mejizo gakwe.

“Nawo wanhu wapitule wose hamonga watye, ‘Heya!’ ”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 28

Wulugutwe wono wukuziza mu wuwa ne cihuliko

(Lawi 26:3-13; Kum 7:12-24)

1 “Holeka hono mumogope Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, hamonga no kwibata ko wihuwilwa i malajilizo gakwe gono nghumulajilizenyi izuwali, ale, nayo komunozenyi muwe yikungugo yiwaha lyono lyowa licizize makungugo ganji gose gono gali mononze yiyi.
2 Na hono mumogope no kumuhulika Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, wulugutwe wu wuno wose wowa wenyu nyenye.

3 “Muwaha Mulungu, kogalugutiza makaya genyu hamonga ne migunda yenyu.

4 “Muwaha Mulungu, komulugutizenyi nyenye, kumupelenyi wana wenji, hamonga ne nhwanga mbaha muno, hamonga ne zimitugo, zing'ombe ne zingholo nyinji muno muno.

5 “Muwaha Mulungu, kowulugutiza wuhemba wenyu hamonga no wugali wono wukulawa mumo wono mowa kulya.

6 “Muwaha Mulungu, kokulugutiza kolo wukwinjila, kolugutizwa kolo wukulawila.

7 “Muwaha Mulungu, komusumilenyi walugu wenyu wose lono wakumuhomenyi. Womuhomenyi ku wulawilila ku lubavu lumonga, ninga wotiza wose hali 'nye weyagale ako na ako.

8 “Muwaha Mulungu, koyilugutiza milimo yenyu, ne madongha genyu kogamemya no wuhemba. Kanyhi komulugutizenyi muno muno mu yisi yono yakumupelenyi.

9 “Na hono mumogope no kumuhulika Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kanyhi mwitume zose zono yakuzilajiliza, ale, nayo komunozenyi muwe wanhu wakwe nha vyono yehuwiza.
10 Nawo wanhu wa mono'nze yose, wowona vyono Muwaha Mulungu yakondya kumuhagulenyi muwe wanhu wakwe, baho nawo womogopenyi muno muno.
11 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, komupelenyi wana wenji, ne mitugo minji muno, hamonga ne nhwanga mbaha muno muno, mu yisi yono yawehuwiza i walezi wenyu, vyono komupelenyi.
12 Nayo komutonyezezenyi mvula mu vipango vyayo, mvula yono yikulawa mu mavunde mono kayiwikaga, kanyhi koyilugutiza ne milimo yenyu yose, ho musume kuwakopesha wanhu we makungugo menji, ninga anye mowa musina wanjile wono womunozenyi sunga nanye mubite mukakope kuli wene.
13 Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu komunozenyi muwe walongola wa muli makungugo gose, ninga anye hamba si molongozwa na yunji yaye. Kanyhi mowa kumoteswa vilo vyose, hamba si mokwanjila cinji yaye, hono mugebate ko wihuwilwa malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, gono nghumulonjelenyi izuwali.
14 Ninga mwilanjilile, mulecele lukulu kugasemwa malajilizo gaga, hanyuma muhembe milungu yinji no kuyitumamila.”

Wulipicizwe ku so wu wula cihuliko

(Lawi 26:14-46)

15 “Ninga hono mulece kumuhulika Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kanyhi mulece no kugebata ko wihulwa malajilizo gakwe gose, hamonga no wulajilize wakwe, nha vyono nghumulonjelenyi izuwali, ale, i zimbi zizino zose zomwijelelenyi.

16 “Muwaha Mulungu, kogalijita makaya genyu, hamonga ne migunda yenyu.

17 “Muwaha Mulungu, kowulijita wuhemba wenyu, hamonga no wugali wono wukulawa mu wuhemba wuwo.

18 “Muwaha Mulungu, komulijitenyi, yamupelenyi wana wacefu muno muno, no wuhemba wucefu, na ng'ombe ne zingholo nyhefu muno.

19 “Muwaha Mulungu, kokulijita kolo wukwinjila, kokulijita kolo wukulawila.

20 “Na hono mwitume zimbi du, no kumuleka Muwaha Mulungu, nayo komuletelenyi wulijitwe, no wongomele, hamonga no wulemwe mu milimo yenyu yose, sunga kolo mwokondya kongomelela lukulu musunho.
21 Nayo komuletelenyi matamwa ge cibehi cibehi, sunga mumalice wose, hawe hasina hamba monga mono yasigaye mu yisi yono mukwinjila muyiteme.
22 Muwaha Mulungu komutowenyi ne matamwa go kwileceza, hamonga ne mapu, ne mhungo. Kanyhi komuletelenyi cibahu ciwaha muno, hamonga ne libeho yiwaha lyono lyohodanga vilimwa vyenyu vyose. Ne magazo gago gomutyatililenyi du sunga, kolo wose mwafwila lukulu.
23 Hamba mvula si yotonya yaye, ne yisi yonyala yiwe kotya yibwe.
24 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, so kumutonyezezenyi mvula, langa mwene komuletelenyi muhangha ne manghundi ku wulawa ku wulanga, sunga wose mongomele.

25 “Nayo Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, kowapela wusume i walugu wenyu, wamusumenyi. Anye mowahoma ku wulawa ku lubavu lumonga, ninga kanyhi motiza no kwiyagala caka caka. Ne wanhu wose wa mono'nze, woziwona wezine muno kuli zono zakondya kumwaganenyi.
26 Na kolo mweza kufwa, zindeje hamonga ne zinimu za mu mbago, zokuza zilye miwili yenyu. Na howa hasina hamba munhu mono koziwinga yaye.
27 Muwaha Mulungu komuletelenyi mapu, nha vyono yawanozeza Wamisili. Koyinoza miwili yenyu yituje malondalonda. Nanye wose motuga mukuje, na howa hasina wunoje.
28 Muwaha Mulungu komulecenyi mahala genyu gajilile, nayo kumuletelenyi wubofu, hamonga no wucelewale.
29 Nanye mowa kwalabata kwalabata mu misi nha vyono munhu mubofu kalabataga mu wutitu. Nanye hamba si mosuma kuyiwona nzila. Kanyhi hamba si momoteswa mu milimo yenyu yose. Kanyhi mowa kwiwonelwa no kusolelwa vinhu vyenyu, ninga hamba nhavivyo, howa hasina mono komuponyenyi.

30 “Nagwe kolo wabanya muhinza, munhu yunji kogona nayo. Kozenga kaya, ninga hamba si kokwikala mu kaya yiyo. Kolima mugunda wa zabibu, ninga hamba si kozilya zabibu zayo.
31 Ne zing'ombe zenyu zowa kuwulajilwa ha meso genyu, ninga anye hamba si mozilya zinyama zayo. Ndogowe zenyu zowa kusolelwa ha meso genyu, ninga hamba si womugomolelenyi kanyhi yaye. Ne zingholo zenyu nazo zopegwa i walugu wenyu, na howa hasina munhu wo wose mono komutazizenyi.
32 Nawo i wana wenyu we cicekulu sunga na we cilume, wopegwa likungugo lijenzi ha meso genyu, wawanoze wawe wawanda wawo. Nanye mowa mwikaye nhondo zose muwulanjiliye wuwuye we wana wenyu ninga wulanjilile wenyu, yowa nghani ya bwete du.

33 “Nalyo likungugo lijenzi, lyoyikungha nhwanga yenyu yose, yono mwayitumamila milimo miwaha muno muno, ha wulimile. Nanye mokwiwonelwa du no kutendelwa vibi.
34 Ne magazo gago, gomunozenyi ne mahala genyu galuzuce.
35 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu komunozenyi mutuje mapu gono gasina wunoje, ku wulawilila ku zinzayo sunga ko kwi 'ndosi.

36 “Muwaha Mulungu komusolenyi hamonga no mutemi wenyu, yamutegulenyi ku yisi yo wujenzi, i hanhu hono nyenye hamba walezi wenyu wakali nekala yaye. Na kuko, anye mohemba no kuyitumamila milungu ye mabici maseswe, na ye mabwe.
37 Na mu ma'si gono Muwaha Mulungu komwiyagazizenyi, i wanhu wono wekaye mu zi'si zizo, wotinuka muno muno kuli zono zamwijelelajenyi, nawo womusekanjenyi no kumubedegalenyi.

38 “Nanye kuko mowa kuhadika mbeyu nyinji, ninga mowa kutuga ndudula nyhefu, hambi zinzije nha zowa kulya vilimwa vyenyu.
39 Kanyhi mowa kulima zabibu no kuzilanjilila muno muno, ninga hamba nhavivyo si mokwambulila cinji yaye, zabibu hamba divayi yayo. Hambi zabibu nha zokwinjilwa na sulenje.
40 Nayo mizeyituni yocindika minji mu yisi yenyu, ninga hamba si mosuma kugatuga mafuta gayo, hambi zizeyituni nha zokung'unhicila hasi zose.
41 Nanye motuga wana we cilume na we cicekulu, ninga hamba si mokwikala nawo, langa wosolwa nha wafungwa wo wulugu.
42 Nayo mibici yenyu hamonga ne vilimwa vyenyu vyose, vyoligwa ne mazije.

43 “Nawo i wajenzi wono wowa mwikalane mu yisi yenyu wocila kuwa ne vilungo muno, ninga anye mocila kucepucilwa ne vilungo vyenyu.
44 Nawo womukopeshenyi mapesa. Ninga anye mowa musina mapesa go kuwakopesha. Na ku ndumo yayo, wowa watemi wenyu.

45 “Ne malijito gaga gocila kumuzizenyi, nago gowa muli nyenye, sunga kolo mwakondya kongomezwa, vyono si mwamuhulika Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, no kwibata wulajilize wono yamupelenyi.
46 Naco cowa co citanjilo co wuling'anizi wo Mulungu kuli 'nye, na kuli wana wenyu, cibitilila.
47 Muwaha Mulungu yali yakondya kumulugutizenyi muli vyose, ninga anye hamba si mwasuma kumwitumamila ko mutima muzelu, hamonga ne nhumbula yono yina nyemo.
48 Holeka luno mojendelela kuwetumamila walugu wenyu wono Muwaha Mulungu kowaleka waze wamuhomenyi. Nanye modabuka muno, kanyhi mokaluka muno, kanyhi mowa kujenda cipwi, mowa mwina wanjile wuwaha muno. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu komupelenyi mikalo mikamu, sunga kolo yakumongomezenyi.
49 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, komuletelenyi likungugo ku wulawilila ku mhelo ye yinze, likungugo lyono hamba ne nonga yawo, anye si muyimanyile yaye. Ne wanhu wawo womuzizenyi nha numba.

50 “Wanhu wawo wakali, hamba si wolosuma kumuwonela lusungu munhu wo wose, mukombi hamba muzelelo.
51 Nawo wololya mitugo zenyu sunga ne vilimwa vyenyu, nanye hamba si molotuga ndigwa, sunga mufwe. Nawo si wolomusigazizenyi hamba mhece monga yaye, hamba divayi, hamba mafuta, hamba ng'ombe, hamba ngholo, anye langa molofwa du.
52 Wanhu wawo, wologahoma makaya genyu gose, mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu yakumupelenyi. Sunga ne magomawakali, i matali muno, gono mwitumbiye, gose gologwa hasi.

53 “Walugu wenyu walawa kuwa wagapilima makaya genyu, mologazika muno ne nzala, sunga muwe kulya ne wana wenyu wono Muwaha Mulungu yamupelilenyi.
54 No munhu muswanu muno kanyhi mutambule, nayo kolowa yasina witumbilo mu cipango cico, lono walugu wagapilime makaya genyu, nayo kosuma kulya sunga ne wanagwe wanji, vyono howa hasina hamba ndigwa yoyose.
55 Hamba si kosuma kumugawila nduguye, hamba mugolece wakwe mono yamwendile muno, hamba kuwagawila i wanagwe wono wasigaye.
56 Hamba yawe mucekulu muswanu muno, kanyhi mutambule, kanyhi mugoli, mono hamba lizuwa linji yakali nakanyaga hasi yajende hanhu hanji, yuyo nayo kokwituma nhavivyo du.
57 Nayo kolo walugu walipilima likaya, kolowa yasina witumbilo kanyhi, holeka nayo ndigwa yakwe, kololya cizilago, u mwanagwe mugalika, hamonga no mutyati muyagwe mono yamuleceye. Kanyhi hamba si kokwenda kumugawila yunji wowose wa muli wanagwe.

58 “Na hono mulece kugebata ko wihuwilwa i mejizo gose go Muwaha Mulungu, gono gandicigwe mu citabu cici, na hono mulece kulipela cogopo litagwa lili lye nhojo ne cogopo, lyo Muwaha Mulungu,
59 nayo kolomuletelenyi nyenye ne wana wenyu i matamwa gono si ganogaga, hamonga ne matamwa go wileceze, gono gowa gasina muganga.
60 Kolomuletelenyi kanyhi sunga ne matamwa go kuwulaga, gono mwagawona ku Misili, nanye hamba si molonojela lukulu.
61 Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu kolomuletelenyi matamwa ge mawala gose, gono si ganogaga, matamwa gono si gatambuligwe hamba kwandikwa mu citabu cici co wulajilize, hamonga ne mejizo go Muwaha Mulungu. Nanye molokongomezwa.
62 Hamba muwe mwakondya kwinjipa no kuwa muli wenji nhe zinyelezi za ku wulanga, ninga hamba nhavivyo, wacefu wa muli nyenye wolopona, vyono si mu muhulice Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu.
63 Nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yasangalala, yakamumotyenyi, no kumwinjifyenyi, yo nhavivyo kanyhi vyo kosangalala, kumongomezenyi no kumuhodanjenyi. Nanye mowuswa mu yisi yono mukwinjila muyiteme.

64 “Nayo Muwaha Mulungu kolomwiyagazizenyi mu makungugo gose, ku wulawilila ndumo ayi ye yinze, sunga ko ku ndumo nzagwe kanyhi ye yinze. Na kuko, molowa kuhemba milungu miseswe ye mabici na ye mabwe, i milungu yono nyenye weneco hamba ne walezi wenyu mukali hamba nayihemba.
65 Kanyhi si molowona mulamu hamba hadodo hanhu ho hose, hamba si molowa na hanhu ho hose hono molotya henyu nyo weneco. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu kolomumemyenyi mahwele no wu wula witumbilo, hamonga no wutukuwika mutima.
66 No wumi wenyu wolowa wuli mu mbali nhondo zose. Na mu vilo vyose, cilo ne misi, molowa mumemigwe no woga, nanye mowa mwikaye akuno mwina no woga we lifwa.
67 Nazo zinhumbula zenyu zolowa zina woga wuwaha muli vyose vyono mowa kuviwona. Nanye nhondo zose hakali mitondo, molowa kusulumila mukutya, liswe mbela. Na mu cinyamihe molowa kutya, wele mbela.
68 Muwaha Mulungu yadahile kumugomolenyi kanyhi ku Misili, mu nzila ye zingalawa, hamba yawe yakondya kulonga vyono si kolokwenda kanyhi anye muwuye kuko ku Misili. Na kuko wanji wa muli nyenye, wolokwenda no kwiguza weneco kuli walugu wawo, wawe wawanda wawo. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, holowa hasina hamba munhu monga mono kolokwenda kumugulenyi yaye.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 29

Lisanduku lye Cilagane lyo Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga ne wanhu Waisilayeli, mu yisi ya Mowabu

1 Azi zo nghani ze cilagane, cono Muwaha Mulungu yamulonjela Musa, yazinoze kuli Waisilayeli mu yisi ya Mowabu. Ne cilagane cici cali cilagane cinji naco ne cilagane acilya, cono Muwaha Mulungu yacinoza kuli wene kwi'tunda lya Sinayi.

2 Nayo Musa yakawa kuwacema wanhu wose Waisilayeli, yakawa kuwalonjela yakutya, “Mwaziwona nyo weneco ne meso genyu, zono Muwaha Mulungu yazinoza kuli Falawo mutemi wa Misili, na kuli wetumami wakwe, sunga na mu yisi yakwe yose.
3 Kanyhi mukaviwona vitanjilo ne mezino mawaha go wogofye, gono Muwaha Mulungu yagetuma.
4 Ninga sunga nalu, Muwaha Mulungu si yamulecenyi mumanye zono zamwijelelenyi kuko ku Misili.
5 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakamutonjenyi mu nyika myaka makumi mane, ninga, i myenda yenyu hamba malatu genyu, si vyasakala yaye.
6 Kanyhi mwali musina ndigwa yo kulya, hamba divayi, hamba cing'wegwa co kugala, ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakamupelenyi visakwa vyenyu vyose, ho yamwijizenyi vyono u mwene yo Mulungu wenyu.
7 Nase cali ceza kufika bahano, u mutemi Sihoni wa ku Heshiboni, no mutemi Ogu wa ku Bashani, wakaza wacihome. Ninga, ase cikawasuma.
8 Cikayisola yisi yawo cikawagawila we milango ya Lubeni na Gadi, ne nusu yayo, cikawapela wo mulango wa Manase.
9 Holeka muzibate muno ko wihuwilwa zinghani zose ze cilagane cici, ho musume kutuga wumoteswe muli zose zono mowa kwituma.

10 “Alu izuwali, mwimile hali Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nyenye hamonga ne walongola wenyu, ne wawaha wenyu, ne wanhu wenyu we cilume, na
11 we cicekulu, ne wana, hamonga ne wajenzi, wono mwikalane nawo, wono wakumutenyelenyi zingwagu no kumunejelenyi ne malenga.
12 Nanye lu muli baha izuwali, ho mucivumile cilagane cici cono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yakucinoza nanye, kanyhi muwubocele no wulajilize wayo.
13 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu ho yamuwicenyi zuwali muwe muli wanhu wakwe, nayo yawe Mulungu wenyu, nha vyono yamwihuwizizenyi nyenye, na vyono yelahiza kuli walezi wenyu, Abulahamu na Isaka na Yakobo.
14 Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu, hamba si yakucinoza cilagane cici hamonga no wulajilize wayo na nyenye du.
15 Langa yakucinoza cilagane cici kuli sese wose mono cimile hali mwene izuwali, hamonga kanyhi na kuli wana wetu wono wakali nalelwa.

16 “Mukumbucile mikalile yenyu vyono yali yinha lono mwali muli ku Misili, na vyono yali yinha lono mwakolaga haciligati ye makungugo ganji.
17 Mwaviwona i vihwani vyawo vinozwe kwe mabici hamba kwe mabwe, hamba kwa fweza, hamba kwa zahabu.
18 Holeka mulanjilile, yilece kuwa yunji wa muli nyenye, munhu mulume, hamba mucekulu, hamba wana, hamba wo mulango wowose mono mwimile bahano izuwali, yamulece Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu, yahembe milungu ye makungugo ganji. Hambi yiyi nha yokwihwana ne lisina lyono likuwiluka no kulela sumu nghali.
19 Kanyhi mulanjilile, halece kuwa na yunji wa muli nyenye zuwali, mono yakuzihulika zizi zo wilahize na vyono yikusakwa, kanyhi mwene ho yezivye, no kwihwesa vyono hamba yalece kuzityatya, zose zomuwela swanu du, hanyuma mwene yawe kubita na zakwe. Mumanye vyono nghani yiyo, yomongomezenyi nyenye wose i wabi ne waswanu.
20 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha si komunenela u munhu mono yenha nhavivyo. Langa likuwo lyakwe lyokwaka muno muli munhu yuyo. Ne malijito gose gono gandicigwe mu citabu cici, gomugwila, sunga Muwaha Mulungu yawe yakondya kumongomezeza lukulu munhu yuyo.
21 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu komusiwanula no kumuletela magazo hamonga ne malijito gose gono gandicigwe mu cilagane, gono gandicigwe mu citabu cici ce mejizo go Muwaha Mulungu ho liwe yifundo kuli milango yose ye Waisilayeli.

22 “Nawo wo lulelo lwa mu nyuma, ne wana wenyu, hamonga ne wajenzi wono wekaye ma'si go kutali, wolowona magazo no wugaye wono Muwaha Mulungu yaviletile mono yisi yenyu.
23 Ne migunda yenyu yolobabala hamba kulela cinji kanyhi, yoloticimizwa no moto we cibiliti no mwino. Yolowa yisina hanji kanyhi hono hosuma kuhadikwa cinhu co cose, hamba mahanze bwete, si galacindika mumo yaye. Yisi yenyu yolokwihwana nhe yisi ya Sodoma na Gomola, hamba nhe yisi ya Adima na Zeboyimu, zono Muwaha Mulungu yazongomeza lono yali yakolwa ne likuwo.
24 Baho, ne makungugo gose golokwiwuza gawe kutya, ‘Alo nhawule Muwaha Mulungu kazinoza zizi mu yisi yawo?’
25 Ne lipitulo nalyo lyolowa nhavi, ‘Ku soko vyono i wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu, wabena cilagane cono wacinoza no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wawo, lono yali yakondya kuwawusa mu yisi ya Misili.
26 Nawo wakayihemba no kuyitumamila milungu yinji, yono ku wulawa na katali wali wakali nayihemba, i milungu yono Muwaha Mulungu yawalemeza walece kuyihemba.
27 Holeka Muwaha Mulungu yakawakolelwa likuwo yiwaha i wanhu wakwe, yakawa kuwaletela mu yisi yawo malijito gose gono gandicigwe mu citabu.
28 Muwaha Mulungu yakawakolelwa likuwo yiwaha muno muno, na mwi'kuwo lilyo liwaha, yakawawusa mu wulawa mu yisi yawo, yakawa kuweyagaziza wose mu yisi yo wujenzi, na vyo yina sunga nalu.’

29 “Hozili nghani zinji zono Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yazihundice, ninga yakacigubulila Wulajilize wakwe. Holeka nase ne wana wetu, yicinojeye ciwibate muno wulajilize wuwu, sunga ko ku cibitilila.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 30

Wulajilize wo kulipa, no wumoteswe wayo

1 “Alu nakondya kumulecelenyi welecele, wo kwihagulila nyenye nyo weneco muli zizi zi nyejete, wumoteswe no wulijitwe. Nanye kolo zinghani zikumwijelelenyi, lono muli kuko mwikaye mu makungugo, gono Muwaha Mulungu yamuwinjilenyi, nhondo, ho molokumbucila nha vyono munojeye muhagule nyenye nyo weneco muli zizi zi nyejete zono nghumulecelenyi.
2 Na hono mwihwese nyenye hamonga ne wana wenyu, mumuwuyile Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kwe zinhumbula zenyu zose, kanyhi mugebate ne malajilizo gaga gono nghumulajilizenyi izuwali,
3 ale, Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nayo komuwonelenyi nanye lusungu. Nayo komukongholezenyi kanyhi hamonga, no kumugomolenyi mu wulawa mu makungugo gono yali yakondya kumwiyagazizenyi kuko, kanyhi komumotyenyi.
4 Hamba muwe mwiyagaye caka ako na ako mono yinze yose, ninga mwene Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, komukongholezenyi du, no kumugomolenyi kanyhi,
5 ho muyihale kanyhi yisi yono walezi wenyu wali wayitemile. Nayo komumotyenyi muno muno, kanyhi komonjelezenyi muwe muli wenji si nhe walezi wenyu.
6 Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, komupelenyi nyenye hamonga ne wana wenyu nhumbula yono yina cihuliko kuli mwene, ho mumwende kwe zinhumbula zenyu zose, kanyhi ho mujendelele kwikala mu yisi yiyo.
7 Kanyhi kowuwusa wulijitwe wono wuli muli'nye, yawutajile muli walugu wenyu wono wamusulilenyi no kumwiwonelenyi,
8 nanye nhondo ho momuhulika Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu kanyhi no kwibata i malajilizo gaga gose gono nghumulonjelenyi izuwali.
9 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, komumotyenyi muno muno muli milimo yenyu yose. Mowa mwina wana wenji ne mitugo minji muno, ne migunda yenyu, yowa kulavya nhwanga mbaha muno muno. Nayo kosangalala muno kumumotyenyi, nha vyono yali yawamotizye walezi wenyu.
10 Ninga munojeye nanye mumuhulice mwene, no kwibata malajilizo gakwe gose gono gandicigwe mu citabu cici, i malajilizo ge mejizo gakwe. Holeka munojeye mumuwuyile, kwe zinhumbula zenyu zose.

11 “Ne lilajilizo lili lyono nghumupelenyi izuwali, hamba si yikamu yaye, kanyhi hamba si lili kutali nanye.
12 Hamba si lili kucanyha mu mavunde yaye. Holeka musina soko yo kuwuza muwe kutya, ‘Alo nani mono kosuma kubita kuko kucanyha yakacitimizye lilajilizo lilyo, nase ho cilihulice no kulikamisa?’
13 Kanyhi hamba si lili ku nhumbi nzagwe ye lilenga yaye. Holeka musina soko yo kuwuza muwe kutya, ‘Alo nani mono kosuma kulowoka lilenga, yakaciletele lilajilizo lilyo, nase ho cilihulice no kulikamisa?’
14 Ng'o yaye, hambi lilajilizo nha lili bahano, kanyhi lili muli nyenye, mu zinhumbula zenyu, nanye mukondizye no kulimanya, alu mukusakwa mulikamise du no kwituma.

15 “Alu izuwali nane nghumupelenyi welecele wo kwihagulila nyenye nyo weneco, muli zizi zi nyejete, muli ziswanu ne zimbi, na muli wumi ne lifwa.
16 Hono mugebate malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, gono nghumupelenyi izuwali, hono mumwende, kanyhi mumuhulice no kugebata malajilizo gakwe gose, mbeka momoteswa, kanyhi mowa nghungugo mbaha muno muno. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, komulugutizenyi muno muno mu yisi yono moyisola yiwe yenyu.
17 Ninga hono mwijelele du muzibedegale zizi, no kuwa musina cihuliko, mukwegwe, muyihembe milungu yinji no kuyitumamila,
18 mumanye vyono mbeka mokongomelela lukulu. Holeka ane nghumusugulenyi izuwali. Hamba si mosuma kwikala vilo vinji mu yisi yiyo yono yili ku nhumbi ye likolongo lya Yoludani yono moyisola yiwe yenyu.
19 Holeka ane izuwali nghumupelenyi welecele, mwihagulile nyenye nyo weneco muli zizi zi nyejete, muli wumi ne lifwa, na muli wulugutwe wo Mulungu no wulijitwe wo Mulungu, nane nghusaka wulanga ne yinze, viwe vihomelezi vyenyu, kuli cono nyenye mocihagula. Ninga ane nghumutyenyi, muhagule wumi, ho mowumipa, nyenye, hamonga ne wana wenyu.
20 Mumwende Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, mumuhulice, kanyhi muwe muli wehuwilwa kuli mwene, holeka nyenye hamonga ne wana wenyu, mosuma kwikala vilo vinji, mu yisi yono yehuwiza vyono kowapela i walezi wenyu Abulahamu, na Isaka, na Yakobo.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 31

Yoshuwa yakuhalila mulimo wa Musa

1 Nayo Musa yakajendelela du kuwalonjela wanhu wa Isilayeli yakutya,
2 “Nye wandugu zangu, ane nakondya kuficila luno myaka yigana limonga na makumi mejete go wikale wangu. Holeka hamba si nosuma kujendelela kuwa mutonji wenyu kanyhi. Hamonga na zizo, Muwaha Mulungu, yakondya kunonjela nha vyono ane si nosuma kulowoka likolongo lya Yoludani.
3 Holeka Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yo mweneco du yo komutonjenyi, kanyhi kogongomeza i makungugo gono gekaye mumo, anye ho muyisole yisi yawo yiwe yenyu. Nayo Yoshuwa yo kowa mutonji wenyu, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalonga.
4 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu kowongomezeza lukulu wanhu wawo, nha vyono yanoza kuli Sihoni na Ogu i watemi we Waamoli, yakayongomeza sunga ne yisi yawo.
5 Muwaha Mulungu komupelenyi cisumisumi ho muwasume, kanyhi muwatendele nha vyono namulajilizenyi muwatendele.
6 Mweze ne vilungo ne nhumbula yo wutung'ati, mulece kuwa no woga hamba kuwogopa yaye. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, nha yali hamonga nanye, si komucepucilenyi, hamba si komulecenyi yaye.”

7 Hanyuma Musa yakawa kumucema Yoshuwa, yakawa kumulonjela ha mwande ye wanhu wose Waisilayeli yakutya, “Weze ne vilungo ne nhumbula yo wutung'ati, hambi nha yo gwegwe mono kowatonga wanhu wawa sunga wayisole yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu yawehuwiza walezi wawo.
8 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, mweneco yo kokutonga kanyhi kowa hamonga nagwe. Nayo si kokucepucila hamba kukuleka yaye. Holeka wulece kutununhika mu nhumbula yako hamba kuwa na woga yaye.”

Lilajilizo lyeze kusomwa mu mwaka wo mupunghati, mu myaka yose

9 Hodu Musa yakawa kulyandika Lilajilizo lyo Mulungu, yakawa kuwapela i Wakohani Walawi, wono wali wo walanjilizi we Lisanduku lye Cilagane co Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga ne wanyamhala wose we Waisilayeli.
10 Yakawa kuwalajiliza yakutya, “Mu mwaka wo mupunghati, wo wukombozwe wa mu myaka yose kolo wafika, ale, mweze kusoma lilajilizo lili kwe lizi yiwaha mwi'daha lye sikukuu ye vibanda.
11 Weze kuwasomela wanhu Waisilayeli, lono wehanguliza mu wuhembi, i hanhu ho wuhembelo wakumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu.
12 Weze kuwakongholela wose, i wanhu mututu walume ne wacekulu, hamonga ne wana, hamonga ne wajenzi wose wono wekalane nanye mu makaya genyu. Wose ho wasume kwihulicila weneco kwe makutu gawo, weyijize kumupela cogopo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, kanyhi ho wasume kugebata mejizo gakwe ko wihuwilwa.
13 Na mu nzila yiyi, i wana wenyu wono wakali nazihulika ze Lilajilizo lili, lyo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, ho wasume kulihulika. Nawo ho weyijize kumuhulika Muwaha Mulungu, mu vilo vyawo vyose vya mu wikale wawo, wa mu yisi yono yili ku nhumbi ye likolongo lya Yoludani yono moyitema.”

Malajilizo ge ndumilizo go Muwaha Mulungu kuli Musa

14 Hanyuma Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kumulonjela Musa yakatya, “Vilo vyo wikale wako vyasigala vicefu, holeka wumucemaje Yoshuwa winjile nayo mwi 'Hema lyono lyelile, ho nimupele malajilizo vyono yowa yimunojeye yetume.” Ale du, Musa na Yoshuwa wakawa kwinjila mwi 'Hema lyono lyelile.
15 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kwilajilila hali wene mwisumbili lye livunde lyono lyali limile ha mulango we Lihema lyono Lyelile.

16 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Alu agwe Musa, weza wuli behi no kufwa, na lono kowa wakondya kufwa, hodu wanhu hamba si wowa wehuwilwa kanyhi kuli'ne, kanyhi wocibena ne cilagane cangu cono nalagana nawo. Wondeka, wawe kuhemba milungu yo wulambi yono yili mu yisi yono wene wokwinjila.
17 Na kolo zizi zikwijela, nane nowakolelwa likuwo, niwalece wongomele. Ne magazo menji go wizine gowaziza, nawo baho ho wolomanya vyono gago gose gakuwejelela, vyono ane no Muwaha Mulungu wawo, si nili hamonga nawo kanyhi.
18 Nane hamba si nolowataza yaye, hambi nha wakondya kwituma wubi so wuhemba milungu yinji.

19 “Aluno wulwandikaje lwimbo luluno, wuwejize wanhu wa Isilayeli, u lwimbo lulu ho luwe wuhomelezi wa kuli'ne, kuli Waisilayeli.
20 Ho yiwe kolo nawenjiza mu yisi yono yina moso, yisi yono nehuwiza vyono nowapela i walezi wawo, nawo kolo walya vya mumo weguta, kanyhi wajina, ninga woloyiwuyila milungu yinji wayihembe no kuyitumamila, wolondeka no kucibena cilagane cangu kuli wene, hambi lu kanyhi nha wakondya kwiguta.
21 Ninga kolo i zimbi zikuwaziza hamonga ne magazo mawaha, ale, u lwimbo lulo lolohomelela nha wuhomelezi kuli wene. Ninga hamba nhavivyo nghondizye kuzimanya za mu mahweso gawo sunga na lono nghali hamba nawafisa mu yisi yono nawehuwiziza.”

22 Nayo Musa yakawa kulwandika lwimbo lulo mwi'zuwa lilyo, no kuwejiza i Waisilayeli.

23 Hanyuma Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kumulonjela Yoshuwa u mwana wa Nuni yakamutya, “Ale, weze ne vilungo ne nhumbula yo wutung'ati. Hambi nha yo gwegwe mono kowatonga i wanhu wawa Waisilayeli sunga wuwenjize mu yisi yono nawehuwiziza, nane nowa nili hamonga nagwe.”

24 Nayo Musa yakalyandika mu citabu Lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu ko wulanjilile wuwaha muno, ho yalece kuyisemwa nghani yinji yo yose.
25 Nayo yali yeza kukondya kulyandika Lilajilizo, yakawa kuwacema i wakohani Walawi wono wali wo walanjilizi we Lisanduku lye Cilagane, yakawatya,
26 “Ale, solenyi citabu aci cono candicigwe Lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, muciwice ha lubavu lwe Lisanduku lye Cilagane co Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, ciwe kwikala baho nhondo zose, ho ciwe wuhomelezi kuli wanhu wakwe.
27 Ane manyile vyono wali cibede kanyhi wasina cihuliko hamba hadodo bwete. Hambi nha manyile vyono wasina cihuliko hali Muwaha Mulungu mu wulawa mu mikalile yangu yose. Kanyhi manyile du vyono wocila kuwa wasina cihuliko sunga na hono niwe nakondya kufwa.
28 Holeka aluno wakongholelaje ha mwande yangu, i walongola wose we milango yose ye Waisilayeli, hamonga ne wanyamhala wose, ho sume kuwalonjela zizi. Nane nghusaka wulanga ne yinze, viwe vihomelezi vyawo.
29 Ninga nane manyile du vyono, kolo nakondya kufwa, wanhu wawa wowa wabi, kanyhi hamba si wozisaka na zono nawejiza. Na mu vilo vyono vyokuza, wolokwitang'anila ne magazo mawaha, hambi nha wolowa wakondya kumukoza likuwo Muwaha Mulungu, so wituma zimbi zono si zinojeye hali mwene.”

U lwimbo lwa Musa

30 Nayo musa yakemba zinghani zo lwimbo lulu ha mwande ye Waisilayeli, lono wali wehanguliza, nawo ho waluhulicize, yakatya,


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 32

1 “Nye nyo'si no wulanga,

muhulicize zinghani zangu,

muteje makutu kuli zono nghulonga.

2 Hambi mejizo gangu, nha golagala

nha mabanhi ga mvula,

gogwisa buhe mu yisi,

zinghani zangu zomuzizenyi nha vibanhi

vya mvula mu mahanze madece,

nha mabanhi ga mvula mu vilimwa.

3 Nane nolikumya litagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu,

ne wanhu wakwe wolonga zo wuwaha wakwe.

4 “Muwaha Mulungu yo mukamisi wenyu

mono yali vilungo, mono si kasinjisikaga,

kanyhi yali ling'ani muli zakwe zose.

Mulungu wenyu mwihuwilwa

kanyhi yo we mbeka.

5 Ninga anye si muli wehuwilwa,

musina cambuzi hamba no

kuwucemwa wanagwe,

muli nghungugo nyituma wubi

kanyhi muli walambi.

6 Alu anye nyo wanhu walele,

kanyhi mono musina lilumbo,

mukwihwesa nzila yiyo yo swanu lo

yo kumwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu?

Ninga, mwene yo sogo yenyu

kanyhi yo muwumbi wenyu,

yo mwene mono yamunozenyi

ho muwe yikungugo.

7 “Alu ta mwihwesaje zono zapuluta,

ze vilo vye katali,

muwawuzaje ta walezi wenyu,

wamulonjelenyi zono

zejela mu vilo vyawo,

muwawuzaje ta ne wakombi,

wamulonjelenyi ze katali.

8 Muwaha Mulungu mono yalawiliciye

muli vyose, yuyo yo

yawagawila zi'si makungugo gose.

Yakawagawila wanhu no wikalo wawo.

Mwene yawawicila mipaka,

mu nghango ye liwalo lye wanhu

wo Mulungu.

9 Ninga yakehagulila we ndelo ya Yakobo

wawe wanhu wakwe.

10 “Yawawona lono wayumbayumbaga mu nyika

kunhu kubi, kumemile mavulavumbi

gose, ninga mwene yawacitila no kuwadima

nha vyono yadahile kwidima mweneco.

11 Yakawa nha 'ndi yikulanjiza mana gayo

ku wuguluce, yikugakumbatila

mu mabawa gayo,

Muwaha Mulungu nayo vyo yawakamisaga Waisilayeli.

12 Muwaha Mulungu yo yawatonga wanhu wakwe

hamba hasina mulungu yunji

mujenzi mono yamutaza yaye.

13 Muwaha Mulungu yakawapela i hanhu

hose hono henuce, weneco wahateme,

wakalya malimwa gose ga mu migunda.

Wakabakula no wuci mu matalawe, wakalya,

ne mafuta gakalawa mwi'bwe.

14 Zing'ombe zawo hamonga ne zimhene zawo

zikalavya mele menji muno.

Nawo wakawa wagoli we zingholo, ne

zimhene ne zing'ombe ziswanu zono zijinile.

Kanyhi wakawa no wusaje wuswanu,

hamonga ne divayi.

15 “Wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu,

wakagolipa muno,

wakajina muno,

wakawa wena ndigwa nyinji.

Ninga kanyhi ho wakawa wafyuzi

wakamuleka Mulungu wawo mono yawawumba,

wakamulema u muponyi wawo mono yali vilungo.

16 Wakamupela Muwaha Mulungu wivu so

milungu yawo mijenzi,

no wubi wawo wono wetuma,

wukamukoza Muwaha Mulungu likuwo.

17 Wakayilavizya vilavywa i milungu yo wulambi,

i milungu mipya yono hamba ne walezi

wawo wali si wayimanyile yaye.

I milungu yono Waisilayeli wali

wakali hamba nayitumamila yaye.

18 Wakamusemwa Muwaha Mulungu wawo,

u mukombozi wawo mono yali vilungo,

mono yawapela wumi.

19 “Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yali yeza

kuziwona zizo, hodu yakakolwa likuwo,

yakawa kuwaleka wanagwe we cilume na

we cicekulu.

20 Yakawa kutya, ‘Alu hodu, nane hamba

si nojendelela kanyhi kuwapela wutazi.

Hanyuma ho mbone cono cowagana,

i wanhu wawano wono wasina cihuliko,

kanyhi si wehuwilwa.

21 Wanghoza likuwo ku soko ye

milungu yawo mijenzi,

nane hamba si nghwendezwa yaye ne

milungu yawo yiyo,

milungu yono mbeka hamba si milungu yaye.

Holeka nane notuma likungugo linji

liwakoze nawo likuwo.

Nowanoza nawo wawone wivu

ne likungugo lyo wulele.

22 Likuwo lyangu nane lyokwaka nha moto

nalyo lyovitakamiza vinhu vyose mono'nze.

Lyovificila na vyose vyono vili hasi

ku yinze ye wafu,

no kuyitakamiza ne misinji ye matunda.

23 “ ‘Holeka nane nowaletela magazo mawaha

kanyhi nowaguma ne zisonga zangu zose.

24 Nawo wofwa ku soko ye nzala ne mhungo,

wofwa ku soko ye matamwa mabi.

Noleta malimu ga mu mbago gawahawanje,

ne mazoka gono gena sumu,

gawalumanje.

25 Nawo wulugu wowaletela mafwa mawaha mu zinzila

no wogofywe wowagana mu zikaya zawo,

i wazelelo hamonga ne wahinza, wose wofwa,

ne wagalika wose ne wakombi,

hasina mono kosigala.

26 Nane nowongomezeza wose lukulu,

hawe hasina yunji hamba monga

mono kolowakumbucila kanyhi.

27 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, si nolowaleceleza

i walugu wawo waze welumbe,

wawe kutya wawasuma wanhu wangu.

Na kumbe nene no mweneco yo

nizinozize zizi zose.’

28 “Waisilayeli lyo likungugo lye walele

wasina hamba wizuce yaye.

29 Hamba si wakusuma kumanya i sokoci yono

yilecile wakusumwa,

hamba kumanya bwete cono ciwejeleye yaye.

30 Hamba kumanya vyono wali wanhu

elufu monga, ninga munhu monga

du kawasuma.

Na wanhu elufu yikumi wasumwa na wanhu

wejete du?

Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo, yakondya

kuwaleka, Mulungu wawo we vilungo,

yakondya kuwagumila.

31 I walugu wawo nawo nha wamanyile

vyono milungu yawo yisoceye, na vyono

hamba si yihwanile no Mulungu we Waisilayeli.

32 I walugu wawo nha wawahodanga,

nha vyono Sodoma na Gomola.

Wehwana zabibu zono zili cisungu,

na zabibu zayo zina sumu.

33 Wehwana divayi yono nozezwe sumu ye zinzoka.

34 “Ninga mwene Muwaha Mulungu,

yaziwene zizo zose zono walugu wawo wazitumile.

Nayo yalindiye lipango lifice lyo

kuwalipiciziza wawo walugu.

35 Muwaha Mulungu kowalipiciza wulipicizwe wuwaha.

Lipango lyofika du lyono wosumilwa lukulu,

hambi lipango lyo wugaye wawo

nha lili behi kuza.

36 Muwaha Mulungu kowaponya wanhu wakwe,

kolo yeza kuwawona vyono walemegwa.

Nayo kowawonela lusungu, wose wono

wakumwitumamila mwene.

Kolo yakondya kuwawona vyono mbeka

wakondya kulemwa.

37 Muwaha Mulungu kowawuza wanhu

wakwe yawe kutya,

‘Alo milungu yono mwali muyitumbiye,

yili hayi kanyhi?

38 Yono mwayilisa vilavywa vyenyu vyono vijinile,

mukayipela ne divayi ho ying'we.

Ale ta, na yize le, yimupelenyi lu wutazi,

yiwe kuli'nye wutizizo wenyu.

39 “ ‘Alu mulanje! Ane yo nene Mulungu du

nili wene, hamba hasina mulungu yunji

kanyhi, langa nene du.

Yo nene mono nghuwulaga,

kanyhi nghuwumifya,

nghutajila nghomele kanyhi nghuponya.

Na hasina munhu mono konyimila

muli yono nghwituma.

40 Na mbeka vyono yo nene Mulungu mumi,

nghunyasula muwoko wangu, nghwilahiza,

41 nha vyono nowunola mumaje wangu

we limuli, nanje hono ling'ani

yikwitumwa hamba ng'o.

Nowalipiciza walugu wangu,

nowalipiciza wono wakusula.

42 Nozing'wesa zisonga zangu ne sakami yawo,

no mumaje wangu nawo, wowawulaga

wose wono wakusula.

Nane si nosigaza hamba monga, muli wawo

wono wakwisumaniza nane,

sunga na we zinghomele,

ne wafungwa, wose wofwa du.’

43 “Nanye nyo makungugo,

muwapele lukumyo i wanhu wo Mulungu,

hambi Mulungu nha kawalipicizaga

wose wono wakuwawulaga.

Kawalipicizaga walugu wakwe.

Langa i wanhu wakwe,

kawanenelaga wubi wawo.”

44 Musa na Yoshuwa u mwana wa Nuni, wakalwimba u lwimbo lulu, i Waisilayeli ho wasume kuluhulika.

Malajilizo ge ndumilizo ga Musa, kuli wanhu

45 Musa yali yeza kukondya kuwejiza wanhu i mejizo go Muwaha Mulungu,
46 hanyuma kanyhi yakawa kuwatya, “Muzikamisaje mu zinhumbula zenyu ze malajilizo gaga gono nali kumulonjelenyi izuwali. Muwejize ne wana wenyu, nawo ho wawe ne cihuliko ne cogopo, kuli mejizo gago go Muwaha Mulungu.
47 I mejizo ga gano, si nghani za bwete du yaye, langa wo wumi wenyu. Holeka munojeye mugatyatye, ho mosuma kuwa na vilo vinji vyo wikale wenyu, mu yisi yiyo, yono yili ku nhumbi ye likolongo lya Yoludani, yono mokwinjila muyiteme.”

48 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo, Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
49 “Ale, wubite mu matunda ga Abalimu, mu yisi ya Mowabu yono vidomile ne likaya lya Yeliko. Wubite mucanyha ye litunda lya Nebo, wuyilanje yisi yose ya Kanani, yono nghuwapela i Waisilayeli, wuyiwone vyono yinha.
50 Nagwe kofwila kuko kucanyha ye litunda lilyo, nha vyono nduguyo Haluni nayo yafwila mucanyha ye litunda lya Holi.
51 Hambi anye wose vyono muli wejete, nha mwambedegalila ha mwande ye Waisilayeli. Hambi, lono mwali muli ha malenga ga Meliba, behi ne likaya lya Kadeshi mu nyika ya Zini, agwe nha wambedegalila ha mwande ye wanhu wa Isilayeli.
52 Holeka luno koyilanjila meso du yisi yiyo, mu wulawa mwi'tunda, yisi yiyo yono nghuwapela wanhu wa Isilayeli, ninga agwe si kokwinjila mu yisi yiyo ng'o.”


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 33

Musa yakuwalugutiza we milango ye Waisilayeli

1 Awu wo wulugutize, wono Musa u munhu wo Mulungu yawalugutiza Waisilayeli, lono yakali si nafwa, yakatya,

2 “Muwaha Mulungu yaza he

'tunda lya Sinayi,

yakelajila kotya yizuwa mu yisi ya Edomu,

yakawabulilila wanhu wakwe mu wulawa

mwi'tunda lya Palani.

Ne wamalayika elufu yikumi,

wakawa wali hamonga nayo.

Na mu muwoko wakwe wo kulume,

yakawa yebate

moto wono wakaga.

3 Na mbeka Muwaha Mulungu

yawendile wanhu wakwe,

kanyhi mwene kawadimaga i wakwe.

Holeka cinojele cimufugamile,

cigebate na malajilizo gakwe.

4 Ciwibate no kuwukamisa wulajilize wono

Musa yacijiza,

hambi nha wo wuhazi wuwaha

we nghungugo yetu.

5 Muwaha Mulungu yakawa mutemi muli

wanhu wakwe Waisilayeli,

Lono milango yawo yose,

hamonga ne walongola wawo wose

wekongholela hamonga.”

6 Musa yakazilonga za wo mulango wa Lubeni

yakatya, “Yawumipe Lubeni,

hamba yalece kufwa.

Langa wanhu wakwe wo wawe wacefu.”

7 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Yuda,

yakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wuhulicize cililo cawo wuwataze,

wuwagomole, wuwalunze kanyhi ne

wandugu zawo,

hanyuma gwegwe wuwalewanile,

wuwataze kuli walugu wawo.”

8 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Lawi,

yakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wuwalajililaje zo wuhile wako,

i wetumami wako wehuwilwa

i Walawi, mu nzila ye Ulimu na Tumimu.

Wono wawajela ku Masa, ho wuwawone hono

wali wanhu we mbeka,

ha malenga ga Meliba.

9 Wakalajila cogopo ciwaha hali'gwe,

si nha kuli walezi, hamba kuli wandugu,

hamba kuli wana.

Kanyhi wakagakamisa ne malajilizo gako.

Kanyhi wakawa wehuwilwa kuli ze

Cilagane cako.

10 Nawo wowejiza ne wanhu wako

webate Lilajilizo lyako.

Wokulavizya ne makumbiko mu

cikumbicilo cako.

11 Holeka go Muwaha Mulungu,

wuwataze wo mulango wawo

wuwiluce wuli vilungo.

Milimo yawo yose yikwendeze

wuwawusize lukulu walugu wawo,

walece kwinuka kanyhi.”

12 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Benyamini, yakatya,

“Awu wo mulango wono Muwaha Mulungu,

yawendile no kuwudima.

Mwene kawakamisaga misi yose.

Kanyhi yali hamonga nawo nhondo zose.”

13 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Yusufu,

yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu

yayilugutize yisi yawo ko

kuyitonyezeza mvula mbaha,

hamonga ne mivwilo ye malenga.

14 Yisi yawo yilugutizwe,

yiwe na mikalo minji ya

mwi'pango lye lizuwa.

Yigolipe ne mikalo ye cibahu ne cifuku.

15 Sunga ne vigongo navyo vimeme mikalo yose.

16 Ne yisi yawo yimeme vinhu viswanu

vye mawala gose.

Wuswanu wo Muwaha Mulungu,

wuyilugute,

Mono yalonjela mu cititika

cono cakaga moto.

Wulugutwe wu wuno wose,

wuwazize wo mulango wa Yusufu.

Hambi mwene nha yo yali mulongola

we wanduguze.

17 Yusufu yali vilungo, yenha

nghambaku, ne mahembe ge nimu ya mu mbago.

Mahembe gakwe viling'aniye na wanhu

maelufu, wo mu mulango wa Manase,

ne wanhu elufu yikumi wo mulango wa Efulayimu.

Ne mahembe gago, go kozihomela

zinghungugo zose,

yaziwinje sunga ko ku mhelo ye yinze.”

18 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Zabuloni

na wa Isakali, yakatya,

“Nayo Zabuloni, milimo yakwe yo

wusuluza ya mwi'lenga, yimoteswe.

Nayo Isakali sawo yakwe yonjelezece

mu mahema gakwe.

19 Wowacema wajenzi, wabite mwi'tunda lyawo

Wakalavye kuko makumbiko gono ganojeye.

Nawo wovituga viwutwa vya mwi'lenga,

sunga na vya mu musawawa mu mwejelele

we lilenga.”

20 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Gadi,

yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu

yalumbwe, mono yawonjeleza yisi

yawo yikawa ngazi muno.

Hambi Gadi nha yekaye kotya simba,

ho yawuzewe muwoko sunga ko kwi 'ndosi.

21 Hambi nha wehagulila mu yisi,

hanhu haswanu.

Ne ligawo lyo wulongola, wakapegwa weneco.

Nawo wakagebata malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu

hamonga no wulajilize wakwe.

Lono walongola wose we Waisilayeli wali

wehanguliza hamonga.”

22 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Dani,

yakatya, “Dani mwana simba

mono yakuzumhilila ku Bashani.”

23 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Nafutali,

yakatya, “Nafutali mono wumemile

wulugutwe wo Muwaha Mulungu,

hamonga no wumoteswe.

Yisi yawo yificile ku lubavu lwa kutakama,

ku wulawa he'lamba lya Galilaya.”

24 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Asheli, yakatya,

“Asheli yo mulugutwe si nhe milango yinji yose.

Yeze yo mwendece kuli wanduguze.

Ne yisi yakwe yigolifywe ko winji

we mizeyituni.

25 Ne makaya gawo,

gadimwe ne malango ga cuma.

Nawo weze kwikala wapolile vilo vyose.”

26 Nye nyo Waisilayeli,

manyenyi vyono, hasina

mulungu yunji mono yacizize

u Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.

Mono yakuziza mu mavunde ho

yamutazenyi.

Yakuziza mu mavunde,

mu lukumyo lwakwe.

27 Muwaha Mulungu yo mudimi wenyu nhondo zose.

Ne miwoko yakwe ye cibitilila, wo wutazi wenyu.

Kawawinjilaga kutali i walugu wenyu

so cambuzi cenyu.

Kanyhi yakumulonjelenyi

muwongomeze wose.

28 We ndelo ya Yakobo

ho wekale no mulamu

kanyhi wapolile, mumo mu yisi

yono yimemile

wuhemba winji, hamonga ne divayi,

i hanhu hono buhe yilawaga ku wulanga,

yiwa kuwudejefya muhangha.

29 Alu muli wamoti 'nye nyo Isilayeli,

hambi nani kanyhi mono

yehwanile nyenye,

nyo nghungugo yono yakondya kuponywa

no Muwaha Mulungu.

Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yo

ngula yenyu no mumaje wenyu,

ho yamudimenyi no kumupelenyi wusume.

Iwalugu wenyu womuzizenyi wamulombenyi

wakusaka muwawonele lusungu.

Nanye baho ho mowakanyanjilila

lukulu ho hasi.


Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 34

U wufwe wa Musa

1 Musa yakawa kuwuka mu yisi yo wuhihi ya Mowabu, yakawa kubita kucanyha mwi'tunda lya Nebo, ku cizuli ce matunda ga Pisiga, kucilima ya Yeliko. Na ku wulawa kuko kucanyha, Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajila yisi yose. Yisi ya Gileyadi ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, sunga ko kwi 'kaya lya Dani.
2 Ne yisi yose ya Nafutali, sunga ne zi'si zose za Efulayimu na Manase, sunga ne yisi ya Yuda ku lubavu lwa kumwezi ko kwi'lenga lya Meditelaneya.
3 Sunga ku lubavu lwa kutakama lwe yisi ya Yuda, sunga ko ku yisi yo wuhihi ya ku wulawa Zowali sunga ko ku Yeliko, kwi 'kaya lyono lina mibici minji ye mitende.
4 Hanyuma Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kumulonjela Musa yakatya, “Ayi yo yisi yono namwihuwiza Abulahamu, na Isaka, na Yakobo, vyono yo nowapela i wana wawo. Alu nghulajila, ho wuyilanjile meso du, ninga agwe si nokukombola winjile mu yisi yiyo.”

5 Hodu Musa u mwitumami wo Muwaha Mulungu, yakawa kufwila baho du mu yisi ya Mowabu, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulonjela.
6 Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kumuzika Musa mwi'lolo mu yisi ya Mowabu, vidomile ne likaya lya Betipeyoli. Ninga sunga nalu, hasina munhu wo wose mono yahamanyile i hanhu baho hono Musa yazikwa.
7 Musa yakafwa yaficile myaka yigana limonga na makumi mejete. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, vyo yali yakali yakamile du, sunga ne meso gakwe gali gakali vyo gakuwona viswanu du.
8 Nawo wanhu Waisilayeli wakamulilila Musa vilo makumi madatu, baho mu yisi ya Mowabu.

9 Nayo Yoshuwa u mwana wa Nuni yakamema mahala menji. Hambi nha Musa mono yo yamuhagula yawe muhazi wakwe kolo mwene yakondya kufwa. Nawo i wanhu Waisilayeli wakamuhulika muno Yoshuwa, kanyhi wakagebata muno muno ne malajilizo gono Muwaha Mulungu yawalajiliziza muli Musa.

10 Na ku wulawa baho, hakali si nejela kanyhi muwizi yunji mu Isilayeli mono yenha Musa, mono Muwaha Mulungu yalonganaga nayo vidomile.
11 Kanyhi hasina muwizi yunji wowose, mono yanoza mapoto ne vitanjilo, vyono vinha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamutuma Musa yakavinoze ku Misili, kuli mutemi Falawo, na kuli wanyamhala wakwe, sunga na mu yisi yose.
12 Kanyhi hasina muwizi yunji wo wose, mono yasuma kwituma nghani mbaha zo wogofye, nha zono Musa yazinoza ha meso ge Waisilayeli wose.
