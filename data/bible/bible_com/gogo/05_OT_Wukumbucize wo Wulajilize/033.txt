Wukumbucize wo Wulajilize 33

Musa yakuwalugutiza we milango ye Waisilayeli

1 Awu wo wulugutize, wono Musa u munhu wo Mulungu yawalugutiza Waisilayeli, lono yakali si nafwa, yakatya,

2 “Muwaha Mulungu yaza he

'tunda lya Sinayi,

yakelajila kotya yizuwa mu yisi ya Edomu,

yakawabulilila wanhu wakwe mu wulawa

mwi'tunda lya Palani.

Ne wamalayika elufu yikumi,

wakawa wali hamonga nayo.

Na mu muwoko wakwe wo kulume,

yakawa yebate

moto wono wakaga.

3 Na mbeka Muwaha Mulungu

yawendile wanhu wakwe,

kanyhi mwene kawadimaga i wakwe.

Holeka cinojele cimufugamile,

cigebate na malajilizo gakwe.

4 Ciwibate no kuwukamisa wulajilize wono

Musa yacijiza,

hambi nha wo wuhazi wuwaha

we nghungugo yetu.

5 Muwaha Mulungu yakawa mutemi muli

wanhu wakwe Waisilayeli,

Lono milango yawo yose,

hamonga ne walongola wawo wose

wekongholela hamonga.”

6 Musa yakazilonga za wo mulango wa Lubeni

yakatya, “Yawumipe Lubeni,

hamba yalece kufwa.

Langa wanhu wakwe wo wawe wacefu.”

7 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Yuda,

yakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wuhulicize cililo cawo wuwataze,

wuwagomole, wuwalunze kanyhi ne

wandugu zawo,

hanyuma gwegwe wuwalewanile,

wuwataze kuli walugu wawo.”

8 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Lawi,

yakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wuwalajililaje zo wuhile wako,

i wetumami wako wehuwilwa

i Walawi, mu nzila ye Ulimu na Tumimu.

Wono wawajela ku Masa, ho wuwawone hono

wali wanhu we mbeka,

ha malenga ga Meliba.

9 Wakalajila cogopo ciwaha hali'gwe,

si nha kuli walezi, hamba kuli wandugu,

hamba kuli wana.

Kanyhi wakagakamisa ne malajilizo gako.

Kanyhi wakawa wehuwilwa kuli ze

Cilagane cako.

10 Nawo wowejiza ne wanhu wako

webate Lilajilizo lyako.

Wokulavizya ne makumbiko mu

cikumbicilo cako.

11 Holeka go Muwaha Mulungu,

wuwataze wo mulango wawo

wuwiluce wuli vilungo.

Milimo yawo yose yikwendeze

wuwawusize lukulu walugu wawo,

walece kwinuka kanyhi.”

12 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Benyamini, yakatya,

“Awu wo mulango wono Muwaha Mulungu,

yawendile no kuwudima.

Mwene kawakamisaga misi yose.

Kanyhi yali hamonga nawo nhondo zose.”

13 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Yusufu,

yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu

yayilugutize yisi yawo ko

kuyitonyezeza mvula mbaha,

hamonga ne mivwilo ye malenga.

14 Yisi yawo yilugutizwe,

yiwe na mikalo minji ya

mwi'pango lye lizuwa.

Yigolipe ne mikalo ye cibahu ne cifuku.

15 Sunga ne vigongo navyo vimeme mikalo yose.

16 Ne yisi yawo yimeme vinhu viswanu

vye mawala gose.

Wuswanu wo Muwaha Mulungu,

wuyilugute,

Mono yalonjela mu cititika

cono cakaga moto.

Wulugutwe wu wuno wose,

wuwazize wo mulango wa Yusufu.

Hambi mwene nha yo yali mulongola

we wanduguze.

17 Yusufu yali vilungo, yenha

nghambaku, ne mahembe ge nimu ya mu mbago.

Mahembe gakwe viling'aniye na wanhu

maelufu, wo mu mulango wa Manase,

ne wanhu elufu yikumi wo mulango wa Efulayimu.

Ne mahembe gago, go kozihomela

zinghungugo zose,

yaziwinje sunga ko ku mhelo ye yinze.”

18 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Zabuloni

na wa Isakali, yakatya,

“Nayo Zabuloni, milimo yakwe yo

wusuluza ya mwi'lenga, yimoteswe.

Nayo Isakali sawo yakwe yonjelezece

mu mahema gakwe.

19 Wowacema wajenzi, wabite mwi'tunda lyawo

Wakalavye kuko makumbiko gono ganojeye.

Nawo wovituga viwutwa vya mwi'lenga,

sunga na vya mu musawawa mu mwejelele

we lilenga.”

20 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Gadi,

yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu

yalumbwe, mono yawonjeleza yisi

yawo yikawa ngazi muno.

Hambi Gadi nha yekaye kotya simba,

ho yawuzewe muwoko sunga ko kwi 'ndosi.

21 Hambi nha wehagulila mu yisi,

hanhu haswanu.

Ne ligawo lyo wulongola, wakapegwa weneco.

Nawo wakagebata malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu

hamonga no wulajilize wakwe.

Lono walongola wose we Waisilayeli wali

wehanguliza hamonga.”

22 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Dani,

yakatya, “Dani mwana simba

mono yakuzumhilila ku Bashani.”

23 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Nafutali,

yakatya, “Nafutali mono wumemile

wulugutwe wo Muwaha Mulungu,

hamonga no wumoteswe.

Yisi yawo yificile ku lubavu lwa kutakama,

ku wulawa he'lamba lya Galilaya.”

24 Na kuli za wo mulango wa Asheli, yakatya,

“Asheli yo mulugutwe si nhe milango yinji yose.

Yeze yo mwendece kuli wanduguze.

Ne yisi yakwe yigolifywe ko winji

we mizeyituni.

25 Ne makaya gawo,

gadimwe ne malango ga cuma.

Nawo weze kwikala wapolile vilo vyose.”

26 Nye nyo Waisilayeli,

manyenyi vyono, hasina

mulungu yunji mono yacizize

u Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.

Mono yakuziza mu mavunde ho

yamutazenyi.

Yakuziza mu mavunde,

mu lukumyo lwakwe.

27 Muwaha Mulungu yo mudimi wenyu nhondo zose.

Ne miwoko yakwe ye cibitilila, wo wutazi wenyu.

Kawawinjilaga kutali i walugu wenyu

so cambuzi cenyu.

Kanyhi yakumulonjelenyi

muwongomeze wose.

28 We ndelo ya Yakobo

ho wekale no mulamu

kanyhi wapolile, mumo mu yisi

yono yimemile

wuhemba winji, hamonga ne divayi,

i hanhu hono buhe yilawaga ku wulanga,

yiwa kuwudejefya muhangha.

29 Alu muli wamoti 'nye nyo Isilayeli,

hambi nani kanyhi mono

yehwanile nyenye,

nyo nghungugo yono yakondya kuponywa

no Muwaha Mulungu.

Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yo

ngula yenyu no mumaje wenyu,

ho yamudimenyi no kumupelenyi wusume.

Iwalugu wenyu womuzizenyi wamulombenyi

wakusaka muwawonele lusungu.

Nanye baho ho mowakanyanjilila

lukulu ho hasi.
