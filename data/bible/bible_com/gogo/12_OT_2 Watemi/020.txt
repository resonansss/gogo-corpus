2 Watemi 20

Wutamwe no wunoje wo mutemi Hezekiya

(Isa 38:1-8, 21-22; 2Zin 32:24-26)

1 Na mu mapango gago, mutemi Hezekiya yakawa yatamwa muno muno yali behi no kufwa. Nayo muwizi Isaya u mwana wa Amozi, yakabita kumuwona u mutemi Hezekiya, yakamulonjela yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu yakukutya wunozeleze zose, hambi nha si kopona yaye mu mhungo yiyi. Holeka wiwikanizaje kufwa.”

2 Mutemi Hezekiya, yakapitucila kwi'jinji, yakawa kulomba yakutya,
3 “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wukumbucile nha vyono, ane nakwitumamila ko wihuwilwa ne cogopo, na mu vilo vyose, nghawa kuhidimbala kwituma zo wuhile wako.” Hezekiya yali yeza kudumya wulombe wakwe, hodu yakanza kulila ko wusungu wuwaha muno muno.

4 Nayo muwizi Isaya, hodu yakawa kuwuka hali mutemi, yakawa kutuluka. Ninga lono yali yakali nacipuluta ciwanza ca mu kaya ya mugati mwi'kulu, Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela vyono,
5 yawuye ne nyuma kanyhi kuli mutemi Hezekiya, u mutemi we wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu, yakamulonjele yatye, “Ane no Muwaha Mulungu, no Mulungu wo sogo Dawudi, nicihulika cililo cako, hamonga no wulombe wako. Holeka alu nokuponya, nagwe mu vilo vidatu kojenda no kobita ko ku Nyumba yo Mulungu.
6 Nane nokuleka wuwe mumi kanyhi, sunga yipulute myaka yikumi na mihano. Na kanyhi, noliponya sunga ne likaya lilino lya Yelusalemi, ku wulawa mu muwoko wo mutemi wa Siliya. Nane nolikamisa likaya lili ku soko yangu, na ku soko yo wihuwizo wangu, wono namwihuwiziza Dawudi u mwitumami wangu.”

7 Hanyuma muwizi Isaya yakawalonjela i wetumami wo mutemi yakawatya, “Alu musole nhonje yo wugali wudecedece, wono munozwe ne mana go mukuyu, muyijelece mucanyha ye lipu lyakwe, nayo kopona.”
8 Mutemi Hezekiya yakamuwuza Isaya yakatya, “Alu citanjiloci cono condajila, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu komhonya, na kanyhi nha vyono kolo vikupuluta vilo vidatu, nane nosuma kujenda no kubita kwinjla mu Nyumba yo Mulungu?”

9 Nayo Isaya yakamupitula yakamutya, “Muwaha Mulungu heya kokulajila citanjilo, cono colajila vyono, mwene kowufisiliza wihuwizo wakwe. Holeka alu nagwe nghusaka wunonjele, hono kokwenda mwenzenze we ngazi zizi wujendelele ku mwande nghanzo yikumi, hamba wuwuye ne nyuma nghanzo yikumi?”

10 Hezekiya yakapitula yakatya, “Ane nghuwona nha vyono kowa kupelu mwenzenze ku wubita ku mwande nghanzo yikumi! Holeka ciba wuwunoze wu wuye ne nyuma nghanzo yikumi.”

11 Nayo Isaya yakamulomba Muwaha Mulungu, nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawunoza mwenzenze we ngazi zono yazenga mutemi Ahazi, wukawuya ne nyuma nghanzo yikumi.

Wanhu wakutumwa ku wulawa ku Babiloni

(Isa 39:1-8)

12 Na mu vilo vivyo u mutemi wa Babiloni, mono yacemwaga Melodaki Baladani, u mwana wa Baladani, yakawa yahulika nha vyono mutemi Hezekiya mutamwa, holeka yakawa kumwandicila baluwa yakamutegulila hamonga ne cipegwa.
13 Nayo Hezekiya yakawabocela viswanu wawo wanhu wono watumwa kuli mwene, yakawa kuwalajila wugoli wakwe wose we zifweza, na zabahu, hamonga na vye mhunjile swanu, ne mafuta maswanu, hamonga ne ciwutwa ce vitwalo vyakwe vyo wulugu. Na hali hasina cinji co cose cono cali ciwicigwe mu zinyumba ze viwutwa vyakwe, hamba vyono vyali viwicigwe hanhu ho hose mu wutemi wakwe, vyono si yawalajila yaye.
14 Hanyuma muwizi Isaya, nhondo yakawa kubita kuli mutemi Hezekiya, yakamuwuza yakutya, “Alo wanhu wawa walawilila hayi, na kanyhi wakulonjela nghanici?”

Nayo mutemi Hezekiya yakapitula yakatya, “Wanhu wawa walawilila kutali muno ku yisi ya Babiloni.”

15 Isaya yakatya, “Alu wawona cici mwi'kulu?” Nayo Hezekiya yakatya, “Niwalajila vinhu vyose vya mu viwutwa vyangu, hasina hamba cinji cono cili mu viwutwa vyose, cono si niwalajiye yaye.”

16 Hobaho baho muwizi Isaya yakawa kumutya mutemi, “Muwaha Mulungu, yakukutya nha vyono,
17 cipango cokuza, lono i vinhu vyose vyono vili mwi'kulu lyako, viwutwa vyose vyono walezi wako waviwika sunga nzuwayi, vyose vyolosolwa vitegulwe ku Babiloni. Hasina hamba cinhu cinji cono colosigala.
18 Sunga ne wanago wanji wolosolwa, wabite kuko wakapaswe, wawe wetumami mu kaya ye likulu lyo mutemi wa kuko ku Babiloni.”

19 Nayo mutemi Hezekiya yakatanga nha vyono kumbe wowa mulamu no wucitilwe mu vilo vyono visigaye vyo wikale wakwe. Holeka yakapitula yakatya, “I nghani yono wunonjeye yono yilawile kuli Muwaha Mulungu, mbeka yo nghani swanu muno.”

Ndumilizo yo wutemi wa Hezekiya

(2Zin 32:32-33)

20 Ne zinghani zono mutemi Hezekiya yetuma, hamonga no wupiligazi wakwe, na vyono yanoza cilamba, yakahimba cilamba, yakawicila ne zibomba zono zenjizaga malenga mwi'kaya. Zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Yuda.
21 Hezekiya yakafwa. No mwanagwe, Manase, yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.
