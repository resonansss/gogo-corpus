2 Watemi 8

U mucekulu mulawa Shunemu, yakuwuya kwawo

1 Ninga Elisha yali yakondya kumulonjela u mucekulu munya Shunemu, munghalo mwana mono mwene Elisha yamuzukula, nha vyono, Muwaha Mulungu koleta nzala mbaha mu yisi, yono yosola myaka mupunghati ho yipulute. Holeka Elisha yakamusugula yuyo mucekulu, nha vyono yinojeye mwene hamonga ne mhuga yakwe yose, wawuce baho wabite wakekale kunji.
2 Nayo mucekulu yuyo, yakatyatya nha vyono Elisha yamulonjela, yakawuka hamonga ne mhuga yakwe yose, wakabita kwikala ku yisi ye Wafilisiti myaka mupunghati.

3 Yali yeza kupuluta myaka mupunghati, hodu, yakawa kuwuya kanyhi kwawo ku yisi ya Isilayeli. Yakamubitila mutemi wa Isilayeli, yakamulomba mutemi yamugomolele kaya yakwe hamonga no mugunda wakwe.
4 Ninga yali yeza kufika hali mutemi, yakamwagana mutemi vikuduwana na Gehazi u mwitumami wa Elisha, mutemi yamuwuzaga Gehazi, yasakaga yamanye ze mapoto gono yakuganoza Elisha.
5 Nayo Gehazi lono yali yakali yali mu wulolose, wa vyono Elisha yamuzukula munhu mono yali yafwiye, ho baho baho nayo mucekulu yuyo nayo, yakawa kwinjila hali mutemi. Nayo Gehazi yakatya, “Gwe go mutemi! Ayu ahano no mucekulu yena no mwanagwe mono Elisha yamuzukula.”
6 Mutemi yakamuwuza mucekulu yuyo nha vyono zinha. Nayo mucekulu yuyo yakahomelela nha vyono Gehazi yali yakondya kulolosa. Hodu hobaho mutemi yakawa kumucema yumonga wa muli wetumami wakwe, yakawa kumutuma yabite yakamupele mucekulu yuyo vinhu vyakwe vyose, hamonga ne cifo ce nhwanga yakwe yose ya mu migunda yakwe ya mu myaka yose mupunghati, yono yali yali hasina.

Elisha na Benihadadi, u mutemi wa Siliya

7 Elisha yakabita ku Damesiki, lono Benihadadi u mutemi wa Siliya yali yatamigwe. Nayo mutemi Benihadadi yali yeza kuhulika vyono Elisha yali baho,
8 hobaho yakawa kumucema Hazayeli yumonga wa muli wetumami wakwe, yakamutya, “Alu Hazayeli, wusole cihumbilizwa wumutegulile yuyo muwizi Elisha, wukamulombe yamuwuze Muwaha Mulungu, hono ane nopona mu mhungo yiyi hamba ng'o.”
9 Hazayeli yakawa kusola ngamiya makumi mane, yakazipaciza i vinhu vyose viswanu vya mu Damesiki, yakawa kubita kuli Elisha. Nayo Hazayeli yali yeza kwitang'anila na Elisha, yakamutya, “U mwitumami wako Benihadadi u mutemi wa Siliya, kanhuma kuli'gwe, nize nghuwuze, hono mwene kopona mu mhungo yakwe, hamba ng'o.”

10 Elisha yakamupitula Hazayeli, yakamutya, “Muwaha Mulungu kandajila nha vyono si kopona yaye, langa kofwa. Ninga agwe wubite wukamulonjele nha vyono si kofwa yaye langa kopona.”
11 Baho Elisha yakawa kumunugulila Hazayeli ko wogofye wuwaha muno, sunga Hazayeli yakenjilwa ne mahwele muno. Hobaho baho nayo Elisha yakanza kulila.
12 Hazayeli yakamuwuza yakatya, “Alu Elisha, nhawule wukulila?” Elisha yakamupitula yakatya, “Heya nghulila, hambi nha manyile i zimbi zono agwe kowatendela wanhu wa Isilayeli. Hambi nha kogaticimiza no moto magomawakali gawo, kowongomeza ne wazelelo wawo hamonga ne wana wadodo wose, ne wacekulu wono wena mida, kowademulanga i mida yawo.”

13 Nayo Hazayeli yakamuwuza yakatya, “Ane nha si nili cinji yaye, alu vilungo vivyo vyose ho novisola hayi?” Elisha yakamutya, “Muwaha Mulungu yakondya kundajila nha vyono agwe yo gwegwe mono kowa mutemi wa Siliya.”

14 Hazayeli yali yeza kuwuya kuli Benihadadi, Benihadadi yakamuwuza zono Elisha yalonjile. Nayo Hazayeli yakamulonjela yakatya, “Elisha kanonjela vyono agwe kononga du, wulece kwihwelela.”
15 Ninga mwi'zuwa lye kejete, Hazayeli yakasola libulanjeti, yakalinimika mu malenga, yakaligubika mu wusu wo mutemi, hodu mutemi yakawa kufwa.

Mwene Hazayeli yakawa kuwuhala wutemi wa Benihadadi, yakawa mutemi wa Siliya.

Yeholamu, u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 21:1-20)

16 Na mu mwaka we 'hano wo wutemi wa Yolamu u mwana wa Ahabu, u mutemi wa Isilayeli. Yeholamu u mwana wa Yehoshafati yakawa mutemi wa Yuda,
17 yali yaficile myaka makumi madatu na myejete lono yakutema. Yakayitema Yelusalemi myaka munana.
18 Mugolece wakwe, yali mwana we cicekulu wa Ahabu. Yeholamu yakazityatya zinzila zimbi ze watemi wa Isilayeli, nha vyono yali yinha nyumba ya Ahabu. Yaketuma zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu.
19 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu hamba si yenda yayongomeze Yuda, hambi nha yali yehuwiza kuli mwitumami wakwe Dawudi, nha vyono lulelo lwakwe lojendelela kutema vilo vyose.

20 Na mu vilo vyo wutemi wa Yeholamu, wanhu wa Edomu wakayigaluka yisi ya Yuda, waketema wali wene.
21 Yeholamu yakatuluka hamonga ne matuka gakwe gose go wulugu, yakabita ko ku Zayili. Nawo Waedomu wakamupilima hamonga ne matuka gakwe go wulugu, ne wawaha we matuka. Ninga mwene Yeholamu hamonga ne wawaha we matuka, wakatiza ne cilo. I wasilikale wakwe, nawo wakeyagalila munhu na kaya yakwe.
22 Nayo yisi ya Edomu yikayigaluka yisi ya Yuda sunga nalu. Na mwi'pango lilyo du, likaya lya Libina nalyo likayigaluka Yuda.

23 Ne zinghani zose ze metumo gono yetuma mutemi Yeholamu, zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Yuda.
24 Nayo Yeholamu yakafwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi, mu kaya ya Dawudi. U mwanagwe Ahaziya yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Ahaziya, u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 22:1-6)

25 Na mu mwaka we likumi na nyejete wo wutemi wa Yolamu u mwana wa Ahabu, u mutemi wa Isilayeli, nayo Ahaziya u mwana wa Yeholamu ho yatemela yisi ya Yuda.
26 Yakatema yaficile myaka makumi mejete na myejete. Ninga yakatema mwaka monga du mu Yelusalemi. U nyina wa Ahaziya yacemwaga Ataliya, u mwana we cicekulu wo mutemi Ahabu, kanyhi yo mwizukulu wa Omuli u mutemi wa Isilayeli.
27 Alu vyono Ahaziya, yakawa wali ndugu no mutemi Ahabu, ku soko vyono Ahaziya yali mukwemulima kwa Ahabu, holeka nayo yaketuma zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono nyumba ya Ahabu yetuma.

28 Hodu mutemi Ahaziya, vikelunza na Yolamu u mutemi wa Isilayeli, wakabita wakamuhome Hazayeli u mutemi wa Siliya, ku Lamoti ya Gileyadi. Ninga mutemi Yolamu, yakatamiswa muno muno mumo mu wulugu.
29 Holeka yakawuya yakabita kwi 'kaya lya Yezeleli, ho wamutamye zinghomele zakwe. Nayo mutemi Ahaziya yakabita kumulanga.
