2 Watemi 12

Yowashi mutemi wa Yuda

1 Na mu mwaka wo mupunghati wo wuteme wa Yehu, mutemi wa Isilayeli, nayo Yowashi mutemi wa Yuda ho yanziza kutema. Yowashi yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi mane. U nyina wa Yowashi yacemwaga Zibiya munya Belisheba.
2 Mwene Yowashi yetuma zono zikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu, mu mikalile yakwe yose, hambi Yehoyada mukohani, nha yo yamutongaga.
3 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, nayo si yawumwagula wukumbicilo we milungu yawo yo wulambi mu vigongo. Holeka wanhu wajendelela du kulavya makumbiko mumo, no kufusa wuvumba.

4 Mutemi Yowashi, yakawalajiliza wakohani, wavikamise vilavywa vyose vye zipesa, hamonga ne makumbiko, vyono vyalavywaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, kanyhi walanjilile no wityatye wo wulavye we makumbiko ge nhondo zose, hamonga ne zipesa zono wanhu walavyaga ko wuhile wawo weneco.
5 No mukohani wo wose, yawe kuzibocela zipesa, zono wakuleta i wanhu wono mwene mukohani yakuwetumamila. Ne zipesa zizo, zikawa zikwitumilwa mu mulimo wo kunozelezeza Nyumba yo Mulungu.

6 Ninga sunga yikapuluta myaka makumi mejete na midatu yo wutemi wa Yowashi, i wakohani wawo, wali wakali si wananoza co cose mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
7 Holeka yakamucema Yehoyada hamonga ne wakohani wanji, yakawawuza yakawatya, “Alu nhawulenyi si mwali kuyinozeleza Nyumba yo Mulungu? Hodu ku wulawa lulu, si mosuma kanyhi kuzikamisa zipesa zono mukubocela kuli wanhu, muzikombolaje, ho Nyumba yo Mulungu yisume kunozelezwa.”
8 Nawo wakohani wakavumila walece kubocela zipesa muli wanhu, hamba walece kunozeleza wumwaguce we Nyumba yo Mulungu.

9 Hanyuma Yehoyada yakasola lisanduku, yakalitobola lilanga mu cigubiko cayo, yakaliwika ku lubavu lo kulume lwe cikumbicilo, hono wanhu wakolelaga lono wakwinjila mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Nawo wakohani wono walindilizaga ha mulango, wakazitajila zipesa zose mwi'sanduku lilyo zono i wahembi waletaga.
10 Na mu mapango go gose lono zipesa zali zakondya kumema mumo mwi'sanduku, u mukohani muwaha, hamonga no mwandici wo mutemi, wazaga wawa kuzisola no kuziwala.
11 Nawo kolo wakondya kuziwala no kuzandika mu citabu liwalo lyayo, nhondo ho wawa kumupela u munhu mulanjilizi wo mulimo wo kunozeleza Nyumba yo Mulungu, nazo ziwa kwitumamilwa so kuwalipa misahala i waselemala ne wazenji,
12 hamonga ne wasesa mabwe. Na zinji, so wugula mbawo, hamonga ne mabwe gono getumamilwaga mumo mu wunozelezi we Nyumba yo Mulungu, kanyhi na ku so visakwa vinji vinji vyose.
13 Ninga hasina pesa zinji za muli zizo, zono zetumamilwa ku wugula vikombe vya fweza, hamba bakuli, hamba ndulele, hamba vitumamilwa vyo kuwicila zitala, hamba kunoza cinhu co cose ca fweza hamba zahabu.
14 Ninga pesa zose ziketumamilwa du so kuwalipa misahala i wetuma milimo, hamonga no kugula vinhu vyose vyono vyasakwaga mu mulimo wo kunozeleza Nyumba yo Mulungu.
15 Nawo i wanhu wono wali wapejigwe mulimo wo kuwulanjilila mulimo wuwo, wali wanhu wehuwilwa muno muno. Holeka hakawa hasina soko yo kuwasaka walavye liwalo lye zipesa zono wetumamiye na zono zikali zisigaye.
16 Langa zipesa zono zalavywaga so vilavywa vyo wusutye, hamonga ne vilavywa vyo wubi, pesa zizo si zatajilwaga mwi'sanduku yaye. Azo wapegwaga wakohani du ziwa zawo.

17 Mu mapango ganji, Hazayeli u mutemi wa Siliya, yayihoma Gati, yakayisola, na hanyuma kanyhi yakasaka yabite yakayihome Yelusalemi.
18 Ninga Yowashi mutemi wa Yuda, yakakonghola vilavywa vyose vyono vyasiwanulwa ne wasogwe nha cinji Yehoshafati, na Yeholamu, na Ahaziya, so Muwaha Mulungu. Mwene yakavihanza hamonga ne vilavywa vyakwe, hamonga ne zahabu yono yali yili mu Nyumba yo Mulungu ne zahabu ya mwi'kulu, yakawa kumupela vyose cipegwa u mutemi Hazayeli. Nayo mutemi Hazayeli, yakawa kuliwusa lipuga lye wasilikale wakwe mu Yelusalemi.

19 Na zose zono mutemi Yowashi yetuma, zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Yuda.

20 Ninga mutemi Yowashi, i wetumami wakwe wakamugaluka. Na muli wawo, i wejete wayawo, wono wakucemwa Yozakali u mwana wa Shimeyati, hamonga na Yehozabadi u mwana wa Shomeli, wawo wakamuwulajila Yowashi ku Milo, ha kaya yono yali yizenjigwe hanhu hono hali henuce, ku lubavu lwa kucilima ya Yelusalemi, mu nzila yono yibitile sunga Sila.
21 Yowashi yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye citemi, mu kaya ya Dawudi. Nayo Amaziya u mwanagwe, yakahala wutemi wakwe.
