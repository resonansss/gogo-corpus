2 Watemi 4

Elisha yakumutaza mucekulu muciwa u mufwile

1 U mucekulu mufwile mono yali mucekulu wa yumonga wa mu cipuga ce wawizi, yakamubitila Elisha, yakamulonjela yakatya, “Gwe muwaha, mulume wangu yakondya kufwa! Nha vyono wumanyile, mwene mulume wangu, yali yo munhu mogopa Mulungu. Ninga lu, yali yena mugawa wa mapesa kuli munhu yunji. Alu munhu yuyo kaza ho yawasole wanangu wejete, wawe lye lipo lyo mugawa wuwo wo mulume wangu, yabite yakawanoze wawe wawanda wakwe.”

2 Nayo Elisha yakamuwuza yuyo mucekulu yakatya, “Alu kosaka nghutendele wule? Kanyhi nghukulomba wunonjele cinhu cono wina naco ku kaya yako.”

Nayo mucekulu yuyo yakamupitula yakatya, “Ane sina cinji co cose ku kaya, langa hogali mafuta madodo du gali mu supa.”

3 Elisha yakamutya, “Alu, wubite mu zikaya zose ze wekalani wayago, wukakonghole zisupa zose zono zisina cinji, ziwe nyinji du mu nghango nha vyono kosuma.
4 Hanyuma agwe hamonga ne wanago, mwinjile mu nyumba mwidindile mumo, hanyuma wusole supa yako yono yina mafuta, wuwe kujidila mafuta mu zisupa zono zili bwete. Na lono supa monga yamema, ale kuwa kuyiteza mbela mbela ha mhelo.”

5 Ale, u mucekulu yuyo, yakawa kwinjila mu kaya yakwe hamonga ne wanagwe, wakedindila, yakasola supa yakwe yono yina mafuta, yakawa kwanza kugajidila mafuta mu zisupa zono zili bwete, akuno nawo wanagwe wakujendelela kumusejelezeza supa zono zili bwete.
6 Nawo wali weza kukondya kuzimemya supa zose zono zali zili baho, nyina yawo yakawawuza wanagwe hono howa ho zili kanyhi supa zinji zono zili bwete, zono zikali zisigaye. Ninga yumonga wa muli wanagwe, yakamulonjela nha vyono yiyo yono wukuyijidila, yo supa ye ndumilizo. Hodu ne mafuta nago gakawa kuduma ku wulawa mu supa mono gajidwaga.
7 Hodu mucekulu yuyo, yakamubitila kanyhi muwizi Elisha. Nayo Elisha yakamutya, “Alu, wubite wukagaguze mafuta, wukalipe migawa yako yose. Ninga mapesa ganji gosigala, gono agwe hamonga ne wanago mosuma kwitumamila.”

Elisha no mucekulu mugoli, munya Shunemu

8 Hanyuma lizuwa linji, Elisha yakabita ku Shunemu, hono yekalaga mucekulu yunji mugoli. No mucekulu yuyo yakamucemela Elisha ku zindigwa. Na ku wulawa mwi'zuwa lilyo, lono Elisha yabitaga kuko ku Shunemu, yalizaga ndigwa ko mucekulu yuyo.
9 Hanyuma mucekulu yuyo yakamulonjela mulume wakwe yakatya, “Ane nitumbiye vyono u munhu yuyu mono kazaga baha hono hetu kenji na kenji, yo munhu mono yelile.
10 Holeka nghuwona viswanu cizenje cinyumba cidodo mwi'tembe, ciwice mumo ne citanda, ne meza, ne ligoda, hamonga ne tala, mwene ho yawe kuficila mumo lono yakuza kucilamusa.”

11 Ale, mwi'zuwa linji Elisha yakabita kuko ku Shunemu, yakaficila mu cinyumba cakwe cico yacehe,
12 yakamutuma Gehazi u mwitumami wakwe, yabite yakamuceme yuyo mucekulu yamuzize. Nayo yali yeza kuza,
13 Elisha yakamulonjela Gehazi yakatya, “Ale, wumuwuzaje mucekulu yuyu cono kosaka nimutendele, so magaziko gono gamwagane so wutazi wono yali kucitaza mu vihilwa vyetu. Na lunji kosaka ane mbite kuli mutemi hamba kuli musilikale muwaha, u mulongola we wasilikale nghamusacile ziswanu.”

Ninga mucekulu yuyo yakatya, “Ane nina baha vyose vyono nghuvisaka hamonga ne wanhu wangu.”

14 Elisha yakamutya Gehazi, “Ale wumuwuzaje cono kosaka nimutendele.”

Nayo Gehazi yakamutya Elisha, “U mucekulu yuyu yasina mwana, no mulume wakwe nayo yeza mukombi.”

15 Elisha yakamutya Gehazi, “Ale mulonjelaje yaze hano.” U mucekulu yuyo yakaza yakema ha cipimbilamulango.
16 Nayo Elisha yakamutya, “Ale, ku mwaka mwi'pango lili, kolowa wupagate mwana mu miwoko yako.”

Nayo mucekulu yuyo yakatya, “Woyi he! Nghukunena muwaha wulece kunghonga, hambi agwe, nha wuli munhu wo Mulungu!”

17 Ninga lyali lyeza kufika lipango lililya du mu mwaka wono watyatya, yakasawa mwana wa cilume, nha vyono Elisha yamulonjela.

18 Nayo mwana yali yeza yawiluka, mwi'zuwa linji hakali mitondo, mu vilo vye nhwanga, yakabita ku mugunda kumulonda sogwe lono yadudulaga yena ne wetumami wakwe.
19 Ninga yakanza nhinicizo, kumulilila sogwe, yakutya, “Mutwe wangu wukutama! Nye, mutwe wangu wukutama!” Nayo sogwe yakamutya mwitumami, “Ale musolaje mwana wumugomole ko nyina.”
20 Hodu u mwitumami yakamugomola mwana kuli nyina, nayo nyina yakawa yamupagate du, sunga mwi'pango lya mu misi, lono mwana yafwila.
21 Hodu yakawa kumusola, yakamutegula ku cinyumba ca Elisha, yakamuwika mu citanda ca Elisha, yakadinda mulango yakamuleka mumo.
22 Hanyuma yakamucema mulume wakwe, yakamutya, “Nghukulomba wundetele yunji wa muli wetumami, hamonga na ndogowe monga, mbite musunho kuli Elisha muwizi. Ninga nowuya kanyhi lulu baha nha vyono nosuma.”

23 Mulume wakwe yakamuwuza yakatya, “Alu socici yiwe yikunojeye wubite izuwali du, nalyo ho si lizuwa lya Sabato, hamba si sikukuu ye Ndawo yo Mwezi?”

Nayo mugolece wakwe yakatya, “Aa! Si nghani yaye.”
24 Hobaho yakawa kupegwa ndogowe yono yiwikanizwe, yakamulajiliza mwitumami yakatya, “Alu nghukulomba, wuyimashaje muno muno ndogowe mu nghango ya vyono yosuma kumangha, wulece kuyileka yijende mhola mhola, sunga nene nghulonjele.”
25 Hodu yakawa kutuluka yakabita sunga ko kwi'tunda lya Kalimeli kono yali Elisha.

Nayo Elisha yakamuwonela kutali lono yazaga, hodu yakamulonjela Gehazi mwitumami wakwe yakatya, “Lanje ta! Ayulya mucekulu wa ku Shunemu yakuza!
26 Holeka wiyohaje mwitang'anile nayo, wumutambuze hono zose swanu hamonga no mwene muswanu no mulume wakwe, hamonga no mwanagwe.”

Nayo mucekulu yuyo, yakamulonjela Gehazi vyono zose swanu du.
27 Ninga yali yeza kufika hali Elisha, yakenama ha mwande yakwe yakayibata migulu ya Elisha. Nayo Gehazi yali yeza kuziwona zizo, yakasaka kumukundujiza yuyo mucekulu yawuce, ninga Elisha yakamutya, “Mulekaje! Alo ho si wukuwona vyono yena wusungu wuwaha mu nhumbula yakwe? Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, hamba si yamanyize zizi.”

28 U mucekulu yakamulonjela Elisha, yakatya, “Gwe muwaha, wukumbuciye vyono nakulomba mwana? Kanyhi wule, si nalonga vyono wulece kunghonga?”

29 Elisha yakamupitucila Gehazi, yakamutya, “Ale, wusunhe! Wusole nghome yangu wubite mbela. Kanyhi wulece kucela mumo mu nzila so wilamusa na wanhu, na hono yunji yakulamuse, ale wulece kusola yidaha mu witambuze. Wubite wugolocile sunga wufice ku kaya, wukayijelece nghome yiyi mucanyha yo mwana.”

30 Nayo mucekulu yuyo yakamulonjela Elisha yakatya, “Nane nghwilahiza kuli Muwaha Mulungu mumi mono nimogopile, sunga na kuli'gwe, nha vyono si nokulecela lukulu!” Hodu Elisha no mucekulu yuyo, vikawa kutulukana hamonga.
31 Gehazi yakawa yalongola ku mwande yadinghuye ne nghome ya Elisha, yakayijeleka mucanyha yo mwana. Ninga hakawa hasina yizi, hamba citanjilo co cose co wumi muli mwana yuyo. Hodu Gehazi yakawuya ne nyuma yakamuhinjiliza Elisha, yakamulonjela yakatya, “U mwana hamba si yenuce yaye.”

32 Nayo Elisha yali yeza kufika, yakenjila yali wene du mu cinyumba, yakamuwona mwana yagonile mu citanda yafwiye.
33 Yakedindila mugati yali wene, yakawa kumulomba Muwaha Mulungu.
34 Hanyuma yakawa kugona mu citanda mucanyha yo mwana yuyo. Yakawika mulomo wakwe mu mulomo wo mwana, ne meso gakwe mu meso go mwana, ne miwoko yakwe yakayigolola mu miwoko yo mwana. Nayo Elisha lono yali yegoloye mucanyha yo mwana, hodu u muwili wo mwana yuyo wukawa kwanza kutuga civuce.
35 Nayo Elisha yakatima hasi mu citanda, yakajendajenda hadodo mumo mu cinyumba, hanyuma yakawuya kanyhi yakegolola mucanyha yo mwana. Hanyuma nhondo u mwana yuyo yakatyamula kali mupunghati, yakanugula ne meso gakwe.
36 Elisha yakamucema Gehazi, yakamulonjela yamuceme nyina yo mwana yuyo yaze. Nayo yali yeza kwinjila, Elisha yakamutya, “Ale, ayu hano mwanago.”
37 U mucekulu yuyo, yakagwa ha magulu ga Elisha, akuno wusu wakwe wupamize hasi. Hanyuma yakawa kumusola mwanagwe yakawa kubita.

I mapoto ganji mejete

38 Na mu mapango ganji, hakawa ho yili nzala mbaha mu yisi yose. Nayo Elisha lulo yali yakondya kuwuya ku Giligali. Nayo lono yawejizaga cipuga ce wawizi, yakamulonjela mwitumami, wakwe yajelece nyungu mbaha mu moto, yawatelecele wawizi.
39 Na yumonga wa muli wene, yakabita mu mawimbi ho yakahepe mboga. Na kuko, yakagawona mayungu ga mu mbago, yakagahepa menji muno mu nghango nha vyono yasuma kugadinghula. Yakagaleta ku wayagwe, yakapandula pandula, yakagateleka mu nyungu, akuno si wagamanyile vyono yali ndigwaci yene.
40 Ala du, yakawatenjela wanhu walye. Ninga lulo baho lono walizaga, wakazoza wakumulonjela Elisha wakutya, “Ndigwa yina sumu.” Hodu, hamba si wajendelela kanyhi kuyilya.
41 Elisha yakalomba wusaje, yakawutajila mu nyungu, yakatya, “Ale, watenjelaje ndigwa wanhu walye.” Na mu ndigwa yiyo mukawa musina kanyhi cinji cibi.

42 Na mwi'zuwa linji kanyhi, u munhu yunji yakaza yakulawa Baali Shalisha, yakamuletela Elisha mikate makumi mejete, yono minozwe ne shayili nongozi kududulwa, ya mu mwaka wuwo, hamonga na matama madece, gono si makubule mabeza gayo. Elisha yakamulonjela mwitumami wakwe yawapele wawizi walye.
43 Ninga mwitumami yakatya, “Wukwihwesa nha vyono ndigwa yiyi yowakwila wanhu yigana limonga?”

Elisha yakamupitula yakatya, “Agwe watenjelaje du walye, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yakutya vyono wolya sunga wegute no kusigaza.”
44 U mwitumami nhondo ho yakawa kuwatenjela wanhu ndigwa. Nawo wakalya, wakeguta, sunga wakasigaza, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalonga.
