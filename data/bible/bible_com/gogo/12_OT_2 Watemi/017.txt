2 Watemi 17

Hosheya, mutemi wa Isilayeli

1 Na mu mwaka we likumi na myejete wo wutemi wa Ahazi, mutemi wa Yuda, nayo Hosheya u mwana wa Ela, ho yanziza kutema mu Isilayeli, nayo yakatema mu Samaliya myaka yicenda du.
2 Ninga nayo yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, ninga mwene si yetuma wubi, nha vyono wetuma i watemi wose wa Isilayeli wono wamulongolela kutema.
3 U mutemi Shalamanesa wa Ashuli, yakehoma na Hosheya. Hosheya yakasumwa na Shalamanesa, yakawa yakulavya kodi kuli Shalamanesa mu myaka yose.
4 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, mu mwaka wunji, nhondo, Hosheya yakatuma wanhu kuli mutemi wa Misili yakulomba wutazi, nhondo yakawa kuleka no kulipa kodi kuli Shalamanesa mutemi wa Ashuli. Ninga nayo mutemi Shalamanesa yali yeza kuzimanya zizo, hobaho yakawa kumwibata Hosheya, yakawa kumufunga mu jela.

U wugwe wa Samaliya

5 Hanyuma Shalamanesa yakayihoma yisi yose ya Isilayeli, yakayipilima Samaliya, yakayihoma myaka midatu. Na mu mwaka we kadatu wo wupilimwe wa Samaliya,
6 mu mwaka we licenda wo wutemi wa Hosheya, nayo mutemi wa Ashuli yakaza, yakayihoma Samaliya yakayisola. Yakawahamya ne Waisilayeli, yakawategula ku Ashuli wawopwe. Yakawawika wanji wayawo wekale mwi'kaya lya Hala, na wanji yakawawika behi ye likolongo lya Haboli mu yisi ya Gozani. Na wanji kanyhi yakawawika mu makaya ga Mediya.

7 Samaliya yikagwa, vyono Waisilayeli wetuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo, mono yawaponya kuli mutemi wa Misili Falawo, yakawa kuwawusa mu yisi ya Misili. Ninga wene 'lu, wakahemba milungu yinji yo wulambi,
8 wakatyatya metumo mabi ge wanhu wono Muwaha Mulungu yawawinga, wene i wanhu wakwe ho wekale mumo. Kanyhi wakatyatya ne myoto mibi yono wayileta watemi wa Isilayeli.
9 Waisilayeli waketuma zinghani zono Muwaha Mulungu si yali yawakomboye wetume. Hambi wene nha wakazenga wukumbicilo we milungu yawo mu vigonge, hanhu hose, mu makaya gawo gose, mu makaya mawaha ne madodo.
10 Wakazenga masumbili ge mabwe, hamonga ne vihwani vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela mu matunda gose, sunga na hasi ye mibeho ye mibici miwaha.
11 Wakajendelela kufusa wuvumba mu makumbicilo gose mu vigongo, wakawa kutyatya myoto mibi, ye wanhu wono Muwaha Mulungu yawawinga mu yisi. Holeka wakamukoza Muwaha Mulungu likuwo, ku soko ye metumo gawo mabi.
12 Kanyhi wakalibeza ne lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu lyo wutya, mulece kuvihemba vihwani vye milungu yo wulambi.

13 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yali yakondya kuwasugula Isilayeli na Yuda, mu nzila ye wanhu wono yawatumaga, hamonga ne wawizi, yakuwalonjela yakutya, “Mupituce muzilece zinzila zenyu zimbi, mwibate wuhile wangu, ne mejizo gangu gono gali mu wulajilize, wono nawalajiliza walezi wenyu, nghamulonjelelenyi nanye mu nzila ye wetumami wangu wawizi.”
14 Ninga wene hamba si wahulika yaye, wakawa wasina cihuliko nhe walezi wawo, wono wali si wakumwihuwila Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo.
15 Wakalemela lukulu kugebata mejizo gakwe, hamba kucikamisa cilagane cono Muwaha Mulungu yacinoza ne walezi wawo, hamba kucelelela bwete, ze suguzi yono Muwaha Mulungu yawasugula. Wene wakahemba vihwani vye milungu yo wulambi, vyono visina cambuzi, wakahodanjika, wakatyatya myoto ye makungugo wono wali wawapilime, wakalibedegala lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulugu lyono yatya, “Mulecele lukulu kuwatyatya.”
16 Wakagabena malajilizo gose go Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo, wakenozeza vihwani vya ndama nyejete nyenjedulwe, wakazihemba. Wakenozeza ne cihwani co mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, wakahemba zinyelezi, wakamwitumamila mulungu Baali.
17 Wakawalavya makumbiko i wana wawo we cilume na we cicekulu cilavywa cinyanywe, kuli milungu yo wulambi, kanyhi wakatowa visangu, waketuma zo wuhawi, wakelavya ku wituma zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu. Na ku soko yiyo, wakamukoza likuwo Mulungu.
18 Muwaha Mulungu yakawakolelwa likuwo muno muno Waisilayeli, yakawawusa hali mwene, yakawasigaza wo mulango wa Yuda du wali wene.

19 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, nawo wawo wo mulango wa Yuda, si wagebata yaye malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo, wakatyatya du myoto mibi yono Waisilayeli wali wakondya kuyisolela.
20 Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kuwaleka Waisilayeli, yakawalipiciza, yakawaleka wasolwe ne walugu wono wowatajila mu magayo, na hanyuma yakawa kuwawusiza lukulu ha meso gakwe.

21 Muwaha Mulungu yali yeza kukondya kuwatemanula Waisilayeli na Yuda, hodu Waisilayeli wakawa kumunoza Yelobowamu u mwana wa Nebati, yawe mutemi wawo. Nayo Yelobowamu yakawanoza wamulece Muwaha Mulungu, yakawenjiza mu wubi muno muno.
22 Nawo Waisilayeli wakatyatya zinghani za Yelobowamu, wakajendelela nazo wubi zose zono yetumaga.
23 Na kundumo ya zose, Muwaha Mulungu nhondo, yakawa kuwawusiza lukulu ha meso gakwe, nha vyono yali yakondya kuwasugula, mu nzila ye wawizi, iwetumami wakwe, na vyono si koleka kwituma zizo. Holeka i wanhu wa Isilayeli wakasolwa wakawahamizya ku Ashuli, kono wakali wali kuko sunga nalu.

Waashuli wakwikala mu yisi ya Isilayeli

24 U mutemi wa Ashuli, yakawasola wanhu ku wulawa mu makaya ga Babiloni na Kuti, na Iva, na Hamati, na Sefalivayimu, yakawenjiza mu makaya ga Samaliya wekale mumo, wagahalile makaya ge Waisilayeli wono yawahamizya ku Ashuli. Nawo wanhu wawo, wakagasola makaya gago ga Samaliya, gakawa gawo weneco, wakekala mumo.
25 Ninga i wanhu wawo nawo wali weza kwinjila no kwikala mumo mu Samaliya, hamba si wasuma kumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu, holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakawatumila simba, zikawawulaga wayawo wanji.
26 Nayo mutemi wa Ashuli, yakalonjelwa zinghani zizo, nha vyono i wanhu wono wawategula ho wakekale mu makaya ga Samaliya, si wamanyile zo wulajilize wo Mulungu we yisi yiyo, holeka yo soko yo yilecile Mulungu yuyo, kawatumila simba zono zali kuwawulaga.
27 Mutemi nayo yakalajiliza yakatya, “Alu hono yili nhavivyo, ale mumutegulaje yumonga wa muli wakohani, wono cawaleta bahano ku wulawa kuko, yabite yakekale kuko, ho yawejize wanhu wawo lilajilizo lyo Mulungu we yisi yiyo.”
28 Holeka mukohani monga we Ciisilayeli mono yahamywa ku wulawa kuko ku Samaliya, yakawa kubita, yakekala ku Beteli, yakawa kuwejiza wanhu nha vyono yinojeye wamuhembe Muwaha Mulungu.

29 Ninga i wanhu wawo wono wategulwa wekale mu Samaliya, vyo wakajendelela du kwiseseza vihwnai vye milungu yawo, no kuviwika mu vigongo, mu wukumbicilo wono Waisilayeli wawuzenga. Mhuga zose, mu makaya gose, wanhu no kwawo, wose wakeseseza vihwani vye milungu yawo yo wulambi mumo mu makaya gono wekalaga.
30 I wanhu wono walawa ku Babiloni, wakenozeza vihwani vyo mulungu Sukoti-benoti, nawo wono walawa ku Kuti, vihwani vya Nelegali. Nawo wono walawa ku Hamati, vihwani vya Ashima,
31 ne wanhu wono walawa ku Iva, vihwani vya Nibuhazi na Talitaki. Ne wanhu wono walawa ku Safalivayimu wakawa kuwalavya i wana wawo makumbiko manyanywe, kuli milungu yawo yo wulambi, Adelamaleke na Anameleke.
32 I wanhu wawa ninga kunji, wakawa wamogopile no Muwaha Mulungu. Na ku soko yiyo, wakawahagula wanji wa muli wene, wetume mulimo wo wukohani, mu vigongo, mu wukumbicilo we milungu yawo, yo wulambi, hamonga no mulimo wo kulavya makumbiko gawo.
33 Holeka wakawa wakumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu hamonga no kuyihemba milungu yawo yo wulambi, wakutyatya myoto ye zisi zawo kono walawilila.

34 Nawo wakali vyo wajendeleye du ne myoto yawo yiyo ye katali sunga nalu. Si wakumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu, hamba kwibata wulajilize, hamonga ne malajilizo gono Muwaha Mulungu yawapela i wana wa Yakobo, mono yakacemelwa litagwa lya Isilayeli.
35 Wawo Waisilayeli wono Muwaha Mulungu yanoza cilagane nawo, wono Muwaha Mulungu yakawalajiliza yakutya, “Mulecele lukulu kuyihemba milungu yinji, mulece kuyifugamila hamba kuyitumamila, hamba kuyilavizya makumbiko.
36 Langa muhulikaje nene du no Muwaha Mulungu, mono namuwusenyi mu yisi ya Misili kwe vilungo viwaha, hamonga no wusume. Holeka mufugamile, no kundavizya makumbiko nene du.
37 Kanyhi munojeye muwibate wulajile wangu, no kugakamisa malajilizo gangu gono nghamwandicilenyi. Holeka mulece kuyityatya milungu yinji.
38 Kanyhi mulece kucisemwa i cilagane cangu cono nacinoza nanye.
39 Mweze muli cihuliko kuli'ne no Muwaha Mulungu u Mulungu wenyu, nane ho nomuponyenyi mu walugu wenyu.”
40 Ninga wanhu wawo, hamba si wahulika yaye, wene vyo wakajendelela du kuyityatya myoto yawo mibi.

41 Holeka wanhu wawo hamba wawe wamogopaga Muwaha Mulungu, ninga vyo wali wakali wajendeleye du no kuhemba vihwani vye milungu yawo. Co cikuleka sunga ne wana wawo, wakali wakwituma nhavivyo sunga ne nalu.
