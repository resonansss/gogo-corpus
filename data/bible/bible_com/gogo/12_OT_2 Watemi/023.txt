2 Watemi 23

Mutemi Yosiya, yakuwuwulaga wuhembi we milungu

(2Zin 34:3-7, 29-33)

1 Mutemi Yosiya yakawakongholela hamonga i walongola wose wa Yuda na Yelusalemi,
2 wakenjila mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, i wakohani ne wawizi, ne wanhu wose wa Yelusalemi, i wawaha ne wadodo, wagoli ne waciwa, hamonga ne wanhu wose we mawala gose. Mutemi yakawasomela zose zono zandicigwe mu citabu ce cilagane, cono cejela mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
3 Mutemi yakema behi ne lisumbili, yakanoza cilagane no Muwaha Mulungu, cilagane vyono kowa na cihuliko kuli Mulungu, kanyhi na vyono kogebata malajilizo gakwe, hamonga no wulajilize wakwe kwe nhumbula yakwe yose, na ko mutima wakwe wose. Kanyhi na vyono kozikamya zinghani ze cilagane zono zandicigwe mu citabu. Nawo i wanhu wose wakehuwiza nha vyono wocibata no kucikamisa cilagane cico.

4 Hanyuma ya zizo, mutemi Yosiya, yakawa kumulajiliza mukohani muwaha Hilikiya, hamonga ne wakohani watazi wakwe, hamonga ne walindilizi wo mulango wo kwinjilila mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, wenjile mu Nyumba yo Mulungu wakawuse viya vyose vyono vyanozezwa Baali, no mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, ne zinyelezi. Mutemi Yosiya yakagasola maviya gago yakagaticimiziza mu moto kunze ye likaya, behi ne lilolo lya Kidiloni, yakagasola mavu gayo, yakagategula ku Beteli.
5 Yakawawusa mu mulimo wo wukohani, i wakohani wose wono u mutemi wa Yuda yali yawawicile, ho wawe kulavya makumbiko mu vikumbicilo mu vigongo, mu makaya ga Yuda, sunga na mu wuhembelo wono wali wuli behi behi na Yelusalemi. Wakohani wose wono walavyaga makumbiko kuli Baali, na kwi' zuwa, no mwezi, ne zisayali, hamonga ne zinyelezi.
6 Yakaciwusa cihwani co mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakacisola yakabita kucikongojela kunze ye likaya mwi'lolo lya Kidiloni. Yakacipondanga ciwe manghundi, yakageyagaza manghundi gago mu ciwanza ce vitenjelo vye wanhu wose.
7 Yakazigwisa zinyumba zono wetumamilaga i walume wono wagonaga ne walume wayawo, zono zali zili mugati ye Nyumba yo Mulungu. Mono wekalaga i wacekulu wono wahonaga matote ge mabupa ge zi Ashela.
8 Yakawasola i wakohani wose wono wali wali mu makaya ga Yuda, yakawategula ku Yelusalemi. Yakagavufya makumbicilo gose mu vigongo, gono walavizyaga makumbiko mu yisi yose hanhu hose. Yakagamwagulanga makumbicilo gose mu vigongo, gono wetumamilaga kuli milungu ye myuhe myavu, yono yali yili behi no mulango, wono Yoshuwa u mulongola wa mwi'kaya yawuzenga. Mulango wono wali wuli ku lubavu lo kucekulu lo mulango muwaha wo kwinjilila mwi'kaya.
9 Ne wakohani wawo wali si wakomboywe kwituma mulimo wunji wa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, ninga walizaga mukate wono wusina cisase, wono wapegwaga i wakohani wayawo.

10 Mutemi Yosiya, yakahavufya nhavivilya, i hanhu hono wahembelaga i milungu yo wulambi hono hacemwaga Tofeti, mwi'lolo lya Hinomu, ho halece kuwa no munhu wo wose mono kosuma kanyhi, kumulavya mwanagwe we cilume, hamba we cicekulu yawe yikumbiko lye cilavywa cinyanywe kuli mulungu Moleki.
11 Yakaziwusa ne zifwalasi zono mutemi wa Yuda, yali yazisiwanuye so wuhembi wo kulihemba lizuwa, yakagakongoga sunga ne matuka gono getumamilwaga mu wuhembi wuwo. (Na vivyo vyose, vyekalaga mu ciwanza ce Nyumba yo Mulungu, behi no mulango, kanyhi hamba si kwali kutali ne kaya yono yekalaga Natani Meleki u mwitumami muwaha.)
12 Mutemi Yosiya, yakavimwagulanga sunga ne vikumbicilo vyono, i watemi wa Yuda, wali wavizenjile mwi'tembe lya mwi'kulu, mu kaya yono yekalaga mutemi Ahazi. Mutemi Yosiya, yakavimwagulanga vikumbicilo vyono mutemi Manase, yavizenga mu viwanza vyejete vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Yakavitulanga vikumbicilo, yakagagumila malowanjila gavyo mwi'lolo lya Kidiloni.
13 Mutemi Yosiya, yakavyavufya vikumbicilo vyose, vyono mutemi Solomona yali yavizenjile ku lubavu lwa kucilima ya Yelusalemi, kutakama ye litunda lyo wuhodanjice, so wuhemba vihwani vye cevu, vyono vikucemwa Ashitolete, u mulungu we Wazidoni, na Cemoshi u mulungu we Waamoni.
14 Mutemi Yosiya, yakagabenanjizanga masumbili ge mabwe, yakavidumulanga ne vihwani vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, yakasola mafupa ge wanhu, yakagamemya i hanhu hono yali misimye milungu yiyo.

15 Mutemi Yosiya, yakawumwagula no wuhembelo wono mutemi Yelobowamu mwana wa Nebati, yali yawuzenjile ku Beteli, mono yawanoza Waisilayeli wetume wubi hali Mulungu. Yosiya, yakacimwagula cikumbicilo, yakagatulanjiza mabwe gayo, yakagatindya, gakawa manghundi. Yakacikongoga cihwani co mulungu we cicekulu Ashela.
16 Yosiya yali yeza kulanga ako na ako, yakawona vitenjelo mucanyha ye cigongo. Yakabita yakagafukula mafupa ge wafu, yakagakongojela mucanyha ye cikumbicilo cico. Na mu nzila yiyi, yakacavufya cikumbicilo. Yakazifisiliza zono muwizi yazilonga katali kuko ku Beteli, mwi'zuwa lono mutemi Yelobowamu, yali yemile ha cikumbicilo. Yosiya yali yeza kulanga langa kanyhi, yakaciwona citenjelo co muwizi yuyu mono yazilonga zizi.
17 Hodu yakawa kuwuza yakutya, “Alu citenjelo cicilya ca nani lo?” Nawo wanhu wanya Beteli, wakamupitula wakatya, “Aco co citenjelo co munhu wo Mulungu, mono yalawa ku Yuda, no kuwila zinghani zizi, zono witumile mu cikumbicilo cici.”

18 Mutemi Yosiya yakawatya, “Ale hodu mucilekaje, mulece kugahimbula mafupa gakwe.”

Hodu, mafupa gakwe wakagaleka, hamba si wagahimbula yaye, hamonga ne mafupa go muwizi mono yalawa ku Samaliya nago nhavivyo.

19 Mutemi Yosiya, yakavimwagulanga vikumbicilo vyose mu vigongo, vyono vyali vitagaliye mu makaya gose ga Isilayeli, vyono wavizenga i watemi wa Isilayeli, wakacila muno kumukoza likuwo Muwaha Mulungu. Mutemi Yosiya yakavinozeza vikumbicilo vyose, nha vyono yacinozeza cikumbicilo ca ku Beteli.
20 Yakawawulajila mu vikumbicilo i wakohani wose, wono wetumaga mulimo mu vikumbicilo. Yakagakongoga mu moto, mafupa gose ge wanhu mu vikumbicilo vyose. Hanyuma yakawa kuwuya ku Yelusalemi.

Yosiya, yakuyinoza Pasaka

(2Zin 35:1-19)

21 Mutemi Yosiya, yakawalajiliza wanhu wose, wamunozeze Pasaka Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo, nha vyono yandikwa mu citabu ce cilagane.
22 Na hakawa hasina Pasaka yinji yo yose, yono yanozwa no mutemi wo wose mu watemi walongozi, i watemi wa Isilayeli, hamba Yuda, nha vyono yikanozwa Pasaka yiyi, ku wulawilila mu vilo vye walamuzi, lono wali walitemile likungugo.
23 Langa mu mwaka we kumi na munana, wa mu wutemi wo mutemi Yosiya, ho Pasaka yiyi yanozezwa mu Yelusalemi.

Zinji kanyhi zono mutemi Yosiya yazikawaniza

24 Mutemi Yosiya, yakawawusa mu Yelusalemi na mu Yuda, we macisi, ne wahawi, ne milungu yose, ne vihwani vyose, vye milungu yo wulambi hamonga ne viya vyose vyono vyetumamilwaga mu wuhembi we milungu yo wulambi. Yakafisiliza zinghani zo wulajilize, zono zandicigwe mu citabu cono Hilikiya mukohani muwaha, yaciwona mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
25 Hakawa hasina mutemi yunji kanyhi mono yakawa yehwanile mutemi Yosiya, ku wulawa muli watemi wose wono wamulongolela kutema, hamba wono watema lono mwene yali yakondya kufwa, mono yamwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu kwe nhumbula yakwe yose, na ko mutima wakwe wose, na kwe vilungo vyakwe vyose, mono yali yebate Lilajilizo lya Musa. Hasina hamba monga mono yakawa yehwanile mutemi Yosiya.

26 Ninga likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu likaka muno ku so Yuda, ku soko ye zinghani zono mutemi Manase yetuma. Holeka sunga nalu, likuwo likali lyacile, hamba si licepuce yaye.
27 Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Nowawusa Wayuda ha meso gangu, nha vyono nawawusa Waisilayeli. Kanyhi nolileka likaya lya Yelusalemi, likaya lyono nalihagula, hamonga ne Nyumba yo Mulungu, i hanhu hono natya, vyono ho hanhu hono nowa kuhembelwa.”

Ndumilizo yo wutemi wa Yosiya

(2Zin 35:20–36:1)

28 Ne zinghani zono Yosiya yetuma, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Yuda.
29 Mu vilo vyono Yosiya yali yakali yatemile, Neko u mutemi wa Misili, yaleta wasilikale sunga ko kwi'kolongo lya Efulata, ho yamutaze mutemi wa Ashuli. Nayo mutemi Yosiya yali yeza kuwawona, yakajela kuwemila wasilikale wawo wa Misili ha Megido, ninga yakawulagwa mumo mu wulugu.
30 I wetumami wakwe wakawupaciza muwili wakwe mwi'tuka lyo wulugu, wakawugomola ku Yelusalemi. Yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi.

Nawo wanhu wa Yuda, wakamuhagula Yowahazi u mwana wa Yosiya, wakamwitila mafuta, wakamutemya yawe mutemi.

Yowahazi, mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 36:2-4)

31 Yowahazi yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na midatu lono yatemela mu Yuda. Yakatema myezi midatu mu Yelusalemi. U nyina yacemwaga Hamutali, u mwana we cicekulu wa Yelemiya munya kwi'kaya lya Libuna.
32 Ninga nayo Yowahazi, yakatyatya musambo mubi we walezi wakwe, yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu.
33 No wutemi wakwe wamalicila mwi'zuwa lono yebatilwa no mutemi Neko wa Misili, yakamufunga ku Libula, mu yisi ya Hamati, ho yalece kutema kanyhi ku Yelusalemi. Yakayilifya yisi ya Yuda kodi ya kilo elufu yidatu na magana mane za fweza, hamonga na kilo makumi madatu na yine za zahabu.
34 Mutemi Falawo Neko, yakamutemya Eliyakimu u mwana wa Yosiya, yawe mutemi wa Yuda, yahale hanhu ha Yosiya. Nayo Eliyakimu yakepitula litagwa lyakwe yakecema mwene Yehoyakimu. Nayo Yowahazi yakategulwa ku Misili no mutemi Neko, yakafwila kuko.

Yehoyakimu, mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 36:5-8)

35 Mutemi yehoyakimu yakakonghola kodi ku wulawa kuli wanhu, munhu ne nghango ye sawo yakwe, ho yasume kutuga liwalo lye kodi yono yasakaga mutemi wa Misili.

36 Yehoyakimu, yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na mihano lono yatemela mu Yuda. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka yikumi na wumonga. U nyina yacemwaga Zebida, u mwana we cicekulu wa Pedaya, munya ku cikaya ca Luma.
37 Na mu witume wakwe, yakatyatya musambo mubi we walezi wakwe. Yehoyakimu yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu.
