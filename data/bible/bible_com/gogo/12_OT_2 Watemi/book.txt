2 Watemi 1

Eliya no mutemi Ahaziya

1 U mutemi Ahabu we Waisilayeli yali yeza kufwa, hodu, i wanhu we yisi ya Mowabu wakalema walece kutemwa ne Waisilayeli.

2 Nayo mutemi Ahaziya lulo, yakagwa hasi, ku wulawa kucanyha ku golofa, ye kaya yakwe ye likulu, ku Samaliya, yaketamisa muno muno. Hanyuma Ahaziya yakatuma wanhu wabite wakawuze kuli Baalizebubu, u mulungu we Wafilisiti, ku yisi ya Ekiloni, ho wamanye hono mutemi Ahaziya kofwa, hamba kowumipa.
3 Ninga malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu, yakamutuma muwizi Eliya, wa ku Tishibi, yabite viketang'anile ne wanhu wono watumigwe no mutemi Ahaziya, yakawawuze yatye, “Alu 'nye! Nhawulenyi lu mukubita mukawuze kuli Baalizebubu u mulungu we wanhu wa Ekiloni? Hamba mukwihwesa 'nye hasina Mulungu mu Isilayeli lo?
4 Ale, mubite mukamulonjele mutemi Ahaziya mutye nhavi, ‘U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, makomele gako gago, gokuwulaga du, hamba si kopona yaye.’ ”

Eliya yaketuma nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza,
5 nawo i wanhu wono wali watumwa, wakawuya kuli mutemi Ahaziya. Wali weza kufika kuli mutemi, u mutemi yakawawuza yakutya, “Alu nhawulenyi kanyhi mwawuya?”

6 Nawo wakamupitula wakatya, “Ase citang'anila no munhu mono yacilonjeye vyono, ciwuye ne nyuma cize cikulonjele zono Muwaha Mulungu yakukutya agwe. Yakutya nhavi, ‘Alu nhawule agwe wukutuma wanhu wabite wakamuwuze Baalizebubu, u mulungu we wanhu wa ku Ekiloni? Hamba mukwihwesa 'gwe hasina Mulungu mu Isilayeli lo? Agwe makomele gako gago gokuwulaga du, hamba si kopona yaye.’ ”

7 Mutemi Ahaziya yakawawuza yakatya, “Alu mwene, u munhu yuyo yali yena yiwalaci?”

8 Nawo wakamutya, “U munhu yuyo, yali yavwalile yijoho lya nyhingo ya nimu, yewopile no musipi wa mukowa mu ciwino cakwe.”

Nayo mutemi yakatya, “Woyi, yo Eliya!”

9 Hodu, hobaho mutemi yakawa kumutuma mwitumami yumonga, hamonga na wanhu we cilume makumi mahano, wabite wakamulete Eliya kuli mwene. U mwitumami yuyo, yakamwagana Eliya yekaye mu cigongo, yakamutya, “Gwe go munhu wo Mulungu, mutemi yakukusaka wutime wuze.”

10 Nayo Eliya yakamupitula yakatya, “Hono ane nili munhu wo Mulungu, ale moto wutime ku wulawa ku wulanga wukuwulaje hamonga ne wanhu wako!” Nawo moto wukawa kutima lwa kamonga du, wukawawulaga yuyo mwitumami hamonga ne wanhu wakwe.

11 Ninga mutemi, yakatuma kanyhi mwitumami yunji hamonga kanyhi na wanhu wanji makumi mahano we cilume. Nawo wakamubitila Eliya wakamutya, “Gwe go munhu wo Mulungu, mutemi yakukusaka wutime mbela mbela wuze!”

12 Nayo Eliya yakamutya, “Hono ane nili munhu wo Mulungu, ale moto wutime ku wulawa ku wulanga, wukuwulaje hamonga ne wanhu wako!” Nawo moto wo Mulungu, wukatima kamonga du, wukamuwulaga yuyo mwitumami, hamonga ne wanhu wakwe wono wali waficile makumi mahano.

13 Ninga mutemi nayo yakawuyajila kanyhi kutuma mwitumami yunji, hamonga kanyhi na wanhu wanji makumi mahano we cilume. Nayo mwitumami yuyo yakabita ko ku cigongo, yakafugama hasi, ha mwande ya Eliya, yakawa kulomba yakutya, “Gwe go munhu wo Mulungu, wumbonele lusungu, nene hamonga ne wanhu wangu, wulece kuciwulaga.
14 Hambi i wetumami wayangu wejete hamonga ne wanhu wawo, nha wawulagwa no moto wono wulawile ku wulanga. Ninga ane nghukulomba wumbonele lusungu!”

15 Nayo malayika wo Mulungu, yakamulonjela Eliya yakatya, “Ale lu mubitane nayo, wulece kogopa.” Hodu Eliya yakawa kutulukana hamonga no mwitumami yuyo wakawa kubitana ko mutemi.
16 Nayo Eliya yakamulonjela mutemi yakatya, “U Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, ‘Alu vyono agwe watuma wanhu wabite wakakuwuzize kuli Baalizebubu, u mulungu we wa Ekiloni, nha kotya muli Isilayeli mwaweza musina Mulungu wo kuwuza, holeka ku soko yiyo, agwe nagwe hamba si konoga yaye, langa kofwa du!’ ”

17 Hodu mutemi Ahaziya yakawa kufwa nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalonjela muli Eliya. Nayo Ahaziya yali yasina wana we cilume, holeka ku soko yiyo, Yolamu u nduguye, yakawuhala wutemi wakwe, yakatema mu mwaka we kejete wo wutemi wa Yeholamu u mwana wa Yehoshafati u mutemi wa Yuda.

18 Nazo zose zono yetuma u mutemi Ahaziya, zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.


2 Watemi 2

Eliya yakunyasulwa no kutegulwa ku wulanga

1 Lipango likawa lyafika u Muwaha Mulungu yamunyasule Eliya mu civulavumbi, yamutegule ku wulanga. Ale mwizuwa linji, Eliya hamonga na Elisha, wakatuluka hamonga wakulawa ku Giligali,
2 na lono wali wali mu nzila, Eliya yakamulonjela Elisha yakatya, “Alu Elisha, agwe wikalaje bahano wunindile, ane Muwaha Mulungu kandajiliza mbite ku Beteli.”

Ninga Elisha nayo yakamupitula yakatya, “Nane nghwilahiza, nha vyono nimogopile Muwaha Mulungu mumi sunga na gwegwe, ane si nokuleka yaye.” Hodu, wakawa kubitana wose sunga ko ku Beteli.

3 Nalyo lipuga lye wawizi wono wekalaga baho ha Beteli, wakamubitila Elisha wakamuwuza wakatya, “Mwene Elisha, wumanyile nha vyono izuwali Muwaha Mulungu komusola muwaha wako Eliya?”

Nayo Elisha yakawapitula yakatya, “Heya manyile, ninga cilece kuzilongalonga zinghani zizi.”

4 Hanyuma kanyhi Eliya yakamulonjela Elisha yakatya, “Alu Elisha, agwe wikalaje bahano wunindile, ane Muwaha Mulungu kandajiliza mbite ku Yeliko.”

Ninga Elisha yakamupitula yakatya, “Nane nghwilahiza nha vyono nimogopile Muwaha Mulungu mumi, sunga na gwegwe, ane si nokuleka yaye.” Hodu wakawa kubitana wose sunga ko ku Yeliko.

5 Nalyo lipuga lye wawizi wono wekalaga baho ha Yeliko, wakamubitila Elisha wakamuwuza wakatya, “Alu mwene Elisha, wumanyile nha vyono izuwali Muwaha Mulungu komusola muwaha wako Eliya?”

Nayo Elisha yakawapitula yakatya, “Heya manyile, ninga cilece kuzilongalonga zinghani zizi.”

6 Hanyuma kanyhi, Eliya yakamulonjela Elisha yakatya, “Gwe Elisha, agwe wikalaje bahano wunindile, ane Muwaha Mulungu kandajiliza mbite kwi' kolongo lya Yoludani.”

Nayo Elisha yakamupitula Eliya yakatya, “Nane nghwilahiza nha vyono nimogopile Muwaha Mulungu mumi sunga na gwegwe, ane si nokuleka yaye.” Hodu wakawa kubitana wose hamonga,
7 nawo wa walya i wawizi makumi mahano, nawo wakawatyatya sunga ho he kolongo lya Yoludani. Eliya na Elisha wakema baho ha nyenje ye likolongo, i wawizi wa walya, nawo wakema kutali hadodo.
8 Nayo Eliya yakavula likoti lyakwe, yakalipalinga, yakagatowa malenga ne likoti lyakwe, malenga gaketemanula, mwene Eliya hamonga na Elisha wakawa kulowoka likolongo hakavu.
9 Hanyuma lono walowoka, Eliya yakawa kumuwuza Elisha yakatya, “Alu Elisha, nghulomba wunonjele cono wukuhila nghutendele lono nghali si nanyasulwa.”

Nayo Elisha yakamupitula yakatya, “Ane nghukulomba mhegwe kali kejete i vilungo vyo muhe mono yali muli'gwe, nozwe niwe muhazi wako.”

10 Nayo Eliya yakamupitula yakatya, “Hee! Ayi langa nghani nghamu yono wukuyisaka. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, hono wusume kumbona du lono nghusolwa ha meso gako, ale kotuga. Ninga hono wulece kumbona lono nghusolwa, hodu si kotuga.”

11 Na lono wali wakali wajendeleye no wulonje wawo, walanje nhavi, likaza kwa nhinicizo muno ligali lyo moto lyono likukwegwa na fwalasi za moto, likema haciligati yawo, likamunyasula Eliya, likamutegula ku wulanga mu civulavumbi.
12 Nayo Elisha yakaziwona zizo, hodu yakawa kumulilila Eliya yakutya, “Gwe baba! Woyi baba! Go mutazi we vilungo we Waisilayeli! Alu ho baha du, wakondya kubita!” Elisha si yamuwona kanyhi Eliya.

Nayo Elisha, ku soko yo wukalale wono yawutuga baho, hodu yakawa kulidemulanga likoti lyakwe likalawa hejete.
13 Yakawa kulitondola likoti lya Eliya lyono lyalagala likulawa mwa Eliya, yakatuluka nalyo, yakema ha nyenje ye likolongo lya Yoludani.
14 Yakagatowa malenga ne likoti lya Eliya akuno yakulonga yakutya, “Alu Muwaha Mulungu yali hayi mwene, u Mulungu wa Eliya?” Hanyuma kanyhi yakagatowa malenga lwe kejete, nago malenga gaketemanula cinya ha lulongozi, nayo yakawa kulowoka likolongo hakavu.
15 Nawo wawizi wawalya makumi mahano, wono walawilila ku Yeliko, wakamuwona, wakawa kutya, “Ale, mwawona! I vilungo vya Eliya, vyekala muli Elisha!” Nawo wakawa kubita kumuhinjiliza. Wakenama ha mwande yakwe, wakatya,
16 “Ase mono cili baha, cili wanhu makumi mahano we vilungo. Holeka wucikombole cibite cikamulondole Eliya, u muwaha wako. Hambi nha lunji kowa u muhe wo Muwaha Mulungu wumusola wuwa kumutegula mucanyha ye matunda ganji, hamba lunji mu malolo ganji.”

Nayo Elisha yakawapitula yakawatya, “Aa, ng'o, mulece kubita.”

17 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, wene wakacila muno kumusinjiliza yawakombole wabite du. Hodu nayo nhondo yakawa kuwakombola wabite. Nawo wose vyono wali wali makumi mahano, wakatuluka, wakabita kumulondola Eliya mu matunda na mu malolo vilo vidatu ninga hamba si wamuwona yaye.
18 Hodu wakawa kuwuya kuli Elisha. Nayo Elisha yali yakali yawalindiye du baho ha Yeliko. Elisha yakawalonjela yakatya, “Alu, wulenyi, ane si namulonjelenyi vyono mulece kubita?”

I mapoto gono yaganoza Elisha

19 Wanhu wanji walawa Yeliko, wakamubitila Elisha wakamutya, “Alu nha vyono wumanyile muwaha, likaya lili lyo likaya liswanu muno, ninga malenga galyo go mabi muno, sunga ne yisi si yilelaga.”

20 Nayo Elisha yakawatya, “Ale mutajile mwino mucefu mu bakuli mhya, mundetele.” Nawo wakamuletela.
21 Elisha yakabita ku ndawililo yo muvwilo we malenga, yakawutajila mwino wuwo mugati ye malenga, akuno yakulonga yakutya, “Azi zo Muwaha Mulungu yakulonga yakutya, ‘Nagaponya malenga gaga, holeka si goleta kanyhi wufwe, hamba wu wula kulela.’ ”
22 Ne malenga gago gakawa kunojela lukulu ku wulawilila baho, nha vyono Elisha yali yalonga.

23 Hodu Elisha yakatuluka ku wulawa Yeliko, yakabita ku Beteli, na lono yali yakali yali mu nzila, wazelelo wanji wakamuziza wakulawa mumo mwi'kaya lya Beteli, wakamwizwajiza wakutya, “Wuwuce baha, agwe go munhu wo muhalu!”

24 Nayo Elisha yakawapitucila, yakawanugulila ne meso go wukali, yakawalijita mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu. Nago madubu mejete macekulu, gakakolomhoka gakulawa mu mbago, gakawehawanga wazelelo makumi mane na wejete muli wene, gakawawulaga.

25 Elisha yakawuka kuko yakabita kwi'tunda lya Kalimeli, na ku wulawa kuko, yakaza sunga ku Samaliya.


2 Watemi 3

Wulugu we Waisilayeli ne wa Mowabu

1 Mu mwaka we yikumi na munana wo wuteme wa Yehoshafati u mutemi wa Yuda, nayo Yolamu u mwana wa Ahabu, ho yatemela mu wa Isilayeli, Yolamu yakatema myaka yikumi na myejete mu Samaliya.
2 Ninga yakamusutizya Muwaha Mulungu. Hamba nhavivyo si yali mubi muno nho sogwe Ahabu, hamba nho nyina Yezebeli. Mwene Yolamu yacikumulanga cihwani cono sogwe yacinoza so wuhembelo wa Baali.
3 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, nayo yakawanoza Isilayeli, waketuma wubi, nha vyono yawasutizya mutemi Yelobowamu u mwana wa Nebati, mono yamulongolela kutema. Nawo wakajendelela du kumusutizya Mulungu hamba kuleka yaye.

4 Nayo Mesha u mutemi wa Mowabu, yali mudima ngholo, nayo yakawa yakumupela mwaka na mwaka, Ahabu u mutemi wa Isilayeli, cipegwa ca ngholo elufu yigana limonga, hamonga no wusinga wa ngholo elufu yigana limonga.
5 Ninga Ahabu mutemi wa Isilayeli yali yeza kufwa, hodu, nayo Mesha yakawa kuwagaluka Waisilayeli.
6 Nayo Yolamu yakawuka kamonga mu Samaliya. Yakalikongholela hamonga lipuga lyakwe lyose lye wasilikale wo wulugu.
7 Yakatuma munhu kuli Yehoshafati u mutemi wa Yuda, yabite yakamulonjele yatye, “Mesha u mutemi wa Mowabu yakondya kungaluka, holeka nghukulomba wuze cilunze nane, cibite cikamuhome. Wule kowa wiwikanize?”

Nayo Yehoshafati u mutemi wa Yuda, yakamupitula yakatya, “Heya nalumba, ane nili mu miwoko yako du, hamonga ne wanhu wangu, hamonga ne zifwalasi zangu zose.
8 Ninga alu cokola nzilaci ho cikawahome?”

Yolamu yakamupitula yakamutya, “Cokolela nzila nhali yono yikoleye mu mbago ya Edomu.”

9 Hodu mutemi Yolamu hamonga no mutemi wa Yuda, no mutemi wa Edomu, wakatuluka wose hamonga. Nawo wali weza kujenda muhinzo wa vilo mupunghati, wakawa wasina malenga so weneco ne mapuga ge wasilikale wawo, hamonga ne zinimu zono zali ziwadinghuliye mitegulo yawo.
10 U mutemi Yolamu yakazoza yakutya, “Nye! Alu langa ho cawona wusungu! Hambi Muwaha Mulungu, nha yakondya kucitajila ase co watemi wadatu, mu miwoko yo mutemi wa Mowabu!”

11 Ninga Yehoshafati u mutemi wa Yuda yakawuza yakatya, “Alu mwene nye, baha hasina muwizi mono cosuma kumwitumamila yaciwuzize kuli Muwaha Mulungu?”

U mwitumami monga wa Yolamu u mutemi wa Isilayeli yakapitula yakatya, “Yali baha Elisha u mwana wa Shafati, mono yali yo mutazi wa Eliya.”

12 U mutemi Yehoshafati yakapitula yakatya, “Ayo Elisha yo muwizi we mbeka.” Hodu watemi wose vyono wadatu, wakawa kumubitila Elisha.

13 Nayo Elisha yakamutya Yolamu u mutemi wa Isilayeli, “Wubite wukawawuze wawizi wono sogo Ahabu hamonga no nyina yako Yezebeli wawawuzaga.”

Ninga Yolamu yakapitula yakatya, “Ng'o ye Elisha! Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha kakondya kucitajila mu miwoko yo mutemi wa Mowabu.”

14 Elisha yakapitula yakatya, “Nane nghwilahiza hali Muwaha Mulungu mumi, nha vyono hono yali yiwe si mwilunzize na ayu Yehoshafati u mutemi wa Yuda mono nimogopile, si nali nghuhulice zono wukulonga.
15 Ninga alu mundetele mutowa lisumbi.”

Nayo mutowa lisumbi yali yeza kutowa lisumbi lyakwe, i vilungo vyo Muwaha Mulungu vikamuziza Elisha,
16 yakawa kulonga yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi, ‘Muhimbe makweme makweme mwilolo lili lyose.
17 Nanye hamba simowona mbeho hamba mvula yitonya, langa lilolo lili lyose lyomema malenga gose, nanye hamonga ne zimitugo zenyu, ne zinimu zono zikudinghula mitegulo yenyu, wose motuga malenga menji muno muno go kung'wa.’ ”
18 Elisha yakajendelela kulonga yakutya, “Kanyhi mumanye vyono nghani yiyi yo mhelu du ku witumwa no Muwaha Mulungu, na vyono komupelenyi wusume sunga na kuli Wamowabi.
19 Nanye mogateka sunga ne makaya gawo gose maswanu, gono gena ne magomawakali. Moyitemanga sunga ne mibici yawo yose ye mikalo, moyidinda ne mivwilo yawo yose ye malenga. Moyihodanga no kuyilundila mabwe migunda yawo yose yono yina moso.”
20 Nawo, wali weza kwela mitondo yaco, hakali mitondo, mu vipango vye nhondo zose vyo kulavizya likumbiko, hodu malenga gakaza gakuhuluma gakulawilila ku lubavu lwa ku Edomu, gakalimemya lilolo lyose.

21 Nawo Wamowabi wali weza kuhulika nha vyono i watemi wadatu wakuza kuwahoma, hobaho, wanhu wose walume, wawaha ne wadodo wono wali wanojeye mu wihomi, wakakongholwa wose, wakawikwa ha mupaka.
22 Nawo wali weza kwinuka, i mitondo yaco hakali mitondo, lizuwa likawa limulice mucanyha ye malenga, likaganoza malenga gakawa kwijela madunghu nha sakami.
23 Nawo wakazoza wakutya, “Nye! Ayilya sakami. Yikwijela nha vyono gago mapuga madatu ge wasilikale wo wulugu gewulaga geneco gose! Holeka alenyi cibite cikayitece nghambi yawo!”

24 Ninga wali weza kuyisejelela nghambi, i Waisilayeli wakawahoma wakawawinga. Nawo Waisilayeli, wakajendelela kuwatyatya no kuwawinga Wamowabi, akuno wakuwawulaga mumo mu nzila,
25 no kugongomeza makaya gawo. Na lono wakolaga mu migunda yawo, Waisilayeli wose wagumilaga mabwe, wakugalunda mumo mu migunda, sunga nhondo migunda yose yikamema mabwe gose. Wakayidinda ne mivwilo yose ye malenga, wakayitemanga sunga ne mibici yawo yose ye mikalo. Na hanyuma, likawa lisigaye likaya liwaha lya Kili-halesi, ninga nawo i watowa ne zinhago, wakalipilima nalyo, wakaliteka.

26 Nayo mutemi wa Mowabu yali yeza kumanya nha vyono yasumwa mu wulugu, hobaho yakasola wanhu magana mupunghati wono wena mimaje, yakajela yapulute kwa vilungo haciligati ye walugu wakwe yeponye, yabite ko mutemi wa Siliya, ninga hamba si yasuma yaye.
27 Hobaho yakamusola u mwanagwe, u mulongozi kulelwa, mono yali yo munya kuhala wutemi wakwe, yakawa kumulavya yikumbiko mucanyha ye lijinji lye likaya, kuli mulungu we Wamowabi. Nawo Waisilayeli wali weza kuziwona zizo, wakogofywa muno, hodu wakalileka likaya, wakawuya ku yisi yawo.


2 Watemi 4

Elisha yakumutaza mucekulu muciwa u mufwile

1 U mucekulu mufwile mono yali mucekulu wa yumonga wa mu cipuga ce wawizi, yakamubitila Elisha, yakamulonjela yakatya, “Gwe muwaha, mulume wangu yakondya kufwa! Nha vyono wumanyile, mwene mulume wangu, yali yo munhu mogopa Mulungu. Ninga lu, yali yena mugawa wa mapesa kuli munhu yunji. Alu munhu yuyo kaza ho yawasole wanangu wejete, wawe lye lipo lyo mugawa wuwo wo mulume wangu, yabite yakawanoze wawe wawanda wakwe.”

2 Nayo Elisha yakamuwuza yuyo mucekulu yakatya, “Alu kosaka nghutendele wule? Kanyhi nghukulomba wunonjele cinhu cono wina naco ku kaya yako.”

Nayo mucekulu yuyo yakamupitula yakatya, “Ane sina cinji co cose ku kaya, langa hogali mafuta madodo du gali mu supa.”

3 Elisha yakamutya, “Alu, wubite mu zikaya zose ze wekalani wayago, wukakonghole zisupa zose zono zisina cinji, ziwe nyinji du mu nghango nha vyono kosuma.
4 Hanyuma agwe hamonga ne wanago, mwinjile mu nyumba mwidindile mumo, hanyuma wusole supa yako yono yina mafuta, wuwe kujidila mafuta mu zisupa zono zili bwete. Na lono supa monga yamema, ale kuwa kuyiteza mbela mbela ha mhelo.”

5 Ale, u mucekulu yuyo, yakawa kwinjila mu kaya yakwe hamonga ne wanagwe, wakedindila, yakasola supa yakwe yono yina mafuta, yakawa kwanza kugajidila mafuta mu zisupa zono zili bwete, akuno nawo wanagwe wakujendelela kumusejelezeza supa zono zili bwete.
6 Nawo wali weza kukondya kuzimemya supa zose zono zali zili baho, nyina yawo yakawawuza wanagwe hono howa ho zili kanyhi supa zinji zono zili bwete, zono zikali zisigaye. Ninga yumonga wa muli wanagwe, yakamulonjela nha vyono yiyo yono wukuyijidila, yo supa ye ndumilizo. Hodu ne mafuta nago gakawa kuduma ku wulawa mu supa mono gajidwaga.
7 Hodu mucekulu yuyo, yakamubitila kanyhi muwizi Elisha. Nayo Elisha yakamutya, “Alu, wubite wukagaguze mafuta, wukalipe migawa yako yose. Ninga mapesa ganji gosigala, gono agwe hamonga ne wanago mosuma kwitumamila.”

Elisha no mucekulu mugoli, munya Shunemu

8 Hanyuma lizuwa linji, Elisha yakabita ku Shunemu, hono yekalaga mucekulu yunji mugoli. No mucekulu yuyo yakamucemela Elisha ku zindigwa. Na ku wulawa mwi'zuwa lilyo, lono Elisha yabitaga kuko ku Shunemu, yalizaga ndigwa ko mucekulu yuyo.
9 Hanyuma mucekulu yuyo yakamulonjela mulume wakwe yakatya, “Ane nitumbiye vyono u munhu yuyu mono kazaga baha hono hetu kenji na kenji, yo munhu mono yelile.
10 Holeka nghuwona viswanu cizenje cinyumba cidodo mwi'tembe, ciwice mumo ne citanda, ne meza, ne ligoda, hamonga ne tala, mwene ho yawe kuficila mumo lono yakuza kucilamusa.”

11 Ale, mwi'zuwa linji Elisha yakabita kuko ku Shunemu, yakaficila mu cinyumba cakwe cico yacehe,
12 yakamutuma Gehazi u mwitumami wakwe, yabite yakamuceme yuyo mucekulu yamuzize. Nayo yali yeza kuza,
13 Elisha yakamulonjela Gehazi yakatya, “Ale, wumuwuzaje mucekulu yuyu cono kosaka nimutendele, so magaziko gono gamwagane so wutazi wono yali kucitaza mu vihilwa vyetu. Na lunji kosaka ane mbite kuli mutemi hamba kuli musilikale muwaha, u mulongola we wasilikale nghamusacile ziswanu.”

Ninga mucekulu yuyo yakatya, “Ane nina baha vyose vyono nghuvisaka hamonga ne wanhu wangu.”

14 Elisha yakamutya Gehazi, “Ale wumuwuzaje cono kosaka nimutendele.”

Nayo Gehazi yakamutya Elisha, “U mucekulu yuyu yasina mwana, no mulume wakwe nayo yeza mukombi.”

15 Elisha yakamutya Gehazi, “Ale mulonjelaje yaze hano.” U mucekulu yuyo yakaza yakema ha cipimbilamulango.
16 Nayo Elisha yakamutya, “Ale, ku mwaka mwi'pango lili, kolowa wupagate mwana mu miwoko yako.”

Nayo mucekulu yuyo yakatya, “Woyi he! Nghukunena muwaha wulece kunghonga, hambi agwe, nha wuli munhu wo Mulungu!”

17 Ninga lyali lyeza kufika lipango lililya du mu mwaka wono watyatya, yakasawa mwana wa cilume, nha vyono Elisha yamulonjela.

18 Nayo mwana yali yeza yawiluka, mwi'zuwa linji hakali mitondo, mu vilo vye nhwanga, yakabita ku mugunda kumulonda sogwe lono yadudulaga yena ne wetumami wakwe.
19 Ninga yakanza nhinicizo, kumulilila sogwe, yakutya, “Mutwe wangu wukutama! Nye, mutwe wangu wukutama!” Nayo sogwe yakamutya mwitumami, “Ale musolaje mwana wumugomole ko nyina.”
20 Hodu u mwitumami yakamugomola mwana kuli nyina, nayo nyina yakawa yamupagate du, sunga mwi'pango lya mu misi, lono mwana yafwila.
21 Hodu yakawa kumusola, yakamutegula ku cinyumba ca Elisha, yakamuwika mu citanda ca Elisha, yakadinda mulango yakamuleka mumo.
22 Hanyuma yakamucema mulume wakwe, yakamutya, “Nghukulomba wundetele yunji wa muli wetumami, hamonga na ndogowe monga, mbite musunho kuli Elisha muwizi. Ninga nowuya kanyhi lulu baha nha vyono nosuma.”

23 Mulume wakwe yakamuwuza yakatya, “Alu socici yiwe yikunojeye wubite izuwali du, nalyo ho si lizuwa lya Sabato, hamba si sikukuu ye Ndawo yo Mwezi?”

Nayo mugolece wakwe yakatya, “Aa! Si nghani yaye.”
24 Hobaho yakawa kupegwa ndogowe yono yiwikanizwe, yakamulajiliza mwitumami yakatya, “Alu nghukulomba, wuyimashaje muno muno ndogowe mu nghango ya vyono yosuma kumangha, wulece kuyileka yijende mhola mhola, sunga nene nghulonjele.”
25 Hodu yakawa kutuluka yakabita sunga ko kwi'tunda lya Kalimeli kono yali Elisha.

Nayo Elisha yakamuwonela kutali lono yazaga, hodu yakamulonjela Gehazi mwitumami wakwe yakatya, “Lanje ta! Ayulya mucekulu wa ku Shunemu yakuza!
26 Holeka wiyohaje mwitang'anile nayo, wumutambuze hono zose swanu hamonga no mwene muswanu no mulume wakwe, hamonga no mwanagwe.”

Nayo mucekulu yuyo, yakamulonjela Gehazi vyono zose swanu du.
27 Ninga yali yeza kufika hali Elisha, yakenama ha mwande yakwe yakayibata migulu ya Elisha. Nayo Gehazi yali yeza kuziwona zizo, yakasaka kumukundujiza yuyo mucekulu yawuce, ninga Elisha yakamutya, “Mulekaje! Alo ho si wukuwona vyono yena wusungu wuwaha mu nhumbula yakwe? Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, hamba si yamanyize zizi.”

28 U mucekulu yakamulonjela Elisha, yakatya, “Gwe muwaha, wukumbuciye vyono nakulomba mwana? Kanyhi wule, si nalonga vyono wulece kunghonga?”

29 Elisha yakamupitucila Gehazi, yakamutya, “Ale, wusunhe! Wusole nghome yangu wubite mbela. Kanyhi wulece kucela mumo mu nzila so wilamusa na wanhu, na hono yunji yakulamuse, ale wulece kusola yidaha mu witambuze. Wubite wugolocile sunga wufice ku kaya, wukayijelece nghome yiyi mucanyha yo mwana.”

30 Nayo mucekulu yuyo yakamulonjela Elisha yakatya, “Nane nghwilahiza kuli Muwaha Mulungu mumi mono nimogopile, sunga na kuli'gwe, nha vyono si nokulecela lukulu!” Hodu Elisha no mucekulu yuyo, vikawa kutulukana hamonga.
31 Gehazi yakawa yalongola ku mwande yadinghuye ne nghome ya Elisha, yakayijeleka mucanyha yo mwana. Ninga hakawa hasina yizi, hamba citanjilo co cose co wumi muli mwana yuyo. Hodu Gehazi yakawuya ne nyuma yakamuhinjiliza Elisha, yakamulonjela yakatya, “U mwana hamba si yenuce yaye.”

32 Nayo Elisha yali yeza kufika, yakenjila yali wene du mu cinyumba, yakamuwona mwana yagonile mu citanda yafwiye.
33 Yakedindila mugati yali wene, yakawa kumulomba Muwaha Mulungu.
34 Hanyuma yakawa kugona mu citanda mucanyha yo mwana yuyo. Yakawika mulomo wakwe mu mulomo wo mwana, ne meso gakwe mu meso go mwana, ne miwoko yakwe yakayigolola mu miwoko yo mwana. Nayo Elisha lono yali yegoloye mucanyha yo mwana, hodu u muwili wo mwana yuyo wukawa kwanza kutuga civuce.
35 Nayo Elisha yakatima hasi mu citanda, yakajendajenda hadodo mumo mu cinyumba, hanyuma yakawuya kanyhi yakegolola mucanyha yo mwana. Hanyuma nhondo u mwana yuyo yakatyamula kali mupunghati, yakanugula ne meso gakwe.
36 Elisha yakamucema Gehazi, yakamulonjela yamuceme nyina yo mwana yuyo yaze. Nayo yali yeza kwinjila, Elisha yakamutya, “Ale, ayu hano mwanago.”
37 U mucekulu yuyo, yakagwa ha magulu ga Elisha, akuno wusu wakwe wupamize hasi. Hanyuma yakawa kumusola mwanagwe yakawa kubita.

I mapoto ganji mejete

38 Na mu mapango ganji, hakawa ho yili nzala mbaha mu yisi yose. Nayo Elisha lulo yali yakondya kuwuya ku Giligali. Nayo lono yawejizaga cipuga ce wawizi, yakamulonjela mwitumami, wakwe yajelece nyungu mbaha mu moto, yawatelecele wawizi.
39 Na yumonga wa muli wene, yakabita mu mawimbi ho yakahepe mboga. Na kuko, yakagawona mayungu ga mu mbago, yakagahepa menji muno mu nghango nha vyono yasuma kugadinghula. Yakagaleta ku wayagwe, yakapandula pandula, yakagateleka mu nyungu, akuno si wagamanyile vyono yali ndigwaci yene.
40 Ala du, yakawatenjela wanhu walye. Ninga lulo baho lono walizaga, wakazoza wakumulonjela Elisha wakutya, “Ndigwa yina sumu.” Hodu, hamba si wajendelela kanyhi kuyilya.
41 Elisha yakalomba wusaje, yakawutajila mu nyungu, yakatya, “Ale, watenjelaje ndigwa wanhu walye.” Na mu ndigwa yiyo mukawa musina kanyhi cinji cibi.

42 Na mwi'zuwa linji kanyhi, u munhu yunji yakaza yakulawa Baali Shalisha, yakamuletela Elisha mikate makumi mejete, yono minozwe ne shayili nongozi kududulwa, ya mu mwaka wuwo, hamonga na matama madece, gono si makubule mabeza gayo. Elisha yakamulonjela mwitumami wakwe yawapele wawizi walye.
43 Ninga mwitumami yakatya, “Wukwihwesa nha vyono ndigwa yiyi yowakwila wanhu yigana limonga?”

Elisha yakamupitula yakatya, “Agwe watenjelaje du walye, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yakutya vyono wolya sunga wegute no kusigaza.”
44 U mwitumami nhondo ho yakawa kuwatenjela wanhu ndigwa. Nawo wakalya, wakeguta, sunga wakasigaza, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalonga.


2 Watemi 5

Naamani yakuponywa

1 Naamani u muwaha we lipuga lye wasilikale wo mutemi wa Siliya, yali munhu we lilumbo yiwaha muno ha mwande yo mutemi wa Siliya. Vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamwitumamila Naamani mu wulugu, yakawapela wusume wasilikale we Siliya. Kanyhi Naamani yali musilikale we citung'ati. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, yagazwaga no wutamwa wo wukoma.

2 Nawo Wasiliya, lono wehomaga ne Waisilayeli, Wasiliya wakamusola mateka mwana we cicekulu we Ciisilayeli, wakamunoza yawe mwitumami wo mugolece wa Naamani.
3 Ale, mwi'zuwa linji, yuyo u mwitumami, u muhinza we Ciisilayeli, yakamulonjela mugolece wa Naamani, yakatya, “Ane nali nyende mutwa wangu Naamani, yabite kuli muwizi mono yekaye ku Samaliya! Hambi nha kosuma kumuponyezya lukulu mu wukoma wakwe.”
4 Nayo Naamani yali yeza kuzihulika zizo, hodu yakamubitila mutemi, yakamulonjela zose zono muhinza yuyo yazilonjile.
5 Nayo mutemi yakamutya, “Ale, wubite kuli mutemi wa Isilayeli wumutegulile ne baluwa ayino.”

Hodu Naamani yakatuluka, yakasoleleza na fweza elufu makumi madatu, na zahabu elufu mutandatu, hamonga ne vivwalo yikumi vyono viswanu.
6 Ne baluwa yono mutemi yamupela Naamani, yali yandicigwe nhavi, “I baluwa yiyi yo yomumanyiza mwitumami wangu Naamani kuli'gwe. Nane nghukulomba wumuponye mu wutamwa wakwe.”

7 Nayo mutemi wa Isilayeli yali yeza kukondya kuyisoma yiyo baluwa, hodu yakavidemulanga vivwalo vyakwe ko woga, akuno yakuzoza yakutya, “Alu mwene, u mutemi wa Siliya yalanjiliye vyono ane nosuma kumuponya munhu yuyu? Alo mwene yakwihwesa ane nili Mulungu lo, mono nowa nina wulajilize wo wumi ne lifwa? Ane nghuwona ayu, yakusaka ho cigombane nayo du!”

8 Nayo muwizi Elisha yali yeza kuzihulika zono zijeye, yakatuma munhu yakamuwuze mutemi yatye, “Mutemi, alo socici wina wukalale? Ale wumuletaje munhu yuyo kuli'ne nane nimulajile nha vyono mu Isilayeli mo yuli muwizi!”

9 Ale, Naamani yakawa kutuluka hamonga ne zifwalasi zakwe, ne mikokoteni yakwe yakawa kubita sunga ho ha mulango we kaya ya Elisha.
10 Nayo Elisha yakatuma mwitumami wakwe yakamulonjele Naamani vyono yabite yakehovuje kali mupunghati mwi'kolongo lya Yoludani, nayo konojela lukulu wutamwa wakwe.
11 Ninga Naamani yakawuka yakoligwe ne likuwo muno akuno yakutya, “Ane waco, nali kwihwesa 'mba, Elisha kolawila kononze, yaze yanombele kuli Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wakwe, yawalaze no muwoko wakwe mono mo wuli wutamwa, yamhonye wutamwa wangu wo wukoma!
12 Kanyhi hamonga na zizo, si ciba 'le Abana na Falefa i makolongo ga ku Damesiki, gono gacizize malenga gose ga mu Isilayeli? Ciba nali nihovuje kuko niyeze!”

13 Ninga i wetumami wakwe wakamulonjela wakatya, “Gwe muwaha, hono muwizi yali yakulonjele nghani nghamu, wali witume. Alu socici wulece kwihovuga du nha vyono yakulonjeye, nagwe wuwe wapona?”
14 Hodu Naamani yakawa kubita kwi'kolongo lya Yoludani, yakenimika mumo kali mupunghati, nha vyono yalonjelwa na Elisha, no wukoma wakwe wukezezwa lukulu. Sunga ne nghuli yakwe yikatelemela yikawa nha nghuli ya mwana mudodo.
15 Naamani yakawuya kuli Elisha hamonga ne wanhu wakwe, yakamulonjela Elisha yakamutya, “Elisha! Alu langa ho namanya vyono mbeka hasina mulungu yunji kanyhi, langa hono si Mulungu wa Isilayeli. Holeka alu nghukunena, wubocele cihumbilizwa cangu cono nghukupela.”

16 Elisha yakamupitula yakamutya, “Nane nghukwilahiza kuli Muwaha Mulungu mumi, mono nghumwitumamila, nha vyono sinobocelela lukulu cihumbilizwa.”

Nayo Naamani yakajela kumudindimiza Elisha ho yabocele, ninga hamba si yasuma yaye.
17 Hodu Naamani yakawa kumutya Elisha, “Alu, hono walema kubocela cihumbilizwa cangu, ale wunghombole nzole malongo, gono gakwiliye mutegulo, wono zosuma kudinghula nyumbu nyejete, mbite nago kwetu. Vyono ane ku wulawilila lulu hamba si nomulavizya kanyhi mulungu yunji makumbiko, hamba vilavywa vinyanywe, langa nowa kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu du yali wene.
18 Holeka nitumbiye nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu konenela, 'ne, vyono nabitaga ku wuhembelo wa Limoni, mulungu wa Siliya no kumuhemba, cili hamonga no mutemi wangu. Nane nghulomba mbeka, Muwaha Mulungu yanenele nghani yiyo!”

19 Nayo Elisha yakamutya, “Ale, wubite wupolile.” Naamani yakawa kutuluka.

Nayo Naamani yali yeza kutelatela cidaha,
20 akuno kono nyuma Gehazi u mwitumami wa Elisha, yakehwesa mu nhumbula yakwe yakatya, “Alu, lanje, muwaha wangu Elisha, yamuleceleza Naamani yabita hamba kulavya cinji! Kumbe yali yinojeye yabocele cono Naamani yali kusaka yamupele. Ninga mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu mumi, ane nomutyatya Naamani lumangho, nghabocele co cose kuli mwene.”
21 Hodu, Gehazi yakatuluka lumangho, yakamutyatya Naamani. Nayo Naamani yali yeza kuwona vyono yunji yakuza lumangho yakumutyatya, hodu yakatima hasi mu mukokoteni wakwe, yakamuhinjiliza, yakamuwuza yakutya, “Wule kupolile kuko?”

22 Gehazi yakamupitula yakatya, “Heya kupolile, ninga muwaha wangu Elisha, kanhuma nize nghulonjele nha vyono lulu baha kaficilwa ne mhuga ye wawizi wakulawa mu yisi ye matunda ya Efulayimu. Holeka yakukulomba wumupele fweza elufu yidatu, hamonga na vivwalo viswanu vyejete.”

23 Nayo Naamani yakapitula yakamutya, “Nghukunena wusole fweza elufu mutandatu.” Yakamusinjiliza yabocele. Hanyuma yakayitajila fweza yiyo mu mifuko myejete, yakayiwopa, yakasola ne vivwalo viswanu vyejete, yakawapela wetumami wakwe wejete wamudinghuze Gehazi, wakawa wakujenda wali ku mwande ya Gehazi.
24 Ninga wali weza kufika mwi'tunda mono yekalaga Elisha, Gehazi yakawa kusola yi yilya mifuko myejete ya fweza, yakawa kuyinjiza mu kaya. Yakawa kuwakombola i wetumami wa Naamani wawuye kwawo.
25 Mwene yakenjila mu kaya, yakema ha mwande ya Elisha, nayo Elisha yakamuwuza yakatya, “Alu Gehazi, wali hayi?”

Nayo Gehazi yakapitula yakatya, “Ane hamba si mbitile kunji yaye.”

26 Ninga Elisha yakamulonjela yakatya, “U muhe wangu wali wuli hamonga nagwe, na lono Naamani yali kutima mu mukukoteni wakwe yakukuhinjiliza, ane nghuwona. Ninga wumanye nha vyono, ali si yipango lyo kubocela mapesa, hamba vivwalo, hamba mizeyituni, hamba zabibu, hamba ngholo, hamba ng'ombe, hamba wetumami yaye!
27 Holeka alu wukoma wa Naamani wokwibata gwegwe hamonga no lulelo lwako cibitilila!”

Gehazi yakawa kuwuka hali Elisha, yakondya kuwa no wukoma, yakawa muzelu swee nha seluji.


2 Watemi 6

Wunimulwe we nhemo mu malenga

1 Na mwi'zuwa linji, i mhuga ye wawizi wono mwene Elisha yali yo mulanjilizi wawo, wakamuziza Elisha, wakamulonjela wakatya, “Alu lanje! I hanhu ahano hono cikaye, heza hafinyu muno si hakucikwila ase wose!
2 Holeka cikukulomba wucikombole cibite kwi'kolongo lya Yoludani cikateme mabici, cize cizenjele hanhu hono hakwiliye hono cokwikala.” Elisha yakawatya, “Ale, heya mubite.”

3 Ninga yumonga wa muli wene yakamusaka nayo Elisha vibitane nawo, nayo Elisha yakavumila,
4 ho baho wakawa kutulukana wose hamonga. Nawo wali weza kufika he 'kolongo lya Yoludani, wakawa kwanza lwa kamonga mulimo wawo wo kutema mabici gono wasakaga.
5 Ninga baho baho, lono yunji muyawo yatemaga libici, nhemo yakwe yikasomoka mu caka, yikazubujila mu malenga mwi'kolongo, yikadodomela kusi ye malenga. Nayo munya nhemo, yakanza kulila, yakumulonjela Elisha yakutya, “Woyi he! Alu hamba nonozaci 'ne, nhemo ya wanhu, hambi nha nali niyazime!”

6 Nayo Elisha yakamuwuza yakatya, “Alu nhemo yigwilila ha wulunji wa hayi? Ndajilaje ta!” Munhu yuyo yakamulajila Elisha i hanhu hono nhemo yigwiliye. Nayo Elisha yakatema cibici yakacigumila mu malenga ha wulunji wu wulya. Ne cibici cicilya cikayinoza nhemo yikelela mucanyha ye malenga.
7 Hodu, Elisha yakamulonjela yuyo munhu yakatya, “Ale wugundame wuyitondole.” Nayo yuyo munhu yakagundama, yakayitondola.

Wasilikale we Cisiliya wo wulugu wakusumwa

8 Na mu mapango gago, u mutemi wa Siliya wehomaga ne Waisilayeli. Yakawawuza i wetumami wakwe i hanhu hono wosuma kuyisimya nghambi yakwe.
9 Ninga Elisha yakatuma munhu yabite kuli mutemi wa Isilayeli yakamusugule, vyono yalece kusejelela behi ne hanhu baho ha nghambi yo mutemi wa Siliya. Hambi Wasiliya nha wali wehundice baho, ho wawatejeze Waisilayeli.
10 Ale, nayo mutemi wa Isilayeli, yakawasugula wanhu wono wekalaga hanhu baho, nawo wose wakawa wali meso, walanjiliye zono zokwijela. Ne nghani yono yinha yiyo, yikejela kali kenji du.

11 Nayo mutemi wa Siliya, yali yeza kuziwona zizi, yakakalala muno muno, yakawacema wetumami wakwe yakawawuza yakutya, “Mwene 'nye! Nani wa muli nyenye mono yemile ku lubavu lo mutemi wa Isilayeli?”

12 Muyawo yunji yakapitula yakatya, “Gwe go mutemi, hasina hamba yunji wa muli'se, mono kosuma kunoza nhavivyo. Langa muwizi Elisha, yo kamulonjelaga mutemi wa Isilayeli zose zono kulongaga, sunga na zose zono kulongaga cinyele sunga na za mu cumba cako co kugona.”

13 Nayo mutemi wa Siliya yakalajiliza yakatya, “Ale, mumulondolaje muwone hono yekaye, nane nomwibata.” Nayo mutemi yali yeza kulonjelwa nha vyono Elisha yali ku Dotani,
14 hodu hobaho du, yakatuma wasilikale wenji wajendela mu zifwalasi, hamonga ne matuka go wulugu, wabite wakamwibate Elisha. Wakafika he kaya lya Dotani mu mapango ge cilo, wakalipilima likaya.
15 U mwitumami wa Elisha mwi'zuwa lilyo, yakelawa kwinuka hakali mitondo muno muno, yali yeza kulawila hanze ye kaya, yalanje nhavi, yakawona mapuga ge wasilikale wa Siliya walipilime likaya wena ne zifwalasi zawo, hamonga ne matuka gawo go wulugu. Hodu, yakawuya ne nyuma kuli Elisha, yakamulonjela yakatya, “Gwe go muwaha wangu, alu langa congomezwa! Hamba cotenda nhawule 'lu?”

16 Elisha yakamutya, “Agwe wulece kogopa, hambi ase ku lubavu lwetu nha cili wenji muno kuciza wene.”
17 Hanyuma Elisha yakalomba yakutya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wumudigule yuyu meso gakwe, ho yasuma kuwona!” Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakamuhulika Elisha wulombe wakwe, nayo mwitumami wa Elisha yali yeza kulanga nhavi, yakawona ha cigongo, zifwalasi hamonga ne matuka go wulugu go moto, gamupilime Elisha.

18 Nawo Wasiliya, wali weza kusaka wamuhungucile Elisha, mwene Elisha yakamulomba Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, watunduwazaje meso wanhu wawa, wawe cibofu.” Muwaha Mulungu nayo yakawuhulika wulombe wa Elisha, yakawatunduwika meso wanhu wawo wakawa cibofu.
19 Nayo Elisha yakawa kuwabitila yakawatya, “Nye wayangu, mwayileka nzila yono yibitile kono mukusaka kubita, hambi aha nha si lye kaya lyono mukusaka kubita. Holeka alu munhyatyaje, nane nomutegulenyi sunga kuli munhu yuyo mono mukumulondola.” Nayo yali yeza kuwasola, yakawategula sunga ko ku Samaliya.

20 Nawo wali weza kwinjila du mwi'kaya, hodu Elisha yakalomba yakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wuwadigule meso gawo wasume kuwona.” Muwaha Mulungu yakawuhulika wulombe wa Elisha, yakawapela kanyhi kuwona. Nawo wali weza kunugula, walanje nhavi, kumbe wali mwi'kaya lya Samaliya.

21 Nayo mutemi wa Isilayeli yakatya, “Alu Elisha, niwawulagaje wanhu wawa hamba ng'o?”

22 Elisha yakatya, “Aa, ng'o mutemi! Wulece kuwawulaga. Sunga ne wasilikale wose wono wali wakondya kuwateka, wulece kuwawulaga yaye. Langa wuwapelaje ndigwa walye, ne malenga wang'we, hanyuma wuwakombole wapolile wabite ko mutemi wawo.”
23 Hodu u mutemi wa Isilayeli yakawanozeza ndigwa nyinji muno muno, wakalya, wakang'wa. Hanyuma yakawa kuwakombola wapolile, wawuye kuli mutemi wawo wa Siliya. Na ku wulawilila baho, i Wasiliya, hamba si wejelela kanyhi kuyihoma yisi ya Isilayeli.

Likaya lya Samaliya, likupilimwa ne walugu

24 Hanyuma ya zizo, Benihadadi u mutemi wa Siliya yakawakonghola wasilikale wakwe wose, yakabita kuyihoma Samaliya, yakalipilima likaya lya Samaliya.
25 Na ku soko yo wupilimwe we likaya ne walugu kwa vilo vinji muno muno hodu wukenjila wanjile wuwaha muno muno wa ndigwa mwi'kaya, ne nzala yikawa mbaha muno muno, sunga yikawa, mutwe wa ndogowe, wukawa kuguzwa fweza makumi munana, nago matondofi ga nghunda, gakawa kuguzwa fweza yihano kwa gulamu magana mejete.

26 Nayo mutemi wa Isilayeli, lono yajendajendaga mu canyha ye lijinji lye likaya, ale, mucekulu yunji yakamuwona yakanza kumulilila yakutya, “Gwe go mutemi! Nghukulomba wunhaze!”

27 Nayo mutemi yakamupitula yakatya, “Alu hono Muwaha Mulungu yalece kukutaza, ane waco ho nokupela wutazici? Hambi nha sina wuhemba hamba divayi yaye?
28 Alu agwe wina wugazice ci?”

Mucekulu yuyo yakamupitula yakatya, “Gwe go mutemi! Mwi'zuwa linji mucekulu ayu yanonjela nha vyono izuwali cimulyaje mwanago, i mitondo comulya ayu mwanangu.
29 Holeka cikawa kumuteleka mwanangu cikamulya. Na mwizuwa lye kejete, nghawa kumulonjela nha vyono, izuwali comulya mwanago. Ninga mwene yali yakondya kumuhundika mwanagwe!”

30 Nayo mutemi yali yeza kuzihulika zizi, hodu yakayidemulanga myenda yakwe ko wukalale wuwaha muno muno. Sunga ne wanhu wono wali wali behi ne lijinji, wakamuwona yavwalile yigunila.
31 Mutemi yakazoza yakutya, “Ale Mulungu wangu yambulagaje, hono ndece kumuwulaga Elisha izuwa lili lono lizuwa likali si naswa!”
32 Ho baho baho, yakatuma munhu yabite yakamulete Elisha hali mwene. Nayo Elisha, mu mapango gago, yali yali ha kaya yakwe, vikuduwana ne walongola wanji, wono wali wazize kumulamusa. Na lono munhu mono mutemi yamutumile yali yakali si nafika, Elisha yakawalonjela i walongola, yakatya, “Ayulya u muwulaji, katuma munhu ho yaze yambulaje! Holeka kolo u munhu yuyo yakufika baha, ale, anye mudinde mulango, mulece kumuleceleza yenjile kono gati. Hambi nha nitumbiye vyono mutemi yakuza nayo mu nyuma yakwe.”
33 Nayo lono yali yakali kulonga zizo, hobaho du nayo mutemi yakawa kufika, yakawa kutya, “Alu mwene nha yo Muwaha Mulungu mono yacileteye wugaye wuwu! Alu nolindila kanyhi cici cilawe kuli mwene?”


2 Watemi 7

1 Elisha yakamupitula yakatya, “Ale wuhulicize zono Muwaha Mulungu yakulonga! Yakutya nhavi, i mitondo kwinha vivi, mumu mono Samaliya mosuma kugula kilo yidatu za wusaje wuswanu kwa fweza monga du, hamba shayili kilo mutandatu, nazo kwa fweza monga du.”

2 U mwitumami mwihuwilwa, mulongola wo mutemi yakamupitula Elisha, yakatya, “Azo si zosumika hamba hadodo! Hamba Muwaha Mulungu mweneco yali yasace yalete wuhemba lwa kamonga!”

Elisha nayo yakamupitula yakatya, “Ale heya! Nagwe koziwona zizi zose kolo zikwijela, ninga si kolila lukulu ndigwa yo yose.”

Wasilikale wa Siliya, wakuwuka

3 Hakawa na wanhu wane wono wali wena wutamwa wo wukoma, wanhu wawa, wali wekaye hanze ye malango ge likaya lya Samaliya, wakawa kulongana weneco wose wakutya, “Ase, yili ciba cikale bahano du sunga cifwe,
4 hambi nha hasina cambuzi co cose co kwinjila mwi'kaya, hambi nha vyo cofwa du ne nzala, na hono cikale bahano naho vyo cofwa du. Holeka mukenhe cibite ku nghambi ye Wasiliya, nawo nha wosaka waciwulaje du, ninga na lunji, wociwonela lusungu walece kuciwulaga.”
5 Hodu wakawa kwilawa kwinuka hakali mitondo muno kukali kutitu-titu, wakawa kubita ku nghambi ye Wasiliya. Ninga wali weza kufika ha nghambi, wakagana hasina hamba munhu monga yaye.
6 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yawanoza Wasiliya wahulice nha mukukunzo we misindo ye wasilikale wenji muno muno wakuwaziza, hamonga ne zifwalasi, ne matuka go wulugu. Holeka Wasiliya wakehwesa nha vyono, mutemi wa Isilayeli kawalalika u mutemi we Wahiti, na we Wamisili, hamonga ne wasilikale wawo wo wulugu, waze wamutaze, ho wawahome Wasiliya.
7 Holeka mu cinyamihe cico Wasiliya wakatiza ho weponye wumi wawo. Wakagaleka mahema gawo, ne zifwalasi, ne zindogowe, wakayileka nghambi yawo, hamonga ne vinhu vyose vyono wali wena navyo mumo mu nghambi yawo.

8 Nawo wa walya i wanhu wane wo wutamwa wo wukoma, wali weza kufika behi ha mhelo ye nghambi, wakenjila mwi'hema linji, wakalya, wakang'wa vyono vyali mumo mwi'hema. Wakasola zifweza, ne zizahabu, hamonga ne vivwalo vyono wavyagana mumo, wakatuluka navyo, wakabita kuvihundika. Hanyuma wakawuya ne nyuma, wakenjila kanyhi mwi'hema linji, waketuma nhavivilya.
9 Ninga hanyuma wakakumbucila, wakalongana weneco wose wakutya, “Ninga si yowa viswanu hono cijendelele kunoza nhavivi! Hambi ayino nha nghani swanu, holeka si yowa viswanu hono cinyamale nayo. Na hono cilindile sunga welelecele, ho cikayilonje nghani yiyi, cimanye vyono cotuga wulipicizwe. Holeka alenyi cibite lulu baha du, cikawalonjele wetumami wo mutemi!”
10 Hobaho wakawa kutuluka, wakawa kubita ko ku Samaliya, wakawalomba walindilizi we milango ya mwi'kaya, wakawatya, “Ase cibita sunga ko ku nghambi yono wali wapanjile wanhu wa Siliya, nase si cagane, hamba kuwona munhu wo wose. Ne zifwalasi hamonga ne zindogowe zose, vyo ziwopigwe du, sunga ne mahema gose, nago malekwe du hamonga na vyose vyono vili mugati yago, hambi Wasiliya nha wakondya kuwuka.”

11 Nawo walindiliza mu milango, wakawa kuyanza nghani yiyo, sunga yikafika nhondo ko kwi'kulu.
12 Na mu mapango gago, yali yikali vyo cilo cilo du, ninga hamba nhavivyo, u mutemi yakenuka du, yakawalonjela wetumami wakwe yakatya, “Alu, mugole ta, nimulonjelenyi nha vyono wawo Wasiliya wakusaka wanoze! Wene wamanyile muno nha vyono mu Samaliya mo yili nzala mbaha muno muno. Holeka ane nghuwona vyono wayileka nghambi yawo ne nyono, ho wakehundice hanji. Vyono wene wakwihwesa ase cowuka wose mwi'kaya cibite ku wulondola ndigwa, ho wasume kuciteka cili womi, hamonga no kulisola ne likaya.”

13 Ninga yumonga wa muli wetumami wakwe, yakapitula yakatya, “Alu wanhu wose mwi'kaya hamba nhavivyo, nha wakondya kulemwa ne nzala, alu waweza nha wafwiye. Holeka ane nghuwona ciba citume wanhu wanji hamonga na fwalasi yihano zono zisigaye, cikalanje cimanye zono zili kuko.”
14 Hobaho du, wakawahagula wanhu, nayo mutemi yakawatuma wanhu wamemile mu matuka mejete go wulugu, yakawalajiliza wabite wakamanye zono ziwejeleye wasilikale we Wasiliya.
15 Wanhu wawo wakatuluka wakawa kubita sunga he kolongo lya Yoludani. Na mumo mu nzila, wakawa kuwona vivwalo hamonga ne vinhu vinji vyono Wasiliya wajendaga wakuvigumila lono wali kutiza. Hodu wanhu wawo wakawuya kuli mutemi no kumuloloseza zose.
16 Hobaho i wanhu wa Samaliya, wakawa kubita lumangho ku nghambi ye Wasiliya, wakawa kuvisola vyose vyono vyali vili mumo mu nghambi. Ne wanhu wakawa kugula kilo yidatu zo wusaje wuswanu kwa fweza monga, hamonga na kilo mutandatu za shayili, kwa fweza monga du.

17 Nayo mutemi wa Isilayeli yakamuwika ha mulango mwitumami wakwe mulongola, ho yalindilize mulango we likaya. Ninga wanhu wakamusambanjila baho ha mulango sunga yakafwa. Yikawa nha vyono Elisha yali yalonga, lono mutemi yabitila kumuwona Elisha.
18 Elisha yamulonjela mutemi yakatya, “I mitondo kwinha vivi, mosuma kugula mu Samaliya kilo yidatu za wusaje wuswanu kwa fweza monga du, hamba Shayili kilo mutandatu nazo kwa fweza monga du,
19 lono yuyo mwitumami yapitula yakatya vyono, ‘Azo si zosumika hamba hadodo! Hamba yiwe Muwaha Mulungu mweneco yali yasace yalete wuhemba lwa kamonga.’ ” Nayo Elisha na vyono yamupitula yuyo mwitumami, yakamutya, “Ale, heya! Nagwe koziwona zizi zose kolo zikwijela, ninga hamba si kolila lukulu ndigwa yoyose.”
20 Na zizo zose mbeka zikamwijelala, nayo ya kasambanjilwa ne wanhu ha mulango we likaya sunga yakafwa.


2 Watemi 8

U mucekulu mulawa Shunemu, yakuwuya kwawo

1 Ninga Elisha yali yakondya kumulonjela u mucekulu munya Shunemu, munghalo mwana mono mwene Elisha yamuzukula, nha vyono, Muwaha Mulungu koleta nzala mbaha mu yisi, yono yosola myaka mupunghati ho yipulute. Holeka Elisha yakamusugula yuyo mucekulu, nha vyono yinojeye mwene hamonga ne mhuga yakwe yose, wawuce baho wabite wakekale kunji.
2 Nayo mucekulu yuyo, yakatyatya nha vyono Elisha yamulonjela, yakawuka hamonga ne mhuga yakwe yose, wakabita kwikala ku yisi ye Wafilisiti myaka mupunghati.

3 Yali yeza kupuluta myaka mupunghati, hodu, yakawa kuwuya kanyhi kwawo ku yisi ya Isilayeli. Yakamubitila mutemi wa Isilayeli, yakamulomba mutemi yamugomolele kaya yakwe hamonga no mugunda wakwe.
4 Ninga yali yeza kufika hali mutemi, yakamwagana mutemi vikuduwana na Gehazi u mwitumami wa Elisha, mutemi yamuwuzaga Gehazi, yasakaga yamanye ze mapoto gono yakuganoza Elisha.
5 Nayo Gehazi lono yali yakali yali mu wulolose, wa vyono Elisha yamuzukula munhu mono yali yafwiye, ho baho baho nayo mucekulu yuyo nayo, yakawa kwinjila hali mutemi. Nayo Gehazi yakatya, “Gwe go mutemi! Ayu ahano no mucekulu yena no mwanagwe mono Elisha yamuzukula.”
6 Mutemi yakamuwuza mucekulu yuyo nha vyono zinha. Nayo mucekulu yuyo yakahomelela nha vyono Gehazi yali yakondya kulolosa. Hodu hobaho mutemi yakawa kumucema yumonga wa muli wetumami wakwe, yakawa kumutuma yabite yakamupele mucekulu yuyo vinhu vyakwe vyose, hamonga ne cifo ce nhwanga yakwe yose ya mu migunda yakwe ya mu myaka yose mupunghati, yono yali yali hasina.

Elisha na Benihadadi, u mutemi wa Siliya

7 Elisha yakabita ku Damesiki, lono Benihadadi u mutemi wa Siliya yali yatamigwe. Nayo mutemi Benihadadi yali yeza kuhulika vyono Elisha yali baho,
8 hobaho yakawa kumucema Hazayeli yumonga wa muli wetumami wakwe, yakamutya, “Alu Hazayeli, wusole cihumbilizwa wumutegulile yuyo muwizi Elisha, wukamulombe yamuwuze Muwaha Mulungu, hono ane nopona mu mhungo yiyi hamba ng'o.”
9 Hazayeli yakawa kusola ngamiya makumi mane, yakazipaciza i vinhu vyose viswanu vya mu Damesiki, yakawa kubita kuli Elisha. Nayo Hazayeli yali yeza kwitang'anila na Elisha, yakamutya, “U mwitumami wako Benihadadi u mutemi wa Siliya, kanhuma kuli'gwe, nize nghuwuze, hono mwene kopona mu mhungo yakwe, hamba ng'o.”

10 Elisha yakamupitula Hazayeli, yakamutya, “Muwaha Mulungu kandajila nha vyono si kopona yaye, langa kofwa. Ninga agwe wubite wukamulonjele nha vyono si kofwa yaye langa kopona.”
11 Baho Elisha yakawa kumunugulila Hazayeli ko wogofye wuwaha muno, sunga Hazayeli yakenjilwa ne mahwele muno. Hobaho baho nayo Elisha yakanza kulila.
12 Hazayeli yakamuwuza yakatya, “Alu Elisha, nhawule wukulila?” Elisha yakamupitula yakatya, “Heya nghulila, hambi nha manyile i zimbi zono agwe kowatendela wanhu wa Isilayeli. Hambi nha kogaticimiza no moto magomawakali gawo, kowongomeza ne wazelelo wawo hamonga ne wana wadodo wose, ne wacekulu wono wena mida, kowademulanga i mida yawo.”

13 Nayo Hazayeli yakamuwuza yakatya, “Ane nha si nili cinji yaye, alu vilungo vivyo vyose ho novisola hayi?” Elisha yakamutya, “Muwaha Mulungu yakondya kundajila nha vyono agwe yo gwegwe mono kowa mutemi wa Siliya.”

14 Hazayeli yali yeza kuwuya kuli Benihadadi, Benihadadi yakamuwuza zono Elisha yalonjile. Nayo Hazayeli yakamulonjela yakatya, “Elisha kanonjela vyono agwe kononga du, wulece kwihwelela.”
15 Ninga mwi'zuwa lye kejete, Hazayeli yakasola libulanjeti, yakalinimika mu malenga, yakaligubika mu wusu wo mutemi, hodu mutemi yakawa kufwa.

Mwene Hazayeli yakawa kuwuhala wutemi wa Benihadadi, yakawa mutemi wa Siliya.

Yeholamu, u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 21:1-20)

16 Na mu mwaka we 'hano wo wutemi wa Yolamu u mwana wa Ahabu, u mutemi wa Isilayeli. Yeholamu u mwana wa Yehoshafati yakawa mutemi wa Yuda,
17 yali yaficile myaka makumi madatu na myejete lono yakutema. Yakayitema Yelusalemi myaka munana.
18 Mugolece wakwe, yali mwana we cicekulu wa Ahabu. Yeholamu yakazityatya zinzila zimbi ze watemi wa Isilayeli, nha vyono yali yinha nyumba ya Ahabu. Yaketuma zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu.
19 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu hamba si yenda yayongomeze Yuda, hambi nha yali yehuwiza kuli mwitumami wakwe Dawudi, nha vyono lulelo lwakwe lojendelela kutema vilo vyose.

20 Na mu vilo vyo wutemi wa Yeholamu, wanhu wa Edomu wakayigaluka yisi ya Yuda, waketema wali wene.
21 Yeholamu yakatuluka hamonga ne matuka gakwe gose go wulugu, yakabita ko ku Zayili. Nawo Waedomu wakamupilima hamonga ne matuka gakwe go wulugu, ne wawaha we matuka. Ninga mwene Yeholamu hamonga ne wawaha we matuka, wakatiza ne cilo. I wasilikale wakwe, nawo wakeyagalila munhu na kaya yakwe.
22 Nayo yisi ya Edomu yikayigaluka yisi ya Yuda sunga nalu. Na mwi'pango lilyo du, likaya lya Libina nalyo likayigaluka Yuda.

23 Ne zinghani zose ze metumo gono yetuma mutemi Yeholamu, zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Yuda.
24 Nayo Yeholamu yakafwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi, mu kaya ya Dawudi. U mwanagwe Ahaziya yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Ahaziya, u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 22:1-6)

25 Na mu mwaka we likumi na nyejete wo wutemi wa Yolamu u mwana wa Ahabu, u mutemi wa Isilayeli, nayo Ahaziya u mwana wa Yeholamu ho yatemela yisi ya Yuda.
26 Yakatema yaficile myaka makumi mejete na myejete. Ninga yakatema mwaka monga du mu Yelusalemi. U nyina wa Ahaziya yacemwaga Ataliya, u mwana we cicekulu wo mutemi Ahabu, kanyhi yo mwizukulu wa Omuli u mutemi wa Isilayeli.
27 Alu vyono Ahaziya, yakawa wali ndugu no mutemi Ahabu, ku soko vyono Ahaziya yali mukwemulima kwa Ahabu, holeka nayo yaketuma zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono nyumba ya Ahabu yetuma.

28 Hodu mutemi Ahaziya, vikelunza na Yolamu u mutemi wa Isilayeli, wakabita wakamuhome Hazayeli u mutemi wa Siliya, ku Lamoti ya Gileyadi. Ninga mutemi Yolamu, yakatamiswa muno muno mumo mu wulugu.
29 Holeka yakawuya yakabita kwi 'kaya lya Yezeleli, ho wamutamye zinghomele zakwe. Nayo mutemi Ahaziya yakabita kumulanga.


2 Watemi 9

Yehu, yakwitilwa mafuta, yawe mutemi wa Isilayeli

1 Hanyuma Elisha yakamucema yumonga wa muli wawizi we cizelelo, yakamutya, “Alu, wiwikanizaje, wubite ku Lamoti ya ku Gileyadi. Wusole ne cupa ayi ye mafuta,
2 na kolo wafika kuko, ale, wumulondole Yehu u mwana wa Yehoshafati, kanyhi yo mwizukulu wa Nimushi. Kolo wamuwona, wumusolaje, wubitane nayo mu cinyumba, kutali ne wanduguze.
3 Na kolo wenjila nayo, wumwitile mafuta aga mu mutwe wakwe, akuno wukutya, ‘Muwaha Mulungu yakukutya nha vyono yakukwitila mafuta gaga wuwe mutemi wa Isilayeli.’ Na kolo wazikondya zizi, wuwuce baho mbela mbela nha vyono kosuma.”

4 Ale, du, u muwizi we cizelelo yakawa kubita ku Lamoti ya ku Gileyadi.
5 Nayo yali yeza kufika kuko, yakawagana wawaha we wasilikale wehangulize wali mu mukung'ano wawo. Yakawatya, “Nye wawaha, ane nina nghani yono nhumigwe nimulonjelenyi.” Nayo Yehu yakawuza yakutya, “Alu wukumusaka wa hayi mwene muli sese?”

Muwizi muzelelo yakatya, “Nghukusaka gwegwe.”
6 Hodu wakawa kutulukana. Nayo muwizi we cizelelo, yakawa kumwitila Yehu mafuta mu mutwe, akuno yakutya, “Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli yakukutya, ‘Nghukwitila mafuta gaga, wuwe mutemi we wanhu wangu Waisilayeli.
7 Holeka yikunojeye wumuwulaje mutemi wako. U mwana wa Ahabu, nane ho sume kumulipiciza Yezebeli ku soko yo kuwawulaga wawizi wangu, hamonga ne wetumami wangu wanji.
8 Holeka wuwawulagaje wose we nyumba ya Ahabu hamonga no lulelo lwakwe lose. Nowongomezeza lukulu wose wa mu nyumba yakwe, wadodo ne wakombi.
9 Nowanoza we nyumba yakwe, nha vyono nawanozeza we zimhuga za Yelobowamu na Baasha, i matemi wa Isilayeli.
10 Nayo Yezebeli, u muwili wakwe si wozikwa yaye, langa woligwa du ne zimbwa mu yisi ya Yezeleli.’ ” Nayo muwizi we cizelelo, yali yeza kukondya kuzilonga zizo, hodu yakawuka lwa kamonga mu cinyumba, yakatiza.

11 Nayo Yehu, hobaho yakawa kuwuya kuli wetumami wayagwe, nawo wakamuwuza wakatya, “Wule, zose swanu? Alu yu yulya wo lukwale, yali kukusacilaci?”

Yehu yakatya, “Mumanyile cono yali kunondolela?”

12 Nawo wakatya, “Aa, ng'o, ase si cimanyile yaye, holeka cikukulomba wucilonjele zono yakulonjeye!”

Yehu yakatya, “Kanonjela katya, ‘Muwaha Mulungu kanyitya nha vyono yakunyitila mafuta niwe mutemi wa Isilayeli.’ ”

13 Hodu ho bahobaho wa walya wayagwe, wakawa kutandalika vivwalo vyawo mucanyha ye zi ngazi, Yehu yakawa kwima mucanyha ye vivwalo vivyo, nawo wakawa kutowa ndulele, akuno wazozize wakutya, “Yehu yo mutemi!”

Yolamu mutemi wa Isilayeli, yakuwulagwa

14 Na ku wulawa baho, Yehu yakawa no wuhile wubi kuli mutemi Yolamu, na lulo, Yolamu yali yakali yali ku Yezeleli, kono yali yabita kwilanjilila zinghomele zakwe, zono yazituga ku wulugu wono wehomaga na Hazaleli, u mutemi wa Siliya, ku Lamoti ya ku Gileyadi.
15 Holeka Yehu yakawalonjela i wetumami wayagwe yakatya, “Anye wose hono muwe muli ku lubavu lwangu, ale, halece kuwa na yunji wo wose wa muli nyenye, mono kotiza kulawa mu Lamoti, yabite ku Yezeleli yakawasugule.”
16 Hodu, mwene Yehu yakapanda mwi'tuka lyakwe lyo wulugu, yakawa kubita ku Yezeleli. Nayo Yolamu yali yakali si nanoga zinghomele zakwe. Na Ahaziya u mutemi wa Yuda, nayo yali yaza kumulanga.

17 Ninga mulindilizi we likaya lya Yezeleli, lono yali yali mu canyha yo munala, yakamuwona Yehu yakuza hamonga ne wanhu wakwe. Hobaho yakazoza yakutya, “Nghuwona lipuga lye wanhu likuza!”

Nayo Yolamu yakatya, “Ale, wutume munhu monga mupanda fwalasi, yabite viketang'anile nawo, yawawuze hono walugu hamba wayetu.”

18 Nayo mono yatumwa, yakamangha mbela sunga kuli Yehu, yakamulonjela Yehu yakatya, “Mutemi yakusaka kumanya hono kuza nha wuli muyetu.”

Nayo Yehu yakamupitula yakatya, “Ayo si nghani yako kumanya, agwe wuze wunhyatyaje du mu nyuma.” Nayo mulindilizi mono yali yali mucanyha yo munala, yakalonga nha vyono, u munhu mono cimutumile kakondya kufika kwi'puga, ninga si yakuwuya ng'o, hamba nhawule magu.
19 Baho yakatumwa munhu yunji kanyhi, nayo yali yeza kufika kuko, yakamuwuza Yehu nha vyono muyagwe yamuwuzize. Nayo Yehu yakamupitula yakatya, “Ayo si nghani yako kumanya, agwe wuze wunhyatye du mu nyuma.”

20 Nayo mulindilizi yakalonga kanyhi nha vyono u munhu mono cimutumile, kakondya kufika kwi'puga, ninga si yakuwuya ng'o, hamba nhawule, magu. “Ninga u muwaha we lipuga, yakujendya mutuka wakwe wo wulugu kotya nha munhu mono yena lukwale, kanyhi hono wukumulanga kotya nha Yehu!”

21 Hodu mutemi Yolamu yali yeza kuzihulika zizo, yakatya, “Mbikanizizenyi mbela mutuka wangu wo wulugu.” Nawo wakamutya, “Cakondya.” Hobaho mwene Yolamu hamonga no mutemi Ahaziya, wakawa kupanda munhu no mutuka wakwe, wabite wakamuhinjilize Yehu. Waketang'anila nayo ha mugunda wono wali wa Naboti.
22 Ale, baho, Yolamu yakamuwuza Yehu yakamutya, “Yehu! Wukuza no mulamu hamba ng'o?”

Nayo Yehu yakamupitula yakatya, “Alu howa ho wuli wule mulamu, na kumbe wuhawi wo nyina yako Yezebeli, hamonga no wusenyha wakwe vyono yetumaga, vikali vili mumu?”

23 Nayo Yolamu yakawa kuwupitusa mbela mbela mutuka wakwe yakutiza, akuno yakuzoza yakutya, “Gwe Ahaziya, ale citize awu mbeka wugaluci.”
24 Ninga Yehu yakawutawika wupinde wakwe, yakawukwega kwe vilungo vyakwe vyose, yali yeza kuyilecela songa, yikaficila mu mugongo wa Yolamu, yikaguma yikatoboza mo mu nhumbula yakwe, hodu hobaho Yolamu yakawa kugwa mugati yo mutuka wakwe, yakondya kufwa.
25 Nayo Yehu, yakawa kumulonjela mutazi wakwe Bidikali yakatya, “Ale, wusolaje muwili wa Yolamu wukawugumile mu mugunda wono wali wa Naboti. Kanyhi wukumbucile nha vyono, agwe na nene, lono cali cipandile hamonga cili mu nyuma ya Ahabu, u sogwe wa yuyu Yolamu, u Muwaha Mulungu yazilonga zinghani zizi kuli Ahabu yakamutya,
26 ‘Nawuwona wuwulaji wono wamuwulaga Naboti yigolo, hamonga ne wanagwe wose. Nane nghehuwiza nha vyono, nagwe nokulipiciziza bahano du hono mugunda wuwuno.’ Holeka wusolaje muwili wa Yolamu, wukawugumile mu mugunda wono wali wa Naboti, ciyifisilize nghani yono yayilonga Muwaha Mulungu.”

Ahaziya u mutemi wa Yuda yakuwulagwa

27 Nayo mutemi Ahaziya yali yeza kuziwona zizi, hobaho yakapanda mu mutuka wakwe, yakawa kutiziza ku Beti Hagani. Ninga Yehu yakamuwinga munyuma, sunga yakamwibata, yakawa kuwalonjela wanhu wakwe yakutya, “Mumuwulagaje na yuyo si!” Hodu nawo wakamutimila, akuno yakumangha, wakamuhoma makomele menji, lono mwene Ahaziya, yatizaga yali mu nzila mu mutuka wakwe, yadomile ku cikaya ca Gulu, behi ne likaya lya Ibeleyamu. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, yakasuma kwikwejelela sunga yakafika kwi 'kaya lya Megido, yakafwila kuko.
28 Nawo i wetumami wakwe wakawusola muwili wakwe, wakabita nawo ku Yelusalemi, wakawuzika mu vitenjelo vye watemi, mu kaya ya Dawudi.

29 Ahaziya u mutemi wa Yuda, yakatema mu Yuda mu mwaka we likumi na monga, wa mu vilo vyo wuteme wa Yolamu mwana wa Ahabu mu Isilayeli.

Yezebeli u mugolece wo mutemi yakuwulagwa

30 Yehu yali yeza kufika mwi'kaya lya Yezeleli, yakamwagana Yezebeli yakondya kuzihulika zose zono zijeye, holeka yakawa yeyola na lanji ndunghu mu zinghope ze meso gakwe, yetonanga ne zimvwili zakwe, yakawa yemile he 'dilisha mu kaya ye likulu yalanjile ku nzila.
31 Nayo yali yeza kumuwona Yehu yakwinjila ha mulango we likaya, hobaho yakawa kwanza kuzoza yakutya, “Gwe Zimuli! Gwe go muwulaji! Alu akuno nako, kuza nozaci?”

32 Yehu nayo yakamugalamila kuko kwi' dilisha, yakazoza yakutya, “Anye! Nani mono yali ku lubavu lwangu?” Hakawa na wetumami wa mwi'kulu wejete hamba wadatu nhavi, wono wahulika vyono Yehu yalongaga baho hasi,
33 Yehu yakawalonjela wawo yakawatya, “Mugumilenyi mucekulu yuyo hono hasi.” Hobaho nawo wakamwibata wakamugumila ho hasi. Ne sakami yakwe yikamizucila mwijinji, sunga na mu zifwalasi mose. Nayo Yehu yakawa kukoza mutuka wakwe hamonga ne zifwalasi zakwe mucanyha yo muwili wa Yezebeli, akuno yakwinjila mwi'kulu.
34 Yakalya zindigwa baho. Hanyuma ho yakatya, “Ale, alu mubite mukawusole u muwili wa ayulya u mucekulu mulijitwe mukawuzice, hamba nhavivyo mwene yo mwana we cicekulu wo mutemi.”
35 Ninga wanhu wawo wono watumwa ho wakamuzice, wagana gasigaye go mafupa ge mawoko ne magulu, hamonga ne lipala hodu.
36 Nawo wali weza kumulonjela zizi Yehu, nayo Yehu yakawatya, “Zo zizi zono Muwaha Mulungu yazilonga vyono zowa, lono yazilonjela muli mwitumami wakwe Eliya, nha vyono, zimbwa zo zolowulya muwili wa Yezebeli mu yisi ya Yezeleli.”
37 Sunga ne mafupa gakwe geyagazwe, gagumilwe ako na ako kotya matope, sunga ne wanhu walece kugamanya watye, “Ayu Yezebeli.”


2 Watemi 10

Wo lulelo lwa Ahabu, wose wakuwulagwa

1 Nayo Ahabu, yali yena wana makumi mupunghati ku Samaliya. Yehu yakandika baluwa, yakawategulila wawaha wose we likaya, hamonga ne walezi we wana wa Ahabu. Ne baluwa yiyo yikandikwa zinghani azi:
2 “Anye yo nyenye mono muli wadimi we wana wo mutemi Ahabu, kanyhi mwina ne matuka go wulugu, ne zifwalasi, ne vitwalo vyose, hamonga ne makaya gono gena ne magomawakali gayo. Holeka ane nghumulombenyi, vyono kolo mukuyibocela baluwa yiyi du,
3 ale, muhagule mwana monga wo mutemi Ahabu, mono yena lilumbo yiswanu, muli wayagwe wose, mumunoze yawe mutemi. Kanyhi mumutazilize mu wulugu, muyilewele nyumba ya Ahabu.”

4 Nawo wanhu wa ku Samaliya wakenjilwa no woga wuwaha muno, sunga wakawa kutya, “Alu se waco, ho cosumila hayi kumwimila Yehu, hambi nha sunga no mutemi Yolamu no mutemi Ahaziya, nha si wasuma kumwimila yaye?”
5 Hodu, u mulindilizi muwaha we likulu, hamonga no mulindilizi muwaha we likaya, wakawa kwihanguliza hamonga ne walongola we wanhu ne walezi wose we wana, wakelumbaniza vyono, wamupitule Yehu baluwa yakwe. Holeka wakamwandicila wakatya, “Ase yo sese mono cili wetumami wako, nase ciwikanize kwituma nghani yo yose yono kosaka gwegwe. Ninga ase akuno hamba si cosuma yaye kumunoza yunji yawe mutemi. Langa ase cilindiye witume nha vyono gwegwe du kowona viswanu.”

6 Yehu nayo yakawandicila yakuwapitula yakatya, “Alu kumbe hono muli ku lubavu lwangu, kanyhi mwiwikanize kwituma nha vyono nolonga, ale nghumusacenyi mundetele mitwe ye wana wose wo mutemi Ahabu, monyagana ku Yezeleli mitondo mu mapango gaga.”

Ne wana wose wo mutemi Ahabu, vyono wali wali makumi mupunghati, wakamiswaga ne wawaha we likaya.
7 Nawo walongola we likaya lya Samaliya, wali weza kuyibocela baluwa yiyo, hodu ho baho baho, wakawebata iwana wose wo mutemi Ahabu, wakawadumula mitwe yawo, wakayipanga mu zinhoto, wakamutegulila Yehu ku Yezeleli.

8 Nayo Yehu yali yeza kulonjelwa nha vyono mitwe ye wana wose wa Ahabu, yakondya kuletwa, mwene yakalajiliza nha vyono, mitwe yiyo yose yilundwe malundo mejete, ha mulango we likaya, yikale baho milunde, sunga wele mitondo.
9 I mitondo yaco, Yehu yakawa kubita kwi'lango hakali mitondo, yakawa kuwalonjela wanhu wose wono wali wemile baho, yakawatya, “Ane yo nene mono namugaluka mutemi Yolamu, nghamuwulaga, nanye yo si mwetuma zizi. Ninga alu nani mono yawawulaje wanhu wawo wose.
10 Ninga nghani yiyi, yo yikulajila nha vyono zolofisilizwa zose, zono Muwaha Mulungu yazilonga, zono zolowejelela i wana wa Ahabu. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha kazinoza zizi, nha vyono yehuwiziza muli muwizi wakwe Eliya.”
11 Hanyuma Yehu yakawa kuwawulaga wanduguze wose wa Ahabu, ne wetumami wakwe, ne wendece wakwe, hamonga ne wakohani wose, hakawa hasina hamba monga mono yasigala.

I wanduguze wo mutemi Ahaziya, wakuwulagwa

12 Hodu, Yehu yakawa kuwuka ku Yezeleli, yakawuya ku Samaliya. Ninga lono yawuyaga, yali yeza kufika hanhu hono hakucemwa “Nghambi ye Wadimi.”
13 Baho, viketang'anila ne wanduze wanji wa malehemu, mutemi Ahaziya wa Yuda, nayo Yehu yakawawuza yakatya, “Nyenye wa nani?”

Nawo wakamutya, “Ase cili wanduguze wa Ahaziya, cikanjile ku Yezeleli, cikamulamuse Yezebeli u mugolece wo mutemi, hamonga ne wana wo mutemi.”
14 Hobaho Yehu yakawalajiliza wanhu wakwe, yakatya, “Ale, muwebataje womi!” Nawo wakawebata womi. Nayo yakawawulajila baho behi ne cilamba. Nawo wali waficile hamonga wanhu makumi mane na wejete liwalo lyawo. Na hasina hamba monga mono yapona.

Wanduguze wa Ahabu wose wono wasigaye, wakuwulagwa

15 Kanyhi Yehu yakawa kutuluka, na mumo mu nzila, viketang'anila na Yonadabu u mwana wa Lekabu, yakuza kumuhinjiliza. Yehu yakamulamusa, yakamutya, “Agwe na ane nghuwona nhumbula zetu zihwanile. Alu wule, kosuma kunhaza?” Nayo Yonadabu yakatya, “Heya nokutaza.”

Yehu yakamutya, “Ale mhelaje muwoko wako.” Hodu wakawa kwilamusa. Nayo Yehu yakawa kumupaciza mu mutuka wakwe,
16 akuno yakumutya, “Wuze wiwonelele go mweneco i nghango yo wilavye wangu kuli Muwaha Mulungu.” Hodu, wakawa kubitana wose mu mutuka, sunga ko ku Samaliya.
17 Wali weza kufika ku Samaliya, Yehu yakawawulaga wanduguze wose wa Ahabu, siyasigaza hamba monga. Na zizi zo Muwaha Mulungu yazilonjela muli Eliya, nha vyono zolokwijela.

I wahemba Baali wose, wakuwulagwa

18 Hanyuma Yehu yakawakonghola hamonga wanhu wa Samaliya, yakawalonjela yakutya, “Mutemi Ahabu, yahembaga mulungu Baali hadodo du, ninga ane nomuhemba Baali kuciza muno.
19 Holeka muwakongholelaje hamonga i wawizi wakwe wose, ne wahembi wose wono wamuhembaga Baali, hamonga ne wakohani wayo wose. Yunji yalece kuwa na cikojelwa ho yalece kuza. Hambi ane nha nghuhila kumulavizya Baali yikumbiko yiwaha muno. Na hono yunji yejelele yalece kuza, munhu yuyo kowulagwa.” Ninga Yehu yanoza cihalahala du, ho yawongomeze wose wono wamuhembaga Baali.
20 Hanyuma Yehu yakatya, “Aluno muliwikaje no kulyanza lizuwa lyo kulavizya likumbiko.” Ale, lizuwa likawa kuwagwa, no wanze wukawa kwanzwa.
21 Nayo mweneco Yehu yakatuma wanhu, wabite wakawalonjele wanhu wose mu yisi yose ya Isilayeli. Nawo wanhu wose wono wamuhembaga mulungu Baali, wakawa kuza, hali hasina hamba monga mono yadila. Ne wanhu wose wahemba Baali, wakenjila mu nyumba yo wuhembelo wa Baali, nyumba yikamema twaa hanhu hose.
22 Hodu, Yehu yakamulajiliza mukohani mulongola, u mulanjilizi we vivwalo vyo wukohani, yalete vivwalo vyo wukohani, yawapele i wahembi wavivwale. Nayo mulanjilizi yakavisolo vivwalo vivyo yakawapela.
23 Nawo wanhu wali weza kuvivwala vivwalo vivyo, ale, nayo Yehu hamonga na Yonadabu u mwana wa Lekabu, ho wakawa kwinjila mumo mu nyumba yo wuhembelo wa Baali, wakawa kuwalonjela wanhu wa mumo mugati, wakatya, “Ale, mwilangaje hono yo nyenye wose du mo wahembi wa Baali, na hawe hasina yunji wo wose mono kamuhembaga Muwaha Mulungu muli nyenye, mono nayo yawe kenjila mono nyumba yiyi.”
24 Hodu mwene Yehu hamonga na Yonadabu, wakawa kwinjila ho walavye likumbiko hamonga ne vilavywa vinyanywe kuli Baali. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, Yehu yali yakondya kuwawikaniza wanhu makumi munana hanze yo wuhembelo, yakawa yawaloloseza zono wonoza, yawatya, “Alu anye mulimo wenyu, nghusaka muwawulaje wanhu wawa wose, mulece kuleceleza yunji hamba monga yatize. Na hono yunji wa muli nyenye yamuponye yunji, ale yuyo kowulagwa yahalile ho munhu yuyo!”

25 Nayo Yehu lono yalavyaga vilavywa, hobaho yakawa kuwalonjela walindilizi hamonga ne wetumami wakwe, yakawatya, “Ale mwinjile muwawulaje wose, mulece kusigaza hamba monga yaye!” Nawo wetumami wakenjila lwa kamonga, akuno webate ne mimaje yawo mu mawoko, wakawawulaga wanhu wose, wakawa kuyigumilanga mivimba yawo kunze. Zizo zali kukondya, wakabita kwinjila mu nyumba ya Baali, mugati ye nyumba yiyo yo wuhembelo,
26 wakagalavyaniza kunze i masumbili go wuhembi ga mumo mu nyumba ya Baali, wakagakongoga mu moto.
27 Wakavitakamiza vyose, i masumbili hamonga sunga ne nyumba yeneco yo wuhembelo wa Baali, wakayinoza cikawa coloni sunga nalu.

28 Na mu nzila yiyo, Yehu yakawuwusiza lukulu wuhembi wa Baali mu Isilayeli.
29 Ninga langa yakawutyatya wubi wo mutemi Yelobowamu, wono yawasutizya wa Isilayeli, wubi wo kuhemba i vihwani vye zing'ombe ze zahabu, zono monga yali mbikwe ku Beteli, ne nzagwe nayo ku Dani.
30 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Yehu yakatya, “Alu vyono wakondya kwituma mu wana wa Ahabu zose zono nali kusaka witume, holeka nane nghukwihuwiza nha vyono, i wanago wowa watemi wa Isilayeli sunga ko ku lulelo lwe kane.”
31 Ninga Yehu siyasaka kulikamisa lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli kwe nhumbula yakwe yose. Langa mwene yakatyatya zi nzila zimbi za Yelobowamu mono yawasutizya Waisilayeli.

U wufwe wa Yehu

32 Na ku wulawilila baho, hodu Muwaha Mulungu yakanza kuwucepula wuwaha we yisi ya Isilayeli. U mutemi Hazayeli wa Siliya, yakayihoma yisi ya Isilayeli yose,
33 yakayisola yose ku lubavu lwa kucilima ye likolongo lya Yoludani, sunga ko ku lubavu lwa kutakama kwi 'kaya lya Aloweli, kwi'kolongo lya Alinoni, hamonga ne yisi ya Gileyadi na Bashani, hono wekalaga we milango ya Gadi na Lubeni hamonga na wa Manase wo lubavu lwa kucilima.

34 Ne zinghani zose zono mutemi Yehu yetuma, hamonga ne metumo gakwe ge vilungo, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.
35 Yehu yakafwa, yakazikwa mu Samaliya. Nayo Yehowahazi u mwanagwe, yakahala wutemi wakwe.
36 Yehu yakatema mu Isilayeli myaka makumi mejete na munana.


2 Watemi 11

Ataliya, u mutemi we cicekulu, wa Yuda

(2Zin 22:10–23:15)

1 Nayo Ataliya u nyina yo mutemi Ahaziya, yali yeza kumanya nha vyono mwanagwe Ahaziya kakondya kuwulagwa, hodu nayo ho baho baho du yakalajiliza nha vyono, i wana wose we citemi hamonga ne zimhuga zawo, wanojeye wawulagwe.
2 Langa Yowashi mwana monga du wa Ahaziya, yuyo du yo yapona. Ninga nayo yali yali behi no kuwulagwa, hamonga ne wayagwe, ninga u nyinehenga wakwe, mono yakucemwa Yehosheba yo yamuponya. Mwene Yehosheba yali mwana we cicekulu wo mutemi Yeholamu, kanyhi yali lumbulye lya Ahaziya. Yehosheba yamusola Yowashi hamonga no mukoci wakwe, yakawahundika mu cumba co kugona, mugati ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, holeka Ataliya si yasuma kumuwona ho yamuwulaje.
3 Yehosheba yakamuhundika Yowashi mumo mu Nyumba yo Mulungu myaka mutandatu mu vilo vyose vyo wutemi wa Ataliya.

4 Ninga mu mwaka wo mupunghati, Yehoyada mukohani, yakawalajiliza wetumami walongola, we walindilizi wo mutemi, hamonga ne walindilizi we likulu, yakawalonjela waze wewone nayo ku Nyumba yo Mulungu. Nayo yakalagana nawo, yakawelahiza kuli zono yasakaga yetume. Yakawalajila Yowashi u mwana wo mutemi Ahaziya,
5 hanyuma yakawalajiliza yakutya, “Alu, kolo mweza kuza ku mulimo mwi'zuwa lye Sabato, ale, nhondo mwigawaje hadatu. Lipuga lilongozi, lilindilize likulu,
6 lipuga lye kejete, lilindilize ha mulango wa Suli, ne lipuga lye kadatu, lilindilize ha mulango muyagwe, weme ku mugongo we walindilizi wono wowa wali baho lulo.
7 Nawo i walindilizi wanji, we vipuga vyejete, wono wowa wali wanojeye wawalecele wayawo mulimo na katali, mwi'zuwa lilyo lye Sabato, wene nawo, nhondo weme ha Nyumba yo Mulungu, wamulindilize mutemi.
8 Ninga lono mwimile mukumulindiliza mutemi Yowashi, mweze mwibate ne mimaje yenyu mu miwoko, kanyhi mweze mwina nayo du mutemi ho hose hono kowa kubita. No munhu wo wose mono kosejelela behi, yinojeye mumuwulaje.”

9 I wetumami wakawubocela wulajilize wa Yehoyada, wakawaleta wanhu wawo wose kuli mwene, awalya wono wali wanojeye welecele ne wayawo mulimo, hamonga na wose wono wawabocelaga wayawo mulimo, mwi'zuwa lilyo lye Sabato.
10 Hodu, Yehoyada yakawagawila wetumami wose, migoha hamonga ne zi ngula. Vitwalo vyono vyali vyo mutemi Dawudi, navyo vyekalaga du mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
11 Yakawatandalika wose ha mwande ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, akuno webate ne mimaje yawo, mu miwoko yawo ho wamudime mutemi, akuno wamupilime mbavu zose.
12 Hanyuma, Yehoyada yakawa kumuleta Yowashi kunze. Yakamuvwika mukumbo mu mutwe wakwe, yakamupela ne citabu cono cina wulajilize wo kuwulongozeza wutemi. Hanyuma, yakawa kumwitila mafuta, yakamunoza yawe mutemi. Nawo wanhu wose wakazoza muno, akuno wakutowa ne maganza, wakutya, “Mutemi yawumipe.”

13 Nayo Ataliya, yali yeza kulihulika lizozo lyono wazozaga walindilizi hamonga ne wanhu, hobaho yakasunha kubita kuko ku Nyumba yo Mulungu, kono wanhu wali wekongholeye.
14 Nayo yali yeza kufika kuko, yakamuwona mutemi mupya, yemile he sumbili ha mulango we Nyumba yo Mulungu, nha vyono wali winha moto. Akuno yapilimwe ne wetumami, hamonga ne watowa zindulele, sunga ne wanhu wose, akuno wakuzoza ne nyemo mbaha wakutowa ne zindulele. Hobaho Ataliya yakawa kuvidemulanga vivwalo vyakwe ko wusungu wuwaha muno, akuno yazozize yakutya, “Wugaluci! Wugaluci!”

15 Nayo Yehoyada, vyono si yali yendile Ataliya yawulajilwe mu ciwanza ce Nyumba yo Mulungu, holeka yakawalajiliza wasilikale yakawatya, “Ale, mumusole Ataliya, mumuwice haciligati yo wulindilizi, no munhu wo wose mono kojela kumutaziza, mumuwulagaje.”
16 Wasilikale wakamwibata, wakamutegula sunga ko kwi'kulu, wakamuwulajila ha mulango wono zinjililaga zifwalasi, lono zikubita ko mutemi.

Yehoyada yakucinoza wupya cilagane

17 U mukohani Yehoyada, yakanoza wupya cilagane muli Muwaha Mulungu no mutemi ne wanhu, ho wawe wose wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu. Kanyhi yakanoza cilagane muli mutemi ne wanhu.
18 Hanyuma, wanhu wakawa kubita mu Nyumba yo wuhembelo wa Baali, wakayimwagula, wakatulanga sunga ne makumbicilo gayo, hamonga ne vihwani vyose vye milungu yawo. Wakamuwulajila Matani u mukohani wa Baali, ha mwande ye makumbicilo.

Yehoyada, yakawika walindilizi ku Nyumba yo Mulungu, wono welecelaga wulindilizi wawo.
19 Na hanyuma yayo mwene, hamonga ne wetumami, hamonga ne walindilizi wo mutemi, ne walindilizi we likulu, wakawa kumusindiciza mutemi ku wulawa ha Nyumba yo Mulungu sunga ko kwi'kulu, akuno ne wanhu wose watyatizye mu nyuma. Yowashi yakenjilila ku mulango wo kwinjilila mutemi, yakekala mwi'goda lyo wutemi.
20 Ne wanhu wose wakamemywa na nyemo mbaha muno, ne likaya likawa litulile muno, hambi Ataliya nha yali yakondya kuwulagwa yali musina kanyhi mwi'kulu.

21 Yowashi yali yaficile myaka mupunghati lono yatemela mu Yuda.


2 Watemi 12

Yowashi mutemi wa Yuda

1 Na mu mwaka wo mupunghati wo wuteme wa Yehu, mutemi wa Isilayeli, nayo Yowashi mutemi wa Yuda ho yanziza kutema. Yowashi yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi mane. U nyina wa Yowashi yacemwaga Zibiya munya Belisheba.
2 Mwene Yowashi yetuma zono zikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu, mu mikalile yakwe yose, hambi Yehoyada mukohani, nha yo yamutongaga.
3 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, nayo si yawumwagula wukumbicilo we milungu yawo yo wulambi mu vigongo. Holeka wanhu wajendelela du kulavya makumbiko mumo, no kufusa wuvumba.

4 Mutemi Yowashi, yakawalajiliza wakohani, wavikamise vilavywa vyose vye zipesa, hamonga ne makumbiko, vyono vyalavywaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, kanyhi walanjilile no wityatye wo wulavye we makumbiko ge nhondo zose, hamonga ne zipesa zono wanhu walavyaga ko wuhile wawo weneco.
5 No mukohani wo wose, yawe kuzibocela zipesa, zono wakuleta i wanhu wono mwene mukohani yakuwetumamila. Ne zipesa zizo, zikawa zikwitumilwa mu mulimo wo kunozelezeza Nyumba yo Mulungu.

6 Ninga sunga yikapuluta myaka makumi mejete na midatu yo wutemi wa Yowashi, i wakohani wawo, wali wakali si wananoza co cose mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
7 Holeka yakamucema Yehoyada hamonga ne wakohani wanji, yakawawuza yakawatya, “Alu nhawulenyi si mwali kuyinozeleza Nyumba yo Mulungu? Hodu ku wulawa lulu, si mosuma kanyhi kuzikamisa zipesa zono mukubocela kuli wanhu, muzikombolaje, ho Nyumba yo Mulungu yisume kunozelezwa.”
8 Nawo wakohani wakavumila walece kubocela zipesa muli wanhu, hamba walece kunozeleza wumwaguce we Nyumba yo Mulungu.

9 Hanyuma Yehoyada yakasola lisanduku, yakalitobola lilanga mu cigubiko cayo, yakaliwika ku lubavu lo kulume lwe cikumbicilo, hono wanhu wakolelaga lono wakwinjila mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Nawo wakohani wono walindilizaga ha mulango, wakazitajila zipesa zose mwi'sanduku lilyo zono i wahembi waletaga.
10 Na mu mapango go gose lono zipesa zali zakondya kumema mumo mwi'sanduku, u mukohani muwaha, hamonga no mwandici wo mutemi, wazaga wawa kuzisola no kuziwala.
11 Nawo kolo wakondya kuziwala no kuzandika mu citabu liwalo lyayo, nhondo ho wawa kumupela u munhu mulanjilizi wo mulimo wo kunozeleza Nyumba yo Mulungu, nazo ziwa kwitumamilwa so kuwalipa misahala i waselemala ne wazenji,
12 hamonga ne wasesa mabwe. Na zinji, so wugula mbawo, hamonga ne mabwe gono getumamilwaga mumo mu wunozelezi we Nyumba yo Mulungu, kanyhi na ku so visakwa vinji vinji vyose.
13 Ninga hasina pesa zinji za muli zizo, zono zetumamilwa ku wugula vikombe vya fweza, hamba bakuli, hamba ndulele, hamba vitumamilwa vyo kuwicila zitala, hamba kunoza cinhu co cose ca fweza hamba zahabu.
14 Ninga pesa zose ziketumamilwa du so kuwalipa misahala i wetuma milimo, hamonga no kugula vinhu vyose vyono vyasakwaga mu mulimo wo kunozeleza Nyumba yo Mulungu.
15 Nawo i wanhu wono wali wapejigwe mulimo wo kuwulanjilila mulimo wuwo, wali wanhu wehuwilwa muno muno. Holeka hakawa hasina soko yo kuwasaka walavye liwalo lye zipesa zono wetumamiye na zono zikali zisigaye.
16 Langa zipesa zono zalavywaga so vilavywa vyo wusutye, hamonga ne vilavywa vyo wubi, pesa zizo si zatajilwaga mwi'sanduku yaye. Azo wapegwaga wakohani du ziwa zawo.

17 Mu mapango ganji, Hazayeli u mutemi wa Siliya, yayihoma Gati, yakayisola, na hanyuma kanyhi yakasaka yabite yakayihome Yelusalemi.
18 Ninga Yowashi mutemi wa Yuda, yakakonghola vilavywa vyose vyono vyasiwanulwa ne wasogwe nha cinji Yehoshafati, na Yeholamu, na Ahaziya, so Muwaha Mulungu. Mwene yakavihanza hamonga ne vilavywa vyakwe, hamonga ne zahabu yono yali yili mu Nyumba yo Mulungu ne zahabu ya mwi'kulu, yakawa kumupela vyose cipegwa u mutemi Hazayeli. Nayo mutemi Hazayeli, yakawa kuliwusa lipuga lye wasilikale wakwe mu Yelusalemi.

19 Na zose zono mutemi Yowashi yetuma, zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Yuda.

20 Ninga mutemi Yowashi, i wetumami wakwe wakamugaluka. Na muli wawo, i wejete wayawo, wono wakucemwa Yozakali u mwana wa Shimeyati, hamonga na Yehozabadi u mwana wa Shomeli, wawo wakamuwulajila Yowashi ku Milo, ha kaya yono yali yizenjigwe hanhu hono hali henuce, ku lubavu lwa kucilima ya Yelusalemi, mu nzila yono yibitile sunga Sila.
21 Yowashi yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye citemi, mu kaya ya Dawudi. Nayo Amaziya u mwanagwe, yakahala wutemi wakwe.


2 Watemi 13

Yehowahazi u mutemi wa Isilayeli

1 Na mu mwaka we makumi mejete ne'datu wo wutemi wa Yowashi u mwana wa Ahaziya, lono yali yatemile Yuda, nayo Yehowahazi u mwana wa Yehu, ho yanziza kutema mu Isilayeli. Nayo yakatema mu Samaliya myaka yikumi na mupunghati.
2 Ninga nayo yuyu Yehowahazi, yakehwana Yelobowamu mutemi mono yamulongolela. Nayo yaketuma zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu, yakawasutizya muno Waisilayeli, waketuma wubi. Nayo hamba si yazilecela lukulu zinzila zakwe zimbi yaye.
3 Na ku soko yiyo, Muwaha Mulungu yakawakolelwa likuwo Waisilayeli, yakawa kumukombola Hazayeli mutemi wa Siliya, hamonga na Benihadadi u mwanagwe, wawe kuwasuma Waisilayeli kenji na kenji.
4 Ninga hanyuma, Yehowahazi yakamulomba Muwaha Mulungu. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yaza wona vyono mutemi wa Siliya yawatendelaga vibi Waisilayeli no kuwewonela, hobaho yakamupitula Yehowahazi wulombe wakwe.
5 Muwaha Mulungu yakawaletela Waisilayeli mukombozi, yakawakomboza mu miwoko ye Wasiliya. Nhondo Waisilayeli wakawa kwikala wapolile cinya ha lulongozi.
6 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, si wazileka zimbi zono mutemi Yelobowamu yali yakondya kuwatonga Waisilayeli, wakajendelela du kuzituma, sunga ne cihwani co mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, cikawa vyo cikali cili ku Samaliya.

7 Wasilikale wa Yehowahazi wakafwa wose, hambi mutemi wa Siliya, nha yawawulaga wose wono wali wasigaye, yakawatindya, wakawa nha manghundi. Yehowahazi yakasigala ne wasilikale wapanda fwalasi waficile makumi mahano hodu, hamonga ne matuka go wulugu gali 'kumi, hamonga ne wasilikale wajenda kwa magulu nawo waficile elufu yikumi du hodu.

8 Na zose zono mutemi Yehowahazi yetuma, hamonga ne metumo gakwe mawaha, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.
9 Yehowahazi yakafwa, yakazikwa mu Samaliya. Nayo Yehowashi u mwanagwe, yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Yehowashi, mutemi wa Isilayeli

10 Na mu mwaka we makumi madatu na mupunghati wo wutemi wa Yowashi mutemi wa Yuda, Yehowashi u mwana wa Yehowahazi, ho yanziza nayo kuwa mutemi wa Isilayeli, yakatema mu Samaliya myaka yikumi na mutandatu.
11 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, nayo yaketuma zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu, yakatyatya musambo mubi wono yaleka mutemi Yelobowamu, mono yawasutizya i wa Isilayeli wetume wubi.
12 Na zose zono yetuma mutemi Yehowashi, hamonga ne milewo, vyono yamuhoma Amaziya u mutemi wa Yuda, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.
13 Yehowashi yakafwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi kuko ku Samaliya. U mwanagwe Yelobowamu we kejete, yakahala wutemi wakwe.

U wufwe wa Elisha

14 U muwizi Elisha, yakawa yatamwa muno wutamwa wono womutegula sunga ku wufwe. Na lono Elisha yali yagonile so mhungo, Yowashi u mutemi wa Yuda yakaza kumulanga. Yali yeza mwagana, yakamulilila muno yakutya, “Gwe baba! Gwe baba! Hambi nha wali wuli mudimi we vilungo muno we wa Isilayeli!”

15 Nayo Elisha yakamutya Yowashi, “Ale ta, wusole wupinde hamonga ne zisonga nyhefu.”
16 Hanyuma kanyhi Elisha yakamutya Yowashi, “Ale, wutawice songa ho wugume.” Nayo mutemi yakayitawika songa mu wupinde, nayo Elisha yakayijeleka miwoko yakwe mu miwoko yo mutemi.
17 Hanyuma muwizi Elisha yakamulonjela mutemi yalidigule lidilisha lyono lilanjile ku Siliya, yakamutya, “Ale lu wugume,” Mutemi yali yeza kuyilecela songa, hobaho nayo muwizi Elisha yakazoza yakutya, “Agwe Yowashi yo gwegwe songa yono Muwaha Mulungu koyitumamila no koyisuma Siliya. Nagwe kowahoma wanhu wa Siliya ku Afeki sunga wuwasume.”

18 Elisha yakamulonjela mutemi Yowashi yasole zisonga zinji yazigume hasi mu muhangha. Nayo mutemi yakaguma hasi mu muhangha kali kadatu, hodu yakawa kuleka.
19 Ne nghani yiyi, yikamukalaza Elisha muno muno, sunga yakawa kumulonjela mutemi yakatya, “Gwe mutemi yali yikunojeye wugume kali kahano hamba mutandatu, nagwe ho wali wusume kuwahoma wanhu, wa Siliya no kuwasumila lukulu, ninga aluno kowasuma kali kadatu hodu.”

20 Nayo Elisha yakafwa, wakamuzika. Nawo we vipuga vye Wamowabi, wakajendelela kuyisola yisi ya Isilayeli mwaka na mwaka.
21 Na mwi'zuwa linji lono wazikaga munhu yunji, wakawona cipuga cinji, hobaho wakawugumila muvimba wo munhu yuyo, mu citenjelo ca Elisha, wene wakawa kutiza. Nawo muvimba wo munhu yuyo, wali weza kupamiza du mafupa ga Elisha mumo mu citenjelo, hobaho u munhu yuyo yakawa kuzuka, yakema wima.

Wulugu we Waisilayeli ne Wasiliya

22 Nayo Hazayeli, mutemi wa Siliya, yakawapela Waisilayeli magazo mawaha muno muno mu cipango co wutemi wa Yehowahazi.
23 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakawa yali nheto no lusungu kuli Waisilayeli. Holeka hamba si yawaleceleza wongomezwe yaye. Langa mwene yawataza du ku soko ye cilagane cakwe kuli Abulahamu na Isaka, na Yakobo. Nayo hamba si yawasemwela lukulu wanhu wakwe.

24 Nayo Hazayeli u mutemi wa Siliya yali yeza kufwa, Benihadadi u mwanagwe, yakahala wutemi wakwe.
25 Ale, baho nhondo Yowashi u mutemi wa Yuda, ho yakawa kumusuma Benihadadi kali kadatu, yakagasola sunga ne makaya gose, gono gali gakondya kusolwa na Benihadadi, mu cipango co mutemi Yehowahazi u sogwe wa Yowashi.


2 Watemi 14

Amaziya u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 25:1-24)

1 Na mu mwaka we kejete wo wutemi wa Yehowashi mutemi wa Isilayeli, u mwana wa Yehowahazi, nayo Amaziya u mwana wa Yowashi ho yanziza kuwa mutemi wa Yuda,
2 lono yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na mihano. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi mejete ne 'cenda. U nyina wa Amaziya, yacemwaga Yehoyadini munya baho ha Yelusalemi.
3 Nayo Amaziya yaketuma zono zamwendazaga Muwaha Mulungu. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, si yatyatya zose zono kukuye Dawudi yetuma, langa mwene yakatyatya du nha vyono sogwe Yowashi yetuma.
4 Nayo hamba si yagamwagula makumbicilo ge milungu yawo, yo wulambi mu vigongo, holeka wanhu wakajendelela du kulavya makumbiko gawo mumo, hamonga no kufusa wuvumba.

5 Nawo wutemi wakwe wali weza kuwa watuga vilungo mu muwoko wakwe, hodu, yakawa kuwawulaga wetumami wose wono wamuwulaga sogwe u mutemi Yowashi.
6 Ninga i wana wawo si yawawulaga yaye, yakatyatya nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalajiliza mwi 'lajilizo lya Musa, nha vyono yatya, “I walezi walece kuwulagwa ku soko yo wubi wono wetumile wana wawo. Ne wana nawo walece kuwulagwa ku soko yo wubi wono wetumile i walezi wawo. Langa munhu wo wose yawulagwe ku soko yo wubi wono yetumile mweneco du.”

7 Amaziya yakawawulaga wasilikale elufu yikumi we Waedomu mwi'lolo lyo Mwino. Yakaliteka likaya lya Sela mu wulugu, yakalicemela litagwa lya Yoketeli, ne litagwa lilyo lyekala sunge nalu.

8 Hanyuma Amaziya, yakawa kumutumila wanhu Yehowashi u mutemi we Waisilayeli, wakamusagale ho wehome.
9 Ninga mutemi Yehowashi yakamupitula Amaziya yakamutya, “Mwi'zuwa linji, cibici co mufuku cono cali cili mu matunda ga Lebanoni, cawulonjela mubici wo muhagata cikatya, ‘Wumhelaje mwanago we cicekulu, yatolwe no mwanangu we cilume.’ Ninga lilimu likali, likakola baho likacibenanjiza cibici co mufuku.
10 Alu Amaziya, mwene wedunhumula, vyono wawona wawasuma Waedomu hongo. Ane nghuwona vyono ciba wulece kwikumya, wucehe du ha kaya yako. Alu 'gwe nhawule wukusaka wiyambuze bwete, sunga wuwaletele wanhu wako wongomele wuwaha?”

11 Ninga mwene Amaziya, hamba si yasaka yazihulicize zizo. Hodu, nayo mutemi Yehowashi yakawa kutuluka hamonga ne wanhu wakwe, yakamwagana Amaziya ha Beti Shemeshi ya Yuda, yakamuhoma.
12 Wasilikale wose wa Amaziya wakasumwa wakatiziza ku zikaya zawo.
13 Mutemi Yehowashi wa Isilayeli, yakamusola mutemi Amaziya wa Yuda, yakenjila mu Yelusalemi, yakalimwagulanga lijinji lye likaya ku wulawilila ku Efulayimu, sunga ko ku mulango wa ha cipeto, wugazi wa kotya mita magana mejete nhavi.
14 Yakazisola zahabu zose, ne zifweza zono yasuma kuziwona, yakavisola ne vitumamilwa vyose vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, ne viwutwa vyose vya mwi'kulu, yakavitegula vyose ku Samaliya, hamonga ne wanhu wose wono yawateka.

15 Ne zinghani zose zono yetuma Yehowashi, hamonga ne vilungo vyakwe vyo wihomi, kuli mutemi Amaziya, mutemi wa Yuda, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.
16 Yehowashi yakafwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi mu Samaliya. Nayo Yelobowamu u mwanagwe, yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Wufwe wa Amaziya, mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 25:25-28)

17 Nayo Amaziya mutemi wa Yuda, yekala myaka yikumi na mihano ku wulawa ha wufwe wa Yehowashi mutemi wa Isilayeli.
18 Ne zinghani zose zono Amaziya yetuma, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Yuda.

19 Hakawa howuli wuhile wubi mu Yelusalemi, wamuwulaje Amaziya, ninga mwene yakatiziza ku Lakishi, hamba nhavivyo, i walugu wakwe wakamutyatya kuko, wakamuwulaga.
20 Muwili wakwe wukadinghulilwa mucanyha ye fwalasi, wukategulwa ku Yelusalemi, wukazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi mu kaya ya Dawudi.
21 Nawo wanhu wakawa kumutemya Azaliya u mwanagwe, mono yali yaficile myaka yikumi na mutandatu, yakawa mutemi, yakahalila ho sogwe Amaziya.
22 Nayo Azaliya, lono sogwe yali yakondya kufwa, mwene yakayihoma no kuyisuma kanyhi Elati no kuyizenga kanyhi no kuyigomola ku Yuda.

Yelobowamu we kejete, u mutemi wa Isilayeli

23 Na mu mwaka we yikumi ne 'hano wo mutemi Amaziya wa Yuda u mwana wa Yowashi, nayo Yelobowamu we kejete, u mwana wa Yehowashi ho yatemela mu Isilayeli, yakatema mu Samaliya myaka makumi mane na wumonga.
24 Ninga nayo Yelobowamu we kejete, yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, yakatyatya zimbi zose zono yetuma Yelobowamu mwana wa Nebati mono yali mutemi wa Isilayeli, mono yawanoza Waisilayeli wetume wubi.
25 Hamonga na zizo, yakayisuma kanyhi yisi yose yono yali ye Waisilayeli, yakayisola, ku wulawilila Hamati Pasi, ku lubavu lwa kusumuka, sunga ko kwi'lenga lyo Mwino, ku lubavu lwa kutakama. Ne nghani yiyi yo Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we Waisilayeli yali yakondya kuyihuwiza muli mwitumami wakwe u muwizi Yona, u mwana wa Amitayi kuko ku Gati Hefeli.

26 Muwaha Mulungu yakagawona magazo gono wagazikaga Waisilayeli, na vyono hakawa hasina hamba munhu wo kuwataza.
27 Vyono wali wo si wuhile wo Muwaha Mulungu, wo kuwaleka wongomelele lukulu cibitilila. Holeka yakawaponyezya muli mutemi Yelobowamu we kejete.

28 Ne zinghani zose zono Yelobowamu we kejete yetuma, hamonga ne citung'ati cakwe ku milewo, hamonga na vyono yazisola Damesiki na Hamati, no kuwagomolela kanyhi Waisilayeli, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.
29 Yelobowamu we kejete, yakafwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi. Nayo Zekaliya u mwanagwe yakahala wutemi wakwe.


2 Watemi 15

Azaliya, mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 26:1-23)

1 Na mu mwaka we makumi mejete na mupunghati wo wutemi wa Yelobowamu we kejete, u mutemi wa Isilayeli, nayo Azaliya u mwana wa Amaziya, ho yanziza kuwa mutemi wa Yuda.
2 Yakatema lono yali yaficile myaka yikumi na mutandatu. Yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi mahano na myejete. Ne litagwa lyo nyina yacemwaga Yekoliya munya ku Yelusalemi.
3 Nayo yaketuma ziswanu zono zikumwendeza Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono yetuma u sogwe Amaziya.
4 Ninga hamba nhavivyo nayo si yahamwagulanga hanhu ho wukumbicilo we milungu yawo mu vigongo. Holeka wanhu wakajendelela kulavya makumbiko mumo, hamonga no kufusa wuvumba.
5 Na holeka Muwaha Mulungu yakamutowa Azaliya ko wutamwa wo wukoma, yakawa nawo sunga yakafwa. Yakawa yekaye du mu kaya yakwe, yakayileka sunga ne milimo yakwe. No mwanagwe mono yakucemwa Yotamu, yo yakawa yayilanjiliye yisi.

6 Ne zinghani zose zono yetuma Azaliya, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Yuda.
7 Azaliya yakafwa, yakazikwa mu ciwanza ce vitenjelo vye watemi, mu kaya ya Dawudi. U mwanagwe Yotamu yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Zekaliya, mutemi we Waisilayeli

8 Na mu mwaka we makumi madatu na munana wo wutemi wa Azaliya mutemi wa Yuda, nayo Zekaliya u mwana wa Yelobowamu we kejete, ho yanziza kutema mu Isilayeli, nayo yakatema mu Samaliya myezi mutandatu du.
9 Ninga nayo yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu nha vyono mutemi mono yamulecala. Yakatyatya luzu lubi lo mutemi Yelobowamu u mwana wa Nebati mono yawanoza Waisilayeli wetume wubi.
10 Nayo Shalumu u mwana wa Yabeshi, yakawa no wuhile wubi kuli mutemi Zekaliya, holeka yakamuwulajila ku Ibileyamu, mwene yakawusola wutemi.

11 Ne zinghani zose zono yetuma mutemi Zekaliya, zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.

12 Hobaho wihuwizo wukaficilicila wono Muwaha Mulungu yawihuwiza kuli mutemi Yehu, wono yatya, “I wanago wojendelela kuwa watemi mu Isilayeli, sunga ko ku lulelo lwe kane.”

Shalumu, mutemi wa Isilayeli

13 Na mu mwaka we makumi madatu ne 'cenda, wo wutemi wa Azaliya u mutemi wa Yuda, nayo Shalumu u mwana wa Yabeshi, ho yanziza kutema mu Isilayeli, yakatema mu Samaliya mwezi monga du.

14 Hobaho Menahemu u mwana wa Gadi yakatuluka ku wulawa ku Tiliza, yakabita ku Samaliya, yakamuwulaga Shalumu, yakawusola mweneco wutemi.
15 Ne zinghani zose zono Shalumu yetuma, hamonga na zo wuhile wakwe wubi, vyose vyandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.
16 Nayo Menahemu lono yali yali mu nzila yakubita ku Samaliya, yakalihoma no kulyongomezeza lukulu likaya lya Tapuwa, ne wanhu wose wono wekalaga mumo, hamonga ne mipaka yalyo yose, yakawademulanga ne wacekulu wose wono wali wena mida, vyono likaya likalema kwilavya kuli mwene.

Menahemu, mutemi wa Isilayeli

17 Na mu mwaka we makumi madatu ne 'cenda wo wutemi wa Azaliya u mutemi wa Yuda, nayo Menahemu u mwana wa Gadi, ho yanziza kutema mu Isilayeli, nayo yakatema mu Samaliya myaka yikumi limonga du.
18 Ninga nayo yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, yatyatya musambo mubi wa Yelobowamu u mwana wa Nebati, mono yawanoza Waisilayeli wetume wubi sunga ko ku wufwe wakwe.
19 Nayo Tigilati Pilesa, u mutemi wa Ashuli, yakenjila kwa vilungo mu Isilayeli, Menahemu yakamupela kilo makumi madatu za fweza, ho Tigilati Pilesa yalumbe kumutaza mu wudime we yisi yakwe.
20 Ne zipesa zizo zono yamupela Tigilati Pilesa, mwene Menahemu, yawadindimiza i wagoli wose wa mu yisi, wahangulize, munhu yalavye fweza makumi mahano. Nayo Tigilati Pilesa, yali yeza kuzibocela zipesa zizo, hodu yakawa kuwuya ku yisi yakwe.

21 Ne zinghani zono yetuma Menahemu, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.
22 Menahemu yakafwa, yakazikwa. Nayo Pekahiya u mwanagwe, yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Pekahiya, mutemi wa Isilayeli

23 Na mu mwaka we likumi ne 'hano wo wutemi wa Azaliya mutemi wa Yuda, nayo Pekahiya u mwana wa Menahemu, ho yanziza kutema mu Isilayeli. Yakatema mu Samaliya myaka myejete du.
24 Ninga nayo nhavivyo yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, yakatyatya musambo mubi wo mutemi Yelobowamu u mwana wa Nebati, mono yawanoza Waisilayeli wetume wubi.
25 Hakawa no mwitumami yunji wa Pekahiya, mono yali yo citung'ati wa Pekahiya we milewo, mono yacemwaga Peka, u mwana wa Lemaliya. Yuyo hamonga ne wanhu wanji waficile makumi mahano wanya Gileyadi, wakawa no wuhile wubi kuli Pekahiya, wakamuwulajila mwigomawakali lya mugati ye likulu, kuko ku Samaliya. Mwene Peka yakahala wutemi wakwe.

26 Ne zinghani zose zono yetuma Pekahiya, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.

Peka mutemi wa Isilayeli

27 Na mu mwaka we makumi mahano na myejete yo wutemi wa Azaliya u mutemi wa Yuda, nayo Peka u mwana wa Lemaliya, ho yanziza kutema mu Isilayeli. Nayo yakatema mu Samaliya myaka makumi mejete hodu.
28 Ninga nayo nhavivyo, yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, yakatyatya musambo mubi wo mutemi Yelobowamu u mwana wa Nebati, mono yawanoza Waisilayeli wetume wubi.

29 Na mwi'pango lyono Peka yali yatemile, lyo lipango lilyo, nayo Tigilati Pilesa mutemi wa Ashuli, lo yagasolela makaya ga lyoni, na Abeli-beti na Maaka, na Yanowa, na Kedeshi na Hazoli, hamonga ne zisi za Gileyadi, na Galilaya, na Nafutali. Yakawasola mateka ne wanhu wose yakawategula ku yisi ya Ashuli.

30 Na mu mwaka we makumi mejete, wo wutemi wa Yotamu u mwana wa Uziya, u mutemi wa Yuda, Hosheya, u mwana wa Ela, yakawa no wubi kuli mutemi Peka, yakamuwulaga. Mwene Hosheya yakasola wutemi wakwe.
31 Ne zinghani zose zono yetuma Peka, zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.

Yotamu, mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 27:1-9)

32 Na mu mwaka we kejete wo wutemi wa Peka, u mwana wa Lemaliya, mutemi wa Isilayeli, nayo Yotamu u mwana wa Uziya, ho yanziza kutema mu Yuda.
33 Yakanza kutema lono yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na mihano. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi, myaka yikumi na mutandatu du. Ne litagwa lyo nyina, yacemwaga Yelusha, u mwana we cicekulu wa Zadoki.
34 Ninga mwene yakatyatya musambo muswanu wo sogwe Uziya. Yotamu yaketuma ziswanu zono zamwendezaga Muwaha Mulungu.
35 Ninga nayo hamba si yahamwagula yaye hanhu ho wukumbicilo we milungu yawo yo wulambi mu vigongo, holeka wanhu wakajendelela kulayva makumbiko gawo du, hamonga no kufusa wuvumba mumo. Nayo yuyu Yotamu, yo yalizenga lilango lyo lubavu lwa kusumuka ye Nyumba yo Mulungu.

36 Ne zinghani zose zono yetuma Yotamu, zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Yuda.
37 Na lono yali yali mu wutemi, baho nayo Muwaha Mulungu ho yawategulila mutemi Lezini wa Siliya, no mutemi Peka wa Isilayeli, wakayihome Yuda.
38 Yotamu yakafwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi mu kaya ya Dawudi. No mwanagwe mono yakucemwa Ahazi, yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.


2 Watemi 16

Ahazi mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 28:1-27)

1 Na mu mwaka we likumi na mupunghati wo wutemi wa mutemi wa Isilayeli, u mwana wa Lemaliya, nayo Ahazi u mwana wa Yotamu, ho yanziza kuwa mutemi wa Yuda,
2 lono yali yaficiye myaka makumi mejete. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka yikumi na mutandatu. Ninga nayo hamba si yatyatya musambo muswanu wo sogwe mutemi Dawudi yaye, mwene yaketuma zinghani zimbi, zono si zamwendeza Muwaha Mulungu,
3 yakatyatya musambo we watemi wa Isilayeli. Sunga yakasuma kumulavya u mwanagwe likumbiko lye cilavywa cinyanywe kuli vihwani vye milungu. Yakatyatya mawifyo ge makungugo, wono Muwaha Mulungu yawawinga mu yisi yono Waisilayeli wenjilaga.
4 Ahazi yakajendela kulavya makumbiko, hamonga no kufusa wuvumba mu wukumbicilo mu vigongo na mu matunda, na mu mibeho ye mibici yose miwaha.

5 U mutemi Lezini wa Siliya, hamonga na Peka u mutemi wa Isilayeli, wakabita ku Yelusalemi ho wakayihome, ninga si wamusuma Ahazi yaye.
6 (Na mu vilo vivyo mutemi wa Edomu yakalisola likaya lya Elati, yakawawinga i Wayuda wose wono wekalaga mumo. Ne Waedomu wo wakenjila no kwikala mumo mu Elati sunga nalu.)
7 Nayo Ahazi, yakatuma wanhu kuli Tigilati Pilesa u mutemi wa Ashuli, wabite wakamulonjele nhavi, “Ane nili mwitumami wako, kanyhi nili munhu wako. Holeka nghukunena wuze wunhaze kanyhi, wumhonye mu miwoko yo mutemi wa Siliya hamonga no mutemi wa Isilayeli, wono wakuhoma.”
8 Ahazi yakasola fweza, hamonga ne zahabu yono yali mbikwe mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne viwutwa vya mu nyumba yo mutemi, yakamutegulila cipegwa mutemi Tigilati Pilesa.
9 Nayo mutemi Tigilati Pilesa, baho baho du yakawa kutuluka hamonga ne lipuga lyakwe lye Wasilikale wo wulugu, yakabita yakayihoma Damesiki, yakayisola, yakamuwulaga no mutemi Lezini. Yakawakunza wanhu wose, yakawategula ku Kili.

10 Nayo mutemi Ahazi lono yabitaga ku Damesiki yakamuhinjilize Tigilati Pilesa, u mutemi wa Ashuli, yakawona kuko ku Damesiki cikumbicilo. Hodu mutemi Ahazi yakamutegulila mukohani Uliya, i hwani yayo ye cikumbicilo, hamonga ne lilumbo lyaco.
11 Nayo mukohani Uliya, yakawa kuzenga cikumbicilo cono cihwanile cikumbicilo cico ca ku Damesiki, yakamalila kucizenga lono mutemi Ahazi yali yakali nawuya ku wulawa ku Damesiki.
12 Nayo Ahazi yali yeza kuwuya ku wulawa ku Damesiki, yakagana vyono cikumbicilo cakondya kuzengwa.
13 Holeka hobaho du yakawa kwanza kulavya mu canyha ye cikumbicilo makumbiko ge cilavywa cinyanywe, na vyo wusaje, yakajida ne cilavywa ce divayi, yakayinyunyiza ne sakami ye cilavywa co mulamu mu canyha ye cikumbicilo.
14 Ne cikumbicilo ce shaba, cono cali cisiwanulwe ku so Muwaha Mulungu, cali cili haciligati ye cikumbicilo cico cipya, ne Nyumba yo Mulungu. Ninga mwene Ahazi yakaciwusa baho haciligati yakaciwika ku lubavu lwa kusumuka ye cikumbicilo cakwe cipya.
15 Hanyuma yakawa kumulonjela Uliya yakamutya, “Alu, mwikumbicilo lyangu lili liwaha, mumo mo weze kulavizya vilavywa vinyanywe vyose vya hakali mitondo, hamonga ne vilavywa vyo wusaje vye cinyamihe, kanyhi hamonga ne vilavywa vinyanywe, ne vilavywa vyo wusaje vyo mutemi, hamonga na vye wanhu, na vye vilavywa vye divayi vye wanhu. Kanyhi, weze kunyunyiza mucanyha ye cikumbicilo, i sakami ye mitugo yose yono yowa kulavywa so makumbiko. Langa cikumbicilo ce shaba, wulece kucitumamila, hambi nha nowa kucitumamila nene no mweneco du, so kulondola wutonji kulawa kuli Muwaha Mulungu.”
16 Nayo Uliya mukohani, yaketuma nha vyono mutemi Ahazi yamulajiliza.

17 U mutemi Ahazi, yakadumula mabici ge shaba go wudinghulilo, gono getumamilwaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakagawusa ne mabeseni gono gali majelece mucanyha yago. Kanyhi yakalitimya hasi ne litanji lye shaba lye malenga, lyono lyali yijelece mucanyha ye vihwani vye zing'ombe yikumi na nyejete ze shaba, yakaliwika mucanyha ye cibalangu ce mabwe.
18 Kanyhi yakahawusa mu Nyumba yo Mulugu i hanhu ho kujeleka ligoda lyo wutemi, yakawudinda no mulango wono mutemi yenjililaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, ati ho yamwendeze du Tigilati Pilesa mutemi wa Ashuli.

19 Ne zinghani zono yetuma mutemi Ahazi, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi, wa Yuda.
20 Ahazi yakafwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi mu kaya ya Dawudi. Nayo Hezekiya u mwanagwe yakahala wutemi wakwe.


2 Watemi 17

Hosheya, mutemi wa Isilayeli

1 Na mu mwaka we likumi na myejete wo wutemi wa Ahazi, mutemi wa Yuda, nayo Hosheya u mwana wa Ela, ho yanziza kutema mu Isilayeli, nayo yakatema mu Samaliya myaka yicenda du.
2 Ninga nayo yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, ninga mwene si yetuma wubi, nha vyono wetuma i watemi wose wa Isilayeli wono wamulongolela kutema.
3 U mutemi Shalamanesa wa Ashuli, yakehoma na Hosheya. Hosheya yakasumwa na Shalamanesa, yakawa yakulavya kodi kuli Shalamanesa mu myaka yose.
4 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, mu mwaka wunji, nhondo, Hosheya yakatuma wanhu kuli mutemi wa Misili yakulomba wutazi, nhondo yakawa kuleka no kulipa kodi kuli Shalamanesa mutemi wa Ashuli. Ninga nayo mutemi Shalamanesa yali yeza kuzimanya zizo, hobaho yakawa kumwibata Hosheya, yakawa kumufunga mu jela.

U wugwe wa Samaliya

5 Hanyuma Shalamanesa yakayihoma yisi yose ya Isilayeli, yakayipilima Samaliya, yakayihoma myaka midatu. Na mu mwaka we kadatu wo wupilimwe wa Samaliya,
6 mu mwaka we licenda wo wutemi wa Hosheya, nayo mutemi wa Ashuli yakaza, yakayihoma Samaliya yakayisola. Yakawahamya ne Waisilayeli, yakawategula ku Ashuli wawopwe. Yakawawika wanji wayawo wekale mwi'kaya lya Hala, na wanji yakawawika behi ye likolongo lya Haboli mu yisi ya Gozani. Na wanji kanyhi yakawawika mu makaya ga Mediya.

7 Samaliya yikagwa, vyono Waisilayeli wetuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo, mono yawaponya kuli mutemi wa Misili Falawo, yakawa kuwawusa mu yisi ya Misili. Ninga wene 'lu, wakahemba milungu yinji yo wulambi,
8 wakatyatya metumo mabi ge wanhu wono Muwaha Mulungu yawawinga, wene i wanhu wakwe ho wekale mumo. Kanyhi wakatyatya ne myoto mibi yono wayileta watemi wa Isilayeli.
9 Waisilayeli waketuma zinghani zono Muwaha Mulungu si yali yawakomboye wetume. Hambi wene nha wakazenga wukumbicilo we milungu yawo mu vigonge, hanhu hose, mu makaya gawo gose, mu makaya mawaha ne madodo.
10 Wakazenga masumbili ge mabwe, hamonga ne vihwani vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela mu matunda gose, sunga na hasi ye mibeho ye mibici miwaha.
11 Wakajendelela kufusa wuvumba mu makumbicilo gose mu vigongo, wakawa kutyatya myoto mibi, ye wanhu wono Muwaha Mulungu yawawinga mu yisi. Holeka wakamukoza Muwaha Mulungu likuwo, ku soko ye metumo gawo mabi.
12 Kanyhi wakalibeza ne lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu lyo wutya, mulece kuvihemba vihwani vye milungu yo wulambi.

13 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yali yakondya kuwasugula Isilayeli na Yuda, mu nzila ye wanhu wono yawatumaga, hamonga ne wawizi, yakuwalonjela yakutya, “Mupituce muzilece zinzila zenyu zimbi, mwibate wuhile wangu, ne mejizo gangu gono gali mu wulajilize, wono nawalajiliza walezi wenyu, nghamulonjelelenyi nanye mu nzila ye wetumami wangu wawizi.”
14 Ninga wene hamba si wahulika yaye, wakawa wasina cihuliko nhe walezi wawo, wono wali si wakumwihuwila Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo.
15 Wakalemela lukulu kugebata mejizo gakwe, hamba kucikamisa cilagane cono Muwaha Mulungu yacinoza ne walezi wawo, hamba kucelelela bwete, ze suguzi yono Muwaha Mulungu yawasugula. Wene wakahemba vihwani vye milungu yo wulambi, vyono visina cambuzi, wakahodanjika, wakatyatya myoto ye makungugo wono wali wawapilime, wakalibedegala lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulugu lyono yatya, “Mulecele lukulu kuwatyatya.”
16 Wakagabena malajilizo gose go Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo, wakenozeza vihwani vya ndama nyejete nyenjedulwe, wakazihemba. Wakenozeza ne cihwani co mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, wakahemba zinyelezi, wakamwitumamila mulungu Baali.
17 Wakawalavya makumbiko i wana wawo we cilume na we cicekulu cilavywa cinyanywe, kuli milungu yo wulambi, kanyhi wakatowa visangu, waketuma zo wuhawi, wakelavya ku wituma zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu. Na ku soko yiyo, wakamukoza likuwo Mulungu.
18 Muwaha Mulungu yakawakolelwa likuwo muno muno Waisilayeli, yakawawusa hali mwene, yakawasigaza wo mulango wa Yuda du wali wene.

19 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, nawo wawo wo mulango wa Yuda, si wagebata yaye malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo, wakatyatya du myoto mibi yono Waisilayeli wali wakondya kuyisolela.
20 Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kuwaleka Waisilayeli, yakawalipiciza, yakawaleka wasolwe ne walugu wono wowatajila mu magayo, na hanyuma yakawa kuwawusiza lukulu ha meso gakwe.

21 Muwaha Mulungu yali yeza kukondya kuwatemanula Waisilayeli na Yuda, hodu Waisilayeli wakawa kumunoza Yelobowamu u mwana wa Nebati, yawe mutemi wawo. Nayo Yelobowamu yakawanoza wamulece Muwaha Mulungu, yakawenjiza mu wubi muno muno.
22 Nawo Waisilayeli wakatyatya zinghani za Yelobowamu, wakajendelela nazo wubi zose zono yetumaga.
23 Na kundumo ya zose, Muwaha Mulungu nhondo, yakawa kuwawusiza lukulu ha meso gakwe, nha vyono yali yakondya kuwasugula, mu nzila ye wawizi, iwetumami wakwe, na vyono si koleka kwituma zizo. Holeka i wanhu wa Isilayeli wakasolwa wakawahamizya ku Ashuli, kono wakali wali kuko sunga nalu.

Waashuli wakwikala mu yisi ya Isilayeli

24 U mutemi wa Ashuli, yakawasola wanhu ku wulawa mu makaya ga Babiloni na Kuti, na Iva, na Hamati, na Sefalivayimu, yakawenjiza mu makaya ga Samaliya wekale mumo, wagahalile makaya ge Waisilayeli wono yawahamizya ku Ashuli. Nawo wanhu wawo, wakagasola makaya gago ga Samaliya, gakawa gawo weneco, wakekala mumo.
25 Ninga i wanhu wawo nawo wali weza kwinjila no kwikala mumo mu Samaliya, hamba si wasuma kumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu, holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakawatumila simba, zikawawulaga wayawo wanji.
26 Nayo mutemi wa Ashuli, yakalonjelwa zinghani zizo, nha vyono i wanhu wono wawategula ho wakekale mu makaya ga Samaliya, si wamanyile zo wulajilize wo Mulungu we yisi yiyo, holeka yo soko yo yilecile Mulungu yuyo, kawatumila simba zono zali kuwawulaga.
27 Mutemi nayo yakalajiliza yakatya, “Alu hono yili nhavivyo, ale mumutegulaje yumonga wa muli wakohani, wono cawaleta bahano ku wulawa kuko, yabite yakekale kuko, ho yawejize wanhu wawo lilajilizo lyo Mulungu we yisi yiyo.”
28 Holeka mukohani monga we Ciisilayeli mono yahamywa ku wulawa kuko ku Samaliya, yakawa kubita, yakekala ku Beteli, yakawa kuwejiza wanhu nha vyono yinojeye wamuhembe Muwaha Mulungu.

29 Ninga i wanhu wawo wono wategulwa wekale mu Samaliya, vyo wakajendelela du kwiseseza vihwnai vye milungu yawo, no kuviwika mu vigongo, mu wukumbicilo wono Waisilayeli wawuzenga. Mhuga zose, mu makaya gose, wanhu no kwawo, wose wakeseseza vihwani vye milungu yawo yo wulambi mumo mu makaya gono wekalaga.
30 I wanhu wono walawa ku Babiloni, wakenozeza vihwani vyo mulungu Sukoti-benoti, nawo wono walawa ku Kuti, vihwani vya Nelegali. Nawo wono walawa ku Hamati, vihwani vya Ashima,
31 ne wanhu wono walawa ku Iva, vihwani vya Nibuhazi na Talitaki. Ne wanhu wono walawa ku Safalivayimu wakawa kuwalavya i wana wawo makumbiko manyanywe, kuli milungu yawo yo wulambi, Adelamaleke na Anameleke.
32 I wanhu wawa ninga kunji, wakawa wamogopile no Muwaha Mulungu. Na ku soko yiyo, wakawahagula wanji wa muli wene, wetume mulimo wo wukohani, mu vigongo, mu wukumbicilo we milungu yawo, yo wulambi, hamonga no mulimo wo kulavya makumbiko gawo.
33 Holeka wakawa wakumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu hamonga no kuyihemba milungu yawo yo wulambi, wakutyatya myoto ye zisi zawo kono walawilila.

34 Nawo wakali vyo wajendeleye du ne myoto yawo yiyo ye katali sunga nalu. Si wakumuhemba Muwaha Mulungu, hamba kwibata wulajilize, hamonga ne malajilizo gono Muwaha Mulungu yawapela i wana wa Yakobo, mono yakacemelwa litagwa lya Isilayeli.
35 Wawo Waisilayeli wono Muwaha Mulungu yanoza cilagane nawo, wono Muwaha Mulungu yakawalajiliza yakutya, “Mulecele lukulu kuyihemba milungu yinji, mulece kuyifugamila hamba kuyitumamila, hamba kuyilavizya makumbiko.
36 Langa muhulikaje nene du no Muwaha Mulungu, mono namuwusenyi mu yisi ya Misili kwe vilungo viwaha, hamonga no wusume. Holeka mufugamile, no kundavizya makumbiko nene du.
37 Kanyhi munojeye muwibate wulajile wangu, no kugakamisa malajilizo gangu gono nghamwandicilenyi. Holeka mulece kuyityatya milungu yinji.
38 Kanyhi mulece kucisemwa i cilagane cangu cono nacinoza nanye.
39 Mweze muli cihuliko kuli'ne no Muwaha Mulungu u Mulungu wenyu, nane ho nomuponyenyi mu walugu wenyu.”
40 Ninga wanhu wawo, hamba si wahulika yaye, wene vyo wakajendelela du kuyityatya myoto yawo mibi.

41 Holeka wanhu wawo hamba wawe wamogopaga Muwaha Mulungu, ninga vyo wali wakali wajendeleye du no kuhemba vihwani vye milungu yawo. Co cikuleka sunga ne wana wawo, wakali wakwituma nhavivyo sunga ne nalu.


2 Watemi 18

Hezekiya, mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 29:1-2; 31:1)

1 Na mu mwaka we kadatu, wo wutemi wa Hosheya u mwana wa Ela, mutemi wa Isilayeli, nayo Hezekiya u mwana wa Ahazi, ho yatemela mu Yuda,
2 lono yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na mihano. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi, myaka makumi mejete ne cenda. U nyina wa Hezekiya, yacemwaga Abija, u mwana we cicekulu wa Zekaliya.
3 Mwene Hezekiya, yakatyatya musambo muswanu wo mulezi wakwe mutemi Dawudi, yaketuma zono zamwendeza Muwaha Mulungu.
4 Hambi nha yahamwagulanga hanhu hose mu vigongo ha wukumbicilo we milungu yawo. Yakagatulanga masumbili ge mabwe, yakavidumulanga vihwani vyose vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela. Yakalitulanga ne lizoka lye Shaba, lyono Musa yalinoza, lizoka lyono walicemaga Nehusitani. Hambi nha sunga na mu vilo vivyo i wanhu wa Isilayeli, wali wakali wakulipela cogopo no kulifusiza wuvumba.
5 Hezekiya yakawa yamwitumbiye muno muno Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli. Sunga ne wanhu wa Yuda, wali wakali si wanamutuga mutemi mono yali yehwanile no mutemi Hezekiya ku wulawa lono Hezekiya, yali yakali si yanatema, hamba si wamutuga yunji, lono Hezekiya yali yakondya kuwuka mu wutemi.
6 Hambi Hezekiya nha yali mwihuwilwa muno kuli Muwaha Mulungu, hasina hamba lizuwa linji lyono Hezekiya yamubeza Muwaha Mulungu. Ninga mwene, yali yagebate muno i malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu gono yamupela Musa.
7 Holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakawa yali hamonga nayo, nayo yakacila muno kumoteswa muli milimo yakwe yose. Yakamubeza mutemi wa Ashuli, hamba siyamwitumamila yaye.
8 Yakawahoma Wafilisiti, yakawusola no wikalo wawo, ku wulawa mu citumbi cidodo, sunga mo mu makaya mawaha, na Gaza, hamonga ne yisi yose yono yipilime.

9 Na mu mwaka we kane wo wutemi wa Hezekiya, nawo wali wo mwaka wo mupunghati, wo wutemi wa Hosheya mutemi wa Isilayeli, yakawa kuza Shalimanesa mutemi wa Ashuli, yakawa kuyihoma Isilayeli, yakayipilima Samaliya.
10 Nayo yali yeza kupuluta myaka midatu, hodu nhondo yakawa kuyisola Samaliya. Wo wutya, wali mu mwaka wo mutandatu wo wutemi wa Hezekiya, kanyhi, wali wo mwaka we licenda wo wutemi wa Hosheya.
11 Mutemi wa Ashuli, yakawasola Waisilayeli, yakawategula ku Ashuli nha wafungwa. Yakawawika wanji wekale mwi'kaya lya Hala, na wanji behi ne likolongo lya Haboli mu yisi ya Gozani, na wanji kanyhi mu makaya ga Mediya.

12 Samaliya yikagwa, vyono wene Waisilayeli si wenda kumuhulika Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo, wakacibena ne cilagane cono yacinoza kuli wene, wakagabeza ne malajilizo gono wapegwa na Musa u mwitumami wo Muwaha Mulungu. Wakalema kuzihulika, hamba kuzitumama kose.

Waashuli wakuyogofya Yelusalemi

(2Zin 32:1-19; Isa 36:1-22)

13 Mu mwaka we likumi na yine, wo wutemi wa Hezekiya, hodu yakawa kuza Senakelibu u mutemi wa Ashuli, yakagahoma makaya ga Yuda ge magomawakali, yakagasuma.
14 Nayo mutemi Hezekiya yakatuma munhu kuli Senakelibu, ku Lakishi, yabite yakamulonjele yatye, “Ane sutya, holeka nghukunena wulece kujendelela kuhoma, nane nokupela co cose cono kosaka.” Nayo mutemi Senakelibu yakamupitula Hezekiya yakamutya, “Ale, viswanu, alu yikunojeye wundetele fweza kilo elufu monga, hamonga na zahabu nazo kilo elufu monga.”
15 Nayo, Hezekiya yakawa kumutegulila Senakelibu, zifweza zose zono zali zili mu Nyumba yo Mulungu na zono zali zili mu viwutwa vya mwi'kulu lyo mutemi.
16 Kanyhi yakazinamatula zizahabu zose zono zali mu milango ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, sunga ne zizahabu zono na mweneco yali yazihiliwe mu milango. Yakazisola zose yakamutegulila Senakelibu.
17 Nayo mutemi wa Ashuli, yakawa kutegula lipuga yiwaha lye wasilikale wo wulugu ku wulawa Lakishi, wabite wakamuhome Hezekiya ku Yelusalemi, wakawa wena ne walongola wawo wadatu. Nawo wali weza kufika ku Yelusalemi, wakema behi no muvwilo wono waletaga malenga kulawa mu cilamba ca hacanyha, cono cili mu nzila mbaha yono yibitile sunga ko ku ciwanza ca dobi.
18 Baho, wakawa kumucema mutemi Hezekiya. Ne walongola wa muli wetumami wakwe wo wakabita kwitang'anila nawo, nawo yali Eliyakimu u mwana wa Hilikiya, mono yali yo mwitumami muwaha wa mwi'kulu, na Shebuna mono yali yo mwandici, hamonga na Yowa u mwana wa Asafu mono yali yo mukumbucizi we zinghani.
19 Ale, baho, yumonga wa muli wa walya wetumami wo mutemi wa Ashuli, yakawalonjela nha vyono, mutemi Senakelibu yakusaka yamanye cinhuci cono mutemi Hezekiya yetumbiye.
20 Yakawa kulonga yakutya, “Agwe wukwihwesa nha vyono wuli munghula, kanyhi wina ne lipuga lye wasilikale wono wali vilungo? Alo wukwihwesa nani 'lo mono kokutaza, hono wakondya kuyibeza Ashuli?
21 Hamba wulanjiliye nha vyono Misili yo yokutaza? Ninga kowa nha wukwitumbila ligwami lyono libenece. Nalyo lyomuhoma u munhu wo wose mono kolitumbila. No mutemi Falawo wa Misili vyo yenha, hono munhu yunji yamwitumbile.”

22 Ale du, yu yulya mwitumami we Ciashuli, yakajendelela kulonga yakutya, “Hamba mononjela mutye vyono, anye mumwitumbiye Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu? Alo si yo yuyu du Hezekiya, mono yakawuhodanga wukumbicilo mu vigongo, yakawalonjela wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, vyono yiwanojeye wawe kuhemba ku cikumbicilo cono cili ku Yelusalemi du?
23 Ale, nghukunena 'lu, cijelane anye na nene, mwi'tagwa lyo mutemi. Nane nomupelenyi fwalasi elufu nyejete, hono mbeka mosuma kuwatuga wanhu wenji wono wosuma kuzipanda!
24 Hamba kosuma nhawule kumuwinga yawuye ne nyuma, yumonga wa muli mwitumami mudodo, wa muli wetumami wo muwaha wangu, hamba wuwe witumbiye vyono, Wamisili wokuletela matuka go wulugu, hamonga ne zifwalasi!
25 Hamba wukwihwesa nha vyono, ane niza ho niyihome yisi yiyi, no kuyongomeza lono si nhumigwe no Muwaha Mulungu? Ninga wumanye vyono, Muwaha Mulungu mweneco yo yanhumile nize niyihome, niyongomeze.”

26 Hanyuma Eliyakimu na Shebuna hamonga na Yowa, wakamutya mwitumami yu yulya, “Alu wayiko cikukunena, wulece kulonga nase mu nonga ye Cihebelaniya, hambi wanhu wose wono wekaya mwi'jinji nha wakuhulika, langa wulonje nase mu nonga ye Ciashuli, nase nha ciyimanyile nonga yiyo.”
27 Nayo yakapitula yakawatya, “Anye mukwihwesa vyono mutemi kanhuma nize nongane na nyenye du hamonga no mutemi? Aa ng'o. Ane nghulongana sunga ne wanhu wose wono wekaye mwi'jinji mucanyha, walye mavi gawo, wang'we ne matunzi gawo, sunga na nyenye.”

28 Hanyuma yu yulya mwitumami yakema wima, yakalonga yazozize muno muno mu nonga ye Cihebelaniya, yakutya, “Ale muhulicize zono mutemi wa Ashuli yakumulonjelenyi!
29 Mwene yakumusugulenyi, vyono mulecele lukulu kumuhuliciza Hezekiya, hambi nha yakumukonjenyi du, hamba si kosumila lukulu kumuponyenyi yaye.
30 Hamba mulece kumuleka yamupepelezenyi, yatye nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu komuponyenyi, hamba kolimila lipuga lye wasilikale we Waashuli ho walece kulisola likaya lyenyu.
31 Holeka mulecele lukulu kumuhuliciza Hezekiya. Mutemi wa Ashuli yakumulajilizenyi mwiyilavye nyo weneco, muwuce mwi'kaya. Nanye mokombolwa mulye zabibu za mu mibici yenyu ye zabibu, hamonga ne zinghuyu mu mibici yenyu ye mikuyu, kanyhi mong'wa ne malenga ga mu masima genyu,
32 sunga kolo mutemi yakondya kuza kumusolenyi, no kumwikazenyi mu yisi yono yihwanile yisi yenyu, yisi yono yina zabibu zono zomupelenyi divayi, hamonga no wuhemba wono mosuma kunoza mikate, yo yisi yono yina mafuta ge mizeyituni, hamonga no wuci. Na hono mwitume nha vyono mutemi yakulajiliza, hodu, hamba si mofwa yaye, langa mokwikala womi du. Holeka mulecele lukulu kumuleceleza Hezekiya yamupuvyenyi, muwe kwihwesa nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu komuponyenyi.
33 Wulenyi, hambi ho yili milungu yo yose, ya mu makungugo go gose, yono yasuma kuziponya zi'si zawo mu miwoko yo mutemi wa Ashuli?
34 Alu milungu yiyo yili hayi 'lu, i milungu ya Hamati, na Alipadi? Hamba milungu ya Sefalivayimu na Hena na Iva? Wulenyi, ho yuli hamba mulungu monga mono yasuma kuyiponya Samaliya?
35 Hamba nili 'lo, lono milungu ye zisi zizi, yono yasuma kuyiponya yisi yawo, ku wulawa mu miwoko yo mutemi wa Ashuli? Alu agwe, cicici cene cono cokunoza, sunga wihwese nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu kosuma kuyiponya Yelusalemi?”

36 Nawo wanhu wakawa wanyamaye du twii, hamba kupitula cinji, nha vyono mutemi Hezekiya yali yakondya kuwalonjela vyono walece kupitula nghani yo yose.
37 Hodu, Eliyakimu na Shebuna, hamonga na Yowa, wakavidemulanga vivwalo vyawo ko wusungu wuwaha, wakabita kumulonjela mutemi Hezekiya zinghani zose zono yalonjile u mwitumami wo mutemi wa Ashuli.


2 Watemi 19

Mutemi Hezekiya, yakulondola wutonji kuli muwizi Isaya

(Isa 37:1-7)

1 Mutemi Hezekiya, yali yeza kuzihulika zizo, hobaho, yakavidemulanga vivwalo vyakwe ko wusungu wuwaha muno, yakavwala yigunila, yakenjila mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
2 Yakamutuma Eliyakimu u mulanjilizi muwaha we likulu, hamonga na Shabuna u mwandici, hamonga ne wakohani wakombi, wabite kuli muwizi Isaya u mwana wa Amozi, akuno wawe wavwalile magunila.
3 Wabite wakamulonjele Isaya, watye nhavi, “Izuwali lyo lizuwa lyo wugaye ne mahong'ongo hamonga ne maligo, hambi nha cehwana mucekulu mono yali mu nghunga yo lusawo, mono yasina hamba ne vilungo vyo kulelela.
4 Hambi u mutemi wa Ashuli, nha kamutuma mwitumami wakwe mulongola, yaze yamutovule Mulungu mumi. Holeka Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yagahulikaje matovulo gaga, yawalipicize wanhu wono wali kutovula. Alu wumulombe Mulungu ku soko ye wanhu wetu wono wakali womi.”

5 Isaya yaza kuzihulika zono mutemi Hezekiya yakuzilonga, nayo yakamupitula yakatya,
6 “Muwaha Mulungu yakukutya vyono, Waashuli walece kukogofya, no wulonje wawo wono wakutya vyono, Muwaha Mulungu si kosuma kukuponya.
7 Muwaha Mulungu komunoza mutemi wa Ashuli yahulice nghani zo wulambi zono zomunoza yawuye ne nyuma ku yisi yakwe, nayo Muwaha Mulungu komuleka yawulajilwe kuko.”

Waashuli, wakuleta wogofye wunji kanyhi

(Isa 37:8-20)

8 Nayo Labushake u mwitumami we Ciashuli, yakamanya nha vyono mutemi wa Siliya yawuka ku Lakishi, alu yakwihoma ne likaya lyono lili behi baho lya Libuna. Holeka Labushake yakabita kuko kuduwana no mutemi.
9 Ninga kanyhi Waashuli wakahulika nha vyono mutemi Tilihaka wa Kushi, yakuza hamonga ne lipuga lye wasilikale wa Misili, waze wawahome. Holeka nayo mutemi yali yeza kuzihulika zizi, hobaho yakamwandicila baluwa Hezekiya mutemi wa Yuda,
10 yakumulonjela yakutya, “Yuyo Mulungu mono wumwitumbiye mono yakulonjeye nha vyono agwe si kogwila mu miwoko yangu, nghani yiyo, yilece kukukonga.
11 Hambi nha wakondya kuzihulika zono mutemi wa Siliya, yakuzituma kuli yisi yo yose yono yakusaka kuyihoma no kuyongomeza. Holeka wulece kwihwesa nha vyono nagwe kosuma kupona, langa hamba si kopona yaye.
12 I walezi wangu wagongomeza makaya ga Gozani, Halani, hamonga na Lezefu. Wakawawulaga wanhu wa Betedeni wono wekalaga ku Telesa, na hali hasina hamba milungu yawo yono yasuma kuwaponya.
13 Alu mwene i watemi we makaya ga Hamati na Alipadi, na Sefalivayimu, na Hena na iva, wali hayi wene?”

14 Mutemi Hezekiya yakayibocela baluwa mu miwoko yo munhu mono yatumwa, yakayisoma. Hanyuma yakawa kwinjila mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakayiwika baluwa yiyo hali Muwaha Mulungu,
15 yakalomba yakutya, “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu we Waisilayeli, mono wikaye mu makelubi, nha yo gwegwe Mulungu mono wutemile mu watemi wose wa mono'nze. Yo gwegwe mono wawumba wulanga ne yinze.
16 Alu 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, nghukunena wuzilanje zizi zono zeza kucijelela. Kanyhi wuzihulice na zose ze maligo zono Senakelibu yakuzilonga, na vyono yakukutovula agwe go Mulungu mumi.
17 Alu nase 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono wawo watemi wa Siliya wakondya kugongomeza makungugo menji, no kuzileka zisi zawo nyhiwa,
18 wakayikongoga mu moto ne milungu yawo yo wulambi, hambi i makungugo gago nha gali gasina milungu, langa gali gena vihwani vye milungu viseswe, vye mabici ne mabwe, vyono visesigwe ne wanhu du.
19 Holeka aluno go Muwaha Mulungu go Mulungu wetu, cikukunena wuciponye mu miwoko ye Waashuli. Ne makungugo gose ga mononze, ho gomanya nha vyono yo gwegwe du wuli wene, go Muwaha Mulungu.”

Muwizi Isaya, yakutuma munhu kuli mutemi Hezekiya

(Isa 37:21-38)

20 Muwizi Isaya yakatuma munhu kuli mutemi Hezekiya, yabite yakamumanyize ze mapitulo go wulombe wakwe, vyono Muwaha Mulungu yateye. Nayo munhu yuyo yakabita yakamulonjela mutemi Hezekiya yakatya,
21 “Ayi yo nghani yono Muwaha Mulungu yakulonga kuli Senakelibu yakutya,

‘Agwe Senakelibu,

likaya lya Yelusalemi likukusekanga no kukubedula.’

22 Alu mwene 'gwe Senakelibu,

wukwihwesa yo nani 'lo,

mono agwe wali kumutovula no kumuzwajiza?

Agwe weza wusina cogopo kuli'ne,

ane no Mulungu we Waisilayeli mono nelile.

23 Agwe Senakelibu,

wawatuma wanhu wako waze wekumye hali'ne,

vyono agwe hamonga ne matuka gako go wulugu,

wukagasuma matunda matali ga Lebanoni.

Na kanyhi wukwikumya nha vyono

wakondya kuyitemanga mibici mitali ye mihagata,

hamonga ne mibici miswanu yose ye misonobali,

na kanyhi, nha vyono wakondya kwinjila

sunga haciligati muno muno ye mbago.

24 Kanyhi wukujendelela kwikumya nha vyono,

wahimba ne masima sunga wukagang'wa ne malenga mu zi'si njenzi,

na vyono migulu ye wasilikale wako yalikanyanjila

likolongo lya Nile sunga likanyala.”

25 Hamba agwe si wahulika nha vyono

nghani zizi nali nizihwesize ku wulawa na katali?

Nalu nakondya kuzifisiliza.

Nghakupela agwe,

wusume kugahodanga makaya ge magomawakali, gawe vyugulu.

26 Ne wanhu wono wekalaga mu makaya gago,

wakawa wasina vilungo,

wakawa wamemile woga,

hamonga no wuhangawe.

Wakehwana nhe mahanze ga mu mugunda,

hamba mahanze gono gacindice mwi'tembe,

lono mbeho mbaha ye livuce ya kucilima yikugaputa kwa cihumbi.

27 “Ninga ane nizimanyile zako zose,

zono kwitumaga na kono kubitaga.

Kanyhi, manyile manyi manyi na vyono wungholeywe likuwo muno muno.

28 Ninga nane nha nakondya kuzihulika zako zose,

sunga na ze likuwo lyako kuli'ne,

hamonga na zo wikumyi wako.

Ninga nane luno, nokuwicila ndobogo mu mhula yako,

yipamize no mulomo wako,

nghukwejenhe nghukoze mu nzila yi yilya du,

nghugomole kono walawilila.”

29 Isaya yakamulonjela mutemi Hezekiya yakatya, “Naco acino co citanjilo cono cokwijela. Mu mwaka wuwu, sunga na mu mwaka wono wukuza, mowa kulya wuhemba wono wowa kwicindicila weneco du mwi'hala. Ninga mu mwaka muyagwe, aho langa ho molosuma kuhadika mbeyu yo wuhemba nyenye weneco no kuwududula. Kanyhi mohadika zabibu, molya ne mikalo yayo.
30 Na wa mu nyumba ya Yuda wono wasigaye wono wopona, wawo wokonjezeka nhe vilimwa, vyono vinjizaga milela yayo kusi mu muhangha, hanyuma viwa kulela mikalo.
31 Mwi'kaya lya Yelusalemi, hamonga na mwi'tunda lya Sayuni, mowa mowali wanhu wono wasigaye wono wopona. Hambi u Muwaha Mulungu nha yo yakuzinoza zizi.

32 “Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakumulonjela mutemi wa Siliya yakutya nhavi, ‘Hamba si kosumila lukulu kwinjila mwi'kaya lili, hamba kuliguma songa monga bwete. Hamba wasilikale wono wena ne zingula zawo, si wo kwijelela bwete kuza behi ne likaya, hamba si kozenga ligomawakali yalipilimye yaye.
33 Langa kowuya ne nyuma kwawo, kanyhi kowuyila mu nzila yono yazize, hamba kwinjila mwi kaya lili yaye. Ane yo nene no Muwaha Mulungu mono nghuzilonga zizi.
34 Nane nolidima likaya lili, kanyhi nolikamisa, ku soko yangu, na ku soko yo wihuwizo wangu wono namwihuwiziza Dawudi u mwitumami wangu.’ ”

35 Na mu cilo cico, malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu yakabita ku nghambi ye Waashuli, yakawawulaga wasilikale waficile elufu yigana limonga na makumi munana na wahano. Na mu welelecele we lizuwa lye kejete, hakali mitondo, wose wakawa wakondya kufwa.
36 Nayo Senakelibu u mutemi wa Siliya, yali yeza kuziwona zizo, hodu yakawa kuwuya ku Ninawi.
37 Na mwi'zuwa linji, lono yali yali mu wuhembi, mu nyumba yo wuhembelo wo mulungu wakwe, mono yakucemwa Nisiloke, i wanagwe wejete, Adelamaleke na Shalezeli, wakaza wakamuwulaga sogo yawo ne mimaje yawo. Hanyuma wene wakatiziza ku yisi ya Alalati. No mwanagwe yunji, mono yacemwaga Esale-hadoni yakahala wutemi wakwe.


2 Watemi 20

Wutamwe no wunoje wo mutemi Hezekiya

(Isa 38:1-8, 21-22; 2Zin 32:24-26)

1 Na mu mapango gago, mutemi Hezekiya yakawa yatamwa muno muno yali behi no kufwa. Nayo muwizi Isaya u mwana wa Amozi, yakabita kumuwona u mutemi Hezekiya, yakamulonjela yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu yakukutya wunozeleze zose, hambi nha si kopona yaye mu mhungo yiyi. Holeka wiwikanizaje kufwa.”

2 Mutemi Hezekiya, yakapitucila kwi'jinji, yakawa kulomba yakutya,
3 “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wukumbucile nha vyono, ane nakwitumamila ko wihuwilwa ne cogopo, na mu vilo vyose, nghawa kuhidimbala kwituma zo wuhile wako.” Hezekiya yali yeza kudumya wulombe wakwe, hodu yakanza kulila ko wusungu wuwaha muno muno.

4 Nayo muwizi Isaya, hodu yakawa kuwuka hali mutemi, yakawa kutuluka. Ninga lono yali yakali nacipuluta ciwanza ca mu kaya ya mugati mwi'kulu, Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela vyono,
5 yawuye ne nyuma kanyhi kuli mutemi Hezekiya, u mutemi we wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu, yakamulonjele yatye, “Ane no Muwaha Mulungu, no Mulungu wo sogo Dawudi, nicihulika cililo cako, hamonga no wulombe wako. Holeka alu nokuponya, nagwe mu vilo vidatu kojenda no kobita ko ku Nyumba yo Mulungu.
6 Nane nokuleka wuwe mumi kanyhi, sunga yipulute myaka yikumi na mihano. Na kanyhi, noliponya sunga ne likaya lilino lya Yelusalemi, ku wulawa mu muwoko wo mutemi wa Siliya. Nane nolikamisa likaya lili ku soko yangu, na ku soko yo wihuwizo wangu, wono namwihuwiziza Dawudi u mwitumami wangu.”

7 Hanyuma muwizi Isaya yakawalonjela i wetumami wo mutemi yakawatya, “Alu musole nhonje yo wugali wudecedece, wono munozwe ne mana go mukuyu, muyijelece mucanyha ye lipu lyakwe, nayo kopona.”
8 Mutemi Hezekiya yakamuwuza Isaya yakatya, “Alu citanjiloci cono condajila, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu komhonya, na kanyhi nha vyono kolo vikupuluta vilo vidatu, nane nosuma kujenda no kubita kwinjla mu Nyumba yo Mulungu?”

9 Nayo Isaya yakamupitula yakamutya, “Muwaha Mulungu heya kokulajila citanjilo, cono colajila vyono, mwene kowufisiliza wihuwizo wakwe. Holeka alu nagwe nghusaka wunonjele, hono kokwenda mwenzenze we ngazi zizi wujendelele ku mwande nghanzo yikumi, hamba wuwuye ne nyuma nghanzo yikumi?”

10 Hezekiya yakapitula yakatya, “Ane nghuwona nha vyono kowa kupelu mwenzenze ku wubita ku mwande nghanzo yikumi! Holeka ciba wuwunoze wu wuye ne nyuma nghanzo yikumi.”

11 Nayo Isaya yakamulomba Muwaha Mulungu, nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakawunoza mwenzenze we ngazi zono yazenga mutemi Ahazi, wukawuya ne nyuma nghanzo yikumi.

Wanhu wakutumwa ku wulawa ku Babiloni

(Isa 39:1-8)

12 Na mu vilo vivyo u mutemi wa Babiloni, mono yacemwaga Melodaki Baladani, u mwana wa Baladani, yakawa yahulika nha vyono mutemi Hezekiya mutamwa, holeka yakawa kumwandicila baluwa yakamutegulila hamonga ne cipegwa.
13 Nayo Hezekiya yakawabocela viswanu wawo wanhu wono watumwa kuli mwene, yakawa kuwalajila wugoli wakwe wose we zifweza, na zabahu, hamonga na vye mhunjile swanu, ne mafuta maswanu, hamonga ne ciwutwa ce vitwalo vyakwe vyo wulugu. Na hali hasina cinji co cose cono cali ciwicigwe mu zinyumba ze viwutwa vyakwe, hamba vyono vyali viwicigwe hanhu ho hose mu wutemi wakwe, vyono si yawalajila yaye.
14 Hanyuma muwizi Isaya, nhondo yakawa kubita kuli mutemi Hezekiya, yakamuwuza yakutya, “Alo wanhu wawa walawilila hayi, na kanyhi wakulonjela nghanici?”

Nayo mutemi Hezekiya yakapitula yakatya, “Wanhu wawa walawilila kutali muno ku yisi ya Babiloni.”

15 Isaya yakatya, “Alu wawona cici mwi'kulu?” Nayo Hezekiya yakatya, “Niwalajila vinhu vyose vya mu viwutwa vyangu, hasina hamba cinji cono cili mu viwutwa vyose, cono si niwalajiye yaye.”

16 Hobaho baho muwizi Isaya yakawa kumutya mutemi, “Muwaha Mulungu, yakukutya nha vyono,
17 cipango cokuza, lono i vinhu vyose vyono vili mwi'kulu lyako, viwutwa vyose vyono walezi wako waviwika sunga nzuwayi, vyose vyolosolwa vitegulwe ku Babiloni. Hasina hamba cinhu cinji cono colosigala.
18 Sunga ne wanago wanji wolosolwa, wabite kuko wakapaswe, wawe wetumami mu kaya ye likulu lyo mutemi wa kuko ku Babiloni.”

19 Nayo mutemi Hezekiya yakatanga nha vyono kumbe wowa mulamu no wucitilwe mu vilo vyono visigaye vyo wikale wakwe. Holeka yakapitula yakatya, “I nghani yono wunonjeye yono yilawile kuli Muwaha Mulungu, mbeka yo nghani swanu muno.”

Ndumilizo yo wutemi wa Hezekiya

(2Zin 32:32-33)

20 Ne zinghani zono mutemi Hezekiya yetuma, hamonga no wupiligazi wakwe, na vyono yanoza cilamba, yakahimba cilamba, yakawicila ne zibomba zono zenjizaga malenga mwi'kaya. Zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Yuda.
21 Hezekiya yakafwa. No mwanagwe, Manase, yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.


2 Watemi 21

Manae mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 33:1-20)

1 Manase yali yaficile myaka yikumi na myejete lono yatemela mu Yuda. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi mahano na mihano, U nyina yacemwaga Hefisiba.
2 Ninga Manase yetuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, so wituma makani ge liwifyo, gono wetumaga we zinghungugo, zono Muwaha Mulungu yaziwinga mu yisi, lono wanhu wakwe wenjilaga mumo.
3 Mwene yakazenga hanhu henji mu vigongo ha wukumbicilo we milungu, i hanhu hono u sogwe Hezekiya yali yakondya kuhamwagulanga hose. Kanyhi yakazenga sunga ne cikumbicilo ca Baali, yakasesa ne cihwani co mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, yakazinoza nha vyono Ahabu u mutemi wa Isilayeli yanoza. Manase, yakawa yakuzihemba sunga ne zinyelezi zose.
4 Manase yakazenga vikumbicilo vya we makungugo sunga na mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, mu Nyumba yono Muwaha Mulungu yalonga yakatya vyono, yo Nyumba yono yinojeye mweneco du yawe kuhembelwa mumo.
5 Kanyhi yakazenga vikumbicilo mu makumbo mejete ge Nyumba yo Mulungu, i vikumbicilo vyo kuhembela zinyelezi.
6 Yakamulavya mwanagwe, yawe cilavywa cinyanywe. Yakatowa cisangu, yakawa kunoza wuganga, yakawa mwilagulizi kuli we mawizi ge macisi ne wahawi. Yaketuma nyinji zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu, yakamukoza likuwo yiwaha Muwaha Mulungu.
7 Yakawika cihwani co mulungu ce cicekulu Ashela, mugati ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, i hanhu hono Muwaha Mulungu yamulonjela Dawudi, hamonga no mwanagwe Solomona, yakatya, “Bahano hono Yelusalemi, mu Nyumba yiyi yo Mulungu, ho hanhu hono nakondya kuhahagula ku soko ye yisi yose ye milango yikumi na myejete ya Isilayeli, hawe ho hanhu hono nene no mweneco nowa kuhembelwa.
8 Na hono wanhu wa Isilayeli wagebate malajilizo gangu, kanyhi wakamise no Wulajilize wose wono Musa mwitumami wangu yawapela, nane hamba si nowaleka wawingwe mu yisi yiyo yono nakondya kuwapela walezi wawo.”
9 Ninga lu, i wanhu wa Yuda, hamba si wamuhulika Muwaha Mulungu. Nayo Manase yakawanoza wanhu wamusutizye muno Muwaha Mulungu, waketuma wubi kuciza wubi wono makungugo getuma, gono Muwaha Mulungu, yakagawinga mu yisi, i wanhu wakwe ho wenjile wekale mumo.

10 Muwaha Mulungu yalonjela muli wetumami wakwe wawizi yakutya,
11 “Mutemi Manase, ketuma makani ge 'wifyo muno muno, kuciza ne makani gono wetuma Wakanani, hambi nha kawanoza wanhu wa Yuda wetume wubi, so vihwani vyakwe vyono yavisesise.
12 Holeka nane 'lu no Muwaha Mulungu, noleta wongomezwe wuwaha mu Yelusalemi na mu Yuda, wongomezwe wono munhu wo wose mono kowuhulika, ne makutu gakwe gopaya.
13 Noyilipiciza Yelusalemi, nha vyono nayilipiciza Samaliya, nha vyono namunozeza Ahabu u mutemi wa Isilayeli, hamonga no lulelo lwakwe lwose. Nowafwagula wanhu wose wa mu Yelusalemi wamalice, hawe hazelu swee, nhe sahani yono yakwemwa no kugubikwa.
14 Kanyhi nowalecela lukulu, sunga ne wanhu wono wowa wakali wasigaye, niwakumhile kuli walugu wawo, wasumwe ne walugu wawo, wayisole ne yisi yawo.
15 Nane nozinoza zizi kuli wanhu wangu, ku soko wakondya kwituma wubi hali'ne, wanghoza likuwo, ku wulawilila lono walezi wawo wawucila ku Misili, sunga nalu.”

16 Mutemi Manase mu vilo vyo wutemi wake, yawulaga wanhu wenji wono wasina wubi, ne sakami yawo yikahuluma mu zi nzila za mwi'kaya lya Yelusalemi. Hamonga na zizo, kanyhi, yakawanoza wanhu wa Yuda wahembe vihwani. Na mu nzila yiyo, wakamusutizya muno Muwaha Mulungu.

17 Ne zinghani zono mutemi Manase yetuma, hamonga no wubi wono yetuma, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Yuda.
18 Manase yakafwa, yakazikwa mu busitani ye likulu, i busitani ya Uza. No mwanagwe mono yakucemwa Amoni, yo yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Amoni, u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 33:21-25)

19 Mutemi Amoni, lono yatemela mu Yuda, yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na myejete. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka myejete. No nyina yacemwaga Meshulemeti, u mwana we cicekulu wa Haluzi, munya ku cikaya ca Yotiba.
20 Ninga nayo nhavivyo, yakamusutizya muno Muwaha Mulungu nho sogwe Manase.
21 Yaketuma metumo gose mabi nha vyono sogwe du yetuma. Yakahemba vihwani vyono sogwe yavihembaga.
22 Yakamulema Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu we walezi wakwe, hamba si yakasaka no kugebata ne malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu.

23 I wetumami wa Amoni, wakamusepa Amoni, wakamuwulajila mwi'kulu.
24 Wanhu wa Yuda, nawo, wakawawulaga wawo wono wamuwulaga Amoni, wakamunoza Yosiya u mwana wa Amoni yawe mutemi.

25 Ne zinghani zono mutemi Amoni yetuma, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Yuda.
26 Amoni yakafwa, yakazikwa mu busitani ya Uza. Nayo Yosiya u mwanagwe yakahala wutemi wakwe.


2 Watemi 22

Yosiya, u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 34:1-2)

1 Nayo mutemi Yosiya, yali yaficile myaka munana du lono yakutema mu Yuda. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi madatu na wumonga. U nyina yacemwaga Yedida, u mwana we cicekulu wa Adaya, munya ku cikaya ca Bosikati.
2 Ninga mutemi Yosiya, yetuma zono zamwendeza Muwaha Mulungu. Yakatyatya musambo wo kukuye mutemi Dawudi, yakawukamisiza lukulu wulajilize wo Mulungu.

Citabu co Wulajilize, cikwijela

(2Zin 34:8-28)

3 Nayo Yosiya, mu mwaka we likumi na munana wo wutemi wakwe, yakamusola Shafani u mwana wa Azaliya u mwizukulu wa Meshulamu, u mwandici, yakamutuma ku Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakamulajiliza yakutya,
4 “Wubite kuli Hilikiya u mukohani muwaha, wukatuje liwalo lye zipesa, zono wakohani wazibocelaga, ku wulawa kuli wanhu, ha mulango we Nyumba yo Mulungu.
5 Wumutye mapesa gago yawapele i walanjilizi, wono wakuwulanjilila mulimo wo wunozeleza Nyumba yo Mulungu.
6 Nawo wawo walanjilizi, wo wosuma kuwalipa misahala i wasesi ne wazenji, hamonga ne wasesi we mabwe. Kanyhi wagule ne zimbawo, hamonga ne mabwe, so wunozelezeza Nyumba yo Mulungu.
7 Kanyhi wumulonjele Hilikiya nha vyono, i wanhu wawo wono wakuwulanjilila mulimo wuwo, wo wanhu wehuwilwa muno, holeka howa hasina soko yo mwene, kusaka kumanya liwalo lye zipesa nha vyono wakuzitumamila.”

8 Hanyuma Hilikiya yakamulonjela Shafani u mwandici yakatya. “Nakondya kuciwona citabu co Wulajilize, cili mumo mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.” Hobaho Hilikiya yakawa kumupela Shafani i citabu cico yakacisoma.
9 Hanyuma yakawuya kuli mutemi yakamuloloseza zose yakutya, “I wetumami wako, wakondya kuzisola zipesa zono zali zili mu Nyumba yo Mulungu wawapela i walanjilizi wo mulimo wo wunozeleza Nyumba yo Mulungu.”
10 Hanyuma kanyhi yakatya, “Sunga ne citabu ceneco, Hilikiya kamhela. Naco co acino 'hano nina naco.” Hobaho yakawa kumusomela mutemi kwe'zi waha zono zali zandicigwe mumo.

11 Nayo mutemi Yosiya yali yeza kuzihulika zo Wulajilize zono zandicigwe mumo mu citabu, hobaho yakavidemulanga vivwalo vyakwe ko woga wuwaha muno.
12 Umutemi yakawalajiliza Hilikiya umukohani, na Ahikamu u mwana wa Shafani, na Akeboli u mwana wa Mikaya, na Shefani mweneco u mwandici, hamonga na Asaya u mwitumami wo mutemi, yakawatya,
13 “Alu mubite mukambuzize kuli Muwaha Mulungu, ku soko yangu, na kuli wanhu wose wa Yuda, ze mejizo gono gandicigwe mu citabu cici. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yacikoleywe likuwo muno, hambi i walezi wetu, nha siwasuma kuzituma zinghani zono zili mu citabu cici zono zali ziwanojeye wetume.”

14 Nawo Hilikiya, hamonga na Ahikamu na Akeboli, na Shafani, na Asaya, wakawa kubita kuli mucekulu yunji muwizi, mono yacemwaga Hulida, mono yali yekaye ha citumbi cono cili behi muno na Yelusalemi. (U mulume wakwe yacemwaga Shalumu u mwana wa Tikiva, kanyhi mwizukulu wa Halihasi, nayo yali yo mulanjilizi we vivwalo vyo witumami mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.) Nawo wali weza kumwagana, wakamuloloseza zose zono zijeye.
15 Nayo yakawatya wawuye kanyhi kuli mutemi wakamulonjele
16 zono Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Ane no Muwaha Mulungu, noyilipiciza Yelusalemi, hamonga ne wanhu wayo wose, nha vyono zandicigwe mu citabu, nha vyono mutemi yazisomile.
17 Hambi i wanhu wawa, nha wakondya kunema, nalu wakuyilavizya milungu yo wulambi makumbiko. Na ku soko yiyo, wakondya kunghoza likuwo yiwaha, ku soko ya zono wetumile. Holeka likuwo lyangu, nilikoleywe muno likaya lya Yelusalemi, hamba si lyocepuka yaye.
18 Nayo mutemi, ane no Muwaha Mulungu we Waisilayeli nghumutya nhavi, ‘Ninga agwe kuzihulika no kuzibocela zinghani zono zandicigwe mu citabu,
19 kanyhi kuwa kunena, no kwinenenyheza go mweneco hali'ne, kanyhi kuwa kuvidemulanga sunga ne vivwalo vyako, akuno wukulila, lono weza kumanya nha vyono ane nokuza niyilipicize Yelusalemi, hamonga ne wanhu wayo wose. Na vyono noyinoza Yelusalemi hawe hanhu ha kogofya, kanyhi halijitwe. Holeka aluno, nawuhulika du wulombe wako,
20 no wulipicizwe wono nali niyilipicize Yelusalemi, alu hodu si noyilipiciza yaye, sunga langa kolo wakondya kufwa. Nane nokunoza wufwe wupolile.’ ”

Nawo wanhu wawo, wakamuziza mutemi Yosiya, wakamuloloseza zizo zose.


2 Watemi 23

Mutemi Yosiya, yakuwuwulaga wuhembi we milungu

(2Zin 34:3-7, 29-33)

1 Mutemi Yosiya yakawakongholela hamonga i walongola wose wa Yuda na Yelusalemi,
2 wakenjila mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, i wakohani ne wawizi, ne wanhu wose wa Yelusalemi, i wawaha ne wadodo, wagoli ne waciwa, hamonga ne wanhu wose we mawala gose. Mutemi yakawasomela zose zono zandicigwe mu citabu ce cilagane, cono cejela mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
3 Mutemi yakema behi ne lisumbili, yakanoza cilagane no Muwaha Mulungu, cilagane vyono kowa na cihuliko kuli Mulungu, kanyhi na vyono kogebata malajilizo gakwe, hamonga no wulajilize wakwe kwe nhumbula yakwe yose, na ko mutima wakwe wose. Kanyhi na vyono kozikamya zinghani ze cilagane zono zandicigwe mu citabu. Nawo i wanhu wose wakehuwiza nha vyono wocibata no kucikamisa cilagane cico.

4 Hanyuma ya zizo, mutemi Yosiya, yakawa kumulajiliza mukohani muwaha Hilikiya, hamonga ne wakohani watazi wakwe, hamonga ne walindilizi wo mulango wo kwinjilila mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, wenjile mu Nyumba yo Mulungu wakawuse viya vyose vyono vyanozezwa Baali, no mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, ne zinyelezi. Mutemi Yosiya yakagasola maviya gago yakagaticimiziza mu moto kunze ye likaya, behi ne lilolo lya Kidiloni, yakagasola mavu gayo, yakagategula ku Beteli.
5 Yakawawusa mu mulimo wo wukohani, i wakohani wose wono u mutemi wa Yuda yali yawawicile, ho wawe kulavya makumbiko mu vikumbicilo mu vigongo, mu makaya ga Yuda, sunga na mu wuhembelo wono wali wuli behi behi na Yelusalemi. Wakohani wose wono walavyaga makumbiko kuli Baali, na kwi' zuwa, no mwezi, ne zisayali, hamonga ne zinyelezi.
6 Yakaciwusa cihwani co mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakacisola yakabita kucikongojela kunze ye likaya mwi'lolo lya Kidiloni. Yakacipondanga ciwe manghundi, yakageyagaza manghundi gago mu ciwanza ce vitenjelo vye wanhu wose.
7 Yakazigwisa zinyumba zono wetumamilaga i walume wono wagonaga ne walume wayawo, zono zali zili mugati ye Nyumba yo Mulungu. Mono wekalaga i wacekulu wono wahonaga matote ge mabupa ge zi Ashela.
8 Yakawasola i wakohani wose wono wali wali mu makaya ga Yuda, yakawategula ku Yelusalemi. Yakagavufya makumbicilo gose mu vigongo, gono walavizyaga makumbiko mu yisi yose hanhu hose. Yakagamwagulanga makumbicilo gose mu vigongo, gono wetumamilaga kuli milungu ye myuhe myavu, yono yali yili behi no mulango, wono Yoshuwa u mulongola wa mwi'kaya yawuzenga. Mulango wono wali wuli ku lubavu lo kucekulu lo mulango muwaha wo kwinjilila mwi'kaya.
9 Ne wakohani wawo wali si wakomboywe kwituma mulimo wunji wa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, ninga walizaga mukate wono wusina cisase, wono wapegwaga i wakohani wayawo.

10 Mutemi Yosiya, yakahavufya nhavivilya, i hanhu hono wahembelaga i milungu yo wulambi hono hacemwaga Tofeti, mwi'lolo lya Hinomu, ho halece kuwa no munhu wo wose mono kosuma kanyhi, kumulavya mwanagwe we cilume, hamba we cicekulu yawe yikumbiko lye cilavywa cinyanywe kuli mulungu Moleki.
11 Yakaziwusa ne zifwalasi zono mutemi wa Yuda, yali yazisiwanuye so wuhembi wo kulihemba lizuwa, yakagakongoga sunga ne matuka gono getumamilwaga mu wuhembi wuwo. (Na vivyo vyose, vyekalaga mu ciwanza ce Nyumba yo Mulungu, behi no mulango, kanyhi hamba si kwali kutali ne kaya yono yekalaga Natani Meleki u mwitumami muwaha.)
12 Mutemi Yosiya, yakavimwagulanga sunga ne vikumbicilo vyono, i watemi wa Yuda, wali wavizenjile mwi'tembe lya mwi'kulu, mu kaya yono yekalaga mutemi Ahazi. Mutemi Yosiya, yakavimwagulanga vikumbicilo vyono mutemi Manase, yavizenga mu viwanza vyejete vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu. Yakavitulanga vikumbicilo, yakagagumila malowanjila gavyo mwi'lolo lya Kidiloni.
13 Mutemi Yosiya, yakavyavufya vikumbicilo vyose, vyono mutemi Solomona yali yavizenjile ku lubavu lwa kucilima ya Yelusalemi, kutakama ye litunda lyo wuhodanjice, so wuhemba vihwani vye cevu, vyono vikucemwa Ashitolete, u mulungu we Wazidoni, na Cemoshi u mulungu we Waamoni.
14 Mutemi Yosiya, yakagabenanjizanga masumbili ge mabwe, yakavidumulanga ne vihwani vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, yakasola mafupa ge wanhu, yakagamemya i hanhu hono yali misimye milungu yiyo.

15 Mutemi Yosiya, yakawumwagula no wuhembelo wono mutemi Yelobowamu mwana wa Nebati, yali yawuzenjile ku Beteli, mono yawanoza Waisilayeli wetume wubi hali Mulungu. Yosiya, yakacimwagula cikumbicilo, yakagatulanjiza mabwe gayo, yakagatindya, gakawa manghundi. Yakacikongoga cihwani co mulungu we cicekulu Ashela.
16 Yosiya yali yeza kulanga ako na ako, yakawona vitenjelo mucanyha ye cigongo. Yakabita yakagafukula mafupa ge wafu, yakagakongojela mucanyha ye cikumbicilo cico. Na mu nzila yiyi, yakacavufya cikumbicilo. Yakazifisiliza zono muwizi yazilonga katali kuko ku Beteli, mwi'zuwa lono mutemi Yelobowamu, yali yemile ha cikumbicilo. Yosiya yali yeza kulanga langa kanyhi, yakaciwona citenjelo co muwizi yuyu mono yazilonga zizi.
17 Hodu yakawa kuwuza yakutya, “Alu citenjelo cicilya ca nani lo?” Nawo wanhu wanya Beteli, wakamupitula wakatya, “Aco co citenjelo co munhu wo Mulungu, mono yalawa ku Yuda, no kuwila zinghani zizi, zono witumile mu cikumbicilo cici.”

18 Mutemi Yosiya yakawatya, “Ale hodu mucilekaje, mulece kugahimbula mafupa gakwe.”

Hodu, mafupa gakwe wakagaleka, hamba si wagahimbula yaye, hamonga ne mafupa go muwizi mono yalawa ku Samaliya nago nhavivyo.

19 Mutemi Yosiya, yakavimwagulanga vikumbicilo vyose mu vigongo, vyono vyali vitagaliye mu makaya gose ga Isilayeli, vyono wavizenga i watemi wa Isilayeli, wakacila muno kumukoza likuwo Muwaha Mulungu. Mutemi Yosiya yakavinozeza vikumbicilo vyose, nha vyono yacinozeza cikumbicilo ca ku Beteli.
20 Yakawawulajila mu vikumbicilo i wakohani wose, wono wetumaga mulimo mu vikumbicilo. Yakagakongoga mu moto, mafupa gose ge wanhu mu vikumbicilo vyose. Hanyuma yakawa kuwuya ku Yelusalemi.

Yosiya, yakuyinoza Pasaka

(2Zin 35:1-19)

21 Mutemi Yosiya, yakawalajiliza wanhu wose, wamunozeze Pasaka Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo, nha vyono yandikwa mu citabu ce cilagane.
22 Na hakawa hasina Pasaka yinji yo yose, yono yanozwa no mutemi wo wose mu watemi walongozi, i watemi wa Isilayeli, hamba Yuda, nha vyono yikanozwa Pasaka yiyi, ku wulawilila mu vilo vye walamuzi, lono wali walitemile likungugo.
23 Langa mu mwaka we kumi na munana, wa mu wutemi wo mutemi Yosiya, ho Pasaka yiyi yanozezwa mu Yelusalemi.

Zinji kanyhi zono mutemi Yosiya yazikawaniza

24 Mutemi Yosiya, yakawawusa mu Yelusalemi na mu Yuda, we macisi, ne wahawi, ne milungu yose, ne vihwani vyose, vye milungu yo wulambi hamonga ne viya vyose vyono vyetumamilwaga mu wuhembi we milungu yo wulambi. Yakafisiliza zinghani zo wulajilize, zono zandicigwe mu citabu cono Hilikiya mukohani muwaha, yaciwona mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
25 Hakawa hasina mutemi yunji kanyhi mono yakawa yehwanile mutemi Yosiya, ku wulawa muli watemi wose wono wamulongolela kutema, hamba wono watema lono mwene yali yakondya kufwa, mono yamwitumamila Muwaha Mulungu kwe nhumbula yakwe yose, na ko mutima wakwe wose, na kwe vilungo vyakwe vyose, mono yali yebate Lilajilizo lya Musa. Hasina hamba monga mono yakawa yehwanile mutemi Yosiya.

26 Ninga likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu likaka muno ku so Yuda, ku soko ye zinghani zono mutemi Manase yetuma. Holeka sunga nalu, likuwo likali lyacile, hamba si licepuce yaye.
27 Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Nowawusa Wayuda ha meso gangu, nha vyono nawawusa Waisilayeli. Kanyhi nolileka likaya lya Yelusalemi, likaya lyono nalihagula, hamonga ne Nyumba yo Mulungu, i hanhu hono natya, vyono ho hanhu hono nowa kuhembelwa.”

Ndumilizo yo wutemi wa Yosiya

(2Zin 35:20–36:1)

28 Ne zinghani zono Yosiya yetuma, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Yuda.
29 Mu vilo vyono Yosiya yali yakali yatemile, Neko u mutemi wa Misili, yaleta wasilikale sunga ko kwi'kolongo lya Efulata, ho yamutaze mutemi wa Ashuli. Nayo mutemi Yosiya yali yeza kuwawona, yakajela kuwemila wasilikale wawo wa Misili ha Megido, ninga yakawulagwa mumo mu wulugu.
30 I wetumami wakwe wakawupaciza muwili wakwe mwi'tuka lyo wulugu, wakawugomola ku Yelusalemi. Yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi.

Nawo wanhu wa Yuda, wakamuhagula Yowahazi u mwana wa Yosiya, wakamwitila mafuta, wakamutemya yawe mutemi.

Yowahazi, mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 36:2-4)

31 Yowahazi yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na midatu lono yatemela mu Yuda. Yakatema myezi midatu mu Yelusalemi. U nyina yacemwaga Hamutali, u mwana we cicekulu wa Yelemiya munya kwi'kaya lya Libuna.
32 Ninga nayo Yowahazi, yakatyatya musambo mubi we walezi wakwe, yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu.
33 No wutemi wakwe wamalicila mwi'zuwa lono yebatilwa no mutemi Neko wa Misili, yakamufunga ku Libula, mu yisi ya Hamati, ho yalece kutema kanyhi ku Yelusalemi. Yakayilifya yisi ya Yuda kodi ya kilo elufu yidatu na magana mane za fweza, hamonga na kilo makumi madatu na yine za zahabu.
34 Mutemi Falawo Neko, yakamutemya Eliyakimu u mwana wa Yosiya, yawe mutemi wa Yuda, yahale hanhu ha Yosiya. Nayo Eliyakimu yakepitula litagwa lyakwe yakecema mwene Yehoyakimu. Nayo Yowahazi yakategulwa ku Misili no mutemi Neko, yakafwila kuko.

Yehoyakimu, mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 36:5-8)

35 Mutemi yehoyakimu yakakonghola kodi ku wulawa kuli wanhu, munhu ne nghango ye sawo yakwe, ho yasume kutuga liwalo lye kodi yono yasakaga mutemi wa Misili.

36 Yehoyakimu, yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na mihano lono yatemela mu Yuda. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka yikumi na wumonga. U nyina yacemwaga Zebida, u mwana we cicekulu wa Pedaya, munya ku cikaya ca Luma.
37 Na mu witume wakwe, yakatyatya musambo mubi we walezi wakwe. Yehoyakimu yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu.


2 Watemi 24

1 Na lono Yehoyakimu yali yakali yatemile, yakaza Nebukadineza mutemi wa Babiloni, yakayihoma yisi ya Yuda, yakayisola. Nebukadineza yakamudindimiza Yehoyakimu, myaka midatu, yawe yali hasi yo wutemi wakwe. Ninga hanyuma Yehoyakimu yakamugaluka yakamubeza.
2 Muwaha Mulungu yakatuma cipuga ce Wababiloni ne Wasiliya, ne Wamowabi, hamonga ne Waamoni, wayongomeze yisi ya Yuda, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalonjela muli wetumami wakwe wawizi, nha vyono kozinoza zizo.
3 Nazo zizi zikejela so wulajilize wo Muwaha Mulungu, ho wanhu wa Yuda wawuswe ha meso gakwe, ku soko yo wubi wose wono mutemi Manase yawituma.
4 Na ko muno, ku soko vyono yawawulaga i wanhu wono wasina masutya. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu hamba si yamunenela Manase mu nghani yiyi.

5 Ne zinghani zono Yehoyakimu yetuma, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Yuda.
6 Yehoyakimu yakafwa, u mwanagwe Yehoyakimu, yakahala wutemi wakwe.

7 Nayo mutemi wa Misili, hamba si yejelela kanyhi kuwuka mu yisi yakwe. Hambi mutemi wa Babiloni lulo, nha yo yali yayilongoze yisi yose ya Misili, ku wulawa kwi 'kolongo lya Efulata, sunga ko ku sumuka ye yisi ya Misili.

Yehoyakimu mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 35:9-10)

8 Yehoyakimu, yali yaficile myaka yikumi na munana, lono yatemela mu Yuda. Yakatema mu Yelusalemi myezi midatu. U nyina yacemwaga Nehushita, u mwana we cicekulu wa Elinatani munya Yelusalemi.
9 Ninga nayo nhavivilya, yakatyatya musambo mubi wo sogwe, yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu.

10 Na mu vilo vyo wutemi wakwe, mutemi Nebukadineza, yatuma wetumami wakwe, hamonga ne lipuga yiwaha lye wasilikale, wakabita wakayipilima Yelusalemi.
11 Na mwi'pango lilyo, nayo Nebukadineza, yakaza mweneco ku Yelusalemi.
12 Baho, u mutemi Yehoyakimu, hamonga no nyina, ne wanagwe wose, ne wetumami wakwe, ne wetumami wa mwi'kulu, wose wakawa kwilavya kuli Wababiloni. Mu mwaka wo munana wo wutemi wakwe Nebukadineza, yakawa kumusola Yehoyakimu mufungwa.
13 Yakavisola viwutwa vyose vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, na vya mwi'kulu, yakavitegula ku Babiloni. Nebukadineza yakavidumulanga viya vyose vye zahabu, vyono vyetumamilwaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, vyono yavinoza mutemi Solomona, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalonga.
14 Nebukadineza, yakawasola wanhu wa Yelusalemi nha wafungwa, hamonga ne wawaha wose, ne walongola wose, waficile wanhu elufu yikumi liwalo lyawo. Yakawasola sunga ne wanghula wose, hamonga ne watyani we vyuma. Langa yakawasigaza i waciwa du mu yisi ya Yuda.

15 Nebukadineza, yakamusola Yehoyakimu nha mufungwa, yakamutegula ku Babiloni, hamonga no nyina, ne wagolece zakwe, ne wetumami wakwe, ne walongola wa Yuda.
16 Nebukadineza, yakawasola i wanhu wose we vilungo, waficile elufu mupunghati, yakawategula wose ku babiloni, hamonga ne wanghula we milimo, ne watyani we vyuma, waficile elufu monga liwalo lyawo. Ne wanhu wose wawa, wali wo wanhu wono wali vilungo ku milewo.

17 Nebukadineza, yakamutemya Mataniya, u mwihwawe wa Yehoyakimu, yawe mutemi wa Yuda. Nayo Mataniya yepitula litagwa lyakwe, yakecemela litagwa lya Zedekiya.

Zedekiya, mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 36:11-12; Yel 52:1-3)

18 Nayo mutemi Zedekiya, yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na wumonga, lono yatemela mu yisi ya Yuda. Yakatema mu Yelusalemi, myaka yikumi na wumonga. U nyina yacemwaga Hamutali, u mwana we cicekulu wa Yelemiya, munya kwi 'kaya lya Libuna.
19 Ninga mutemi Zedekiya, nayo yaketuma wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, nha vyono yetuma u mutemi Yehoyakimu.
20 Muwaha Mulungu yakakolwa ne likuwo muno muno ku soko ye wanhu wa Yelusalemi na Yuda, sunga nhondo yakawa kuwaleka wali wene.


2 Watemi 25

Wugwe wa Yelusalemi

(2Zin 36:13-21; Yel 52:3-11)

1 Ninga hanyuma yayo, mutemi Zedekiya, yakamubeza mutemi Nebukadineza, mutemi wa Babiloni. Na ku soko yiyo, mutemi Nebukadineza, yakawa kuza ku Yelusalemi, hamonga ne lipuga lyakwe lye wasilikale, mwi'zuwa lye likumi, lya mu mwezi we likumi, wa mu mwaka we licenda, wo wutemi wa Zedekiya. Wakasita nghambi kunze ye likaya, wakazenga magomawakali, wakalipilimya likaya lyose.
2 Wakawa walipilime likaya sunga mu mwaka we likumi na monga.
3 Na mwizuwa lye licenda lya mu mwezi we'kane, wa mu mwaka wuwo, yikawa yagwa nzala, ne nzala yiyo yikawa nzala mbaha muno, sunga ne wanhu wakawa wasina hamba cinji co cose co kulya.
4 Hodu, wanhu wakawa kugakumula majinji ge likaya ho weponye, hamba yiwe wasilikale we Cibabiloni, wali wakali wali baho walipilime likaya, ninga wasilikale wose wa mwi'kaya wakatiza wose ne cilo. Wakakolela mu nzila yo mulango wa haciligati ye majinji mejete, gono gadomile ku busitani yo mutemi, wakatiziza ku lubavu lwe lilolo lya Yoludani.
5 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, wasilikale we Cibabiloni, wakamutyatya mutemi Zedekiya, wakamwibata, lono yali yafika mu yisi yono yitendemaye, behi na Yeliko. Ninga i wasilikale wakwe wose, wakamuleka wakatiza.
6 Wasilikale wawo, wakamusola Zedekiya, wakamutegula ku Libula kuli mutemi Nebukadineza, hambi mwi'pango lilyo, Nebukadineza nha ko yali yali kuko. Nayo mutemi Nebukadineza yakawa kumuling'aniza Zedekiya.
7 Wakawawulajila i wana wa Zedekiya ha meso gakwe. Mwene nayo, Zedekiya wakamukopozola meso gakwe, wakamuwopa ne manyolo, wakawa kumutegula nha mufungwa ku Babiloni.

Wongomele we Nyumba yo Mulungu

(Yel 52:12-23)

8 Na mwi'zuwa lyo mupunghati, lya mu mwezi we'hano, wa mu mwaka we likumi ne'cenda, lyo wutemi wa Nebukadineza mutemi wa Babiloni, Nebuzaladani u mutazi wo mutemi, mono kanyhi yali yo mulongola muwaha we lipuga lye wasilikale wakwe, yakenjila mwi'kaya lya Yelusalemi.
9 Yakayikongoga Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne likaya lye likulu, hamonga sunga ne zikaya ze wanhu watambule wose wa mu Yelusalemi.
10 Nawo wasilikale wakwe wakagakumulanga i majinji ge likaya.
11 Hanyuma yuyo Nebuzaladani, yakawakunza wanhu wose wono wali wakali wasigaliye, hamonga na wono watiziza mu miwoko yo mutemi wa Babiloni, yakawategula wose ku Babiloni.
12 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, yasigaza mumo mu Yuda, i waciwa wono wali wasina sawo, yakawenjiza mu milimo yo wukamisa migunda ya zabibu, hamonga no kulima migunda.

13 Wababiloni, wakagabenanjiza i masumbili ge shaba, ne mikokoteni, vyono vyali vili mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne litanji liwaha lye malenga lye shaba. Wakayisola shaba yose, wakabita nayo ku Babiloni.
14 Wakasola ne zikoleyo ne viya vyono wetumamilaga so kuwusiza mavu mu cikumbicilo, hamonga ne viya vyose vyono wetumamilaga kukoleleza zitala, ne zibakuli zono wambilaga sakami ye zimitugo ze makumbiko, ne zibakuli zono wetumamilaga kufusiza wuvumba, hamonga ne viya vyose vye shaba, vyono wetumamilaga mu wuhembi, mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
15 Wakavisola ne vinhu vyose vye zahabu hamba fweza, hamonga sunga ne vibakuli vidodododo vyose ne visahani vyono wetumamilaga kupalila makala go moto.
16 Ne vinhu vyose vye shaba, vyono mutemi Solomona yavinoza so Nyumba yo Mulungu, nha cinji, masumbili mejete ge shaba, ne mikokoteni, ne litanji liwaha, navyo vyose vyali vina shaba ya wuzito wuwaha muno.
17 Ne masumbili gago mejete, limonga lyali lina wutali wa mita munana, no mukumbo wayo we shaba nawo wutali wa mita monga na lobo. No wutande we vyuma, ne makomamanga mucanyha yo mukumbo wayo kose kose, vyose vyali vya shaba.

Wanhu wa Yuda wakuhamywa, no kutegulwa ku Babiloni

(Yel 52:24-27)

18 Hamonga na zizo, yuyo Nebuzaladani u muwaha we wasilikale walindilizi, yakawasola nha wafungwa, Selaya mukohani muwaha, na Zefaniya mukohani mutazi, hamonga na wanji wadatu i wetumami wawaha wa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
19 Kanyhi na mu wulawa mumo mwi'kaya, yakamusola muwaha yumonga mono yali muwaha we wasilikale, hamonga na wanji wahano, wono wali wo watazi wo mutemi, wono wali wakali wasigaye mwi'kaya, hamonga no muwaha yunji we kejete we wasilikale, mono yali yo mukamisa zinghani zose zo wulugu, hamonga na wanhu wanji makumi mutandatu, we lilumbo.
20 Yuyo Nebuzaladani yakawasola yakawategula kuli mutemi wa Babiloni, mono mwi'pango lilyo yali yali ku Libula,
21 mu yisi ya Hamati. Nayo mutemi Nebukadineza yakawabocela, yakawatowanga, yakawawulaga wose. I wanhu wa Yuda, wakasolwa wakahamywa mu yisi yawo.

Gedaliya u muwaha we yisi ya Yuda

(Yel 40:7-9; 41:1-3)

22 Nebukadineza u mutemi wa Babiloni, yakamuwika Gedaliya u mwana wa Ahikamu, u mwizukulu wa Shafani, yawe yo muwaha we yisi ya Yuda, kanyhi yawe yo mulanjilizi we wanhu wose wono wasigala, wono si wasolwa no kutegulwa ku Babiloni.
23 Nawo i wetumami, hamonga ne wasilikale, wono si weyilavya mu miwoko yo mutemi wa Babiloni, wali weza kuzihulika zizo, hodu, wakawa kubita kwilunza na Gedaliya ku Mizipa. Nawo wetumami wawa wali wo wano: Ishumayeli, u mwana wa Netaniya, na Yohanani u mwana wa Kaleya, na Selaya u mwana wa Tanhumeti munya cikaya ca Netofa, hamonga na Yezaniya munya Maaka.
24 Nayo Gedaliya, yakawalonjela yakatya, “Ane nghamulonjelenyi nghatya vyono, musina soko yo yose yo kuwogopa i wetumami we Cibabiloni. Anye mwikale du mono yisi yiyi, mumwitumamile mutemi wa Babiloni. Nanye zose zomuwelenyi swanu du.”

25 Ninga mu mwezi wo mupunghati wa mu mwaka wuwo, Ishumayeli u mwana wa Netaniya kanyhi yo mwizukulu wa Elishama, we mhuga yo wutemi, yakawasola wanhu yikumi, yakabitana nawo ku Mizipa, wakamuhoma Gedaliya wakamuwulaga. Yakawawulaga hamonga ne Waisilayeli na Wababiloni, wono wali wali hamonga nayo.
26 Hanyuma Waisilayeli, i wagoli ne waciwa, hamonga ne wawaha we wasilikale, wakawuka wakabita ku Misili, hambi nha wawogopaga Wababiloni.

Yehoyakimu yakuwopolelwa ku wulawa mu jela

(Yel 52:31-34)

27 Mu mwaka wono yatemela Evilimelodaki, mutemi wa Babiloni, yakamuwonela lusungu Yehoyakimu, u mutemi wa Yuda, yakamuwopolela yawuce mu jela. Na zizi zejela mwi'zuwa lye makumi mejete na mupunghati, lya mu mwezi we likumi na nyejete, wa mu mwaka we makumi madatu na mupunghati, ku wulawa mwi'zuwa lyono Yehoyakimu yasolelwa nha mufungwa.
28 Yuyo Evilimelodaki, yakamutendela viswanu muno Yehoyakimu, yakamuwika mu mulimo we lilumbo yiwaha muno kuciza watemi wayagwe wose wono wali wafunjigwe hamonga nayo kuko ku Babiloni.
29 Na ku soko yiyo, Yehoyakimu yakakombolwa yayivule myenda yakwe yo wufungwa, yasume kuhanga ndigwa no mutemi ha meza, mu vilo vyakwe vyose vyo wikale wakwe, vyono vyali visigaye.
30 Nayo nhondo zose, yakawa yakupegwa pesa zo kwitumamila mu visakwa vyakwe, mu vilo vyose vyo wikale wakwe.
