2 Watemi 18

Hezekiya, mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 29:1-2; 31:1)

1 Na mu mwaka we kadatu, wo wutemi wa Hosheya u mwana wa Ela, mutemi wa Isilayeli, nayo Hezekiya u mwana wa Ahazi, ho yatemela mu Yuda,
2 lono yali yaficile myaka makumi mejete na mihano. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi, myaka makumi mejete ne cenda. U nyina wa Hezekiya, yacemwaga Abija, u mwana we cicekulu wa Zekaliya.
3 Mwene Hezekiya, yakatyatya musambo muswanu wo mulezi wakwe mutemi Dawudi, yaketuma zono zamwendeza Muwaha Mulungu.
4 Hambi nha yahamwagulanga hanhu hose mu vigongo ha wukumbicilo we milungu yawo. Yakagatulanga masumbili ge mabwe, yakavidumulanga vihwani vyose vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela. Yakalitulanga ne lizoka lye Shaba, lyono Musa yalinoza, lizoka lyono walicemaga Nehusitani. Hambi nha sunga na mu vilo vivyo i wanhu wa Isilayeli, wali wakali wakulipela cogopo no kulifusiza wuvumba.
5 Hezekiya yakawa yamwitumbiye muno muno Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa Isilayeli. Sunga ne wanhu wa Yuda, wali wakali si wanamutuga mutemi mono yali yehwanile no mutemi Hezekiya ku wulawa lono Hezekiya, yali yakali si yanatema, hamba si wamutuga yunji, lono Hezekiya yali yakondya kuwuka mu wutemi.
6 Hambi Hezekiya nha yali mwihuwilwa muno kuli Muwaha Mulungu, hasina hamba lizuwa linji lyono Hezekiya yamubeza Muwaha Mulungu. Ninga mwene, yali yagebate muno i malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu gono yamupela Musa.
7 Holeka nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yakawa yali hamonga nayo, nayo yakacila muno kumoteswa muli milimo yakwe yose. Yakamubeza mutemi wa Ashuli, hamba siyamwitumamila yaye.
8 Yakawahoma Wafilisiti, yakawusola no wikalo wawo, ku wulawa mu citumbi cidodo, sunga mo mu makaya mawaha, na Gaza, hamonga ne yisi yose yono yipilime.

9 Na mu mwaka we kane wo wutemi wa Hezekiya, nawo wali wo mwaka wo mupunghati, wo wutemi wa Hosheya mutemi wa Isilayeli, yakawa kuza Shalimanesa mutemi wa Ashuli, yakawa kuyihoma Isilayeli, yakayipilima Samaliya.
10 Nayo yali yeza kupuluta myaka midatu, hodu nhondo yakawa kuyisola Samaliya. Wo wutya, wali mu mwaka wo mutandatu wo wutemi wa Hezekiya, kanyhi, wali wo mwaka we licenda wo wutemi wa Hosheya.
11 Mutemi wa Ashuli, yakawasola Waisilayeli, yakawategula ku Ashuli nha wafungwa. Yakawawika wanji wekale mwi'kaya lya Hala, na wanji behi ne likolongo lya Haboli mu yisi ya Gozani, na wanji kanyhi mu makaya ga Mediya.

12 Samaliya yikagwa, vyono wene Waisilayeli si wenda kumuhulika Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo, wakacibena ne cilagane cono yacinoza kuli wene, wakagabeza ne malajilizo gono wapegwa na Musa u mwitumami wo Muwaha Mulungu. Wakalema kuzihulika, hamba kuzitumama kose.

Waashuli wakuyogofya Yelusalemi

(2Zin 32:1-19; Isa 36:1-22)

13 Mu mwaka we likumi na yine, wo wutemi wa Hezekiya, hodu yakawa kuza Senakelibu u mutemi wa Ashuli, yakagahoma makaya ga Yuda ge magomawakali, yakagasuma.
14 Nayo mutemi Hezekiya yakatuma munhu kuli Senakelibu, ku Lakishi, yabite yakamulonjele yatye, “Ane sutya, holeka nghukunena wulece kujendelela kuhoma, nane nokupela co cose cono kosaka.” Nayo mutemi Senakelibu yakamupitula Hezekiya yakamutya, “Ale, viswanu, alu yikunojeye wundetele fweza kilo elufu monga, hamonga na zahabu nazo kilo elufu monga.”
15 Nayo, Hezekiya yakawa kumutegulila Senakelibu, zifweza zose zono zali zili mu Nyumba yo Mulungu na zono zali zili mu viwutwa vya mwi'kulu lyo mutemi.
16 Kanyhi yakazinamatula zizahabu zose zono zali mu milango ye Nyumba yo Mulungu, sunga ne zizahabu zono na mweneco yali yazihiliwe mu milango. Yakazisola zose yakamutegulila Senakelibu.
17 Nayo mutemi wa Ashuli, yakawa kutegula lipuga yiwaha lye wasilikale wo wulugu ku wulawa Lakishi, wabite wakamuhome Hezekiya ku Yelusalemi, wakawa wena ne walongola wawo wadatu. Nawo wali weza kufika ku Yelusalemi, wakema behi no muvwilo wono waletaga malenga kulawa mu cilamba ca hacanyha, cono cili mu nzila mbaha yono yibitile sunga ko ku ciwanza ca dobi.
18 Baho, wakawa kumucema mutemi Hezekiya. Ne walongola wa muli wetumami wakwe wo wakabita kwitang'anila nawo, nawo yali Eliyakimu u mwana wa Hilikiya, mono yali yo mwitumami muwaha wa mwi'kulu, na Shebuna mono yali yo mwandici, hamonga na Yowa u mwana wa Asafu mono yali yo mukumbucizi we zinghani.
19 Ale, baho, yumonga wa muli wa walya wetumami wo mutemi wa Ashuli, yakawalonjela nha vyono, mutemi Senakelibu yakusaka yamanye cinhuci cono mutemi Hezekiya yetumbiye.
20 Yakawa kulonga yakutya, “Agwe wukwihwesa nha vyono wuli munghula, kanyhi wina ne lipuga lye wasilikale wono wali vilungo? Alo wukwihwesa nani 'lo mono kokutaza, hono wakondya kuyibeza Ashuli?
21 Hamba wulanjiliye nha vyono Misili yo yokutaza? Ninga kowa nha wukwitumbila ligwami lyono libenece. Nalyo lyomuhoma u munhu wo wose mono kolitumbila. No mutemi Falawo wa Misili vyo yenha, hono munhu yunji yamwitumbile.”

22 Ale du, yu yulya mwitumami we Ciashuli, yakajendelela kulonga yakutya, “Hamba mononjela mutye vyono, anye mumwitumbiye Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu? Alo si yo yuyu du Hezekiya, mono yakawuhodanga wukumbicilo mu vigongo, yakawalonjela wanhu wa Yuda na Yelusalemi, vyono yiwanojeye wawe kuhemba ku cikumbicilo cono cili ku Yelusalemi du?
23 Ale, nghukunena 'lu, cijelane anye na nene, mwi'tagwa lyo mutemi. Nane nomupelenyi fwalasi elufu nyejete, hono mbeka mosuma kuwatuga wanhu wenji wono wosuma kuzipanda!
24 Hamba kosuma nhawule kumuwinga yawuye ne nyuma, yumonga wa muli mwitumami mudodo, wa muli wetumami wo muwaha wangu, hamba wuwe witumbiye vyono, Wamisili wokuletela matuka go wulugu, hamonga ne zifwalasi!
25 Hamba wukwihwesa nha vyono, ane niza ho niyihome yisi yiyi, no kuyongomeza lono si nhumigwe no Muwaha Mulungu? Ninga wumanye vyono, Muwaha Mulungu mweneco yo yanhumile nize niyihome, niyongomeze.”

26 Hanyuma Eliyakimu na Shebuna hamonga na Yowa, wakamutya mwitumami yu yulya, “Alu wayiko cikukunena, wulece kulonga nase mu nonga ye Cihebelaniya, hambi wanhu wose wono wekaya mwi'jinji nha wakuhulika, langa wulonje nase mu nonga ye Ciashuli, nase nha ciyimanyile nonga yiyo.”
27 Nayo yakapitula yakawatya, “Anye mukwihwesa vyono mutemi kanhuma nize nongane na nyenye du hamonga no mutemi? Aa ng'o. Ane nghulongana sunga ne wanhu wose wono wekaye mwi'jinji mucanyha, walye mavi gawo, wang'we ne matunzi gawo, sunga na nyenye.”

28 Hanyuma yu yulya mwitumami yakema wima, yakalonga yazozize muno muno mu nonga ye Cihebelaniya, yakutya, “Ale muhulicize zono mutemi wa Ashuli yakumulonjelenyi!
29 Mwene yakumusugulenyi, vyono mulecele lukulu kumuhuliciza Hezekiya, hambi nha yakumukonjenyi du, hamba si kosumila lukulu kumuponyenyi yaye.
30 Hamba mulece kumuleka yamupepelezenyi, yatye nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu komuponyenyi, hamba kolimila lipuga lye wasilikale we Waashuli ho walece kulisola likaya lyenyu.
31 Holeka mulecele lukulu kumuhuliciza Hezekiya. Mutemi wa Ashuli yakumulajilizenyi mwiyilavye nyo weneco, muwuce mwi'kaya. Nanye mokombolwa mulye zabibu za mu mibici yenyu ye zabibu, hamonga ne zinghuyu mu mibici yenyu ye mikuyu, kanyhi mong'wa ne malenga ga mu masima genyu,
32 sunga kolo mutemi yakondya kuza kumusolenyi, no kumwikazenyi mu yisi yono yihwanile yisi yenyu, yisi yono yina zabibu zono zomupelenyi divayi, hamonga no wuhemba wono mosuma kunoza mikate, yo yisi yono yina mafuta ge mizeyituni, hamonga no wuci. Na hono mwitume nha vyono mutemi yakulajiliza, hodu, hamba si mofwa yaye, langa mokwikala womi du. Holeka mulecele lukulu kumuleceleza Hezekiya yamupuvyenyi, muwe kwihwesa nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu komuponyenyi.
33 Wulenyi, hambi ho yili milungu yo yose, ya mu makungugo go gose, yono yasuma kuziponya zi'si zawo mu miwoko yo mutemi wa Ashuli?
34 Alu milungu yiyo yili hayi 'lu, i milungu ya Hamati, na Alipadi? Hamba milungu ya Sefalivayimu na Hena na Iva? Wulenyi, ho yuli hamba mulungu monga mono yasuma kuyiponya Samaliya?
35 Hamba nili 'lo, lono milungu ye zisi zizi, yono yasuma kuyiponya yisi yawo, ku wulawa mu miwoko yo mutemi wa Ashuli? Alu agwe, cicici cene cono cokunoza, sunga wihwese nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu kosuma kuyiponya Yelusalemi?”

36 Nawo wanhu wakawa wanyamaye du twii, hamba kupitula cinji, nha vyono mutemi Hezekiya yali yakondya kuwalonjela vyono walece kupitula nghani yo yose.
37 Hodu, Eliyakimu na Shebuna, hamonga na Yowa, wakavidemulanga vivwalo vyawo ko wusungu wuwaha, wakabita kumulonjela mutemi Hezekiya zinghani zose zono yalonjile u mwitumami wo mutemi wa Ashuli.
