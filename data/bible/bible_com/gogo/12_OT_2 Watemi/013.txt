2 Watemi 13

Yehowahazi u mutemi wa Isilayeli

1 Na mu mwaka we makumi mejete ne'datu wo wutemi wa Yowashi u mwana wa Ahaziya, lono yali yatemile Yuda, nayo Yehowahazi u mwana wa Yehu, ho yanziza kutema mu Isilayeli. Nayo yakatema mu Samaliya myaka yikumi na mupunghati.
2 Ninga nayo yuyu Yehowahazi, yakehwana Yelobowamu mutemi mono yamulongolela. Nayo yaketuma zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu, yakawasutizya muno Waisilayeli, waketuma wubi. Nayo hamba si yazilecela lukulu zinzila zakwe zimbi yaye.
3 Na ku soko yiyo, Muwaha Mulungu yakawakolelwa likuwo Waisilayeli, yakawa kumukombola Hazayeli mutemi wa Siliya, hamonga na Benihadadi u mwanagwe, wawe kuwasuma Waisilayeli kenji na kenji.
4 Ninga hanyuma, Yehowahazi yakamulomba Muwaha Mulungu. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yaza wona vyono mutemi wa Siliya yawatendelaga vibi Waisilayeli no kuwewonela, hobaho yakamupitula Yehowahazi wulombe wakwe.
5 Muwaha Mulungu yakawaletela Waisilayeli mukombozi, yakawakomboza mu miwoko ye Wasiliya. Nhondo Waisilayeli wakawa kwikala wapolile cinya ha lulongozi.
6 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, si wazileka zimbi zono mutemi Yelobowamu yali yakondya kuwatonga Waisilayeli, wakajendelela du kuzituma, sunga ne cihwani co mulungu we cicekulu Ashela, cikawa vyo cikali cili ku Samaliya.

7 Wasilikale wa Yehowahazi wakafwa wose, hambi mutemi wa Siliya, nha yawawulaga wose wono wali wasigaye, yakawatindya, wakawa nha manghundi. Yehowahazi yakasigala ne wasilikale wapanda fwalasi waficile makumi mahano hodu, hamonga ne matuka go wulugu gali 'kumi, hamonga ne wasilikale wajenda kwa magulu nawo waficile elufu yikumi du hodu.

8 Na zose zono mutemi Yehowahazi yetuma, hamonga ne metumo gakwe mawaha, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.
9 Yehowahazi yakafwa, yakazikwa mu Samaliya. Nayo Yehowashi u mwanagwe, yakahala wutemi wakwe.

Yehowashi, mutemi wa Isilayeli

10 Na mu mwaka we makumi madatu na mupunghati wo wutemi wa Yowashi mutemi wa Yuda, Yehowashi u mwana wa Yehowahazi, ho yanziza nayo kuwa mutemi wa Isilayeli, yakatema mu Samaliya myaka yikumi na mutandatu.
11 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, nayo yaketuma zimbi hali Muwaha Mulungu, yakatyatya musambo mubi wono yaleka mutemi Yelobowamu, mono yawasutizya i wa Isilayeli wetume wubi.
12 Na zose zono yetuma mutemi Yehowashi, hamonga ne milewo, vyono yamuhoma Amaziya u mutemi wa Yuda, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi wa Isilayeli.
13 Yehowashi yakafwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi kuko ku Samaliya. U mwanagwe Yelobowamu we kejete, yakahala wutemi wakwe.

U wufwe wa Elisha

14 U muwizi Elisha, yakawa yatamwa muno wutamwa wono womutegula sunga ku wufwe. Na lono Elisha yali yagonile so mhungo, Yowashi u mutemi wa Yuda yakaza kumulanga. Yali yeza mwagana, yakamulilila muno yakutya, “Gwe baba! Gwe baba! Hambi nha wali wuli mudimi we vilungo muno we wa Isilayeli!”

15 Nayo Elisha yakamutya Yowashi, “Ale ta, wusole wupinde hamonga ne zisonga nyhefu.”
16 Hanyuma kanyhi Elisha yakamutya Yowashi, “Ale, wutawice songa ho wugume.” Nayo mutemi yakayitawika songa mu wupinde, nayo Elisha yakayijeleka miwoko yakwe mu miwoko yo mutemi.
17 Hanyuma muwizi Elisha yakamulonjela mutemi yalidigule lidilisha lyono lilanjile ku Siliya, yakamutya, “Ale lu wugume,” Mutemi yali yeza kuyilecela songa, hobaho nayo muwizi Elisha yakazoza yakutya, “Agwe Yowashi yo gwegwe songa yono Muwaha Mulungu koyitumamila no koyisuma Siliya. Nagwe kowahoma wanhu wa Siliya ku Afeki sunga wuwasume.”

18 Elisha yakamulonjela mutemi Yowashi yasole zisonga zinji yazigume hasi mu muhangha. Nayo mutemi yakaguma hasi mu muhangha kali kadatu, hodu yakawa kuleka.
19 Ne nghani yiyi, yikamukalaza Elisha muno muno, sunga yakawa kumulonjela mutemi yakatya, “Gwe mutemi yali yikunojeye wugume kali kahano hamba mutandatu, nagwe ho wali wusume kuwahoma wanhu, wa Siliya no kuwasumila lukulu, ninga aluno kowasuma kali kadatu hodu.”

20 Nayo Elisha yakafwa, wakamuzika. Nawo we vipuga vye Wamowabi, wakajendelela kuyisola yisi ya Isilayeli mwaka na mwaka.
21 Na mwi'zuwa linji lono wazikaga munhu yunji, wakawona cipuga cinji, hobaho wakawugumila muvimba wo munhu yuyo, mu citenjelo ca Elisha, wene wakawa kutiza. Nawo muvimba wo munhu yuyo, wali weza kupamiza du mafupa ga Elisha mumo mu citenjelo, hobaho u munhu yuyo yakawa kuzuka, yakema wima.

Wulugu we Waisilayeli ne Wasiliya

22 Nayo Hazayeli, mutemi wa Siliya, yakawapela Waisilayeli magazo mawaha muno muno mu cipango co wutemi wa Yehowahazi.
23 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakawa yali nheto no lusungu kuli Waisilayeli. Holeka hamba si yawaleceleza wongomezwe yaye. Langa mwene yawataza du ku soko ye cilagane cakwe kuli Abulahamu na Isaka, na Yakobo. Nayo hamba si yawasemwela lukulu wanhu wakwe.

24 Nayo Hazayeli u mutemi wa Siliya yali yeza kufwa, Benihadadi u mwanagwe, yakahala wutemi wakwe.
25 Ale, baho nhondo Yowashi u mutemi wa Yuda, ho yakawa kumusuma Benihadadi kali kadatu, yakagasola sunga ne makaya gose, gono gali gakondya kusolwa na Benihadadi, mu cipango co mutemi Yehowahazi u sogwe wa Yowashi.
