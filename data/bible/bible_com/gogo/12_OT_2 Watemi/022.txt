2 Watemi 22

Yosiya, u mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 34:1-2)

1 Nayo mutemi Yosiya, yali yaficile myaka munana du lono yakutema mu Yuda. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka makumi madatu na wumonga. U nyina yacemwaga Yedida, u mwana we cicekulu wa Adaya, munya ku cikaya ca Bosikati.
2 Ninga mutemi Yosiya, yetuma zono zamwendeza Muwaha Mulungu. Yakatyatya musambo wo kukuye mutemi Dawudi, yakawukamisiza lukulu wulajilize wo Mulungu.

Citabu co Wulajilize, cikwijela

(2Zin 34:8-28)

3 Nayo Yosiya, mu mwaka we likumi na munana wo wutemi wakwe, yakamusola Shafani u mwana wa Azaliya u mwizukulu wa Meshulamu, u mwandici, yakamutuma ku Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakamulajiliza yakutya,
4 “Wubite kuli Hilikiya u mukohani muwaha, wukatuje liwalo lye zipesa, zono wakohani wazibocelaga, ku wulawa kuli wanhu, ha mulango we Nyumba yo Mulungu.
5 Wumutye mapesa gago yawapele i walanjilizi, wono wakuwulanjilila mulimo wo wunozeleza Nyumba yo Mulungu.
6 Nawo wawo walanjilizi, wo wosuma kuwalipa misahala i wasesi ne wazenji, hamonga ne wasesi we mabwe. Kanyhi wagule ne zimbawo, hamonga ne mabwe, so wunozelezeza Nyumba yo Mulungu.
7 Kanyhi wumulonjele Hilikiya nha vyono, i wanhu wawo wono wakuwulanjilila mulimo wuwo, wo wanhu wehuwilwa muno, holeka howa hasina soko yo mwene, kusaka kumanya liwalo lye zipesa nha vyono wakuzitumamila.”

8 Hanyuma Hilikiya yakamulonjela Shafani u mwandici yakatya. “Nakondya kuciwona citabu co Wulajilize, cili mumo mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.” Hobaho Hilikiya yakawa kumupela Shafani i citabu cico yakacisoma.
9 Hanyuma yakawuya kuli mutemi yakamuloloseza zose yakutya, “I wetumami wako, wakondya kuzisola zipesa zono zali zili mu Nyumba yo Mulungu wawapela i walanjilizi wo mulimo wo wunozeleza Nyumba yo Mulungu.”
10 Hanyuma kanyhi yakatya, “Sunga ne citabu ceneco, Hilikiya kamhela. Naco co acino 'hano nina naco.” Hobaho yakawa kumusomela mutemi kwe'zi waha zono zali zandicigwe mumo.

11 Nayo mutemi Yosiya yali yeza kuzihulika zo Wulajilize zono zandicigwe mumo mu citabu, hobaho yakavidemulanga vivwalo vyakwe ko woga wuwaha muno.
12 Umutemi yakawalajiliza Hilikiya umukohani, na Ahikamu u mwana wa Shafani, na Akeboli u mwana wa Mikaya, na Shefani mweneco u mwandici, hamonga na Asaya u mwitumami wo mutemi, yakawatya,
13 “Alu mubite mukambuzize kuli Muwaha Mulungu, ku soko yangu, na kuli wanhu wose wa Yuda, ze mejizo gono gandicigwe mu citabu cici. Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yacikoleywe likuwo muno, hambi i walezi wetu, nha siwasuma kuzituma zinghani zono zili mu citabu cici zono zali ziwanojeye wetume.”

14 Nawo Hilikiya, hamonga na Ahikamu na Akeboli, na Shafani, na Asaya, wakawa kubita kuli mucekulu yunji muwizi, mono yacemwaga Hulida, mono yali yekaye ha citumbi cono cili behi muno na Yelusalemi. (U mulume wakwe yacemwaga Shalumu u mwana wa Tikiva, kanyhi mwizukulu wa Halihasi, nayo yali yo mulanjilizi we vivwalo vyo witumami mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.) Nawo wali weza kumwagana, wakamuloloseza zose zono zijeye.
15 Nayo yakawatya wawuye kanyhi kuli mutemi wakamulonjele
16 zono Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Ane no Muwaha Mulungu, noyilipiciza Yelusalemi, hamonga ne wanhu wayo wose, nha vyono zandicigwe mu citabu, nha vyono mutemi yazisomile.
17 Hambi i wanhu wawa, nha wakondya kunema, nalu wakuyilavizya milungu yo wulambi makumbiko. Na ku soko yiyo, wakondya kunghoza likuwo yiwaha, ku soko ya zono wetumile. Holeka likuwo lyangu, nilikoleywe muno likaya lya Yelusalemi, hamba si lyocepuka yaye.
18 Nayo mutemi, ane no Muwaha Mulungu we Waisilayeli nghumutya nhavi, ‘Ninga agwe kuzihulika no kuzibocela zinghani zono zandicigwe mu citabu,
19 kanyhi kuwa kunena, no kwinenenyheza go mweneco hali'ne, kanyhi kuwa kuvidemulanga sunga ne vivwalo vyako, akuno wukulila, lono weza kumanya nha vyono ane nokuza niyilipicize Yelusalemi, hamonga ne wanhu wayo wose. Na vyono noyinoza Yelusalemi hawe hanhu ha kogofya, kanyhi halijitwe. Holeka aluno, nawuhulika du wulombe wako,
20 no wulipicizwe wono nali niyilipicize Yelusalemi, alu hodu si noyilipiciza yaye, sunga langa kolo wakondya kufwa. Nane nokunoza wufwe wupolile.’ ”

Nawo wanhu wawo, wakamuziza mutemi Yosiya, wakamuloloseza zizo zose.
