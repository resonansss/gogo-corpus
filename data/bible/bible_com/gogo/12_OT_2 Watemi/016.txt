2 Watemi 16

Ahazi mutemi wa Yuda

(2Zin 28:1-27)

1 Na mu mwaka we likumi na mupunghati wo wutemi wa mutemi wa Isilayeli, u mwana wa Lemaliya, nayo Ahazi u mwana wa Yotamu, ho yanziza kuwa mutemi wa Yuda,
2 lono yali yaficiye myaka makumi mejete. Nayo yakatema mu Yelusalemi myaka yikumi na mutandatu. Ninga nayo hamba si yatyatya musambo muswanu wo sogwe mutemi Dawudi yaye, mwene yaketuma zinghani zimbi, zono si zamwendeza Muwaha Mulungu,
3 yakatyatya musambo we watemi wa Isilayeli. Sunga yakasuma kumulavya u mwanagwe likumbiko lye cilavywa cinyanywe kuli vihwani vye milungu. Yakatyatya mawifyo ge makungugo, wono Muwaha Mulungu yawawinga mu yisi yono Waisilayeli wenjilaga.
4 Ahazi yakajendela kulavya makumbiko, hamonga no kufusa wuvumba mu wukumbicilo mu vigongo na mu matunda, na mu mibeho ye mibici yose miwaha.

5 U mutemi Lezini wa Siliya, hamonga na Peka u mutemi wa Isilayeli, wakabita ku Yelusalemi ho wakayihome, ninga si wamusuma Ahazi yaye.
6 (Na mu vilo vivyo mutemi wa Edomu yakalisola likaya lya Elati, yakawawinga i Wayuda wose wono wekalaga mumo. Ne Waedomu wo wakenjila no kwikala mumo mu Elati sunga nalu.)
7 Nayo Ahazi, yakatuma wanhu kuli Tigilati Pilesa u mutemi wa Ashuli, wabite wakamulonjele nhavi, “Ane nili mwitumami wako, kanyhi nili munhu wako. Holeka nghukunena wuze wunhaze kanyhi, wumhonye mu miwoko yo mutemi wa Siliya hamonga no mutemi wa Isilayeli, wono wakuhoma.”
8 Ahazi yakasola fweza, hamonga ne zahabu yono yali mbikwe mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne viwutwa vya mu nyumba yo mutemi, yakamutegulila cipegwa mutemi Tigilati Pilesa.
9 Nayo mutemi Tigilati Pilesa, baho baho du yakawa kutuluka hamonga ne lipuga lyakwe lye Wasilikale wo wulugu, yakabita yakayihoma Damesiki, yakayisola, yakamuwulaga no mutemi Lezini. Yakawakunza wanhu wose, yakawategula ku Kili.

10 Nayo mutemi Ahazi lono yabitaga ku Damesiki yakamuhinjilize Tigilati Pilesa, u mutemi wa Ashuli, yakawona kuko ku Damesiki cikumbicilo. Hodu mutemi Ahazi yakamutegulila mukohani Uliya, i hwani yayo ye cikumbicilo, hamonga ne lilumbo lyaco.
11 Nayo mukohani Uliya, yakawa kuzenga cikumbicilo cono cihwanile cikumbicilo cico ca ku Damesiki, yakamalila kucizenga lono mutemi Ahazi yali yakali nawuya ku wulawa ku Damesiki.
12 Nayo Ahazi yali yeza kuwuya ku wulawa ku Damesiki, yakagana vyono cikumbicilo cakondya kuzengwa.
13 Holeka hobaho du yakawa kwanza kulavya mu canyha ye cikumbicilo makumbiko ge cilavywa cinyanywe, na vyo wusaje, yakajida ne cilavywa ce divayi, yakayinyunyiza ne sakami ye cilavywa co mulamu mu canyha ye cikumbicilo.
14 Ne cikumbicilo ce shaba, cono cali cisiwanulwe ku so Muwaha Mulungu, cali cili haciligati ye cikumbicilo cico cipya, ne Nyumba yo Mulungu. Ninga mwene Ahazi yakaciwusa baho haciligati yakaciwika ku lubavu lwa kusumuka ye cikumbicilo cakwe cipya.
15 Hanyuma yakawa kumulonjela Uliya yakamutya, “Alu, mwikumbicilo lyangu lili liwaha, mumo mo weze kulavizya vilavywa vinyanywe vyose vya hakali mitondo, hamonga ne vilavywa vyo wusaje vye cinyamihe, kanyhi hamonga ne vilavywa vinyanywe, ne vilavywa vyo wusaje vyo mutemi, hamonga na vye wanhu, na vye vilavywa vye divayi vye wanhu. Kanyhi, weze kunyunyiza mucanyha ye cikumbicilo, i sakami ye mitugo yose yono yowa kulavywa so makumbiko. Langa cikumbicilo ce shaba, wulece kucitumamila, hambi nha nowa kucitumamila nene no mweneco du, so kulondola wutonji kulawa kuli Muwaha Mulungu.”
16 Nayo Uliya mukohani, yaketuma nha vyono mutemi Ahazi yamulajiliza.

17 U mutemi Ahazi, yakadumula mabici ge shaba go wudinghulilo, gono getumamilwaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, yakagawusa ne mabeseni gono gali majelece mucanyha yago. Kanyhi yakalitimya hasi ne litanji lye shaba lye malenga, lyono lyali yijelece mucanyha ye vihwani vye zing'ombe yikumi na nyejete ze shaba, yakaliwika mucanyha ye cibalangu ce mabwe.
18 Kanyhi yakahawusa mu Nyumba yo Mulugu i hanhu ho kujeleka ligoda lyo wutemi, yakawudinda no mulango wono mutemi yenjililaga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, ati ho yamwendeze du Tigilati Pilesa mutemi wa Ashuli.

19 Ne zinghani zono yetuma mutemi Ahazi, zose zandicigwe mu citabu ce zinghomo ze Metumo ge watemi, wa Yuda.
20 Ahazi yakafwa, yakazikwa mu vitenjelo vye watemi mu kaya ya Dawudi. Nayo Hezekiya u mwanagwe yakahala wutemi wakwe.
