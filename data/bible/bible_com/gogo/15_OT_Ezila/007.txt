Ezila 7

Ezila yakufika ha Yelusalemi

1 Myaka minji yali yeza kupuluta ya mu wuteme wo mutemi wa Alitashasita mutemi wa ku Pelisiya, kukawa na munhu yunji yitagwa lyakwe Ezila. Nayo mutemi yakazityatilila zinghani za Ezila ku wulawilila kuli wakukuze sunga ko kuli Haluni, u mukohani muwaha, nha vyono wityatye wayo wukulolosa. Ezila, yali mulelwe na Selaya mwana wa Azaliya, mwana wa Hilikiya.
2 Mwana wa Shalumu u mulelwe na Zadoki mwana wa Ahitubu,
3 Mwana wa Amaliya, mwana wa Azaliya, mwana wa Melayoti,
4 Mwana wa Zelahiya, mwana wa Uzi, mwana wa Buki,
5 Mwana wa Abishuwa, mwana wa Finehasi, mwana wa Eleyezali mwana wa Haluni.

6 Ezila yali yamanyile viswanu malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu we Waisilayeli, gono Muwaha Mulungu yali yamupeye Musa. Ale, vyono Ezila yali mulugutwe no Muwaha Mulungu wakwe, u mutemi muwaha yamupelaga co cose cono Ezila yacisakaga.
7 Mu mwaka wo mupunghati wa mu wuteme wakwe Alitashasita, Ezila yakawuka ku wulawilila ku Babiloni, yakabita ku Yelusalemi, hamonga ne lipuga lye Waisilayeli, ne wakohani Walawi, ne wembi wa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, ne walindilizi we Nyumba yo Mulungu hamonga ne wetumami.

8 Wakatuluka ku wulawilila ku Babiloni, mwi'zuwa lilongozi lya mu mwezi mulongozi.
9 Muwaha Mulungu yakawa yali hamonga nawo sunga ku Yelusalemi mwi'zuwa lilongozi lya mu mwezi we hano.
10 Ezila yali yelavizye mu mikalile yakwe yose, kwiyijiza no kutyatya malajilizo go Mulungu, no kuwafunda wanji wa mu nghungugo ye wanhu wa Isilayeli zo wulajilize ne malajilizo gago.

Lwandiko lono Alitashasita yamupela Ezila

11 Alu lo lwandiko lono Mutemi Alitashasita yakamupela Ezila mukohani, u munghula we malajilizo hamonga no wulajilize vyono Muwaha Mulungu yawapela Waisilayeli.

12 “Lwandiko luluno lukulawilila kuli mutemi muwaha Alitashasita, kuli'gwe Ezila go mukohani, no munghula wo kumanya malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu wa ku wulanga.

13 “Nghulavya wulajilize mu wutemi wangu wose, vyono, wanhu wose wa Isilayeli hamonga ne wakohani ne Walawi wo wose wono wena wuhile wo kubitana nagwe ku Yelusalemi, ale, wakombolwe wubitane nawo.
14 Ane hamonga ne wanyamhala wangu mupunghati cikukutuma wukalanje ku Yelusalemi na ku Yuda, wukawone hono mbeka malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu wako, gono yakupela, wakugatyatya viswanu.
15 Yikunojeye wubite ne vilavywa vye zizahabu na vye zifweza, vyono nene hamonga ne wanyamhala wangu cokupela, ho wukamulavizye Muwaha Mulungu wa Isilayeli, mono i Nyumba yakwe yili ku Yelusalemi.
16 Hamonga na zizo, yikunojeye kanyhi wusoleleze ne zizahabu ne zifweza zono kozisola mu matumbi gose ga mu Babiloni, hamonga ne vilavywa vye Waisilayeli, na vye wakohani wawo, vyono wovilavya so Nyumba yo Mulungu wawo yono yili ku Yelusalemi.
17 Yikunojeye witumamile sawo zizi viswanu, wugule zinghongolo ne zinduwilu, ne zinyana ze zingholo no wuhemba, hamonga ne divayi, ho wuvilavye mu cikumbicilo ca mu Nyumba yo Mulungu ku Yelusalemi.
18 Nayo zahabu hamonga ne zifweza zono zosigala, mosuma kuzitumamila gegwe hamonga ne wayago mu nzila yo yose, yono yikumwendeza Mulungu wenyu.
19 Kanyhi, yikunojeye wumulavizye Mulungu kuko ku Yelusalemi, i viya vyose vyono wupejigwe, ho vitumamilwe mu wuhembi mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.
20 Kanyhi, cinhu co cose cono kocisaka ho witumamile mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, kosuma kucituga mu wulawa mu zisawo zo wutemi.

21 “Nghuwalajiliza i walanjilizi we zisawo zo wutemi, mwi'tumbi lya ku mhelo ye mwezi ye likolongo lya Efulata, vyono, yiwanojeye wamupele Ezila u mukohani no munghula wo kumanya i malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu wa ku wulanga, cinhu co cose cono kosaka yapegwe.
22 Mumupelaje sunga ha nghango ya kilo elufu yidatu na magana mane ga fweza, na kilo elufu yikumi za wuhemba, na lita elufu nyejete za divayi, na kilo elufu nyejete za mafuta, hamonga no mwino wuwe mwinji.
23 Kanyhi munojeye mulanjilile mu wulavye we vinhu vyono Muwaha Mulungu wa ku wulanga yakuvisaka so Nyumba yakwe, Muwaha Mulungu ho yalece kumbonela likuwo, hamonga ne watemi wanji wono wolotema kolo ane nawuka mu wutemi.
24 Kanyhi mulecele likulu kuwatoweza kodi wakohani, hamba Walawi, hamba wembi, hamba walindilizi we Nyumba yo Mulungu, hamonga ne wetumami, hamba kuli munhu wo wose mono yakwituma mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.

25 “Nagwe Ezila, witumamilaje mahala gono wupejigwe no Muwaha Mulungu wako, wuwahagule walongola ne walamuzi, ho wakawateme wanhu wose wono wekaye ku mhelo ye mwezi ye likolongo lya Efulata, wose wono wagamanyile malajilizo go Mulungu wako. Kanyhi wuwafundaje viswanu na wono si wagamanyile, nawo ho wagamanye malajilizo gaga.
26 Hono yunji yalece kugebata no kugatyatya malajilizo go Mulungu wako, hamba i malajilizo go wutemi, ale, munhu yuyo yalipicizwe wulipicizwe wuwaha muno, lunji yawulagwe, hamba yawingwe mu'si, hamba lunji yadindilwe mu jela, hamba yasolelwe zisawo zakwe.”

Ezila yakumulumba Mulungu

27 Ezila yakatya “Muwaha Mulungu we wasogo zetu yalumbwe! Mono yamunozize mutemi, ho yendezwe no mulimo wo kuyizenga Nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu ku Yelusalemi.
28 Na ku soko ye lihewa lyo Mulungu, nakondya kuwutuga wendezwe wuwu wo mutemi, ne wanyamhala wakwe, hamonga ne walongola wakwe we vilungo. Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wangu, kamhela wusume wo kusuma kuwakwega i walongola wenji wa mu zindelo za Isilayeli, sunga ciwuyane nawo ku Yelusalemi.”
