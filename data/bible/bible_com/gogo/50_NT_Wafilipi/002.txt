Wafilipi 2

U winenenyhezi no wuwaha wa Kilisito

1 Alu wulenyi mikalile yenyu ya muli Kilisito, yikumupelenyi vilungo? Hamba wendo wakwe wukumusindamicenyi? Aluno ma, mwina wumonga no Muhe Mono Yelile, kanyhi mukwiwonela lusungu no kwikalalila nyenye wose?
2 Ale muyifisilizizaje i nyemo yangu mu wuwa na mutima monga, wendo monga, nhumbula monga no wuhile wumonga.
3 Mulece kwitumila nghani yo yose mu mutima wo wivu, hamba mu widunhumuzi wa bwete, mweze mwina winenenyhezi nyenye wose, na munhu wo wose yamuwonaje muyagwe yo yamucizize lilumbo sinha mweneco.
4 Halece kuwa na yunji mono yakulondola cambuzi cakwe mweneco du, langa cambuzi co muyagwe.
5 Muweze no wuwe wuwulya wono yali yena nawo Kilisito Yesu.

6 U Mwene ku cihuma, nhondo zose yali Mulungu

ninga si yehwesa vyono u wuwa sawa no Mulungu

ceze nha cinhu cono cidahile kudindimilwa kwa vilungo.

7 Ninga yakenda kuzileka zizo zose mweneco,

yakasola u wuwe wo mwitumami,

yakawa yenha wanhu

yakehwana kotya wanhu.

8 Yakenenenyheza, yakawa na cihuliko sunga ko ku wufwe,

heya, wufwe wa mu musalaba.

9 Holeka u Mulungu yakamukumya muno

yakamupela i litagwa lyono licizize i matagwa gose.

10 Ku so lilumbo lye litagwa lya Yesu ho vinhu

vyose vya ku wulanga,

na vya kono'nze na vya kusi ye yinze,

vifugame hamwande yakwe,

11 Na munhu wo wose yavumile vyono Yesu Kilisito yo Muwaha,

mu lukumyo lo Mulungu Baba.

U wuzelu we yinze

12 Ale anye wendece wangu, lono nali nili hamonga nanye, mwahulikaga nhondo zose, sunga na lulu hambe nili kutali nanye, anye mujendelele ne cihuliko. Mweze kwituma milimo akuno mwina ne cogopo no wucicime ku soko ye mhonelo yenyu,
13 hambi u Mulungu nha yo ketumaga u mulimo muli nyenye nhondo zose, no kumupelenyi u wusume wo kusaka no kufisiliza zose zono zili mu wulunji wo wuhile wakwe mweneco.
14 Mwitumaje zose akuno musina wung'ung'ulice hamba wiwanganile,
15 ho muwe wanhu waswanu, wono wasina yilema, kanyhi mweze watelevu nha wana wo Mulungu wono wekaye mu yinze yiyi yo wubi yono yajiliye. Mobulila muli wene, kotya i zinyelezi vyono zikuwa kuwumulika u wulanga,
16 hono muyibate mukamile i nghani yo wumi. Nane baho ho notujila i nghani yo kwilumbila mwizuwa lilya lya Kilisito, hambi nha ho yolokwijelela vyono u wuhidimbazi wangu hamonga no mulimo wangu si vyajilila bwete.

17 Ninga hamba ndavywe hamonga no wihuwilo wenyu wono lye kumbiko kuli Mulungu, hodu nosecelela muno kanyhi nosangalalana na nyenye wose mu nyemo yiyo.
18 Holeka na nyenye nhavivyo, yiwanojeye musecelele no kusangalalana nane mu nyemo yenyu.

Timoteyo na Epafulodito

19 Mu wilunze no Muwaha, nina witumbilo vyono nosuma kumutuma Timoteyo kuko kwenyu vilo vivi, ho sinyhimale nane kolo nghumanya zenyu.
20 Hambi nha sina munhu yunji mono yenha yuyo, mono kozilanjilila zenyu nha yuyu.
21 Hambi wanji wose nha wakulondola i zawo weneco, si nho kulondola za Yesu Kilisito.
22 Nyenye weneco mumanyile vyono Timoteyo yakamile, u mwene na nene, cihwanile vyono yenha u mwana no sogwe, cali kwitumama hamonga ku soko yo Luganuzi Luswanu.
23 Holeka nitumbiye vyono nomutuma yaze kuko kwenyu nila neza kuwona vyono zowa zinha.
24 Nane nitumbiye muli Muwaha vyono si noleka kuza kuko kwenyu vilo vivi.

25 Ninga nawona cinojele nimutumilenyi ndugu yetu Epafulodito, u mwitumami muyangu no mulewani muyangu, mono mwanhumila, nayo yakanhaza mu visakwa vyangu.
26 Yena wuhile wuwaha wo kumuwonenyi nyenye wose, nayo yakukalala muno nha vyono mwahulika yali yatamigwe.
27 Heya nha yali yatamigwe behi no kufwa. Ninga u Mulungu yamuwonela lusungu, kanyhi si mwene du, langa na nene, ho ndece kuwa mu wukalale wono wucizize.
28 Holeka nghuhila muno nimutumilenyi, nanye kolo mukumuwona kanyhi, ho musecelele, nane u wuzinguce wangu wucepuce.
29 Ale mumubocelaje na nyemo kotya ndugu muli Muwaha. Kanyhi yiwanojeye muwapelaje lilumbo wanhu wono wenha mwene,
30 vyono yali yeza kufwa so mulimo wa Kilisito, ho yasume kumhela u wutazi, wono anye si mwasuma kumhela.
