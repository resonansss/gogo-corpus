Zabuli 67

Lwimbo lwo wulumbe

1 Wuciwonele lusungu go Mulungu, wucilugute,

wucilanje ko wuswanu.

2 I yinze yose ho yimanye wutonji wako,

ne makungugo gose ho gamanye vilungo vye mhonelo yako.

3 Wanhu wose wakulumbe, 'gwe go Mulungu,

makungugo gose wakukumye.

4 Ne makungugo gose gasangalale no kusecelela,

hambi agwe nha wukuwaling'aniza wanhu we ling'ani.

No kugatonga makungugo ga mono'nze.

5 Wanhu wose wakulumbe 'gwe go Mulungu,

ne makungugo gose gakukumye.

6 Yisi yacindya vilimwa vyayo, u Mulungu,

Mulungu wetu kacilugutiza.

7 Mulungu kacipela vye lihewa lyakwe,

holeka wanhu wose,

wa mono'nze yiwanojeye wamogope.
