Zabuli 99

Mulungu yo mutemi we lilumbo

1 Muwaha Mulungu yo yatemile,

nago makungugo gakucicima!

Mwene yekaye mu makelubi,

nayo yinze yikusinjisika!

2 Muwaha Mulungu yo yalawiliciye mu Sayuni,

nayo yali mucanyha ye makungugo gose.

3 Wanhu wose walikumye litagwa lyakwe,

lyono lilawiliciye kanyhi lyo wogofye.

Hambi Mono Yelile, nha yo mweneco du!

4 Gwe go mutemi mono wulawiliciye,

go mwenda wiling'ano!

Kuwukangaza wiling'ano muli Isilayeli,

kuleta wiling'ano ne ling'ani.

5 Mukumyenyi Muwaha Mulungu,

u Mulungu wetu, mugwe funama hali mwene.

Hambi Mono Yelile, nha yo mweneco du!

6 Nawo Musa na Haluni wo wali wakohani wakwe,

nayo Samwili yali yali mu mhuga,

ya wono wamulililaga Muwaha Mulungu,

nayo yakacihulika cililo cawo.

7 Nayo Mulungu yakalongana nawo,

ku wulawa mwi'vunde,

nawo wakawukamisa muno wuhile wakwe,

hamonga ne malajilizo gono yawapela.

8 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wetu,

agwe wawahuliciza. Ninga kuli wene,

agwe wukawa Mulungu munenezi,

hamba yiwe nha wawalipiciza,

ku soko ye masutya gawo.

9 Mukumyenyi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu,

muhembe mwi'tunda lyakwe lyono lyelile!

Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu mwene,

yo Mulungu mono yelile.
