Zabuli 86

Kulomba wutazi

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wuteje likutu lyako wuhulicize,

hambi ne nha sina cinji, kanyhi nili musocele.

2 Holeka wumhonyaje mwi'fwa,

hambi 'ne nha nghogopile gwegwe.

Wumhonye ane no mwitumami wako,

mono nghwitumbiye.

3 Agwe yo gwegwe go Mulungu wangu,

holeka wumbonele lusungu,

ane nghukulilila misi yose.

4 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wusangalaze ane no mwitumami wako,

hambi wulombe wangu nha wuli kwako du gwegwe.

5 Hambi agwe go Muwaha Mulungu, nha wuli muswanu,

kanyhi wuli munenezi nhondo zose,

kanyhi wuli lusungu kuli wose wono wakukulomba.

6 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu wuhulice wulombe wangu,

kanyhi wuhulice ne cililo co wulombe wangu.

7 Nane nghucemelezaga mu vilo vyo wugaye,

hambi 'gwe nha kuhulicizaga.

8 Gwe Muwaha Mulungu,

hasina mulungu mono yehwanile gwegwe,

hamba hasina mono kosuma kuzinoza,

zono agwe kuzinozaga.

9 I makungugo gose gono wagawumba,

gokuza no kukuhemba gwegwe,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nago gokwanza zo wuwaha we litagwa lyako.

10 Hambi nha yo gwegwe mukala wuwaha,

mono kunozaga mapoto mawaha,

agwe yo gwegwe Mulungu we ciwene.

11 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wunyijize ze nzila yako,

nane noyityatya ko wihuwilwa,

wuyitongaje nhumbula yangu yikogope gwegwe.

12 Nane nokulumba kwe nhumbula yangu yose 'gwe,

go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wangu,

nane nokwanza zo wuwaha we litagwa lyako cibitilila.

13 Hambi lusungu lwako kuli'ne nha lwinji muno!

Nagwe kumhonya nhumbula yangu mu citenjelo.

14 Gwe go Mulungu,

wanhu wedunhumuzi wose wamhinjiye nene,

lipuga lye wanhu wawulaji,

wono si wakogopile gwegwe wakusaka wambulaje.

15 Ninga agwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wuli Mulungu wo lusungu, kanyhi wina lihewa,

agwe wuli mukangalizi muno,

kanyhi wumemile lusungu ne mbeka.

16 Wumhitucile kanyhi wumbonele lusungu,

wumhele vilungo vyako wumhonye,

hambi ane nha nghwitumamilaga,

nha vyono yaya yanozaga.

17 Wundajile citanjilo co wuswanu wako,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, awalya wono wasulile,

kolo weza kuwona vyono wakondya kunhaza,

no kusindamika, wene ho wapuwe.
