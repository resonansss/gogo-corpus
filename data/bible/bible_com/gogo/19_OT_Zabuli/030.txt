Zabuli 30

1 Nghukukumya go Muwaha Mulungu,

hambi nha wambusa mu mbali,

hamba si wuwaleceleze walugu wangu wasimanje.

2 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wangu,

nakulilila, nagwe wukamhonya.

3 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wakondya kumhonya mu wulawa mu citenjelo,

wakondya kumhonya no kumbusa mu muhinzo,

wo kugwila mugati ye likombo litali.

4 Mukumyenyi Muwaha Mulungu na nyimbo,

anye mo wanhu wakwe wehuwilwa.

Muwukumbucilaje wele wakwe no kumukumya.

5 I likuwo lyakwe nha lili hadodo vivi du,

langa lusungu lwakwe, lwikalaga cibitilila.

I cililo ciwezaga mu cilo du,

ninga hakali mitondo, yiwezaga nyemo.

6 Mu wumote wangu natya,

“Sinosinjisika yaye!”

7 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

ku soko yo lusungu lwako,

wakondya kunghamya nhe'tunda yiwaha.

Ninga hamba nhavivyo,

wukehundika kutali nane,

sunga nane nghawa kwanza kutuga woga.

8 Nghakulilila gwegwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nghakunena gwegwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

9 “Alu hono nifwe, agwe kotuga cambuzici?

Alu cowa cambuzici hono ngwile mwikombo?

Hamba manghundi lo, gosuma kukukumya?

Hamba kwanza zo wuswanu wako wuwaha?

10 Hulice go Muwaha Mulungu,

wumbonele lusungu,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wunhazaje!”

11 Gwegwe kuwupitula wukalale wangu,

wuwa wuvina wa nyemo,

kumbusiza wukalale wangu wose,

kumhela nyemo mbaha kuciza.

12 Holeka nane sinonyamala twii, aa ng'o,

langa nojendelela kukwimbila nyimbo zo lukumyo.

Nowa kukulumba cibitilila,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wangu.
