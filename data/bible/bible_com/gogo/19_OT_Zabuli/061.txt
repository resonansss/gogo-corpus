Zabuli 61

Kulomba wudimwe

1 Gwe go Mulungu, wuhulice cililo cangu,

wuhulice wulombe wangu.

2 Ku wulawa ku mhelo ye yinze,

nghukulilila ne nhumbula yangu yizilice.

Wunhegule mu canyha ye litalawe liwaha.

3 Hambi agwe, nha yo gwegwe go wutizizo wangu,

kanyhi yo gwegwe go ligomawakali lyangu,

kuli walugu wangu.

4 Alu wunghombole,

nikale mwi'hema lyako cibitilila,

ho nikale mholile hasi ye mabawa gako.

5 Hambi 'gwe go Mulungu,

nha kuhulika zo wihuwizo wangu,

kanyhi kumhela ne ziswanu,

zono wawehuwiza wono wakogopile gwegwe.

6 Wumonjeleze mutemi vilo vyo wikale wakwe,

i myaka yakwe yo wikale yiwe minji.

7 Yateme cibitilila hali'gwe go Mulungu,

u lusungu no wihuwilwa wako, vimudime.

8 Nane baho nhondo,

ho nokwimbila zinyimbo zo lukumyo,

akuno nghuwufisiliza no wihuwizo wangu nhondo zose.
