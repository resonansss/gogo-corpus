Zabuli 124

Mulungu yo mukamisi we wanhu wakwe

1 “Mhali Muwaha Mulungu yali yalece,

kuwa ku lubavu lwetu,

2 Go Isilayeli wulonje wutye, Muwaha Mulungu,

yali yalece kuwa ku lubavu lwetu,

mu cidaha cicilya cono cahungucilwa ne wanhu,

3 mhali wacimele cili womi lululya,

lono wali wacikolelwa makuwo.

4 Baho langa mhali cisolelezwe,

ne lihwazi lye malenga,

Likolongo lyali lizigubicize nhumbula zetu,

5 ne malenga menji,

go mukukunzo gali gacigubicize!”

6 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe,

mono si yacileceleza,

i walugu wetu wacongomeze.

7 Alu capona nha vyono ndeje yisukulaga,

mu mutego we wapelembi mutego wubeneka,

nase capona.

8 Wutazi wetu wulawaga kuli Muwaha Mulungu,

mono yawumba wulanga ne yinze.
