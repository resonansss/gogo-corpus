Zabuli 52

Wuling'anizi wo Mulungu ne lihewa lyakwe

1 Gwe go munhu 'gwe,

socici wukwilumbila wubi wako,

kuli wanhu waswanu?

2 Agwe kwihwesaga zo wongomeza wayago du,

nhondo zose, no lulimi lwako,

lwihwanile liceto likali!

Agwe kubotaga zo wulambi du mapango gose.

3 Kanyhi agwe, wendile zimbi si nhe ziswanu.

Agwe wuwendile u wulambi si nhe mbeka.

4 Gwe go mukonji muwaha,

agwe wendile zinghani zo kuwatamisa wanji.

5 Holeka u Mulungu nayo kokongomeza cibitilila,

kokusola no kukuwusa mu nyumba yako,

kokukula wuwuce mono'nze, mu yisi yo womi.

6 Nawo we ling'ani woziwona zizo,

wokogopa, hanyuma wokuseka wawe kutya,

7 “Lanjenyi ta zono zimwagane munhu ayu!

Mwene si yenda Mulungu yawe wutizizo wakwe,

langa mwene yaketumbila winji we sawo yakwe,

no kuwulondola mumo wihundiko wakwe!”

8 Ninga ane, nihwanile muzeyituni,

wono wudedewaye mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.

Nane nilwitumbilaga lusungu lwakwe,

cibitilila ne cibitilila.

9 Gwe go Mulungu,

nokulumba vilo vyose,

ku soko ye metumo gono wetuma.

Nane nokwanza hali wanhu wako,

we ling'ani, zo wuswanu wako.
