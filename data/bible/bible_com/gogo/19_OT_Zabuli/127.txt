Zabuli 127

Lukumyo so wuswanu wo Mulungu

1 Muwaha Mulungu hono yalece kuyizenga kaya,

wono wakuyizenga wakwigaza bwete.

Muwaha Mulungu hono yalece kulidima likaya,

wono wakulilindiliza, wokwikala meso bwete.

2 Mulimo wenyu wa bwete.

Mwilawaga kwinuka hakali mitondo,

no kucela kugona, no kulya ndigwa ye lituguti.

Hambi Mulungu nha kawapelaga vyose,

wono yawendile lono wali mu nhongo.

3 I wana co cipegwa,

cono Muwaha Mulungu kacipelaga nawo,

co cipegwa cakwe kuli 'se.

4 Wehwanile nhe zisonga zono musilikale yebate,

vyo wenha wawo wana wono munhu kawatugaga,

lono yakali muzelelo.

5 Holeka mumoti munhu,

mono yamemizye songa nyinji,

hambi nha si kosumilwa lukulu,

kolo yakusitakwa,

no mulugu wakwe mu mahakama.
