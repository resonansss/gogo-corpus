Zabuli 93

Mulungu mutemi

1 U Muwaha Mulungu yatemile yena ne nhojo yakwe!

Muwaha Mulungu yevwicile nhojo ne vilungo!

Nayo yayisimya yinze hanhu hayo,

hamba si yosinjisicila lukulu yaye.

2 Ne ligoda lyako lyo wutemi,

lyawikwa ku wulawa katali.

Nagwe wali wuli baho,

ku wulawa cibitilila ne cibitilila.

3 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

visolo vye lilenga vinuye mazi gayo, heya,

vinuye mazi hamonga no mukukunzo wayo.

4 Muwaha Mulungu, yena wutemi wuwaha ku wulanga,

yali vilungo yacizize mawingo ge lilenga.

5 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wulajilize wako wo we cibitilila,

ne Nyumba yako, yelile cibitilila ne cibitilila.
