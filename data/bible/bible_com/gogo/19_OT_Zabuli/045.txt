Zabuli 45

Lwimbo lo kumukumya Mutemi

1 Nhumbula yangu yimemile mahweso maswanu,

nane nghumutulila Mutemi lwimbo lwangu,

no lulimi lwangu lwihwanile,

nhe kalamu yo mwandici munghula.

2 Agwe wuli muswanu kuciza wanhu wose ne zinghani zako,

zimemile wulonji wuswanu, nayo Mulungu,

kakulugutiza cibitilila.

3 Gwe go citung'ati, wuwikanize mumaje wako,

agwe yo gwegwe mono wuli we nhojo,

kanyhi yo gwegwe munghalo wuwaha wose.

4 Wujendelele kumwande so lukumyo lwako ko ku wusume.

Wuwutetele wiling'ano ne mbeka.

Ne vilungo vyako vyokupela wusume wuwaha muno.

5 Ne zisonga zako nha nghali muno muno,

nazo zipuguzaga mu zinhumbula ze walugu wo mutemi,

sunga ne makungugo gakugwa hasi yako.

6 Ne ligoda lyako lyo wutemi,

lye 'goda lyo Mulungu,

likalaga cibitilila ne cibitilila.

Nagwe wukuwatema wanhu wako ko wiling'ano.

7 Agwe mbeka wendile wiling'ano, ninga si wendile wubi,

na ku soko yiyo, holeka Mulungu,

u Mulungu wako yakakuhagula,

nayo yakakujidila nyemo mbaha muno kuciza wayago.

8 Ne vivwalo vyako vina mhunjile,

wudi ne manemane,

ne watowa masumbi nawo wakukwimbila,

mu zinyumba zono zihambigwe kwe zimbali ze zinhembo.

9 Ne wana we cicekulu we watemi, wo wamonga,

na wawo wono wakukuhinjiliza, no mugolece wo wutemi,

yemile ku muwoko wako wo kulume,

yavwalile wihambo we zahabu za Ofili.

10 Wuhulicize ta go mwana we cicekulu wihwese!

Wuteje likutu lyako,

wusemwele lukulu i wanhu wako,

hamonga na we mhuga yako wose.

11 Lanje ta wuswanu wako vyono wukumwendeza mutemi,

mwene yo mulume wako,

holeka yikunojeye wumuhulice.

12 Ne wanhu wanya Tulo,

nawo wokuletela vipegwa,

sunga ne wagoli wose wokwenda wavumilwe nagwe.

13 Umwana we cicekulu wo mutemi yakuza,

lanje vyono muswanu ta!

Civwalo cakwe cihonwe kwa mhuti za zahabu.

14 Yakutegulwa kuli mutemi,

yavwalile civwalo ce mawala menji,

akuno yakusindicizwa ne wahinza wayagwe,

nawo wakutegulwa kuli mutemi nhavivilya.

15 Nawo wakubita kuko wena nyemo no wusangalale,

no kwinjila mwi'kulu lyo Mutemi.

16 Gwe go mutemi, i wanago,

wohalila ligoda lyo wutemi lye walezi wako,

nagwe kowatemya mononze yose.

17 Ne lilumbo lyako lyomanyika,

ku zindelo zose vilo vyose,

ne makungugo gose gokukumya cibitilila.
