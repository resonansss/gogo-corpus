Zabuli 56

Kumwitumbila Mulungu

1 Gwe go Mulungu, wumbonelaje lusungu,

hambi wanhu wa nha wakusaka wambonele,

madaha gose walugu wangu nha wamhilime.

2 Misi yose walugu wangu wakunhowanga,

nawo lu ho wenji muno muno,

wawo wono wakwihoma nane.

3 Gwe go Mulungu u Mudaha Vyose lono woga wunyibate,

ane nghwitumbiye gwegwe.

4 Nane nghumulumba Mulungu,

ku soko ye nghani yakwe,

holeka nimwitumbiye du Mulungu,

hamba si nghogopa yaye.

Hambi munhu nha musocele konhenda cici?

5 Misi yose wekalaga wakwijiliza zinghani zangu,

hambi mahweso gawo gose,

nha gali mu wutamisa nene du.

6 Wetang'anilaga ho waziwice zawo hamonga,

ho wasepe 'ne, wazisunjililaga zose zono nitumaga,

wakusepa ho wambulaje.

7 Gwe go Mulungu,

wuwalipaje wawo i wabi mu nghango yo wubi wawo,

wuwagwise hasi kwe likuwo lyako.

8 Agwe wuwumanyile wusukangwe wangu,

wuwicile cikumbucizo ce mahozi gangu.

Wule, zose si zili mu citabu cako?

9 Na lono nghukulomba wutazi wako,

baho ne walugu wangu wawuyaga ku nyuma.

Nane manyiye lukulu,

vyono Mulungu yali hamonga nane.

10 Nane nghumulumba Mulungu ku so nghani yakwe,

nghumulumba Muwaha Mulungu,

ku so wutazi we nghani yakwe.

11 Nimwitumbiye Mulungu,

hamba si nghogopa cinji yaye,

Hambi munhu konhenda cici?

12 Gwe go Mulungu, nowufisiliza wihuwizo wangu kuli'gwe,

nane nokulavizya vilavywa vye malumbo.

13 Hambi nha wamhonya mwi'fwa,

heya kunghamisa ho ndece kugwa hasi,

ho njendelele kuwa hali gwegwe go Mulungu,

nikale mu wuzelu we womi.
