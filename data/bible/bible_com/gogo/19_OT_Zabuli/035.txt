Zabuli 35

Kulomba wutazi

(Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wisumanizaje nawo wono wakwisumaniza nane,

wulewane nawo wono cikulewana nane.

2 Wibate ngula hamonga ne cigula,

winuce wuze wunhaze.

3 Wusole mugoha ne nhemo yako,

wihome na wono wakunhyatilila.

Wunonjele nha vyono komhonya.

4 Wawo wono wakulondola wambulaje,

wawone cevu, wapuwe.

Nawo wono wena wuhile wubi kuli'ne wakolwe cevu,

wawuye ne nyuma.

5 Wehwane nhe mawaje vyono gapemheluswaga ne mbeho,

lono malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu yakuwawinga!

6 Ne nzila yawo yeze yimemile citi ne cinheleza,

lono malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu yakuwawinga.

7 Hambi nha wali wanhejile du mbula soko,

kanyhi wakahimbila yikweme, akuno ho wasina soko.

8 Holeka lece wongomele wuwagane musunho,

webatwe mu mitego yawo weneco, wagwile mwi'kombo lyawo weneco!

9 Langa baho nane nhondo,

nomusangalila Muwaha Mulungu,

nosangalala ku soko vyono yamhonya.

10 Nane, ko mutima wangu wose nomutya Muwaha Mulungu,

“Agwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

mbeka hasina mono yehwanile gwegwe!

Agwe kuwaponyaga i wasocele mu miwoko ya wono wali vilungo,

i waciwa nawo kuwaponyaga mu miwoko ye wahizi.”

11 I wanhu wabi wakuhomelela ane zo wulambi,

wakusitaka kuli zinghani zono ane si nizimanyile yaye.

12 Wene wakundipa zimbi kuli zangu ziswanu,

nane nekala nili mu wuciwa,

13 Ninga wene lono wali watamigwe,

ane nghavwala magunila,

nghulajila vyono nghalaye,

neyigaza ko wuleka kulya ndigwa.

Nghawa kulomba akuno ninamizye mutwe wangu.

14 Nghawa nha nghumulilila mwendece wangu,

hamba ndugu yangu,

nghawa nha munhu mono yakulila ku soko yo wufwe wo nyina.

15 Lono nali nagwa, wene wakawa kusecelela,

wakawa kwikongholela hamonga, wakusimanga,

Ne wanhu wono si niwamanyile, nawo wakanhowanga,

hasina hamba monga mono yasuma kuwemila.

16 I wanhu wono wendile kuwaseka we vilema,

nawo wakanghang'anhilila meno gawo ko lusulo.

17 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

kojendelela kuwalanga du sunga nili?

Wumhonyaje ne milewo yawo,

wumhonyaje wumi wangu kuli simba zizo.

18 Nane nokulumbila mu wihangulize wuwaha we wanhu,

nokukumizya mwi'puga yiwaha lye wanhu.

19 Wulece kuwaleceleza wawo walugu wangu,

wajendelele kusimanga,

na wawo wono wakusula 'ne bwete,

ho wawusangalalile wugaye wangu.

20 Sunga ne zinghani zono wakuzilonga,

si nghani zono zikulawa mu wendo yaye,

wakutula zinghani zo wulambi,

kuli wono wendile mulamu.

21 Ati wakusitaka wazozize du wakutya,

“Ale! Ale! Caziwona zono witumile!”

22 Nagwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wakondya kuyiwona nghani yiyo.

Ale, lu, wulece kunyamala,

go Muwaha Mulungu,

kanyhi wulece kwikala kutali nane.

23 Wuze go Muwaha Mulungu wunhetele,

wuze go Mulungu wangu,

wuzilanje, zinghani zangu.

24 Gwe Muwaha Mulungu,

go Mulungu wangu,

yo gwegwe du mono wuli ling'ani,

holeka wuning'anizaje sina wusutye,

kanyhi wulece kuwaleceleza i walugu wangu wasimanje.

25 Wulece kuwaleceleza walonje watye, “Alu he,

si nhavi vyo cali kuhila!”

Hamba lunji watye, “Alu cakondya kumumalila!”

26 Walekaje 'gwe wawo wono wakusecelela,

so wutamiswe wangu,

wasumwe wose, wakolwe cevu.

Na wawo wose wono wakwiwona nha waswanu wanyhizize,

wakolwe cevu, kanyhi wapuwe.

27 Awo langa wono wakusangalala,

ku soko yo wiling'ano wangu,

waweze na nyemo nyinji,

wewuyajize kulonga kali kenji watye,

“U Muwaha Mulungu yo Muwaha muno!

Kendezwaga no wumoteswe wo mwitumami wakwe.”

28 Ale, baho nane langa, ho nokwanziza zo wuswanu wako,

nane nojendelela kuzilonga zo lukumyo lwako, misi yose.
