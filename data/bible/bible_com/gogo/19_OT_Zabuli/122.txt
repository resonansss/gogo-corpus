Zabuli 122

Lukumyo kuli Yelusalemi

1 Nasangalala waza kunonjela wakutya,

“Ale cibite ku kaya yo Muwaha Mulungu.”

2 Alu ase cakondya kufika,

nalu cimile ha milango ya Yelusalemi!

3 Gwe Yelusalemi, go likaya lyono

lizenjigwe wuzengwe wuswanu muno,

kanyhi wukamile!

4 Ale, mumo mo makungugo gakuza, heya,

makungugo ga Isilayeli,

gakuza gawe kumulumba Muwaha Mulungu,

nha vyono yalajiliza.

5 Na mumo mo mogali mahakama go,

kuwaling'aniziza wanhu mu ling'ani,

i mahakama ge nyumba ye citemi ya Dawudi.

6 Holeka muyilombele Yelusalemi mulamu,

“Nawo wose wono wakwendile, wamoteswe.

7 Mwi'kumbo lyako, muweze no mulamu,

na ku zinyumba zako kuweze kupolile!”

8 Ku soko ye wayangu hamonga ne wandugu zangu,

holeka nghulomba mulamu wuweze muli gwegwe,

agwe go Yelusalemi!

9 Na ku soko ye nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu wetu,

holeka nghukulombela wutuje wumoteswe!
