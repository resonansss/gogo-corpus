Zabuli 88

Kulomba wutazi

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go mhonelo yangu,

ane nikalaga du nghulila misi yose,

na ku cilo, nghuzizaga gwegwe.

2 Nghunena wulombe wangu wukuficile,

wuhulice ne cililo cangu, wunhaze,

3 Magayo menji gono gakondya kunyagana,

sunga neza nili behi no kufwa.

4 Ne vilungo vyangu vyose vyakondya kumalika,

nehwana na wose wono wakubita kwi'fwa.

5 Nakondya kulekwa muli wono wafwiye, nehwana,

na wono wawulagwe, wono wali mu vitenjelo,

wono agwe si kuwakumbucilaga kanyhi,

wono wali kutali no wudimi wako.

6 Wanhaga mu cisolo ce citenjelo,

mono mowuli wutitu, ne cisolo citali.

7 Likuwo lyako limvunile muno muno,

nagwe wunhyatiliye ne mawingo gayo.

8 Wanhemanula ne wendece wangu,

kunoza niwa nha cinhu cavu hali wene.

Alu mbopigwe, hamba si nosuma kwiwopolela.

9 Sunga ne meso gangu gatunduwaye,

ku soko yo wukalale.

Nane nghukulilila nhondo zose,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

akuno nghukwalila ne maganza gangu.

10 Wule, kosuma 'mbi kuwanozeza mapoto wono wafwa?

Ma, wono wafwiye wosuma kwinuka, ho wakukumye?

11 No lusungu lwako 'mbi lotambulwa mu citenjelo?

Hamba ne mbeka yako ma,

hambi yotambulwa kono kowali wafu?

12 Ma, i mapoto gako,

gowa gamanyice 'mbi mumo mu wutitu?

Hamba wuswanu wako nawo,

wowa wumanyice kuli wawo wono agwe wawasemwa?

13 Ninga ane nghulililaga agwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nane nghuletelaga wulombe wangu,

hakali mitondo nhondo zose.

14 Alu so cici wukunhaga,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu wangu,

alo socici wukundeka?

15 Hambi ku wulawa wudodo wangu nha ngazagwa,

mbikanizizwe kufwa,

sunga nalu natimba so wugazwe wo wulipicize wako.

16 Likuwo lyako likali,

alu lyanyagana no wutowe wo wogofye wako,

alu wukunyongomeza.

17 Vyanhejela nha malenga misi yose,

vimhilime vyose hamonga.

18 Kunhemanula ne wendece wangu,

sunga ne wayangu wose,

alu'ne nekala mu citi du, sina yunji kanyhi.
