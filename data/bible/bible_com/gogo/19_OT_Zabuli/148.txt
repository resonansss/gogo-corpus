Zabuli 148

Yinze yose yimulumbo Mulungu

1 Mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu!

Ale, mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu,

wulawa ku wulanga,

mumulumbe anye mono mwikaye ku canyha.

2 Mumulumbe nanye nyo wamalayika wakwe,

mumulumbe nanye nyo lipuga lyose lya ku wulanga.

3 Mumulumbe nanye nyo mwezi ne lizuwa,

Mumulumbe nanye nyo zinyelezi,

zose zono zikung'alala.

4 Mumulumbe nanye nyo wulanga ku canyha,

sunga ne malenga gono gali ku canyha yo wulanga.

5 Litagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu linojeye lilumbwe muno,

hambi mwene nha yo yalajiliza na vyose vikawumbwa.

6 Nayo yakavimya hanhu hayo sunga cibitilila,

navyo hamba si vyosuma kumubedegala yaye.

7 Mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu wulawa kono'nze,

nanye nyo visolo vye lilenga,

hamonga ne mazoka gayo.

8 Mumulumbe nanye nyo moto,

ne mvula ye mabwe hamonga ne seluji,

hamonga nagwe go mbeho yo mukukunzo,

mono wibate wulajilize wakwe.

9 Mumulumbe nanye nyo matunda ne vigongo,

sunga nanye nyo mibici,

ye mikalo hamonga ne mbago yose!

10 Mumulumbe nanye nyo vilimu vya mu mbago,

sunga nanye nyo mitugo yose,

sunga nanye nyo viwumbwa vyono vitambalaga hasi,

hamonga ne zindeje zose!

11 Mumulumbe nanye nyo watemi,

we'nze hamonga ne wanhu wose walongola,

ne walamuzi wose we'nze.

12 Mumulumbe nanye nyo wazelelo ne wahinza,

nyo wakombi hamonga ne wana wadodo!

13 Mulilumbe litagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu,

anye nyo wanhu wose hambi litagwa lyakwe,

nha lilawiliciye, no lukumyo lwakwe,

lucizize kono'nze na ku wulanga.

14 Yawapela vilungo i wanhu wakwe,

wose ho wamulumbe mwene,

ne wana wa Isilayeli wono yawendile.

Mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu!
