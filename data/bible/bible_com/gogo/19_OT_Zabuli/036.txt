Zabuli 36

U wubi wo wanhu

1 U wubi wulonganaga no munyo wubi,

mu nhumbula yakwe mugati.

Wukutya, uwogopo Mulungu musina muli mwene.

2 Na vyono kekalaga yakwihwesa zo wiwoni wakwe du,

holeka kehwesaga vyono wavu wakwe,

si womanyika no kulijitwa no Mulungu.

3 Mwene kalongaga nghani za wavu zose kanyhi za wulambi,

yaleka ku witumamila mahala no kwituma ziswanu.

4 Na lono yagonile hose,

mwene kehwesaga wituma zimbi du,

sunga ne mijendele yakwe yose,

musina hamba yinji yono swanu, kanyhi,

sikasumaga kwilega ne nghani yo yose yono 'mbi.

U wuswanu wo Mulungu

5 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

lusungu lwako lutandaye sunga ko ku wulanga,

no wihuwilwa wako wuficile sunga ko ku mavunde.

6 Ne ning'aniza yako,

yihwanile visolo vye lilenga.

Agwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

kuwadimaga wanhu wose hamonga ne vilimu vyose.

7 No lusungu lwako go Muwaha Mulungu, luwaha muno!

Ne wanhu watizizaga mu mubeho we mabawa gako.

8 Nagwe kuwegutyaga no wugoli wa mu nyumba yako,

kuwang'wesaga mwikolongo lyo wuswanu wako.

9 Agwe yo gwegwe mwamulizo wo wumi,

na mu wuzelu wako, nase cikuwona wuzelu.

10 Wujendelele kuwawonela lusungu wose wono wakumanyile,

kanyhi wujendelele kuwa muswanu, kuli we ling'ani.

11 Wulece kulumbila lukulu,

wanhu wedunhumuzi wahungucile,

hamba i wanhu wabi waswe wakumasha.

12 He! Kumbe i wetuma zimbi wakondya kugwa,

wagwiswa hasi, hamba siwokwinucila lukulu.
