Zabuli 28

Kulomba wutazi

(Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Nghukulilila gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

agwe gwe 'talawe lyo wutizizo wangu!

Wuhulicizaje hamba wulece kuwa nha si wukuhulika,

ho ndece kwihwana na wono wakubita ku yisi ye wafu.

2 Wuhulicize lono nghukulomba wutazi,

lono nghuyinula miwoko yangu,

niyidomizye hono helile hako.

3 Wulece kuning'aniza hamonga ne wahodanji,

hamonga ne wanhu wetuma zimbi,

iwanhu wono walongaga ziswanu,

ninga mu zinhumbula zawo wamemile lusulo.

4 Wuwalipicize nha vyono genha metumo gawo,

nha vyono genha masutya gono wetumile.

Wuwalipicize mu nghango ye metumo gawo,

wuwatendele nha vyono yiwanojeye.

5 Hambi wene nha si wakugacelelela,

i metumo go Muwaha Mulungu,

hamba kuzimanya kose, zono yetumile.

Holeka ku soko yiyo, nayo kowamwagula,

hamba si kowazenga yaye cibitilila.

6 U lukumyo lweze kuli Muwaha Mulungu,

hambi nha yawuhulika wulombe wangu.

7 U Muwaha Mulungu vyo vilungo vyangu,

ne ngula yangu,

nane nimwitumbiye mwene kwe nhumbula yangu yose.

U mwene kanhazaga no kusangalaza,

holeka nane nomukumya, no kumwimbila,

zinyimbo zo wusangalale.

8 U Muwaha Mulungu, vyo vilungo vye wanhu wakwe,

nayo kamucitilaga no kumuponya u mutemi wakwe muhagulwa.

9 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wuwaponye wanhu wako,

wuwalugute i wanhu wono yo sawo yako,

wuwadime no kuwacitila sunga cibitilila.
