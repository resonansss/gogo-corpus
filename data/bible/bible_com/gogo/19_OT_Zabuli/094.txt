Zabuli 94

Mulungu yo muling'anizi wa wose

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

go Mulungu mulipicizi, ale,

wulajile likuwo lyako!

2 Ale, winukaje wime wima agwe,

go muling'anizi we wanhu wose,

wuwalipicize wose,

i wedunhumuzi wono wasina cihuliko,

nha vyono yiwanojeye!

3 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu alu i wabi,

wojendelela kwikumya sunga nili lo?

Alu wajendelele kwiyikumya sunga nili?

4 Alu i wabi wojendelela kwikumya so wubi?

Hamba wokwidunhumula no kwikumya sunga nili?

5 Wakuwongomeza wanhu wako 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

no kuwewonela wawo wono yo sawo yako.

6 Wakuwawulaga i wacekulu wafwile,

hamonga ne wajenzi,

wakuwawulaga ne wana wono wasina walezi!

7 Wakutya ti, “Muwaha Mulungu si yakuwona yaye,

u Mulungu wa Yakobo siyamanyile yaye!”

8 Anye mo walele, mwihwesaje ta hadodo!

Anye nanye mono musina mahala,

alu ho motuga nili wizuce?

9 Alu mukwihwesa mono yalinoza likutu,

hongo si kosuma kuhulika yaye?

Hamba mono yaliwumba liso,

alu hongo si kosuma kuwona ng'o?

10 Mono yagatemile makungugo,

alu hongo si kosuma kulipiciza?

Hamba mono kafundaga wanhu,

wukwihwesa yasina wizuce lo?

11 U Muwaha Mulungu,

yagamanyile mahweso ge wanhu,

yamanyile vyono si gakwiliye hamba nhavi bwete.

12 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

yo mumoti munhu mono kumufundaga cogopo,

munhu mono kumwijizaga wulajilize wako.

13 Hambi nha komuceheza mu vilo vyo wugaye,

ninga i wanhu wabi wakuhimbilwa yikweme.

14 Muwaha Mulungu,

si kowalecela lukulu wanhu wakwe,

hamba si kowasemwa yaye wono yo sawo yakwe.

15 Holeka sunga ne ning'aniza,

yowa ye ling'ani,

nawo we ling'ani woyityatya.

16 Hamba nani 'mbi mono yema,

ho yanhaze kuli wetuma zimbi?

Hamba nani mono yema ku lubavu lwangu,

yanhaze kuli wetuma zo wubi?

17 Na hono Muwaha Mulungu yali yalece kunhaza,

mhali nakondya kubita ku yisi ye wafu.

18 Nali neza kutya, “Neza kugwa,”

lusungu lwako 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

lukasimya'ne wima.

19 No wuhangawe wa mu nhumbula yangu,

lono wukucila,

agwe kunhuzaga kanyhi kusangalazaga.

20 Agwe si kokwilumbaniza ne mahakimu,

wono si watyatyaga ze ling'ani,

wono wawuwikaga wulajilize mu nzila,

yo kuwutetela wubi.

21 Wene wawo welumbanizizaga mu zinzila,

zo kuwongomezeza wanhu we ling'ani,

no kuwaling'aniza wufwe,

wanhu wono wasina wusutye.

22 Ninga u Muwaha Mulungu wangu,

yo kanyhitilaga muli zose,

kanyhi wo wihundiko wangu.

23 Nayo kowagomolezeza weneco wubi wawo,

kanyhi kowapatila lukulu ku soko yo wubi wawo.

Heya, Muwaha Mulungu,

u Mulungu wetu, kowongomeza!
