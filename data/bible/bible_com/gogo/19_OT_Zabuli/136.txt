Zabuli 136

Lwimbo lo wulumbe

1 Mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu,

hambi nha muswanu, hambi lusungu lwakwe,

nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

2 Mumulumbe Mulungu,

mono Yalawiliciye mu milungu yose,

hambi lusungu lwakwe,

nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

3 Mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu,

mono yacizize wawaha wose,

hambi lusungu lwakwe,

nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

4 Yo mweneco yali wene du,

mono ketumaga mapoto mawaha,

hambi lusungu lwakwe,

nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

5 Yawuwumba wulanga ko wizuce wakwe mweneco,

hambi lusungu lwakwe, nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

6 Yayitandaza yisi,

mucanyha ye visolo vye malenga,

hambi lusungu lwakwe, nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

7 Yawumba lizuwa, no mwezi, hamonga ne zinyelezi,

hambi lusungu lwakwe, nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

8 Lizuwa liteme mu misi, hambi lusungu lwakwe,

nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

9 Mwezi ne zinyelezi ziteme ku cilo,

hambi lusungu lwakwe,

nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

10 Yawawulaga i wana wose,

we Wamisili iwalongozi kulelwa,

hambi lusungu lwakwe, nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

11 Yakawawusa wanhu wa Isilayeli ku Misili,

hambi lusungu lwakwe, nha lwikalaga cibitilila,

12 ko muwoko wakwe we vilungo,

hamonga no wuwaha wakwe,

hambi lusungu lwakwe,

nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

13 Yalitemanula lilenga lya Shamu,

hambi lusungu lwakwe,

nha lwikalaga cibitilila,

14 yakawakoza mumo wanhu wa Isilayeli,

hambi lusungu lwakwe, nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

15 Ninga yakawongomezeza mumo Falawo,

hamonga ne wasilikale wakwe,

hambi lusungu lwakwe, nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

16 Yawatonga wanhu wakwe mu nyika,

hambi lusungu lwakwe, nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

17 Yawawulaga watemi we vilungo,

hambi lusungu lwakwe, nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

18 Yawawulaga watemi we lilumbo,

hambi lusungu lwakwe, nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

19 Yakamuwulaga sunga na Sihoni,

u mutemi we Waamoli, hambi lusungu lwakwe,

nha lwikalaga cibitilila,

20 sunga na Ogu nhavivyo nayo,

u mutemi wa Bashani, hambi lusungu lwakwe,

nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

21 Yakazisola zisi zawo, yakawapela wanhu wakwe,

hambi lusungu lwakwe, nha lwikalaga cibitilila,

22 ho ziwe sawo ya Isilayeli u mwitumami wakwe,

hambi lusungu lwakwe, nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

23 Mwene yacikumbucila mwidaha lyo wusocele wetu,

hambi lusungu lwakwe nha lwikalaga cibitilila,

24 yakaciponya kuli walugu wetu,

hambi lusungu lwakwe, nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

25 Mwene kavilisaga ndigwa viwumbwa vyumi vyose,

hambi lusungu lwakwe nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

26 Mumulumbe Mulungu wa ku wulanga,

hambi lusungu lwakwe, nha lwikalaga cibitilila.
