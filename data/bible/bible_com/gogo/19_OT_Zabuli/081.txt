Zabuli 81

Lwimbo lwe sikukuu

1 Mwimbilenyi zo lukumyo, u Mulungu wa Yakobo,

mumusangalalile Mulungu vyo vilungo vyetu.

2 Mwimbilenyi lwimbo lwe nyemo,

towenyi ne zing'oma,

towenyi masumbi ne zindonondo,

ze mazi maswanu.

3 Towenyi ndulele mu ndawo yo mwezi

na lono mwezi wukutula,

mu sikukuu yetu.

4 Ali lyo lilajilizo kuli Isilayeli,

na yiyo yo nghong'onheza yo Mulungu wa Yakobo.

5 Mulungu yakamupela sunga na Yusufu,

lilajilizo lilyo, lono yayihomela yisi ya Misili.

Nghuhulika lizi lyono si nghulimanya yaye, likutya,

6 “Ane nghayinenula mitegulo yako mu mawega gako,

nghakuwusiza kanyhi sunga ne mitegulo,

yono wali wudinghuye mu miwoko yako.

7 Na lono wanyhemeleza,

lono wali wuli mu wugaye, ane nakuponya.

Nane nghakupitula lono nali nihundice mwididimo.

Nghakujela ha malenga ga Meliba.”

8 Ale muhulicize zono nghumusugulenyi,

anye nyo wanhu wangu.

Yali yiwe ciba, hono wali wuhulice,

'gwe Isilayeli!

9 Wulecele lukulu kuwa no

mulungu wo wose kuli'gwe.

Wulecele lukulu kuhemba mulungu yunji.

10 Hambi ane, nha yo nene Muwaha Mulungu,

u Mulungu wako,

mono nakuwusa kulawa mu yisi ya Misili.

Holeka wahame mulomo wako, nane nokulisa.

11 “Ninga woyi hee!

Wanhu wangu hamba si wasaka kuhulika 'ne,

Isilayeli wakambedegala.

12 Hodu nane nghawa kuwaleka wajendelele,

no wukamu we zinhumbula zawo,

wetume zono wakuhila weneco.

13 Ninga yali yiwe ciba, wanhu wangu wali wahulice!

Yali yiwe ciba hono Isilayeli wali watyatye u wutonji wangu!

14 Nane nali niwawulaje lwa kamonga walugu wawo,

nali niwasumile lukulu, wono wakuwewonela.

15 Nawo wono wasulile, wali wafugamile ko woga wuwaha,

no wulipicizwe wawo, wali wuwe wa cibitilila.

16 Ninga nali nghulise agwe, ko wuhemba wuswanu,

kanyhi nali nghwigusye no wuci wa mwi'talawe.”
