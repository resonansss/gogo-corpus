Zabuli 64

Kulomba wudimwe

1 Wuhulice go Mulungu wuhulicize cililo cangu.

Wumhonye wumi wangu,

hambi ne nha nghuwogopa walugu wangu.

2 Wunyhitile no wuhile wubi we wanhu wabi,

hamonga ne lizozo lye wanhu wavu.

3 Wakuzinola zinimi zawo kotya mumaje,

wakulonganjika makani ga wusungu kotya zisonga.

4 Wakulawilila mu wihundiko wawo,

wawa kumuhoma munhu mwinenenyhezi,

wakumuhungucila no kumuhoma wasina,

hamba cogopo bwete.

5 Wakwilumbaniza no kuhangana mumo,

mu wuhile wawo wubi,

wakwilumvya ne hanhu,

hono woyihundika mitego yawo.

Wakwihwesa vyono,

“Hasina mono kosuma kuyiwona yaye.”

6 Wakwilumbaniza mu zinghani zawo zo wuhile wubi,

akuno wakutya, “Cakondya kugawikaniza viswanu,

i mahweso getu!

Alu nani kanyhi 'mbi mono kosuma,

kucimanya zo wuhile wetu?”

Ilihweso lye cinyele lyo munhu,

mbeka lihundice mu nhumbula yo munhu!

7 Ninga Mulungu kowaguma ne zisonga,

wotuga nghomele nhinicizo.

8 Kowongomeza ku soko ye zinghani zawo,

no munhu wo wose mono kowawona,

kosinjisa mutwe wakwe.

9 Ne wanhu wose wokogopa no kwanza,

ze metumo gono yetumile Mulungu,

wokwihwesa ze metumo gakwe.

10 Nawo we ling'ani,

wozisecelelela zono Muwaha Mulungu yetumile,

nawo wotiziza kuli mwene.

Ne wanhu waswanu wose, wokwikumya.
