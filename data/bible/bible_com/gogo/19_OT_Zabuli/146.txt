Zabuli 146

Lukumyo kuli Mulungu Mukombozi

1 Mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu!

Gwe go nhumbula yangu,

wumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu!

2 Nomulumba Muwaha Mulungu,

mu vilo vyo wikale wangu wose,

nomwimbila Mulungu wangu,

vilo vyose vyono nghali mumi.

3 Mulece kuwetumbila wawaha,

hambi muli wanhu nha musina mhonelo.

4 Hambi nha kolo wakufwa, wakugawuyila manghundi,

kanyhi na mwizuwa lilyo mahweso gawo gakwajilila.

5 Holeka yo mumoti mono yakumunoza Mulungu wa Yakobo,

kuwa yo mutazi wakwe, no witumbilo wakwe,

wukuwa wuli muli Muwaha Mulungu wakwe,

6 mono yawumba wulanga ne yinze, ne lilenga,

sunga na vyose vyono vili mumo.

Mwene kawukamisaga wihuwizo wakwe cibitilila.

7 Mono yakuwanozeza ling'ani wono wakugazwa,

kanyhi yakuwapela ndigwa wono wali nzala,

Muwaha Mulungu kawawopolelaga,

wono wadindiywe mu jela,

8 kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu,

kawanuguzaga meso wono wabofuye.

Muwaha Mulungu kawenulaga wono wagwiye,

Muwaha Mulungu yawendile wono wali ling'ani.

9 Muwaha Mulungu kawadimaga wajenzi,

wono wekaye mu yisi yetu,

kanyhi kawatazaga wacekulu wafwile hamonga,

ne wana wono wasina walezi,

ninga nzila ye wabi, mwene kayibidyangaga.

10 Muwaha Mulungu yatemile cibitilila,

Mulungu wako 'gwe go Sayuni,

yo mutemi muli zindelo zose.

Mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu!
