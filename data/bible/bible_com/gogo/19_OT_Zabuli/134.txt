Zabuli 134

Lucemo lo kumukumya Mulungu

1 Ale nzonyi mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu,

anye nyo wetumami wakwe wose,

anye wose mono mumwitumamilaga,

ne cilo mu Nyumba yakwe.

2 Mwinule miwoko yenyu,

muyilanzye kono koyili Nyumba yo Mulungu,

mumukumye Muwaha Mulungu!

3 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu,

mono yawumba wulanga ne yinze,

yamulugutizenyi ku wulawa ku Sayuni,
