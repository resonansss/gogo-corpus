Zabuli 24

U Mutemi Mono Yalawiliciye

(Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Yinze hamonga na vyose vyono vili mugati yayo,

yo sawo yo Muwaha Mulungu,

yinze hamonga na wose wono wekaye mumo, yo sawo yakwe.

2 Hambi mwene nha yo yayizenga yinze mu cisolo ce lilenga,

yakayisimya mu makolongo ge malenga.

3 Alo nani 'mbi mono kosuma kubita mucanyha,

ye litunda lyo Muwaha Mulungu?

Hamba nani mono kosuma kwikala hanhu hakwe hono helile?

4 Yo munhu mono yasina wuhoswe,

ne nhumbula yono yelile.

Mono si yakwiyinjiza mu makani ge caka caka.

Hamba kuhomelela ko wulambi.

5 Mono yena lilumbo linha lilyo,

yuyo yo kotuga wulugute ko Muwaha Mulungu,

Kotuga ling'ani yakwe kuli Mulungu mono kamuponyaga.

6 Wawo wo wanhu wono wakumulondola Mulungu,

wo wanhu wono wakumulondola Mulungu wa Yakobo.

7 Ale, Nyo milango digucenyi,

digucenyi 'nye nyo milango ye katali,

u Mutemi we nhojo ho yenjile.

8 Alo yuyo yo nani lo u Mutemi we nhojo?

Yuyo yo Muwaha Mulungu, we vilungo no Wusume.

U Muwaha Mulungu, citung'ati mu milewo.

9 Digucenyi nyo milango

digucenyi 'nye nyo milango ye katali,

u Mutemi we nhojo ho yenjile.

10 Alo yuyo yo nani lo u Mutemi we nhojo?

Yuyo yo Muwaha Mulungu, we mihanga,

Mwene yuyo yo Mutemi we nhojo.
