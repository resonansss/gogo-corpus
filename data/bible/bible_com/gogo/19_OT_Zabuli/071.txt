Zabuli 71

Wulombe wo mukombi

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

ane nghutiziza kwako,

holeka wulece kundeka ho ngholwe cevu.

2 Wunimule, wumhonye,

ku soko yo wiling'ano wako,

wuteje likutu lyako!

3 Weze wuli wutizizo, no wihundiko wangu,

ne vilungo vyono vikamile vyo kumhonya,

hambi 'gwe nha yo gwegwe go mudimi wangu,

kanyhi yo gwegwe go wihundiko wangu.

4 Wumhonye 'gwe go Mulungu wangu,

mu miwoko ye wabi.

Wumhonye mu wulawa mu wanhu wabi ne wawulaji.

5 Hambi 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nha yo gwegwe du mono nghwitumbiye.

Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

ane nghwitumbiye gwegwe ku wulawa wudodo wangu,

6 nghwitumbiye ku wulawa vyono nalelwa,

hambi nha yo gwegwe mono wandavya mu'nda ya yaya.

Yo gwegwe du mono nghukumyaga vilo vyose.

7 Nane naweza colecezo co wizine kuli wenji,

ninga agwe yo gwegwe go wutizizo wangu wono wukamile.

8 Mulomo wangu wumemile zo lukumyo lwako,

hamonga ne lilumbo lyako misi yose.

9 Kanyhi wulece kundeka kolo nakombipa,

na kolo vilungo vyangu vyamalika wulece kundeka.

10 Hambi i walugu wangu nha wakunonganga,

nawo wono wakusepasepa, wakwilumbaniza hamonga,

11 wakutya, “Ale, Mulungu yamuleka,

holeka mutyatyenyi mukamwibate,

hambi lu nha hasina kanyhi wo kumuponya!”

12 Holeka wulece kwikala kutali nane go Mulungu,

wuze mbela wunhaze, 'gwe go Mulungu wangu,

13 ho wakozwe cevu no kongomela,

wose wono wakwisumaniza nane,

nawo wose wono wakusacila zimbi,

wakozwe cevu no kuzwajizwa.

14 Ninga ane nolindila, akuno nitumbiye,

kanyhi nokukumya kali kenji muno muno.

15 Nane nosimula ze metumo gako ge ling'ani,

kanyhi nokwanza ze metumo gako,

ge mhonelo misi yose,

hamba gawe nha gapulute mahala gangu.

16 Nane, go Muwaha Mulungu,

nokwamuliziza mu metumo gako mawaha,

nokwanza zo wuswanu wako gwegwe du wuli wene.

17 Gwe go Mulungu, yo gwegwe du,

mono wanyijiza ku wulawa wudodo wangu,

holeka nane si nolecela lukulu,

ku wanze we metumo gako go wizine.

18 Wulece kundecela lukulu 'gwe go Mulungu,

hamba niwe nawa nili mukombi nina ne zimvwi,

sunga langa kolo nakondya kuwalonjela ze vilungo vyako,

wanhu wo lulelo lono lukuza.

19 Gwe go Mulungu i vilungo vyako,

hamonga no wiling'ano wako,

vificile ko ku mavunde.

Agwe yo gwegwe mono wanoza zimbaha muno.

Alu go Mulungu yo nani 'mbi,

kanyhi mono yehwanile gwegwe?

20 Kundetela magayo menji mabi,

ninga hamba yiwe nhavivyo,

agwe komhela kanyhi wumi no kunyinula.

21 Konyonjelezeza kanyhi ne lilumbo lyangu,

hamonga kanyhi no kusindamika.

22 Nokwimbila zo lukumyo kwe lisumbi,

ku soko yo wihuwilwa wako,

'gwe go Mulungu wangu,

nokwimbila nyimbo kwe lisumbi,

agwe mono welile wa Isilayeli.

23 Nane nowa no wusangalale,

lono nghwimba zo lukumyo lwako,

nokwimba kwe nhumbula yangu yose,

hambi nha wamhonya.

24 Nane nosimulila zo wiling'ano wako misi yose,

hambi nha wanyihwesezaga zimbi ninga lu,

wakozwa cevu hamonga ne zisoni.
