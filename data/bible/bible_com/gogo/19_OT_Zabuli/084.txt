Zabuli 84

Nyhilu yo wusaka kuwa mu Nyumba yo Mulungu

1 Ane niyendile muno muno Nyumba yako,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga.

2 Nghusulumila niwe mu Nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu.

Nane nghumusangalalila Mulungu,

mumi kwe nhumbula yangu,

na ko muwili wangu wose.

3 Sunga ne vikobobole vizenjile manhuli gavyo,

nazo zisamamba zina wikalo,

hanhu hono zikuwika zinyana zayo,

ha vikumbicilo vyako, 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

u Mulungu we mihanga,

go Mutemi wangu no Mulungu wangu!

4 Wamoti wono wekalaga mu Nyumba yako,

mono wosuma kukukumya cibitilila.

5 Wo wamoti wono wakupegwa vilungo kulawa kwako,

wono wakusulumila kubita kwi'tunda lya Sayuni.

6 Nawo lono wakukolela mwilolo,

lyono linyalile lya Baka,

walinozaga liwa mivwilo,

ne mvula ye mhinga,

yilimemyaga makombo-makombo.

7 Nawo wajendelelaga du kutuga vilungo,

mu nghango vyono wakucila kujendelela ku mwande,

nawo womuwona Mulungu we milungu mu Sayuni.

8 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

go Mulungu we mihanga,

wuhulice wulombe wangu, wuhulice

'gwe go Mulungu wa Yakobo.

9 Gwe go Mulungu wetu,

wumulanjilile mutemi wetu ko wuswanu wako,

heya, wumulanjilile viswanu mutemi mono wamuhagula.

10 Hambi lizuwa limonga,

hono litumamilwe mu Nyumba yako,

nha yili ciba muno muno,

si nha witumamila vilo elufu monga hanhu hanji,

hamba no wima ha mulango we Nyumba yako, du,

nha kuswanu muno kuciza wikalo,

wo kwikala hamonga ne wanhu wabi.

11 U Muwaha Mulungu yo mudimi wetu,

kanyhi yo mutemi wetu wo lukumyo,

mwene kacimotyaga vye lihewa lyakwe,

kanyhi yakucipela no lukumyo.

Muwaha Mulungu si yakuwa kuwema cinji co cose

ciswanu, wono wetumaga ze ling'ani.

12 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga,

wo wamoti wono wakwitumbiye gwegwe!
