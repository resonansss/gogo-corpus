Zabuli 23

U Mulungu yo mudimi wangu

(Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 U Muwaha Mulungu, yo mudimi wangu,

holeka sinocepucilwa na cinji.

2 Kanyhehezaga mu wudimilo we mahanze madece,

kanhongaga ku vilamba vyono

visunjile vye malenga maswanu,

3 no kumhela vilungo vipya.

Kanyhi kanhongaga mu nzila yo mugoloka,

nha vyono yehuwiza.

4 Holeka hamba nghole mwi'lolo lyo mwenzenze

we lifwa, sinokogopa cinji, hambi 'gwe,

agwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nha wuli hamonga nane, ne nghome yako,

hamonga ne shale yako, vikundima.

5 Wukunhenjela ndigwa hamwande ye walugu wangu,

wukumbaka ne mafuta mu mutwe wangu,

sunga ne cikombe cangu wukucimemya twaa no mwitiko.

6 U wuswanu wako hamonga no lusungu lwako, mbeka,

vyowa hamonga nane mu vilo vyose vyo wikale wangu.

Nane nokwikala mu nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu cibitilila.
