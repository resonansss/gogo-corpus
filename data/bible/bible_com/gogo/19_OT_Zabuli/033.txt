Zabuli 33

Lwimbo lwo lukumyo

1 Anye mo wanhu we ling'ani,

musangalalilenyi Muwaha Mulungu,

nanye mo wenenenyhezi, mukumyenyi Mulungu.

2 Mukumyenyi Muwaha Mulungu ko wutowa ndonondo,

mwimbilenyi ne lisumbi lye zinghunzi yikumi.

3 Mwimbilenyi lwimbo lupya,

towenyi zing'oma viswanu, no wusangalale.

4 Inghani yo Muwaha Mulungu yo ye mbeka,

ne nyituma yakwe yose, yo nyihuwilwa.

5 U Muwaha Mulungu,

yendile wiling'ano hamonga no wuling'anizi,

ne yinze yose yimemile lusungu lwakwe.

6 U Muwaha Mulungu,

yawuwumba wulanga ko wulajilize wakwe,

ne vinhu vyose vya ku wulanga,

vyawumbilwa mu wahulile wakwe.

7 Yagakongholela hamonga,

malenga ge lilenga ne visolo vye lilenga,

yakavidindila mu nyumba yo wuwiko we viya.

8 Yinze yose yimogope Muwaha Mulungu!

Nanye mo wanhu wose wa mononze,

mumupelaje Muwaha Mulungu lilumbo lyakwe!

9 Hambi mwene nha yalonga du,

ne yinze yikawa kuwumbwa,

mwene yalajiliza du,

na vyose vikawa kwijela.

10 U Muwaha Mulungu kawuwembulaga wuhile we makungugo,

no kugapata i mahweso gawo.

11 Wuhile wo Muwaha Mulungu,

wikalaga cibitilila,

no wudumule wakwe we zinghani wikalaga ko ku zindelo zose.

12 Na mbeka, limotile likungugo lyono Mulungu wayo,

yo yuyu u Muwaha Mulungu!

Wamoti wono Mulungu yawahaguye wawe wanhu wakwe!

13 U Muwaha Mulungu kasunjililaga,

kono hasi ku wulawa ku wulanga,

no kuwawona wanhu wose.

14 Nayo, ku wulawilila mwigoda lyakwe lyo wuwaha,

yakuwalanga no kuwawona wanhu wose wono wekaye mononze.

15 Mwene Mulungu yo muwumbi we zinhumbula ze wanhu wose,

holeka mwene yazimanyile zose zono wakwituma.

16 Hasina mutemi mono kekalaga yapolile,

ku soko yo winji we mapuga ge wasilikale wakwe,

wo wulugu gono yena nago,

hamba citung'ati, si kawasumizaga wayagwe,

ku soko ye vilungo vyakwe mweneco.

17 Hamba fwalasi mu wulugu,

si yokwila yeneco kusuma ne vilungo vyayo viwaha,

hamba si vyosuma kumuponya munhu.

18 U Muwaha Mulungu,

kawalanjililaga wose wono wakumuhulika,

wanhu wose wono wetumbiye lusungu lwakwe.

19 Mwene kawaponyaga mwi'fwa no kuwalisa mu nzala.

20 Ase cimwitumbiye Muwaha Mulungu,

mwene yo mukamisi wetu,

kanyhi yo mutazi wetu.

21 Nase cisangalaye ku so mwene,

kanyhi cilitumbiye litagwa lyakwe lyono lyelile.

22 Lusungu lwako luweze muli'se 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nha vyono nase cikwitumbiye gwegwe.
