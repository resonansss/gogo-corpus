Zabuli 80

Wulombe wo kuligomola likungugo

1 Wucihulicize 'gwe go mudimi wa Isilayeli,

mono kuwatongaga wana wa Yusufu, nhe zingholo.

Agwe mono wikaye mwi'goda lyako lyo wuwaha,

mucanyha ye makelubi,

2 Wuwamulicilaje ku mwande wa Efulayimu,

Benyamini, na Manase.

Wulajile vilungo vyako, wuze wuciponye!

3 Gwe go Mulungu,

cikukunena wucilanje ko wuswanu wako,

nase coponywa.

4 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

go Mulungu we mihanga,

alo kojendelela sunga nili kuwusimbila,

wulombe we wanhu wako?

5 Alu wawunoza wukalale waweza yo ndigwa yetu,

kanyhi wacing'wesa mahozi ko winji muno.

6 Wagaleceleza makungugo gono gacipilime,

geza kuyihoma yisi yetu,

ne walugu wetu wakucitovulanga.

7 Gwe go Mulungu we mihanga,

wucilanje ko lusungu lwako, nase coponywa.

8 Waleta muzabibu wu monga ku wulawa Misili,

nagwe wukawawinga wanhu we makungugo ganji,

hanyuma yayo, wukawuhadika mu yisi yawo.

9 Wawuzuwila ho wuwiluce viswanu,

nawo wukota milela no kutandala mu yisi yose.

10 Wagagubika matunda no mubeho wayo,

ne manhambi gayo gakawa nha mihagata miwaha.

11 Ne manhambi gayo,

gakatandala sunga ko kwi'lenga lya Meditelaneya,

ne mahapilwa gayo,

nago, gakatandala gakafika ku nhumbi,

ye likolongo lya Efulata.

12 Alu socici, wukagakumula majinji,

gono gali gawupilime?

Alu yeza wo wose mono yakupuluta mu nzila yiyo,

yahepe zabibu zayo,

13 sunga ne zingubi za mu mbago,

nazo zawa kuwuhodanga,

ne vilimu vya mu mbago navyo, vyeza kuwulya.

14 Holeka go Mulungu we mihanga,

cikukunena wucilanje kanyhi!

Wucilanje ku wulawa ku wulanga,

wuze wuwudime wuwuno muzabibu!

15 Wono wawuhadika ko muwoko wako wo kulume,

na cico cinhambi cidodo, cono wacinozeza vilungo.

16 Na wawo walugu wetu,

wono wawudumulanga, no kuwuticimiza mu moto,

wuwalangaje ko wukali, no kuwongomezeza lukulu.

17 Muwoko wako wumucitile no kumudima munhu wako,

mono wamuhagula, ho yawe yakamile ku soko yako.

18 Nase hamba si cokulecela lukulu,

no kuwuya ne nyuma kanyhi,

Wuciwumifye du, nase cokukumya gwegwe.

19 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

u Mulungu we mihanga, wucigomole,

wucilanje ko lusungu lwako, nase coponywa.
