Zabuli 129

Lilombo ku soko ye walugu wa Isilayeli

1 Isilayeli yakutya, “Walugu wangu,

wakuwa kungaza kali kenji,

ku wulawilila wuzelelo wangu!”

2 “Heya mbeka, i walugu wangu,

wangazaga wulawilila wuzelelo wangu,

ninga hamba si wasumiza yaye.

3 Wandelemulanga mu mugongo wangu,

wakawunoza nha mugunda wa kulimwa.

4 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yo we ling'ani,

kamhonya mu wulawa mu wuwanda.”

5 Holeka wasumilwe lukulu kanyhi wakozwe cevu,

wagomolwe ku nyuma,

wawo wose wono walisulile likaya lya Sayuni.

6 Kanyhi wose wehwane mahanze,

gono gali mwi'tembe lye kaya,

gono ganyalaga na lono gakali,

hamba si natalimha,

7 hasina hamba yunji mono kogakongholeza,

yagawope mu madulu madulu.

8 Na hasina hamba yunji wo wose,

mono kokolela baho behi mono kosuma kutya,

“Muwaha Mulungu yamulugutizenyi!

Cikumulugutizizenyi mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu!”
