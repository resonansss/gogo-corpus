Zabuli 95

Lwimbo lwe malumbo

1 Muze cimwimbile Muwaha Mulungu,

cimusangalalile Mulungu we mhonelo yetu!

2 Cinjile kuli mwene ne zinyimbo zo wulumbe,

cimusangalalile ne zinyimbo zo lukumyo.

3 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu,

nha yo Mulungu Mono yalawiliciye.

Mutemi mono yali vilungo mu milungu yose.

4 Mwene yayitemile yinze yose,

sunga ne malolo hamonga ne vizuli vye matunda.

5 Lilenga lyakwe, yo mweneco, mono yalinoza.

Ne yisi nghavu, yo mweneco mono yayiwumba.

6 Nzonyi cifugame no kumuhemba,

cimufugamile Muwaha Mulungu,

Muwumbi wetu!

7 Hambi mwene nha yo Mulungu wetu,

nase cili wanhu mono mwene yakucidima,

ase cili ngholo zakwe zono yakuzilisa.

Holeka izuwali mulihulice lizi lyakwe,

8 “Mulece kuwa musina cihuliko,

nha vyono wali wenha i walezi wenyu ku Meliba,

nha vyono wali mwizuwa lilya ha Masa ku nyika.

9 Kuko kono walezi wenyu wanjela no kumhima,

hamba wawe wali wakondya kugawona metumo gangu,

gono nali nawatendela.

10 Na mu myaka makumi mane,

hamba si nendezwa nawo,

nghawa kutya, ‘Mbeka wanhu awa,

wo wanhu wono wajiliye!

Hamba si wakuwusacila lukulu no wutonji wangu!’

11 Hodu nane nghasimba,

nghawa kwilahiza nghatya,

‘Hamba si wokwinjilila lukulu kanyhi,

mu yisi yono nali niwapele wuceho!’ ”
