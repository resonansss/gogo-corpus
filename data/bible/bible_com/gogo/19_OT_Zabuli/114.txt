Zabuli 114

Lwimbo lwe Pasaka

1 Lono Isilayeli yawucila ku Misili,

i wana wa Yakobo,

lono wawucila mu yisi yo wujenzi,

2 Yuda wukawa wikalo wono

welile wo Mulungu,

Isilayeli yakawa sawo yakwe mweneco Mulungu.

3 Lilenga lya Shamu lyaziwona zizo,

likawa kutiza,

likolongo lya Yoludani nalyo,

likaleka kuhuluma!

4 Ne matunda nago,

gakazumhazumha nha nduwilu,

vigongo navyo,

vikazumhazumha nhe zinyana ze zingholo.

5 Alu 'gwe go lilenga,

ho yikawa wule, nagwe wukatiza?

Nagwe, go likolongo lya Yoludani,

nhawule lo nagwe wukaleka kuhuluma?

6 Nanye mo matunda, nhawulenyi

mukawa kuzumhazumha nha nduwilu?

Nanye ma mo vigongo,

nhawulenyi mukawa kuzumhazumha,

nhe zinyana ze zingholo?

7 Holeka 'lu agwe go yinze,

wucicime hali Muwaha Mulungu,

heya, hali Mulungu wa Yakobo,

8 mono kalipamizaga du litalawe,

nalyo liwa yilamba lya malenga,

ne mabwe makavu gawa mivwilo ya malenga.
