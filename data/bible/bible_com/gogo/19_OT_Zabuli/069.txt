Zabuli 69

Kulilila wutazi

1 Gwe go Mulungu, wumhonye,

hambi malenga nha gaficila mu singo yangu.

2 Alu neza kudodomela mu nhope mbaha,

sunga na ha wusambo we magulu hasina.

Alu ngwiliye ha ciligati ye cisolo ce malenga,

neza kusolwa ne mawingo.

3 Alu natimba muno so cililo cangu,

sunga ne malaka gangu ganyala muno.

Ne meso gangu geza si gakuwona viswanu,

lono nghuwulondola wutazi wako.

4 Nawo wanhu ho wenji wono wasulile bwete,

wenji muno wacizize no winji we zimvwili zangu.

Ne walugu wangu wakunonga za wulambi,

wali vilungo, wakusaka wambulaje.

Wakusaka ati ngomole vinhu vyono'ne si solile.

5 Gwe go Mulungu,

agwe yo gwegwe mono wuwumanyile wulele wangu,

ne masutya gangu gose,

hasina hamba limonga lyono yihundikwe hali gwegwe.

6 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, u Mudaha Vyose,

wulece kuvumilila lukulu,

awalya wono wakwitumbiye wakolwe cevu ku soko yangu.

Wawo wono wakukulondola,

'gwe go Mulungu wa Isilayeli,

walece kupuwa ku soko yangu.

7 Hambi nha nghozwa cevu ku soko yako gwegwe,

soni zifunicize mu wusu wangu.

8 Sunga neza nha nili mujenzi kuli wandugu zangu,

i wawaha zangu hamonga ne walumbu zangu,

hamba si wakumanya yaye.

9 Uwendo wangu so Nyumba yako, wukumbulaga.

Ne matovulo gose gono wakutovulaga gwegwe,

gose gambuyila nene.

10 Na lono nenenenyhezeza mu wuleka kulya,

wanhu wakahosa muno.

11 Nali neza kuvwala ligunila,

ho ndajile wukalale wangu,

wene wakasekanga muno sunga wakambedegala.

12 Ne wanhu nhondo weza wakuhehela, mu zinzila du,

sunga ne wagala wujimbi wose,

wakutula nyimbo zo kunyimba nene.

13 Ninga'ne nghukulomba,

agwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wuvumile wulombe wangu,

nha vyono wendile gwegwe,

lono nghukulomba.

Wuhulicize, 'gwe go Mulungu,

hambi agwe nha wuli lusungu muno,

kanyhi yo gwegwe du,

mono yo mhonelo ye mbeka.

14 Nghukulomba wumbuse mu nhope yiyi,

ho ndece kudodomela,

wumhonye kuli wawo wono wasulile,

wumhonye mu visolo vye malenga.

15 Wulece kundeceleza solwe,

ne lihwazi lye malenga,

wulece kundeka ndodomele ha cisolo,

hamba kudodomela mu citenjelo.

16 Wuhulicize 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

so wuswanu we lihewa lyako,

wunanje ku so winji wo lusungu lwako.

17 Wulece kwihundika kutali,

nane no mwitumami wako, wuhulicizaje 'lu,

hambi nha nili mu wugaye.

18 Wunyizize, wumhonye, wumhonye kuli

walugu wangu wenji.

19 Hambi agwe nha wumanyile vyono nghutovulwa'ne,

wumanyile ne cevu,

hamonga ne zisoni zono zikunyagana,

sunga ne walugu wangu, agwe wuwamanyile wose.

20 Zinhovu nhondo zanhukuwika,

sunga no mutima wangu, nane natimba!

Nondola wutuzwe, hamba kuwuwona yaye,

hamba si yejeye no munhu mono konhuza yaye.

21 Lono nali ndabuce,

wene wakamhela sumu yo nilye,

nali nghaluce,

wene wakamhela siki yo ning'we.

22 Ne zindigwa zawo,

ze zi sikukuu ze makumbiko,

ziweze mutego kuli wene,

Ne zi sikukuu zawo ziwagwize weneco.

23 Meso gawo gatukuwikwe ho walece kuwona,

ne migongo yawo wuyitimvye.

24 Wuwajidile likuwo lyako,

no wukali wako wuwagane.

25 No wikalo wawo wikale bwete, no munhu wo wose,

yalece kwikala kanyhi mu mahema gawo.

26 Hambi nha wakuwagaza,

wono agwe wakondya kuwalipiciza,

kanyhi wakuwonjelezeza nghomele,

awalya wono agwe wakondya kuwapela zinghomele.

27 Ale, wuwalipicize mu nghango yo wubi wawo,

hamba wulece kuwanenelela lukulu.

28 Wuwapatilaje lukulu mu citabu ce womi,

walece kuwa mwi'walo lya we ling'ani.

29 Langa wunyinulaje 'ne 'gwe go Mulungu,

wumhonye, ane mono nili mu wusungu,

kanyhi mono nakondya kutukuwika mutima wangu.

30 Nane nolikumya litagwa lyo Mulungu kwa nyimbo,

kanyhi nomutogoza ko kumulavizya yilumbo.

31 Ne nghani yiyino,

yo yomwendeza muno Muwaha Mulungu,

si nha kumulavizya vilavywa vya ng'ombe,

hamba si nha cilavywa ce nghongolo mututu.

32 Nawo wono wakwiwonelwa,

kolo weza kuziwona zizino,

wosecelela muno muno.

Nawo wono wakumulondola Mulungu,

wotuga vilungo.

33 Muwaha Mulungu kawahulicizaga waciwa,

hamba si kosuma kuwasemwa yaye wanhu wakwe,

wono wawopigwe.

34 Nanye nyo wulanga ne yinze, mumukumye Mulungu,

lilenga na vyose vyono vili mumo, nanye mukumyenyi.

35 Hambi Mulungu nha koliponya likaya lya Yelusalemi,

no kulizenga kanyhi likaya lya Yuda.

Ne wanhu wakwe, wokwikala mumo no kutema,

36 no lulelo lwe wetumami wakwe,

wawo wo wolihala likaya lilyo,

na wose wono wamwendile,

wawo wo wokwikala mwi'kaya lilyo.
