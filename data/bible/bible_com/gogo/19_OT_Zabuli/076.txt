Zabuli 76

U Mulungu yo yali cisumisumi

1 U Mulungu yo yamanyice mu yisi ya Yuda,

ne litagwa lyakwe lye 'tambule mu Isilayeli.

2 Ne lihema lyakwe lili ku Salemi,

no wikalo wakwe wuli ku Sayuni

3 Kuko ko yabenanga zisonga ze walugu,

yakabena ne ngula, no mumaje, heya,

sunga ne vitwalo vyose.

4 Agwe wina nhojo, wulawiliciye,

si nhe matunda ge cibitilila.

5 Na wono wena nhumbula ye vilungo,

wabokwa mateka gawo,

nalu wagonile zinhongo zawo zo mudumilizo,

i vilungo hamonga no wunghula wawo wose,

wuwa nghani ya bwete du.

6 Nagwe go Mulungu wa Yakobo,

wali weza kuwakalipila du, hodu du, zifwalasi,

hamonga na wono wali wali mucanyha ye zifwalasi,

wose wakafwa yitenene.

7 Agwe go Mulungu, mbeka wuli wa kogofya muno,

alu nani kanyhi 'mbi,

mono kosuma kukangalila hono wuwe wakolwa likuwo?

8 Nagwe nha kuyilajila ning'aniza yako,

vyono yinha, ku wulawa ku wulanga,

nayo yinze yiwa kogopa no kunyamala twii,

9 lono wali weza kwinuka no kuling'aniza,

kuwaponya wose wono wakwiwonelwa mono'nze.

10 Ne likuwo lye wanhu,

likuletelaga lukumyo lono lucizize muno;

nawo wono waponile ku wulugu,

wowa kuzikumbucila zi sikukuu zako.

11 Alenyi mumupelaje Muwaha Mulungu,

u Mulungu wenyu, vyono mwehuwiza,

nanye mono muli behi, mupelenyi vipegwa Mulungu,

mono yanojeye wanhu wamogope.

12 Mwene kawawusiza wumi i wawaha wedunhumuzi,

kawogofyaga muno muno watemi we yinze.
