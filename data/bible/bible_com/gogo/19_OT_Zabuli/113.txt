Zabuli 113

Kumukumya Mulungu so waswanu wakwe

1 Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe!

Anye nyo wetumami wo Muwaha Mulungu,

likumyenyi litagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu!

2 Litagwa lyakwe lyeze kukumywa,

ku wulawa lulu, sunga ko ku cibitilila.

3 Litagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu,

lijendelele kukumywa,

ku wulawa kucilima, sunga ko kumwezi!

4 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu,

nha yo yatemile makungugo gose,

no lukumyo lwakwe luli kucanyha yo wulanga wose.

5 Hambi nani kanyhi, mono kowa yehwanile,

no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wetu?

Mwene nha yekaye mwigoda lyo wutemi kucanyha,

6 hamba nhavivyo,

kekalaga yalanjile za hono hasi, heya,

kazilangaga wulanga ne yinze.

7 Mwene kamwinulizaga muciwa,

ku wulawa mu manghundi.

Kanyhi kamunyasulizaga muciwa,

ku wulawa mu wosocele wakwe,

8 hanyuma kawa kumwikaza,

hamonga ne wawaha, heya,

hamonga ne wawaha we wanhu wakwe.

9 Mwene kanyhi kamupelaga,

lilumbo mucekulu mono si kalelaga,

kamusangalazaga ko kumupela wana.

Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe!
