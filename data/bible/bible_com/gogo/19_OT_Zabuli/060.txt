Zabuli 60

Kulomba wukombozwe

1 Gwe go Mulungu, wacitaga no kucipondajiza,

wakolwa ne likuwo,

ninga alu, cikukunena wucigomolele zo wuswanu wako.

2 Kuyicicimya yisi no kuyipandula,

alu wukande mazatu gayo,

hambi nha yeza kupanduka.

3 Kuwaleka wanhu wako wenjile mu magayo,

nalu ceza pepesuka nha wono wagalile divayi.

4 Kuwasugula awalya wono wakogopile,

ho wasume kuyityega songa.

5 Wuciponye kwe vilungo vyako,

wucipitule no wulombe wetu,

i wanhu wako wono wuwendile, ho wekale wapolile.

6 U Mulungu kalonga ku wulawa hono helile hakwe katya,

“Alu nobita sangalaye nghayigawe yisi ya Shekemu,

ne lilolo lya Sukoti nalyo,

noligawa kuli wanhu wangu.

7 Gileyadi yo yangu,

sunga na Manase nayo yangu nhavivyo,

ayi nayo Efulayimu, nha yo kofiya yangu ye cuma,

nayo Yuda, yo nghome yangu yo wutemi.

8 Ayi nayo Mowabu,

yo bakuli yangu yo kwizuguziza.

I cilatu cangu naco,

nocigumila ku Edomu, ho niyiteme.

Nane baho langa ho nozoza kwe lizi,

lyo wusume kuli Wafilisiti.”

9 Alo nani lu, mono konhegula,

kwi'kaya lye ligomawakali?

Hamba nani mono konhonga sunga fice ku Edomu?

10 Gwe go Mulungu,

alu wacilecela lukulu du hongo?

Hamba lu sikojendela lukulu,

kanyhi hamonga ne mapuga getu?

11 Wucipele wutazi kuli walugu wetu,

hambi wutazi wono wukulawa kuli wanhu,

nha si wukwiliye cinji yaye.

12 Ase cosuma du ko wutazi wo Mulungu wetu,

Mwene kowapondanjizanga i walugu wetu.
