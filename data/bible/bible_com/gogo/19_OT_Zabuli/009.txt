Zabuli 9

Lilumbo kuli Mulungu so ling'ani yakwe

(Kuli mwimbizi: Nho wimbe wa Muti-labeni Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Gwe Mulungu go Muwaha wetu,

ane nokukumya gwegwe ko mutima wangu wose,

kanyhi nokwanza ze metumo gako go wizine.

2 Nane nosecelela no kusangalala ku soko yako,

nokwimba zo lukumyo lwako,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu Mono Wulawiliciye Muli Vyose.

3 I walugu wangu mwali mweza kwidoma nawo,

wakawuya ne nyuma, wakagwa,

no kwajililila lukulu.

4 Kunhetela no kumhela ling'ani yangu,

wikaye mwigoda lyako lyo wutemi,

wukuling'aniza kwe ling'ani,

5 Nagwe kuwatacila we makungugo,

kuwongomeza i wanhu wabi,

kuwapatila lukulu litagwa lyawo cibitilila.

6 I walugu wongomela, wajililila lukulu.

Nagwe kugamwagula ne makaya gawo,

nawo wakondya kusemelwa lukulu.

7 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yatemile cibitilila,

yaliwicile ligoda lyakwe lyo wutemi,

ho yasume kuling'aniza.

8 Nayo yakuyiling'aniza yinze kwe ling'ani,

kanyhi yakugaling'aniza makungugo mu wulunji.

9 Muwaha Mulungu, wo wutizizo kuli munhu,

mono yakwiwonelwa,

kanyhi ho hanhu hono hapolile,

mu vilo vyo wugaye.

10 Nawo wose wono walivumilaga litagwa lyako,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu wawo wo wakwitumbilaga gwegwe,

hambi 'gwe nha si kumutagaga yaye,

u munhu wo wose mono yakukulondola gwegwe.

11 U Muwaha Mulungu yo yayitemile Sayuni,

holeka mumwimbilaje nyimbo ze lilumbo,

kanyhi muwanzizaje wanhu wose ze metumo gakwe.

12 U Mulungu kawakumbucilaga wose wono wakugazwa,

kanyhi hamba si kacisemwaga cililo ca wono wakwiwonelwa.

13 Wumbonele lusungu 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu!

Lanje ta wugaye wono nghugazwa na wono wasulile,

wumbuse mu wulawa mu vilungo vye lifwa,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu.

14 Ho simulile zo lukumyo lwako,

hali wanhu wa Yelusalemi,

kanyhi ho sangalale vyono wakondya kumhonya.

15 I wanhu we makungugo wahimba likombo,

ninga kanyhi wagwila weneco mumo,

wegwiza mu mutego wono wawutejile weneco.

16 U Muwaha Mulungu kelajila mu ning'aniza yakwe ye ling'ani,

Ne wanhu wabi wegwiza weneco mu metumo gawo mabi.

17 I wabi wose wobita ku yisi ye wafu,

heya, wanhu wose wono wakumubeza Mulungu.

18 Langa u munhu muciwa, hamba si kosemwelwa lukulu,

u witumbilo wo muciwa,

hamba si wokwajililila lukulu.

19 Ale, wuze, 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu!

Wulece kumuleceleza munhu ho yewone nha yasuma.

Wugakongholezezaje makungugo

hali gwegwe, wugaling'anize.

20 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu wuwogofyaje makungugo,

ho wamanye vyono wene, wanhu du.
