Zabuli 74

Kulomba likungugo liponywe

1 Go Mulungu, alu nhawule wacitajila lukulu?

Alu so cici likuwo lyako likwaka kuli zingholo zako!

2 Wukumbucile wihangulize wako,

wono wawahagula gwegwe ku wulawa na katali,

mbeyu yono wayikomboza yiwe sawo yako,

litunda lye Sayuni lyono walinoza liwe wo wikalo wako.

3 Ale winuce wukole mucanyha,

ye matongo gagano ge vilo vyose.

Nawo walugu wakondya kuhodanga vinhu vyose,

vya mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.

4 Walugu wako wakuzoza mu Nyumba yako,

ne lizozo lyo wusume!

Waziwicile mumo ne zibendela zo wusume wawo!

5 Wehwana no mutenya zingwagu,

mono katemaga mabici ne nhemo yakwe.

6 Wakawubenanjiza mulango we Nyumba yo Mulungu,

kwe zinhemo zawo hamonga ne zinyondo zawo.

7 Wakayiticimiza no moto Nyumba yako yono yelile.

Wakahatovula i hanhu hono agwe wukogopwa.

8 Vyono wali welahize sunga wacimale ase wose,

holeka wakahaticimiza mu yisi yose,

hanhu hose hono cekongholelaga kukuhemba.

9 Vitanjilo vyetu vyose vyono vyelile,

sicikuviwona lu, alu ceza cisina hamba muwizi.

Hamba si cimanyile zizi,

zojendelela kuwa nhavivi sunga nili!

10 Alu 'gwe go Mulungu,

u mulugu ho kojendelela kukusekanga sunga nili?

Alu mwene ho wolitovula litagwa lyako sunga cibitilila?

11 So cici lo wukulema kucitaza lu?

Nhawule wuyiwicile ku mugongo miwoko yako?

12 Agwe go Mulungu, yo gwegwe mono wuli

mutemi wetu ku wulawa katali na katali,

mono kunozaga mhonelo mono'nze yose.

13 So vilungo vyako, watemanula lilenga,

wukayitijinha mitwe ye mazoka ga mwi'lenga.

14 Wayitijinha mitwe ye lilimu,

lyono likucemwa Lewiyatani,

wukawa kuzipela zinimu za mu mbago muvimba walyo.

15 Agwe yo gwegwe mono wanoza mivwilo ne vikolongo,

wukaganyaza i makolongo mawaha.

16 Misi yo yako, sunga cilo naco co cako,

waviwika mwezi ne lizuwa hanhu hayo.

17 Agwe yo gwegwe,

mono wayiwika mipaka yose ye yinze,

yo gwegwe mono wawika vipango vye cibahu,

ne vipango vye mbeho.

18 Ninga wukumbucile 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wubedegazi wono wukulawa kuli walugu wako,

likungugo lyono lipuwile,

likulitovula litagwa lyako.

19 Wulecele lukulu 'gwe go Mulungu,

kugagumilila malimu, wumi we wendece wako,

kanyhi wulece kuwasemwa wanhu wako,

wono wali mu wugaye.

20 Wucikumbucilaje i cilagane cako kuli'se!

Yisi nayo yimemile wuwulaji wose,

hanhu hose ha citi,

21 Holeka wulece kuwaleceleza wali wene,

ho wakozwe cevu, wose wono wakwiwonelwa.

Nawo waciwa hamonga ne wanjile,

wakukumye gwegwe.

22 Ale winuce go Mulungu, witetele,

wukumbucile wubedegazi we vilo vyose,

wono wukulawa kuli wono si wakumanyile.

23 Wulece kugasemwa mazozo ge likuwo,

gono gakulawa kuli walugu wako,

hamonga ne liyowo lye vilo vyose,

lya wono wakwisumaniza nagwe.
