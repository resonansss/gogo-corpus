Zabuli 54

Wulombe wo kwicitila kuli walugu

1 Gwe go Mulungu wumhonezye mwi'tagwa lyako,

wumbice niwe nili mwelecele kwe vilungo vyako.

2 Wuhulice wulombe wangu,

'gwe go Mulungu wuzibocele zinghani zangu.

3 Hambi wanhu wedunhumuzi, nha wakunhowanga nawo,

i wawulaji nha wakuwulondola wumi wangu,

wanhu wono wasina cogopo hamba cidodo ca kuli Mulungu.

4 Ninga, ane Mulungu wo wutazi wangu,

Muwaha Mulungu, yo kawusikamyaga wumi wangu.

5 No wubi we walugu wangu,

wuwawuyile weneco, ko wihuwilwa wako,

'gwe go Mulungu wuwongomeze.

6 Nane nokulavizya likumbiko kwa nhumbula nzelu,

kanyhi nolikumya litagwa lyako so wuswanu wako.

7 Hambi nha wakondya kumhonya mu magayo gangu gose,

nane nakondya kuwona vyono walugu wangu wose,

wakondya kusumwa.
