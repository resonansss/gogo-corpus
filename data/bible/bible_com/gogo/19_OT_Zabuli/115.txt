Zabuli 115

Mulungu yumonga du we mbeka

1 Kuli'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu wuli wene,

yo si sese,

langa yo gwegwe du wuli wene,

mono wunojeye lukumyo,

ku soko yo lusungu lwako,

no wihuwilwa wako.

2 Ku sokoci makungugo gakuciwuza gakutya,

“Alu Mulungu wenyu yali hayi?”

3 Mulungu wetu'se, yali ku wulanga,

nayo mwene ketumaga zose,

nha vyono yendile mweneco.

4 I milungu yawo nha ya fweza na zahabu du,

yono yinozigwe ne miwoko ye wanhu.

5 Yina milomo, ninga hamba si yikulonga bwete,

yina meso, ninga hamba si yikuwona yaye,

6 sunga ne makutu yina nago,

ninga hamba si yikuhulika ng'o,

ne zimhula zose yina nazo,

ninga hamba si yihevyaga,

munungho wa cinji co cose yaye,

7 'gwe, sunga ne miwoko yose yina nayo,

ninga si yisumaga hamba no kwalabata bwete du,

ati sunga ne magulu gose yina nago,

ninga hamba si yikusuma kujenda yaye.

Si yikusumila lukulu hamba kulavya yizi du kose.

8 Holeka na wose wono wayitumbilaga milungu yiyo,

nawo wehwanile nha yeneco, heya,

sunga na wose wono wayihuwiye milungu yiyo.

9 Nye nyo Isilayeli,

mwitumbilenyi Muwaha Mulungu,

mwene yo mutazi wenyu,

kanyhi yo ngula yenyu.

10 Nanye mo wana wa Haluni,

mwitumbilenyi Muwaha Mulungu,

mwene yo mutazi wenyu,

kanyhi yo ngula yenyu.

11 Anye nanye mono mumogopile Muwaha Mulungu,

mwitumbilenyi Muwaha Mulungu mwene,

yo mutazi wenyu, kanyhi yo ngula yenyu.

12 Mwene Muwaha Mulungu, yacikumbuciye du,

kanyhi kocilugutiza, kowalugutiza Waisilayeli,

kowalugutiza wana wa Haluni.

13 Mulungu kowalugutiza wose wono wamogopile,

kowalugutiza wawaha sunga ne wadodo.

14 Muwaha Mulungu yacile kumonjelezenyi wana,

anye mo weneco, sunga ne wana wenyu!

15 Ale, Muwaha Mulungu yamulugutizenyi,

mono yawumba wulanga ne yinze.

16 Wulanga wose, yo sawo yo Muwaha Mulungu,

langa yinze, yakawapela wanhu.

17 Kanyhi wafu, si wamukumyaga Muwaha Mulungu,

hamba wo wose yulya, mono yakondya kubita ku yisi ye wafu,

18 Langa sese du mono cili womi,

yo comukumya Muwaha Mulungu,

ku wulawilila lulu baha,

sunga ku cibitilila.

Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe!
