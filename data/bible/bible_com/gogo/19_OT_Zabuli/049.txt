Zabuli 49

Wulele wo witumbila sawo

1 Hulicizenyi zizi, anye nyo wanhu wose!

Tejenyi makutu genyu,

anye wose mono mwikaye mono'nze,

2 anye wose nyo wawaha ne wadodo,

nanye wose nyo wagoli, sunga nanye nyo waciwa.

3 Izinghani zangu zowa za mahala,

nane nolonga zinghani zo wizuce.

4 Nane notega likutu lyangu hulice fumbo,

no kuciloloseza cidalicizo cangu mu wutowa lisumbi.

5 Ane si nghogopa mu vilo vye zimbali,

lono nowa mhilimwe ne walugu mbavu zose.

6 Wanhu wabi, wono wetumbilaga zisawo zawo,

no kwilumbila winji wo wugoli wawo.

7 Mbeka, munhu si kosuma kwikomboza mweneco,

hamba kulipa kuli Mulungu cifo co wumi wakwe,

8 vyono cifo co wumi ciwaha muno muno.

Si kosuma kulavya cinhu co cose,

cono cosuma kuhomela,

9 cono cosuma kumunoza ho yajendelele kuwa mumi,

yalece kucijeza citenjelo.

10 Hambi wose nha wamanyile vyono,

sunga na we mahala nha wafwizaga du,

i walele, sunga ne wawula mahala.

Wose wawano, wawalecelaga wanji zisawo zawo.

11 Ne vitenjelo, wo wikalo wawo cibitilila,

wo wikalo wawo sunga ku zindelo zose,

hamba wawe ha mwamulizo wali watemile yinze.

12 U munhu, si kosuma kwikala mwi'lumbo lyakwe,

langa kofwa du nhe zinimu.

13 Azino zo za mu wulele wawo, zono ziwaganaga,

na wuwo wo mudumo wa wose,

wono welumbilaga zisawo zawo.

14 Kuli wene, lifwa yo mudimi wawo,

lyono lyowategula sunga ko ku citenjelo,

nhe zingholo zono ziwiciywe kuwulagwa.

Nawo we ling'ani, wokwilumbila muli wabi,

i miwili yawo yilawa yeza kuwola kuko ku yisi ye wafu,

kutali muno ne zikaya zawo zono wali wekaye.

15 Ninga ane, Mulungu konhaza,

komhonya mu vilungo vye yisi ye wafu.

16 Holeka agwe wulece kuhangawa,

lono wukuwona munhu yunji yakugolipa,

hamba lono sawo yakwe yikonjelezeka muno,

17 hambi lono yakufwa,

nha sikosoleleza cinji co cose,

hamba si vyobitana ne sawo yakwe

kuko ku yisi ye wafu yaye.

18 Holeka hamba yedunhumule mu mikalile yiyi,

sunga ne wanhu wamukumye ku soko

yo winji we sawo yakwe.

19 Ninga hamba yatye, vyo kobita du

vikahanje ne walezi wakwe kuko ku yisi ye wafu,

kono wutitu wutemile cibitilila.

20 Gwe, munhu hamba yatye, si kosuma kwikala

mwi'lumbo lyakwe vilo vyose,

langa kofwa du nhe vilimu.
