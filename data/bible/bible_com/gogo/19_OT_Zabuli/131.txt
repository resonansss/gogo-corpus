Zabuli 131

Wulombe wo witumbilo wo winenenyhezi

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

ane nakondya kuwuleka widunhumuzi wangu,

nawuleka sunga no wiwoni wangu wose.

Alu, hamba si nghwisacila mawaha,

hamba zo wizine zono zikufutila.

2 Langa 'ne nhulile twii, kanyhi nina no mulamu,

mu nhumbula yangu, nha vyono mwana ketuzaga,

mu mhambaga yo nyina, nhavivyo ne nhumbula yangu,

vyo yitulile muli'ne.

3 Gwe Isilayeli wumwitumbile Muwaha Mulungu,

ku wulawa lulu, sunga ko ku cibitilila.
