Zabuli 58

Kumulomba Mulungu yawaling'anize i wabi

1 Nanye mono mutemile!

Alu, mulig'anizaga mu mbeka?

Wulenyi, mbeka muwaling'anizaga wanhu mu ling'ani?

2 Hamba hadodo!

Anye langa mwikalaga mukwihwesa,

zo wituma zimbi du nhondo zose,

kanyhi yo nyenye mono mukuleta,

nghani ze mewonezi mu'si.

3 Hambi i wabi, nha wahodanjice du,

ku wulawa vyono walelwa,

wekalaga wakulonga zo wulambi du,

kulawa vyono walelwa.

4 Wamemile sumu yose,

sumu yinha ye zinzoka kanyhi wali ciziwi,

wenha lizoka linyamwilu lyono lili ciziwi.

5 Lyono silihulikaga hamba lizi lyo muhawi,

hamba lwimbo lo muganga, u munghula ku wuhawi.

6 Gwe go Mulungu, wugabenaje meno gawo,

wugakule i meno ge masimba gago,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu.

7 Wajililile lukulu nhe malenga,

gono gang'welelaga hasi mu muhangha, wawe nhe mahanze,

wakanyanjilwe, wanyale.

8 Wajililile lukulu,

nha vyono nghola yinjilaga mu nhope yajilila,

wawe nho muda wono wahodanjika,

hamba kuliwona ne lizuwa yaye!

9 Holeka, lono wakali du,

hamba si wanamanya cinji agwe wuwakulaje nhe mamivya,

hamba mabeleje.

Na kwe likuwo lyako go Mulungu,

wapemhelusizwe kutali muno muno akuno ho wakali womi.

10 Nawo we ling'ani mbeka wosangalala,

lono wakuwona i wabi vyono wakulipicizwa.

Nawo wojenda wakugazika,

mucanyha ye sakami ye wanhu wabi.

11 Ne wanhu wose wolonga wotya,

“Heya mbeka wanhu we ling'ani wahumbilizwaga!

Na mbeka u Mulungu,

ho yuli mono yakuyiling'aniza yinze!”
