Zabuli 132

Lukumyo lwe Nyumba yo Mulungu

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, mukumbucile Dawudi,

wukumbucile magazo gose gono yagatuga.

2 Kanyhi 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wukumbucile sunga no wihuwizo wono yakwihuwiza,

no wilahize wono yakwilahiziza,

agwe go Mudaha Vyose, go Mulungu wa Yakobo,

3 nha vyono yatya si kokwinjila mu kaya,

hamba kugona mu citanda cakwe,

4 vyono si kosaka yagone nhongo,

hamba kusinzila,

5 sunga kolo yakondya kukuzenjela,

wikalo wako agwe go Mudaha Vyose,

go Muwaha Mulungu wa Yakobo!

6 Nase cazihulika kuko ku Efulata,

ze Lisanduku lye Cilagane,

cikalyagana mu mawimbi ga ku Yeyalimu.

7 “Heya, alenyi ku kaya yo Mulungu,

cikahembe he 'goda lyakwe lyo wutemi!”

8 Wuze ku Nyumba yako ne Lisanduku lye Cilagane,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

ciwe co citanjilo ce vilungo vyako,

nagwe wukekale kuko cibitilila.

9 Ne wakohani wako waweze ling'ani nhondo zose,

ne wehuwilwa wako, nawo, watowe nyhenze zo wusangalale!

10 Na ku soko ya Dawudi mwitumami wako,

go Muwaha Mulungu,

holeka wulece kumulema yuyo mutemi wako,

mono wamuhagula.

11 Hambi nha yo gwegwe mono wamwihuwiza Dawudi,

wihuwizo wono wukangaye,

wono si kowubena yaye, lono watya,

“Nomutemya yumonga wa muli wanago yawe mutemi,

nayo kotema agwe kolo wakondya kufwa.

12 Na hono wanago wasume kwibata cilagane cangu du,

hamonga no kukamisa malajilizo gangu,

gono nawalajiliza, ale ne wana wawo nawo,

nhavivilya wojendelela kuwuhala wutemi wako,

vilo vyose.”

13 Muwaha Mulungu yalihagula likaya lya Sayuni,

yakasaka wuwe wo wikalo wakwe, yakatya,

14 “Ahano langa, ho wowa wo wikalo wangu,

cibitilila, ho hanhu hono notema.

15 Nane nolipela likaya lya Sayuni,

visakwa vyalyo vyose,

kanyhi nowegutya zindigwa iwaciwa wayo.

16 Ne wakohani wayo nawo,

nowamotya mu milimo yawo,

ne wehuwilwa wayo nawo,

wotowa nyhenze zo wusangalale.

17 Na mumo namo, mo nomunozeza yumonga,

wa mu wana wa Dawudi, yawe mutemi mu waha,

kanyhi nowusikamya wutemi,

wo mutemi wangu muhagulwa.

18 Nane nowakoza cevu walugu wakwe,

ninga wutemi wakwe womoteswa muno muno.”
