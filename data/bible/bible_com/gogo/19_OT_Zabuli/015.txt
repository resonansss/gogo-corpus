Zabuli 15

Mikalile yo munhu yono yikumwendeza Mulungu

(Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nani 'mbi mono kosuma kwikala mwi'hema lyako?

Hamba nani mono kosuma kwikala mwi'tunda lyako,

lyono lyelile?

2 Yo munhu du mono yasina wuhoswe,

mono ketumaga ze ling'ani nhondo zose,

mono kalongaga ze mbeka kulawa mu nhumbula yakwe.

3 Munhu mono si muheha wanhu.

Kanyhi yo munhu mono si kanozezaga muyagwe vibi,

hamba kumuvila muyagwe makani go wulambi.

4 Kanyhi munhu yuyu kawabedegalaga wose,

wono Mulungu yakuwalema,

langa kawapelaga cogopo wose wono,

wamogopilo Mulungu.

Nayo kawufisilizaga wihuwizo wakwe,

hamba wuwe wukumuletela wambuce.

5 Kanyhi mwene kakopeshaga hamba kudaya cambuzi yaye,

hamba si kabocelaga mafupo ho yamutamise munhu,

mono yasina wuhoswe, no munhu we liwala lilino,

kowa yakamile cibitilila.
