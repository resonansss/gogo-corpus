Zabuli 57

Kulomba wutazi

1 Wumbonele lusungu, 'gwe Mulungu wumbonele

lusungu, ane nghutiziza kuli'gwe.

Hambi mu mubeho we mabawa gako,

nha mo mowuli wihundiko,

sunga kolo magazo gaga mawaha gakupuluta.

2 Nane nghumulilila Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye,

u Mulungu mono kafisilizaga visakwa vyangu vyose.

3 Nayo kohuliciza ku wulawa ku wulanga no kumhonya,

kowakoza cevu wawo wono wakunyiwonela.

Mwene Mulungu kondajila zo lusungu lwakwe,

hamonga no wihuwilwa wakwe.

4 Nawo walugu wamhilime wenha masimba malya wanhu,

ne meno gawo, gehwanile migoha ne zisonga,

ne zinimi zawo zihwanile mumaje mukali.

5 Wunojeye wukumywe ko ku wulanga 'gwe go Mulungu,

ne nhojo yako yiweze mu yinze yose.

6 Hambi i walugu nha wanhejile no lwavu,

wangwize, nane nghuzizidika muno so wukalale.

Wahimbiye likombo mu nzila, ninga hamba nhavivyo,

weneco wo wakugwila mumo.

7 Gwe go Mulungu, nhumbula yangu 'ne yimile,

yikamile, heya,

nhumbula yangu yimile yikamile, holeka nane nokwimba,

no kukusangalalila gwegwe!

8 Holeka winuce 'gwe go nhumbula yangu!

Mwinukaje nanye nyo ndonondo ne lisumbi!

Nane nosinhuka hakali mitondo muno muno.

9 Nane nokulumbila muli makungugo,

go Muwaha Mulungu,

kanyhi nokwimbila ze malumbo muli wanhu.

10 Hambi lusungu lwako nha luficile ko ku wulanga,

no wihuwilwa wako wuficile ko kucanyha.

11 Wunojeye wukumywe,

ko ku wulanga 'gwe go Mulungu,

ne nhojo yako yiwe mu yinze yose.
