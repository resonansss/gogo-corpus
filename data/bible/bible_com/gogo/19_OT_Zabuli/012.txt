Zabuli 12

Kulomba wutazi

(Kuli mwimbizi: Nho wimbe wa Sheminithi. Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Ale, wucitaze, 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu!

Hambi wono wakogopile gwegwe nha wamalika,

i wehuwilwa wose nha wakondya kwajililila lukulu.

2 Wose welonjelaga nghani za wulambi du,

ne zinghani zawo zose za wiyendezi du kanyhi za wukoci.

3 Gwe Muwaha, wuyidindaje milomo yiyo yo wulambi,

wuzinyamazye zinimi zizo zono zilongaga zo widunhumuzi.

4 Wakutya, “Kwe milomo yetu, cina wusume,

nase colonga nha vyono cendile nani 'mbi mono kocisuma?”

5 Ninga u Muwaha Mulungu yakutya nhavi,

“Ku soko vyono wasocele wakwiwonelwa,

ne waciwa wakung'ung'ulika, alu neza kuza,

nane nowapela wuwo mulamu wono wakuwulanjilila.”

6 Wihuwizo wo Muwaha Mulungu wa mbeka,

kanyhi welile,

winha fweza yono yilenguywe mu moto,

winha fweza yono yilenguywe nghanghulo mupunghati.

7 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu wucikamisaje,

no kucicitila kuli lulelo lulu.

8 I wabi wakupilima hanhu hose,

no wajilile wukulumbizwa muli wanhu.
