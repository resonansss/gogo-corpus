Zabuli 90

CITABU CE KANE

(Zabuli 90–106)

Mulungu we cibitilila no munhu

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

agwe yo gwegwe kuwezaga wikalo wetu,

vilo vyose mu zindelo zose.

2 Agwe yo gwegwe go Mulungu,

we cibitilila ne cibitilila,

mono wali wuli baho du,

sunga na lono matunda gali gakali si nawa,

hamba yinze kuwumbwa.

3 Agwe kumulonjelaga munhu,

yawuye kono yalawilila,

kumupitulaga kawa manghundi.

4 Myaka elufu monga kuli'gwe,

yiwezaga nhe zuwa limonga,

yinha yigolo yono yapuluta,

yinha lidaha lidodo du lye cilo.

5 Kuwakwenghwelezezaga kutali nhe lihwazi.

Na hakali mitondo wawa wenha,

mahanze gono gakuwiluka.

6 Hakali mitondo gakusipuka no kudedewala,

ninga cinyamihe gakuwa gakondya kunyala.

7 Likuwo lyako likucongomeza,

cikogofywa no wukali wako.

8 Hambi'gwe nha wuwumanyile wubi wetu,

nagwe kuwanzalikaga wubi wetu ko wuzelu wako.

9 Na kwe likuwo lyako, wumi wetu wihemaga,

wuwa kumalika, nha vyono muhe wumalikaga.

10 Lunji cidahile kufisa myaka makumi mupunghati,

mu wikale wetu, na lunji makumi munana,

hono ciwe cina vilungo, ninga hamba nhavivyo,

mu myaka yose, cili mu magayo du no wukalale.

Navyo vilo, vikumalika mbela mbela,

nase cikupuluta musunho!

11 Alo nani 'mbi,

mono kosuma kumanya zo wukali we likuwo lyako?

Hamba nani lu mono yaziceleleye zo wukali wako?

12 Wucijize zo wucefu we vilo vyo wikale wetu,

ho cisume kuwa ne nhumbula ye mahala.

13 Ale wuwuye go Muwaha Mulungu!

Alo kojendelela kusimba sunga nili lo?

Wuciwonele lusungu ase co wetumami wako.

14 Wucigushe zo lusungu lwako hakali mitondo,

nase ho cisecelele,

no kusangalala vilo vyetu vyose.

15 Wucipele lu myaka minji yo wusangalale,

mu nghango ye myaka minji ye magayo

no wugazice, yono wacipela.

16 Wucilajile ze metumo gako ge vilungo,

ase co wetumami wako.

Wuwalajile wana wetu vilungo vye nhojo yako.

17 Gwe Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wetu,

wuciwonele lusungu wucipele cambuzi muli zose,

zono cikwituma.
