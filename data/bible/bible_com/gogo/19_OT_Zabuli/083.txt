Zabuli 83

Wulombe ku soko ye walugu wa Isilayeli

1 Gwe go Mulungu, wulece kunyamala, wulece kutula,

hamba kunyamala twii 'gwe go Mulungu!

2 Lanje ta walugu wako vyono wasagahaye,

na vyono wakukubeza wono wakusulile.

3 Wakuhila wawasepe wanhu wako no kuwatamisa,

wakusaka wawatendele vibi wawo wono kuwadimaga.

4 Wakutya nhavi,

“Alenyi muze cilyongomezeze lukulu likungugo lyawo,

ne litagwa lya Isilayeli lyajililile lukulu.”

5 Nawo welumbanizize kumonga welunze ho walewane nagwe,

6 wanya Edomu, ne Waisimayeli,

Wamowabi, ne wanhu wa Hagali,

7 wanya Gebali, Amoni na Amaleki,

ne wanhu wa Filisiti,

sunga ne wekala ku Tulo wose.

8 Sunga ne Ashuli nayo yelunza nawo,

wakuwataza wana wa Loti!

9 Wuwanozeze nha vyono wawanozeza wanhu wa Midiyani.

Nha vyono wawanozeza wa Sisela,

na Yabini he kolongo lya Kishoni,

10 wono wawongomeza ku Endoli,

sunga ne miwili yawo yikawa mboleya ya yisi.

11 Wuwanozeze wawaha wawo,

nha vyono wawanozeza wa Olebu na Zebu,

ne watemi wawo wose, wawe nha Zeba na Zalumuna,

12 wono watya,

“Cokwisolela sese yisi yo Mulungu.”

13 Gwe go Mulungu wangu,

wuwanoze wawe nha manghundi,

wawe nhe mahanze gono gakupemheluswa ne mbeho,

14 nha vyono moto wuticimizaga mbago,

nha vyono malambi-lambi go moto,

gatakamizaga matunda.

15 Wuwawingaje wawo kwe cihumbi cako,

wuwogofye ne libeho lyako liwaha lyo mukukunzo,

16 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wuwanozaje wawone cevu ciwaha,

ho wawulondole wuwaha wako.

17 Wawone cevu no kugazika cibitilila,

wakozwe soni no kwajililila lukulu.

18 Ho wamanye vyono yo gwegwe du wuli wene,

go Muwaha Mulungu,

Mulungu Mono Wulawiliciye mu yinze yose.
