Zabuli 149

Lwimbo lo lukumyo

1 Mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu!

Mumwimbile Muwaha Mulungu lwimbo lupya,

mumukumizye mu mhuga ye wanhu we ling'ani!

2 Wusangalale, 'gwe Isilayeli,

hambi mwene nha yo muwumbi wako,

nanye nyo wekala ku Sayuni, sangalalenyi,

hambi mwene nha yo mutemi wenyu.

3 Mulikumizye litagwa lyakwe,

mu wuvina, mumwimbile kwe zing'oma ne masumbi.

4 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu,

nha kendezwa ne wanhu wakwe,

kawapelaga i wapolo wusume.

5 Nawo i wehuwilwa, wasecelelele mu lukumyo,

wasangalale cilo cose sunga wele.

6 Nhondo zose weze kwanza,

zo lukumyo luwaha lo Mulungu,

akuno webate ne mimaje mikali,

kose kose mu miwoko yawo,

7 ho wasume kuwalipiciza we makungugo,

no wulipicizwe we wanhu,

8 wawawope watemi wawo ne manyolo,

wawawope ne walongola wawo ne manyolo ge cuma,

9 wawalipicize we makungugo,

nha vyono Mulungu yalajiliza.

Na wuwo wo wusume we wanhu wo Mulungu.

Mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu!
