Zabuli 125

Wucitilwe we wanhu wo Mulungu

1 Wono wamwitumbiye Muwaha Mulungu,

wehwanile ne litunda lya Sayuni,

lyono si lisinjisikaga yaye,

langa lisikame vilo vyose.

2 Nha vyono Yelusalemi yipilimwe ne matunda,

ale, no Muwaha Mulungu nayo,

vyo yawapilime wanhu wakwe,

ku wulawa lulu, sunga ko ku cibitilila.

3 Hambi nha si kowaleka i wabi,

ho wateme yisi ya we ling'ani,

ku soko hono wateme,

wowambuza weling'ani nawo wetume wubi.

4 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

weze muswanu kuli wanhu waswanu,

kuli walya du wono webate malajilizo gako.

5 Ninga lono wukuwalipiciza i wanhu wabi,

ale wulipicize sunga,

na wono si wakutyatya zo wutonji wako.

Mulamu weze kuli Isilayeli!
