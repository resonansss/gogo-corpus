Zabuli 66

Lwimbo lwo lukumyo ne lilumbo

1 Anye nyo wanhu wose mono'nze,

mumusangalalilaje Mulungu!

2 Mwanze zo lukumyo lwakwe kwa nyimbo,

mulavizyenyi lilumbo lyo lukumyo!

3 Mumulonjele Mulungu mutye, “Imetumo gako,

mbeka go metumo go wizine muno muno!

Na ku soko ye vilungo vyako viwaha,

sunga ne walugu wako lu, weza kukufugamila gwegwe.

4 Ne yinze yose yikukuhemba gwegwe,

sunga ne wanhu lu, wakwimba zo lukumyo lwako!”

5 Ale, muze ta muziwone zimbaha,

zono yetumile Mulungu,

Kanoza nghani zo wizine muli wanhu.

6 Mwene yalinyaza lilenga hakawa yisi nghavu,

ne walezi wetu wakasuma kulowoka mumo,

wakujenda kwa migulu, ale nase baho,

cikawa kusecelela muno.

7 Kagadimaga cibitilila kwe vilungo vyakwe.

Mwene yo kagadimaga makungugo gose.

No mubedegazi wo wose yalece kumupinga-pinga ku mwande.

8 Nanye nyo makungugo gose, ale,

mumulumbe Mulungu wetu,

mwanze zo lukumyo lwakwe,

zihulikwe hanhu hose.

9 U mwene yo kacipelaga wumi,

hamba si yacileka cigwe.

10 Hambi 'gwe go Mulungu, nha wacipima,

wacijela nha vyono fweza yikuwa kujelwa mu moto.

11 Wakondya kucigwiza mu lwavu,

wacidinghuza mutegulo muzito muno.

12 Kuwaleka walugu wetu wacikanyanjile,

cikolela mu moto na mu malenga.

Ninga luno wakondya kucileta hanhu hono hapolile.

13 Nane nokuletela mu Nyumba yako,

vilavywa vinyanywe,

no kukufisiliziza wihuwizo wangu.

14 Nane nokulavizya nha vyono nehuwiza nene no mweneco,

mu cipango cono nali nili mu wugaye.

15 Nokulavizya vilavywa vinyanywe vyono vijinile muno,

vilavywa vinyanywe vye zingholo zinume,

nolavya zinghongolo hamonga ne zimhene.

16 Anye mono mumogopilo Mulungu,

alenyi muze wose, muhulicize,

nane nomulolosezenyi zono yanhendeye Mulungu.

17 Ane namulomba wutazi,

kanyhi nghamukumya no lulimi lwangu.

18 Na hono nali nihwese zimbi mu nhumbula yangu,

nayo Mulungu hamba si yali yahulicize ng'o.

19 Ninga alu mbeka Mulungu kahulika,

heya mbeka, kawuhulika wulombe wangu.

20 U Mulungu yalumbwe,

hambi nha si yawulemile wulombe wangu,

hamba kuluwusa lusungu lwakwe kuli'ne.
