Zabuli 51

Kulomba wunenelwe

1 Wumbonele lusungu, 'gwe go Mulungu,

mu nghango yo wendo wako wuwaha,

wugapatile lukulu masutya gangu,

ku soko yo winji wo lusungu lwako.

2 Wunyezeze lukulu wavu wangu,

wunghweme wubi wangu.

3 Nghuvumila wubi wangu,

hambi nha nikaye nghuwuwona wubi wangu,

calinze vilo vyose.

4 Nane nghusutizya gwegwe du wuli wene,

nituma zimbi hali gwegwe.

Holeka lono wukuning'aniza, wukunoza viswanu,

hambi nha wina ling'ani lono wukundipiciza.

5 Ane nha nili musutyi ku wulawa vyono nalelwa,

nili mubi ku wulawilila mu'nda ya yaya.

6 Ninga agwe wendile winenenyhezi wa mugati,

holeka wunyijizaje mahala mu nhumbula.

7 Wumbusize wubi, niwe nyelile, wunghweme,

ne Hisopo niwe muzelu swee.

8 Wumemye nyemo no wusangalale.

Wusangalaze kanyhi, ane mono wamhondanga.

9 Ninga wulece kuwulanga wubi wangu,

wupate masutya gangu gose.

10 Gwe go Mulungu, wumumbile nhumbula swanu,

wumbicile muli'ne nhumbula mhya yono yikamile.

11 Wulece kunhaga kutali nagwe,

hamba kumbusiza Muhe wako Mono Yelile.

12 Wundekaje sangalale,

vyono wamhonya, wumhele nhumbula ye cihuliko.

13 Nane ho nowejiza i wasutyi ze nzila yako,

nawo wanyo wubi, wokuwuyila gwegwe.

14 Gwe go Mulungu, u Mulungu we mhonelo yangu,

wumhonye ne lifwa lyono nojeye nifwe.

Nane nokwanza ko lwimbo, vyono

agwe kwitumaga ling'ani.

15 Wundigulaje mulomo wangu, 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

ho sume kuzanza zo lukumyo lwako.

16 Agwe, i mbeka, si wukwendezwa ne

likumbiko, yali yiwe si nhavivyo,

ane nali nghulavizye.

Agwe si wendile vilavywa vinyanywe.

17 Langa cilavywa cangu kuli'gwe go Mulungu,

yo nhumbula du yono yidejepe,

hambi 'gwe go Mulungu,

nha si kulemaga nhumbula

yono yidejepe na ye cihuliko.

18 Gwe go Mulungu,

wulinozezaje ziswanu likaya lya Sayuni,

wugazengaje kanyhi wupya majinji ga Yelusalemi.

19 Ale, baho langa,

ho kokwendezwa ne vilavywa vinyanywe,

sunga ne vilavywa vinyanyilwe lukulu,

sunga ne zinghongolo, nhondo,

ho zolavizywa mu cikumbicilo cako.
