Zabuli 112

I nyemo yo munhu muswanu

1 Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe!

Yo mumoti munhu mono yamogopile Muwaha Mulungu,

mono yakusangalalila kugebata malajilizo gakwe.

2 No lulelo lwakwe lowa ne vilungo kononze,

ne wanagwe nawo wolugutizwa.

3 Ne mhuga yakwe wowa wagoli nayo,

kowa yamotile nhondo zose.

4 Wuzelu wuwamulicilaga mu wutitu,

i wanhu waswanu, heya,

wanhu wono wali lusungu,

hambi Muwaha Mulungu,

nha yali lusungu kanyhi mupolo,

kanyhi yali ling'ani.

5 Yo mumoti u munhu mucemezi,

mono kakopeshaga hamba kusaka cambuzi,

mono ketumilaga milimo yakwe mu ling'ani.

6 Nayo we ling'ani hamba si kosumwa yaye,

langa kokumbucilwa nhondo zose.

7 Na hono yaganwe na nghani 'mbi,

hamba si kokogopa yaye,

hambi nhumbula yakwe nha yikamile,

kanyhi yimwitumbilaga Muwaha Mulungu.

8 Yakuwa yasina mahwele,

hamba si yakogopa yaye,

hambi nha yamanyiye lukulu,

vyono walugu wakwe wosumwa.

9 Kalavyaga ko wuswanu, kawapelaga bwete waciwa,

no wuswanu wakwe, wikalaga cibitilila.

No munhu we liwala lilyo, kolumbwa cibitilila.

10 Ninga i wabi waziwonaga zizo wawa kusimba,

wawa kugwezula meno gawo, ko lusulo no kwajilila,

witumbilo wawo, hodu wahodanjika.
