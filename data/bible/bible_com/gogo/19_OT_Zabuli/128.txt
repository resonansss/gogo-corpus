Zabuli 128

Cihumbilizwa kuli mono yamogopilo Muwaha Mulungu

1 Wamoti wose wono wamogopilo Muwaha Mulungu,

wono wakugatyatya malajilizo gakwe.

2 I milimo yako, yo yokutujila ne visakwa vyako,

kanyhi kowona nyemo no wumoteswe,

3 No mugolece wako, kowa yehwanile mu zabibu,

wono wukulela muno, mu kaya yako,

ne wanago wowa wehwanile vinhambi,

vyo mu zabibu vyono vikutula,

woyipilima meza yako yo wulilo.

4 Heya, nhavivyo vyo komoteswa munhu,

mono yamogopilo Muwaha Mulungu.

5 U Muwaha Mulungu, yakulugute ku wulawa ku Sayuni!

Nagwe wuwuwone wumoteswe wa Yelusalemi,

mu vilo vyose vyo wikale wako.

6 Heya, wikale sunga wuwawone wana we wanago!

Mulamu weze kuli Isilayeli!
