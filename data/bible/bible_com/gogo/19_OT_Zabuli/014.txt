Zabuli 14

U wubi we wanhu

(Zab 53)

(Kuli mwimbizi: Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 I walele welonjelaga caka wakutya,

“Hasina Mulungu.”

Wose wahodanji, wetuma zinghani ze cikomaza,

holeka hasina hamba monga mono yakwituma ziswanu.

2 U Muwaha Mulungu yakuwasunjilila wanhu,

ku wulawa ku wulanga,

ho yawone hono ho yuli yunji we mahala,

hono ho yuli munhu mono yakumulondola Mulungu.

3 Wose wapatuka, wose watipiye,

hasina mono ketumaga ziswanu, hasina hamba monga.

4 Alu Mulungu nayo yakutya, “Mwene wawo wono

wakwituma makani wubi, alo wasina wizuce lo?

Wono wakuwahiziza wanhu wangu,

hamba si wakuhemba ane yaye.”

5 Baho 'lu langa, wocicima ko woga,

hambi u Muwaha Mulungu,

nha yali hamonga na wose wono wali ling'ani.

6 I wanhu wabi, wahodangaga,

wuhile wo munhu muciwa, ninga Muwaha Mulungu,

wo wutizizo wakwe.

7 Yali yiwe ciba le,

hono mhonelo ya Isilayeli yilawe ku Sayuni!

Wolowa wusangalale wuwaha kuli Isilayeli,

kolo Muwaha Mulungu yakuwagomolela

wumoteswe wawo kanyhi,

i wanhu wa Yakobo wosangalala,

wanhu wa Isilayeli wowona nyemo.
