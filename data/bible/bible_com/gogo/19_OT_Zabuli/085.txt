Zabuli 85

Kulilombela likungugo wumoteswe

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, kwendezwa ne yisi yako,

kuwamotya kanyhi Isilayeli.

2 Kuwanenela wanhu wako wubi wawo,

wawusemwa wubi wawo wose.

3 Kuwimila wukali wako wose,

hamba si wukuwakolelwa likuwo kanyhi.

4 Wucigomole, 'gwe go Mulungu,

go Mukombozi wetu,

wulece kuciwonela likuwo kanyhi.

5 Alu 'mbi kojendelela kucisimbila cibitilila?

Alu mwene, wukali wako,

wojendelela sunga ku zindelo zose lo?

6 Alu hongo, si kocipela kanyhi mikalile mipya,

i wanhu wako ho wakusangalalile?

7 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wucilajile wuswanu wako,

wucipele ne mhonelo yako.

8 Nghuhulika zono yakutya Mulungu, u Muwaha Mulungu,

yakwihuwiza kuwapela mulamu wanhu wakwe wehuwilwa,

hono walece kuwuwuyila kanyhi wulele wawo.

9 Mbeka, mwene yewikanize kuwaponya wose,

wono wamogopile, no lukumyo lwakwe,

lokwikala mu yisi yetu.

10 Lusungu lwakwe ne mbeka yakwe vyokwitang'anila,

nayo ling'ani no mulamu vyokwikumbatila.

11 I mbeka yocindika mono yisi, iling'ani nayo,

yotima kulawa ku wulanga.

12 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, kociletela wumoteswe,

ne yisi yetu yocitujila ndigwa nyinji.

13 I ling'ani nayo yolongolela hali Muwaha Mulungu,

no kumunozelezeza nzila yakwe.
