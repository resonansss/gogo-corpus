Zabuli 48

Sayuni likaya lyo Mulungu

1 Muwaha Mulungu, yo Muwaha,

mono yanojeye kulumbwa mu Nyumba yakwe,

2 Mucanyha ye litunda lyakwe,

lyono lyelile Sayuni.

Litunda lyo Mulungu,

yitali kanyhi yiswanu,

likaya lyo Mutemi muwaha,

lyo liletaga nyemo ye yinze.

3 Na mu zikaya ze likaya lilyo lya Sayuni,

mumo mo Mulungu,

yakwilajila, vyono mwene wo wihundiko wono wukamile.

4 Watemi wakekonghola hamonga,

wakabitila wose hamonga kwihoma.

5 Ninga wali weza kuliwona du likaya lya Sayuni,

wakezina muno muno,

wakebatwa no woga, hodu wakawa kutiza.

6 Woga wukawebata, wakanza kuhangawa muno,

wakawa nho mucekulu,

mono yali mu nghunga yo lusawo.

7 Wakawa nha wono waputigwe,

ne libeho liwaha lya kucilima,

lyono lizibenanjizaga zingalawa zimbaha.

8 Zose zono cazihulikaga vyono Mulungu yo yetumile,

alu nhondo sese co weneco caziwona,

mwi'kaya lyo Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga,

nayo Mulungu kolidima likaya lilyo sunga cibitilila.

9 Gwe go Mulungu, ase cikuzihwesa muno zo

lusungu lwako mu Nyumba yako.

10 Agwe yo gwegwe mono wukukumywa muno hanhu hose,

lukumyo lwako lumanyice hanhu hose mono'nze yose.

Nagwe wutemile kwe ling'ani.

11 Holeka wanhu wa ku Sayuni,

yiwanojeye wasangalale!

Agwe kuling'anizaga muling'ano,

holeka wanhu wa mwi'kaya lya Yuda, wasecelele!

12 Ale, mubite mukalipilime likaya lya Sayuni,

bitenyi mukaviwale vizuli vyalyo.

13 Mugalangaje majinji galyo,

musunjilile sunga ne magomawakali galyo,

ho mosuma kuziganulila zindelo,

zono zikuza muwe kutya nhavi,

14 “Ayu yo Mulungu, Mulungu wetu cibitilila!

Nayo kowa mutonji wetu sunga cibitilila!”
