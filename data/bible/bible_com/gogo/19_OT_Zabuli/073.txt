Zabuli 73

CITABU CE KADATU

(Zabuli 73–89)

Wiling'ano wo Mulungu

1 Mbeka Mulungu yo muswanu kuli Isilayeli,

muswanu na kuli wono wena nhumbula nhelevu.

2 Nali neza nili behi,

no kwikobala, na hadodo du nali nhelele,

3 hambi nha nali kuwawonela wivu i wedunhumuzi,

lono nali neza kuwona i wabi wakumoteswa.

4 Hambi wawo nha si waganagwa no wugaye yaye,

i miwili yawo mitelevu,

kanyhi wena ne vilungo du, wakamile.

5 No wugaye we wanhu,

hamba si wuwaganaga wene,

kanyhi hamba si wagazikaga,

nha vyono wanji wagazikaga.

6 No widunhumuzi waweza kuli wene,

nha mukufu mu singo zawo,

no wukali nawo, wawa nha wo mwenda wawo wo kuvwala,

7 Ne meso gawo gavimbile so wujine,

zinhumbula zawo zilavyaga ze mahweso gawo mabi,

8 Wendile kuwezwajiza wanhu wanji, no kuwalonga makani mabi,

wedunhumulaga no kupanga zo wiwonela wanhu wanji.

9 Na kwe milomo yawo, wawulongaga vibi wulanga,

na kwe zinimi zawo, wetapaga mono'nze.

10 Sunga ne wanhu wo Mulungu nawo wakuwatyatya,

no kwihuwila zose zono wakuzilonga.

11 Akuno wakutya, “Mulungu si kozimanya yaye!

Hamba Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye,

si kosuma kuziwona ng'o!”

12 Nhavivi vyo wenha i wanhu wabi,

wakwizize vyose no kuciziliza.

13 Alu ne hongo, neyeza bwete nhumbula yangu,

no kwilanjilila ndece kwituma wubi?

14 Misi yose nhuga magazo,

nagwe kumhelaga wulipicizwe,

hakali mitondo nhondo zose.

15 Nane mhali nonje nha vyono wene wakulonga,

si nali niwe nili munhu we mbeka muli wanhu wako.

16 Nane nghajela kuyihwesa nghani yiyi,

ninga yikawa nghani nghamu muno muno kuli'ne,

17 sunga langa nali neza kuhelela hono helile hako,

baho langa ho namanyila zono zowagana i wabi.

18 Mbeka, agwe kuwawikaga i hanhu ha cinheleza,

wukuwanoza wagwe wajilile.

19 Wakongomezwa nhinicizo,

no kupatilwa kutali ko wogofye.

20 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, kolo wukusinhuka,

wene wakwajilila kamonga,

hamba kukumbucilwa kanyhi,

wawa kwihwana nha ndoto,

zono munhu kazisemwaga,

lono yakwinuka hakali mitondo.

21 Nali neza kuwuwona wusungu mu nhumbula yangu,

no kuhomeka mu ciselinji cangu.

22 Nghawa mhuwile hamba kumanya cinji,

nghehwana nhe lilimu hali'gwe.

23 Ninga hamba yiwe nhavivyo,

ane nili kuli 'gwe du vilo vyose,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu!

Agwe, wunyibate muwoko no kumhela vilungo.

24 Agwe yo gwegwe du. Mono wukunhonga ko wulanjizi wako,

na ku mudumo, kombocela mu lukumyo lwako.

25 Alu nani 'mbi kanyhi ku wulanga,

mono kosuma kunhaza, langa hono si gwegwe du?

Na hono niwe nili hamonga nagwe mono'nze,

hamba sinosuma kwanjila cinji yaye.

26 Hamba niwe nyhepuciywe vilungo,

mu muwili na mu nhumbula,

agwe go Mulungu, yo gwegwe go vilungo vyangu,

ne ligawo lyangu yo gwegwe du cibitilila.

27 Na muli wose mono kokwitemanula nagwe, ale,

yuyo mbeka kofwa du.

Nayo mono si yakukuvumila,

yuyo nayo agwe komongomezeza lukulu.

28 Ninga viswanu kuli'ne,

niwe behi nagwe go Mulungu,

hambi agwe go Muwaha Mulungu nha yo mhonelo yangu.

Nane nozanza ze milimo yakwe yose yono witumile!
