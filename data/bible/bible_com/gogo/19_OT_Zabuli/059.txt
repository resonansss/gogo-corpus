Zabuli 59

Kulomba wudimwe

1 Gwe go Mulungu wangu,

wumhonye kuli walugu wangu,

wunyhitile kuli wawo wono wakwisumaniza nane.

2 Wumhonye kuli wawo i wanhu wabi,

wunghomboze kuli wawo wawulaji!

3 Alu, lanje, ta,

vyono wakusepa wambulaje, i wanhu,

wawulaji wakwilunza hamonga ku soko yangu.

4 Lanje vyono wakumangha, wakwiwikaniza ku soko yangu,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

kumbe nane ho sina lisutya, hamba wuhoswe,

hamba wubi muli'ne,

holeka winuce 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu wunhaze!

5 Ale winuce 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

Mulungu u Mudaha Vyose,

yo gwegwe go Mulungu wa Isilayeli.

Winuce wuwalipicize wawo wanhu,

wono si wakumanyile yaye,

wulece kuwalecela lukulu i wabi,

hamba yumonga, wose i wetuma zimbi.

6 Walugu wawa, wawuyaga nhondo zose cinyamihe,

wakufwema nhe zimbwa,

akuno wakulipilima-pilima likaya.

7 Wulanje ta,

wogofye wawo hamonga ne maligo gawo!

Zinimi zawo zihwanile mimaje mikali,

kanyhi wene wehwesaga vyono,

hasina mono yakuwahulika.

8 Ninga agwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

agwe wukuwaseka du,

hamba si wukuwacelelela bwete,

wanhu wawo wono si wakumanyile.

9 Ane nokwimbila zinyimbo zo kukukumya gwegwe,

agwe mono yo wuli yigomawakali lyangu.

10 Nayo Mulungu wangu,

komhela zo wendo wakwe,

kondajila niwawone walugu wangu,

vyono wakondya kusumwa.

11 Wulece kuwawulaga musunho,

i wanhu wangu ho walece kusemwa, langa,

wuwayumvyaje du kwe vilungo vyako,

kanyhi wuwagwise hasi, hambi gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nha yo gwegwe go ngula yetu!

12 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wuwalekaje wongomele,

mu nghango yo wubi we zinghani zawo,

webatwe weneco mu widunhumuzi wawo!

Ku soko yo wulijitwe,

hamonga no wulambi wawo,

13 wuwongomezezaje mwi'kuwo lyako, nawo

hamba si wokwijela kanyhi yaye, ne wanhu wose

wamanye vyono Mulungu yo yawatemile,

mu yisi ya Yakobo,

sunga ko ku ndumo ye yinze.

14 Walugu wawa,

wawuyaga nhondo zose cinyamihe,

wakufwema nhe zimbwa, akuno,

wakulipilima-pilima likaya,

15 wakuyumba-yumba akuno, na akuno

wenha zimbwa, wakulondola wulye,

na hono walece kwiguta,

hodu, wawa kwikala wakukoloma du.

16 Ninga ane nokwimba zo lukumyo lwe vilungo vyako,

kanyhi, nosangalalila zo lusungu lwako hakali mitondo,

hambi nha yo gwegwe go wihundiko wangu,

no wutizizo wangu mu vilo vyo wugaye.

17 Nokulumba, agwe mono wuli vyo vilungo vyangu,

'gwe go Mulungu, gwegwe go wihundiko wangu,

go Mulungu mono kumbonelaga lusungu!
