Zabuli 91

U Mulungu yo Mudimi wetu

1 Hono wuwe wuli mu wudimi wo Mulungu,

Mono Yalawiliciye,

na hono wiwicile lukulu muli mwene,

mono yali vilungo,

2 kosuma kumulonjela Muwaha Mulungu wutye,

“Agwe yo gwegwe go wutizizo wangu,

kanyhi go wihundiko wangu, go Mulungu wangu,

mono nghwitumbiye!”

3 Mbeka u Mulungu kokuponya mu mutego,

kanyhi kokucitila na muli matamwa mabi.

4 Mwene kokuhundiciza ne mabawa gakwe,

nagwe kodimwa no mwene viswanu,

no muwoko wakwe wokudima no kukucitila.

5 Nagwe kowa kanyhi wusina soko yo kogopa,

wogofye wo wose ku cilo,

hamba lunji wuhungucilwe we nhinicizo ku misi.

6 Wusina soko yo kogopa lihuhuji,

lyono lidahile kwijela nhinicizo ne cilo,

hamba mabi ge liwala lyolyose lilya,

gono gadahile kukuziza mu madaha ge misi.

7 Hamba wawe wanhu elufu monga,

wono wafwiye wagwe behi nagwe,

hamba lunji wanhu elufu yikumi,

wagwe hali'gwe mbavu zose,

agwe hamba si kotamiswa yaye.

8 Nagwe kowawona ne meso gako gwegwe go mweneco,

i wanhu wabi vyono wakulipicizwa.

9 Agwe kumunoza Muwaha Mulungu wutizizo wako,

kanyhi kumusaka Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye yawe,

wo witumbilo wako.

10 Holeka nghani yo yose 'mbi, si yokwagana yaye,

hamba nghani yo yose ye mawulaji,

siyosejelela ha kaya yako.

11 Hambi Mulungu,

nha kowalajiliza wamalayika wakwe,

wakudime hanhu ho hose hono kowa kubita.

12 Nawo wokudinghulila mu miwoko yawo,

ho wulece hamba kwikobala mwi'bwe.

13 Nagwe kogakanyanjila masimba ne mazoka,

kozitijinha zinyana ze masimba ne mazoka.

14 Mulungu yakutya,

“Ane nomuponya mono yanyendile.

Kanyhi nomudima mono yakumanya vyono nili Mulungu.

15 Nane nomupitula lono yakunyhemeleza,

na lono yali mu wugaye,

ane nowa nili hamonga nayo.

Nomuponya no kumupela cogopo.

16 Nomupela vilo vinji vyo wikale wakwe mono'nze,

kanyhi nomuponya.”
