Zabuli 133

Lukumyo kuli wendo wo wundugu

1 Lanje ta vyono yo nghani swanu muno,

kanyhi yono yikwendeza,

hono we zindugu wawe wekalane hamonga ko wumonga.

2 Wakuwa wehwanile mafuta go wuguzi wukamu,

gono gakuhuluma mu mutwe na mu ndevu za Haluni,

gakuhulumila sunga mo mu singo,

mu vivwalo vyakwe vyo wukohani.

3 Gakwihwana buhe ya mwi'tunda lya Helimoni,

yono yigwizaga mu matunda ga Sayuni.

Baho ho hanhu hono Muwaha Mulungu,

yehehuwiziziza u wulugute wakwe.

Heya, wumi we cibitilila.
