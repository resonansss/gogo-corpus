Zabuli 32

Wunene no wunenelwe

(Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Mumoti munhu mono yaneneywe lisutya lyakwe,

munhu mono wubi wakwe wapatilwa lukulu.

2 Yo mumoti munhu mono u Muwaha Mulungu si yakumuwalila,

hamba kugakumbucila masutya gakwe,

u munhu mono yasina wukonji wo wose mu nhumbula yakwe.

3 Lono nali nghali si nanena wubi wangu, mbeka,

nali soceye so wulila misi yose.

4 Wali kundipiciza ku misi na ku cilo,

sunga ne vilungo vyangu vikawa kuwuka muli'ne,

nane nghawa kunyala nha vyono muhangha wunyalaga

mu cibahu, lono mvula yawuka.

5 Nghawa kuwuvumila wubi wangu hali'gwe,

hamba sinawuhundika wavu wangu.

Nghawona viswanu, mvumile wubi wangu hali'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nagwe wukawa kunenela wubi wangu wose.

6 Holeka yimunojeye munhu wo wose mono yakogopile,

yakulombe mwi'daha lyo wugaye,

hamba gaze magaziko menji,

ninga si gomuficila yaye.

7 Agwe yo gwegwe go wutizizo wangu,

kunghamisaga mu wugaye wangu.

Nina wusangalale wuwaha

vyono wakondya kumhonya.

8 Mulungu yakutya, “Nokulajila nzila yono kokola,

na kanyhi, nokwijiza no kukutonga.

9 Wulece kuwa mulele nha vyono yinha fwalasi hamba nyumbu,

yono yiwopelezwaga matandiko ne mikowa mu mulomo,

hono wulece kuyinoza nhavivyo, si yokusejelela yaye.”

10 Nawo i wanhu wabi, wotuga magayo menji,

ninga wono wamwitumbiye Muwaha Mulungu,

awo wakudimwa ko lusungu lwakwe.

11 Nanye mo wanhu we ling'ani, musecelele,

no kusangalalila i metumo gono yetumile u Muwaha Mulungu,

anye nanye mo wenenenyhezi,

mutowe nyhenze za nyemo.
