Zabuli 79

Kulomba likungugo liponywe

1 Gwe go Mulungu,

i wanhu wono si wakumanyile, wenjila mu yisi yako.

Wayitipiza Nyumba yako yono Yelile.

Wayileka Yelusalemi yeza matongo.

2 Wayileka miwili ye wetumami,

wono wafwiye, yiligwe caka ne zindeje,

miwili ye wanhu wako,

yiwe ndigwa ye malimu ga mu mbago.

3 Sunga ne sakami yawo,

yimwajikanjice caka caka kotya malenga,

mwi'kaya lya Yelusalemi,

ne miwili yawo mitagwe mumo,

hamba hasina munhu wo kuwazika.

4 Caweza wanhu mono cikutovulwa ne wekalani wayetu,

ne wekalani wayetu weza kucisekanga no kucizwajiza.

5 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, alu kosimba cibitilila?

Alu likuwo lyako lyokwaka nha moto sunga nili?

6 Wuwajidile likuwo lyako

i wanhu wono si wakumanyile,

sunga no wutemi wose,

wono siwukulyogopa litagwa lyako.

7 Hambi nha wawawulaga i wanhu wako,

wayihodanga ne yisi yako.

8 Wulece kucilipiciziza wubi we walezi wetu.

Cikulomba lusungu lwako lucizize mbela,

hambi nha cenamywa muno muno.

9 Gwe go Mulungu go muponyi wetu,

wucitaze ku soko ye lilumbo lyako.

Wuciponye no kucinenela no wubi wetu,

ku soko ye litagwa lyako.

10 Alo socici we makungugo wacilonje, wawe kutya,

“Alu Mulungu wenyu yali hayi lu?”

Wucitaze sunga ciwone ne meso getu co weneco,

vyono wukuwalipiciza wanhu we makungugo,

ku soko yo wita sakami ye wetumami wako.

11 Cililo ca wono wafunjigwe cikuficile,

na kwe vilungo vyako,

wuwaponye wono waling'anizwe wawulagwe.

12 Wuwalipaje kali mupunghati wawo,

wekalani wayetu, 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

ku soko ye zinhovu zono wakutovuye gwegwe!

13 Nase co wanhu wako,

mono cili ngholo ze litewa lyako,

cokulumba cibitilila,

hamonga no kukukumya vilo vyose.
