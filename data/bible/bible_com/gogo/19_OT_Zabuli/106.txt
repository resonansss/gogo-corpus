Zabuli 106

U wuswanu wo Mulungu kuli wanhu wakwe

1 Yalumbwe Muwaha Mulungu!

Mulumbenyi Muwaha Mulungu,

hambi nha muswanu,

no lusungu lwakwe nha lwikalaga cibitilila!

2 Hambi nani mwene mono kosuma,

kuzilonga zimbaha zose zono yetuma?

Hamba nani lo mono kosuma,

kuzilolosa zo lukumyo lwakwe?

3 Wo wamoti wono webate malajilizo gakwe,

wono wakwituma nhondo zose zo mugoloka.

4 Ale, wunghumbucilaje na nene 'gwe,

go Muwaha Mulungu, lono wukuwataza wanhu wako,

wuhanzilizaje nawo, lono wukuwaponya,

5 nane ho sume kuwuwona,

wumoteswe we wahagulwa wako,

nane ho niyisangalalile nyemo,

ye nghungugo yako,

kanyhi ho niyilumbile lukumyo,

hamonga ne wanhu wako.

6 Cinoza wubi nhe walezi wetu,

cituma zo wavu, cituma zimbi.

7 Walezi wetu lono wali wali ku Misili,

hamba si wazicelelela ze mapoto go Mulungu,

hamba kukumbucila zo lwinji lo lusungu lwakwe,

lono yawalajilaga, wakamubeza Mulungu

ha nhumbi ye lilenga lya Shamu.

8 Ninga hamba yiwe nhavivyo,

mwene yawakomboza du,

nha vyono yali yehuwize,

ho yalajile zo wuwaha wakwe.

9 Yakalikalipila lilamba lya Shamu, likanyala,

yakawa kuwakoza mumo nha mu yisi nghavu.

10 Yakawaponya mu wulawa,

mu wanhu wono wali wawasulile,

yakawaponya mu wulawa,

mu vilungo vye walugu wawo.

11 Malenga ge lilamba,

gakawagubiciza walugu wawo,

si yasigala hamba yumonga bwete.

12 Baho nhondo, nawo wanhu wakwe,

ho wehuwilila ze vilagane vyakwe,

wakawa kumwimbila ne zinyimbo zo lukumyo.

13 Ninga hanyuma hadodo,

wakawa kuzisemwa ze metumo gakwe mawaha,

wakawa kuleka kumuhuliciza.

14 Wakakwegwa ne masulumizi ku nyika,

wakamujela Mulungu ku nyika.

15 Hodu nayo Mulungu,

yakawapela cono wasakaga,

ninga hamba nhavivyo,

yakawagwisiza nhamwa 'mbi muno muno.

16 Wakamuwonela wivu Musa ku nghambi yawo,

hamonga na Haluni,

u mwitumami mono yelile wo Muwaha Mulungu.

17 Nayo yisi yikahama mulomo wayo,

yikamumela Datani,

yikamuzika Abilamu,

hamonga ne mhuga yakwe yose,

18 no moto wukawaziza watyatyi wawo,

nawo wukawaticimiza wawo wanhu wabi.

19 Wakenozeza weneco nyehe ya zahabu ku Sinayi,

wakacihemba cihwani cico,

20 wakalukawaniziza lukumyo lo Mulungu,

ku cihwani ce nimu 'ndya mahanze.

21 Wakamusemwa Mulungu mono yawakomboza,

mono yetuma mezino mu yisi ya Misili,

22 ne mapoto mu yisi yiyo ya Misili,

wakazisemwa sunga ne zinghani zose,

zo wogofye zono yetuma mwi'lamba lya Shamu.

23 Mulungu nayo yakasaka yawongomeze,

ninga Musa, muhagulwa wakwe,

yakema hagati yakalimila likuwo lyo Mulungu,

ho yalece kuwongomeza.

24 Nawo hanyuma wakayibedegala yisi swanu yiyilya,

ku soko vyono si wehuwila wihuwizo wo Mulungu.

25 Wakang'ung'ulicila mu mahema gawo mugati,

hamba si wasaka kanyhi kumuhulika Mulungu.

26 Holeka nayo Mulungu yakawasugula,

vyono kowongomezeza wose mu nyika,

27 kanyhi na vyono zindelo zawo,

koziyagaziza muli wanhu,

wono si wamumanyile Mulungu,

koweyagaza mu yinze yose.

28 Nawo wakawa kwilunza,

mu wuhemba Baali ku Peyoli,

sunga wakalya ne makumbiko ge wafu.

29 Na mu nzila yiyo,

wakacila muno kumukoza likuwo Muwaha Mulungu,

ku soko ye metumo gawo, na ku soko yiyo,

matamwa mabi muno gakawagwila.

30 Ninga yakenuka Finehasi yakanoza wuling'anizi,

ne matamwa gago hodu gakawa kuwuka.

31 Ne nghani yiyo yikali yikumbuciywe so cogopo cayo,

ku wulawa mwidaha lililya sunga cibitilila.

32 Wamukoza likuwo Mulungu, ha malenga ga Meliba,

nayo Musa yakagazika muno,

mu nhumbula yakwe ku soko yawo.

33 Wakamukalaza muno muno Musa mu nhumbula yakwe,

sunga nhondo yakalonga zinghani zono,

si zinojeye, wula kwihwesa.

34 Hamba si wawawulaga wanhu we makungugo ganji,

nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yali yawalajilize.

35 Ninga wene vikelunza hamonga ne wanhu we makungugo,

sunga wakeyijiza no kwituma metumo gawo.

36 Wahemba vihwani vye milungu yawo,

navyo vihwani vivyo vikawongomeza.

37 Wakawa kuwawulaga wana wawo we cilume,

sunga na we cicekulu,

no kuwalavya so makumbiko kuli masetani.

38 Wakawawulaga wana wono wasina,

hamba masutya bwete,

no kuwalavya makumbiko kuli vihwani vya Kanani,

sunga ne yisi yikatipizwa so wuwulaji wuwo.

39 Holeka wakehodanga ku soko ye metumo gawo,

wakawa wasina wihuwilwa kanyhi kuli Mulungu,

wakawa nhe wasenyha.

40 Holeka likuwo lyo Muwaha Mulungu,

likawacila wanhu wakwe,

hamba si yendezwa kanyhi na wawo wanhu wakwe.

41 Yakawatajila mu miwoko ye wanhu we makungugo,

nawo wawo wono wali wawasulile, wakawatema.

42 Wawo nawo walugu wawo,

wakawewonela wakawetumya kwe vilungo.

43 Mulungu yawaponya wanhu wakwe kali kenji,

ninga wene wakamubeza,

na ku soko yiyo, holeka Mulungu nayo yawaleka,

ku soko yo wubi wawo.

44 Ninga hamba nhavivyo,

yali yeza kucihulika cililo cawo,

no kugawona magazo gawo, gono wagazagwa.

45 Mulungu yakacikumbucila cilagane cakwe,

na ku soko yo lusungu lwakwe lwinji,

hodu yakawa kulituza likuwo lyakwe.

46 Kanyhi yakawanoza wawonelwe lusungu,

ne wanhu wose wono wawetumyaga.

47 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu wuciponye,

go Mulungu wetu.

Wucikongholezaje wose,

ciwuce muli we makungugo, nase ho cikulumbe,

no kulikumya litagwa lyako lyono lyelile!

48 Yalumbwe Muwaha Mulungu,

Mulungu wa Isilayeli,

ku wulawa cibitilila, sunga cibitilila!

Ne wanhu wose walonje watye, “Heya!”

Yalumbwe Muwaha Mulungu!
