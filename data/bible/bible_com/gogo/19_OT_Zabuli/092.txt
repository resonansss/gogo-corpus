Zabuli 92

Lwimbo lwo kumukumya Mulungu

1 Yo nghani swanu muno kukulumba agwe,

go Muwaha Mulungu, kwimba so cogopo cako,

'gwe go Mulungu Mono Wulawiliciye.

2 Yo nghani swanu kwanza zo lusungu lwako,

hakali mitondo, no wihuwilwa wako vilo vyose,

3 kuzanza ko wutowa masumbi,

hamonga no mulizo we ndonondo.

4 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, i metumo gako mawaha,

nha gakusangalaza muno, nane nokwimba na nyemo mbaha,

ku soko ye metumo gako.

5 Hambi metumo gako nha mawaha

muno muno 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu!

Sunga ne mahweso gako,

nha ga mahala menji muno muno!

6 U munhu mulele, hamba si kosuma kwizuka,

nayo muwula mahala,

hamba si kosuma kuyimanya nghani yiyi yaye,

7 nha vyono nawo i wabi,

wosuma kuwiluka nhe mahanze,

nawo wono wetumaga zo wubi,

vyono wosuma kumoteswa,

ninga hamba yiwe nhavivyo,

ndumilizo yawo wawo wose,

yowa wongomele we cibitilila,

8 Nagwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

yo gwegwe du Mono Wulawiliciye cibitilila.

9 Wawo i walugu wako go Muwaha Mulungu,

heya i walugu wako wose mbeka wokongomezwa,

na wose wono wetumaga zo wubi, wokwiyagala!

10 Agwe kumhela vilungo vinji,

vinha vilungo vye mbogo,

kunjidila mafuta go wusangalale.

11 Nane niwawona nene no mweneco,

i walugu wangu vyono wakondya kusumwa,

hulika ne cililo cawo i wetuma zimbi.

12 We ling'ani langa,

awo wakuwa kumoteswa nho mutende,

kanyhi wawilukaga wawa,

nho muhagata wa ku Lebanoni.

13 Wakuwa nhe mibici yono mihadikwe,

mu nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu,

yikumota mu Nyumba yo Mulungu wetu.

14 Hamba wawe wakombipe,

ninga wojendelela kulela mana sunga ku wukombi,

womema moso, nawo wodedewala, no kuwa ne vilungo

ho wasume kulwanza u wiling'ano,

lo Muwaha Mulungu,

15 na vyono kanyhi, musina wubi wo wose kuli mwene,

mono wo wihundiko wangu.
