Zabuli 140

Kulomba wudimi wo Mulungu

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wumhonye kuli wanhu wabi,

wunyhitile kuli wanhu wawulaji.

2 Hambi wanhu we liwala lilyo,

nha wehwesaga zimbi du nhondo zose,

kanyhi wekalaga wali ciwuma-wuma du nhondo zose.

3 Wena nimi nghali,

zihwanile nha sumu ye zinzoka.

Ne zinghani zawo zihwanile,

ne sumu ye lizoka li nyamwilu.

4 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wunyhitile kuli vilungo vyo mubi,

kanyhi wunyhitile sunga na kuli wawulaji,

wono wena wuhile wo kunyajiliza.

5 I wedunhumuzi wanhega ne mitego yawo,

watandaze milejehe nha nyavu,

kanyhi wahundice mitego yawo,

mu nzila ho wangwize.

6 Nane nghamulonjela Muwaha Mulungu nghatya,

“Agwe yo gwegwe go Mulungu wangu du.”

Holeka wuhulicize lizi lyo wulombe wangu, 'gwe

go Muwaha Mulungu wunhaze.

7 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

go vilungo vye mhonelo yangu,

kunyhitila mu vilo vyo wulugu.

8 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wulece kuwapela i wabi visakwa vyawo,

kanyhi wulece kuwamotya mu wuhile wawo wubi,

9 Wulece kuwapela wusume walugu wangu,

ne zimbali zawo ziwawuyile weneco!

10 Ne makala go moto gawagwile,

wagwilizwe mu makombo, walece kulawila kanyhi.

11 I wabalalizi si wosuma kwikala mono'nze,

no wubi wuwagane i wawulaji,

no kuwongomeza lwa kamonga!

12 Nane manyiye lukulu,

vyono Muwaha Mulungu komudima musocele,

kanyhi koyitetela ne ling'ani yo muciwa.

13 Mbeka wono wali ling'ani wokulumba muno,

kanyhi wokwikalana na gwegwe.
