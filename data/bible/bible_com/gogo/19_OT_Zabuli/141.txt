Zabuli 141

Wulombe we cinya Mihe

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nghukucemeleza wuze wumhonye mbela!

Wuhulicize lizi lyangu kolo nghukucemeleza!

2 No wulombe wangu wukuficile nha wuvumba,

sunga no winule we mawoko gangu,

wuweze nhe likumbiko lya mwi'daha lye cinya mihe.

3 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu wuwudime lulimi lwangu,

wuwice mulindilizi ha mulomo wangu.

4 Wunyhitile kuli zo wituma zimbi,

ndece kwilunza no wajilile wa wetuma zo wavu,

kanyhi ndecele lukulu kuhanga nawo,

mu zindigwa za mu zi sikukuu zawo.

5 Na ku soko ye cambuzi cangu,

nokwenda munhu u muswanu,

yafundile mu nzila yo kunhowa ninga,

si nokwendela lukulu i wabi wanghumye,

hambi 'ne nha nikalaga nghulomba,

ku soko ye metumo gawo mabi.

6 Ninga i wawaha wawo,

kolo wapondwapondwa mwi'talawe baho,

langa ho womanyila,

vyono wulonje wangu wali wa mbeka.

7 Nha vyono vinha vidumulwa-dumulwa vye zingwagu,

ne mafupa gawo gowa geyagaye,

ha mulango we citenjelo.

8 Ninga'ne nghwihuwiye gwegwe du,

go Muwaha Mulungu,

agwe yo gwegwe go wutizizo wangu,

holeka wulece kundeka nili wene.

9 Nghukunena wunyhitile,

kuli mitego yono wanhejile,

wumhonye kuli zi nonga zawo,

zono wakunhega wawo i wanhu wabi.

10 I wabi wegwize weneco mu mitego yawo,

lono ane nghukola mholile.

Kulomba wutazi.
