Zabuli 82

Mulungu yo mutemi we lilumbo

1 Mulungu yo muwaha we libalaza lya ku wulanga,

mwene kalamulaga hagati ye milungu.

2 “Lecelenyi lukulu ning'aniza,

yono si yili ling'ani!

Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kuwahijila mbejele,

no kuwendeza iwabi!

3 Watetelenyi wono wasoceye,

hamonga ne wana wono wasina walezi,

muwanozeze ling'ani waciwa na wono wasina cinji.

4 Waponyenyi wono wasoceye hamonga ne waciwa,

muwakomboze mu miwoko ya wono wakuwewonela.”

5 “Ninga 'nye hamba si mukutanga,

hamba kwizuka yaye!

Anye mwikalilaga mu wubocela mafupo du,

mwayihodanga sunga ne ling'ani yose mononze!

6 Ane nghatya, ‘Yo nyenye milungu,

anye wose muli wana wo Mulungu,

Mono Yalawiliciye.’

7 Ninga hamba yiwe nhavivyo, nanye mofwa du,

nhe wanhu wanji wose, no wumi wenyu woduma,

nha vyono wudumaga wumi we watemi wose.”

8 Gwe go Mulungu, wuze wuyiling'anize yinze,

hambi i makungugo gose, nha sawo yako gwegwe.
