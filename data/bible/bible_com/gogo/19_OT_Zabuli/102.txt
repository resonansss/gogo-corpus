Zabuli 102

Lilombo lyo muzelelo mono yali mu wugaye

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wuhulice wulombe wangu,

ne cililo cangu cikuficile.

2 Wulece kuwa kutali nane lono nili mu wugaye!

Wuteje likutu lyako, wuhulice mbela lono nghulomba!

3 I vilo vyangu nha vikupuluta mbela mbela nha lyosi,

ne mafupa gangu gakwaka nha moto.

4 Natemangwa, nawa nhe mahanze gono ganyalile.

Nawa sina hamba nzala ya ndigwa.

5 Ku soko ye lizi lyo wugule wangu,

mafupa gangu ganamatiye ne nghuli yangu.

6 Nehwana nhe zindeje za mu nyika,

kanyhi nhe zinhuwi mu matongo.

7 Nghugona nuguye du, nihwanile,

nhe ndeje nyhiwa,

yono yili mwi'tembe mucanyha.

8 Nawo walugu wangu,

wekalaga wakusimanga misi yose.

Nawo wono wakunzwajiza,

wakwitumamila litagwa lyangu ko kulilijitila.

9 Na ku soko yo wukali we likuwo lyako,

mavu gaweza nha yo ndigwa yangu,

10 Nane nagahanza-hanza mahozi gangu,

na co kung'wa cangu.

Nagwe kunhondola no kunhaga kutali.

11 No wumi wangu, wihwanile,

nho mubeho we cinyamihe,

nghunyala nhe mahanze.

12 Ninga agwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wutemile cibitilila,

ne litagwa lyako lyokumbucilwa mu zindelo zose.

13 Agwe kokwinuka,

koliwonela lusungu likaya lya Sayuni,

hambi cipango nha cafika cono cinojeye,

co lusungu lwako,

naco aci co cipango cono cinojeye muno.

14 Ninga wetumami wako,

walyendile likaya lilyo lya Sayuni,

hamba liwe lyawa matongo gose,

ninga hamba nhavivyo,

wakuliwonela lusungu du,

hamba liwe lyakondya kuweyanjika muno.

15 Nago makungugo, gomogopa Muwaha Mulungu,

ne watemi nawo, wose wolwogopa lukumyo lwakwe.

16 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu,

kolo yakondya kulizenga,

kanyhi likaya lya Sayuni,

baho langa ho kokwijelela,

vyono mwene yo wo lukumyo.

17 Nayo kowuhuliciza wulombe wo muciwa,

no wulombe wawo hamba si kowulema yaye.

18 Ale, wuzandikaje zizino,

ku soko yo lulelo lono lukuza,

i wanhu wono wolelwa hanyuma,

nawo wamukumye Muwaha Mulungu.

19 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu,

ku wulawilila hono helile hakwe, yakalanga hasi,

ku wulawilila ku wulanga, yakayilanga yinze.

20 Yakacihuliciza cililo ca wono wafunjigwe,

yakawawopolela wono wali waling'anizwe kufwa.

21 Ne litagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu,

lyokwanzwa ku Sayuni, heya,

sunga no lukumyo lwakwe,

nalo lokwanzwa ku Yelusalemi,

22 mwipango lono makungugo gose,

gokwikongholelela hamonga,

ne matemi gamuhembe Muwaha Mulungu.

23 Mulungu kanyhepulila vilungo vyangu,

lono nghali muzelelo,

kacepula vilo vyo wikale wangu.

24 Gwe go Mulungu wangu,

wulece kusola lulu lono nghali muzelelo.

Gwe go Mulungu, yo gwegwe,

mono kwikalaga cibitilila,

25 wayiwumba yinze katali na katali,

wulanga wo mulimo we miwoko yako.

26 Azo zokwajilila no kusakala nha mwenda,

nazo zopuluta no kwajililila lukulu,

ninga agwe kosigala baho du vyo winha.

27 Hambi agwe, nha yo gwegwe du cibitilila,

no wikale wako wusina ndumo.

28 Ne wana wetu wokwikala wapolile,

ko wudimi wako, ne wana wawo nawo,

wowa wakamile hali'gwe.
