Zabuli 126

Kulomba wukombozwe

1 U Muwaha Mulungu, lono yacigomolela,

kanyhi ku Yelusalemi,

cali nha wanhu wono wali mu ndoto!

2 Baho cikagwa mu seko, cikemba ko wusangalale.

Sunga ne wanhu we makungugo ganji wakawa kutya,

“Muwaha Mulungu mbeka, kawatendela zimbaha!”

3 Na mbeka Muwaha Mulungu yacitendela mezino

mawaha, sunga na sese co weneco,

cikasangalala muno muno.

4 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

cikukunena kanyhi wucimotese,

nha vyono vikolongo vihulumilaga malenga,

mu malolo gono ganyalile.

5 Wono wakuhadika zimbeyu zawo,

wakulila mahozi, wolodudula wasangalaye!

6 Wono wakubita ku wuhadici akuno wakulila,

wowuya wasangalaye wadinghuye ne ndudula yawo.
