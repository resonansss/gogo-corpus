Zabuli 39

Wunene wo munhu mono yali mu wugaye

1 Ane natya, “Noyilanjilila mikalile yangu,

ho ndece kwituma wubi mu wulonje wangu.

Nowimila mulomo wangu lono wanhu wabi,

wali behi nane.”

2 Nali nha we cibwibwi,

hamba kulonga cinji, nghanyamala,

hamba si natuga ciba, wusungu wangu, wukacila muno.

3 Wusungu wangu wukaka kotya moto mu nhumbula yangu.

Na mu nghango yo wihwese wangu,

na vyo nacila kukalala,

hanyuma nghalonga nghani azi nghatya,

4 “Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

ndumilizo yangu yowa nili?

Manyizaje i vilo vyo wikale wangu,

nha vyono vikumalika mbela!”

5 Kumbe wunjelanizize vilo vicefu muno vyo wikale!

No wumi wangu kumbe yo nghani ya bwete du hali'gwe,

kumbe, mbeka, wanhu wose wehwanile cihumbi ce mbeho du.

6 Na mbeka, munhu wo wose kapulutaga nho mwenzenze du.

No wuhidimbazi wakwe wose,

wukuwa nghani ya bwete du,

mwene kakongholaga sawo,

hamba kumanya mono koyitumila nani.

7 Nalu, 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

alu nghali nindiye cici kanyhi?

Witumbilo wangu nha wuli kuli gwegwe du!

8 Holeka wumhonye mu wubi wangu wose,

hamba wulecele lukulu kuvumila,

kanyhi, i walele waswe wakunhovulanga.

9 Ane nyamaye du, si nghulonga cinji kanyhi,

hambi nha yo gwegwe mono witumile zizo.

10 Holeka wulece kundipiciza kanyhi,

hambi nha neza nili behi no kufwa,

ku soko yo wutowe wako.

11 Na lono wukumulipiciza munhu,

ku soko yo wubi wakwe ko kumukalipila,

mbeka kotya wukuhodanga cinhu cono lipulaputya licendile.

Na mbeka, i wanhu wose wehwanile cihumbi ce mbeho du.

12 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wuhulicize wulombe wangu,

hamonga ne cililo cangu,

wulece kunyamalila lono nghukulilila.

Hambi ane nha ninha mujenzi du mono yakupuluta,

nane nili mujenda nzila,

nha vyono wali wenha walezi wangu wose.

13 Wundekaje, nane niwe na nyemo,

lono nghali nafwa, no kuwucila lukulu.
