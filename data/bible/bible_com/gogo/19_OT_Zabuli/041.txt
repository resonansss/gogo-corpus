Zabuli 41

Wulombe wa mono yatamigwe

(Kuli mwimbizi: Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Yo mumoti munhu mono yakuwataza waciwa,

u Muwaha Mulungu komutaza nayo,

mu vilo vyo wugaye.

2 U Muwaha Mulungu komudima no kumuwika ca wumi,

no kumupela wumoteswe mono'si

hamba sikomuleka mu miwoko ye walugu wakwe.

3 Muwaha Mulungu komutaza mu wutamwa,

no kumuponya mu wutamwa wakwe.

4 Nane nghawa kutya nhavi,

“Ane nghusutizya, 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

holeka wumbonele lusungu, wumhonye.”

5 Hambi walugu wangu nha

wakunonga nghani zimbi,

wakuhila nifwe, najililile lukulu.

6 Na lono wakuza kunamusa,

si wawezaga no wuhile wuswanu yaye,

langa wanyihwesezaga zimbi du,

ho wabite wakawalonjele wanji.

7 Nawo wose wono wasulile,

wekalaga wakwipwepwela i zangu,

wakunyihweseza i zimbi zono zosuma kunhamisa.

8 Akuno wakutya,

“Lece ta, wutamwa awu nhondo womuwulaga,

hamba si kokwinucila lukulu kanyhi, mu citanda cakwe!”

9 Sunga no mwendece wangu muwaha mono nali nimwitumbiye,

mono nali kuhangana nayo zindigwa zangu,

nayo yakondya kumhituka no kwihoma nane!

10 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wumbonele lusungu!

Wungomolele vilungo vyangu, nane niwalipicize.

11 Na mu nzila yiyo, mo nomanyila vyono mbeka

wukwendezwa nane.

Hono walugu wangu walece kusumiliza.

12 Kanyhi vyono ane nitumaga ze ling'ani,

holeka agwe kosikamya,

nokumbika hali gwegwe cibitilila.

13 Yalumbwe Muwaha Mulungu,

u Mulungu we Waisilayeli wulawa cibitilila

ne cibitilila! Heya! Heya!
