Zabuli 100

Lwimbo lwo lukumyo

1 Mwimbilenyi Muwaha Mulungu,

'nye nyo we yinze wose!

2 Mumuhembe Muwaha Mulungu ne nyemo,

mumubitile akuno mukwimba no wusangalale!

3 Mumanye vyono Muwaha Mulungu, yo Mulungu du.

Yo mweneco mono yaciwumba, nase cili sawo yakwe,

cili wanhu wakwe,

kanyhi cili ngholo ze madimo gakwe.

4 Mwinjilaje mu milango ye Nyumba yakwe,

akuno mukulumba,

mwinjilaje mu wikalo wakwe no wulumbe.

Mumupelaje lilumbo, no kulikumya litagwa lyakwe.

5 Hambi u Muwaha Mulungu nha muswanu,

no lusungu lwakwe lwikalaga cibitilila,

ne mbeka yakwe yikalaga mu zindelo zose.
