Zabuli 135

Lwimbo lo lukumyo

1 Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe!

Likumyenyi litagwa lyakwe,

anye nyo wetumami wo Muwaha Mulungu,

2 anye mono mwimaga mu Nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu,

mu Nyumba yo Mulungu wetu.

3 Mukumyenyi Muwaha Mulungu, hambi nha muswanu,

mulimbile litagwa lyakwe zo lukumyo,

hambi nha vyo yinojeye.

4 Muwaha Mulungu yakehagulila Yakobo,

yawe munhu wakwe, ne wanhu wa Isilayeli,

wawe sawo yakwe mweneco.

5 Manyiye lukulu vyono,

Muwaha Mulungu yo muwaha yacizize,

u Muwaha Mulungu wetu,

yo Yalawiliciye mu milungu yose.

6 Mwene Muwaha Mulungu wetu,

kanozaga nha vyono yendile mweneco,

ku wulanga, hamba mono'nze,

hamba mwi'lenga, sunga na mu visolo vyayo.

7 Mwene kagaletaga mavunde,

ku wulawa ku mhelo ye yinze,

kayiletaga mvula mbaha mu nzila ye mamuli,

kanyhi kayiletaga mbeho mbaha muno,

ku wulawa ha wikalo wayo.

8 Yawawulaga i wana walongozi kulelwa wose,

ku Misili, i wana we wanhu,

sunga ne mana ge zinimu nhavivyo.

9 Nayo yakanoza mapoto,

ne vitanjilo kuli'gwe go Misili,

kuli Falawo hamonga ne wetumami wakwe wose.

10 Yakagongomeza makungugo menji,

yakawawulaga sunga,

ne watemi, wono wali wali vilungo,

11 Sihoni, mutemi we Waamoli,

na Ogu mutemi wa Bashani,

sunga ne watemi wose wa mu Kanani.

12 Yasola zisi zawo, yakawapela wanhu wakwe,

yawapela Isilayeli wuwe wuhazi wawo.

13 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

litagwa lyako lyowa kukumbucilwa cibitilila,

nagwe kokumbucilwa ne wanhu we zindelo zose.

14 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu,

nha kowatetela wanhu wakwe,

kanyhi kowa no lusungu kuli wetumami wakwe.

15 I milungu ye makungugo,

nha milungu ya fweza na zahabu,

minozwe ne miwoko ye wanhu.

16 Yina milomo, ninga hamba si yikulonga yaye,

yina ne meso, ninga hamba si yikuwona ng'o.

17 Ne makutu yina nago,

ninga hamba si yikuhulika bwete,

hamba kwehela kose, si yehelaga yaye.

18 Holeka wose wono wayinoza,

na wose wono wakuyitumbila,

wokwihwana ne milungu yiyo!

19 Nye nyo wanhu wa Isilayeli,

mukumyenyi Muwaha Mulungu!

Nanye mo wakohani, mo lulelo lwa Haluni,

mukumyenyi Muwaha Mulungu!

20 Nanye mo Walawi,

mukumyenyi Muwaha Mulungu!

Sunga nanye mono mumogopile,

Muwaha Mulungu, mukumyenyi!

21 Muwaha Mulungu yakumywe mu Sayuni,

yakumywe sunga na mu wikalo wakwe ku Yelusalemi.

Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe!
