Zabuli 103

Wendo wo Mulungu

1 Gwe go nhumbula yangu,

wumukumye Muwaha Mulungu.

Na vyose vyono vili muli'ne,

vilikumye litagwa lyakwe lyono lyelile!

2 Gwe go nhumbula yangu,

wumukumye Muwaha Mulungu!

Wulece kuwusemwela lukulu wuswanu wakwe.

3 Mwene kanenela wubi wangu wose,

kanyhi kamhonya ne matamwa gose.

4 Kamhonya mu wulawa mwifwa,

yakamhela ze lihewa no lusungu lwakwe.

5 Ne mikalile yangu yose kayimemya wumoteswe,

no wuzelelo wangu wukusipulwa,

ho njendelele kwikala nili muzelelo du,

kanyhi nili vilungo nha'ndi.

6 Muwaha Mulungu kaling'anizaga mu ling'ani,

kawanozaga wasocele, ho watuje ling'ani yawo.

7 Yamumanyiza Musa zinzila zakwe,

yawalajila wanhu wa Isilayeli ze metumo gakwe.

8 Muwaha Mulungu yo munghala lihewa no lusungu,

mwene si kakolwaga ne likuwo mbela,

kanyhi yena lusungu lwinji muno.

9 Mwene si kakalipilaga-kalipilaga nhondo zose,

hamba ne likuwo lyakwe si likalaga cibitilila yaye.

10 Kanyhi si yakuwa kucilipiciza,

nha vyono yicinojeye a a ng'o!

Hamba si kacilipaga mu nghango ye masutya getu.

11 Nha vyono winha wulanga,

wuli kutali ne yinze, ale,

no wuswanu wakwe vyo winha kuli wono wamogopile.

12 Nha vyono yinha kucilima,

na kumwezi vili kutali kutali, ale,

nayo Mulungu,

vyo kawuwikaga kutali nase wubi wetu.

13 Nha vyono sogo yo munhu,

kawawonelaga lusungu wanagwe, ale,

nha vivyo no Muwaha Mulungu nayo,

vyo kawawonelaga lusungu wose wono wamogopile.

14 Hambi Mulungu nha yacimanyile wimo wetu,

yamanyile vyono ase cili manghundi du.

15 Munhu nha yehwanile mahanze du,

kasanulaga nhe liluwa lya mu mugunda,

16 ninga kolo mbeho yikuliputa, hodu,

liwa kwajilila du, hamba kwijela,

kanyhi na hono lyali holili.

17 Ninga lusungu lo Muwaha Mulungu,

lwikalaga cibitilila,

kuli wose wono wamogopile,

no wuswanu wakwe wikalaga mu zindelo zose,

18 kuli walya wose wono webate cilagane cakwe,

no kugetuma malajilizo gakwe ko wihuwilwa.

19 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yaliwicile ku wulanga,

ligoda lyakwe lyo wuwaha, nayo yo yatemile muli vyose.

20 Nanye nyo wamalayika mono muli vilungo,

mumukumye Muwaha Mulungu,

mukumyenyi anye mono mwibate ze malajilizo gakwe,

mono mwibate ne nghani yakwe!

21 Nanye nyo we vilungo ku wulanga,

mukumyenyi Muwaha Mulungu,

mukumyenyi anye nyo wetumami wakwe,

anye mono mwibate zo wuhile wakwe!

22 Mukumyenyi Muwaha Mulungu,

nanye nyo viwumbwa vyakwe vyose,

mukumyenyi anye wose mono muli mu wutemi wakwe.

Gwe go nhumbula yangu, mukumyaje Muwaha Mulungu!
