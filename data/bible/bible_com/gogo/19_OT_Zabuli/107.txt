Zabuli 107

CITABU CE HANO

(Zabuli 107–150)

Lukumyo so wuswanu wo Mulungu

1 “Mulumbenyi Muwaha Mulungu vyono muswanu,

hambi lusungu lwakwe nha lwikalaga cibitilila!”

2 Ale mweze kulonga nhavivyo,

anye wose mono mwakombozwa no Muwaha Mulungu.

Yakamuponyenyi mu vilungo vye walugu wenyu,

3 kanyhi, yakamukongholezenyi,

ku wulawa mu zisi zo wujenzi,

ku wulawilila kucilima na kumwezi,

na ku wulawilila kusumuka na kutakama.

4 Wanji wakapilima-pilima,

caka mwi'hala na mu nyika,

hamba kufika ku kaya yo wikalo.

5 Wakawona nzala ne nyhilu,

sunga ne zinhumbula zawo zikazilika.

6 Na mumo mu wugaye wawo,

wakamulilila Muwaha Mulungu,

nayo Mulungu, yakawaponya mu magazo gawo.

7 Yakawatonga mu nzila yono yigolocile,

sunga wakayificila kaya yo wikalo.

8 Holeka yiwanojeye wamulumbe Muwaha Mulungu,

ku soko yo lusungu lwakwe lwinji,

ku soko ye mapoto gono yawatendela.

9 Mwene kawacesaga wono wena nyhilu wawa kuca,

nawo wono wena nzala, kawegutyaga ziswanu.

10 Wanji wakekala mu cinyhiwa na mu wutitu,

akuno wawopwe ne magazo hamonga ne manyolo,

11 ku soko vyono wabeza malajilizo,

go Mulungu no kubedegala zo wutonji,

wo Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye.

12 Wakakatala muno muno,

so milimo mizito yono wetumywagwa,

sunga wakagwa ho hasi,

ninga hasina hamba yunji mono yasuma kuwataza.

13 Holeka wakawa kumulilila,

Muwaha Mulungu mumo mu magazo gawo,

nayo yakawaponya mu magazo gawo.

14 Yakawawusa mu cinyhiwa no wutitu,

yakagabenanjiza ne manyolo gawo.

15 Holeka yiwanojeye wamulumbe Muwaha Mulungu,

ku soko yo lusungu lwakwe lwinji,

na ku soko ye mapoto gono yawatendela.

16 Mwene kayibenanjizaga,

no kuyidigula milango ye shaba,

no kugadumulanga madindilo ge vyuma.

17 Wanji wakalelepa ku soko yo wubi wawo,

wakagazika ku soko yo wavu wawo,

18 sunga ne ndigwa yose yikawipa mu milomo yawo,

wakawa wali behi no kufwa.

19 Holeka wakawa kumulilila,

Muwaha Mulungu mumo mu magazo gawo,

nayo yakawaponya mu magazo gawo.

20 Yawaponyezya mu nghani yakwe,

yakawaponya ho walece kongomela.

21 Holeka yiwanojeye wamulumbe Muwaha Mulungu,

ku soko yo lusungu lwakwe lwinji,

na ku soko ye mapoto gono yawatendela.

22 Wamulavizye vilavya hamonga ne lilumbo,

wasimulile ze metumo gakwe ko wimbe we nyemo.

23 Wanji wakenjila mu zi ngalawa,

wakawa wakwilondolela ndigwa zawo mumo mwi'lenga,

24 sunga na wawo nawo wagawona,

metumo mawaha go Muwaha Mulungu,

i metumo gakwe go wizine mwi'lenga.

25 Yakalajiliza,

yakaleta libeho yiwaha lyo mukukunzo,

likagenula kucanyha mawingo ge lilenga.

26 Wakagumilwa mu mwanya mucanyha,

hanyuma sunga ko ku visolo kusi,

na ku so nghani yiyo,

wanhu wakazilika mu nhumbula zawo.

27 Wakayumba-yumba no kupebesuka,

nha wono wagalile wujimbi,

no wusugu wawo wose wa mu malenga wukamalika.

28 Holeka wakawa kumulilila Muwaha Mulungu,

mumo mu magazo gawo,

nayo yakawaponya mu magazo gawo.

29 Yakawutuza mukukunzo wunyamale twii,

nago mawingo gakanyamala.

30 Nawo wakasangalala muno vyono hanyamala twii,

nayo yakawafisa i hanhu hono wabitaga.

31 Holeka yiwanojeye wamulumbe Muwaha Mulungu,

ku soko yo lusungu lwakwe lwinji,

ku soko ye mapoto gono yawatendela.

32 Kanyhi wamukumizye mu wihangulize we wanhu,

no kumulumba mwi balaza lye wanyamhala.

33 Mulungu yakaganyaza makolongo hakawa hakavu,

sunga ne mivwilo nayo,

yose yakayinyaziza lukulu,

34 ne yisi yono yilelaga,

yakayinoza yikawa yihala lya mwino,

ku soko yo wubi we wanhu wono wekalaga mumo.

35 Yakayipitula nyika, yikawa malamba ga malenga,

ne yisi nghavu, hakawa mivwilo ya malenga.

36 Yakawahamizya kuko wono wali nzala,

nawo wakazenga wikalo wawo kuko.

37 Nawo wakalima migunda,

no kuhadika mizabibu yawo kuko,

wakatuga mikalo minji kuciza.

38 Nayo yakawalugutiza wanhu wakwe,

nawo wakonjezeka muno,

sunga ne liwalo lye mitugo yawo,

nalyo Mulungu yakalyonjeleza muno muno.

39 Nawo wali weza kwanza kucepuka,

no kuwula vilungo,

ku soko yo wiwonelwe no wugazwe wawo,

40 Hodu, Mulungu yakawabedegala,

wono wawagazaga,

holeka yakawa kuwakoza mu nyika,

kono kusina nzila.

41 Ninga hamba nhavivyo,

yawaponya waciwa mu wugazwe wawo,

yakawa kuyonjeleza ne mhuga yawo,

wakawa nhe'tewa lya ngholo.

42 Nawo we ling'ani, wakuziwona zizo,

kanyhi wakusangalala, ninga i wabi wose,

milomo yawo yodindwa.

43 We mahala, ale wazihwesaje zizo,

kanyhi walumanye no lusungu lo Muwaha Mulungu.
