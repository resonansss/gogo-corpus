Zabuli 55

Wulombe wo munhu mono yakwiwonelwa

1 Gwe go Mulungu, wuhulicize wulombe wangu,

wulece kuwutumbila mugongo wulombe wangu.

2 Nghukunena wuhulicize kanyhi wumvumile,

nakondya kukatazwa no wugazice wangu.

3 Nane nogope muno so wulonje,

wokogofya we walugu wangu,

na ku soko yo wiwonelwa ne wanhu wabi.

Wawo wanhu wabi wakungaza muno,

wakumbonela likuwo kanyhi wasulile.

4 Ne nhumbula yangu yimemile woga,

no wogofye we lifwa wundomile nene du.

5 Nane nghucicima so woga wuwaha,

nenjililwa no wogofye wuwaha muno.

6 Yali yiwe yili ciba,

hono nali niwe ne mabawa nha nziwa!

Nali nguluce mbite kutali nghacehe kuko.

7 Heya, nali mbite kutali muno muno,

nghacehe mu nyika.

8 Nali nhizize hanhu hono hapolile,

niwe kutali muno ne libeho lyo mukukunzo.

9 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wuwatemanulaje milonjele yawo,

hambi nha nghuwona kowuli,

wuwulaji no wugombe kuko kwi'kaya,

10 vilipilime likaya misi ne cilo,

no kuliletela likaya wuwulaji wuwaha,

11 Wuhodanji watandala mwi'kaya lyose,

hamonga no wisuzi no wiwonelwe hanhu hose.

12 Hono mulugu wangu yali yasimanje lunji,

nali sume kuzikangalila zizo,

na hono mono yasulile yali yambedegale,

nali sume kwihundika niwe kutali no mwene.

13 Kumbe lu, yo gwegwe go muyangu,

yo gwegwe mwiyendi muyangu mono cizoweye muno!

14 Ase cali ciyizoweye,

ciduwanaga nha munhu na mwiyendi muyagwe,

kanyhi cali cihembanaga,

hamonga mu Nyumba yo Mulungu.

15 Ale ciba walugu wangu wafwe i vilo lono vikali,

wabite ku yisi ye wafu lono wakali womi,

hambi wubi nha wuwamemile twaa,

sunga mo mugati mu zinhumbula zawo.

16 Ninga ane nghumulilila Mulungu,

nayo Muwaha Mulungu komhonya.

17 Ane nikalaga du nghulila ko wukalale,

cinyamihe, hakali mitondo, na mu misi mugati,

ninga mwene kolihulika lizi lyangu du.

18 Mwene komhonya no kumbika mholile,

mu wulugu wono cidomile nawo,

hambi walugu wangu nha wenji muno.

19 U Mulungu mono yatemile ku wulawa cibitilila,

mwene kotega likutu lyakwe, kowasuma,

hambi wene nha si wawukamisaga wulajilize,

hamba kumogopo Mulungu kose.

20 U muyangu yakondya kuwatowanga i weyendi

wayagwe, yakondya kubena ne vilagane vyakwe.

21 Ne zinghani zakwe zitelemeye,

si nhe tilazi, ninga nhumbula yakwe,

ayo yisulumilaga wulugu du.

Nghani zakwe zili telele zinha mafuta, ninga,

zitemaga kotya mumaje mukali.

22 Mulecelaje Muwaha Mulungu,

wugazice wako wose,

nayo kokucitila muli zose,

hambi mwene nha si kamulekaga munhu,

wakwe we ling'ani yasumwe.

23 Ninga i wanhu wawulaji,

hamonga ne wanhu wabi,

u Mulungu kowategula mu vitenjelo vyawo,

lono wakali hamba si wanaficila nusu,

ye vilo vyo wikale wawo mono'nze.

Ninga ane nokwitumbila gwegwe du go Mulungu.
