Zabuli 25

Kulomba wutonji no wudimi

(Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nghukulomba kwe nhumbula yangu yose!

2 Ane nghwitumbiye gwegwe, go Muwaha Mulungu wangu,

wulece kundeka nghozwe cevu,

i walugu wangu walece kuwusangalalila wusumwe wangu.

3 Na wose wono wakwitumbiye gwegwe, si wokozwa cevu yaye.

Langa wono wakubedegaye wokozwa cevu.

4 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wumanyize nzila yako, heya,

wumhelaje wutonji wako.

5 Wunhongaje mu mbeka yako no kunyijiza,

hambi nha yo gwegwe du go Mulungu mono kumhonyaga,

nane nghwitumbiye gwegwe du vilo vyose.

6 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wukumbucile zo wuwaha wo wuswanu wako,

wulukumbucilaje no lusungu lwako,

hambi nha vikalaga cibitilila na cibitilila.

7 Wulece kuwukumbucila wubi wo wuzelelo wangu,

wunenelaje ne masutya gangu,

wunghumbucilaje so lusungu lwako,

ko wuswanu wako 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu.

8 Muwaha Mulungu yo muswanu, kanyhi yali ling'ani,

mwene kawejizaga wahodanji zo wutonji.

9 Mwene kawatongaga i wenenenyhezi mu wiling'ano, heya,

kawejizaga wawo inzila yakwe.

10 U Muwaha Mulungu,

kawatongaga ko wihuwilwa na ko wendo,

wose wono webate cilagane cakwe ne malajilizo gakwe.

11 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wunenele masutya gangu gose,

hamba gawe vyono genha ku soko ye lilumbo lye litagwa lyako.

12 Munhu wo wose mono kamogopago Muwaha Mulungu,

yuyo kokwijizwa no Mulungu inzila yo kutyatya.

13 No munhu we liwala lilyo,

yo komoteswa nhondo zose na wo lulelo lwakwe,

woyihala yisi.

14 U Muwaha Mulungu,

yawendile wose wono wakumuhulika,

nayo kawamanyizaga ze cilagane cakwe nhondo zose.

15 Nimulanjililaga Muwaha Mulungu ho yanhaze,

mwene yo kogasopola magulu gangu mu mutego.

16 Hambi 'ne nha nili wene kanyhi soceye,

holeka wunangaje go Muwaha Mulungu wumbonele lusungu.

17 Wumbusize wugazice wa mu nhumbula yangu,

kanyhi wumhonye mu magayo gangu.

18 Wuwulangaje wulungule wangu no wugazwe wangu.

Wunenele wubi wangu wose.

19 Lanje, ta, walugu wangu vyono wenji.

Lanje, ta, vyono wasulile vibi muno.

20 Wuwukamisaje wumi wangu,

wumhonye nghutiziza kwako,

ho ndece kukozwa cevu.

21 U wuswanu wako no wiling'ano wako vinghamise,

hambi 'ne nha nghwitumbiye gwegwe.

22 Gwe Mulungu, wuwaponye wanhu wako Waisilayeli,

wuwaponye mu magayo gawo gose.
