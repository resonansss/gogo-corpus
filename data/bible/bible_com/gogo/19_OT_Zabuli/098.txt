Zabuli 98

Mulungu yo mutemi we yinze yose

1 Mumwimbile Muwaha Mulungu lwimbo lupya,

hambi nha ketuma nghani zo wizine!

Nayo ketujila wusume,

so vilungo no wuwaha wakwe vyono vyelile.

2 Muwaha Mulungu kalajila zo wusume wakwe,

kawanzalicila we makungugo,

ze vilungo vyakwe vye mhonelo.

3 Kawukumbucila wuswanu wakwe,

no wihuwilwa wakwe kuli wanhu wa Isilayeli.

Nazo zimbavu zose ze yinze zakondya,

kuwuwona wusume ne vilungo vyo Mulungu wetu.

4 Yinze yose yimusangalalile Muwaha Mulungu,

yimukumye kwe zinyimbo ne zinyhenze.

5 Mumukumye Muwaha Mulungu kwa nyemo,

kwe lizi liswanu hamonga ne masumbi.

6 Mutowelenyi zinyheze Muwaha Mulungu mutemi wetu,

musangalalilenyi kwa ndulele,

hamonga ne mazi ge zifilimbi.

7 Nalyo lilenga litowe cihumbi,

hamonga na vyose vyono vili mumo,

yinze hamonga na wose wono wekaye mugati yayo.

8 Nanye mo makolongo, towenyi maganza,

nanye mo matunda, mwimbe hamonga ne nyemo.

9 Sangalalenyi hali Muwaha Mulungu,

hambi nha yakuza kuyitema yinze.

Nayo koyiling'aniza yinze mu ling'ani,

i wanhu nawo kowatemela mu ling'ani.
