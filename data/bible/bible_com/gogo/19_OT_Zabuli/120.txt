Zabuli 120

Kulomba wutazi

1 Lono nali nili mu wugazice,

namucemeleza Muwaha Mulungu, nayo yakahulika.

2 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wumhonye kuli wanhu wakonji kanyhi walambi.

3 Anye nyo wanhu walambi,

mwene mopegwa cinhuci no Mulungu?

Hamba Mulungu komulipicizenyi vye wule,

mumo mu wulambi wenyu?

4 Mbeka komulipicizenyi,

kwe zisonga zinghali ze wasilikale,

kanyhi na kwe makala go moto!

5 Sunga no wikala muli'nye, wambuce du,

hambi nha winha wikala mu Mesheki,

hamba kotya nha wikala mu wanhu wa Kedali.

6 Ku wulawa katali,

nekalana ne wanhu wono si wendile mulamu yaye!

7 Ane nili wo mulamu,

lono nghulonga zo mulamu,

wene wakusaka wulugu.
