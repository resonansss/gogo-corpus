Zabuli 150

Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe

1 Mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu!

Mumulumbile Mulungu mu Nyumba yakwe!

Mumulumbe ku soko yo wutemi wakwe ku wulanga!

2 Mumulumbe ku soko ye metumo gakwe,

ge vilungo gono yetumile,

mumulumbe ku soko yo lukumyo lwakwe luwaha.

3 Mumulumbe kwe lizi lye ndulele,

mumulumbe kwe masumbi ne zindonondo!

4 Mumulumbe kwe zing'oma no wuvina,

mumulumbe kwe zifilimbi ne zindonondo!

5 Mumulumbe ne visesele vye mazi mawaha,

mumulumbe ne visesele vilizi muno muno.

6 Ale, viwumbwa vyose vyumi,

vimulumbe Muwaha Mulungu!

Mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu!
