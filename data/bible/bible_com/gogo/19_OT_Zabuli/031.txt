Zabuli 31

Wulombe wo kumwitumbilo Mulungu

(Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Nghutiziza kuli'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

so wutazi wako, ho ndece kukozwa cevu,

wumhonye ko wiling'ano wako.

2 Wuteje likutu lyako, wumhonye mbela!

Wuweze kuli'ne mudimi mono wukamile,

wihundiko wo kumhonya.

3 Heya, hambi agwe nha wuli talawe lyangu,

ne gomawakali lyangu.

Ku soko ye litagwa lyako,

wunongolele, wunhonje.

4 Wundavyanize mu mutego wono wanhejile,

wunyhitile mu mbali.

5 Nghuwuwika muhe wangu mu miwoko yako,

wumhonye 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

go Mulungu we mbeka.

6 Nghuwasula wono wakuyihemba milungu yo wudesi,

ninga ane nghwitumbiye gwegwe,

agwe go Muwaha Mulungu.

7 Nosangalala, no kulusecelelela lusungu lwako,

agwe nha kuwuwona wugaye wangu,

kuwumanya no wugazice wangu.

8 Agwe nha si wandeceleza nibatwe ne walugu wangu,

kumbika hanhu hono hapolile.

9 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wumbonele lusungu, hambi nha nili mu wugaye,

meso gangu gavimba so wulila muno,

ne vilungo vyangu vyose vyatimba.

10 Hambi wikale wangu nha wukumalicila mu wukalale, heya,

sunga ne myaka yangu nayo, mu cililo.

Nasocela so wugaye sunga ne mafupa gangu gakutama.

11 Sunga ne walugu wangu wose, wakumbedegala, naweza

nha linhu du hali wayangu. Ne wendece wangu,

weza kumbona nili wa kogofya,

nawo lono wakumbona mu nzila, wakunhiza.

12 Nawo wose wakondya kusemwa nha munhu mono yafwiye,

neza nha nili ciya cono citulice.

13 Nghuwahulika walugu wangu wakwipwepwela,

wogofye wumhilime mbavu zose,

wakwihehemeza, ho wambulaje.

14 Ninga ane nghwitumbiye gwegwe,

agwe go Muwaha Mulungu nghutya,

“Agwe yo gwegwe Mulungu wangu!”

15 Nane nhondo zose, nili mu wudimwe wako du,

holeka wumhonye mu miwoko ye walugu wangu,

na muli wawa wono wakungaza.

16 Wunanje ko wuswanu wako no mwitumami wako,

wumhonye ko lusungu lwako.

17 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nghukulomba wulece kundeka nghozwe cevu,

ninga wuwalekaje i wabi wakozwe cevu,

wajilile kuko ku yisi ye wafu.

18 Wuyidinde milomo ye wanhu wawo walambi,

i wanhu wedunhumuzi kanyhi wewoni,

wono wakuwabedegala i wanhu we ling'ani.

19 Lanje ta, wuswanu wako vyono winji,

wono wuwawiciye wose wono wakogopile!

Kuwanozezaga hali wanhu wose ziswanu,

wose wono wakukuziza.

20 Kuwahundikaga hali'gwe hono hapolile,

wawa wali kutali no wuhile wubi we wanhu.

Kuwahundika hali'gwe wapolile no wuwanji we walugu wawo.

21 U Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe!

Hambi mwene nha kandajila zo lusungu lwakwe,

lono nali mhilimwe akuno wakunhowanga.

22 Nane nghawa kogopa,

no kwihwesa vyono wakondya kundeka,

kumbe agwe wali wakondya kucihulika cililo cangu

lono nakulilila wunhaze.

23 Mumwendaje Muwaha Mulungu,

anye mo wanhu wakwe!

U Muwaha Mulungu,

mwene kawadimaga wanhu wose iwehuwilwa.

Ninga i wedunhumuzi, kawalipicizaga

nha vyono yiwanojeye kulipicizwa.

24 Muweze muli vilungo no kuwa

ne nhumbula yono yisina woga,

anye wose mono mumwitumbiye Muwaha Mulungu.
