Zabuli 29

I lizi lyo Mulungu mu mbeho yo mukukunzo

(Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Mulavizyenyi lukumyo Muwaha Mulungu,

anye mo viwumbwa vyose vyono vili ku wulanga.

Heya, mumukumyaje no kuwuvumila wuwaha wakwe.

2 Likumyenyi litagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu,

mumuhembe Muwaha Mulungu lono yakwilajila.

3 Lizi lyo Muwaha Mulungu likuhulikwa mu malenga,

u Mulungu wo lukumyo yakulavya mabalalusi,

ne lizi lyo Muwaha Mulungu,

likuhulikwa mwi'lenga mucanyha!

4 Lizi lyo Muwaha Mulungu lili vilungo,

lizi lyo Muwaha Mulungu limemile wuwaha.

5 Lizi lyo Muwaha Mulungu,

libenanjizaga i mibici ye mihagata, heya,

sunga ne mihagata ya ku Lebanoni.

6 Liganozaga i matunda ga Lebanoni gazumhe-zumhe,

kotya nyehe ya ng'ombe, ne matunda ga Helimoni,

nago kotya nyana ye mbogo.

7 Lizi lyo Muwaha Mulungu lilavyaga malambi go moto.

8 Lizi lyo Muwaha Mulungu lisinjisaga nyika,

u Muwaha Mulungu yakulisinjisa linyika lya Kadeshi.

9 Lizi lyo Muwaha Mulungu,

liyisinjisaga mikambala,

nalyo likung'unhaga mahamha ge mabici mu mbago,

na wono wali mu Nyumba yakwe wakutya,

“Lukumyo lweze kuli Mulungu!”

10 U Muwaha Mulungu yo yatemile lihwazi lye malenga

Muwaha Mulungu yo mutemi, yatemile cibitilila.

11 U Muwaha Mulungu kowapela wanhu wakwe vilungo,

kowalugutiza wanhu wakwe ko mulamu.
