Zabuli 53

Wusocele wo munhu

(Zab 14)

1 I walele welonjelaga caka wakutya,

“Hasina Mulungu!”

Wose wahodanji,

wetuma zinghani ze cikomaza,

holeka hasina hamba monga,

mono yakwituma ziswanu.

2 Mulungu yakuwasunjilila wanhu,

ku wulawa ku wulanga,

ho yawone hono ho yuli yunji we mahala,

hono hawe ho yuli munhu wo wose,

mono yakumulondola Mulungu.

3 Wose wapatuka, wose watipiye,

hasina mono ketumaga ziswanu,

hasina hamba monga.

4 Alu Mulungu nayo yakutya,

“Mwene wawo wono wakwituma makani mabi,

alo wasina wizuce lo?

Wono wakuwahiziza wanhu wangu,

hamba si wakuhemba ane yaye!”

5 Baho 'lu langa, wocicima ko woga,

u woga wono wakali hamba si nawuwona yaye,

hambi Mulungu nha kogeyagaza i mafupa ge walugu,

nawo wokolwa cevu, hambi Mulungu nha yakondya kuwaleka.

6 Yali yiwe yili ciba,

hono mhonelo ya Isilayeli,

yali yize kulawa mu Sayuni!

Mulungu kolo yawagomola wanhu wakwe,

i wana wa Yakobo wosecelela, Waisilayeli wowona nyemo.
