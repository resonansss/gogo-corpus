Zabuli 6

Kulomba wutazi mwi'daha lyo wugazice

(Kuli mwimbizi: Hamonga ne vitumamilwa vye zinghunzi vye masumbi nha vyono winha wimbe wa Shemininti Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wulece kunhacila kwe likuwo lyako,

hamba wulece kundipiciziza mwi'kuwo lyako.

2 Wumbonelaje lusungu, 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

hambi ane nha nili musocele,

holeka wumhonye 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

hambi nha nghucicima sunga mo mu mafupa mugati.

3 Nghugazika muno mu nhumbula yangu,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

alu kocela kunhaza sunga nili?

4 Wunangaje, 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu

wumhonye nhumbula yangu,

wumhonye mwi'fwa so lusungu lwako.

5 Hambi kuko ku yinze ye wafu,

nha kusina mono kakukumbucilaga yaye,

hamba hasina munhu kuko mono kosuma kukulumba.

6 Nghataye muno muno ku soko ye cililo cangu co wusungu,

vilo vyose ku cilo nghucisuluziza citanda cangu mahozi,

na mu wulile wangu, nghuwusuluziza muto wangu.

7 Ne meso gangu gakatala so wukalale,

gavimba ku soko yo wugazwe ne walugu.

8 Muwuce ha mwande yangu,

anye wose mo wetuma wubi!

Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yacihulika cililo cangu.

9 U Muwaha Mulungu kawuhulika wulombe wangu,

u Muwaha Mulungu kavumila wulombe wangu.

10 Ne walugu wangu wose wokolwa cevu,

kanyhi wogaya muno,

wowuya ne nyuma no kuwona cevu nhinicizo.
