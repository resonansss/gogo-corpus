Zabuli 104

Kumukumya muwumbi

1 Gwe go nhumbula yangu, wumukumye Muwaha Mulungu!

Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu wangu,

agwe yo gwegwe Mono wulawiliciye muno muno!

Kanyhi wivwicile nhojo ne lilumbo.

2 Wipilimye wuzelu nhe bulanjeti,

kanyhi wuwutandaze wulanga nhe hema,

3 wuzenjile wikalo wako,

mucanyha ye malenga mu wulanga,

wunozize mavunde gawa wo mutuka wako,

kugulucilaga mucanyha ye zimbawa ze libeho.

4 Kuyinozaga mbeho yiwa yo munhu wo kutuma,

u moto nawo hamonga ne malambi gayo,

kawa yo mwitumami wako.

5 Wuyisimizye yinze mu musinji wayo,

ho yilece kusinjisika cibitilila.

6 Nagwe wukayigubiciza yinze,

ne lilenga nha civwalo,

ne malenga nago, gakagagubiciza,

sunga ne matunda matali.

7 Nagwe wali weza kugakalipila malenga,

hodu gakawa kutiza lumangho,

gali geza kulihulika lizi lyako du,

gakakulumhuka lumangho.

8 Gakawa kuhuluma ku wulawilila,

mu matunda sunga mu malolo,

gakawa kubita sunga,

i hanhu hono wagawikaniziza.

9 Wagawicila mupaka, malenga gago,

ho galece kulapuka no kuyigubika kanyhi yinze.

10 Walavya mivwilo mu malolo,

yikolele muciligati ye matunda.

11 Ne mivwilo yiyo,

yizing'wesaga, zinimu za mu mbago.

Ne zinyhenje nazo,

mumo mo zidumulilaga nyhilu yazo.

12 Sunga ne zindeje nazo, zizengaga manhuli gazo,

mu mibici yono yili baho,

kanyhi zitimaga mu manhambi gazo ziwa kwimba.

13 Kanyhi kugatonyezezaga mvula,

i matunda ku wulawa kucanyha,

kuwa kuyimemya yinze no wumoteswe wako.

14 Kucindyaga mahanze, ndigwa ye vilimu,

ne vilimwa viwe ndigwa ye wanhu,

nayo munhu ho yetujile ndigwa yakwe,

ku wulawa mu muhangha,

15 nha cinji, divayi yono yomusangalaza,

ne mafuta gono gomupela nyemo,

sunga ne ndigwa yono yomupela vilungo.

16 Sunga ne mibici miwaha yo Muwaha Mulungu,

yose yikutuga malenga gono gakwiliye muno muno, heya,

sunga ne mibici ye mihagata ya ku Lebanoni,

yono yayicindya mweneco.

17 Ne zindeje nazo, zizengaga mumo manhuli gazo,

ziyobwa nazo, zinozaga wikalo wazo mu misonobali.

18 Na mu matunda matali,

mumo wo wikalo we zimhene za mu mbago.

Na mu matalawe namo, wo wutizizo we zimhimbi.

19 Agwe go Mulungu,

wawuwumba mwezi wucipimile mapango,

ne lizuwa nalyo, limanyile lyeneco,

lipango lyayo lyo wugwile.

20 Agwe kuletaga citi, ne cilo ciwa kuza,

ne zinyana ze zisimba nazo zikolomaga,

zituje mapelembo gazo.

21 Nazo zose zimulindilaga du,

Mulungu yazipele ndigwa yazo.

22 Nalyo lizuwa kolo likusosomoka du,

hodu nazo ziwa kuwuya kuceha,

mu zimhango zayo.

23 Ale, baho nayo munhu ho kawa kwinuka,

no kubita kwanza milimo yakwe,

nayo yakwituma sunga lizuwa ligwila.

24 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, milimo yako,

nha minji muno kanyhi yo wizine muno muno!

Wavinozeza vinhu vyose mu mahala mawaha!

Ne yinze yimemile viwumbwa vyako du!

25 Na kuko ko lili lilenga yiwaha,

kanyhi yigazi muno, na mugati yalyo,

vikalaga viwumbwa vinji muno,

vyono hamba ne liwalo lyavyo,

si limanyice yaye,

i viwumbwa vyumi, viwaha ne vidodo.

26 Sunga ne zingalawa zikolaga mumo mucanyha yalyo,

sunga ne lilimu lyono likucemwa Lewiyatani,

lyono waliwumba, nalyo lidawalaga mumo.

27 Ne viwumbwa vyose nha vikwitumbiye gwegwe du,

ho wuvipele ndigwa mu madaha gayo.

28 Navyo vilizaga co cose du cono kovipela gwegwe,

na lono wukufumbatula muwoko wako,

vyene vikwiguta vyono vijinile.

29 Na hono wihundice hadodo vivi du,

hodu vyene vyagaya,

na hono wuviwusize muhe wavyo wo wumi,

hodu vyofwa lwa kamonga,

no kuwuya mu manghundi mono vyalawilila.

30 Ninga hono wuviputile muhe wako,

hodu vikuwumipa kanyhi.

Agwe yo gwegwe mono kuyisipulaga yinze,

yiwa kutuga futa mhya.

31 Lukumyo lo Muwaha Mulungu lwikale cibitilila,

Muwaha Mulungu, yendezwe ne metumo gakwe mweneco.

32 Mwene kayilangaga du,

yinze nayo yiwa kucicima caka,

kanyhi kagapamizaga du matunda,

nago gawa kulavya lyosi!

33 Nane nowa kumwimbila du Muwaha Mulungu,

mu mikalile yangu yose,

na mu wikale wangu wose, wa mononze,

nowa kumukumya Mulungu wangu.

34 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

u lwimbo lwangu luluno lukwendezaje,

hambi i nyemo yangu yose nha yilawaga kwako.

35 Wanyo wubi wose, wali wawuswe ta mono'nze si,

ho halece kuwa no wavu wo wose kanyhi!

Gwe go nhumbula yangu, wumukumye Muwaha Mulungu!

Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe!
