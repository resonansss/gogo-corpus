Zabuli 68

Lwimbo lwo wusume

1 U Mulungu kenuka,

ne walugu wakwe weyagala,

na wono wamusulile, wose wakumutiza!

2 Nawo wakupemheluswa,

nha vyono lyosi lipemheluswaga ne mbeho,

ne wabi wose wokwajililila lukulu,

hali Mulungu, hamba kwijela yaye,

nha vyono lihula lyenjedukaga ha moto.

3 Ninga we ling'ani wasangalaye,

no kusecelela hali Mulungu,

wakusecelela akuno wakwimba so nyemo.

4 Mwimbilenyi Mulungu,

kanyhi mulikumye ne litagwa lyakwe,

nozelezenyi nzila yakwe,

mwene mono yakujendela mu mavunde,

litagwa lyakwe yakucemwa, Muwaha Mulungu,

ale, musangalale hali mwene.

5 Mulungu mono yekaye hono helile hakwe,

mwene yo Sogo ye wana wono wasina walezi,

kanyhi yo mudimi we wafwile.

6 Nayo Mulungu yo kawapelaga waciwa,

wikalo wono wikalaga,

kanyhi kawawopolelaga wawopwa,

no kuwasangalaza, ninga i wabedegazi,

wojendelela kwikala mu yisi yono yinyalile.

7 Gwe go Mulungu,

lono wawatonjela wanhu wako,

lono wali wali mu muhinzo wawo mu nyika,

8 Yinze yacicima,

sunga no wulanga nawo wukatonya mvula,

ku so wuze wako,

go Mulungu wa Sinayi, heya,

so wuze wako go Mulungu wa Isilayeli!

9 Gwe go Mulungu,

wayitonyeza yisi mvula mbaha,

wukayidejefya lono yali yeza kusakala.

10 Ne wanhu wako wakatuga mumo wikalo,

na so wuswanu wako,

wukawapela waciwa visakwa vyawo.

11 U Muwaha Mulungu kalavyaga wulajilize kamonga du,

nawo wacekulu yipuga yiwaha,

wawa kuzitandaza zinghani zizo,

12 “I watemi hamonga ne mapuga gawo,

wose wakutiza!”

Iwacekulu nawo ku zikaya,

wakugawana vyono vitecigwe.

13 Alu nhawule wanji wa muli nyenye wagonile mu mabululu,

mu wugono we zinziwa mwizuwa lyo wulugu?

Mono movili vihwani vye zinziwa vya fweza,

vyono vina mabawa gayo gakung'alala zahabu.

14 U Mulungu Mudaha Vyose, yali vilungo,

yali yeza kukondya kuweyagaza watemi,

mwi'tunda lya Salimoni,

ne seluji nayo yikawa kutonya mwi'tunda lilyo.

15 Matunda ge Bashani, go matunda matali muno,

matunda ge Bashani, gena vizuli vinji muno!

16 Nanye mo vizuli!

Nhawule lo mukuliwonela wivu litunda,

lyono Mulungu yalihagula,

ho yekale mucanyha yalyo?

Ninga Muwaha Mulungu,

kokwikala kuko cibitilila!

17 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakulawa ku Sinayi,

yakuza hono helile hakwe,

yali hamonga ne lipuga yiwaha,

lya maelufu na maelufu lye matuka go wulugu.

18 Yakubita kucanyha,

akuno yali hamonga ne mateka gakwe,

kabocela vipegwa kuli wanhu,

heya nhavivyo sunga na kuli wanhu i wabezi,

nayo Muwaha Mulungu kokwikala muli wene.

19 Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe,

mono kacidinghulilaga mitegulo yetu mwizuwa ne zuwa.

Mwene yo Mulungu mono kaciponyaga.

20 Mulungu wetu, yo Mulungu we mhonelo,

nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yo kaciponyaga mwi'fwa.

21 Nayo Mulungu,

koyipondanjiza mitwe ye walugu wakwe,

heya, i mitwe ya wose,

wono wakali wadindimiye zo wituma wubi.

22 Muwaha Mulungu yalonga yakatya,

“Ane nowagomola i walugu ku wulawa ku Bashani,

nowagomola ku wulawa mu visolo vye lilenga.

23 Agwe ho wusume kukola mu zisakami zawo,

sunga ne mabwa nago gayinamhe nha vyono gosuma.”

24 Gwe go Mulungu i mijendele yako,

yo wusume nha yijeye calinze,

i mijendele yo Mulungu wangu, u Mutemi wangu,

sunga ko ku hanhu hakwe hono helile!

25 I wembi walongoye ku mwande,

mu nyuma kowali watowa masumbi,

haciligati naho, wahinza watowa vigoma.

26 “Mukumizyenyi Mulungu haciligati,

ye lipuga yiwaha lye wanhu.

Mukumyenyi Muwaha Mulungu,

anye nyo lulelo lwa Isilayeli!”

27 Yamule Benyamini, u mudodo wa wose,

ho watyatye i walongola wa Yuda ne lipuga lyawo,

ha nyuma ho waze wawaha wa Zabuloni,

na wa Nafutali.

28 Gwe go Mulungu,

ale wulajilaje wuwaha wako,

wuwaha wono wetumamila ku soko yetu,

29 mu wulawa mu Nyumba yako ku Yelusalemi.

Ne watemi wokuziza du,

wena ne vipegwa vyawo mu mawoko!

30 Wugakalipilaje le, malimu gago gono

gekalaga mwi'lamba,

litewa lye zindama hamonga ne zinghongolo,

sunga makungugo gago gakupele cogopo,

hamonga no kukulipa kodi.

Ale, wuweyagaze wanhu wawo i wenda wulugu!

31 Wawaha watambule, wokuza ku wulawilila Misili,

Waetiyopiya nawo,

womwinulila Mulungu miwoko yawo.

32 Nanye mo wutemi wa mono'nze,

mwimbilenyi Muwaha Mulungu,

mwimbilenyi Muwaha Mulungu zinyimbo zo lukumyo,

33 mwimbilenyi mwene mono yekaye ku wulanga,

wulanga wono wali katali na katali.

Muhulicizenyi lono yakulavya lizi lyakwe kwe vilungo.

34 Mwanze zo wuwaha wo Mulungu!

Lukumyo lwakwe luli muli Isilayeli,

lukumyo lwakwe luli mu wulanga.

35 Mulungu yo we cogofyo muno,

i hanhu hakwe hono helile, heya,

mwene yo Mulungu wa Isilayeli!

Nayo kawapelaga wanhu wakwe vilungo no wuwaha.

Mulungu yalumbwe!
