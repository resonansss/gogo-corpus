Zabuli 26

Wulombe wo munhu muswanu

(Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wumbalaje'ne sina wubi,

hambi nha nitumaga du ziswanu,

nane nghwitumbiye gwegwe,

hamba sina mahwele yaye.

2 Go Muwaha Mulungu, nghulomba wunjele,

wumhime, wugalanje mahala gangu,

ne nhumbula yangu.

3 Hambi lusungu lwako nha lo lukunongoza nane,

njendelaga mu mbeka yako.

4 Sinikalanaga ne wanhu we caka caka yaye,

hamba sinilunzaga hamonga ne wanhu wakoci.

5 Sinyendile hamba hadodo,

kuhangahanga ne wanhu wono wetumaga zo wavu,

hamba sinokwilunziza lukulu ne wanhu wabi.

6 Ane nghwizuguza miwoko yangu,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

ho ndajile vyono ane nili mutelevu,

nghwilunza no wihangulize wo wuhembi,

mu wupilime we cikumbicilo cako,

7 akuno nghwimba no lwimbo lo kukulumbiza gwegwe,

hamonga no kwanza ze metumo gako go wizine.

8 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nyendile wikalo wa mu nyumba yako,

ihanhu hono lwikaye u lukumyo lwako.

9 Wulece kumbaliliza hamonga ne wanyo wubi,

hamba wulece kunhajiliza hamonga ne wanhu wawulaji,

10 i wanhu wono wekalaga wakulya mafupo vilo vyose.

11 Ninga 'ne, nitumaga zo wiling'ano du,

wumbonele lusungu wumhonye.

12 Nimile mholile, kanyhi nghamile,

baho ho nomukumizya Muwaha Mulungu,

mu wihangulize wuwaha we wanhu.
