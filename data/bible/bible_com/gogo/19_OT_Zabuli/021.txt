Zabuli 21

Wulumbe ku soko yo wusume

(Kuli mwimbizi: Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

U Mutemi yakusangalala ku soko ye vilungo vyono wumupeye,

yakusecelela ku soko yo wusume wono wumupelile.

2 Kumufisiliziza wuhile wa mu nhumbula yakwe,

hamba si wumwimile cisakwa cakwe.

3 Kumuziza kuwa kumupela wulugute no wumoteswe,

kumuvwika mu mutwe mukumbo we zahabu yono yilenguce.

4 Yakakulomba wumi, nagwe wukamupela vilo vinji,

vyo wikale cibitilila.

5 Yena lukumyo ku soko yo wutazi wako,

yo gwegwe mono wumupelile lilumbo no wogopwe.

6 Wukumunoza yawe mulugutwe cibitilila,

u wijele wako, wukumumemya nyemo.

7 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

mutemi yakwitumbiye gwegwe,

nayo kowa yapolile so lusungu lwako,

go Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye Muli Vyose.

8 U muwoko wako wowaficila i walugu wako wose,

u muwoko wako wo kulume,

wowebata wose wono wakusulile.

9 Nagwe kolo wukuza,

kowaticimiza mwi'kozo lyo moto,

i likuwo lyako go Muwaha Mulungu,

lyowongomeza, no moto wowatakamiza.

10 Lulelo lwawo lopatilwa lukulu mono'nze,

ne wana wawo, si wolosigala mu wanhu.

11 Hamba wanoze wusugu,

hamba wawe no wuhile wubi wo kukutamisa,

awo wowa wibahule wa bwete,

hamba siwosuma kwituma cinji.

12 Hambi agwe nha kowakulumhusa lumangho,

ko kuwalunga ne zisonga zako, mu wusu wawo.

13 Wulumbwe 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

ku soko ye vilungo vyako!

Nase cokwimba no kuvikumya i vilungo vyako.
