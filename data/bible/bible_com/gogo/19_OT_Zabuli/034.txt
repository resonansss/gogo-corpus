Zabuli 34

Lukumyo kuli wuswanu wo Mulungu

1 Ane nomukumya Muwaha Mulungu madaha gose,

hamba si nolecela lukulu kulutambula lukumyo lwakwe.

2 Mutima wangu wokwilumbila muli Muwaha Mulungu,

nawo wono wakwiwonelwa, wahulice, wasangalale.

3 Mumukumyaje Muwaha Mulungu hamonga nane,

wose hamonga cilikumye litagwa lyakwe.

4 Nghamulomba Muwaha Mulungu,

nayo yakahuliciza, yakambusiza woga wangu wose.

5 Mumupitucilaje Mulungu,

ho muwe ne nyemo, ho simokolelwa lukulu cevu.

6 Nayo muciwa yamulilila Muwaha Mulungu,

nayo yakamuhuliciza,

yakamuponya mu wugaye wakwe wose.

7 Nayo malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu,

kawadimaga wose wono wamogopile,

kanyhi kawaponyaga mu zimbali.

8 Jezenyi nyo weneco,

muwone vyono Muwaha Mulungu muswanu!

Wamoti wono wakumwitumbila mwene.

9 Ale, mumogopaje Muwaha Mulungu,

anye nyo wanhu wakwe, wose wono wamogopile,

si wanjilaga cinji yaye.

10 Sunga ne zisimba nazo hanji,

si zitugaga co kulya, ninga wono

wamogopilo Muwaha Mulungu, wakwizize vyose.

11 Nzonyi muhulicize anye nyo wadodo,

nomwijizenyi mumogope Muwaha Mulungu.

12 Wule, wendile wuwe na nyemo mu mikalile yako,

wikale vilo vinji mu wumote?

13 Ale, wulecele lukulu kuzilonga zinghani,

zimbi, kanyhi wulecele lukulu kulonga wulambi.

14 Wilegaje no wubi we mawala gose,

witume ziswanu, wulondole mulamu, wuwutyatye.

15 Hambi u Muwaha Mulungu,

nha kawalanjililaga i wanhu we ling'ani,

no kuwahuliciza cililo cawo.

16 Ninga iwetuma zimbi kawasininghalilaga,

nawo kolo wafwiza,

si wakukumbucilwa kanyhi yaye.

17 I wanhu we ling'ani,

kolo wakumulilila Muwaha Mulungu,

nayo kawahulicizaga,

no kuwaponya mu magayo gawo gose.

18 U Muwaha Mulungu yali behi na wose wono wena mahwele,

kawaponyaga wose wono wasina witumbilo.

19 U munhu we ling'ani, katugaga magazo menji,

ninga Muwaha Mulungu kamuponyaga muli gose.

20 Muwaha Mulungu kamudimaga muno muno,

munhu we ling'ani, na hasina hamba lifupa lyakwe,

limonga lyono lyobeneka.

21 Wubi wowongomeza i wanhu wabi, na wono wawasulile,

i wanhu we ling'ani, wolipicizwa.

22 Muwaha Mulungu kowaponya wanhu wakwe,

na wose wono wakutiziza kuli mwene, si wolipicizwa yaye.
