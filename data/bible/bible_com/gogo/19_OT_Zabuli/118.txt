Zabuli 118

Lilombo lyo wulumbe so wusume

1 Mulumbenyi Muwaha Mulungu,

hambi nha muswanu.

No lusungu lwakwe nha lwikalaga cibitilila.

2 Wanhu wa Isilayeli walonje watye,

“Lusungu lwakwe lwikalaga cibitilila.”

3 Nawo Wakohani wo Mulungu walonje watye,

“Lusungu lwakwe lwikalaga cibitilila.”

4 Na wose wono wamogopilo Muwaha Mulungu,

nawo walonje watye,

“Lusungu lwakwe lwikalaga cibitilila.”

5 Nane mu wugaye wangu,

nghamulilila Muwaha Mulungu,

nayo yakahulika, yakamhonya.

6 Muwaha Mulungu, yali hamonga nane,

holeka nane si nghogopa cinji co cose,

hambi munhu konoza cici?

7 Muwaha Mulungu yali hamonga nane,

nayo yo yakunhaza,

holeka nowawona walugu wangu wakusumwa.

8 Yili ciba kumwitumbila Muwaha Mulungu,

sinha kumwitumbila munhu.

9 Yili ciba kumwitumbila Muwaha Mulungu,

sinha kuwetumbila walongola we yinze yiyi.

10 Walugu wenji wali wamhilime,

ninga nghawapondanjiziza wose,

mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu.

11 Wali wamhilime mbavu zose,

ninga nane nghawapondanjiza wose,

kwe vilungo vyo Muwaha Mulungu.

12 Wakahungucila ko winji kotya nzuci,

ninga wakatakamila wose,

nhe cititika mu moto,

nane nghawapondanjiza wose,

kwe vilungo vyo Muwaha Mulungu.

13 Wakanzumhila, sunga nghawa nili behi,

no kusumwa nawo,

ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakanhaza.

14 Mbeka Muwaha Mulungu,

yo kamhelaga vilungo no wusume,

yo mweneco du mono yamhonizye.

15 Ale, hulicizenyi ta mazi,

ge nyemo no wusume gono gakulawa,

mu mahema ge wanhu wo Mulungu, gakutya,

“Vilungo vyo Muwaha Mulungu,

vyo vitumile zizi!

16 Vilungo vyo Muwaha Mulungu,

vyo viletile wusume wo wulugu,

Vilungo vyo Muwaha Mulungu yali wene,

vyo vitumile zizi!”

17 Ane si nofwa yaye, langa nowumipa,

nane nozanza ze metumo mawaha,

go Muwaha Mulungu.

18 Muwaha Mulungu kandipiciza muno muno,

ninga si yandeceleze nifwe.

19 Ndigulilenyi milango ye Nyumba yo Mulungu,

nane nokwinjila, nimulumbe Muwaha Mulungu!

20 Awu wo mulango wo Muwaha Mulungu,

wono wadahile kuwukolela we ling'ani.

21 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nghukulumba vyono wuhulicize,

hambi nha yo gwegwe du mono wumheye wusume.

22 Libwe lyono walilema wazenji,

lyo lyeza yibwe liwaha lyo musinji.

23 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu,

yo yetumile nghani yiyino,

nayo yo nghani yo wizine muno muno kuli'se.

24 Ali lyo lizuwa lyo wusume wo Muwaha Mulungu,

holeka cisecelele kanyhi cisangalale.

25 Wuciponye, 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu wuciponye!

Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu cikukunena wucipele wumoteswe!

26 Yalugutwe yuyo mono yakuziza,

mwi'tagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu!

Nase cikumulombelenyi wumoteswe,

ku wulawa mu Nyumba yo Muwaha Mulungu.

27 Muwaha Mulungu, yo Mulungu du yali wene,

nayo kacimulicilaga mu wuswanu.

Holeka ale mwibataje manhambi,

mu miwoko yenyu, musangalale,

mujendele hamonga sunga ko ku cikumbicilo.

28 Agwe yo gwegwe Mulungu wangu,

nane nghukulumba,

kanyhi nokwanza zo wuwaha wako.

29 Mulumbenyi Muwaha Mulungu,

hambi nha muswanu,

no lusungu lwakwe nha lwikalaga cibitilila.
