Zabuli 50

Wuhembi we mbeka

1 Muwaha Mulungu,

u Mulungu Mudaha vyose yakutya nhavi,

yakuwacema wanhu wose we yinze yose,

ku wulawa kucilima sunga ko kumwezi.

2 Ku wulawa ku Sayuni,

likaya liswanu muno u Mulungu,

yakwilajila no kumulika.

3 U Mulungu wetu yakuza,

hamba si konyamala yaye,

no moto wono wutakamizaga, nawo wumulongoleye,

no mukukunzo we libeho likali, wumupilime.

4 Nayo yakuzicema wulanga ne yinze zize zihomelele,

ziwone vyono yakuwaling'aniza wanhu wakwe.

5 Yakutya, “Ale, munghongholele iwehuwilwa wangu,

wono wawika cilagane nane mu wukumbice!”

6 Wulanga wukwanza zo wiling'ano wakwe,

nayo Mulungu mweneco yo hakimu.

7 “Ale, hulicizenyi,

anye nyo wanhu wangu, nane nolonga!

Ane nokuhomelela vyono wuli musutyi agwe Isilayeli,

Ane nili Mulungu, u Mulungu wako!

8 Ane si nghukukalipila ku soko ye makumbiko gako,

hambi nha wukali kundavizyaga vilavywa vyako vinyanywe.

9 I mbeka, ane si nghusaka,

ho wumhele zinghongolo za mwibululu lyako,

hamba zimvulati za mwi'tewa lyako, a a ng'o,

10 hambi zinimu zose za mu mbago,

nha sawo yangu,

sunga ne maelufu ge zinimu zose za mu matunda,

zose nha zangu nene.

11 Sunga ne zindeje zose za mu mbago,

nha zangu, sunga ne viwumbwa vyumi vyose,

vyono vili mu mawimbi, vyangu nene.”

12 “Hamba na lono nali mbone nzala,

si nali nghulonjele gwegwe yaye,

vyono yinze yose nha yangu,

sunga na vyose vyono vili mumo, vyangu vyose.

13 Alu agwe wukwihwesa,

vyono ane ndizaga nyama ye zinghambaku lo,

hamba lunji nghung'wa i sakami ye zimhene?

14 Lilumbo lyako,

liweze lyo likumbiko lyako,

kuli Mulungu wumufisilizize wilahe wako,

u Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye Muli Vyose.

15 Ale wunyhemaje lono wuli mu wugaye,

nane nokuponya, nagwe konghumya.”

16 Ninga Mulungu yakumulonjela u munhu mubi yakutya,

“So cici lo wukuzitambula-tambula zinzila zangu?

Hamba socici wukuzilonga-longa ze cilagane cangu?

17 Agwe nha kulemaga hono yunji yakuhose,

sunga ne zinzila zangu,

agwe si wendile kuzityatya yaye.

18 Agwe hono wumuwone munhu muhizi,

hodu du, mwelumbaniza nayo,

sunga ne wasenyha mukuhanga nawo.”

19 “Agwe kwikalaga nhondo zose,

wiwikanize du ku wulonga zimbi,

na ku wulonga wulambi nako, agwe wo mulimo.

20 Agwe kwikalilaga mu wulonga ze wayago du,

heya, wukumusonjeza sunga no nduguyo go mweneco.

21 Agwe wetuma zizo zose, nane nyamaye du,

wukwihwesa ane ninha gwegwe lo?

Ninga alu nghukutacila,

kanyhi nghukukalipila calinze.”

22 “Ale ta mwihwesaje viswanu ze nghani yiyi,

anye wose mono mumbedegaye,

ale mwilanjililaje, ane nomongomezenyi wose,

hamba hasina mono kosuma kumuponyenyi yaye.

23 Nayo mono yakumhela lilumbo liwa lyo

likumbiko lyakwe,

yuyo langa yo yanyogopile, no munhu wo wose mono

yakuhulika ane, nane nomuponya.”
