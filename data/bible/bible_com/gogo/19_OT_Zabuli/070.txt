Zabuli 70

Kulomba wutazi

(Zab 40:13-17)

1 Wumhonye, go Mulungu,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu wunhaze mbela!

2 Ho wawone soni, kanyhi wakozwe cevu,

wawo wono wakusaka kunyongomeza!

Wagomolwe kunyuma, wakozwe cevu,

wawo wono wakwendezwa no wutamiswe wangu!

3 Nawo wono wakwendezwa no wuzwajizwe wangu,

wapuwe no kukolwa cevu!

Wono watezaga, “Agwe! Agwe!”

4 Ninga awalya wose wono wakulondolaga,

wasangalale, no kusecelela ku soko yako.

Na walya wose wono wakwendezwa,

ne vilungo vyako vyo kuponya,

weze kulonga nhondo zose wakutya,

“Mbeka Mulungu yo we vilungo muno!”

5 Wunyizize mbela 'gwe go Mulungu!

Ane mono nili musocele kanyhi nili muciwa.

Hambi nha yo gwegwe go mutazi wangu,

kanyhi go mukombozi wangu,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wulece kutinya kunhaza.
