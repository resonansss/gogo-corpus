Zabuli 20

Kulomba wusume

(Kuli mwimbizi: Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 U Mulungu yakuhulicize lono wuli mu wugaye,

i litagwa lyo Mulungu wa Yakobo,

lyeze wo wutizizo wako.

2 Yakuletele wutazi ku wulawa hono helile hakwe,

yakusikamye kulawa mwi'tunda lya Sayuni.

3 Yagakumbucile makumbiko gako gose,

yalibocele ne likumbiko lyako linyanywe.

4 Yakupele vyono wukuhila mu nhumbula yako,

yagafisilize i mahweso gako gose.

5 Baho, nase ho cosecelela ku soko yo wusume wako,

cozinula zibendela ko kulikumya litagwa lyo Mulungu wetu.

U Muwaha Mulungu yakupelaje visakwa vyako vyose!

6 Alu nakondya kumanya vyono Muwaha Mulungu,

kamupelaga wusume u mutemi muhagulwa wakwe,

kanyhi kamuhulicizaga ku wulawa hono helile hakwe ku wulanga,

kanyhi kamupelaga wusume ko muwoko wakwe we vilungo.

7 Wanji wetumbilaga mituka yo wulugu,

wanji nawo wetumbilaga zifwalasi zawo.

Ninga ase, cimwitumbiye u Muwaha Mulungu,

u Mulungu wetu.

8 Wene wawo wekobalaga no kugwa hasi,

ninga ase cikwinuka no kwima wima.

9 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wumupelaje mutemi wusume,

kanyhi wucihulicize lono cikukulomba.
