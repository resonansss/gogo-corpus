Zabuli 4

Kulomba wutazi mwipango lye cinyamihe

(Kuli mwimbizi, hamonga ne vitumamilwa vye zinghuzi vye masumbi Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Gwe go Mulungu, go mudimi wangu!

Kolo nghukulomba, wuhulicizaje.

Lono nali nili mu wugaye, agwe wukanhaza,

holeka wumbonele lusungu,

wuhulicize wulombe wangu.

2 Mwene anye nyo wanhu,

alu mojendelela kunhovula sunga nili?

Alu ku sokoci mwendile makani go wulele,

no kulonga wulambi?

3 Mumanye nha vyono, Muwaha Mulungu yakondya,

kwihagulila we ling'ani wakwe.

Hambi Muwaha Mulungu,

nha kahulicizaga ane lono nghumulomba.

4 Mucicime kwe cogopo, mulece kwituma zo wubi.

Mwihwesaje muno muno,

lono muli mu vitanda vyenyu mutulile.

5 Mulavye makumbiko go wiling'ano,

akuno mukumwitumbila Muwaha Mulungu.

6 Kowali wenji wono walombaga wakutya,

“Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, wucimotyaje muno muno!

Kanyhi wucilanje ko lusungu!”

7 Kumhela nyemo mbaha mu nhumbula yangu,

kuciza wene wawo,

hamba wawe wena wuhemba winji hamonga ne divayi.

8 Na lono nghutambalala mu citanda,

ngonaga nhongo ko mulamu muno,

hambi nha yo gwegwe go Muwaha Mulungu wuli wene,

mono kundimaga nikala mholile.
