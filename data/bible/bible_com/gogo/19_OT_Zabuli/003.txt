Zabuli 3

Kulomba wutazi mwipango lye hakali mitondo

(Zabuli ya Dawudi lono yamutizaga Abusalomu)

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

i walugu wangu nha wenji,

kanyhi wenji muno wono wali suta nane!

2 Howali wenji muno wono wakutya,

“Ati agwe go Mulungu sikosuma kunhaza!”

3 Ninga agwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wuli ngula yangu,

nane nghwilumba no kutuga wuluwuce ku soko yako.

4 Nane nghumulilila Mulungu kwe'zi, ho yanhaze,

nayo kamhitulaga mu wulawa mwi'tunda lyakwe lyono lyelile.

5 Holeka alu nane nosuma

kugona nhongo no kwinuka kanyhi.

Hambi u Muwaha Mulungu nha yakunghamisa.

6 Nane hamba sinowogopa maelufu ge wanhu,

wono wamhilime kose kose.

7 Wuze 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu!

Wumhonye, gwe go Mulungu wangu!

Hambi nha yo gwegwe mono kuwabenaga

nzagasa i walugu wangu wose,

kanyhi kuwabenangaga meno gawo i wanhu wabi,

ho walece kunhamisa.

8 Agwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

yo gwegwe du mono kuponyaga,

ale wuwamotyaje wanhu wako.
