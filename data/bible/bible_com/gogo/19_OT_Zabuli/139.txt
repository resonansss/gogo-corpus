Zabuli 139

Wumanye wo Mulungu no wukamisi wakwe

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, agwe wamhima,

wakondya kumanya sunga,

zono zili mu nhumbula yangu.

2 Agwe wuzimanyile zose zono nitumaga,

kanyhi wugamanyile sunga,

ne mahweso gangu gose.

3 Kanyhi wukondizye kumbona,

lono nili mu mulimo, hamba hono nyhehile,

wuyimanyile nyipiganza yangu yose.

4 Sunga na lono nghali,

sinalonga cinji co cose,

agwe wakondya kuzimanya zi nyhici zono nolonga.

5 Hambi 'gwe nha wumhilime mbavu zose,

kanyhi kundimaga kwe vilungo vyako.

6 Hambi wumanye wako,

nha wunyhizize mahala gangu,

wumanye wuwo wuli kucanyha muno,

hamba si nosuma kuwuficila.

7 Alu notiziza hayi, ho niwe kutali nagwe?

Hamba nhizize hayi, kono kowa wuli kusina 'gwe?

8 Na hono nitye, ta nhizize ku wulanga,

agwe wuli baho,

na hono nitye, ta nhizize ku yisi ye wafu,

nako agwe wuli kuko du,

9 hamba nitye, nguluce sunga,

kono lisosomocelaga lizuwa,

hamba mbite nghekale kutali muno,

ku lubavu lwa kumwezi.

10 Kuko nako, nha vyo konyagana du,

kowa wuli kuko, ho wusume kunhaza.

11 Na hamba nali sace wutitu wungubize,

no wuzelu wono wumhilime ciwe cilo.

12 Hamba nhavivyo cilo nha si cinji kuli'gwe,

ne cilo kuli'gwe nha yinha misi du,

hambi wutitu no wuzelu nha co cimonga du kuli'gwe.

13 Hambi nha yo gwegwe,

mono wamumba muwili ne nhumbula,

wamumbila mu'nda ya yaya.

14 Nane nghukulumba muno, hambi,

nha wunojeye wupegwe cogopo,

hambi metumo gako nha ga wizine muno,

nane nigamanyiye lukulu mu nhumbula yangu.

15 Na lono mafupa gangu gawumbwaga,

no kulunzizwa mu'nda ya yaya,

na lono nawilucilaga mumo cinyele,

agwe wali wumanyile vyono nili mumo,

16 hambi 'gwe nha wali wumbwene,

na lono nali nghali nalelwa.

Sunga ne vilo vyangu vyose,

wukavyandika mu citabu cako,

lono kwali kukali kusina hamba cinji.

17 Gwe go Mulungu,

mbeka kukamu kumanya ze mahweso gako,

nago si gasumikaga hamba ku wuwalwe kose.

18 Na hono nali nitye nigawale,

gali gawe menji kuciza no musawawa,

Hambi nha lono nghusinhuka du,

nghali nili hamonga nagwe.

19 Yali yiwe yili ciba le go Mulungu,

hono wali wuwawulaje wawo i wabi!

Yali yiwe yili ciba,

wawulaji wawo hono wali wambucile.

20 Hambi nha wakukulonga agwe makani mabi,

kanyhi wakulilonga litagwa lyako makani ga cevu!

21 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

ane niwasulile mbeka,

i wanhu wono si wakwendile,

kanyhi niwabedegaye muno muno,

wono si wakuhulice gwegwe!

22 Holeka niwasuliye lukulu.

No kuwanoza nha walugu wangu.

23 Wulondole mu nhumbula yangu 'gwe go Mulungu,

ho wumanye vyono ninha, wumhime,

wugamanye mahweso gangu.

24 Wunanje hono nina nghani yo yose,

'mbi mu nhumbula yangu,

wunhonje mu nzila ye cibitilila.
