Zabuli 145

Lwimbo lo lukumyo

1 Gwe go Mulungu, ane nozanza zo wuwaha wako,

go mutemi wangu, nolikumya litagwa lyako,

nhondo zose cibitilila.

2 Nowa kukulumba nhondo zose,

nolikumya litagwa lyako nhondo zose cibitilila.

3 Muwaha Mulungu yo muwaha mono,

yanojeye kukumywa muno,

no wuwaha wakwe wucizize wumanye wose,

4 Ne zindelo na ndelo,

zojendelela kukumya ze metumo gako,

gono wetuma, no kwanza ze metumo gako mawaha.

5 Zojendelela kuzilonga,

zo wuwaha wo lukumyo lwako,

nane nojendelela kuzihwesa,

ze metumo gako go wizine.

6 Kanyhi wanhu wozilonga,

ze metumo gako go wogofye,

nane nokwanza zo wuwaha wako.

7 Wokwanza zo lukumyo lo winji wo wuswanu wako,

hamonga no kwimba kuli zo wiling'ano wako.

8 Muwaha Mulungu yo munya nheto no lusungu,

mwene si kakolwaga ne likuwo mbela,

yena lusungu luwaha.

9 Muwaha Mulungu, yo muswanu kuli wanhu wose,

kanyhi yali lusungu kuli viwumbwa vyakwe vyose.

10 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

viwumbwa vyako vyose vyokulumba,

ne wanhu wako wehuwilwa nawo wokukumya.

11 Kanyhi wokwanza,

zo lukumyo lo wutemi wako,

no kwanza ze vilungo vyako.

12 Ho wasume kuwamanyiza wanhu,

ze metumo gako mawaha,

hamonga no lukumyo lo wutemi wako.

13 No wutemi wako, wo wutemi we cibitilila,

no wulajilize wako, nha wikalaga cibitilila.

U Muwaha Mulungu nha mwihuwilwa,

mu wihuwizo wakwe,

kanyhi yali lusungu mu nyituma yakwe.

14 Muwaha Mulungu kawatazaga wose,

wono wali mu wugaye,

kanyhi kawenulaga wose wono wagwiye.

15 Viwumbwa vyose vikulanjiliye gwegwe du,

nagwe kuvipelelaga ndigwa mu vipango vyayo.

16 Nagwe kuvipelaga vyose vyono vikwiliye,

mu nghango ye visakwa vyayo.

17 Muwaha Mulungu,

yo yali ling'ani mu zinzila zakwe zose,

kanyhi mwene muswanu muli milimo yakwe yose.

18 Muwaha Mulungu yali behi,

na wose wono wakumucemeleza,

kuli wose wono wakumucemeleza,

kwe nhumbula yo winenenyhezi.

19 Muwaha Mulungu,

kawapelaga visakwa vyawo wose wono wamogopile.

Kacihulikaga cililo cawo no kuwaponya.

20 Muwaha Mulungu kawadimaga wose wono wamwendile,

ninga i wabi, kowongomeza wose.

21 Nokwanza zo lukumyo lo Muwaha Mulungu,

ne viwumbwa vyose,

vilikumye litagwa lyakwe lyono lyelile,

ku wulawa lulu, sunga cibitilila.
