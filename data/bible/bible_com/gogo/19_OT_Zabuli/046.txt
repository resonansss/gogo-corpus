Zabuli 46

U Mulungu yali hamonga nase

1 Mulungu wo wutizizo wetu ne vilungo vyetu,

nayo yewikanize nhondo zose,

kuleta wutazi mwidaha lyo wugaye.

2 Holeka nase hamba si cokogopa cinji,

hamba yinze yisinjisice,

sunga ne matunda gagwile mwi'lenga,

3 hamba lilenga libume no kugala,

sunga ne vitunda vicicime ku soko yo wubume walyo.

4 Holili likolongo lyono malenga galyo,

galisangalazaga likaya lyo Mulungu,

wikalo wono welile wa Mono Yalawiliciye Muli Vyose.

5 Nayo Mulungu yali mwi'kaya lilyo,

holeka si lyosuma kusinjiswa,

hambi mwene nha kaliletelaga,

wutazi hakali mitondo muno.

6 U Mulungu kenulaga lizi lyakwe,

sunga ne yinze yiwa kwenjeduka,

ne makungugo gakolwa yikuwo,

sunga no wutemi wose wuwa kusinjisika.

7 U Muwaha Mulungu,

u Mulungu we mihanga yali hamonga nase,

u Mulungu wa Yakobo, wo wutizizo wetu.

8 Ale nzonyi mugawone metumo go Muwaha Mulungu,

lanjenyi ta mapoto gono yetumile mono'nze.

9 Mwene yakuwudumya wulugu mono'nze yose,

mwene kabenanjizaga wupinde ne migoha,

sunga ne matuka go wulugu gose kagaticimizaga.

10 Yakutya nhavi, “Ale munyamale!

Mumanye vyono ane yo nene Mulungu!

Ane nghukumywa mu makungugo gose,

ane yo nene muwaha we yinze yose!”

11 U Muwaha Mulungu, Mulungu we mihanga,

yali hamonga nase,

u Mulungu wa Yakobo, wo wutizizo wetu.
