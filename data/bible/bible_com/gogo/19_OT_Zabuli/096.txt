Zabuli 96

Mulungu yo mutemi we lilumbo

(1Zin 16:23-33)

1 Mumwimbile Muwaha Mulungu lwimbo lupya!

Mwimbilenyi Muwaha Mulungu mo we yinze wose!

2 Mumwimbile Muwaha Mulungu,

no kulikumya litagwa lyakwe.

Mwanze nhondo zose ze nghani yakwe swanu,

vyono mwene yo mhonelo yetu.

3 Mugalonjele makungugo zo lukumyo lwakwe,

hamonga na ze metumo gakwe,

mawaha kuli wanhu wose.

4 Hambi u Muwaha Mulungu,

nha yo yalawiliciye nayo,

yo yakukumywa muno muno,

kanyhi yo yanojeye kogopwa muno,

kuciza milungu yose.

5 Milungu ye makungugo ganji si cinji yaye,

ninga Muwaha Mulungu yo yawumba wulanga.

6 Lilumbo no wuwaha vimupilime vilungo no wuswanu,

vimemile mu Nyumba yakwe.

7 Mumupelaje cogopo Muwaha Mulungu,

anye wose we zimhuga zose ze wanhu,

mupelenyi Muwaha Mulungu lukumyo no wuwaha wakwe.

8 Likumyenyi litagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu,

mulete cilavywa co kwinjilila mu Nyumba yakwe.

9 Mumuhembe Muwaha Mulungu lono yakwilajila,

wucicime hali mwene, 'gwe go yinze yose!

10 Mugalonjele makungugo mutye nhavi,

“Muwaha Mulungu yo Mutemi!

Yayisimya yinze hanhu hayo,

hamba si yosinjisicila lukulu yaye.

Nayo kowaling'aniziza wanhu mu ling'ani.”

11 Sangalalenyi anye mo wulanga ne yinze!

Nalyo lilenga litowe cihumbi,

hamonga na vyose vyono vili mumo!

12 Nanye mo migunda musangalale,

hamonga na vyose vyono vili mumo!

Holeka sunga ne mibici yose ya mu mbago,

nayo yimbe ko wusangalale!

13 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu kokuza no kuyitema yinze.

Heya, mwene kowatema wanhu,

mu ling'ani hamonga no wiling'ano.
