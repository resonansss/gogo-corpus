Zabuli 11

Kumwitumbila Muwaha Mulungu

(Kuli mwimbizi: Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 U Muwaha Mulungu wo wutizizo wangu

Alu anye mosuma nhawule kunyitya,

“Ale wuguluce nha ndeje sunga ko kwi'tunda?

2 Hambi wulanje ta i wabi

vyono wakuwukunga wupinde,

wakondya kutawika zisonga mu lukunzi lwo wupinde,

ho wawagume i wanhu waswanu mu wutitu.

3 Alu hono musinji wuhodangwe,

u munhu muswanu ho kotenda nhawule?”

4 U Muwaha Mulungu,

yali mu Nyumba yakwe yono Yelile,

ne ligoda lyo wutemi lyo Muwaha Mulungu,

lili ku wulanga.

Meso gakwe gakuwalanga wanhu wose,

nayo yazimanyile zose zono wakwituma.

5 Muwaha Mulungu kawapimaga we ling'ani ne wabi,

nayo kawasulaga muno muno i wanhu wakali.

6 Nayo kowalagaziza i wabi,

makala go moto ne cibiliti,

ne mbeho nghali muno muno,

yowa co cilipicizo cawo.

7 U Muwaha Mulungu, mwene yo we ling'ani,

nayo yendile ling'ani,

ne wanhu wono wakwituma ze ling'ani,

wawo wo wokwikalana nayo.
