Zabuli 147

Kumukumya Mulungu u Mudaha Vyose

1 Mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu!

Hambi nha viswanu,

kumwimbila Mulungu wetu ze lilumbo, heya,

viswanu kanyhi vyo yinojeye muno kumulumba.

2 Muwaha Mulungu,

yakulizenga likaya lya Yelusalemi,

yakuwakongholeza wapolile Waisilayeli,

wono wali weyagazwa.

3 Yakuwaponya wono wadejepe zinhumbula,

no kuziwopa zinghomele zawo.

4 Mwene kaziwalaga zinyelezi,

no kuzicemela zose matagwa.

5 Muwaha Mulungu wetu,

yo Muwaha mono yalawiliciye,

kanyhi yo we vilungo muno.

No wizuce wakwe wusina ndumo.

6 Muwaha Mulungu kawasimyaga

i wenenenyhezi ninga i wedunhumuzi,

kawagwisaga.

7 Ale, mwimbilenyi Muwaha Mulungu,

zi nyimbo zo wulumbe,

mutowelenyi ndonondo Mulungu wetu!

8 Mwene kawugubicizaga wulanga ne mavunde,

kawa kuyinozelezeza yinze mvula,

hamonga no kucindya mahanze mu matunda.

9 Mwene kazipelaga zi nimu ndigwa yazo,

sunga ne mana ge mahowe lono gakulila.

10 Mwene langa si yakuwa kwendezwa,

ne vilungo vye zifwalasi,

hamba si kasangalalilaga wutung'ati,

we wasilikale,

11 langa mwene kendezwaga,

ne wanhu wono wamogopile,

wanhu wono wetumbilaga lusungu lwakwe.

12 Gwe Yelusalemi,

wumulumbaje Muwaha Mulungu!

Wumulumbe Mulungu wako, 'gwe go Sayuni!

13 Hambi nha kayikamyaga milango yako,

kanyhi kawalugutiza ne wanhu wako.

14 Kawika mulamu mu mipaka yako

kanyhi yakukwigutya no wuhemba wuswanu muno.

15 Mwene kayilajilizaga yinze, ne nghani yakwe

yifisilizwaga musunho muno muno.

16 Kanyhi kalavyaga seluji

yiwa nha wuhamba,

kayanzazaga buhe kotya nha mavu.

17 Kaletaga mvula ye mabwe,

na hasina munhu mono

kosuma kuyikangalila mbeho yayo!

18 No kulavya wulajilize,

malenga gawa kwenjeduka.

Kaletaga mbeho mbaha,

malenga gawa kuhuluma.

19 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu kawamanyizaga,

i wanhu wakwe ze nghani yakwe,

nayo Isilayeli, zo wutonji wakwe

hamonga ne ning'aniza yakwe.

20 Ninga hamba si yawanozeza,

makungugo ganji zono zinha zizi,

holeka makungugo ganji,

hamba si wazimanyile ze malajilizo gakwe.

Mumulumbe Muwaha Mulungu!
