Zabuli 108

Lilombo ku so wucitilwe kuli walugu

(Zab 57:7-11; 60:5-12)

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

inhumbula yangu yikamile,

heya nghamile mu nhumbula yangu,

holeka nokwimba, kanyhi nokusangalalila!

Gwe go nhumbula yangu, alu wusinhuce!

2 Musinhuce nanye nyo ndonondo ne masumbi,

nane no mweneco nokwilawa kwinuka hakali mitondo!

3 Nane nokulumbila haciligati ye makungugo,

'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nokwimbila zo lukumyo haciligati ye wanhu.

4 Lusungu lwako lwinji lucizize wulanga,

no wihuwilwa wako,

wuficile sunga ko ku mwanya.

5 Gwe go Mulungu,

wulawiliciye ku wulanga kucanyha,

no lukumyo lwako luficile mono'nze yose.

6 Holeka wucikombolaje,

co wanhu wako mono wucendile,

wuciponye ko muwoko wako we vilungo,

kanyhi wucihulicize no wulombe wetu.

7 Mulungu kalonga ku wulawa,

hono helile hakwe katya,

“Alu langa nosangalala,

hono niyigawanule yisi ya Shekemu,

ne lilolo lya Sukoti nalyo,

noligawa kuli wanhu wangu.

8 Hambi Gileyadi nha yangu,

sunga na Manase nayo yangu,

Nayo Efulayimu nha yo kofiya yangu ye cuma,

na Yuda nayo, yo nghome yangu yo wutemi.

9 Nayo Mowabu lye bakuli lyangu lyo kwizuguziza,

nane noyigumilila Edomu cilatu cangu,

ciwe co citanjilo vyono nayo yangu,

kanyhi nozoza lizozo lyo wusume kuli Filisiti!”

10 Alu nani lo Gwe go Mulungu mono kosuma,

kunhegula sunga kwi'kaya lye ligomawakali?

Hamba nani mono konhonga sunga ko ku Edomu?

11 Hamba lu wo wutya,

wakondya kucilecela lukulu 'gwe go Mulungu?

Alu yo hodu du, hamba sikowa wuli hamonga,

kanyhi ne mapuga getu?

12 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

cikukunena wucitaze kuli walugu wetu,

hambi wutazi wono wukulawa kuli wanhu,

nha si wokwila cinji yaye.

13 Nase mbeka cosuma du,

hono Mulungu yawe yali ku lubavu lwetu.

Mwene kowapondanjiza i walugu wetu.
