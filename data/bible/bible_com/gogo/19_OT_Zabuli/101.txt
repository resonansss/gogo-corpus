Zabuli 101

Wihuwizo wo mutemi

1 Lwimbo lwangu ku soko yo wihuwilwa ne ning'aniza,

nane nolwimba so lilumbo lyako 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu.

2 Ne mikalile yangu yowa yisina yilema lyo lyose.

Alu 'gwe ma, konyiziza nili?

Mu kaya yangu, nokwikala mu mikalile,

yono yikwilaniciye,

3 hamba sinoyikangalilila nghani yo yose yimbi.

I mijendele ye wanhu wabi, ane si niyendile yaye.

Sunga ne mikalile yawo, ane si noyityatya ng'o.

4 Ne nzila yo yose yo wajilile, yeze yili kutali nane,

nane nokwilega no wubi we mawala gose.

5 Nowapezezya lukulu,

wose wono wendile kuwaheha wayawo,

hamba si nosuma kuwakangalila,

i wedunhumuzi, ne wepelelezi.

6 Wanhu wehuwilwa, awo langa wo wayangu,

ne wetumami wangu,

wowa wo walya wono wasina wubi.

7 No munhu wo wose mono mulambi,

ayo si kosuma kwikala mu kaya yangu,

hamba mukoci wo wose si cokwikalana nayo yaye.

8 Nane nhondo zose, nowongomezeza lukulu,

i wabi wose mu yisi yetu,

nowawusiza lukulu i wabi wose,

mwi'kaya lyo Muwaha Mulungu.
