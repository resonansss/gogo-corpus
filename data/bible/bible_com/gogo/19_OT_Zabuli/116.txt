Zabuli 116

Lilumbo lyo munhu ku soko yo wuponywa mwi'fwa

1 Ane nimwendile Muwaha Mulungu,

hambi nha kahulikaga,

hambi nha kahuliciza cililo co wulombe wangu.

2 Mwene nha kahulicizaga lono nghumulilila.

3 Zimbali ze lifwa zali zimhilime,

no wogofywe we citenjelo wukanyibata,

nghawa no wugazice hamonga no wukalale.

4 Holeka nghamulilila Muwaha Mulungu nghatya,

“Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, nghunena wumhonye!”

5 Muwaha Mulungu yo munghala wuswanu wose,

no wihuwilwa, Mulungu wetu,

yo Mulungu wo lusungu.

6 Muwaha Mulungu kawakamisaga,

wono wasina wutazi,

hambi nha lono nali nili mu mbali,

mwene yamhonya.

7 Holeka go nhumbula yangu, wutule muli'ne,

hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha kanhendela ziswanu.

8 Kamhonya mwi'fwa, kagapata ne mahozi gangu,

yakawa kundima ho ndece kanyhi kugwa mu musutye.

9 Holeka nane lu,

nowa kujenda hali Muwaha Mulungu,

mu yisi ye wanhu womi.

10 Nghawa nihuwiye du,

sunga lono nalongaga nghutya,

“Alu langa ane, nhondo neza kwajilila.”

11 Nhondo sunga nghawa kulonga ko woga nghutya,

“Mbeka wanhu hamba si wehuwilwa yaye.”

12 Alu nomugomolela ci cici Muwaha Mulungu,

ku soko ye ziswanu zose zono yanhendeye?

13 Nomulavizya Muwaha Mulungu cilavywa ca divayi,

nimulumbe ku soko ye mhonelo yono yamhonizye.

14 Nomufisiliziza Muwaha Mulungu vipingo vyangu,

ha mwande ye likungugo lyakwe lyose.

15 Muwaha Mulungu kawukalalilaga muno muno,

wufwe wa wono wali ling'ani,

16 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, ane nili mwitumami wako,

nili mwitumami wako du nha vyono yenha yaya.

Nagwe wukanoza nghawa mwelecele.

17 Holeka nane lu,

nokulavizya likumbiko lyo wulumbe,

kanyhi nowa nghogopile muno muno.

18 Nane nokufisiliziza zose,

zono nehuwiza, go Muwaha Mulungu,

ha mwande ye likungugo lyako lyose,

19 kuko ku Yelusalemi mu Nyumba yako.

Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe!
