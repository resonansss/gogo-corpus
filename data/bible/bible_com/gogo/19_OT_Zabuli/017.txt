Zabuli 17

Wulombe wo munhu muswanu

(Ulombe wo Dawudi)

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wuhulicize cililo cangu ce mbeka,

ubocele wulombe wangu wa mu nhumbula,

wono wusina wulambi.

2 Yo gwegwe mono kunhetelaga,

agwe kuwonaga ze ling'ani.

3 Agwe wuyimanyiye lukulu nhumbula yangu kunyiziza ne cilo,

kunyihenga hamba kuwona cinji cibi muli'ne.

4 Kuli ze metumo ge wanhu, ane sinongaga zimbi,

nha vyono wanozaga wanji,

nilibate lilajilizo lyako,

singhutyatya nzila ze wawulaji.

5 Nhyatyaga nzila yako nhondo zose,

nghali si ninayileka hamba hadodo.

6 Nane nghukucemeleza, 'gwe go Mulungu,

hambi nha kuhulicizaga, holeka wuteje likutu lyako,

wuzihulicize zinghani zangu.

7 Wuwulajilaje lusungu lwako, wuwakomboze kuli

walugu wawo, wose wono wakutiziza kuli'gwe.

8 Wunghamisaje, nha vyono kuyikamisaga nenje ye liso lyako,

wuhundice mu mubeho we mabawa gako,

9 ho niwe kutali no wihomi we wabi i walugu wamhilime,

10 wasina lusungu hamba hadodo,

wamemile nghani zo widunhumuzi.

11 Alu wanhejile wamhilime kose kose wakuhila wangwise hasi.

12 Wewikanize wahawanje nha simba,

nha vyono nyana ye simba yisepaga mhelembo yayo.

13 Wuze, 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu mwihome nawo,

wuwagwise hasi wumhonyaje kuli wanhu wabi ko mumaje wako.

14 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wumhonyaje no muwoko wako kuli wanhu wawo,

i wanhu wono walangaga za mu sawo ya mononze yiyi du.

Wuwalipicizaje kwe magayo gono wawawicila, gawe

gawakwiliye ne wana wawo,

wawalecele sunga ne wezukulu zawo.

15 Ninga ane nokuwona, hambi nha sina wusutye,

nane kolo neza kwinuka,

nowa nina nyemo ku soko yo kukuwona.
