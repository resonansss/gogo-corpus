Zabuli 111

Kumukumya Muwaha Mulungu

1 Muwaha Mulungu yalumbwe!

Nomulumba Muwaha Mulungu,

kwe nhumbula yangu yose,

mu wihangulize we wanhu wakwe we ling'ani.

2 Metumo go Muwaha Mulungu,

nha ga wizine muno muno!

Na wose wono wakugasangalalila,

wakusaka wagamanye viswanu.

3 Hambi co cose cono ketumaga,

nha cimemile lukumyo no wutemi.

Ne ling'ani yakwe, yikalaga cibitilila.

4 Muwaha Mulungu nha kacikumbucizaga,

ze metumo gakwe go wizine,

Muwaha Mulungu muswanu, kanyhi yali lusungu.

5 Mwene kawalisaga wono wamogopile mwene,

hamba si kacisemwaga cilagane cakwe yaye.

6 Kawalajila wanhu wakwe vilungo vyakwe,

ko kuwapela zi'si ze makungugo ganji.

7 Metumo gakwe ga ling'ani, kanyhi ga mbeka.

Ne mejizo gakwe gose nha mehuwilwa nhondo zose.

8 Ne malajilizo gakwe,

gekalaga cibitilila ne cibitilila.

Nago galavizwa mu mbeka na mu wiling'ano.

9 Mwene yawakomboza wanhu wakwe,

kanyhi yakanoza cilagane nawo ce cibitilila.

Mwene yelile, kanyhi wa kogofya muno muno.

10 Holeka mu wumogopa Muwaha Mulungu,

wo musinji we mahala gose,

nawo wose wono wakugebata malajilizo gakwe,

wo wawo wono wakupegwa wizuce.

No lukumyo lwakwe lwikalaga cibitilila.
