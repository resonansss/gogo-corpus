Zabuli 38

Lilombo lyo munhu mono yali mu wugaye

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wulece kunhacilila mwi'kuwo lyako,

hamba kundipiciziza mu wukali wako.

2 Nagumwa ne zisonga zako,

no muwoko wako wunghandamize vibi.

3 Na ku soko ye likuwo lyako,

ane neza nili mu wusungu wuwaha muno,

hamba na mu mafupa gangu, mweza musina wuswanu,

ku soko yo wubi wangu.

4 No wubi wangu wungubicize, wanhulila,

wawa nha mutegulo muzito muno muno.

5 Ne zinghomele zangu alu zawola,

zeza kunungha, ku soko yo wulele wangu.

6 Nekunga, nenama ho hasi,

nghulila ko wukalale misi yose.

7 Mhungo yanhama, neza nili behi no kufwa,

neza sina hamba wuswanu wowose.

8 Alu natimba, nadejepa muno,

nghuzizidika muno so wusungu wa mu nhumbula.

9 Go Muwaha Mulungu, agwe wuwumanyile wuhile wangu,

hambi cililo cangu nha cili calinze hali'gwe.

10 Ne nhumbula yangu yikukukunza muno,

sunga ne vilungo vyambucila,

ne meso gangu si gakuwona viswanu.

11 Sunga ne wayangu weza kunhiza,

ku soko ye zinghomele zangu,

na we mhuga yangu, nawo wekala kutali nane.

12 Nawo wono wakusaka wambulaje,

vyo wajendeleye du kutega mitego yawo.

Na wono wakunyihweseza zimbi,

nawo wakusaka wambulaje.

Misi yose, wekalaga wakwihwesa vyono wonhendela.

13 Ninga ane, si nghuzihuliciza yaye,

neza nha nili ciziwi,

neza nha mono yali cibwibwi,

mono singhusuma kulonga cinji.

14 Heya, mbeka! Neza nha munhu mono si

yakusuma kupitula hamba cinji,

hambi nha si ya kuhulika.

15 Ninga ane nokwitumbila gwegwe du

go Muwaha Mulungu.

Agwe go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mulungu

wangu, kohuliciza.

16 Nghulomba I walugu wangu ho walece

kuwusecelelela wugaye wangu,

hamba kwilumbila mu wugwe wangu.

17 Neza nili behi nhondo no kugwa hasi,

hambi nha nili mu wusungu du vilo vyose.

18 Alu nghunena masutya gangu gose,

no wubi wangu wunghalaze muno muno.

19 Ninga walugu wangu,

wali vilungo, kanyhi wakamile.

Kanyhi wo wenji muno, wono wasulile mbula soko.

20 Nawo wawo wono wakungomolela zimbi,

ku soko yo wuswanu wangu,

wakumhingamhinga ku soko vyono ane nitumaga ziswanu.

21 Holeka wulece kundeka 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wulece kuwa wuli kutali nane go Mulungu wangu.

22 Wiyohe, wunhaze mbela,

go Muwaha Mulungu, go Mukombozi wangu.
