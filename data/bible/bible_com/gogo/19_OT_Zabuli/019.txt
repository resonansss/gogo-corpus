Zabuli 19

Lukumyo lwo Mulungu kuli viwumbwa

(Kuli mwimbizi: Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Wulanga wukulajila lukumyo lwo Mulungu,

no wulanga nawo wukwanza i milimo ye miwoko yakwe.

2 Misi nayo yikuyilonjela i misi ye mitondo,

cilo cikucimanyiza cilo cono cikutyatya,

3 Ninga hasina wulonje hamba nghani zono zikwitumamilwa,

hamba lizi lyono likuhulikwa.

4 Hamba nhavivyo,

lizi lyazo litandaye mononze mose,

ne zinghani zayo zificile sunga ko ku mhelo ye yinze.

U Mulungu yaliwiciye lizuwa wikalo walyo mu mwanya,

5 nalyo lilawilaga kunze lihwanile bwana halusi,

lihwanile i citung'ati mono yewikanize ku wisumanize.

6 Nalyo lisosomocelaga lubavu lumonga,

no kupilima sunga ku lubavu luyagwe,

na hasina cono cosuma kwicita ne livuce lyayo.

Lilajilizo lyo Mulungu

7 I lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu likwilaniciye,

nalyo limupelaga munhu vilungo.

Nago malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu gakamile,

gawapelaga mahala i wenenenyhezi.

8 U wutonji wo Muwaha Mulungu, wo wulunji,

wusangalazaga nhumbula, lilajilizo lyo Muwaha Mulungu,

lyo lye ling'ani, limwijizaga munhu.

9 U wogopo Muwaha Mulungu, yo nghani yono yelile,

cogopo co kumogopa Muwaha Mulungu cikalaga cibitilila,

ne malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu, go go wulunji,

gose gali ling'ani.

10 Maswanu muno si nha zahabu,

gali muzinzi si nho wuci muswanu muno muno.

11 Gakunyijiza ane no mwitumami wako, vyono,

hono nghugatyatya,

gakundetela wuhumbilizwe wuwaha.

12 Ninga 'lu nani 'mbi,

mono kewonaga i masutya gakwe mweneco?

Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wumhonye ne masutya gono sinigamanyile.

13 Wunyhitilaje no mwitumami wako,

kuli masutya go kusutya ne nyono,

wulecele lukulu kuvumila gago gateme muli'ne.

Na baho ho nowela nghwilaniciye,

hamba si nowa no wusutye wuwaha yaye.

14 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, nghukulomba zinghani zangu,

hamonga ne mahweso gangu, vikwendeze gwegwe,

mono wuli 'talawe lyo wutizizo wangu,

kanyhi go Mukombozi wangu!
