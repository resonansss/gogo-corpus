Zabuli 109

Wung'ung'ulice wo munhu mono yali mu wugaye

1 Ane nghukukumya 'gwe go Mulungu,

holeka wulece kunyamala!

2 I wanhu wabi ne wakonji, nha wakunzumhila,

wakumbalalila zinghani zo wulambi.

3 Wakumbalalila makani ge cevu,

kanyhi wakunzumhila bwete,

nane ho si hodanjile cinji yaye,

4 hamba yiwe ane niwendile,

ninga wene hamba si wanyhelelela yaye,

hamba nhavivyo, ane nghawalombela du.

5 Wene wakundipa zimbi,

ku soko yo wuswanu wangu,

kanyhi wakusula ku soko yo wendo wangu.

6 Wumuhagulile hakimu mono si yanojeye,

wumulece yasitakwe na yumonga wa muli walugu wakwe.

7 Na ha wuling'anizwe wakwe ho yejele yena masutya,

no wulombe wakwe nawo, wuwe yisutya linji nalyo.

8 Ne vilo vyo wikale wakwe, navyo viwe vicefu,

na munhu yunji yasole witumami wakwe!

9 Ne wanagwe, wawe wana wono wasina walezi,

no mugolece wakwe nayo, yawe mufwile!

10 I wanagwe nawo wangalime,

kanyhi wawe welombelezi,

kanyhi wawinjilizwe,

mu matongo ge zikaya zawo!

11 Nayo mono yakumudaya cinji,

yamuboce sawo yakwe yose,

no mujenzi nayo yetujile vinhu,

vya mu nhuguti yakwe!

12 Kanyhi halece kuwa na munhu wo wose,

mono komuwonela lusungu,

hamba mono kowalanjilila,

i wanagwe wono weza wasina walezi!

13 No lulelo lwakwe lose longomele,

sunga ne litagwa lyakwe lilece kukumbucilwa,

kanyhi mu ndelo zono zikuza!

14 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yawukumbucile wubi,

we walezi wakwe, hamba no wubi wo nyina,

wulece kupatilwa lukulu!

15 Muwaha Mulungu yawukumbucile wubi wawo,

nhondo zose, langa wene,

wo walece kukumbucilwa kanyhi mono'nze.

16 Hambi u mubi yuyo, nha si yacelelela,

hamba hadodo kuwa no lusungu,

langa mwene yakawewonela no kuwawulaga,

i waciwa, no mutoci hamonga no musocele.

17 Yakawa yendile muno kuwalijita wanhu,

ninga 'lu, 'lece wulijite wakwe wumuwuyile,

kanyhi mweneco.

Hambi nha si yenda wanji ho walugutwe holeka,

lece wulugutwe wuwo wumuwucile mweneco.

18 Hambi wulijite kuli mwene,

nha winha wuvwala mwenda, holeka,

lece ta, wulijitwe wuwo wumulowece,

nha malenga, kanyhi wulijitwe wuwo,

wumwinjile sunga mo mu mafupa gakwe kotya mafuta.

19 Leka wulijitwe wumugubice nha civwalo,

wumupilime nhondo zose nha musipi.

20 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

'lece zizo ziwagane wawo wono wakusitaka.

Wanhu wono wakunonga zono si zinojeye.

21 Gwe go Mulungu,

go Muwaha Mulungu wangu,

wunhendele nha vyono wendile gwegwe,

wumhonye ku soko yo wuswanu wo lusungu lwako!

22 Ane nha nili muciwa kanyhi nili mutoci,

nhamiswe mo mu nhumbula yangu mugati.

23 Nghwajilila nho mubeho we cinyamihe,

kanyhi nghupemheluswa nhe zinzije.

24 Sunga ne mafugamilo gangu nhondo gatimba,

ku soko yo wuwula kulya ndigwa,

alu nhondo nasigala nghuli ne mafupa.

25 Nawa nili munhu wa kusekwa hali wanhu,

na lono wakumbona, wakumbedegala,

no kusinjisa mitwe yawo.

26 Holeka wunhaze 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

go Mulungu wangu, wumhonye,

mu nghango yo lusungu lwako.

27 Wuwamanyize walugu wangu,

vyono yo gwegwe du mono kumhonyaga,

na vyono yo gwegwe du,

go Muwaha Mulungu mono witumile zizo.

28 Ale, walekaje du wene walijite,

ninga agwe wundugutize,

wawo wono wakunzumhila bwete ho wakozwe cevu,

ninga ane langa no mwitumami wako, ho sangalale.

29 Walugu wangu wavwikwe cevu,

wefunice cevu cawo weneco nhe bulanjeti.

30 Nane nomulumba Muwaha Mulungu,

kwe 'zi yiwaha, kanyhi nomukumizya,

mwipuga yiwaha lye wanhu,

31 hambi mwene nha kawatetelaga waciwa,

kanyhi kamuponyaga kuli walya,

wono wakumuling'aniza yafwe.
