Zabuli 130

Kulomba wutazi

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

mu wukatale wangu ane nghukulilila.

2 Holeka go Muwaha, wuhulice cililo cangu,

wuhulicize wulombe wangu wunhaze.

3 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

hono wali wuwe kukumbucilaga zo wubi wetu,

nani 'mbi mono yali yavumilwe nagwe?

4 Ninga 'gwe, kucinenelaga wubi wetu,

ho cikwitumamile kwe cogopo.

5 Niwulindiye wutazi wo Muwaha Mulungu,

na mu nghani yakwe nitumbiye.

6 Nimulindiye Muwaha Mulungu,

kuciza walindilizi vyono walanjililaga,

wele wa hakali mitondo, heya,

kuciza walindilizi wa hakali mitondo.

7 Gwe Isilayeli, wumwitumbile Muwaha Mulungu,

hambi mwene nha yena lusungu,

yewikanize nhondo zose ho yacikomboze.

8 Heya, kowakomboza wanhu wakwe Isilayeli,

mu wulawa mu wubi wawo wose.
