Zabuli 47

Mulungu, u Mutemi we yinze yose

1 Nye nyo wanhu wose, towenyi maganza,

mukumyenyi Mulungu ko wusangalale!

2 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu,

u Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye, mono yakogofya.

Yo Mutemi muwaha kono'nze kose.

3 Kacipela wusume wo kuwasuma wanhu we makungugo,

kagawika makungugo hasi yo wulajilize wetu.

4 Kacipela yisi yiyi yiwe wuhazi wetu,

yisi yono wo lukumyo lwa Yakobo mono yamwendile.

5 U Mulungu kabita kucanyha akuno yakusangalalilwa,

Muwaha Mulungu kabita kucanyha,

akuno yakutowelwa ne zindulele.

6 Ale, mwimbilenyi Mulungu zi nyimbo zo lukumyo,

heya mwimbilenyi!

Mwimbilenyi Mutemi zi nyimbo,

zo lukumyo heya mwimbilenyi!

7 Mulungu yo mutemi we yinze yose,

holeka mwimbilenyi zi nyimbo zo lukumyo.

8 Mulungu yatemile makungugo gose nayo yekaye,

mwi'goda lyakwe lyo wutemi lyono lyelile.

9 Sunga ne watemi we makungugo,

wakwikonghola hamonga ne wanhu wo Mulungu wa Abulahamu.

Mwene Mulungu, yali vilungo yacizize

mapuga gose ge wasilikale wo wulungu,

mwene yamanyice muno muno muli wose.
