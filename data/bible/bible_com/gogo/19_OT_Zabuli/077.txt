Zabuli 77

Wutuzwe mwidaha lyo wugaye

1 Nghumulilila Mulungu kwe'zi yiwaha,

nghumulilila nayo kohulika.

2 Nane nimulombaga Muwaha Mulungu, lono nili mu wugaye,

nghumwinulila miwoko yangu ne cilo,

hamba kukatala yaye,

ninga si nhugaga wutuzwe wo wose.

3 Nane lono nghumukumbucila Mulungu,

nghutuga wuzinguce, na lono nghwihwesa muno,

nhumbula yangu yikuzilika.

4 Yakunyimila ndece kutuga nhongo hamba hadodo,

'nye, ane nili mu wugaye mbeka,

no kulonga kose hamba si nghusuma yaye.

5 Niwa kwihwesa sunga ne vilo vye katali,

nghumbucila sunga ne myaka yono yapuluta.

6 Na mu cilo cose, nili mu wihwesehwese,

nghwihwesa no kwiwuza nghutya nhavi,

7 “Alu mwene, Muwaha Mulungu, hongo yacilecela lukulu?

Hamba si kokwendezwa nase kanyhi lo, hamba hadodo?

8 No lusungu lwakwe luwaha ma, alu hodu lwamalika?

Alu hodu, hamba si kowufisiliza,

kanyhi wihuwizo wakwe?

9 Hamba Mulungu yasemwa lo vyono yali lusungu?

Hamba wule, alo likuwo lyakwe,

lyowa lyo lyawuwusa wupolo wakwe lo?”

10 Hanyuma kanyhi nghatya,

“Alu cono cikunhamisa muno,

mu nhumbula yangu cocino, wo wutya,

u Mulungu lu mono yali vilungo,

yaweza yasina vilungo kanyhi!”

11 Ninga 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nozikumbucila ze metumo gako,

nozihwesa zinghani zako ze katali zo wizine.

12 Nokwihweseleza muno muli zo mulimo wako,

hamonga ne metumo gako mawaha.

13 Gwe go Mulungu,

vyose vyono kwitumaga 'gwe, vyelile.

Hasina mulungu muwaha kanyhi mono yehwanile gwegwe.

14 Agwe yo gwegwe Mulungu mono kwitumaga mapoto,

walajila kuli makungugo wuwaha wako.

15 Wawakomboza wanhu wako ko muwoko wako, heya,

u lulelo lwa Yakobo na lwa Yusufu.

16 Ne malenga nago gali geza kukuwona go Mulungu,

heya, gali geza kukuwona,

mbeka gakogopa muno muno,

lilenga likacicima sunga ko ku visolo vyayo.

17 Ne mavunde gaketa malenga,

mabalalusi gakabalaluka muno muno,

ne zisonga zako, zikamulika ako na ako,

18 Lizi lye libalalusi lyako, likaza,

limuli lyayo, likayimulika yinze nayo,

yinze yikasinjisika, yikacicima.

19 Wayiwika nzila yako mucanyha ye lilenga,

wukajenda mwi'lenga liwaha,

ninga nzayo zako hamba kwijela yaye.

20 Wukawatonga wanhu wako nhe zingholo,

ko wutonji wa Musa na Haluni.
