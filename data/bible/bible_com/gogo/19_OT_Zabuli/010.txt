Zabuli 10

Wulombe wo munhu mono yakwiwonelwa

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, so cici wuli kutali?

Nhawule wukwihundika lono cili mu wugaye?

2 I wabi wakwidunhumula no kuwatyatilila i waciwa.

Ninga 'gwe lece, mitego yawo yiwagwize weneco.

3 U munhu mubi kedunhumulilaga mu masulumizi gakwe mabi,

nayo we masulumizi,

kamukanaga Muwaha Mulungu no kumubeza.

4 U munhu mubi, mu wulawilila mu widunhumuzi wakwe,

hamba si kamucelelelaga Mulungu,

ne mahweso gakwe gose katezaga,

“Hasina Mulungu.”

5 U munhu mubi katugaga cambuzi nhondo zose,

hamba si kayimanyaga ne ning'aniza yo Mulungu,

kanyhi kawabedegalaga i walugu wakwe wose.

6 Nayo kehwesaga mu nhumbula yakwe,

“Ane si nokwihwelela cinji,

hamba sinotuga magayo mu mikalile yangu.”

7 Mulomo wakwe wumemile wulijite no wukonji mupelu,

ku wulonga makani mabi na ge cikomaza.

8 Yendile kwihundika mu vikaya,

ho yawasepe no kuwawulaga cinyele,

wanhu wono wasina yisutya lyolyose.

Kawasepaga wanhu wono wasoceye,

9 kawazumhilaga nhinicizo ku wulawa mumo,

mu wihundiko wakwe nha simba.

Katejelaga ho yawebate waciwa,

nayo kawagwizaga ko mutego wakwe, kawa kuwasola.

10 Kamuzumhilaga musocele no kumugwisa hasi,

yakugwiswa ko wukali wo munhu mubi.

11 Nayo munhu mubi kehwesaga mu nhumbula yakwe yakutya,

“U Mulungu si kacelelelaga cinji,

yafufunize, hamba si yakumbona yaye.”

12 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wuze wuwalipicize,

kanyhi wulece kuwasemwa wono wali mu wugaye.

13 Gwe go Mulungu,

alu so cici u munhu mubi yakukubedegala,

no kutya, “Hamba si kosuma kundipiciza yaye?”

14 Na kumbe agwe ho kuwawonaga wono,

wali mu wugaye hamonga ne magaziko,

nagwe wiwikanize nhondo zose kuwataza.

Nayo mono yasoceye kakwitumbilaga gwegwe,

nayo mwana mono yasina walezi katugaga wutazi kuli'gwe.

15 Alu wuvibenanjizaje vilungo vye wabi,

wuwudumyaje wubi wose ho wulece kwijela kanyhi.

16 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yo mutemi,

cibitilila ne cibitilila!

Ne makungugo gono si gamumanyile mwene,

gokwajililila lukulu,

hamba kwijela kanyhi mu yisi yakwe.

17 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

kubocelaga cililo co musocele,

kumuhulicizaga no kumusikamya mu nhumbula yakwe.

18 Kowataza i wana wono wasina walezi,

na wono wakwiwonelwa,

munhu we nghuli ho yalece kuwogofya kanyhi.
