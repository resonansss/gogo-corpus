Zabuli 37

Ndumilizo ye wabi ne waswanu

(Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Wulece kwigaza ku soko ye wanhu wabi,

wulece kuwawonela wivu i wetuma zimbi.

2 Hambi wene nha wokwajilila musunho nhe mahanze,

wonyala nhe vilimwa videce.

3 Wumwitumbilaje Muwaha Mulungu,

witume ziswanu, holeka kokwikala wupolile mononze.

4 Wuyilondolaje nyemo yako kulawa kuli Muwaha Mulungu,

nayo kokupela vyose vyono wukusaka mu nhumbula yako.

5 Wumulecelaje Muwaha Mulungu mikalile yako yose,

wumwitumbilaje, nayo kokutaza.

6 Nayo kowunoza wuswanu wako wung'alale wuwe winha wuzelu,

ne ling'ani yako yiwe yinha lizuwa lya mu misi mugati.

7 Ale, wutule hali Muwaha Mulungu,

wumulindilaje ko wukangalizi,

hamba wulece kwigaza na awalya wono wakumota muli vyawo,

hamba kuli walya wono wakwitujila vinhu mu zinzila zimbi.

8 Wulecele lukulu kukolwa ne likuwo,

hamba kwihangavya, hambi mu wunoza nha vivyo,

nha muletaga wugazice du.

9 Ne wanhu wose i wetuma zimbi,

nha wokongomezwa,

langa wono wamwitumbiyo Muwaha Mulungu,

wo wohala yisi.

10 Hambi nha cikali cidaha cidodo du,

nayo mubi kowa yajililila lukulu,

nagwe komulondola hanhu hono yali yekaye,

hamba si kohawona kanyhi.

11 Ninga i wapolo wo wohala yisi,

kanyhi wo wowusecelela mulamu we mbeka.

12 U munhu mubi, kamwihwesezaga,

i zimbi du u munhu we ling'ani,

no kumwikang'anhilila meno ko lusulo.

13 Ninga mwene Muwaha Mulungu,

kamusekaga u mubi,

hambi nha yamanyile du vyono u mudumo wakwe weza wuli behi.

14 I wanhu wabi, wakwegaga mimaje yawo,

wakukunga no wupinde wawo,

ho wawawulaje waciwa na wono wasina cinji,

no kuwacinja wanhu we ling'ani.

15 Ninga mimaje yawo yiyo,

yowacinya weneco mu zinhumbula zawo,

no wupinde wawo wobenanjizwa-benanjizwa.

16 Isawo ndodo yo munhu we ling'ani yili ciba,

si nhe sawo nyinji yono wena nayo i wanhu wabi.

17 Hambi u Muwaha Mulungu,

nha kovibenanjiza i vilungo vye wanhu wabi.

Langa wanhu we ling'ani,

Muwaha Mulungu kawakamisaga muno.

18 Muwaha Mulungu kawakamisaga wanhu wehuwilwa,

no wuhazi wawo wokwikala cibitilila.

19 Hamba vize vilo vibi,

wene si wogazika yaye,

hambi nha wowa wena ndigwa nyinji mu vilo vye lizala.

20 Ninga i wanhu wabi, wokongomela wose,

ne walugu wo Muwaha Mulungu,

wonyala nhe maluwa ga mu mbago, na mbeka,

wokwajililila lukulu nha lyosi.

21 U munhu mubi, kakopaga hamba si kalipaga yaye,

ninga munhu we ling'ani,

kanozaga du lihewa no kulavya bwete.

22 Wono wakondya kulugutizwa no Muwaha Mulungu,

wawo wo wohala yisi.

Ninga wono wakondya kulijitwa no Muwaha Mulungu,

wawo wowinjilwa kutali.

23 U Muwaha Mulungu, yo katongaga zinghanzo zo munhu,

nayo kamudimaga munhu mono yakwendezwa nayo,

24 na hamba yagwe hasi,

si kogwila lukulu yekalilile baho hasi,

vyono Muwaha Mulungu yo yamwibatilize.

25 Ane lu, nakombipa,

no wikale wangu weza wa myaka minji muno,

ninga nghali hamba sininawona munhu,

we ling'ani Mulungu yamulece,

hamba ne wanagwe,

wawe wakwilombeleza ndigwa, aa ng'o.

26 Langa mwene kalavyaga du bwete madaha gose,

na wanji yakuwakopesha,

ne wanagwe wakuwa wulugutwe.

27 Wuwuce mu wubi, witume ziswanu,

holeka nagwe kokwikala mu'si cibitilila.

28 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yendile wiling'ano,

hamba si kawalekaga wehuwilwa wakwe.

Mwene kawadimaga wawo cibitilila,

ninga zindelo ze wabi, wokongomezwa.

29 We ling'ani, wohala yisi,

nawo wokwikala mumo cibitilila.

30 U munhu we ling'ani,

kalongaga nghani ze mahala.

Kanyhi kalongaga ze ling'ani no lulimi lwakwe.

31 Kawibataga wulajilize wo Mulungu mu nhumbula yakwe,

si kawulekaga yaye hamba hadodo.

32 U munhu mubi, kamusepaga u munhu muswanu,

no kujela kumuwulaga,

33 ninga Muwaha Mulungu sikomuleka,

u munhu muswanu mu vilungo vyo mulugu,

hamba si komuleka yaling'anizwe, hono yasitakwe.

34 Wumwitumbilaje Muwaha Mulungu,

kanyhi wukamise no wutonji wakwe,

nayo kokupela yisi,

nagwe kowawona i wabi vyono wakongomezwa.

35 Nimuwona u mubi yakalipe muno muno,

yetandaze nhe libici lyo muhagata lya Lebanoni.

36 Ninga ha nyuma nali neza kukola baho,

nghamwagana yeza yali hasina,

nghamulondola, ninga hamba siyejela yaye.

37 Ninga lanje ta,

u munhu muswanu munhu we ling'ani,

u munhu mono yendile mulamu,

yo kapegwaga lulelo.

38 Ninga wanyo wubi wose,

wokongomezezwa lukulu,

sunga no lulelo lwawo,

nalo lopatilwa lukulu.

39 U Muwaha Mulungu kawaponyaga we ling'ani,

no kuwadima mwipango lyo wugaye.

40 U Muwaha Mulungu kawatazaga no kuwaponya,

kawaponyaga mu wanhu wabi,

vyono nawo wamwitumbiye mwene.
