Zabuli 97

Mulungu yo mutemi we lilumbo

1 Muwaha Mulungu yo yatemile!

Yinze yisangalale, nanye,

mo vizingwa sangalalenyi!

2 Mavunde ne citi ciwaha vimupilime, nayo,

yatemile ko wuswanu ne mbeka.

3 No moto wumulongoleye ku mwande yakwe,

wukuwaticimiza i walugu wakwe mbavu zose.

4 Ne mamuli gakwe gakuyimulicila yinze,

nayo yinze yikugawona yiwa kucicima.

5 Nago matunda gakwenjeduka kotya yihula,

ha mwande yo Muwaha Mulungu.

Heya, ha mwande yo Muwaha we yinze yose.

6 Wulanga wukwanza ze ling'ani yakwe,

ne makungugo gose gakuluwona lukumyo lwakwe.

7 Na wose wono wakuhemba vihwani,

wakukozwa cevu, heya,

sunga na wose wono wakwikumizya milungu,

yono yisina cambuzi,

milungu yose nha yimuhembaga Muwaha Mulungu du.

8 Wanhu wa ku Sayuni waseceleye,

ne makaya gose ga Yuda nago gakusangalala,

ku soko yo wuling'anizi wako go Muwaha Mulungu.

9 Hambi 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu

Mono wulawiliciye nha wutemile mu yinze yose,

kanyhi yo gwegwe du,

mono wuli vilungo muno muno wucizize milungu yose.

10 U Muwaha Mulungu, yawendile wono wawusulile wubi,

kanyhi mwene kawudimaga wumi wa wehuwilwa wakwe,

no kuwaponya mu vilungo vye wanhu wabi.

11 No wuzelu, wumumulicilaga munhu,

we ling'ani ne nyemo nayo,

kuli wanhu waswanu.

12 Nanye mo we ling'ani,

muzisangalalilaje zono Muwaha Mulungu yetuma!

Mukumbucile zo lukumyo lwakwe, mumulumbe!
