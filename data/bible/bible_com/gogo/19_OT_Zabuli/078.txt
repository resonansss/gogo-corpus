Zabuli 78

Mulungu ne wanhu wakwe

1 Ale hulicizenyi wijize wangu,

anye nyo wanhu wangu,

muteje likutu muhulicize zinghani zangu.

2 Ane nolonga nanye mu nzila ye vihwanicizo,

nomulolosezenyi ze zifumbo ze katali,

3 ze zinghani zono cazihulika,

kanyhi no kuzimanya,

zinghani zono walezi wetu waciloloseza.

4 Nase hamba si coleka kuwasimuliziza,

i wana wetu, cowasimuliziza lulelo,

lono lukuza kuli zo wuwaha wo Muwaha Mulungu,

kuli ze mapoto gakwe gono yagetuma.

5 Hambi nha yawika wulajilize kuli Waisilayeli,

na kuli lulelo lwa Yakobo,

yakasimya wuhomelezi.

Yakawalajiliza walezi wetu,

vyono wawejize ne wana wawo malajilizo gakwe.

6 Ho wanhu we ndelo yono yikuza wagamanye,

heya, i wanhu wono wolelwa hanyuma, wagamanye,

nawo ho wawejize i wana wawo.

7 Nawo ho wasume kumwitumbila Mulungu,

ho walece kugasemwa i metumo go Mulungu,

langa ho wagebatilile lukulu i malajilizo gakwe.

8 Walece kuwa nha vyono wali i walezi wawo,

wono wali wabedegazi kanyhi wafyuzi,

lulelo lono lusina wulunji we nyikala, wono wukamile,

u lulelo lono lusina wihuwilwa kuli Mulungu.

9 I wanhu wa Efulayimu, mwizuwa lyo wulugu,

wakatiza wakawuya ne nyuma,

akuno ho webate no wupinde hamonga ne zisonga mu mawoko.

10 Si wasaka kwibata cilagane co Mulungu,

kanyhi walema no kugatyatya malajilizo gakwe.

11 Wakasemwa zono Mulungu yali yetumile,

ze mapoto gono yali yawalajila.

12 Yanoza mapoto hali walezi wawo,

mwilolo lya Sowani mu yisi ya Misili.

13 Yalitemanula lilenga, yakawalowosa wene,

yakaganoza malenga geme wima nhe lijinji.

14 Mu mapango ge misi,

yawatonga ne livunde ninga mu cilo cose,

yawatonga no wuzelu wo moto.

15 Yagapandula matalawe mu nyika,

yakawang'wesa malenga, mu wulawa mu visolo.

16 Kanyhi yalavya malenga wulawa mwi'talawe,

yakagahulumya nha makolongo.

17 Ninga hamba nhavivyo,

vyo wakacila du kumusutizya Mulungu,

sunga na mu nyika,

wakamufyulila u Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye.

18 Wakamujela Mulungu mu zinhumbula zawo,

wakusaka wapegwe ndigwa yono weneco wasakaga.

19 Wamulonga Mulungu wakutya,

“Alu mwene mbeka,

Mulungu kosuma kucipela ndigwa mu muno mono nyika?

20 Hamba yiwe, heya mbeka yatowa litalawe,

malenga gakahuluma nhe kolongo,

ninga aluno ma, mbeka kosuma kucipela ndigwa,

hamonga no kuwapela wanhu wakwe nyama?”

21 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yali yeza kuzihulika zizo,

yakawa kukolwa ne likuwo,

likuwo lyakwe likawakolelwa i wana wa Yakobo,

no kumwinucila Isilayeli,

22 vyono si wamwihuwila Mulungu,

hamba kwihuwila kose vyono kosuma kuwaponya.

23 Ninga hamba yiwe nhavivyo,

yagalajiliza mavunde ga kucanyha,

yakayidigula milango ya ku wulanga,

24 yakawagwisiza Mana ho walye,

yakawapela wuhemba wa ku wulanga.

25 Hodu wakalya ndigwa ye wamalayika,

nayo Mulungu, yakawapela ndigwa,

mu nghango nha vyono wasaka.

26 Yakayinoza mbeho ya kucilima yipute muno muno,

na kwe vilungo vyakwe, yakayitonga mbeho ya kutakama,

27 yakawaletela zindeje nyinji muno wanhu wakwe,

zikawa nyinji kotya musawawa wa mwi'lenga,

28 ne zindeje zizo zikagwa haciligati ye nghambi yawo,

zikapilima mahema gawo gose.

29 Ne wanhu wakalya wakeguta.

Mulungu yawapela cono wasakaga.

30 Ninga na lono wali wakali wena,

no wuhile wo kulya,

ne ndigwa lono yali yikali mu milomo yawo,

31 lo lulo baho likuwo lyo Mulungu,

likawacila, Mulungu,

yakawawulaga i wanhu,

wono wali wali vilungo muli wene,

yakawagwisa ne wazelelo wa Isilayeli.

32 Ninga hamba nhavivyo,

vyo wakacila du kumusutizya Mulungu.

Si wehuwila yaye,

hamba yawe yali yetuma mapoto mawaha.

33 Holeka Mulungu nayo,

yakavidumulila i vilo vyawo nho muhe,

ne myaka yo wikale wawo,

mu wugaye wo kogofya.

34 Na mu nghango yono yajendelelaga kuwawulaga,

awalya wono wasigalaga,

wakajendelela kumupitucila,

wakanena wubi wawo wakamupitucila Mulungu,

kwe zinhumbula zawo zose.

35 Wakumbucila vyono Mulungu, yo mudimi wawo

na vyono u Mulungu Mudaha Vyose,

yo mukombozi wawo.

36 Ninga wamukonga Mulungu, na muli zose zono

wamulonjelaga, zali za wulambi du.

37 Hamba si wali wehuwilwa kuli mwene,

hamba si wasuma no kwibata cilagane cakwe.

38 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, Mulungu yakawa yali

lusungu, yakawanenela wubi wawo,

hamba si yawongomeza yaye.

Yeyimila likuwo lyakwe kali kenji, hamba si

yalileceleza likuwo lyakwe limukole muno.

39 Mwene yakumbucila vyono wene wawa wanhu du,

wehwanile mbeho yono yipulutaga yiwa kwajilila.

40 Wihwesaje ta,

kali kalinga wamufyulilaga mu nyika!

No kumukalaza mumo mu nyika!

41 Wakamujela Mulungu kali kenji muno muno,

wakamukalaza muno muno yuyo,

Mono Yelile wa Isilayeli.

42 Hamba kukumbucila i vilungo vyakwe,

hamba za mwizuwa alilya lono yawaponya,

mu miwoko ye walugu wawo,

43 lono yetumila mezino mawaha mu yisi ya Misili,

hamonga ne mapoto ga mwilolo lya Sowani!

44 Yagahundusa malenga ga mu makolongo,

gakawa sakami,

Wamisili ho walece kutuga malenga go kung'wa.

45 Yawagumilila mawumbi ge mahazi gono gawagaza,

hamonga ne mabula gono,

gawambuza wakatuga wambuce wuwaha.

46 Yakazikombola zi nzije,

zikalya vilimwa vyawo,

hamonga no kuyihodanga migunda yawo.

47 Yayihodanga migunda yawo ya mizabibu,

ne mvula ya mabwe,

hamonga ne mikuyu yawo ne mbeho nghali muno.

48 Yawulaga zing'ombe zawo,

ne mvula ye mabwe zingholo zawo,

yakaziwulaga ne limuli.

49 Yalileka likuwo lyakwe liwazize,

hamonga no wukali, ne simbili no wugaye,

hamonga ne litewa lye wamalayika wawulaji.

50 Hamba si yalimila likuwo lyakwe,

hamba si yenda kuwatetela yaye,

langa yawawulaga no wutamwa we lihuhuji.

51 Kanyhi yawawulaga i wana wose wa Misili,

i walongozi kulelwa, heya,

ndawililo ye vilungo vyawo mu mahema ga Hamu.

52 Hanyuma yawahamya wanhu wakwe,

nhe zingholo, yakawalongoza ku nyika,

nhe litewa lye zimitugo.

53 Yawajendya wapolile, hamba si wogopa yaye,

langa lilenga likawagubiciza walugu wawo.

54 Nayo yakawahamizya ku yisi yakwe,

yono yelile, mu matunda gono yagateka mweneco,

kwe vilungo vyakwe.

55 Yagawinga makungugo ku mwande yawo,

yakawapela Isilayeli yisi zawo ziwe zawo,

yawapela no wikalo we zimbeyu zakwe.

56 Hamba nhavivyo, wakacila kumujela,

no kumufyulila Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye,

hamba si webata zo wuhile wakwe yaye.

57 Wakawa wasina cihuliko, hamba kuwa wehuwilwa,

wakawa nha vyono wali wenha walezi wawo,

wakayumba-yumba nhe songa yono si nghangale.

58 Wamukalaza Mulungu,

mu wuhemba milungu yinji mu wuhembi wawo,

holeka wamunoza Mulungu yawe,

no wivu ku soko ye makumbicilo ge milungu yawo,

ye vihwani.

59 Nayo Mulungu yali yeza kuziwona zizo,

hodu yakakolwa ne likuwo,

no kumulemela lukulu Isilayeli.

60 Yakawuleka no wikalo wakwe wa ku Shilo,

wikalo wono yali yekaye muli wanhu.

61 Yakalileka ne Lisanduku lye Cilagane,

lisolwe ne walugu,

Lisanduku lyono lina vitanjilo vye vilungo vyakwe,

hamonga no lukumyo lwakwe.

62 Yawasimbila wanhu wakwe mweneco,

yakawakombola ho wawulagwe ne walugu wawo.

63 Ne wazelelo wawo wose, wakamalicila ku wulugu,

i wahinza nawo, wakekala si wawatujile,

wo kuwatola yaye.

64 I wakohani wawo nawo, wakawulagwa wose,

ne i wacekulu zawo, si wakombolwa ho wawalilile yaye.

65 Ninga ku ndumilizo yayo,

u Muwaha Mulungu yakenuka nha yakulawa mu nhongo,

nha citung'ati mono yasangalazwe ne divayi.

66 Yakawatowanga walugu wakwe,

no kuwagomola ne nyuma,

yakawakozeza cevu lukulu cibitilila.

67 Hamba nhavivyo ninga, yayilema mhuga ya Yusufu,

hamba si yawahagula wo mulango wa Efulayimu.

68 Langa yawahagula wo mulango wa Yuda,

kanyhi yakalyenda muno ne litunda lya Sayuni.

69 Nayo yakayizenga Nyumba yakwe kuko,

yikawa yinha nho wikalo wakwe ku wulanga,

yikawa yihwanile ne yinze,

yono yayisimya yikamile cibitilila.

70 Yamuhagula Dawudi mwitumami wakwe,

mono yamusola ku wulawa kwi dimo,

71 yamucema lono yali yali mu wudima litewa

lyakwe, yakamunoza ho yamudime Yakobo,

mono lye kungugo lyakwe,

yawadime Isilayeli wanhu wakwe.

72 Nayo Dawudi yakawadima,

mbeka kwe nhumbula yakwe yose,

hamonga no kuwatonga ko wunghula,

wuwaha muno muno.
