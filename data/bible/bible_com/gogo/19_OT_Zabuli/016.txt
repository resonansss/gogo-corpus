Zabuli 16

Wulombe wo witumbilo

(Zabuli ya Dawudi)

1 Wunghamisaje 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

nghwihundika muli gwegwe.

2 Nimulonjela u Muwaha Mulungu,

“Agwe yo gwegwe go Muwaha Mulungu wangu,

sina cinji ciswanu, langa hono si gwegwe du.”

3 Kuli ze milungu yo wulambi yono yili mononze,

ane nehwesaga vyono yili vilungo!

4 Ninga wono wakuyihemba milungu yinji,

wakwiyiletela weneco wugaye wuwaha.

Nane sinokwiyinjiziza lukulu mu makumbiko gawo.

Hamba ne milomo yangu siyotambulila lukulu,

matagwa ge milungu yiyo.

5 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, ane nghuhaguye gwegwe du,

yo gwegwe du mono kumhelaga visakwa vyangu vyose,

no wumi wangu wuli mu miwoko yako.

6 Wumhelile ciwagwa ciswanu muno muno, heya,

na vyose vyono kumhelaga nha viswanu vicizize.

7 Nane nghumukumya u Muwaha Mulungu,

mono kanhongaga sunga na mu cilo,

ciselinji cangu cikuwa kunyijiza.

8 Holeka nane nghumuwika Muwaha Mulungu,

hamwande yangu nhondo zose,

nayo vyono yali hamonga nane,

holeka hamba sinosinjisika yaye.

9 Holeka nosangalala no kusecelela,

kwe nhumbula yangu yose.

Nane nokwikala mholile muno.

10 Hambi nha wunyhitiye ne vilungo vye lifwa,

hamba sikomuleka mono yelile wako yawolele mu citenjelo.

11 Agwe kondajila nzila yono yibitile ku wumi,

hali'gwe yimemile nyemo,

na mu muwoko wako mo mozili ziswanu ze cibitilila.
