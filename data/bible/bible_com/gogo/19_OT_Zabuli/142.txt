Zabuli 142

1 Nghumucemeleza Muwaha Mulungu yanhaze,

nghumunena akuno nghulila.

2 Nghumuwicila wulilanzice wangu,

nghumuloloseza zo wugazice wangu wose.

3 Lono nili behi kukatala mu nhumbula,

mwene yazimanyile zose ze mijendele yangu.

Nawo walugu wanhejeze no mutego.

4 Na lono nghulanga ako na ako,

si nghumuwona hamba munhu wo wose,

mono komhela wutazi,

hamba ne hanhu ho kutiziza, hose sina,

hamba munhu yumonga bwete hasina,

mono yakumbona nha vyono nane nili munhu.

5 Holeka nane nghukucemeleza agwe,

go Muwaha Mulungu wunhaze.

Hambi nha yo gwegwe du,

go wutizizo wangu,

yo gwegwe du mono wina vyose,

vyono nghuhila mu mikalile yangu.

6 Holeka wuhulice wucemeleze wangu wunhaze,

hambi nha natimba muno,

wumhonye kuli walugu wangu,

hambi nha wali vilungo wanyhizize.

7 Wumbusize magaziko gono gali muli'ne nane,

ho nghulumbile mu wihangulize, wa we ling'ani,

hambi 'gwe nha kunhendela ziswanu.
