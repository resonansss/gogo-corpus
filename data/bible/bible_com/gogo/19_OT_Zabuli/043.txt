Zabuli 43

Wulombe wo munhu mono yawingwa kwawo wujendeleye

1 Gwe go Mulungu, wime ku lubavu lwangu,

wunhetele ling'ani yangu,

wunyhitile kuli wanhu wono wakusulile,

i wanhu walambi kanyhi wabi.

2 Agwe Mulungu,

yo gwegwe mono wuli wutizizo wangu,

alu kanyhi nhawule wanhaga kutali?

Alu nha najendelela kugazika muno,

nghugazwa ne walugu wangu?

3 Wundetele wuzelu wako,

hamonga ne mbeka yako,

vinhonje, vifise sunga kwi'tunda lyako,

lyono lyelile, ku wikalo wako.

4 Baho nane go Mulungu,

nobita ha wukumbicilo wako,

nokuza kuli'gwe go Mulungu,

mono wo mwamulizo we nyemo yangu.

Nane nokukumya kwe lisumbi 'gwe go Mulungu,

go Mulungu wangu.

5 Alo socici nghalaye nhavivi mu nhumbula?

Hamba socici nghugazika nhavivi?

Alu hodu, nomwitumbila Mulungu,

nomukumya kanyhi Mulungu wangu,

no muponyi wangu.
