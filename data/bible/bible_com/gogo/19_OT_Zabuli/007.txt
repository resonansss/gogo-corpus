Zabuli 7

Wulombe wo munhu mono yakwiwonelwa

(Cililo ca Dawudi ku soko ya Kushi, wo mulango wa Benyamini)

1 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

Mulungu wangu, ane nghwitumbiye gwegwe,

umhonye kuli wose wono wakunhyatilila,

wumbuse muli wene.

2 Na hono wulece kunozeza nhavivyo,

wene wokuza wahawanje nha simba,

wanghwejele kutali, kono kowa kusina mono komhonya.

3 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

Mulungu wangu!

Hono niwe nitumile yinji ya muli zizi,

na hono niwe niyitipiza ko wubi miwoko yangu,

4 na hono yiwe lunji

nimutendela vibi muyangu,

hamba lunji niwe nimutamisa

mulugu wangu mbula soko,

5 Ale hodu lece mulugu wangu yanhyatilile, yanyibate,

yawukanyanjile wumi wangu, yambulaje.

6 Ale, wuzize mu wukali wako 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wulimile likuwo lye walugu wangu.

Winuce 'gwe go Mulungu wangu, hambi 'gwe,

nhondo zose nha kusakaga ling'ani yifisilizwe.

7 Wugakonghole hali gwegwe makungugo gose,

nagwe ho wugateme ku wulawa ku wulanga.

8 Gwegwe, 'gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

kugaling'anizaga makungugo,

ale wuning'anizizaje nane mu nghango yo wiling'ano wangu,

mu nghango yo wutelevu wangu, wono nina nawo.

9 Wavu we wanhu wabi wudume,

wumukamye munhu we ling'ani,

'gwe go Mulungu mono kwitumaga ling'ani,

mono wuzimanyile zose ze cinyele za mu zinhumbula,

ne mahweso ge wanhu.

10 U Mulungu, yo ngula yangu yono yikunyhitila

mwene kawaponyaga i wanhu watelevu.

11 U Mulungu, yo hakimu we ling'ani,

mwene kamulipicizaga munhu mubi nhondo zose.

12 Ne wanhu nawo hono walece kupituka,

u Mulungu kowunola mumaje wakwe,

kowutawika wupinde ku wulunji wawo,

13 nayo kowikaniziza lukulu vitwalo vyakwe,

vyo kuwulajila,

no kuzikangaliza mu moto zisonga zakwe.

14 Ninga i wanhu wabi wamemile wubalya,

wegute wubi, nawo wolela wukonji,

wahimbaga yikombo yitali.

15 Ne mitego yono wawatejile wayawo,

wakwigwiza kanyhi mumo weneco.

16 No wubi wawo, wowawuyila weneco du,

kanyhi wokwitamisa weneco no wukali wawo.

17 Nane nomukumya Muwaha Mulungu,

ku soko yo wuswanu wakwe,

kanyhi nokwimba zo lukumyo lwakwe Muwaha Mulungu,

u Mulungu Mono Yalawiliciye.
