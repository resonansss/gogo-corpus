Zabuli 22

Cililo co wusungu no lwimbo lwo lukumyo

1 Mulungu wangu, Mulungu wangu!

So cici wukundeka?

Nhawule wuli kutali ku wunhaze,

wuli kutali ne zinghani ze cililo cangu?

2 Mulungu wangu!

Mu misi nghukulilila ninga hamba si wukuhuliciza,

na ku cilo nako nhavivyo, ninga hamba singhutuga wucehe yaye.

3 Hamba nhavivyo, agwe welile;

mono wanhu wa Isilayeli wakukukumya.

4 Ne walezi wetu wali wakwitumbiye gwegwe,

wali wakwitumbiye, nagwe wukawaponya.

5 Wakulilila lono wali wali mu zimbali, wakaponywa,

wakwitumbila, hamba si wawona cevu.

6 Ninga ane nili ng'onyo du,

hamba si nili munhu yaye,

nghubedegalwa no kuhoswa ne wanhu wose.

7 Wose wono wakumbona, wakunzwajiza du,

wakufyonza no kusinjisa mitwe yawo,

8 Wakutya, “Alu, si wali wumwitumbiye Muwaha Mulungu,

Nhawule lu si yakukuponya?

Hono Mulungu yawe yendezwe nagwe, ale yakuponye!”

9 Ninga agwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

yo gwegwe mono wandavyaniza mu'nda ya yaya,

no kundima lono nali nghali kongha.

10 Nghwitumbiye gwegwe ku wulawa wulelwe wangu,

ku wulawilila lono nalelelwa,

gwegwe du yo wuli Mulungu wangu.

11 Wulece kuwa wuli kutali nane,

hambi wugaye nha wasejelela,

holeka weze wuli behi,

hambi nha sina mono konhaza.

12 Walugu wenji wamhilime, wenha zinghambaku,

wambaninyhize, wenha zinghambaku zinghali za Bashani!

13 Wakwahama i milomo yawo nha simba,

wewikanize ho wanhowanje caka caka, no kundemulanga.

14 Ane namalika nhe malenga gono gamwajice,

ne mafupa gangu gose gatenguka,

ne nhumbula yangu yeza nha kotya nha yono yenjeduce.

15 Mumelo wangu wanyala nhe lizaye,

no lulimilwangu lukwinamatila mu zinzagasa zangu,

wandeka fwiye mu manghundi.

16 Wihangulize wa wetuma zo wavu, wumhilime,

wamhilime kose kose kotya litewa lya mabwa,

wanhobola miwoko ne magulu.

17 Alu nasigala mafupa gose du,

ne walugu wangu wananjile akuno wakusimanga.

18 Wakugawana vivwalo vyangu mu nzila yo ko kutowa kula.

19 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu,

wulece kuwa kutali nane.

Agwe go mutazi wangu, wiyohaje wunhaze.

20 Wumhonye no mumaje!

Wumhonye wumi wangu ku wulawa mu mabwa gaga!

21 Wumhonye mwi'lomo lye simba,

wumhonye ku wulawa mu mahembe ge mbogo.

22 Nane nowanziza wandugu zangu ze metumo gako,

nokulumbila muciligati co wihangulize wawo.

23 “Ale, mumupelaje lukumyo,

anye mo wetumami wo Muwaha Mulungu!

Nanye mo lulelo lwa Yakobo,

mumutogozaje Mulungu,

nanye mo wanhu wose wa Isilayeli, mumuhembaje.

24 Hambi mwene nha si kawabedegalaga waciwa mu wusocele wawo,

hamba sikagabedegalaga i magazo gawo,

si kalemaga kuwahuliciza yaye,

langa kawapitulaga lono wakumulomba wutazi.”

25 Gwe go Mulungu, kumhelaga wusume wo kukukumya,

hagati yo wihangulize wuwaha we wanhu,

nane nofisiliza wilahize wangu hali wono wakogopile.

26 I waciwa wolya sunga wegute,

nawo wono wakumulondola u Muwaha Mulungu, womukumya.

Ale, wamoteswe cibitilila!

27 Ne yinze yose yokumbucila,

no kumuwuyila Muwaha Mulungu,

ne zimhuga zose ze makungugo, zomuhemba.

28 Hambi u Muwaha Mulungu nha Mutemi,

nayo yagatemile makungugo gose.

29 Ne wanhu wedunhumuzi wose,

womuhemba mweneco du,

na wose wono si wakusuma kwiwumifya,

hamba wahwele, womuhemba mweneco du.

30 Ne zindelo zono zikuza, zomwitumamila,

ne wanhu nawo, woziganula zinghani zo Muwaha Mulungu,

kuli zindelo zono zikuza,

31 Sunga ne wanhu wono wakali nalelwa,

nawo wolololonjelwa nhavi,

“U Muwaha Mulungu yo yawaponya wanhu wakwe!”
