Hoseya 1

1 Ayi yo nghani yono Muwaha Mulungu yamulonjela Hoseya, Mwana wa Beyeli, mu vilo vya Uziya, na Yotamu, na Ahazi na Hezekiya, lono wali watemi wa Yuda, na Yelobowamu mwana wa Yehowashi yali mutemi wa Isilayeli.

Mucekulu wa Hoseya ne wanagwe

2 Baho lulongozi lono Muwaha Mulungu yalonga ne Waisilayeli mu nzila ya Hoseya, Muwaha Mulungu yakamutya Hoseya, “Wubite wukatole, mucekulu musenyha, sunga ne wanagwe nawo wowa wasenyha nha vyono yenha nyina yawo. Nhavivyo ne wanhu wangu Waisilayeli, nawo wakondya kundeka, alu waweza wasenyha.”
3 Holeka Hoseya yakawa kutola mucekulu mono yacemwaga Gomeli, u mwana wa Dibulayimu. Yali yeza kulelwa mwana wawo mulongozi we cilume,
4 Muwaha Mulungu yakamutya Hoseya yamucemele litagwa lyakwe Yezeleli, ku soko cikali cipango cidodo du, nomulipiciza mutemi wa Isilayeli, so wuwulaji wono kukuye Yehu yetuma kuli Yezeleli, aluno nane noludumya lulelo lwa Yehu.
5 Na mu cipango cico, nolovihodanga vilungo vyo wulugu vye wasilikale we Waisilayeli mwi'lolo lya Yezeleli.
6 Gomeli yakalela mwana we kejete wa cicekulu. Muwaha Mulungu yakamutya Hoseya “Mucemelaje Loluhama (nhunizo yayo, ‘Mono si yakuwonelwa lusungu’) ku soko sinolajila wendo kuli Waisilayeli, hamba kuwanenela.
7 Langa kuli wanhu wa Yuda, nolajila wendo. Ane no Muwaha Mulungu wawo, nowaponya wene, ninga sinozinoza zizo mu nzila yo wulugu wo mumaje, hamba wo wupinde ne zisonga, hamba we zifwalasi ne wapanda fwalasi.”
8 Baho Gomeli yali yeza kumulesa mwanagwe we cicekulu. Yakadinghula 'nda kanyhi, yakatuga mwana yunji wa cilume.
9 Muwaha Mulungu yakamutya Hoseya, “Mucemelaje litagwa lyakwe ‘Loami’ (Nhunizo yayo, ‘Si wanhu wangu.’) Ku soko wanhu wa Isilayeli, si wanhu wangu, nane si nili Mulungu wawo.”

Isilayeli wologomolwa

10 Ninga i wanhu wa Isilayeli wolowa wenji liwalo lyawo nho musawawa wa mwi'lenga, wono siwusumikaga ku wupimwe hamba ku wuwalwe. Aluno Mulungu yakuwatya, anye si muli wanhu wangu, ninga lizuwa likuza lyono mwene kolowatya, “Anye muli wana wo Mulungu mumi!”
11 I wanhu wa Yuda ne wanhu wa Isilayeli, wolokwilunza hamonga. Nawo wolokwihagulila weneco mulongola, na kanyhi, wolokonjelezeka no kumoteswa mu yisi yawo. Heya, lizuwa lya Yezeleli lyolowa yizuwa yiwaha muno!


Hoseya 2

1 Holeka muwacemaje wandugu zenyu Waisilayeli “Ami” (Nhunizo yayo, “Wanhu wangu.”) Ne walumbu zenyu muwacemaje “Luhama.” (Nhunizo yayo, “Mono yawonelwa lusungu.”)

Wu wula wihuwilwa wa Gomeli, no wu wula wihuwilwa wa Isilayeli

2 Wanangu, mumunene nyina yenyu hamba yiwe mwene, yaweza si mugolece wangu, nane neza si nili mulume wakwe. Mumunenaje yalece zinghani zakwe zo wusenyha, no wumalaya wakwe.
3 Na hono si yakuleka, nomuwopoza vivwalo vyakwe yekale cipwi, yawe nha vyono yali mwi'zuwa lyono yalelelwa. Nane nomunoza yawe nhe yisi nghavu ne ye nyika, nayo kolofwa ne nyhilu.
4 Nane hamba si nilalajila lusungu kuli wanagwe,
5 hambi wene nha wana wo mucekulu malaya, mono yasina hamba cevu. Hambi nha yalonga mweneco yakatya, “Nane nobita kuli wono wanyendile wono wakumhela ndigwa ne malenga, no wusinga, no wuhamba, hamonga ne mafuta ne divayi.”

6 Holeka ku soko yiyo, nomuwicila ligomawakali lye mamivya no kuzenga lijinji ho liyidinde nzila yakwe, yalece kuwuwona wukolo wakwe.
7 Nayo kowamanyhilila wendece wakwe, ninga hamba si kowatuga yaye. Kowalondola, ninga hamba si kowawona. Hanyuma kotya, “Alu neza kuwuya kuli mulume wangu u mulongozi, hambi u wuwe wangu wulongozi, nha wali wuwe wuswanu muno, si nha wuwe wono nina nawo aluno.”
8 Hamba si kosuma kumanya vyono, ane yonene mono namupela wuhemba, ne divayi, ne mafuta, ne zifweza zose, hamonga ne zahabu, vyono yetumamilaga mu wuhemba Baali.
9 Holeka ku soko ya zizo, mu cipango ce nhwanga, nolowusa vipegwa vyangu vyo wuhemba ne divayi. Na kanyhi, nolosola myenda yangu yono namupela ho yavwale.
10 Nolomuwopoza myenda yakwe ha mwande ye wendece wakwe, yekale cipwi, hamba hasina yunji mono yalasuma kumuponya ku wulawa mu vilungo vyangu.
11 Noloyidumya nyemo yakwe yose ye zisikukuu, ne vilo vyakwe vye ndawo yo mwezi ne zi Sabato, hamonga no wihangulize wawo wose we mikung'ano ye cidini.
12 Noloyihodanga mizabibu yakwe ne miganhi, yono yakutya wendece wakwe wamupela ho yawetumamile. Nolopitula migunda yakwe ye mizabibu, ne migunda ye mibici yono yilelaga mikalo, yiwe mbago, ne vilimu vya mu mbago vyoloyihodanga.
13 Nolomulipiciza ku soko ye cipango cono yakasemwa 'ne, lono yafusaga wuvumba kuli Baali, no kuvwala wihambo wo wuguzi wukamu, no kuwatyatya wendece wakwe. Vyo yakutya Muwaha Mulungu.

U wendo wo Muwaha Mulungu kuli wanhu wakwe

14 Holeka nomugomola sunga ku nyika kanyhi, na ku wulawa kuko, nolomugomola mu nzila yo kulonga nayo nghani zo wendo.
15 Na ku wulawa kuko, nomupela kanyhi migunda ye mizabibu yono yali yena, no kulinoza lilolo lyo wugazwe, liwe mulango wo witumbilo. Nayo kohulika 'ne, nha vyono yahulikaga mu vilo vyo wudodo wakwe, lono yaziza ku wulawa Misili.
16 Kanyhi mwi'zuwa lilyo kolonyhema “Ishi.” Nhunizo yayo, “Mulume wangu.” Hamba sikolonyhema kanyhi “Baali.” Nhunizo yayo “Mbuya yangu.”
17 Nane nolowusiza lukulu matagwa ge zi Baali, si nolomuleka kanyhi yagatambule matagwa gawo.

18 Mu vilo vivyo, nolonoza cilagane ne vilimu vyose vya mu mbago ne zindeje, ho vilece kuleta wugaye muli wanhu wangu. Nane nolowusa vitwalo vyose vyo wulugu mu'si, mimaje yose no wupinde. Nolowaleka wanhu wangu wekale wapolile.

19 Gwe Isilayeli,

ane nolokunoza wuwe mugolece wangu,

Nolowa nili wa mbeka no wihuwilwa,

Nolokulajila wendo wuwaha no lusungu.

20 Nane nolowukamisa wilahize wangu,

no kukunoza agwe wuwe wuli wangu,

nagwe kolomanya 'ne,

vyono yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.

21 Mu vilo vivyo nolowupitula wulombe we

wanhu wangu Isilayeli.

22 Nolotonyeza mvula mu'si,

nayo yisi yololela wuhemba,

ne zizabibu hamonga na zeyituni.

23 Nolowasikamya wanhu wangu mu'si,

no kuwanoza wamoteswe.

Nane nolomuwonela lusungu

mono si yakuwonelwa lusungu,

na kuli wawo wono wacemwaga si wanhu wangu,

nolotya, “Anye muli wanhu wangu,”

nawo wolomhitula watye,

“Agwe yo gwegwe Mulungu wetu.”


Hoseya 3

Hoseya no mucekulu musenyha

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakanyitya, “Ale, wubite kanyhi wukalajile zo wendo wako, kuli mucekulu mono yakwituma zo wusenyha no mbuya yakwe. Yikunojeye wumwende nha vyono ane niwendile Waisilayeli, hamba yiwe wene nha wayipitucila milungu yinji, no kusola vilavywa vye zabibu zinghavu no kuvilavizya vihwani vye milungu.”
2 Holeka nane Hoseya, nghalavya fweza yikumi na yihano, hamonga na kilo yigana limonga na makumi mahano za shayili, nghawa kumugula mucekulu yuyo.
3 Hanyuma nghamulonjela nghamutya vyono, yimunojeye yanindile sunga vilo vipulute, ninga yelece kwituma zo wumalaya wakwe, hamba zo wusenyha wakwe lono yali mu wulindile. Na mu cipango cico, nane nowa nimulindiye.
4 Hambi Waisilayeli, nha wokwikala vilo vinji wasina mutemi hamba walongola, wowula hamba vilavywa, hamba masumbili ge mabwe maswanu, wowula vihwani, hamba hwani bwete yo kutowela cisangu.
5 Ninga cipango cikuza cono Waisilayeli wolomuwuyila kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo, no lulelo lwa Dawudi mutemi wawo. Nawo wolomogopa Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo, no kubocela vipegwa vyakwe viswanu.


Hoseya 4

Muwaha Mulungu yakuwahosa Waisilayeli

1 Muwaha Mulungu, yena nghani yo kuwahosa wanhu wono wekaye mu'si ayi. Muhulicize, Isilayeli, muli zizo zono yakulonga, “Kweza kusina wihuwilwa hamba lusungu mu'si, ne wanhu hamba si wakumanya 'ne vyono nili Mulungu.
2 Wakunoza cilagane no kucibena, kanyhi wakulamba, no kuwulaga, wakuhiza no kwituma zo wusenyha. Wubena wulajilize, nawo wukonjezeka, na ko wuli wuwulaji ako na ako.
3 Holeka yisi yonyala, sunga ne vinhu vyose vyono vicindice mumo, vyonyala. Ne vilimu vyose vya mu mbago, hamonga ne zindeje, sunga ne zisomba zose, vyose vyofwa.”

Muwaha Mulungu yakuwahosa wakohani

4 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Yunji yalece kuwahosa i wanhu hamba kuwatacila. Hambi wung'ung'ulice wangu nha wuli kuli'nye nyo wakohani.
5 Vyono cilo na misi, anye mwikalaga mukuhodanga du, sunga ne wawizi nawo si wakwituma ziswanu. Nane nomongomeza u nyina yako, 'gwe, Isilayeli.
6 Wanhu wangu wongomezwa ku soko vyono si wamanyile'ne. Nanye mo wakohani si mukusaka mumanye 'ne, kanyhi mukagalema ne mafundo gangu. Holeka nane nghumulemenyi nanye, hamba si nowavumila ne wana wenyu wawe wakohani wangu.
7 Na mu nghango yono mukucila kuwa wenji, yo nghango yiyo du kanyhi, yono ucila kusutizya 'ne. Holeka nane nolipitula lilumbo lyenyu liwe cevu.
8 Hambi nha mukugolipila mu wubi we wanhu wangu. Na ku soko yiyo holeka mukuwona viswanu wene wajendelele kwituma zo wubi.
9 Ninga mulanjilile! Nanye motuga wulipicizwe wuwo du wono wanhu wolipicizizwa. Nomulipicizenyi ho mulipe mabi gose gono mukwituma.
10 Molya lifungu lyenyu lye vilavywa, ninga hamba simokwiguta ng'o. Nanye mohemba milungu yono yina lulelo, ninga hamba simotuga wana ng'o. Ku soko vyono mwakondya kundeka 'ne, no kuyihemba milungu yinji.”

Muwaha Mulungu yakuwulemeza wuhembi we vihwani

11 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Divayi yose, mhya ne huma, yikuhodanga mahala ge wanhu wangu!
12 Wakulomba wugubulilwe ku wulawa mu cihwani ce libici! Ne nghome yawo yo wuganga, yo yikuwalonjela zono wakuhila wene kuzimanya! Mbeka, wakondya kundeka 'ne. Wakondya kwiyilavya kuli milungu yinji, kotya mucekulu malaya.
13 Wakulavya makumbiko, mucanyha ye vizuli vye matunda, kanyhi wakufusa no wuvumba mucanyha ye matunda na ha masina ge mibici mitali. Vyono mibeho yayo yikwendeza muno! Ku soko yiyo, holeka wana wenyu we cicekulu, wekaye nha malaya, ne wakwe zenyu we cicekulu nawo wakusenyha.
14 Hamba nhavivyo, nane si nowongomeza i wana wenyu we cicekulu so zizo ng'o, hamba i wakwe zenyu we cicekulu, hambi nha nyenye nyo weneco mono mukujendana hamonga ne wasenyha, no kuhanga hamonga mu wulavizya vihwani makumbiko. Nha vyono mbazi yikutya, ‘Wanhu wakongomela ku soko yo wu wula kumanya kwawo.’
15 Ale, sunga na nyenye nyo Waisilayeli, si muli wehuwilwa kuli'ne, ninga wanhu wa Yuda walece kunoza nhavivi. Mulece kuhemba ku Giligali hamba Betaveni, hamba kwihuwiziza kuko mwi'tagwa lyo Mulungu mumi.
16 Wanhu wa Isilayeli wakamu, wehwanile nyumbu. Alu, nane ho nowadima nhawule nha ngholo, mwi'lolo?
17 I wanhu wa Efulayimu, wasumigwe ne vihwani. Holeka lece vyajililane kuko.
18 Kolo wakung'wa divayi nyinji, wakwendezwa no wusenyha wawo, nawo wendile cevu si nhe lilumbo.
19 Wolosolwa nha wakukwenghwelezwa ne mbeho, nawo wolokozwa cevu ku soko ye makumbiko gawo ge vihwani.”


Hoseya 5

1 “Ale, muhulicize zizi, anye mo wakuhani! Muhulicize nyo Waisilayeli! Muteje makutu genyu anye nyo we nyumba yo mutemi! Hambi nha munojeye nyenye mulavye ning'aniza ye mbeka. Ninga alu, wuling'anizwe womuwuyilenyi nyenye nyo weneco. Hambi nha mweza muli mutego ku Mizipa, no lwavu lono lugoloywe mwi'tunda lya Taboli.
2 Ne yikombo yitali mwi'kaya lya Akasiya, nane nomulipicizenyi nyenye wose.
3 Hambi nha niyimanyile Isilayeli vyono yinha, holeka si yosuma kwihundika yaye hali'ne. Hambi nha yeza si nyihuwilwa yaye, sunga ne wanhu wayo hamba si wanojeye wahembe nene.”

Hoseya yakulavya suguzi kuli wukumbika vihwani

4 Wubi wono wanhu wetumile, wukuwemila ho walece kumuwuyila Mulungu wawo. Hambi wukumbika vihwani, nha wuwebate muno muno, holeka si wamumanyile no Muwaha Mulungu yaye.
5 No wiwoni we Waisilayeli, wukuhomelela muli wene. No wubi wawo wukuwakobaza no kuwagwisa, ne wanhu wa Yuda wakugwizana hamonga nawo.
6 Nawo wakusola zingholo zawo ne zing'ombe, ho wamulavizye Muwaha Mulungu likumbiko, nazo hamba si zikuwanoza ho wawe waswanu ng'o. Hamba si wosuma kumuwona Mulungu, hambi Mulungu nha yakondya kuwaleka wene.
7 Vyono wene wali si wehuwilwa hali Muwaha Mulungu, sunga ne wana wawo wali si wakwe yaye. Holeka nalu wene hamonga ne'si yawo, wokongomezwa musunho.

Wulugu wa Isilayeli na Yuda

8 Mutowe ndulele yo wulugu mu Gibeya! Ne zifuluti mu Lama! Nanye kuko ku Betaveni, mucenje so wulugu! Nanye mo wanhu wa Benyamini, mwinjile mu wihomi!
9 Lizuwa lyo wulipicizwe likuza, na Isilayeli yokongomezwa. Anye nyo Waisilayeli, zizi mbeka zokwijela!
10 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Ane ngholigwe yikuwo ku soko wawaha wa Yuda, wenjila mu mipaka ya Isilayeli, no kuyihiza yisi yawo.” Holeka nane nowetila likuwo lyangu kotya malenga.
11 Isilayeli yakugaya so wiwonelwe, hambi nha yakondya kwajiliza yisi yono yali ling'ani yakwe, vyono mwene yabita kulondola wutazi, kuli wono si wosuma kumupela wutazi wo wose.
12 Holeka nane noleta wuhodanje kuli Isilayeli, no wongomezwe muli wanhu wa Yuda.

13 “Nayo Isilayeli yali yeza kuwona vyono yatamwa, nayo Yuda yali yeza kuziwona zinghomele zakwe, hodu Isilayeli yakawa kubita ku yisi ya Siliya kulondola wutazi kuli wutemi wuwaha, ninga hamba nhavivyo, nayo hamba si kosuma kuwaponya, hamba kuzinoja zinghomele zawo.
14 Nane nowahungucila wanhu wa Isilayeli na Yuda kotya simba. Nowahawanga no kuwaleka baho. Na lono nowa nakondya kuwasola, hasina munhu yunji kanyhi mono kusuma kuwaponya.

15 “Nane nowaleka wanhu wangu wagaye mu nghango yo wubi wawo, sunga waze wanondole 'ne. Na mumo lunji mu wugaye wawo, mo wolosuma kunondola wahidimbaye sunga wambone.”


Hoseya 6

Wunene wo wukoci, we wanhu

1 Wanhu wakutya, “Alenyi ciwuye kuli Muwaha Mulungu! Mwene yacitamisa yakondya, ninga yamanyile vyono kociponya! Yacipela zinghomele, ninga kociponya zinghomele zetu.
2 Na mu vilo vyejete hamba vidatu, kocinula, nase cokwikala womi hali mwene.
3 Alenyi cimumanye Muwaha Mulungu. Mwene kokuza kuli'se lono wela hakali mitondo muno, nayo kokuza kuli'se kotya mvula ye mhinga yono yidedewazaga yisi.”
4 Ninga Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Isilayeli na Yuda, alo nghanici yono nomutendelenyi? Wendo wenyu kuli'ne wawuka musunho nha vyono nghungulyosi lye hakali mitondo, kotya buhe, yono siyitinyaga kunyala.
5 Ayi yo soko yono yaleka nghamutumilenyi wawizi wangu, hamonga no luganuzi lo wuling'anizwe, no wongomezwe kuli'nye. Na yono nghusaka kuli'nye yoyi.
6 Ane nghusaka wendo wenyu we mbeka kuli'ne, hamba si nghusaka makumbiko genyu ge zimitugo. Kanyhi ane nghuwona yili ciba hono wanhu wangu wamanye 'ne si nho kundavizya vilavywa vinyanywe.

7 “Ninga wene weza nha Adamu, wacibena cilagane cono calagana nane.
8 Gileyadi, lye 'kaya lyono limemile wanhu wabi ne wawulaji.
9 Ne wakohani wenha litewa lye wahizi wono watejeye munhu wamwibate. Wakwituma zo wuwulaji, sunga ko ku hanhu hono helile ku Shekemu. Kanyhi wakwituma wubi wuwo ne nyono du.
10 Kanyhi niyiwene nghani 'mbi yo wogofye muli Isilayeli, wanhu wangu wetajila mu wavu weneco, so wuhemba vihwani.

11 “Nanye kuli'nye anye nyo wanhu wa Yuda, nakondya kuwika lipango lyo kumulipicizenyi, ku soko ya zizo zono mukwituma.


Hoseya 7

1 “Mu madaha gono nghuhila kuwaponya wanhu wangu Isilayeli no kuwanoza wamoteswe kanyhi, ninga zono nghusuma kuwona muli wene, wo wavu wawo no wubi wono wakwituma. Wakwiyihiziza weneco wose, wakubena mu zikaya no kuhiza, wakuwahiziza wanhu mu zinzila.
2 Hamba siwakwihwesa mu zinhumbula zawo, vyono nowukumbucila wubi wuwu wose, no wubi wawo wuwapilime, hamba si wihundice ho ndece kusuma kuwuwona.”

Wilumbanize wo kuyinozeza zimbi kaya yo wutemi

3 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Wanhu wamukonga mutemi ne wawaha wakwe, ko wuhile wawo wo wubi.
4 Wene wose wakoci, kanyhi hamba si wehuwilwa. Ne likuwo lyawo, likwaka nha moto mu mafigwa, wono si mufwanghane, sunga muteleci kolo yakondya kuwutiwa wusaje we cisase, na kolo wakondya kutumba, ho wutelekwe.
5 Mwi'zuwa lye sikukuu yo wukumbucile wo wuwikwa mwi'goda mutemi, wakamunoza mutemi hamonga ne wawaha wakwe, wakang'wa muno divayi sunga wakagala.
6 Na mbeka wuhile wawo wubi, wukoleye nha moto mu mafigwa. Ne likuwo lyawo lifunghaga, na ko muno ku cilo, na kolo wela, lyakaga nha malambi ga moto.

7 “Mu moto we likuwo lyawo wakawawulaga walongola wawo. Ne watemi wawo wakawa kuwulagwa yumonga yumonga, ninga hasina hamba monga mono yakulomba wutazi kuli'ne.”

Isilayeli ne makungugo

8 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Waisilayeli weza kotya mukate wono si mupitulwe. Wene wetumbiye makungugo gono gawapilime.
9 Hamba si wakumanya vyono witumbilo wawo wuwo wa muli wajenzi, wakondya kuwahiziza vilungo vyawo. Na vyono vilo vyawo viwale, ninga wene hamba si wakumanya yaye.
10 No wiwoni we Waisilayeli wukuwahomelela weneco, ninga muli zizo zose zono zejela, hamba siwasuma kumbuyila ane no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wawo.
11 Isilayeli yakuyumbayumba ako na ako kotya nziwa ndele, wanhu wakwe ha lulongozi wakasaka wutazi kuli Misili, na aluno kanyhi wakumanyhila ku Siliya.
12 Ninga ane notandaza lwavu ho niwagwize kotya ndeje za mucanyha. Kanyhi nowalipiciza ku soko yo wubi wono wetumile.

13 “Woyi he nye! Hambi nha wandeka 'ne, kanyhi wambedegala. Ninga walekaje, wokongomezwa du. Nali nyendile niwaponye, ninga wuhembi wono wakuhemba 'ne, wo wuhembi wo wulambi.
14 Hambi nha si wakusuma kulomba kuli 'ne kwe nhumbula yo winenenyhezi yaye, langa wakwigwisa no kwigalagaza hasi nha vyono wanozaga wono si wamumanyile Mulungu. Na lono wakulomba so wuhemba ne divayi, wakwisalanga-salanga mu miwili no kwipela nghomele nha wono si wamumanyile Mulungu. Nhondo hamba watye, wo wubedegazici waco wuwu.
15 Ninga hamba yiwe yo nene mono nawafunda, nghawakamya ne vilungo, hamba nhavivyo wene vyo wakunyihweseza zimbi du.
16 Vyo wakali du wakuwuya ne nyuma wakundeka, wakumutyatya mulungu wo wulambi, mono yasina vilungo. Waweza si wehuwilwa hamba hadodo, waweza nha wupinde wono si wukwiliye cinji. Hambi walongola wawo, nha wakulonga ko wivuni, ninga walekaje, wolofwa cifo cibi muno, sunga ne Wamisili wolowaseka.”


Hoseya 8

Mulungu yakuwalemeza Waisilayeli walece kuhemba vihwani

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Towe ndulele lizi lye citanjilo! Hambi walugu nha wakuzumha hasi, nha numba, mu'si yangu! Wanhu wangu wabena icilagane cono nalagana nawo, wabedegala no wulajilize wangu.
2 Hamba wawe wakunyhema no kunyitya ane nili Mulungu wawo, no kwiyitya wene wanhu wangu, na vyono wamanyile 'ne,
3 ninga wazilema ziswanu. Na ku soko ya zizo, i walugu wawo wowatyatilila.

4 “Wanhu wangu wakwihagulila watemi, ninga wakwituma zizi wali wene. Wakwiwicila wawaha hamba kwilumbaniza nane ng'o, wakusola fweza zawo na zahabu, wawa kunoza vihwani so wongomele wawo weneco.
5 Ane si nghucisacila lukulu cihwani ce ndama ye zahabu, yono wakuhemba wanhu we likaya lya Samaliya. Ngholigwe ne likuwo muno muno ku soko yawo. Alu mwene wojendelela sunga nili ho waze walece wuhemba vihwani vyawo?
6 No munhu munghula mu Waisilayeli, yo munoza cihwani. Ninga yiyo ndama ye zahabu, yono wakuyihemba kuko ku Samaliya, yolobenanjizwa-benanjizwa tici-tici.
7 Ku soko vyono wakuhadika mbeho, holeka wolodudula mukukunzo! Migunda yo wuhemba siyolopisa hamba siyolosuma kulavya ndudula yo yose. Na hamba yize yilavye, iwajenzi wo woloyilya wayimale.
8 Waisilayeli weza wenha makungugo ganji, wono si wakwiliye cinji, weza wenha ciya cono cisakaye.
9 Wena nhumbula nghamu nha ye nyhenje. Waisilayeli wakujendela mu zinzila zawo weneco. Wakondya kubita kulondola wutazi kuli Wasiliya, no kugalipa i makungugo ganji ho gawadime kuli walugu.
10 Holeka luno ane nowakonghola hamonga, ho niwalipicize. Nawo wokwinyongola ko wusungu musunho, lono mutemi wa Siliya yakuwatendela ko wukali.

11 “Na mu nghango yono Waisilayeli wasuma kuzenga vikumbicilo vinji muno, yo nghango yiyo du kanyhi, yo wakacila kuhonjeleza i hanhu ho kwitumila zo wubi!
12 Hambi ane nha nawandicila wanhu mafundo gangu menji sigakuwala, ninga wene wakagalema no kugawona nha cinhu cipya, kanyhi cijenzi.
13 Wakundavivya makumbiko ge zinyama, no kulya weneco kanyhi zinyama zizo ze makumbiko. Ninga ane no Muwaha Mulungu singhwendezwa nawo. Naluno nowukumbucila wubi wawo, no kuwalipiciziza mu wubi wawo, nowagomola kanyhi sunga ko ku Misili!
14 I Waisilayeli, wazenga makaya go wutemi, ninga wamusemwa u Muwumbi wawo. Nawo wanhu wa Yuda, wazenga makaya gono gena magomawakali. Ninga nane notegula moto wono woganyanya no kugagwisa hasi makaya go wutemi hamonga ne makaya gawo.”


Hoseya 9

Hoseya yakulonga zo wulipicizwe we Waisilayeli

1 Nye nyo Waisilayeli, mulece kusangalalila zi sikukuu zenyu nhe makungugo ganji, mwapituka mwamuleka Mulungu wenyu, mweza si muli wehuwilwa kanyhi kuli Mwene. Hambi nha mweyiguza nyo weneco mu yisi yose kuli mulungu Baali kotya muli wasenyha, mukenda zo wuhemba wono mwehwesa vyono go malipo genyu!
2 Ninga mumanye vyono mu vilo vicefu du mowa musina wuhemba wono wukondizye, hamba mafuta ge zeyituni, kanyhi howa hasina hamba divayi bwete.
3 Ne Waisilayeli siwokwikala mu'si yo Muwaha Mulungu, langa wowuya ku Misili, nawo wowa kulya ndigwa yono si swanu kuko ku Ashuli.
4 Hamba siwomulavizya Muwaha Mulungu vilavywa vye divayi mumo mu zi'si zo wujenzi. Ne zindigwa zawo zolomwavufya munhu wo wose mono kolozilya, kotya nhe ndigwa ye nghunguza yo wumala lifwa. Nayo yokwitumamilwa so wigusha weneco du. Ninga hamba cinji si cokwila kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu mu Nyumba yakwe yo wuhembelo.
5 Ninga cipango cilawa kufika ce sikukuu yo kumulavizya cogopo Muwaha Mulungu, wolonoza nhawule?
6 Wuhodanjice kolo wakondya kuza, ne wanhu wakwiyagala, wabite ku Siliya. Ne Wamisili wolowakonghola hamonga wazikwe ku Memufisi! Ne viwutwa vyawo vye fweza, hamonga ne hanhu hono zikaya zawo zali nzenje ha mwamulizo, holocindika mabalwe ne mamivya.

7 Ninga cipango co wulipicizwe cakondya kuza, icipango cono wanhu wolozituga zono ziwanojeye. Kolo zizi zikwijela, Isilayeli wolozimanya, nawo wololonga watye, “Muwizi ayu yapuwile! Munhu ayu mono yakutongwa no Mulungu yena lukwale.” Anye nyo wanhu, musulile 'ne ku soko yo winji wo wubi wenyu.
8 Ninga Mulungu yanhuma ane nha muwizi, ho niwakaje wanhu wakwe Waisilayeli. Ninga ho hose hono nghubita, anye mukujela kunhega kotya ndeje. Sunga na mu Nyumba yo Mulungu yo wuhembelo, i wanhu wayo, wo walugu wo muwizi.
9 Weyihodanga muno ku soko yo wubi wono wetuma lono wali wali ku Gibeya. Ninga Mulungu kowukumbucila wubi wawo, nayo kowalipiciza ku soko yo wubi wawo.

U wubi we Waisilayeli, hamonga ne cambuzi cayo

10 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Ha mwamulizo lono namwaganila Isilayeli, yikawa nha mbwene zabibu zono zikuwilucila mu nyika. Na lono nawawonela walezi wawo ha mwamulizo, nali nha mbwene mikalo yo mukuyu, yono yipiliye mu cipango cayo. Ninga wali weza kuza kwi'tunda lya Peyoli, wakawa kwanza kumuhemba Baali. Ninga kanyhi hadodo bwete, wakawa kuyisemuna yiyo milungu yono wali wayendile.
11 Nalo lukumyo lwa Isilayeli lologuluka kotya ndeje, na kolowa kusina wana wanji wono wololelwa kuli wene, kolowa kusina wacekulu wono wolodinghula 'nda, hamba wana wono wolodinghulwa 'nda.
12 Hamba waze watuje wana, ane nolowawulaga. Sinolomuleka hamba yunji yekale mumi. Na lono niwe nawaleka wanhu awa, mbeka nghani 'mbi yolowagana.”
13 Gwe Muwaha Mulungu, nghusuma kuwawona wana wawo vyono wakusepwa no kuwulagwa.
14 Alu nghulombe cinhuci cono wuwatendele wanhu wawa? Wanozaje wacekulu zawo wawe wagumba ne matombo gawo galece kulavya mele!

Wuling'anize wo Mulungu kuli Isilayeli

15 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Metumo gawo gose mabi, ganziza ku Giligali. Baho nane ho nanziza kuwasula. Na ku soko yo wubi wono wetuma, nane nowawusa mu'si yangu. Hamba sinowenda kanyhi yaye. Hambi nha sunga ne walongola wawo wose, wakondya kumbedegala.
16 Waisilayeli wehwana mubici wono wunyalile milela yayo, wono si wukulela mikalo kanyhi. Holeka wowa wasina wana, na hono wawatuje, ane nowawulaga i wana wono wendigwe muno nawo.”

U muwizi yakulonga ze Waisilayeli

17 Mulungu mono ane nghumwitumamila, kowalema wanhu wakwe, vyono siwakumuhulika mwene. Nawo wowa wanhu wono wowa kupilima-pilima mu makungugo.


Hoseya 10

1 Waisilayeli wali wenha kotya nha muzabibu wono wulelile zabibu nyinji muno. Mu nghango yo wumoteswe wawo, yikawa yo nghango yo wonjeleze wo kuzenga vikumbicilo vinji. Na mu nghango yo wulezi we yisi yawo vyono wali winha, ale, yo nghango yo wuswanu we masumbili ge mabwe maswanu gono wanoza, gono wahembaga.
2 Ne wanhu wono nhumbula zawo nghoci, alu wogaya so wubi wawo. Mulungu kovibenanga vikumbicilo vyawo, no kugahodanga ne masumbili gawo.
3 Wanhu wawo siwotinya kutya, “Alu cisina mutemi, ku soko vyono sicikumogopa Muwaha Mulungu. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, u mutemi kocitumilaci?”
4 Wakulonga nghani zono zisina nhunizo, no kwihuwiza wihuwizo wo wulambi, no kwituma nghani zono zisina cambuzi. Ne ling'ani, yeza si ling'ani, yikuwiluka kotya mabalwe gono gena sumu mu mugunda wono wakondya kulimwa.

5 Ne wanhu wono wekaye mwi'kaya lya Samaliya, wotuga woga no kulila so wajilize we ndama ye zahabu ya ku Beteli. Wene hamonga ne wakohani wono wacitumamilaga cihwani cico, wolocililila muno. Wololila walaza kuwa wawusizwa nhojo yawo yo lukumyo.
6 Ne cihwani cico ce ndama, colosolwa sunga ko ku Ashuli, ciwe kodi kuli wutemi wa Ashuli. Nayo Isilayeli yolokolwa cevu no kuwona soni so nghani yayo yeneco.
7 No mutemi wayo kolosolwa kotya lifulo mucanyha ye malenga.
8 Na mucanyha ye cigongo ca Aveni, hono Waisilayeli wakuhemba vihwani, naho holokongomezwa. Mamivya ne mabalwe, vyolowiluka mu makumbicilo gawo. Ne wanhu wologalonjela matunda wawe kutya, “Mucihundikaje!” Ne vigongo wakutya, “Mucigubikaje!”

Muwaha Mulungu yakulonga zo wuling'anizi kuli Isilayeli

9 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Waisilayeli siwakuleka kuwituma wubi kuli'ne, ku wulawa mu cipango co wubi wawo wa ku Gibeya. Holeka wulugu wolowagana ku wulawa ku Gibeya.
10 Nowebata wanhu awa wanya wubi niwalipicize. Ne makungugo golokwihanguliza hamonga ho gehome nawo, wololipicizwa so wubi wawo vyono winji.

11 “Nayo Isilayeli yenha ndama yono yakondya kwijizwa, yono yendile kutowa wuhemba, ninga nghwihwesa vyono, ciba niyiwicile lubano mu singo yayo swanu, ho niyitonje yisume kwituma mulimo muwaha. Nomunoza Yuda yawe yo mukwega lisili, nayo Isilayeli yawe yo mutulanga malowanjila mu nyuma yo muyagwe.”
12 Nane nghutya, “Muyilime yisi mhya, so nyenye nyo weneco, muhadice ling'ani no kududula wendo no wulugutwe wono wolawa mu wuhembi wenyu wa kuli'ne. Alu co cipango cenyu co kumbuyila, ane no Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, nane nokuza nimumwajilenyi wulugutwe wangu.”
13 Ninga anye mwahadika wavu, no kududula nhwanga yayo. Alu mwakondya kulya mikalo yono yikulawilila mu wulambi wenyu. Ku soko vyono mwali mwitumbiye matuka genyu go wulugu, ne wasilikale wenyu vyono wenji.
14 Holeka wulugu wolowaziza i wanhu wenyu, ne magomawakali genyu golokongomezwa. Yolowa nhe vilo vyo mutemi Shalimani, vyono yalyongomeza likaya lya Betalibeli ko wulugu. Wanyina za munhu, hamonga ne wana wawo, wakatowa sunga wakafwa.
15 “Azi zo zilejela kuli'nye wanhu wa Beteli, ku soko yo wubi wenyu wono mwetuma. No wihomi wulawa kwanza, mutemi wa Isilayeli kolowulagwa.”


Hoseya 11

Wendo wo Muwaha Mulungu kuli wanhu wakwe wono wamulema

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya,

“Lono Isilayeli yali mudodo, nali nimwendile,

nghamucema mwanangu yawuce ku Misili.

2 Ninga nha vyono nacila kumucema,

nayo vyo yakacila kundeka no kutyatya zakwe.

I wanhu wangu wakawalavizya makumbiko Mabaali,

wakafusiza wuvumba ivihwani.

3 Ninga ane yonene mono nawafunda Isilayeli wajende.

Nawadinghula wanhu wangu mu mawoko gangu,

ninga wene hamba siwamanya vyono ane

nawaponya, no kuwadima.

4 Nane nghawakwega ko wuhile wangu na ko wendo wangu.

Nghawasola no kuwakamisa kwe vilungo vyangu.

Nghenama no kuwalisa.

5 “Ninga wene wakalema kumbuyila, holeka yiwanojeye wawuye ku Misili, nayo Mwashuli yo kowatema.
6 No wulugu wolokwinjila mu makaya gawo, no kugabenanga i malango ge likaya lyawo, wulugu wolowongomeza.”
7 Wene wakucila kuwuya ne nyuma. Ninga wololila ku soko yo lubano lono luli muli wene, hamba hasina munhu mono kololuwusa muli wene.

8 “Nosuma nhawule kukuleka 'gwe Isilayeli?

Nosuma wule kukutaga?

Hamba si nosumila lukulu kukongomeza,

nha vyono nayongomeza Adima.

Hamba kukunozeza nha vyono nayinoza Zeboyimu?

Nhumbula yangu siyonghombola ho nitume zizo!

Hambi wendo wangu kuli'gwe, nha wuwaha muno.

9 Holeka si nomulipicizizenyi mwi'kuwo lyangu ng'o.

Hamba sinoyongomeza Isilayeli kanyhi.

Hambi 'ne nha nili Mulungu, hamba si nili munhu yaye.

Ane yonene Mono Yelile, mono nili hamonga nanye.

Hamba sinomuzizenyi mwi'kuwo lyangu yaye,

ho nimongomezenyi.

10 “Wanhu wangu wolonhyatya kolo nghukoloma nha simba kuli walugu wawo. Wolonyiziza musunho ku wulawa ku lubavu lwa kumwezi.
11 Wolokuza ku wulawa Misili, wolokuza musunho nha ndeje, na ku wulawa ku Ashuli, wolokuza nha nziwa. Nane nolowagomola ku zikaya zawo kanyhi. Vyo nghutya ane no Muwaha Mulungu.”

Isilayeli na Yuda wakuhoswa

12 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Waisilayeli wamhilima hamonga no wulambi no wukonji. Ne wanhu wa Yuda nawo, wakali wakunema 'ne no Mulungu mwihuwilwa, Mono Yelile.


Hoseya 12

1 “Nghani zose zono Waisilayeli wakwituma, cilawa hakali mitondo, sunga cinyamihe, zisina cambuzi, kanyhi za wuhodanji du. Wukoci hamonga ne metumo go wu wula lusungu, gakonjelezeka muno muli wene. Wakulagana ne wanhu wa Ashuli no kusuluza hamonga ne Wamisili.”
2 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu, yena nghani yono yakuhila kuleta kuli wanhu wa Yuda, na kanyhi kowalipiciza Waisilayeli, ku soko ye metumo ge wanhu wayo. Mwene kolowalipa so wubi wawo wono wetuma.
3 U sogo yawo Yakobo yesumaniza no muwahawe Esawu, lono wose wejete wali wakali mu'nda ye nyinawo. Na lono Yakobo yali yeza wiluka, yakesumaniza no Mulungu.
4 Yakesumaniza na Malayika, yakasuma, yakalila no kulomba wulugutwe. Mulungu yakaza kuli sogo yetu Yakobo, no kulonga nayo lono yali yali ku Beteli.
5 Ayu yali yo Muwaha Mulungu we mihanga, Muwaha Mulungu lye 'tagwa lyono linojeye wanhu wamuhembe.
6 Holeka anye nyo lulelo lwa Yakobo, mwitumbilenyi Mulungu wenyu, kanyhi mumuwuyile mwene. Mweze ne cihuliko, kanyhi mweze muli ling'ani, akuno mumulindiye Mulungu ko wukangalizi, nhondo zose.

Nghani zinji zo wuling'anizi

7 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “I Waisilayeli, si wehuwilwa, wenhwanile Wakanani, wendile kuwahiziza wanhu wono wakuza kugula, mu nzila yo witumamila mizani yo wulambi.”
8 Akuno wakutya, “Ase cili wagoli, citujila sawo nyinji, cina lyuwa lyetu. Hamba hasina munhu mono kocihosa so wutuga wugoli wuwu mu nzila yo wupujila.”
9 Ninga 'ne yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu mono namulongozenyi ku wulawa Misili. Nomwikazenyi kanyhi mu mahema nha vyono mwali lono namuzizenyi mu nyika.

10 “Nalonga ne wawizi no kuwapela wugubulilwe winji, na mu nzila ye wawizi, nawakaga wanhu wangu.
11 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, ku Gileyadi, vyo wakali du wakuhemba vihwani, ninga wono wakuvihemba, wolofwa. Ne zindama zono wakuzilavya makumbiko mu Giligali, hamonga ne makumbicilo gawo, golowa masumbili ge mabwe mwi'hala.”

12 No mulezi wetu Yakobo, yatiziza ku Mesopotamiya, ho yatuje mugolece kuko. Yaketuma mulimo wo kudima zingholo kuli munhu yunji.
13 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakatuma muwizi yawaponye wanhu wa Isilayeli ku wulawa mu wuwanda ku Misili, ho yawadime.
14 Waisilayeli wamukoza Muwaha Mulungu yikuwo yiwaha, na yali yiwanojeye wufwe so masutya gawo. No Muwaha Mulungu kolowalipiciza so cevu cono wamuletela.


Hoseya 13

Wuling'anizi we ndumilizo kuli Isilayeli

1 Vipango vyono vyapuluta, lono wo mulango wa Efulayimu walongaga, ninga we milango yinji ya Isilayeli wogopa, wakawa wamulanjile Efulayimu. Ninga wanhu wetuma wubi so kumuhemba Baali, na ku soko yiyo, wofwa.
2 Sunga nalu wakucila kwituma wubi, wakwinozeza vihwani ho wavihembe. Wakwinozeza vihwani vya fweza, vinozwe ne miwoko ye wanhu kwe mahala gawo weneco. Nawo wakutya, “Ale muvilavizyaje makumbiko!” Alu wanhu ho wovinonela wule vihwani vivyo, i vihwani vyono vine futa ye zindama!
3 Holeka wanhu awa wokwajilila nhe nghungulyosi lya hakali mitondo, nhe buhe yono yikunyala musunho. Wowa kotya mawaje gono gapemheluswaga ne civulavumbi mu cuga. Kanyhi kotya lyosi lyono likulawa ha mafigwa.
4 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, mono namuwusenyi Misili. Musina Mulungu yunji langa nene du.” Ane yo nene du mono nili muponyi wenyu.
5 Nane nghamudimenyi mu yisi nghavu ya mu nyika.
6 Ninga lono mwali mweza kwinjila mu yisi swanu, yono mukulya no kwiguta, hodu imitima yenyu yikekumya no kusemwa 'ne.
7 Holeka ku soko yiyo, nane nomuhungucilenyi nha simba. Nolambalala hasi, nimutejezenyi nha suwi mu zinzila zenyu.
8 Nane nomuhungucilenyi nhe dubu lyono litajile wana wayo, kanyhi nomudemulanjenyi. Nomuhawanjenyi kotya simba, no kumudemulanjenyi vidodo vidodo kotya nimu ya mu mbago.

9 “Nane nomongomezenyi anye nyo Waisilayeli! Nayo nani mono kosuma kumutazenyi?
10 Mukalomba mutemi ne walongola, ninga woliponya nhawule likungugo?
11 Nane nghamupelelenyi watemi mwi'kuwo lyangu, ninga kanyhi niwawusiza mu wukali wangu.

12 “Wubi wa Isilayeli ne masutya gakwe, vyose vyandikwe no kuwikwa ciwutwa.
13 Isilayeli yena cilejeho co kwikala mumi. Ninga yo nghani yo wulele kuwihwesa havivi, hambi nha yenha mugalika mono yali mu cidaha co kulelwa, ninga kawa kulemelela mu'nda yo nyina.
14 Nane hamba si nowaponya wanhu we liwala lili, mu yinze ye lifwa, hamba kuwaponya mu vilungo vye lifwa. Agwe go lifwa, wulete lihuhuji lyako! Nagwe go yinze ye lifwa, wulete magazo gako! Nane hamba si nowawonela lusungu yaye wanhu wawa.
15 Nayo Isilayeli, hamba yawe yaficile nhe mabalwe, ane noleta mbeho ya kucilima kulawa mu nyika yono yili moto, nayo yonyaza mivwilo yawo ye malenga, hamonga ne masima. Nayo yolovipemhelusa vinhu vyose vyono vikwendeza.
16 Nayo Samaliya yinojeye yilipicizwe, ku soko yo kundeka 'ne. Ne wanhu wakwe wolofwa ku wulugu, ne wana wawo wagalika, wolowa kubamilizwa hasi, ne wacekulu wono wena mida, awo nawo wolotumbulwa.”


Hoseya 14

Wulombe wa Hoseya kuli Isilayeli

1 Ale, muwuyilenyi Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, anye nyo wanhu wa Isilayeli. Wubi wenyu wo waleka mukekobala no kugwa.
2 Muwuyilenyi Muwaha Mulungu, no wulombe awu wuwe co cilavywa cenyu kuli mwene. “Gwe Muwaha Mulungu, wucinenelaje wubi wetu wose, kanyhi wubocele wulombe wetu nase cokukumya agwe nha vyono cehuwiza.
3 Hambi Ashuli nha sikociponya yaye, hamba fwalasi zo wulugu sizosuma kucitaza. Hamba sicolotya kanyhi kuli vihwani vyetu vye milungu, vyono vyene yo Mulungu wetu. Gwe Muwaha Mulungu, wulajile lusungu kuli wawo wono wasina ku wubito.”

Muwaha Mulungu yakwihuwiza mikalile mipya kuli Isilayeli

4 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya,

“Nane nowagomola wanhu wangu wawuye kuli'ne

nowenda kwe nhumbula yangu yose,

hamba sinolowakolelwa likuwo kanyhi.

5 Nane nowa nha mvula mu'si nghavu kuli Waisilayeli.

Nawo wosanula nhe 'luwa,

kanyhi wohimbiza muno milela hasi,

kotya mibici ya Lebanoni.

6 Nawo wokwikala no kujendelela kuwiluka wupya,

no wuswanu wawo,

wowa nho wuswanu we mizeyituni.

Nawo wowa na mhunjile,

yinhe miyelezi ya Lebanoni.

7 Na kanyhi wokwikala hasi yo wudimwe wangu,

kanyhi wotula nha ngano.

Nawo wowa na mhunjile swanu,

nhe divayi ya Lebanoni.

8 Waisilayeli,

wowa wasina kanyhi cinji co kwituma kuli vihwani,

nolowupitula no wulombe wawo

no kuwadima nolowacitiza

no mubeho kotya mubici wono wudedewaye.

Hambi nha yo nene mono nili mwamulizo

wo wulugutwe wawo wose.”

Ze ndumilizo

9 Nghulomba, wono wena wizuce, wazitanje zizi zono zandicigwe baha, no kuziwika mu zinhumbula zawo. Nzila zo Muwaha Mulungu zo ze ling'ani, ne wanhu we ling'ani, wo wakuzityatya. Ninga wanyo wubi, wakukobala no kugwa, ku soko vyono siwaziceleleye yaye.
