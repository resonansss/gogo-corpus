Mika 1

1 Ayi yo nghani yo Muwaha Mulungu yono yamuziza Mika, mono yali munya Molesheti mu vilo vya Yotamu, na Ahazi na Hezekiya i watemi wa Yuda. Muwaha Mulungu yamugubulila Mika zizi zose za muli Samaliya na Yelusalemi.

U Muwaha Mulungu yakuza kuling'aniza

2 Ale muhulicize zizi anye nyo makungugo gose; muhulice zizi wose mono mwikaye mono'nze!

Muwaha Mulungu,

komuhomelelenyi, muhulicize!

Yakulonga ku wulawa mu Nyumba yakwe

yo wuhembelo ku wulanga.

3 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu

nha yakuza yakulawilila hono helile hakwe,

kokuza hasi,

kanyhi kojenda mu vizuli vye matunda.

4 Ne matunda gokwenjeduka hali mwene,

kotya lihula vyono lyenjedukaga mu moto,

gohulumila mu malolo,

kotya malenga gakuhuluma gakulawa mu cigongo.

5 Azi zose zokwijela, ku soko ye wanhu wa Isilayeli, wono wetuma wubi no kumuleka Muwaha Mulungu. Alu nani 'mbi mono kohoswa ku soko ye lisutya lya Isilayeli? Hono si Samaliya du! Hamba nani mono yena lisutya lyo kuhemba vihwani mu'si ya Yuda? Hono si Yelusalemi!
6 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Holeka ku soko yiyo, nane noyinoza Samaliya, yiwe nha matongo go wongomezwe, mu yisi ye lihala, i hanhu hono hakuwa kupanhwa mizabibu. Nogagwisa makaya, ne mabwe gayo nogataga mwi'lolo, noyileka misinji yayo yili bwete.
7 Ne vihwani vyawo viseswe, vyose vyobenanjizwa-benanjizwa. Ne vinhu vyawo vyose vyono wavitujila mu nzila zo wusenyha, vyohodangwa no moto, ne vihwani vyose, novinoza viwe visina cambuzi. Hambi Samaliya, nha yavitujila vivi vyose, mu zinzila zo wusenyha. Holeka walugu wakwe wovisola, no kuvitegula hanhu hanji, nawo wavitumamile nhavivyo mu zinzila zo wusenyha.”

U muwizi yakuzililila Yelusalemi na Yuda

8 Mika yakatya, “Ku soko ya zizo, ane nolila muno muno, ndajile wusungu wono nina. Nojenda kwa magulu sina malatu, kanyhi nili cipwi, nowa kubweka nha nyhewe, kanyhi nolila nha nyacihema.
9 Hambi zinghomele za Samaliya, nha si zolonoga yaye, na Yuda nayo nhavivyo, yakondya kuficilwa no wugaye, heya, wugaye wakondya kufika sunga mo mu milango, hono wekaye wanhu wangu ku Yelusalemi.”

10 Mulece kuwalonjela walugu wetu wono wali ku Gati, zo wusumwe wetu, kanyhi mulece kuwaleka wawone vyono mukulila. Wanhu wa ku Bete-leyafula, mulajile nha vyono mwina wusungu, mu nzila yo wigalagaze mu manghundi!
11 Anye nyo wanhu wa Shafila, mubite ku wuhamywe, muli cipwi, ne cevu. Na wawo wono wekaye mu Zanani, walecele lukulu kulawila mwi'kaya lyawo. Kolo mukuhulika wanhu wa Betizeli wakulila, ale momanya nha vyono kusina wutizizo kuko.
12 I wanhu wono wekaye Maloti nha wakulondola wutazi, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yaleta magazo ko ku Yelusalemi.
13 Anye wanhu mono mwikaye Lakishi, muwopeleze zifwalasi mu magali go wulugu. Anye mono mwawutyatya wubi wa Isilayeli, kanyhi mukayambuza Yelusalemi yitume wubi.
14 Holeka 'lu wanhu wa Yuda, welagaje mu makaya ga Molesheti-gati. Hambi watemi wa Isilayeli, nha si wotuga wutazi ku wulawa mwi'kaya lya Akizibu.

15 Nanye nyo wanhu wa Malesha, Muwaha Mulungu komuwicenyi mu miwoko yo mulugu, mono kolitema likaya lyenyu. Ne watemi wa Isilayeli, wobita kwihundika mu mapango ga Adulamu.
16 Anye nyo wanhu mono mwikaye Yuda, mucete zimvwili, mulile, ku soko ye wana wono muwendile. Mwinozezaje nyo weneco mihalu kotya 'ndi, hambi wana wenyu nha wohamywa no kutegulwa kutali na nyenye.


Mika 2

Mika na wono wakwisumaniza nayo

1 Woyi he, kuli wono wagonaga meso wakwihwesa zo wavu! Na lono wela hakali mitondo, kolo wakutuga cilejeho du, hodu wawa kwituma zizo zo wavu zono wali wazipanjile, hambi nha zili mu vilungo vyawo.
2 Na lono wawe wakusaka migunda, hodu, wakuyisola kwa vilungo, na lono wakusaka zinyumba, nazo wakuzisola, hasina mhuga yo yose, hamba sawo, yono yisigaye yipolile.

3 Holeka Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Nghwihwesa nimuletelenyi magazo, gono si mosuma kupona, langa mokwiwona mwenjila du mu wugaye, wono womunozenyi mulece kwilumba kanyhi hamba hadodo.
4 Ne vilo vivyo, kolo vikuza, wanhu wolosimulila ze mikalile yenyu yo wugaye, ciwe colecezo, nawo wawe kwimba no lwimbo aluno lwe cililo, lono lukulajila zono zamutujenyi, wawe kutya,

‘Cakondya kuhodanjilwa lukulu!

Muwaha Mulungu yakondya kuyisola yisi yetu,

no kuwapela wono wacinozize mateka.’ ”

5 Holeka i vilo vilawa kuza, vyo kugomolwa yisi kuli wanhu wo Muwaha Mulungu, kolowa kusina kanyhi yunji, mono kolowa yena lifungu muli'nye.

Wono wakwisumaniza no muwizi

6 Nawo wanhu wakunganulila wakutya, “Wulece kuganula hali'se. Wulece kuganula muli zizi zose. Hambi Mulungu nha si kocikoza cevu.
7 Wukwihwesa lo, vyono wanhu wa Isilayeli wali mu wulijitwe? Hamba Muwaha Mulungu yawutaga wukangalile wakwe? Hamba mbeka kokwituma zizi? Hamba mwene lo, si kalongaga ko wupolo kuli wose wono wakwituma ze ling'ani?”

8 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakupitula yakutya, “Mukuwahoma wanhu wangu nha walugu. Lono wakulawa ku wulugu, wakuwuya ku zikaya, wakwihwesa vyono luno wapolile, ninga kumbe anye mukwihundika mu nzila, ho muwahizize i makoti gawo gono wavwalile mucanyha.
9 Mukuwataga i wagolece we wanhu wangu, kudeha ye zinyumba zawo zono wazendile, kanyhi mukuwaboka i wana wawo wo wulugutwe wangu cibitilila.
10 Muwuce mubite, hambi ahano nha hasina kanyhi wuceho. Hambi u wubi wenyu, nha wahaletela wufwe hanhu baha, hamonga no wongomezwe.

11 “Wanhu awa, wakusaka muwizi mono yamemile wulambi no wukonji, mono yakutya ‘Nowila, divayi no wujimbi vilawile ko winji ku soko yenyu.’

12 “Ninga ane nomukongholenyi kanyhi hamonga, anye nyo wanhu wa Isilayeli, mono musigaye. Nomukongholelenyi hamonga, kotya ngholo zono zikuwuya kwi'bululu. Kotya wudimo wono wumemile ngholo, ne yisi yenyu vyo yowa yimemile wanhu wenji muno.”

13 Nayo Mulungu kowadigulila nzila, no kuwalongolela, wawuce mu wulawa mu yisi yo wujenzi. Wogabenanjiza malango ge likaya no kubita wapolile. No mutemi wawo, u Muwaha Mulungu mweneco, yo kolowatonga.


Mika 3

Mika yakuwahomelela i walongola wa Isilayeli wubi wawo

1 Ale muhulicize, anye nyo walongola wa Isilayeli! Yinojeye kuli'nye muyimanye ling'ani,
2 ninga 'lu 'nye muzisulile ziswanu, langa mwendile izimbi. Mukuyihinza nyhuli ye wanhu wangu akuno wali nyumi, no kuyitema nyama mu mafupa gawo.
3 Kanyhi mukuwalya nyama wanhu wangu, mukuyihinza nghuli yawo, no kugabenanga mafupa gawo, no kuwaguza wene, nha nyama nhelekwe mu nyungu.
4 Hambi icipango nha cikuza, cono anye nanye molomulilila Muwaha Mulungu, ninga mwene si kolomupitulenyi yaye, hamba si kolowuhuliciza no wulombe wenyu, ku soko 'nye mwetuma zimbi.

5 Wanhu wangu nha wakukongwa ne wawizi wo wulambi, wono wakuwehuwiziza vyono wokwikala wapolile, wawo wono wakuwalipa, na wono siwakwituma zizo, wakuwakajiziza wulugu. Ninga kuli wawizi wawa, u Muwaha Mulungu yakuwatya.
6 “Anye nyo wawizi, lizuwa lyenyu lili behi malika, nalyo lizuwa lyeza kumuswelenyi. Hambi nha mwawajiliza wanhu wangu, holeka hamba simowa no wugubulilwe wunji wo wose, hamba simosuma kuwila kanyhi cinji co cose.”
7 Na wawo wono wakuwila zono zikuza, wolokolwa cevu ku soko yo wuwizi wawo wo wulambi, nawo wose wolonyetywa, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha si kolowapitula yaye.

8 Ninga ane, Muwaha Mulungu kamhela vilungo vyo muhe wakwe, hamonga no wusume, no wuling'anizi, kamhela 'ne vilungo, ho niwalonjele Isilayeli u wubi wawo vyono winha.
9 Ale muhulicizaje anye nyo watemi wa Isilayeli, na anye wose mono muyisulile ling'ani, no kuyipitulanya imbeka liwa yisutya.
10 Anye mono mukuzenga likaya lyo Mulungu Yelusalemi, mucanyha ye misinji yo wuwulaji no wuwula ling'ani.
11 Nawo i walongola, wakulitema likaya kwe mafupo, nawo wakohani, wakuwejiza wanhu Wulajilize ho watuje musahala, nawo wawizi, wakulonga zo wugubulilwe wawo ho walipwe mapesa. Nawo wawa wose wakwiyitya ti, vyono Muwaha Mulungu yali hamonga nawo. Kanyhi wakutya, “Hasina zimbi zono zocagana, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yali hamonga nase.”

12 Holeka ku soko yenyu, Sayuni yololimwa nha mugunda, nayo Yelusalemi, yolowa yilundo lya matongo, ne litunda lye Nyumba yo Mulungu, lyolowa mbago.


Mika 4

Muwaha Mulungu kowatema wanhu wose ko mulamu

1 Mu vilo vyono vikuza,

litunda lyono Nyumba yo Mulungu yizenjigwe,

lyolokwinulwa muno hali mayagwe gose,

ne cizuli calyo colowa cipulute matunda gose.

Ne wanhu we makungugo gose, wololibitila.

2 Ne wanhu wayo wolotya,

“Alenyi cibite kwi'tunda lyo Muwaha Mulungu,

ku Nyumba yo Mulungu wa Isilayeli mwene

yo kocifunda ase zono yakuhila cizitume.

Nase cojendela mu zinzila

zono mwene yazihagula.

Hambi i mejizo go Muwaha Mulungu,

nha galawaga ku Yelusalemi,

kanyhi yakulonga ne wanhu wakwe

ku wulawa ku Sayuni.”

3 Nayo kologalamula makungugo,

muli makungugo ge vilungo,

gono gali behi na gono gali kutali.

Nawo wolotyana mimaje yawo yiwe masili,

ne migoha yawo yiwe hengo zo kutemanjila.

Ne makungugo,

si golokwihoma yaye,

hamba kwiwikaniza

hamba kwiyijiza kanyhi zo wulugu.

4 No munhu wo wose kolokwikala yapolile,

yena ne nyemo mu migunda yakwe

ye mizabibu ne mikuyu,

hasina munhu yunji mono kolomogofya.

Muwaha Mulungu we Mihanga yo yehuwize zizi.

5 Likungugo ne kungugo lihembaga no kogopa u mulungu walyo, ninga mu vilo vivyo, colomuhemba no kumogopa Muwaha Mulungu wetu cibitilila na cibitilila.

Isilayeli wolowuya ku wulawa ku wuhamywe

6 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Ivilo vikuza, lono nolowakonghola hamonga wanhu wangu wono nawalipiciza, wakagazwa ku wuhamywe.
7 Wakawa wakusenzegula, kanyhi wali kutali no kwawo. Ninga hamba nhavivyo nowanozeza mwamulizo mupya wawo wono wasigala, nawo wowa likungugo yiwaha lye vilungo. Nane notema hali wene mwi'tunda lya Sayuni, ku wulawa mu vilo vivyo sunga cibitilila.”

8 Nagwe go Yelusalemi, i hanhu hono Mulungu yo mudimi, mono kawalanjililaga wanhu wakwe, kolowa kanyhi likaya yiwaha lyo wutemi wono wali wako.
9 Alu so cici wukulila kwe 'zi yiwaha? Hamba so cici wukuwona wusungu nho mucekulu mono yali mu nghunga yo lusawo? Ku soko vyono wusina mutemi, ne watazi wako wono wakutazaga zinghani, wose wakondya kufwa?
10 Muwone wusungu mwinyongole anye wanhu wa Yelusalemi, nho mucekulu mono yali mu lusawo, hambi aluno nha mowuka mwi'kaya, mubite mukekale mwi'hala. Kanyhi mowa munojeye mubite ku Babiloni, ninga kuko ko Muwaha Mulungu kolomuponyezyenyi ku wulawa kuli walugu wenyu.
11 Hambi makungugo menji nha gekonghoye ho gamuhomenyi. Gakutya, “Yelusalemi yinojeye wuhodangwe! Nase coliwona likaya lili lyawa yitongo!”
12 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, i makungugo gaga, sigamanyile zono Muwaha Mulungu yakwihwesa. Hamba si gakumanya vyono, mwene yagahangulize hamonga ho yagalipicize, nha vyono wawulundaga wuhemba mu cuga wawa kuwutowa.
13 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Anye nyo wanhu wa Yelusalemi, ale, mubite mukawalipicize walugu wenyu, nane nomunozenyi muwe ne vilungo nhe nghambaku, yono yina mahembe ga cuma ne mabangho ga shaba. Nanye mogahoma makungugo menji no kugasumiza, ne sawo yawo yose yono wayituga kwe vilungo, ayo mondavizya nene, ane no Muwaha Mulungu we yinze yose.”


Mika 5

1 Anye nyo wanhu wa Yelusalemi, mwikongholaje so wihomi! Hambi nha cakondya kupilimwa! Nawo wamuhoma u mulongola wa Isilayeli!

Mulungu yakwihuwiza vyono, Mutemi kolawa ku Betelehemu

2 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Agwe Betelehemu Efulata, agwe yo gwegwe wuli cikaya cidodo mu Yuda, ninga nomuhagula yunji ku wulawa muli'gwe mono kowa mutemi wa Isilayeli, mono wulawo wakwe wo we katali na katali.”
3 Holeka Muwaha Mulungu, kowaleka wanhu wakwe kuli walugu wawo, sunga mu cipango cono mucekulu kolelela mwana we cilume. Ale baho i wanhu wono wowa wakali wasigaye ku wuhamywe, ho wolokwilunza kanyhi ne wanhu wawo weneco.
4 Nayo yalaza kuza, kolowatema wanhu wakwe, kwe vilungo vyono vikulawa kuli Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga ne nhojo yo Muwaha Mulungu mweneco. Ne wanhu wakwe, wolokwikala wapolile, hambi wanhu wose wa kono'nze, nha wolowuvumila u wuwaha wakwe, nayo yo kololeta mulamu.

Wukombozwe no wulipicizwe

5 Hono Waashuli waze wayisole yisi yetu no kugabena magomawakali getu, nase cowategula watemi wetu we vilungo wakawahome.
6 Nawo wolowasuma Waashuli mu yisi ya Nimulodi ko wukali wo mumaje, nawo wolociponya mu miwoko ye Waashuli, kolo wakwinjila no kuyihoma yisi yetu.
7 Ne wanhu wa Isilayeli wono wasigaye, wolowa nhe buhe yono yikulawa kuli Muwaha Mulungu so makungugo menji, kotya mahalavu ga mvula, mu migunda ye vilimwa vyono vikuwiluka. No witumbilo wawo, wolowa kuli Mulungu hamba si kuli munhu yaye.
8 Nawo wo lulelo lwa Yakobo wono wolowa wakali wasigaye mumo mu makungugo, wolowa nha simba yono yili mu mbago mu wupelembi, hamba mu wudimilo, yikwagana zingholo no kuzizumhila no kuzihawanga, hawa hasina hamba no witumbilo wo kupona yaye,
9 Isilayeli kolowasuma walugu wakwe, no kuwahodanjila lukulu wose.

10 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Mu vilo vivyo, noloziwusiza lukulu zifwalasi zenyu, no kuyihodanga mituka yenyu yo wulugu.”
11 Kanyhi nologahodanga makaya ga mu yisi yenyu, no kugagwisa hasi magomawakali genyu gose.
12 Noloziwusiza lukulu zimhiji zono muzitumbiye, nimulecenyi musina kanyhi no mutowa cisangu.
13 Nolovihodanga ne vihwani vyenyu, hamonga ne masumbili genyu ge mabwe gono mugasiwanuye, nanye si molovihembela lukulu kanyhi, i vinhu vyono mwinozeze nyenye nyo mweneco.
14 Nolovigwisa hasi vihwani vyo mulungu we cicekulu Ashela mu'si yenyu, no kugahodanga makaya genyu.
15 Nane nologalipiciziza mwi'kuwo lyangu liwaha, makungugo gose gono gasina cihuliko kuli'ne.


Mika 6

Wulalame wo muwizi

1 Ale muhulicize calo co Muwaha Mulungu kuli Isilayeli. Ale winuce 'gwe Muwaha Mulungu wulete calo cako. Matunda ne vitunda vihulice zizo zono wukulonga.
2 Anye nyo matunda, nanye nyo misinji ye cibitilila ye yinze, muhulicize calo co Muwaha Mulungu kuli'nye, Muwaha Mulungu yena calo kuli wanhu wakwe, mwene yakubita kuyileta nghani yo kwilolosa kuli Isilayeli.
3 Muwaha Mulungu yakulonga yakutya, anye nyo wanhu wangu, ane namutendelenyi ci? Hamba nawa nili mutegulo kuli'nye so nghani ci? Ale ta mumhitulaje.
4 Ane nghamusolenyi no kumuwusenyi ku wulawa ku yisi ya Misili, no kumuponyenyi ku wulawa mu wuwanda. Nghawatuma Musa, na Haluni, na Milyamu, ho wamutonjenyi ku wulawa kuko.
5 Anye nyo wanhu wangu, mukumbucile zizo zono Balaki u mutemi wa Mowabu, yali yewikanize ho yamutendelenyi 'nye, na vyono Balamu mwana wa Beyoli yamupitula. Kanyhi mukumbucile zono zejela, lono mwali muli mu nzila, ku wulawa ku nghambi ya Shitimu sunga ku Giligali. Holeka momanya zono nghamutendelenyi ho nimuponyenyi 'nye.

I liwuzo lye wanhu

6 Cinhuci cono nomutegulila Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wa ku wulanga, lono nghuza kumuhemba? Hamba sole nzeku swanu yono yina mwaka monga, nimulavizye nha cilavywa cinyanywe hali mwene?
7 Muwaha Mulungu kokwendezwa hono nimutegulile matewa na matewa ge zingholo, hamba makolongo elufu na elufu ge mafuta ge mizeyituni, yono si yidumaga? Hamba nimulavizye mwanangu mulongozi kulelwa, ho ndipile wubi wangu?

Lipitulo lyo muwizi

8 Aa ng'o, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yacilonjela ziswanu zono zinojeye hali mwene, zono yakuhila kuli'se, nha zozi, Citume zono zili ling'ani, cilajile wendo kuli mwene, no kujenda ko winenenyhezi hamonga no Muwaha Mulungu wetu.
9 Hambi nha go mahala, mu wumogopa Muwaha Mulungu. Mwene yakucema mwi'kaya yakutya, “Muhulicize anye nyo wanhu wa Yuda, mono mwikongholeye mwi'kaya!
10 Mu zikaya ze wanhu wabi, movili viwutwa vyono wavitujiye mu zinzila zimbi. Hamby nha wakwitumamila vipimo vyo wukonji vyono ane si nivyendile.
11 Alu nowanenela nhawule wanhu wono wakwitumamila vipimo ne mizani yo wukonji?
12 Wanhu wako i wagoli, wakuwegalila waciwa, na muli nyenye, wose muli walambi.
13 Holeka nane nakondya kwiwikaniza so wongomezwe wenyu no wuhodangwe wenyu ku soko yo wubi wenyu.
14 Nanye molya, ninga simokwiguta yaye, langa mokwikala vyo mudabuce du. Na mu luhamo lwenyu, si mosuma kusola cinji citelevu, na cono mocisoleleza citelevu, ane nocihodanga mu wulugu.
15 Molohadika zimbeyu, ninga simolodudula cinji yaye. Molominya mafuta ge mizeyituni, ninga si mologetumamila yaye. Kanyhi molonoza ne divayi, ninga hamba si moloying'wa ng'o.
16 Na zizi zolomuzizenyi ku soko vyono mwagatyatya metumo mabi go mutemi Omuli, na go mwanagwe mutemi Ahabu. Vyono mukujendela mu zinghani zawo, holeka nane nomutegulenyi sunga ko ku wongomezwe, ne wanhu wose womubedegalenyi, no kumwitumilenyi vyono wakuhila.”


Mika 7

U wulalame wo muwizi

1 Woyi hee! Hambi ane nha neza nha munhu mono yadabuce, mono yagana musina mikalo yono yisigazwe mu mibici, hamba zabibu mu manhambi ge mizabibu. Hambi zabibu zose hamonga ne mikalo yo mukuyu, nha vyakondya kuhepwa.
2 Hasina munhu mwihuwilwa mono yasigaye mu'si, hamba munhu mono yali cihuliko hali Muwaha Mulungu. Munhu wo wose kalondolaga cilejeho ho yetume zo wuwulaji. Munhu wo wose kamupelembaga nduguye ho yamugwize.
3 Wose wanghula muno ku wituma zo wavu. Wanhu wawaha hamonga ne mahakimu, wakulomba mafupo. Nayo u munhu muwaha yakuwalonjela zono yakuhila mu nhumbula yakwe nawo wawa kwilumbaniza nayo.
4 Sunga no munhu muswanu muli wene, yakuwa yasina cambuzi nhe maselegwa.

Ninga lizuwa lyakondya kuza, lyono Mulungu kowalipiciza wanhu, nha vyono yawasugula mu nzila ye walindilizi wawo ne wawizi wakwe, ku wulawa katali. Alu weza wali mu mahwele.
5 Wulece kumwihuwila mwikalani muyago, hamba kumwitumbila mono mwiyendile nayo. Wilanjililaje muli zono wukulonga, hamba yiwe kuli mugolece wako.
6 Na mu vilo vivyo, i wana wolowa kuwatendela wasogo zawo nha wanhu wono wasina mahala, nawo i wana we cicekulu, wakwiwanganila ne wanyina zawo. Ne wahinza wono watoligwe, nawo wakugombana ne wakwe zawo we cicekulu. Hambi i walugu wo munhu, nha wo wawo wa mu kaya yakwe mweneco.
7 Ninga 'ne, nomulanjilila Muwaha Mulungu, nomulindila Mulungu, mono komhonya 'ne. Nayo Mulungu wangu kohulika 'ne.

Witumbilo no wulombe we wanhu

8 Ne walugu wetu wowa wasina soko yo kucisimanga. Heya, hamba ciwe cagwa, ninga cokwinuka kanyhi. Alu cili mu wutitu, ninga Muwaha Mulungu kociletela kanyhi wuzelu.
9 Ase cetuma zo wubi hali Muwaha Mulungu, holeka aluno yicinojeye cilikangalile kwa cipango likuwo lyakwe. Ninga ku ndumilizo kocitaza no kucipela ling'ani kuli ze masutya gono cali citumile. Mwene kocilavya, kociwika ku wuzelu, holeka cowa cili womi, nase cimuwone na vyono yakuciponya.
10 Nawo walugu wetu, woloziwona zizi wakolwe cevu. Ne walugu wanji wolocilonga wawe kutya, “Alu yali hayi u Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu?” Nase colowawona wakondya kusumwa, wakukanyanjilwa hasi nhe madabaga, mu zinzila za mwi'kaya.

11 Nye nyo wanhu mono mwikaye mu Yelusalemi, I cipango co kuzenga kanyhi majinji ge likaya cikuza. Na mu cipango cico i mipaka ye yisi yenyu yologazimhywa.
12 Ne wanhu wenyu wolomuwuyilenyi ku wulawa mhelo zose, wolokuza ku wulawa kucilima ku Ashuli, na ku wulawa kutakama ku Misili, ku wulawa mu mipaka ye likolongo lya Efulata, ku wulawa kwi'lenga sunga mu matunda go kutali.
13 Ninga yisi yolowa nyika, ku soko yo wubi we wanhu wono wekaye mumo.

U wulombe

14 Gwe Muwaha Mulungu, weze mudimi kuli wanhu wako, iwanhu wono wawahagula. Hamba wawe wekaye kutali mwi'bago. Ninga yisi yose yono yiwapilime yina moso muno. Walekaje wabite wakaliswe ku Bashani na ku Gileyadi nha vyono wanoza mu vilo vye katali.

15 Gwe go Muwaha Mulungu, witumaje mapoto ku soko yetu, nha vyono wetuma mu vilo vyono waciwusiza ku yisi ya Misili.
16 Ne makungugo goziwona zizi, gawe kogopa no kuviwonela soni vilungo vyawo, na ku so woga, hamba si wolonga cinji kanyhi, langa wodinda makutu gawo.
17 Nawo wolowa kutambala hasi nha nzoka. Wokuza ku wulawa mu makaya gawo ge magomawakali, akuno wakucicima wena no woga. Wowuya kuli Muwaha Mulungu u Mulungu wetu, akuno wena no woga kuli mwene.

18 Gwe Muwaha Mulungu, hasina mulungu yunji mono yehwanile gwegwe, hambi 'gwe nha kuwanenelaga u wubi wawo wanhu wako wono wasigala, hamba si kwikalaga ne likuwo cibitilila. Ninga kuwezaga ne nyemo, ho wulajile lusungu lwako kuli'se.
19 Kowa no lusungu kanyhi kuli'se nhe vilo vyono vyapuluta. Kanyhi kowusambanjila wubi wetu hasi ye magulu gako, no kuwutaga wubi wetu ku visolo vye lilenga!
20 Agwe kolajila wihuwilwa wako no lusungu lwako kuli wanhu wako, mu lulelo lwa Abulahamu na Yakobo, nha vyono wawehuwiziza i walezi wetu ku wulawa mu vilo vye katali.
