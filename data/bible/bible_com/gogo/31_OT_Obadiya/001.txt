Obadiya 1

1 Awuno wo wugubulilwe wa Obadiya, wa zono Muwaha Mulungu yazilonga kuli likungugo lya Edomu.

Muwaha Mulungu koyilipiciza Edomu

U Muwaha Mulungu yamutegula mutumwa wakwe

kuli makungugo,

nase cayihulika i nghani yakwe,

yakutya, “Ale, wiwikanizaje!

Cibite cikayihome Edomu!”

2 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakutya kuli Edomu,

“Ane nokunoza wuwe wusina vilungo,

na wose wokusekanga.

3 Hambi wiwoni wako nha wakukonga,

mono wikaye mwi'kaya liwaha

lyono lipilimwe ne magomawakali ge mabwe,

wikalo wako wono wuli mucanyha ye matunda,

holeka go mweneco wukutya,

‘Nani 'mbi mono kosuma kunhimya hasi?’

4 Hamba wuwunoze wikalo wako

wuwe kucanyha nhe nhuli lye 'ndi,

ho yijele kotya nha wuli hamonga ne zinyelezi,

ninga hamba nhavivyo,

ane nokutimya du ho hasi.

5 “Ne wahizi lono wakuza ne cilo,

wasolaga vyono wendile weneco,

na lono wanhu wakududula zabibu,

wasigazaga nyhefu zo kuhalaliza.

Ninga i walugu wako wakondya kugongomezeza lukulu.

6 Nanye wo lulelo lwa Esawu,

zisawo zenyu zakondya kuhizwa.

7 I wanhu wono mwalagana,

wamukongajenyi, alu lanje,

wakondya kumuwusenyi mu wulawa mu yisi yenyu.

Wo wanhu wono mwekalanaga nawo viswanu,

ninga luno wamusumajenyi.

Wawo wono mwali mwiyendile,

mukulizana hamonga nawo,

alu wamuwicilajenyi mutego,

nawo wakumulonjenyi wakutya,

‘Alu vilungo vili hayi vyono wali wena navyo?’

8 Na mwi'zuwa lyono noloyilipiciziza Edomu,

nolowahodanga i wanhu wawo we citung'ati,

kanyhi nololuwusiza lukulu no wizuce wawo.

9 Ne wanhu vitung'ati wo wulugu

wono wali ku Temani,

wolokogofywa muno muno,

ne wasilikale wose wono wali mu Edomu,

wolowulagwa.”

Soko yono yoleka Edomu yilipicizwe

10 “Kolipicizwa agwe Edomu,

ku soko vyono wali wuli muhizi,

kanyhi wukawawulaga sunga ne wanduguzo,

i wana wa Yakobo,

holeka nagwe luno kohodangwa

no kuwembulilwa lukulu cibitilila.

11 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo,

agwe wali wimile ha mhelo,

lono walugu wabenela i malango gawo.

Na gwegwe wali wuli yumonga na wawo wajenzi,

wono wayipumha sawo ya Yelusalemi,

no kugawana weneco wose.

12 Holeka si wali wunojeye wuwasece

i wanduguzo wono wali mu Yuda

ku soko yo wugaye wawo.

Hamba kuwa no wusangalale

mwi'zuwa lyawo lyo wugaye.

Hamba kuwasekanga ku soko yo wugaye wawo.

13 Hamba si yali yikunojeye

winjile mwi'kaya lye wanhu wangu,

wuwasimanjile mu magayo gawo,

no kusola zo wugoli wawo mwi'zuwa

lyo wongomezwe wawo.

14 Hamba si yali yikunojeye wime

ha nzila malekano,

ho wuwebate wawo wono wakuhila kutiza.

Hamba si yali yikunojeye wuwawice

mu miwoko ye walugu,

mwi'zuwa lyo wongomezwe wawo.”

Mulungu kologaling'aniza i makungugo

15 “Lizuwa lyasejelela lyono ane,

no Muwaha Mulungu nologaling'aniza makungugo gose.

Nagwe Edomu zizo zono wetuma,

zo zizo zono kolonozezwa

na gwegwe nhavivilya.

Nagwe zolokuwuyila zizilya du

zono wawanozeza wanji.”

16 Wanhu wangu wakang'wa cikombe

co wusungu wo wuling'anizwe,

mucanyha ye litunda lyangu lyono lyelile.

Ninga imakungugo gose gono gapilime,

golong'wa cikombe co wusungu

cono cicizize co wuling'anizwe,

wolocing'wa wose no kwajililila lukulu.

Wusume we Waisilayeli

17 “Ninga mwi'tunda lya Sayuni,

wanji wolopona.

Na ho baho,

holowa hanhu hono helile.

Ne wanhu wa Yakobo wolokwikala mu yisi,

yisi yono yo ling'ani yawo.

18 Wanhu wa Yakobo ne wanhu wa Yusufu,

wolowa nha moto,

wolowahodanga wanhu wa Esawu kotya

nha moto wono wukutakamiza lisici.

Nawo wo lulelo lwa Esawu,

hasina hamba monga mono yalasigala.

Ane no Muwaha Mulungu vyo nghutya.

19 “Wanhu wa ku Yuda ya kutakama,

woloyisola Edomu,

nawo wono wololawa

ku lubavu lwa kumwezi mu vitunda,

woloyisola yisi ye Wafilisiti.

Nawo Waisilayeli woloyitema

yisi ya Efulayimu ne yisi ya Samaliya,

i wanhu wa Benyamini nawo,

woloyisola Gileyadi.

20 Ne lipuga lya wono wasolwa wakahamywa

ku wulawa Isilayeli ya kusumuka,

nawo wolowuya no kuyitema Foyinike,

sunga ko kusumuka kutali,

ku Zelefati.

Na wono wahamywa ku wulawa Yelusalemi,

wawo wono wali Sefaladi,

wologasola makaya ga Yuda ya kutakama.

21 Wanhu wo wusume wa Yelusalemi,

woloyihoma Edomu no kuyitema.

No Muwaha Mulungu mweneco,

kolowa yo mutemi.”
