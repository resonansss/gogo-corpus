Imetumo ge Watumwa 14

Pawulo na Balanaba mu Ikoniyo

1 Kuko ku Ikoniyo, zinghani zikawa nha vyono zali ku Antiyokiya. Wa Pawulo na Balanaba wakenjila mu nhang'anilo yo wuhembelo ye Wayahudi, wakalonga wapetuce, sunga Wayahudi wenji hamonga ne wanhu wono si Wayahudi, wakehuwila.
2 Ninga Wayahudi wanji wono si wasaka kwihuwila, wakawasagahaza wanhu wono si Wayahudi no kuwatajila nhumbula yo lusulo ho wawabeze we zindugu.
3 Holeka wa Pawulo na Balanaba wakajendelela kwikala kuko vilo vilo. Wakulonga wasina woga muli Muwaha, nayo Muwaha yakayikamya i mbeka ye lihewa lyakwe yono wayiganulaga, ko kuwapela vilungo vyo kunozeza mapoto ne mezino.

4 Ninga wanhu wa mwi'kaya lilyo, wakegawa hejete, wanji wakawa ku lubavu lwe Wayahudi, na wanji wakawa ku lubavu lwe watumwa.
5 Hanyuma yayo, iwanhu wanji wono wi Wayahudi, hamonga ne Wayahudi, wakelunza hamonga ne wawaha wawo, ho wawatendele vibi wawo iwatumwa, no kuwatowanga ne mabwe.
6 Ninga iwatumwa waza kuyimanya nghani yiyi, wakatiziza ku Lusitela na Delibe, i matumbi ga Lukawoniya, na mu mhelo mhelo ya baho.
7 Na kuko wakajendelela kuluganula u Luganuzi Luswanu.

Pawulo na Balanaba ku Lusitela na Delibe

8 Kwali na munhu yunji kuko ku Lusitela, mono yali yali citewe kulawa vyono yalelwa, nayo yali yakali si yanajenda hamba hadodo.
9 U munhu yuyo, yamuhulicizaga Pawulo lono yaganulaga. Nayo Pawulo yakamulanga muno, na yaza kumuwona vyono yena wihuwilo wo kusuma kuponywa,
10 yakalonga ne'zi iwaha, yakutya, “Winuce wime wima.” Nayo yuyo mono yali yali citewe, yakenuka musunho, yakanza kujenda.
11 Imapuga ge wanhu gaza kuwona yono Pawulo yayinozize, wakanza kuzoza, wakulonga mu nonga ye Cilukawoniya, wakutya, “Imilungu yicitimila yihwanile wanhu.”
12 Balanaba wakamucema Zewusi, na Pawulo, vyono mwene yali yo mulonji muno, wakamucema Heleme.
13 Nayo mukohani mono yamwitumamilaga yuyo mulungu Zewusi, mono nyumba yakwe yo wuhembelo vyali vidomile ne likaya, yakaleta ng'ombe no wihambo wa maluwa, sunga ho ha mulango muwaha we likaya, nayo yasakaga vihanje ne wanhu wawalya, wawalavizye yikumbiko iwatumwa.

14 Ninga Pawulo na Balanaba wali weza kuzihulika zizo, wakademulanga myenda yawo, wakenjila hagati ye lipuga lye wanhu, wakazoza wakutya,
15 “Nhawulenyi mukwituma zizi? Nase nha cili wanhu du kotya nyenye. Nase cili baha ho cimuganulilenyi Luganuzi Luswanu, ho muzilece zizi zo wulele, mumupitucile Mulungu mumi mono yawumba wulanga ne yinze, lilenga na vyose vyono vili mugati.
16 I katali u Mulungu yagakombola i makungugo gose getume nha vyono gendile.
17 Hamba nhavivyo, u Mulungu si yaleka kwilajila, so metumo maswanu gono yakumunozezenyi, yakumupelenyi mvula ya ku wulanga, kanyhi ya kumupelenyi ne vipango vye matika, yakumupelenyi zi ndigwa, no kuzigutya zinhumbula zenyu na nyemo.”
18 Hamba wawe walonga zizo, ninga wakawona kukamu kuwemila mapuga, ho galece kuwalavizya likumbiko.
19 Ninga Wayahudi wanji, wakaza wakulawa ku Antiyokiya na Ikoniyo wakawakwega wanhu ho welunze nawo, wakamutowanga Pawulo ne mabwe, wakamukulunhya sunga ko kunze ye likaya, wakawa kwihwesa vyono yakondya kufwa.
20 Ninga wejizwa wakekonghola, wakamupilima, nayo yakenuka yakenjila mwikaya. Imitondo yaco yakatuluka, mwene na Balanaba, wakabita ku Delibe.

Wa Pawulo na Balanaba, wakuwuya kwi'kaya lya Antiyokiya ya ku Siliya

21 Waza kuwaganulila Luganuzi Luswanu wa mwi'kaya lilyo lya Delibe, no kutuga wejizwa wenji, wakawuya ku Antiyokiya, ninga wakakolela ku Lusitela na Ikoniyo.
22 Wakawasinyhimika zinhumbula i wejizwa, no kuwasikamya wekalaje mu wihuwilo. Wakawalonjela wakutya, “Yicinojeye ase wose kukolela mu magayo menji, mu winjile mu wutemi wo Mulungu.”
23 Wakawahagula wanyamhala kulawa mu makanisa gose, ho walidime i Likanisa. Nawo waza kuwa walomba no kuleka kulya, wakawawika kuli Muwaha mono wamwihuwila.

24 Wakakola mu yisi ya Pisidiya, wakafika ku Pamufuliya.
25 Waza kukondya kuganula nghani mu Peliga, wakabita ku Ataliya,
26 na ku wulawa kuko, wakajendela mu ngalawa, wakawuya ku Antiyokiya, ihanhu bahalya hono walombelwa ha mwamulizo, no kuwikwa mu wukamiswe wa mwi'hewa lyo Mulungu, so mulimo wono wali wakondya kuwufisiliza.
27 Waza kufika kuko ku Antiyokiya, wakacema mukung'ano we Kanisa ya baho. Wakawasimuliziza zose zono Mulungu yetumile hamonga nawo, kanyhi vyono Mulungu yawadiguliye mulango, iwanhu wono si Wayahudi, ho wenjile mu wihuwilo.
28 Wakekalana hamonga na wawalya iwejizwa vilo vikatela.
