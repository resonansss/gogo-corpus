Imetumo ge Watumwa 10

Petelo na Kolineliyo

1 Kwali na munhu ku Kayisaliya, litagwa lyakwe Kolineliyo, muwaha we mhuga ye wasilikale yigana, yono yacemwaga “Mhuga ye ci Italiya.”
2 yali munhu mwihuwilwa, u mwene hamonga na wa mu nyumba yakwe wose, wali wehuwilwa na kanyhi wali wogopo Mulungu, nayo yetuma zi nyinji, ko kuwataza iwaciwa we Ciyahudi, kanyhi yali yendile kumulomba u Mulungu nhondo zose.
3 Yali nha saa tisa ne misi, yakawona ciwonelela mu wugubulilwe, Malayika wo Mulungu yakwinjila mugati no kumutya, “Kolineliyo.”
4 Nayo Kolineliyo yakamunugulila meso yuyo Malayika kuno yakogopa, yakatya, “Alo ko cilici Muwaha?” Nayo Malayika yakamutya, “Mulungu kawubocela wulombe wako, hamonga no wulavye wako kuli waciwa.
5 Alu, wutume wanhu wabite ku Yopa, wakamusole yunji Simoni, litagwa linji kacemwaga Petelo.
6 Yekaye kwa yunji Simoni munoza zinyhingo, kaya yakwe yili ha mwejelele we lilenga.”
7 Yuyo Malayika mono vyalonganaga nayo, yaza wuka, Kolineliyo yakawacema iwetumami wakwe wejete, no musilikale mwihuwilwa, muyagwe na wono wamwitumamilaga nhondo zose,
8 yakawasimuliziza zizo zose, yakawatuma wabite ku Yopa.

9 Imitondo yaco, lono wanhu wawalya i wadatu wali wakali mu muhinzo, ninga wali wali behi no kufika ku Yopa, Petelo yakabita kucanyha ku golofa mu saa sita ye misi, kulomba.
10 Yakadabuka, yakasaka kulya zindigwa, na lono zali zikali kunozelezwa, yakebatwa na nhongo nzito, yakalota ndoto.
11 Yakawona wulanga wudiguce, na cinhu kotya yimwenda yiwaha, cikutimywa mu mamhembo gayo mane ho hasi.
12 Na mugati ye limwenda lilyo, mwali mogali mawala gose ge vilimu, ivilimu vye migulu mine, ne vilimu vyono vitambalaga hasi, ne zindeje za mucanyha.
13 Yakahulika lizi likumutya, “Petelo, winuce wuwulaje vilimu vivyo, wulye.”
14 Petelo yakatya, “Ng'o, Muwaha, hambi ane nha nghali sinalya co cose icisemunwa, hamba cavu.”

15 Ilizi lililya likahulikwa kanyhi likamutya “Wulece kutya visemunwa, ivinhu vyono Mulungu yakondizye kuvyeza.”
16 Inghani yiyi, yiketumwa nghanghulo yidatu, hanyuma limwenda lilya, likagomolwa ku wulanga.
17 Petelo lono yali yakali kwizina muli zono yagubuliywe, nhunizo yayo cici, wawalya wanhu wono wali watumwa na Kolineliyo waza kuyiwona nyumba ya Simoni, wakema ha mulango,
18 wakacema kwe lizi wakutya, “Ati ho yuli mujenzi baha mono yakucemwa Simoni Petelo?”

19 Na Petelo lono yali yakali kwihwesa zo wugubulilwe, ho baho u Muhe yakamutya, “Hulicize! Howali wanhu wadatu baha wono wakukulondola.
20 Ale winuce mbela mubitane nawo, hamba wulece kwihwelela, hambi nha nene niwatumile.”
21 Hobaho Petelo yakatima hasi, yakawatya wawo wanhu, “Ane baha mono mukunondola, nhawulenyi muza?”
22 Wanhu wawo wakamutya, “Kolineliyo u muwaha we wasilikale yigana, munhu mono yali ling'ani, kanyhi mwihuwilwa, mono kamogopago Mulungu, kanyhi mono yena lilumbo mu nghungugo yose ye Wayahudi, kacituma. Yalonjelwa na Malayika mono yelile, yakuceme wubite ku kaya yakwe, ho yahulice nghani yo yose yono kowa wina nayo yo kumulonjela.”
23 Petelo yakawacemela kugati, yakawapela hanhu ho kugona mu cilo cico. Imitondo yaco, Petelo yakenuka yakabitana nawo, na wenji we zindugu wa ku Yopa wakajendana nayo.
24 Na mwizuwa lye kejete, wakenjila ku Kayisaliya. Nayo Kolineliyo yawalindilizaga, yali yawakonghola ne wendece wakwe.
25 Baho Petelo lono yenjilaga, Kolineliyo yakamuhinjiliza, yakafugama ha mwande yakwe, yakenama ho hasi.
26 Ninga Petelo yakamwinula, yakamutya, “Winukaje, hambi nane nha nili munhu du.”
27 Na Petelo walonganaga na Kolineliyo, akuno wakwinjila mu nyumba, na mumo mu nyumba, wakawagana wanhu wenji wekonghoye.
28 Petelo yakawatya, “Nyenye nyo weneco mumanyile vyono Muyahudi wo wose, wulajilize wakwe we dini wukumulemeza yalece kwilunza ne wanhu wono si Wayahudi. Ninga u Mulungu kandajilila 'ne vyono ndece kumutya yunji musemunwa, lunji mwavu.”
29 Ku soko yiyo holeka mweza kunyhema si nemile kuza. Holeka alu nghusaka kumanya cono munyhemeye?
30 Kolineliyo yakatya, “Alu vyapuluta vilo vidatu vyono mwipango kotya lili, lye saa tisa ne misi, nalombaga mu nyumba yangu, kumbe kwa nhinicizo du, munhu mono yali yavwalile vivwalo vyono vikung'alala, yakema hali nene,
31 yakatya, Kolineliyo, Mulungu kawubocela wulombe wako, hamonga ne vilavywa vyako kuli waciwa.”
32 Alu, wutume munhu yabite ku Yopa, yakamuceme munhu yunji litagwa lyakwe Simoni, ne litagwa linji kacemwaga Petelo, yekaye mu nyumba ya Simoni munoza zinyhingo, ha mwejelele we lilenga.
33 Holeka nane nghawa kukutumila wanhu lwa kamonga, nagwe kunoza viswanu kuza. Alu ase wose cili baha hali Mulungu, cizihulicize zose zono Muwaha yakulajilize wulonje.

U wuganule wa Petelo

34 Hobaho Petelo yakatya, “Alu mbeka ho natanga vyono u Mulungu yasina wuwaguzi.
35 Ninga munhu we nghungugo yo yose, mono kamogopago Mulungu, no kwituma ze ling'ani yo yakubocelwa nayo.
36 Ayi yo nghani yo Mulungu yono yawategulila iwanhu wa Isilayeli, yakuganula u Luganuzi Luswanu lono lukuleta mulamu muli Yesu Kilisito, mono yo Muwaha wa wose.
37 Anye muyimanyile inghani yilya yono yejela mu yisi yose ye Wayahudi, ku wulawilila ku Galilaya, hanyuma yo wubaputizo wono waganulwa na Yohana.
38 Yo Yesu wa Nazaleti, mono mumumanyile vyono u Mulungu yamwitila Muhe Mono Yelile ne vilungo. U Mulungu yali yali hamonga nayo, umwene yakola ako na ako yakwituma viswanu, no kuwaponya wose wono wagazagwa na Setani.
39 Nase cili wahomelezi wa zose, zono yatendaga mu yisi ye Wayahudi, na mu Yelusalemi. Wamuwulaga, wakamutumbika mu musalaba,
40 ninga u Mulungu, yakamuzukula mwizuwa lye kadatu, yakamunoza yejele,
41 ninga si kuli wanhu wose, langa kuli wawo wono u Mulungu yali yakondya kuwahagula wawe wahomelezi wakwe, wo wutya, yo sese mono calizana no kung'wezana hamonga nayo, lono yali yazuka muli wafu.
42 Yakacilajiliza ciwaganulile wanhu wose u Luganuzi Luswanu, no kuhomelela vyono u mwene yo yahagulwa no Mulungu, yawe Muling'anizi we womi ne wafu.
43 Sunga ne wawizi wose walonga zo Mwene, vyono munhu wo wose mono komwihuwila, kopegwa wunenelwe wo wubi wakwe mwi'tagwa lyakwe.”

Kolineliyo na wa mu nyumba yakwe, wakumubocela Muhe Mono Yelile

44 Na lono Petelo yali yakali yakulonga zinghani zizi, u Muhe Mono Yelile yakawatimila wose wono wayihulicizaga nghani yiyo.
45 Wawalya Iwayahudi wono wali wehuwila, wono wazana na Petelo kuwulawa ku Yopa, wakezina muno, waza wona vyono Mulungu yawetiye icipegwa co Muhe Mono Yelile iwanhu wono si Wayahudi nhavivilya,
46 hambi nha wawahulika wakulonga mu nonga hayo hayo, wakumukumya Mulungu. Hobaho Petelo yakatya,
47 “Wanhu wawa wamubocela u Muhe Mono Yelile nha vyono sese co weneco vyono camubocela. Wulenyi hoyuli yunji mono kosuma kuwemila wawa ho walece kubaputizwa?”
48 Hobaho yakawatya, wabaputizwe mwi'tagwa lya Yesu Kilisito. Wakamunena yekale nawo baho vilo vitela tele.
