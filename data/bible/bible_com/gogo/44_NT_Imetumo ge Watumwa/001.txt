Imetumo ge Watumwa 1

Yesu yakuwelaga i wejizwa wakwe

1 Ndugu yangu Teyofilo, Mu citabu cilongozi nandika zose zono Yesu yetuma no kwijiza, ku wulawo mwamulizo wo mulimo wakwe,
2 sunga mwi'zuwa alilya lyono yanyasulilwa no kuwuya ku wulanga. Na lono yali yakali nanyaswulwa no kuwuya ku wulanga, yawalajiliza mu Muhe Mono Yelile, wawalya iwatumwa wono yawahagula.
3 Na lono, yali yatamiswa yakondya, yakelajila kuli wene vyono mumi, mu vitanjilo vinji vye vilungo, lono yejelaga kuli wene yidaha lya vilo makumi ma'ne. Wamuwona, nayo yakaduwana nawo muli zo wutemi wo Mulungu.
4 Na lono wali wetang'anila hamonga, yakawalonjela yakutya “Mulece kuwuka ku Yelusalemi, langa mucilindilaje icipegwa cono yehuwiza Baba, icipegwa cono mwakondya kucihulika nghucilonga.
5 Hambi Yohana nha yabaputizaga mu malenga; ninga anye mu vilo vicefu, mobaputizizwa mu Muhe Mono Yelile.”

Yesu yakuwuya ku Wulanga

6 Baho wene, waza kwikonghola hamonga na Yesu, wakamuwuza wakutya, “Ati Muwaha, kwinha nhavivi ho kowakombolela Isilayeli Wutemi?”
7 Ninga Yesu yakawatya, “I madaha ne vilo, vili mu wulajilize wa Baba, holeka si zenyu kumanya zowa nili.
8 Ninga mopegwa vilungo, kolo yakumuzizenyi u Muhe Mono Yelile, nanye mowa wahomelezi wangu mu Yelusalemi, na mu yisi yose ya Yudiya, na Samaliya, sunga ko ku mhelo ye yinze.”
9 Nayo yaza kondya kulonga zizi; nawo lono wamulangaga, yakanyasulilwa ku wulanga, livunde likamugubiciza ha meso gawo, hamba kumuwona kanyhi.
10 Nawo lono wali wakali wagalamiye ku wulanga lono yabitaga, lanje, wanhu wejete wono wali wavwalile vivwalo vizelu, wakema behi nawo;
11 nawo wakatya, “Nye nyo wanhu wa Galilaya, alo so cici mwimile mugalamiye ku wulanga? Ayu Yesu mono yasoligwe kuli'nye no kuwuya ku wulanga, kolokuza kanyhi nhavivyo vyono mumuwene yakubita ku wulanga.”

Matiya yakuhagulwa yahalile witumami wa Yuda

12 Baho Iwatumwa wakawuya ku Yelusalemi, wakulawa ku cigongo cono citezagwa ca Mizeyituni, cono cali cihinzo ca kotya kilometa monga du ku wulawa ku Yelusalemi.
13 Nawo waza kufika, wakenjila mu nyumba ya ku golofa, mono wekalaga, nawo wali wa Petelo na Yohana na Yakobo na Andeleya, Filipo na Tomaso, Batolomayo na Matayo, Yakobo mwana wa Alifayo, na Simoni zelote, na Yuda mwana wa Yakobo.
14 Wawa wose, waswezanaga hamonga mu wulombe ko wuhile wumonga, hamonga ne wacekulu wanji, hamonga na Malyamu u nyina wa Yesu, ne wanduguze wa Yesu.
15 Hanyuma ya vilo vicefu, hakawa na mukung'ano wa wono wali wakondya kwihuwila, wali waficile nha wanhu yigana limonga na makumi mejete. Petelo yakema hamwande yawo,
16 yakatya, “Wandugu zangu, cinojele mandiko gafisilizwe, gono yamula kulonga u Muhe Mono Yelile ko mulomo wa Dawudi, za Yuda mono yali mutonji wa wono wamwibata Yesu.
17 Hambi Yuda nha yali yawalilizwe hamonga nase, hambi nha yahagulwa yahanje nase mu witumami wuwu.”

(
18 Mumanyile vyono mwene yawugula mugunda, ne zipesa zizilya zono yazitujila mwi'tumo lyakwe ilibi. Yakagwa hasi, yakabahuka, no wutumbu wakwe wukalawila kudeha.
19 Ne wanhu wose wa mu Yelusalemi, wakazihulika zizi, holeka wakawucema mugunda wuwo mu nonga yawo, wakawutya, “Hakeli Dama nhunizo yayo Cigundu ce Sakami.”)

20 “Hambi nha yandikwa mu citabu ce Zabuli:

‘Wikalo wakwe liweze 'tongo,

Hamba munhu wo wose yalece kwikala mumo,’

Kanyhi yandikwe,

‘Munhu yunji yahalile u witumami wuwo.’

21 “Holeka yinojeye munhu yunji, yawe muhomelezi hamonga nase zo wuzuce wakwe u Muwaha Yesu.
22 Yimunojeye yawe yumonga wa muli awalya wono cijendanaga nase mu vilo vyose, vyono u Muwaha Yesu yajendanaga nase, wulawa mwipango lyono Yohana yabaputizaga, sunga lizuwa lilya lyono Yesu yanyasulilwa kuwulawa kuli'se no kubita ku wulanga.”
23 Wakatambula matagwa ga wanhu wejete; u mulongozi Yusufu mono yacemwaga Balasaba (hamba liyagwe Yusito,) na we kejete Matiya.
24 Hanyuma wakalomba wakatya, “Gwe go Muwaha, mono wumanyile zinhumbula ze wanhu wose, alu wulajile muli wawa vyono wejete, mono wumuhaguye,
25 yahalile witumami no wutumwe wuwu, wono yawuleka Yuda, ho yabite kwakwe mweneco.”
26 Baho wakatowa kula, ne kula yikamwihaga Matiya, nayo yakawalilizwa ne watumwa we likumi na monga.
