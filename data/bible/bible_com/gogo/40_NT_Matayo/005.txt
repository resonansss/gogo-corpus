Matayo 5

U wuganule wa Mwitunda

1 Nayo Yesu yaza kugawona imapuga ge wanhu, yakabita kucanyha ye litunda yakekala hasi. Iwejizwa wakwe wakamuziza,
2 nayo yakawa kwanza kuwejiza yakatya,

I nyemo ye mbeka

(Luk 6:20-23)

3 “Wamoti wono wakwiwona nha waciwa hali Mulungu,

hambi u wutemi wo Mulungu nha wawo.

4 Wamoti wono wena wukalale,

hambi nha wosindamikwa.

5 Wamoti iwapolo,

hambi nha wopegwa yinze.

6 Wamoti wono wena nzala ne nyhilu,

yo kwituma zo wuhile wo Mulungu,

hambi u Mulungu nha kowegutya.

7 Wamoti wono wena lusungu kuli wanji

hambi nawo nha wowonelwa lusungu no Mulungu.

8 Wamoti wono wena nhumbula nzelu,

hambi nha womuwona u Mulungu.

9 Wamoti wono wakwituma so wulondola mulamu,

hambi nha wocemwa wana wo Mulungu.

10 Wamoti wono wakugazwa

ku soko yo wituma zo wuhile wo Mulungu,

hambi u Wutemi wa ku wulanga nha wawo!

11 “Muli wamoti nyenye, kolo wanhu wakumulijenyi, no kumugazenyi, no kumuvilenyi, kanyhi no kumulonjenyi makani mabi ku soko vyono muli watyatyi wangu.
12 Musecelele, kanyhi muwe musangalaye, hambi nha mwina wuhumbilizwe wenyu wuwaha ku wulanga. Hambi nha vivyo nha vyo wanhu wawagaza i wawizi wono wamulongolelenyi.

U mwino no Wuzelu

(Mlk 9:50; Luk 14:34-35)

13 “Anye mwinha muli mwino mu yinze! Ninga u mwino hono wususulice, wogomolezezwa na cici kanyhi ndaso yayo? Awo wukuwa si wukwiliye cinji kanyhi, langa wutagwaga kunze no kukanyanjilwa ne wanhu.

14 “Anye muli wuzelu we yinze! Kaya yono yizenjigwe mwi'tunda mucanyha, siyosuma kwihundika.
15 Hamba ne wanhu si wakolelezaga tala no kuyigubiciza ne nhoto, langa yiwikwaga hacanyha, ha wuwiko wayo, ho yiwamulicile wose wono wali mumo mu nyumba.
16 Nha vivyo vyo yinojeye no wuzelu wenyu wumulice hali wanhu, ho wagawone imetumo genyu maswanu, nawo wamulumbe u sogo yenyu wa ku wulanga.

Kuli zo Wulajilize

17 “Mulece kwihwesa vyono ane naza ho niwupate u Wulajilize wa Musa, hamba lunji ne mejizo ge wawizi. Ane si naza ho nizipate zizo, langa naza ho nizisikamye.
18 Nghumulonjelenyi mbeka, vyono sunga u wulanga ne yinze zilapuluta, ninga hasina hamba nghani ndodo, hamba cipinga cidodo co Wulajilize cono cowuswa, sunga kolo zose zakondya kufisilizwa.
19 Holeka muli wose mono kobena ililajilizo hambe lidodo sinha gose, no kuwejiza ne wanji wetume nha vivyo, yuyo yo kowa mudodo muno mu wutemi wa ku wulanga. Ninga mono kosuma kuzibata, sunga no kuwejiza iwanji, yuyo kowa muwaha mu wutemi wa ku wulanga.
20 Holeka nghumulonjelenyi nghutya, u wuswanu wenyu, hono wulece kuciza awulya we Wafalisayo ne wejizi wo Wulajilize, nanye si mokwinjilila lukulu mu wutemi wa ku wulanga.”

Kuli ze likuwo

(Luk 12:57-59)

21 “Mwahulika vyono iwanhu we katali walonjelwa nhavi, ‘Wulece kuwulaga munhu! Nayo mono kowulaga, yimunojeye yaling'anizwe.’
22 Ninga ane nghumutyenyi, muli wose mono yakumuwonela likuwo u nduguye, yuyo yimunojeye yaling'anizwe. Nayo mono yakumubedegala u nduguye, yuyo kotegulwa ku wuling'anizwe. Na mono yakumutya u nduguye ‘Go mulele gwe,’ yuyo yanojeye yenjile mu moto we Gehena.
23 Ale, hono wuwe wukulavya icilavywa cako ha cikumbicilo, na kuno hono wukumbucile vyono u nduguyo yena nghani na gwegwe, ale,
24 wucilekaje icilavywa cako, ha mwande ye cikumbicilo, wubite wamule mukelamule no nduguyo, hanyuma ho wuze, wukacilavye icilavywa cako.

25 “Mwilumbanizaje mbela mbela na mono yakukusitaka lono mukali nayo mu nzila, lono mukubita kuli mahakama. Na hono wulece kulanjilila, mono yakukusitaka, kokuwika mu miwoko ya hakimu, nayo hakimu, kokuwika mu miwoko ye wasilikale, nagwe kudindilwa mu jela.
26 Mbeka nghukulonjela, vyono sikolawaniziza lukulu mumo sunga kolo weza kukondya kulipa sendi ye ndumilizo.”

Kuli zo wusenyha

27 “Mwahulika vyono iwanhu walonjelwa, ‘Wulece kusenyha!’
28 Ninga ane nghumulonjelenyi vyono, umunhu mono komulanga mucekulu sunga yamusulumile, yuyo yakondya kusenyha nayo mu nhumbula yakwe.
29 Holeka, hono iliso lyako lyo kulume likusutizye, ale, wuliwusaje wulitaje kutali. Yili ciba muno kuli'gwe, wajilize cilunzwa cimonga co muwili, si nho kutagwa u muwili wako wose mu moto.
30 Na hono u muwoko wako wo kulume wukukusutizya, ale, wuwudumulaje no kuwutaga kutali. Yili ciba muno wajilize cilunzwa cimonga co muwili, si nho u muwili wako wose wutegulwe mu moto we Gehena.”

Muli ze talaka

(Mat 19:9; Mlk 10:11-12; Luk 16:18)

31 “Kanyhi yalongwa vyono, ‘Mono komuleka u mugolece wakwe, ale yamupelaje talaka.’
32 Ninga ane nghumutyenyi, mono komupela u mugolece wakwe talaka, langa hono si ku soko yo wusenyha, yuyo yakumwambuza du yasenyhe, no munhu yunji hono yamutole u mucekulu mono yapejigwe talaka, nayo yakusenyha.”

Muli zo wilahize

33 “Mwahulika kanyhi iwanhu we katali vyono walonjelwa, ‘Wulece kukawaniza uwilahize wako, langa yikunojeye wuwufisilize u wilahize wako kuli Muwaha.’
34 Ninga ane nghumulonjelenyi nghutya, mulece kwilahiziza lukulu mwi'tagwa lya cinji co cose, hamba ku wulanga, hambi nha lyo ligoda lyo lukumyo lyo Mulungu,
35 hamba cinji ca kono'nze, hambi nha wo wukanyago we magulu gakwe, hamba ku Yelusalemi, hambi nha yo kaya yo Mutemi muwaha.
36 Hamba wulece kwilahiza mu mutwe wako, hambi nha sikosuma kulunoza u luvwili lwako lumonga, luwe luzelu hamba lutitu.
37 Hono wutye, ‘Heya’ ale yiweze ‘Heya du!’ Na hono wutye, ‘Ng'o’ ale yiweze mbeka ‘Ng'o.’ Na yo yose yono yitimile zizo, yilawaga kuli ayulya u Mubi.”

Muli zo kwilipiciza

(Luk 6:29-30)

38 “Mwahulika vyono yalongwa, ‘Liso na liso liyagwe, lino na lino liyagwe.’
39 Ninga ane nghumutyenyi, wulece kumulipiciza u munhu mubi. U munhu hono yakutowe kofi mu luzagasa lo kulume, ale wumupitusizaje no luzagasa luyagwe.
40 Munhu hono yakutegule kuli mahakama, ho yakusolele shati yako, wumulecelaje yasole ne likoti lyako.
41 No munhu hono yakudindimize wumudinghulile u mutegulo wakwe mu cihinzo ca kilometa monga, agwe wumudinghulilaje wumufise kilometa nyejete.
42 Nayo mono yakukulomba, wumupelaje, kanyhi wulece kumulemeza mono yakusaka kukukopa cinji.”

Muli zo kuwenda iwalugu

(Luk 6:27-28, 32-36)

43 “Kanyhi mwahulika vyono yalongwa, ‘Wumwendaje u muyago ninga wumusulaje u mulugu wako.’
44 Ninga ane nghumutyenyi, ‘Muwendaje iwalugu wenyu, kanyhi mweze kuwalombela wono wakumugazenyi nyenye,
45 ho musume kuwa wana wo Sogo yenyu mono yali ku wulanga. Hambi u Mwene nha kawamulicilaga ilizuwa lyakwe iwanhu waswanu ne wabi, no kuwatonyezeza imvula iwanhu waswanu ne wabi.’
46 Alu motuga wuhumbilizweci hono muwe kuwenda du wono wakumwendenyi nanye? Hasina! Ku soko sunga ne watoweza kodi nawo wanozaga nhavivyo.
47 Na lono muwe mukuwalamusa iwandugu zenyu du, wulenyi mowa mwanoza cinhu cijenzi? Ne wanhu wono si wamumanyile u Mulungu, nawo nha wanozaga nhavivyo.
48 Holeka mweze mukwilaniciye, nha vyono u Senyu wa ku wulanga yakwilaniciye.”
