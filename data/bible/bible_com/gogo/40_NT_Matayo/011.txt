Matayo 11

Wono watumwa kulawa kuli Yohana u Mubaputizi

1 Nayo Yesu yali yeza kukondya kuwalajiliza i wejizwa wakwe, hodu yakawuka kuko, yakawa kubita kwijiza no kuganula, mu makaya gawo.

2 Nayo Yohana mubaputizi, yakazihulika ze metumo ga Yesu lono yali yali mu jela. Holeka yakawa kuwatuma i wejizwa wakwe,
3 wamuwuze watye, “Alo yo gwegwe yulya mono yakuza, hamba cimulindilaje yunji?”
4 Nayo Yesu yakawapitula yakatya, “Mubite mukamulonjele Yohana zose zono mukuzihulika no kuziwona,
5 vyono i wabofu wakuwona kanyhi, we vitewe nawo, wakujenda, nawo wono wena wukoma, wakwezwa, na we ciziwi nawo wakuhulika, kanyhi sunga na wono wafwiye, nawo wakuzukulwa, ne waciwa nawo wakuganulilwa u Luganuzi Luswanu.
6 Holeka, yo mumoti u munhu mono si yakuyiwona nghani yo yose yimbi muli nene.”

7 Ale, i wanhu wawo wono watumwa na Yohana, wali weza kuwa watuluka, baho Yesu yakawa kwanza kugalonjela i mapuga ge wanhu, i zinghani za Yohana, yakutya, “Alu mwene mwabitaga kulanga cici kuko ku nyika? Lidete lyono likuyumvywa ne mbeho?
8 Alo mwabitaga ho mukawone ci cici? Mwabitaga ho mukamulanje u munhu mono yavwalile i vivwalo vye cimalidadi? Ninga i wanhu wono wavwalaga i vivwalo vye cimalidadi wekalaga mu zikaya ze watemi.
9 Holeka alu anye mwabitaga ho muwone ci cici? Alo u Muwizi?” Heya mbeka u munhu mono yamucizize muno u muwizi.

10 “Yuyu yo yalongwa mu Mandiko Gono Gelile vyono,

Lanjenyi ta,

nghumutuma u mutumwa wangu,

ho yakulongolele no kukunozelezeza i nzila yako.”

11 Nane nghumulonjelenyi mbeka, vyono mu wana wose we wanhu, yakali nejela mono muwaha yamucizize Yohana Mubaputizi. Hamba nhavivyo, ayulya mono yakwijela nha yo mudodo muno mu wutemi wa ku wulanga, yo muwaha yamucizize yuyo Yohana.
12 Kuwulawilila mu vilo vya Yohana sunga nalu, u Wutemi wa ku wulanga wukwinjilwa kwe vilungo muno muno, ne wanhu i wahidimbazi, wo wakujela kuwutuga kwe vilungo.
13 Ne mejizo gose ge wawizi no Wulajilize wa Musa, vyose vyawila muli ze madaha gaga.
14 Holeka hono musume kuvumila, ale, yuyu Yohana yo ayulya Eliya mono yali yaze.
15 Ale muhulice, hono mwina makutu!

16 “Alu 'mba niluhwanicizaje u lulelo lulu na cinhuci? Na lunji lwinha i wazelelo wono wali wekaye mu sokoni, nawo wakawa wakwilonjela i cipuga acino na acilya, wakutya,
17 Cimutowelenyi zifilimbi, ninga hamba si muvinile, cimwimbilenyi i zinyimbo zo wukalale, nanye hamba kulila ng'o!”
18 Hambi Yohana lono yaziza, nha yaleka kulya, kanyhi hamba si yang'wezaga divayi, nawo wakatya, “Ayo yena yicisi.”
19 Alu, u Mwana wo Munhu yakaza, yakulya no kung'wa, nawo wakatya, “Mulanjenyi ta ayu munhu mubuguci, kanyhi mung'wa divayi, mwendece we watoweza kodi ne wahodanji. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, i mahala go Mulungu gakuhomelelwa vyono maswanu ku so i metumo gakwe.”

I makaya gono si gehuwiye

(Luk 10:13-15)

20 Baho yakawa kwanza kugatacila i makaya gono, hamba yawe yanoza mapoto menji mugati yago, ninga iwanhu wayo hamba si wasaka kuwukawaniza zo wuhile wawo wubi, yakutya,
21 “Woyi he, kuli'gwe go Kolazini, woyi he, kuli'gwe, go Betesayida. Hambi i mapoto gono ganozezwa kuli'gwe, hono gali ganozezwe ku Tulo na Sidoni, iwanhu wa mumo mhali wavwala katali vivwalo vye magunila, no kwibaka i mavu, ho walajile vyono wanena.
22 Hamba nhavivyo ane nghumulonjelenyi vyono, Mwizuwa lye ning'aniza mbeka anye, molotuga wulipicizwe wuwaha muno si nha Tulo na Sidoni.
23 Nagwe go Kapelanawumi, wukwihwesa vyono kotegulwa sunga ku wulanga mbi? Aa, ng'o, langa kovulumuswa sunga kono kowali wafu! Ku soko i mapoto gono ganozezwa kuli'gwe, hono gali ganozezwe ku Sodoma, mhali likaya lilyo lili baho sunga nalu.
24 Ninga nghumutyenyi mbeka, vyono i lizuwa lye ning'aniza lyolowa lili ciba muno muno kuli wanhu wa Sodoma si nho kwako go mweneco.”

Muze kwangu mucehe

(Luk 10:21-22)

25 Mwipango lilyo Yesu yakatya, “Nghukulumba 'gwe Baba, go Muwaha wo wulanga ne yinze, hambi nha kuwahundika we mahala i zinghani zizi, langa wukawagubulila i wana wadodo.
26 Heya, Baba, vyo wali wendile go mweneco.

27 “Baba yamhela vinhu vyose. Na hasina mono yamumanyile Mwana, langa hono si Baba du, hamba hasina mono yamumanyile Baba, langa hono si Mwana du, kanyhi na wo wose mono u Mwana kokwenda kumumanyiza.
28 Ale, muze kuli'ne anye wose mono mukugazika, na mono muvunice ne mitegulo yenyu, nane nomucehezenyi.
29 Mwiwicilaje u lubano lwangu, mwiyijize kuli'ne, hambi ane nha nili mupolo, kanyhi nili mwinenenyhezi mu nhumbula, nanye motuga wutuzwe mu zinhumbula zenyu.
30 Hambi u lubano lono nghumupelenyi ane, nha lupelu, hamba no mutegulo wangu si muzito yaye.”
