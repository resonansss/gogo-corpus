Matayo 12

Muli ze Sabato

(Mlk 2:23-28; Luk 6:1-5)

1 Mu madaha gago, Yesu yakolaga mu migunda yo wuhemba mwizuwa lye Sabato. Nawo i wejizwa wakwe wakawa wadabuka, hodu, wakawa kwanza kubena i maso go wuhemba, no kulya.
2 Nawo Wafalisayo waza kuziwona zizo, wakawa kumulonjela Yesu wakutya, “Lanje ta i wejizwa wako wakwituma i nghani yono yili mwiko ku witume mwi'zuwa lye Sabato.”
3 Nayo Yesu yakawapitula yakawatya, “Wulenyi, mukali nasoma vyono yavinozanoza Dawudi, hamonga na wono yali yali hamonga nawo, mu cipango lono wali wadabuka?
4 U mwene Dawudi, yenjila mu Nyumba yo Mulungu hamonga ne wayagwe, wakawa kulya i mikate yiyilya yono yali yiwicigwe hali Mulungu. U mwene, hamba wawo i wayagwe, si wali wakomboywe kuyilya i mikate yiyo, langa i wakohani du wali wene.
5 Hamba wulenyi, mukali nasoma mu Wulajilize, vyono mu mazuwa gose ge Sabato, i wakohani wakuwa kuwubena u Wulajilize mu Nyumba yo Mulungu, ninga hamba nhavivyo, si wakuwa kwihwesezwa vyono wena wusutye?
6 Holeka nghumulonjelenyi vyono, bahano hoyuli u muwaha muno, mono yayicizize sunga ne Nyumba yo Mulungu.
7 Mwali muwe mumanyile nhunizo ye zinghani zizi zono zikutya, ‘Nghusaka lusungu, hamba si yikumbiko,’ si mwali muwahose i wanhu wono wasina masutya.
8 Hambi u Mwana wo Munhu, nha yo Muwaha we Sabato.”

U munhu wo muwoko wono wudejepe

(Mlk 3:1-6; Luk 6:6-11)

9 Yesu yakawuka baho, yakabita mu nhang'anilo yawo yo wuhembelo.
10 Na mumo mwali na munhu yunji mono yali yena muwoko wono wudejepe. Baho i wanhu wakamuwuza Yesu wakutya, “Ati, yikomboligwe kumuponya munhu mwizuwa lye Sabato?” Nawo wamuwuzaga nhavivyo, ho watuje i soko yo kumusitacila.

11 Nayo Yesu yakawatya, “Alu citye nhavi, lunji yunji muli nyenye, yawe yena ngholo yakwe yono yowa yigwiliye mwikombo, alu sikosuma kuyilavyaniza mumo mwikombo mwizuwa lye Sabato?
12 Ninga alu, munhu yayicizize ngholo! Holeka si yilemezwe kwituma nghani swanu mwizuwa lye Sabato.”
13 Hobaho yakawa kumulonjela u munhu yuyulya wo muwoko wono wudejepe, yakamutya, “Wugolole u muwoko wako.” Nayo yakawugolola, nawo wukawa muswanu telele nha vyono winha u muwoko muyagwe.
14 Hodu i Wafalisayo wakawa kulawila kunze, wakawa kulongana vyono wosuma kumongomeza Yesu.

U mwitumami u muhagulwa wo Mulungu

15 Ninga Yesu yaza yimanya nghani yiyo, yakawuka i hanhu baho. Ne wanhu wenji wakamutyatya, nayo yakawaponya watamwa wose.
16 Yakawalajiliza vyono walece kuwalonjela wanhu zinghani zakwe,
17 ho zifisilizwe zono yazilonga u muwizi Isaya, zono yatya,

18 “Langa ta, ayu yo mwitumami wangu mono nimuhaguye,

u mwendece wangu

mono yakunyendeza mu nhumbula yangu.

Nane nowuwika u Muhe wangu muli mwene,

nayo koganula ze ning'aniza yangu kuli wanhu wose.

19 Si kowanga, hamba kuzoza,

hamba i lizi lyakwe, si lyohulikwa mu zinzila.

20 Ilidete lyono libinuce, si kolibena yaye,

hamba si kowuzimya u wutambi wono wukulawa lyosi,

sunga kolo yakondya kuyitemya i ning'aniza ye ling'ani.

21 Ne makungugo gowa no witumbilo

ku soko ye litagwa lyakwe.”

Yesu na Belizebuli

(Mlk 3:20-30; Luk 11:14-23)

22 Baho i wanhu wakamuletela u munhu yunji mono yali yali cibofu kanyhi yali cibwibwi, ku soko vyono yali yebatwe ne licisi. Yesu yakawa kumuponya, nayo yakasuma kulonga no kuwona kanyhi.
23 Ilipuga lyose lye wanhu, likezina muno muno, no kutya, “Ati, Wulenyi, alu yuyu mbeka yadahile kuwa Mwana wa Dawudi?”
24 Ninga i Wafalisayo waza kuzihulika zizo wakatya, “U munhu yuyu, kagalavizyaga macisi mu vilungo vya Belizebuli u muwaha we macisi.”
25 Ninga Yesu vyono yali yagamanyile i mahweso gawo, baho yakawatya, “U wutemi wo wose wono witemanuce weneco wose vipuga vipuga vyono si vikwilumvya vyeneco vyose, u wutemi wuwo si wosuma kujendelela hamba hadodo, ne likaya lyo lyose hamba mhuga yo yose yono yitemanuce vipuga vipuga vyono vitemanuye, lilyo lyogwa.
26 Holeka hono Setani yakumuwinga Setani, ale, yakwitemanula mweneco. Alu u wutemi wakwe ho wokwima nhawule?
27 Nha vyono anye mukutya, ati ane nghugawinjila macisi mu vilungo vya Belizebuli, alu ma i wana wenyu wakugawinjila mu vilungo vya nani? Holeka ku soko yiyo, wawo wo womuling'anizenyi nyenye.
28 Ninga hono ane niwe nghugawinjila macisi mu vilungo vyo Muhe wo Mulungu, ale, mumanye vyono u Wutemi wo Mulungu wakondya kumuficilenyi nyenye.

29 “Hamba u munhu kosuma nhawule kwinjila kwa vilungo mu nyumba ya mono yali vilungo, ho yasume kumuboka i viya vyakwe, lono si yamuye kumuwopa yuyo u munhu we vilungo? Langa baho ho kosuma kumuboka i viya vyakwe.
30 U munhu wo wose mono si yakwilunza nane, yuyo yali suta nane, yuyo keyagazaga.
31 Holeka nghumulonjelenyi, nghutya nhavi, i wanhu wosuma kunenelwa u wubi wawo, hamonga no wutovule wawo wose, ninga si wosuma kunenelwa hamba hadodo u wubi wo kumutovulo u Muhe Mono Yelile.
32 Kanyhi sunga na mono kolonga i nghani yo kumutovulo Mwana wo Munhu, yuyo nayo konenelwa, ninga langa mono kolonga i nghani ya kumutovulo Muhe Mono Yelile, yuyo langa si konenelwa hamba hadodo ku wulawa lulu sunga cibitilila.”

U mubici ne mikalo yayo

(Luk 6:43-45)

33 “U mubici hono wuwe muswanu, sunga ne mikalo yayo nayo yowa miswanu, na hono wuwe mubi, ne mikalo yayo yowa mibi. No mubici nawo wumanyicilaga mu mikalo gayo.
34 Nye mo lulelo lwe zinzoka! Alu mosuma nhawule kuzilonga i ziswanu, na kumbe nyenye mo weneco muli wabi?” Ku soko u munhu, nha kalongaga zono zimumemile mu nhumbula yakwe.
35 U munhu muswanu nha kalavyaga i zinghani ziswanu mu ciwutwa cakwe ciswanu, no munhu mubi nayo, nha kalavyaga izinghani zimbi mu ciwutwa cakwe i cibi.

36 “Holeka ane nghumulonjelenyi, nghutya, Mwizuwa lyo wuling'anizwe i wanhu si wololeka kupitula muli ze zinghani zose zono si zinojeye, zono wakuzilonga.
37 Holeka mu wulawilila mu zinghani zako, lunji kovumilwa vyono wuli muswanu, hamba lunji koling'anizwa nha wuli muhodanji.”

U wusaka vitanjilo

(Mlk 8:11-12; Luk 11:29-32)

38 Hanyuma yayo, wanji wa mu wejizi wo Wulajilize hamonga ne Wafalisayo, wakamulonjela Yesu wakutya, “Ase cikusaka ciwone citanjilo ku wulawa kwako.”
39 Nayo Yesu yakawapitula yakawatya, “Ululelo lubi kanyhi lono si lumogopile Mulungu! Mukusaka citanjilo, si mopegwa i citanjilo co cose, langa citanjilo ca Yona u muwizi.
40 Nha vyono Yona yakekala mu'nda ye lisomba liwaha vilo vidatu misi ne cilo, ale nha vivyo u Mwana wo Munhu nayo, si koleka kwikala vilo vidatu misi ne cilo mugati ye yisi.
41 Ne wanhu wa Ninawi, wolokuza he zuwa lyo wuling'anizwe nawo walaluhosa u lulelo lulu nha vyono lusutizye. Hambi Waninawi nha wanena, so wuganule wa Yona. Na kumbe yali baha mono yamucizize Yona!
42 U mutemi we cicekulu wa kutakama kolokuza mu cipango lono u lulelo lulu lweza kuling'anizwa, nayo kololuhosa nha vyono lwina masutya. Hambi u mwene nha yajenda muhinzo mutali, kulawa kutali, yakaza kuzihuliciza zinghani ze mahala no wizuce wa Solomona, na kumbe, ahano hoyuli u muwaha mono yamucizize Solomona.”

U wuwuyajile we licisi lyavu

(Luk 11:24-26)

43 “I licisi lyavu, kolo likuwingwa no kulawila muli munhu, likuwa kujenda jenda mu nyika, likulondola i hanhu ho kuceha, hamba kutuga.”
44 Hanyuma likutya, “Nowuya ku wikalo wangu wono nekalaga. Nalyo kolo likuwuya no kuwagana u wikalo wuwo wusina yunji, kanyhi wuhambigwe,
45 hobaho likubita kugasola macisi ganji mupunghati, gono mabi muno muno kuciza lyeneco, nago gose gakwinjila muli munhu yuyo. Alu nhondo i mikalile yo munhu yuyo, yikuwa mibi muno muno si nha ya halulongozi. Na nhavivyo vyo yowa kuli wanhu wawa i wabi.”

I wanduguze weneco du wa Yesu

(Mlk 3:31-35; Luk 8:19-21)

46 Nayo Yesu lono yali yakali yakulonga ne mapuga ge wanhu, u nyina, hamonga ne wanduguze wakawa waza, no kwima hanze, wakuhila kulongana nayo.
47 Holeka munhu yunji yakabita yakamulonjela Yesu yakutya, “U nyina yako hamonga ne wanduguzo, wali aho hanze, wakusaka kulongana nagwe.”
48 Nayo Yesu yakamutya yuyo munhu mono yamulonjela nhavivyo, yakatya, “Alu yaya yo nani? Hamba i wandugu zangu wo wa nani?”
49 Hanyuma yakawa kwinula muwoko wakwe yakawa kuwasonhelela i wejizwa wakwe akuno yakutya, “Awa wo wa yaya, ne wandugu zangu!
50 Vyono u munhu wo wose mono yakwituma nha vyono yakuhila Baba mono yali ku wulanga, ale, yuyo yo muwaha wangu, yo lumbu lyangu, kanyhi yo nyina yangu.”
