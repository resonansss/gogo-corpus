Walawi 3

Cilavywa co mulamu

1 “Na lono yunji yawe yakulavya ng'ombe monga ya mwi'tewa lyakwe, yiwe cilavywa co mulamu, ng'ombe yiyo hono yiwe nume hamba nyhekulu, yeze nhelevu yisina cilema co cose.
2 Nayo yuyo munya ng'ombe, yajelece muwoko wakwe mu mutwe we ng'ombe yiyo, hanyuma yayiwulajile ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile. Nawo wakohani i wana wa Haluni, wayimize sakami yo mutugo wuwo, mu cikumbicilo mu zimbavu zayo zose vyono yine.
3 Hanyuma wamulavizye Muwaha Mulungu mhama yose ya mu'nda,
4 hamonga ne zifigo ne mhama yayo, ne nhandamala yono yili mwi'toga, yiwe ndigwa ye cilavywa kuli mwene.
5 Nawo wakohani wavikongojele vivyo vyose mu cikumbicilo hamonga ne vilavywa vinyanywe. Ne mhunjile ye cilavywa ce ndigwa yiyi, yikwendeza muno kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

6 “Na hono ngholo hamba mhene yiwe yikwitumamilwa mu cilavywa co mulamu, yidahile yiwe nume hamba nyhekulu, ninga yinojeye yiwe nhelevu yono yisina cilema co cose.
7 Na hono munhu yunji yawe yakulavya ngholo,
8 munhu yuyo yajelece muwoko wakwe mu mutwe we ngholo yiyo, hanyuma yayiwulajile ha mwande ye Lihema lyono lyelile. Nawo wakohani i wana wa Haluni wayimize sakami yayo mu cikumbicilo mu zimbavu zayo zose vyono yine.
9 Ninga mhama yose yo mutugo wuwo, hamonga no mucila wayo wose wono mudumulile ha cigugu ce ngholo, ne mhama yose ya mu wutumbu,
10 ne zifigo ne mhama yayo, ne nhandamala yono yili mwi'toga lyayo vivyo vyose, wamulavizye Muwaha Mulungu.
11 Nayo mukohani mono yakulongoza wuhembi, yavikongojele vyose mu cikumbicilo, yiwe yo ndigwa ndavye kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

12 “Na hono munhu yunji yalavye mhene,
13 munhu yuyo yajelece muwoko wakwe mu mutwe we mhene yiyo, hanyuma yayiwulajile ha mwande ye Lihema lyono lyelile. Nawo wakohani i wana wa Haluni, wayimize sakami yayo mu cikumbicilo mu zimbavu zayo zose vyono yine.
14 Wamulavizye Muwaha Mulungu mhama yose ya mu'nda,
15 hamonga ne zifigo ne mhama yayo, ne nhandamala yono yili mwi'toga lyayo, viwe ndigwa ye cilavywa kuli mwene.
16 Nayo mukohani yavikongojele vivi vyose mu cikumbicilo, yiwe ndigwa ya cilavywa, yono yikwendeza kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Mhama yose yo yo Muwaha Mulungu.
17 Kanyhi hasina Muisilayeli wo wose mono yakomboywe kulya mhama hamba kung'wa sakami. Ne lilajilizo lili munojeye mulikamise cibitilila mu zindelo zenyu zose ho hose hono mowa kubita.”
