Walawi 1

Kuli ze makumbiko ge cilavywa cinyanywe cinhu mututu

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamucema Musa mu wulawa mwi 'Hema lyono lyelile.
2 Yakawa kumulonjela ze malajilizo gono Waisilayeli wanojeye wagatyatye lono wakulavya i makumbiko gawo.

Munhu wo wose lono yakulavya mutugo so likumbiko lye cilavywa cinyanywe, yinojeye yiwe yono yikulawa mu mitugo zakwe mweneco, hono yiwe ng'ombe, hamba mhene, hamba ngholo.
3 “Na hono yawe yakulavya ng'ombe yakwe, yiwe lyo likumbiko lye cilavywa cinyanywe, yimunojeye yalavye ng'ombe nume, yono yisina cilema cinji. Yayilete ha Mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile, no Mulungu nayo ho kosuma kumubocela.
4 No munhu yuyo yimunojeye yawujelece muwoko wakwe mu mutwe wo mutugo wuwo, nawo mutugo wuwo ho wovumilwa ciwe cilavywa co kumunozeza wilamule wakwe.
5 Nayo koyiwulajila ng'ombe yiyo baho hali Muwaha Mulungu. Nawo wana wa Haluni i wakohani, woyilavya sakami yayo kuli Muwaha Mulungu, hanyuma wawe kuyimiza mu cikumbicilo cono cili ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile, mbavu zose vyono yine.
6 Hanyuma yuyo munhu, yayihinze ng'ombe yiyo, yayitemasetemase,
7 nawo Wakohani i wana wa Haluni, wawikanize zingwagu mu cikumbicilo no kuzikoleleza.
8 Hanyuma wawe kuzijeleka mucanyha yo moto zinyama zi nhemase, hamonga no mutwe, ne mhama yayo.
9 Kanyhi yimunojeye munhu yuyo, yasole wutumbu, hamonga ne vima, ne viwoko vye ng'ombe yiyo, yavikweme ne malenga. Nayo mukohani, yacikongoje cilavywa cose hamonga, mu moto mucanyha ye cikumbicilo. Ne mhunjile swanu muno ye cilavywa cico, yikwendeza muno kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

10 “Na hono munhu yawe kulavya cilavywa ce ngholo hamba mhene, yimunojeye yalavye nduwilu hamba mvulati yono yisina cilema cinji.
11 Nayo yayiwulajile ku lubavu lwe sumuka ye cikumbicilo; Nawo Wakohani i wana wa Haluni, wayimize sakami yayo mu zimbavu zose vyono yine, ze cikumbicilo.
12 Nayo munhu kolo yakondya kuyitemasha-temasha, mukohani yayisole nyama yose, hamonga no wutumbu, no mutwe wayo, ne mhama yayo, vyose yavijelece mu moto mu cikumbicilo.
13 Ninga munhu yuyo, yamule yavikweme ne malenga, wutumbu hamonga ne vima. Nayo mukohani ho yalilete likumbiko lilyo hali Muwaha Mulungu, yalikongoje mu moto mucanyha ye cikumbicilo. Ne mhunjile swanu muno ye cilavywa cico, yikwendeza muno kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

14 “Na hono munhu yawe kulavya cilavywa ce ndeje, ciwe co cilavywa cinyanywe, yimunojeye yalavye nghunda, hamba macinda ge nziwa.
15 Nayo Mukohani yayilete ha cikumbicilo, yayinyongotole singo, yawe kuwukongoga mutwe wayo mu moto mucanyha ye cikumbicilo. Langa sakami yayo, yayisuluzize ha mhelo, ha lubavu lwe cikumbicilo.
16 Ninga yawuse lisolelo hamonga ne mazoya gayo, yavitaje ku lubavu lwa kucilima ye cikumbicilo, kono walundaga.
17 Hanyuma yayitandakule ndeje yiyo mabawa gayo no kuyikata, ninga yalece kugadumula mabawa gayo. Hanyuma yawe kuyikongoga yose mu moto mucanyha ye cikumbicilo. Ne mhunjile swanu muno ye cilavywa cico, yikwendeza muno kuli Muwaha Mulungu.


Walawi 2

Cilavywa co wusaje

1 “Na lono yunji yawe yakulavya cilavywa co wusaje kuli Muwaha Mulungu, yimunojeye yalavye wusaje wono wunamuce viswanu. Kanyhi yimunojeye yawuhanzahanze ne mafuta hamonga no wuvumba,
2 ho yawe kuwuleta kuli wana wa Haluni i wakohani. Nayo Mukohani mono yakulongoza wuhembi, yasole yiganza limonga lyo wusaje wuwo, hamonga ne mafuta, no wuvumba wose, yavikongoje vyose mu cikumbicilo, ho ciwe citanjilo cono cikulajila nha vyono vyose vilavywa kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Ne cilavywa ci cino ce ndigwa, co cikwendeza kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
3 No wusaje wono wowa wusigaye, awo wowa sawo ya Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, hambi nha welile, vyono wusigala mu wulawa mu ndigwa yono yilavigwe kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

4 “Na hono cilavywa ciwe ca mukate wono wuteleceligwe mu nyungu, weze mukate mutelece, wono wusina cisase. Kanyhi yeze mikate yono yina njiga mbaha, minozwe no wusaje wono wunamuce, wono muhanzahanzwe ne mafuta, hamba lunji ziwe bisikuti zono njidile mafuta.
5 Na hono cilavywa ciwe ca mukate wono wuteleceligwe mu cikalango, weze mukate wono wutelecigwe no wusaje wono muhanzahanzwe ne mafuta, ninga weze wusina cisase.
6 Wutiwaje wuwo wusaje ho wuwe kujidila mafuta mucanyha yayo, lono wukuwulavya kuli Muwaha Mulungu nha cilavywa.
7 Na hono cilavywa ciwe ca mukate wono mutelecele mu nyungu, mukate wuwo weze wa wusaje wono wunamuce, wuhanzahanze ne mafuta.
8 Nagwe wuwuletaje nha cilavywa, mu nzila yo mukohani mono yo kosuma kuleta cico cilavywa cako ku cikumbicilo.
9 Nayo mukohani yasole cibegulwa cidodo co mukate, yacikongoje mu cikumbicilo, ciwe nha citanjilo cono cikulajila nha vyono, vyose vyakondya kulavywa kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Ne mhunjile ye cilavywa ci cino ce ndigwa, yikwendeza kuli Muwaha Mulungu muno muno.
10 Ne cilavywa cono cowa cisigaye, cowa sawo ye wakohani, hambi nha celile muno, vyono cisolwa ku wulawa mu ndigwa yono yilavigwe kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

11 “Holeka halece kuwa na cilavywa co cose co wusaje cono wukumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu, cono cowa cina cisase. Wulecele lukulu kwitumamila cisase hamba wuci mu ndigwa yono yilavigwe kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
12 Kanyhi weze kumulavizya malimbuko gako Muwaha Mulungu mu mwaka na mwaka, ninga langa wulece kugakongoga mu cikumbicilo.
13 Langa du weze kumbucila kutajila mwino mu vilavywa vyako vyo wusaje, hambi wuwo mwino, nha wo wukulajila i cilagane co Mulungu nagwe. Holeka yikunojeye weze kutajila mwino mu vilavywa vyako vyose.
14 Na lono wukumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu vilavywa vye malimbuko, yikunojeye wulavye cimhombogwa, hamba mhece zo wuhemba, zono fijihwe ku wulawa mu maso gayo.
15 Ninga langa, wujidile mafuta hamonga no wuvumba.
16 Nayo mukohani yasole mhece nyhefu, yazikongoje mu moto hamonga ne mafuta, ciwe cikumbucizo, kanyhi yakongojeze hamonga no wuvumba, yiwe ndigwa ndavye kuli Muwaha Mulungu.”


Walawi 3

Cilavywa co mulamu

1 “Na lono yunji yawe yakulavya ng'ombe monga ya mwi'tewa lyakwe, yiwe cilavywa co mulamu, ng'ombe yiyo hono yiwe nume hamba nyhekulu, yeze nhelevu yisina cilema co cose.
2 Nayo yuyo munya ng'ombe, yajelece muwoko wakwe mu mutwe we ng'ombe yiyo, hanyuma yayiwulajile ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile. Nawo wakohani i wana wa Haluni, wayimize sakami yo mutugo wuwo, mu cikumbicilo mu zimbavu zayo zose vyono yine.
3 Hanyuma wamulavizye Muwaha Mulungu mhama yose ya mu'nda,
4 hamonga ne zifigo ne mhama yayo, ne nhandamala yono yili mwi'toga, yiwe ndigwa ye cilavywa kuli mwene.
5 Nawo wakohani wavikongojele vivyo vyose mu cikumbicilo hamonga ne vilavywa vinyanywe. Ne mhunjile ye cilavywa ce ndigwa yiyi, yikwendeza muno kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

6 “Na hono ngholo hamba mhene yiwe yikwitumamilwa mu cilavywa co mulamu, yidahile yiwe nume hamba nyhekulu, ninga yinojeye yiwe nhelevu yono yisina cilema co cose.
7 Na hono munhu yunji yawe yakulavya ngholo,
8 munhu yuyo yajelece muwoko wakwe mu mutwe we ngholo yiyo, hanyuma yayiwulajile ha mwande ye Lihema lyono lyelile. Nawo wakohani i wana wa Haluni wayimize sakami yayo mu cikumbicilo mu zimbavu zayo zose vyono yine.
9 Ninga mhama yose yo mutugo wuwo, hamonga no mucila wayo wose wono mudumulile ha cigugu ce ngholo, ne mhama yose ya mu wutumbu,
10 ne zifigo ne mhama yayo, ne nhandamala yono yili mwi'toga lyayo vivyo vyose, wamulavizye Muwaha Mulungu.
11 Nayo mukohani mono yakulongoza wuhembi, yavikongojele vyose mu cikumbicilo, yiwe yo ndigwa ndavye kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

12 “Na hono munhu yunji yalavye mhene,
13 munhu yuyo yajelece muwoko wakwe mu mutwe we mhene yiyo, hanyuma yayiwulajile ha mwande ye Lihema lyono lyelile. Nawo wakohani i wana wa Haluni, wayimize sakami yayo mu cikumbicilo mu zimbavu zayo zose vyono yine.
14 Wamulavizye Muwaha Mulungu mhama yose ya mu'nda,
15 hamonga ne zifigo ne mhama yayo, ne nhandamala yono yili mwi'toga lyayo, viwe ndigwa ye cilavywa kuli mwene.
16 Nayo mukohani yavikongojele vivi vyose mu cikumbicilo, yiwe ndigwa ya cilavywa, yono yikwendeza kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Mhama yose yo yo Muwaha Mulungu.
17 Kanyhi hasina Muisilayeli wo wose mono yakomboywe kulya mhama hamba kung'wa sakami. Ne lilajilizo lili munojeye mulikamise cibitilila mu zindelo zenyu zose ho hose hono mowa kubita.”


Walawi 4

Cilavywa so masutya go kusutya wula kumanya

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajiliza Musa,
2 “Yawalonjele wanhu wa Isilayeli vyono, munhu wo wose mono kosutya, no kubena lilajilizo limonga, lya muli malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu wula kumanya, munhu yuyo yowa yimunojeye yatyatye wulajilize awuno.
3 Hono yiwe mukohani mweneco yo yasutya no kuwasutizya wanhu, yowa yimunojeye yalavye nzeku nhelevu, yamulavizye Muwaha Mulungu so wubi wuwo wono yawitumile.
4 Yayilete nzeku yiyo sunga ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile, yajelece muwoko wakwe mu mutwe wayo, yawe kuyiwulajila baho hali Muwaha Mulungu.
5 Hanyuma yayisole sakami nyhefu ya yiyo nzeku, yayitegule mwi 'Hema mugati.
6 Yazabice cidole cakwe mu sakami, yayimize kali mupunghati mwi 'bupa lyo mwenda lye hanhu hono helile.
7 Hanyuma yuyo mukohani yasole sakami yinji kanyhi, yayitajile mu mahembe ge cikumbicilo, mu zinhuzi zose ze cikumbicilo co kufusiza wuvumba mumo mugati ye Lihema. Ne sakami yono yowa yikali yisigaye, yayite hasi ha cikumbicilo ce vilavywa vinyanywe cono cili hanze yo mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile.
8 Nayo mukohani yasole mhama yose ya mu nzeku yiyo, ne mhama ya mu'nda,
9 ne zifigo ne mhama yayo, ne nhandamala yono yili mwi'toga.
10 Mukohani yavisole vinhu vivyo vyose yavikongojele mu cikumbicilo ce vilavywa vinyanywe, yanoze nha vyono kanozaga zi mhama zo mutugo wono wukuwa kuwulagwa so cilavywa co mulamu.
11 Langa nyhingo yayo ne nyama yose, no mutwe wayo, ne vima ne viwoko vyayo, ne nhumbula, ne mapupu gayo, hamonga no wutumbu wayo,
12 avi vyose yavisole yavitegule kunze ye nghambi, i hanhu haswanu hono wagamwagaga mavu, yavikongojele baho mucanyha ye zingwagu.

13 “Na hono yiwe mhuga yose ya Isilayeli wo wasutizye, no kubena lilajilizo limonga lyo Muwaha Mulungu wula kumanya,
14 no wubi wuwo vyono wukumanyika du, ale mhuga yose walete nzeku lwa kamonga, wayilavye cilavywa co wubi. Wayilete sunga he 'Hema lyono lyelile.
15 Nawo wanyamhala we mhuga, wajelece miwoko yawo mu mutwe we nzeku yiyo, hanyuma wawe kuyiwulajila baho hali Muwaha Mulungu.
16 Nayo mukohani yayisole sakami yayo yinji, yayinjize mwi 'Hema.
17 Yazabice cidole cakwe mumo mu sakami, yayimize kali mupunghati ku mwande ye libupa lyo mwenda.
18 Kanyhi yayitajile sakami yinji mu mahembe ge cikumbicilo, mu zinhuzi zose ze cikumbicilo co kufusiza wuvumba mumo mugati ye Lihema. Ne sakami yono yowa yikali yisigaye, yayite hasi ha cikumbicilo ce vilavywa vinyanywe, cono cili hanze yo mulango we Lihema.
19 Hanyuma yasole mhama yayo yose yayikongojele mucanyha ye cikumbicilo.
20 Mukohani yayinozezaje nzeku yiyo, nha vyono kanozaga mu nzeku yono yikuwa kuwulagwa so cilavywa co wubi. Na mu nzila yiyo, mukohani kowa yawanozeza wilamule, nawo wanhu wonenelwa wubi wawo.
21 Hanyuma yayisole nzeku yose mututu, yayitegule kunze ye nghambi, yakayikongojele kuko, yanoze nha vyono kakongogaga nzeku yono kalavyaga so wubi wakwe mweneco. Na cico co cilavywa co wubi so mhuga yenyu yose.

22 “Na hono mulongola yo yasutye, no kuwa musutyi so wubena lilajilizo limonga lyo Muwaha Mulungu wula kumanya,
23 ale, yimunojeye baho baho du lono yakumanya vyono yasutizye, yalavye cilavywa ca mvulati nhelevu yono yisina cilema co cose.
24 Nayo yajelece muwoko wakwe mu mutwe wayo, hanyuma yabite yayiwulajile ku lubavu lwe sumuka ye cikumbicilo kono wawulajilaga mitugo ye cilavywa cinyanywe. Hambi ayi nha cilavywa co wubi.
25 Nayo mukohani yazabice cidole cakwe mu sakami yiyo ye mvulati, yayibace mu mahembe ge cikumbicilo, mu zinhuzi zose vyono yine, hanyuma sakami yono yisigaye yayite baho hasi ye cikumbicilo.
26 Na hanyuma yawe kuyikongoga mhama yayo yose mu cikumbicilo, nha vyono kakongogaga mhama ye mitugo zose, zono zikuwa kulavywa no kuwulagwa so vilavywa vyo mulamu. Na mu nzila yiyi mukohani mo komunozeza wilamule so wubi, no mulongola yuyo konenelwa wubi wakwe.

27 “Na hono munhu yunji wowose yasutye no kubena lilajilizo limonga muli malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu wula kumanya,
28 yimunojeye baho baho du lono yakumanya vyono yasutizye, yalavye cilavywa ce mhene nyhekulu nhelevu yono yisina cilema co cose.
29 Nayo yajelece muwoko wakwe mu mutwe wayo, hanyuma yabite yayiwulajile ku lubavu lwe sumuka ye cikumbicilo, kono wawulajilaga mitugo ye cilavywa cinyanywe.
30 Nayo mukohani yazabice cidole cakwe, mu sakami yiyo ye mhene, yayibace mu mahembe ge cikumbicilo, mu zinhuzi zose vyono yine, hanyuma sakami yono yowa yisigaye, yayite baho hasi ye cikumbicilo.
31 Hanyuma yawe kuyiwusa mhama yayo yose, nha vyono kanozaga mhama ye mitugo zose, zono zikuwa kulavywa no kuwulagwa so vilavywa vyo mulamu. Nayo mukohani yayikongojele mu cikumbicilo. Mhunjile yayo ho yimwendeze Muwaha Mulungu. Na mu nzila yiyi, mukohani mo komunozeza wilamule so wubi wo munhu yuyo, no munhu yuyo konenelwa wubi wakwe.

32 “Na hono munhu yalavye ngholo, yiwe co cilavywa co wubi, ngholo yiyo yiweze ngholo nyhekulu nhelevu, yono yisina cilema co cose.
33 Nayo munhu yuyo yajelece muwoko wakwe mu mutwe we ngholo yiyo, hanyuma yabite yayiwulajile ku lubavu lwe sumuka ye cikumbicilo, kono wawulajilaga mitugo ye cilavywa cinyanywe.
34 Nayo mukohani yazabice cidole cakwe mumo mu sakami ye ngholo yiyo, yayibace mu mahembe ge cikumbicilo, mu zinhuzi zose vyono yine, hanyuma sakami yono yowa yisigaye, yayite baho hasi ye cikumbicilo.
35 Hanyuma yawe kuwusa mhama yayo yose, nha vyono kanozaga mhama ye zingholo, zono zikuwa kulavywa no kuwulagwa, so vilavywa vyo mulamu. Nayo mukohani yawe kuyikongojela mu cikumbicilo, hamonga ne cilavywa ce ndigwa yono yilavigwe kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Na mu nzila yiyi, mukohani mo komunozeza wilamule so wubi wo munhu yuyo, no munhu yuyo konenelwa wubi wakwe.”


Walawi 5

Nghani zono zosakwa so cilavywa co wubi

1 “Vilavywa so wubi, vyosakwa vilavywe so nghani azi zono zityatizye.

“Hono munhu yawe yacemigwe ne mahakama, yakalavye wuhomelezi, kuli zono yamanyile, nayo ho yaleme kulavya wuhomelezi kuli zono yawene, hamba kuli zono yahulice, munhu yuyo, kowa yanojeye yatuje wulipicizwe so nghani yiyo.
2 Hono munhu yapamize cinhu co cose cavu wula kumanya, lunji mudunhu we nimu yono yifwiliye, hodu munhu yuyo kowa yakondya kwiyavufya mweneco no kwinjila mwi'sutya. Nayo kolo yakondya kumanya vyono kumbe kasutya, ale ho kowa musutyi.
3 Hono munhu yapamize cinhu co cose cavu cono cilawile muli munhu wula kumanya vyono cavu, munhu yuyo kowa yakondya kwiyavufya mweneco no kwinjila mwi'sutya. Nayo kolo yakondya kwizuka vyono kumbe kasutya, ale, ho kowa musutyi.
4 Hono munhu yelahize musunho muli cinhu co cose wula kwihwesa, kowa yenjila mwi'sutya kolo yakwizuka du cono yetumile.
5 Na kolo munhu yavumila vyono musutyi, yimunojeye yanene wubi wakwe,
6 kanyhi yimunojeye yalavye ngholo nyhekulu hamba mhene, yiwe co cilavywa cakwe co wubi wakwe kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Nayo mukohani komunozeza munhu yuyo wilamule, so wubi wakwe.

7 “Na hono munhu yawe siyakusuma kulavya ngholo hamba mhene, kosuma kuleta nghunda nyejete, hamba nziwa nyejete kuli Muwaha Mulungu, monga yiwe cilavywa co wubi wakwe, ne nzagwe nayo yiwe so cilavywa cinyanywe.
8 Nayo yavilete kuli mukohani. Nayo mukohani yamule yayilavye ndeje ye cilavywa co wubi. Ninga yayibenaje singo yayo, yalece kuwunyomhola mutwe wayo,
9 yayimize sakami yayo yinji mu lubavu lwe cikumbicilo. Ne sakami yono yowa yikali yisigaye, yayisuluze baho hasi ye cikumbicilo. Ne sakami yiyo, cowa co cilavywa co wubi.
10 Hanyuma lu, ho yasole yi yilya ndeje ye kejete, nayo yayilavye so cilavywa cinyanywe, nha vyono winha wulajilize. Na mu nzila yiyo du, mukohani vyo komunozeza wilamule so wubi wakwe. Nayo munhu yuyo konenelwa wubi wakwe.

11 “Na hono munhu yawe si kosuma kulavya nghunda nyejete, hamba nziwa nyejete, kosuma kuleta kilo monga yo wusaje, wuwe so cilavywa co wubi. Ninga yalece kutajila mafuta hamba wuvumba wo wose mu wusaje wuwo, hambi nha cilavywa co wubi, hamba si cilavywa ca wusaje du yaye.
12 Nayo yawulete kuli mukohani, no mukohani nayo yawuneje yiganza limonga, ciwe co citanjilo vyono wusaje wuwo wulavywe kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Hanyuma yawukongoje mu moto mu cikumbicilo, nawo wowa co cilavywa ce ndigwa. Kanyhi co cilavywa co wubi.
13 Nayo mukohani komunozeza wilamule so wubi munhu yuyo wono yawitumile. Nayo munhu yuyo konenelwa wubi wakwe. No wusaje wono wowa wukali wusigaye, wowa sawo yo mukohani, nha vyono yinha du mu cilavywa co wusaje.”

Cilavywa co wusutye

14 “Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajiliza Musa yakatya,
15 Hono munhu yasutye wula kumanya, so wulemwa kuleta cilavywa co wusutye, cono cisiwanuliywe Muwaha Mulungu, ale, munhu yuyo yanojeye yalete nduwilu nhelevu, yono yisina cilema co cose, hamba mhene yono yisina cilema. Ne cifo cayo, ciweze ce nghango ye mapesa gono gakwitumamilwa mwi 'Hema lyono lyelile.
16 Yahalize ku so wubi wono yawitumile, kanyhi yimunojeye yahalize hamonga no wonjeleze we ligawo lye nghango ye makumi mejete kwe 'gana. Nayo yayilete kuli mukohani, nayo mukohani komunozeza wilamule so wubi, munhu yuyo. Nayo munhu yuyo konenelwa wubi wakwe.

17 “Hono munhu yasutye wula kumanya, yabene lilajilizo limonga lya muli malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu, ale munhu yuyo kowalwa musutyi, yimunojeye yalavye cilavywa co wusutye.
18 Nayo yimunojeye yalete nduwilu hamba mhene nhelevu yono yisina cilema co cose kuli mukohani, ciwe co cilavywa cakwe co wusutye. Ne cifo cayo ciweze ce nghango ye mapesa gono gakwitumamilwa mwi 'Hema lyono lyelile. Nayo mukohani komunozeza wilamule ku so wubi wo munhu yuyo wono yawitumile wula kumanya. Nayo munhu yuyo konenelwa wubi wakwe.
19 Naco cowa co cilavywa co wusutye so wubi wakwe wono yetumile hali Muwaha Mulungu.”


Walawi 6

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
2 “Cilavywa, cinojeye cilavywe lono munhu yamusutizya Muwaha Mulungu, so wulema kumugomolela Mwiisilayeli muyagwe, cinhu cono yaleceywe no muyagwe nha mupinga, hamba cono yamuhizize muyagwe,
3 hamba kwilahiza ko wulambi, yakutya vyono si yaciwene cinhu co muyagwe cono cajiliye.
4 Na hono munhu yasutye nghani monga muli zizi, yimunojeye munhu yuyo yahalize co cose cono yena, ku soko yo wula wihuwilwa wakwe.
5 Na mwi'zuwa lyono kokwijelela vyono musutyi, yowa yimunojeye munhu yuyo, yamuhalize cinhu mututu yuyo munya cinhu, kanyhi yowa yimunojeye yahalize hamonga no wonjeleze we ligawo lye nghango ye makumi mejete kwe 'gana.
6 Nayo yanojeye yalete kuli mukohani, nduwilu hamba mhene nhelevu yono yisina cilema co cose. Ciwe co cilavye co wusutye kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Ne cifo cayo, ciweze ca nghango ye mapesa gono gakwitumamilwa mwi Hema lyono lyelile.
7 Nayo mukohani komunozeza wilamule munhu yuyo so wubi wakwe. No munhu yuyo konenelwa wubi wakwe.”

Wulavye we cilavywa cinyanywe

8 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
9 “Ale wumulonjelaje Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe wutye, cilavywa cinyanywe, cinojeye cikongogwe mu cikumbicilo, cilo cose sunga wele. No moto wa mu cikumbicilo weze kwaka vilo vyose.
10 Nayo mukohani yavwale vivwalo vyakwe vyo wukohani, yavwale musweko we bafuta, hamonga ne suluwale ye bafuta. Hanyuma yazole mavu ga mu cikumbicilo gono gahangahanjile ne mafuta, yagamwaje baho hasi ye cikumbicilo.
11 Hanyuma, yawe kukawaniza vivwalo vyakwe, yawe kugasola mavu gago, yawe kugamwaga hanhu haswanu hono hanojeye gamwagwe.
12 Nawo moto wono wuli mu cikumbicilo, awo weze kujendelela kwaka du, wulece kulecelezwa hamba hadodo wuzime. Nayo mukohani yeze kujendelela kutajila zingwagu mucanyha yayo nhondo zose, hakali mitondo, hamonga ne cilavywa cinyanywe mumo mu cikumbicilo, no kukongoga mhama so cilavywa co mulamu.
13 Ninga yinojeye moto weze kwikala wacile du mu cikumbicilo nhondo zose, wulece kuzimila lukulu.”

Cilavywa co wusaje

14 “Awu nawo wo wulajilize wono wunojeye kuli vilavywa vyo wusaje. Yiwanojeye wakohani walawi, walavye cilavywa co wusaje kuli Muwaha Mulungu, wacilavizye ha mwande ye cikumbicilo.
15 Hanyuma yasole yiganza limonga lyo wusaje ne mafuta, yawunyunyile no wuvumba hacanyha yayo, ho yawe kuwukongoga mu cikumbicilo, ciwe co citanjilo nha vyono wusaje wose walavywa kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Ne mhunjile ye cilavywa cici yikwendeza kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
16 Nawo wakohani wosuma kuwulya wusaje wono wusigaye. Ninga wusaje wuwo, wunojeye wutelekwe mukate wono wusina cisase, kanyhi yinojeye wulilwe i hanhu hono helile, mugati ye luwa ye Lihema lyono lyelile.
17 Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yawakomboleye wakohani, liwe lyo ligawo lyawo mu ndigwa ye vilavywa. Nawo mukate wuwo, welile nha vyono celile i cilavywa co wubi, kanyhi nha vyono celile i cilavywa co wusutye.
18 Na mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza, i wana wose wa Haluni wowa kulya, nalyo lyowa lyo ligawo lyawo lyono lyojendelela, lye ndigwa yono yilavigwe kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Na munhu wo wose mono koyipamiza ndigwa yiyo yono yilavigwe, u munhu yuyo kotamiswa ne ndigwa yiyo ku soko yo wele wayo.”

19 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajiliza Musa wulajilize awu,
20 “Kuli zo witilwe mafuta we wakohani Walawi. Mwi'zuwa lyono yakwitilwa mafuta mukohani Mulawi, yimunojeye yalete kilo monga ya wusaje, yiwe co cilavywa cakwe kuli Muwaha Mulungu, yalavye nusu yayo, hakali mitondo, ne nusu nzagwe, yayilavye cinyamihe.
21 Ninga wusaje wuwo, wunojeye wuhanzahanzwe ne mafuta, wukalinjilwe mu cikalango. Na hanyuma, ho wuwe kuletwa hali Muwaha Mulungu, yiwe nha cilavywa co wusaje. Ne mhunjile yayo yikwendeza kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
22 Na mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza, yinojeye cilavywa cici ceze kulavywa no munhu wo wose wa mu wana wa Haluni, mono yakwitumama mulimo wo Wukohani. Ninga yinojeye cilavywa cico, ceze kukongojezezwa lukulu, hambi nha yikumbiko kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
23 Kanyhi hasina cibegulwa co cose ca mu cilavywa co wusaje, cono mukohani yatelece, cono cosuma kuligwa, langa cinojeye cilavywa cico cikongogwe cose.”

Cilavywa co wubi

24 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa,
25 “Yamulonjele Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wulajilize wuwu kuli cilavywa co wubi. Cilavywa co mutugo wono wukulavywa so cilavywa co wubi, mutugo wuwo wunojeye wuwulajilwe ku lubavu lwe sumuka ye cikumbicilo, i hanhu hono yikuwa kuwulajilwa mitugo ye vilavywa vinyanywe. Na cici co cilavywa cono celile muno muno.
26 No mukohani mono yakwituma mulimo wo kulavya cilavywa cico ce likumbiko lyo mutugo wuwo, yimunojeye yayilile nyama yayo i hanhu hono helile, mugati ye luwa ye Lihema lyono lyelile.
27 Na co cose cono cowupamiza mudunhu wo mutugo wuwo cowa cela. Na hono mwenda wowose wukwile sakami yo mutugo wuwo, yinojeye mwenda wuwo wukakanzizwe i hanhu hono helile.
28 Ne nyungu yo yose yono yotelecelwa nyama yiyo, yinojeye nyungu yiyo yitulwe. Na hono yiwe nyama yiyo yitelecelwe mu sufuliya, ale sufuliya yiyo, yinojeye yikwemwe ne malenga ko kuyikusuwa muno muno sunga yele swee.
29 No munhu wo wose we cilume wa mu mhuga ye wakohani, kosuma kuyilya nyama yiyo ye cilavywa, nayo yelile muno muno.
30 Ninga hono sakami yo mutugo wuwo yinjizwe mwi 'Hema no kwitumamilwa so mulimo wo kunoza wilamule, hodu nyama yayo yilece kuligwa na munhu yaye, langa yikongogwe yose mu moto.”


Walawi 7

Cilavywa co wusutye

1 “Awu nawo wo wulajilize kuli cilavywa co wusutye, cono celile muno.
2 Mutugo wono wukulavywa ku so cilavywa cicino, yinojeye wuwulajilwe ku lubavu lwe sumuka ye cikumbicilo, i hanhu hono ziwulajilwaga mitugo ze cilavywa cinyanywe. Ne sakami yayo yimizizwe mu zimbavu zose vyono yine ze cikumbicilo.
3 Mhama yayo yose, yinojeye yiwuswe yilavywe mu cikumbicilo, hamonga ne mhama ya mu mucila wayo, ne mhama ya mu wutumbu,
4 ne zifigo ne mhama yazo, ne nhandamala ya mwi'toga.
5 Nayo mukohani yavikongoje vyose mu cikumbicilo, ciwe cilavywa kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Naco co cilavywa co wusutye.
6 No munhu wo wose we cilume wa mu mhuga ye wakohani, kosuma kuyilya nyama yiyo. Ninga yinojeye yililwe i hanhu hono helile, vyono yene yelile muno muno.
7 Kanyhi ko wuli wulajilize wono wihwanile na we cilavywa co wubi, ne cilavywa co wusutye, nha cinji, i nyama yayo yikuwa yo mukohani mono yetumile mulimo wuwo wo kuwunozeza wilamule.
8 Sunga ne nyhingo yo mutugo wuwo wono, walavywa so cilavywa cinyanywe, wowa wo mukohani mono yetumile mulimo wuwo we likumbiko.
9 Kanyhi, sunga ne vilavywa vyose vyo wusaje, vyono vikalinjigwe mu nyungu, hamba mu nghalango, vyowa vyo mukohani, mono yetumile mulimo wuwo we likumbiko kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
10 Ninga cilavywa co wusaje cono si cikalinje, hono ciwe cihanzahanzwe ne mafuta hamba ng'o, aci cowa so wakohani wose Walawi. Ninga yiwanojeye wagawane wose muling'ano.”

Cilavywa co mulamu

11 “Awu nawo wo wulajilize wa kuli vilavywa vyo mulamu vyono vikulavywa kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
12 Hono munhu yawe yakulavya cilavywa cono cinha ci cino, so kumulumba Mulungu, yimunojeye yalete cilavywa co mukate wono wusina cisase, hamba mikate yono yijinhaye yono mitelece ko wusaje wono muhanzahanzwe ne mafuta, hamba bisikuti zono zijidiywe mafuta, hamba keki zono nhelece ko wusaje wono muhanzahanzwe ne mafuta. Hamonga ne vivyo vyose, munhu yuyo yaleteleze no mutugo we likumbiko.
13 Ninga hamonga na zizo, munhu yuyo yadahile kulavya hamba mikate yono yina cisase.
14 Kosuma kuleta cibegulwa cimonga cimonga ku wulawa mu mikate ye mawala gose, yiwe co cilavywa cinulwe hali Muwaha Mulungu. Navyo vibegulwa vivyo, vyowa vyo mukohani mono koyisola sakami no kuyimiza mu cikumbicilo.
15 Ne nyama yo mutugo, yinojeye yiligwe mwi'zuwa lilyo du lono linozezwe likumbiko, yinji yilecele lukulu kusigala sunga wele hakali mitondo.
16 Na hono munhu yawe yakuleta cilavywa co mulamu ku so wilahize wakwe, hamba lunji ku soko vyono keyendela mweneco du, ale, si nghani yaye, hamba hono nyama yayo yilece kuligwa yose mwizuwa lilyo lyono yilavigwe, na hono nyama yiyo yisigale, yosuma kuligwa hamba mitondo yaco hakali mitondo.
17 Langa hono nyama yayo yinji yiwe yikali yisigaye sunga lizuwa lye kadatu, ale nyama yiyo yikongogwe mu moto.
18 Vyono nyama yiyo yono yowa yikali yisigaye sunga mwi'zuwa lye kadatu, hono yiligwe, Mulungu nayo hamba si kocivumila cilavywa cico co munhu yuyo. Ne cilavywa cico hamba si cosuma kuwalwa vyono yakondya kuhaliza, langa cowa iwifyo du, no munhu wo wose mono koyilya nyama yiyo, kotuga wulipicizwe so nghani yiyo.

19 “Ne nyama hono yiwe yipamize cinhu cocose cavu, yilece kuligwa yaye, langa yitakamizwe mu moto. Kanyhi munhu wo wose mono si yeyavufye, yuyo yo yadahile kulya nyama ye likumbiko,
20 ninga munhu mono yakondya kwiyavufya, hono yayilye nyama ye likumbiko, munhu yuyo si kowalilizwa kanyhi hamonga ne wanhu wo Mulungu.
21 Kanyhi munhu wo wose hono yayilye nyama yiyi ye cilavywa, lono yakondya kupamiza cinhu cavu, lunji mu wulawa muli munhu hamba mu mutugo, munhu yuyo si kowalilizwa kanyhi hamonga ne wanhu wo Mulungu.”

22 Muwaha Mulungu yakamupela Musa wulajilize awuno,
23 “Kuli wanhu wa Isilayeli yakatya. Walonjele wanhu wa Isilayeli vyono, walecele lukulu kulya mhama ya ng'ombe hamba ya ngholo, hamba ye zimhene.
24 Ne mhama yo mutugo wono wifwiliye weneco, hamba wono wuwulagwe ne zinimu, walece kuyilya. Langa mhama yiyo yosuma kwitumamilwa so mulimo wunji du.
25 No munhu mono koyilya mhama yo mutugo, wono wali mulavywe nha cilavywa ce ndigwa kuli Muwaha Mulungu, munhu yuyo si kowalilizwa kanyhi hamonga ne wanhu wo Mulungu.
26 Ne hanhu ho hose hono mowa kwikala, mulecele lukulu kulya sakami ye zindeje, hamba ye zimitugo.
27 No munhu wo wose mono kobena wulajilize wuwu, munhu yuyo si kowalilizwa kanyhi hamonga ne wanhu wo Mulungu.”

28 Muwaha Mulungu, kanyhi yakamupela Musa wulajilize awuno kuli wanhu wa Isilayeli yakatya,
29 “Munhu wo wose mono yakulavya cilavywa co mulamu, yimunojeye yalete cibegulwa cayo, ciwe co cilavywa cakwe co mulamu kuli Muwaha Mulungu,
30 kanyhi yimunojeye yacilete kwe miwoko yakwe mweneco nha cilavywa ca ndigwa. Yalete mhama yo mutugo wuwo hamonga ne nyama ye ng'hwa, ho yinulwe, yiwe cilavywa cinulwe hali Muwaha Mulungu.
31 Nayo mukohani yayisole mhama yayitakamize mu moto mu cikumbicilo, ninga nyama ye ng'hwa, ayo yowa ye wakohani.
32 Langa nyama yo mugulu we cima co kulume co mutugo,
33 ayo yapegwe mukohani yuyo, mono kokwituma mulimo we cilavywa ce sakami, hamonga ne mhama ye cilavywa co mulamu.
34 Nayo nyama ye ng'hwa yo mutugo wuwo cowa cilavywa cinulwe, hamonga nayo ne nyama yo mugulu we cima co kulume nyinulwe, navyo Muwaha Mulungu yakondya kuvisola ku wulawa mu wanhu wa Isilayeli, no kuwapela wakohani. Ne zinyama zizo, zo wanojeye wanhu wa Isilayeli wawe kuwapela wakohani vilo vyose.
35 Nalyo lyo ligawo lye ndigwa yono yilavigwe kuli Muwaha Mulungu, ndigwa yono yapegwa Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, mwi'zuwa lyono wetililwa mafuta wawe wakohani.
36 Mwi'zuwa lilyo Muwaha Mulungu yawalajiliza wanhu wa Isilayeli, wawapele Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, ligawo lili lye cilavywa. Ne lilajilizo lili linojeye wanhu wa Isilayeli walikamise vilo vyose.
37 Awu nawo wo wulajilize wa kuli cilavywa cinyanywe, ku so cilavywa co wusaje, ne cilavywa co wubi, ne cilavywa co wusutye, ne vilavywa co witilwe we mafuta, sunga ne likumbiko lye vilavywa vyo mulamu.”
38 Muwaha Mulungu yalonga na Musa kwi'tunda lye Sinayi, yakamupela malajilizo gaga, mwizuwa lono yawalonjela wanhu wa Isilayeli wawe kulavya vilavywa vyawo.


Walawi 8

Witilwe we mafuta wa Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe

(Wut 29:1-37)

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
2 “Ale musolaje Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wuwategule sunga ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile. Wuleteleze ne myenda yo wukohani, ne mafuta go witile, ne ng'ombe ye cilavywa co wubi, hamonga na nduwilu nyejete, ne nhoto yono yina mikate yono yisina cisase.
3 Hanyuma wuwakongholele hamonga baho mhuga yose.”

4 Nayo Musa yakanoza nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza yanoze. Na lono mhuga yali yekongholela hamonga,
5 yakawalonjela yakatya, “Alu ane neza kwituma nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yandajilize nitume.”

6 Hodu Musa yakawa kuwaleta Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe ha mwande, yakawa kuwahovuga mu malenga cihembihembi.
7 Hanyuma yakawa kumuvwika Haluni shati ne lisweko lya mucanyha yayo, yakawa kumuwopa no musipi mu ciwino. Yakamuvwika ne efodi, yakayiwopeleza yiyo efodi ko musipi wayo mu ciwino.
8 Yakamuvwika ne cifuko ca ha mhambaga, yakawa kutajila Ulimu na Tumimu mu cifuko cico.
9 Yakamuvwika no mukumbo mu mutwe wakwe, na ha mwande yo mukumbo yakawicila wihambo wa zahabu, co citanjilo co wusiwanulwe, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza.

10 Hanyuma Musa, yakawa kusola mafuta go witile, yakalitila Lihema lyono lyelile hamonga ne vinhu vyose vyono vili mugati yalyo. Na mu nzila yiyo, yakawa kulyeza so Muwaha Mulungu.
11 Yakasola kanyhi mafuta ganji go witile, yakagamiziza mu cikumbicilo kali mupunghati, hamonga na mu viya vyayo, na mu ciya ce shaba co wizuguzizo, ne litende lyayo, ho yalyeze so Muwaha Mulungu.
12 Musa yakamusiwanula Haluni ko kumwitila mafuta go witile mu mutwe wakwe.
13 Hanyuma Musa ho yakawa kuwasejeleza ne wana wa Haluni ha mwande, yakawa kuwavwika zi shati, yakawawopa ne misipi mu viwino vyawo, yakawavwika ne zikofiya mu mitwe yawo, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza.

14 Hanyuma, Musa yakawa kuyileta ng'ombe so cilavywa co wubi. Nayo Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wakayijeleka miwoko yawo mu mutwe we ng'ombe yiyo.
15 Musa yakawa kuyisola ng'ombe yiyo, yakawa kuyiwulaga. Yakayisola sakami yayo, yakayibaka mu mahembe ge cikumbicilo gose vyono mane kwe cidole cakwe, yakaceza cikumbicilo. Hanyuma i sakami yono yali yikali yisigaye, yakawa kuyita baho hasi ye cikumbicilo. Na mu nzila yiyi, yakawa yacisiwanula no kuceza cikumbicilo.
16 Hodu, Musa yakawa kuyisola mhama yose ya mu wutumbu, ne nhandamala ya mwi'toga, ne mhama ya mu zifigo ne zifigo zayo, yakawa kuvikongoga vyose mu cikumbicilo.
17 Yakawa kusola kanyhi sunga ne nyama yono yali yisigaye, hamonga ne nyhingo yayo, no wutumbu wayo, yakawa kubita kuvikongojela kunze ye nghambi, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza.

18 Hanyuma kanyhi Musa yakasola nduwilu ye cilavywa cinyanywe. Nayo Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, wakajeleka miwoko yawo mu mutwe we nduwilu yiyo.
19 Musa yakawa kuyiwulaga, yakayisola sakami yayo, yakayimiziza mu cikumbicilo mu zimbavu zayo zose vyono yine.
20 Yakawa kuyisola nyama ye nduwilu, yakawa kuyitemasa.
21 Yakawa kuwusola wutumbu wayo, yakawa kuwukwema ne malenga hamonga ne migulu yayo. Hanyuma yakawa kuyisola mhama, hamonga no mutwe, na vinji vyose vyono vyali visigaye vya mu mudunhu we nduwilu, yakawa kuviticimiziza mu cikumbicilo, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza. Ne cilavywa cicino cinyanywe cikawa co cilavywa ca ndigwa. Ne mhunjile yayo yikwendeza kuli Muwaha Mulungu.

22 Hanyuma Musa yakawa kuyileta nduwilu ye kejete, yono yali cilavywa so wusiwanule. Nayo Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe wakajeleka miwoko yawo mu mutwe we nduwilu yiyo.
23 Musa yakawa kuyiwulaga, yakayisola sakami yayo, yakamubaka Haluni ku sonje ye likutu lyakwe lyo kulume, na mwi'dole kululu lyo muwoko wo kulume, na mwi'dole kululu lyo mugulu wakwe wo kulume.
24 Hanyuma yakawa kuwasola i wana wa Haluni, nawo yakawabaka sakami mu visonje vye makutu gawo go kulume, na mu madole kululu ge miwoko yawo yo kulume, na mu madole kululu ga mu migulu yawo yo kulume. Musa yakawa kuyisola sakami yono yali yisigaye, yakayimiziza mu cikumbicilo mu zimbavu zayo zose vyono yine.
25 Hanyuma yakawa kuyisola mhama, no mucila wayo, ne mhama yono yifunicize wutumbu, ne nhandamala yono yili mwi'toga, ne zifigo, ne mhama yayo hamonga ne cima co mugulu wo kulume.
26 Yakasola no mukate monga mu nhoto yono yina mikate yono yisina cisase, yono milavywe kuli Muwaha Mulungu, no mukate monga, munoze hamonga ne mafuta, na bisikuti monga, yakavijeleka mucanyha ye mhama ne cima co mugulu wo kulume.
27 Yakawa kumupela Haluni vinhu vivi vyose, hamonga ne wanagwe. Nayo yakawa kuvinula, ho viwe cilavywa cinulwe hali Muwaha Mulungu.
28 Hanyuma kanyhi Musa ho yakawa kuvisola ku wulawa mu miwoko yawo, yakawa kuvikongoga mu cikumbicilo, mucanyha ye cilavywa cinyanywe, ciwe co cilavywa co wusiwanule. Navyo vyali co cilavywa ce ndigwa, ne mhunjile yayo yikwendeza kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
29 Hanyuma Musa yakawa kuyisola nyama ye ng'hwa, yakawa kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu, ho yiwe cilavywa cinulwe. Nayo nduwilu yiyo, yikawa lye 'gawo lya Musa ku wulawa mu mulimo wo wusiwanule. Nayo Musa yakanoza zose, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza.

30 Musa yakawa kusola mafuta go witile ne sakami ndodo, yono yali yili mu cikumbicilo, yakawa kuwamiziza Haluni, ne wanagwe, hamonga na mu myenda yawo. Na mu nzila yiyino, yakawa yawasiwanula so Muwaha Mulungu Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, sunga ne myenda yawo yose yo wukohani.

31 Hanyuma Musa yakawa kuwalonjela Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, yakawatya, “Ale, musole nyama yiyi, muyiwice ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile. Muyitelece, hanyuma muyilile baho du, hamonga no mukate wono wuli mu nhoto ye vilavywa vyo wusiwanule, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalajiliza.
32 Ninga nyama yoyose, hamba mukate, vyono movisigaza, nhondo muvikongogaje vyose mu moto.
33 Kanyhi mulece kuwuka baho ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile, sunga kolo vyakondya kupuluta vilo mupunghati sunga vifisilizwe vilo vyo wusiwanulwe wenyu.”

34 “Hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha yacilajiliza, cizitume zizino zono cizitumile zuwali, nha cinji cimunozezenyi wilamule.
35 Holeka munojeye muwe baho du, ha mulango we Lihema vilo mupunghati cilo na misi, mukufisiliza zono Muwaha Mulungu yalajilize. Na hono mulece kuzifisiliza, mofwa. Nazo zizi zo Muwaha Mulungu yandajilize.”
36 Nayo Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, waketuma zose nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yazilajiliziza muli Musa.


Walawi 9

Haluni yakulavya makumbiko

1 Vyali vyeza kupuluta vilo mupunghati, vyo wusiwanulwe wawo, mwizuwa lyo munana, Musa yakawa kuwacema Haluni ne wanagwe, hamonga ne wanyamhala wa Isilayeli.
2 Yakawa kumulonjela Haluni yakatya, “Ale, wulete nzeku hamonga ne nduwilu nhelevu vyono visina cilema co cose, wuvilavye kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Wulavye nzeku so cilavywa co wubi, nayo nduwilu yiwe so cilavywa cinyanywe.
3 Hanyuma wuwalonjele nawo wanhu wa Isilayeli, walete mvulati so cilavywa co wubi, kanyhi walavye ne nyehe ya mwaka monga, ne nyana ye ngholo ya mwaka monga, zono nhelevu zisina cilema co cose, viwe so cilavywa cinyanywe.
4 Walavye kanyhi nzeku ne nduwilu, so cilavywa co mulamu. Kanyhi yiwanojeye walavye likumbiko lilyo kuli Mulungu hamonga ne cilavywa co wusaje wono muhanzahanzwe ne mafuta. Yiwanojeye wazitume zizi, vyono Muwaha Mulungu kokwilajila kuli wene izuwali.”

5 Nawo wakawa kuvileta vinhu vyose ha mulango we Lihema, nha vyono Musa yalajiliza. Ne wanhu wose wakehanguliza hamonga ho wamuhembe Muwaha Mulungu.
6 Nayo Musa yakawalonjela yakatya, “Muwaha Mulungu kamulajilizenyi mwitume zizi zose, holeka ne nhojo yakwe yomulawilenyi.”
7 Hanyuma yakamulonjela Haluni yakamutya, “Ale, wubite ha cikumbicilo, wukalavye cilavywa co wubi, hamonga ne cilavywa cinyanywe, so wubi wako winozeze wilamule, hamonga no wubi we wanhu. Wulavye cilavywa cico wuwanozeze wanhu wilamule, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalajilize.”

8 Nayo Haluni yakawa kubita ha cikumbicilo yakawa kuyiwulaga nzeku yono yali so cilavywa co wubi wakwe mweneco.
9 Nawo wanagwe, wakawa kumupeleza sakami, nayo yakawa kuzabika cidole cakwe mu sakami, yakawa kuyimiza mu mahembe, mu zinhuzi zose ze cikumbicilo. Na yono yali yisigaye, yakayita hasi ye cikumbicilo.
10 Hanyuma yakawa kusola mhama, ne zifigo, ne nhandamala ya mwi'toga, yakawa kuvikongoga mucanyha ye cikumbicilo, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.
11 Ninga nyama yose ne nyhingo yayo, avyo yakavikongojela kunze ye nghambi.

12 Hanyuma yakawa kuwuwulaga mutugo wono wali so cilavywa cinyanywe. Nawo i wanagwe, wakawa kumupeleza sakami. Nayo yakawa kuyimiza mu mbavu zose vyono yine ze cikumbicilo.
13 Hanyuma wakawa kumupeleza mutwe wo mutugo, hamonga ne zinyama zinji, nayo yakawa kuvikongojela vyose mu cikumbicilo.
14 Yakawa kuwusola wutumbu ne vima vyayo yakavikwema, yakawa kuvikongojela mucanyha ye cikumbicilo hamonga ne cilavywa cinyanywe cono cali cisigaye.

15 Nhondo ho yakawa kusola vilavywa vye wanhu, yakawa kuvilavya. Nha cinji, yakavisola mhene yono yali so wubi we wanhu yakayiwulaga, yakayilavya mu nzila yono yetumamila mu wulavya cilavywa so wubi wakwe mweneco.
16 Yakawa kusola no mutugo we cilavywa cinyanywe, nawo yakawulavya nha vyono wali winha wulajilize.
17 Yakaleta ne cilavywa co wusaje, yakawumemya mwi 'ganza, yakawukongoga mu cikumbicilo. Na yiyi yali nyonjeza ye cilavywa cinyanywe ce nhondo zose.
18 Hanyuma yakawa kuyiwulaga nzeku, hamonga ne nduwilu, so cilavywa co mulamu kuli wanhu. Nawo wanagwe, wakawa kumupeleza sakami, nayo yakawa kuyimiza mu zimbavu zose vyono yine ze cikumbicilo.
19 Hanyuma yakawa kuyisola mhama yose ye nzeku, yakayiwika mugati mu ng'hwa yayo, mhama ye nduwilu, nayo yakayiwika mugati mu ng'hwa yayo.
20 Yakawa kuvisejeleza ha cikumbicilo. Yakasola mhama zayo yakazikongoga mu cikumbicilo.
21 Ninga zi ng'hwa hamonga ne cima co mugulu wo kulume, Haluni, yakavinula, vikawa cilavywa cinulwe hali Muwaha Mulungu, navyo vikawa vye wakohani, nha vyono yalajilizwa Musa.

22 Nayo Haluni yali yeza kuwukondya mulimo wuwo wo kulavya cilavywa co wubi, ne cilavywa cinyanywe, ne vilavywa vyo mulamu, yakawa kuwagololela wanhu miwoko yakwe yakawa kuwalugutiza, hodu yakawa kutima mu wulawa mu cikumbicilo.
23 Musa na Haluni wakenjila mwi 'Hema. Na lono walawilaga kunze ye Lihema, wakawalugutiza wanhu, ne nhojo yo Muwaha Mulungu yikawalawila wanhu wose.
24 Nayo Muwaha Mulungu yakagumila moto nhinicizo, wukacitakamiza cilavywa cinyanywe hamonga ne mhama yose ya mu cikumbicilo. Nawo wanhu wali weza kuziwona zizo, wakazozeza wose hamonga, wakafugama, wakenamya wusu wawo sunga ho hasi.


Walawi 10

U wubi wa Nadabu na Abihu

1 Nawo wana wa Haluni, Nadabu na Abihu, wakasola munhu ne zaye lyakwe, wakapala moto munhu na wakwe, wakawa kusola wuvumba wakawutajila mu myoto yawo, wakawa kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu. Ninga wuwo moto wono wene wawupala, si wali moto wono welile, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha si yali yawalajilize wetume zizo.
2 Holeka Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kuwagumilila moto nhinicizo, wukawa kuwawulajila baho ha mwande yo Muwaha Mulungu.
3 Hodu, Musa yakamulonjela Haluni yakatya, “Gwe Haluni, ayi yo nghani yono Muwaha Mulungu yayilongaga, lono yatya nhavi, ‘Munhu wo wose mono yakunyitumamila, yimunojeye yawupele cogopo u wele wangu, nane holeka noyilajila nhojo yangu kuli wanhu.’ ” Ninga mwene Haluni hamba si yapitula cinji yaye.

4 Hodu Musa yakawa kuwacema Mishayeli na Elizafani i wana wa Uziyeli, u mwihwawe wa Haluni, yakawalonjela yakatya, “Nzonyi muyidinghule miwili ye wandugu zenyu, muyiwuse mwi Hema lisiwanulwe, mubite mukayiwice kunze ye nghambi.”
5 Nawo wakaza wakawa kuyidinghula miwili, wakawa kuyitegula kunze ye nghambi, nha vyono Musa yalajiliza.

6 Hanyuma Musa yakamulonjela Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, Eleyezali na Itamali, yakawatya, “Mulece kuzileka zimvwili zenyu zikale si swamule, hamba mulece kuyidemulanga myenda yenyu muwe kulajila vyono mwina cililo. Vyono hono munoze nhavivyo du, mofwa, kanyhi moleka no Muwaha Mulungu yawakolelwe likuwo mhuga yose. Langa wayenyu Waisilayeli wose wakomboywe, wasumile kulila ku soko ye lifwa lili, lyono liletigwe no moto, wono Muwaha Mulungu yawugumiye.
7 Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kuwuka ha mulango we lihema, hono muwuce, hodu mofwa, hambi anye nha mwasiwanulwa so mafuta go witile go Muwaha Mulungu.” Nawo waketuma, nha vyono Musa yalonga.

Malajilizo kuli Wakohani

8 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Haluni yakatya,
9 “Mulecele lukulu kwinjila mwi 'Hema lyono lyelile lono mwakondya kung'wa divayi mwakolwa, gwegwe hamba wanago. Vyono hono mwinjile, mofwa. Ne lilajilizo lili linojeye likamiswe muno mu zindelo zenyu zose.
10 Kanyhi munojeye mwiyijize kuzisiwanula, muli zono zimunojeye Mulungu, na muli zono zinojeye mu witumamilwe we nhondo zose, kanyhi kuzisiwanula muli zono zelile, na muli zono si zelile.
11 Kanyhi munojeye muwejize wanhu wa Isilayeli, imalajilizo gose gono namupelenyi mu nzila ya Musa.”

12 Musa yakamulonjela Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, Eleyezali na Itamali, yakatya, “Ale, musole cilavywa co wusaje, cono cisigaye ku wulawa mu ndigwa yono yalavywa kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Munoze mukate wono wusina cisase, muwulile baho ha cikumbicilo. Hambi i cilavywa cici, nha celile muno.
13 Muwulile baho i hanhu hono helile, hambi nha lyo ligawo lyako hamonga ne wanago, ligawo lyono lilawiliye mu ndigwa ndavywe kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Na vyo Muwaha Mulungu yandajilize 'ne.
14 Ninga anye ne zimhuga zenyu, molya nyama ye ng'hwa, hamonga ne cima, vyono vinuywe hali Muwaha Mulungu, hambi nha lyo ligawo kuli wakohani. Nanye mosuma kuvilila hanhu ho hose hono helile. Hambi vilavywa vivi, nha kupegwa gwegwe, hamonga ne wanago we cilume na we cicekulu, ligawo lyako lyono likulawilila mu vilavywa vyo mulamu, kuli wanhu wa Isilayeli.
15 Yiwanojeye walete cico cima cinulwe hamonga ne nyama ye ng'hwa, sinjiswe mu cipango cono mhama yikulavywa nha cilavywa ce ndigwa sinjiswe hali Muwaha Mulungu. Na gago gowa go magawo gako, gwegwe hamonga ne wanago cibitilila, nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yalajilize.”

16 Musa yakawuza ze mhene yono yali so cilavywa co wubi, nayo yakamanya vyono mhene yiyo yakondya kukongogwa. Na ku soko yiyo, Musa yakawakolelwa likuwo muno muno Eleyezali na Itamali. Musa yakawawuza yakatya,
17 “Alo nhawule si mucililiye cilavywa co wubi mu hanhu hono helile? Hambi nha celile muno. Nayo Muwaha Mulungu kamupelenyi, ho mwitumamile so mulimo wo kudinghula wubi we zimhuga no kuwanozeza wilamule hali Muwaha Mulungu?
18 Vyono sakami yayo si yaletwa mugati ye Lihema lyono lyelile, holeka yali yinojeye mucilile mumo mu hanhu hono helile, cico cilavywa, nha vyono nalajiliza.”

19 Nayo Haluni yakapitula yakatya, “Wule! Hono nali nilye cilavywa co wubi izuwali, Muwaha Mulungu yali yendezwe? Hambi wanhu nha waleta cilavywa co wubi kuli Muwaha Mulungu izuwali, kanyhi waleta ne cilavywa cawo cinyanywe, holeka zizi ziwa kunyagana.”
20 Nayo Musa yali yaza kuzihulika zizo, hodu yakendezwa nazo.


Walawi 11

Vilimu vyono vinojeye kuligwa

(Kum 14:3-21)

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakawapela Musa na Haluni malajilizo
2 gono getyatizye, ku so wanhu wa Isilayeli, yakatya, Mosuma kulya nyama ye zinimu zose mono'nze.
3 Nimu zono zina mabangho gatemanuce, na zono zina magulu gapanduce, kanyhi zono zikusindula, zizo zo mudahile kulya.
4 Ninga mulecele lukulu kulya ngamiya,
5 hamba mhimbi,
6 ne sungula wugombe. Azi zose nha nyavu hali 'nye. Hamba ziwe zisindulaga, ninga mabangho gazo si gatemanuce yaye.
7 Kanyhi mulece kulya ngubi, hambi nha si zelile kuli 'nye, hamba ziwe zina mabangho gatemanuce, ninga sizisindulaga yaye.
8 Holeka mulecele lukulu kulya zinimu zizino, hamba kuyipamiza midunhu yazo, hambi nha nyavufywe.

9 Ninga mosuma kulya zisomba ze mawala gose, zono zina malebeto ne makaka.
10 Langa, cilimu co cose cono cikalaga mu malenga, cono cisina malebeto ne makaka, aco mulece kulya avi iwifyo kuli'nye.
11 Vilimu vye liwala lilyo, si vitelevu yaye. Holeka mulecele lukulu kuvilavya, hamba kuyipamiza midunhu yavyo.
12 Holeka mulecele lukulu kulya cinhu co cose cono cikalaga mu malenga, cono cisina malebeto ne makaka.
13 Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kulya zindeje ze liwala alino, hambi nha iwifyo kuli'nye, 'ndi, numba, mwanziwa,
14 nyaciko, nyacikwanghwala ku luto lwayo.
15 Ne mahowe gose ku luto lwayo.
16 Ne nyacihema ne maluwatu, no mulundi, ne mwewe
17 No mugulo, ne limulowa, ne nhuwi,
18 ne limuzinji, no mujimbo, ne mwambogo.
19 Ne mhuje, ne nyhinyinha, ne li mung'wa ne libudibudi.

20 Navyo vitambala hasi vyose vyono vina mabawa, vyono vijendaga ne migulu mine, vivyo navyo iwifyo kuli'nye.
21 Ninga, avi vitambala hasi vinji, vyono navyo vina mabawa, kanyhi vikujenda ne migulu mine, kanyhi vina ne milundi, ne vima mu mazayo gayo vyo kuzumhila zumhila, avyo mosuma kulya.
22 Kanyhi mudahile kulya zinzije, nhendelefute, nyenye, na mhanzi.
23 Ninga vitambala hasi vyose, vyono vina mabawa avyo langa iwifyo kuli'nye.

24 Na muli wose, mono koyipamiza midunhu ye vilimu avino vyono vityatizye, yuyo kowa yeyavufya, sunga kono lyoswela lizuwa.
25 Vilimu vyose vyono visindulaga vyono vina mabangho, na vyono vina magulu gapanduce.
26 Ne vilimu vyose vyono vina migulu mine, hamonga ne makombazala.
27 Munhu wowose mono kodinghula mudunhu wavyo, yimunojeye munhu yuyo yayikanze myenda yakwe.
28 Ninga hamba nhavivyo, kojendelela kuwa yeyavufye sunga kono lyoswela lizuwa.

29 Navyo vitambala hasi vyono vitambalaga kononze, vyono vyowa iwifyo kuli'nye nha vyovi. Funde, ne ngule, ne mbulu,
30 ne nhunu, nyhodo, ne libandagazi, ne likaka, no lwivu.
31 Na muli wose mono koyipamiza midunhu yavyo, kowa yeyavufya sunga kono lyoswela lizuwa.
32 Na hono midunhu yazo yigwile muli cinhu co cose, i cinhu cico naco, hodu cowa cavufywa. Ne nghani yiyino, yowa yili muli vinhu vye mawala gose, iviya vye mabici, myenda, nyhingo, hamba mhinda hamba cinhu cocose du cono citumamilwaga, hamba ciwe vyono cinha, cinhu cico cinojele cizabikwe mu malenga, hamba nhavivyo cowa cavu sunga kono lyoswela lizuwa.
33 Na hono midunhu yavyo yigwile mu nyungu, hodu vyono vili mumo vyowa vyawa iwifyo, holeka yowa yikunojeye wuyitule nyungu yiyo.
34 Ne ndigwa yoyose yono yali yinojeye yiligwe, ninga ho yiwe yimwajiciywe malenga gono gakulawa mu njilo yono yina liwifyo, ne ndigwa yiyo yaweza iwifyo, ne malenga gono gali mu njilo yiyo gose gaweza iwifyo.
35 Ne ciya cocose cono cogwilwa ne midunhu yiyo, ciya cico cowa iwifyo, lunji nyungu hamba mafigwa, yinojeye vinhu vivyo vitulangwe.
36 Ninga muvwilo we malenga, hamba lisima, mono malenga galawile, avyo vyowa vitelevu du, hamba yiwe ivinji vyose, vyono vipamize midunhu yazo, vyawa iwifyo.
37 Na hono cimonga ca muli vitambala hasi vivyo, cigwile mu mbeyu yono yali yakondya kuwikanizwa yikahadikwe, i mbeyu yiyo, yowa nhelevu du.
38 Langa hono yiwe mbeyu yakondya kulowekwa mu malenga, na cinji co cose co mudunhu cigwile mu mbeyu yiyo, hodu mbeyu yiyo yowa yeza iwifyo.

39 Na hono mutugo wo wose wono wunojeye wuligwe, ninga hono wifwile weneco, munhu wo wose mono kowupamiza mudunhu wayo, u munhu yuyo kowa yeyavufya, sunga kono lyoswela lizuwa.
40 Na hono munhu wo wose yejelele yalye mhonje ye nyama yo mutugo wuwo, munhu yuyo yanojeye yakanze myenda yakwe. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, kowa yeyavufya sunga kono lyoswela lizuwa. No munhu wo wose mono kodinghula mudunhu wayo, yimunojeye munhu yuyo yayikanze myenda yakwe. Ninga hamba nhavivyo, kojendelela kuwa yeyavufye sunga kono lyoswela lizuwa.

41 Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kulya i vilimu vyo vyose i vidodododo vyono vikujenda hasi.
42 Hamba viwe vyono vikutambala hasi, hamba viwe vyono vikujenda kwe migulu yavyo mine, hamba vyono vina migulu minji.
43 Holeka mulecele lukulu kwiyavufya so wulya vilimu vye liwala lili.
44 Hambi 'ne, nha yo nene mono nili Muwaha Mulungu wenyu. Holeka munojeye mweze mwelile, hambi 'ne nha nelile.
45 Hambi 'ne nha yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, mono namuwusenyi ku yisi ya Misili, ho niwe Mulungu wenyu. Holeka munojeye mweze mwelile, hambi 'ne nha nelile.

46 Nalyo lilino lyo lilajilizo lya kuli vilimu vyose hamonga ne zindeje, na kuli vinhu vyose vyono vikujenda mu'si.
47 Holeka yinojeye mulanjilile muno muno, ho musume kusiwanula muli vinhu vye liwifyo na vyono vyelile. Na muli vilimu vyono vidahile kuligwa, na vyono si vidahile kuligwa.


Walawi 12

U wezwe we wacekulu, lono wakulawa mu lusawo

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
2 “Walonjelaje wanhu wa Isilayeli wulajilize awu, wutye nhavi, ‘U mucekulu hono yatuje 'nda yasawe mwana we cilume, mucekulu yuyo kowa yeyavufya, sunga vipulute vilo mupunghati, nha vyono kawezaga yeyavufye, lono yali mu vilo vyakwe vyo mwezi.
3 Na mwi'zuwa lyo munana u mwana yuyo yanojeye yategulwe ku calamo, yalame.
4 Kanyhi mucekulu yuyo yajendelele kuwa yali mu wavufywe du, sunga vipulute vilo makumi madatu ne 'datu, lono yakali neyeza mu wulawa mu sakami yo wezwe wakwe. Kanyhi yimunojeye yalecele lukulu kupamiza cinhu co cose cono celile, hamba yalece kwinjila mwi Hema lyono lyelile, sunga kolo vyakondya kufika i vilo vyo wezwe wakwe.’ ”

5 Langa hono mucekulu yasawe mwana we cicekulu, mucekulu yuyo, kokwikala yeyavufye vilo yikumi na vine, nha vyono kawezaga mu vilo vyakwe vyo mwezi. Kanyhi yajendelele kuwa yali mu wavufywe wakwe du, sunga vipulute vilo makumi mutandatu na mutandatu, lono yakali neyeza mu wulawa mu sakami yo wezwe wakwe.

6 Na kolo vilo vyo wezwe vyakondya kufika, hono yiwe so mwana we cilume hamba so mwana we cicekulu, yimunojeye mucekulu yuyo, yaze kuli mukohani ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile. Yaleteleze ne nyana ye ngholo ya mwaka monga, so cilavywa cinyanywe, hamonga ne nziwa, hamba nghunda, so cilavywa co wubi.
7 Nayo mukohani yawe kucilavya cilavywa cico kuli Muwaha Mulungu, no kumunozeza wilamule. Nayo mucekulu, kowa yezwa mu wulawe we sakami yakwe. Nazo zizi zo zinojeye yazitume mucekulu mono yakulawa mu lusawo.

8 Na hono mucekulu yalece kusuma kulavya nyana ye ngholo, hodu, kosuma kuleta nziwa nyejete hamba nghunda nyejete. Na muli zizo, monga yowa so cilavywa cinyanywe, ne nyagwe nayo yowa so cilavywa co wubi. Nayo mukohani komunozeza wilamule. Nayo mucekulu kowa yezwa.


Walawi 13

Malajilizo ku so wutamwa wo wukoma

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakawapela malajilizo gaga Musa na Haluni, yakawatya,
2 Hono lunji munhu wo wose yawe yena wupele hamba wukulutu, hamba wulalawuce, vyono vyosuma kuwa nha wutamwa wo wukoma. Munhu yuyo yanojeye yategulwe kuli Haluni mukohani.
3 Nayo mukohani yalilanje livimbe lilyo. Na hono yijele zimvwili zono zili he 'vimbe, zagaluka zawa nzelu, ne futa ye livimbe likwijela yikombo mu nghuli yakwe, ale wuwo weza wutamwa wo wukoma. Holeka mukohani komwanza munhu yuyo vyono mwavufywe.
4 Ninga hono livimbe liwe yizelu, kanyhi likwijela lisina yikombo mu nghuli, sunga ne zimvwili zayo ziwe si zizelupe, ale, mukohani komukoma munhu yuyo vilo mupunghati.
5 Ninga hanyuma mukohani ho yamulanje kanyhi mwi'zuwa lyo mupunghati, na hono yawone vyono livimbe likali lili nhavivilya du hamba si lipituce liwala, hamba si lyonjelezece yaye, hodu mukohani komukoma kanyhi vilo mupunghati.
6 Nayo mukohani yamulanje kanyhi mwi'zuwa lyo mupunghati. Na hono yawone livimbe likucepuka hamba si lyonjelezece yaye, hodu mukohani komwanza munhu yuyo vyono si mwavufywe yaye, langa lyali yivimbe du. Nayo munhu yuyo yayikanze myenda yakwe no kwiyeza.
7 Langa livimbe hono liwe likujendelela kuvimba kanyhi, lono mukohani yali yakondya kumulanga munhu yuyo, no kwanzwa zo wezwe wakwe, ale munhu yuyo yowa yimunojeye yelajile kanyhi kuli mukohani.
8 Nayo mukohani yamulanje kanyhi, na hono mukohani yawone vyono livimbe likonjelezeka, hodu komwanza munhu yuyo vyono mwavufywe, yawa nhamwa yo wukoma.

9 Na hono munhu wo wose yawe yena wutamwa wo wukoma, yimunojeye munhu yuyo yaletwe kuli mukohani,
10 ho yalangwe. Na hono hawe holili yivimbe yizelu mu nghuli yakwe munhu yuyo, lyono lina mvwili nzelu hamonga no wufila,
11 ale lilyo lyakondya kuwa wutamwa wo wukoma. Nayo mukohani komwanza munhu yuyo vyono mwavufywe. Hamba howa hasina kanyhi soko yo kukomwa ne wayagwe. Hambi nha yaweza mwavufywe cagalanze.
12 Na hono wutamwa wuwe wakondya kumutandala munhu mu muwili wose ku wulawilila ku migulu sunga ko ku mutwe,
13 ale, mukohani yamulanje kanyhi. Na hono mukohani yawone vyono, mbeka wutamwa wutandeye mu muwili wose, hodu komwanza munhu yuyo vyono yakondya kwezwa. Vyono hono nghuli yose yo munhu yuyo yiwe yawa nzelu swee, hodu yakondya kwezwa.
14 Ninga hono livimbe likopozoce, hodu munhu yuyo kowa mwavufywe.
15 Nayo mukohani komulanga kanyhi, na hono yawone livimbe likopozoce, hodu yamwanze vyono mwavufywe. Hambi wukopozoce nha wukulajila vyono awo weza wutamwa wo wukoma, sunga no munhu yuyo yawa mwavufywe.
16 Ninga hono livimbe linoje no kupituka kuwa kanyhi yizelu, hodu munhu yuyo yanojeye yabite kanyhi kuli mukohani,
17 ho yamulanje kanyhi. Na hono mukohani yawone vyono livimbe lyapituka lyawa yizelu, hodu mukohani komwanza munhu yuyo vyono yakondya kwezwa.

18 Na hono munhu yawe yali yena yipu, lyono ninga alu lyakondya kunoga,
19 ninga kanyhi ho yejele yatuga wuvimbe wuzelu, hamba vipelepele vyono vihanjile wuzelu no wudunghu, ale munhu yuyo, yanojeye yabite kuli mukohani.
20 Nayo mukohani yamulanje, na hono mukohani yawone vyono lyahimba yikombo, sunga ne zimvwili zayo zawa nzelu, hodu mukohani yamwanzaje munhu yuyo vyono mwavufywe. Hambi nha lyaweza wutamwa wo wukoma wono wukwamuliziza mwi'pu.
21 Ninga hono mukohani yalilanje yawone vyono zimvwili si zinawa nzelu, kanyhi liwe si lihimbile yikombo, ninga lyakondya kwanza kutukuwala, hodu mukohani yamukomaje munhu yuyo mu wayagwe vilo mupunghati.
22 Ninga hono vipelepele viwe vikujendelela kutandala, hodu mukohani yamwanzaje munhu yuyo vyono mwavufywe, hambi nha yakondya kwibatwa ne nhamwa.
23 Ninga hono liwe si lipituce liwala, hamba si litandaye yaye, langa lisigaye yikovu du baho he 'pu, ale mukohani yamwanze munhu yuyo vyono yakondya kwezwa.

24 Na kuli munhu mono yapiye moto, cilonda co moto, hono ciswelulile, hamba lunji ciwe no wuswelulile wo wudunghudunghu,
25 ale, mukohani yamulanje munhu yuyo. Na hono yijele zimvwili za mu cilonda zisweluliye, na liwe lyahimba yikombo, ale wuwo waweza wutamwa wo wukoma wono wamulizize mu cilonda, so wupye wo moto wono wumunyanyile. Holeka mukohani yamwanzaje munhu yuyo vyono mwavufywe.
26 Ninga zimvwili zayo, hono ziwe si zisweluliye yaye, kanyhi hamba si lihimbile yikombo, ninga lyakondya kwanza kutukuwala, ale mukohani yamukomaje munhu yuyo mu wayagwe vilo mupunghati.
27 Ninga mukohani yamulanje kanyhi mwizuwa lyo mupunghati. Na hono lijele lilonda likujendelela konjelezeka, ale wuwo weza wutamwa wo wukoma. Holeka mukohani yamwanzaje munhu yuyo vyono mwavufywe.
28 Ninga hono liwe si lipituce yiwala, hamba si litandaye yaye, ninga lyakondya kwanza kutukuwala, ale wutamwa wuwo wowa wutamwa wono si wa wukoma yaye. Holeka mukohani yamwanze munhu yuyo vyono yakondya kwezwa. Hambi nha lisigaye lyo likovu du lyo wupye wo moto.

29 Na hono munhu we cilume hamba we cicekulu, yawe yatuga cilonda mu mutwe wakwe, hamba mu cidevu,
30 ale mukohani yamulanje munhu yuyo cico cilonda cakwe. Na hono cilonda cico cijele cihimbile yikombo, ne zimvwili zayo ziwe zawa zili manjano, kanyhi mugati yayo muwe movili viwembe wembe visisili, ale wuwo waweza wutamwa wo wukoma. No munhu yuyo yanzwe no mukohani vyono mwavufywe.
31 Na lono mukohani yawe yamulanga, yawone vyono cilonda si cihimbile yikombo, ninga zimvwili zayo ziwe zikali si zinatukuwala yaye, ale mukohani yamukomaje muli wayagwe vilo mupunghati.
32 Ninga mukohani yamulanje kanyhi mwi'zuwa lyo mupunghati. Na hono yawone vyono lilonda si litandaye yaye, kanyhi lisina zimvwili ze manjano mugati yalyo, kanyhi hamba si lihimbile ng'o,
33 hodu mukohani yamunyonyole munhu yuyo zimvwili zakwe zose, ninga yazinje du hono holili lilonda. Hanyuma mukohani yamukomaje kanyhi munhu yuyo mu wayagwe vilo mupunghati.
34 Ninga mukohani yamulanje kanyhi kolo vyeza kupuluta vilo mupunghati. Na hono lilonda liwe si litandaye hamba si lihimbile yikombo, hodu mukohani yamwanzaje munhu yuyo vyono yakondya kwezwa. Nayo munhu yuyo yayikanze myenda yakwe, nayo kowa yezwa.
35 Ninga lono yakondya kwanzwa vyono yezwa, na kanyhi lilonda lyakwe ho liwuye ne nyuma no kutandala kanyhi,
36 ale, mukohani yamulanje kanyhi. Na hono yijele vyono lilonda lyakwe lyatandala kanyhi, hodu kowa kusina soko kanyhi yo kuwona hono zimvwili zawa zili manjano, hambi munhu yuyo nha yakondya kwijela cagalanze vyono mwavufywe.
37 Na mu wulanje wo mukohani, hono yawone vyono lilonda si likutandala sunga ne zimvwili zinhitu zakondya kucindika, hodu lilonda lilyo lyakondya kunoga, na mukohani yamwanzaje munhu yuyo vyono yakondya kwezwa.

38 Na hono munhu we cilume hamba we cicekulu, yawe yatuga wulalawuce mu nghuli yakwe,
39 mukohani yamulanje munhu yuyo. Hono yijele vyono wulalawuce wuwo wuli buli yono yihanzize no wuswelulile, ale gago go malila-ga-mbuli gono, galalawuce du mu nghuli, no munhu yuyo yelile.

40 Na hono munhu yatuje cipala ku cigosi
41 hamba muhalu, awo si wavufywe yaye.
42 Langa hono cilonda cimwime mu cipala hamba mu muhalu, ale, awo langa weza wutamwa wo wukoma.
43 No mukohani yamulanje munhu yuyo, na hono mukohani yawone cilonda cico cina wuswelulile wo wudunghudunghu,
44 hodu mukohani yamwanzaje munhu yuyo vyono mwavufywe, ku soko yo wutamwa wo wukoma wono yena nawo mu mutwe wakwe.

45 No munhu mono yena wutamwa wo wukoma, yimunojeye yavwale myenda yono yidemukanjice, kanyhi yalece kuswamula ne zimvwili zakwe, kanyhi yagubicize no mwenda mulomo wakwe wa kucanyha, yawe kuzoza yakutya, “Ane nili mwavufywe! Ane nili mwavufywe!”
46 Nayo kojendelela kuwa mwavufywe mu vilo vyakwe vyose vyono yakali mutamwa. Kanyhi yimunojeye munhu yuyo, yekale kunze ye nghambi kutali ne wayagwe.

Wulajilize kuli zo mumvulya

47 Hono hawe howuli mwenda wawe watuga mumvulya, hono wuwe mwenda wa bafuta hamba mwenda wa wuhamba,
48 hamba yiwe mu mwenda we 'wala lyo lyose, lunji mu mwenda we bafuta, lunji wo wuhamba, hamba mwenda we nguwo, hamba muli cinhu co cose cono cinozwe na nguwo.
49 Hono mumvulya wuwo wuwe we 'wala lye dedede, hamba lidunghu, awo weza wutamwa wo wukoma. Holeka yowa yinojeye wulajilwe kuli mukohani.
50 Nayo mukohani kowulanga mwenda wuwo, nayo yawusole yawuwice kutali muno ne wanhu, wikale kuko vilo mupunghati.
51 Nayo mukohani yawulanje kanyhi mwi'zuwa lyo mupunghati, na hono yawone vyono mumvulya wujendeleye kutandala, hodu mwenda wuwo waweza iwifyo.
52 Mukohani yawusolaje yawutakamize mu moto, vyono weza mumvulya wono wukutandala, wono wunojeye wutakamizwe mu moto.

53 Ninga mukohani lono yakucilanga cinhu cico, ho yawone vyono mumvulya si wutandaye mu cinhu cico,
54 hodu yalajilize cinhu cico cikwemwe ne malenga, yaciwice kutali ye wanhu vilo mupunghati kanyhi.
55 Hanyuma ho yacilanje kanyhi, na hono yawone vyono mumvulya wu wulya wukali hamba si napituka liwala, hamba wuwe si wutandaye mu cinhu cico, ninga cinhu cico cowa cikali cili iwifyo du. Nanye munojeye mucitakamize mu moto, hamba cinhu cico ciwe cakondya kuwola mugati yayo, hamba munze yayo.
56 Ninga mukohani lono yakucilanga kanyhi, yagane mumvulya hono ho wuli wakondya kunyala, hodu mukohani yahademulaje baho hono hali ho wuli, mu mwenda hamba mu nguwo.
57 Na hono mumvulya wuwo wijele kanyhi, no kutandala, ale, munya cinhu cico yacitakamizaje mu moto.
58 Na hono yacikweme mu malenga sunga libanhi lyajilile, ale, yowa yimunojeye yawuyajile kucikwema kanyhi lwe kejete, ne cinhu cico, cowa cakondya kwezwa.

59 Nawo wu wuno wo wulajilize wa kuli zo mumvulya wono wuli mu mwenda. Hamba wuwe mu mwenda wo wuhamba, hamba mu mwenda we bafuta, hamba wuwe mu cinhu co cose cono cinozwe na nyhingo. Nawo awu wo wulamuzi wono wunojeye wukamiswe kuli vyose vye liwifyo, na kuli vyono si vye liwifyo.


Walawi 14

U wezwe, lono wakondya kunoga wutamwa wo wukoma

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
2 Aga go malajilizo go wezwe wa kuli munhu mono yakondya kunoga, mono yali yena wutamwa wo wukoma. Mwi'zuwa lono yuyo munhu yakwanzwa vyono yakondya kwela, ale yimunojeye yaletwe kuli Mukohani,
3 nayo mukohani yamusole yamutegule kunze ye nghambi yakamulanje kuko. Na hono yejele vyono wutamwa wakwe wakondya kunoga,
4 hodu mukohani yalajize ziletwe ndeje nyejete zono zelile zono zokwitumamilwa so wezwe, ne cibici co mubici wo muhagata, no mulejehe musisili mudunghu, ne cinhambi ce ezobu.
5 Nayo mukohani yalajilize ndeje monga muli zindeje zizilya zi nyejete, yayiwulajile mu ciya ce lilongo, mucanyha ye malenga momi.
6 Nayo mukohani yayisole ndeje yi yilya nyumi ye kejete, ne cibici ci cilya co mubici wo muhagata, no mulejehe wu wulya musisili mudunghu, ne cinhambi ce ezobu, yavizabice vyose hamonga mu sakami yiyilya ye ndeje yono yiwulagwe.
7 Hanyuma, mukohani yayisole sakami yiyo, yamumizize kali mupunghati, u munhu yuyo mono yakuhila kwezwa mu wulawa mu wutamwa wakwe. Hobaho yamwanze munhu yuyo vyono yakondya kwezwa. Hanyuma yayilecele yiyilya ndeje nyumi, yiguluce yibite mu mawimbi.
8 Nayo munhu yuyo yayikanze myenda yakwe, yacete ne zimvwili zakwe, kanyhi na mweneco yehovuje. Nayo hodu kowa yakondya kwezwa. Nayo lu yadahile kwinjila mu nghambi, ninga yalece kuhelela mwi'hema lyakwe, sunga vipulute vilo mupunghati.
9 Na mwizuwa lyo mupunghati, yazicetaje kanyhi zimvwili zakwe hamonga ne zindevu zose, sunga ne zinyhiti zakwe, ne zimvwili zakwe zose zono zili ako na ako mu muwili wakwe. Hanyuma yayikanze kanyhi myenda yakwe, yehovuje no muwili wakwe. Baho nhondo ho kowa yakondya kwezwa.

10 Na mwi'zuwa lyo munana, munhu yuyo yalete nduwilu nyejete, na ngholo nyhekulu monga, ya mwaka monga, zono nhelevu zisina cilema co cose. Hamonga na kilo yidatu zo wusaje wono muhanzahanzwe ne mafuta. Kanyhi ne mafuta cikombe cimonga.
11 Nayo mukohani yamusole munhu yuyo hamonga ne vilavywa vivyo vyose, yamutegule sunga ko ku mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile.
12 Mukohani yasole nduwilu monga ya muli zizo, hamonga ne mafuta cikombe cimonga. Yavilavye viwe cilavywa co wusutye. Yavinule ho viwe cilavywa cinule hali Muwaha Mulungu.
13 Mukohani yayiwulajile ngholo yiyo i hanhu hono helile, i hanhu hono zikuwa kuwulajilwa mitugo ye cilavywa co wubi, ne cilavywa cinyanywe. Mukohani yanojeye yetume nhavivi, hambi nha vyono cinha cilavywa co wusutye, ale, nhavivyo vyo cinha ne cilavywa co wubi, vyose vikuwa vyo mukohani, hambi nha vyelile muno muno.
14 Nayo mukohani yasole sakami ndodo ye ngholo, yayibace mu cisonje ce likutu lyo kulume, na mwi'dole kululu lyo muwoko wo kulume na mwi'dole kululu lyo mugulu wo kulume, vyo munhu yuyo, mono kokwanzwa vyono yakondya kwezwa.
15 Hanyuma mukohani, yasole mafuta madodo yagajidile mwi'ganza lyakwe lyo muwoko wo kucekulu,
16 yazabice cidole cakwe co muwoko wo kulume yagamize mafuta gago kali mupunghati hali Muwaha Mulungu.
17 Hanyuma yasole mafuta madodo gono gali mwi'ganza lyakwe, yagahanze hanze hamonga ne sakami ye ngholo, yamubace mu cisonje ce likutu lyo kulume, na mwidole kululu lyo muwoko wo kulume, na mwidole kululu lyo mugulu wo kulume vyo munhu yuyo mono kokwanzwa vyono yakondya kwezwa.
18 Mukohani kanyhi yagajidile mu mutwe wo munhu yuyo, i mafuta gono gakali gasigaye mwi'ganza lyakwe. Na mu nzila yiyi mukohani kowa yamunozeza wilamule u munhu yuyo hali Muwaha Mulungu.

19 Hanyuma mukohani ho yalavye cilavywa co wubi no kunoza wilamule. Hanyuma ya zizo, yawulaje mutugo so cilavywa cinyanywe.
20 Yawe kucilavya hamonga ne cilavywa co wusaje, mu cikumbicilo. Na mu nzila yiyi, mukohani kowa yanoza wilamule kuli munhu yuyo. No munhu yuyo nayo, kowa yakondya kwezwa.

21 Na hono munhu yawe muciwa yasina cinji, mono si yakusuma ku wulavya cinji, hodu munhu yuyo, kosuma kuleta nduwilu monga du, so cilavywa cakwe co wusutye, so kumunozeza wilamule. Ho ciwe cilavywa cinulwe hali Muwaha Mulungu so mukohani. Kanyhi kosuma kuleta kilo monga du yo wusaje wono muhanzahanzwe ne mafuta, so cilavywa co wusaje, kanyhi yalete ne mafuta cikombe cimonga.
22 Kanyhi munhu yuyo yalete na nghunda nyejete, hamba nziwa nyejete. Yimonga yiwe so cilavywa co wubi, ne nzagwe nayo yiwe so cilavywa cinyanywe.
23 Nayo yavilete kuli mukohani ha mulango we Lihema, mwi'zuwa lyo munana lyo wezwe wakwe.
24 Nayo mukohani koyibocela yiyo ngholo ye cilavywa co wusutye, hamonga ne mafuta, yavinule, ho viwe cilavywa cinulwe hali Muwaha Mulungu.
25 Nayo mukohani yayiwulaje ngholo yiyo, yasole sakami yayo, yayibace mo munhu yuyo mu cisonje ce likutu lyo kulume, yamubace na mwi'dole kululu lyo muwoko wo kulume, na mwi'dole kululu lyo mugulu wo kulume.
26 Hanyuma mukohani yasole mafuta madodo, yagajidile mwiganza lyakwe lyo muwoko wo kucekulu,
27 hanyuma yazabice cidole cakwe co muwoko wo kulume. Yagamize mafuta gago kali mupunghati, mucanyha hali Muwaha Mulungu.
28 Nayo mukohani, yasole mafuta ganji, yagabace mo munhu. Yamubace mu mulya du mono yamubacile sakami, nha cinji, yamubace mu cisonje ce likutu lyo kulume, na mwi'dole kululu lyo muwoko wo kulume, na mwi'dole kululu lyo mugulu wo kulume.
29 Ne mafuta gono gowa gakali gasigaye mwi'ganza lyakwe lyo muwoko wo kucekulu, yagajidile mu mutwe wo munhu yuyo. Na mu nzila yiyi, mukohani kowa yanoza wilamule kuli munhu yuyo.
30 Na hanyuma yayo, mukohani yayilavye nghunda monga hamba nziwa monga ya muli zi zilya,
31 ho yiwe cilavywa co wubi, ne nzagwe nayo yiwe cilavywa cinyanywe, hamonga ne cilavywa co wusaje. Na mu nzila yiyi, mukohani kowa yanoza wilamule kuli munhu yuyo.
32 Na lili lyo lilajilizo lya kuli munhu mono yena wutamwa wo wukoma, ninga kuli munhu mono si yakusuma kulavya vilavywa vyono vikusakwa so wezwe wakwe.

Mumvulya wa mu zikaya

33 Muwaha Mulungu yakawalonjela Musa na Haluni yakatya,
34 Aga go malajilizo gono ganojeye gakamiswe kuli zo mumvulya wa mu zikaya, wono wutandalaga. (Na aga gali go malajilizo gono wanhu wa Isilayeli wali wanojeye wagakamise, lono wali wakondya kwinjila mu yisi ya Kanani, yono Muwaha Mulungu yawapela wayihale yiwe yawo).
35 Hono munhu yunji yawone vyono Muwaha Mulungu yaleta wutamwa wo mumvulya mu kaya yakwe, ale munhu yuyo, yimunojeye yabite yakamumanyize mukohani nghani yiyo lwa kamonga.
36 Nayo mukohani yalajilize vyono vinhu vyose vya mu kaya yiyo, viwuswe lwa kamonga, lono mwene yakali si nabita ho yakawulanje wuwo mumvulya. Na hono vinhu vilece kuwuswa mu kaya, hodu vinhu vyose vyokwanzwa vyono vyawa iwifyo.
37 Ale nayo mukohani yabite ku kaya yiyo, yakawulanje wuwo mumvulya. Na hono gawe mogali mabandagu mu majinji, ge liwala lye dedede, hamba go wudunghudunghu, gono geza kuhimba na mu majinji,
38 ale, munya kaya yahame mu kaya yiyo, nayo mukohani yayilanjilile vilo mupunghati.
39 Na mwi'zuwa lyo mupunghati, mukohani yabite yakayilanje kanyhi kaya yiyo. Na hono yawone vyono mumvulya wakondya kutandala.
40 Hodu mukohani yalajilize mabwe gose gono gakondya kwibatwa no mumvulya, gakumulwe, gatagwe i hanhu havufywe, kunze ye likaya.
41 Na hanyuma ya zizo yinojeye majinji gose ga mugati, gakung'unhwe wuhiliwe wayo. Ne malongo gayo, gatagwe i hanhu havufywe kunze ye likaya.
42 Hanyuma gasolwe mabwe ganji maswanu, gagomolezwe hanhu hono gawusigwe mayawo. Hanyuma majinji ge kaya ho gahiliwe wupya kanyhi.
43 Na hono mumvulya wuwuyajile kanyhi mu kaya yiyo lono mabwe go mumvulya gakondya kuwuswa, sunga ne kaya yakondya kukumulwa wuhiliwe wayo, no kuhiliwa wupya kanyhi,
44 ale mukohani yabite yakayilanje kaya yiyo kanyhi. Na hono yayagane mumvulya wutandaye kanyhi, hodu kaya yiyo yawa iwifyo.
45 Ne kaya yiyo yikumulangwe yose i mabwe gayo, hamonga ne mabici gayo, sunga ne malongo go wuhiliwe wayo wose, gakunghwe gabite gakatagwe i hanhu havufywe kunze ye likaya.
46 No munhu wo wose hono yejelele yenjile mu kaya yiyo lono yikali ndinde milango, hodu munhu yuyo kowa yeyavufya, sunga lizuwa kono lyoswela.
47 No munhu wo wose mono kotambalala hasi mu kaya yiyo, hamba mono kolila zindigwa mu kaya yiyo, munhu yuyo yanojeye yakanze myenda yakwe.

48 Na hono mukohani yaze yayilanje kaya yiyo, nayo yawone vyono mumvulya si wuwuyajiye kanyhi yaye, hodu mukohani yayanze kaya yiyo vyono yakondya kwezwa. Hambi mumvulya nha wakondya kuwucila lukulu.
49 Nayo mukohani lono yeza kuhila kuyeza kaya yiyo, yasole ndeje nyejete, ne cibici co mubici wo muhagata, no mulejehe mudunghu musisili, hamonga na ezobu.
50 Nayo mukohani yayiwulajile ndeje monga, mucanyha ye ciya ce lilongo mucanyha ye malenga momi.
51 Hanyuma mukohani yasole ci cilya cibici co mubici wo muhagata, na ezobu, na wuwulya mulejehe musisili mudunghu, hamonga na yiyilya ndeje yono yikali nyumi, yavizabice vyose hamonga mu sakami ye ndeje yi yilya yono yayiwulaje, kanyhi na mu malenga momi. Nayo yayimizize kaya yiyo kali mupunghati.
52 Na mu nzila yiyo kowa yakondya kuyeza kaya yiyo ne sakami ye ndeje, ne malenga momi ne ndeje nyumi, ne cibici co mubici wo muhagata, na ezobu, hamonga no mulejehe musisili mudunghu.
53 Hanyuma hodu yayilecele ndeje yi yilya i nyumi, yiguluce yibite kono yikusaka yeneco kunze ye likaya mu mawimbi. Na mu nzila yiyi kowa yanoza wilamule we kaya yiyo. Nayo yowa yakondya kwezwa.

54 Na wuwu wo wulajilize wa kuli matamwa go wukoma.
55 Nha vyono genha, malonda, mapu, hamba wukulutu,
56 hamba muli mumvulya wono wuli mu myenda hamba mu zikaya.
57 No wulajilize wuwu, wunojeye wukamiswe no kwitumamilwa, kuli cinhu cocose ce liwifyo na kuli cono si ce liwifyo.


Walawi 15

Wavufywe so wufila wa mu muwili

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakawalonjela Musa na Haluni yakatya,
2 Walonjele wanhu wa Isilayeli wulajilize awuno wutye nhavi, Hono munhu yejele yakulawa wufila mu muwili wakwe, ale munhu yuyo yaweza mwavufywe so wuwo wufila wakwe,
3 hamba yiwe wufila wakwe wukulawila hamba wuwe si wukulawila, mwene yawa mwavufywe du so wufila wakwe.
4 Ne citanda co cose cono munhu yuyo kukwikalila, hamba kutambalala mucanyha yaco, ne citanda cico naco cawa iwifyo.
5 No munhu wo wose mono kocipamiza cico citanda cakwe,
6 hamba kwikala mucanyha ye cinhu co cose cono cakondya kwikalilwa no munhu yuyo, yinojeye yakanze myenda yakwe, kanyhi yehovuje no muwili wakwe. Kanyhi yajendelele kuwa mwavufywe sunga lizuwa kono lyoswela.
7 No munhu wo wose mono kumupamiza yuyo munhu mono yakulawa wufila, yimunojeye yakanze myenda yakwe, yehovuje no muwili wakwe. Ninga kojendelela kuwa mwavufywe sunga lizuwa kono lyoswela.
8 No munhu mono yena wufila, hono yamufunyile mate munhu muswanu, munhu yuyo mono yafunyiywe mate, yimunojeye yakanze myenda yakwe, yehovuje no muwili wakwe, ninga kojendelela kuwa mwavufywe sunga lizuwa kono lyoswela.
9 No munhu wo wose hono yekalile matandiko ge ndogowe, hamba cikalilo co cose cono munhu mono yena wufila yakondya kwikalila, munhu yuyo nayo, yaweza mwavufywe.
10 No munhu wo wose mono kopamiza cinhu co cose, cono yakondya kwikalila munhu yuyo wo wufila, hodu yaweza mwavufywe sunga lizuwa kono lyoswela. No munhu wowose mono kodinghula cinhu co cose, cono yakondya kwikalila munhu mono yena wufila, yimunojeye yakanze myenda yakwe, yehovuje no muwili wakwe, ninga kojendelela kuwa mwavufywe sunga lizuwa kono lyoswela.
11 No munhu mono yena wufila, lono yakali nezuguza miwoko yakwe, hono yunji wo wose yamupamize munhu yuyo nayo mono yamupamize, yimunojeye yakanze myenda yakwe, yehovuje no muwili wakwe, ninga kojendelela kuwa mwavufywe sunga lizuwa kono lyoswela.
12 Ne ciya co cose ce lilongo cono munhu yuyo mono yena wufila kocipamiza, cinhu cico cinojeye citulangwe. Langa cinhu cocose ce mabici, cono kocipamiza munhu yuyo mono yena wufila, aco cinojeye cizuguzwe du mu malenga.

13 No munhu kolo yakondya kunoga mu wutamwa wakwe wo kulawa wufila mu muwili wakwe, yimunojeye yamule yekale vilo mupunghati, hanyuma yakanze myenda yakwe, yehovuje muwili wakwe mu malenga momi, nayo kowa yakondya kwezwa.
14 Na mwizuwa lyo munana, yasole nghunda nyejete hamba nziwa nyejete, yazilete ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile, yamupele mukohani.
15 Nayo mukohani yayilavye monga, yiwe so cilavywa co wubi, ne nzagwe so cilavywa cinyanywe. Na mu nzila yiyi, mukohani kowa yanoza wilamule kuli munhu yuyo hali Muwaha Mulungu.

16 Na hono munhu yalavye mbeyu yakwe yo lulelo, yimunojeye yehovuje muwili wakwe, kanyhi yajendelele kuwa mwavufywe sunga lizuwa kono lyoswela.
17 Ne cinhu co cose cono colagalilwa na yiyo mbeyu yakwe yo lulelo, hono ciwe cinozwe na nyhingo hamba mwenda, cinhu cico cinojeye cikanzwe, kanyhi cojendelela kuwa iwifyo sunga lizuwa kono lyoswela.
18 Kanyhi mulume lono yawe yakondya kugona no mucekulu, no kulavya mbeyu yo lulelo yiwanojeye wose vyono wejete, wehovuje miwili yawo, kanyhi wojendelela kuwa wavufywe sunga lizuwa kono lyoswela.

19 Na hono mucekulu yawe yali mu vilo vyakwe vyo mwezi, yekalaje mwavufywe sunga vilo mupunghati vipulute. No munhu wowose mono komupamiza, nayo kowa yakondya kwiyavufya sunga lizuwa kono lyoswela.
20 Sunga ne cinhu cocose cono kokwikalila, hamba kutambalala mucanyha yaco, lono yali mu vilo vyakwe vyo mwezi, i vinhu vivyo vyowa iwifyo.
21 No munhu wowose mono kocipamiza citanda cakwe, hamba cinhu co cose cono yakondya kwikalila,
22 munhu yuyo yanojeye yakanze myenda yakwe, yehovuje no muwili wakwe,
23 ninga kojendelela kuwa mwavufywe sunga lizuwa kono lyoswela.
24 Na hono munhu yagone nayo, lono yakali yali mu vilo vyakwe vyo mwezi, munhu yuyo yakwitajila wavufywe no lukwe lwakwe. Holeka munhu yuyo kokwikala mu wavufywe vilo mupunghati. Sunga ne citanda cono yakugonela naco cawa iwifyo.

25 Na hono mucekulu, yawe yakuzaza sakami mu vilo vyono si vya mu vilo vyakwe vyo mwezi, hamba hono yawe yakujendelela kuzaza sakami na lono vilo vyakwe vyo mwezi vyakondya kupuluta. Hodu, mucekulu yuyo yajendelele kuwa mwavufywe, mu nghango ye vilo vyakwe vyose, vyono yajendeleye kuzaza sakami yakwe, nha vyono kawezaga lono yali mu vilo vyakwe vyo mwezi.
26 Sunga ne citanda cakwe cono yakugonela, ne vinhu vyose vyono yakwikalila mwidaha lilyo, vyose vyawa iwifyo.
27 No munhu wo wose mono kovipamiza vinhu vivyo, nayo yawa mwavufywe, na kanyhi yimunojeye yakanze ne myenda yakwe, yehovuje no muwili wakwe, ninga kojendelela kuwa mwavufywe, sunga lizuwa kono lyoswela.
28 Na lono wuzaze we sakami yakwe wuwe waduma, yamule yekale vilo mupunghati. Ne vilo vivyo mupunghati kolo vyapuluta, hodu nayo kowa yakondya kwezwa.
29 Na mwi'zuwa lyo munana yasole nghunda nyejete, na nziwa nyejete yazilete kuli mukohani ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile.
30 Nayo mukohani yayilavye monga, yiwe so cilavywa co wubi, ne nzagwe so cilavywa cinyanywe. Na mu nzila yiyi, mukohani kowa yanoza wilamule kuli mucekulu yuyo hali Muwaha Mulungu.

31 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yawalonjele wanhu wa Isilayeli, welanjilile muno muno kuli zo wiyavufye, ho walece kulyavufya Lihema lyakwe lyono lyelile, lyono lyali yisimye haciligati ye nghambi, vyono hono wejelele du, wofwa.

32 Nago gaga go malajilizo ga kuli munhu mono yakulawa wufila, hamba mono yakulavya mbeyu yo lulelo ya mu muwili wakwe,
33 na kuli mucekulu mono yali mu vilo vyakwe vyo mwezi, hamba kuli munhu mono yakugona no mucekulu mono yali mu wavufywe.


Walawi 16

Lizuwa lyo wilamule

1 U Muwaha Mulungu, yakamulonjela Musa, lono i wana wejete wa Haluni, wali wakondya kufwa, so wulavya moto wono si welile kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
2 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya, “Mulonjelaje muwahawo Haluni, vyono yimunojeye yenjile kugati ye libupa lyo mwenda, ku Hanhu hono helile, helile muno, mu cidaha cono cinojeye du. Hambi hanhu baho nha ho hanhu hono nosuma kwilajilila mwi'vunde, hacanyha ye ligoda lyo lusungu mucanyha ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane. Na hono yalece kuwa ne cihuliko, kofwa.
3 Nayo kosuma kwinjila i Hanhu hono helile, helile muno, lono yena ne nzeku ye cilavywa co wubi, ne nduwilu so cilavywa cinyanywe.”

4 Muwaha Mulungu yakawa kumupela Musa wityatye awu, yakatya, “Lono Haluni yakali si nenjila mu Hanhu hono helile helile muno, yimunojeye yamule yehovuje, hanyuma yavwale ne myenda ye cikohani, nha cinji musweko we bafuta wono welile, ne suluwale ye bafuta, hamonga no musipi, no mukumbo.”

5 Nawo mhuga ye Waisilayeli, wamuletele Haluni mvulati nyejete, so cilavywa co wubi, na ngholo nyejete so cilavywa cinyanywe.
6 Nayo Haluni yalavye nzeku so cilavywa co wubi wakwe yenozeze wilamule, hamonga na we mhuga yakwe.
7 Hanyuma ho yazitegule zi zilya zimhene zi nyejete ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile.
8 Nayo baho, yatowe kula mu nzila yo kwitumamila mabwe mejete, gono limonga lyandicigwe, “Ku so Muwaha Mulungu.” Ne liyagwe lyandicigwe, “Ku so Azazeli.”
9 Nayo Haluni yayilavye mhene yono yiwajigwe ne libwe lyono lyandicigwe “Ku so Muwaha Mulungu,” yiwe so cilavywa co wubi.
10 Ne mhene ayilya yono yiwajigwe so Azazeli, yayilavye kuli Muwaha Mulungu yili nyumi. Hanyuma yayitegule ku nyika kuli Azazeli, ho yinoze wilamule kuli wanhu.

11 Na lono Haluni yakulavya nzeku, cilavywa co wubi kuli mwene hamonga ne mhuga yakwe,
12 yasole lizaye yalimemye makala gono gakulawa mu cikumbicilo. Yasole no wuvumba wuswanu muno maganza mejete. Yenjile navyo mu Hanhu hono helile, helile muno.
13 Na mumo mugati mwi Hema lyono lyelile, yasole wuwo wuvumba yawutajile mu moto, ne lyosi lyo wuvumba lyotitiliza cigubiko ce Lisanduku lye Cilagane, ho mwene yalece kuliwona, ho yalece kufwa.
14 Hanyuma ho yasole sakami ndodo ye nzeku ne cidole cakwe, yayimize mucanyha ye cigubiko ce Lisanduku, na yinji yayimize kali mupunghati ku mwande ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane.

15 Hanyuma ho yayiwulaje mhene so wubi we wanhu, yalete sakami yayo i Hanhu hono helile, helile muno, yayimize mucanyha ye cigubiko ce Lisanduku, na yinji yayimize ku mwande ye Lisanduku nha vyono yanozize mu sakami ye nzeku.
16 Na mu nzila yiyi, kowa yakondya kunoza wilamule we Hanhu hono helile, helile muno, ku wulawa mu wavufywe we wanhu wa Isilayeli, na mu wulawa mu wubi wawo wose. Kanyhi yimunojeye yanoze nhavivi sunga na mwi 'Hema, hambi nha yisimye hagati ye nghambi, hono si hezwe yaye.
17 Na lono Haluni yakwinjila mugati ye Hanhu hono helile, helile muno, ho yenozeze wilamule, munhu wo wose yalecele lukulu kuwa mugati ye Lihema mwi'pango lilyo, sunga kolo yakondya kwinozeza wilamule mweneco hamonga na we mhuga yakwe, ne mhuga yose ya Isilayeli no kulawila kunze.
18 Hanyuma ho kosuma luno kubita ku cikumbicilo ca kunze, co kulavizya cilavywa cinyanywe, no kucinozeza wilamule. Ninga yimunojeye Haluni, yasole sakami ndodo ye nzeku, hamonga ne sakami ndodo ye mhene, yayitajile mu mahembe ge cikumbicilo mbavu zose vyono yine.
19 Kanyhi yazabice cidole cakwe mu sakami, yayimize mu cikumbicilo kali mupunghati. Na mu nzila yiyi, kowa yanoza wezwe we cikumbicilo mu wulawa mu wubi we wanhu wa Isilayeli, no kucinoza ciwe celile.

Mvulati yo kwipunjila wubi

20 Nayo Haluni kolo yakondya mulimo wo kuhanozeza wilamule i Hanhu hono helile, helile muno, hamonga ne Lihema lyono lyelile, ne cikumbicilo, ale, yayiletaje kuli Muwaha Mulungu yi yilya mvulati yono yikahagulwa ku so Azazeli.
21 Nayo yayijelece miwoko yakwe yose vyono myejete, mu mutwe we mvulati yiyo nyumi. Yawe kuvumilila mu mutwe we mvulati, masutya gose, no wubi wose, hamonga no wubedegazi wose we wanhu Waisilayeli. Na mu nzila yiyo, kowa yagawika masutya gose mu mutwe we mvulati yiyo. Hanyuma yayisole mvulati yiyo, yamwibatize munhu mono yamuwikanize mweneco, nayo munhu yuyo yayitegule sunga ko ku nyika.
22 Nayo mvulati yiyo yowudinghula wubi wawo wose sunga ku yisi yono si wekalaga wanhu wanji, nayo koyilecela mvulati yiyo mu nyika.

23 Hanyuma Haluni yenjile mwi 'Hema, yavule myenda yakwe yo wukohani, yono yali yayivwala lono yali yakali nenjila mugati ye Hanhu hono helile, helile muno, yayilece mumo.
24 Nayo yimunojeye yehovujile muwili wakwe i Hanhu hono helile, yavwale ne myenda yakwe yono si yo wukohani. Kolo yeza kukondya, yalawile kunze, yalavye cilavywa cinyanywe co wubi wakwe, hamonga no wubi we wanhu. No kunoza wilamule ku so mweneco, na ku so wanhu.
25 Yasole mhama yo mutugo wuwo, yayikongojele mu cikumbicilo, so cilavywa co wubi.
26 Nayo munhu yuyulya mono yayiteguye ku nyika mvulati kuli Azazeli, yimunojeye yakanze myenda yakwe, yehovuje no muwili wakwe, lono yakali si nenjila mu nghambi.
27 Nayo nzeku, hamonga ne mvulati zono zitumamiywe mulimo we cilavywa co wubi, zono ne sakami yazo yitumamilwa mugati mu Hanhu hono helile, helile muno, so kuleta wilamule, zizo zisolwe zitegulwe kunze ye nghambi, zibite zikakongogwe kuko. Nyama yazo, ne nyhingo, ne matumbu gazo, vyose vinojeye vikongogwe.
28 Nayo munhu mono kowituma mulimo wuwo wo kukongoga, yimunojeye yamule yakanze myenda yakwe, yehovuje no muwili wakwe, ho yenjile mu nghambi.

Wukumbucile we lizuwa lyo kunozezwa wilamule

29 Awu nawo wowa wulajilize, wono munojeye muwukamise sunga mu vilo vyose vyono vyokuza. Mwi'zuwa lye likumi lya mu mwezi wo mupunghati, yinojeye wanhu wose Waisilayeli, hamonga ne wajenzi wono wekalane nawo, wose walece kulya mwi'zuwa lilyo, hamba kwituma mulimo wunji.
30 Hambi mwi'zuwa lilyo, nha monozezwa wilamule, so wezwe wo wubi wenyu, ho muwe mwelile.
31 Ne lizuwa lilyo, yo sabato yo wucehe no kunoza wuhembi munojeye mulinoze liwe lyelile muno muno, lyo lizuwa lyono woleka kulya, kanyhi walece kwituma hamba mulimo wunji wowose. No wulajilize wuwu wunojeye wukamiswe sunga mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza.
32 No Mukohani Muwaha mono yetiywe mafuta no kusiwanulwa, ho yawe mukohani mu hanhu ho sogwe, yuyo yo konoza wilamule yavwalile ne myenda yo wukohani,
33 ho yahanozeze wilamule i Hanhu hono helile, helile muno, ne Lihema lyono lyelile ne cikumbicilo, ne wakohani, hamonga ne wanhu wose we mhuga yose.
34 No wulajilize wuwu wunojeye wukamiswe sunga mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza. No wuhembi wuwu wunojeye wuwe kunozwa kamonga mu mwaka, so kuwanozeza wilamule i wanhu Waisilayeli ku so wubi wawo wose.

Nayo Musa yaketuma zose nha vyono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza.


Walawi 17

Wele we sakami

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa,
2 nayo Musa yamulonjele Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, ne Waisilayeli wose wulajilize awu,
3 Mwiisilayeli wowose mono kowulaga ng'ombe, hamba ngholo, so kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu,
4 hono yalece kuyiwulajila ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile, munhu yuyo kowa yabena wulajilize. Kamwaga sakami caka, holeka nayo, si kowalilizwa ne wanhu wo Mulungu.
5 Ne nhunizo ye lilajilizo lili, yoyi, i wanhu wa Isilayeli ku wulawa zuwali, weze kuzileta mitugo zawo kuli Muwaha Mulungu, mitugo zono waziwulajilaga ako na ako mu'si. Alu yiwanojeye wazilete zose kuli mukohani, ha mulango we Lihema lyono lyelile. Nawo waziwulajile baho, so cilavywa co mulamu.
6 Nayo mukohani yasole sakami, yayimize mu zimbavu zose ze cikumbicilo ca baho ha mulango we Lihema. Hanyuma yayikongoje mhama, ho yilete mhunjile swanu yono yikwendeza kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
7 Holeka aluno wanhu Waisilayeli, walecele lukulu kuwa si wehuwilwa kanyhi kuli Muwaha Mulungu, walece kuziwulaga kanyhi mitugo zawo caka caka ako na ako mu mawimbi, nha vyono walavyaga kuli milungu ye mvulati yono wayihembaga. Holeka i wanhu wa Isilayeli, yiwanojeye walikamise lilajilizo lili sunga mu vilo vyono vikuza.

8 Holeka Mwiisilayeli wo wose, hamba mujenzi mono kekalanaga nawo, mono yakulavya cilavywa cinyanywe, hamba cilavywa ce liwala lyo lyose,
9 kuli Muwaha Mulungu, mono kolavizya cilavywa cico caka caka ako na ako, yalece kuleta no kulavizya ha mulango we Lihema, munhu yuyo si kowalilizwa kanyhi hamonga ne wanhu wo Mulungu.

10 Na hono Mwiisilayeli wo wose, hamba mujenzi mono wekalane, hono yalye nyama hamonga ne sakami yayo, hodu Muwaha Mulungu komuleka, si kowalilizwa kanyhi hamonga ne wanhu wo Mulungu.
11 Vyono, wumi wa muli vyose vyumi vya mono'nze, nha wuli mu sakami, na yiyi yo soko yono yikuleka Muwaha Mulungu yakulajiliza vyono sakami yose, yinojeye yitilwe mucanyha ye cikumbicilo, ho yinoze wilamule. Sakami yono wo wumi, yo yikunoza wilamule.
12 Na yiyi yo soko yono yikuleka Muwaha Mulungu yakuwalonjela wanhu wa Isilayeli vyono, weneco hamba mujenzi mono yekalane nawo, walecele lukulu kulya nyama hamonga ne sakami yayo.

13 Na hono Mwiisilayeli hamba mujenzi mono yekalane nawo, yapelembe nimu, hamba ndeje, yono si iwifyo, ale yimunojeye yayite sakami yayo hasi, yayifucize no muhangha.
14 Vyono wumi we vyumi vyose vya mono'nze, nha wuli mu sakami. Na yiyi yo soko yono yikuleka Muwaha Mulungu yakuwalonjela wanhu Waisilayeli, vyono walecele lukulu kulya nyama hamonga ne sakami yayo. Na hono yunji yejelele, hodu munhu yuyo si kowalilizwa kanyhi ne wanhu wo Mulungu.

15 No munhu wo wose, hono yawe Mwiisilayeli hamba yawe mujenzi, mono kolya nyama ya nimu yo yose yono yifwiliye yeneco, hamba yono yiwulagwe ne zinimu za mu mbago, yimunojeye munhu yuyo yakanze myenda yakwe, yehovuje no muwili wakwe, ninga kojendelela kwikala nhavivyo sunga lizuwa kono lyoswela, nayo ho kowalwa vyono yakondya kwezwa.
16 Na hono yalece kuwutyatya wulajilize wuwu, munhu yuyo kolipicizwa.


Walawi 18

Wulemezwe we makani go wusenyha

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa
2 yawalonjele wanhu Waisilayeli yatye nhavi, “Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.
3 Holeka mulecele lukulu kutyatya i mikalile ye wanhu wa ku Misili kono mwekalaga, hamba mikalile ye wanhu wa ku Kanani, kuko kono neza kumutegulenyi.
4 Langa muzikamisaje zose zono nghumulajilizenyi. Hambi ane nha yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.
5 Holeka muyityatyaje mikalile hamonga ne malajilizo gono nghumupelenyi, holeka nanye mowumipa hono mugakamise. Hambi ane nha yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”

6 Muwaha Mulungu yakalajiliza yakatya, “Yili mwiko mukali, mulecele lukulu kugona na yunji wo wose wa muli wandugu zenyu.
7 Wulecele lukulu kumukoza sogo cevu, so kugona no nyina yako. Kanyhi wulecele lukulu kumukoza cevu u nyina yako.
8 Wulece kumukoza cevu sogo so wugona na yumonga wa muli wagolece zakwe.
9 Wulecele lukulu kugona no lumbulyo, hamba yawe lumbulyo mutumulwa mono yaleligwe no sogo hamba no nyina yako, mono yawilusizwe mu nyumba yo sogo, hamba yawe si muwiluciye wose hamonga mu nyumba yo sogo.
10 Wulecele lukulu kugona no mwizukuluwo we cicekulu, hambi nha nghani ya cevu muno kuli'gwe.
11 Wulecele lukulu kugona no lumbulyo mono muhanjile nayo sogo yenyu, hamba nyina yenyu, hambi nayo yuyo nha lumbulyo.
12 Wulecele lukulu kugona no lumbulye lyo sogo,
13 hamba muwahawe hamba mwanakwawe wo nyina yako.
14 Wulecele lukulu kugona no mugolece wo sogo muwaha, hamba wo sogo mudodo, hambi 'gwe, nha nyina yako.
15 Wulecele lukulu kugona no mugolece wo mwanago,
16 hamba mugolece wo muwahawo.
17 Wulecele lukulu kugona no mwana we cicekulu, hamba mwizukulu wo mucekulu mono mwakondya kugona nayo, hambi wawa nha wanduguzo. Hambi nha yowa nha munhu mono yakugona no mwanagwe we cicekulu.
18 Wulecele lukulu kumutola muwahawe hamba mwanakwawe wo mugolece wako, lono mugolece wako yakali mumi.

19 “Kanyhi wulecele lukulu kugona no mucekulu, lono yali mu vilo vyakwe vyo mwezi, hambi nha yakali yeyavufye.
20 Wulecele lukulu kugona no mugolece wa munhu yunji, hambi nghani yiyo nha yokunoza wuwe wuli mwavufywe.

21 “Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kuwalavya i wana wenyu we cicekulu, wetumamilwe mu wuhembi wo mulungu Moleki. Hambi nghani yiyo, nha yoliletela cevu litagwa lyo Muwaha Mulungu.

22 “Kanyhi munhu wo wose we cilume, yalecele lukulu kugona no mulume muyagwe, hambi Mulungu nha si yakuzisacila lukulu ze liwala lilyo, hambi nha iwifyo kuli'nye.
23 Kanyhi go munhu wo wose we cilume hamba we cicekulu, wulecele lukulu kugona ne nimu ye liwala lyo lyose, hambi awu nha wuhodanji.

24 “Holeka, wulecele lukulu kwiyambalila ko kwiyavufya go mweneco, so wituma limonga lya muli makani gago. Hambi makani ge liwala lilyo, nha go gakuwavufya we makungugo wono wekaye mu yisi yono mukuyibitila kuyihala, yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu yakuwawinga, anye ho mukayihale.
25 Hambi metumo gawo nha gayinoza yisi yiwe iwifyo. Holeka Muwaha Mulungu yakuyilipiciza yisi, no kuyinoza yisi vilemwe ne wanhu wono wekaye mumo.
26 Nawo wakagetuma makani ga gano, no kuzipitulanya zono zinojeye, no kuyinoza yisi yiwe iwifyo. Ninga anye mulecele lukulu kugetuma makani ge liwala lilyo.
27 Nanye wose muli nyenye, hono nyenye mo Waisilayeli, hamba wajenzi wono mwikalane nawo, munojeye wose mugakamise malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga no wulajilize wayo.
28 Holeka nanye si mokwilemwa ne yisi yiyo, nha vyono vyelemwa na we makungugo, wono wamulongolelenyi kwikala mu yisi yiyo.
29 Kanyhi mumanye vyono, munhu wo wose mono kokwituma linji lya mu mawifyo gaga, munhu yuyo si kowalilizwa kanyhi hamonga ne wanhu wo Mulungu.”
30 Muwaha Mulungu yakalonga kanyhi yakatya “Ale, mugakamisaje malajilizo gose gono namupelenyi, kanyhi mulecele lukulu kwiga mikalile ye wanhu wono wamulongolelenyi kwikala mu yisi yiyo. Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kwiyambalila ko kwiyavufya nyo weneco, so wituma limonga lya muli makani gaga. Hambi ane nha yo nene Mulungu wenyu.”


Walawi 19

Wulajilize so wele, ne ling'ani

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa,
2 yawalonjele wanhu we mhuga ya Isilayeli yatye nhavi, “Mweze mwelile hambi 'ne no Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, nha nelile.
3 Na wose muli nyenye, yimunojeye munhu yawogope nyina no sogwe. Kanyhi muyibataje Sabato nha vyono namulajilizenyi. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.
4 Mulece kundeka no kuhemba milungu yo wulambi. Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kwinozeza milungu myenjedulwe no kuyihemba. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.

5 “Na lono mukumuwulajila Muwaha Mulungu mutugo, so cilavywa co mulamu, ale mulanjilile, mutyatye nha vyono namulajilizenyi, nane ho nosuma kucibocela cico cilavywa cenyu.
6 Ne nyama yo mutugo wono wuwulagwe, yinojeye yiligwe mwi'zuwa lilyo lyono yiwulajiywe, hamba mwi'zuwa lye kejete. Ne nyama yo yose yono yowa yikali yisigaye sunga mwi'zuwa lye kadatu, nyama yiyo yowa yinojeye yikongogwe mu moto,
7 hambi nha yakondya kuwa iwifyo, na hono yunji yejelele yayilye, nane hamba si nocivumila ne cilavywa.
8 Nayo munhu wowose hono yayilye nyama yiyo, hobaho kowa musutyi, vyono kenjiza liwifyo muli zo Muwaha Mulungu zono zelile. No munhu yuyo si kowalilizwa kanyhi hamonga ne wanhu wo Mulungu.

9 “Na lono muli mu wududuzi we migunda yenyu, mulece kududuliza vyono vili mu mhelo mhelo ye migunda yenyu. Hamba mulece kutondoleza ne mahalalwa gayo.
10 Hamba mulece kuwuyajila mu migunda yenyu ye mizabibu muwe kuhalaliza zabibu zono zisigaliye, hamba kutondoleza zono zigwiye hasi. Munojeye azo muwalecele wanhu waciwa hamonga ne wanhu wajenzi. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.

11 “Wulece kuwa wuli muhizi hamba wuli mungwele, hamba wuli mulambi.
12 Kanyhi wulecele lukulu kwilahiziza mwi'tagwa lyangu, hono wuwe wumanyile vyono si kosuma kuwufisiliza wihuwizo wako. Hambi wunoza nhavivyo, nha wukuliletela cevu litagwa lyangu. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.

13 “Wulece kumugalila muyago, hamba kumuhiziza yaye. Kanyhi wulece kucela kumulipa musahala munhu mono yakwitumiye mulimo, wulece kugona hamba lizuwa limonga, wuwe wukali si namupela.
14 Wulece kumulijita munhu we ciziwi, hamba kumuwicila cikobazo we cibofu ho yekobaze. Wunyogopaje, hambi 'ne nha yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wako.

15 “Weze mwihuwilwa, kanyhi wuli ling'ani, lono wukulamula mu vyalo. Wulece kuhijila mbejele kuli muciwa, hamba kumuwonela cevu munhu mugoli.
16 Wulece kutandaza zinghani ze maheho kuli munhu wo wose. Na hono hawe hoyuli munhu yunji mono yena calo cono comwambuza yawulagwe, nagwe hono wuwe wina wuhomelezi wono wosuma kumutaza wumi wakwe, ale, wuhomelele calinze ho wumutaze. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.

17 “Wulece kuwa no lusulo kuli yunji wo wose, ninga viswanu mwilamule nayo, ho wulece kutuga wubi ku soko yakwe.
18 Wulece kwilipiciza kuli munhu wo wose, hamba kujendelela kumusula, langa yikunojeye wumwende muyago nha vyono wiyendile gwegwe go mweneco. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.

19 “Mugakamisaje malajilizo gangu, mulece kulezya mitugo zenyu ne mbeyu yono yisimajiye. Mulece kuhadika mbeyu yono yisimajiye mu mugunda monga. Mulece kuvwala mwenda wono wina mawala mejete gono gesimajiye.

20 “No munhu hono yagone no muwanda we cicekulu wo munhu yunji, mono yakali nakombozwa hamba kulekwa mwelecele, ale wose vyono wejete wanojeye walipicizwe, ninga walece kuwulagwa, hambi mucekulu yuyo nha muwanda du.
21 Ninga munhu yuyo, yanojeye yalete nduwilu ha mulango we Lihema lyangu lyono lyelile, yiwe co cilavywa co wusutye.
22 Nayo mukohani yayisole nduwilu yiyo yamunozeze wilamule munhu yuyo, ku so wubi wakwe. No Mulungu komunenela.

23 “Nanye kolo mweza kwinjila mu yisi ya Kanani, na kolo mwahadika mawala gose ge mibici yono yikulela mana, ale mukumbucile, mugawonaje mana ge mibici yiyo vyono iwifyo, holeka mulece kugalya sunga yipulute myaka midatu. Na mu cipango cico cose mulecele lukulu kugalya.
24 Na mu mwaka we kane, mana gose ge mibici gosiwanulwa, gawe cilavywa cono cikulajila lilumbo lyenyu kuli'ne no Muwaha Mulungu.
25 Ninga mu mwaka we 'hano, mosuma kulya mana ge mibici. Na hono munoze nhavivi, mibici yenyu yolela mana kuciza. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.

26 “Mulece kulya nyama hamonga ne sakami yayo. Mulece kwitumamila zinghani ze cigangaganga, hamba kutowa cisangu.
27 Mulece kuyisilula mitwe yenyu, hamba kuceta cidevu.
28 Mulece kwisalanga masale mu miwili yenyu, hamba kwikakata no moto so wulilila wono wafwiye. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.

29 “Mulece kuwakoza cevu wana wenyu we cicekulu ko kuwanoza wawe wasenyha. Hambi hono munoze nhavivyo, nha moyiwuyila milungu yo wulambi, no kuyinoza yisi yimemywe no wusenyha.
30 Wuyikamise Sabato, kanyhi muhapelaje cogopo i hanhu hono ane nghuhembelwa. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.

31 “Mulece kuwabitila we macisi, hamba i wahawi, no kulondola wutazi kuli wene. Hambi hono munoze zizo, nha mowa mweza wavufywe. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.

32 “Mweze kuwapela cogopo i wakombi, no kuwapela lilumbo lyawo. Munyogope nene, ane no Muwaha Mulungu.

33 “Mulece kuwegalila i wajenzi wono mwikalane nawo mu yisi yenyu.
34 Muwanozeze viswanu nha vyono mukuwanozeza Iwaisilayeli wayenyu, kanyhi muwendaje nha vyono mwiyendile nyenye nyo weneco. Mukumbucile vyono nanye mwali wajenzi mu yisi ya Misili. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.

35 “Mulece kwitumamila vipimo vyo wukonji mu wuzito, no wutali na mu winji.
36 Langa mwitumamile vipimilo vye ling'ani, vipimo vyo wuzito, ne mizani ye ling'ani. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu. Nane yo nene mono namuwusenyi mu yisi ya Misili.
37 Mugakamisaje malajilizo gangu gose, hamonga no wulajilize wangu. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”


Walawi 20

Wulipicizwe ku so cibede

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa,
2 yawalonjele wanhu wa Isilayeli yatye nhavi, “Yunji wa muli nyenye, hamba wa muli wajenzi wono mwikalane nawo, mono komulavya yumonga wa muli wanagwe, yetumamilwe mu wuhembi wo mulungu Moleki, munhu yuyu yatowangwe ne mabwe ne mhuga yose ya Waisilayeli sunga yafwe.
3 No munhu wo wose mono kumulavya yumonga wa muli wanagwe kuli mulungu Moleki, no kulinoza Lihema lyangu lyono lyelile liwe iwifyo, no kulipela cevu litagwa lyangu, nane nomuleka munhu yuyo hamba si nomuwaliliza kanyhi hamonga ne wanhu wangu.
4 Na hono mhuga ye Waisilayeli walece kuzicelelela zizo zono yetumile, hamba kumuwulaga,
5 nene no mweneco nomwizila munhu yuyo hamonga na we mhuga yakwe wose, sunga na wose wono wowa vihanjile nayo mu wu wula wihuwilwa kuli'ne, wono wakumuhemba Moleki. Ane nane si nowawaliliza kanyhi hamonga ne wanhu wangu.

6 “Hono munhu wo wose yabite kuli we macisi, hamba kuli wahawi, kulondola wutazi kuli wene, nane nomwizila, hamba si nomuwaliliza kanyhi hamonga ne wanhu wangu.
7 Mweze mwelile, hambi 'ne nha yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.
8 Mugakamise malajilizo gangu, hambi ane nha nili Muwaha Mulungu, nane nghumunozenyi muwe mwelile.”
9 Muwaha Mulungu yakalajiliza yakatya, “Hono munhu wowose yamulijite sogwe hamba nyina, munhu yuyo yanojeye yawulagwe. Hambi nha kesacila kufwa mweneco.

10 “Na hono munhu yebatwe yakusenyha no mugolece wo Mwiisilayeli muyagwe, awo wose mulume no mucekulu wanojeye wawulagwe.
11 No munhu wowose mono kogona no yumonga wa muli wagolece wo sogwe, yakumukoza cevu sogwe. Awo nawo vyono wejete mulume no mucekulu, wanojeye wawulagwe. Hambi nha wesacila lifwa weneco.
12 No munhu hono yagone no mugolece wo mwanagwe, wose vyono wejete wanojeye wawulagwe, hambi nha vigona munhu no mwanagwe. No wuwulagwe wawo yowa nghani yawo weneco.
13 Na hono munhu mulume yagone no mulume muyagwe, wawo wetuma nghani ya cevu muno muno, holeka wose yiwanojeye wawulagwe. Hambi nha wesacila lifwa weneco.
14 Na hono munhu yawatole yawahanze, mwana no nyina yakwe, awo wose vyono wadatu, wanojeye wakongogwe mu moto sunga wafwe, ku soko ya nghani ye cevu yono wayitumile. Ne nghani ye liwala lili, yinojeye yilece kulecelezwa hamba hadodo, yitumwe muli nyenye.
15 Na hono munhu yagone ne nimu, munhu yuyo hamonga ne nimu yiyo wose wawulagwe.
16 Na hono mucekulu yejelele nayo yagone ne nimu, mucekulu yuyo hamonga ne nimu, wose wawulagwe. Hambi nha wesacila lifwa weneco.

17 “Na hono munhu yamutole lumbulye hamba u lumbulye mono wahanjile sogo yawo hamba nyina yawo, wanojeye wakozwe cevu calinze, kanyhi wakomwe mu mhuga. Hambi nha vigona no lumbulye, holeka wanojeye wawubocele wulipicizwe wayo.
18 Na hono munhu yagone no mucekulu mono yali mu vilo vyakwe vyo mwezi, awo wose vyono wejete wanojeye wakomwe mu mhuga, hambi nha wabena wulajilize wo wavufywe.
19 Na hono munhu yagone no muwahawe wo nyina yakwe, hamba no lumbulye lyo sogwe, wose vyono wejete wanojeye wabocele wulipicizwe wawo.
20 Na hono munhu yagone no mugolece wo sogwe muwaha, hamba wo sogwe mudodo, wose vyono wejete wokwidinghulila wubi wawo, kanyhi wofwa wasina wana.
21 Na hono munhu yatole mugolece wo muwahawe hamba wo mwanakwawe, nawo wofwa wasina wana. Hambi nha ketuma nghani ye 'wifyo, no kumukoza cevu muwahawe.”

22 Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Ale mugakamisaje malajilizo gangu hamonga no wulajilize, ho yisi ya Kanani yilece kumulemenyi, yisi yono nghumutegulenyi.
23 Mulece kusolela myoto ye wanhu wono wekaye mumo. Hambi 'ne nha nghuwawinga we makungugo, anye ho musume kwinjila mu yisi yiyo. Hambi nha wanghoza cevu ku so metumo gawo mabi gono wakwituma.
24 Ninga lu, nghondya kumwihuwizizenyi yisi yiyo, yono yina wugoli wose hamonga no moso mwinji, ho yiwe yisi yenyu, ane nomupelenyi nyenye. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu, yo nene mono namusiwanulenyi ku wulawa mu makungugo ganji.
25 Ho musume kumanya kuzisiwanula zi nimu ne zi ndeje, zono nyezwe na zono si nyezwe. Nanye mulecele lukulu kulya zi nimu ne zindeje ze liwifyo. Hambi nha nakondya kuzanza vyono zili iwifyo, na hono mulye, mokwavufywa.
26 Munojeye muwe mwelile, kanyhi muli wangu, hambi 'ne nha yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, nelile. Nane nghamusiwanulenyi mu wulawa mu makungugo ganji, ho muwe muli wangu nene du nili wene.

27 “No munhu wo wose hono yawe mulume hamba mucekulu, hono yabite kuli we macisi ne wahawi kulondola wutazi kuko, munhu yuyo yatowangwe ne mabwe sunga yafwe. No munhu wo wose mono kokwituma zono zinha zizi, kokwisacila lifwa mweneco.”


Walawi 21

Wele we Wakohani

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajiliza Musa yawalonjele wana wa Haluni, yawatye nhavi, “Mukohani wowose yalece kwiyavufya so wulongoza wuhembi we maziko, lono yafwilwa no nduguye.
2 Langa hono sogwe yawe yo yafwiye, hamba nyina, hamba mwanagwe, hamba mwanagwe we cicekulu, hamba muwahawe,
3 hamba lumbulye mono yakali natolwa, mono wekalane mu kaya monga.
4 Hamba yalece kwiyavufya mu vililo vye wanduguze wa mwi'zenjele.
5 Mukohani wo wose yalece kwisilula ho hose mu mutwe wakwe, hamba kuceta ndevu, hamba kwisalanga masale mu muwili wakwe, ho yalajile vyono yali mu cililo.
6 Mwene yanojeye yawe yelile, hamba yalece kulikoza cevu litagwa lyangu. Hambi mwene nha kalavyaga cilavywa ce ndigwa kuli 'ne, holeka yimunojeye yeze yelile.
7 Kanyhi mukohani yimunojeye yalece kutola mucekulu musenyha, hamba mucekulu mono yakondya kumanya walume, hamba mucekulu mono yalecigwe no mulume wakwe, vyono mwene mukohani yelile kuli Mulungu wakwe.
8 Ne wanhu, wanojeye wamuwone mukohani vyono yo munhu mono yelile, hambi nha yo kandavizyaga ane no Muwaha Mulungu i vilavywa vye ndigwa. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu mono nimwezajenyi 'nye, nha nelile.
9 Na hono mwana we cicekulu wo mukohani yenjile mu wusenyha, yuyo yakumukoza cevu u sogwe, holeka yanojeye yakongogwe mu moto sunga yafwe.

10 “Mukohani Muwaha yetilwa mafuta go witile mu mutwe wakwe, nayo musiwanulwe, ho yavwale myenda yo wukohani, holeka si yanojeye yalece mvwili zakwe si swamule, hamba lunji yademulanje myenda yakwe, yakulajila vyono yali mu cililo.
11 Hambi mwene nha musiwanulwe so nene. Holeka si yinojeye yeyavufye mweneco, hamba kulyavufya Lihema lyangu lyono lyelile,
12 so wu wuka mwi 'Hema, no kwinjila mu kaya mono mowuli muvimba wo munhu mono yafwiye, hamba yawe sogwe hamba nyina.
13 Kanyhi yimunojeye mukohani, yatole mucekulu mono yakali namanya walume.
14 Yalece kutola mucekulu mufwile, hamba mono yalecigwe no mulume wakwe, hamba mucekulu mono kawezaga musenyha. Langa yatole du mono yakali namanya walume, mono kolawa mu wanhu wakwe.
15 Hono si nhavivyo, iwana wono kolela, wowa wavufywe, hamba si wowa welile, nha vyono wakusakwa wawe. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu nimusiwanula nha Mukohani Muwaha.”

16 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajiliza Musa yakatya,
17 ya mulonjele Haluni yatye, “Mu lulelo lwako lose, munhu mono kowa yena cilema mu muwili wakwe, ayo si kowa yanojeye yandavizye cilavywa ce ndigwa. Ne nghani yiyi yinojeye yikamiswe na mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza.
18 Hamba hasina munhu wo wose mono yena cilema mu muwili wakwe, mono kosuma kulavya yikumbiko, nha cinji, mubofu, hamba mono yakusenzegula, hamba mono yahodanjice mhula, hamba mono yena cilebeto,
19 hamba mono yabenece mugulu, hamba muwoko.
20 Hamba mono yafunhuce mugongo, hamba munghulimba, hamba mono yena likopo mu liso limonga, hamba mono yena wutamwa wo wupele, hamba mono mupaswe.
21 Holeka munhu mono kowa yena cilema mu lulelo lwa Haluni mukohani, yuyo si kosuma kundetela cilavywa ce ndigwa.
22 Munhu we liwala lilyo, mwene kosuma kulya ndigwa yono ndavye kuli'ne, hamonga ne ndigwa yono yelile, sunga ne ndigwa yono yelile muno muno.
23 Ninga ku soko ye cilema du cono yena mu muwili wakwe, holeka si kosuma kusejelela behi ne libupa lyo mwenda, hamba kusejelela ha cikumbicilo. Ho yalece kuvitajila iwifyo vinhu vivyo vyono vyelile. Hambi ane, nha yo nene Muwaha Mulungu. Nane yo nene mono nghuvyeza.”

24 Na zizi zo Musa yamulonjela Haluni, hamonga ne wanagwe, sunga na kuli wanhu wose Waisilayeli.


Walawi 22

Wele we vilavywa

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajiliza Musa,
2 yamulonjele Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, yatye nhavi, “Mulecele lukulu kuliletela cevu litagwa lyangu lyono lyelile. Holeka muvinozeze cogopo vilavywa vyono wanhu wa Isilayeli wakusiwanulila 'ne, ane no Muwaha Mulungu.
3 Holeka yumonga wa mu lulelo lwenyu, hono yasejelele behi ne vilavywa vyangu vyono vyelile, vyono wanhu wa Isilayeli wasiwanuliye 'ne akuno ho yeyavufye. Munhu yuyo si kolosumila lukulu kanyhi kwituma ha cikumbicilo. Na zizi zinojeye zikamiswe sunga mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza.”

4 “Kanyhi mu wana wa Haluni, hasina munhu mono kosuma kulya vilavywa vyono vyelile, akuno ho yena no wutamwa wo wukoma, hamba mono yakulavya wufila mu muwili wakwe. Langa yalindile sunga kolo yakondya kwezwa. No mukohani wo wose hono yapamize cinhu co cose cono cili 'wifyo, nha cinji, mu wupamiza muvimba, hamba munhu mono mbeyu yakwe yo lulelo yikulawila,
5 hamba kupamiza nimu ye liwifyo, hamba u munhu mwavufywe.
6 No mukohani wo wose hono yeyavufywe, hodu yimunojeye yajendelele kuwa mwavufywe sunga lizuwa kono lyoswela. Hamba nhavivyo, si kosuma kulya vilavywa vyono vyelile, langa sunga kolo yehovuga muwili wakwe.
7 Nalyo lizuwa kolo lyakondya kugwila, hodu nayo kowa yakondya kwezwa. Nayo ho kosuma kulya nhondo vilavywa vyono vyelile, ku soko yo ndigwa yakwe.”
8 Kanyhi mukohani yalecele lukulu kulya nyama ye nimu yono yifwiliye yeneco, hamba yono yiwulagwe ne malimu ga mu mbago, hambi nha yomwavufya. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.
9 “Yiwanojeye wakohani wose wagebate malajilizo gose gono namupelenyi. Na hono si nhavivyo, wowa wasutyi, na wofwa, vyono si wagakamise malajilizo gono gelile. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, yo nene mono nghuweza.

10 “No munhu yunji wo wose si yakomboywe yalye vilavywa vyono vyelile, langa wa mu mhuga yo mukohani du, yo yakomboywe yalye. Ninga hamba yunji wo wose mono vikalane hamonga no mukohani, hamba mwitumami wakwe, awo si wakomboywe walye yaye.
11 Langa i wawanda wo mukohani, wono yawagulile ne sawo yakwe mweneco, hamba wono waleleywe mumo mu kaya yakwe, awo wosuma kulya i ndigwa yono yakupegwa mukohani.
12 Nayo mwana we cicekulu wo mukohani, hono yatolwe no munhu mono si mukohani, nayo yalecele lukulu kulya vivyo vilavywa vyono vyelile.
13 Nayo muhinza mufwile, hamba mono yalecigwe no mulume, na kanyhi yawe yasina wana, hono yawe yawuya kwikalana no sogwe, kanyhi yawe yajendeleye kwikala yali wene du, ale, yuyo kosuma kulya ndigwa yono sogwe yakupegwa yo wukohani. Ninga munhu wo wose mono si Mwiisilayeli yalece kuyilya.

14 “No munhu wo wose mono si wa mu mhuga yo wukohani, hono yalye cilavywa co cose cono celile wula kumanya, ale munhu yuyo yimunojeye yahalize cono celile mu nghango ye cifo cayo yamupele kuli mukohani, hamonga ne ngomola ye makumi mejete kwe 'gana.
15 Nawo wakohani yiwanojeye walanjilile, walece kuvyavufya i vilavywa vyono vyelile,
16 mu nzila yo kumuleceleza munhu mono si yanojeye yalye. Ne nghani yiyi, yomuletela munhu yuyo masutya hamonga no wulipicizwe wuwaha muli mwene. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu, yo nene mono nghuweza.”

17 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulajiliza Musa
18 yamupele malajilizo aga Haluni hamonga ne wanagwe, sunga ne wanhu wose wa Isilayeli, “Hono hawe hoyuli munhu Mwiisilayeli, hamba mujenzi mono vikalane hamonga ne Waisilayeli, mono yawe yakulavya cilavywa cinyanywe, hono yawe yakulavya so wufisiliza wilahize wakwe, hamba wula wilahize. Ale mutugo wuwo weze mutelevu wusina cilema co cose.
19 No mutugo wuwo ho wusume kubocelwa, yeze nume nhelevu yono yisina cilema cocose.
20 Na hono mutugo wuwe wina cilema, hodu mutugo wuwo yalece kuwulavya, hambi Muwaha Mulungu nha si kowubocela yaye.
21 Na hono munhu yunji yakumulavizya cilavywa co mulamu Muwaha Mulungu, hono yawe yakulavya so wufisiliza wilahize wakwe, hamba cilavywa ce lihewa, ale mutugo wuwo weze mutelevu wusina cilema, hono yisakwe yivumilwe.
22 Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu mutugo wono wuli cibofu, hamba yono yibenece mugulu, hamba ye cilema, hamba yono yina malonda, hamba yo wupele, hamba yono yina wukulutu. Mulece kulavya mu cikumbicilo mitugo ye liwala lilino ho yiwe cilavywa ce ndigwa.
23 Langa mu vilavywa vye lihewa, mosuma kulavya mutugo we cilema hamba yono yicepuciywe vilunzwa vyayo vinji. Ninga mu vilavywa vyo wilahize, mutugo wuwo si wovumilwa yaye.
24 Kanyhi mulece kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu mutugo wono mupaswe hamba wono mutulangwe hamba zono zitemigwe. Azi zose si zikomboligwe mu zitumamile mu yisi yenyu.
25 Mulece kulavya cilavywa ce ndigwa co mutugo wono wulawile kuli munhu mujenzi. Hambi mitugo ze liwala lilyo nha zikuwalwa vyono zina vilema, holeka si zikuvumilwa yaye.”

26 Kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
27 “Nzeku hamba nyana ye ngholo, hamba nyhinda, kolo yalelwa, yilece kugobozwa ko nyina yayo, sunga vipulute vilo mupunghati. Na kolo vyapuluta vilo vivyo, ale ho yosuma kuvumilwa yisume kulavywa yiwe cilavywa ce ndigwa.
28 Mulece kuziwulaga hamonga ne nyana yayo, hamba mhene hamonga ne nyana yayo mwizuwa limonga.
29 Na lono mukulavya yikumbiko lyo wulumbe kuli Muwaha Mulungu, yinojeye muwutyatye wulajilize vyono winha ho musume kuvumilwa.
30 Kanyhi munojeye muyilye nyama yiyo mwi'zuwa lilyo du, mulece kuyisigaza sunga mitondo yaco. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”

31 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Mukamise malajilizo gangu. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.
32 Mulecele lukulu kulikoza cevu litagwa lyangu lyono lyelile. Ne wanhu wose wa Isilayeli yiwanojeye wamanye vyono, ane nelile. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu mono nghumwezenyi.
33 Nane yo nene mono namuwusenyi mu yisi ya Misili, ho niwe Mulungu wenyu. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”


Walawi 23

Zi Sikukuu ze ci Dini

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamuloloseza Musa,
2 “Wulajilize wono wunojeye wukamiswe lono wanhu wa Isilayeli wakunoza zisikukuu ze cidini, lono wakwihanguliza hamonga mu wuhembi.
3 Ho vili vilo mutandatu du vyono vinojeye mwitume milimo yenyu, ninga mweze kukumbucila vyono lizuwa lyo mupunghati, lyo lizuwa lye Sabato, ne lizuwa lilyo lyo lizuwa lyo kuceha. Holeka mwi'zuwa lilyo, mulece kwituma mulimo wunji, langa mwihangulizaje du so wuhembi. Hambi Sabato nha Sabato yo Muwaha Mulungu, hanhu ho hose hono mowa mwikaye.
4 Nanye munojeye muzanzalice zi sikukuu zizino zose nha vyono zinha, monga mwi'zuwa lyayo.”

Pasaka no mukate wono wusina cisase

(Wuw 28:16-25)

5 “Sikukuu ye Pasaka, yeze kunozwa so cogopo ca kuli Muwaha Mulungu, nayo yeze kwanziza hono lizuwa likugwila, mwi'zuwa lye likumi na yine lyo mwezi mulongozi.
6 Na mwi'zuwa lye likumi ne 'hano, i sikukuu yo mukate wono wusina cisase baho ho yowa kwanziza. Na mu vilo mupunghati, mulecele lukulu kulya mukate wono wina cisase.
7 Na mwi'zuwa lilongozi lya mu vilo vivyo, munojeye mwihangulize hamonga so wuhembi du, hamba mulece kwituma mulimo wenyu wowose.
8 Mujendelele kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu, cilavywa ce ndigwa nhondo zose, mu vilo vivyo vyose mupunghati. Ninga mwi'zuwa lyo mupunghati mwihangulize kanyhi hamonga muwe no wuhembi. Ninga mulece kwituma mulimo wunji wowose.”

9 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
10 “Na kolo mwenjila mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu yakumupelenyi, na kolo mwadudula wuhemba wenyu, ale mumuletele mukohani nhoto monga ye malimbuko.
11 Nayo mukohani yagalavye nha cilavywa cinule hali Muwaha Mulungu, nanye ho musume kuvumilwa. Mukohani yanojeye yacilavye cilavywa cico mitondo yaco lono Sabato yakondya kupuluta.
12 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo lono wukulavya cilavywa co wuhemba, ale wulavye hamonga ne cilavywa cinyanywe, ca nduwilu monga, ya mwaka monga, nhelevu yono yisina cilema co cose.
13 Hamonga na vivyo, wuleteleze no wusaje, lete no wusaje wa kilo nyejete wono muhanzahanzwe ne mafuta, wuwe cilavywa ce ndigwa. Ne mhunjile ye cilavywa cico, yikwendeza kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Kanyhi hamonga ne cilavywa cico, wulavye na lita monga ya divayi.
14 Ninga wulecele lukulu kuwulya wuhemba wuwo wupya, hamba hono mufijihe, hamba hono mukalinje, hamba wono mutelecelwe mukate, sunga langa kolo wakondya kumulavizya cilavywa cico Muwaha Mulungu, nagwe ho kosuma kuwulya. No wulajilize wuwu wunojeye wukamiswe mu zindelo zenyu zose sunga mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza.”

Sikukuu ye nhwanga

(Wuw 28:26-31)

15 “Na mu wulawilila baho, ale wuwale sunga gapulute majuma mupunghati, mu wulawilila lizuwa lyono wuleteye cilavywa co wuhemba wupya, kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu.
16 Na mwi'zuwa lye likumi ne hano, mitondo yaco, lono lizuwa lye Sabato yo mupunghati lyakondya kupuluta, ale wumulavizyaje kanyhi Muwaha Mulungu cilavywa co wuhemba wunji wupya.
17 Na kanyhi, yinojeye mhuga zose zimuletele Muwaha Mulungu mikate myejete myejete, ciwe cilavywa ce mikate minulwe hali Muwaha Mulungu. Ne mikate yiyo yose, yeze minozwe ko wusaje wono wunamuce, wa kilo nyejete nyejete, kanyhi yeze mitelekwe hamonga ne cisase. Nayo yiletwe kuli Muwaha Mulungu nha cilavywa cono cikulawa mu malimbuko.
18 Kanyhi hamonga no mukate wuwo, mhuga yose yinojeye yilete nyana mupunghati za ngholo, za mwaka monga monga, hamonga na nyehe monga, na nduwilu nyejete, na zose zeze nhelevu zisina cilema co cose. Navyo vinojeye vyose vilavywe nha cilavywa cinyanywe kuli Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga ne cilavywa co wusaje, ne cilavywa ce divayi. Ne mhunjile ye cilavywa cico yikwendeza kuli Muwaha Mulungu.
19 Hanyuma ho kanyhi, walete mvulati yiwe cilavywa co wubi hamonga na nyana za ngholo nyejete za mwaka monga, ziwe cilavywa co mulamu.
20 Nayo mukohani yalavye mukate hamonga ne zinyana zinyejete ze zingholo, ziwe cilavywa cinulwe hali Muwaha Mulungu so wakohani. Ne vilavywa vivi vyelile.
21 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo, mulece kwituma mulimo wunji, langa mwihangulizaje hamonga du so wuhembi. Ne zindelo zenyu zose, yiwanojeye wawukamise wulajilize wuwu sunga mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza.

22 “Na lono muli mu wududuzi we migunda yenyu, mulece kududuliza vyono vili mu mhelo mhelo ye migunda yenyu. Hamba mulece kutondoleza ne mahalalwa gayo. Langa muwalecelaje wanhu waciwa, hamonga ne wanhu wajenzi. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.”

Sikukuu yo mwaka mupya

(Wuw 29:1-6)

23 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
24 “Na mwi'zuwa lilongozi lya mu mwezi wo mupunghati, lizuwa lilyo lyowa yizuwa lyo wucehe. Nanye kolo mukuhulika mazi ge zindulele, munojeye mwihangulize hamonga so wuhembi.
25 Mulete ne cilavywa ce ndigwa mumulavizye Muwaha Mulungu. Ninga mwi'zuwa lilyo mulece kwituma mulimo wunji.”

Lizuwa lyo Wilamule

(Wuw 29:7-11)

26 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
27 “Lizuwa lye likumi lyo mwezi wo mupunghati, lyo lizuwa lyono munojeye munoze sikukuu yo mwaka, hambi lizuwa lilyo nha lyo lizuwa lyo wilamule. Na mwi'zuwa lilyo mulecele lukulu kulya cinhu cocose, langa mwihangulize du hamonga so wuhembi, muwe kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu ne cilavywa ce ndigwa.
28 Ninga mwi'zuwa lilyo mulece kwituma mulimo wunji wowose. Hambi nha lyo lizuwa lyo wilamule, lyo lizuwa lyo kunoza wilamule hali Mulungu wenyu.
29 Na munhu wo wose mono kokwijelela yalye cinhu co cose mwi'zuwa lilyo, u munhu yuyo si kowalilizwa kanyhi hamonga ne wanhu wo Mulungu.
30 Kanyhi, munhu wo wose hono yetume mulimo wunji wo wose mwi'zuwa lilyo, Mulungu komuwulaga munhu yuyo.
31 No wulajilize wu wuno wowa so nyenye, hamonga ne zindelo zenyu zose, hanhu ho hose hono mowa mwikaye.
32 Mukumbucile vyono munojeye mulisiwanule lizuwa lilyo, mulece kwituma mulimo wunji hamba kulya cinji co cose, ku wulawilila kolo lizuwa likugwila mwizuwa lye licenda, sunga kolo lizuwa likugwila mwizuwa lye likumi.”

Sikukuu ye Vibanda

(Wuw 29:12-40)

33 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
34 “Nayo sikukuu ye vibanda, yinojeye yanze mwi'zuwa lye likumi ne hano lya mu mwezi wo mupunghati. Nayo yijendelele sunga vilo mupunghati.
35 Na mwi'zuwa lilongozi lya mu vilo vivyo, munojeye mwihangulize hamonga so wuhembi du, hamba mulece kwituma mulimo wo wose.
36 Mujendelele kumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu cilavywa ce ndigwa nhondo zose, mu vilo vivyo vyose mupunghati. Na mwi'zuwa lyo munana mwihangulize kanyhi hamonga so wuhembi, mumulavizye no Muwaha Mulungu cilavywa ce ndigwa. Ne lizuwa lilyo lyo lizuwa lyo wuhembi du, nanye mulece kwituma mulimo wunji.

37 “Zizino zo sikukuu ze Cidini, zono zinojeye mumupele cogopo Muwaha Mulungu, mu nzila yo wihanguliza hamonga wenyu, so wuhembi wa hamonga, no kumulavizya vilavywa vye ndigwa, ne vilavywa vinyanywe, ne vilavywa vyo wusaje, ne makumbiko, hamonga ne vilavywa vye divayi, mu nghango nha vyono vikusakwa mwi'zuwa ne 'zuwa.
38 Ne zi sikukuu zizo, zili hamonga ne mazuwa ge Sabato. Navyo vilavywa vivyo, vili hamonga ne vilavywa vyenyu vye nhondo zose, vilavywa vyono mukulavya so wufisiliza wilahize wenyu, sunga ne vilavywa vye lihewa vyono mukumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu.

39 “Nanye kolo mwadudula migunda yenyu mwakondya, munoze sikukuu yiyino vilo mupunghati, mwanze kuyinoza mwi'zuwa lye likumi ne hano, lya mu mwezi wo mupunghati. Ninga lizuwa lilongozi, lyeze lizuwa lya kuceha du.
40 Na mwi'zuwa lilyo, musole mikalo yinji miswanu ya mu mibici yenyu, ne manhambi ge mitende, ne manhambi ge mibici, gono gena mahamha menji, ho muwe kwanza kunoza sikukuu ze cidini, so kumupela cogopo u Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.
41 Nanye muyinoze sikukuu yiyi vilo mupunghati. Ne zindelo zenyu zose, yiwanojeye wawukamise wulajilize wuwu, sunga mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza.
42 Ne wanhu wose wa Isilayeli, yiwanojeye wawe wekaye mu vibanda sunga vilo mupunghati.
43 Zindelo zenyu ho waze wamanye vyono Muwaha Mulungu yawanoza i wanhu Waisilayeli wekale mu vibanda lono yawawusiza mu yisi ya Misili. Yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.”

44 Nayo Musa, yakawa kuwalonjela i wanhu wa Isilayeli wulajilize wono wunojeye, mu wibata i vilo vye zi sikukuu so kumupela cogopo Muwaha Mulungu.


Walawi 24

Wulajilize so wukamise we Tala

(Wut 27:20-21)

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa,
2 yawalonjele wanhu Waisilayeli wityatye awuno wono wityatizye yawatye, “Ale letenyi mafuta malengu maswanu getumamilwe so tala yono yili mugati ye Lihema lyono lyelile, ho yiwe kwikala yacile du nhondo zose.
3 Nayo Haluni yowa yimunojeye yawe kuyikoleleza cinyamihe nhondo zose. Yayilece yikwaka sunga wele, kononze ye libupa lyo mwenda lye Lihema lyono lyelile ha mwande ye Lisanduku lye Cilagane, lyono lili i Hanhu hono helile, helile muno. Ne lilajilizo lili linojeye likamiswe sunga mu vilo vyono vikuza.
4 Nayo Haluni, yimunojeye yazikamise zitala mu wuwiko wayo we zahabu nengu, kanyhi yazilanjilile ziwe kwikala zacile du hali Muwaha Mulungu.”

Kuli ze mikate milavywe kuli Muwaha Mulungu

5 “Ale, wusole wusaje wa kilo yikumi na nyejete, wunoze mikate yikumi na myejete.
6 Wuyitandalice mu micilili myejete, mucilili monga mikate mutandatu, no muyagwe nawo nhavivyo, mucanyha ye meza i hiliwe zahabu nengu, yono yili mwi Hema lyono lyelile.
7 Hanyuma wusole wuvumba wono wunamuce muno, wuwutajile mucanyha ya yiyo micilili, yiwe citanjilo ce cilavywa ce ndigwa kuli Muwaha Mulungu, cono cikuhalila hanhu ha mikate.
8 Na mu Sabato zose, mu vilo vyose vyono vikuza, yinojeye mukate wuwe kuwikwa ha mwande yo Muwaha Mulungu. Na wuwu wowa wo mulimo we Waisilayeli cibitilila.
9 No mukate wuwo, wowa wa Haluni hamonga ne zindelo zakwe. Nawo yiwanojeye wawulile i hanhu hono helile du, vyono mukate wuwo welile muno, we ndigwa ndavye kuli Muwaha Mulungu so Wakohani.”

Wulipicizwe wo muling'ano

10 Kwali na munhu mono u sogwe yali Mumisili, ninga u nyina yali Mwiisilayeli, litagwa lyakwe Shelomiti, u mwana we cicekulu wa Debili wo mulango wa Dani. Nayo munhu yuyo, vikagombana no Mwiisilayeli mumo mu nghambi.
11 Na mu wugombane wawo wuwo, munhu yuyo yakamutovula Mulungu. Nawo wakamusola wakamutegula kuli Musa,
12 wakamuwika hasi yo wulindilizi, wakawa kulindila Muwaha Mulungu yawalonjele vyono wamutendele.

13 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa yakatya,
14 “Musolaje munhu yuyo, wumutegule kunze ye nghambi. Ne wanhu wose wono wamuhulice vyono yakutovula, wabite wakajelece miwoko yawo mu mutwe wakwe, wuwe wo wuhomelezi vyono munhu yuyo kanoza masutya. Hanyuma, mhuga yose ye Waisilayeli, wamutowanje ne mabwe sunga yafwe.
15 Wuwalonjele ne wanhu wa Isilayeli, vyono munhu wowose mono komutovula Mulungu, munhu yuyo kokwidinghulila wubi wakwe mweneco,
16 nayo kowulagwa. No munhu wowose mono komutovula Mulungu, hono yawe Mwiisilayeli hamba mujenzi mono mwikalane, munhu yuyo yatowangwe ne mabwe ne mhuga yose sunga yafwe.

17 “No munhu wowose mono kowulaga munhu, ale munhu yuyo nayo yawulagwe.
18 No munhu wowose mono kowulaga mutugo wo muyagwe, munhu yuyo yimunojeye yayihalize. Ninga nghani mbaha muli zizi, wo wumi kwa wumi.
19 Na hono munhu wo wose yamupele muyagwe cilema, munhu yuyo yatendelwe nha vyono yamutendeye muyagwe.
20 Hono yawe kamubena muyagwe lifupa, nayo yabenwe lifupa lyakwe. Na hono yawe kamutula muyagwe liso, nayo liso lyakwe litulwe. Na hono yawe kamubena lino, nayo lino lyakwe libenwe. Cinhu co cose cono munhu komutendela no kumupela muyagwe cilema, ale cico nayo co kotendelwa du.
21 No munhu wo wose mono kowulaga mutugo, munhu yuyo kohaliza yinji, ninga munhu mono kowulaga munhu muyagwe, u munhu yuyo langa yawulagwe du.
22 Ne lilajilizo lilino lili so nyenye wose kono mwalila, kuli Waisilayeli, na kuli wajenzi wono mwikaye nawo. Hambi ane nha yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.”

23 Nayo Musa yali yeza kukondya kulonga zizo kuli wanhu Waisilayeli, hodu wakawa kumusola munhu yu yulya, wakawa kumutegula kunze ye nghambi, wakawa kumutowanga ne mabwe sunga yakafwa. Na mu nzila yiyi, i wanhu wa Isilayeli wakazifisiliza zono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa.


Walawi 25

Mwaka wo mupunghati

(Kum 15:1-11)

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakalongana na Musa mwi'tunda lya Sinayi, yakamulajiliza,
2 yawalonjele wanhu wa Isilayeli yatye, “Kolo mwenjila mu yisi yono Muwaha Mulungu yakumupelenyi, munojeye mumupele Muwaha Mulungu cogopo, mu nzila yo kuleka kuyilima yisi, mu mwaka wo wose wo mupunghati.
3 Muhadice migunda yenyu, muyidumulile ne mizabibu yenyu, mukonghole ne vilimwa vyenyu, mu myaka mutandatu du.
4 Ninga mu mwaka wo mupunghati, wowa wo mwaka wo kuwuceheza muhangha we migunda yenyu, wo mwaka wono musiwanulwe kuli Muwaha Mulungu. Holeka mu mwaka wuwo, mulecele lukulu kupanha no kuhadika mu migunda yenyu, hamba mulece kuyidumulila ne mizabibu yenyu.
5 Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kuhalaliza wuhemba wono wukwicindicila wuli wene yunji si yawuhadice, hamba kuhepa zabibu mu mizabibu yono si midumulile, hambi nha mwaka wono wunojeye muhangha wa mu migunda yenyu wuceheze.
6 Hamba yiwe migunda si yilimigwe mu mwaka wuwo, ninga yosuma kulavya ndigwa yono yomukwilenyi nyenye ne wawanda wenyu, ne wetumami wenyu, ne wajenzi wono mwikalane nawo,
7 ne mitugo zenyu, hamonga ne zinimu za mu mbago zono zili mwi'hala. Na co cose cono colelwa mumo mu muhangha, yowa yo ndigwa yenyu.”

Mwaka wo wuhogolwe

8 “Nanye muwale myaka mupunghati yiwe kali mupunghati, no wutowelele wayo, yowa myaka makumi mane ne 'cenda.
9 Ale, mwi'zuwa lye likumi lyo mwezi wo mupunghati, mwi'zuwa lyo wilamule, wutume munhu yatowe ndulele mu yisi yose.
10 Na mu nzila yiyo, kowa wakondya kuwusiwanula u mwaka we makumi mahano, no kwanza zo wulecelwe wa kuli wose wekazi wa mu yisi yose. Na mu mwaka wuwu we makumi mahano, sawo yo yose yono yowa yali yaguzwa, yinojeye yagomolelwe munya sawo, hamba kuli wana wawo. Sunga na muli wose mono yali yaguzwa no kuwa muwanda, yuyo nayo yanojeye yalecelwe yawuye kuli wanduguze.
11 Kanyhi mulecele lukulu kupanha no kuhadika mu migunda yenyu, hamba mulece kududula wuhemba wono wicindiciye wuli wene, hamba kuhepa zabibu mu mizabibu yono si midumulile.
12 Mwaka mututu wuwo wose, weze musiwanulwe kuli'nye. Nanye molya cono migunda yolela yili wene.

13 “Na mu mwaka wuwu, sawo yo yose yono yowa yali yaguzwa, yinojeye yagomolelwe munya sawo.
14 Na lono wuwe wukuguza mugunda kuli Mwiisilayeli muyago, hamba lono wuwe wukugula kuli mwene, wulece kunoza ko wukoci.
15 Langa wuguzi wayo, weze wiling'aniye no wuwale we nghango ye myaka yono mugunda wosuma kulavya ndudula yono yileceye mwaka wuwo wo wuhogolwe.
16 Na hono yiwe yikali yisigaye, myaka minji ku wuficila mwaka wuwo, ale no wuguzi wudahile kuwa wuwaha. Ninga hono yiwe yikali myaka micefu du yono yisigaye, no wuguzi nawo wowa wudodo, vyono u wuwale we ndudula wono mugunda wosuma kulavya, no wuguzi wayo nawo wowa vyo winha.
17 Holeka mulece kwikopa nyenye wose. Langa mweze muli cihuliko kuli Muwaha u Mulungu wenyu.”

Zi nghamu zono zili mu mwaka wo mupunghati

18 “Holeka munojeye mweze ne cihuliko kuli malajilizo go Muwaha Mulungu, hamonga no wulajilize wakwe. Nanye ho mwikale mupolile mu yisi.
19 Nayo yisi yolela mana gayo, nanye mosuma kuwa na vinhu vyose vyono mohila kulya. Kanyhi mokwikala mupolile.
20 Lunji yunji yakuwuza yawe kutya, alu ho cowa kulya cici mwidaha lilyo lyo mwaka wuwo wo mupunghati, lono hasina mugunda wo wose wono wohadikwa, hamba kuwa na wuhemba wono codudula.
21 Ninga Mulungu koyilugutiza yisi mu mwaka wo mutandatu, yisi ho yisume kulela ndigwa yono yokwila mu myaka myejete.
22 Na lono mowa mweza kupanha no kuhadika, mu mwaka wo munana, mowa mukali kulya ndigwa yono mwayidudula mu mwaka wo mutandatu. Na mu mwaka wuwo mojendelela kulya ndigwa yiyo sunga kolo mwadudula ndudula yo mwaka wuwo wo munana.”

Wukomboze we zi sawo

23 “Wulanjilile, wulece kuwuguziza lukulu mugunda wako, vyono yisi si yenyu nyenye, langa yangu nene no Mulungu. Anye nha muli wajenzi du mono mukomboleywe yisi ho muyitumamile.

24 “Holeka lono mugunda waguzwa, yinojeye ling'ani ya munya mugunda yiwe yikali yimanyice, vyono yo mwene mono kowukomboza kanyhi.
25 Na hono Mwiisilayeli yawe yaciwapa, sunga yadindimizwe no wuciwa wakwe, yawe kuwuguza mugunda wakwe, ale u nduguye we behi, yo yawukomboze mugunda wuwo.
26 No munhu hono yawe yasina nduguye we behi, mono kosuma kuwukomboza wuwo mugunda, ninga hanyuma, ho kasuma kutuga wulugutwe no kuwa ne vinhu vyono vikwiliye, vyo kuwukombozeza mweneco mugunda wuwo.
27 Na mu nghani yono yinha yiyi, munhu yuyo, yimunojeye yamugomolele mono yamuguziza, mu nghango ye myaka yono yisigaye, ku wuficila mwaka wo wuhogolwe, nayo ho yasume kuwusola mugunda wakwe kanyhi.
28 Ninga hono yawe yasina mapesa gono gokwila, go kuwukombozeza mugunda wakwe, hodu, mugunda wuwo, wikalaje du na mono yawugula, sunga mu mwaka wo wuhogolwe. Na mu mwaka wuwo wo wuhogolwe, mugunda wuwo wo wuhogolwe wugomolwe kuli munghalaco.

29 “Hono munhu yaguze kaya yakwe, yono yili mwi'kaya lyono lipilimwe ne lijinji, munhu yuyo, kosuma kuyikomboza yiyo kaya yakwe, mu mwaka monga, mu wulawa lizuwa lyono yayiguziza kaya yiyo.
30 Na hono yalece kuyikomboza mu cidaha cico, hodu kowa yakondya kuyajiliza, mono yayigulile kaya yiyo, hodu koyisawila lukulu, yowa sawo yakwe hamonga ne wanagwe. Si yosumika kanyhi kukombozwa hamba mu mwaka wo wuhogolwe.
31 Ninga zikaya zono zizenjigwe mwihala, azo zinozezwe nha vyono migunda yikunozezwa. U munya mugunda kowa yena ling'ani yo kuwukomboza mugunda wakwe. Kanyhi yowa ling'ani zigomolwe kuli wanghalaco mu mwaka we makumi mahano wo mwaka wo wuhogolwe.
32 Hamba nhavivyo, i Walawi wena ling'ani yo kuzikomboza mu mapango gogose zisawo zawo, zono zili mu makaya, zono wali wakondya kuziguza.
33 Na hono hawe hoyili kaya mu makaya gaga, yono yali yaguzwa no Mulawi, nayo kaya yiyo yiwe yikali nakombozwa, ale, kaya yiyo yinojeye yigomolwe kuli munghalaco mu mwaka wo wuhogolwe. Ku soko vyono zikaya zono zili mu makaya gawo, zono Walawi wezenjeye weneco, zizo nha sawo yawo du vilo vyose, mu wanhu wa Isilayeli.
34 Ninga mugunda wo wudimilo wawo, wono wupilime makaya ge Walawi, awo wulecele lukulu kuguzwa, hambi wuwo nha sawo yawo cibitilila.”

Nzila yo kuwakopesha i waciwa

35 “Na hono hawe hoyuli nduguyo Mwiisilayeli, mono mwikalane behi nayo, ho yawe yaciwape, mono si yakusuma hamba kwitaza mweneco, ale yikunojeye agwe wumutaze mu wanjile wakwe, wumunozeze nha vyono wali wumunozeze mwitumami wako, nayo holeka kosuma kujendelela kwikalana behi nagwe.
36 Kanyhi wulecele lukulu kumusolela cambuzi, langa wumogope Mulungu wako. Kanyhi wumulekaje yuyo nduguyo yekalane behi nagwe.
37 Wulece kumusolela cambuzi mu mapesa gono wumukopeshe, hamba kutuga cambuzi mu ndigwa yono wumuguzize.
38 Ane yo nene Muwaha, u Mulungu wenyu mono namuwusenyi ku yisi ya Misili, ho nimupelenyi yisi ya Kanani, kanyhi ho niwe Mulungu wenyu.”

Wulecelwe we wawanda

39 “Na hono nduguyo Mwiisilayeli mono mwikalane behi nayo, yawe yaciwapa hawaha, sunga yawe kwiguza kuli'gwe nha muwanda, ninga agwe wulece kumwitumya nha muwanda.
40 Langa kosuma kwikalana nagwe nha mwitumami du, nayo kokwitumamila sunga mu mwaka wo wuhogolwe wono wukutyatya.
41 Na mwidaha lilyo yikunojeye wumulecele mwene, hamonga ne wanagwe, yawuye kuli walezi wakwe na kuli wanduguze.
42 Weze kumanya vyono i wanhu wa Isilayeli, wo wetumami wo Muwaha Mulungu, vyono yo yawawusa mu yisi ya Misili. Holeka si wanojeye waguzwe ku wuwanda.
43 Holeka, wulece kumwiwonela, langa wumuhulice Mulungu wako.
44 Ninga langa hono muwe mwendile kuwa ne wawanda, ale, mosuma kuwagula mu wulawa mu makungugo gono gamupilimenyi.
45 Na hono mwende, mosuma kuwagula i wana we wajenzi, wono mwikalane nawo. Ko muno i wana walelelwa mu yisi yenyu, awo wadahile kuwa sawo yenyu.
46 Nanye mosuma kuwanoza wawe wuhazi kuli wana wenyu, wono wosuma kuwetumamila mu vilo vyose vyo wikale wawo. Ninga mulanjilile, mulece kuwewonela Waisilayeli wandugu zenyu.

47 “Nalunji u mujenzi mono mwikalane nagwe yagolipa, lono nduguyo Mwiisilayeli kawa muciwa, no kwiguza mweneco yawe muwanda kuli yuyo mujenzi, hamba lunji kuli yumonga wo lulelo lwe mhuga ya yuyo mujenzi.
48 Ninga hamba yawe yakondya kwiguza kuko, kowa yakali yena ling'ani yo kukombozwa na yumonga wa muli wanduguze wono vihanjile walezi,
49 hamba kukuye, hamba mwana wo kukuye, hamba yumonga wa muli wanduguze we behi, wosuma du kumukomboza. Hamba lunji mweneco hono yawe yakondya kutuga sawo, ale, kosuma kwikomboza mweneco.
50 Nayo yowa yimunojeye yalongane no munhu yuyo mono yali yamugulile, ho wawalane myaka, ku wulawilila mwi'pango lyono yeguziza mweneco kuli munhu yuyo, sunga mwi'pango lyo mwaka wo wuhogolwe wono wukutyatya. Ne nghombola yakwe, vyeze viling'aniye ne nghango yono viling'aniye no musahala wono wukuwa kulipwa ko mwitumami.
51 Hono yiwe yikali yisigaye myaka minji, sunga lono yoficila myaka makumi mahano yo wuhogolwe, hodu, kohaliza mu nghango ye cifo co wukombozwe wakwe mu sawo yo wuguzi wakwe.
52 Na hono yiwe yikali yisigaye myaka micefu sunga mu mwaka we sikukuu ye Jubilii, komuwalila munghango ye myaka yono yowa yikali yisigaye,
53 yiwe kotya, nha mono yali mwitumami wa mwaka monga monga. Ninga nayo muwaha wakwe, yalece kumwiwonela.
54 Na hono yalece kusuma kwikomboza mweneco, muli zinzila zizi zose, hodu, u mwene hamonga ne wanagwe, wanojeye walecelwe mu mwaka wo wuhogolwe.
55 Mumanye vyono, si yisumice kuli Mwiisilayeli yawe muwanda cibitilila. Hambi Waisilayeli nha wetumami wo Muwaha Mulungu. Mwene yawawusa mu yisi ya Misili, nayo yo Muwaha u Mulungu wawo.”


Walawi 26

Wulugutwe so wuwa ne cihuliko

(Kum 7:12-24; 28:1-14)

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakatya, “Mulece kwinozeza vihwani vye milungu yo wulambi, hamba mulece kwiyinulila cihwani ciseswe, hamba lisumbili lye libwe, hamba mabwe makakate, ho mugahembe. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu wenyu.
2 Holeka muzikamisaje zi sikukuu ze cidini. Kanyhi muhapelaje cogopo i hanhu hono mukuhembela 'ne. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.

3 “Hono muwukamise wulajilize wangu no kwibata malajilizo gangu.
4 Nane nomutonyezezenyi mvula mwi'daha lyono likwiliye, i mibici ho yilele mana, ne yisi nayo yilele nhwanga mbaha.
5 Ne nhwanga yenyu yokonjelezeka muno, nayo yomunozenyi mujendelele kududula du sunga na mu mapango go kuhepela zabibu. Nanye mowa mukali mujendeleye kuhepa zabibu, sunga na mwidaha lyono lyowa lyo kupanha no kuhadika wuhemba. Nanye movituga vyose vyono mukuvisaka ku wulye, kanyhi mokwikala mupolile mu yisi yenyu.

6 “Nane nomupelenyi mulamu mu yisi yenyu. Nanye mowa kugona mupolile, musina kanyhi mono komogofyenyi yaye. Nane noziwinga zinimu zose zono zina mbali ziwuce mu'si, kanyhi mowa musina wulugu wo wose mumo.
7 Nanye mowasumiza walugu wenyu wose.
8 Ne wanhu wahano du muli nyenye, wosuma kuwasumiza wanhu yigana, ne wanhu yigana limonga, wosuma kuwasumiza wanhu elufu yikumi.
9 Nane nomulugutizenyi, kanyhi nomupelenyi wana wenji. Nane nocikamisa cilagane cangu cono nalagana nanye.
10 Sunga ne nhwanga yenyu yowa mbaha yono yosuma kulimuka sunga ku mwaka. Hamba nhavivyo, si mosuma kuyimala yaye, langa mowa kuwumwaga wuhemba wuhuma, ho mutuje cilejeho mu madongha go kutajila wuhemba wupya.
11 Nane nowa hamonga nanye mwi Hema lyangu lyono lyelile, kanyhi hamba si nolomulecenyi yaye.
12 Nowa nili hamonga nanye. Nane nowa Mulungu wenyu, nanye mowa wanhu wangu.
13 Ane, no Muwaha Mulungu, u Mulungu wenyu, yo nene mono namuwusenyi mu yisi ya Misili, ho mulece kuwa wawanda kanyhi. Nghavibenanjiza vilungo vyono vyali vimwibatenyi. Nghamunozenyi mujende mugoloka.”

Wulipicizwe ku so wu wula cihuliko

(Kum 28:15-68)

14 Muwaha Mulungu yakutya, “Hono mulece kugebata malajilizo gangu, motuga wulipicizwe.
15 Na hono mulece kuwibata wulajilize wangu, hamonga ne malajilizo gangu, kanyhi mubene ne cilagane cono nacinoza nanye.
16 Mbeka molipicizwa. Ane noleta wongomezwe muli'nye. Noleta matamwa gono gasina miti, gono si gosumika no kuwulagulwe, hamonga ne mapungo gono gomunozenyi mubofule, no kumuwulajenyi. Kanyhi mohadika zimbeyu, ninga hamba si mokwambulila cinji, hambi walugu wenyu nha womusumizenyi, no kulya vyose vyono mwali mwalima.
17 Nane nomulecenyi, walugu wenyu wamusumizenyi, na wono wakumusulenyi, wo wolotema muli'nye. Nanye molokogofywa muno muno, sunga muwe manghanga caka, na lono hasina munhu mono yakumuwinjenyi.

18 “Ninga hono zizi zose ziwe zakondya kupuluta, nanye ho muwe mukali vyo muli bamba du, hamba si mukuhulika, nane nokonjeleza wulipicizwe wenyu kali mupunghati.
19 Nociwusa cibede cenyu, hamonga no wu wula cihuliko wenyu, na howa hasina mvula, ne yisi yenyu yonyala yiwe nghavu kotya cuma.
20 Sunga ne milimo yenyu yose yono mokwituma muhidimbaye, yowa nghani ya bwete du, hamba si mokwambulila cinji co cose, hambi yisi nha si yosuma kulela kanyhi, sunga ne mibici yenyu siyolela mikalo yaye.

21 “Na hono muwe mukali mujendeleye kwisumaniza nane, no wu wula cihuliko wenyu kuli'ne, nane nokonjeleza wulipicizwe wenyu kanyhi kali nghanghulo zinji mupunghati.
22 Noleta malimu makali muli'nye, nago gowawulaga i wana wenyu, gohodanga ne mitugo zenyu, no kumusigazenyi muli wacefu, ne zinzila zenyu zowa zisina munya kuzitumamila.
23 Ne magazo gaga gose, kolo geza kupuluta, nanye muwe vyo mukali du si mukuhulika 'ne yaye, kanyhi muwe mukali vyo mumbedegaye du,
24 ale, nane nomuzizenyi, nimulipicizenyi muno muno kali mupunghati si nha ha lulongozi kusoko yo wubi wenyu.
25 Nane noleta wulugu muli'nye, ho nimulipicizenyi so wubena cilagane wenyu. Na kolo mukwikongholela mu makaya genyu, ho mutuje mulamu, nane noleta matamwa gono gasina miti, gono si gosumika na ku wulagulwe yaye. Nanye baho ho modindimizizwa mwikombole kuli walugu wenyu.
26 Nane nocepula zindigwa zenyu, ne wacekulu yikumi, wose wosaka watelecele mukate mwi 'figwa limonga. Hanyuma wamugawilenyi cipimo co wanjize, nanye molya hamba kwiguta yaye.

27 “Na zizi zose kolo zapuluta, nanye muwe vyo mukali mujendeleye du kumbedegala, no kuwa musina cihuliko kuli'ne.
28 Nane nomuzizenyi mwi'kuwo lyangu, nokonjeleza wulipicizwe wenyu mu nzila mbi muno muno kali mupunghati si nha wa halulongozi.
29 Ne zinzala zenyu zowa mbaha, modabuka muno muno, nanye mowalya sunga ne wana wenyu we cilume na we cicekulu.
30 Nane nohahodanga sunga ne hanhu henyu ho kuhembela mucanyha ye matunda, nogatulanjiza ne makumbicilo genyu go kufusiza wuvumba, noyigumila ne mivimba yenyu, mucanyha ye vihwani vyenyu vye milungu, vyono vigwiye hasi, no mutima wangu womusulenyi.
31 Noganoza makaya genyu gawe matongo, nohahodanga hanhu henyu ho wuhembelo, nogalemela lukulu no kugabocela ne makumbiko genyu.
32 Nane noyihodanjila lukulu yisi yenyu, sunga ne walugu wenyu wono wakwikala mumo, wezine so wongomezwe wayo.
33 Noleta wulugu muli'nye, kanyhi nomwiyagazenyi mwiyagalile mu zi'si zo wujenzi. Ne yisi yenyu yowa matongo, ne makaya genyu golekwa gagwiye caka.

34 “Nayo yisi yosangalalila wucehe wono si mwali mudahile kuyipela.
35 Yosigala nyhiwa, nayo yoceheza, lono anye muli mu wuwinjilizwe, muli mu yisi ye walugu wenyu.
36 Nawo wayenyu wono wosigala, nowapela woga wuwaha muno, u woga wono wowanoza, hamba wahulice lizi lye mbeho du yikuputa lihamha, hodu wene wowa kutiza du. Nanye mowa kumangha nha munhu mono yakuwingwa ku wulugu, mogwa caka sunga na hono hasina mulugu mono yali behi nanye.
37 Kanyhi mowa kwikobala munhu no muyagwe na hono hasina munhu wo wose mono yakumutyatililenyi mu nyuma yenyu. Mowa musina vilungo vyo kwihoma ne walugu wenyu.
38 Nanye mofwila kuko ku wuwinjilizwe, momelwa ne yisi ye walugu wenyu.
39 Na wawo wacefu wa muli'nye, wono wowa womi mumo mu yisi ye walugu wenyu, nawo wolokwambuka bwete, ku soko yo wubi wenyu na so wubi we walezi wenyu.

40 “Ninga i wana wenyu wo wolonena u wubi wawo, no wubi we walezi wawo, vyono wesumaniza nane, wakambedegala,
41 wakeyambuza weneco, nane nghawaleka wabite ku wuwinjilizwe mu yisi ye walugu wawo. Ninga hanyuma, kolo i wana wenyu wakwinenenyheza no kuvumila wulipicizwe, so wubi wawo, hamonga no wubedegazi wawo,
42 nane ho nolocikumbucilila cilagane cangu kuli Yakobo na Isaka, hamonga na Abulahamu. Nane nolowusipula wihuwizo wangu wo kuwapela yisi wanhu wangu.
43 Hamba nhavivyo, yinojeye yisi yilekwe ne wanhu wayo, ho yisume kuwusangalalila wucehezwe wayo. Nawo wanhu, yowa yiwanojeye wavumile vyono walema wulajilize wangu hamonga ne malajilizo gangu.
44 Ninga hamba yiwe nhavivyo, lono wakali wali mu yisi ye walugu wawo, ane si nosuma kuwaleka hamba si nowongomeza yaye. Hambi nghani yiyo nha yocidumya cilagane cangu kuli wene, nane kumbe ho nili Muwaha, u Mulungu wawo.
45 Ninga ane nolocisipula cilagane cono nacinoza kuli walezi wawo, lono nalajilila vilungo vyangu kuli makungugo gose, mu nzila yo kuwawusa wanhu wangu ku Misili, ane, ho sume kuwa Mulungu wawo. Ane yo nene Muwaha Mulungu.”

46 Nawo wuwu, wo wulajilize hamonga ne malajilizo, gono Muwaha Mulungu yamulajiliza Musa mwi'tunda lya Sinayi, ho yawalonjele i wanhu wa Isilayeli.


Walawi 27

Wulajilize ku so vilavywa vya kuli Muwaha Mulungu

1 Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Musa,
2 “Wityatye, wono wityatizye ho yawalonjele wanhu wa Isilayeli. Hono munhu yalavywe cilavywa kuli Muwaha Mulungu, so wufisiliza wilahize wakwe. Munhu yuyo kosuma kukombozwa ne liwalo ali lye zipesa zono zikwitumamilwa mwi Hema lyono lyelile nha vyono nhavivyo.
3 Kuli munhu mulume mono yena myaka makumi mejete sunga makumi mutandatu, zeze fweza makumi mahano.
4 Mu nghango ye zipesa zono zikwitumamilwa mwi 'Hema lyono lyelile. Nayo munhu mututu we cicekulu, zeze fweza makumi madatu.
5 No mwana mudodo we cilume, mono yena myaka mihano sunga myaka makumi mejete, yiweze fweza makumi mejete. Nayo mwana mudodo we cicekulu, ayo zeze fweza yikumi limonga.
6 Kuli wana wagalika, u mwana we cilume, mono yakali naficila myaka mihano, ayo zeze fweza yihano. Nayo we cicekulu, zeze fweza yidatu.
7 Na hono munhu yawe yaficila kuciza myaka makumi mutandatu, kuli walume, zeze fweza yikumi ne 'hano. Na kuli we cicekulu, zeze fweza yikumi limonga.
8 Na hono munhu mono yelahize yawe muciwa, si yakusuma kulavya liwalo lyono kosakwa yalavye, hodu yamulete munhu yuyo kuli mukohani. Nayo mukohani yamucepulile liwalo, yamuwalile munhu yuyo liwalo yidodo lyono kosuma kulavya.

9 “Na hono wilahize wuwo, wuwe wilahize wa kuli mutugo wono wuvumiywe kumulavizya cilavywa Muwaha Mulungu, ale ne vilavywa vyose vyono munhu yakumulavizya Muwaha Mulungu, vyowa vyelile.
10 No munhu yuyo mono yelahize, si yimunojeye kanyhi yawukawanize mutugo wuwo no mutugo wunji. Na hono yejelele yanoze nhavivyo, hodu mitugo zizo zose vyono nyejete zowa sawo yo Muwaha Mulungu.
11 Langa hono wilahize wuwe wuli so mutugo wono mwavufywe, wono si wukuvumilwa wuwe cilavywa kuli Muwaha Mulungu, u munhu yuyo yawusolaje mutugo wuwo, yawutegule kuli mukohani.
12 Nayo mukohani kotemya wuguzi wayo, mu nghango yo wuswanu wayo wo muwili hono yijinile hamba yigandile, no wuguzi wayo vyo wowa winha.
13 Na hono munhu yuyo yasace yayikomboze, yimunojeye yalavye wuguzi wuwo kanyhi yonjeleze hamonga ne makumi mejete kwe 'gana yo wuguzi wayo.

14 “Na hono yunji yawe kuyisiwanulila kaya yakwe kuli Muwaha Mulungu, yinojeye mukohani yayitemezezye wuguzi wayo, mu nghango yo wuwe we kaya vyono yowa yinha, hono yikamile hamba yisakaye, no wuguzi wayo vyo wowa winha.
15 No munhu yuyo mono yayisiwanuliye kaya yakwe kuli Muwaha Mulungu, hono yawe yena wuhile wo kuyikomboza, ale yimunojeye yalavye wuguzi wuwo, kanyhi yonjeleze hamonga ne makumi mejete kwe 'gana yo wuguzi wayo.

16 “Na hono munhu yawusiwanulile mugunda wakwe kuli Muwaha Mulungu, wuguzi wayo wuwagwe mu nghango yo winji we mbeyu yono yohadikwa mu mugunda wuwo, zeze fweza yikumi, kwa kilo nyejete nyejete ze mbeyu ya shayili.
17 Ninga hono yawusiwanule kamonga du, lono u mwaka wo wuhogolwe wakondya kupuluta, baho langa, yanojeye yalavye wuguzi wose wo mugunda weneco.
18 Ninga langa hono yawusiwanule lono vilo vinji viwe vyakondya kupuluta ku wulawa ha wumalilicile wo mwaka wo wuhogolwe, ale mukohani yatemye wuguzi wayo, wuwe mu nghango ye myaka yono yisigaliye, sunga wuze wufice kanyhi mwaka we sikukuu ye Jubilii wono wukutyatya, ninga yacepule liwalo lye makumi mejete kwe'gana.
19 Na hono munhu mono kowa yawusiwanuye mugunda wakwe, nayo ho yasace kuwukomboza, ale, yalavye wuguzi wayo wose, kanyhi yonjeleze hamonga ne liwalo lye makumi mejete kwe 'gana.
20 Na hono yawuguze mugunda wuwo kuli munhu yunji, lono yakali si nawukomboza ku wulawa kuli Muwaha Mulungu, hodu munhu yuyo kowa yakondya kwajiliza ling'ani yakwe yo kuwukomboza kanyhi.
21 Kolo weza kufika mwaka wo wuhogolwe, mugunda wuwo hodu wowa weza sawo yo Muwaha Mulungu vilo vyose, nawo wowa sawo ye wakohani.

22 “Na hono munhu yawusiwanule mugunda wono nayo mweneco kumbe yawugulile,
23 ale mukohani yatemye wuguzi wayo mu nghango ye myaka yono yisigaliye sunga wufice mwaka wo wuhogolwe. Ninga munhu yuyo yimunojeye yalavye wuguzi wuwo baho baho du lwa kamonga. Ne zipesa zizo yo sawo yo Muwaha Mulungu.
24 Na mu mwaka wo wuhogolwe, mugunda wuwo wugomolwe kuli munghalaco, hamba kuli wanagwe.
25 No wuguzi wose weze wuli mu nghango ye mapesa gono gakwitumamilwa mwi 'Hema lyono lyelile.

26 “Ninga zinyana ze mitugo zinongozi kulelwa, azo zakondya kuwa sawo yo Muwaha Mulungu, holeka yunji yalece kwisiwanulila ciwe cilavywa cakwe ce lihewa. Nha cinji, ng'ombe, hamba nyana ye ngholo, hamba nyhinda, avyo vyose yo sawo yo Muwaha Mulungu.
27 Langa zinyana zinongozi kulelwa ze zinimu zono nyavufywe, azo zosuma kukombozwa mu nzila ye mapesa gono gakwitumamilwa mwi 'Hema lyono lyelile, hamonga ne nyonjeza ye makumi mejete kwe 'gana. Na hono yilece kukombozwa, ale yiguzwe kuli munhu wo wose mu wuguzi wa mu zipesa zono zikwitumamilwa mwi 'Hema lyono lyelile.

28 “Ninga munhu wowose si kosuma kuguza hamba kukomboza cinhu cono yamusiwanuliye Muwaha Mulungu, hono yawe munhu, hamba mutugo, hamba mugunda. Hambi avyo i visiwanulwe nha sawo yo Muwaha Mulungu vilo vyose.
29 No munhu wo wose mono yasiwanuywe ne wanhu, si kosuma kukombozwa, langa yanojeye yawulagwe du.
30 Ne zaka ya muli vyose vye vilimwa vyenyu, hono wuwe wuhemba, hamba mikalo, vivyo vyose yo sawo yo Muwaha Mulungu.
31 Na hono munhu yasace kuvikomboza cimonga ca muli vinhu vivyo, yowa yimunojeye yagule ko wuguzi wono wuling'aniye, kanyhi yonjeleze hamonga ne liwalo lye makumi mejete kwe 'gana.
32 Kanyhi, mutugo monga wa muli mitugo yikumi, ayo yo sawo yo Muwaha Mulungu. Na lono mukuwala zi mitugo zenyu, mweze kumanya vyono, muli mitugo yikumi, mutugo monga yo sawo yo Muwaha Mulungu.
33 Kanyhi mulanjilile, munya mitugo yalecele lukulu kuhagulila 'ne mutugo wono wutimbile, hamba yalece kuyikawa na yunji. Na hono yejelele yayikawe na yunji, hodu mitugo zizo zose vyono nyejete, zowa sawo yo Muwaha Mulungu, hamba si zosuma kanyhi kukombozwa yaye.”

34 Nago gaga go malajilizo gono Muwaha Mulungu, yamulajiliza Musa mwi'tunda lya Sinayi, ho yawalonjele i wanhu wa Isilayeli.
