Mwamulizo 16

Kuli za Hagali na Ishumayeli

1 Salayi u mugolece wa Abulamu, yali yakali namulelela Abulamu mwana. Ninga lulo, mwene Salayi, yali yena mwitumami we cicekulu munya Misili, ne litagwa lyakwe yacemwaga Hagali.
2 Ale, Salayi yakamulonjela mulume wakwe Abulamu yakatya, “Gwe mulume wangu, wumanyile vyono Mulungu yakali namhela wana. Holeka nghukunena, winjile kuli mwitumami wangu we cicekulu Hagali, na lunji yalandelela nane mwana.” Nayo Abulamu yakawuvumila u wulonje we mhuga yakwe Salayi.

3 Hodu Salayi yakawa kumukombola Abulamu yenjile kuli u mwitumami wakwe Hagali mu Misili, yawe mugolece wakwe. Nazo zizino zejela lono Abulamu yali yekala mu yisi ye Wakanani myaka yikumi.
4 Nayo Abulamu yakawa kumusola Hagali, nayo Hagali yakawa kudinghula 'nda. Ninga lulo baho Hagali yali yeza kuwona vyono yakondya kudinghula 'nda, hodu yakawa kwanza kumubedegala Salayi.
5 Ale, Salayi yakamutya Abulamu, “U wubi wono nghutendelwa na Hagali, ale, weze wako gwegwe. Hambi ane nha nakukombolela u mwitumami wangu Hagali, ninga alu, yali yeza kuwona vyono yatuga 'nda, hodu du, ane neza si nili cinji kanyhi ha meso gakwe! Ale du, u Muwaha Mulungu, yo koling'aniza hagati yetu na gwegwe!”
6 Ninga nayo Abulamu yakamutya Salayi, “Gwe mugolece wangu, ayu mwitumami wako, kanyhi yali mu miwoko yako. Holeka wudahile kumunozeza nha vyono wukusaka gwegwe du go mweneco.” Hodu Salayi yakawa kwanza kumukobyanza Hagali, sunga nhondo Hagali yakawa kutoloka.

7 Malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu yakamwagana Hagali mu nyika, hono howuli muvwilo we malenga, u muvwilo wono wuli mu nzila yono yibitile ku Shuli.
8 Malayika yakamuwuza Hagali yakutya, “Gwe Hagali go mwitumami wa Salayi, alu wukulawilila hayi, na kanyhi wukubita hayi?” Nayo Hagali yakatya, “Ane nghumutiza yaya Salayi.”
9 Nayo Malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu yakamutya Hagali, “Ale wumuwuyilaje yuyo nyina yako Salayi, ninga kolo wawuya, wumwitumamilaje ko winenenyhezi.”
10 Hamonga na zizo kanyhi, Malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Hagali yakatya, “Nane nolwinjifya muno muno u lulelo lwako, sunga kuwe kukamu no kuluwala kose, ku soko yo winji wawo.”
11 Hanyuma kanyhi yuyo Malayika wo Muwaha Mulungu yakamulonjela Hagali yakatya, “Agwe alu wina muda, na kolela mwana wa cilume, nagwe komucemela litagwa lya Ishumayeli. Hambi u Mulungu nha kawuwona u wugaye wako.
12 Nayo yuyo Ishumayeli, kokwikala nhe zindogowe za mu mbago. Nayo kowa mulugu kuli munhu wowose, no munhu wowose kowa mulugu wakwe. Nayo mu mikalile yakwe yose kowa yetemanuce ne wanduguze wose.”

13 Nayo Hagali yakehwesa yakatya, “Mwene mbeka nimuwona Mulungu, na vyo nghali nili mumi du?” Holeka Hagali yakamucemela litagwa u Mulungu mono vyaduwana nayo kuko, yakamutya, “U Mulungu mono kawonaga.”
14 Ayi yo soko yono yaleka lisima lililya, likacemwa Belilahayi-loyi. Ne lisima lilyo lili haciligati ye Kadeshi na Beledi.
15 Ale, Hagali yakamulelela Abulamu mwana wa cilume. Nayo Abulamu yakamucemela mwana yuyo litagwa lya Ishumayeli.
16 Nayo Abulamu yali yaficila myaka makumi munana na mutandatu, lono Hagali yamulelela Ishumayeli.
