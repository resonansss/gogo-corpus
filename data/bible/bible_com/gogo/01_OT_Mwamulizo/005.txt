Mwamulizo 5

I zindelo za Adamu

(1Zin 1:1-4)

1 Awuno wo wityatye we zindelwa za Adamu. Mwi'pango lyono Mulungu yamuwumbila u munhu, yamuwumba mu futa yakwe mweneco.
2 Nayo yawawumba u mulume no mucekulu, hanyuma yakawa kuwalugutiza, yakawacemela ne litagwa lya “Munhu.”
3 Na mwi'pango lono Adamu yali yaficila myaka yigana limonga na makumi madatu, vilo vyo wikale wakwe, yakatuga mwana we hwani yakwe mweneco, nayo yakamucemela litagwa lya Seti.
4 Yali yeza kukondya kumulela Seti, u mwene Adamu yakekala myaka magana munana, nayo yakawa yatuga wana wanji we cilume na we cicekulu.
5 Adamu yakafwa yena myaka magana yicenda na makumi madatu, yo wikale wakwe mono'nze.

6 Na lono Seti, yali yafisa myaka yigana limonga na mihano, yakamulela Enosi.
7 Nayo yaza kuwa yamulela Enosi, mwene Seti yakekala kanyhi myaka magana munana na mupunghati, nayo yakawa yena wana wanji we cilume na we cicekulu.
8 Nayo Seti yakafwa yena myaka magana yicenda na yikumi na myejete.
9 Na lono Enosi, yali yaficila myaka makumi yicenda, baho yakamulela Kenani.
10 Na lono yali yamulela Kenani, mwene Enosi, yakekala kanyhi myaka magana munana na yikumi na mihano, nayo yakawa na wana wanji we cilume na we cicekulu.
11 Nayo Enosi yakafwa yena myaka magana yicenda na mihano, yo wikale wakwe wa baha mono'nze.
12 Nayo Kenani, lono yali yaficila myaka makumi mupunghati, yakamulela Mahalaleli.
13 Nayo yali yeza kumulela Mahalaleli, mwene Kenani yakekala kanyhi myaka magana munana na makumi mane, nayo yakawa na wana wanji we cilume na we cicekulu.
14 Nayo Kenani yakafwa yena myaka magana yicenda ne 'kumi limonga, yo wikale wakwe wa mono'nze.
15 Nayo Mahalaleli, lono yali yaficila myaka makumi mutandatu na mihano, yakamulela Yaledi.
16 Nayo yali yeza kumulela Yaledi, mwene yakekala kanyhi myaka magana munana na makumi madatu, nayo yakawa yena wana wanji walume na we cicekulu.
17 Mahalaleli yakafwa yena myaka magana munana na makumi yicenda na mihano, yo wikale wakwe wa mono'nze.

18 Nayo Yaledi, lono yali yaficila myaka yigana limonga na makumi mutandatu na myejete, yakamulela Enoci.
19 Nayo yali yeza kumulela Enoci, mwene Yaledi yakekala kanyhi myaka magana munana, nayo yali yena wana wanji we cilume na we cicekulu.
20 Yaledi yakafwa yena myaka magana yicenda na makumi mutandatu na myejete, yo wikale wakwe wa mono'nze.
21 Nayo Enoci, lono yali yaficila myaka makumi mutandatu na mihano, yakamulela Metusela.
22 Nayo yali yeza kumulela Metusela, mwene Enoci, yakekala kanyhi myaka magana madatu, akuno vili hamonga no Mulungu. Nayo yakawa yena wana wanji we cilume na we cicekulu.
23 Nayo Enoci yekala myaka magana madatu na makumi mutandatu na mihano yose hamonga.
24 Nayo vikajendana no Mulungu mu vilo vyakwe vyose, hanyuma yakahama hamba kwijela kanyhi. Hambi u Mulungu nha yamusola.

25 Nayo Metusela, lono yali yaficila myaka yigana limonga na makumi munana na mupunghati, nayo yakamulela Lameci.
26 Nayo yali yeza kumulela Lameci, mwene Metusela yakekala kanyhi myaka magana mupunghati na makumi munana na myejete, nayo yakawa na wana wanji we cilume na we cicekulu.
27 Nayo Metusela yakafwa yena myaka magana yicenda na makumi mutandatu ne'cenda, yo wikale wakwe wa mono'nze.
28 Nayo Lameci, lono yali yeza kuficila myaka yigana limonga na makumi munana na myejete, nayo yakamulela mwana wa cilume.
29 No mwana yuyo, Lameci yakamucemela litagwa lya Noha, akuno yakutya, “U mwana yuyuno, yo kocisindamika mu milimo yetu mizito, yono cetuma kwe miwoko yetu, mono yisi yiyino yono u Mulungu yayilijita.”
30 Nayo Lameci yali yeza kumulela Noha, mwene Lameci yakekala kanyhi myaka magana mahano na makumi yicenda na mihano, nayo yakawa na wana wanji we cilume na we cicekulu.
31 Nayo Lameci yakafwa yena myaka magana mupunghati na makumi mupunghati na mupunghati, yo wikale wakwe wa mono'nze.
32 Nayo Noha, lono yali yaficila myaka magana mahano yo wikale wakwe, yakawalela wa Semu na Hamu na Yafeti.
